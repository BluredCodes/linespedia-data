In a deep slumber I see your face
A dream before my eyes
but then in sudden waking
the colors blur into reality

a reality more blissful 
then all my dreams combined

when you are neat life is wonderful
the pure white of caring
the deep reds of passion
melt with the star filled blue above us
to become this violet night??/??/00, -just like every other love poem the only thing I liked about this one enough to submit it was the color metaphor, just throwing it out there to see what you think
