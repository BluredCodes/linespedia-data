title: "How to Bake Bagels in Hell"

Take one-part dreams
One part hopes
Mash thoroughly 
Add denial
Mix until you have an equal
 consistency 
throughout
then mix in memories
and forget completely
(if you don’t forget all of it an undesirable bittersweet taste may result)
divide this mixture equally
between heart and soul
and repress at the temperature of loneliness
until it is warm and rotting
chill until it is resentful
and eat while it is still seethingMy favorite of the last few months, enjoy!
