Eyes dripping with blood
the goddess cries crimson red
it all fades to blackthis lost in a "most distrubing haiku" contest to someone who wrote about licking dirty dishes.... I was bitter.   I hope you enjoy it I just thought I would put it up for anyone who wanted to see my attempts at classical forms of poetry
