Let me kiss the tears from your eyes,
The pearls for tears you cry,

Let me feel your arms around me,
Feeling the sweet, gentle, caress
And having the days troubles drift away,

Let me taste your soft sweet lips like wine,

Let me run my hand through your soft downy hair,

Let me feel your great warmth near me,
Always near when life is cold,
Let your presence comfort me,
Like a raft in a sea of trouble,

And may our hearts become one.??/??/99, early early work, first attempt at non-rhyming way back in 8th grade.   It has a lot of cliches and a weak ending
