I care for you

               I dream of you
 
                              I miss you



I stab you
               
               I abandon you
   
                              I don't want you near me

Kiss me
                                            Stab me

KISS ME
STAB ME

                           Stab through me
                           feel me cringe
                           watch me bleed

and when my heart is about to fall
to be destroyed

                             when it looks as though I can 

stand
no
more



                        Stab deep into my heart
                          with love??/??/01, The kissing hurt more sometimes....
