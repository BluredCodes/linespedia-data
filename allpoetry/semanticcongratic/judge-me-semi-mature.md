Judge me
Let me fill the stereotypes
thriving in your rotting mind

I can be who you think I am

I can fit the mold you set for me

I can be the monster that you make me

Judge me
and I will fit your judgement

I will be the horny a*shole boy
the heartbreaker
the fag

judge me

I am nothing more then what you say
no one without your words,
cover me with your curses
your lies

judge me
let me drown in the catagorized hell 
you have created

let me be the sexist
the racist
the chauvinist

anything YOU think an angry white Christian boy can be

THAT is who I am

I am not a human being
I have no past
No emotions
dreams, hopes
I am just the world's b*tch to blame
When all minorities cry out
I am that b*tch and no one else

Judge me
Pass your eyes over me once
from a distance
And put me in the back of your mind
where fear and hatred grow


and then

Just leave 

me
