Run                       A    W   A    Y                   from me


                          run all over me


                                                             and don't look

back
 

      for then                                       you'll see the real me

                        the tears I have cried
                              they are yours
                           you gave them to me
                         and now

I cry them

                                                      for you


you never did                                                  see me

                          did you?

                                                                  look

back
                          you will not recognize me
 
                        when you saw me then
                      do you remember?
                           when we were happy?
           
                    You couldn't see my lips making words
of caring                                                          of love

                       just my tongue
                   the needle of your addiction

                            to
                    K                 T
                        I        A
                            S
                        S       T
                     I            I
                  N                  N
                G                      G

                        touching

                 can you see I have a heart
              it may have been broken and bruised
               but it was full of the purest care


                        for you

"you're good" you say

                            good for what?

for my heart

                 my dreams

                                my emotions

or good for my breasts

                                                          my tongue

things of rancid beauty

objects of your desire


                             I wanted to show you
                              my soul

but that is not what you lust to see


                                                                is it?

Someday I will be able to say
"you're no good"

                                   I will be held in arms much warmer
more caring then yours

                             He will taste my lips AND

the sweetness of my words

                         he will hold me passionately



in love                                          not merely in desireThis poem was written from the perspective of a girl friend of mine who all too often gets objectified.   Its a little long and redundant but I like the form and theme so I wanted to know what you think
