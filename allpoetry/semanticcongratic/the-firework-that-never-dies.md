I reach out and hold your gentle hand,
The fire lights the fuse,
I put my arm around you,
A crackle and a bang,
And off goes the streaming angel’s light,
Into the cold black of the night,
I touch each of your fingers,
The rocket touches heaven,
I run my hand through your soft hair,
A flash of lightning blue,
I kiss you on the forehead,
An explosion of a million colors,
You kiss me back and then,
The heavens and earth collide,
For this is the firework that never ends,
