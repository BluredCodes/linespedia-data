Kiss

As my lips touch yours,
My heart’s fire ignites,
And burns away my fears,
My troubles,
My worries,
My woes,
All melt away,
With your passion,
 With that oh so wonderful kiss,
The world turns to a blur,
And all is silent...
 Time just seems to stop,
With your lips to mine,
And mine to yours,
The world can touch us no morea classic, dedicated to a girl I have always wanted to kiss, one of the many but I am sure this is exactly what it will be like if it ever happens
