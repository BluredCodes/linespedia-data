The disenchanted

                    pornograhic wit of one who's lost
murmurs in my head

"her lips were pink, full, curved..."

but her eyes they were terrified

"her breasts were pink

full 

curved"

her body shivered  when I touched it, cold and lonely for lack of passionless



warmth


                                          where in my heart

does this desire come from?


                                              what chasm cries out

for a body

                                                               tobelostin

to
be
found
in

                         to make love to?

eyes filled with longing
with lust
whisper in my mind

SCREAMS

        of rancid beauty

"taste her and be whole"

"fill her and be whole"


and apples red as gold does glow
fall like cherries                                  from


the


sky-what part of your heart does desire come from?
