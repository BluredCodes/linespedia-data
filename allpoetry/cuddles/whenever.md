---
title: "~Whenever~"
writer: "CuDdLeS"
slug: "whenever"
source: "AllPoetry"

Whenever i am with you i am the happiest person alive.
When we are apart i miss you so much.
I get so used to being with you that when we are not together i get sad.
I know that i should not get so used to being with you,
But i cant help it.
You are the only thing that makes me happy.
So many people try to make me happy when i am with them but they can't,
only you can babe.
When we are not together i worry about you and how i dont want no one to hurt you.
I wish that we could be together every second of every day and we could then i would.
I would hold you in my arms and never let go...
Just like when we are together.
