What the fuck is everyones problem?
Why cant they just leave me at alone.
They all butt into my buisness,
and call me and my best friend dykes.
Even if we are it is no one elses buisness,
If we wanted people to know then we would tell them.
Until then they should all just butt out.
So many people talk shit,
Even those who i thought were my friends.
I guess that this is the time that i find out who my real friends are.
Which i guess that i have none but brittney and melissa, 
They knwo what is happening,
And they underatand.
Everyone else can go fuck off.
What the fuck is there problem it is my likfe not theres!!i wrote this when i was upset cause ppl were talkin shit about me. enjoy!!
