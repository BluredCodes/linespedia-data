All i seem to do is piss you off.
Why cant i make you happy?
I used to be able to make you happy every time that we tlaked.
It seems as tho everyone else can make you smile but not me.
God i am such shit.
I am such a bitch to everyone that ilove.
I guess that it is just my personality.
I cant change it.
I AM SHIT!!
I AM SORRY FOR RUINING YOURT LIFE!!
