I wish that I could tell people about me and you.
I really do love you and i want them to know how happy i am.
I know that they may not like it but i dont care.
Nothing will ever seperate us no matter what.
I love you more then anything, 
Nobody will ever change how i feel no matter how hard they try.
I know that we do not tell anyone becasue we are afraid that they will seperate us,
But you make me so happy and i want everyone to know.
I know that they may not like that we are gay,
But they will have to except it.
It is our lives not theres.
They should be happy for us,
I mean as long as we are happy that is all that should matter.
I love you babe, 
You know who you are.
