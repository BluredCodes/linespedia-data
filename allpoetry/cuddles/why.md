Why do so many people hurt me?
Why do so many people not care?
Why doesn't any body talk to me?
Why do they all call me names?
Why doesn't everyone just mind their own buisness?
Why don't they butt out of mine?
Why can't people love me?
Why do so many people hate me?
Why don't they tell me what i did wrong so i can fix it?
Why am i always the one who is apologizing when you do something wrong?
Why Why Why??
