---
title: "Should We Come Out??"
writer: "CuDdLeS"
slug: "should-we-come-out"
source: "AllPoetry"

I am not sure what i am,
All i know is that i am in love with another girl.
Nobody knows about us but one person.
I want to come out,
But i do not want to be seperated from her.
She means the world to me.
Some people may say that this is wrong and i should not be with her,
But i cant help what i feel,
Even if i could i would not.
Without her i do not know where i would be at right now.
We just started out as friends,
Then we feel in love.
Now we are going out.
What do you think,
Should we come out with our feelings??
