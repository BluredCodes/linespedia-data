i miss you so much when we are apart..
every time that we are not together i miss you..
i cant stand not being without you..
something is different about tonight tho..
i miss you more then ever.
i am not sure what it is..
i jsut have this feeling and i want to be with you..
i was to be with you and cuddle with you rite now.
i never want to let you go.. 
i love you bub and i cant wait till i see you again!!
