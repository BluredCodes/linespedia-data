i am telling my parents bout me and you today.
and i am not sure how they will react.
i hope that they accept it.
even if they dont it will not matter.
i will not change how i feel.
nothing will..
our love is to strong for anyone to ruin,
they will see that.
i am scared of how they will react.
i dont want to hurt them.
i have to tell them tho,
i have waited long enough,
they deserve to know.
it is my life tho so i hope taht they will realize that,
and just let me be happy..
WITH YOU!!this is not really a poem,
i wrote it in a hurry, i just felt like this when i was writing so i put it in words.
sorry if it dont make no sence to no one, but iot makes perfect sence to me..
