title: "~UnSuRe~"

I am so unsure about so many things,
But there is one thing that i am sure about.
That is my love for my friend.
She is the best thing that has ever happened to me.
She has helped me through the good and the bad.
I know that she will always be there for me no matter what.
I am glad that she is here or i know that i would be dead.
She has saved my life and i can not thank her enough.
I know that i should not be unsure about so many things,
But i can not help it.
I am so sick of being hurt,
So i just hurt people first so they cant hurt me.
There are so many things that i am unsure about,
How can i make my self be sure about things?This may not make sence, but it was just things that i was thinking at the time that i wrote it...
