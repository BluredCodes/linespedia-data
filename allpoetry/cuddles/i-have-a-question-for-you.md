---
title: "I Have A Question For You!!"
writer: "CuDdLeS"
slug: "i-have-a-question-for-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

You have stood by me through all my troubles.
Have i stood by you?
You cheared me up whenever i was depressed.
Did i ever chear you up?
You helped me with all of my problems.
Did i help you?
You have talked me out of suicide.
Have i talked you out of it?
You have showed me the many different paths that i can take in life?
Have i shown you any paths?
You helped me hold my head up high.
Did i help you hold your high?
You have made a HUGE difference in my life.
Have i made a difference in yours?
I really hope that i have helped you as much as you have helped you.
