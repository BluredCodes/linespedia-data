Why the fuck doesnt everyone just leave me alone. 
Why do they all have to mess with my life.
They all try to make me go insane.
And it is working.
They mess up my friendships,
Then i will mess up there faces. 
I hate everyone and i hope they all just die.
No actually i just want to die.
Then they will have to find someone else to talk shit about,
And they will know that i killed myself because of them!!
Then they will be sorry,
Sorry for not excepting me,
Sorrry for talkin all their shit,
Sorry for everything.
FUCK THE WORLD ANS ALL WHO LIVE IN IT!!!!!!!!
