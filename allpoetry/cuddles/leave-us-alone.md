i think that my ma knows about me and you.
she keeps askin me a lot of questions,
i do not tell her though,
i am to afraid that she will seperate us.
why cant her and everyone else just leave us alone and let us be happy??!!
cant they see that you are the only thing that makes me happy??
if they tear us apart i will commit suicide.. 
that is not a joke either.
i am on the verge of running away,
and taking you with me.
i am not sure where we will go,
or how we will get money...
all i know is that i will be with you and i will be happy!!
that is all that matters to me!
fuck what people say about us,
they will not be able to seperate us,
nothing will..
our love is to strong,
you are my sould mate bubba!!
screw everyone else,
you are all that matters to me!i wrote this when ppl started to find out about me and my bub! they are trying to seperate us but they have to realize that our love is to strong and that it will not work!! nothing will!!
