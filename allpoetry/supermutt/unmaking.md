What is it about you
That I can't go away?
What is it that's keeping me
That's making me stay?

Why can't I say no
When you ask me again
I just keep coming back
Like I love the sin
Again and again

((And all the while
Unknown to me
Behind my back, in secret
You were unmaking me))

My vision is clear now
My heart has grown cold
Weary from your desperate trials
It's you that's made me old

Gone separate roads
"The road not traveled"
That's the one I'll be taking
To redo what you unraveled

((And all the while
Unknown to me
Behind my back, in secret
You were unmaking me
You were unmaking me))February  2002,
