Catching the train
And collapsing in the seat
How could I imagine
Such a wonderful person I'd meet?

Six hours we spent
Chasing conversation down the track
But not a word since
And now there's no looking back

((I guess that's why I've never seen
A window in back of a train
To make it that much easier
To forget about the pain))

So many words have come and gone
I saw you just the other day
But then I saw another's name
Upon your lips begin to play

I bit my tongue, grit my teeth,
And forced a smiled at you
Then my courage welled up inside
And independence made a debut

((I guess that's why I've never seen
A window in back of a train
To make it that much easier
To forget about the pain))

So play the part of the pander
That you've come to play so well
And I'll play my part of the apathetic, aesthetic,
Silent little belle
