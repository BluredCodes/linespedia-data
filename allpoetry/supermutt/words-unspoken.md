Wrap myself in words unspoken;
The heavy cloak broods in shades of
Black.
It is heavy but
It is too cold without it.
Damned if you
Do,
Damned if you
Don't;
Why bother?
Because I'm that
Desperate.
But hey,
If it wasn't for bad luck
I wouldn't have any luck at all.
Might as well take what I can
Get.  And so
I'll wrap it just a little tighter around me,
Trudge a few more steps,
And take a seat in the night sky.
I blend in there.
Would you like to help me find
My rainbow cloak?
HE stole it, and I want it back
Because now I can't be seen
In my night sky anymore.
