title: "My Wings Have Failed Me"

The flurry, the flutter, of the fledgling's feathers
Indicates her readiness to fly
Overestimating her powers
She hops from the nest
Into
   the
      sparkling
          blue
              sky
And for a moment, she floats
Weightless for the time;
The glittering sun on her back,
Breathing in the white clouds;
But the moment is gone
And her wings fail
And she tumbles to the



                                 ground.
