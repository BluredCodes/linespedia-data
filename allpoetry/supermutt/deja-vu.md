Is this the beginning of a new story
Or just the never-ending?

I could swear I've been here before;
Deja vu? Could be.

Is this the beginning of a new story
Or just the never-ending?

One-track course, narrow but never straight
One winding turn after turn after turn

Is this the beginning of a new story
Or just the never-ending?

I stop running, to see what happens
And the ground moves under my feet

Is this the beginning of a new story
Or just the never-ending?

An unstoppable trek, longest journey ever
The only journey, that is

Is this the beginning of a new story
Or just the never-ending?
