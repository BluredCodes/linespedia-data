---
title: "//Side of the Road\\"
writer: "supermutt"
slug: "side-of-the-road"

This kind of weather
Seems like it's suited for a longer ride better
Evening's misting, I'm missing
A few little pieces of me
Must've left them there on the side of the road
When I stopped up, dropped up, picked that weary hitcher up

Asked where he was going, he replied "To the stars"
Made a little small talk
But we didn't get far
Before the car heated up, blew up, tripped, fell down
Passenger and I thrown to the side of the road
Bruised and shaken, shattered, forsaken

Didn't know entirely where I was
Wandered around aimlessly
Dazed and confused, battered and bruised
Uttering curses shamelessly
Saw him laying on the side of the road
Mouth open, eyes closed, heart exposed

Left him there on the side of the road
Laying on the side of the road

Now the sky is grey
The thunder's chasing me
Down this long ribbon of cement
Nothing before and after
Only bordered by the sides of the road
And the sneaking suspicion that I'm really in for it this time...February 2002,
