The first time you asked
What my talents are
I couldn't think of a single thing
Besides something so bizarre

"It's crazy," you thought,
"You can't do that"
Well you saw it happen,
You saw me at bat

In our crazy little waltz of love
With the steps so foreign and unknown
I was so busy watching out for your toes
I forgot, and tripped over my own

So you see how it is,
This little endowment of mine,
It's got me going crazy,
Always trying to refine

As for my talent,
I have a certain flair
For breaking my own heart
Lindsey Nunn, self-breaker, extraordinaire
