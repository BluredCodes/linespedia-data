title: "Don't blame me"
writer: "allagory"

Look at me
look at me when you talk to me

Is it arrogance
Is it arrogance if i am smarter than you

Is it conceit
Is it concetited if I am better looking than you

Is it pride
Is it prideful if I am too good for you

Am I a jerk
Am I a jerk if I turn you down

Why are you angry
why are you angry if you really know

You see
you really see
