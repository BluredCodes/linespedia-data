title: "The last Time ( I Impassioned you)"

When last I thrilled your wanting soul,
And impassioned you beyond control.
Burning brighter than the sun,
Two of us becoming one.
My love I find to my regret,
That life goes on and I forget.
Have we grown so far apart?
That I no longer touch your heart?

Toil and save and build and grow,
Where love does the passion go?
That secret sadness in her eyes,
Does that come when passion dies?
If I reach my hand through the loveless air?
Will you reach in turn and take it there?
I fear it might fall to my side,
Unheld because our love has died.

Across a room we've known for years
Sits two strangers with their fears,
Cast apart and made afraid.
By the love they never made.
Grant me strength to loose these chains,
That I might find your love remains.
That love remove all sense of doubt,
And neither ever be without.

I bless you darling with my kiss,
And pledge my very life on this.
That I will love you every day,
Til forever come what may.
Oh sweet forever angel mine,
That age just like the sweetest wine.
This my heart found to be true,
Last time I impassioned you.For Bonnie (Just because)LMAO@myself again.
