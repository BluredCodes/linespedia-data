Some dreams are so very bizzare,
Or maybe its the way I am.
I dreamed they "came" from near and far,
To the naked poetry jam.
The rhymes were few, But hey! the veiw,
Was all you could desire.
The night was right and oh the sight,
Did so much to inspire.

Of doodiebutts and flagpole mutts,
And panties for a hat.
And being wary of the pp faeiry,
Who wants to shower like that?
We laughed and sang, and the Goddess brang,
Us all a little snack.
For on her arms were lucky charms,
Someone had given back.

Now you might say, uhuh no way,
But I tell you I was there!
The poets all came out to play 
And all of them were bare,
It went so fast but I saw it pass,
Like a lucious candy gram.
And I saw some of the sweetest ass,
