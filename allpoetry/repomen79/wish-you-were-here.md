title: "Wish you were here"

Within this aching empty night,
I dream of laughter love and light.
come fair flower, I bid you bloom,
Beside me in this empty room.
Come seal my sorrow with tender kiss,
Our every moment lead to this.
Dream and drama, toil and tear,
Oh my love to have you here.
Was there just a bit of passion in the way you spoke my name?
Did you love in your own fashion, hoping I would do the same?
The pattern of the lives we weave,
Binds our hearts and I believe,
Mine will always hold your's dear,
How I wish that you were here.

A man must kneel,
Before he knows.
The tender feel,
Of a yellow rose.
The silken touch of Glory and all possibility.
The great and endless story, fragrant of eternity.
The tenderest reflection that a mortal man can feel,
Who finds on close inspection that his every hope is real.
The wanting like a fire in the vein,
The twilight of all passions til only you remain.
Desolation, in the absence of a tear,
The only consolation is to wish that you were here.
Treasure all the visions that I keep,
Waking up to find I have been crying in my sleep.Dedicated to: who ever it speaks to LOL@myself
