Night is dark and rain is wet,
And taters eyes don't see.
First we get, and then forget,
Its all the same you see.
We tear our hair in deep despair,
And eat when we are hurt.
The first to stare, is the last to care,
We're all Ernie's to someones Burt.

Phones don't ring, nor ovens bake,
Regardless of the label.
The loving thing is the best to make,
We do it when we're able.
To bad shoes, just come in twos,
A third would seem more just.
When flapping lips just plain refuse,
To shut up when they must.

Bought the car, and sold the farm,
At least this time around.
The other way would sure alarm,
Some quiet peice of ground.
Heard the news and paid my dues,
Could feel my nose get brown.
Refuse service, serve as refuse,
Its enough to bring you down.HI EVERYONE!!!!!!!HUGS TO ALL.........
