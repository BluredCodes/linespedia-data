Let come a day of jubillee,
Let all who toil be set free.
This day the fearful doubting stops.
Proclaim it from the mountain tops.
For He is Lord and does abide.
May laughter walk here at your side.

Good women, lay your burden down.
Go don the snow white wedding gown,
For in this tiome of jubillee,
Your every hope shall come to be.
That true love find you sanctified,
May laughter walk here at your side.

You Who've faced the breath of hell.
Heaven knew your name as well,
Your patiant father bids you still,
To shelter in his blessed will.
And live the hope you always hide,
That laughter may walk at your side.

Oh futile fears of human kind,
Why do you run so far to find?
That all you love and hope and care,
Lies safe within your saviors care.
He waits to free you from your pride,
That laughter may walk at your side.

Every month, you don't forget,
You pay your bills and meet your debt.
Your credit card will not go through,
When the bill for sin falls due.
For this our king was crucified,
That laughter might walk at your side.For Tamara 88 (a DOG???????)
