The light was dizzy serenade, 
Of silken silver blue,
And all I was did blurr and fade,
Til there was only you.
The very breath of paradise,
Did shine as treasure will,
And found me lost within your eyes,
My love as time stood still

Like children we were innocent,
Our hearts could do no wrong.
We followed where our passions went,
And trusted all along.
It seemed so fine, like sweetest wine,
As love did over fill.
To know that you were ever mine,
My love as time stood still.

Let silk caress, and snow white dress,
As angels walk beside.
And tenderness and loving bless,
This radiant new bride.
I take your part and hold your heart,
From this hour forth until.
Just as Ive held it from the start,
My love when time stood still.For Tilly May Just because shes nice to me.
