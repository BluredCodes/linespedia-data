Take me to a place of laughter and light,
Make me a dream in the dead of night,
Sing in my sadness, a sweet lullabye,
That I not remember the fire must die.
All of my issues, and every desire
Find joy in the comfort that your words inspire.
I make my wishs on a poetry star,
And send my love wherever you are.
I could not leave, my home is here.
I've never held so many dear.
This planet darkens with despair,
And there are few to comfort there.
In the tide of deception the real must hide,
In the place where the poets hearts abide.
Where goes the dreams that men did share?
Where is the truth in this affair?
Who shall speak of the things we must?
In that we all return to dust.
Speak out and let your heart be known,
