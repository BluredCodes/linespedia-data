The spirals wind the weave behind,
The tapestry we are.
The circle stairway that defined,
The gulf between the stars.
The bridge between the now and then,
The random and exact.
The lives and deaths of mortal men,
In fiction and in fact.

Pharohs In their golden hour,
Set spirals into stone.
That men beware the dreadful power,
Of the terrible unknown.
Down ever down the spiral went,
Up ever up the way.
From pyramid to beggers tent,
The spiral Seemed to say,

Between the things that truley are,
And the things we wish that might.
Within the spiral, near and far,
We journey to the light.
That every joy of earth and sky,
And every dream and prayer.
Will live once more and never die,
