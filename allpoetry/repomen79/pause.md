Fear not most graceful loving friend,
Your light will never die.
For love is ever without end,
And loves the reason why.
Oh for the beauty of your sorrowing heart,
Most tender soul, don't weep.
The love you gave me from the start,
Is the love I'll always keep.

Had a dream of a stream,And a river ran through,
And I heard the island cry,
I woke in the night and I called for you,
As the traffic hurried by.
Felt like a gear just lying here,
As the machine hummed in the night.
And I wept the sad and single tear,
Kept hidden from your sight.

Hear me when I cry to thee,
For love does so amaze.
You bless the very soul of me,
In all your loving ways.
Beyond my window, evening falls,
We are, and then are not.
Within this heart, your poetry calls,
I love you a whole lot.Its all good angel.
