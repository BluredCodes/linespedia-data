Blameless you
Idiot me
With all this animosity
You know you lie
At night I cry
You know it’s not the truth

You get others to believe
Because you’re the adult
You’ll tell them anything,
Even that I’m in a cult

But you know it’s not true
And I didn’t even have a clue
Up until this moment-
And now I know

Maybe you’re jealous,
Maybe you’re insane
But let me tell you,
I’m not taking this blame!

I’m sick and tired of your games
And I wish that you would stop calling me names
And this all happens behind my back,
You know you need to stop- but how?
Let’s backtrack

You as a child had the same abuse
Your mother and father were into drug use
You didn’t have a person to talk to
It was hard for you as a person, but still grew
Although this wasn’t the person God knew
It wasn’t supposed to be you.

Now isn’t it true? 
That you couldn’t be you?
Do you want me to end up the same?
Playing the same stupid mind games?
And if you do, then you’re just sick,
Because I’m losing friends over it.
You foolish fuckers,
You know I’m not a sucker!
So stop it now or you’re going down.Maybe a little too bubble-gum sounding at the end. Some of the in-between stuff i really how it turned out.
