Cold….
Laughter fills the room
But I cannot see them laughing
Or what they laugh about.
I wish they would stop
Stop the happiness
Happiness that I just cannot comprehend
The touch of my hand is cold

Nothing needs me
Nothing wants me
Nothing you can do for me
Nothing I can do for you
And I don’t want you

Stay out of my home I shout
But no one can hear
No one can hear the sound of my heart
Shouting out
Shouting and shouting
LEAVE! LEAVE! 
But they cannot hear me
Am I also mute?
Is it so that I cannot speak?
I utter words but they cannot hear

I look around the room for something to hold
But I see nothing, 
Only darkness
I wish someone would turn on a light

The voices soften to a mere whisper
A whisper I cannot understand
Raspy and quick
No comprehension

I ask them what they are whispering about
But they do not answer
They will not answer my commands

LEAVE! I shout. LEAVE!
But they do not.
They stay as they are.
And I stay where I am.
