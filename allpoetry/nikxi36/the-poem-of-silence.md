Crisp cool fresh
And the milk runs dry and the milk runs dry
You look at my with your pretty eyes
And they milk turns cold and the milk turns cold
Crushing, slushing, brushing
And we all turn awayI'm not sure how anyone will react to this since it's very abstract. If you don't understand it and would like some clarificaton you can ask me!
