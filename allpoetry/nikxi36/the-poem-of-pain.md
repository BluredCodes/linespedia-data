Scrapes and bruises
Black and blue- ugly too
And you weep and your cheap
Cry your heart out!
The sun will set soon
So why do you worry so?!

Mental, physical, consciously quizzical
You weep and your cheap
So cry your heart out because no one is looking
anymoreThe poem is supposed to seem like the person who is saying it is recieving the pain through the words, but it's also supposed to bestow pain on the listener. Keep that in mind! ;)
