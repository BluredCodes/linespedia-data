I look to you for a higher learning
A learning i can only learn from you
you teach me through deep tones
and it almost scares me but
at the same time, lures me even closer.
Your eyes captivate me, 
i hear your heart and mine tries to repeat it's rhythm
you...captivate me


you...captivate me
