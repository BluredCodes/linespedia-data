Look Into My Hazel Eyes

    

   Look into my hazel eyes
   and tell me what you see.
  
   Do my eyes tell you of my
   thoughts an desires untold?
   Do they dance in merriment?
   Or do they reflect a stormy scene?

   My eyes mirror the reflection
   of what I'm going through
   All my feelings an emotions
   Come over me
  
   And if you really look 
   you will see.
   My eyes cry tears of sorrow an regrets.

   To know what state of mind
   I'm in, look into my eyes.

   My eyes bore into your eyes deeply.
   Seeking your soul an secrets.
   My eyes open the way.

   They say your eyes are the 
   Gateway to your soul.

   Look into my hazel eyes
   And tell me what you see.
