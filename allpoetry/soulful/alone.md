Alone
   


   Have you ever felt really alone?
   I mean so comletely alone.
   where all you hear is your own breathing.
   and an ache starts to swell up inside you.


   All you really need is for someone
   to really hold you, and listen to you.
   To kiss you so deeply that nothing else matters.
   Needing someone to say that you are
    their everything.
  

   Ever feel really alone?
   And all you need is a hug.
   Ever feel really alone?
  Hoping that one day someone
   will say "i care"
  
   Ever feel really alone?
   I mean have you ever felt really alone??
   So alone that it hurts?
   have you?
