I've looked at you
    a million times
    But right now
    this very minute
    I feel I'm looking
    at you for the
    very first time
    I can't believe
    what i see
    As I look
    into your eyes
    and see the rage
    The look on your face
    is easy to read
    Why must you inflicked
    this pain on me?
    And I ask what 
    have I done?
    Do you in some way
    enjoy hurting me?
    where did the person
    I knew an loved go?
