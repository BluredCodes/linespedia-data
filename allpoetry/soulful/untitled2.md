I hear the rain falling outside
   Pitter patter,pitter patter.
   Like it is knocking to come in.
   I already feel the stinging of tears.
   As I scream into my pillow
   Silently but I scream.
   While tears flow from my eyes 
   like a waterfall.
   And my sobs rack my body
   with shivers and shakes.
   I pull the covers up to my neck
   and scream once more into my pillow
   I thought I felt you reaching for me
   but when I turn, you are not there
   Just an empty place beside me 
   where i lay
   My ears ache to hear you
   call my name
   No more ' I love you's '
   no more kisses
   There is an emptiness here
   that will never go away
   I long to see the sight
   of you ,sometimes I think
   I smell you, but when
   I turn you are not there
   My eyes mist up and
   I think I see you there
   then i blink an you are not
   Was it my heart playing tricks on me
   Oh yes it was
   I'm sorry to say it was
   I cant see you,feel you
   hold you, or hear you anymore
   I scream but this time a little louder
   This time an i let the teardrops fall
    I hug my pillow to me tightly
   As my sobs rack my body
   OH yes i let the rain in
