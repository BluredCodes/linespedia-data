Sex
  


   There is all kinds of sex
   The strong bond sex
   with all the love
   involved
   and makes you want
   to lay in the ectasy
   then there is the sex
   that you want
   to wash an scrub off
   in the shower
   the filth of it
   makes your skin crawl
   Then there is best friend sex
   where you never have
   to actually follow
   through on it if you
   don't want to
   Its never ending
   Everyone enjoys sex
   There is all kinds of sex
   So I say sex on people
