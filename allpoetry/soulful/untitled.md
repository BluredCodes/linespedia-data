She sat quietly cowering against
    The harsh words an angry voice.
    the screaming rang in her ears.
    No other sound could penetrate

    The horrific mas of shouts.
    The tears finally came staining
    Her face,soaking her hair,her clothes.
    She wanted to help but herself
    But could not
    
    And with each blow she lost
    Part of the innocence
     Of her womanhood.
