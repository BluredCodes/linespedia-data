Is it true

    
     Is it true what I heard
     That everytime a bell chimes
     An angel has earned its wings?

    
     Is it true what i heard
     that with evry tear shed
     An angel is born?
    
     
     I just heard a tinkling of a bell
      Maybe there is a  set of wings awarded.
    And I just cried my tears was there
      An angel just born was it a girl or a boy?
