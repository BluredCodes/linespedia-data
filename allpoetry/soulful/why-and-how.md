Here I am again
   In my hiding spot
   It's so dark an quiet in here
   No yelling do I hear
   I feel no hurt in here
   Ive locked the door
   So the pain can't get in
   I sit an wrap my arms tight
   Around myself
   It's so peaceful in here
   I begin to breathe
   Oh! But wait
   There is were I made the mistake
   I see a tiny light
   There is banging on the door
   I begin to hear the screams
   The hurt is causing an ache
   Why and How
   Why do you do it?
   How do you do it?
   Why do you keep invading
   my hiding spot?
   How do you keep invading
   my hiding spot?
   Why do you keep hurting me?
   

  Maybe next time I'll get a dead bolt!
