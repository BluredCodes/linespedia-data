To Rest

  She sleeps in a little windswept
  Graveyard on a hill.
  Next to strangers never 
  met in life.
  But she lives on in
  the fabric of the many
  lives she cherished
  and I believe enobled.
  And with something more
  than a wistful longing
  I believe that she
  lives and works in
  another of the far flung
  empires over which
  the Creator rules
  It is in the dimension 
  of that empire
  that grief comes
 tremutously to rest.
