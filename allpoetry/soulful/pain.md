Pain

 I'm struggling to get up now.
 After falling so hard.
 I feel as if I am fighting
 a terrible storm.
 Can no one see the tears
 behind my smile?
 Can you hear the cries 
 I silently hold back?
 When I say " I'm ok",
 can't you still feel the pain?
 Of things I won't bother
 you with in vain.
 My tears still get in the way,
 An my heart lies heavy with pain.
