Rain

 As I sit by my window
 Watching the rain fall
 I see the drops slide on
 The window pane
 And let my tears fall
 My heart slowly breaks
 As the memories flood back
 The hurtful words I still
 Hear ringing in my ears
 The look in your eyes
 Say it all
 I turn an walk away
 As my heart shatters in two
 I wrap my arms around
 Me an let my tears 
 Slide down my face
 And watch
 The rain fall
