Hands


  Hands can do so much
  from a simple carress  
  to holding a tiny hand
  Hands can say so much
  from i love you 
  to thank you
  
  Hands can be as gentle
  as a lamb
  they can also cause 
  pain an hate
    

  Our two tiny instruments
  can do so much
  what we choose to do
  with those hands
  shows who we are
