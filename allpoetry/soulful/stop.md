Stop
  

    Stop this I scream
    I beg you please
    Stop the pain
    The hurtful words
    You fling in the air
        like rocks
      
    The tears will
      Not stop
    Your words cut
    Like a knife
   I don't ask
     for this
  
   I never dreamed
   the dreams I
   Once had would 
     be shattered
   
   With just the 
    touch of you
  You have taken
  a part of me
   Why are lauching at me?
   
   Your like some crazed
    Caged animal
   One that feds
    upon my Soul
  My Soul is dying

   You have shattered
      my innocence
    Stop this I say
   I'm down on my knees
   
    Head bowed
    Hands clasped
   Will this nightmare
     ever cease
   I am already bleeding
   
   Don't you see?
    Stop this I say
     I beg you please
        
        STOP
