Come my love
And we shall
Explore the mountains
And see what we can see
We'll take long walks in the woods
We'll explore the rocks trees and water falls
We'll make love on a bed of leaves and moss
By the gurgling stream
We'll swim naked in the streams
Come my love and.....
Explore with me all that Mother Nature has to give.
