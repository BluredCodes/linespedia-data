What I would give to be a Eagle
and fly on the winds of time
To fly on the winds of past present and future
The Eagle is timeless and majestic and held in aww
To some its just another bird....
To some its majesty and beauty is a symbol of freedom and peace
But to me its my totem
The Eagle is my spirit guide to another world
I am able to fly the winds of time upon its wings
Along with brother wolf I am guided through this world
And into the world to come
Where I  will find peace beauty and freedom to be what I want to be
