What would you do, if I told you
I am Black Magic Woman
Would you call we witch or soceress
Would you run from my presence
screaming into the night
For I am Black Magic Woman
and I want you under my spell
I want you to love me and crave me
As I love and crave you
Come and let me weave a spell of love 
as we dance around the camp fire
Let me be your lover tonight
Come my love
and let's weave that spell of love
that will last forever and a day
For I am Black Magic Woman
