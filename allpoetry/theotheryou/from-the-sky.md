---
title: "From The Sky"
writer: "TheOtherYou"
slug: "from-the-sky"
source: "AllPoetry"

They push you around and they shove you down
And then they make you feel like you're nothing
They hold you back from everything you love
And then they strip you of your dignity and spit on your name
They melt you down and turn you inside out
And then they make you hate them forever
They force you to be exactly like them
And then they realize that they're not in control anymore

It makes you as sick as it makes me
I know because i can see it in your eyes
And i can hear it in your words
So let the rain of your anger fall from the sky

They act like you owe them your life and your blood
And then they don't appreciate what you give them
They think that you will shut up and take it
And then they tell you that you're not strong enough for yourself
They walk with their heads so high and so proud
And then they look down if you lift your eyes
They snap your bones in pieces and break you
And then they tell you how wrong you are and it's your fault

It makes you as sick as it makes me
I know because i can see it in your eyes
And i can hear it in your words
So let the rain of your anger fall from the sky

So let them know with raging fists and flaming guns how you feel!

It makes you as sick as it makes me
I know because i can see it in your eyes
And i can hear it in your words
So let the rain of your anger fall from the sky
