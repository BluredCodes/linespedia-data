this poem was not directed towards anyone. i just had some rage in me at the time and well what better to do than write it out. hope you enjoy




If i were to drift away
far across the sea,
Would i still matter to you?,
would you still have love for me

Well the way i felt last night
was as if you would love it so,
Now im wondering if your love was true
i guess ill never know

How many lies have you told me?
have you gone back on your word?
" oh you can trust me with anything"
those words are so obserd

I loved you with everything i had
i gave all i had to give
All i wanted was to make you happy
give you a great life to live

But you screwed it all up
you want all the pleasure for yourself
What i wasnt good enough?
you had to go to everyone else

But you will feel the pain
you will surely see
That never again will you have
a man thats like me.
