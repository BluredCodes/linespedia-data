I stare deep into the monnlight sky
wishing you were here.

I hold my pillow tight and close to me at night
wishing it was your body.

I regret not calling you at the times I could have
I wish i was better at it.

Out of all those wishes , none of them has come true. so far the only thing that has caem true was when i wished for the girl of my dreams, and i suddenly found you.
