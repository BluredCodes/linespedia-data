Last night i dreamt of a girl
who sent chills right down my spine,
a girl who really cares
and is willing to put in the time

One that makes me smile
even when shes not around,
One that brings my spirits up
when i am feeling down

A girl whos beauty is incredible
and appealing to my eyes,
The thought that good people are still out there
she has made me realize

The brightness of her smile
her perfect size hips,
The sparkle in her eyes
the softness of her lips

The warmth when im around her
only explainable by the touch,
All the little things she does
to me mean so very much

This girl that i have dreamt of
is unlike any girl i ever knew,
well in case you havent noticed
last night i dreamt of you.
