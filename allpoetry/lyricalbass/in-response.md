ok this poem was written in response to a poem written to me. maybe  behind the smile would post the poem she wrote to me so you all can read hers and then come to mine. well along with the reading...........

If there was a day i was sad, just the thought of you would comfort me
And if there was a day that i was mad, just be you and ill be happy
If at times you feel lost. i wouldnt stop looking till i found you
And if a person should cover my eyes, i would peel right through
If you ever lied to me, sure it would be the same
And if you were truly sorry, then i wouldnt have a soul to blame
If we ever get into a fight, i would surely accept your apology
And if we had never met before i would probably be out looking for me
When ever im around you, happy is how i stay
If you go through some bad times, ill try to comfort you in every way
If i were to ever make you cry , i would gently wipe away your tears
And if we stay together, i will be yours for endless years
Not a soul can steal me from you, because your i will stay
And if you feel helpless without me, i will surely keep you sane
If the time comes for you to be mad at me , and you let out an attack
In  way i would be happy because you finally fought back
But i would feel bad inside cause i made you feel that way
And i just hope i dont bring you to that point , on any given day
And well if you said that you love me, you want to know if ill say it back
There is only one way to find out, i guess youll have to wait for the answer to that
