The secrets kept inside
are putting me to the test,
I sit at night and think about them
the thoughts disturb my rest.

My close friend's sister
my ex-girlfriends friend,
Knowing that in some sort of way
Part of thier life has come to an end.

I can't say anything to anyone
People have trust in me,
I can't say it to my girlfriend
not even my family.

The people that this involves
have no idea that i know,
These secrets I can't help in anyway
I have to let that pain go.

I don't want to be around
when my close friend finds out,
I'm sure he is going to loose his mind
I'm sure without a doubt.

But I will be there to help him through
help him ease the pain,
I want to take some stress off his back
and just leave some to remain.

My ex-girlfriends best friend
is also one of mine,
And we never thought a thing like this
could come at any time.

I have'nt been able to talk to her
we have'nt been able to get in touch,
But i want to be there to talk her through
I care so very much.

The secrets kept inside
are putting me to the test,
Some emotions I have dealt with
I just have to cope with the rest.
