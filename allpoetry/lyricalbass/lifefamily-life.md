The anger and the sadness
the suffrage and the pain,
All the questions have been changed
yet the questions remain the same.

Just when you thought you knew
the right path to take,
Troubles come amongst you
some you cant escape.

Everyone experiences obstacles
thats just the way it is,
But never would i imagine
people can cause as much pain as this.

The yelling, The cursing
the calling of the names,
It's not the actions, but it is the words
that cause all of the pain.

Instead of taking out the time
to hear things through from me,
They just assume everything i do is wrong
they dont open thier eyes to see.

Each and every word from them
effects me throughout the day,
It follows me wherever i go
it will not go away.

"I'M trying to help you"
-"Leave me alone get away",
"Your suggestions are not helping
but hurting me in everyway".

I dont care to listen to what you have to tell
I'll pay no attention at all,
You have eaten away at my insides
and made me feel so small.

All i ask is to let me be
and i dont ask much of you,
Please just respect what i have to say
and let me go about doing what i do.
