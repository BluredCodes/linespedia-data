The pain inside me is cause
by stress, anger, and hate,
All of this pain is mixed
into an unimaginable state.

With control im holding back
an attach that will do no good,
I feel like running and hitting
anything i could.

The sound of my fathers yell
is unpleasing to the ear,
And i still havent grown accustomed to it
throughout all these years.

I need someone to talk to
but communication is not a choice,
All my means of communication
have been shattered by his voice.
