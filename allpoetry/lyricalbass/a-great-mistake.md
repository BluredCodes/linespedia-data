Tonight i sit down and think
of all the problems in my life,
How something could turn bad
from somthing that is so right?

The thought of a friend
who is supposed to be there,
Is now just a friend
who tends to not even care.

I kind of know whats going through his head
and it really isnt good,
But why cant he just be happy for me
like a good friend would?.

In a way I did him wrong
but i hope in the end it will be okay
I wish for this all to be gone
and just move to the next day.

With this crap going on in the world
and the thoughts running through my mind,
I noticed we take our lives for granted
we should be thankful we are alive.

He may not think it effects me
but it does deep in my heart,
I will be there for him till the end
Like i have been frokm the start.
