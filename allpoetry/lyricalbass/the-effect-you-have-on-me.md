---
title: "the effect you have on me"
writer: "lyricalbass"

When i think of all the things you do
that make me feel good inside,
Like the letters you write, and the kisses you give
and the smiles that you can not hide

My body is filled with pleasure
all the pain is no longer there,
When i turn towards your beautiful face
all i can do is stare

If you wre only looking through my eyes
and you could see what i do,
You would see a wonderful young woman
you  would see an image of you

I remember the times it was cloudy
and to me, you became my sun,
I remember all the times I've been put down
but you make me feel like I've won

I forget the days ive been lonely
because those days are no longer here,
I hate when we arent together
i cherish the time when you are near

Tonight i sit down and think
about the change you have had on my life,
You brought me from the times that were bad
to the present which feels so right.

Thankyou Victoria!!
