the noise disturbing
i cannot rest
parents yelling
another test

i have to control myself
not get involved
i will cause more problems
none will be solved

i like the fact
that shes getting yelled at
some pressure is off me
she'll deal with the rest of that

she says shes moving out
well i wont mind that at all
she will be no longer under these roofs
no longer inside these walls

i guess im in the mood to write
but now its time to go
when will all this yelling stop
the answer, i do not know
