To you im nothing but a boy
But to me your something more,
Everytime I see your eyes
all my heart can do is twirl.

Knowing you have a man
makes my heart ache and sore,
Every oppurtunity i have to tell you
he comes and shuts the door.

I have nothing to offer but love
hopeing youll find a way,
You are just all im thinking of
theres nothing else to explain or say

But to me your everything.
