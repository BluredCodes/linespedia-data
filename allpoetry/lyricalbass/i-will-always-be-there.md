---
title: "I Will Always be there"
writer: "lyricalbass"
slug: "i-will-always-be-there"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58362-I-Will-Always-be-there-by-lyricalbass"

Today is so right, i know ill sleep tight
for you will be in my dreams tonight
and in my thoughts forever you will stay
always in my thoughts, youll never go away
the thought of you makes me feel good inside
the feeling of which cannot be passed by
please take it to mind that about you... i truly care
and whenever you need me i will always be there
