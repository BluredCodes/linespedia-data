Yesterday afternoon i told you i loved you
and i meant it with every bit of my heart
i know you will be a great person till the end
like you have been from the start

Every thought of you makes me happy
in an undescribable way
your tenderness and loving care
has an effect on me everyday

Sometimes we may be happy
sometimes we may want to cry
But no matter what kind of mood i am in
im better when i look in your eyes

To have someone i know cares
standing right along my side
can not give me any reason
to let this love pass by

The feelings that i show
will continue until the end
After all whats shared love with out 2 people?
its like a line of a cricle without a bend
