heaven was in my hands that night.
whatever HEAVEN means

we looked at the Sun as it settled into the hills at the end of that busy street
just for a moment,
the liquidy perfection of the sky... 

The evening performance makes it easy for me
to believe that you are who i so wish you were
it lets me look into your eyes and see the purest of intentions
it lets me believe you are a little boy

suddenly i can dismiss everything
i can forget, yes i can forget
you only want to be near me and the others do too
Nobody tries to break anyone else, It just happens.

We put such faith in sunsets, though.
we examine the beauty and then we sigh
as if, maybe, in that perfect sacred moment we could fix everything...
we can pretend that "moving on" is really moving on
such clinging to "the simple things,"  is it escaping or is it true?
oh a penny for my stupidity & one for your denial
that's what it comes down to when i think of me and you

Look! over your shoulder
there's that one, that one and that one's easy to love 
But there's your delusion, your heart-breaker fantasy
Her head's ablaze and her fists are before her...
she throws her darts of poison with her sinister gentle hands
i'm struck--i know she's got me
i'm no better off than you.a work in progress....
