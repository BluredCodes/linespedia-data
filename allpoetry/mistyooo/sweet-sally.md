sweet sally is on the hunt she never knew
why they put it in her head cause she never
knew it was there.

sally sees the world with foggy eyes 
but all seems clear to a paper doll
picture-perfect patron of prosperity

and all seems clear to you and me 
WE NEVER SEE REALITY

ReAlitE reality the opposite of insanity
that lie we dream of at night... 
See Sally sweet sally
lying in her bed 
picturing the events the sweet and the sour

her tommorrow is never and her yesterday is forever
glassy eyes overlook at the cloudy skys
SALLY STICKS HER HAND IN FIRES 

she yanks it out, can't stand the blood
no it's not pyromania not a primal need
she just THINKS IT FEELS GOOD.just a tiny bit experimental...i'm working with some ideas here, some metaphors...this will probably change again but please tell me what you think!
