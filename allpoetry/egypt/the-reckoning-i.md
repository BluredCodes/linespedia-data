---
title: "The Reckoning I"
writer: "Egypt"
slug: "the-reckoning-i"
source: "AllPoetry"

Water dripped from her iridescent skin to pool on the floor
Looking up at the moonless sky, realizing the opening of the door
Standing in her nakedness, fresh from her cleansing bath
Understanding with perfect clarity of her appointed path

The Dark One walked behind her, overwhelmed with beauty and dark grace
Sensing her troubled emotions, he gently turned her, to look on her face
`You must not let the past cloud your future.`  He quietly said
Taking her in his arms, he closed his eyes, `You need your rest, go to bed.`

She said nothing and moved not an inch when she realized
His words of beauty, comfort and confidence had never been lies
He pulled her away and looked deep into icy blue eyes
He furrowed his brow and frowned when he saw the tear slide

'Mourning our past deaths is something I must do.'
'We left our daughters helpless, without US, as our soul's flew.'
'Destined or not, we should have fought for their sake.'
'And for that, there is something that I've learned to do, and that's hate.'

'Not for those who took us, or our girls - I could never do that.'
'But for you and me, we failed, were weak and that's a fact.'
'I've pondered since remembering if I shall ever see them again.'
'Not in this lifetime, but further lives we'll lead, since time began.'
