title: "Lost Remembered II"
writer: "Egypt"

Gravel crunching underfoot, a shadow lurched toward her
The body fell against her and they fell, her heart finally stirred
Holding in her arms, her Lord gasped and moaned
Feeling his body covered in blood, she let out a groan

'Protect you, he couldn't, his life now worthless.'
'He lives long enough to watch you die, Sweet Mistress.'
'The last of your kind you are, the others are all dead.'
'Evil you are, and it ends when I take off your head.'

As her Lord laid in her arms, she slowly began to smile
'You think you have won, but sadly, you've one more mile.'
Her Lord let out a final gasp, then felt him deep in her core
Laughing, she stood and from the wound, blood began to pour

'My death is eminent, and I welcome it with open arms.'
'Our work will continue, without doubt, this I warn.'
Her eyesight completely gone, she dropped her head down
She cried and thought of her Lord, without making a sound

She laid down beside him, her head on his bloody chest
Strongly feeling the much needed Eternal Rest
Her last words angered them, the one angered further
'Our work will continue...with our newborn daughters.'

Screaming in pain, she sat up in bed
Crying til blinded, she quickly turned her head
Standing above her, the Dark One looked as of stone
'Feel this and own it, your revenge deep in your bones.'
