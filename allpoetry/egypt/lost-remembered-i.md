---
title: "Lost Remembered I"
writer: "Egypt"
slug: "lost-remembered-i"
source: "AllPoetry"

Standing alone, a room so small
High above the world, not wanting to fall
Shadows and movement was all she could see
Blinded in attack, she accepted and let it be

Heart pounding her chest, fear tightened its grip
Blood oozing from the wound deep inside her hips
Strange noises and unfamiliar scent stopped her cold
Heart stopped beating, her senses on overload

'Mistress, come, you are not safe here.' An unfamiliar voice in her ear
'You'll be found for sure, then your death would soon be near.'
Sensing no hostility, no malice, she closed her eyes and sighed
Whatever was to happen, she would soon take her long flight

Down the winding stairs, her hand on his back
Sweat ran down her body and filled with pain from the attack
Taking precious moments away from her safety
They descended slow and awkward, her Lord she wished to see

'He is waiting for you, Mistress; he sent me to get you.'
His statement calmed her immediately, the ordeal almost through
She allowed a brief smile and through hazy sight
The unfamiliar smiled back, one more step and they ended the flight

He led her outside, the sun warm on her face
Furrowing her brow, she grew frightened of the space
'No, please NO!'  She screamed and grew weak
'It ends now, Mistress, it is you whom I seek.'
