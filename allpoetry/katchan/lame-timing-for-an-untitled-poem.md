These eyes of failure,
these eyes of defeat,
these eyes that have seen the world.
The battle,
the space between death and cruelty.
They’ve seen the sweat and blood of a solider
on the life forsaken ground.
The merciful ground.
They are they eyes of a worrier.
Some soul that's been in one battle too many,
one joke too long,
and put up with too much.
There just isn't anymore fighting left to do.
