title: "dragons for ends (long)"
writer: "katchan"

One more time...I'll face the dragon
Those fierce hurtful teeth
innocent helpless eyes.
The red love of my life.
In to the pit again
Where I lost my armor before
demanded by a damsel in 'distress'.
My shroud, submissive
My sheild, passive
My will, weak
Srtong only, is love
There is reason for my shame;
The kiss of flames.
Burns deserved.
My weight carried on my knees, 
colapsed in exhaustion.
The rusted sword screams aloud
'not again'
The dragon takes no excuses
no explinations
no appoligies,
I shall be slayed.
The flames will swallow me, 
just as they should.
My heart groans unimportantly
'no more battles fair maden,
no more fires till dawn,
I shall suffer no more.
Piece dear dragon
piece.'
Let me burn on my knees
let me burn away. 
to slain ashes
to dust. 
Into the pit again
one more battle.....I never want to be in love again.  what a mess that was.
