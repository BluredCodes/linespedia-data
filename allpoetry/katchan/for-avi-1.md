title: "for avi (1)"
writer: "katchan"

whatever it is you see in me
I don't see it. 
whatever it is you like in me
I don't like it.  
whatever it is you cherish about my presence 
I certainly don't cherish it. 
I'm not recognizing whatever it is you love
they love
you claim to enjoy. 
but whatever it is you hate about yourself
I don't understand.
call me a hypocrite.
to me you're amazing, surprising
an unexpected release, 
embrace, 
breath.  
so be it. 
you do it so unconsciously and so well. 
you're so niece and uneducated
to your own genuine beauty.
you deserve so much more
because you are so much more.
