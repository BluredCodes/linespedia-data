title: "here's your payment"
writer: "katchan"

Yes, you're right
I won't fight back with strength or might.
I am lower than low
and I am slower than slow.
I am pathetic, 
and aesthetic.
An amazing transparent fake
to be dumped in a polluted lake
accompanied by beautiful cement shoes.
They'll all forget the news;
"The art of imperfection mastered!"
Take me tanked and plastered-
so I might not feel the extreme pain.
Though, I'll still die in life long vein.
You'll get your wonderful way
I'll pay.
My overhead debt to you
will be permanently through
and so will these daffodils.
