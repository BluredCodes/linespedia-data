Tonight I will make no wishes upon stars.
I  will no longer pray to find what I have found.
I have struck gold.
I  have found what I was
looking,
searching,
praying,
wishing for.
And I found it in a most unexpected spot!
I found a heart as hurt as mine
and we've mended together.
my love,
my only,
my true love.
I will no longer wish.
I know what perfection can be.
I know what beauty can be,
and how powerful a gesture is,
a simple gesture.
I knew he'd be this easy to love.
But easier to love than was expected.
He is amazing in all aspects
and I hope I never have to wish on bright stars again.
Now,
I shall only thank them.
