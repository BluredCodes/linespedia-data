I sit here and think of you
your beautiful face and it's orbs of blue
the carved anarchy symbol
away on the table causes memories to mingle
my mind regrets the last few encounters
your degradation causes pounders
my body aches with need
forgiveness, apology, respect i desperately plead
there is no mercy for people like us
the world rolls on without our fuss
my days run together, long and slow
I can't tell the difference between lower and low
I try not to dwell on the things that i miss
example; when you shamelessly piss
my e-mail is checked every day
just to see if there's anything you might say
