---
title: "Soulmate Defined"
writer: "Kym"
slug: "soulmate-defined"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61678-Soulmate-Defined-by-Kym"

Reach into me
Pull out my strength
Find my beauty
Discover the child in me

Hold a mirror to me
Show me what's inside
Uncover my demons
And exorcise them

Cover me 
With wings of steel
Armor of petals
Protection like a tiger

Be all things to me
Be my lover
My friend
My muse
And help me love myself
