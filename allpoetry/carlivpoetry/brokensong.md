(Verse1)
Outspoken, try to bite my tounge
it seems jealousy has claimed this one.
maybe it's the sober that killed all our fun?
Dont' wanna hear our song no more,
it's time to move along...

(Chorus)
Broken, only wasting time...
Dont look back, when you pass me by...
just pass on by.

(Verse2)
too predictable, easy to ignore,
been there before.
On the devils side of my head I even out the score.
Longing for our ending, defending, my old-school style.
Lets be friends...enemies for awhile...

(Chorus)
