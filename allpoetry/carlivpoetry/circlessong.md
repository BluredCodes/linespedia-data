(Verse1)
For the rush a must to touch your skin
stop this ride I'm sticking to the wind
lost your smile in sight, cant see the sound
round and round, up-down, we're finally found

(Chorus)
I'll keep climbing and you keep whining
these circles never cease.
Where this ends she starts again
it's geometry... 
and you failed... you failed.

(Verse2)
Study us, the way we live, and we'll try to co-exist
sex was good but not that great, caught in love we crave the hate
more abuse to use for this muse
another lie she stretched into the truth

(Chorus)
