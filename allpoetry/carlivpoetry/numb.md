Pray to you?
You stole my greatest abilty.
I can only release my feelings in words as of now I cant use my hands.
Paralyzed through anxiety would've seemed an unlikely way to end my career...

but look at what I have...

Hands that no longer work.

You fucking bastard.


Recently suffered some medical problems.

Wont be able to write.  Hopefully I'll regain feeling in my hands.

Goodbye, Carl IV
