he seems as if he wants to be more than just
"friends"
he complements me, telling me i'm sweet,
and that he loves me and that he misses me when i'm not
around
he says when i aint sick at home with a cold 
wanna try and give it a shot
as in a lil bit of lovin
not knowing how to resond i say sure
always up for lovin.

he eyes are to die for
there the most prettyest eyes i've ever seen
the softest shade of blue extremly
breath taking
hes sweet and nice and treats me well

but what do you do if you 
dont feel as if your ready for this 
or if you dont feel the same as he does for you?theres this guy at school and hes so sweet and nice to me and everything and i think he likes me but i aint to sure and i think he might want to get together with me and if he asks me i may not know what to say or do?? confuzing i might just let it play out and see what happens if he still says these things....
