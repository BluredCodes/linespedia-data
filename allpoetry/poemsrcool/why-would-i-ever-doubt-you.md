why would i ever doubt you 
the way i did only a few days ago
i sometimes had these feelings
that you would do something to me
but you always told me you would never do such a thing
you said " you have had someone do this to you
before but that doesn't mean every guy that 
you meet and have in your life will do that to you"
and i believed him and i am so glad i did
i have no reason to doubt you in any way
you would never do anything to me
you are the greatest and yet so gentil
you have expressed your thoughts on this 
in alot of ways and i have believed you in every way
i wonder if i every doubted you because
i used that feeling of doubt to try and not
get too close to you because i was
afraid to get to close then
be ripped away from you and be so heartbroken....
i wonder why i ever doubted you, 
that would never happen.....huh i dunno what happened here but it turned out kinda weird....and the person here i am talkin about knows who he is....
