i never dreamed of being away from you
i thought we would always be together 
the thought of you and I being apart scared me
and once i learned the horrible truth that you had
to leave me i felt dead inside
not wanting to go on thinkin i would never see you again
i felt completely alone and thinking that i would
never find anyone again.

But once i learned you were traveling back to see me
the joy i felt over comed the sadness i felt and
i once felt whole and complete again
seeing you for the first time in months
made me extremly nervous
i didnt know how to act or what to say to you ,
wondering thinking if you still loved me
scared to know the truth that you might of moved on
leaving me behind to hold up on my own

as you walk up to me and kiss my as you always
have even before you left i knew everything would be ok
you were mine forever even if you were thousands of miles away!
