have you ever known someone who
made you feel so complete and whole
like nothin could go wrong 
that he would love you forever and ever
never letting you fall
he would always be there to catch you
he'd fight off the bad people in my life telling
me they are not worth the time and wait
making me the best i can be everyday
never mixing his own life with mine
always being so gentil and caring of my needs
never pushing to far
or asking to much
just enough so he can know what he needs to know
to help with the problem or whatever i may have at the time
have you ever known someone so great and loving as 
sweet as candy and as loving as a father to his own child??i have and it is dedicated to a very special person who means everything to me!!!
