the window to my soul 
is in my eyes
you see right into something
very mysterious that you really don't know
what to do or how to react to it
my soul at times can be very much hurt
and may crumble to pieces any second
but what is most unique about my soul is that
no matter how much i have endured in my life so far
this pain, heartache and loss
i seem to pick myself back up and
be as stronge as i can be each and everyday
and i find myself to be lucky to be able
to do this all on my ownsince i havnt really had anyone there for me
well, i have had my parents
but i've always had this feeling that they 
never really understood
but now i think they are starting to.
