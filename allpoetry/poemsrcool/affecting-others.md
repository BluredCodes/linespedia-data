people around me seem to be fighting 
all the time not paying attention
to who they really are hurting
they yell and bitch at one another
for as long as it takes for them to feel
satistfied and once that satisfaction weres off
they then start to bitch again
can't you give it a rest, and just resolve whatever you are fighting
about i know it kills you to fight with one of your friends
over something so very worthless
but dont you see it hurts me just as much as it hurts you
to see you go through this with your friend which also
happens to be mine also 
if this doesnt end soon between you too i'm
gonna turn my head and walk away 
cause i dunno how much longer i can take
of this bitching and back stabbing!!!!for my sister who is mad at a guy who is also my friend and also for my sister who is fighting with another one of her friends which i personally dont like but she seems to mean alot to her i just hope it shorts itself out sooN!!!
