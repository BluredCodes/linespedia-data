---
title: "beneath the surface"
writer: "TeKajo"
slug: "beneath-the-surface"
source: "AllPoetry"

slowly slice carve a line
across the soul's selfish shell
feel the flowing warmth of mine
from where love dwells (hell)
one single thin sweet line
tracing racing away
down...

slowly pry into my old soul
both hands digging feeling
eight fingers sliding finding 
their hold......
peel back rip and tear
so i can finally see within
why i do not care again
peel back rip and tear
beneath the surface 
i can no longer stare inside me
the emptiness why i hate
i close my eyes cry try and fade.

slowly pry into my torn soul
beneath the surface no smiles
heartless too worn to show
the inside truths of mine
slowly slice carve a line
just to see if i feel
just to see...if i am real.'.'
