---
title: "This Time"
writer: "Objection"
slug: "this-time"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Curses,
I thought you could show me
A single breath of compassion
Just a little emotion you really felt
Not what you were supposed to feel.

Damn you,
Only a man, but I thought I knew you.
I dreamed of days when we could just sit
I could just be in your arms
And allow you to hold me close..

Shit.
I don't know why I gave up
Why was it me that threw in the dry towel?
You meant and still are the world to me.
What did I throw it all away for?

Please-
Let me be your destiny,
Have a chance to hold you again
I don't want to give up on you yet, 
Not when I still have a bit of a chance...

Curses, 
You made me love you
Fallen under your spell
I don't nknow why I did this to myself,
Maybe I just don't care about now...

Damn you,
I want you so badly.
Just to kiss you, caress you, 
To hold you again and be yours forever...
I just want to make love to you,

Shit.
I threw it all away again
I had another chance and blew it
Let me kiss you and let me love you
I want to be under your spell..

Please-
Curse me, condemn me, 
Make me feel like shit...
At least in the end of all of this crap I'll know
That I had another chance, that it's all my fault This Time.
