---
title: "Hold on"
writer: "Objection"
slug: "hold-on"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Hold on, my heart
   Hold onto this feeling
You know what you could start
   Let my mind do her dealings.

Hold on, my mind
   Remember my heart?
Don't do anything unkind,
   You play an important part

Hold on, my soul
   Wait for a sign from me
Let time take his toll.
   Whatever will be, will be
