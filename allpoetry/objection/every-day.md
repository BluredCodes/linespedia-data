---
title: "Every Day"
writer: "Objection"

This burden of reproach I gladly bear.
For it is self-inflicted.  
I draw the knife, pull back my hair.
What a way to "live"-contradicted.
Across my wrist-
The end never begins, irony is bliss.
Here's my proof, 
Ive been shown the truth,
I'm nothing, I'm shit
I'm glad I went through with it.
As I drift of to sleep, I pray one last time:
"Please let me die before I wake,
Please end this sorrow of mine. 
I pray the Lord my soul to take".  
What a shock, I never arise,
No one notices my absence.
Every damned day someone dies, 
because they were never given a chance.
