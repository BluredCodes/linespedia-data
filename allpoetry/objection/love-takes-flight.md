title: "Love Takes Flight"

When love takes flight
It feels so right
The pains of being alone
Forever are gone
He'll reach for my hand
And my heart can't withstand
The strength of his arms
Keeps me safe from harm.
I'll never forget
His eyes, they were wet
With tears of gladness-
An end to my sadness
'Twas not only a dream of mine,
But also of thine.
His voice can barely supress
The love and tenderness
We feel for each other
I don't feel smothered 
As I usually would,
This embrace just feels good.
I don't want to leave
Our touch cannot be cleaved.
We're together forever
No one could sever
This thing that we feel 
It really is real.
