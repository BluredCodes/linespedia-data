title: "Chain on My Arm"

Wishes are worthless
They never come true
I never thought this
'Til I lived it through.

This girl's best friend,
I hate to admit-
Isn't hardly a diamond
Nothing like it.

My most faithful companion
Is sharp, grey, and strong.
When it peirces a canyon-
The scar will remain for long.

Just a few swipes
Could end several lives.
But I'll wait for a night
When I'm too tired to try.

I've been sick of effort for years
Trying just to fit in
Living my life through tears,
It's impossible to win.

People say not to let you get to me,
As if I could choose.
If I could decide I'd already be free.
But relentlessly, I'll just lose.

With this chain on my arm
And this chip on my shoulder,
I'll never be safe from harm
And my life will become ever colder.Yes, I feel this way.  No, you can't save me.  You probably can't do anything at all.  But, yeah, now it's out there
