title: "Free My Mind, I Don't Mind"

~:*:~:*:~:*:~
Where did I go wrong?
What made you see this in me?
How can you be so strong,
When all I am is weak?

Where is the origin of your strength-
How can you be so independent
Doesn't masculinity limit to length?
I'm too young to be so intent on you.
~:*:~:*:~:*:~
Why don't I see
What you do to me?
You make me crazy 
I become insane when I see you
I don't know what to do
Baby, you free my mind...
I don't mind it at all.
~:*:~:*:~:*:~
Please help me understand you
I don't know what you want from me.
What I want is to make it through
It's my only dream for us to be.

I don't want to be alone anymore.
I've been knocking forever
Please, just open the door.
My arms are weak from reaching over.
~:*:~:*:~:*:~
Why don't I see
What you do to me?
You make me crazy,
I become insane when I see you
I don't know what to do
Baby, you free my mind...
I don't mind it at all.
~:*:~:*:~:*:~
Please come
To me
Call this home
Let's be...
I want you
I need you
Please let me
Love you.....
Let me see...
What you do to me...
~:*:~:*:~:*:~
Why don't I see
What you do to me?
You make me crazy
I become insane when I see you
I don't know what to do
Baby, you free my mind...
I don't mind it at all
