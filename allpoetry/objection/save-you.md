writer: "Objection"

You said you'd look out for me
Y'know, when I "needed a friend"
Because you 'love' me,
You'll always be there to 
Ensure what little happiness I can muster
 
Buddy, I hate to say this-
But words like Love and Happiness
Never seem to stand up
when they're called upon.
So, I suppose that oath wasn't worth much.

What about that time 
When you were in a bit of a bind,
and you needed an out.
Who sacrificed her time, her effort
her ASS, just so you wouldn't get caught?

Man, that girl's name just escapes me.
Did I start with a T?
and end with an ARA?
That's not possible-is it?
How could Tara be so dumb to risk everything?

Oh, but she was.
Was she ever.
And the things she-I-lost because of you.
Because of your stupid-ass antics.
Always trying new things...

Each thing is less bright than the last.
So, tell me-explain to me:
How are you there for me
If I'm always rescuing you?
It doesn't make much sense.

So, next time you're in trouble
and you need someone to save you
Call on someone else.
Because I'll be too busy for you.
re-alphebetizing my recipes.
