---
title: "Let Me Know"
writer: "Objection"

We've been
the best of friends
For a few years
that'll never end
But what if I 
Started to love you?
or care about you
More than before?

What if I told you
I'm falling for you?
What would you do if I
felt like you were the best 
for me?
How would you take that?
Could you do it if I said
"Be with me, please, baby..."

We've talked
'bout this before
I said "Let's be friends"
Nothing more right now
I was so stupid
You're what i've wanted...
I want to hold you
Just let me love you

Please try for me
You said you'd do it
In the past
I know you care
Just...show me, ok?
Let me know 
How you really feel...
Do you want to be my guy?
