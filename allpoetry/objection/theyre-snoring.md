title: "They're SNORING"
writer: "Objection"

I'm so bored
Too much so for my own good
I've been thinking of stupid things.
Why doesn't Donald Duck wear pants?
Where did the word "word" come from?
Who thought to make Marshmallows for sandwiches?

I hear the voices in my head,
I swear to God it sounds like they're snoring.
If I could stay awake all night,
I would.
I'm eating a sandwich and I'm stil hungry
I get hunger pains as I eat.  Weird.

If I were a hot dog, 
I would have yummy mustard on me.
I would eat myself.
Yum,
Yum,
Yummy.
