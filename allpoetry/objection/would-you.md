I don't know how to say 
All of the terrible things I've thought today.
Not just sure why I care 
Though this whole song is heartfelt I swear.
I thought that I could always have
All the parts of you I adore.
I wish it occured before.

If it had  I would have kissed you
I would have held you one last time.
I would have made sure I knew you more
And that you were really mine.
I would have loved you
Would have told you.
I need you...
In my life.

Because I was scared to let you in
To allow you to really know me,
In a way I can't ever really win,
You'll never get to get to know me.

If I had would you have kissed me? 
Would you have held me one last time?
Would you have made sure you knew me ore?
Or that I was really yours?
Would you've loved me?
would you have told me you need me...
In your life?

Why can't O love you 
Or be near you
Without fear of certain pain.
Why can't I see you
Or talk to you?
I'll never be the same...I wrote this one about a guy, duh.  No one in particular...just boredom.
