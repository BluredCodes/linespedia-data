This is not another love poem
Though I wish that it could be
The boy-I don't really know him
But freakishly-he knows me.

Reached across the abyss for me
and never quite grabbed hold.
I'm very glad he couldn't you see,
His hands are supernaturally cold.

Many rhetorical questions
I shot over in his way
He always tried to answer them
Then would ask if I was ok.

Just once I wish I'd told 
the complete answer to that
But instead, emotions I with hold
He'll never know when my thoughts were at.

Though some things he'll know never
He'll always know one thing-
I'll care for him forever
Though I know not what he means.
