---
title: "I Smile"
writer: "Objection"

No one understand me-
Nodody really tries.
"Why does she smile all the time?"
No one really knows...
I do.
I smile because I'm unaware,
I've no clue what's going on around me.
Can't discover why I have to hide;
Why's everyone else allowed to have feelings?
And not I...
Please, could someone just pretend to care
That I die each time I laugh?
My heart disenegrates when people look at me
Damn it, why do the stare?
I'm really not all that different you know, I'm just misunderstood.
Don't say you know how I feel.
I you did, would I still be alone?
I smile because I doubt my happiness.
If I smile I look happy.  
If I don't, well
I don't.
More people respect me when I've got it all together
Than when I fall apart or break down.
No one doesn't anything to stop my sobbing
They just point at me and giggle.
Snicker all ou want, I don't care anymore.
I've grown immune.
I smile because I don't have the strength to frown.
Does  that make you feel secure?
