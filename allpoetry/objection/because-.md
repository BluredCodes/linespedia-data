---
title: "Because. . ."
writer: "Objection"
slug: "because-"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Because of all that he is
Because of all that he could someday be 
Because he doesn't really know
That I am in love with he.

Because I fall apart at a glance,
I die each time we touch.
Simply because he doesn't know
Honesty would be too much

Too much to say,
Yet so much I wish I could do.
I don't know where or when or how
I don't know how to...

To tell him that I fall apart 
Each time I see his face
To let him know that even though
He's oblivious, in my heart he has a place.

I needed to get this off my chest;
I needed to let it out.
Now that I have done my best, 
I've proven I can't live without.

I can't live without what we could be
I can't live without who he could make of me.
I shan't go on dreaming that one day he'll know,
For to him my feelings I'll ne'er show. 

I guess I should forget it all-
Pretend I never cared.
And if one day, I pay the price
It will be because I never dared.
