---
title: "Dance"
writer: "Objection"

Whisper a little louder,
It's becoming harder to hear.
I know, you didn't mean to overcrowd her,
She'll easily fall into fear.
Don't speak too loudly, now, for she'll know.
You slightly care for her,
Though I know you truly don't...
You just want to her hurt.
Stop following me around, 
I can't help who she is.
Don't tell her to be different, 
Her ignorance brings her bliss.
Just leave her to be the wierdo she'll be
If you'd just give her another chance.
I know how she feels-she's really me,
And all she wants to do is dance...
