I never imagined
I'd die alone
In my room tonight.
Never dreamed
No one would care
I gave up the fight.
So, I'll fall into fatigue
Due to loss of blood from my wrists
And never wake up
No one'll even know it
Nobody will miss me.
I wasn't needed, I waste oxygen.
I laughed the loudest, 
Who would've thought of something like this
From me?
Well, now you do.
Mom-it IS your fault
Hanna-I love you
Drake-Never knew you, bro.
Don't bother missing me
I never imagined
I'd die alone
In my room tonight...
