I don't know where I'm coming from
I don't know where you are
I don't know why I don't care anymore
I don't know what to think.

I can't explain what you do to me.
I can't explain why I want out.
I can't explain all of the things in my mind
I can't explain the things I've told myself.

Stop falling further that you can go,
Stop falling for people so much
Stop falling in love, Tara, it's not nice
Stop falling and just falling out..

Don't say "I love you" unless you do
Don't say "I'll always be there" if you won't.
Don't say that you've never felt like this before
Don't say you care if you just can't

I wish I could turn back the tables of time
I wish I could change what I said,
I wish I could tell you I like you a lot
I wish I could take back 'love'.

But I can't stop how I felt
But I can't change what's been done
But I can't explain why I said I was in love
But I can't truthfully tell you I lied.I like it a lot..just how I feel about this guy.  Probably because another one offered to be my 'kissing teacher'...uh yeah.  Just tell me if it's too redundant..I bet I got the points across!!
