title: "Now"
writer: "Objection"

I shall break free
From all this pain
I will, really flee
And feel it moreso once again.
This hurt, like all other
Will come to an end.
I will get over you, 
Becuase you said 
I really have to.
Though now some of me's dead.
I can, I swear I will.

Although I cry when I think of you now
Although I sigh after dreams of you now.
It will all end
It already has.
I just continue to hang on,
I can never let go for someday I'll know.
I'll know what we could have been, 
But I will let go because I have to.
You told me to there and then.

I refused to give up but you told me to
I suppose I will always succomb to you.
Give me a word, I'll give you my life
If only your spell didn't cause so much strife.
The pain I feel, is immense above all,
Will I pull through or fall further away?
Just to let the world know, you decide my every day.
