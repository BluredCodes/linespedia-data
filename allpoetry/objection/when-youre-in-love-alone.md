title: "When You're in Love Alone"

Life's a prison when you're in love alone.
When you can't live without someone, 
And he could continue on without.
It's as though you're watching the soap opera of you
At the part where the main guy kisses the main girl's twin.
But it's you....but he feels nothing
That's why it doesn't seem like she's you.
Why would you fall for someone who didn't want you?
Why would he pretend?
Can he help me...I am lost
Help me out, please
I need to leave this penitentiary.
Life's a prison when you're in love alone.
