title: "Part of the sky"

Have you ever looked into the night? 

  Not looked at it, but become part of it. 
Seen the pink and orange clouds of sunset, 
but were part of them as well. Looked at 
the stars and felt like you could almost 
reach up and touch one.

  If only it could be like this. 
You could be part of the sky, part of 
the beauty, and still part of the world.

  Next time your out at night, and 
you're part of the sky, try and look 
back down at yourself. See yourself as 
part of the world.

  If it doesn't work and you can't 
see youself as part of the world, remember, 
you're never alone in the sky.
