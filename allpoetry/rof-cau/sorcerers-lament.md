---
title: "Sorcerer's Lament"
writer: "Rof Cau"
slug: "sorcerers-lament"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62548-Sorcerers-Lament-by-Rof-Cau"
---

Under a dark, leaden sky
I saw him digging her grave
slowly
for the labour and tears
served only to impede
the healing still far arears

I saw love lost, saw it sadly die
now buried deep under an empty sky
With head still bent he called to me
"Scribe, by magic I'm letting you see:

How with all my power and might
I was defeated by cloaked friends
whose dealings dark as night
resulted in my love's untimely end

Scribe, you see her eyes so bright
- eyes in which angels took flight? 
By their light I will hunt the dark
by my love's end they are now marked"

Then he cried and within something died
the gods help me but I cannot lie:
I felt his hate inside blossom and grow 
mountains quaked, mighty kings did tremble!

In eternal damnation there is no hope
with the blood spilt he is still alone
by her grave for his poison he groped
I witnessed his last breath - his last smile

In solitude now a stone does stand
in swirling fog where cloud meets land
on moonlit nights young lovers do come
to swear their undying love
and wait for the sun
