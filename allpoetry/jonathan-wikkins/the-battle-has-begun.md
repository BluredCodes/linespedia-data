title: "The Battle has Begun"
writer: "Jonathan Wikkins"

It is I, 
     the gentle muse…
Once again, 
     I have returned…
This time, 
     with a report… 

The battle has begun…
Evil has tried 
     to gain the upper hand…
But good is fighting back…
It won’t be an easy fight, 
     but hopefully, 
          good will win true…
For all mankind… 

This fight is 
     to keep the forces of good 
          from joining together…
Mistrust is its method, 
     this time… 
And it fights dirty… 

It fights within our minds…
It fights within our hearts…
It fights to even win our souls…
So those it can’t turn, 
     will not trust those others 
          who need them… 

It is an almost invisible fight…
That is what makes it 
     such a difficult battle…
You never know 
     whether you are 
          fighting someone,
               or someone speaking
                    through them…
Until, suddenly,
     it becomes crystal clear… 

Then you almost 
     don’t know what to say… 
But you must keep on fighting, 
     and keep your motives good… 
And, perhaps, 
     the battle can be won…
For us all… 

I shall continue 
     to report things
          as they come…
Keep checking back…

It is I, 
     the muse…
I shall return…
