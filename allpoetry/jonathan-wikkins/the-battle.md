title: "The Battle..."

It is I,
     the muse…
I have returned,
     once again…
For another message
     has been given… 

This time it is one
     on how the battle
          will be fought…

Parts of the battle coming,
     will be bloody…
Trouble is,
     when someone defeats
          the Evil they see,
               Lucifer’s power could 
                    change them to look “normal”
                         in death…
Not as they truly were in life… 

Those minions of Evil
     could be what is known
          as “shape shifters”…
And when the Evil is defeated,
     the person they inhabited
          is what is left, in death…

A person who is “possessed”,
     is not always obvious
          to the naked eye…
In fact most are not obvious,
     they could even be
          your next door neighbor…

But as those
     who are to fight them
          awaken,
               they will have
                    the ability to see them,
                        as they truly are… 

But if you don’t have that ability,
     to see Evil as it truly is,
          you may never see the demons inside… 
Even when that Evil is defeated,
     and the person possessed has
          been killed… 

That is the Evil’s greatest weapon,
     deception… 
It will deceive you
     whenever it can…
So that you do
     its fighting for it,
          whenever possible…
And you don’t even know
     you’ve been deceived…

Be careful what you believe,
     for it could be YOU,
          who has been deceived…

It is I,
     the muse…

I shall return again…
