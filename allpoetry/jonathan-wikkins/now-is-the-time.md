---
title: "Now is the Time"
writer: "Jonathan Wikkins"
slug: "now-is-the-time"

---

It is I, 
     the muse… 
As I have said, 
     I have returned…
This time with a new message,
     and a warning… 

Be prepared… 

For the ancients 
     speak to us all…
Prophecy has been set forth,
     but it’s biased… 

For the fight
     will not be as easy
          as some would 
               have you believe… 
For the forces of evil, 
     will not give up easy…
We must defeat them… 

Evil’s advantage over good 
     is that, people think 
          that its powers 
               will be obvious, 
                    But they won’t be… 

Some would be called crazy, 
     if they witness what Evil will do…
But that is Evil’s advantage,
     for those that see its magick 
          will be persecuted… 

We must believe 
     that Evil is there…
For if we don’t,
     it can win… 

It is written that Lucifer 
     was given dominion 
          over the Earth…
Do you honestly think 
     he will give it up 
          without a fight? 
The forces of good 
     must use their strength 
          and majick to defeat it…
Some fights will be fought 
     between  flesh and blood…
Some will be invisible; 
     they may even seem 
          there is only one participant…

Wake up, wake up people,
     before it’s too late… 
Those of you who read the Bible,
     read it, understand it… 
For even the Bible says 
     that we will fight…
Not against flesh and blood, 
     but against principalities of power…
And those powers 
     don’t come from Tinker Bell… 

They come from the same source
     that gave Lucifer his powers… 
They come from God Himself…
Those of us who have powers 
     given to us,
          understand one thing…
It’s not what we do, 
     but how we do it,
          that speaks for whether 
               or not 
                    good is done…
It is the intention 
     behind the magick 
          that speaks 
               to its possible outcome…

Keep up the good fight…
Fight if you are one of those 
     destined to fight…
If you are not, 
     and if you witness 
          what appears to be a battle… 
Pray for the one 
     who is on the side of good…
Just a general little prayer, 
     you don’t have to even 
          know their name
               or even which one is for good… 
It will be heard… 

Listen to this message…
For it is one 
     of great importance… 

It is I, 
     the muse…
I shall return…
