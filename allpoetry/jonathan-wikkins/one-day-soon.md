---
title: "One Day Soon"
writer: "Jonathan Wikkins"

It is I,
     the gentle muse, 
I have returned
     once again…

A new message 
     has arrived… 
This time it is one of deception… 

One day soon…

A battle shall begin…
Some think it has already,
     and in some sense 
          it has…
But not in the intensity 
     that it will be… 

Times have changed…
The battle is joined…
Evil has begun
     to win souls…
Souls that will fight
     on its side…

Some of those souls,
     don’t even realize 
          that they have been taken…
Their teaching hasn’t allowed them 
     to see… 
They won’t even know who 
     they’re fighting,
          or even why,
               but fight they will… 

They’ll deceive you…
They’ll lie about you…
They won’t believe you…
That is how the Evil will fight,
     dirty… 

For that is all it knows,
     to fight dirty…
It fights within your dreams…
It fights within the courts…
Some will even be “put away”,
     for their visions of reality… 

Not all who are 
     “put away” see reality correctly,
          but, who knows…
Perhaps some of them do,
     and the Evil saw them 
          as a threat,
               and put them 
                    where they will not 
                         be believed…. 

It calls them “crazy”…
It calls them “insane”… 
But it could be,
     because they don’t want 
          YOU to believe those 
               who they see as a viable threat…

Would not even Jesus Christ, Himself,
     be considered insane today?
And think about it,
     He was Evil’s greatest enemy,
          in His day…

And still is… 

It is I
     the muse…
I shall return…
