In life…

We find love
     in the most unlikely
          of places…
It can come easily,
     or sometimes
          it can be difficult
               to find… 

Some of us look,
     and look some more,
          yet never really find it… 
But the lucky few do… 

And an even luckier few
     find it not once, 
          but twice… 
It can even come
     quite easily,
          like it was waiting
               for you
                    all along… 

When love appears to you,
     it could be for someone
          you’ve known for quite a while…
Or it could be
     for someone you just met…
And that love 
     is to become
          that special kind of love…
Something just seems to say to you, 
     “Here they are…”

For when you find it,
     that love just jumps out 
          and grabs you,
               by the hand…
And then
     it leads you
          into a new life…

When you find a wife, 
     and you loose a wife,
          then find another…

As I did…

You see how truly lucky you are…
