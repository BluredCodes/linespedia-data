On black moon night,
     I set forth light,
          to waken up my soul…
For on this day,
     Evil stay away,
          for this is now my goal…

And now for-ward
     I take up sword,
          to wield it now just-ly…
To make it so,
     and to and fro,
          for I now defend thee…

Wake up, dear soul,
     from days of ole,
          for we are now at war…
Wake up, wake up
     and fill your cup,
          I open now your door…
