title: "You May Know..."

It is I, 
the gentle muse…
Once again,
I have returned…

This time with a message
to a few out there…

I am told
there are those, 
Who will take particular heed
to my words, 
those who are
in need of them… 

Some of you
know who you are…
Some of you do not…
It is those, who do not,
who have particular need
of these words… 

You are needed
in the fight,
upcoming… 
The battle has begun… 
Others out there
are looking to find you… 

But they do not know
who you are, yet… 
But your wisdom and power
must be combined with theirs,
for the struggle ahead…

For without you,
all could be lost,
to the minions of Evil… 

You could be
Christian,
or
Wiccan, 
or
Druid,
or
Buddhist, 
or
whatever faith you may follow…
That part does not matter… 
The forces of Good need you… 

Awaken your power
as you can…
Sharpen your swords…
Temper your tongue…
Find the courage
you were born with,
for that is also needed… 

The battle comes to us all…
Sometimes within our minds… 
Sometimes within our hearts…
Sometimes within our souls…
And sometimes,
right in front of us… 

Quoth the muse, 
I shall return…
