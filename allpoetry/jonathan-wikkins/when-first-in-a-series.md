It is I, 
     the muse…
I have returned, 
     once again…

This time, 
     with some questions, 
          and answers… 

You’ve heard me speak 
     of many things, 

The Evil… 

The Alliance… 

The Good… 

You have heard me 
     warn of deception, 
          and 
               greed, war, dark days, etc… 

I’ve raised a few questions 
     within some of you…
Within others, 
     the truth seems 
          to awaken within their souls… 

When is the time of these, 
     and future prophecies? 
The answer is NOW… 
Global war is upon us…
But that is only a prelude, 
     to the true battle,
          the one between 
               Good and Evil… 

Some have said 
     the battle was won, 
          when Jesus died 
               on the cross for our sins…
Nothing could be 
     further from the truth…
That was only 
     the start of the battle, 
          the one that will determine 
               the true fate of the Earth… 

It will determine who 
     will control the Earth, 
          throughout the rest of history… 

It is written, 
     that Lucifer was given 
          dominion over 
               the inhabitants of the Earth…
Which is true…
And he won’t give up 
     his toys without a fight…
Yes, toys, that’s what 
     he thinks of us… 
We are nothing more 
     than mice in a maze,  to him… 
But he’s the maze keeper… 

It would be nice, 
     if we could pick the time,
          for these battles to occur…
But, Lucifer seems 
     to always be there, 
          when we’re not ready…
And, it’s no telling 
     how many other worlds, 
          he’s done this in… 
For, you see, 
     he gets everyone’s soul, 
          if he wins… 

When all who are to fight 
     the minions of Lucifer have awoken, 
          we will be ready to fight, 
               and hopefully, to win… 
But, do not dare to think 
     that the outcome is certain, 
          for it is not… 
Only time will tell 
     what that outcome 
          shall be… 

This time, 
     I have returned,
          with a series of messages, 
               on when, who, how, where, 
                    and what will occur, 
                         in the upcoming battle…

It is I,
     the muse…

I shall return…
