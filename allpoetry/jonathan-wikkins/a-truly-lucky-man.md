---
title: "A Truly Lucky Man"
writer: "Jonathan Wikkins"
slug: "a-truly-lucky-man"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55502-A-Truly-Lucky-Man-by-Jonathan-Wikkins"
---

There was a man,
who one day met a lady,
a lady who would
change his life,
for the better.

They had a daughter
a short time later,
One destined
for something grand,
without a doubt… 

They grew together,
loved each other,
more than life itself.

But their daughter,
would keep him straight.
He did not know
he would need
that straight path,
one day… 

For one day,
much against her will,
the angels came,
and took his lady away.

He knew from
the very day she left,
that it was her choice,
Her choice for him,
to live,
to love again.
But he would
always remember
his love
for the lady he lost.

One day,
after she left,
he went to a friend,
of theirs
and said,
“Have you thought 
about writing a song
in tribute to her?”
The friend said, 
“No, but it would be
a good idea,
I think, 
But I don’t know
what to put in it.
So why don’t YOU
put some ideas down on paper;
and I’ll see what
I can do with it.”

And he did.

And now,
147 (at this writing) poems later,
and many poems
being very highly popular,
and very well read,
his writing career was born.

Along the way,
one night,
he was talking to his son.
He knew his son
was a gifted saxophone player.
He’s even been called
a young Kenny G.
But one thing he didn’t know
was his son
was a poet too,
and a very good one
indeed.

But he was lonely,
and he missed
the companionship
of a lady,
Almost as much
as his own lady he had lost.
Unfortunately, he made
some mistakes
along the way,
because of that loneliness.

But one day,
fate struck once again.
He moved back
to his hometown, 
To be with someone
he thought he wanted
to be with.
But that fell through,
almost as soon
as he moved.

But… 
There was someone else
waiting out there for him.
Someone who
would change his life,
once again.
He even found out,
quite by accident,
that she knew the lady
he had lost.

They had never met,
before,
but right from the start,
love came running
after them.

So, as I’ve said
before,
what a lucky man
he is.

How do I know?
Easy my friend.
easy.

That man,
that man,
is me!
