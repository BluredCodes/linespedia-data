It is I, 
     the muse…
Once again, 
     I have returned…
With another 
     highly controversial message… 

This time it is who 
     we are against, 
          and what they 
               are all about… 

The Evil you have heard me refer to, 
     also known as Lucifer, 
Would not be so tough 
     to win against,
          if not for a few things…
Some you have been taught
     others you will realize 
          as you begin to see 
               what is written…
Yet, one question I have is this, 
     why have we not been taught
          the rest?

First, Lucifer was 
     the first angel
          God created in Heaven…
In him, He created 
     the most beautiful
          of His creations to follow… 
Also in him was 
     the most powerful, 
          and the wisest 
               of all the angels…

Since that time, 
     there was a major war, 
          for control of Heaven…
In which the forces of Lucifer,
     were defeated,
          in that plane…
Yet the battle still rages on,
     this time, in our plane… 

When Lucifer was defeated,
     and banished to what 
          is known as Hell…
He took with him 
     1/3 of all the angels in heaven…
Some also, 
     of the most powerful,
          known as the Archangels, 
               the warrior angels…

This is quite well known,
     but what follows, is not…

Since that time, 
     other angels that 
          did not take his side, 
               have been turned… 
Making our fight, 
     just that much tougher… 

Even more bad news,
     more of God’s archangels, 
          were also turned, 
               who became Lucifer’s 
                    most powerful allies… 
And the worst possible demons 
     you could ever imagine… 

But, even mankind 
     has helped Lucifer 
          in his causes… 
By allowing him 
     to turn some of them 
          into demons… 

Truly a bleak picture, 
     isn’t it? 
Well, 
     it gets even worse… 
For other worlds 
     have been lost, 
          to Lucifer and his minions… 

No, this is not 
     the only world 
          that God created… 
Do you think He 
     would stop at just one? 

And how Lucifer fights,
     well, it is at best, dirty… 
By having you disbelieve 
     that the forces of magick
          are all evil, and/or impossible… 
Not all magick is evil, 
     mind you, 
          for the same spells,
               that he works 
                    for his purpose, 
                         can also be used 
                              to work good magick…
And then used against him… 
And that black magick he uses
     is just one of his weapons… 

Speaking of magick, 
     it is not the fact 
          that a spell is used…
It’s the intent of the person or entity 
     working the spell, 
          that determines 
               whether it is good, 
                    or white magick…
Or if it is evil or
     black magick… 

I shall return, 
     with more messages, 
          when they are given…

It is I, 
     the muse…
