It is I, 
     the muse…
I have returned,
     once again…

This time with an even more
     controversial message…
It is one on how
     the battles will be fought,
          and hopefully won…

You have heard me tell 
     of the fight, not against flesh and blood,
          but between Principalities of Power… 
A decidedly more difficult fight… 

Sometimes you don’t even know 
     who, or what, or where
          you’re fighting…
If you even know,
     you just simply know you are… 

The battles will be fought, 
     sometimes,
          within our own minds…
These battles are for control, 
     and belief, and can be Invisible… 

Sometimes they’ll be fought
     between two entities,
          sword to sword… 
One human, one not… 
But they won’t be fair fights,
     for the demons 
          will have advantages
               the humans don’t have… 

But first the battles 
     will be between armies…
Both thinking they’re right…
And who knows, 
     they may be, 
          within their own cultures 
               and belief systems
                    believe me, the Evil ones 
                         will use anything…

Beware of the things you have 
     always wondered about, 
          if there were such things as magick…
For there is, 
     and it could be 
          your worst nightmare… 
For magick works in ways 
     you don’t even realize… 

Through the black arts,
     also known as black magick,
          Lucifer turns those 
               who could do him 
                    the most damage, 
                         against each other, 
                              through disbelief, discord, etc…
For “a house divided against itself, cannot stand” 

For he knows, 
     that if you don’t believe 
          he truly exists, 
               or that his power 
                    is as great as it truly is, 
                         then half his battle 
                              is already fought for him… 

Magick is not something only out 
     of a strange fiction novel,
          neither are all the other 
               so-called psychic powers, 
                    they do truly exist, 
                         within us all,
                              along with our other gifts…
But Lucifer knows, 
     as long as he can keep us 
          disbelieving they even truly exist, 
               we can’t or won’t use them against him… 

I don’t profess to say 
     that all persons who “see” things 
          are sane, but, who’s to know? 
It is written, 
     that a prophet 
          is not to be believed, 
               in his own home, 
                    but far and wide, 
                         the good which can come of it, 
                              can be far reaching… 

Another advantage 
     that Lucifer has is this, 
          the battle lines, in our mortal minds, 
               are not clearly defined…
It would be so easy 
     if we knew exactly 
          who, what, when, where and why 
               we are fighting… 

Sometimes Lucifer’s minions 
     will “possess” persons 
          in positions of power, 
               Law enforcement, Judicial, Medical, or others…
To fight against those 
     Lucifer has determined 
          that are dangerous 
               to his cause…
Those “persons” could 
     arrest, convict, lock away, or commit 
          people who speak out 
               against his cause… 
He doesn’t really care 
     what happens to them,
          as long as, 
               they’re out of his way… 

So, be on guard, 
     this is what we’re fighting… 
Even though one day, 
     possibly soon, 
          the fight will turn physical, 
               and most likely, bloody… 

It matters not what faith, 
     or religion you follow…
Because we’re all fighting 
     for the same thing, 
          truth, justice, and goodness 
               for all mankind…
