title: "In Memoriam"

It’s been so long,
     since last I saw you…
Perhaps one day,
     we will see each other,
          once again…

I remember so much,
     now, of what we had…
It was so special,
     so loving, such a wonderful life,
          together… 

My life has been
     somewhat of a shambles
          since then…
I’m getting better,
     my love,
          I truly am…

I’ve made many mistakes,
     many wrong turns…
I’ve lost out
     on “good” opportunities,
          jumped in on “bad” ones…
But I guess that’s life,
     we make mistakes…

As I said many times,
     from right after your death…
I’ll always love you…
I’ll always miss you…
I’ll always wish it hadn’t happened…
And I’ll always wonder
     what was coming next…

To you, I say, 
     in loving memory,
          of you, my wife… 

You will never be far
     from my thoughts…
Your name will always
     be near my tongue…
Rest assured, you will never
     be forgotten…

Please wait for me…
Come for me,
     when my day comes…

Where we will spend
     the rest of eternity,
          together…

You and I…
