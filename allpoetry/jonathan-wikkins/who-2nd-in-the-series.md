It is I, 
     the muse…
I have returned,
     once again…

This time with 
     my most controversial writings, 
          to date… 

Now is the time, 
     I shall reveal 
          who is to fight 
               this fight, 
                    for the Earth… 
It goes far beyond 
     what you’ve been taught,
          all your life… 

For in this fight, 
     it is not the Christians 
          against everyone else, 
               as you might believe
                    or may have been taught… 
Although, we are making way 
     for Jesus’ return… 
That is true to what you believe… 
But it is not just the Christians 
     that are the only 
          people of God…
Others are too… 

Before you continue,
     take a look 
          at your individual faith…
               because, I’m about to shock you…

NO ONE believes more in God,
     and His Power, 
          than a true follower
               of Lucifer… 

For it is His Power,
     that they will be fighting…
And they know more 
     about what we have, 
          against them,
               than we even know 
                    about what we have, 
                         against those forces of Evil, 

So it is time, 
     for those of us 
          destined to fight, 
               to awaken… 
Join together for the fight…
The fight for our souls 
     and the planet itself… 
That is only one 
     of the reasons, 
          the outcome is 
               so uncertain…
But, there are others… 

As most of you have learned
     through most of your individual faiths, 
In the beginning, 
     there was God…
Who is also known as Allah, Zeus,
     and by many other names… 
But the concept, 
     is still the same…
The universal principal 
     of 
          Truth, Goodness, and Light…

As some of you may know,
     all faiths have 
          “helpers” of the Supreme Powerful…
Some faiths called them 
Gods and Goddesses, 
Prophets, 
Angels, 
Disciples, 
Saints, 
and 
many others… 
INCLUDING THE CHRISTIAN RELIGION… 

The ONLY difference is this, 
     the NAMES…
Yet, the principles are the same,
     Truth, Justice, and the Way… 

All those faiths, 
     including the so called
          Pagan religions, 
               including Wicca, Druidism, Buddhist, 
                    and many others, 
                         have one thing in common…
And that is to fight Evil, 
     wherever it is found… 
For all those faiths 
     have the same ultimate goal, 
          Paradise… 
The ONLY difference 
     is the path taken… 

For if you take 
     a serious look 
          at those religions…
You will find there is 
     little difference
          between their principles,
              and those
                   of the Christian faith… 
They ALL believe in being good
     to and for one another…
And taking care of this planet
     the way it SHOULD be… 

I shall end this now,
     and allow you to digest
          what I’ve written here… 

For in the next writing, 
     I shall detail 
          what we are up against…

And it is truly scary… 

It is I, 
     the muse…

I shall return…
