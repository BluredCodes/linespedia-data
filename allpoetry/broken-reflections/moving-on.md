---
title: "Moving On?"
writer: "Broken Reflections"
slug: "moving-on"
source: "AllPoetry"

Basically it feels as if im holding on to something that was never there
That the love i feel so deep inside, will go forever unreturned
So i swallow my pride, and walk away with my heart,
Never letting you see inside the person you may have at one time loved
So im sorry, but without you, i just have to move on,
If i couldnt find love with you, i must go find it within someone else
And i want you to know that me leaving isnt completly your fault
But being your friend just hurts to much,
Seeing you, talking, touching, being around you kills me inside
And though you, i just have to move on without you,
Or should i leave the decsion up to you?
