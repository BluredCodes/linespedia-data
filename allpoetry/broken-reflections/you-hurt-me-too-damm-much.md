title: "You Hurt Me Too Damm Much"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

I never thought the day would come,.
That I would have to be the one to say good-bye,
If anything I thought you would be the one to leave me,
But I now  realize that I have to leave,
Because frankly,
You hurt me to damn much,
I thought I could love you despite all the mind games,
Through all the pushing away, ,
I thought that I could love just you 
I even promised you that I would stay with you no matter what,
I just wanted my love to heal you, 
Not change you,
But you never gave me a chance,
You didn’t even try to meet me half way,
I was the always the one loving,
And always the one getting hurt
But you know what
I can at least say that I tried,
But like everyone else,
I have to give up on you,
I just have to say good-bye,
Because  frankly,
Being with you, 
Hurts me too damn much.
