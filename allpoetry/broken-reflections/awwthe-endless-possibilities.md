You say so confidently that you could do things,
Do things to my mind and body thats never beeen done before.
That you could put your tounge in places that i never even knew existed,
That after onr taste, 
I wont need or want anything else ever again
And i must admit that i like what i hear,
That you have me wondering, thinking,...
About endless positions,
I mean about our endless possibilities
You know,
About the things you could do to me,
The things that i would let you do to me,
Because i have no problem with submission,
I mean with trying new things,
Because my imagination is running wild with the things i could do to you
About how you could......
And than i would.........
And than we should.......
