---
title: "When The Day Comes"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

When the day comes that you decide, 
That you are once again in love with me, 
I want you to know right now, 
That i wont be waiting for you 
The truth is i hope that im with someone else 
So that you can lay in your bed,..alone 
Only thinking about me, 
Kissing, touching, and holding someone else 
Looking into their eyes, 
And promising them forever, 
A forever that was once yours, 
So when you do decide that once again your in love with me 
Don't concern or worry yourself with telling me 
Because ill be a lil' to buzy, 
Loving someone else a whole lot more than i ever loved you 
I just cant wait for that day to come
