title: "Im Not Asking For Much"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Im not asking you For much,
Just for you to look into my eyes,
And tell me that you no longer love me,
And i promise that not a moment later i will walk away,
That i will never look back,
So if dont want me,
If you dont need me,
As much as you say that you do,
Than saying that you no longer love me shouldnt be a problem
Right?
