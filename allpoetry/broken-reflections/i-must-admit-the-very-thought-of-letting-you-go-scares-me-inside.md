I must admit the very thought of letting you go scares me inside
But holding on to something that i know won't last,
Will hurt me much worse later
Because the eyes that once promised me forever,
Are now the same eyes that cant even promise me tomorrowshort sweet to the point, or just bad poem , i dont know
