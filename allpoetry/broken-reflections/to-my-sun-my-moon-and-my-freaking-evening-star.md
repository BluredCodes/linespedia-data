I promised you that no matter,
I would never hurt you and i would never leave you,
And though no matter how hard you pushed away,
No matter how much you hurt me,
I never did anything in return because i loved you,
And now you tell me that you no longer love me,
That you no longer care about how i feel,
Well did you ever care?,
Did ever even love me?,
Or did you just use me to pass the time,
Did you just see me here for your damm entertainment,
Because i loved you,
If you asked me for the world,
I would have spent my whole life trying to obtain it for you
You were my sun, my moon, my freaking evening star
And in return you played me like a clown
