title: "To Hear Nothing But Your Heartbeat"

Dont promise me the moon and the stars,
Dont try to give me the world,
Just give me your smile,
Let me have your heart
And if you want to promise me anything,
Than promise me your forever,
And that will be enough,
Because the moon and the stars belong in the sky,
And theres not much that i could do with the world,
Just give me your everything,
Let me be all you need,
And promise me that each nite i will lay next to you,
And hear nothing but your heartbeat
