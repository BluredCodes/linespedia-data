---
title: "Im Afriad To Be Alone"
writer: "Broken Reflections"
slug: "im-afriad-to-be-alone"

Im so use to being strong,
Because i cant take a chance on being weak,
I cant allow myself to be hurt,
I cant allow myself to feel,
But now im asking you without hesitaion,
To just hold me,
Please just hold me and do nothing else,
Because i need this cold heart to feel,
I need to finally allow myself to cry,
Inside your arms i need to heal,
Because im so afriad of being alonei know its not that good, but i try
