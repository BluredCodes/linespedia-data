I mean I tried,
Lord knows  how hard I tried
But I just don’t love you,
I twisted my heart in almost every direction,
I tired to make myself feel something if anything when I kiss you,
I tired to make your touch bring me some type of emotion
When I look in to your eyes,
I don’t see anything I want,
I basically feel just about… nothing,
The plainest truth is, 
You just don’t do anything for me,
And I tried,
Believe me,
I really, really, tried 
But I really just … don’t  love you
So could you  please stop saying that you love mei really really did try
