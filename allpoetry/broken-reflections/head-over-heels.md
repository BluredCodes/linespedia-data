---
title: "Head Over Heels"
writer: "Broken Reflections"
slug: "head-over-heels"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59752-Head-Over-Heels-by-Broken-Reflections"
---

For the First time in my life,
I'm not afraid of being hurt,
Not by anything,
Or even by  this special someone,
Who seem to  have captured my heart in more ways than they will ever know,
Who has dreaming thing, I never thought I would dream,
Doing things I never thought I would do,
And feeling things I didn’t even know existed,
Who simply enough has head over heels in love
