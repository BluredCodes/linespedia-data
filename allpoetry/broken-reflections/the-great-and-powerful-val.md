Hey dont you know who i am,
I am the great and powerful val
No emotions,
No feeling,
Nothing comes in,
Nothing goes out,
I dont belive in love,
I dont take the time to care about happiness
So why do you feel that you have to change that,
Why do you say that its ok to trust you,
That its alrite to love you,
Because i dont need your...
Oh i kinda like you toouch,
And i dont want your.....
Could you please kiss me again?
Hey wait,
Dont you know who i am,
I am the great and powerful val
The great and powerful val,
In love
