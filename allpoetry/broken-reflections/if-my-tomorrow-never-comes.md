If my tomorrow never comes,
Well than,
That  would really be bad,
There so much I have yet to experience,
Things to see,
Places to go ,
Um , people to do,
Since I have happily kept my virginity,
For Mr. or Miss. right, (or at least  half decent)
I mean it would be bad if I die before I get some,
So if my tomorrow was to never come,
Well than damn,
 That would really, really sucki dont know, im bored
