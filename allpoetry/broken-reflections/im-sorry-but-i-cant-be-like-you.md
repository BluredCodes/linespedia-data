Im sorry that i can't be more like you,
That i only know how to dream with my eyes open,

Because i see the world for what is,
Why you try to fix it into something that it could never be,

But all my heros already dies,
I already know the truth behind Santa's Claus
And the easter bunny hasnt visited my house for all long time

So im sorry, but i can never be like you
I kinda like dreaming with my eyes open

Because while you work so hard to fix the world,
I'll sit back and enjoyed it for what it isi know this sux, so its ok
