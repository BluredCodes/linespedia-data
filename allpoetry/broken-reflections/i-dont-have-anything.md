title: "I Dont Have Anything"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

I don’t have everything
A matter a fact I don’t have anything,
But a broken heart,
And a bunch of shattered dreams,
Though I  sometimes wish that I could give you something more,
Like my never ending trust,
And my everlasting love,
 I cant, and I wont
Because I don’t know how to,
And I don’t think I ever will,
Because even though  I want so bad you give you my everything,
The sad fact is I don’t have anything,
But a unfixable broken heart,
And a bunch of  burnt ashes,
That I once called dreams
