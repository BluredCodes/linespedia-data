title: "A Heart Not Worth Having"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Let it be known,
That I don’t have a heart worth having,
And of anyone wants it,
It's pretty much up for sale,
Because it doesn’t do much but get you into trouble,
And if you believe in it to light your way,
You will soon find out that you were better off lost in darkness,
So let it be known, that for now and forever,
I have a heart not worth having,
So if anyone wants it,
Or believes that they could do something with it,
I start the bid at $5
