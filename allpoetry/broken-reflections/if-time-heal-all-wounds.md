I never thought that I would fall in love
Or at least thought I had the time to,
But when I heard you say that you loved me
I gave you  my heart in that exact minute,
And in the second we were through

But am I  wrong for still wanting something more
For wishing that things were back to the way they were before
Because without you I feel like I'm half losing my mind
And if time heals all wounds?
Than why hasn't  it yet healed mine?

Because  I keep hearing your voice over and over in my mind
I wake up each morning still thinking your there
Because I miss your touch, I miss your kiss,
I spend nights and days thinking about how to get you back
Hoping inside that your heart still cares

But am I wrong for still wanting something more
For wishing that things could be the way they were before
Because without you I feel like I' m losing  what's  left of my mind
And if people keep telling me that time heals all wounds?
