---
title: "The Best Thing I Never Had"
writer: "Broken Reflections"
slug: "the-best-thing-i-never-had"

Even though you say that you love me,
I knew that i never had your whole heart,
That no matter how hard i tried,
You never felt free inside my arms,
So i know inside that i must let you go,
And if you return,
Than i know that we are truly in love,
That i will now and forever have your heart,
But if you dont return,
I want you to know that no matter what i do,
No matter what i say,
You were the best thing i never hadI hope this is somewhat of a good poem,
