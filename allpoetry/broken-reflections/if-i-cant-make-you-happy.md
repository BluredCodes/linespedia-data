If I cant make you  happy,
Than tell me, who can?
Because I tried,
But you won't let anyone close enough to get a chance to,
Do you even know what happiness is?
Because I don’t think you do,
And I think that’s why you fear it,
I think that’s why you don’t trust it,
Or I at least I know,
You trust it as much as you trust me,
So if I can't make you happy,
Well than answer this question,
Who can?,
Because no matter how hard I try,
