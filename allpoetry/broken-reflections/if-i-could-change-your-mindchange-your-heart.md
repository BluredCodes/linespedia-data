I wish that i could change your heart,
But more than anything,
I wish that i could change your heart,
Because before you knew me,
Before you even shook my hand,
You already already assumed who i am,
You already assumed what i was about,
If I was too black, 
If I was too white,
If I wasnt enough,
Before i even had a chance to sat hello,
You already determine my wealth,
Already figured i wasn't worth your time,
So i wish that i could open your mind,
But more than anything in this world,
I wish that i could open your heart,
But your not ready, so i cant
But I hope one day God soon will
