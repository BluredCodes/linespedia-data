title: "To My Friend"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Your my friend and i know thats the way it has to be,
Because i dont know what it will take for you to see,
That my deep and dark secret is that im in love with you,
So deep in love that i dont know what to do,
I wish that i could tell you, but i dont know what words to say
Scared you will only see me as a friend, scared you will only push me away
So i sit next to you so quietly,
Hoping one day that you will notice me,
Because you are my friend and i want to tell you whats on my mind, 
What lies inside my heart,
Yet there is so many way to tell you that i dont know where to start,
But you are my friend, and thats the way it has to be,
Because i dont what it will take for you to love meTo my friend, you know who you are
