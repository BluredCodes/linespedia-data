You are so right,
I am dumb, 
I am so stupid,
For allowing myself to fall in love with you,
I mean I should have known better,
I should have known that you don’t even  know how to love,
Sadly enough,
You don’t even know how to feel,
That inside that cold heart,
All there is  more ice,
All there is more pain,
And despite everything,
I tried to look past that,
I tried to love you anyway,
And all you did was hurt me,
You couldn’t  even give me a chance,
So you are so right,
I am dumb,
Beyond all that I am stupid,
For believing in you,
For having faith in you,
