Im trying really hard not to cry,
Im trying just as hard not to feel,
Because without you i feel so lost,
Without you i feel as if i will always be alone,
But i have to come to terms with the fact that you never loved me,
That the only person you ever loved was yourself,
And that the only thing that you were good for,
Was causing me pain,
That for some strange reason the person you said you loved,
Was the same person you took so much pleasure in hurting,
But right now im trying very hard not to cry,
Im not giving you the satifaction of knowing,
That once again you have made me cryok i know its bad, but what can i say
