title: "[Why arent you here with me?,]"

Why arent you here with me?, 
Why cant i touch you, feel you?, 
Why must i long for you to be in my arms?, 
Why I must live day after day, 
Nite after nite without you? 
Tell me, 
Why arent you here with me?, 
Why cant i love you, kiss you?, 
Why must i long to lay inside your arms?
