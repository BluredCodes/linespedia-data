title: "You Dont Have To Stay"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

I never asked you to fall back in love with me,
I never asked you to stay,
Your the one that promised not to leave me,
But if you want to go so bad,
Theres the door, im not stoping you
Its not as if i belived you when you made that promise
Because no one stays ,
Everyone leaves me,
What made you think that you were so differnt?,
What made you think that you could stay in love with me?,
What makes you believe that i could love anyone in return?
So if you want to leave,
Go ahead, im not going to stop you,
As long as you know,
That when you walk out that door,
Theres no comming back
