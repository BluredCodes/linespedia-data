If you ever loved me,
At any time,
At any moment, 
In your in entire life,
Than you would listen to me,
when i ask you,
To just please walk away,
You dont need to ask me any questions,
No if, and, buts, ot why's
No explanation would be needed,
Because if you ever loved me,
At any time, 
At any small moment,
In your whole entier life,
Without a second thought,
You would just simply walk awayok i dont where this came from, but its written so i dont know
