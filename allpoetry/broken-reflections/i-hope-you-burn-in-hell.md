I hope that you burn in hell
I hope that you really, really die
I hope that you end up in pain
I hope one day someone makes you cry

I hope that you burn in hell
I hope someone falls in love with you again
I hope they turn around and tell you that they no longer love you
I hope they say they just want to be your friend

I hope that you burn in hell,
I hope your whole world crashes around you
I hope that someone treies to sleep with you, and than leaves you
I hope someone makes you feel the same way i do

I hope that you burn foever in hell
I hope that you really, really, really die
I hope that you spend the rest of your life in pain
I hope im there for the day someone make you cryTHIS WAS ORGINALLY WRITTEN FOR SOMEONE ELSE, BUT NOW SINCE THIS CERTAIN PERSON REALLY PISSED ME OFF, I REVISED IT FOR HIM
