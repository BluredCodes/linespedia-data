Hey love,
I know i havent known you that long,
And you know how i hate to be so forward,
But i just have to tell you right quick,
That i love your smile,
I love your eyes,
Let me just cut to the chase,
I think im in love with you,
Its like when i look at you,
I see your soul,
And it speaks the most beautiful things to me,
Your heart fills me up and sets me free,
Without doing anything at all,
And when someone like me ,
Feels somthing like that,
I just have to compliment,
So love,
While im sitting here talking, 
Have something that would interest you,
One thing that would interest you in me,
Because i dont think that i could live one more day without you
I mean i dont think that i could go on without an answer,
So tell how you feel, becasue either way i have to know,
Because though i hate to come on so strong,
And you know how i hate to be so forward,
But if you dont love me,
Let me stop talking,
let me at least walk away with my pride,
If i cant walk away with your heartits not my usual style, but i guess it will do, i dont know
