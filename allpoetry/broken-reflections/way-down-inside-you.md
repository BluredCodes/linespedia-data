---
title: "Way Down Inside You"
writer: "Broken Reflections"
slug: "way-down-inside-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

When i look way down inside you,
Beyond the masks,
Beyond the scares of pain,
Way beyond the cold person you pretend to be,
I see a heart,
I see the most beautiful soul,
But most os all i see the person i fell in love with,
It just to bad that when you look inside yourself,
You dont see the same things I do
