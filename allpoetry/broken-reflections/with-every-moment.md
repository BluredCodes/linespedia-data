---
title: "With Every Moment"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Please forgive me for saying this, 
But I think that I'm starting to fear your presence , 
I think im starting to fear your touch                      
Because every time I see you, 
Every time you touch me, 
It's as if I can't speak, 
As if  I can't  even breathe
So than I can't help but want you, 
Can't help but listen to my heart as it cries out for you
Because I can honestly say,
 That I never meet anyone who brought me so much pleasure,
Whose single caressing finger can  heighten every one of my five senses, 
And though I  still  fear  it,
I can't explain the trill  I get each time I feel the slightest  stroke of your hand
The sensational  enjoyment your touch sends throughout  my soul
But the problem is,
How do you tell someone who at one time meant nothing to you,
That you can't live without them
That everything he says, 
Everything he does,  
Means so much more than you can express, 
So  much more than you can define,
How can you so casually, so easily, call him your friend, 
When everything inside of  you desires for something more 
Because with every moment  I'm with you,
I want to captivate your mind, 
Fascinate  your soul, 
Mesmerize the very core of your being,
I just simply want to be your everything .
