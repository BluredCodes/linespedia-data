---
title: "To little of Too Much"
writer: "Broken Reflections"
slug: "to-little-of-too-much"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58894-To-little-of-Too-Much-by-Broken-Reflections"
---

So much pain,
So much hurt,
So much misery,
So much loneliness,
So much of nothing at all,

Because it hurts to feel,
It even starting to hurt  when I write,
Nothing seems to make sense,
Nothing ever really seems to matter

Because I'm dying inside,
Sadly enough I might already be dead,
Because I feel nothing within,
I don’t even think I know how to feel,

Because there's  too much pain,
To much hurt,
To much misery,
With just so little company ,
I have just a little too much of nothing at all
