What would make me happy??
Do You really want to know?,
Let's see, ummmmmm,
Everyone leaving me the hell alone,
Stop making my decsion,
Quick telling me how to feel,
And telling me that the few feelings i do have are wrong,
Better yet stop telling me that you know how i feel
Because,...wow...you dont,
Your not me,
So stop trying to fix me,
Thers nothing wrong with me,
Maybe theres something wrong with you,
Fix your own damm life,
Take care of your own damm problems,
Im not a barbie doll,
Or just another cute thing to play with,
I dont need you to help me,
I dont want your pity,
I dont want or need anything from anyone,
And once everyone figures that out,
Stop trying to save me,
And that will make me happy,
Or at least the closest i ever been
