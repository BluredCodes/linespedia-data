title: "Somewhat Human"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Im sorry if i tried to make you,
I dont know,...
Feel,
To act somewhat human,
To have an emotion,
To try to show you more than just hate,
To fill your heart up with something else besides pain,
I just wanted to give you something to hold on to,
Something to believe in,
For you to believe in me
And i guess that makes me less of a person,
At least in your eyes anyway,

So im sorry if i tried to make you feel something
If anything at all,
I just wanted you to act,...
i dont know,
Somewhat human
To just try to melt that cold heart,
That you try so hard to hold on to,
I just wanted to give you something to love,
Ijust wanted to try to love mehmmmmmmmm
