Have you ever woke up,
Wishing for a car to hit you?,
For a gun to find the back of your head?,
For the strength to just to slit your wrist?

Have you ever woke up,
Hoping to just die?,
To have someone poison you food?,
To just not see the light of another day?,

Have you just ever woke up,
Hoping for the pain to go away?
For the strength to smile through another day?
For yuor whole world to just please end?
