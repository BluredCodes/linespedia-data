I know i should'nt miss you,
That its wrong for me to even think about you,
But i can't stop,
Because no matter how hard i try,
Im still in love with you,
Even if your no longer in love with me,
Even though without tying,
You still hurt me,
I dont know if i could live without you,
I just keep thinking of all things that we could have done,
All things that we never said,
All the ways we could have tried to stay togther,
But you let me go, you let me down,
But most of all,
You hurt me,
And the sad part was that you didnt even care,
Now i know that i should'nt miss you,
That its so wrong for me to even miss you,
For me to even care that you are gone,
But no maater how hard i fight it,
No matter how bad i want it stop,
Deep down inside,
Im still in love with you
