Im not in the mood,
For who blames who,
Who hurt you,
Who should be sorry,
Who's right,
Who's wrong,
Why cant you do this?,
Why cant you do that?,
I want to go,
I need to stay,
I love you,
I want you,
But i dont love you,
But i need you,

Im really not in the mood,
For who blames who,
Who hurt who,
Who should be sorry,
Who's right,
Better yet who's wrong,
Come on,
Really...,
Lets not play the gamei dont know why i wrote this, it sounded good in my head
