---
title: "Im Way Beyond You"
writer: "Broken Reflections"
slug: "im-way-beyond-you"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61813-Im-Way-Beyond-You-by-Broken-Reflections"

Could you please stop pretending to be 20 million people
And for once just try to be you
If you even know who you are,
Because im tired of all the games,
I mean just because I don’t analyze things,
I don’t get mad every time you say a nasty comment
Doesn’t make me slow, stupid or dumb,
It just makes me an adult,
And makes you look more like a child,
Oh and by the way could you please stop throwing the fact,
That I don’t know or understand you in my face,
Maybe if you stopped playing all the games,
And give me a chance to get close to you, I would
But I'm fed with trying,
So don’t worry yourself with trying to change,
I mean not that you ever cared enough about me to try,
But that’s  ok ,
Because I'm beyond all the high school games,
But most of all I'm way beyond you.
