title: "I Cant  Write"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

I cant write,
I cant feel,
i cant do anything
No words in my head to write on paper
No emotions to analize,
Nothing is comming in,
Nothing is flowing out
I cant write,
I cant feel,
I cant do anythingi dont know what this means, im just bored
