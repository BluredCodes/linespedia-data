title: "Can We Please Not Fight"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Lets not fight,
Lets not argue,
For once can we just chill,
And talk about simple things,
Back when days were simpler,
Before love was a problem,
Before feeling got in the way of judgement
Back when all i wanted to do was make you smile,
And all you did was make me happy,
When nothing mattered but the moment,
And tomrrow was so far away in our minds,
Can we please not fight,
Im so not in the mood to argue,
For once can we just lay back and chill,
And talk about more simplistic things,
Back when days when simple,
Can we go back,
To when we were just friends
