---
title: "Be Weary"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Please be weary of those who only show you one side of the world,
Because its usally only there side they are showing you,
They will break you down untill you dont remember who you are anymore
And take away from you what they dont understand
They just want to bask in your glow,untill your star fades away,
And as soon as it does, so will they,
So they can move to the next victim, the next smiling face,
Because in this world when your unique, your frown upon
But yet none of them know the defination of normal,
Funny enough no ones does,
So be weary of those who only show you only one side of this world,
Because its usally only thier side there showing you
