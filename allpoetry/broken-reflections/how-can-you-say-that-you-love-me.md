title: "How Can You Say That You Love Me?"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

How can you say that you love me,
That you need me,
Sit there and beg me to stay,
And than turn around and cause me so pain ?,
I love you, without question, without doubt,
But you hurt me to much,
You make me feel as if im in love alone,
As if im the only one feeling anything,
Sometimes i feel so lonely for you,
My heart longs for a kiss,
Just for a simple touch,
And in return you just push me away,
In return you give me nothing,
So how can tell me that you love me,
Say that you want me,
That you need me so stay in your life
And than just turn around,
And just cause me so much pain?
