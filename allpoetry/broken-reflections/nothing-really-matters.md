Nothing really matters,
Why cant anyone in this world see,
That no matter how hard you try,
No matter how much you give of yourself,
The people you love, wont always love you back 
The people you want, wont always want you,
And now matter how much you try,
Some poeple in this world were just meant to be alone
So nothing really matters,
Why cant everyone in this world see,
That nothing really matters,
Nothing really matters at alli have no idea what im talking about, i was just writing
