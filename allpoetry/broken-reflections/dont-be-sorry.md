Tell me, Why are you sorry?
As far as i can see, you didnt do anything wrong
Its not your fault that you no longer love me,
To tell you the truth, its mine
Becasue i knew better than to belive in love and happiness 
I knew that i cant trust anyone to stay,
That you cant trust anyone period
So what are you sorry for?
You did nothing more than try to melt my cold heart
Dont be sad because like everyone else you failedok this is wack poem, but i dont know i wrote itanyway
