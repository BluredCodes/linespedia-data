---
title: "Why Won't  You Try???"
writer: "Broken Reflections"
slug: "why-wont-you-try"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57866-Why-Wont--You-Try----by-Broken-Reflections"
---

It seems like I'm the only one trying,
The only one fighting,
To keep us together
I guess forever was just a little to long to ask for
But I still want you to stay`
But lately you keep pushing me away
Am I wrong for wanting my friend again
Why must you make things so tough
Inside I feel as if I had enough
Are you worth me fighting for?
I really don’t know anymore,
Because I'm losing my mind without you,
I just can't compute in my mind that were through
Because I still love you,
I still need you around,
So tell me why you feel  like you can't stay
Why must you try to keep pushing  me away,
Why won't you try as hard to keep us together
Or is forever a little to long to ask of you,
Because I feel as if I'm the only one trying
The only one fighting,
To make things right
