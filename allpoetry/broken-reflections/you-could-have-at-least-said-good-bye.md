So what if your gone,
So what if you left,
I mean everyone leaves me,
I didnt expexct you to be differnt, 
But you were,
Because at least everyone else said good-bye,
At least they told  me what i did to push them away,
But not you, 
You walked away without saying a word,
You just walked away and never turned back,
You left me in pain,
You left me alone,
But you promised you will always be there for me,
And now that i need you,
Where are you,
I loved you,
And i never loved anyone before,
or loved anyone who has hurt me so much
But that all right,
Because i dont need you anymore
I dont need anyone,
So what if your gone,
So if you left me,
Its not like i care,
Everyone leaves me eventually,
I really didnt expect you at all to be differnt,
But for some strange reason you were,
Because when you left,
You also took my heart,
When you left,
You didnt even say good-byeThe pain of losing the one you love
