Hey wait,.....
You left me,
You left me feeling so alone,
Left me in so pain, longing for you to come back
And now you tell me that you were wrong,
That you need me,
That "baby i still love you"
But when i needed you, you werent there,
When i creied, when i was hurting,
Your shoulder was no where to be found,
So dont be mad that i dont want to take you back
Because wait,...
You left me,... remember,
You lef me feeling so alone,
Left me feeling so cold and empty imside,
So dont be sad that i went and found someone else
