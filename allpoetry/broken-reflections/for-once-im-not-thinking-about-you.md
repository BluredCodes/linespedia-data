---
title: "For Once Im Not Thinking About You"
writer: "Broken Reflections"
slug: "for-once-im-not-thinking-about-you"

I woke up,
Took a shower,
Brushed my teeth,
Made me some breakfast (frosted flakes)
And for once I'm not thinking about you,

Did some chores,
Wrote some poems (not about you)
Watched some T.V,
Called up some friends,
But I didn’t call you,
Because for once I'm not thinking about you

Hung out with some friends,
Went to see a movie,
One with a sad love theme,
But that’s quite alright,
Because for once I'm not thinking about you

Go home,
Make some dinner (frosted flakes)
Brush my teeth,
Lay in my bed, thinking about how successful I was,
How for a whole day I only thought about…not…thinking about…..you
Damm!!!!
