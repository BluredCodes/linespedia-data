I have no reason to believe that you ever loved me,
Im not sure if you even liked me,
But i am sure that you used me,
That i allowed myself to be used,
Because i believed that you loved me,
I believed you when you said that you would never hurt me,
But right before my very eyes you played me like a fool,
Right in front of everyone you destroyed me,
But yet i continue to come back for more,
It was like i was addicted to you,
Maybe i just like you making me hurt,
But i was in love,
And love makes you do some crazy things,
