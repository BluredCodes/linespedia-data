I have no greater want or need in this world,
Than to be by your side,
And the fact that I cant be,
Tears me up inside, more than you will ever know
Because I would live to hold you,
Die to kiss you,
I would do anything,
Just to lay beside you,
To wtach you as sleep
But your there, and I'm here,
And we lead two completely  different lives,
But yet our hearts beats as one,
And though you don’t care that I love you,
Or at least you don’t feel the same way,
I don’t know,
It really doesn’t bother me,
Because I have no plans of changing how  I feel about you
Or at least wait for the day finally hits,
And you realize that you feel the same wayIt amazing that i wanted to revised this poem , when the girl i wrote it for, took my heart, broke it into a million pieces, and danced on each one
