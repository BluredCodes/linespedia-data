Why can't you be more like me?
Why can't you even try to see things my way?
Why can't you be more blunt?
Why can't you say what you think or feel,
As soon as you think and feel it?
Why must you play so many games?
Why must you play with my heart?
Why can't you see things more black and white,
Why the hell must everything have shades of gray with you?
Just explain to me,
Why can't you be more like me?
Why must you try so hard to be like you?
