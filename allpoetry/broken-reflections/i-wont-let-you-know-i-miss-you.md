Nope,
I wont,
I cant,
i promised myself that i wont call you,
That i wont talk to you,
And i can do it
I think,
I hope,
I have to,
I just have to,
i cant let you know that i miss you,
Ok,
So put down the phone 
what the hell are you doing,
Dont dial,
Dont be weak,
Ok phone is down,
I cant ,
i wont,
I dont need to call you,
i dont ned to hear your voice,
I can do this this,
I am strong, 
I can do this,
Oh dammit i cant,
Oh god i want you, 
i need you,
But i cant let you know,
I cant let you know that i miss youi dont know this is the things that running through my head, so i just wrote it down
