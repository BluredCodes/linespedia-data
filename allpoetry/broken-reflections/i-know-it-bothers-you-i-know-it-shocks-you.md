I know that it bothers you,
And i know that it shocks you,
That no matter what mean things you do,
No matter how many hurtful things you say,
Im not leaving,
That for as long as you need me,
You will never be alone,
Because behind the masks you wear to the world,
I see the beautiful person you are inside,
The beautiful person that i fell in love with,
Even though most people run from you,
I see you for who you really are
So go ahead,
Do your worse to me,
I really dont care,
Because it really doesnt bother me at all
But i know it really bothers you,
Deep down i know it shocks you,
No matter how many times you hurt me,
No matter how much you try to push me away,
Im not leaving,
Because in you arms,
