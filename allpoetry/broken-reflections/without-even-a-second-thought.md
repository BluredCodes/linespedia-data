---
title: "Without  Even A Second Thought"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Anything you ever asked of me,
Without a second thought,
I would have just given it,
My heart, have it,
My mind, dont need it,
My soul, well damm leave me with something
But as much as i have given,
I recieved nothing in returned,
Your heart, your love
Wasnt mine to have,
Though my soul longs to be intertwined with yours
My body yearns to be satisfied by your touch,
I know that i cant have you,
That you will never give me your heart,
But whatever you would have asked of me,
I would have done,
With out even a second thoughtok i know this bad, but its off the top of my head, so be kind
