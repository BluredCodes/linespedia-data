Please wake me when i am free
Because my creative mind cant bear captivity,
It seems like everyone is trying to fix me,
When the only person im trying to be is me,
But if they took one second to look, they'd see
That the only person i need to make to me happy is me
So could someone please wake me up when im finally free,
Bcause theres nothing nothing worse than a creative mind,
Trapped in captivityi hope i answerd your question
