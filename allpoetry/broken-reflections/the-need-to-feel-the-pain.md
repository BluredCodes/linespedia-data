---
title: "The Need To Feel The Pain"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Did you ever need the feel of pain?
To just have the sense of it, the taste of it ?,
To need it to the point where you were addicted to it,
Because inside there is nothing wrong with a lil' cut here,
With just a lil' cut there,
I just love to watch myself bleed,
I like the pleasure of pain,
To feel the comfort in each cut of the blade,
I just need to feel something,
To just keep cutting till I get closer to my wrist,
Because nothing but my loneliness seems real,
And nothing but my need to feel the pain matters
