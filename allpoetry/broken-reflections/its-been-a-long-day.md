Its been a long day, 
And i must say, 
Thats you all thats on my mind, 
All its keeps doing is rewinding, 
To the last time, 
To the last time you touched me, 
To the last time we kissed, 
The last time i felt so happy, 
I cant remember the last time i felt like this 
Your deep brown eyes, 
They just keep me calling me, 
Has my body and mind crazy for you, 
Has me so deep in love with you, 
That i cant explain, 
Dont wanna have to, 
Because its been along day, 
And i must say, 
Thats your all thats on my mind, 
And it just keeps rewinding, 
To the last time, 
To the last time you kissed me, 
To the last time i felt so free
