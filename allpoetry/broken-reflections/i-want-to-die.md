Right now,
At this exact moment,
I really want to die,
I really wish that i could just end it all,
I have no reason to live,
But to much strength to kill myself,
My belief in God stops me picking up the knife,
But my lack of faith had me close to my edge,
So right now,
At this very exact moment,
I really just want to die,
I want to kill myself,
I want to stop myself from seeing the light of another dayumm..i dont think im that depressed, so dont worry
