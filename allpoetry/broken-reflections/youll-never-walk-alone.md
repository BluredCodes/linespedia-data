---
title: "You'll Never Walk Alone"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

I know along the way your gentle has been broken
That the people you loved, weren't always the right people to trust
But no matter how bad I want to,
I can't mend your broken heart,
I can only promise to be there for you, whenever you call,
To stand by you, 
To always love the quiet, sweet, and sometimes insecure guy,
With the most beautiful smile
Because just as much as you say I inspire you,
You don’t know this, but you also inspire me,
To not just become a better writer,
But to become a better person,
So if at anytime you ever feel lonely,
Just look to your side,
And realize that with me,
You'll never walk aloneThis is for buddy, you know who you are
