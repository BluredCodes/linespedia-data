Im begging you, 
Please dont give up on me,
Please dont leave me,
I need you,
I want you
I have to have you in everyway,
Dont listen to the negitive things i say,
Dont pay attention to the hurtful things i do
I dont mean them,
I swear, i promise,
I never wanted to cause you any pain
Please come back,
Im begging you,
I am pleading with you,
To just dont give up on me,
Please dont leave me,
Because i need you,
I want you,
I love you,
I cant live without you,
Please, please, please
No matter what,
Dont give up on medont pay attention to me, i dont know what im talking about
