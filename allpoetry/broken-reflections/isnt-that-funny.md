title: "Isnt That Funny"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Its funny how the ones who say they love you 
The ones who promise to stay with you forever 
Who say that they will weather the paroles of any strom for you 
Are the first ones to leave the minute the storm comes 
The first ones to hurt you, 
The first ones to act like they dont know you, 
The minute you say how you feel,
 Well than, i guess foreve was a lil' to long for you 
Your heart, your pride was a lil' to much for my sensitivity 
That you could'nt say how you felt, 
Becasue you dont know how to feel, 
So you go around promising people the world, 
And when given the chance you break them,
 And than you walk away with the hearts and souls, 
Of the people you decided to leave behind, 
Isnt that funny,
Doesnt that make you wanna laugh?
