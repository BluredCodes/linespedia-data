So explain this to me,
You just woke up one morning,
And decided that you were no longer in love with me
Well wow,..that.. really... sucks for me, huh
But dont do me any favors,
By telling me that you still wanna be my friend,
Because i dont want to be your friend,
I want you,
And if i cant have you,
Than i want nothing to do with you
So please dont do me any favors by sticking around,
Because if you dont love me anymore,
Than why do i still need you aroundwell i know that sounds mean, but im a mean person,
