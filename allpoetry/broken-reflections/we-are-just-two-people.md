We are..
Two people in pain,
Two peope hurting,
Two people very lost,
And yet by some sick twist of fate,
We seemed to have found each other,
We seem to have found our own weird version of love,
The kind that keeps pushing away,
But always find it way back home,
The kind we just cant for some reason walk away from,
Because believe me we have tried,
And yet for some strange reason we cant,
 Because we are just two people,
Two people endless pain,
Two people who cant stop hurting,
To people who are very lost,
Who believe that we are meant to be alone,
