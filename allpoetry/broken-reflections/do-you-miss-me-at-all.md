Do you miss my voice?,
My touch?,
The cute things I would say?,
The small things I would do?
Do you miss me at all?
How many times do I cross your mind?
How much  do  you wish at this very moment I was laying beside you?
Am I your first thought as rise in morning?
Your last thought as you lay your head down at night?,
Do you think about the dreams we once had?,
The dreams you so quickly destroyed?
Do you sit around for hours staring at my picture?,
Running over every moment of us together in my mind?,
Am I still everything you ever wanted and more?,
Do you miss the warmth of my embrace?,
The fire of my kiss?,
Do you miss the very way i made you feel?,
Are you still wishing that you never  hurt me?
Do you know how much of a fool you were for letting  me go?
Because now that I am happy,
And well.. you’re the one all alone
I  quickly wonder to myself,
Do you miss me at all?
