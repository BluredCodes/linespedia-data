title: "So What If I Lied"
writer: "Broken Reflections"

Ok,…so what if I lied
So what if for the first time my heart wasn't true
I only wanted to keep us together
You act like my intentions were to pull us apart

So really,  don’t look at me like that,
Dammit you can still look me in the eye,
Don’t treat me like the enemy,
Like I'm not worthy to be in your presence 

Don’t tell me about how hurt you are
How you're not sure if you can trust me any more
I'm not in the mood to hear about how much pain I caused
Even though I know I'm far from the only one causing it

I mean really…so what if I lied,
I have no intentions on saying I'm sorry,
So please just stop acting like the victim,
Like your were the only one whose been lied to in this relationship
