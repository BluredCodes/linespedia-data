title: "[Call me selfish or self-centerd,]"

Call me selfish or self-centerd,
But i never wanted someone's touch,
Someone kiss,
Someone smile,
As much as i want yours,
I never dreamed about someone so much,
Needed someone so bad,
So call me selfish or self-centerd,
But i never wanted someone as much,
As i want youerrrr, this is bad.....
