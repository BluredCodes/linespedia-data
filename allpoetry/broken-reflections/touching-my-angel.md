---
title: "Touching My Angel"
writer: "Broken Reflections"
slug: "touching-my-angel"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59044-Touching-My-Angel-by-Broken-Reflections"
---

When I touch your face,
I feel like I'm touching my guardian angel,
When you touch my hand I know that I have found my soulmate,
When I look into your eyes,
You take my breath away,
Because you see inside my soul, see inside my heart
You mean the world to me, you make me feel so complete
When you're near me, I feel my loneliness slowly slip away,
You made me something that I never thought I could be,
You made me see the beauty I have inside,
You’re my shoulder to cry on,
My happiness when I'm sad,
My light in the worlds darkness,
with you by my side I know that hope is never to far away,
Because I know no matter where I go,
No matter what I do,
I know my angel won't be too far behind,
Because with you in my life, with you in my heart,
I know that I will never be lonely
