Have you ever went to sleep,
Wishing that tomorrow the sun would never touch your eyes?
Because when it does that mean you have to live another day,
Another day, in another pit of depression,
If you want to call it living , 
Because everything inside is already dead

Have you ever felt,
So cold, so empty,
so lost, so confused,
Too much pain,
Too much hurt,
Too much of nothing at all?

Have you ever just wanted to die?,
Because you finally relized thats theres nothing and nobody to live for?
That everyone and everthing you once did live for was a lie?
 
Have you ever went to sleep
