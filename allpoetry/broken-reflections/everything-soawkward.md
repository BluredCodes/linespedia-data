Maybe it just me,
But ever since we said,
That we dont love each other anymore,
It's been well......Awkward

We dont talk the same,
We dont act the same,
In one small moment,
Everything felt so ....Awkward

Is it me,
Is it you,
Did something change,
Did we change,
Because everything inside feels so....Awkward

Are we just friends,
Can we just be friends,
Is this what was meant to be,
Because nothing feels the right anymore,
Everything feels...so...so,
Well...AwkwardI know that is bad, but i wrote so oh well
