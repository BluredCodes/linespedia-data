When it comes to shattered love affairs-
They always end the same-
It really doesn’t matter,
Who’s at fault, or who’s to blame.

The pain is no less bitter,
If a person’s right or wrong-
The tears are no less painful-
And the night’s are no less long.

Why can’t we be an island-
With no need of other’s love?
Why can’t we walk a loner’s path-
When push turns into shove.

It really doesn’t matter…
Human’s hearts can’t stand the strain-
Perhaps that’s why we’re frightened…
To ever love again.
