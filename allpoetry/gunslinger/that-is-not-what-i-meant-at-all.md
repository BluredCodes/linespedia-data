* That is not what I meant at all
Nor thought to see a teardrop fall…
Alas, I find my tongue too sharp-
Too prone to criticize and harp.

I meant no harm, and that is true-
More so to such a one as you…
Who only kindnesses have shown,
The fairest I have ever known.

Alas, I find that once they’re said-
They fill the heart with nameless dread-
And trust and confidence mistreated…
From being hurt, have both retreated.

* That is not what I meant at all…
Now I am feeling very small…
I sorrow that I’ve made you cry-
And now I fear, it is “goodbye”.
