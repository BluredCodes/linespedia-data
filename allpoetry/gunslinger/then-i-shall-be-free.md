title: "Then I Shall Be Free"
writer: "Gunslinger"

I guess I rode too many trails-
Too many bridges burned...
Too many wrong decisions made-
Too much I haven't learned.

Too many times I thought I've loved-
To find that I was wrong.
The trail that I am traveling-
Will end before too long.

I once was young and reckless-
And now I'm old and hard...
A cynic, and a loner...
With his heart and body scarred.

My only friend, the gun I own-
On which I can depend...
It seems I've carried all my life,
And will until the end.

I know that he is waiting-
An old persistent foe...
Who's haunted me, and hounded me
For twenty years, or so...

Sometimes it seems he is so close,
I feel his fetid breath...
In case you haven't figured out,
I speak of old man Death.

Some people fear his boney hand-
And shun his company...
I seek that long awaited meet...
For then I shall be free.
