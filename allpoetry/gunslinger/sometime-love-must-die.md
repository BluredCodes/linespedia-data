title: "Sometime Love Must Die"
writer: "Gunslinger"

I thought about my feelings-
Then I tried to sort them out.
To put my thoughts in order...
Find out what life's all about.

Was what we, lightly, once called love-
A real and lasting thing?
Or was it merely passion?
A flighty, passing fling?

Then I recalled the tear drops-
The heartache and the loss,
When at last I realized-
The line I must not cross...

I'm sorry that I hurt us-
I fully take the blame...
There was a time or two I woke...
Up. Calling out your name.

I'm glad that we're still speaking-
Though we can feel the strain-
We keep our conversation light,
To keep from causing pain...

It's best to let the old wounds heal-
Let sleeping problems lie...
But still it is a tragedy-
That sometime love must die.
