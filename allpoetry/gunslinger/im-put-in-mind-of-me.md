---
title: "I'm Put In Mind of Me"
writer: "Gunslinger"

I thought about the years I spent-
Out on the open road.
When roadside parks, and culverts
Were the place of my abode.

When meals were few and far between-
But miles were nearly free...
And rodeo, gold buckle dreams-
Mattered most to me.

A million miles of highway-
A lot of eaten dirt...
I burned the candle from both ends-
And rode when I was hurt.

I never made the big time-
Like Frost, and Donnie Gay...
I guess I'm just an "Also rode"-
Who couldn't make it pay.

I wouldn't take a million bucks-
For all that I have learned...
Friends I met, and girls I loved,
And bridges I have burned.

Those days are gone forever-
But every time I see...
A cowboy going down the road-
I'm put in mind of me.
