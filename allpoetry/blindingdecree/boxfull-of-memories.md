Grasping the box covered in paper hearts
down from her closet it comes
so that now the "grieving" period starts
as she heads toward her bed

Boxfull of memories...
5 dried flowers from him
1 wrist band
10 pictures in a tiny album
6 movie stubs
countless pages of notes
1 letter written to him
1 gift bag from him
1 glowstick
1 score sheet from laser tag
1 receipt from his birthday gift
1 sheet of directions to his house from when I met him
2 notes
1 plastic wrap from the flowers
1 peice of paper with movies he wanted to see
1 brown bag
1 card
1 ticket from laser tag
1 peice of paper with cologne on it
in 1 boxfull of memories

1 girl left to swoon and cry
over loves lost
