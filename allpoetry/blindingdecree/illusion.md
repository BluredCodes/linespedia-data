paradise seeks me out, 
maybe im batting it away
illusions are my dreams, my fantasies
stuck in a world of a fairy tale
i want it all to be perfect
the sunsets, the oceans the waterfalls of emotion love will bring
drenched in the mirrage of love
i waded in the pool of hate
wishing upon a star
hoping it was the one
illusions of love in his eyes
parading in my mind
a glimps of the future,
now buried in time
now left with only the memory,
of warmth and a so-called-love
was it all an illusion, a dream that plagued my mind
now that it is gone
a nightmare, that is left to haunt."Love can sometimes be magic. But magic can sometimes...just be an illusion."
-Javan
