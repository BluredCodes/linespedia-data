Love 
admire care
 talking cherishing feeling 
suddenly blinded by decree
 crying hurting pondering
 hate fury 
Detest

Women
Considerate, sentimental
Gossiping, sharing, loving
In love with a man
Lying, loathing, neglecting
Fickle, heartless 
Men
