She walks in a shadow of fear
On the brim of death
she walks the tightrope between silence and madening screams
treading softly through this 
all-to-well-known forrest of forgotten love
now comes a discovery of blackness,
it engulfs her everymove
nothing here has survived
blacknes and wilderness all around
impervious to the shadows around her,
she trudges on
a howl in the background
a scream on the trail
darkness still engulfs her
satisfied with this and nothing more,
she sits upon the remainings of a lightening stricken tree
hands folded in her lap
eyes fixed on the path she has come from
a blank stare remains on her face
she will sit and wait here
for his answer
she will sit here for countless days
waiting for his gentle outline,
to apear between the darkness and the billowing trees
here she will remain,
here she will stay
here she will die.
