title: "I Dont Know Why..."

but you still hold a place in my heart... 
you remain here,
temping me with my memories,
klling me slowly,
you are here in my heart my mind my soul,
you will always be here,
in my tears
they sting my face like the pain you inflicted stung my soul,
I dont know why but i cant stop thinking about you
and oh how i wanted to see you tonight
but my heart is holding me back
for to see you laugh
or to see you look at me with hate in your eyes
that will kill me in a more slow and painful death
unknown to my already broken heart
whose tiny peices
are now swept up into my box of memories
tucked neatly under my bed
where i dream of the day when we will laugh once again...
