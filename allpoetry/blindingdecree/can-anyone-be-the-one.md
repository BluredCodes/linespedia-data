"You arent the one, but you are the only one who can make me feel like this, you arent to one, but you are the only one who can make me feel like shit!" -Foo Fighters


would you be my one?
be everything i want
everything that makes me love you
touching in gentle places
grasp for whats not there
tell me you love me
will you be my one?
love too ambiguous?
life to stressful?
people too controlling?
you are too paraniod

can just anyone be the one?
thats all i need
someone to be there
not only to care
but the share
to be there

can just anyone be my one?
thats all i need
someone to love me
the way love is supposed to be
just guarentee
you will there for me
on every degree
ingnore decree

Can anyone be my one?
thats all i need...
