faintly speaking
gently weeping...

I think i've been here before
I cant help that feeling
I know what is coming next
but I keep hoping, maybe there will be more...
I know what they will all say
I know more hearts are to be broken
I knew mine was next
I've had this feeling before
feeling felt before
words spoken before
tears cried before
doomed to repeat the past!
doomed to repeat the past...!
I've told them all 
Now I've shaped up
now i want more
I've felt this 
feeling in my heart
broken broken broken...I know it sucks, but ah well...
