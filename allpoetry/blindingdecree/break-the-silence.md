title: "Break The Silence"

Now it kills me to not hear your voice, 
to feel a slight warmth,
of breath next to my cheek
How it hurts 
to know you are out there somewhere,
away from me, 
living your life, 
Kill me slowly with silence, 
a life of solitude from you, 
and drag my heartbreak out, 
Forget what i meant to you, 
what you still mean to me,
forget all we ever shared,
and you say you never did
Turn your back on those who love you, 
only to share the petty pleasures in life
And leave me here alone and dying,
nolonger knowing the sweet taste of love, 
Please break this deafening silence,
and utter one word to me, 
just peasure me with the music that is your voice,
Break the silence,
And talk to me
