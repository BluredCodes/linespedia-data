You are so inferior to the lesser species you surround yourself with aren't you?
   Or Is that just what you think that because you don't wear pants and makeup and keep your hair long God will love you more?
 Or is it that you think that because you go to church 4 times a week and try to recruit everything that breaths that that is your ticket to heaven?
Okay so why is it that you allow a T.V. in your house? Other apostolic's would never let that happen, it is a sin of the eyes they say
 And why is it that we all sat down for easter dinner and then sat down to gamble, for money, after, true you werent a part of it but you let it in your home.
  And why is it that you preach that God said to love everyone and yet you think that every other religion that is not your own is some kind of cult and that they will all go to hell.
 And now I am moving out with a man that is not my husband, and you think that I am just the most evil person you have ever met and you will have no part in it.
  So what now, you disown me and never talk to me again? you shut me out of your life becasue I am living in sin?
 But would you rather my son grow up with no kind of father figure in his life then grow up with a good man there to guide him?
 The bible was written thousands of years ago when you got married at 13 then moved in with a guy then had a baby, we are living in a world now where girls are having babies at 13 not knowing who the father is and then throwing them in trash cans and here I am trying to make a better life for my son and you look down on that?
 I am not asking for approval, I am asking for you not to be upset, you may be, but I am asking for you to just be accepting.this is a rant to my grandmother, crazy situation, amazing how you find out who your true family is in times like these huh?
