If nothing else, you are strong to me
when that man put bruises on you, you held your composure
for the sake of your family
for the sake of your freinds
you walked out your door
every morning after
and even though you were hurting on the inside
you were kind on the outside

If nothing else, you are strong to me
when your grandbabies were not being cared for
the way you thought properly
you still kept your composure
you did what you could do
you took them in
and for your sanitys sake
you kept smiling

If nothing else, you are strong to me
you still gave the advice
while the whole time your soul was crying out 
for some of your own
you have been a wonderful teacher and guider
the whole time needing a little guidance of your own
and you still hold your head high

If nothing else, you are strong to me!This is just a little something I wrote for a freind of mine that I have worked with over the years, she is such a good person and has been through hell and back, angie, this is to you sweetie!
