title: "Determine Me"

Is it that you excpect me to let you run the overflowing thoughts of abandonment that run through my head like the carelessness that runs through yours?
Is it that I have been formed into this being that you can play with at your will and then when your body has it's fuel you can throw me down like an overused overabused toy and be done with me?
         (determine me)
Is it that the words that come out of my mouth form as clouds before you and with the breath inside your head you blow them out of your way so you won't have to be bothered with my mumblings?
                                (DETERMINE me)
Is it that the caring that I have given throughout the years have only been cushions for your hurt and when your feelings have rested enough you throw me with the rest of the dirty laundry to be washed and then used again?
Is it that these tears have been the river that you have floated on again and again to ease the restfulness of your well-being all the while using your paddles to beat the eyes which these tears have been formed?
 How long can I let you
                     (...DETERMINE ME!)
