Sitting in the dark
Alone.
I wonder what you are doing.
I wonder who.
I worry...
Taking a sip of my too hot tea
Alone
I wonder if I'll ever feel better.
I wonder when
I worry...
Smoking yet another cigarette
Alone.
I wonder why the light fades to dark
I wonder how.
I worry...
Stealing another shattered glance at the clock
Alone.
I wonder if something will come my way.
I wonder what.
I worry...
Staring blankly at the wall
Alone.
I wonder if I'll see better things.
I wonder where.
I worry...
Touching my tears running down my face
Alone.
I wonder if they will ever stop.
I wonder why.
I worry...
Alone I worry about my fate.
Alone I confront the deamons inside my head.
Alone I will win battles and lose wars.
Alone I will find happiness or pain.
