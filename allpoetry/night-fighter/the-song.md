What is this song I can not hear?
Whispering across my barren cheek,
Daintely caressing my ear?

Who is the one so far away?
Fighting for salvation,
Hearing all that I say?

Unwanted,
Unbidden,
Uninvited,
He wanders into my thoughts.

Where is that sweet thing that deliciously
Curved my lips,
Into an esctatic grin?

How will I find my way without him?

Frightened,
Worried,
Stressed,
I search for the song that was...
