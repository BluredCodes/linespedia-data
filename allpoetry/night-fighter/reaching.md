Reaching
Out to the one
I have always loved and
Finding myself alone in the
Darkness of this horrible aching night.
Sighing, screaming, waiting for you.
Wretching into the dark.
Spitting love out.
Reaching.This is another experiment with the rictameter format that tina taught me...
