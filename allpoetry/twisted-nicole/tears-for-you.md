---
title: "Tears For You"
writer: "Twisted Nicole"
slug: "tears-for-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

Is it ironic, that these tears are for you?
Do you feel worthy of each drop?
I'm not going to sensor them,
Because you're worth every one,
And I'm not ashamed to cry, 
Over you.
One tear for every smile lost 
Every kiss taken for granted
Every word unheard
Every response unforgiven
Every moment forgotten.
And would you forgive me,
If every tear is for you?

Is it ironic, that these tears are for you?
And only sunshine,
Your sunshine,
Can dry them away.
Is it ironic that it's cloudy every day?

I tell myself, it's all okay,
It's okay to cry over you,
It will eventually just drowned me
And that's okay.

But will you forgive me love,
If I cried everyday?
Too late
I already do,
And It's all okay,
Because these tears are for you.
