title: "Locked Inside My Head"
writer: "Twisted Nicole"

I stay here locked inside my head
Wondering about what you had said
And I'm sure you didn't consider the notion
I'll just wander what's wrong with my devotion...

Why do I always manage to stick around?
When every time, I'm just get shot to the ground?
Maybe you're just too dumb to realize
I don't see you in everyone elses' eyes.

But why do I always get shoved aside,
When I'm the one with true feelings to hide?
It's not fair to yourself
Or me, this whole thing is hell.

So I'll just stay here locked inside my head,
Thinking about every word you said,
And wonder if it'll ever happen again,
Or if maybe this time I won't drown in pain.

What's the matter with you boy?
I don't see you as a useless toy,
You could be mine, all mine,
And we'd have the time of our lives...

Meanwhile, I'll just stay here locked inside my head
Remembering everything you said,
Because that's all that's left to hold on to,
And there's no one else like you.
