title: "To Be Happy"

Does it scare you now, that I am free?
Happiness is being the person you want to be...
Have you ever felt a childs face,
Apprciated their warm embrace...
Or do you still live your life
Like nothing is even alive.  

You burn me cold
With your words...
The shouting's old
That's all I've heard

And I don't need it anymore
You had your chance when I was four
But you chose to leave
Don't tell me what not to believe...
All the times I saw you
You acted like you never knew
You had a child
And she was me...

And is money really the most important thing?
All I want from you is love...
There's already too many children that don't have enough,
But I'm yours.. and I know that you're trying the best that you can,
But to be happy, you have to understand,

That I'm not as old as I was when you left off
I don't need for you to say you're the boss
It's bullshit...don't lie to me,
Don't tell me what not to believe 
Because all I want is for you to be happy
Just be the person you want to be
Even if that means...
That you don't know me.
