---
title: "Hear Me"
writer: "Twisted Nicole"

Do you hear me?
If I say it one more time, I'll lose my nerve
Did you hear me?
Say that I love you, and I'm all you deserve.

Respond,
Please don't leave me standing alone
Just come on,
And I'll put you on your throne...

You asked me what I was thinking
I told you, 
Lastnight I was just wondering what you were dreaming
Then I realized that you didn't care anymore
And my heart's shattered on the floor.

You could respond,
Just one last time,
Tell me the vibe you're on
Then i'll stop giving the signs.

It hurts so bad when you need someone
And they don't need you...
And it hurts so bad when you tell someone
You love them, but they don't hear you.

Stop making me search for the answers
If they're not there,
Stop making me hope for the better 
If love can't be found anywhere,
And all I'm asking for you is to go beyond
What you really want,
Right now you could just stop taunting,
And answer me, but I want you to see

That I love you,
But you don't hear me.
