title: "Undone"
writer: "Twisted Nicole"

I've lost it now
I've come undone
I really believed that you were the one
And I wished that everything you said was true
Because no one will love you the way I do.

You've lost it now
You've come undone
You're not the same man that I thought I would want
You've got another girl but it's just for fun,
The love's not true,
Because I know you...

Don't deny the feelings that you hide,
Tell me no, but I know what's inside,
I've known you too long for you to lie
You keep saying things that make me want to cry...

Do you care at all?
Tell me do you care at all?
Did you forget it all?
It seems like you forgot it all...
Why don't you call?

Too many times I see you,
And I wish you knew...
That all the things that we used to do
Still remain in my memory like something new

And if there's love
I want to have something to do with it
I just want to feel young,
Because I've lost it now
And I don't want to be undone...

And if there's love
I want you to have something to do with it 
Just like we used to
Because you've lost it now
And you don't deserve to be left undone.
