You've inspired me to be human
To be the person I never was,
I almost feel complete now, 
And you know it's just because...
You inspire me in everything you do
Make me see myself in a

Different          View

And I see the world as it was my last day living
And every day, expectations are always new.
And you wonder why I've got so much to say,
You know it's because of you...
Because you've showed me how to
Discover the world in a 

Different          View

I think I'll do something today
That I've never done.
And if you wonder how I got there,
Just know that I've won...
So far, untill you came along,
Not many things were right
But now you keep me strong
And I'm willing to fight
To keep an open mind 
About anything new
And If I succeed again just know 
It's because of my life, I've got a 

Different          View

And all I want to say 
Is thank you.
