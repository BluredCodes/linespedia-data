title: "One of those people..."

Have you ever meet one of those people?
You see they merely appear nice at first to foul you,
Yes, they'll compliment you on clothing or even weight,
They'll ask you about your family,
Or if they're working in a store, 
They'll offer their help,
But don't cross them,
Because like a bat out of hell,
And right before your eyes,
It appears that they have grown horns,
The real them comes out, the devil within,
The words become more crisp and cutoff like,
And you're left standing wondering what just happened,
Well, you've just meet one of those people.
Do you know anyone like this?????
