title: "The way to know love"

The best way to know love,
Is to be loved by one,
But if none will love me,
Then let me know none,

May that love that escaped me,
Come back with time,
Strangely I hope to be selfish,
To put claim to what is mine,

I simply want to be loved,
But really don't we all,
Just someone to hold and comfort,
And make me just feel, beautiful.
