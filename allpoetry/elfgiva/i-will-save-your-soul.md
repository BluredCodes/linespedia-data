title: "I will save your soul"
writer: "Elfgiva"

I will save
If you need me to
I will be there through all the famine
by your side
I wont let you be sad
You can be the lost fool in need and ill be your savior
I can save your soul
I will help through the trials of this existence
I will love you forever
