Call me Cinderella 
I guess that who I am
Waiting for her Prince Charming
In the ashes expecting to be rescued 
Waiting to Escape
Just Call me Cinderella 
That who I am
Waiting for her Prince Charming


I can be your Rapunzel 
and let down my long hair to you
I can be Snow White 
and awaken to your kiss
I can be Ariel
and walk on land with you
I can be your Sleeeping Beauty
and sleep until you come
I can be Jasime
and ride on magic carpet

But most of all Im Cinderella 
Awaiting in rags for my Prince
Why dont you come

I wish for a fairy godmother to transform me
into a beauty
And leave my glass slipper for you....

Just Call me Cinderella 
That who I am
Waiting for her Prince Charming
