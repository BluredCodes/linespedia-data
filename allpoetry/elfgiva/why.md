I sit here alone 
Doomed
To walk alone into death
I sit without out you
I loved you
I would have loved you 
If you gave me the chance
Your beautiful face 
Upturned with a smile
Sayin "Trust me"

Flash
Your gone
Why'd you have to do that?

I yearn for your love 
your sweet grin

I need to feel you
Ineed us to be together
Why cant you come back?
