Goodbye cruel world,
 Past injustices still sting me. But that will all end. This is my last last soliquey to thee. My home I'm leaving. Hoping for promise is the valley of darkness. He walks with me beside me. He is only seen by my soul.
i'm going to join him. I can ascend with the angels.Not stuck in this melancholy existence. I will not be seen agasin.
