I try to speak out agaisnt injustices and wrongs
I am charged with sedition
I am labeled an anarchist
An usurper
Well I guess i am
I protest the things that need to be avenged and righted
Maybe noone listens but I can go sleep at night knowing that I tryed
I didnt become a mindlees leming
I stood out
I denied them right over me 
I refused to Give in 
I stuck to my causes
Noone cared but I kne w I was right
