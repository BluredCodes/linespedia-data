Ok That lifeless metal box in front of me is being slow
I cant load pictures over trillions of times of trying
I cant find 'hotel california' Of lime wire
The keyboard keeps freezing dangit
I cant even get the darn website to load
I hate you box evil malicous box
You hate me Dont you
Please help me meanie computer
I think that your gonna go to the trash 
ANd when I can type better Ill get a typewriter
Yes Typewriterplease dont tell my computer I wrote this LOL
