I am ok
Just my world is accelereating into decline
I cant breath
I am deppressed 
I dont sleep 
I dont eat
I dont live
Only your kiss can save me from the vortex
I need you 
I cant BE without you
I love you with every grain of my heart and soul 
Why did you leave me 
Your beautiful face grinning
Saying you'd come back
But you didnt 
You're lost in the afterlife
