In the rain I sat
Letting it fall over me
Thinking 
no hoping that its purity would clease my sins
Tears stream openly down my face 
The wrongs I have done
Haunt me
I cry out in deprression
I ran in the field
I tryed to say I was sorry
The rain brings me pain
It reminds me i'm alone
Without anyone
I am shivering now 
But I am numb
I cant feel anything
I lost track of time 
I sat there for hours
I cant see how it will geet better
This desolation
I'm now sobbing so quickly i cant breathe
The rain piiter patters endlessly
I cant escape This feeling of claustrophobia
I feeling alone
