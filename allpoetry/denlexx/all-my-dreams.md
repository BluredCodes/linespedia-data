I keep all my dreams 
Stored in bottles
That I drink from
When ever I'm blue

I've got shelves filled
With all assorted
And a cupboard 
Filled with one's just of you

When ever I'm lost or lonely
Or maybe with nothing to do
I get out my dream filled bottles
And I drink till I'm filled up with you
