Woman of the mystic mountain 
Come into my dreams tonight 
Love me like no other 
Hold me ho so tight 
Whisper that you love me 
I'll make you mine forever more 
Woman of the mystic mountain 
It's you that I adore 

Woman of the mystic mountain
Give all your love to me
Hold me tight all through the night 
Never set me free
Lock me in your wild embrace
Never let me up for air 
Let our soul's be as one
We'll live without a care 

Woman of the mystic mountain
To you I'll always be true
Stay by your side forever more
I'll love and cherish you 
In my dreams you'll live forever more
My one and only love 
Till we meet again my darling
Sweet dreams, and all my love
