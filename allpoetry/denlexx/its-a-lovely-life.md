It's a lovely life being a cat
Lying all day by the fire on a mat
Catching a bird if I want some fun 
Scratching the dogs nose
And making him run 

Climbing up a tree 
To look at the world
Lying on a branch 
With your tail uncurled

Getting all your food 
Put nicely on a plate
Staying out all day 
And them coming in late

Sitting in the best chair
Getting nice and fat
It's a lovely life being a cat
