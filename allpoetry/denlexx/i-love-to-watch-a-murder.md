I love to watch a murder
But only on TV
It's the way they always work it out
And who killed whom you see

I love to think, "who did it"
And it gives me quite a fright
When I'm sitting by the telly
In a room without a light 

They always catch the bad guy
Or he gets it with a gun
He's only got an hour 
So he can't go on the run

The cops they always get their man
It's nothing like real life
They catch the bad guy in an hour 
And then go home to the wifethought this one up cos the wife loves to watch anything with a murder in it on tv ...gets me worried sometimes ..lol
