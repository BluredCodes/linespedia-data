Here I am trapped in the bath
I fell in here and it's not a laugh
Can't get out no matter how I try
Would come in very handy if I could fly
No one wants to get me out 
They just try and wash me down the spout
Got eight  legs but I can't hold tight 
If i were a lot bigger they'd get a fright
I'm a poor little spider without a friend in sightwould you help a spider out of the bath ;-)
