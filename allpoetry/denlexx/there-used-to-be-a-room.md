There used to be a room 
In the back of my head 
And I filled it with things 
That filled me with dread

This room it would fill
Till it was fit to burst 
Then whoever upset me 
They sure got the worst

These days it's empty
I let it all out
When I get upset
I scream and I shout 

I'm a much better man 
Than I used to be 
I feel so happy 
And at last I'm freethis used to be so true of me ;-(
