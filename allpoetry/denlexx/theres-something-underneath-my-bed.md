There's something underneath my bed
It wasn't there last night
When I awoke this morning
It gave me such a fright

As I bent down to get my socks
It looked right back at me
All I saw was two big eyes
It's dark under there you see

I got right down upon my knees
To get a better look 
Put my hand under the bed
My first mistake "to book"

It grabbed my hand 
And held on tight
It tried to pull me under
This I was not having
So I pulled back, by thunder 

This pull and push 
Went on all day 
No way would it let go
Then at last I dragged it out
And gave a mighty throw

It hit the wall, and then the door
And ran off down the stairs
The only thing I ever found 
Was a big hand full of hairs

Now before I get in bed
Under the bed I'll look
If I ever see another spider like that
I'll flatten him with a bookkeep looking under the bed if I was you ...lol
