My special moments
Come when I'm asleep
You float in through my window
And into my dreams you creep

You put your arms around me
Kiss and hold me tight
Whisper that you love me 
And everything will be all right

We spend the night together
Wrapped in each others arms
Sharing tender moments
As you show me all your charms

Then as the morning sun comes up
And bird song fills the air
I awake to find an empty bed
With a feeling of despair
