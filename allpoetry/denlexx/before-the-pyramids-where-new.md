A love that transcends time and space
That's what I have with you
We've been in love forever 
Since before the pyramids where new

It started in a garden 
Before time had began
You gave me an apple
And we both went on the run

We made love sailing down the Nile
When you where a queen
You saved me from an arrow
When I was seventeen

Your father gave you to me
Back in 1862
And we lived below the Rockies
With our kids Simon and Sue

Our love, it will out last the stars
To you I will, and have been true
No time can stand between us 
There'll always be me and you
