You're my black magic woman
Come work your spells on me
I love you, crave you, and want you
So never set me free

Hold me with your magic spells
Spellbind me with your love
Work your web of magic
And I'll be your one true love

Mix your magic potions 
I'll drink the glasses dry
Hold me in your loving arms
Teach me how to fly

Hubbell bubble toil and trouble
Yours I'll always be 
Black magic woman 
Never set me free
