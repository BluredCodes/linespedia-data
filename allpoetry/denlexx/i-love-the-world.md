I love the world 
When I'm in bed
All cuddled up to you
With your arms wrapped around me
I'm never ever blue

We melt into each other 
And feel as warm as toast
It's nice to be wrapped up in bed
With the one you love the mostthought this one up when I  was in bed with the wife this morning :-)
