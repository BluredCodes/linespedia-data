Waiting at the computer 
For the one I love to call
Wondering and wondering 
Will she call at all?

Has she found another?
Is her computer working right?
Can she get away to chat
In the middle of the night

Seems it's getting late now
Is she gonna come
Waiting on MSN
My bum is getting numb

Don't look like she's gonna make it
It's well past 2am 
Better get to bed now
Wife will wonder where I amNo I'm not having an affair ok I was just thinking ...lol
