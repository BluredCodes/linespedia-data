Mister moon, ho mister moon
You shine up there so bright
Please shine upon my lover
And keep her safe tonight

Please look after her while I'm away
Smile down on her each night
Blow her a kiss and tell her
I'll soon be home to hold her tight

If you can, tell mister sun
To brighten up her day
Keep her warm and free from harm
Till I come back to stay
