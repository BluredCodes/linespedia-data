My Angels Up in Heaven 

God took away my Angels and new told me why

They'll hold a specail place that can never be replaced

I'll keep my head strong and try so hard not to cry

God always held your hands and completed u with faith



 My Angels Up in Heaven

I hide my tear, I hide my fears an i try and let u know

Your missed here on Earth but your memorys will live on

I break down in tears and rememember my fears but still I cant let u go

I'll miss you dear friends but your Legacy will live on and on and on



 My Angels Up in Heaven 

Run with God, and wait till he sets me free

I'll jump up and down like a silly little clown and a hive of honey bees

Oh dear friends I am missing you, no more sorrowing like the birds or waving like the trees

I know you'll catch my tears and make my fears disapear I'll one day be free.

Glory to God for he has set you free and carried you home to be



   IN LOVING MEMORY OF (People I lost in almost 2years)

 JEAN J. IRVING (My Grandmother) April 23.-August 6,2001 

 ANNA (My Best-friend) Febuary 23.-Febuary 20,2002 almost 16 years old.

ANDREA (My Friend) 1985-2002 almost 17 years old.
