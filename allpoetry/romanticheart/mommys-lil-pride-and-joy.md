Mommy's little pride and joy
Works all day and sings all night
She talk and talks till all hours of the night with a boy
Tide down and beat down mommy little angel is in love and it takes a flight

Mommy little pride and joy 
Jumps around from house to home
She make enough noise to make her self a little boy
Playing with knifes playing with men, mommy little angels eyes start to rome

Mommy's little pride and joy 
Lets her feet crul and make grown men wherls
She kissing and huging on some guy name Roy
He takes her into the moon like a star she herls

Mommy's little pride and joy is all grown up
Mommy's little pride and joy is all safe and sound
Mommy's little pride and joy is now barried and found
