I met a friend that was just like me
He never was sad and never was alone
He took me every where he went he told me I was just like him
We made each other laugh and play in a little kiddy tone


He keeps me in his heart and we shall never bring each other pain
I'ma daddy's lil girl I'm true to that game
He keeps the love coming like a mountain of rain 
He looks at me and sees no wrong, i'm as sweet as a gental rain

Daddy's little baby will always be the same 
Your gentle and so kind and can always make me smile
The others maybe jealous cause I think so much of you, I tell the all the same its your fame
Its your grace and you smile that helps complete your style, I'm daddys lil girl didnt't you see my chain?.... 

 TO MY DADDY WHO HELPED ME TO BE ME!!!!!!!
