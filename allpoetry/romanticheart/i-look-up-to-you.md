I Look Up to You 
I Look Up to You for guidence
I Look Up to You for Advice 
I Look Up to You for Love 
I Look Up to You because You helped me Grow


I Look Up to You
Mom I Look Up to You because You love me uncondustionaly
Mom I Look Up to You for Honor 
Mom I Look Up to You for Trust
Mom I Look Up to You because Your everything a "Black Women" should be "Successful" 

I Look Up to You 
I Look Up to You because Your a Friend
