title: "Inkwells"

The ink that dries upon these pages
Blackens my heart
With the thickest pigments
And all the wrong words
I keep from flowing
Out onto paper.
Each letter of sadness
Curls these leaves 
So that they will never lie flat.
These blotches splatter
Like my sputtering tears
Leaving dried blemishes
Upon white perfection
Causing my own wells
To overflow and trickle 
Down my face
In search of a pure surface
To stain with blackened tears.I'm so sick of my writing---yarg---growl---I'm so frustrated.
-zuzu
