Was this what you envisioned;
Am I a great experiment
To taste a new type of flesh,
To turn smoother rocks
Upon your palms
And torment yourself with new senses,
Softer lips, and seamless fingers?

Did you picture it this way;
Your head resting 
On the knot between my navel and belly
That you yourself had tied tight
Then fought to loosen? 

When you dreamt of me
Did you imagine me unraveled;
Easily picked apart 
Like a curious child disassembles 
A clock that no longer keeps time,
Or a music box with no song?

Have you exhausted 
Each way to twist and turn me;
To stretch me across a distance
I dare not measure
Or rearrange my body
To fit perfectly in your arms?

I don’t know
If I can live bound up this tight,
Sore and tired from waiting to see 
If my pieces fit exactly
Against the warmth of your skin
Or if you can find a way
