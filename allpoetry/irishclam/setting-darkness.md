The doll running twards the door
cracks apart, falls into pieces.
Psycopathic deamons
burst into flames,
while the lights are let out of lives.

Confusion runs through the minds of children,
they're always being let down,
......and beaten.
The darkness sets in
as the blood dries to the floor.i was in a weird poetic mood this mornin'
