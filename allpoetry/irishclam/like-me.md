Childhood reminisence
thinking about death and deception,
and seeing figments of my imagination.

I see my brother
lying there dead,
he's exactly like me,
except,.........not alive in anyway.this is about the poem that's the most true about me


about my twin that died at birth

~RIP~ Matheu Andrew
