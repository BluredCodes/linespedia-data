My love you are so beautiful,
you make me happy when I am down
you are the light of my life.

your love can never be counted apon,
your true emotion twasrds me.
will never lessen.

When we talk,
I can trust you like no other.
When you say you love me,
I feel like the luckiest man alive.
When you kiss me,
I know i am the luckiest man alive.

I must be special, expecially since you chose me.

       Thank you for your love.thank you irish-pumpkin for loving me>
