~Life it’s self~

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

I see our future,
laying here under the perfect moon light.
Cold winds blow,
but I find warmth within you.
You warm my heart like no other.

The ideal love that you have,
never resolves,
always is consistent.
That is what I have always needed.

Your seductive heart,
pulls me closer every time you speak
those three comforting words,
"I love you"

See now,
that I see our future
I can say,
I love you more than life it’s self.
