i could sit and ponder on this day forever
but confuse myself for i don't know if i am dreaming

pinch me
awaken me

are you not but a figment of my own imagination?

your words are mine
in my heart

becuase today we have connected

even if you never know my name
or spea your love and heart to me again

i will always remember your words
