starving for affection
I run
from all humanity
i must hide

for i am ashamed
afraid and curious

where is my other self?
with love? with heart?

i see the distant stras
in my nights sky

to them i must 
run

far far away form all my lonliness

am i a fool?

a fool in need of so much affection
i run and hide to their rejection

hiding from the ghosts of pasts
insecure

confusion makes my mind run wild

i turn around
i'll face my day
