lost in a madness of this destruction
for the sky has fallen down on me
am i breathing?
i hear nothing but falling
falling of our sanity and togethorness
i hear my heros
i yell but i am mute unto them
i have fallen with the sky
and yet i still sit with my whole body intact
i can pull through
my breaths begin to become shorter
becuase i think the sky has just fallen again
i am being pounded into an undeserving hell
and i begin to again hear nothing but falling
i look to my left
i knew her today
but her face has turned demonic shades of blue and red
she no longer breaths
my breath fades as i try to scream my last breath away
why? my god! why have you forsaken me?
