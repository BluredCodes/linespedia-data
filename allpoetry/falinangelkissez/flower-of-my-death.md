i watched a you grew into a something
yet you still decided to throw it all away
you always thought you were nothing
becuase of peoples mindless games

yet i saw you from nother point of view
you were a something

something speical and one day the world will come to see you just as that

as i sit adn wait for you to come to this realization
you continue to inspire
my eyes become somewhat of my own muse
for in your thoughts you are a nothing

but nothing is a something to me
