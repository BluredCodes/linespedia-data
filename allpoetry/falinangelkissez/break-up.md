i could leave now
with out you i would die

youve torn my heart
precisely into two

you intrusted your love
and that love i bestwoed

you took my hand
then left me alone

i love you
i don't
i love you
i won't

your lies 
your shame

you have left me on the darkest roads
alone
on this forever cold and forbidden night

time for you to leave
now
go
