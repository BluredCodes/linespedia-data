Two blankets Contourted around our skin
as i ponder on this situation
i turn to my left
you were there 
and i began to wonder

where am i?
this smell is all to new to me
the scent of a man
one i have never smelled before

what am i doing here?
i hear the fire crackle down the hall
and the warmth of your breath is fire in my ear

Nothing seperates our once independant bodies
now we hold each other, you'll never let me go

i turn to my right
window
night is upon us
and my clock reads 
Its Late

with the 2 blankets around us
and the warmth of your skin pressed against my arms
i have never felt so free
such an independant me
