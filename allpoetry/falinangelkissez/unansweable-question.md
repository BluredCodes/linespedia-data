questions without answers

answers with no explaination

shoot me, heal me, tell me I'm o.k.

don't deny im here
don't deny you are too
kiss my forbidden lips
as i lick your forbidden tongue

these are my answers
give me an explaination
as to why they seem to be

this is my question with no answer
why
