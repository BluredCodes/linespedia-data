Isicles Oblivious
the only bad thing is the numbness of my fingers
yet somehow its almost like an awakening to my situation

the street duster slowly paces himself
but i wish he'd leave 
my wonderland alone

i can't play 
i am too amazed with this shining beauty

a beauty beyond that of my own

i am rich now
i am swaremed with the most beautifully cut diamonds

all around me life is glistening
and my world seems oblivious
