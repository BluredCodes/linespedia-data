fooled me yet again
oh laugh all you want

god i hate this paun
do you sit and laugh at mu luck
becuase once was too much
but i don't deserve this torture

this pain
my destruction

is this part of my life on repeat?
let me go?

3 times was a charm
then again and again harm

4th time
im giving up
love
destruction

yet another day to day 
occurance for me

smile for me
becuase my smile always makes me cry
