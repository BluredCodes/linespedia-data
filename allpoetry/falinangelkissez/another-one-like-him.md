title: "Another One Like Him"
writer: "FalinAngelKissez"

Shedding my soul
kiss my insecurities

good-bye i say to you

as i put on my conscience and drive away

with a kiss of the lips
and a chorus to our song
it was done

he was all over me with
his soul
his-self

and i cried

he shed himself upon me
and every other one
stupid enough just like me
