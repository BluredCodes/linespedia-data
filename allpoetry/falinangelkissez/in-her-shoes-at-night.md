scream my name
scream my name

oh well tell me what I'm doing right
tell me how i make you feel

call me baby
call me yes

how do i make you feel?
pretty well from the sound of your voice amungst the other obvious

scream my name
oh! scream my name

how do i make you feel?
good...don't stop

but when time comes 
just push pause 
we'll take our break

then scream my name with repitition
