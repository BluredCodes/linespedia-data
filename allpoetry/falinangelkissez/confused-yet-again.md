my emotions have yet to kill me
as i wait for another chance
i weakin in my days
and strengthn in my nights
love cannot save me now
for it is demolished in numbers

the love of mine is great, but he small amount of seemingly hate over rides
why?
Lucky am i for what i ave
thankful i am not dead but i am alive
no one said it was supposed to be easy
but so young
it hurts so bad

hate is not yet to be said but anger is still leathl
can i live so long as to be forgiven?
or will that only come to me at my death?

i cannot hold my head so high as to show my front

my head hangs with humility
and all i can do is cry
