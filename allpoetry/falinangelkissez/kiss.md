10 seconds till we come to our first blissful kiss

9 times tonight i haevlooked deeply in your eyes and you have dreamed back into mine

8 total past lovers between us

7 seconds and we'll kiss

6 hours ago i saw you last and ive missed you already

5 days ago i professed my true love unto you

4 hands, paired up to romantic touches

3 seconds and we'll kiss

2 lips seem to quiver, but turn fast away

1factor remaind for lovers 1 st kiss was or shared becuase we refused to take a chance
