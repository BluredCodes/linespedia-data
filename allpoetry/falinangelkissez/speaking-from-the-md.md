Medically speaking could you could call me a compulsive liar?
oh well! ive been broken hearted 
yet someone will take YOUR side because i told my miniscule lie!

Screw that

im sick of the pain
of the torture
of having to live the life brokenheartedly

i need "love" to take me away
to love me away
carry me away

i wanna be someones one and only
becuase all men have become my one and only

i wanan be someones one and only
i wanna be taken away
love
i wanna be taken away

take me away
lift me off this pain
take me away
