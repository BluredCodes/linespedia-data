Sitting here along the wall
Waiting for the invitation to dance
Is it coming?
No. 
Good, I didn't want to be here anyway
I prefer a quiet evening...alone...I
Want to be alone.  Solitude.
A relationship would only mess up my
Life.  Too complicated...All I know
Of people, and of parties, and of fun, and
Action-not for me.  I sit at home, in endless
Stagnation.
Is this poem fooling anyone?
Not me!  My real fear is there is no Love
For
