© 04/09/2002 Dennis L. Pickering

Jingle, Jingle, Jingle, Jingle
It goes off so you can mingle
With all others who aren't single
And with whom you intermingle.

Ignite the candle of your life
Although it can fill you with strife
Don’t have to cut it with the knife
That may be brandished by your wife.

A wife not of the human sort
The word may cause you to purport
An idea that you will abort
It is a system that’s not short!
