---
title: "Saturn"
writer: "kay a"
slug: "saturn"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Secrets kept in my heart
buried there deep away from you
try to dig into my soul 
break open my heart
with your bare hands 
I wont let you in
I am afraid 
but I won't show it
no one shall know of what 
is in my mind
nothing but misery and pain 
surrounds me so 
it's not good for you 
anyway 
stop trying to 
step into me
your boots can leave a mess 
that I can't clean
it wont be long before I
go into the pure darkness
that was meant for me 
and you can't hassle me
anymore 
you don't care
it doesn't matter to you
anyway 
so I'm 
better off
pain and misery has 
consumed me
so let me 
go....
