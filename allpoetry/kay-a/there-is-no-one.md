---
title: "...there is no one..."
writer: "kay a"
slug: "there-is-no-one"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56942-...there-is-no-one...-by-kay-a"
---

there is no one here
to hold me
while i cry 
about the fact that i'm always
lonely
and i can't write
anymore
because i'm afraid
and
there is no one here
to understand 
how my heart feels when
i am unable to express myself
and 
there is no one here
for me
to complain to whenever i do 
something
because everything i do is 
wrong
and
there is no one here
while i scream
while i cry
while i dwell in the lonliness 
of my life
