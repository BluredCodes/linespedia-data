---
title: "not enough"
writer: "kay a"
slug: "not-enough"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

you love me too much 
i think
you go out of your way
to let me know
you give me things
that money can't buy
though sometimes
you try to buy my love
even though i want to
i can't make myself
feel the way you want me to 
our lives are different
somehow 
we come together for the 
same cause
which makes us think that
we should be together
but it's not that 
easy
you love me too much
you have ambitions that only
you can achieve
even though you don't take the 
time to
you think that promising the world
will keep me by your side
the world is not mine
i'll be there for you
regardless
in my own way
we have many things in common
but our lives are different 
somehow
that doesn't make any difference
to you
cause your eyes only see 
the love in your heart for me
you love me too much
but i can't recipricate
your hopes and dreams put me high
on a pedestal
in your life
when you should put yourself 
first 
our lives are much too different
somehow 
you hold on to faith
of us being together
even though i 
belong to another
so i can't love you 
enough...
