PLEASE DON'T TAKE ADVANTAGE OF ME
I DON'T DESERVE IT 
PLEASE DON'T LIE TO ME WHEN
I TELL YOU NOTHING BUT THE TRUTH
PLEASE DON'T PATRONIZE ME TELLING
ME THINGS YOU THINK I WANT TO HEAR
PLEASE DON'T ACT LIKE YOU CARE
I KNOW YOU DON'T GIVE A SHIT
I NEVER ASK FOR ANYTHING IN LIFE
BUT TO BE HAPPY
PLEASE DON'T TAKE THAT AWAY FROM ME 
IT'S HARD TO GET UP IN THE MORNING
PLEASE DON'T MAKE ME WISH TO SLEEP FOREVER
PLEASE JUST LET ME BE ME 
AND DON'T ASK FOR MORE
PLEASE DON'T MAKE LOVING YOU SUCH A CHORE
IT'S NOT FAIR TO ME 
IT'S NOT FAIR TO YOU
PLEASE DON'T MAKE THIS ANY HARDER THAN IT ALREADY IS
PLEASE STOP HURTING ME...
