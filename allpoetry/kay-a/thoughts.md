as she waits for him
to come home
she runs her fingertips
slowly across her 
flat stomach
knowing that he when he 
gets there
she'll be wet and ready
for anything
with a bit of reluctance she 
goes down a bit 
further
getting excited at even the
thought of what 
she can do
her breathing gets a little
heavier as she gets 
closer and closer
to being touched
and as the key turns in 
the door
her legs are wide open 
ready to be taken
by him...
