---
title: "relentless"
writer: "kay a"
slug: "relentless"
source: "AllPoetry"

it's hard to think
that 
you will ever hurt me
after all 
we've been through
but it's true
you're relentless
in your efforts
to shoot my heart
it's hard to feel what
I feel
for you
you take it for granted
it seems
lying to my face about 
love
honesty
loyalty
that I have for you 
but you refuse to give 
to me
it's hard to be in this
position
with you
loving without conditions
without getting any
back
getting me to trust you
again
splitting me in two
what am I to do? 
you're 
relentless 
in your efforts to hurt me
again 
and it's hard to think that
about you...
