i feel the sweat
sliding down my forehead
i look around my room
and things begin to change
pictures come alive
THER'RE TRING TO KILL ME
no, please no more
i want it to stop
the carpet crawls up my leg
i scream in horror
pulling free i trip
i hit my head on a chair
its legs of the chair
begin to stomp around my head
i jump to my feet
ignoring the pain
this has to end
i grab the windowsill
and squeeze until my
hands begin to bruise
i pull my body forward
and slam my head into the glass
i pull back and look at the shattered pane
i don't even care about the pain
or the cut across my forehead
the sweat stings but i ignore it
all i care about is the glass
i grab a shard and pull if from the frame
it cuts my hand but i don't care
i plunge it into my chest
and i feel relieved happiness
because i made it endi have never done LSD. just for the record. but i know lots of people kill themselves while on it.
