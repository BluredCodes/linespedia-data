---
title: "i hate"
writer: "Seer"
slug: "i-hate"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55069-i-hate-by-Seer"
---

i hate you
for you pick on me
and you attack me with your words

i hate you
because you find flaws in me
while you look so very perfect

i hate you
because you are better than me
in every way i can think of

i hate you
because it's easier to blame you
for my mistakes and flaws

i hate you
because you point out my imperfections
so really all i hate is me
