All alone
And feeling a trap
All recoils back
To my mishaps

I never thought
It would come to this
With me in a bathtub
And cuts on my wrists

The water is
As thick as paint
No can help me
Not a doctor or saint

So i leave this world
With its storm unbraved
And i end me
With my soul unsaved
