Again and again
I feed my children
And over and over again
My hand is bitten

So what do I do?
Do I punish them?
Or should I abandon
Feeding them?

Both seem cruel
But they must learn
If they shun me
They will burn

I do not forget
And can forgive no more
My patience is tried
To limits unseen before

It is so very sad
For I can no longer be just
But man has sinned
So I will do what I must
