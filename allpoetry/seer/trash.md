---
title: "Trash"
writer: "Seer"
slug: "trash"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60538-Trash-by-Seer"
---

look at you
so unkempt and unclean
with no morals at all

i hate you
and all the others
who are just like you

you disgust me
your so eager
just to fit in

for you,
nothing is too high priced
to be accepted

not your body
not your mind
not even your soul

but remember
your "friends" cant help you
when your burning in HELL!

so be careful
when choosing your friends
they affect this life and the next

and be careful in choosing
what you want in life
it may come at a price you can't pay

becaus if you don't
you'll end up trash
just like everone else
