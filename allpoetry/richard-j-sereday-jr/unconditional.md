I thought of you today,
for it had been a while
since we did last meet
eye to eye,
heart to heart,
soul to soul,

and from out of the blue,
there stood you.

A vision of beauty
to once again behold. 

And I compared this vision I saw
with the one in my heart
where it lives so deep,
and they were one in the same.

And I then realized that
I could not love you more,
but with ever meeting of you,
each time more.

And you asked of me,
“will you treat me badly
as I do return your way?”

and I wondered how
you could not know
of how I feel for you,
in this unconditional love.
