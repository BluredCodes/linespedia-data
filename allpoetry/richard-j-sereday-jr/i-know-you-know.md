title: "I know you know"

I know you know
and 
you know I know
and

the craziest of things about it all
is as my desire for you
does comes forth from my soul,
it meets your desire half way

and these desires do frolic and play
in the form of the smiles on our faces,
and the twinkle in our eyes,

as two innocent children
who first learn of love,
we giggle because we both know
what our hearts can not deny.
