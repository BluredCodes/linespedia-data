title: "you did question"

There was a time that you questioned
my ability to love you,
as your own purity, you did question.

and I did laugh to myself,
for what matters most to me,
in these things can not be found.

What I value is the feelings
that in my heart you fill. 

The longing, the desire,
I taste of these things
as I gaze into your beckoning eyes.

So just let me say to you,
I do love you, I truly do.
