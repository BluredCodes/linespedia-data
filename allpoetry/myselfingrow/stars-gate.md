The ship is coming in...I can see the near future
Chaos and destruction..painted on a field of terror
16102 I wish we lived to see it, but this year
it is said to be the end of the world. 
War of the Universe; to be the leading race.
Human kind with an ego just a little bit to big,
won´t even get passed the first 69.000 miles
Elimination, start a new race who will play God 
and who will lead us into the end of what is us?

Why am I here
Only to test life´s faith?
Why am I not here,
To safe the human race?

Shelters build will soon thereafter be found,
16101 is the year we will fade away. 
Children, Elderly, Man and Women will be send into Annialation
"Why didn´t we listen to the fifth coming?"
Lessons will never be learned, five books filled with lessons,
Will the sixth ever come?
There will be nothing left, We are the weakest kind.
Survivors will be prisoners and prisoners will look back and say
"We were in top condition".This piece is written driven by my facination about the universe that we live in. It obviously is fiction
