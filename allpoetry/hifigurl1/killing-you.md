As i set here and play with my knife, i think, what if i just turned around and took away your pathetic little life. I'd slit your fucking throat and watch your blood pore to the floor. You make me so sick i can't take it anymore.I'd set there and stare at the cut in your neck, are you alive and breathing, there's no need to check. I'v drempt about this day forever,my planes are true and very clever! I warned you not to fuck with me, my intentions you did not know or see.In your locker i'd leave a black rose, were i would hide your body, noone knows. This is it, this is what i have to do, to get the satifaction of killing you.
