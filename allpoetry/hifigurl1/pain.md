The pain inside is hiding in my veins. I spit in the fire and blew out the flames. The lonliness is flowing in my blood. My cries dripped in the dirt and turned into mud. The anger is tangled in my hair, i screamed but my voice disapeared in the air. The ugliness is poking at my eye, everything i look at seems to decay and die.
