Have you ever fallen in love but knew they didnt care? Have you ever wanted to cry your self to sleep at night, but knew it'd get you nowhere. Have you ever watched someone walk away, not wanting them to go, whisped "i love you" softly not wanting them to know? Have you ever cried all night and almost gone insane, theres nothing in this world that causes so much pain. If i could choose between love and death, i think i'd rather die, love is fun but it hurts too much and the price you pay is high. So i sy dont fall in love you'll be hurt before it's through, you see my friend i ought to know cuz i've been hurt by you.
