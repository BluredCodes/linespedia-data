I've come to see my name in lights
to have my proverbial fame
grind it between my eyes
mix in stale prophecies
and inject it in my veins


and


I lay here the inanities
this life believes me dead
but crawling through the tunnels
dragging behind, my executioner
has camouflaged my blood


but 


You may not recognise
your failure is the test
and when the lights ignite
emblazoned by my bones
our eyes seek tongues confessmy first

be honest from the beginning
I will appreciate it
and respect your words
