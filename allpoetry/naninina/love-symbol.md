There was a road once, 
Where only 
The two of us walked 
Under the moon.
The stars had witnessed our
Presence, although
We didn't particularly 
Notice (or thank for) their very existence. 

Love (Affair)
(that is often depicted by the shape 
Of an organ: the heart) 
Was modern to me, though not to you. 

What exactly is a "true love"? 
I haven't lived, long enough to comprehend,
But if I didn't ask, I wouldn't be able
To live curiously (and not bitterly).

...Once (upon a time)...
...a road...
...the two of us...
We tasted Beauty for a minute
Then everyone and everything
Became jealous.
Now I find the road 
Has become too crowded. 

There are your sister, your brother,
Your Mother and your God Daughter.
There are your guitars, your bills, and
That old truck. Your 
Duties as a loyal friend to your
Amigos. 
There is you, 
With your ten more thousand personalities, 
As yet to be discovered. 

They keep coming
Like violent rain that 
Never stops falling on a wide sea.
I am drowning. You are floating. 
Even though you are often 
Out of breath. 

(My)  
Insecurity
And excessive Jealousy 
Could cause a great storm 
In this whole sea of you.
(My)
Fresh, restless brain needed to be stabled.  
Confused mind needed to be rationalized.
Throbbing heart needed to be calmed. 

Take/ this heart/ out of/ my chest
And lay it/ on the table. 
 
(And you will see that
The shape of a human's heart 
Is quite different from, 
(not as perfect as), 
The red-blooded, softly curved
Valentine's balloons, or
The exaggeratedly-and-
Prettily-decorated
(with-hearts) 
Love cards)  
 
 ---    Don't believe what
 ---    you see and what
 ---    you hear, until
 ---    you see and hear it
 ---    yourself.

 ...Everybody and everything...
 ...Your (schizophrenic) mother... 
 ...Your (unpredictable) characteritistics...
We realized this is your road,
I was passing by,
I decided to stay,
It gave me more good than bad cries
---(Perhaps) It has helped me find my road. 

I am calm(er) now.
