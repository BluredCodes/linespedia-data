We were all wearing well-worn gray uniforms.
Stated by the strictly ruled high school's
principal, 
"A female student's skirt
can not be shorter than her knees" 

***

Cameline we were always together 
I wasn't always very honest with you
Only because I wanted your attention
I've never gotten closer to anyone 
like I was to you
We could make each other laugh
till we cry and laugh at the same time

Tia I've known you longer than I could remember
I am always amazed by your strength
of your standing strong against the harsh wind
that seemed to blow often, 
in the air that you are breathing
This strong bond between us will always
keep us near 

Novi you are funny as hell, bold and intuitive 
You could be a roaring lion when you're upset
but a lion's sensitive heart is 
the sweetest and most loyal I've ever known
Thank you for all the letters you sent me 
since I moved here 
(Are you still a Leonardo-Dicaprio crazy fan?) 

San we had a lot fun sitting next to each other
in that classroom where we first met 
Your motherly affection always did
(and still does) mean a lot to me 
I was always buying you gifts 
(as if everyday was a friendship day)
I had realized I could only buy a true friendship
with respect and honest and good intentions

Finny you were a new friend
The boy I had a crush on 
admired and liked you so much 
I checked you out and 
ended up liking you, too 
I felt I could be totally open to you 
I wish you would write more often nowadays 

***

Somebody forgave us for being loud and hysterical
Someone understood why there's so much politics
in girlie friendships 
There is nothing like high school (girl)friends 
Sisterly smiles and sisterly love among us are 
what I treasure the mostDedicated to: all the names mentioned in the poem (and others that are not mentioned)
