title: "I Hear"

outside the windows
cars' wheels rotate fast 
on the thick asphalt 

barking dog 
tied to the pole 
on the corner of the street

chirping birds
perhaps they utter,
"night's approaching, 
where 
should we sleep?"

scraping match,  
someone 
is about to smoke 

paul and john
on the local radio, 
"you've got to hide 
your love away"

odwalla fridge,
breathing
white and 
foamy milk,
being steamed

young woman 
like myself 
says hello and 
can-i-help-you

my sweetest friend
inhaling,
and exhaling 
right beside me

my subtle voice, 
back in the depth 
of my mind,
"sometimes 
little things 
like being here
can widen your lips"

a smile 
is born.Originally titled: "Hanging Out at a Cafe"
