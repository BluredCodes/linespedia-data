---
title: "Good and Bad Food"
writer: "naninina"
slug: "good-and-bad-food"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55484-Good-and-Bad-Food-by-naninina"
---

Rough, or joyful times 
All should be suitable 
for you and for me
It really doesn't matter
who's wrong and who's right
Just because,
this is Life 
and let us fall in love with it 

We can relax and be mellow
and feel the gentle breeze
We can pretend that we are strong
and stand in the storm

Whoever we are 
All is acceptable
Just because,
this is Life
and it's full of everything, 
good and bad

What we see, is what we swallow
'Cause the world is one big, mysterious plate
from where we get our soul food 
to fill up these precious, forever starving hearts
We chew and we chew again
everything on this big plate, 
whether it tastes
good or bad 

It doesn't really matter 
who's to blame
Just because,
this is Life 
Let us fall in love 
with it
