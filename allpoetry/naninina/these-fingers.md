---
title: "These Fingers"
writer: "naninina"

These desirous fingers 
always want to do 
something,
anything

Dial a number
Dance with a lovely tune
Feel somebody's forehead
Grab a fork   
Open a present 
Pick up a child
Write down or type up
something,
anything
Reach out to somebody,
anybody

Aren't you glad...
these fingers are yours?
