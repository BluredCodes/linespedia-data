For Laura V. 


Four years ago, I was seventeen,
a child who was
somewhat lost and needed guidance.
On the first day of school, 
you came to me and
started a conversation. 
I wasn't afraid to open up with you.
You were my English teacher,
but also a dear sister. 

Back in Summer 98,
I was foreign to this place,
this country called 'America'.
I was like a new plant,
you helped me grow. 
You never did let me die. 
I was withering away,
and you gave me water.
You always made me smile.

~*~ 

Season after season,
we've walked different paths.
Somehow we've always remembered
each other,
and reunited at the end of each road.
Thank you  
for keeping in touch with me.

Now I am still a child.
My heart is growing and will forever be.
You are there near this heart, 
watching me ripen, 
guiding me so I don't fall.
Thank you
for always being there from the start. 


April 6, 2002
