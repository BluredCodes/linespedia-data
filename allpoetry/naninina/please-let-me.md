When the time is appropriate,
The old tree releases its children, its leaves

Fresh, green leaves fall down to the ground
Some remain on that ground for a long time
Some are blown by and travel with the wind
Some end up in a trash can 

Young, carefree children are delighted to 
see the real and forever-growing world
Some manage to reach their dreams
Some discover happiness and some don't 

Mother, I am 21
Please let me find my own path, and destinationThis is written for parents who are over protective to their children, including my very own. Sometimes they just don't believe in me and won't let me find my own way.
