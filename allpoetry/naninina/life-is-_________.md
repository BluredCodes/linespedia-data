To you who let 
Jadedness
swim under your skin
and 
operate the wires 
in your brain. 

Let me tell you this:
Life is ___________
(whatever adjective word 
you choose)
Y-O-U pick, not I nor s/he. 

(The blank) is erasable.
It is not your fault 
you didn't have a happy childhood.

Please,
don't fill in 
the blank for me.
You have no right to.

Success didn't perch on
your station (yet). 
(Know that) Hope 
stays in your heart forever.  

You are not content, I see that.
But let the innocent and the 
promising ones be. 
Don't envy us. 

This society is
as cruel as a wild forest,
full of young antelope
