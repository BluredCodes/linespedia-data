he is man#1. 
man#1 is not my lover. 
he sits right next to me
everyday. 
he turns his head 
to the left
when he wants to 
look at my eyes.

when this place is 
crowded, 
i lose sight of him. 
then he will walk 
right by me and 
touches my shoulder, 
slightly, but gently. 
his touch gives me peace 
like a friendly breeze
does, when it blows
against my face. 
 
man#2 is someone i kiss 
in the middle 
of a romantic dinner. 
while he's driving
his right hand 
holds my left hand.
he asks me 
to shave my legs 
on friday nights. 
and i walk with him
whether it's sunny 
or stormy.

man#1 touches 
the chin of his girl 
the way 
he touches my shoulder. 
man#2 never seems
to be satisfied.  

the curve of his lips 
when man#1 smiles 
kills me softly, 
though not 
e n t i r e l y.ps. Man#1 is someone I work with. Man#2 is my boyfriend.
