You come home and you feel a bit grouchy
It is always a long day I know 
Your cat stays home all day, resting on
top of your folded green blanket 

You play guitar like you watch the sunset
To fill your soul up are both of them 
Once in a while your cat will go outside,
to stretch and greet the Sun 

Oh baby don't be down
'Cause up and down we ride this carousel
Oh baby don't bite your lips
'Cause whatever it is, 
It comes and goes just like the daylight  

You go outside and you spill coffee 
on your brand new shirt 
You go inside your room and you find
your bills, piling up like a mountain 

You wake up early but not early enough
You take care of your mother like
she's your own child, but oh she won't listen
and stop eating sugar 

Oh baby don't blame yourself 
'Cause our mouths were once wide open
Oh yes darlin we were ready to
swallow the rain on a sunny day   

Up and down we ride this tote-go-round
Up and down and up again
And down again 
Up and down we ride this carouselWould this make a good song, you think?
