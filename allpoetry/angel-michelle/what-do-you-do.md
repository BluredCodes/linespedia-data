---
title: "What do you do?"
writer: "Angel Michelle"
slug: "what-do-you-do"
source: "AllPoetry"

What do you do when you are put in a fix,
or find a small glitch in your life?
What do you do when you can't cry anymore,
and your crying because 
the one you love has just 
walked out the door.
What do you do when you feel theres no end?
What I always do is look for a friend.
