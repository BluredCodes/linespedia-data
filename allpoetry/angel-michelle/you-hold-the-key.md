title: "You hold the key"
writer: "Angel Michelle"

So many things that I wish I could say,
Baby I love you more and more every day.
You say that I dont 
and from our embrace we do part 
but do you really know what I hold in my heart?
If not then Ill tell you whats hidden within
its something that can not be written in pen
In my heart I hold a locked up safe
full of wasted happiness as well a wasted hate,
and in your hand you hold the key
to open up that safe.
You posess the power to unleash all of my smiles
and turn my hate to love.
You must be an angel sent to me from up above.
