---
title: "End"
writer: "Angel Michelle"
slug: "end"
source: "AllPoetry"

One day 
One night 
One wish 
One dream
Lost forever in an ocean of greed,
All it takes is a minute
to let it settle in and
then your dreams and wishes 
come to an unforgetable end
One day 
One night is all it takes
to lose everything you love in life.
