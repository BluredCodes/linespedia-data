did u know that by looking at an object like a mirror it supposed to reflect
an image of u?
probably knew that since before first grade.
but i bet u never thought of it like this before

the things u say
the things u do
the things u speak
the things u hear
the things u touch
the things u smell
are just an image that u portray

but what it reflects to others is something bigger
a reflection of what ur thinking can also be used as a wepon
it has slain many people
what a mirror knows 
no body will ever know

u walk down a street 
thinkn everythings okay
u bump into a stranger
a vibe is sent
he runs away
theres something that was projected towards him
how do describe and image that looks like death in a form of an angel

whose that angel u ask?
is it a girl or boy?
why would u care
its just a person taking responsibility
or is it here to kill the ones that reject him or her
but i think its just how the image is shown

this image is sent in the form of a being
some one is walking next to death himself
u may think that this image of death is strange
u may also think that my image of death is amusing
the blood i carry is black for the color of death beating at my door
i open it
hell is loose
the heat just takes me
i dont care about life anymore
i become dead, but in another realm
where no one can take me

relationships are nuthning but meaningless bondages 
that turn u mindless at ur own thinking
u then do shit u regret
just like i did
i regret meeting a few people here and there
death isnt supposed to feel emotion

death walks all over emotion
as if its nothing and cant do anything now anyway
an image of death is the same image of truth
cant seem to hide it anymore
i must be let loose in the dark
where i can be made a sacrifice

im no longer worth this shameful body ive been givin
take me away and lock away the key
just burn my heart and heal it in the thickness of the dark realm called hell
its where i belong since i cant seem to step in a right area
my image is now gone
but u thought i was kidding
reflections can send a powerful meaning if u just look into my eyes 
u can see all the hate i have in me. 
my eyes burn with fury and rage
im now sick of everything and everyone

and u say its just a mirror
ur eyes are and obstacle or reflection
ur eyes are the mirror of ur existance 
it tells others what ur made of inside without a word said
and u say a mirror is just a mirror
whatever man.....
