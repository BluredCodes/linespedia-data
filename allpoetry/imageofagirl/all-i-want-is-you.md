---
title: "all i want is you"
writer: "imageofagirl"

humor me with soft
            words that delite my senses 
      and slowly, remove 
      the layers that hide my emotions 
                      when you get close enough 
       i can feel your breath 
                            like warm feathers, tickling and exciting 
      
                  quick beats of my heart 
                          your hands caress my fears 
        and mold them into understanding 
                               your body takes me over and crushes 
            my pessimistic views 
         let your touches take me to the final 
                                    assent to realization 
                   that all i want is you.
