break it out 
i        have           the               courage 
                                             to push on 
            I can make it           (watch me) 
                                              if you laugh( i will cry) 
but      i             will              survive (optimistic lyrics)
                   see these arms they are 
strong
                       like a bull 
i        can do what        i want with          my might 
                    see these legs they can 
run 
                     faster then a cheetah 
                                      (as fast as I have to, to get away) 
          see these eyes 
 they can see all of your secrets 
                                and mysterious deeds ( someone is always watching) 

                                    see this heart it can keep beating 
            with o r
     without you near. 
                          but it's so much easier when you're around. (don't leave)
