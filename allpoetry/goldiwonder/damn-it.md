Here I go with something so metaphorical 
Yet you people keep looking for something rhetorical 
I'm trying to get my deep stuff out of the box 
But all your straight forwardness supposedly rocks 
Now all of a sudden I'm respectable with fame 
But I won't forget the depth of which I came 

Rest your head, the salesmen never sold me 

I'm still myself, take it or not 
You're still you, but your simplicity will rot 
I'll cut you some slack and give you a break 
Explaining in small words would be a mistake 
So I'll break out with crayons and thick paper 
And draw you a Picasso that imagination will stir 
Are my ideas finally getting though?
