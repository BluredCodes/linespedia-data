Strap me here to this pole 
Delight me, tease my soul 
Constrict my breath 
Some fated yoke of pain 
The mere idea could drive a man insane 
Now strap it around her gentle curving lips 
Chain her legs, arms, throat and hips 
Give me a sign, lead me by your whip. 
I'll walk the plank of your sunken ship
