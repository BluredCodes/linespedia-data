Little Red Riding Hood   
Should have stayed where she stood    
Never walk those woods alone   
Immoral now, stand in stone
Revert me Back             
Thunder of Revertions   
She was just a child 
Leave her alone 
Just a child 
Innocent and mercy mild 
Stay in her mama's house 
Harm never touched that child
Don't walk the woods alone 
Never know when the wolf gonna come 
Never walk the woods alone 
Never know when the wolf's gonna comeI just want to know what you think
