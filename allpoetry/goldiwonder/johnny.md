The day I saw the gold
I realized it wasn't so old
To die at this age
Is such a rage
Not to me, but to other's
Who once had caring mothers
One who cares is a friend
But when it's over you'll find the end
Now I look down on my best friend 
looking for the pieces to bring together to mend
Yet he who could not comprehend
Brought his own life to an end 
I told Pony, to Stay Gold 
And hope forever this he may hold
Now I dedicate this to you Pony. . .
Yes Natures first green is GoldI wrote this when I was in 7th grade for the book The Outsiders, which is now my favorite book. If any of you have read it you probably know what it's about, but if not I hope you can still enjoy it's 7th grade quality
