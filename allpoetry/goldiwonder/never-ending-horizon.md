It is a towering inferno on a midsummer's eve;
It showers us with water throught it's endless sieve;
A golden fleece racing dawn;
An eternal cover, all day long;
Darkening with night;
Such a breath taking sight;
Helping sunshine pour its glaze;
Helping plants through their phase;
Sheltering Heaven from it's uncanny view;
With stars shining brightly through, and through,
Guarder of the winds, the clouds, the sun, the moon, the stars,
If this blanket should happen to fall;
It will surely mean the death of us all.
