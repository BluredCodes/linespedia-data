title: "My Chamber"
writer: "GoldIWonder"

Monster's reaping through my brain 
Intense demons guide my navigational chart 
Some raging savior barely keeps me sane 
Curses of the earth rape my heart 
My knees grind the dust, shake my pain 
I walk with everyone so far apart 
I look at myself an unwanted stain 

Scars burning the inside, sewn on the out 
These menaces like cancer, just smile and wave 
Oh for the gut to stand up and shout 
But no, just stay in a shallow grave 
Keep eyes shut while still staring about 
Ignorance is bliss, innocence will save 

Come whisper bravery in my ear 
Good-Bye hindering, sacred hive 
I'll stand up and shout, Can they hear? 
They hear me! Attention does arrive
They stare at me! Mockery now does steer
They strike me! But still I do survive.
