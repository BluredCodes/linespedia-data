I see your face in my mind but never imanged 
that we come face to face. Now and
forever you hold my heart as for me 
i hope to hold yours for now and forever.
As time goes by remember me for i will always 
remember you as a drean that suddenly came true.this is one of the first poems that i ever wrote.
