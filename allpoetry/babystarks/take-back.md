writer: "babystarks"

What image of me can i sell for you to understand what
i was going through.
 You may not even care that i tried so hard to make you
understand.
 I did it out of love nothing i ever did was to hurt you,
but you took it the wrong way what can i do or say.
 Tell me how i can get things back to normal cause i'm 
losing my mind just sitting here waiting for you to call.
 I cant take back what was in the past but i can try to 
fix the future but only if you let me.
 Please dont shut me out of youre life cause i know i cant
live without you, and i know deep down inside you fee the 
same way..
