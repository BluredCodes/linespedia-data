I think of you always but never know what to say around 
you.
 I love you i know i have done some terrible things in
the past but now it's time for you and me to work on 
the future.
 If  we dont work together nothing will become of our
time and time we cant waste.
 Every mintue count's every second is so important
to start a new begining.
 As we became closer to each other we will know that
this is the last time we will have to start a new bebegining 
all over again.
