Today was a day unlike the others.
This day was greeted with such mixed emotions.
My heart beating intensly with just the thought of you.
I had pictured us together in my head over and over again.
Not leaving one single detail out, but to my surprise after all
the planning still things played out differently from how i had expeceted
not saying that all was bad just noticing that it was different.
I tried to hide my feelings with wit and humor which actually turned into
 a form of agression.
Not completly meaning what i said.
I dont know what to do with my self i'm at my wits end.
I know you dont feel the same but it's ok because i'm not 
trying to pressure you. 
I'm just letting you know what's on my mind.
