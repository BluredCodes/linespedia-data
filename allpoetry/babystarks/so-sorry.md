title: "So sorry"
writer: "babystarks"

I know i'm sorry doesnt cut it but what can i do to 
make you understand i did what was right for the both
of us.
 You may not see it now but you will and if you do understand
what i'm saying understand why i'm saying it.
 I'm  saying it because i cant change the way i feel the same way 
you cant but if i could how fair would it be to you.
 Knowing that i had to change the way i feel about you i hope, wish,
pray that you understand i would hate to lose someone like you.
 I need you in my life you know me better then anyone else i just
hope we can remain friends.
 That's all up to you, you dont have to answer me anytime soon or
ever if you dont want to but if you ever need a friend you will 
always have me..
