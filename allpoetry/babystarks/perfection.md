Struggling to become the ideal person spending all your time 
trying to correct all your mistakes that were left in your past, 
while at the same time losing focus on the positives that you
 have built. 
The battles become more intense each time and you cant help but to 
blame your self for all of your flaws which in actuality are just 
little imperfections that you wish you could get rid of.
Beat your self up for not being skinny enough,your not smart enough, your not tall enough, your not this and your not that.
All the things that you were given were gifts and not punishments so why can't we accept them for what they are or we are. 
Perfection is unattainable it is the mer thought of being the perfect you
and since that's the case than everyone is perfect just the way the are 
because you can only be you nothing more and certainly nothing less.
