---
title: "Just for You"
writer: "babystarks"
slug: "just-for-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

I wish i could start time all over again just to see you.
Even if it's only for a mintue.
You have changed my life so much in such a short time i can't
imagine what life would be like with out you.
You are the center of my life as i am your protector now and 
forever this will always be.
No one has ever came close to knowing the true me i keep my inner
feelings locked away from the world and only you have the key.
You have broken down every single barrier that i have put up.
There is no point in denying my feelings for you because anyone
can see how much you mean to me.
In every way shape or form i love you forever yours.i wrote this for someone who is very dear to my heart on the spur of the moment..
