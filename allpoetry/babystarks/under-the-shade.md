title: "Under the shade"

Sitting back getting lost in the day just like we use to.
We would lay under the shade and dream about our future.
So many plans the world was ours for the taking.
Seasons came and went and our love grew stronger and stronger.
College driffted us apart for a short period of time.
But it could never tear us apart we are and will always be 
connected.
While i struggled your career took off but you never let me fall.
Pushing and encouraging me to follow my dreams.
Never allowing me to give up on my self.
Now our lives have come round in a full circle.
OUr dreams have now become our reality.
We started out in the world as half's and now we are whole.
My love for you has never wandered my heart has always been with you.
Under the shade we look one another in the eye and  see
how fortunate we are to have one another as we watch the sun set forever.
