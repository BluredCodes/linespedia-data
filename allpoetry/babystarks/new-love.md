Thinking back to the first day we meet, just a regular day.
No intense feelings no secert sparks no love for one another. 
We were just friends no more and no less,as the months flew by
As emotions kicked in the force between us was like a magnet to
strong to ignore there was no point in hiding our feelings.
It was there, the love, the trust, the respect all signs pointed
to yes.
I stay on the defensive side not ready to let my guard down.
Then with one touch you seemed to break all my insecurities in one
blow.
You never seize to amaze your openness your heart and soul in which 
you gave to me will always be with me.
