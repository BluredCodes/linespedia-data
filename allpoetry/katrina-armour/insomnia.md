Hello dear sir,
glad to see you're awake.
I hope you've had a 
nice sleep.

Dear sir,
I'm glad you've admitted your problem.
I hope you can find
your cure.

I've found an elixir
Doctor said it could mend this obsession
"What if we could fix her?"
No use killing yourself over depression

Hello dear sir,
glad to see you're alive.
I hope you've found a pill
to comfort the sleeping...

to comfort the sleeping
in all that you do.
