I hate myself for making sure
the phone is plugged in.
You said eight-ISH
so naturally I've been here since six.
If I had a rosary I'd be
saying my Hail Mary's,
though I've never been a Catholic
and I probably never will.
Did you forget my number?
I thought you'd written it on your mirror,
with your finger in the dust.
I always told you
you should clean that off.
