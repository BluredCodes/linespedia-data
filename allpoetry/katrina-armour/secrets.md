title: "Secrets"

Madmen speak in tongues
and I
just wanted you to know
that I
think medication might be useful
for you.

You want to know
if I see
your ghosts
and I tell you
I just saw someone leave.

Do you lie,
Tigerlily,
when you know the truth
can hurt?
Do you hurt,
Tigerlily,
when you know the truth
can kill?

I do.

You want to know
if I'm doing well;
I don't know
the truth.
