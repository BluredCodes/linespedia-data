Are you
running in water,
covered in tracks?
Do you
hear voices
where there should be none?

Psst...

Hey.

Hey you.

Can you panic?

4 white walls
You're in a box now
Cut off mom
Cut off dad
They were getting blurry anyhow
Wipe the smudges off your photographs
(as long as you don't get compulsive)

Psst...

Hey.

Hey you.

Start panicking.
