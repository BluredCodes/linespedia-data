In time,
everything is;
time breeds patience
the hurry of youth
becomes wisdom
and, a need
to see more
than the everyday
happenstance 
brought by life
this young
can't wait attitude 
becomes quiet
with age
a knowledge
and acceptance
clocked by seasons
and circumstance
experience is master
the eons the vehicle
and in the end
it all comes
down to this;
you have to
pay to ride 
the pony.....
