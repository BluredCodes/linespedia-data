---
title: "Shadow Dance"
writer: "butch"
slug: "shadow-dance"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54103-Shadow-Dance-by-butch"
---

Her eyes upon me
penetrating, touching 
examining

I turn to her
she looks away
pretend game

I watch her
in conversation
she hovers

To catch her eye
my intention
my secret goal

I circle her
like a hunter
zeroed in

Motile, slowly
cautiously focused
determined

She ignores me
though aware
feigning indifference

I get closer
almost tasting
almost scenting

Her voice
sings the words
velvet rhyme

At last 
I reach her
my voice grows

My courage 
leaves me
voice weakens

One last glance
as I sink back
amongst the shadows
