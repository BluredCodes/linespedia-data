title: "Common"
writer: "butch"

I walk upon this earth in awe
things in place
world eruption
tagging me it
chasing me towards
an unknown
I try to remain anonymous
but, cannot
beauty and tenderness
keep my focus
peachtree fantasy
my created music
forward of the realm
pique my interest
a rollercoaster ride
avoiding perdition
by moments and chance
twisted, kamikaze
swerving, uncontrollable
almost into the abyss
age slows me
sanity be found
saved from the edge
sweet restraint
at last
