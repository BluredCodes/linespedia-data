---
title: "Two For Time"
writer: "butch"
slug: "two-for-time"

I met an old woman today
I helped her with
the laundrymat machines.
she was very happy,
sweet little thing,
she reminded me of
my grandmother.
my grandma, when drinking
would give me a big 
mushy kiss, and I always
wiped it off,
and she would laugh,
I remember one time 
we got in a taxi
and went to the 
Horseshoe club,
to get my grampa.
they argued all the way
home, and she went to bed
and he reheated some chili
and fell asleep.
smoked up the whole house.
everyone woke up
panicked, and he still slept.
Grandma was so mad.
he laughed the next day.
I used to go with him
when he visited 
the local clubs
in the daytime,
to fix the beer boxes.
he knew them all
and, he never charged.
he was a good man,
she a good woman.
married for almost
60 years, they both
died too young,
for me anyway.
