title: "Cloud Toucher"
writer: "butch"

Daydreamer;
always thinking, always dreaming
never in one place for long
have to hide my pain
put myself somewhere
in a rainbow, find the gold 
the hero, the winner
everybody loves me
unravel the mystery
invented it, praise me
stopped the war
no one died
undid all the wrongs
replaced animosity
gave them understanding
never hungry
never tired
tears disappear
I touch clouds
I fly.
