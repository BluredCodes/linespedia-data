God, it's Saturday
serene, and peaceful
in early spring morn'
as I lay back and
listen to the
neighbors dog
barking at the
neighborhood  kids
playing and laughing
sun on their backs
I reflect on faces
as they return
to memory alive
as they were
bringing at once
warmth and sadness
but, refreshing
my resolve
glad to be here
so much more
to see and learn
and love to gain
I can go on
afterall......
