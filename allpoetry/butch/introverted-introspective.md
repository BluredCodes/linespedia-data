title: "Introverted Introspective"

Methods in mayhem, 
miracles and madness,
wicked wanderings, 
weighted wastes.
casual confabulation, 
cackled confessions,
baffled bemusement, 
battled bartering.
life in lonliness, 
lost and lachrymose,
groped gregariousness, 
goofs and goons.
holy habitat, 
hellishly haggard,
runaway railings, 
ripened raging.
found facade,
former furies,
justified joining,
jack-off jealousy.
yesterday's yearning,
yeilded youth,
needed none,
near naiveness.
paltry paltering,
pacing peace,
saved sanity,
secret sanctuary.This is an experiment. I hope none of you get too sick, when you read it. Butch
