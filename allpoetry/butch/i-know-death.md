---
title: "I Know Death"
writer: "butch"

I know Death;
he rides a dark horse
in flowing dark robe
he comes with the darkness
he shows no mercy
Death knows me;

He knows my name;
he follows me
comes to my dreams
wakens me in fright
points his finger
he calls me;

He laughs at me;
counts my minutes
hides in shadows
bides the time
patiently smug
he smiles;

Death awaits me;
casually remaining
his eyes on me
as the end nears
resigned I become
I await Death....
