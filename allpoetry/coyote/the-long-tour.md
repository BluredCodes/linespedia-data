found a lot to drink--
found a lot to smoke--
found a lot to pop--
and only a year left to go.

lost twenty pounds--
lost my virginity--
lost a stripe--
and only six months to go.

lost another partner--
lost faith in the machine--
lost faith in me--
and now it's time to go.

found where I was goin'--
found what I was doin'--
found where I belong--
and another six months to go.any old vet will understand the breakdown of the one year tour, but I want to know if what I am saying is clear to all age ranges. Talk at me, please!
