I try & pretend I don't need people around, they speak but I react not~
this man isolated and lost, even through success of the bad habits that I've dropped
there is no more reason to crop, I die slow and live as eternal corpse~
so I seek an end to all thoughts, to eliminate my pain from it's very source

~But since it'll never cease, there's no use in even trying....

Respectfuly Dead,
~Timmy G.
