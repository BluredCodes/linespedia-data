Life is a stick of gum
once the flavor is gone
Its no more good

But theres more to life
then just life and death
theres several steps to it
Its like climing stairs 
step my step
learning what things you should
and shouldn't do

things that must be done 
and follow through
Family must be loved
Friends must be cherished
You must be noticed

populairty is nice to have
but then you often need a break
hang with people who don't care
about populairty
people wo are in the middle
people who care about things
that are not really talked about
by the backstabers and stakers

Just go on and be happy on who are 
Your life isn't over yet
Always wait and see what happens next
You never know
It could be something good....
