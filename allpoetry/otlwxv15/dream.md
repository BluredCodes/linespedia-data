I often wonder aobut things I shouldn't 
Things that wonder in my head
over, and over again
Thats just me I guess
I think

Will I ever find that special someone
will I ever get married
will I always be alone
will I always stay singled

I have dated
this one special guy he was
for two years
then it ended
I've had a few flings
but their nothing
nothing compared to that special guy

His kindness
his touch
It just warmths me when I think of
the good days
good days that are special

Though
I go on
gon day after day
wishing for something good to happen
which things do happen
good things
but its ok


I'll go on and see what happens next
to the future
My life is very enjoying
yeah, sure
theres ups
and downs
but that come with the whole package
And I'll just go on dreaming
with high hopes
and continue with my life
cuz there is always something
excting that gonna happen
just wait and seeIs it to boring?
