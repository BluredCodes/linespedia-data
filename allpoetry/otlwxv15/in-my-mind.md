I start to think about thinks that
happend in the past
things that are storage in my mind
thinks that i'll never forget

Pictures of us having fun
Thoughts of each other
Memories of the good and bad things

things that make my day
things that make me cry
etc.......

Oh how wonderful those things are
And I think you for each day
we spent together
things, thoughts, gifts, that
i'll remember till the end of time

You who I'll cherish
You who I'll always be
at side with
Because it is you 
Who lights my dayI hope you enjoyed it *
