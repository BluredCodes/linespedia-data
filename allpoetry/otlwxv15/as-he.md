As he tells me that he did it…
I smile to myself saying,
“I knew he could do it.”
The words had changed 
On what he told me Sunday and today…
So, I start to read,
Wanting to know what’s about…
As I start to read it,
I said, “ I’m glad he found someone.”
Then when I start to read more…
I start to cry on the sweet words he wrote…
It made me realized that it was me…
Me who God has put before him…
