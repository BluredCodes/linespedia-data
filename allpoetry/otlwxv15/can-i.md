Is their someone wishing for me out their
Someone who thinks I' someome then just
one person out of the entire world
Who will think of me  always
who will stay by my side till the end

Would I be able to wish for things
I've always wanted
will I ever get the chances that I want
Am I being too comfusing in my life
Am I confusing

Things will come and go
they'll want to stay or not
thats's how it is
to me or to everyone one else
its ether can't or can
which one to choose
I would like "can"
But can it always be "can"
Or can it be "can't"

Oh why do things have to be
so difficult
can't it be simple
can't it be fun
is it all above
I'm just going on
Thinking over, and over, and over
The same-o questions I would want answered
The same questions that I ponder
And wonder about

Can I do this or that
Can I be someone famouse
Can I go to the stars
Can I be everything I ever
wanted to be
CAN'T I JUST BE MEhow is it?
