The moment of it is harsh 
It hits you bad
You feel empty
And something pounding on
Your chest

Tears fall down your face
One by one
You try to prevent them
But you can't stop
Why oh Why can't it stop

Please stop
Please lord make it stop
I should be happy
I should be glad
He's free now
Why am I so sad
