I hold my my breath waitng
I just want you to hold me
Tkae me in your arms
Never letting me go
An unspoken bond

I wants you to live each day
Knowing that I don't have to 
worry you'll be there
And me being there for you
You don't ever have to worry
About that

I pray you'll be there
Making my life a better place
Comforting me when sad
Smilling at me when I'm happy
Just adapting to me

I want to be there or you
Always and forever
Working everything out
Listening to you
Memorizing every word

I want to be the perfect being
The one you've dreamed of
The perfect mold
This way our love would never
End
