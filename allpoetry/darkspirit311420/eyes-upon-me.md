title: "Eyes Upon Me"

I wake every morning and gaze into the mirror.
A pair of eyes stares back at me.
And as I look back,it makes me wonder.
What do others eyes see when they gaze upon me?
I know when i see me,I see a big,ugly monster,
But what do my friends,family,or even total strangers see?
Do they see my determination to succeed in life?
How bout the scared child inside of me?
Maybe they see me happy on the outside,
When truly,until I find my true love,I'm just empty on the inside.
Is it easy for people to look at me the way I am?
If it was me i wouldn't,I would turn and walk the other way.
So as any eyes are upon me,
I understand why everyone looks the other way.
