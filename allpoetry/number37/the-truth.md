how do i react to your choices? 
i do react but i dont know how to 
so what do i do? i run. 
i want  to please everyone. 
have him ,take me, love him , hate me! 
I CANT LOVE! 
i'll run just a little further away 
and maybe i mean just maybe you'll leave me alone!
stay away! 
leave me in my darkness where i feel COMFORTABLE

cant you see i am SCARED!
i said it! i'm scared!
go to him becaus i cant love you
i cant love.......i am sorry about the grammor. i amnot perfect and i suck at it anyway.
