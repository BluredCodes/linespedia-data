---
title: "Anything For You"
writer: "Maharet"
slug: "anything-for-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

I love you
I worship you
I’d do anything you’d want me to

Taking a risk
I’ve got nothing to lose
As long as you’re always by my side

I’ll take any religion if you wanted me to
Or if you wanted I’d burn down all the churches 
Just for you

I’ll kill someone for you if you wanted me to
Get sentenced to jail
I’d run from the cops for you

I don’t have any money 
But I’ll steal anything for you
I’d give anything to you

Crawl on my knees
I’d kiss your feet
Just to feel close to you...baby

Surrender my soul to you
Break my bones
I’d die for you

Put chains around my neck
I’d be a dog for you
I’ll be a loving pet too

I’ll be strong for you
Watch over you
I’d protect you

I’ll feel the misery for you
I’d cry your tears for you
Feel sad just for you

Lend you my shoulder
Frighten away all your fears for you
I’d take care of you

Make up silly jokes
I’ll laugh for you
I’d make you smile too

Get naked for you
I’ll lie across your bed just for you to stare
I’d pleasure you if you wanted me too

Sing my songs for you
I’ll even dance to them too
I’d be your personal entertainer

Every word you speak
I’d listen to you
I’ll comfort you as much as you need

Enter through hell’s gates
I’d walk over burning coal for you
Feel pain if you wanted me too

I’ll be a slave for you
Let you whip me too
I’d work hard for you

Open my mind up to you
I’d tell you anything you wanted me too
Everything is for you

Sail across stormy oceans
I’d be seasick over you
I’d go far away if you wanted me too

I obey you
I pray to you
You are my god too

Everything
I’d take in to my hand
And give you all I had

I’ll do anything for you
Just as long as you are mine
And love me too
