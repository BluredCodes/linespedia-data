And Jesus said unto her, "I am the resurrection and the life, whosoever believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live.  And I say unto you he that liveth in me shall never die."  John 11:25

In Loving Memory of:

Miss Wanda Hamilton
"Mine eyes have seen the glory of the coming of the Lord!"



My heart it aches when I think of you

You've gone away, now no more can we do.

Fallen soldier, after a battle well fought

Living the eternal life that long you've sought.


Fallen soldier who fought up to the end

A loyal companion, leader, and friend.

Fallen soldier who surrendered never 

You're etched in the clay of my heart forever.


Your life was well spent and happy indeed

You never turned away whenever I had need.

Fallen soldier I send prayers for you now

Fallen soldier, my heroine art thou.
