Leni-Lenape Trail  -  A Sonnet

A Persian carpet spread amongst the trees
 Of red and gold mosaic tapestry,
collage of leaves spun by an autumn breeze
from sycamore and oak surrounding me

I climb the upward pathway to the crest
of foothills plush with colors, wet with dews
Though trees are bare, my path is finely dressed
in Appalacian’s range of autumn hues

This trail was etched by footsteps of a tribe
in winter white and bronze of autumn tones
And as I walk the pathway they inscribed
I feel somehow I do not walk alone

The brightly painted hillsides mesmerize
As I gaze out through someone elses’s eyes
