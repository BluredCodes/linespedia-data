Soft as a Flower

Soft as a flower to open its bloom
Sweet as a rose to bestow its perfume
Slow as a child to waken from sleep
Mystic as  stars of the evening to creep

Gentle as seeds of the earth come alive
Wet by the tenderest tears from the skies
Everything changing with purpose and grace
Going unnoticed by moments of haste

God sets the hour for petals to bloom
Man who determines what eyes will consume
All of life’s treasures enticing the eye
So soft and so silently tiptoeing by
