Relinquishing the Night

The gentle glow of dawn
leans into the early hour,
pushing against the horizon
luring me to its blush of rose
that blooms slowly above 
the crest of the hill.

just as a fragile flower
slowly turns its petals,
revealing the unique design
of its delicate slihouette,
enticing me to gaze deeply
into the sensual eye
of an orchid.

I am surrounded by
the softness of the moment, 
the power of nature 
ever moving 
toward newness,
that enfolds me in
the pastel pleasures of dawn.

And I am no longer hesitant
to relinquish the night
as its starry bliss disappears into
a purple veil of peace
