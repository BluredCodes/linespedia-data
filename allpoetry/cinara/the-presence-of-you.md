The Presence of You

You are the fragrance of the wind
In April dawn I breathe you in
to soothe the deep and longing ache
and savor every breath I take

From early mist I gather dew
and taste the essence that is you
I drink you with a lover’s lust
from brimming cup of love and trust

You brush against my cheek and lips
with tender phantom fingertips
caressing skin and silken hair
with gentle puffs of springtime air

Your presence is embracing me
with warming arms of ecstasy
that bind two lovers far apart
with distance to entice the heart

Your essence flies the April wind
and fills the emptiness within
