Field of Green

The moning haze is lightly spread
Across the meadow’s April bed
Beneath the mist a field of green
And woodland’s edge soon to be seen

Behind the cloud of foggy dew
A white tailed deer within my view
A frilly fringe of purple lace
Of redbud’s spattering limbs of grace

And just beyond this field I’ve known
An ancient barn of granite stone
Will soonl appear when morning haze
Dissolves into remembered days

And brings to view as crystal clear
As childhood memeories held so dear
A field of green in early morn
A place where many dreams were born
