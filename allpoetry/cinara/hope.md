HOPE

Hope is the garden
surrounding a dream,
filling its eyes
with colors of desire

it is moonlight soaking into
every crevice of passion

it is a soft breeze that caresses the
the visions of
a kiss

and the wings of a nesting lark
harboring songs 
yet unsung,

Thr eyes of a dream
gather summer flowers,
 bathe in opal glows of moonlight
and dance to the silent music
that is sketched
on the fringes of longing

HOPE gives life to a dream
and makes it rejoice
in its delight
even before
 the first dawn
 of possibility
