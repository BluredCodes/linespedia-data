You Came to Me Gently

I lowered my head
in the pillowy down

my eyes bearly awake,
my mind weary and muttled,
my aching bones
sinking into the softness
of my bed

The pain in my limbs
flowed down my body
from head to toe,
and was 
suddenly released
outof my feet

and my bones now
floated in comfort

You came to me
so gently
in the dark
caressing my memories

my mind took a step 
backward in time
for a few moments
of pleasure
which pulled the darkness
from my eyes
and filled them
with the figure of you

In a solitary breath
I drank you in
and my heart skipped 
a slight beat
as I felt a pang 
of silver lightening
piercing my chest
with ectascy and sorrow

I pulled my quilt up
over my shoulders
savoring your warmth
for just a moment

and cuddled
into a light slumber,
directing my visions
toward the rapture 
of the moment

as I fell off
to sleep
