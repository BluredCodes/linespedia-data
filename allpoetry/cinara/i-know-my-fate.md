I Know my Fate


I know what fate awaits me
What fields I have to plow
But it’s not written in the stars
Nor etched upon my brow

I know the future burdens
To which God assigned my name
But this does not consume my thoughts
Nor flow within my vein

For I possess abounding love
And peace within my soul
Treasures much more certain
Than the future they foretold

When waters flow from mountain springs
I cannot stop the stream
I can only live the moment
I can only live the dream
