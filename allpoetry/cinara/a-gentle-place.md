This Gentlle Place

Thank you for occupiying
this gentle place
in my heart
A space that no one else
has ever touched
It is filled with all the good things
on earth, the sea gulls, the laughter
the sweet midnight moments
fragrant boquets
the everlasting kisses
the tender sound of your voice
our favorite music
your love, your smile
YOU  
and the day you left me 
for another
The years of pain , the time it took
to become my own person again
All of this dwells in a gentle place
in my heart
I shall never relinquish that 
sacred chamber for anything else
for it is the most
wondrous love I have ever known
It is unseen, unattainable
unknown to others,
It is a bittersweet memory
that occupies
a very gentle place
within my heart
