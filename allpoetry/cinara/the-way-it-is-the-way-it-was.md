The Way It Is, The Way It Was

Dressed in summer’s dusty gown
The centuries old Brazilian town
So far removed from present day
Still living the forgotten way

The spoken word remains the proof
Beneath a terra-cotta roof
The way it was, the way it is
Still living life as it was lived

On top the hill ’gainst sunset sky
A sihouette of deathly cry
The sacred source of weekly food
A bounded steer his future rued

From dusk to dawn the beast is tied
Aware that he’s the next to die
The day was marked, the time is his
To live his life the way it is

The ritual stays the way it was
The reason being, just because
