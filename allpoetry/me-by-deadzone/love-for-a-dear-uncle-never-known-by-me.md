A person I have admidered but have not met
Is my mothers brother Joe, who died by a car
If I were to meet him, in the past, in a malt shop is where we would take a seat
On a night wher there is a moon but not one star
The moon would be bright, his suit new
Maybe a light rain
So that in the morning the grass be full of dew
We would talk and sip on old fashioned malts, while what gose by is a trian
I would tell him about my childhood
He would tell me about his boxing and his life and life and thoughts
His hair would be combed back , as best, as good
We might both enjoy the food we bought
While in eachothers company he tells me about my mother as a child
I would be overjoyed just to see him with my own eyes
His life was really cool and mild
Then I leave with a sigh
I will miss him so much, but at least I got to see that face and hear that voice and see that smile
And at least he got to know me
He will be in my memory for more than a while
And at last he got to know and see
The person he never got to know aout and that felt a love for him without knowing the person he was
