---
title: "In Need of Cleansing"
writer: "sonrisa"
slug: "in-need-of-cleansing"
source: "AllPoetry"

twisted wrists
bleed deep
beyond the iron
that binds

a million reasons
to hate you
on the tip of my tongue

a thousand questions
of why
confuse and torture 
hold me back

in need of cleansing.....

take me 
to the place that can
clean away the poison 
of hurt in my mind 

look to the sky 
for the way
bow my head
in response to refusal

desperately in need of cleansing.....

follow the pills
with the acid
that burns away
the pain

cut 
and bleed away
the liquid words
of a love gone

forever in need of cleansing.....
