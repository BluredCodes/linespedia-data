Deliver me some evil 


Deliver me some evil Father 
As you unzip me 
Deliver me some love 
As you start to suck 
Deliver some love 
Onto my teen body 
Deliver me some shame 
As you betray my trust 
Deliver me onto 
Years of therapy 
Years of lies 
Years of pain 
Deliver onto me some truth 
I never knew 
Of your sickness 
Of your transfers 
Even after the decree 
Signed and sealed 
Not like the young lips 
You forced open 
Ignoring my pleas 
Father no 
Father no 
Father no 
Father 
Deliver me some peace 
I thought I was 
The only one 
Til I read the paper 
Saw the story 
And learned what had happened 
Deliver me an answer 
Archbishop Cover-up 
How many boys 
Is a priest 
Supposed to have 
Until he has bagged his limit 
Deliver some jail time 
For Father and the 
Archbishop 
Deliver me a faith 
That protects its young 
And faces 
Not hides 
Its sins 
Deliver me some reason 
To overcome my anger 
And my grief 
For what was given 
To me 
As my birthright 
Has been taken 
Help me learn 
The beauty 
Of true love 
Please someone 
Anyone who cares 
If not God 
Please deliver us 
From this evilA difficult topic
