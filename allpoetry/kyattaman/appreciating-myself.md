Appreciating Myself


Appreciating myself
A meditation for Kenyatta
Things are things to remember
when bills depress me
employers stress me
muscles ail me
memory fails me
checks bounce me
I just had a prostate exam
how embarrassing 
to bend over
drop my pants
and be touched
by a stranger
my goodness
but bottom line
my friend
you are here
and are now
of a certain age
when you must
if you are to
remain
on this earth
take care
of your body
think of
the things
you could not have done
had you drowned
years ago
in a panic
the proposals
never written
the connections
never made
the love
not enjoyed
and so what of
this unpleasantness
to detect a disease
whose symptoms
I am sure
you will not want
to experience
impotence
sleeplessness
urine problems
and then
the final
symptom
the exam
is a sign 
of your life
your ease
your ability to travel
your access
to health care
your tax dollars
paying for those human needs
you promote
with friends
with strangers
with party members
with politicians
but there is more
you were so proud
to be mentoring
to be teaching
younger people
to be leaders
taking up your causes
you called out to them
to represent
to be antiracist
without shame
or guilt for being
what they could not change
the color of their skin
the size and shape
of their features
their family history
so now you are
beyond racism
venturing
into other areas
mental health
gays and lesbians
creativity
drumming
nonprofits
good works
ourselves
our lives
your love
for a woman
that you tried
so often
to throw away
walk away
run away from
appreciate also
that you are
writing
a gift
not everyone possesses
nor do they possess
their own computer
in a nice house
heated
and with electricity
in a quiet neighborhood
where you know
the computer
will be waiting 
for you
when you return
from therapy
from your work
and from the world
and lest you forget
the senses that it takes to write
my friend
although there is Braille
your eyes can see
your hands can move to type
your brain can send
the messages
to your eyes
and hands
and know
that they will respond
the music in the background
that you hear puts you
in the mellow mood
a woman singing
what’s new
but the question
is not what’s new
but what’s still there
and that’s you
and that’s you baby
so jus’ enjoy it!Reposted from Story Area
