If Jerry Springer Calls, Hang Up
 


scorned lovers attack 
men 
women 
black and white 
gay and straight 
and bisexuals 
who don’t know 
don’t care what you think 
they are here to tell it all 
bare their souls 
their breasts 
their butts and 
their lovers’ lives 
for a precious few moments 
jerry jerry jerry 
today’s guest 
has been sleeping 
with her brother 
and thinks it’s 
wrong 
that her mother 
wants to horn in 
I just can’t take it jerry 
incest whores 
ya’ll folks 
just need to back off 
cause ya’ll don’t know 
what’s truly goin on here 
question from the audience 
ya’ll folks are sick
jerry jerry jerry
mom lifts up her shirt 
you’re just jealous 
cause 
my son’s gettin all of this 
audience groans
laughs and howls
shake the studio
and twenty men
of different ages
sizes and colors 
come onstage
as the biggest breasts
this side of the Grand Canyon
flop down to mom's waist
there's audience
participation
there's true love
there's the future 
as we know it
but if your girl friend says
i gotta tell you a secret
beware
you just might find out
she's a guy
jerry jerry jerry
