You Will Pass Out Before You Die

You weren’t supposed to die Corey
Just pass out
From the heat
From 300 plus pounds
From being so much meat
On the hoof
From the inner logic
Of football
That says
Play through it
Get bigger
Get tougher
And above all
Stay on the field
After you had died
I read about
Coaches
Who told players
Like you
That in the case
Of heat exhaustion
You will pass out
And it 
Will be 
All right
And I said
Oh no
They could not say that
Not in
This day and age
Of medicine
And training
And it 
Is lucky
Oh so lucky
That we
Do not have
Three hundred
Plus 
Pound
Bodies
In hospitals
More
Insurance
Checks
Being written
To widows
More letters
Of regret
More children
Without fathers
And so
I am
Wondering
What was the
Price of the ticket
I just boughtSomething to consider
