The Ghetto Business Rap Version 2
 
 
 What kind of business
 Take care of business
 The ghetto business rap
 
 It’s time to talk about
 A plague
 On our community
 It's time talk about
 Rebuilding cities
 It's time to talk about
 Our families
 
 What kind of business
 Take care of business
 The ghetto business rap
 
 When we talk about plague
 We’re not just talking
 About just disease
 We’re talking about
 A Dis Ease
 And an I can’t get no ease
 
 What kind of business
 Take care of business
 The ghetto business rap
 
 Yes brothers and sisters
 The plague I’m talkin' 'bout
 Is the ghetto business
 What kind of  business
 Take care of business
 The ghetto business rap
 
 Now first off clear your minds
 Of everything that you
 May know about what makes a ghetto
 It’s how you treat others
 That puts you in the ghetto
 
 What kind of business
 Take care of business
 The ghetto business rap
 
 If your workers
 Don’t know
 When, if or even how
 They will be paid
 You are sure enough
 Full of ghetto
 
 What kind of business 
 Take care of business
 The ghetto business rap
 
 Ghetto business
 May be white, black, red or yellow
 It’s how you act that makes you ghetto
 You may be friend
 You may be foe
 Could be a John or even Jane Doe
 
 What kind of business
 Take care of business
 The ghetto business rap
 
 I worked in a factory
 It nearly killed me
 No ventilation no air condition
 No family
 No inspection
 Then came a fire
 Right through the wire
 
 What kind of business
 Take care of business
 The ghetto business rap
 
 They blocked the exits
 We could not get out
 Many died from smoke inhalation
 No OSHA visits
 Saw the violations
 So many died couldn’t get out
 
 What kind of business
 Take care of business
 The ghetto business rap
 
 We've got to take back our lives
 Too many fires waiting to happen
 There are workers
 Who are breathing poison
 There are factory owners
 Who ought to be in prison
 
 We’ve got to close them down
 The ghetto business
 We’ve got to shut them down
 The ghetto business
 Take care of business
 It’s time to shut it down
 The ghetto business rap
 
 Rap on Brothers and Sisters
 No matter what color or race
 Yeah, stare injustice in the face
 And rap on...
 Fade to black
 
 KyattamanThis one has a beat, the other didn't
