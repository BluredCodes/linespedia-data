The Ghetto Business


Let’s talk for a moment
About a plague
That is ravaging our communities
Breaking up families
Spreading crime
And increasing poverty
Yes it is the ghetto business
And this is a phenomenon
That we must deal with
As a people
If we are to progress
But I don’t want to give you
The impression
That the ghetto business
Is strictly a black thing
Ghetto business can be white, yellow
Red, black or some of all
It can be male or female
A relative
Or someone you wish
You had never met before
And one thing for sure
Ghetto business is too bad
Too cold
And too sick
For you to allow it any sympathy
The only common factor
That these ghetto businesses have
Is green
The color of money
And the bile
In your stomach
As you think about
The hours that you wasted 
Working for one of these ghetto businesses
It is easier to tell you
What can and very often
Does happen in these ghetto businesses
As a way of defining them
One of the ways to know
If you are in a ghetto business
If you have to beg for your paycheck
You're in a ghetto business
If you thought you were ired
And thought you knew
Your payrate but then you don't
You're in a ghetto business
If you have to threaten
To sue
To make them see the light
You're in a ghetto business
Ghetto business can be big business
Hiding behind a small business
Check out
Who stands behind
Those small factories
Could be K Mart
WalMart
And other marts
Big discounts
Big profits
And be onto you
In their fact   ories
Because it
May be the
Twenty first century
But it's like 
The Nineteenth century
In some of these
Ghetto businesses
And don't count
On inspectors
Because your complaint
May lead to 
INS or IRS
And 
Being dropped off
Somewhere in the 
Middle of nowhere
Wondering how to 
Get back 
To your family
All from thinking
You have rights
In the 
Ghetto business
Watch out for those fumes
But that little mask
Won't save your lungs
In the ghetto business
Ghetto business
May fire you
Today tomorrow or the next
For any reason
At any time
At any place
The Christmas party
The day of your bonus check
The day after
Your evaluation
Ghetto business
Ghetto business
Could be for profit
Non profit
Christian
Muslim
Or atheist
Every day
Ghetto business
Takes down
Lives
Smokes out careers
Leaves babies
Without parents
And moves on
They have no shame
Ghetto business
May take your dignity
And if they get you once
Shame on them
But if they get you twice
Shame on you
Don't be ashamed
To admit it
And move on
Because it isn't right
Ghetto business
Cause they will take your life
Ghetto business
Ghetto businessman
You have no right
To that money
That you made from the sweat of my brow
It's gonna be another day
There has got to be a better way
I'm gonna leave today
Ghetto business
I'm gonna make it right
Listen  to what I say
Around the corner
In the next room
in the next hour
Is another boss
Ready to do it to you
You've got to beware
Of ghetto business
Of ghetto business
Fade outLife in the city
