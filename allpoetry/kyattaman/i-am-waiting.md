I am waiting


I am waiting
Ever so
I am waiting
For priorities
Of our nation
To include
Children
I am waiting
For no child
To be left behind
I am waiting
For America
To truly foster children
To care
And spend
As much for children
As we spend on 
Missiles
Helicopters
And gunships
I am waiting
For a time
When advocates
Don't need to sue
File endless
Depostions
Pleading for help
I am waiting 
For a time
Of peace
When the children
Of other lands
Are not 
Seen as 
Other
But are as 
Valued
And precious
As our own
I am waiting
For leaders
I am waiting
For questions
I am waiting 
For justice
I am waiting
For peace
In my lifetime
I am waiting
To wake up one day
And hear
Not of 
More war plans
But of 
Irrigation dtiches
In Palestine
Factories in Rwanda
Education in 
Angola
Farmer co-ops
In Iraq
And so much more
I am waiting
For joy
I have been
Waiting
And marching
And singing
And wanting
My leaders
To catch up
To stop denying
The truth
So that the
World can truly 
Foster its children
Why should we
Keep them waiting?




Kenyatta YamelI'd Rather Make Toys
