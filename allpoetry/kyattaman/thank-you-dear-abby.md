Thank You Dear Abby

I’ve got to eat my sister in law
I can’t deny it
I just can’t hide it
Next time she comes
It’s her face or mine

I wrote Dear Abby
A few weeks ago
Telling her of my urgent need
My own dear sister in law
Was coming to town
And staying with my hubby and me

I’ve got to eat my sister in law
I can’t deny it
I just can’t hide it
Next time she comes
It’s her face or mine

I was too shamed to admit it
I played around like I was timid
I told Dear Abby
She’s really a sweet girl
I just can’t stand
All her nudity

I’ve got to eat my sister in law
I can’t deny it
Don’t knock it until you try it
Next time she comes
It’s her face or mine

In our small flat there’s little room
A kitchen for the three of us
Hubby and me have the bedroom
The first time I saw her
 I thought I would swoon
When June said I sleep in the buff

I’ve got to eat my sister in law
I can’t deny it
I just can’t hide it
Next time she comes
It’s her face or mine

I couldn’t eat I couldn’t sleep
Knowing the truth was gnawing at me
I am so straight
I’ll even masturbate
How can I think of toast and jam
With that pus staring at me

I’ve got to eat my sister in law
I can’t deny it
I just can’t hide it
Next time she comes
It’s her face or mine

Dear Abby said take my advice
Next time she comes
Give her a robe
But when I  watched  TV
All I could see
Were June’s  golden globes

I’ve got to eat my sister in law
I can’t deny it
I just can’t hide it
Next time she comes
It’s her face or mine

One day when June came and my hubby was out
I confronted her
Dear God please June for my sanity
And the sake of my marriage
Put on some clothes
She laughed and said
You’re bi like me

We have progressed
Thanks to Dear Abby
One day it’s Phil
Next day it’s June
Sometimes we’re 3
No need for modesty

We’ve got to eat my sister in law
We don’t deny it
Don’t knock til you have tried it
