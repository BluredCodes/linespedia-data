It held her


It held her
something we don’t tell mama
it held her
don’t tell papa
it held her
not no teacher neither
it held her
it’s just an us thing
it held her
our little joy
it held her
can’t run away
it held her
who’s that outside
it held her
some fool shouting
it held her
no one protected me
it held her
no one knew my shame
it held her
from childhood to adulthood
it held her
from victim to perpetrator
it held her
stopped her from crying
it held her
it held her daughter
why she never got help
it held her
til it was too late
oh mama
it held her
her daughter cried
it held her
til the trial ended
it held her
til after the verdict
it held her
til we visited
her
the last time
in prison
to claim her body
til it was too late
to know 
what had been done to her
and now it held us all
