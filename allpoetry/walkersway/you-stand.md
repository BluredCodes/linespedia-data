My moods shift like the tides
and yet you stand with me
against all reason.
Beyond thought and selflessly
you stand with all your friends and lover
While others say run
still you stand

My words may elevate you 
to a level beyond compare
still they are only words
and actions are where feelings can be seen
And yet my words can be scary
and still you stand

Your passion and energy is immeasurable
Your simpilcity and wisdom is the voice
I need to hear
And you stand
Your love and friendship is what I seek
it is clarity in my murky waters
you stand as my beacon

I drown in uncertainty
and you stand as my preserver
You stand so I don't fall

So I stand beside you
or no where at allThis is about a friend who has stuck by me through all of life's mishaps.
