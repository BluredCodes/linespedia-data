Gazing out across the frozen lake

listening  to the guggrle of the manmade fountain.
I reflect and huddle deep in my cotton sweater.

So here I am. 
Life experience in my mind
And an empty horizon

I still fee some of my Childhood angst and anxiety
But now they are overshadowed by a feeling of peace and tranquility.

Sure I have to pay the bills
Sure I have to work
Sure I must eat
Sure I must die
But for now I am 
Happy with the way things are…

Gazing out across the frozen lake.I wrote this at my cottage, after a long and harrowing month, to remind myself that things aren't always as bad as they seem.
