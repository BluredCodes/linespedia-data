If I Knew Then What I Know Now

I have often wondered if given the chance 
to go back and learn it all again 
Would I?

If I knew now, what I knew then
Could I teach it to my younger self?
Would I want to?

Life is about pain and discovery
It is about hopes and failures
Should I take that away?

I consider myself the eternal student
Though the classroom has changed
And the grades are different
So when does the learning stop?

Now tardiness comes with a price
And achievement comes with reward
I’m no longer the awkward teenager
So why should I be sad?

It is in those moments between one and wonderment
That I learned a lot about my family, my friends, the world, the universe
and myself.

No I don’t think I could go back
Because I’m always looking forwardIf we could vist ourselves in the past to warn us of the future would we? This is dedicated to someone really special who is about to go on his own journey.
