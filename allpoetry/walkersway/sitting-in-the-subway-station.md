Sitting in the Subway
Listening to the clickity-clack 

From the training going the wrong way

That figures!

I always miss the one going my way.

A past manifesto of my life.
But I’m no longer trying to play catch up

Instead I am waiting for the train to come to me.Just came to me while I waited...
