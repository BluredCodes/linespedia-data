Cold is the world around us, living a line.
Drawn out by GOD, or is it the wine?

"We make our own fate", I said to the man.
Yelling, "I'm goin to be late!", he ran through the land.

The busy streaks and bodies of these cold towers,
have anyone time for sun flowers?

Withering is this life we have,
share with love to one another.
For these days are our time to play,
