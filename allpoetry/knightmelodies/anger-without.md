---
title: "Anger without"
writer: "KnightMelodies"
slug: "anger-without"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61994-Anger-without-by-KnightMelodies"
---

Hidden secrets now revealed,
love was lost without doubt.
False lies about the past,
I live a dark life with his spit.
From the mouth this foul stench,
in which it spews the wrath of man.
Both fathers fate of anger,
men without their honor.
In this anger devours man,
child, woman, and his land.Dedicated to my fathers . . . don't hurt your children; love your wife.
