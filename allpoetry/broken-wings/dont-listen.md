---
title: "Don't Listen"
writer: "Broken Wings"
slug: "dont-listen"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59662-Dont-Listen-by-Broken-Wings"
---

Cheat death,
Skip work,
Hold your breath,
Take a dive,
Close your eyes,
Spill your salt,
Chase a black cat,
Kick a ladder,
Step on cracks,
Spin really fast,
Fall without thinking,
Say what you want to,
Punch a pillow,
Scream at the sky,
Talk back,
Be good,
Be bad,
Plan your day,
Drive without steering,
Choose your path,
Hop on one foot,
See this world,
Through your own eyes,
Don't listen,
Help that old lady,
Take chances,
Hug a stranger,
Make someones day,
Make your own,
Love and Hate
Make mistakes
Be yourself and Live
