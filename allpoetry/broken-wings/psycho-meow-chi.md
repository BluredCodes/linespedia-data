A meow-chi is a new robo pet kinda like a furby
but more annoying


I swear it's out to kill me 
Those cute eyes only lie 
I wish I could make it fly 
Through the sky make it die 
Bye bye Meow-chi now you'll be quiet 
I'm putting my cats on a Meow-chi diet 
This makes no sense but I want it gone! 
It's kept me up for oh so long 
-smash smash- Finally it's still! 
Oops I forgot..It's not mine to kill[glares at the meow-chi] I'm not going to sleep while this thing is in the room
