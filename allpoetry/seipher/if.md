title: "If...."
writer: "Seipher"

If I could,
give you wings.
I'd watch
as you fly o'er me.
You'd soar off,
and leave me here.

If I had.....
just one wish.
I'd make it better for you.
I'd take away pain and sorrow.
Make you happy,
for the rest of your life.

If....
Just if I did.
This would make you happy,
and keep you that'a way.
But I can't.....
So you aren't.
