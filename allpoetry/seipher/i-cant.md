---
title: "I Can't"
writer: "Seipher"
slug: "i-cant"

---

I wish I could talk too.
  Just go away,
  don't need to stay.
I don't want to hurt you.

I don't know what's with me.
  I am the way I am,
  for reasons I don't understand.
Why can't you just see?

I tried to talk to you.
  Tried to tell my life,
  how troubles are rife.
But now what do I do?

Oh.... I wish I could.
  I don't know what to say,
  about why I feel this way.
Oh, if only you understood.
