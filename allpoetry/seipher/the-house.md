if I could talk,
If my walls could.
They would tell....
many'a things.

They'd speak of death.
People diminishing in health.
That is tll they are gone.
But their spirit remains.

They'd speak of life.
Life that is present,
Or new life.
Both are good.

They'd tell of birthdays,
Young getting older.
Those bringing others....
bright, happy, little ones to celebrate.

These wall would say....
Say all this plus more.
But my walls can't,
So you don't know.
