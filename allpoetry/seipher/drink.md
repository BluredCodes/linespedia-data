title: "Drink"
writer: "Seipher"

Wheter it's the fizz of coke,
or the warmpth of hot tea.
Whether the mixture of a milkshake,
or the aroma of sweet coffe.

It's a drink!
One that I've down'd.
It's a drink!
For everyone around.
