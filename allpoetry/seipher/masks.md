---
title: "Masks"
writer: "Seipher"
slug: "masks"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54723-Masks-by-Seipher"
---

We all have them
It's what we show the wold
mine is happy,
carefree, no worries.
Though it's just a mask.
You don't want people to see....
see what's on the inside.
Though it slips,
Some times that mask falls
The tough are shown weak,
the geekky, cool.
But no matter who you are,
the second the mask falls,
there's always a hand to catch it.
A hand to put it back.
One day that hand won't be there.
The whole world'll see.....
see the inner you,
the inner me.....
