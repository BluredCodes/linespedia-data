Why is it like this?
Yor can open yur heart,
pour all of your feelings on me.
This makes you a happeier person.

You want me to do the same.
I can't.
It's a flaw in my character
A curse you might say.

This curse is that I cant's be open.
I can't share troubles.
I don't know why.....
It's just me.

I wish I could
Trust me I do
You mean the world to me 
And I want you to be happy.

Your my everything
Whu can't I open to you?
Why Not?
Please understand I whish I could.
