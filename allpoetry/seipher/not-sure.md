She said I was great,
That i was the best.
But did she mean it?

She made me belive.
she made me trust.
But was it real?

I heard what she said.
It's just a rumor.
Or is it?

I don't know anymore.
Dis-belife is rife. (widespread)
My heart has been sruck.

My streangth is shaken.
Just like the oaks,
great oaks by the tempest. (stong wind)

I'm not sure.
Just not anymore.
