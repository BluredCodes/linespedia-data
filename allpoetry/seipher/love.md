---
title: "Love?"
writer: "Seipher"
slug: "love"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54691-Love--by-Seipher"
---

You're the one I adore
I care for you more than you know.
I love your voice
hair, face, everything about you.
I can't stand to not hear you
It kills me when you not around.
But Love?
Do I Love thee?
Yes I adore you 
But are you The One?
My true soul mate?
