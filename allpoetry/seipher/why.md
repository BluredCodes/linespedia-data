Why does life have to be like this?
Why must it beat me?
I'm in a state of confusion
It's obvious.. Can't you see?
Life sends on then another.
I can't take... take it all.
Life gives me a special person
then another before I'm done.
It's happened once before,
it hit me and still am i sore. 
Why must I hame two of a love?
It's like a dual gift from a dove.
Why it happens, it makes no sense.
Sice it's happened my life's all tense.
I don't want it to be this way
Why can't it all go away?
