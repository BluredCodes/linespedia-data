I will never
give you bad advice.
Lead you wrong,
or turn my back..
when you come to me.

I will always
go get the paper,
even if it's a little wet,
as long a i get a 
small rub on the head.

I won't ever 
bring home a girl
with a pierced nose.
But she might smell.

I will always 
give you presents
that are yellow
on your tire.this was a poem that was in my head all day long. i don't expect to have good reviews on this cause i know it's not good. but this was written on the bus on the way home,(wierd poems are written then).
