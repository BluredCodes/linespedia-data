Oh my lady
you cheer up my day
you make me happy in everyway.
I know i upset you 
when it's you I won't hold,
I guess my heart's a little cold.
I can't be sad,
or ever mad around you.
Hpe you feel the same too.
you brighten my day
if it's not goin so well,
or if I even feel as if in hell.
I adore you so much.
For the first time I'm all sappy,
for I'm with you.
