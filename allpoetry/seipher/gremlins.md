Little little gremlin,
running here and there.
Only want and need ....
is for you to scare.

You're keeping your load,
all nice and light.
As so you could....
stay out of sight.

Og this evil bastard,
it killed porr Tim.
The only reason...
that he saw him.

Oh stay away...
away from this thing.
For if you don't,
You shall feel his sting
