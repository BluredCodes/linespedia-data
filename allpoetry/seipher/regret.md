---
title: "Regret"
writer: "Seipher"
slug: "regret"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

You surround me with fear and hate,
turn me like a dog on a chain.
People try and help... too late,
I'm full of anguish,pain, and hate.

I've changed....no longer a nice guy.
In your ways your done and set.
You are to me like sand in the eye.
You push.. I snap..... You regret.
