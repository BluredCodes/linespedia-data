---
title: "Depression"
writer: "Seipher"
slug: "depression"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59626-Depression-by-Seipher"
---

I feel it,
it's coming.
I fought it off,
once long ago.
I don't know.....
if I can do it again,
all on my own.
I need your help,
anyone's help.

Hurry!
Please!
T'is not long
before it's here.
I can't fight it off,
at least on my own.
I haven't the will to.
Forget what I said.
Too late... it's here.
