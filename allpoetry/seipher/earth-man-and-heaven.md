---
title: "Earth, Man, and Heaven"
writer: "Seipher"
slug: "earth-man-and-heaven"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Earth,
ohh... Mother Earth.
It gives us life
and gives us birth.
Torn, burned, turned into sand
Inspite of this, it's still mans land.

Man, 
this evil one.
All work, all day
till the day's done.
Tools of evel: gun and saw.
All to disobey heaven's law.

Heaven,
this godly place.
Some say it's the stars
some... it's out there in outer space.
Tobe here is a great gift,
follow the law and your soul'll lift.
