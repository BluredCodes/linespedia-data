title: "Jagged Pill"
writer: "Seipher"

A stupid thig it is.
This tiny little pill.
I took you down
with any drink found.
You swim in my stomache.
make it twist and churn.

After this initial pain
you make me happy.
make my mind float.
I forget my pain,
forget my sorrow.
Till you're gone.

You leave me with pain,
make my body and heart ache,
I go crawling back
to make the pain stop.

But no more!
Your stone grip has no streangth.
You're no linger my master.
I'm free for ever.This is about a time in my life that i'm not proud of. but i guess if it didn't happen i wouldn't be the man i am today.
