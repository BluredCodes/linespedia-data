You came into my work
About one week ago
I was tryin’ not to flirt
Cause I thought you’d be a jerk
You’ve got all the style
The patches and the pins
I should have asked you for a date
But it’s no use, I never win
You’re wearing the blue dickies
And the glasses Rivers got
It comes down to this statement
Emo boy, I think you’re hot!
Today you bought a hamster
And took her home with you
Never knowing that the salesgirl
Wanted you to screw
You’re perfection in a boy
So sweet and charming too
You may cry too much
But damnit, I still love you!
I wish that I could say
How much this stranger means to me
But I think I’ll keep him at a distance
Though he fills me completely
