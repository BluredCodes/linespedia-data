---
title: "My confession"
writer: "Riverette"

So you may have heard
And yes, it’s true
Over the past few months
I’ve developed feelings for you
The first time I laid eyes
On your sweet face
I knew I needed nothing more
Than your tight embrace
And to hear you sing
My favorite blink song 
As I drift off to sleep
Knowing there’s nothing wrong
Because I don’t see how 
In a world with you
There could be any pain or misfortune
Or anyone sad and blue
Cause you’re so great
A mere mold of perfection
And it’s hard to think I’m the only one
That loves your pasty complexion
When I met you 
Those six months ago
I would have never believed
I’d have this much to show
I’m constantly happy
Never shed a tear
Just because I know
In my heart, you’re always near
