---
title: "Kidney Kupkakes"
writer: "Riverette"
slug: "kidney-kupkakes"

You let your palm hit me in the head
But I could care less anyways
Cause I'm not sure, I may be dead
No words from me can make you stay
I've handed you my heart on a platter
Yet you chopped it up nice, julienne
Added eggs and made pancake batter
The leftovers still sit in your oven
I didn't know when I gave you my heart
You wouldn't be grateful, you'd just pretend
That you'd open a bakery with my body parts 
And share me with all your dumb friends
Yet you never enjoyed the best of me
So when I go bad just throw me away
I hope to God that it kills you inside
It could be in a week but I hope it's today
But what the hell, time doesn't matter
Cause when I gave you my heart, that's when I died
