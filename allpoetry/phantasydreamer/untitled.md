Thoughts of you 
creep through my mind
and they take me back 
to a time
When I was happy
without a care in the world,
and the only thing that mattered
was that you were there
to hold me as I fell asleep
in your arms.
I dont know what went wrong
and I would do anything
to take it all back.
But the happy days are gone
and so are you.
And now all I have left
are memories
and thoughts of you creeping through
my mind.
