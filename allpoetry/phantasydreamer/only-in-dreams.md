title: "Only In Dreams"
writer: "phantasydreamer"

Only in my dreams
would I feel you close to me
Only in my dreams
would I see you smile
just for me.
Only in my dreams
could I ever call you mine
And
Only in my dreams
could I hold you till the end of time.
I can only hope
one day these dreams come true
but until then I'll be satisfied 
dreaming of you.
