Do you ever wake up reaching for me?
Do you ever see me in your dreams?
Do you ever wonder what might have been?
Do you ever wish I was there for you to love?
Or am I just the one you'll never miss?
These are the questions I ask myself every day...
And cry myself to sleep with every night.I'm still working on this one...I think it's kinda dumb or/and whiny, but I seem to be having a poetry block, I guess you could call it...anyone wanna help me out??
