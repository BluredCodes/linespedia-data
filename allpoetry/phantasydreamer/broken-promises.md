---
title: "Broken Promises"

You wispered promises 
of forever in my ear
and you told me 
the things I always wanted to hear
But those didnt make up for
what you put me through.
All the unwanted touches
and forbiddon kisses
haunted me every night as I cried 
myself to sleep.
I didn't understand
how you could do this to me 
and still carry a normal life.
You left without saying goodbye,
and you didnt give me any time
to scream at you
for all the shit you put me through
and all the broken promises you
made me.
