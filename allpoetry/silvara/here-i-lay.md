I lay here cold and shivering
Here I lay, lost and alone
Crying out to all those that listen
“Help me! Help me from this, my hole”

As I lay here, tears drop from my face
As I realize that my hopes and dreams fall from their place
Here I hold my heart again
Broken and shattered, unable to mend
Here I lay cold and shivering
Here I lay in my grave

I had felt love and warmth return to my veins
But now I freeze, a broken heart and only pain remains
My soul is breaking, and my heart is bleeding
I lay alone, confused and broken
