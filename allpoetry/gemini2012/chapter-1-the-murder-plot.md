We were all sitting in class when it hit me, the coolest idea for a book, I murder plot. I smiled at myself and started writting down names and what would happen. Terrie Williams looked over my shoulder. "Whatcha doing?" She picked up the peice of paper. She smiled, "Ooooh! A murder plot! Make me the killer!" She smiled, "No make it the least likely person!" She pointed to Kagan Phillips. "Nooo! Wait I want to be the killer!" She looked at the peice of paper.

I blinked and smiled, my newest idea, Terrie is gonna be the killer. Terrie put the peice of paper down on my desk. She smiled, "Lemme read your book!" She picked up my moon and star book and plopped it on her desk. Crossing her legs she began to read. She was intriged.

I sighed. Maybe it wouldn't be so bad, I smiled, making a book. It was a plot a murder plot. I deciced right then and there Lex was first to die for calling my Fish, or a 2 cent prostitute, at the lockers. I really wasn't going to put up with him. The bell finally rang and we left.

Kagan smiled at me, "I want my name to be Kaylee in your book." Kagan blined as I wrote it down silently. "So who's your killer Mel?"

I smiled as I blinked at her, "It's an surprise. A big one that you'll never guess!" I smiled. Terrie handed me back my book.

Terrie smiled, "For real!?" She smiled, "It should be Bailey! She's a big blond." Which she was the biggest ever.

Kagan frowned, "Bailey, couldn't kill a fly." She sat back down properly in her desk and took down her pony tail. Jenna leaned over and whispered in her ear and they both began to laugh.

I knew then I was going to make a duo, Jenna and Kagan. I smiled. They were always giggling and laughing and talking why not. I wrote it down on a seperate sheet and placed it into my note book and smiled. Maybe, maybe not. There was so much I had to plan. I knew that the murders would be grousome.
