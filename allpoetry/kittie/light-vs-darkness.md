title: "Light vs. Darkness"

Don’t lose face
Let go of the existence you once knew

Remember 
Even when you feel
Crushed by the weight of the world
There’s someone out there who loves you

Things aren't random
Maybe everything is
...Inevitable...

Light and Darkness are
Really the same 

If you close your eyes
You can see them both

-The sadness in happiness-
-Hope at the end of pain-

Stand up
Find that place of light
The place we all dream of...

No matter how far apart
I take the friendship you give
To my heart

And we will find the Place of Infinite Light...dedicated to my good friend! ((hugs))
