---
title: "~Forbidden Lover~"
writer: "Kittie"
slug: "forbidden-lover"
source: "AllPoetry"

Child of Darkness
Princess of Ice
Don’t relinquish your Love

Child of Light 
Peasant Boy of Fire
Never give up Hope

O Mother Earth can you
Not hear your children weep
Why are they miles apart

Fate why must you play
The Assassin of Love
And keep them out of
Each other’s arms

There Love should conquer
Any obstacle that may come their way
Yet...

She – a princess – 
Weaving dreams of Darkness
And Living the Life of Ice

He – a peasant – 
Whispering thoughts of Light
And Breathing the Air of Fire

Destiny made them opposite
From birth

*Ice could never embrace Fire
With a gentle kiss...*

*Light could never caress Darkness
With an adoring touch...* 

Perchance in the Spring twilight 
Fortune will have them meet...this is about me...i am the Ice Princess...and my Fire Boy...is just someone i long to be with...yet Fate and Destiny are keeping us apart...not true to life...tell us what you think!!
