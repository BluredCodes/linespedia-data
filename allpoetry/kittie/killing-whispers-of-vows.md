title: "Killing Whispers of Vows"

Hidden beneath
Under tainted memories
Lies the secret of your heart 
All your Love
Drenched in forgotten sin

I use the night to hide my dreams of you
Fear of what they may be
Fear of your Love returning to me

I use the morning to see my dreams
Black swirling nightmares
Engulfed in a freezing sea

In order to get something
You have to lose something
And someone will be hurt

-Love is pain
When freedom is found...-

-Freedom is found
When you lose Love...-

Beyond the light 
Love lies 
The unexpecting Fate
Reaching for the darkness
