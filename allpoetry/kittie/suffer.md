I Loved you too much
Myself of that time
Shall remain forever locked away

I got bored with seeing you
Now I’ll have another in my arms

Soon I won’t Love you anymore
The hatred will increase
And I will want to destroy you
Don’t you like my selfishness?

I search more than just
My mind for answers
I searched your body

A ghost staring through the mirror
Who can make you suffer?
I or he?
Alone whispered my heart

I hurt you 
And it went unnoticed
We cannot understand each other
You cannot understand me...

A phantom lying lonely
In the mirror
It slowly comes toward me
Who can make you suffer more?
Me or yourself?

Finally you suffer so much 
Your spirit is going away from your body 
In front of my eyes you're yelling of laugh
