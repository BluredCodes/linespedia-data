Never going back
Wanting to forget
Your Love that never faltered

In the meadow
Where the flowers of poison
Bloomed in profusion

In this meadow 
You looked up at the sky
We saw it together
In this sky I look down
At this meadow
We were to-gether

I want the faded flowers
To bloom
Only one more time
This night my heart and ideals
Float in the darkness

That day
 I disappeared from your eyes
I can’t go back
I want to forget all that happened

I want to forget even the memories
My heart that loved you hurts
Forever – until now there was no Love
Forever – never will our Love come back

Although I can't go back to the past
 I’ll continue looking for you
You – who have not changed in my heart 
Until this time 
I’ll be waiting for you
Still in the meadow...*sigh* ah spring!
