Sitting 
Thinking thoughts of what may be
The angels carry my feelings on their wings

Inside of the continually falling rain
Hollowly I looked up into the sky
With the ends stretching out into eternity

Only your lost smiling face
Could never do anything like betray
Over and over I murmured this to my heart

No my own thoughts
My innocuous dreams of Love
This is what betrayed me

And no matter how much I’ve been hurt
And what became the thing that hurts me
I’ll continue to Love

The angels see my dreams
Slight light of Hope gleams 
In the dark distance
 
It’s all right
I only find some light
Though the sun has yet to rise
