Unexpectedly
One day at my door 
A face came to me

A person from my past
A Love I once knew
-Shock...no surprise-

He gazed at me
With a warming look behind his eyes
He told me ‘I’m different from other guys’
I smiled and nodded as tears welled up in my eyes

My Love didn’t care that I was not 
Quite human 
He was merely shocked that I had not told him 
Sooner...
-Astonished...no confused-

He told me he had
Time to think about my...transformation
And someday he might want to be like me
Not now but someday

He said he cared for me
Still in his heart
…a Love deep in his heart...
-Love...yes Love-um...blah...
