My Love and I
Lived the perfect life together

I told him –once- before
‘I’m different from other girls’
He’d nod and smile ‘I know...’

At the time...
Perhaps he didn’t know how different

I decided to show him
-Foolishness...no trust-

There was no rush of wind
Or other falsities of ‘magic’
There was just me
Showing him
My truest form

The silver moon
Burns my flesh and
Reveals fur and claws

He had a look 
That was hard to see at first

-Surprise...no horror-

As his two mortal legs
Ran as fast as they could
I on my four ran after him

I decided to stop
Why scare him more
Than he already was

After that night I never
Saw him again

-Hate...no Sadness-

I decided to run
Run from everything and everyone
...especially from myself...

-Run...yes run-um...akay!
