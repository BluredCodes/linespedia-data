---
title: "For You"
writer: "Kittie"
slug: "for-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

Watching from a distance
Never gathering the strength to say my true thoughts

I talk to him – as casual friends
While my lips burn with the desire to kiss upon his

My heart beckons for him to caress it
For he holds it in the palm of his hands

I can never tell him how I feel
For the fear that our friendship should end

Far out of reach you are
Yet you stand beside me...

I welcome the illusion of you and me
One day this shall become a reality

But until that day my heart will burn
From afar

My lips shall go untouched 
By yours

And my arms shall hold just the memories of 
When we hug as friends
And I wish for more...i wrote this while talking to a veri good friend of mine...he feels this way so i just wrote it out for him!
