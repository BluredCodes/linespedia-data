A plethora of pleonasm,
What exactly is the defining meaning of that?
It is not that illusive when you know the answer -
Plethora meaning over abundance or more than what is necessary,
And Pleonasm meaning a plethora of words.
I guess its like saying two twins,
Or the eyes that see.
More subtly you could say, 
A dumb blonde,
Or a corrupt Politician.
How about the movie title the Sound of Music?
But the best example of all is a Plethora of Pleonasm 
Which should by now have a defined meaning
Or else my whole entire poetic poem has been a wasteful useless effort and ambition!Had a friendly competition with my sister to write a poem each, but not just simply any poem. By randomly flicking to a page in the dictionary i chose a title for myself "A Plethora of Pleonasm" (with plethora and pleonasm being on the same page) and a simpler title for my sister. In about 15 mins i came up with this! I hope it makes sense! :) one of my more ambitions poems...BTW no offence intended to Blondes or Politicians
