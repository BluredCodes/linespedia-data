title: "a question af faith"
writer: "fastfingerz"

if our paths crossed but one time, and the forces that be should make me fall in love, what would you say? if weshould then again pass years later, would you forsake everthing for love? or would you realize that one moment was not worth your entire life spent hoping to one day feel the same again.
