writer: "redmoon"

To say I'm sorry would be too easy.
I wanna put up a fight,
I wanna stand solid.
Im absorbed by this new ground,
I can't withstand these temptations, these commitments,
I won't endure in suffering.
This task too much to bear, 
this mold to hard to fit,
this witness beras no trust, seeking vengence;
Frightening.
