---
title: "Disgusting"
writer: "redmoon"
slug: "disgusting"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

You
disgust me
repulsive and pukish.
Immature as it sounds,
you're faulted,
and I can't deal with your flaws anymore.
I look at you, I
feel nauseas,
as it you were a sea of gross and grime.
You are empowering to my self-esteem,
a bad thing,
it means you're
disgusting.
