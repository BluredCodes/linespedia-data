---
title: "God versus Goddess"
writer: "crestfallen"
slug: "god-versus-goddess"

Fates so weaved on ivory thread
maked light the hearts of destined dead,
Though many bodies rise and fall
amid the flesh of Satan they call.

'God, oh Lord, please save our souls
forgive our failure to reach your goals!'
But their God will not come today
they still believe Jesus will pay.

Tainted by the virgins tale
Sisters put thier souls for sale,
Sin is spun by obsessive sterility
condemning the rapture of fertility.

Satan is Christs own creation
wrought by faithless imagionation.
Forsaken Goddess hides her face
Ashamed of all the human race.
