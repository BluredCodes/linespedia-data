---
title: "So in love"
writer: " Jimmy "
slug: "so-in-love"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

every second without you is a living hell
i want to kick and scream, cry and yell
you were the only person who made me want to live
i gave you all the love i was able to give
i don't want to live anymore, not without you here
cuz i am living in pain and in constant fear
what if some day, when i am still waiting for you
you realize this is not what you want to do
what if you find someone else that is better than me
then who will i have and what will i be
i feel like im nothing if we are not one
i can't be happy and i can't have fun
we're together in school, so i love going there
cuz i can show you i love you, i can show you i care
when i am at home and i'm thinking of you
i just sit and cry because i don't know what to do
when you are not home when i try to call
i sit around the house and bawl
because without you i feel so alone and scared
because i have never had love that has compared
to the love i have and hold for you
babe without you i can't make it through
life is so hard and i feel so alone
and my only escape is when we talk on the phone
the only other escape i have is going to school
even though the people there are harsh and cruel
i get to see you there and it makes things okay
i'd love to go to school and stay there all day
but you and me would be the only ones there
we will be able to talk and we'll be able to share
i'll be happy there because you'd be there with me
i love you more than anything babe and soon they will see
that if they take you away, i will be lifeless and dead
and they will regret all the bad and mean things they said
they'll regret taking you away for as long as they did
and only because i kept my feelings all hid
i only did that because i knew they'd take you away
i shouldn't have told them, i regret it everyday
i miss you so much now that they did this to me
but someday soon you'll be back here where you're supposed to befor the only one i love
