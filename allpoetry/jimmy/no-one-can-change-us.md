---
title: "No One Can Change Us"
writer: " Jimmy "

no one in this world can break us apart
so don't worry baby cuz you're safe in my heart
i won't let them change my mind about how i feel about you
because without you here i won't be able to make it through
you are my hope and strength to carry on
i won't make it baby if you are gone
my life won't be the same without you here
i will beg and plead and cry my every last tear
you're my love, my babe everything good in my life
i wanna be with you forever, maybe you'll be my wife
i'll be here for you forever, all throughout the years
through happy and sad, through laughs and through tears
nothing can happen to change the way i feel
cuz i know only my love for you is real
no one else makes me this happy, no one makes me feel this way
i love you more than life babe and i just wanna say
no matter what anyone says or what they do 
no one can change how much i love youfor the only one i love
