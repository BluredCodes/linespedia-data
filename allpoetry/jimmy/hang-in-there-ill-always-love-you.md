title: "Hang In There, I'll Always Love You"

i will never give up on me and you
no matter what they say or do
no one can change how i feel
they say i'm confused, i know my love's real
who else could know this better than me
i know it for sure and soon they will see
i will not quit being with you just because they don't approve
we will run away, i will take u with me and we'll move
we'll go to someone who accepts who we are
we will go anywhere, near or far
i'd go to the end of the world just to be with you
i will do anything that i have to do
i will go to hell and back if you can be mine
cuz even in hell, you'll be my sunshine
i will never leave your side, i will always be there
i will have your back and i will always care
i will love you forever and i mean what i said
just screw life without you cuz i'd rather be deadfor the only one i love
