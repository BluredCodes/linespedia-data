title: "This is how it'd be if you were gone"

i was in love with this girl, but she put her life to an end
she was the love of my life, and also my best friend
i would have done anything to help change her mind
she showed me so much love i wasn't able to find
she was the most beautiful person god ever made
she told me all the time our love would never fade
i guess she never lied, and that much is true
but how can i live on without being able to say i love you
you were my everything and always will be
my life won't be the same because you are not here with me
knowing inside i can not ever see you anymore
knowing you're the only one i'll ever adore
you were so gorgeous, everything about you was right
there were never problems, and we never did fight
we'd lay around and hug and cuddle and kiss
out of everything that is the most i will miss
kissing you was the best feeling i've had
and now that you're gone i will be forever sad
i never thought you'd end it, not in this way
i thought i helped u a lot babe, so why couldn't you stay
constant pain is what i have without you here my love
you were my only angel, sent down from above
i told you i loved you a billion times a day
i was in love with you in every single way
you touched my heart every time you were near
i'll never love again, that is my biggest fear
because no one could compare to the perfections of you
or help me through things that you've helped me through
no one could be as gorgeous or have those beautiful eyes
no one could compare to you babe, cuz they only told me lies
even though you're gone and i don't know what to do
i still want you to know that i'll always love youfor the only one i love
