title: "Best Friends Forever And Always"
writer: " Jimmy "

we are together again, even if it is just as friends
cuz yer so special to me and i hope this friendship never ends
i feel so comfortable around you, this feeling is so neat
god picked us as best friends, he chose us to meet
he picked a certain day when he knew i needed you
he picked that certain day cuz he knew u needed me too
he chose us for each other cuz we are so much the same
he knew we were forgiving, but we both took all the blame
we said sorry for things we didn't even do
i said i forgive you and you said i forgive you too
he knew i'd stand by you and he knew u'd stand by me
he knew we'd see the way things needed to be
god knew i'd have your back and he knew u'd have mine
he knew if we were together, everything would be just fine
he knew that there would be tough times, but we'd make it through
god knew we were sposed to be best friends, that i am meant for you
