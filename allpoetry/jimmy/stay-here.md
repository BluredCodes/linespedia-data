---
title: "Stay here"
writer: " Jimmy "
slug: "stay-here"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

suicide is wrong even though i do it too
but all i want is the best for you
let me tell you how it'd be if you were gone
for one i know i'd never move on
you are everything to me, everything that is good
i said i wanna take away your problems, you know i would
there would be no joy in my life if you were to leave me
i would never be happy again, the way i used to be
i'd cry forever and i'd die with a broken heart
because u told me babe that we'd never be apart
i've never been in love like this, not ever this much
i've never felt so careless, when i felt someone's touch
your touch takes away my problems and takes away my fears
your hand is perfect for my holding and you wipe away my tears
babe, without u here, i would not know what to do
cuz no one could replace the love i feel for you
so every time u think about taking away your life
think about me and then put down your knife
think twice about dying, because you make my life complete
i love you more than anything babe, damn you are so sweet
please live for me even if thats the only reason you do
because babe, im in love, and i can't live without you
