---
title: "I Love You"
writer: " Jimmy "
slug: "i-love-you"

things are getting messed up and they're tryin to take you away
i am dying inside and i am dreading that day
you make me so happy why don't they understand 
that i won't beg to be with you cuz it's a demand
they can't take away my happiness which would be you
they can't tell me i can't see u, they can't tell me what to do
they won't change my mind about who i love and who i don't
and if they think they will, they're wrong cuz they won't
you can not change something that is so meant to be
and one thing that is, is that you're here with me
you are who makes me happy, you are who makes me smile
and to get you in the long run, this hurt is worth my while
i'd go through any sort of pain or hurt if it means that you can stay
because i won't be able to survive if they take my love away
i won't be able to eat without you because you're what stopped me from dying
i won't be able to be happy because you're what stopped me from crying
i won't ever smile again, cuz you are the only one who makes me 
i won't be able to move on without you, knowing we're meant to be
so if they try to separate us, we will run somewhere and hide
it's not like we did anything wrong, it's not like we lied
i can't help who i love, and i won't even try
but baby the day they take u away is the day i will just die
cuz babe without you here i won't know what to do
but no matter what they do, i'm still in love with youto the only one i love
