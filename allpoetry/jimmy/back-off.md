---
title: "Back off!!!!"
writer: " Jimmy "
slug: "back-off"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

there is just this one person that makes me feel this way
makes me wanna make her life miserable each and every day
she hangs around my girl, the one i call my love
my sweet baby, my angel sent from above
i don't like this bitch by my girl cuz it makes me scared
i've never had a love like this that could even be compared
i love her more than life, no one makes me feel this way
so i want to make this clear, here's some things i wanna say
back off bitch you're movin in on MY girl
the sweet love of my life, the girl who rocks MY world
you aren't her friend you are just obsessed with her beauty
so tellin u to back off just seems to be my duty
i get pissed off when i see that you're around
it makes my adrenaline rush, makes my heart pound
i wanna just tell her to fuck off and leave my girl alone
wanna tell her to quit writin notes, don't call her on the phone
back off my girl you bitch or i'll make you wanna die
i know u know i hate you, i just hope u don't wonder why
