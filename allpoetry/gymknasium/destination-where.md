Oh god way up there, 
help me persevere.
The expanse of fate.
For this is what I abominate.
How can I help me?
If  I sail on the vessel Destiny.
Titanic was filled with fortune,
but fell to the depth of misfortune.
Is this what I deserve?
No,  No, I will not serve.
A simple Idea
That has to do with a fear.
