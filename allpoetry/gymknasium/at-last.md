---
title: "At last"
writer: "Gymknasium"

Told I lacked capability,
but I new I had assiduity.
Taboo, shared with that of Keats;
thus, I have to defeat the biggest of all feats.
Trumans diligence, for now I see the light.
For endowment will not play the factor in this fight.
Now that I have found the dint within.
I will be able to conquer like Napoleon.
The offensive has commenced on the enemy fleet.
Now the battle has taken a different beat.
As my ship sails out to sea with victory.
At last, serenity.
