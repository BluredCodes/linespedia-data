title: "Finding Amour"

Much Have I been round.
Perhaps nowhere I be bound.
Looked high and low,
but never found that glow.
Where to find amour?
Maybe she exists no more.
The mien of a flame.
Is what vexes my aim.
Should it be an eyeful,
or ought to it be a careful.
It's this between,
to what I mean.
I know what's shallow,
but still fall fallow.
One day I shall control
and fill in the empty hole.
For then I espied Amour,
like never ever before.
