---
title: "Sly Dog"
writer: "Gymknasium"
slug: "sly-dog"
source: "AllPoetry"

Sly dog sly.
Sly dog pry.
Sly dog spy.
Sly dog deny.
Sly dog lie.
Sly dog cry.
Sly dog die.
Sly dog sly?
