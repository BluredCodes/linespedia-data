Lying down in bed,
reading thread by thread.
The hours continue to march.
as I look at my watch.
Soon after, the magicians trick, 
Really did not make me sick.
Now everything I see 
looks like a bee.
Dreaming up pictures with the ink well.
That are really swell.
They are not just zebra prints.
Rather money mints.
Now that I learned the magic.
I suddenly became a fanatic.
Maybe I could cast the spell on you
Then you can construe.
That its really true.
Even to you.
