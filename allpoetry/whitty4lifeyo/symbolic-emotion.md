Emotion is like an ocean 
As a potion is like an antidote
Emotion is compassion
As love fulfills your satisfaction
Emotion just as Langston Huges wrote
If your look at it, its all symbolic to emotion

Emotion is peaceful and loving
As an ocean is to those in it swimming
Emotion is sad and stressful
As to those angry stressed and unhelpful

Emotion is also black and blue
The colors, peace, or hurtfulness 
Emotions within me and you
Emotion is what gives our hearts to say I love you
Emotion is what makes us feel nothing less

But if you loook at it, tis all symbolic to emotion

Emotion is scared and nervous
As someone horrified and panicking
Like a baby, so gentle to us
Emotion is such a blessing
But if you look at it, its all symbolic to emotion......
