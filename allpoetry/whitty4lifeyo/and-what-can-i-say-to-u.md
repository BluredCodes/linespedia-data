what can i say to u
i love ur amazing new u
with ur beautiful eyes 
u walk with a smile
what can i say to u

what can i say to u
to make ur self say i love u
the friend i really needed
when i talk u listen
what can i say to u

what can i say to u
i wish i was makn this outta tha blue
sugar and spice
and everything's nice
but what can i say to u

what can i say to u
my mind just blew a fuse
when ever we talk i have so much fun
but im sure by now ur not amused
but how can i say this to u

but how can i say this to u
im lost in my world of neverland
i just steped on my hand
but what can i say to u

but what can i say to u
my vision is in tha blue
i pray for one more night
but what can i say to u

what can i say to u
how can i say this to u 
im so confused
but what can i say to u

what can i say to u
ur grace and beauty take me away
it sux were not the same age
but what can i say to u

my mind has said this to u
dear friend if u need me
u can then call 
when ever u feel like u need me
and ill never let u fall
my mind has spoken to u
