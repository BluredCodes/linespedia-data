i wish that i could be the one for u
i wish i could be next to u
i wish i could be the one to tell u i love u
i wish that this could be all true
but nothing in this world comes close to u

i wish that my words would heal ur pain
i wish we could ever be the same
i wish i wasnt so lame
i wish sometimes u never came
but nothing comes remotely close to 

i wish for tomorrow,
i wish for yesterday
i wish for no more sorrow
i wish for this everyday

i wish for pain
i wish for rain
i wish for death
but at the same time i wish for my breath

i wish u good luck
i wish the very best
i wish we could be together
but theres not a chance like this forever

i wish i could heal my wounds
i wish i could run away from my problems
i wish i could be left alone
i wish for another day
but most of all i only wish the wish of having u by my side
