Emotion is a feeling you get in certain situations
the way you feel about something or how you feel about something
im emotional, i feel so many things just like everyone else, but im unique
as a black male i express my feelings/emotions in ways no one can.
and other black males that people know now and days 
are not as sensitive as i am
im more receptive to others feelings and expressing my own in the open
sensitive you say i am huh?
maybe, but thats what i say, you may think differently
Being emotional is not all it is cracked up to be
emotion is like the water flowing from a stream, a lake, or sea, or an ocean
the sea and ocean tie together
people feel many things from anger to depression
well if you look at the ocean you can see all forms of emotion with the waters of a sea, stream, lake, river and even an ocean.
think how the greeks saw it
when lightning struck they thought zeus was mad.
when a storm came, pysidon was agitated and furious
when the waters was still, he was peacefull
but when eht waves grew bigger hes just calm
when it rained he is just sad and depressed
that just shows how emotion works inside you and your heart
you feel what you feel and if you let it all show thats cool as well
but emotion works whether you let it show or not
being emotional is something great 
it lets you be able to show who you are inside as a person it also shows your heart and your sweet side as well
