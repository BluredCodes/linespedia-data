why me all of a sudden...

u make me feel like jello...
whenever u tell me hello...
u make me smile ...
its long as a mile...
u even make me mello...
why me all of a sudden...

why me all of a sudden...

its b/c of a stupid number 
that keeps me from slumber
when thinking that i was under
when all i needed was ur smile
