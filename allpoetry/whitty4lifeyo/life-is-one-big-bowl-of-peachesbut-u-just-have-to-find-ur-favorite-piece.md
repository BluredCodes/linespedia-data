if u look at the clouds
the clouds look back at u in distress
if tha clouds look at u in distress
the cloud of distress is going to rain

if the cloud of distress is going to rain
then the rain becomes showering down
if the rain begins showering down
then the rain is the tears left from my broken wound
if my broken wound is tearing
the tears come from a empty space
if that space is not filled the space leaves a whole

that whole seems to leak
if there is a leak
fix it
life is like this example

life is like a bowl of peaches
its allways full of surprises and important choices
but if u just look at it, im kinda happy
happy is not much for me right now
but im glad to be this sad
i realize that my life belongs to GOD himself
god is everything i need at this point
so i leave on this note that if i die of pain its because of my health
and id rather it be that way
cuz soon ill be with tha lord and all of my memories ill carry forever....
i love the ones who even care but i care enough about something, someone, enough to say life is too precious to waist on me... live on. life is too precious to waite. life is like a bowl of peaches
always full of important choices
