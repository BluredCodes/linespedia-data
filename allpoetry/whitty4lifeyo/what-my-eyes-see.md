title: "what my eyes see"

what does my eyes see anyway,
i may wear glasses sometimes but i see nothing but pure beauty
ur eyes resemble a glance at the sea
the way ur hair resembles the horizon at sunrise
makes me feel like i cant do anything im in a trance
to visualize u next to me is shocking
to see u makes me go upscale
how can i express this feeling u bring 
when only a small picture or visualisation in my head can sing
but in due time things will spring up again
how do i see u
what do my eyes see anyway
just lookn at u makes me feel like im floating on cloud 9
when im distressed i know that the image of u will make me feel fine
there is a moment that passes by
that i wish i could just see u
but the pain of blocking me is frusterating
but if u could see what i see u would be in love too!!!
