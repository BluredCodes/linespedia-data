the sweet sound of the robin in spring
makes everything inside me sing
my heart flows with everlasting happiness
to feel ur love and tenderness
would u just stay with me
and listen to the ocean breeze
listen to the heart of the waters 

this sweet serenity is more than words
its a feeling of romance, and a feeling of my heart
with out u my life would never start
so i thank u in more ways than words

im at the point where i cant even say anything more
i sometimes think ur all i ever look for
u make my heart soar 

how can i express this emotion
its like the ocean
i hope this never ends
this messege u send


just remember this letter
and one day we'll be together
maybe one day somewhere
in space and in time
but when that day comes i hope its for eternal
my heart glows like a firey inferno 
just for u my heart skips beating ocasionally
i hope that emotionally we last permently 

one day.....
