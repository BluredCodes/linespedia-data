the story ~*chapter 2: ELE*~


Extinction Level Event


there was this girl i used to like
she was all i thought of that mad sense
we were like one as an item
then she left to a relm of misunderstanding
her interpretations were nothing but words of confusion
taking everything out of my heart tearing it to a pulp

fateful as it may seem, the reality of relationships hurt
but even more so than the ones that seek in tha wrong direction
the ones u crush will arise in happiness one day
relieved from the stress that was brought

take a risk and relax ur mind 
its amazing what shocking surprise ull find
but its not like i havent said this before
its just a little something more

please understand that my existance here was to make u find better
please understand that my existance has no meaning now
im just reapn what ive sown
my mind was blown
ive lost touch with reality
the electricity
that flows threw my vains
of that shocking memory
that floats in my precious mind

think about the ones u hurt will be ur biggest night mare in the end
as u think what happend
u cant remember 
but the pain is still there
stop thinking
ur brain feens for energy
but the power of someone keeps u floatn on tha edge

emotions are not to toy with
suddenly ur mind wanders
and u cant control it
u want to find a solution
but in the end its already gone

thoughts approach ur mind
memories will pass
haunting u on why u made ur choices
were they correct?
can u concentrate on ur feelings?
just wait and see
ur time is nigh
although u cant speak
u gotta play silent
but it will somhow end
ur misery
to the point where u will never hurt others again
ull come back and ask why
no response
ur now miserable
cant think
u want to end life as we speak
u then wish it all a dream
maybe it is or maybe its not
just think the thing that kept u sane
was under ur nose
stop searching
its there for u
in a moments time it will be gone
how will u explain the destruction

just wait
ELE is on its way
u cant even describe it

it will tear up this world
all life forms will have to evacuate
from the likes of the demon himself
takes peoples souls and burns them like nuthing


then its all gone what can u say 
u lost ur chance
stop looking for something u cant get
its not there
all is lost
look at me
im gone and its so much fun
the pain is relaxing
then the world comes to an end.

judgement day comes and when asked why
the choices u made cant be taken back
and neither will i

the story ends like this folks.....
