title: "jenny is my baby"
writer: "whitty4lifeyo"

no one knows love as a true friend 
its something that will never bend
its something that is shared
and it will never end
only a friend will know what i mean
some times i scream
"JENNY!!!!"
where are ya

when i needed something or just a hug
she would never be there to poke fun or bug
she expresses her love
always will she be in my heart for ever
she is so smart and cleaver
beautiful as a soft black little kitty
her name brings more joy than tha world
makin me melt like chocolate in tha sun
when ever i recieve a hug
a friend will never end i love my friends
but no 1 comes close to my baby jenny....
