how can i express this pain i feel
sometimes i wish god could just heal
it tears me to know i cant be with u
but it cures me to know u love me

ur all i think about at night
the vision of u in my sight
ur face resembles every pedal in the garden
the colors are remarkable

so when i think of the one
ur presence around me is so much fun
and when u say u love me...
how do u want me to react

ur eye's resembles the beauty of my first true love
my heart seems to soar with grace as a white dove
my heart sings as a robin in spring
u make me feel like no other, this feeling u bring

how i love the one u see
there is no other place id rather be
than in ur arms until we depart
so in ending my first poem, 

i love u my one, true, sweetie
