.,.,.,

I felt as if we were one 
melting into eachother, when you held me tight
I could feel your heart beat so fast aginst my chest
I was a blood cell runnring through your veins
drowning inside you...melting into you
the world around us dissapeard
all my troubles evaporated into the skies
became clouds and rained down on us
as we stood there holding eachother
where nothing and no one mattered
but the taste of your lips
the touch of your fingertips
and the heart that beat so fast against my chest

Love
xoxoxo
NAZ
