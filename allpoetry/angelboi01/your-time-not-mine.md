---
title: "Your Time Not Mine"
writer: "Angelboi01"

You give me attention on your time not mine.
Instead of making this a two-sided relationship, you make it one sided.
You want it to be hidden and secret.
I can't take it right now.
I want you to hold me the way you did when we are alone.
To be wrapped in your arms is life to me.
When you throw me to the side, i feel sad and betrayed.
I want you, more and more, every day.
I like the way your body feels close to mine and the way your smell penetrates my senses.
I lose my self in you.
I almost hate myself for that because sometimes you're not worth it.
