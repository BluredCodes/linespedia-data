title: "Games of the Heart"

I gave my all to your persona.
To be crushed by you.
I let you have something no one else has.
And this is how you treat me?
Letting me know this is how you felt for so long.
But still letting me be intimate with you.

How dare you play games with my heart.
I felt fragile inside before you,
Now I feel completely empty without you.
I have nothing now,
You have stolen it all.
My virginity, my hope, my heart.

I loved before you and I will love after you.
Next time I will give my heart in a different order.
I will let him see my heart and my mind before I let myself go.

I won't fall that deep so fast.
The next one will last.
You are now just part of my past.

I let you play games with my heart.
I felt fragile inside before you.
I feel stronger inside without you.
I will regain all that you have taken from me.
But the one part will still remain yours.I wrote this peom for a friend who is in need of comfort.  It will be all right babe.  Leave him were he lays.
