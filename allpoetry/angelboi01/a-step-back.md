I feel I need to step back from you.
I have gotten way too attatched for my own good.
I hadn't realized it until you mentioned it to me.
How could i expect you to change over nite.
I know it is harder to be yourself in a world full of ignorante people.
I fell as if I have been pushing you to an answer.
You don't owe me one.
I refuse to walk away from you.
However, I think it is time I take a step back.I wrote this poem a month or so back.  If it is about you you would know.
