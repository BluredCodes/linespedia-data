I can't stand to see you with him.
The way you let him touch you,
Play with your breast.
The way you give him attention,
Just to putup a front.
I can't stand the way you let him immatuely chew on your snatch.
I see the way you look at me,
Why stay with him?
I know you want an expert mouth,
Tounge to show you pleasure.
Why stay with him when he shows you less than I?
You confuse me with your picture perfect world.This poem just came to me as i was going to sleep.  It really has no meaning.  I guess it portrays envy over something you don't have.
