Standing back
Observing from a far hill
Watching the herd
Disgusted by the idea
That they are the ones that lead
That they are the ones that decide
That they are the ones who determine
What is right
and what is wrong
No shepherd and one herd
But I will not be part of them
I refuse to be numbered
I have been rejected
Because I reject the norm
I take one last look,
Crest the hill
And set out on my journey
To become the shepard
