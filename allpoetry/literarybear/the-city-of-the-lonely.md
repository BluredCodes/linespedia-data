---
title: "The City of the Lonely"
writer: "LiteraryBear"

For the first time
in my two year living here,
the city welcomes me
and reveals her inner nature
She only reveals herself to those who are alone
those who need a family
It is when walking through the city alone 
that you are welcomed 
into the the family of the lonely
and as you walk the streets
you encounter many others in your state
a fleeting look of recognition is exchanged
and it is in this glance that the city reveals herself
It is in this glance that you are taken up in her maternal embrace
It is in this glance that something within you is forever changed
and filled.I wrote this when I disappeared into the city of Boston one night when I was feeling remarkably depressed and alone.
