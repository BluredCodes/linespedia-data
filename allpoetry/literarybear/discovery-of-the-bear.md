Confused
Searching
Trying to find something to believe in
To eliminate the void
Years of searching
To reveal something within

The Bear reveals itself
Showing me its gentleness
Its compassion
Its quiet Strength
Its beauty
Its ability to enjoy life
Both in moments of joy
and melancholy

I finally understand...

I am the BearA poem about my entry into shamanism
