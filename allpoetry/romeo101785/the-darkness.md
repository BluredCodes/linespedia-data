The darkness is where i am
and the darkness is what I know
the darkness loves me
as i am layed in black

I am laying on my bed 
staring at the endless
darkness that covers
my room 

As I lay there I think
why do they critisize me
why do they joke
they dont know me

I know the dark
and it is my bestfriend
The dark helps me 
get away from my fear

The darkness helps me out
when I'm in a a jam
and when i need to think
The dark is where i am
