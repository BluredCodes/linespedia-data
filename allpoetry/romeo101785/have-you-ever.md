have you ever layed in bed 
thinking of what could have been
and then wishing you could
just turn around and redo
everything in life 
and then cryed cause you relized
what you have lost everything 
that has ever mattered
to you in life and you know
that you will never get it back
I have and I cant bare to loose it again
