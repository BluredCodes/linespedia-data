I dont know what to do
She talks bout death 
and I cant take much more

I dont want to loose her
but a you can only take 
so much of the pain

I never know if she will
be there when I call
or if she's gone

I dont know what to do
all I know is that
I cant take much more

I am scared that when
I call someone will say
"She's dead" that isn't what I want

So what should I do
When that is what
may possibly happen
