The passion burns inside of me
I want to let it out
But I cant let it loose

The passion is eating away at me
The voices are telling me to do it
and the knife is right there

This passion is not leaving
Its just begging to be let out
Thell tell me its alright

I know diffrent than that
I would get caught 
But I cant help it
I just have to do it
