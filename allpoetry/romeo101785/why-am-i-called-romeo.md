Why is it they call
Romeo when all I know
is the pain and agony
no love at all

I hear them say it
then wonder why
if I really am Romeo
Why I have no love

I would be happy
to be with someone 
that loved me
and I could stay with

Now with all this pain
I am in why am I called
This name Romeo with
only one feeling inside
which is pain
