Its not like yours
Or anyone else's
Its mine and I love it

Its deep and dark
Its violent and brutal
And it is not for the weak of heart

Its more deadly than imagined
and no one can survive
not even me

Its filled with torture and torment
And the most brutal acts
now I challenge you

Take a step out of your world
and try to live in mine
and you'll see its not easy

Now take a chance 
and risk your life
and step over to my world
just for one night
