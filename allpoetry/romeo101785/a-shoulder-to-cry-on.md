Everything is driving me
insaine and insaine i am

Nothing is a cure 
and my life is hell

I just need someone
to lean on 

All i wish for is
a shoulder to cry on

A person I can turn to
to help with decisions

I am being royally screwed
and stressed out

I dont know what
I want to do

I am scared to 
loose her 

but scared that if
i stay she will die
