My friend was nice
and he was funny
He is not like
anyone else

but when he was 15 
He was to depressed
He called me up crying
saying she dumped him

He was very scared
but he diddnt want to live
and he told me to come over
he wanted to say good bye

I told him I was on myy way
but I was too late
when I arived I saw his car
it was in flames

He layed in the yard
only 15 feet from his baby
She came over to tell him 
she made a mistake and wanted him back

But he was already dead
she took his lighter
and light it up
opened the gas tank
and then threw it in

See I lost 2 of my friends
But one was more
He was like a brother
and he was taken from me
Because love destroyed him
