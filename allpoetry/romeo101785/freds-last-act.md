The urge to bleed is back
Its stronger than I remember
I dant know what's happening
But I know it feels good

My arm starts to tingle
As I feel something cold
The wind picks up
As I look down

My arm is red
No whit showing
I start to black out
and I know what I did

I woke up in what seems
Lke only 5 minutes
But I know its longer
Then I stand up 

I swore never again
But it just did
He came out to kill me
But he failed once againFred is my split personality
