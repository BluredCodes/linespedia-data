As I look at the sky 
I hear foot steps
None that I have heard before

They sounnd lighter than 
human but heavier than
any animal in the town

I turn to see
A dark figure standing there
I felt a cold chill run down my spine

Not knowing what would happen
or what would become of me
I just stood there 

She asks me if i wanted to be dead
If I wanted out of the world
or if I wanted to live forever

I asked her to make me like her
A pale sickly white with the hunger
A hunger for blood and death

She grasped my arms 
and tilted my head
she sunk the fangs in deap

She drank my blood 
as I saw the world go black
and my body went limp

Now the story isn't over
It's just begun 
Because I am here for eternity
