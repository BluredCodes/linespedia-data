My life has turned to hell
in a very short 2 hours
Nothing is worth it

I just want to die
and leave this place
Nothing is worth living for

I dont know why
everyone must lie
About me and people I care about

No one relizes how much it hurts
when they tell me that they
hate me or despise me

They told me to go to hell
and now i am here
and they want me back

Why am I alive and going
when I wish I was dead
and nothing can change my life
