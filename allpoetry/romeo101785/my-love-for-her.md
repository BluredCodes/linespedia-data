My love for her
is like none 
I've had before
I love her with all my heart
And with alot more passion

She's all I can think about
Every time I awake
She's all I can see
When I go to sleep
Every night

Her voice is all I hear
When I try and think
about what will happen
with our love 
and I know it is a love
I never felt before
