I hear them yelling 
Then her scream
I look up as 
The door slams shut

I'm sitting on her bed
As she lays down beside me
she's in tears
So I ask why

She told me he hit her
that she doesnt want me in it
But i told her to stay there
As i got up and left

I guess she was scared 
scares that he would hurt me
But i had no fear
As i walked up to him

I see him sitting there
with a beer in his hand
as he stand up
he asks what i want

I told him that we need to talk
and it has to be now
He and I walked out to the lawn
And we got in my truck

We go to the river
And had my little talk
But he decided to swing on me
But got a little surprise

See 2 of us left the house
But only one returned
No one knows what happened
That crazy night
