They all said
I would amount to nothing
and then I ask my self

Why am I still hear then
Why am I alive
If I will be nothing why am I living

I hear them mock me 
as I cry out why 
then they are silent

As they see me laying 
In a pool of blood
they caused my death

All of them scared
They run to their homes
and crawl into their beds

See I was told i would be nothing
Now it is true because I am dead
But if I am dead then how can I be something
