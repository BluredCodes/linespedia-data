Not a day goes by
in which I dont think
of where we'll be
in the future

I wish to have 
her here with me
No matter what 
I have to do

I love her so
That I need to hear
Her sweet voice
every day

I had the chance
to be with someone else
but I cant stand the thought
of lossing her

Through out my life
I never knew what I wanted
that is until now
and I want her with me
