title: "What am I?"

What do you see
when you gaze into my eyes
do you see who I am
or better yet
do you see what I am

Do you see my pain
or even my agony
The torture I've had
and the stress I am under

Do you see
the real me
or just someone you think
that you know

Can you see my fear
the fear of loosing
yet another love
to this crazy act

so I ask
What am I to you?
