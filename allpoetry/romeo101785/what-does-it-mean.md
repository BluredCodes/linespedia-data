When I say I love you
what does it mean
When we are together
are you playing a game

When you tell me 
you want me and miss me
are you just saying that
or is it true

Every time I hear it 
I get scared because
I don not know if
you are telling me the truth

Is it a game 
that I dont know of
is it a game of my heart
and that i cant play

I need to know
are you using me
as a piece in a game
or are you sincere

so what does it mean
when you tell me you love me
or that you want me 
or even that you miss me
