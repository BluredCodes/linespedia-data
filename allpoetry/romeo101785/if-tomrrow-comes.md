If tomorrow come and I'm not here
To see your face dont you cry
For I am still here

If tomorrow comes and I'm not there
To see the sunrise dont you fear
For I am still here

If the sun somes up and I'm not there
To wipe the first tear from your eyes
Dont be sad for I am right there

If tomorrow comes and I'm not there
For you to hear those three little words
Believe this that I do

So if tomorrow comes and I'm not there
I Just want to say 
I love you and always willthis is dedicated to Zach My cousin who died in 2001
