I am sitting here
where i feel safe and secure
its totally dark
just the way i like it

No one can tell me 
what to do now
or even what to say

The dark is my friend
and it has not left me
for a single moment

The voices are with me
so I'm not alone
The dark helps me 
clear my head

The dark is where I am
and where I'll stay
from now till death
