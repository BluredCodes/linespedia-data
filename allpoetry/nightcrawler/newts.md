---
title: "Newts"
writer: "Nightcrawler"
slug: "newts"
source: "AllPoetry"

My crystal hangs in the window.
It gathers the light of the rainbow.
With its sparkle and shine,
A vision sublime.
My crystal hangs in the window.

A finger painting hangs up high.
It's main focus octopi.
My mother and me,
Went on a Picasso spree.
A finger painting hangs up high.

A statue of a cat wears a crown.
Its eyes are a piercing golden brown.
With a proud and fierce stare,
All she does is sit there.
A statue of a cat wears a crown.

Japanese fire newts are swimming.
I've had them since the beginning.
Named Zaphod and Ford,
They never get bored.
Japanese fire newts are swimming.

Now we come to the bed.
The place where I must lay my head.
A frog of light green,
Cats, Gonzo, a Fraggle and dreams.
Now there is nothing more to be said.

((A note.. one of my newts recently passed away (Ford) He led a short but happy life, may he rest in peace.))
