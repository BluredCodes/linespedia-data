Ramen noodles are a college student's best friend.
Even though they probably turn your insides completely yellow.
You can eat them in HUGE bites.
Or delicately slurp them.

I make mine in a coffee pot.
At least 5 bowls a day.
I have no idea what's in them.
But boy do they smell great!
