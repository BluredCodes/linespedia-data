Something is There.
I cannot say why.

Something is There.
Formless and sly.

Something is There.
Deep in the dark.

Something is There.
Lit by a spark.

Something is There.
Not moving away.

Something is There.
Starting to sway.

Something is There.
I cannot say why.

Something is coming.
And I'm going to die.
