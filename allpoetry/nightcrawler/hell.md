I had a Hell of a lot of problems yesterday...

I fell down the stairs
Continued on out a window
Landed in a tree
The fire department had to help me down
While I was up there I saw my boyfriend
He was kissing another girl
One highlight of the day:
The ground opened up and swallowed them whole
But she was my sister
And he had the notes I needed for a class
So I failed geometry.
I was only passing that time because the teacher thought I was cute.
Next year the teacher I will have is blind
I really don't know how that's going to work out
Anyways, when I opened my locker I hit myself on the head and passed out.
I woke up in the nurse's office and promptly died from eating bad fish.
Somehow there was a mix-up and I ended up in hell
Some murderer and I had the same name and the paperwork got messed up.
At least the devil is kinda cute
So I might rule hell in a week or two.

So Hey...

Things are looking up!No this never happened, but I wrote it my senior year of high school.
