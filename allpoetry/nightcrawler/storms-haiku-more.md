Snap of radience
Roar of giants dancing
Chill of ice water.

The light never ceases to amaze me
As I gaze furtively into the night.
The other's seem to fear it,
But I know not their plight.

I am one with the electricity!
The fire, the pounding crack!
I want to dance among it.
While others are curled in their sack.

My eyes are lit likes cat's are,
As I try to feel the sense,
Of loosing all humanity.
The scene is so intense.

Why do they fear its beauty so?
Is there a reason that they cower?
I treat the storm as an equal,
And crave part of its power.
