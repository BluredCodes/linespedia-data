Here we march along as a single mind 
Thump Thump Thump... 

Our souls are gone but flesh stays behind 
Thump Thump Thump... 

Now we stop to feast. 

Our victim screams yet we hear no plea 
Munch Munch Munch... 

Cannibal corpses that is we 
Munch Munch Munch... 

Alas all joy is fleeting. 

He is dead and forced to walk 
Thump Thump Thump... 

No mouth to move to scream or talk 
Thump Thump Thump... 

More meals lie ahead. 

The door is the shell for food within 
Scratch Scratch Scratch... 

We try to knock but we can't get in 
Scratch Scratch Scratch... 

No way to enter. 

So here we cry our vague duress 
We hope our words do not cause distress...This is also listed as a challenge poem.
