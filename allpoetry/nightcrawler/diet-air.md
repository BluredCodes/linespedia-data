Less filling than regular air?
Makes you thinner with only one sip!

You have to love its flair
It's wonderfully great for the hip!

Sweet and tart
It's bad for the heart.

No one anywhere
Can breathe without its help!

Its bottles are disappearing off the shelves!

Buy it today!

No one should be without!

Just what are YOU breathing?
