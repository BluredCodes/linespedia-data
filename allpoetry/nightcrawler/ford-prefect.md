A Tribute to the Newt who 
helped me through my 
first year of college.


Ford

You were first in my life
The day before the world
Went into its great strife.

A day before 9/11.

You were with me through it all
The good and the bad
The large and the small.

Both you and Zaphod.

Some may say
"It's just a Newt"
You weren't there that day.

I needed something to hang on to.

They kept me sane
In an insane world.
The day of terrible pain.

Now you are gone.

To be buried not like a thing.
But in a golden box scented with jasmine.
You will be buried like an ancient king.

Good night sweet prince.

May your small soul
Find peace.
