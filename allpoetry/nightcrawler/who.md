Who am I?
Who?

I am a knight in shining armor.
I have my sword and shield.

Who am I?
Who?

I am a wretch in the alley.
I carry the world on my back.

Who am I?
Who?

I am a famous performer.
I can see my name in lights.

Who am I?
Who?

I am a dead rat in the gutter.
I possess the nameless plague.

Who am I?
Who?

I am part of the rainbow.
I am the invisible colors.

Who am I?
Who?

I am all and nothing.
I exist only in my dreams.

Who do you think I am?
