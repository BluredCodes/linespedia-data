title: "The sea above"

Lost within a deep blue sea,
Moving white waves all around me.
A vision of beauty within the sky,
The sea I speak is above you and I.

The waves are clouds of white puff,
Sometimes calm and sometimes rough.
A breeze it follows these clouds of white,
Sailing through both the day and night.

A beautiful sight behind clouds the sun,
A brilliant array of colors compared to none.
Silently these move before my eye's,
It's more than anyone can realize.
