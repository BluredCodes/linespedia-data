---
title: "My heart needs to know"
writer: "Dale"

Light the candle within your soul,
Ignite the flames within your heart.
Take from me the heart you stole,
Bring us together never to part.

Give to me your warm sensuous touch,
Give to me your heart complete and all.
Show me you love me and just how much,
Show me why for you I truely did fall.

Give me a reason to love only you,
Give me a reason for me to stay.
Give to me all your love ever true,
Give it to me each and every day.

Never question my love only for you,
Never question why with you I do stay.
My love for you it is all the way through,
And my love for you it is in every way.

So give me a signal give me a sign,
Show me there is love between you and I.
Show me that you are all but mine,
Show me with me your heart does lie.

Seal our love with a special kiss,
One in which is sure to last a lifetime.
Embrace me to make me not want to miss,
Take me as one to stand all of time.

Dedicated to: Diana Lynn
