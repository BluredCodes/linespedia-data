---
title: "A child's love"
writer: "Dale"

Blessed are our children as they grow,
Treasured are thier memories for us to know.
So fast they grow through a quickened time,
The moments are with me and all mine.
They give love and expect nothing in return,
Childhood youth and fun what they yern.
As they drift off into a slumber sleep,
I know that the love shared is deep.
