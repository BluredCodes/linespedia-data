title: "You are my lover"

Hello my friend I needed this,
Time with you I cannot miss.
We hugged and laughed and even kissed,
Oh Lord how I needed this.
For above all the sadness there,
I needed to know that you still care - for me.
Now together after this I realize,
Which has broght tears to my eyes.
You are my lover - My only lover
I just had to feel you were there
I just had to feel you were there
You sleep beside me in our bed,
Dreams of you all through my head.
Thoughts of you and thoughts of me,
There's not another place I'd rather be.
For above all the sadness there,
I've needed to know that you still care - for me.
Now together everything has turned around,
My love for you can always be found.
You are my lover - My only lover
I just had to feel you were there
I just had to feel you were there
We both woke up with a smile,
For that its been awhile.
Now I see you as my friend,
With a love that will never end.
For above all the sadness there,
I needed to know that you still care - for me.
Now together I've come to realize,
Which has brought tears to my eyes.
You are my lover - My only lover.
I just had to feel you were there,
I just had to feel you were there.
We are together in loving bliss,
Times like these I cannot miss.
For now I feel you are back,
With a love that cannot lack - a thing.
For there is no sadness there,
I feel that you still care - for me.
For you are my lover - My only lover.
I just had to feel you were there,
I just had to feel you were there.
Happiness now it follows me,
This love it feels like destiny.
You are my love you are my friend,
With a love that could never end.
You are the one I need to love,
With feelings that fly high above.
When we are together,
I feel like its all forever.
You are my lover - My only lover.
I just had to feel you were there,
I just had to feel you were there.
You are my lover - My only lover.
You are my lover - My only lover.
My only lover - Like no other,
You are my lover - My only lover......
