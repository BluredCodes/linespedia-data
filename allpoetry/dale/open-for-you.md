---
title: "Open for you"
writer: "Dale"

Contentment belongs to me,
and what I believe we believe,
for every fragment that is mine
is ours.

I wait and open my heart,
I give the openess...and view
you in the morning light.


Dedicated to: Diana Lynn
By: Dale
