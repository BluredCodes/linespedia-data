---
title: "My wonderful wife"
writer: "Dale"
slug: "my-wonderful-wife"
source: "AllPoetry"

If I were to have a lover, it could
be one and that is you. For there is
not one woman who could take me
away from the one I love and that's you!

For there is not a woman who could
connect with me as only you can do.
There is not a woman who could make
me feel like a man better than you!

There is not a woman who could
make me feel loved more than you.
There is not a woman who could
ever make me want to leave you!

Dedicated to: Diana Lynn

By; Dale
