all is calm all   is

padded here   isthisallyouwanted

along the western fronts is

this all you expected

sob until your eyes come off



there will be no ghosts tonight



never againim new... please dont hurt meow too much

this piece is part of a cycle about the conscious journey from day to dream... this being the completion of waking light
