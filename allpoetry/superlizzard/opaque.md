I love you

I hate you



I'm confused

I'm afraid



You're the only person I can't see through



and you see--

that's why I'm afraid of youI know it's cheesy, but it's so true, so true.. I changed the ending, I think it makes more sense this way. If you're wondering who it's about.. well.. it's about my Tuna Boy, hehehee...
