Here's where this story begins
Me staring at you, me liking you
But getting nothing back
Though I heard you liked me, but it wasn't true

Thanks to friends we got "closer"
but not too close to love each other
In a way I loved you
You didn't love me, you didn't even bother

Indeed, you did something
In my heart you made a hole
YOU worth nothing
YOU are such an ASSHOLE

First lie, but more to come
"can't go, have to do an assignment with my class"
Then found you somewhere else with friends
Oh! what a JACKASS

Forgave you in vain
"I have to study, I'm not doing well at school"
Kid wanting to be a bad boy, eh?
You are a big FOOL

I was so happy having you, Damn!
I was in love thanks to cupid
But now that it's over
I start thinking: "Oh my god, he was so STUPID"don't rush people to love you =(  
   - Lesson learned -
