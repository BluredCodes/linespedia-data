Beautiful song, where you belong- 
Is soul's sinless chamber!
Your tone a bliss, the sweetest kiss,
Love is what you render.
Your lovely rhythm, free from all '-ism'-
Spells only true harmony,
Your sweetmost tune, to us a boon
Is enchanting a symphony!
Its love you love, the hearts you surf
Out spell all their sins,
Your flawless stream, bestow sweet dreams
Dreamland's you're the prince.
You fret our tear, your only fear,
Our deviation from peace,
So long you're here, bless has no fear
You utter eternal bliss!
