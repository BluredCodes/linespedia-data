---
title: "Little Bits"
writer: "Defallene Engel"
slug: "little-bits"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56675-Little-Bits-by-Defallene-Engel"
---

There's little bits of you
Everywhere I go
I still smell your scent
On my hands, on my skin
Through the stench of my nail polish
Bloodred - you said you love it
My bed is a mess
From where you've slept
It annoys me at night
The way you breath against my neck
But I love it in the morning
To find your arm about my waist
And the blankets tangled about my legs
Joined with yours
A kiss stolen at night
With tiny thoughts of love
To be returned in the morning
After dreams have drifted
And faded with the coming sun
And those thoughts of love
Grow with each passing hour
When the sun reflects in you eyes
Letting me know it's all real
