The tears come... ?

From a tormented land
Of barren wastes
And celestial monuments
Forgotten....

They stop
Halted mid step
Enough to trip the trip 

Into

Another place


Time
Smell
Awkward situation

Sleep tames
Lions feed
And

Water lilies still float forever onwards
