title: "Dream me a Dream..."
writer: "FaeSinner"
slug: "dream-me-a-dream"

Ride me up a tune 
From somewhere south of here
Anything that sooths my mind
Something other than these torments of late
String me up a harp
Multi stringed instrument of light
Dance oh invisible fingers 
Make use of music deep
So I to can sing
Pure and unadulterated
Whole in soul and song
Join with me a symphony
A plethora of magic delight
So that I might relax this tenuous hold
Upon this reality
Wish me a magic
So strong it carries me home
Into the arms of those I love
No longer lost
Found and whole

Dream me a dream
Truer than false this time
Make it worth
Every thought I have dreamed.....

Sleep me a remembering
Refreshing this patterned blueprint
Synapse the connection
And open of my soul
