This night speaks strangely
In whispers of eternity
Of sweet seduction
Bound by shadows and pale thoughts
Fear chases away
Those to timid to reach further
Into the inky darkness
Perhaps it mirrors their souls
Deep places they know not
Only that they must not face
It's velvet caress

Where have all the people gone
Scurring home to light and safety
Protected by fragile santury
They not know the peace
Of walking alone
Moving from moment to moment
Tasting the night
And it's hidden slendour
Secret truths
And dreams

Ancestral memories
Bring about
A cleaner exsistance
Of when survial was fought
With hand and instinct
Alone
Eyes in the darkness
Reflecting the moon
And intent
Of the hunt
And of living
Until morning

Cool air relinquishing
The sun's warm breath
As I breath in
This liquid
Called night
Fear sits in the pit of my stomack
Swirling around
Creating mild unease
My mind floats just above reality
Body tense and ready
Waiting
Intent soon to be discovered

Modern day instincts
Sharpen
As I merge with
An older self
Remembering times
Indistinctly
Settling with my own subconscious
Sniffing the air
Feeling my enviroment
Every instinct alive

I set off
To walk
Alone

My only companion
Is the nightYumbo!
