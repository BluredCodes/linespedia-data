Here let me help you 
She says as she leans against and over me
Pointing to the program on the desktop
Her unfamiliar form pressing against my back
Feminine curves offering a brief informal touch

A sign of intimacy

Not a come on
But certainly a turn on

As I agree with what she is saying
Quering her instructions
Voice calm and secure
But body alive and tingling
I fight the urge to relax 
Rest my head back 
To be cradled by breasts warm and inviting
Reach around and stroke office nylons
Create static electricity
To spark the connection

Right I say
Uhuh
as she finishes...

With a you understand don't you
Which of course I don’t

The program yeah that’s no problem
Picked up what she was saying straight away
But everything else
The smell of her perfume
That voice....

And a million nerves saying...

Hello!

This sterile environment
Partitions
And air conditioning
Black and gray
Cotton and polyester
Time precision existence
Calculated programmable mathematics
Fixed faces dreaming of somewhere else
Processed coffee
Stress

A moment in a crazy world
Of body memory
And a casual trusting intimacy
An underlining need to be....

Open

With one another

She says something
To which I politely agree
Not hearing amongst the plethora
And walks off
Absently straightening her shirt

I get a grip
Refocus
Switch off the preferred existence 
And get back to it

The memory fading
Perhaps to be talked about later


Perhaps not























On a crowded standing room only tram after work
I recognize her
And as the tram sways along rickety winding tracks
We are momentarily pushed together
Alighting a familiar feeling

Eyes lock

And she smiles

A warm inviting smile
That brings understanding at lastOh Ahhhhhh.....:)
