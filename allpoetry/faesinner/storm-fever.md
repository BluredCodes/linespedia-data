There's a storm outside
I wanna go play
But there's lightning and thunder
So inside I'll stay

The funny thing is
That it hurts like hell
My body's aching
Ringing like a bell

It's building up
I wish it would rain
Pour down hard
And end this pain

There's a storm inside
I have to say
And until it rains
I won't be able to playaaaaaooooouuuu!
