Will u take this last dance?

The lion roars it's mighty song

Across a wooden floor
Jumping little entities
Giggle in a thundered step
Winding up long mesmerizing beats
Floating falling spinning

Tracing slipstreams with hands and empty thoughts

Lights glow and glitter
Smiles joyous and alive
Bodies

Oh bodies

To capture such
In pen and ink and painted texture
What a ride of the sweaty sublime 


Native indigenous shaman dances
Shaking his step
Around and around
Never pausing 
Just liven' it

Base beat RAOR
Best beat dancin' a slick floor
Feeling my world form mindless

At last

The impossible task of describing bliss
The sheer love joy of feeling with every inch of I
The song in my veins
My body insane loving it lovin' it being it grovin' it

Wild reckless tangent of spiraling desire 
For the next beat to take me take me take me 
Into another even more intense sublime diva

Carried with masterful mind sets playing dance sets
As the acid licks a trilling sonnet
Movement insistent
Madness willing given
gimme gimme gimme

ahhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh

The kick rises unexpectant

And endorphins rock my burning mindset
Into anothererereererereeeeeeeeeeerererrrrrreereeeeeeeeeeeee!!!!







Beat.

Can’t describe this
Lets grove baby
