She rocks me solid
Excites my existence
Reals my dreaming vision
Steams a heart to boiling
Dubs my internal core
Runs an invisible current
Into my system and explodes
Against my will
Leaving me to whimper
And pant
And reach out of the abyss
That is my blissed out
True innermost depths
Of funkadalic love
And embraces
Nurtures

Feels me
As I have never 
Never ever 
Been felt before
Secret places 
Once hidden 
Now delighted
And free

She moves me
Body sublime
A rhythm
A knowing
I dance with her
And I know now
That I have never
Been danced with
Like this 
Before

Her curves capture

My male
 
Her mind sings 
Along lines of
Understanding
Of acceptance

Love
 
She laughs and i melt
And she melts with me
Like chocolate in the sun
On a warm summer's night

Crying
Because nothing this good 
has ever happened
Like this before

Dying
Because now I know
That there is
A soul destined
To join in
