Softly now sweet memories
Sleep has come even for you
Wish away times fused 
With magical starlight
And dream the whispers 
Of words I say to you
Majestic wonders
Inescapable melodies
Ancient steeps bound
By snow and ice
Sweeping plains carrying 
Nomadic peoples
Jungles filled by
Giant cats and sleeping snakes
Seas rolling in thunderous
Storms with nature's furious momentum
Light cascading in earth veins
And crystal caves
Children voices singing in chorus
Smiling shared delight
Butterflies and fireflies 
And green tree frogs and dragons 
And unicorns
And white elves tall and lithe
Dark gnomes and phosphorous fairies
Forget not the mother
Her time here forever
Supporting her children
Nature's loving glow
Sleep now my love
Let thine light shine
For forgetting is remembering
Upon this starlit night
Softly now sweet memories:)
