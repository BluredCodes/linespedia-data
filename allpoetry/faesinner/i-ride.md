What is beauty?
Is it a face that awakens I?
A voice that whispers to me of love?
A storm filled night?
An uncontrollable

Feeling?

What is this passion that resides
This stacato beat in my heart
That rises when inspired
This inner lord

where is the muse 
Who boils my blood
Sends thoughts racing
To find haven
Amongst the gods

When are you coming home?
My love and light
Sign of my soul
Lapis and gold
I wear this pennant
Before all
Not for I

Loyalty disguised

I ride the wind's back
Zephyrs chase and tempests fly
Clouds riot across a crimson
Tempting death this side of night

Token I ask of thee
Of thy love
Be it so true
That I show to you
My truest?

Thunder echos 
Surround those who seek to find
Their truest quest

Oh!

Where for art thou?
Grail
I seek thee everywhere
And find thee not
My muse
My colours own
All my grace
Maddness
Sight
Wonder
I give to the finding of you
In this arrid waste that is my

Is my





Is my heart













I think not

I ride

Mind
Soul 
Body

Enriched by this love for life that is in


In


My Heart

Is my Spirit




Look Out!
