I had forgotten
That we were supposed to be in love
Two people joined 
Together at the heart
To many things had gone wrong
Doubts transformed into a sad reality
An unforgiving bondage that kept me away from you
I loved you then
And I love you now
Yet I realise
That it's time to move on
To let you love another
All I ask
If you deem it possible 
In that heart of yours

Is one last kiss

Few are the truely magic moments in life
When one falls in love
Truely for the first time
It is an awakening
To the very meaning of life it's self
That moment
When I look into your eyes
As you said yes
I knew that we 
Could capture some of that essence
Once more

You blinked slowly
As if coming to life
Your eyes widening slightly
As I leant towards you
Over the low rail fence that separated us
My hands drifting up to your face
Wanting to cradle you
Gently and lovingly
Your hands hesitant
And unsure
You eased yourself into my embrace
Looking up into my eyes
Yours glistening with 
The beginnings of tears
Gazing down at you
I realised how much I loved you
Knowing that we could not go back
Wanting to start our time again
Savouring this moment
As if it was my last
Dropping my chin
As you tilted up towards me
I grazed my lips across your forread
The tip of my tougue
Memorizing furrowed brow
Bringing them down
Past eyelashes fluttering 
Cheeks reddened
To rest at last
Upon the velvet richness of your own
Trembling together
As if our bodies remembered
Whom we once were
Tentively I opened my mouth
Pushing against your fragile resistance
Tongue protuding to taste at last
The salt of your tears
Your own joining mine
In a dance of love's sharing
Deeper and deeper we embraced
Lips and tongues merging
Hands caressing and loving
All forgotten in that timeless moment
Each wanting it to last forever
Transforming our past
Into a wonderous present

I don't know who pulled away first
We each stood back
As if released form restraints
Stupified and dazed
Smiling 
I looked at her
Those eyes that had enchanted me always
She looked up at me
Tears in her eyes
She embraced me
Squeezing the life 
That I had just so recently discoved
Out of my soul
As she turned crying
And walked back
Into the houseyes.....another kiss challenge
