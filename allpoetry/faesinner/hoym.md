There is someone I think of 
When days are long
And I struggle with turmoils
When nights reach into forever
And my head bows with burdens
All I have to do is

Remember

Kind loving words given freely

Unconditional

Sincerely


She means alot to me
This poet
When saddened
Her words bring life 
And an ancient heritage
To those who need
When happy
She brings light
And an innonce
Bound by wisdom


 
Mesmerised I have watched her grow

The quiet voice
Has grown strong
Echoing her totem
Her magic written
For all to see
She offers
Humble thanks
And quiet joy

Her friendship
Is valued highly
Her tears bring pain
Her laughter joy
Time spent with her
However fleeting
Is truly
Golden

For although I pushed you away
With words of deepest sorrow
My heart was there with you
As yours was with mine

Thank you my friend
