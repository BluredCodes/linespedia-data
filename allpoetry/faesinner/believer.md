title: "Believer."

Speak thunderous voice so that I might hear you
Not whispered thoughts to trap and steal
But words clear and wild to capture my heart
Propel me into a moment of pure forms and ideologies

Rise up from hidden depths that seek to bind
Those words chiseled out of stone
Fear not this present bondage
Shake that wild man's cloak and ride the wind

Shaking I tremble unsure that I walk the true
Knowing that this power is to simple to construe
Instinctive and intuitive at last nature's irony prevails
Eyes glowing magical delight singing a tempest or two

Long ago I wept pure the frustration of loss
A four-walled hovel called me antidote
Now I know for whom love calls
And it awakes for thee, the believer:)
