Do you hear it
He said lowering his head to her
That sound 
It’s coming from somewhere
Under us i think
She cocked her head to the side
Listening

As waves beat up against a distant shore
And snow fell down a mountainside
Rain swept across a desert plain
And wind flew it's forever path

Yes I can feel it
More than hear it 
She said looking at him
Her eyes brightened
Understanding
Intuitively

What’s it mean do you think
At a loss he turned to her for direction
Trusting her and her woman's wisdom

A soul cry
Keening the loss of a million years
Tormented by the ill use of it's hearth
Vibrated alarming
Heard by all
Understood by few

It’s the death of the world
She said, deep sorrow in her words 
There’s nothing we can do
It has started at last
Tears well up in her eyes as she crouches down
Unable to bear the weight of the world
And it's demise

There must be something we can do
Aren’t we an advanced race of people?
Conscious in our choices
Able to give back what we have taken
He saids shaking with realization and determination

The cry has reached a peak
To loud in every sense not to be heard
From all around people are seen to collapse
Unable to understand

There is
She saids tears streaming
Voice taking on a newer deeper purpose
We must work together
To heal this planet
And what we have taken from her

She

Gia

Is asking us to..








Silence
