title: "Tribulation"
writer: "FaeSinner"

Romantic moments

Forever days

Timeless thoughts

Forgetful ways

Lasting memories

And smiling stories

Laughing friends

Sharing past glories

Friendships say

Their gentle truth

And in so many ways

Are honored thus

So goodnight to you

Friend and foe

This day has spent

It’s final

Wonder
