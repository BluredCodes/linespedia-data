title: "To Be Born Thus."
writer: "FaeSinner"

Calling to you of sacred wonders
Upon these tides of bliss
Leave behind turmoil’s sorrows
Awaken to this moment and dive deep

Yesterday was but a time when we didn't understand
Lost in the mysteries of our pasts
Now is our time to live again
Where two are one and two again

What comfort I find in your embrace
This melding of you and I
Flesh to flesh and heart to heart
Of silent rejoice streaming to all

I feel this reality now
And such is this feeling that I can hardly breath
Lost amongst you and I on this ephemeral plane
Looking no more to answers needed so dearly

Rest and recover now my light
To shine again the brightest day
Tomorrow we shall see, my love
Why we are for today:).....:)
