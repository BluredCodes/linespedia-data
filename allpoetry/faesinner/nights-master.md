I am the night's master
Of succulent desire
The sudden gasp of cool air
As your fingers caress
Those hidden places
The other hand that toys
Imagining
Wanting more
I am the bead of perspiration
That travels undulating
Across your 
Warm 
Flushed flesh
Seeking
To embrace
A lover's kiss
I am the night's lover
Calling to you
Formed from your secret desires
Ready to pleasure
You’re mortal 
Flesh
You’re very essence
You seek me out
With every breath 
And sigh
You know I'm there
With you
As you move deeper
Deeper into euphoria
Stroking that bliss 
That builds inside
Rocking with tension
As you climb
Ascension
I am with you
Silently guiding you
Ephemeral hands and loving embrace
I am yours 
The night's master
I am here 
Only for you
