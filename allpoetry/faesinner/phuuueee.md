A hole fills the place 
Where all understanding used to be.
Big 
Black 
And 

Frantic


The weight of memory loads cyclic
Crushing my heart
Leaving me cold
Frozen in my ineptitude
Unbalanced
Surreal

Waiting for the inevitable

Analysis



The unnamed




Rules it’s frozen kingdom

Impersonal abandonment imperialist uncontrollable need…


And then some…
