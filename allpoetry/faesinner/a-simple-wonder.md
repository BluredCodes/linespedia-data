Raw this tingling
Along nerves and sinew
Taunt with desire

Bodies locking together
With tension's aching need
Seeking reconstruction

Shaped thoughts realized
At last we ride this wagon
Steep the climb to heaven

Creation in a moment's song
As two lights give to lust
And love sings of all things wonderful

Breathing together
In and in again
Will it ever ebb?

Over the precipice
Shaking and clenching the wave
Spasmodic understandings of the universe

Clear at last
Need steeped in liquor's golden laze
Inevitable embrace of rebirth:)
