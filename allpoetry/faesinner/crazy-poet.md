Poet
Crazed soul under a merciless mind
That whips and cranks it up

Baby

Fire rains from the sky
Too rich to take it in all at one time

Sip it gently lest it
Rips you
Apart

Rending you differently from the rest

Mindscapes ride rapidly
From one moment to the next
Of times and moments 
And silly adventures amongst the

Bytes

And frights

Oh 

And such thrilling delights

Water cascades
Drenching you after pain
Smelling of sunlight

Evaporated

Windswept sadness of past's
Indelible hurts

Cannot escape such

And the flint's all wet






Beat and song
Rising up
From memories dream
Takes earth
Bodies
Mind
That needs no books

Just love......

    Slipping through the 

Cracks

Home
Is somewhere

Out there

Amongst burning chrome

And no mans land
As the heat chases the wizard
And the sorcerous
Evalopes the madman

A poet of secret skill
Rides a challenge all the way 


To nevermind
