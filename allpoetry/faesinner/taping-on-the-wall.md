title: "Taping on the wall"

I don't understand any more
I just get to sit back 
And watch
The opening and closing
Of doors
And life

I walked my path
I still do
Regret and sorrow 
I have moved through
But sometimes I wish 
I had not been the one
To break the game
I never won

Now I just hover
And try to care
Knowing my place 
Is no longer there
But I smile often
and remember
a friend
With a loving heart
Who cares*............*
