Whoa.....

That wasn't expected
A quick kick aimed just so
Out of silence
And oh so low
What a laugh
Shall I dance 
Another farce
Never to lead again
Well maybe I've changed
Yet again
Yet again
Yet agian
Yet again
Yet have I?
Not sending this mind
Around any bend
Not of my making
Anyway

Can't help but grin though.Ummmmm...just being Ummmmmmmm...just Being Ummmmmmmm..just be
