Sit back and relax in the seat of Cybertime
Do not fear this Ice runner
It’s just different
From real time interfacing
It’s an uplifting experience

As an interwoven mesh encapsulates your body
Crawling across flesh like ants invading a sweet morsel
Electrodes and namo glands and serial sequences
All load simultaneous
A sharp edge of invasion followed 
By uninterrupted euphoria
Spiral coded cords and IntelliSense of cyber codecs
Run riot across my mind

Why we have had many of your type here
Seeking answers to questions far beyond their own minds
They ride the to the moon seeking lost time
And find it as easily as you will

Pores open up in your skin
Portals to allow access
To leads that snake sentient towards intercourse
Mesh leaps to embrace as insertion completes
Locking into you as
The first emotion hits you
Shock!
Raw energy erupts along nerves and sinew
I strain at invisible bonds
Not in body
Not in mind
Somewhere in between
Helpless to chosen course

It’s just a simple process Ice runner
You are gently nestled in a shock resistant
Inter mesh cell woven of highest quality
Nano-cybertime encapsulation
And then as we hook up to your senses
All that was will be and all that will be is

Rage tears loose fragile feelings from ancient cores
Joy sears through body and then soul! if soul it be
Fear loosens bowls as times expands beyond comprehension
Hate love envy joy lust happiness anger desire sadness 
Swirl amongst the plethora as you rocket into being

It’s your chance to travel time it's self!
Have you ever felt it possible to go beyond what’s known 
And explore the conceivable and inconceivable

Time expands and loops on it's self
Creating a window into the impossibility of being
Unexplainable yet very real
I let go of bonds both imagined and concrete
Leap forth
Wings aglow with manic energy
At home at last
In cybertime
Acceptance surreal as the doubt behind it

Quickly now ice runner
Submerge your self
That’s it
Just relax
It won’t be long now

A new door opens up in my heightened awareness
I fly unheeded towards it
A new feeling envelops
Euphoria a pale companion
I change
Beyond expectations of modern science 
And nano technology derived spirituality

We’re losing him
Shouts across a busy terminal
As techs run about punching buttons
And entering master codes to bring back
a disappearing Icerunner
It’s never happened before!
It’s our first rogue!
The tech so bent on selling the product
Is now quivering with the weight of new found 
Responsibility and guilt
What will we do?
As the machines slow down to standby
The lights and sounds of the interface
Wink out one by one
And there is silence
Amongst those whom know not what to doand now for something completly different...:)
