If it was I whom was served this blow 
In my country
My heart would break
My mind would beget thunderous revenge 
And I would wish to strike out in blind rage 
Claiming pride and right!

But then as the toll of innonce deaths 
And shattered lives came to my realisation 
Would I weep with shame and guilt? 
At actions beneath a land of free people 
People lucky enough to choose their choices..

Would I then send out my thoughts
Actions and love
To clense the deeper evil?
To heal those devestated souls
Both those whom now know the loss and agony 
Of living in a third world country
And those who live it

Not those who seek war
Glory and self gain
But those innocence people whom weep everyday 
At the pain they live with
LIVE WITH.....
Every day

The smoke has cleared

The damage has been done

Is there healing amongst all this?

Or is the ANGER still intent upon wiping out 

Anyone whom threatens at all costs?

What cost is it being number one?

Does it take that terrible a statement 

To say somethings not right?

As we bask in our glory

Stay warm and safe

With computers and beds and electricity 

And creature comforts

And and and 

And and and 

And and and 

And and and 

And and and 

And

And 

And

And

And 

Enough!


















May we all know peace

May we all have the strength to give and protect and love

Everyone regardless of the situation

May God or Goddess come on down 

And help us so that one day 

I may stand with my brother and sister

From all lands and say

With pride

We are the lands of the brave and free
