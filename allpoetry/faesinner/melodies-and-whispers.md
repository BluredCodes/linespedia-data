title: "Melodies and Whispers"
writer: "FaeSinner"

Floating's a strange place
This time out of mind
When I hover between myself
Nowhere and this place

Somewhere between caring 
And not wanting to cause hurt 
For words said 
And actions done

Stumbling over a silence heard shouting
Or a whisper guilded in the shape of lost love
Not being what I could have been
And doing what I am

I wonder at myself
My intent in this life
Where I stand
And whom am I now

Am I a follower
Needing that faint blush of joy
Or am I the leader
Lost in the essence of it all

I am neither or ither
Only one step at a time
But for all my silly mistakes
I am just whom I can and try

Unconditionally I learn
Caring in my way
I cannot change my past
But I live for the thinking of it

everyday

For better or for ill
Life is what it has
Melodies and their whispers
Are the very songs of hidden greatnessjust a quiet rambling to fill the space....
