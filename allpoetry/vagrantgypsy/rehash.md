I listen contently as the rain slides down my window
And I smell the breeze come in washing away regret
I was almost asleep,
The first time in two days,
But I had too many thoughts running through my head:

Eight months ago was the last time I got high
And then two month after that
Every time is my last time
Just like on Saturday when I smoked some pot
It’s raining softer but I still think about past mistakes
My eyes are closing, must be the meds. I wasn’t supposed to take
One year ago I said farewell, fuck you
It was the last time we’d ever hang out
Just like eight months ago and last weekend when we drank at your house
There's no more rain to clear my head
So I'll fall asleep w/ visions of what I'll never do again
