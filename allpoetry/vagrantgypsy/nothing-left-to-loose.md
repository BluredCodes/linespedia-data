title: "Nothing left to loose"
writer: "VagrantGypsy"
slug: "nothing-left-to-loose"
source: "AllPoetry"

Everything seems worth it when you’ve got nothing left to loose but yourself and the broken promises You’re trying to forget the small town people
Meaning nothing but seeming able to destroy you
So you sit around and suffer with all these narrow minded people because you know once you leave there’s no coming back
And the thought of a new place scares you more than what would happen if you stayed here
Nothing seems worth it any more
So keep thinking small people only live in small towns as you walk into a world full of close minded people
