---
title: "World's child"
writer: "VagrantGypsy"

Innocent kisses fall soft
Even though you're spying someone else from across the room
You still smile at me with those huge eyes and pale skin
I completely adore you
But you're the World's child and you belong to everyone
Wish you'd tell me you love me one more time,
Even though it's just a taunting thought 
That makes me hold onto your waist tighter as we fall asleep
