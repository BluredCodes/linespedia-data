title: "Trippy Me"

I want to dance and sing and write all day in poppy fields 
With my best friends, an acoustic, and a pair of bongos
I want to feel sublime and remember that lovin’ is what I got
But there's so much angst inside I still gotta mosh
I wanna break free of everything constricting me
And run away to a state where no one knows my name

There's this place inside my head
I visit when I need to get away
There's a field of wheat and some magnolia tress, plaid blankets in the shade
Painted faces and long hair
Music floats through the breeze

But then the bell rings and I wake up as everyone runs out the door
Wish I could remember that the deaf, dumb, and blind kid plays a mean pinball
So I’m back on the streets of downtown
A pissed off chick from middle class suburbia
With all this rage inside
So I’ll go to the show with my spiky collar 
And scream with sweat blood and booze running down my face
Until there's nothing left

There's this place inside my mind
No one knows about
Where I go to escape
Happiness comes from smiles not the other way around
The sun never rises, never sets
There are just rays of light and clouds
There's this person inside my head that I’d like you to meet,
She's me only better
She's free
