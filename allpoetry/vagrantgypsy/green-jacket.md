You walk so self-assured 
Pretending you’re not afraid of the world
You know exactly who you are and what you want
But try to be everything you’re not
You’re so grown up; you’re so much more mature
But really, you’re just a girl with big brown curls and a green jacket
Living in a wonderland when she needs to escape reality
A psychedelic princess born in the wrong decade
You stand out in a room full of people
Maybe it’s the bracelets, maybe the sandals
It’s in what she says, the way she moves
Too bad she's unaware of the beauty that flows from beneath a green jacket
