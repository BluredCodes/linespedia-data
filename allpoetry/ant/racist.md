Racist
Face it
While others
Embrace it

Racist
Take it
So often
Mistake it

Look at you in your

Big family with your

Big house

And your

Wide screen TV

Racist
Face it
While others
Embrace it

Watching you as you

Breed incessantly and you

Feed over zealously

With your

Wide screen TV

Racist
Face it
While others
Embrace it

Pity you with your

Lack of self control and your

Betrayal of mother nature as you

Defile the name of purity with your

Acts of mental insecurity and your

Hatred of the differences that make

People individuals

Racist 
Face it
You will never
Make itI observed a racail slur and the devestating effect that ONE word can have on a adolescent Asian footballer who is still unsure of his place in society. Is he asian? Is he British? he has to decide for himself, and racist bigots like the one I saw today make his tough decision harder to come to terms with. In all honesty he was twice the footballer and three times the man that any of us were out there today.
