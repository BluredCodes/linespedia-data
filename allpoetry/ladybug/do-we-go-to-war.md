To his kingdom we come
The prince of peace
where victory is won
And mercy is a feast

the table is set
let all have a turn
in the Lords supper
where there is no return

the wine is bitter
the bread is meager
but the forgiveness
is bountiful

He asks only one thing in return
to let go of this land
full of bitter and rage
trampling over our hate

the kingdom is near
the times are hard
the world is in an awful place today
it is peace that we seek

A rainbow of hope
a cloud of forgiveness
the mercy in us 
 cries out to this land

We need to come together
help solve this riddle
that the politics cast 
another world war is not our requestwe do pay their salaries and vote them in the office they govern, so why are we allowing this war to continue??
