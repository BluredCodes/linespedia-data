---
title: "Koom By Yha"
writer: "Ladybug"
slug: "koom-by-yha"
source: "AllPoetry"

Humble me to my knees
Humble me to the One 

Humble me to kiss this pain away
Humble me to accept all that I am

Humble me to be all I can
Humble me to set this star ablaze

Humble me to be the light
whether it be dark or day

Humble me to extend a lending hand
for those that are stumbling

Humble me to be 
the castle you are carving

Humble me to be content
To serve, only for your grace
