We blow out the candles
slice the cake
toast a merry
off in the coach of married

Five years later
we dine with no wine
share a kiss
but no card

I still need you to know
my undying love for you
grows a bit more every day
And will forever more

Just when I think I'm bored
A new rose comes along
To help me remmeber
All the thoughtful things you do.

If granted one wish
it would be to stay in love forever
You are my knight and shining armor
That has caught me every time I fell.

               I adore in you
               your strenth and courage
               but most of all
               the twinkle of your eye
               when you say I love you

Pains have come and gone
the years have been gentle
All I can say on this special day
Is, I do still love you
 
               Happy 5th Anniversaydoes time relax you so much that romance is not a pleasure?
