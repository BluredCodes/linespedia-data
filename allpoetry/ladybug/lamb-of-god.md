Jesus, Lamb of God
Worthy is your name
Taking all my sins
Taking all my shame


To the cross you died
To release me from my cross
I bless your name
You are my all in all


When I am weak
You are my strength
You are the treasure that I seek
You are my all in all


Lord I give up
Being this fool that I am
I give up being of this world
Your love has captured methe freedom bestowed to be just Me!
