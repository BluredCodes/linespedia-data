title: "Crimes of Divorce"

Let me count the ways 
to a dark and unseen way
Freedom at last....
to do whatever you like

Guilt sets the stage
What if I had
What if I had not
As I sleep less and eat no more

Years pass so slow
The children grow
as fast as the grass
Time waits only for the next check

Loss of hair, loss of teeth
The insurance that once was
Is now no more
Cash will only go so far

The days are long and empty
Not much laundry in the hamper
The schools feed our kids
As we scrape together enough coins for the meter

Work is work
no matter what company the name is
A long sigh of relief
As the children smile and hug Mommie

The crimes of Divorce 
Are Many
Some may avoid the trap
Some may heal their wounds
But the guilt, does it ever leave?

Can time really heal these scars
Not visible to anyone's eye
The ones that play your heart strings
And sting the eye

Loved you were not
Missed you are not
Crimes of a divorcejust rambling, no pity required
shedding some old memories of yesteryears
