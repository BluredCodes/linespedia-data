In this life we live in two worlds,
Television world.
Ordinary world.

In dreams life seems to be transparent like glass,
Then the wake strikes,
And everything appears dull and gray,
Waiting for the morning light in a darkness that never leaves,
Walking through the days are routine and repetitive,
Work, eat, laugh, drink, smoke, sleep,
Endless miniscule elaborations of yestraday,

I want to wake to the dream.Just a small complaint from a small person in a huge world.
