---
title: "Freedom of the Artists"
writer: "VocoKool"
slug: "freedom-of-the-artists"

The freedom of expression is that to God's gold ring,
It is the feeling of the need to push is what drives the artist,
Being in a place where warm winds play silent songs through your hair while you float in mid air,
It is this freedom that I hold deep,
With my paper, my pen and my mind is where everything disappears, 
I then settle to a calm natural quiet world with heavenly notes of serenity,
And in this world a fire burns also,
Like a crisp fresh flame of passion that can never be explained because it is only felt,
Mixed emotions run and clarity is solidarity,
So let us roam free forever,
Us, the artistry of expressive being.This is to the artists on this site who are easily distracted by their own defensive mannerisms.
