The dawn brings new light each day,
And with that light is a promise;
That at the fall of each day
A new one will rise in it's place.

Even the darkest deepest night
Will at some point end.
The eye of the storm,
The end of the rain and snow.

The winter brings forth spring,
With new birth and sunlight
And the simple parts of life
That we all live for.
