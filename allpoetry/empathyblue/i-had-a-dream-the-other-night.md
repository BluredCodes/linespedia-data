I had a dream the other night,
A dream of you and me;
I was in a gilded boat,
Across a silver sea,
I came up to a castle,
Made up of frozen halls,
I put my hands up as i walked,
And touched the icy walls.
It was so very dark,
And as cold as night could be,
And we felt an icy wind
From across the silver sea.
You were standing at the end
Of the very longest hall,
And as I walked toward you
The ice began to fall
And we were in a garden,
And you reached for my hand
And we began to walk,
And the sea took over land
But I wasn’t afraid
Of the shining, silvery sea,
I felt forever safe,
