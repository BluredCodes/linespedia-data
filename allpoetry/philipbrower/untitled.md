Unconditional Love


Where were you?
to dry my tears
Where were you?
to love and comfort
Where were you?
When i needed you most
A heart without a soul


If only you had seen my tears,
the tears i cried within
If only you had seen my hurt,
the hurting deep within
If only you had seen me!
This heart without a soul
