Different Paths

He is where he is supposed to be.
He is where I want him to be.

He and I are not each other’s destination, 
but detours along the way 
that changed the course of where we are each headed. 

He will always be a part of my life, 
because who I am today has been shaped so much 
by the things he taught me when I had so much to learn. 

I hope the same is true for him.

I am where I am supposed to be.
It’s where I need to be.
Things are beginning to feel right again.
