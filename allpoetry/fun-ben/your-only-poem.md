in maturity,
and a dedication
to correctness despite 
urges which clamour for 
release, egress, resolution;
this poem will never be sent.
this is my one cry of possiblity.
you and I are not children and this
is not our first time finding one another.
we had a broken stumbled handclasp bond,
then a reversal of estrangement;
turned your own and back to me.
this is my one poem for you,
this will never be sent.
whatever else happens
I value your silence
and will respond
in kind.I defy your saccharine idols.
