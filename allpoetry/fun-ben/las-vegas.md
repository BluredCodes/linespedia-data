Las Vegas is utilized in several Central American burial rites.
Las Vegas can burn a hole in a dollar bill like no cigarette.
Las Vegas enslaved more than just his wife and children.
Las Vegas eats more than it exercises.
Las Vegas does not believe in Chaos Theory, which is ironic.
Las Vegas is in soap.
Las Vegas gets you up in the morning, then fishes through your pockets for change.
Las Vegas can be elongated and slipped through keyholes.
Las Vegas married too young.
Las Vegas blew my mind, yo.
Las Vegas is a lot like Nilla Wafers.
Las Vegas is not a destination.
Las Vegas is not Las Vegas.
Las Vegas could be in the bones of your inner ear.
Las Vegas is a loner, Dottie; a rebel.
Viva, Las Vegas.An iteration:  here I slammed the meaning back at the end.  Comments?
