title: "Schnitzel"
writer: "Fun Ben"

Schnitzel is tubular and extensive.

Schnitzel has been approved by the American Association of Dentists.

Schnitzel has three illegitmate children.

Schnitzel used to be called "hairbrush," before there were hairbrushes.

Schnitzel is aware of your deformity.

Schnitzel found a stray dog, today.

Schnitzel once flew from the top of Buckingham Palace.

Schnitzel is taking a day to reassess.

Schnitzel can fit in the space at the tip of your shoe, 

...................if you are a size eight, but wear a size nine.

Schnitzel was discovered, then lost, then discovered again.

Schnitzel is spelled 'wheelbarrow.'

Schnitzel helps aging veterans.

Schnitzel can be used for good and for evil.

Schnitzel has a button on it that doesn't do anything.

Schnitzel was a gift from God in the late seventies.

Schnitzel lines the cases of expensive cigars.

Schnitzel climbed a tree this Wednesday and could not get down, 

...................but the fire department didn't come until today.

Schnitzel was the real reason for Lincoln's assassination.

Lincoln was a good man, but very bony.experimenting.
