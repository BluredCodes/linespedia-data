Nilla Wafers have been calling here for you all week.
Nilla Wafers left telltale clues, but in the end walked free.
Nilla Wafers have been found measuring several stories high.
Nilla Wafers are the little discs that are in your blood but do nothing.
Nilla Wafers rock loud and hard.
Nilla Wafers are wasting their life on that scumbag, Brian.
Nilla Wafers have a wide circulation 
...................amongst the disaffected of Rhode Island.
Nilla Wafers will stop you if you try to touch them.
Nilla Wafers are products of a clash 
...................between your children’s overactive imagination, 
......................................and your overactive realism.
Nilla Wafers opened on Eighth Avenue in 1950. 
Nilla Wafers bring home the bacon.
Nilla Wafers open horizontally, and read left to right.
Nilla Wafers have been known to scurry 
...................when the lights are turned on in the kitchen.
Nilla Wafers congregate in woodland glens.
Nilla Wafers will reap the whirlwind.
