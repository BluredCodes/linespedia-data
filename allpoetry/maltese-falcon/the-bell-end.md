title: "The Bell End"

It is Sunday Morning,
Ding-Dong,
On the day of rest,
Ding-Dong,
For Sunday lunch.
Ding-Dong,
In Your Sunday Best,
Ding-Dong,
I want to lie in,
Ding-Dong,
But i'm driven 'round the bend,
Ding-Dong,
Please call the church,
Ding-Dong,
And make the ringing bell end.
