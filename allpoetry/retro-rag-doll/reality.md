---
title: "Reality"
writer: "retro rag doll"

Reality's too real for me,
but someday soon, just wait and see,
what's real to me will be all that will be.
That's all that I need, 
and then maybe I'll lead
you to see it too.
so that what's real to you,
will actually be,
what was in the beggining real to me.
