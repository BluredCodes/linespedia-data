---
title: "The Love I Want"
writer: "retro rag doll"
slug: "the-love-i-want"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59585-The-Love-I-Want-by-retro-rag-doll"
---

The love I want's the kind you find,
in some love story, so divine,
it lives in the hearts and dreams of me,
and maybe someday this love I will see.
It's unlikely I'll ever see such love,
which seems to come from up above,
the fact I'm just a wannabe,
of people who live in love stories.
I have a so-so love life,
full of pain, and full of strife,
truth is I never had the time,
so now I speak 'bout love in this rhyme,
I want a relationship that needs less work,
one with a man that's never a jerk,
one that let's me be myself,
and doesn't worry about my health.
He'd be so kind and sweet to me,
but in my heart he'll always be,
'cause the relationship I want is,
possibly, impossible.....ok, I'm having a bad day and this guy I like began raving about this girl he's in love with..... she lives on the other side of the country..... I just don't get it..... but I'll let him go I guess.... he never did get the fact that I liked him.....
