Give me a pen and a paper,
and I'll draw you a beutiful scene,
of the handsomest knight and his lady,
and of places which never have been.

Give me the right inspiration,
and in turn I will pen,
the magics that can live forever,
and continue to write them again.

Give me a reason to love you,
and in turn I will give love to you,
turn an walk away right now,
and in turn I will feel very blue.

Give me a reason to hate life,
and any reason will do,
and I will attempt to kill myself,
all because you told me to.

Give me a pen and some paper,
and I'll write you the story of my life,
of the sadness that being me feels like,
