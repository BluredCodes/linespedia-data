Love's Castles

our love was like a fantasy
a beautiful castle upon a sundrenched shore
first there was nothing
then from the ground up the foundations were laid
slowly but surely we took form
it was safe...sound....and wonderful
then the walls came up
the tide came and went
grain by grain we disintergrated
'til no evidence of our love remained

By Ginny
