Dad,I remember your unsmiling face
Grim look and thoughtful countenence;
As a growing kid,
It frightened me no end!

"Whop",school-teacher's slap landed on my cheek,
Punishment for indulging in mischief.
When you heard this
I saw fire in your eyes.

Bed-ridden with fever and cough,
At midnight,I woke up
To see you sitting by the bedside,
Fondly stroking my forehead.

Now,in middle age,I recollect
The love you would have felt
Behind the facade of toughness,
Towards your son!Walking down memory lane,I remember: Our Father,
Sri.K.M.C.GANESAN was a strict disciplinarian.  Whatever he
did,was only for the welfare of his children.  As kids,we 
were afraid of him and there was a big communication gap!
Father died at the age of 62,during 1989.  Now as a middle-aged man,I recollect his actions and see only love and
affection behind everything he did.
