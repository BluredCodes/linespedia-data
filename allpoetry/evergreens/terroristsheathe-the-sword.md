Terrorism, is a product of ignorance
Poverty,frustration and laziness!
Terrorist is a pawn in the hands
Of greedy and insensitive thugs-
Masquerading as leaders!

You could intimidate others
by sheer violence;
If you have an iota of wisdom,
Will realise the extent of hatred
Generated by your deeds,oh terrorist!

God alone has the power
To create or destroy:
One who defies this dictum
Will be the next victim!
Sheathe the sword,holster the gun.

Want to win the opponent over?
Negotiating table is the best arena;
Sit down and talk it out.
Ambience of give-and-take
Will result in lasting peace!Terrorism is seen wide-spread all over the world.

Selfishness and short-sightedness of leaders,pave the way

for this despiceable growth.  What is the remedy?

I've tried to find it out.
