i sat poised and i felt your fingertips tapping gently on my knee
i coyly smiled as your warm palm slid gently along my thigh
'this play isnt so bad' i thought

i held my breath as your thumb carressed my skin
i inhailed in subtle satisfaction as your hand slid further and further up
'this play isnt so bad at all" i thought

my breath quickened as your fingers moved skillfully and knowingly
i exhaled with a sigh of release and your soft lips kissed my ear
(applause) 'what did i miss?'i was inspired by one of birchwood's poems that was written from the challenge: write about the most incredible place you have been intimate with your lover.
