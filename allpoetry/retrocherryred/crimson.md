a single drop
 of crimson blood
  fell to its perish
   tumbled to the ground

more drops scatter
 as it hits the dirt
  a blood curtling scream
   peirced the ears of the unexpecting

a shot of terror flashed in those eyes
 those thousand eyes
  looked worriedly around
   no trace from where it came

except a single drop
 of crimson blood
  that fell to its perish
   tumbled to the groundi wrote this when i was 14.
