Do you know what it's like
not to matter
to the person that matters most?

do you know what its like
not to care about her?
she loves you.

do you know what its like
to be daughter with a loving father?
i don't

do you know what its like 
to turn away from anyone like him?
i hate men

do you know what its like
to love the touch of her lips
just because of what she's not?I wrote this when i was still a very confused about my sexuality, i am still confused just not as confused...because i've decided not to use labels or the current ideas of what sexuality is.
