the playing of bells
is heard in his cell
he tries not to dwell
only time will tell

he cant remember if he killed her
after he filled her
her scream was shriller
he tried to still her

he hears the bells
and sees where she fell
where he knelt
and undid his belt

no matter how he tries
he cant remember why
as he looked in her eyes
he wanted her to die

that young innocent face
he first wanted to embrace
his killing thoughts he cannot trace
it wasn't much of a chase

she wasn't yet 11
and on her way to heaven
because of a man named kevin
who'll be free by '27

he hears the bells
and sees where she fell
where he knelt
and undid his belt

no matter how he tries
he cant remember why
as he looked in her eyes
he wanted her to die

and on her way to heaven
because of a man named kevin
