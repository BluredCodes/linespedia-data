in the shade i hide
under a dark tree
in an evil forest

avoiding the world
i remain in a dream
trying to deny reality

afraid of the truth
terrified by fear
i cower alone

confused in a lie
denying the truth
living with no light

crying at laughter
laughing at death
contradicting every mood

turning from the crowd
ignoring existence
dancing in an endless shadow of lightthis is another that i wrote when i was 14
