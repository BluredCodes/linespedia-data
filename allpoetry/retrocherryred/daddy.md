im sorry i dont get straight A's dad
and i sorry that i dye my hair

im sorry im not as skinny as my sister
yes dad im sorry im not perfect for you

im sorry that you cant brag about your daughter
you'll have to keep bragging about your son
forget about your three daughters
they failed
when they were born as girls

im sorry that i pierced my nose dad
and im sorry that i dont like chemistry

yes and im sorry that i like girls dad
im sorry that you cant brag about me

but daddy
most of all
im sorry
that i was born a girl

im sorry
so sorry
that im not what you want me to be
im sorry 
that you cant find it in your heart to love me
