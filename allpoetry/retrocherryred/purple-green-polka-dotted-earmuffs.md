to my earmuffs:

its pretty tough
living without my earmuffs
stolen by a ninth grader and stuff
hope he's not buff

cause im gonna kick his ass
and if he gives me any sass
he better run really fast
cause ill put him in a full body cast

and then ill dash
with all his cash
and his stash
perhaps his pink sash

more importantly i'll have my muffs
his family might be sad but tough
he forced me to get rough
probly thought it was a bluff

i'll have my prize
and he'll be circumsized
i wont have to say "i hope he dies"
even though he's twice my size

but oh can i live another day
oh what prices i would pay
what words i could say
for hours i could lay

with my earmuffs oh so fuzzy
whose theif is probly pretty scuzzy
and ill make him give me all his money
even if i have to punch his tummy

i must thank him for showing me who my true love is after all
"it is better to have loved and lost than never to have loved at all" (shakespeare)
but the louder with his pleading voice he calls
the harder ill crash his head against the wallthis is based on a true story....just the  part about my purple green polka dotted earmuffs being stolen by a ninth grader and stuff.....and yes i was devistated.
