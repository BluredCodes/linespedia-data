it hurts me

that i could be fooled so easily

by your plastic sincerity

by your strategically placed tears for me



why do you cage yourself up

in high heels and make up?

your diet smoothie needs to be shaken up

why dont you see yourself for who you are wake up

see all the time that trying to be the same takes up



i dont know about you

but i need to fly

i have no acrylic tears to cry

now i see through

that cut and paste smile

i want to shatter you denial



why do you mask your face

just once leave the house without your make-up case

he doesnt love you because your bra is lace or because of the way your edible body oil tastes



just give up the act

because he'll always love the fact

that you love him back

not that you have a prada backpack
