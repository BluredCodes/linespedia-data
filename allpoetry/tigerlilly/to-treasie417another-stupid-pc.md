With pc pangs, I sympathize,
Inside my beast too…
A dark soul lies.

It bytes me each time
I sign on, if I complain
Ram is gone…

Amnesia mechanics
It maintains, within
It’s evil c-drive brain.
Tormenting me…
With message errors,
Can’t reach support
To report the terrors.

I run the wizards
And utilities,
All in vain…
No problems to see.

Nothing to fix
It does infer…
That the problem
Lies with me…???

So then I refer
To books and guides,
The stakes are high,
All success rides
On my ability
To translate,
Pc Greek…
I hesitate.

And it’s then I know
I’ve lost the battle,
When windows
Loads with a death rattle.

Such as it is,
This is my fate,
No pc whiz am I.

I curse and swear at
This beastie’s whim
Reach for the phone
Instead, 
to talk with him…
