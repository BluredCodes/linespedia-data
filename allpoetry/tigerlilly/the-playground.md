...O
....W
......N   the slide, feet hit the ground

rrrruuunnn to the MERRY – GO – ROUND…

wait your turn, 
and after that…

at S
///// W
\\\\\\\\ I
////////// N
\\\\\\\\\\\\ G
////////////// See the treetops as you fly

a child’s spring day goes by…all comments and critiques welcomed
