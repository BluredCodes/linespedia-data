Dawn and Dusk
Find me alone as I watch
Lapping tides
Lift a twisted piece of driftwood
Caught there for a time.
 
One snag 
Secures a portion in wet sand,
While moving waters invite the rest,
Encouraging strange voyages 
To
Uncharted lands.

High noon finds the branch…
Covered with an aqueous weight,
Swaying with the undercurrent…
Dreaming maybe
Of some place to rest with creatures, 
All exotic,
In some rockbound tidal pool.

Midnight
Moonbeams lancing through the depths,
Enhancing visions… 
Freeing snag, 
Letting go the sand…
Yet 
Knowing that with the sun’s returning
I’ll still find this piece
Of driftwood
Turning 
Aimlessly in the sea of life.all comments and critiques welcomed
