THE MOONLIGHT WAS BEAUTIFUL TONIGHT
OUT OVER THE OCEAN
WHERE THE BOATS LIT UP
THE WATERS WERE CALM
THE BIG FULL MOON
TOWARDS END OF JANUARY
THE MOONLIGHT WAS MINE
IT COULD BRIGHTEN UP A DREAM
OF A GLAMOROUS, GORGEOUS NIGHT
THE MOONLIGHT WAS MINE
IN THE DEEP SHADOWS
NOT A FIGURE,BUT OF A MAN
THE WATERS BECAME RIPPLES
OUT OVER THE OCEAN
THE FULL MOON DISAPPEARED
THE MOONLIGHT WAS MINE 
UNTIL HE TOOK IT ALL AWAY
THE MOONLIGHT WAS OURS TO SHARE
HE DROWNS ME IN FEAR
NO LONGER A MAN
OUT OVER THE OCEAN
IN SEARCH FOR ANSWERS
NO ONE IS THERE
ONLY A DARK SHADOW OF A MAN
THE MOONLIGHT WAS COLD
