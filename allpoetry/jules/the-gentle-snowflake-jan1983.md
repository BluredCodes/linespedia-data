title: "The gentle snowflake       (jan.1983)"
writer: "jules"
slug: "the-gentle-snowflake-jan1983"

EARLY THIS MORNING THE SNOW FELL
SO GENTLY,SO SOFTLY
THE SHINY GLITTER
SPARKLING BACK AT ME
IT TOUCHED MY HAND
LIKE A GLAMOUROUS DIAMOND
SO DELICATE SO FRAGILE
THE PRECIOUSNESS I HOLD 
SLOWLY IT MELTS AWAY
I CAN SEE OTHERS ALONG THE WAY
GENTLY FALLING UPON THE GROUND
SOON IT COVERS THE EARTH
SUCH A BEAUTY TO SEE AND TOUCH
ESPECIALLY WHEN AT THE END OF THE DAY
AS THE PURPLE SKY MEETS THE SUNSET
SLOWLY FADING FROM THE DISTANCE
PILES OF DIAMONDS IT SEEMS 
SPARKLING BACK AT ME
THE TOUCH NO LONGER A TOUCH
MILES AND MILES AWAY
THE EARTH IS COVERED IN A WHITE BLANKET
KEEPING MYSELF SECURE AND WARM
THIS GENTLE SNOW THAT FELL TODAY
TOO PRECIOUS TO JUST LET IT GO
HOLD ONTO THE DREAM
IT TOUCHED MY HEART
I KNOW IT WOULD TOUCH YOURS
REACH OUT FOR THE GENTLENESS
TAKE THE LOVELINESS
CHERISH THE DELICATE TREASURE
ON SUCH A BEAUTIFUL MORNING
AS THE SNOW FELL
SO GENTLY, SO SOFTLY
AND KEEP IT CLOSE TO YOUR HEART
AS IT TOUCHED MY HEART
SO GENTLY,SO SOFTLY
