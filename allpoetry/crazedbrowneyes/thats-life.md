title: "That's Life"

There isn't a thought that hasn't been thunk,
There isn't a ship that won't be sunk,
There isn't a word that can't be spoke,
There isn't a heart that can't be broke,
There isn't a lie that can't be told,
There isn't a thing to be but bold,
There isn't a life that can't be saved,
There isn't a thing that can't be made,
There isn't a world wich can be true,
But, well, that's life, it's up to you.
