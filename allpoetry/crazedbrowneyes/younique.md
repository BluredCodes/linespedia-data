You are YOUnique, 
That's right YOUnique.
So you drive that popular car,
and wear those big brand name jeans,
and listen to the same music,
as everybody else in this life does.
But still you are YOUnique.
I say YOUnique instead of unique beacuse of this:
Not a person on this earth will see thing the way you see them,
think the way you think,
express themselves in the same manner as you,
no one is like you.
Your perspective on life is different,
from me or him.
And that makes you YOUnique.okay, not exactly poetry...... but hope you like it..... I made up the word...... he he
