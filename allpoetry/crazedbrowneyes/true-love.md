title: "True Love"

This is true love,
and I can tell,
from the kiss of a dove,
by the wishing well,
and the way I hated you,
but not at all,
to the way I knew 
I was starting to fall.

When we speak of a kiss,
the perfect one yes,
in my mind I imagine,
that yours are the best,
and that moment in time's,
better than all the rest.

This is true love,
and I can tell,
before there was you,
my life was a hell,
nobody to confide too,
no inspiration on which to dwell.

I'll love you forever,
and that's a fact,
and no one can ever,
say more than that.This is nothing special, no specific guy this goes out to, but I do dedicate this to the many authors of love stories that touch our hearts and make us wish and dream, maybe someday your magic will come through to reality for some lucky one of us on the outside...... but I digress.... I hope you liked it.
