---
title: "If I'm Gone"
writer: "CrazedBrownEyes"
slug: "if-im-gone"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56448-If-Im-Gone-by-CrazedBrownEyes"
---

You won't miss me if I'm gone,
please correct me if I'm wrong.
Even as I start to leave,
to this home my heart will cleave.
But still I will just go away,
unless you convince me to stay.
Your life goes on after I go.
if you say stay I'll just say no.
as life goes on my memory will lessen,
just as your dearest childhood posessions.
You won't miss me if I'm gone,
I know I'm right, it's YOU who's wrong.will be explained in time.....
