'Tis words that make the world go round.
'Tis words that let the heart be found.
'Tis words we use to decribe snow,
or those feelings we wish to show.
'Tis words that make this poem up,
and words we use like eat and sup.
'Tis words that let us question WHY?,
and words to answer in reply.
'Tis words that make the world go 'round,
to words we are forever bound.
