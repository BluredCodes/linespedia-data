My Jesus is my true friend
I can talk to him about anything
My Jesus loves me and forgives me
No matter what I do
My Jesus cradles me when I'm scared
And helps me to overcome my fears
My Jesus died on the cross
Rose on the third day
Set me free 
And He lives forever.
