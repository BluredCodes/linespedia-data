If I Had Been At the Cross

If I had been at the cross
I would have twitched at every crack of a whip
My eyes would have become blurred
From the tears that poured down my face
Sometimes I would have had to turn my head
Away from the unbearable sight
I would have cried so hard for so long
Until my stomach ached
Weakened from crying 
I would have fallen to the ground
And buried my face in my hands
But I would have pulled my hands away from my face
And the tears would have stopped
As Jesus cried out to God,
"Father please forgive them for they know not what they do."
Astounded by his words
I would have buried my face in my hands again
And start to weep some more
As I would have heard him gasping for his last breaths
I would have noticed myself struggling too
And as Jesus hung there
Life-less on the cross
I would have found myself to be one of the last people to leave
The death eaten scene
But I know that I would have fully understood the concept of 
That pain
That suffering
That blood 
All that was for me
