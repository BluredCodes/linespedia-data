I saw Jesus hanging on the cross
And I cried
I saw Him suffer for me
And I cried harder
I heard Him gasping for his last breaths
And I almost fell to my knees
I saw Jesus die before my eyes
And I reluctantly walked away 
Wiping the tears from my face
When He arose the third day
I cried tears of joy
Because I knew He would live
