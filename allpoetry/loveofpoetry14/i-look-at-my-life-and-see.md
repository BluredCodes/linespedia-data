title: "I Look At My Life and See"
writer: "loveofpoetry14"

I look at my life and I see 
That there are some things I need to work out
And I look at my life and I see
That I'm strong in the Lord, no doubt
And because of that
I look at my life and I see
That it's just the way it should be
