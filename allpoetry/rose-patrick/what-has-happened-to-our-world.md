There was a little world, 
A pretty little world… 
It had not a care… 
It was such a nice little world… 

Then the gods, 
Decided to have a little war. 
And now our pretty little world, 
Has to pick which side it wants. 
It’s inhabitants have the choice between 
The side of all things that are of good. 
Or the side of all things that are dark. 

This pretty little world is now in a mess. 
Woe to all the people, 
That live on this pretty little world. 
For your sprits are the prize… 

Old Sprit’s and new sprit’s, 
Awaken to what is happening. 
For you are to fight for the peace, 
Of your immortal sprit’s… 

We can join each other in this, 
With a fight for the good of all… 
Or we can stay with our head in the sand, 
Only to be given to the dark side, 
And the evil one’s pleasure… 
So the choice is yours…. 
Do we fight and make it better, 
And make our pretty little world, 
A good place again for all, 
Are do we just give it up, 
With out a fight, 
And let the enemy win?
