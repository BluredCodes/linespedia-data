title: "What is Reality?"

We are told, 
About reality. 
We are told, 
What is real and what is not. 
We are told 
Things as a small child 
We are told 
There is no boogie man 
We are told 
That it is just our imagination 
We are told 
Things have an order to them 

So what is it, 
When things move by themselves? 
So what is it, 
When it seems to be a knock on the wall? 
So what is it, 
When it seems to be someone is there, who is not? 
So what is it, 
When you can smell something awful, 
But there is no reason? 

That something moving by itself, 
Was just our imagination. 
That knock we heard 
Was just the house settling 
Or a tree branch hitting the house. 
That feeling of someone there 
Was just the light playing tricks on our eyes 
That awful smell 
Was just the factory across town… 

We are also told 
To be good, if not, something bad will happen to us 
We are also told 
That the devil is going to get us if we don’t be good 
We are also told 
That if we don’t be good when we die we will go to hell 
We are also told 
When something good happens, it was the will of God. 
We are also told 
When something bad happens, it was also the will of God. 
We are told 
That mankind has been fighting the battle 
Of good and evil, since the beginning of time. 

What is reality? 
We know what we saw, felt, and heard… 
We know we didn’t get the straight truth. 

It is time to set things straight. 
We are going into battle. 
We must be ready for anything. 
Whether or not we think it’s real. 
When we fight the Devil 
We must be ready to fight the way he does 
And that is down and dirty.
