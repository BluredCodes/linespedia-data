title: "The Time of Red Days"

The time has arrived, 
And now that it has, 
We will either learn the path of truth and light, 
Or we will fall 
Into the path of the Red Days… 
Oh sweet people, of this world, awaken! 

Awaken before it is too late, 
For the Red Days are here… 

In this time, there will be no in-between, 
You will either awaken to the truth of love, 
Light, and goodness born in all mankind, 
Or you will be lost to the Evil, 
And the darkness will consume you, 
Where there will be only helplessness and despair… 

Oh rise to the voices of the people 
Who are calling for you to be ready, 
With their messages of hope, 
For the Red Days are here, 
Now… 

Arise good people, 
And awaken to the battle 
That is at hand, 
Don’t be caught on the wrong path, 
Stay in the light…
