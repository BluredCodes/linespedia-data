What is a spell? 
Some say it is a rhyme, 
Some sat it is a verse. 

What is a spell ? 
Is it good? 
Is it bad? 

What is a spell? 
Is it just words? 
Is there meaning  in it? 

What is a spell? 
What good or they? 
What kind are they? 

What is a spell? 
Who uses them? 
Who makes them? 

What is a spell? 
What good are they? 
What use are they? 

What is a Spell? 
Is it white magic? 
Is it black magic? 

What is a Spell? 
It is what ever, 
The person wants it to be… 

So it maybe good, 
So it maybe bad, 
So make it what you want, 
To happen…

 
BLESS BE
