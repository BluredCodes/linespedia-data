title: "Just Like a Flower"

Love is like a flower. 
A flower starts as a seed. 
Love also, starts with the first meeting. 

As a flower grows in time, 
Love also grows… 
As long as it is being care for, 
It can become great beauty, 
Just like a flower… 

If love is cared for, 
It grows and blossoms. 
Moreover, with each year, 
Its beauty becomes even more intense. 
Just like a flower. 

If a flower is not nurture properly can wither and die 
so is it with love if love is not work at to be make strong. 
Just like a flower, You will never see its beauty…
