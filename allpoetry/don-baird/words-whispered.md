*
*
*
to you my sweet 
I give a rose from my garden of peace and love

and I dare 
paint a colorful card of hearts and kisses

revealing 
how lovingly my heart beats just at my thought of you

words whispered
in the secrecy of a thousand flowers express the depth of my feelings

so special
you are the everything love for me my completion my other half

a dream
that has its reality based deeply within my soul

I reach out
to offer my internal kingdom as a dwelling place for our love

hoping
deeply that what I have written here is a mirror of your feelings as well

and ask
for a moment of your time under the stars to say so perfectly again 
I Love You
*
