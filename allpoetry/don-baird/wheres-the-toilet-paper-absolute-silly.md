sitting on a toilet waiting for my duty

come on, come on let's shake this booty



reading a magazine and hoping for the best

when in the heck am I gonna see the rest



looking for tissue of course it's gone

I'll be here now until it's fricking dawn



anybody there to help me out

I guess I'll sit here and simply pout



well I don't really need my underwear

what do you think should I not or should I dare



sliding them off and folding them well

I assure you this experience no one will I tell



wiping and wiping until I am clean and squeaky

fastidiously caring to the very last leaky



thank goodness I'm done and ready to leave

it remains that no toilet paper is still my pet peeve
