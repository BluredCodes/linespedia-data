*
*
*
lying next to you
holding you so close
I sense the gentle heat of your skin
and the dreamy beat of your heart

I love your fingers running my hair
and the sound of your breath in my ear

this rich moment between us is becoming more intense
as tonight our forever will be shared with a single kiss

what tenderness you have for me my love
as your lips and tongue glide upward from my neck
I remember the many enchanted times we’ve enjoyed
filled with incredible intimacy and exotic tantric moments
   
your inviting vanilla cream radiance is luring and sweet
beckoning me to taste your body’s delicate flavors
  
I trace your jaw with my more than willing tongue
while your gentle lips are in anticipation of a special moment

I’m sensing you’re body becoming weak and fatigued
I know the truth of tomorrow and the emptiness it will possess 
and when tonight passes by our kiss will be gone forever
as this journey to be is for you and you alone

but for the moment we’re in one another’s arms bonded
our mouths remain passionate and search their way

nervous anticipation of this one final kiss to come
so deep in love and yet we’re not meant to be

just now as our lips so very lovingly touch
we discover a wonderful moisture already present
while our tongues tangle together gliding smoothly
we lick the erotic heat of each other’s breath 

shifting closer and pressing tighter together
I feel your tongue move more intensely and deeper

it’s clear that we both know destiny’s dark intention
but our fiery passion has crashed through taking us over

then the clock struck an inevitable tone
and your body went completely limp
a calmness suddenly overcame us both
yet our mouths remained pressed tightly together

In a stillness profound I felt your final breath 
and your soul slipped through my mouth into our heart

this one perfect kiss we shared
was now the most you could ever giveEven in a final moment..... there must be time for a Perfect Kiss.
