---
title: "And I melt"
writer: "Soulslulluby"
slug: "and-i-melt"
source: "AllPoetry"

As your brown eyes penetrate mine
Desire runs through my veins
My breathing grows heavy
I melt into you

Your warm touch raises my senses
I’m speechless with your kisses
Taken into your arms
I melt into you

Laying next to you as we talk
Holding my hand in yours
Aware of your cologne
I melt into you

Staring up at your bright brown eyes
And smiling sweetly at you
You smile back at me
I melt into you

Thinking about you day and night
Awaiting our next encounter
Waiting for that moment
I melt into you

Butterflies visit my tummy
Any time I think of you
When your name is mentioned
I melt into you

As you wrap your arms around me
Tickling me by surprise
I squirm and laugh out loud
I melt into you

Listening to sappy chick songs
I dance alone in my room
Imagining you are here
I melt into you

When you are asleep I whisper
Soft words of adoration
Stroking your soft red hair
I melt into you

I am happy for no reason
I smile when I’m alone
I giggle in the dark
I melt into you
