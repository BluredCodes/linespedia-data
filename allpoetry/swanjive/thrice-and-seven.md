title: "Thrice and seven"

Earthen: fabric whirling you resemble
Orbs: revolving, vacuum celestial.
Fortune: Thrice and seven times around spins  
Wheel: sphinx, serpent, Anubis, Sapiens.

Above: score luminary written that
Below: our dancing feet tread in rhythmic. 
Alphabet: verse sacrosanct combined gives
Words: who sing stones to move perpetual.
Lyre: of seven spheres splash on fingers, these
Threads: reverberate continuously.This poem is based on the Tarot card The Wheel of Fortune. It is a form of ten lines with ten syllables per line.
