Way back in the republic 
I left my farm behind 
Went to join the legion 
And stepped in well-tuned line 
With the pounding of the onagers 
beating out the time. 

covering fifty miles of ground 
lugging a sixty pound pack around 
Though we will not grow weary 
Upon our journey long 
While our ears are ringing 
With this Fascist marching song: 

We will march into your village 
We will ravage and pillage 
We will lay siege to your town 
Head of gold on feet of clay 
We will march until our days are done. 

'et sic transit 
Gloria mundi 
ubi pecunia regnet 
Populi sunt servi 
In hoc signo vinces 
Fors bellique omnia vincit'My bad Latin:  
"and so passes the Glory of the world. Where riches are allowed to rule, the people are slaves. By this sign ye shall be victorious. The force of war conquers all." An onager was a very large catapult.
