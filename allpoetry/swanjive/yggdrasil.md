Well Thats the story
'bout how it's all endin'
'bout that's the story
birth of the Yggdrasil tree
growing out cracks withered 
where be people inside
Low thy sound goes
down when Down wind
Where stands i
float eye of the deep within
Metal wood dirt flame air
is where Far from slikckening 
sweat of animals
and brine bashing
brocken-bracken footbound
pyth of centuries.The Yggdrasil tree is the universal tree in norse mythology that connects the three worlds. it protected the last of the human race after Ragna Rok when the Gods fought the giants for supremacy..
