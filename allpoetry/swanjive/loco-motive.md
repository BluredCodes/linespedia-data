Sam crack them cans
Your voice'll smash 'em to trash!
Some goofed to wig city driverman
must have knocked the breakman a benny tea
'Cause I can see  you ain't had a cowcatcher before
And those ironshod dogs click-clack on the train track like no before daddy ever did. with a pulse like mine
 keepin' time keepin'  time machine pace can't replace a heartbeat your wheels devour  amd burn this body, saguine coal to reach your goal 
cuts a trainwide valley through the center of the senses

(Wish you were a heap of color on the strand
Water flowing in and out . rivulets material corporeal.
Cover me oh salt brine and sandy eyed I will return}

and kicks cans down a tin cave mouth!
singing the delicate pearly swirl of a goddamm bottlecap!
