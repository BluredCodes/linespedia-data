Daddy please stop hurting me.
Mommy wont dont you stick up for me, please dont turn your head again.

Daddy please stop touching me, i may be young but i know this isnt right.
Mommy i know you see what goes on why cant you just leave him.

Daddy dont you touch my little brother, I'd rather you touch me.
Mommy please this will fuck him up for life open your mouth god dammit.

Daddy please stop drinking.
Mommy dont enable him anymore.

Daddy please come home at night.
Mommy dont worry he'll be fine, he will stumble in eventually.

Mommy daddy is bleeding very bad.
I know sweetheart daddy will no longer hurt you, how did this happen tho?
I'm sorry daddy if i was bad. I had to save them from you, i had to save myself from you.
