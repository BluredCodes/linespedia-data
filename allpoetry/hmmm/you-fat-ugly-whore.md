Your eyes sparkle like glistening pools of
    urine on a hot summer's day
Their shit brown color makes me want
    to flush you away.
"You ugly whore."
You've got so many rolls it makes me
    want to call you the pills
You eat so damn much you can hardly pay
    the bills.
"You fat whore."
I'm gonna kick you in the teeth if you
    don't put down that food
You overweight bitch you ever chug like
    a dude.
You need to buy some Slim Fast or go
    On the Jenny Craig plan
You Fat, Ugly, Whore, You look like a man.long time ago,
