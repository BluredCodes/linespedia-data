Cold tounge, pale and pink,
Cut it off, put it in the sink.
Look at the mess all over the floor,
Red and bloody; want some more?
So much gore, get the mop.
Dismembered youself, and the blood won't stop.
Getting weak, gonna black out;
So much pain, you scream and shout.
Crying out loud, lying down on your bed;
Close your eyes, you wake up dead.a long time ago,
