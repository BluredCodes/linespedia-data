Devil's wing fly high above
Red eyes glow, can you feel the love?
Don't say no! have your choice,
Speak your words, Feel your voice.
Devil's god a sober man,
Your anxious choice, you have a plan.
Never meant, I said no more,
Dacaying mind open door.a long time ago,
