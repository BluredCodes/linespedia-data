What the hell, why am I hear
Where are the memories that I hold
So dear?
Lost time out on a limb
Chances of survival looking slim
Hat, misery, fire and brimstone
Souls of the Devil bleed and morn.
get the picture this is real
Inside this is how I feel
Confused and running I dont know where to turn
Afraid to die but inliving I burn.short time ago,
