The gods are fighting
The clouds are wicked
The angles told me
I'm gonna die.
So I'm gonna go in the dark of night
The lights dim
And I don't know why.
The gods are fighting
The clouds are wicked
The angels told me
I'm gonna die.
Living is dying
Please believe me
And I dont know why.a long time ago,
