---
title: "By the Lake, Asleep"
writer: "Brazos"
slug: "by-the-lake-asleep"
source: "AllPoetry"

I awake with a start, aside the lake
It is early, the moon still shows
The air is misty, short sight can I take
Sun shines thru the mist, and it glows

I have slept against a tree, you see
Drunken man, no cares I do show
The ducks are running, round the sea
Do they quack to me? I don’t know

For I have been so drunk, for so long
That I am not sure that I am awake
The story of my life make’s a good song
But the end of it, is anyone’s take

But I am still alive today, here by the lake
And it seems like I have been here oft before
I reach for my bottle, my thirst to slake
But ‘tis empty, I “killed” it afore

To drunk to move, I now lay still
Waiting for whatever comes up                                                           Perhaps I’ll drown in my own swill
Perhaps I’ll sleep, and ne’er wake up

Wait, I see a man and woman
Standing betwixt me and the lake
He’s talking to me, is the man
Handing me money that I take

Says it’s from Jimmy, my lost son
Who I drove off with my drinkin’
Infantry now, he carries a gun
Across a desert, he’s slinking

I close my eyes, I see him now!
Huddling on the side of a hill
Jimmy, I will change, I do vow
But, unhearing, he remains still.

God save his soul, and God curse mine
He would be here if not for me
I lie by a lake in decline
He lies in Afghanistan, you see?3rd in a series "by the lake", the first being "Jimmy's Song". While this is fictional, it does reflect upon my life.
