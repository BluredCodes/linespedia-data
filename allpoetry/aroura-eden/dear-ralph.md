Noise
Rhythm
Throbbing hearts
Striking body parts

Pelt the climatic booms and dramatic tones

Twisted bodies
Popping thighs
Taught Caves
Erie eyes 

This commands for the dogs to follow

Flash
Blink
Stars shining
Beings perspiring

Then the sweet swig of heat

And continues our blood to boil in our guts
These nights are when our kidneys scream
No one hears, none can be felt
But our dear Ralph is always here to help
