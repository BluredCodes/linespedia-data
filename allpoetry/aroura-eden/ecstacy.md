Can you hear him whispering
He breath us all a different life
A life of malicious misery
He talks of things I desire
He spoons the lush of bitter fruit
I want him for my own
But he won’t have that 
He has other mistresses
I can already feel the jealousy bubbling and changing into some nasty disease
I am coveting my neighbor’s daughter

He’s so beautiful
He’s so exciting
He’s oh so full of danger
He's an addiction
I like that in a man

I know of his assailants
He could and will have me killed when he is through with me
But that’s not all 
He wants to melt me 
That’s his trade-mark
When he sleeps in my bed he always says he will melt my heart
That’s not all he’ll melt

But how can I leave him
He’s what I wake for every day
And the reason I sleep in late
He keeps me awake with vile things
Lustful and erotic fantasy
Moments of passion and pain
Blood and blister
Hate and greed

He holds me in with his voice
He pulls me back with his everlasting lies
He binds me in to sin
He makes me an irresistible temptress
The world wants me and accepts me when he is around
How can I turn away from him 
He’s made me a star*
He is ecstasyTrue, this guy is real, and I've got to dump him.  He's a real drag.  But I just can't seem to do that.  He's so up lifting, but he will always bring me down.
Roury
