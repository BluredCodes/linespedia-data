Drunk with a plan 
Gonna get canned
You think you’ll be loved for words
Binding my will 
Killing my skill
Been labeled a puppet of society
I’m branded a teen 
Its killing my dream
I’m not what they think I’ll be
You say yes, I oppose
Obviously no one knows
There exists two, you and I
If I open my mouth they’ll put us away
I will leave it un said, at least for today
I’ll leave you hidden for now
I wish you would leave
I’m getting some what peeved
Drowning here in this crap called confusion
Why must you put up residence here
Your causing me to build up fear
I’ll be rid of you soon ( I hope )
I will always love you 
I will always love you
We’ll stun them in the end
