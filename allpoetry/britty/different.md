If I wasn't born this way
Would my life be different?
Would people still call me "that word"?
Mutter it under their breaths
"That word" full of controversy
Would I be taken more seriously?
Would people see me as a humam?
Rather than a suspect?
No.
Probably not
Each race deals with its own stereotype
People often ridiculed for bringing up the past
How they were oppresed
Their long fight for independence
"Why bring that up?" they say
It's over with, right?
Wrong.
Many still fight for their respect everyday
It never ends
If we all just knew what it's like
To be different
Who knows
Maybe the world would be normal.
