title: "Scars"

Each one writes its own story
Essays of joy
Letters of pain
Whether it signifies your greatest win
Or your lowest defeat
They come and go like rain
When you least expect it
Some you try hard to show
Others you fight to hide
But don't fight that battle alone
No matter how hideous the scar may be
Over time it will heal
Remember
Scars don't last forever.this isn't a sad-like poem. but it's not completely happy. I have a scar from flying off my grandma's bed when i was 3.... although it hurt, it was one of the funnest things i've ever done. so, just think about it.
