---
title: "Goodbye"
writer: "Britty"
slug: "goodbye"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Each passing moment
I dread it more
Soon i'll never return
Through that door

I'll go away
And leave behind
All the things I worked
So hard to find

My life, my calling
The ones I truly love
My only guidance
Comes from above

It's hard for me
To face the fact
I'm going away
But i'll always look back

Putting friendships on hold
To you it's not fair
But no matter where I am
I'll always be there

My feelings towards this
My heart can't attain
I pretend nothing's coming
Just to ease the pain

My mind imagines things
To take the grief away
I want to speak of better things
But my mouth has nothing to say

That sad day will come
I'll try not to cry
The only left for me to say is...
Goodbye.*sigh*.... i'm moving.
