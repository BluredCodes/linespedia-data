Things seem a little different
Your world seems to be changing
Everything is going your way
The world is..... perfect

You always catch green lights
People actually hold the door for you
You don't have that annoying run in your panty hose
People actually respect you.

Your generosity is not taken for granted.
There's no longer a struggle to survive
Things seem too good to be true
This new agenda boggles the mind 

Overwhelmed from the perspiration of shock
You awaken in a cold sweat
Realizing that world you once knew
Was just a dream

You wipe the sweat from your face 
And lay back down to sleep
Only to dream of that wonderful place
