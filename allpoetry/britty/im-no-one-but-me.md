---
title: "I'm no one but me"
writer: "Britty"

Why do people feel the need
To tell me how to live?
I'm not a kid 
I can think for myself
Because i'm no one but me

I'm supposed to be
Some famous star
And be like others
But that serves no purpose
Because i'm no one but me

I can't be what everyone else wants
It's a burden in itself
"Why don't you play sports?" 
Well, i'll tell you why
Because i'm no one but me

Don't always expect
That because of my appearance
I will fit this looks persona
I'm trying my best to be apart from its usual
Because i'm no one but me

Many times I look in the mirror
And I don't like what I see
I wanted to change into "normal"
But I didn't
Because i'm no one but me

The rest of my life is MY choice
I want to prove the things i've been taught
Nothing or no one will stop me
I've set MY path
Because i'm no one but meThis is just about how people always tell me that cuz i'm tall i should do this, and do that.... it's really kept me from doing many things. Finally, i get to do what i want to do.
