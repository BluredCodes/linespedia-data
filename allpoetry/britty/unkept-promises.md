Unkept promises.
That's been our story so far
I remember how so many times
I looked forward to your visits
The smile on your face
The little squeak in my voice
You would pick me up 
And put me on your shoulders
I felt on top of the world
You always seemed to visit me at the right time
But that was then
This is now
You changed
All of a sudden 
I was of no significance to you at all
Just some monthly bill
Remember our annual Chuck E Cheese visit?
How could you forget
Of course you called me
"I'll be there at 3, babygirl. I promise.."
Yeah, right.
Of course back then, "daddy never lied"
So, I sat on the porch
In my little pigtails
With my packed Barbie nightbag
And my new pair of Keds on...
I sat there and waited...
And waited... and waited.
You never came.
"But daddy never lies!" I cried.
That broken promise has broken my heart for 8 years
Sometimes, I still sit on my porch waiting for you to come.
Trying to mend the bruise in my heart from your last 
Unkept promise.I haven't mentioned my dad like that in years... *sigh* tt hurts so much.
