Ever since I came in this worldI've had a room of my own So I guess I kind of got used to the ideaOf me always being aloneI'm always on the insideTrying to find my way outThere's no one to confide inBecause they don't know what lonely is aboutOccasionally there's an instanceWhen you have excitement to shareYou want to tell the person nearest youBut realize no one's thereIt's hard when you feel one wayBut to others you can't feel that sameThey have no earthly ideaHow much you are in painThere are some you can always count onWhenever you need them, they're aroundBut lately they're too busyWith the "other" people they've foundThey always seem to have someoneWith whom they can share their joyWhich leaves me in a predicamentOne that continues to annoyI've already been hurt onceAnother on the wayIt could happen to me tomorrowI wait here alone todayI can't help to wonderOf what my future will bringWith this situation nowMy fate could be anything
