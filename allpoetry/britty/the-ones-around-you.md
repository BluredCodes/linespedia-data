---
title: "The Ones Around You"
writer: "Britty"
slug: "the-ones-around-you"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54399-The-Ones-Around-You-by-Britty"
---

In this world
There are people I know
My friends
The ones with much love to show

We're very much different
We don't see color
We've laughed, cried
And grown with one another

But things have changed
There's a loss of pride
We are all hiding 
a secret feeling inside

Is it love?
Is it hate?
Is it a confused ponder?
Maybe joy,
Maybe envy,
My mind is filled with wonder

I look at us now
There's no unity at all
Although the group is big
We interact so small

I thought it was me
My expectations were long
Because at times I feel
Like I just don't belong

It wouldn't be a problem
If there wasn't so much anger
Some of my friends
Feel like complete strangers

I can deal with some of this
Here's the only place I want to be
But I can't deal with the lies....
Because that's just not me
