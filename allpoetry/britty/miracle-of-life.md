title: "Miracle of Life"

The time has come!
A birth of a new day
After nine long months
A new baby is on the way

Taking care of a child
Is something you can't ignore
I hope it's something
My aunt is ready for

I'm trying my best comfort her
It's very hard without a doubt
I hold her trembling hand
As she squeezes all the life out

I can see on her face
All the suffering and strife
This painful experience
Is one of the most beautiful things in life

The labor is over
I can't help but to shed a tear
I hold this little bundle of joy in my hands
My new nephew is finally here.My aunt's having her baby! This is what'll probably happen except w/ 10 times more chaos.
