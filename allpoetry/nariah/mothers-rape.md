---
title: "Mother's Rape"
writer: "Nariah"
slug: "mothers-rape"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58647-Mothers-Rape-by-Nariah"

Leaves under bare feet 
wet dirty cold 
and so outstretched thumbs 
of two innocent teenagers 

Goosebumps sharing leg space with short sexy cutoffs 
long thin pasty white 
ducking under tree branches 
looking desperate to passer-bys 

Red Ford pickup truck 
rusty loud shaking 
pulls over "where ya headed" 
soon I am crushed against our hero 

Radio crackling county music 
loud sad droning 
energy in the cab changes 
as he makes a wrong turn 

Dirt road to nowhere familiar 
lonely dark winding 
into a hidden driveway 
truck idles briefly, stops 

Trailer parked on concrete squares 
small musty deserted 
"come on in for a few" insistent 
my head shaking a concerned "no" 

Brown leather belt 
cracking heavy unbuckled 
slams against my lap 
pocket knife unto the floor 

Hand grips the knife 
battered soiled gently 
as if not to scare me 
"come inside" again he shouts 

Four young thighs 
sticky weak naked 
peel off the seat 
march unwillingly inside 

Three trailer steps 
metallic loose grimy 
graced with four young calloused feet 
and two cowboy boots behind them 

"Sit down" his voice 
gentle excited demanding 
we do as we are told 
our hero begins undressing 

Stained white underwear 
tight sweaty fragrant 
hugs one enlarged penis 
both of us are crying 

"Who first" end of a beer can hollers 
echoing hollow laughing 
we hold tight to one another 
he chooses her at random 

Face of my beloved 
red withdrawn resigned 
rises to her feet 
at his request undressing 

Her body thrown on the kitchen table 
bruised limp naked 
I can feel her soul join mine 
outside our bodies watching 

A pause too short to notice 
crisp breathless divine 
through with her, not finished 
I assume my friend's position 

Hand grasping my breast 
pale confused hurting 
I feel it ripping off my body 
he squeezes as he comes 

Grey smelly loveseat 
respite comfort overstuffed 
scramble to put our clothes on 
watching hero's every move 

Four young thighs 
feeble shaking dirty 
stumble down three steps 
seek safety in the woods 

Two young souls 
damaged wandering heartful 
pass these wounds unto their daughters 
who are born twice as strong
