---
title: "Child Support"
writer: "Nariah"
slug: "child-support"

I am becoming aware
of the time warp I must have been
a part of for-
who knows-
over an hour now. 
The walls in the place are
ridgid and hard marble 
like my stomach before
his sperm ever entered my body 
"just a bump in the road" 
the words from his lawyer, 
an old family friend 
defending
his parents' hard earned
$25 a month. 
He sits too 
new clothes, 
pierced, 
perfect child 
capitalist 
dead beat 
trench coat to dress coat 
porn star 
defended 
ever-protected. 
Angry pacifist 
I cling to my being
my mother magic 
my truth in blue jeans 
Brown vs. Bernagozzi 
we enter the courtroom. 
Then triumphantly 
$25 a month awarded 
temporarily 
for my baby 
my motherwork 
and court ajurned.
