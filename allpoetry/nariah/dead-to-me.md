..



car door slammed
stopped briefly at the light
our last exchange 
echoed

I told you my plan of abandonment
how I was no longer
     wanted you
     needed you
how I wished you
     D
      E
       A
        D
and how already you're dead to me

I watched your still sunken face
puff up
and a few heavy tears 
washed away streaks of fake color

Now your skeleton slips away
running-
stressing pneumonia-filled lungs
to catch the transit
and avoid hitchhiking
away from your neglectful daughter

Damnit
a tear highlighting my plump red cheek
we are in such different places

out of sight
I pray briefly
you've caught your bus
wipe my face

move on
