title: "uprooted"

The unnamed plant
	in the corner
		I repotted
is dying

it must not
like the soil
or its new place
in my living room

she stands up straight but her leaves are wilting

I noticed today
        I’m not who I was
	         just a few months ago

I am aging drastically

I can’t say
that I really regret it
it is nice
to have some security

life has been easier but my spirit is fading

Maybe some miracle grow
	a little sleep
a few days of sunshine
and just some readjusting
will have us both
looking up again
