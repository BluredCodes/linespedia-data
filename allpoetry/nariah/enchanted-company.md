title: "Enchanted Company"
writer: "Nariah"

Your smell fills the room 
soft, textured and devine 
like it is love itself 
and I am melting 

Everything you look at is illuminated 
tickled- 
like a teased nipple finally licked 
it has exsisted merely for this moment 

Your voice causes earthquakes 
throughout my body 
ripples in time 
commanding the attention of the admiring Universe 

When you are near me 
my soul lives larger 
begging for more of you 
and I experience God
