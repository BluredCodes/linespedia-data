---
title: "calorie count"
writer: "Nariah"
slug: "calorie-count"

diet pills
quick shakes
belly rolls
back aches

chubby neck
monstrous thighs
stretch marks
getting high

breakfast muffin
tight clothes
chocolate kisses
candy rose

old photos 
shame shame
avoid the camera
avoid the nameQuick on fat frustration.
