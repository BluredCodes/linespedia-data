I remember the first time I met you
nervous and in love with your son
you had to stop at K-Mart
it was then I realized
this was the family I'd been waiting for

I can remember the way the car smelled
cigarettes and laundry
and a hint of baby goodness
I could never tell you but I hated getting out

And now I am so lucky! 
my mother-in-law is also
my favorite poet
          and a friend!

I love you Bonnie.
Thanks.
