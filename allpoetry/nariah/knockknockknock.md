title: "*knock*knock*knock*"

*knock*knock*knock*
avoid the front door
it's the NYSEG man again
he doesn't understand me
and you've been gone some days now

*knock*knock*knock*
that man you loved once
is here too
he won't answer 
he's been drinking and he's creepy

*knock*knock*knock*
mom just come on home now
just explain that I'm alone here
I am cold and I am hungry
they'll let you out I'm sure

*knock*knock*knock*
excuse me she is not home now
it's been quite a few years 
nevermind you see her
passed out there on the bathroom floor

*knock*knock*knock*
they're taking me away now
five years too late they get it
she *not* home now
I'm alone here

*knock*knock*knock*
can someone get that?
it's the po-lice now
they've found her
face-flat on the sidewalk bleeding

*knock*knock*knock*
miracles! she's alive again
but they have no idea
this ghost that has been haunting
for something like ten years

*knock*knock*knock* 
mommy it is me
here with all your meds now
come on take them 
then we're done here
go now, rest in peace mom

*knock*knock*knock*
run away 
they'll never understand you
they can't see the mercy killing
they'll just lock the door....
