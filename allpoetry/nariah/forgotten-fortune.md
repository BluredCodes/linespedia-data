---
title: "Forgotten Fortune"
writer: "Nariah"
slug: "forgotten-fortune"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59591-Forgotten-Fortune-by-Nariah"
---

chalky Easter chocolate
forgotten in the bottom
of the last-minute basket

egg wanes
saliva thickens
*swallow*
*swallow*
icky film covering my teeth
leaves my fingers foraging 
for more shiny metallic goodness
