cold wooden floor
hard, harsh reminder
of a loving god
a balance

"come back
(whispers)
come back
I am here too
No hurry
No worries
You’ve created this world
for a reason
be here now
enjoy it
all of it"

god as escapism
(ha!)
oh, I am!
mantra repeated
 and with the cyclic nature of everything
reality slips
oh, and I am cold!
I am!
I am!
