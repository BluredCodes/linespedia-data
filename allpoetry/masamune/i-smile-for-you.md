title: "i smile for you"
writer: "Masamune"

i pushed them all away
everyone who loved me

i did it to myself
and i wasnt helping them anyways

and i cry tears of blood for them
tears from my wrists

that's why i wear long sleeves
in the florida spring

i hope they're happy now
as my corpse walks by them
