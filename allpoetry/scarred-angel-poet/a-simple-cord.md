Clamor comes outside the tavern,
Dragons arrive from Fiery Cavern!

People run and people shout,
Arms flail and swords pulled out.

Quivers are filled, arms bared,
I get my book, some have stared,

I race outside, murmuring fast,
Freezing time with the spell I cast.

I rose my arms, sliding down robes,
Rereading the steps in ancient codes.

"Shtoth," I whisper, then I cry,
The dragons vanish when I blink my eye.

What have I done, are they dead?
What peers from the sky; a head!

A ruby red dragon winks at me,
"Thank you," she rumbles, "we're free!!"

"Elf!" a man shouted from behind,
"What have you done?" a voice ver' kind.

I motioned to my old spellbook,
With a word of praise came a look.

"Thank you elf, you freed those creatures,"
The man grinned with unmistakable features.

When he walked away, I glanced at his sword,
"Excalibur" was embossed there, just in simple cord.
