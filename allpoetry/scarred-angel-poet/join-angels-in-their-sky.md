My lips are stiched to each other,
No secrets to tell to you, brother.

Pain is weakness leaving the body,
And tears only leave ink blotty.

I bound my very own mouth closed,
To leave behind words and idiotic "so's"

Swords cut and leave you bleeding,
The sight of your blood leaves you needing.

Now I think it's time for the eyes,
I need needle and thread of great size.

Slash goes the sword, blood spills from a vein,
And that blood carrier was one of my main.

I cannot see, cannot, will not cry out,
Nor will I spill tears, or will I shout.

Vaguely I hear people near me as I die,
Gone to join the angels in their sky.
