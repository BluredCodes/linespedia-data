title: "...and you're now gone"

So you're now gone
and i'm left with the baggage
of moral psychosis
And you don't care
For the things we had
and the lack of insecurity

But can i ever remember
the dreams that i've had for ages
you said we've turned pages
so why do i feel like 
i'm locked in the cages
of your head
of your head

So you've been had
and for the contentious behaviors
I get shit
And you're now gone
for the train to El Segundo
and the 'movielife' along with it

But will you repent
and realize my ever present lack of motivation
there was replication
on your god damn standard vacation
the word 'fornication'
the word 'fornication'

And now i've grown older
but poorer and colder
by far
by far

But maybe you aren't the best
because if words can't describe you
why am i writing this?
