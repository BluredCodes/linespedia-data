Give me back the days, minutes, and hours
I spent them pining
and you will give back when you die
they'll take back the dead flowers
three is a party i'm not invited to
I hope he is happy, now that he has you

But did he fuck you?
did he make you bleed?
I hope you realize
that the blood on the floor
is just a measure of how much you think he loves you

We've gone nowhere and you're out of time
I'll watch it go
I'll see you become the hooker, the whore
I'll see you commit the fucking crime
then I'll think that it was a shame
I hope you realize that I still love you

Did he fuck you, enough to love you?
did you cry for him?
I cried for you
did he make you bleed?
there will always be blood on my floor

Well, who you fuckin' now?
I'll never wonder
I've fucked myself, enough to know
you'll have to fuck yourself a way out
and when you fuck yourself
will i cross you mind?

Did he leave you in the rain?
send you down the drain?
so you say, so you say
absence makes the heart go yonder
but your kids will never know
