---
title: "Understanding"
writer: "GlassBrains"

I've wondered for hours about this
I stayed asleep today
when slovenly placed shots never miss
its nothing i can ever say
but definitely worth waiting for

Because I just can't understand
why the winner is always the sinner
I won't bother it now
let the sleepers lie

Maybe I'll forget
the names and faces
of everyone I've ever met
it would be much easier
to let all the kids run away

And I will never understand
why the winner is always the God damn sinner
I've been in the race for days
and look at me, I'm still not you

All I want to do
is dream premonitions
and see whats inside you
stay clean and be different
stand in line and have a happy fucking New Year

But I still can't understand
why the winner is always the God damn sinner
save a place for me
because I'm still thinking

I'll come home
from a trip to the west
I'll see you play the 'sinner'
you'll lock it in a box
there is no place like home

You can't make me understand
why the winner is just a sinner
why its good to glow
and why bridges lead home
