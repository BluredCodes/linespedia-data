---
title: "Seeing in Stripes"
writer: "GlassBrains"
slug: "seeing-in-stripes"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62246-Seeing-in-Stripes-by-GlassBrains"
---

Beat the day
and figure out what you've done
my hands go tremulous
when looking down the barrel of a gun
don't pass it to me
I'll just miss and end up with a needle through my throat

And I swear
I'd rather hold your head underwater
than see you like this
your friends now total a simple list
and your gun means the same as a kiss

Throw your voice
to see what you really sound like
your self-destructiveness is contagious
looks like I'm the only one around
I'm done sympathizing at close distance
I'll just miss the promenades and the beautiful nights

And I swear
I'd rather hold your head underwater
than let the locusts kill my first-born daughter
I would have shot her
I'm just another dysfunctional father

It was all too new for her
I was all to cruel to her

And I pray
that the barrier is just an open locker
then I can rejoice at seat's edge
and see that broken promises are a well-played pledge
livid dreams are a crumbling ledge
