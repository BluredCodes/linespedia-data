title: "kneel"
writer: "GlassBrains"

I've buried my heart
it hurt too much to see you there
the price of maturity
it will grow one day, if you come back to me
til then, i wish you were here in the first place

as if i'm your demi-god
i'll reside on the edge
waiting for them to realize

You cut me in two
and taped me to your little blackbook
the name for your converted matchbook
i think one day i'll understand
but til then, everything is right 
and the rain in my eyes feels nice

and i'll be your god
kneel and heave away your pain
then i'll realize

there's better things to do
than sit and wonder what i'm supposed to do
i gave it to you
you will say you never knew
then you will let it go
