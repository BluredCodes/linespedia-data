I'd like to close my eyes and look away
but there will always be debts to pay
for as long as there is something more
I think you know what the masses believe
something hauntingly predilectory
filled with hate, angst, and an unhealthy lack of fear

but if you open your eyes
and don't let them paralyze
then the things you want in the end
will not always depend
on the action of others
blurted out undercovers

so don't let the hive lure you in
stay with me and i'll take you there

saying this predicts the end
so if you blink, you'll miss the best part
the credits are going down
the leaders and their psuedo-crowns
for every person who opens your door
another is waiting to knock you to the floor
i'll watch out for that one 
but don't rescind the others gratitude

so don't pretend to lose
the point is not to cruise
through everything thats thrown your way
in the end
you'll have your day
pardon yourself before you leave
and you will recieve

but don't let the hive lure you in
stay with me, i'll take you there
