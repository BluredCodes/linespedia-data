[]Wonderer's Wish []



In wishes how much we had

We had crowned them
with fancy gold, and the best  smells of flowers

We breathe the fresh morning breeze

we played when light's stares raise

we played with the wet sands on the showers, and build high demolishing castles

The shower that got tiered
from the sea waves

How the sand looks clear and crestless?
How the dusts and contamination of land did not affect it?

How not different colors or have the sea bleach change it?

On the ways lots of sands and stones
Sometimes hidden at the valley or the well or a cave

And the size of land stays the same,
cold without heat

The squeezed unknown fear
Surrounded the meaning of the excitement

Go back one day and see
Today
And little backward--}}}

Time of fear is gone far away 
Disappeared

Look out!!!
Wake up and take the lead, 
Accomplish what you wish

Don't be hesitating
Hold the stick 
And
Follow the tracks
You'll fined

It's only
a
Wonderer's wish that you aim to have
