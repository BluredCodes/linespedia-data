i am not your girl, so please don't call me that
you think i'm just angry all the time
you've been greatly mistaken
for i'm not as angry as you may think
i'm just misunderstood in my simple dispostions and complicate manerisms

you assume so little of me? why?
jelous because i can control my life and still let fate do its job?
i'm just an angry girl, so theres nothing left here for you
run along and find yourself a simple minddamn ex
