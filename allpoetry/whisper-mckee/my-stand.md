---
title: "My Stand"
writer: "Whisper Mckee"

The world's hidden in darkness.
Souls trapped outside the door.
A land scared and blood drenched,
By the ravage of war.

We have given up,
Without even a fight.
Forgetting his grace,
And his saving light.

Simply shrugging our shoulders.
We say it's all meant to be.
But that's when I take my battle,
Down on my knees.

I know the greatest change,
Cannot be brought about by man.
So I fall to my knees,
And it's there I take my stand.
