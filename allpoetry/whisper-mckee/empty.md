---
title: "Empty"
writer: "Whisper Mckee"
slug: "empty"

I feel you,
That ache,
It's called,
Empty.

I know,
You'll return
To me.
Simple.

Yet the night,
Whispers,
Your name.
Softly.

Sighing,
I whisper,
Your name,
To the stars,
They Hear.

The moon,
Alone,
Up there.
As I am.
Here.

What to say,
Simple words,
Honey,
Without you,
I'm empty.
