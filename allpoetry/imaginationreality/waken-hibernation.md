title: "Waken Hibernation"
writer: "ImaginationReality"

I find myself waking in the morning

to the frost dew and yawning

the morning at first light

has given risen from another night

and with this first daybreak

my eyes they do not fake

from another night of slumber

to another day in life numbered

my feet follow the way to the door

because I want to adore

this fine thing of nature

and cannot wait till later

the air is cold this day

after waking up from my lay

I see a squirl in a tree

gathering food not for me

I see a bird fly to his stoop

pecking just above some root

the wood is dead and full of hole

you could say the tree has given up its' soul

I came out here and seen my shadow

and now find my way back to my hollow
