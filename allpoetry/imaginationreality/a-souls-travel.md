I wish to travel

the vast knowledge of your mind

that's where I may find

the soul is too kind



I wish to show

a love not yet known

my heart not thrown

like a seed is sewn



I wish to feel

moments caressed by hands

toes in the sands

when reached foreign lands



I wish to belief

nothing to lose

this to chose

live by this rule



I wish to witness

the different seasons

believed to my reason

behold no treason



I wish to love

consumed by affection

showing soul direction

this my mission
