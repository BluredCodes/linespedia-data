title: "Wooden Knows"
writer: "ImaginationReality"

I am not made of wood

I am flesh and blood



I am not made of clay

you cannot mold me your way



I am not a puppet on a string

my finger is in need of ring



I am not a piece in a game

I have a soul and a name



I am not your slave or martyr

my mind dreams further and further



I am not just words on your screen

my person is just to you unseen



I am not less than thee

you are you and I am me



so when you look into your mirror

let you judge thyself



and when I look into my mirror

let me see myself
