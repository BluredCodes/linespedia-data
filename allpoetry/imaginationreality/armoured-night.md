My pleasure armor
Ready to protect your swollen organs
A fight of desires
Some intentions, strong intentions
Without judgment are pleasure reigns
Wishing, not enough
Without missing are love subtracts
Too much, go down, slow Down
Without meaning
Love to feel none
Captured inside thoughts unreal
Waiting for your love
 
Unconsciencess rises to rule the night
Waiting for reality to overcome my dreams
Thoughts of propaganda enter my mind
Though unholy obsession I find my faith
Dreams subpress my minds eye
Settle, sleep, no noise, no sound
Emptyness, temptress
No Surprise
