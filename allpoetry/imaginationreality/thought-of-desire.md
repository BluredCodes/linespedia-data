Tell me what it is you desire

For this I may give you

Wrapped in sweat and full of sin

To these desires we are forbidden

The lust is must for we're above

this romantic dream called love

Another verse, another poem

Make me not part a groan

But a scream of desire

Not a funeral pyre

 

I'm waiting online

For it is you that I miss

A word or too I may caress

Not another word is what I say

Listen to these thoughts that I play

For a stairway of knowledge could be gained

It is not book but it is my brain
