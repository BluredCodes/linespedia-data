Simulated moments through disruptive reality

I feel the reactions, I see the thoughts

Ever wary of judgement authority

Cast through these eyes I walk this earth

In search of space for my rebirth

Calm nerves caress my soul

I speak in syllables

In a ever evolving future

Trapped in time, Alone

I forget my intellect

questioning my intuitions

Constantly searching

Running, Sleeping, Moving
