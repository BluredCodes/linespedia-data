I believe we have found the correct time in space.

My thoughts meandering through digital reality,

I find your magnetic existence.

Could this be phenomenon and not just coincidence.

The Heavens' ablaze with cracks of thunder & flashes of light.

The lights go out with a violent might.

Pleasures of the flesh flow over my mind.

My strengths and weaknesses fighting to decide.

Could this be a dream.

Is this really what it means?

Could you be the lady of the lake of tears.

Alone I clutch a piece of breath waiting for my fears.

To melt inside or to subside.

To cast a light and burn it bright.

On my soul the candle waits

as the hours draw late.

Engulf my desires and caress my soul.

Dancing flames can warm a frozen soul.

Bless this night, this night tonight.

For is has brought a new connection.

Through the dreams and my thoughts.

My Hearts yearns for deep expression.

Through these words are no depression.

Look into my eyes to see your reflection.
