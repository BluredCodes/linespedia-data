title: "As Was, May Be"
writer: "ImaginationReality"

As I walk down this path

this path behind my eyes

Memories believed in once

but now have turned to lies



In these times of treason

I find within my mind

looking for the reasons

and searching for my kind



I continue my journey

looking at the earth

walking through a forest

searching for rebirth



As I make my way

my way through some trees

a bird looks down in wonder

gliding in the breeze



The bird begins a tune

The bird begins to tweet

and I look up to the sky

to hear the sound so sweet



There is no visible moon this day

up above in the sky

instead some clouds moving

no beauty to my eye



The wind is still blowing

and loosens off a tree

a leaf start a twirling

and is heading just for me



I stop to watch it dance

I watch it all in calm

open my hand to catch it

but it lands within my palm



I begin my travel again

as the clouds move across the sky

I stop to look up

then a cloud forms before my eye



this seems so strange

the cloud takes your silhouette

this seems to be arranged

that much I can bet



I close my eyes then awaken

cause I can't believe what's done

the sky starts to open

a brilliant ray of sun



Now that I am blind

I close my eyes again

to see your silhouette

traced in my sight again



A thought then comes over me

that spring has just begun

the thoughts of me worshiping you

that time it has to come
