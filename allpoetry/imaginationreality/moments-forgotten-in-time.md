Tonight I found new thoughts

as I browsed upon your writings

unknowing to my intellect

written without my knowing

my spirit captured your senses

and teased your soul

I tempted your desires

My shadow danced in your view

Now I see what could become

we must gather our knowledge together

to rewaken our minds

and live our enjoyments in each other
