title: "Book of Mind"
writer: "ImaginationReality"

An open book is like I feel

all the words emotional steel

Left open and uncovered

Or upon a shelf undiscovered

Just a touch is all I need

Or just the urge for you to read

A new world could be found

just by opening my leather bound

so get some coffee and the kettle

Take no mind to the dust that settled

A gaze down into my black and white

will make you unbutton that dress so tight

so take that hand that just fed

the pets so loved were put to bed

and pull me from that place above

and feel your soul fill with love

you'll find your eyes look down

into my words that show no frown

as you wander through my pages

you'll see the time that turned to ages

Words so sweet and words so kind

you'll finally realize it is my mind
