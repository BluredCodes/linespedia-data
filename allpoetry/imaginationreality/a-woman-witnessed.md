title: "A Woman Witnessed"

This woman that I met

she is amazing in her glory

with just a word of her story



This woman that I met

she sees things with her eyes

that which are not lies



This woman that I met

she hears things with her ears

and feel things with her tears



This woman that I met

her souls bears the witness

and her hand writes fineness



This woman that I met

she has a magical existence

and not even this instance



This woman that I'll meet

I met by chance

definite circumstance



This woman that I'll meet

I wait hours and endure

just moments to adore



This woman that I'll meet

I would give her the sun

which cannot be done



This woman that I'll meet

I will worship her needs

these are my deeds



This woman that I'll meet

so like my very kind

I would do anything to have her mine
