~O~

we could be lovers
incomplete

make love without contact
subtle and sweet

I would desire you
beyond all reason

drink of your essence
in momentary illusion

casting my spell
scent of anticipation

springing the trap
mental copulation

I dream of your flesh
soft and inviting

the warmth of your eyes
soul enticing

lay your mental bonds
against my mind

rest your weary emotions
salt on my palm

encircling your presence
dreaming soft embrace

entering your existence
open hearts relate

nothing matters here
insubstantial

unspoken ties
strong and gentle

cast aside reality
superceded

we could be
love completed

~O~argh mushy pulp blah
