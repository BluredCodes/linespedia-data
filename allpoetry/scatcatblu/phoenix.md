~O~


little phoenix how I count the screams
as the petals I tug plead in voices of rusty steel
tiny flower breaths ignite falling as ash in my mind
I dream the roses blood red
tainted with the scent of feathers singed

~

insert your tongue inside my mind taste honeyed pain
lifeless but exisiting all the same
initial my soul with your breathless pleas
I will wrap you in the darkest grip of tenderness
defying you to leave 

~

sanctified by your presence torn from the pit of absolution
dreams of fiery columns crashing down upon me
bending to the rhythm of your unending need
I stoke the embers in numb expectation
I raise the knife of unquestioned temptation
bleeding a spark for you to steal

~

captured in meek submission
which one of us is the demon
unending torture in a fiery hell
creating gardens of screaming souls
flinging together charcoaled humanity
listening for answers to your own anonymity

~

clasping one another with push and shove
drinking of a sordid past shared as one
riding on the wind of insecurities
who would crash and burn in flames of inadequacy
tell me truthfully when to let go


~O~
