I don't need you, I don't need anyones aggravation

I accepted myself long ago in this synopsis

like treacle

spread me thin and I still taste good and strong



I'm kinda cauterised in my responses

hanging loose with the tides

banging my head against the occasional wall



Who says you should be lonely

just because you want to be alone

I've constructed a wall of attitude and indifference

to keep this world at bay



I stripped back the components

but I still can't figure how I work

and anyway I have grown smaller than my parts



thinking on the responses

I throw them just to see them flyexperimental change of tact (just because) please tell me what you think (really) :)
