~O~

this is a box
encoded within

perception of rhythm
slumber of misconception
intent on translation
an instant gratification
undisciplined generation
partaking of creation
the substance of understanding
a well of ineptitude
cremating theories
needless descriptions
posing as hypersensitive
signs of idealism
stylised idiosyncracies
afraid of sunlight
cascading repertoires
consuming perception
inane intricacies
of suicidal insight
reality superceded
imagination precluded
originality mislaid
cognitive conclusions
faithless parades

do not open
under any circumstances

unless specified conditions
outlayed in detail
on reverse transmission
are precisely concurrent
with relevant delineations
subject to expected delay

~O~
