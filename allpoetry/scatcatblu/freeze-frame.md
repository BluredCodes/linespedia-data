title: "'freeze frame'"

hit me harder, next time

with your ego

you might spark a reply

ignite a flame of truth

to combust your frigid mind

consuming stale aggression



I know I'm just a cliche

depressed and full of inhibition

sad and scarred and lonely

too busy framing shadows

in need of mediation

absorbed in self analysis



and we've all heard this story before

except this one is mine

and I'm attached to the prognosis

the history that drives the frames

I constructed a therapeutic solution

got all caught up in perception

which side of the projection did I stand



in the end I'm only an occassional visitor

to my own sense of my self

lingering momentarily in sanity

confused and out of place

I'm addicted to the reruns

caught up in the hidden art



and you say that I've been better

not so deep, not so dark for so long

less time spent in the downward spiral

less frequently absorb in memories

and you're right, my dealing is improving

and I don't seem so uninvolved

but in my mind the reel is turning

and the playlist is the sameand again - a flood of emotional diatribe - in need of an edit
