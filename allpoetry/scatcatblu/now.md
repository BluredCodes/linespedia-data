I don't need consideration
don't treat me like a girl
I'm an independent woman
an individual force
you do your thing get happy
and I'll oblige the same
when we get connected
I ain't wasting time swapping baggage
or old love stories gone wrong
so let's get busy
do that happening thing
share some action
hanging and chilling
surfing and sliding
round this now scene

you got something going down
hey I'm cool
I got movement on the side
don't ask for my permission
if you wanna go hang
golfing with your buddies
or cruising around town
I'm competent at entertainment
I'm cool with myself
I prefer to make my own decisions
and have my own turf
I don't need no interaction
when it comes to what I want
I'm not planning integration
I'm perfetly in touch with myself
don't grill me 'bout my contacts
commenting on my style
get all possessive about me
clocking up those miles

I'm available if you ask me
unless I'm getting busy somewhere else
and I'm always glad to see ya
when we're having fun together
doing something crazy
or just fooling around
I'm here for you as companion
as lover or as friend
but don't be giving me no agro
about how I live my life
or where this story ends

so let's swing this thing together
or not
'cause now is where it's atum yeah - editing to come :o]
