knotch me on your bed then baby
you cut me deep
I thought friendship was important
obviously the sex was too much
too good to refuse

	better luck next time
	(in) being understood

step back and play at recognition
divine truth two paces behind self
neglect to admit the omissions
behold the deception
finding substance in the empty space

	better luck next time
	(at) being real

contrasting with your inhibitions
an inate sense of ego prevails
dodging between personas
the enticing vulnerability ebbs
self preservation flows

	better luck next time
	(by) being yourself

dressed in suspension
shrouded in mystique
grasping at opportunities
caught up in contrivances
playing love game strategy

	better luck next time
	(re) your current losing streakhmmm . . .
