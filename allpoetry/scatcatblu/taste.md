~O~



i want to lick the taste of tomorrow from your lips

push my tongue against your teeth



savouring coffee coated amphetamine 

spiked with your obeisant lies

drape my hand up and down your spine

cupping and clinging to your neck as i

plunge my fawning tongue into your mind

and grind myself against your responsive sighs 

marking the rhythm of nonexistant time

fucking you endlessly with my eyes

and all you do is look at me and smile

speaking to me of souls and numbing lives

how you bleed and need me to cry

neither venturing across the line



and all i want is to lose myself

in the taste of yesterdays sorrow



~O~
