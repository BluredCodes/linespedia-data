~O~

I hate coming down
back to the planet
leaving behind
my real life jacket

tomorrow brings reality
assume crash positions
the atmosphere thickens
without my connections

cloaked in convention
I land on my feet
shrug off dissension
provision for sleep

I make the transition
from back to beyond
once again standing
below solid ground

~O~the plane came in to land
the world came rushing forward
the threads of my life
not long enough to sustain me
*sigh* a walking fragment
