I am what I want to be
I wonder if dreams really come true 
I hear people who cry
I see things I would never want to lose 
I want more than life can give
I am what I want to be

I pretend that life always keeps going
I feel the heat of eyes that stare
I touch the stars in the sky
I worry that not all people know how to care
I cry inside, but I dont like to show it
I am what I want to be

I understood more than you think I do
I say things that I dont mean, there off the wall
I dream about a sinless life
I try to reach my goals but fall
I hope everyday for something new
I am what I want to be
