so cold... so cold
it feels against my skin
i shiver as i trace
an outline of my vein

my muscels tensen
but it says relax, relax
i loosen my grip on it
but still i hold it in my hand

you need me..
you need me...
it seems to say
i cant! i cant!

i stop fighting the urge
as i bring it's silver body
toward my chest
i scream as the blade
penetrates my heart
