The sound of your voice
                  is enough to make me break.
       It makes me wanna rip
                         all reality apart.
            When I even close my eyes,
                    I see your face.
            Sneering at me, 
                 how much longer will this last.
     I tick like a time bomb,
          Your gonna make me go off.
                  I'll blow up in your face
            and stop hurting myself.
         You put me over the edge,
        every shred of patience leaves me. 
    This wouldn't have happened,            
            You just had to decieve me.
        I'm at the point of no return.
              Now I'm gonna break.
