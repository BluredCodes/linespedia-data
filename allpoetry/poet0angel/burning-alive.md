burning...
  screaming..
       burning....

fire grabs at you 
from every corner
                
                 trapping....
              suffocating.....
trapped 
in the inferno's prison
        
  crying....
               praying...

crying for someone
to set you free

               dying...
           burning alive......

the flames
devour you 

            heaven....
                  hell....

to which 
will you go?
