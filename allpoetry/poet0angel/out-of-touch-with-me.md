title: "Out of Touch with Me"

I'm so afraid,
my life is out of touch.
Living can be senseless
if your heart has been cut.

Do I trust anyone 
or live alone.
If I do,
how long will it hold?

I'm lost 
in the sky of my own mind.
I search for myself
but never I find.

I'm just afraid
of getting hurt again.
So give me some time
until my wounds mend.

I found myself once
then my mind made me fall.
To the whole world,
I put up a wall.

And my fear doesn't phase me,
but my anger consumes.
Everyway I look at it,
I'm just gonna lose.
