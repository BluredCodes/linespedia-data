so black
so cold your mind?
untold
the anger 
the hurt
your heart?
a desert
your life
runs away
your soul?
just can't say
innocent?
not me
what am I?
guilty?

passion?
love?
something i talk myself out of
betrayal
decieving
my sanity
keeps on leaving
my goals
keep on passing
my mouth keeps on ranting

...My Life
