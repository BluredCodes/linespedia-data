title: "Astray from Desire"

So easily you fall short of it,
passing you by.
It is so easy to deny,
for the emotionless soul.

The ira of your mind,
pushes it to recede.
Is it to mislead,
your mortal self?

And if it is to fade
into the distance.
Will you not listen
to your heart?

Do you ever find it?
You search away from the ire.
But fall short of a desire.
Do you look for love, and passion's bloom?

When you find it,
will you accept?
And take into your heart's depth,
a new light.
