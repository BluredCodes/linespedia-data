creeping closer, it follows me
everyday of my short life
it doesn't smile
it just snickers, making me miserable

I crawl away everytime
it just keeps getting closer
reaching fo me with its fingers from a fiery pit
following....following...following me

clothed in the darkeness 
that is vailed in the night 
at me, it continues to
try and bite, as it follows me

like pain, it will never go away
so I try to flee
grabbing at me, I tug and pull
as death follows me,
everyday
