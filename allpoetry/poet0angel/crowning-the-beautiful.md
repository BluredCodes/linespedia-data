title: "Crowning the Beautiful"

Does she truly exceed the world?
Is her value higher than that of a queen?
So crown her, but with what?

She has everything
but the poor have nothing.
Is she self-less?
To crown her, with what?

Portraying the admiration
of everyone she meets,
is her soul as light as her body?
But to crown her, with what??

Then her true colors come to face.
Beautiful? Not anymore.
Her soul dark as coal,
her crown of brimstone.
