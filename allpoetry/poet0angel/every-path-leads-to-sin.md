Dreaming of a beautiful life,
a path away from sin.
You travel so many different roads,
all lead to a dead end.

One day you think you get it right
but then you float away.
You will continue to pick yourself up again
each and everyday.I think this is my shortest poem and too much rhyme[oh well]
