I'm a fool
cause I thought you could change.
I thought you were different
but you just the same.
As every other playa in my life,
you lucky I dont cut yo head off with a knife.
I'm sick of this crap
and I wont take this anymore.
Go around and play
around wit the slutty lil whores.
I'm better than that 
and this is what I make of it,
You and yo ego are just full of sh*t.
so I'm through with this.
