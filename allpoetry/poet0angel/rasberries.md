Rasberries are alluring 
just by the looks of them.
You say you gotta have em,
but how will they taste?

You grab the first one you see
and then you try it out.
It's seems pretty good,
but bittersweet.

Then you realize 
there's an after taste.
It wasn't as good 
as it seemed.

Get real people, 
do you think I would write about rasberries?
This is 'bout people lookin' good on the outside
but being real cruel inside.
