Dwindle away pettey mistakes
for when was anyone perfect?
Perserve the blossoming song in the heart
because it's song sings of love.

Rebuke the slander 
that slaughters the mind,
for the fool planted it's seeds
in your head.

Scorn not the young
for they are not all ignorant
and can see beyond 
one's heart.

Cherish the life that you live
for tomorrow you might part.
And look at everything in a different way,
then kiss the moon,the sun,and a star.
