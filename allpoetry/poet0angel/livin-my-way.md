I live my own life
so dont tell my how to live
I dont care what you think
or what you gotta give

Got a problem with it?
good for you.
Your not my boss
so dont tell me what to do.

This aint new,
and dont call me immature.
Cause unless you get to know me
you'll never be sure.

And dont ever judge me,
I dont take that crap.
You stab me
I'll stab you right back.

So you can be my friend
or my enemy.
It really doesn't matter
but I'll be what I wanna be

Only me
Cause I'll be what I wanna be
