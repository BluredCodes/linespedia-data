---
title: "Kiss My Sinning Soul"
writer: "poet0angel"

source: "AllPoetry"

Kiss my lips of poison
My mind that seems to bleed
Kiss my heart, no passion
My choler it seems to feed

Kiss my hand of hurt
My temptation it obeys
Kiss my soul of darkness
Kiss the sin away
