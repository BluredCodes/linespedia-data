My heart,
light as a feather.
When I looked into your eyes.
I told myself
I wasn't in love,
but how could I let you pass me by?

I don't know what made me change,
but then my heart started to glow.
In an essence
it was your sweet smile,
to which I now well know.

I think about you every moment,
thank you for setting me free.
From the person I never should have been,
my mind was in misery.

Never I wanna be alone,
never how I was.
All I'll ever ask of you
is for you sweet gentle love.
