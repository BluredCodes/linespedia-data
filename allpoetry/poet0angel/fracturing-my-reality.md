title: "Fracturing My Reality"

I trusted you
with everything in me
this goes through my mind
over and over and over

I can't get you out of my head
that sneer 
that less than innocent smile
why???

I took out everything on myself,
but now I see it was you
who ruined me
the real me

I'm different because of you 
now my anger drives me
I just can't control it
I know I should be the bigger person

sometimes I wish I could
strangle the life out of you
to rip your hair ou
beat that smile off your face

just look at me now!
I'm supposed to be that innocent
little girl I was
I had to grow up

why did you do this?
why?
why?
why?

I just can't take the fact
that you sleep good at night
everything is different for me
you shattered my realityI had to blow off a LOT of steam
