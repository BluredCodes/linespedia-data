title: "To Whom I Battle Most"

The look on your face 
says so many things.
You fought many emotional battles,
the guilt inside that overcame.

You cry
but you hide it.
Because you cry on the inside,
you deny it.

Turning the pain physical
on yourself, you take it out.
There's scars on your body.
Will they go away, I doubt.

Your family is your shelter
and your friend was supposed
to be your support,
but fell.

Your heart hurts so bad,
Your mind is all wrong.
Everyday you ask yourself,
how much longer will this last? How long?
