I wanna drown you
in the water you bathe in
Strangle you in your sleep
I'll laugh while you scream
What? do you think I'm mean?

Put a hole in your heart
like in away you did mine
Poison you food?
Believe me, I'm feelin fine

Rip your life apart
with my own hands
Spit on your grave
that's a plan

Destroy your dreams
demolish your goals
Believe me b*tch 
you aint never gonna grow old

I'm gonna smile
while you rot away
It wont hurt,
dont be afraid.

Just Die
