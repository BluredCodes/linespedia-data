I Want 

I want green leaves rustling on the tress. 
Sun shimmering down thought them flickering off my skin;
Like a gigantic wave of sound; 
Whispering in the world’s ear.

I want the sweet aroma of a spring flower;
Rising up to my senses of smell;
Filling my head with the sweetness of spring:
Like innocence of a new born baby fawn.

I want to watch the dewfall of a flower petal;
Grazing a fluffy lush layer of moist grass;
Watching as a small rabbit runs in and open field.
As if no one is watching, making small circular trails.

I want to watch the fog rise of a small stream;
Filling the sky with it’s wonderful beauty;
Like a fluffy blanket, growing larger, drifting deeper into the heavens;
As it slowly disappears.

I want to watch a new born baby calf come into the world;
Taking it’s first breath of this air on earth;
As I watch, snuggles its mother looking for comfort;
As she licks the life giving fluids of it’s warm moist skin.

I want to watch a small child pick dandelions;
As it runs thought the grass with it treasurer?
Getting to its destination only to find out the flower is gone;
And watching the child run back for more.

I want to watch my children grow old.
And them having children watching my family tree grow.
Loving each and ever grandchild if it was my own;
Giving them the hope of love that can happen, if let be.

I want to see the beauty of their sparkling eyes;
As I play with them on a blanket in the sun.
Having them say in a soft sweet voice I love you Mama;
As they tell me I chase all their fears away.

I want to grow old watching God's wonder;
With hope of you being by my side sharing the beauties with me;
Watching every little movement in this God given heaven, called earth.
But most of all I want You, My love, here beside me.
