The Luckiest Guy


Some girls want to be that woman
slinky legs with skirts so tight
crawl'n to a new Mercedes
all the fellas sayin' "Ooh, My, My!"
And that's why, I'm The Luckiest Guy.


And some girls want to be so macho
pumpin' iron almost every night
Flexin' hard all those new grown muscles
workin' out just to catch his eye,
and that's why, I'm The Luckiest Guy.


I've seen the type that'll bake their bodies
fantasies burnin'in the sun
on the shores you'll always find them
sun-spot eyes waitin' for the one,
and that's why, I'm The Luckiest Guy.


Some girls are not quite happy
until they claw to some social high,
up the piles over shattered lovers
she breaks their hearts with a wink of her eye,
and that's why, I'm The Luckiest Guy.


Some girls live just to make the contact,
they dance 'til wet in those late night bars
go to bed with whoever's buyin'
she rates her man based upon his car,
and that's why, I'm The Luckiest Guy.


I've been with these and I'm glad I've found you,
you're just right, can I tell you why?
Your heart is pure and I'll always love you,
you'll be my one thought 'til I die,
and that's why, I'm The Luckiest Guy!


@2001 Dave Adam
