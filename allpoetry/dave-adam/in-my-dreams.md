In My Dreams

I'd Return to you each sleep
after every daily nightmare
those moments in my dreamworld
were all that kept me goin'.

Sleepin' with a memory,
wakin' in that hell,
of holding you beside me
then the morning always killed.

I'd try all day to hold on,
I knew this had to be,
our love was all but over,
until I went to sleep,
so I'll, just stay, Asleep.

What happened doesn't matter,
it probably was my fault,
but pieces of our good times,
were stuck there in my thoughts.

I hope they'll let me keep them,
they meant so much to me,
We'll always be together,
since I have gone to sleep,
and I'll, remain asleep

My daytime life was surreal
I knew it wasn't true,
I'd lost my only reason,
I'd lost a life with you,
I thought, "What sense is tryin',
This never will be right",
my life was filled with heartache,
"But I'll be home with you tonight",
'cause I'll, just go to sleep.

Now I return to you each sleep,
after every daily nightmare...Original artwork entitled: "Night Fish" by Dave Adam
