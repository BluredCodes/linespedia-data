BoOm KaBoOm!   



There's a real me, 

inside of me

inside here, see feel me

Put your hand here

and see for yourself

there's something alive

within me.

Its that same spirit

that wakes me from sleep

makes my heart beat

that loves you.

KaBoOm, KaBoOm

it must be my heart

KaBoOm,KaBoOm

I love you.

So put your hand here

and feel for yourself

its really in love

believe me.

There's a spirit in my heart

that makes me breathe

a spirit in my soul that's

just beneath.

So put your hand here

and feel for yourself

how slowly it beats

without you.

Yes, there's a real me

inside of me,

its really in love,

I need you...
