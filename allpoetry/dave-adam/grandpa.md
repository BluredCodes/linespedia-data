My grandfather was a really wise man
he taught me things as a child,
we'd seen people planting flowers by the road
he turned to me and smiled,
he said, "Always plant flowers with love in your heart,
'cause that's the only way they'll grow, if you count on the blossoms to bring you a profit, then you wont have much to show"

Grandpa use to work at Daytona Racetrack
he took me with him sometimes,
I think his job was collecting pop-bottle caps,
and carrying those big bags of dimes.

Grandpa, Grandpa, my Grandpa
Can I go with you Grandpa?
Grandpa, My Grandpa

His heart was so fragile, yet patient and kind
he answered me all the time,
no matter what I'd ask him, he'd stop what he's doing,
to explain things with a smile, he said,
"Life is like a flower that grows in your heart,
with the warmth from her sunshine, and
hate is like a poison that will sneak into your garden,
and burn it up inside, and so, 
always plant your flowers, with love in your heart,
'cause that's the only way they'll grow, if you
count on her promise to last you a lifetime then,
you'll be walking home alone".

Grandpa, Grandpa, my Grandpa
I really loved my Grandpa
Grandpa, My Grandpa

I still remember the flowers
they sent to our house
the week my Grandpa died,
all wrapped up in plastic
and sold for a profit,
I guess that's why I cried.

Grandpa, Grandpa, my Grandpa
I really miss my Grandpa
Grandpa, My Grandpa

My grandfather was a really wise man
he taught me things as a child,
we'd seen people planting flowers by the road,
he turned to me and smiled,
he said, "Always plant flowers with love in your heart,
'cause that's the only way they'll grow, if you count on the blossoms to bring you a profit then you wont have much to show".
