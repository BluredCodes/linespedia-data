Moonlight tickler
spins spicy-hot nightdreams
past bedposts and cool sheets
to soft-blinking eyes

light-strings her highway
she rides 'cross the evening
to dangle her fingers
in warm pools of passion

Midnight tickler
twist-wrestles with pleasure
brings warm themes and wet dreams
to heartbeats afire

Awake with her memory
as if you've been dreaming
those warm pools of passion
now soil the linen.
She's a moonlight tickler
a moonlight tickler.
