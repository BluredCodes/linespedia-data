Aliens

(Uncle David, what's that in the sky?)

Aliens
Who've come here from another world
Their Aliens
Flying in their spaceships.

(Here they come)
From a distant planet
Those Aliens
They're coming from another world

(Far Away)
Hope they take me with them
Those Aliens
From troubles in my home world.

They're Aliens
They're Aliens

Aliens,
