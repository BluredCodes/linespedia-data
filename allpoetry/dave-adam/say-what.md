What's this you say?
	The time has past
	the world has changed
	we can't go back...
	What's this you say?
	I can't believe
	that you've moved on
	but I'm still here...
	Okay,Okay,Okay
	I understand
	Okay,Okay,Okay
	You're in command!
	What's this you say?
	the time has past
	the world has changed
	we can't go back...
