Anti-Matter

Every morning now he wakes with the notion
that all can be reversed in the world that he lives,
"The electrons should travel the opposite direction,
and wood should stick to glass"
Anti-Matter composed of these deviant atoms
would cast no reflection with time,
absence of mass would seem heavy
as the heat would certainly freeze,
all that is out can be measured
as a portion that's yet to be seen,
The speed of our lives is encountered
when reality seems like a dream...

and is the coffee ready yet?23june1982,
