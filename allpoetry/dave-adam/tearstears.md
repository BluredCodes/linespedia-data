And can they see into my mind,
with their books
and their pens?

Do they think they'll ever find
what's inside,
deep within?

Long ago in pain
I built these walls
'round my soul,
on my own

And, I'll be damned
they won't tear down
what's been built
from these tears!

And,can they know
what's in my heart
from the words
that I speak?

Do they think they stand a chance,
'cause inside 
its just me?

I'll never think to let them in,
it will hurt
I will cry.

So here behind my solid walls
I will keep
what is mine.

Doctors ask me what is wrong,
I refuse
I reject
