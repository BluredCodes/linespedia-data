See my heart there on your hand 
sure looks good there on your finger 
like the ring that I once gave you 
twisting there for him to love... 

Small circle of truth 
plain and unassuming 
perfect trust established 
hanging,dangling in a dump... 

Crying only hurts my finger 
my eyes have long dryed up 
golden symbol of devotion 
Hanging, dangling in a dump...
