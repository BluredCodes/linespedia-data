WHOA!!!! Very balanced , steady and insane!
Whoops, there goes the citybus- gotta' go :::
focused delusion, speak to me
patterned confessions
at large.
Hairsprayed women with perfume like Ortho
like squirrels in the Park
on the southside that fall
Mimes beg for casual kindness
as we made our way to the zoo.

Taxis reminder of peril after dark
through the tunnels near 98th
Peanut vendors sold out to the monkeys
the day before the rally. 
For coffee, and french fries
and a wall street journal

Went to the pond for the ducks
Went to the pond for the ducks
