Dot da dee dot ta
Prospering Princess

Short change focus
recapture your faith
(you're almost gone)

Balance/remember
They're on to you...
Dot da dee dat ta
(and your neutral zone!)

Hold it! I love you
are you for real?
(I'm not really sure)
Dot da dee dot ta

Prospering Princess
touch my wallet
(my money is pure)
Dot da dee dot ta

I see you obey
the ways of cash,
so what's it cost these days?
Dot da dee dot ta

Thump,thump,pop!
take it all down...

Thump,thump,pop!
take it all down...
