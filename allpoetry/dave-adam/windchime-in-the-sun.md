---
title: "Windchime in the sun"
writer: "Dave Adam"
slug: "windchime-in-the-sun"
source: "AllPoetry"

Just in at last
must sing and fast
this song of then
and you

We had some times
like windblown chimes
strung high and much
too loose

We shared you know
a rythum flow
we never meant to clash

but as we did
our song began
each breeze another lash

'till they broke above
those strings of love
that held our tune
as one

you stayed up high
as I fell off
our windchime in the sun

But I'll be back
some summer's night
to climb those
strings again

On your front porch
a firefly, a windchime
and a friend.
