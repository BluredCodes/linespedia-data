Time is Love
For those who wait...
                          Time is Love
its true...
                          Time is love
On Summer nights...
                          Time is Love
With You!
                          Time is Love
When you're alone...
                          Time is Love
its True...
                          Time is Love
most anytime...
                          Time is spent...
With you!
