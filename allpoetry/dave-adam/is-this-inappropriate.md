title: "Is this inappropriate?"
writer: "Dave Adam"

I'm a poet
I'm a freak
I'm a writer
I'm a geek
I'm a loser
Uh-huh

I'm living in the streets
I'm happy to be me
I'm a loser
Uh-huh

I grow just what I smoke
your culture is a joke
'cause I'm a loser
Uh-huh

I live the way I choose
'cause I ain't got much to lose
and I like it
Uh-huh

I say what's on my mind
If you hate me, that is fine
'cause I'm a loser
Uh-huh

While others will go far
I don't even have a car
'cause I'm a loser 
Uh-huh

People ask me all the time
If I will change this life of mine
no. I'm a loser
that's all

See I once had a good life
three children and a wife
then I lost them
Uh-huh

Don't ask me to forget
'Cause this isn't over yet
I will have them one day

Don't ask me to believe
This is how its meant to be
I don't buy that
Nuh-uh

See, I'm a poet
I'm a freak
I'm a writer
I'm a geek
I'm a Loser
