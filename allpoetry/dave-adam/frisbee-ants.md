I think I saw a Beatle only once
following a party in Florida
Or I could've been High
Could' been almost anyone

Damn its been a while
Since I took a pen in hand
had to tell you of a story
can't remember Monday Night Football
PACKERS vs. JAGUARS
@7:02 MST MON 2 DEC 2001
actually written: 25 JUNE 1982
Watchin' Miller and the Boys
talkin' Favre and Batman
final score 18-5
Sports NFL imagine yearly drive

In no-huddle offense to Conklin
Sherman and Holmgren w/ Elvis Mack @ 15YD line
right Back @ Florida State- Boom!
Masterful jewel Determined at Scrimage
From Chapel Hill,NC- Say it Again!

@ pittsburgh 2 weeks ago now at
2271 Franklin Street
Chapel Hill, NC-  OH yeah, no-huddle w/ Miller and the Boys
Back on Monday Night Football-

Remember that moment, ya'know 7:02 pm MST 
Monday Night Football Party,
That second in time poem you coded
for others to read 
in random scattered journal entries
you wrote between classes @ DBCC (when you were in college)
and copyrighted the true date as 25 June 1982 4:27 pm EST
this is the true time, picture the instant what were you doing?

My poem is this:
I told you before
Let me ask if I could
what were you doing
that moment in time?

You know I must ask you,
it wears on my thinking
I'm stuck in that time
Seems better if I drop it
and go on w/what I'm doing
I made a new first
If you know what I mean
but still that moment froze in time
The day I got married
I shared it with you
My heart was on fire!
Seems crazy I'm dying
without her by my side
before I got shot
fighting this war...
