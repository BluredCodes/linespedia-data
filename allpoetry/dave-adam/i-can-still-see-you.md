---
title: "I can still see you"
writer: "Dave Adam"
slug: "i-can-still-see-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

Is it that important
	this thing you must do?
	Can it hold to your heart,
	is it all that to you?
	Like trying to say
	what you never will hear,
	reach out to relate,
	put words to these tears,
	and
	I,I,I,I,I,I, Can still, see you
	I,I,I,I,I,I, Can still, see you
	Its that when I loved you
	I wanted to so madly
	to grab onto your soul
	but you hurt me so badly,
	and
	I,I,I,I,I,I, Can still see you
	I,I,I,I,I,I, Can still see you
	I can still see you my darlin'
	most times its through pain
	just when I get over
	It kills me again,
	and
	I,I,I,I,I,I, Can still see you
	I,I,I,I,I,I, Can still see you
	You don't understand
	but maybe you do
	I gave a big part of myself
	to love you
	You don't understand
	but maybe you do
	I gave a big part of myself
	to love you
	to love you- oo-oo-oo-oo
	and,
	I,I,I,I,I,I, Can still see you
	I,I,I,I,I,I, Can still see you...
