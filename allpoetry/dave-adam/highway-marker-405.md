@highway marker #405
	a rabbit falls
	couldn't quite get the speed
	to make the cross
	Frozen horror
	grabbed his brain
	now its too late
	Sleep now bunny
	along the shoulder
	give up your pain
	@Highway marker#405
	a victim falls
	put to death
	by selfish others
	who's time will come
	And can you see them
	fevered pitch- bounding
	racing 'long the tufted turf?
	I want to be a dad
	one day
	and love the ones
	I've made
	We'll sail the meadows
	off to venture
	beyond the roads of man
	Come my children
	mother's waiting
	mustn't dally now!
	@Highway marker #405
	a rabbit falls
	wonder if the culprit
	here can see the loss?
