Our Friendship means so much to me
Better friends we could'nt be,
We always treat eachother with care
Our feelings we always share,
We cheer eachother up when we are down
We can take away a frown,
We always know when to give a smile
And we definently have a sense of style,
Our secrets we will always keep
And our friendship will always remain deep,
Our friendship is really true
And i will always be there for you.Any comments?
