---
title: "Forgotten"
writer: "Fallen Girl"

I have forgotten why I came here,
I forgot how to leave,
I did not wish to be thrown into this,
I won't let myself believe.

I know I have done something wrong,
But my heart just had to see
What was right and what was true
What was, me and you.

Just know your life isn't a game 
I play
But I want to join in.
So with this said, I bow and leave
It's time to die again.
