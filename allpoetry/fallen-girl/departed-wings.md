---
title: "Departed Wings"
writer: "Fallen Girl"
slug: "departed-wings"
source: "AllPoetry"

if i had wings 
I would fly away from here
high above the mountains
and the carnage in the fields

if i could fly
i would take you away from here
high above the mountains
and the carnage that you fear

i would show you the splenders
and remove all of your tears
we would glide above the clouds
by the wind we would steer

if i had wings
i would take you to the stars
i would show you the beauty 
and the joy of being who you are

if i could fly
i would teach you to feel
holding your heart close to mine
with my wings as your shield

we would soar until you surrendered
and then 
i would drop you!

i would watch the terror in your eyes
as you spirl downward, lacking control
your screams gone unheard
to you horror no yield
and then

just before you impacted into the ground
and shattered into a million pieces
i would smile and softly whisper
now you know the feeling it seems
when i slammed into the ground
for your departed wings.

lacey*
