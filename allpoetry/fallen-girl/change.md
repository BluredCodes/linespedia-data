Today I'am beautiful
5 hours from now I'll be special
5 minutes from now I'll be yours
Tomorrow I'll be willing
1 hours from now I'll be waiting
1 minute from now I'll be gone

30 minutes can change your life
so 30 minutes from now you'll
be alone.

lacey*
