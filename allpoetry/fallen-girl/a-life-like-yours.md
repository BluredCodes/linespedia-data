title: "A Life Like Yours"
writer: "Fallen Girl"

will you sit here beside me?
can I tell you a story, the story of me?
who Iam, where I've been, what I know?
I see you standing here, so close, touching
your face, and tears roll down...

my life is one that you will never know,
but nonetheless, you should always
know, what I did to get me here, standing
trembling, in front of you.

alone in the limelight for all of you to see.

I know I've come so far, just to be
in the company of human beings, please
don't turn me away now, it has
been a long journey.

if you will listen, you will know and 
learn, you were worth the journey,
the struggle, the endless wait.

I came alone, but here, I wish to
rest my soul with you.
