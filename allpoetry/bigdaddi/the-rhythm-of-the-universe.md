---
title: "The rhythm of the universe"
writer: "BigDaddi"

There’s a hum that fills the gap between
Thoughts and silence
God whispering softly in your ear
It’s hangs there on the air
In the air
As the air
Dancing slowly through your body like wine
Warming the spirit and letting all else loose
Eyes closed and mind empty
The hum is there
Composing it’s masterpiece
The heart comes in first
Giving the symphony it’s pulse
A smooth rhythm slowing every second
The hum reigns the hearts recklessness in
Restoring order
Then the lungs come in from the back
Breathing life in to stillness
Pushing and pulling the melody along
To and fro but always in the same place
Soon the body vibrates and pulsates along
Dancing with the hearts steady pace
Always calm
Cellular peace in A minor
It’s the hum that ties it all together
The ribbon round the package of creation
A breadcrumb trail back to the source
Beckoning the listener to let go
There is no listener
In the web of activity and motion
It looms ever present
Unrecognized but unrelenting
Always there
Drowning in ignorance
It plays on softly like God’s harp
Or Divine Mother’s voice
Calling her children home
It’s there at the edge of thought
A doorway to the other side
Listen with your ears and you’ll miss it
A lost child wandering aimlessly in the
Dessert of cognition
This hum lies beyond the mire of perception
It is in the illusion but not of it
But then nothing really is
There is a hum that hangs there on the edge of enlightenment
Pervading all things
Directing all things
Holding the universe together
It is the sound of love
Low and soft
Steady and constant
Often unacknowledged and ignored
Yet always there
