---
title: "My beautiful dreamer"
writer: "FranticFad"

Locked away in the prison of a torn, bleeding heart
Will you feel the soft winds of a promise of spring?
Will you touch the warm light of the fresh living day?

I have seen your true beauty, felt your great charm.
Softly seduced by your vision and spectacular words,
My heart you hold lightly in you soft shapely hands,
My soul is entangled with yours on the bed

The gulf lies between us, too far to engage
My heart leaps aloft as you dance through my dreams
In my dreams I will love you and secretly touch
In the day I shall wake and live the sad truth

Never to reach across that unfathomable gulf
