title: "Fall of night"

The sirens scream
My bruised heart wails in agony
Sharp rocks in the surf, becon
My hand turns the helm
Worry crosses my brow
Resolution strengthens my hand

As my ship is pierced throught the heart
The rocks bear no forgiveness
I question again
For what have I done as the light fades away
