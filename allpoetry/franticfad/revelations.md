Rising from the horrid primordial muck
He stretches
Gathers into himself
The screaming, horrified souls of the damned

The beast cries out, hurtful to hear,
He is gathering his chosen few in heaven above!
At last!
At long last!
The days are upon us; my time has come

Upwards he drives,
rising from perdition’s pit in a nightmare maelstrom
Delivering unto the Earth horrors unimagined
Terrors unrelieved

Come to me my children!
Find peace at my feet, together.
Peace I grant you, the peace of the damned!
Mine!
All mine!

And so did they gather
The tired and the scared, unknowing, unwise
At the feet of the beast
The great squandered lies

See what I have wrought?
Shaking his great gnarled fist
Your playthings are mine
To keep
To devour

But some did hold fast
Stood trembling, but strong
Possessed of the truth
Hope lives with us still

Flashing great light
Shown down on the Earth
The master had come
His children to save

You hold no domain
Not here in this place
Your place lies beneath
Your chains they do waitThought I would try somrthing truly different.....
