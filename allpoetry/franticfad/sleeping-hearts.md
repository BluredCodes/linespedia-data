title: "Sleeping Hearts"

Marching madness, freedom cries
Strung along on endless lies
Compassion's lost, love's demise

Rending, silent, heartfelt pain
Yearning efforts, all in vein
A life of struggling smiles to feign

Furtive, quiet, secret touch
A stolen moment means so much
Your sleeping heart I tried to clutch
