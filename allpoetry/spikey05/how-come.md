title: "How come?"

What's the big idea?
Who can tell me what to do,
How to act,
How to dress?
No one, or
That's what I thought at least.

I guess my so-called 
Friends had their own agendas.
At my expense of course. 
Yes, the guy that listens 
And advises,
Sits and stares while being 
Made fun of and sneered at.

Yes, these words are true.
The cool guys prey on me
Like lion on antelope.
Easy target for their 
Malicious Lies.
