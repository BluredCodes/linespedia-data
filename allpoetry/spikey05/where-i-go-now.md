title: "Where I Go Now..."

Given the freedom
To make my own choices,
I find myself dying
To go back to
The way things were.

Unknown directions
In which to go,
Leading away
From where I am now.

Which leaves me this question,
To answer alone,
Where is there to go
From where I am now.Didnt really have a direction for this, but hopefully the subtle beat saved it.
