---
title: "Flightless"
writer: "Gwion"
slug: "flightless"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58593-Flightless-by-Gwion"
---

I remember
When time was wingless,
Flightless,
Held to the ground
By our own immortality,
By our existence
Lengthened purely
By love.
I remember touches
In the night
That made seconds
Last for hours,
Made an evening of moonlight
Into an eternal calm bliss.
I felt the distance
Pass as wind under my soaring body,
I felt every step, every mile
That I traveled 
To be by your side.
And I remember every second
Of skin against skin,
Of eyes reflecting eyes,
Grasping to hold onto
A fleeting moment,
Stretching it thin
Over time,
Knowing
It would never happen againWritten for S about M.
