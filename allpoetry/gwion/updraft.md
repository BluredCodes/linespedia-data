---
title: "Updraft"
writer: "Gwion"
slug: "updraft"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59609-Updraft-by-Gwion"
---

The first drowning gasp
Of flesh ripped from flesh,
Each quivering wail of Man,
Moves invisible threads of energy.

Desparate gulps become air
Heated by angry bulbs,
By fumes of the family.

Even in vast clouds
Can we pull faces from
Massed moisture,
We can see beginnings
Of thought,
Of lightning between lobes.

Wind
Moves water through air,
Starts currents.

Sends static shock ideas
Radiant blue spectres
To plummet
From towering potential
To smash pellets of anger
Into the dust.

Cycles break bones
Hurtling ice
Thrown from Nimbus hands
To wreck
What Man was so bold
As to create.

Breathing sparks
Chaos,
The beginning of all things
And the eventual ruin
