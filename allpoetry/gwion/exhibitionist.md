Slutty little boy
Wrapped in hot
Fetish,
Sticky longing.
Netting
Captured thick limbs,
Such frail threads
Holding a Man
Together.
Fanning lace
Across flexed muscle
Androgenous thighs,
Testosterone
Pumping velvet throbbing hunger
Over skin.
His body
Defied shape
And curved to the forced
Efforts
Of corsets.
There were Women
In his eyes,
Under his skin
Bursting to be set free.
Kohl and burgundy
Slapped on moistness,
He painted femininity
Over a 5 o’clock shadow,
Pursed his lips
And spread his legs
And invited
The whole world to watch.About a fetish.
