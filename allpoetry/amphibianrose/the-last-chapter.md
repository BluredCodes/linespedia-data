title: "The Last Chapter"

You flipped through the first,
skimmed right on through.
You laughed at the story,
it wasn't about you.

My pages were crisp,
no tears or creases.
Now pages are missing,
pages torn off in pieces.

The first chapter was boring,
you said it was a sleeper.
You got to the middle,
then said it was a keeper.

You were in a hurry
to see how it ended.
Didn't understand the meanings.
Oh, how you pretended.

You finally gave in,
counldn't wait no more.
Didn't care how you did it,
you're rotten to the core.

You thought the ending,
was Happily Ever After.
What you didn't know,
You ended in the Last Chapter.
