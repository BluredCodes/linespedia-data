title: "The Night From Hell"

It was bright and sunny
as we put up the tent.
Camped by the river
where there's tubes you can rent.

WE were all boozed up
and ready for some fun.
Started floating down the river
and noticed there was no sun.

The clouds above darkened,
the sun had gone away.
We kept on floating,
to drunk to care anyway.

WE got to the end,
no bus was in sight.
We walked back slowly
we were staying the night.

Back at the campsite,
we all sat around.
Talking about stupid
stuff, for we were all
tightly wound.

WE quickly passed out,
one person an hour.
After awhile the sky
opened up, and it started
to shower.

We were not all asleep in
out tents, so we did not
notice that two tents
went.

There we were all huddled
in one. Hoping that soon
the damn rain would be done.

The next morning we woke,
wet and cold we did shiver.
Went to find our tents,
oops found them on the river.

Now this story is true,
but you never can tell.
But that is when it
became "The night from hell".


It was damn fun though. LOL
