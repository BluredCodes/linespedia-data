My mom made me cereal,
she burned up my piece of toast.
She tried to make me an omelet,
I thought it was a roast.

My mom made me cereal,
pancakes were hard to make.
She tried to make me some french toast,
and poured in a breakfast shake.

My mom made me cereal,
said bacon would clog my heart.
She was going to make me bisquits,
to open up the cans a start.

My mom made me cereal,
oatmeal is just too bland.
She tried to make me malt-o-meal,
and bought the Cup-o-soup brand.

I tried to eat the cereal,
I was just out of luck,
for a bowl without the cereal,
kind of begins to suck.
