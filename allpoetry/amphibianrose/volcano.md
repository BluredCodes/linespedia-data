---
title: "Volcano"
writer: "amphibianrose"
slug: "volcano"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

I'm raging deep inside,
the end is near.
My tears swell over,
my end, I fear.

I have lain dormant,
so long in this life.
I'm boiling intensely,
cuts like a knife.

I'll spew my toxic poison,
suffocation draws nigh.
My river of hurt finds you,
I don't even cry.

I'll burn all who dare,
even think it's a game.
I leave ashes behind,
No more an old flame.

I'll leave devastation,
that years cannot recover.
To put a stop to life,
the part I didn't smother.

After I have conquered,
again I'll lay to rest.
Now all the world around me,
is sucking upon my breast.
