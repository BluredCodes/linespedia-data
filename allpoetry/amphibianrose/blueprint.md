---
title: "Blueprint"
writer: "amphibianrose"

Everything in my life
and my heart is laid
out for you to see.
Meant for us to build
together, only for a
small fee.

We weren't the same in
many ways, we were to
meet in between.
I didn't plan a playground
here, don't care what
you've seen.

My whole life was laid
on the line, I've shared
my darkest parts.
Now, you can't make up
your mind, you're playing
with two hearts.

So now I've got to say,
these months,were they a waste?
I feel that maybe this whole
game was played in extraordinary
haste.

I'm sorry that's it's come to
this, you will find out soon.
THis was a blueprint of my life,
not a child's cartoon.
