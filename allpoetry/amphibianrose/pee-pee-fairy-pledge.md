We are Pee Pee Fairies,
Just us girls you see.
We pee pee all together,
or sometimes just me.

Our life is dedicated
to pee pee all year round.
We promise to wipe the dribble
away,when TP we have found.

We pee pee here, we pee pee
there, we pee pee all over.
Our pee pee is your good luck
charm, forget the 4 leaf clover.

We Pee Pee Fairies are beautiful,
We're just a chosen few.
If you tend to pee pee right,
we just may recruit you too.

Ms.Trayseelynn is the founder,
of the Pee Pee Fairy Committee.
So talk to her about an application,
signed only in your pee pee.

One more thing before I go,
I know I am brand new.
But if I learn pee pee well, 
can I become one too??
