title: "I'm A Cracker"
writer: "amphibianrose"

I am a cracker,
Not Saltine,
nor Ritz.
How did this name
come about?
It is the pits.

Walking upstairs,
just the other day.
I was called a cracker,
the only one I'd say.

I'm not square,herbed
or cheesy.
I'm not eaten with 
sandwiches, nor am
I greasy.

I do not come in a
box, nor am I shaped
like a fish.
I'm not eaten at a party,
well, I wish.

I may be dry, but I don't
crumble when eaten.
I'm not baked nor fried,
no dough for heatin'.

I just want to know, why
I'm called a crispy wafer.
If that's what we really
are, I don't feel much safer.
