title: "Calgon, Why Didn't You Take Me Away"

I've seen your dumb commercials,
on the damn t.v.
I've sat in the bathtub with
this powder,
relaxed I just can't be.

My child is still screaming 
outside, the dog is still
barking.
My job is no less hectic,
the boss is still harking.

What makes those people
come up with this lie, that
this powder works it's magic.
I still hear of people going insane,
My God, is this not tragic.

I hardly ever see it on t.v.,
though it's still on shelves today.
The only question I'd like to ask,
Is "Calgon, Why Didn't You Take Me Away??"
