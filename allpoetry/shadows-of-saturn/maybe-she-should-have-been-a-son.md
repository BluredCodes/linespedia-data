little sara
bright and pretty
forgot her self sometimes
playing with the older men

little sara 
tried a little rum
and to have some fun
got in the back of a car

little sara
was found today
rotting in a dump
how did she stray so far?

little sara
raped and murdered
by a drunk, like her 
who got off of her innocence

little sara
would have been 15 today
little sara,
gave her life away
little sara,
always bright and pretty

little sara,
her mom can only cry
as she thinks to her self
