he took you to his home
ate through your skin to your bones
and now you feel so alone

but never truely alive 
dropped down from the darkened sky 
never again to be free and fly

I've seen you changed
you were dead for just a day
but a price you must pay

dump your heart into a glass jar
now you are all rotten inside
but you've come to far

you've lost your wings
and forgotton to fly
to become A bat is to lose your wings
and now you can never come homeI guess this poem is about how people try to hide from the truth but lose a lot in the process
