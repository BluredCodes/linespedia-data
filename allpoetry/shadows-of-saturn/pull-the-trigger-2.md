they've changed say she's still got a chance
stuck between now and then
 she'll never remember the past

the bed is warm but she's not there
maybe she's just in the bathroom
but she's stuck in this tough snare

how long will this last they ask
a suicidal freak in the color
they don't think they will get her back

no ones there to help her out
a girl easy to get by the road side
her life is gray in doubt

she's on this downward slide
a bullet found through the window
a ruling for a bloody homicide

she's running running to her past
a cobweb path follows her home
she's running out of gas too fast

Mary Mary now is all fed up
doctors find her in the trash
her wrists and throat all cut up

maybe it was all meant to be
just a tired soul trying to sleep
but now there is nothing to see

Mary was buried today
this burial rite all down now
they can find nothing  to say
