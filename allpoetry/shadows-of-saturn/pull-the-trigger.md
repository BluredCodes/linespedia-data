She's in paradise-
a fool in the dark
just a sparrow in a poisioned bird bath

she's found him a little
house; on the edge of 
the earth dive off

she's lost herself again
now she can never go back
she's got to pull the trigger for him

now there is nobody left
so lick your wounds and start to turn back
but she's sold her heart to end her in debt

anonymous caller on the phone
says he knows from way back then
before she flew from her home

a forgotten friend from the other side
now he's found her by the ocean
she's swept out with the tide

she pulls the trigger - blasts his bones
she thinks she knows her way back home

now they are both in hospital beds
her white frail body all dead
and now they say she will never come backman this took me sooo long to write :)
