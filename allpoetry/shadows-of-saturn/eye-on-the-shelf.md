Midnight eyes on the 
shelf; forever waiting but
always shut 

a broken sigh rises
a blink in time, never found
last dream drowned

A star in the rough
she looks to far, always sought
moonlit nights

red blood shed tonight
but forgot the stars still bright
-bide a ride

a freedom taken-
never won, someones lover
dies tonightI really cant think of a title
