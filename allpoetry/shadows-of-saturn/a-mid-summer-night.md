---
title: "A Mid-Summer Night"
writer: "shadows of saturn"

when I'm alone
the sun never sets
when I'm with you 
I can cry and laugh

but you say you didn't miss me
that you don't remeber
anything but bittersweet days
that we have faded in the sun

now its too late to seal
this frozen gap 
going to sleep knowing
I will never see you

but if I say I missed you
that I remeber everything
would you agree too,
or just become blind again?

now were are lost in the spring mud
washed away by yet another flood
healing the dying birds 
by getting them off the ground

maybe things are meant to be that way
only remebered when the sun is high
and when the stakes are low
but I can never forget
how I have changed
