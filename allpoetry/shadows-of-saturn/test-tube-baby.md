title: "Test Tube Baby"

Reaching for something lost
a mother finds
shes lost her wings

lock your heart into a
test tube; watch it
explode

sell your soul for nothing
feel it rot inside
and turn you inside out

fall off the world-
come to the surface
breathing fire

a lonly mother watches
each wing
be torn and shredded
she only waits for tomarrow
and the new sunlight
