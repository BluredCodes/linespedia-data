title: "Random Acts of Kindness"

Throw my burdens to the wind
Release all anger, hate and sin
Love, show me compassion
Before time comes crashing

Melt my bitter cold heart of snow
So a temperate one may soon grow
Behold, while Mother Nature dies
New life unfolds before our eyes

Random acts of kindness
Delicate as lace and fineness
Pride, depart this aching soul
I dislike feeling frail and small

A single, selfless unimportant favor
Delivered by a gracious mortal savior
Shines brighter than a blazing star
Bringing happiness near and far

Like apple seeds upon a tree
Good deeds long to be set free
Sing a humble song of peace
May humanity never cease
