I need a permanent vacation
I’ve got not one drop of patience
Hop a space taxi to the moon
I need a relaxing vacation soon

I think I am going crazy
I’m a neurotic pink daisy
Deliver me now, anywhere
Not to Hell, been there

I really need a quiet vacation
Without the slightest hesitation
A charming, deserted seashore nook
Somewhere I don’t do chores or cook

A cozy, warm, human-free niche
Red roses in bloom, mushroom quiche
I want to run to lands so far away
Damn it, fly me to nirvana today!

I need a nice vacation
To end all my frustration
Send me up to the skies
A vacation in paradise
