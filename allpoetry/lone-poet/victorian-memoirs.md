Elegant burgundy velvet boudoir pillows,
Soirees and plays, wintry ice-skating follies
Hope's promise of enticing new tomorrows,
Cheery remembrances, enchanting holiday jollies

Frosty nipped noses, mulled apples and spice,
Lavender sachets, wool rugs of homespun threads
Yule sugarplums, tiny tots, all sweet and nice,
Slumber warm and snug in spacious canopy beds

Jack Frost’s wonderland of joys, 
Dickens and miserly old Scrooge 
Grand festivities for girls and boys,
Séances, tearooms, Moulin Rouge

Sleigh bells jingle, galloping hooves,
Fresh pines loom over the town square
Smoke billows from chimneystack grooves,
Smell of excitement lingers in the air

Delicate alabaster, damask rose beauties,
Fine strands of lovely embroidered filet lace
Woven silk twine, crocheted baby booties,
Fate's ghosts etched upon time and space… 

…Antiquated memoirs of Victorian grace
