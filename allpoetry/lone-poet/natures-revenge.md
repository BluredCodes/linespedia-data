title: "Nature's Revenge"

Mother Nature has her way
Of seeking vengeance on Man,
We pollute her sacred land every day
Not part of Gaia's higher plan 

Our blatant lack of self-respect,
Hiding behind evil, greed, and shame
Seedlings drown in tidal waves of neglect,
Not part of humankind's grand game

Selfless and divine won't win
Blame won't do us any good,
We mortals are so full of sin
Hell's flames burnt all our food

Earthquakes, hurricanes, floods, smoggy skies,  
Forsaking helpless children and animals of joy
When will these uncivilized beasts ever realize,
That forests and rivers weren't meant to destroy?

Took any free haven we could find
And gave back not one honorable thing,
Terrorists went and blew out peace's mind
As Democracy's finger pulled fate's trigger 

Someday this whole sorry planet will blow
Nobody who's still alive will want to be found,
Disease, war, hate, and pain are all we know,
The only sound will be bombs hitting the ground

No love, no babies, no pets, no fun,
No heaven, no oceans, moon, sun, stars
Only fools with missiles, knives and guns,
An army of crusaders feathered and tarred

Comes the world's end, Earth's final judgment day
Will we naively bow down upon our knees and pray,
Or will we all just turn around and slowly walk away?
Can our unworthy eternal souls be saved anyway?
