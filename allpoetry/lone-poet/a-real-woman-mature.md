How does modern society define
A real female, what is "womanly"?
One whose virgin soul is of the divine
Or whose pale existence is chosen anonymously?

Given no voice, choice or purpose, trapped in her life
Submissive and frightened, blindly follows along
Cluelessly serves and obeys as a "good little wife"
All she knows to be right is dismissed as sinfully wrong

Is it "unfeminine" for girls to wear pants?
Do big, buxom boobs and curls bed more guys?
Do giggly blondes have more fun and romance,
Or do they just hear more chauvinistic lies?

Smart, strong, pretty, talented, charming, well-versed
Not weak, groveling, unheard, raped, abused, and defiled
But sensual Goddess; clever, sincere words unrehearsed
Whose role isn't only to breed and breastfeed a child

In my eyes, a real woman is someone
Who holds head high, clings tight to faith
She doesn't settle for just mere common
Follows her own righteously destined path

Keen sense of humor, heart of 14k gold
Positive attitude between hormonal flows
Glowing inner beauty, innocence never grows old
Creative genius, bold, sensitive, an ethereal rose 

To me, a truly complete woman means so much more
Than monthly cycles, orgasms, chores, or birth
Equality bleeds a river of love that freely pours
Needs no slovenly male to determine her untold worth

No ego-bruised slave driver or disgruntled ex
Her cunt desired only for cheap, amusing sex
Comes and goes as she chooses, reaping her tired life
As brave warrior, poet, mother, sister, wife

A compassionate person of honor who fights hard to keep
What belongs held tight inside earthy, weathered hands
Untethered spirit allowed to laugh, hurt and weep
Never hides her tears and intuitively understands;
A REAL woman is one who lives and dies, who feels passion down deep

What does it mean to be a female living in a male dominated society, and how do you define what a "real" woman is?
Here is what I think it means...I'd like to hear your ideas, too.
