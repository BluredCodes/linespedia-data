I found a new game we big kids can play
Hold in tiny hands a squishy ball of clay
Roll it all around and mold 'til you form
An itsy-bitsy, wiggly, creepy crawly worm

Now close snugly your sleepy eyes
Gaze deeply into the moonlit skies
Feel your magical creation of art
Imagine it has a real soul and heart

Let it dance and hug your arm
Pretend you’re on a fairy farm
And bugs are your fairy friends
Where no great fantasy ever ends
