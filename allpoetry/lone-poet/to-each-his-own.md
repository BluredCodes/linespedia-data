We hate each other
Sister and brother
We say awful things
That bigotry brings

To each his own
Just leave me alone
The root of all evils
Isn’t money, but believers

We righteously pray to loving pseudo-gods
Yet sin and kill, fight fate with steel rods
Truth lies in humanity's open heart and mind
As faith and hope get shoved up our closed behind


© 2/12/02, 3/11/04 SmSThis was not to imply that anyone's deity is better or more "real" than others...or that there are "fake" gods.
I respect almost all religions, even 'tho I may not agree with them...unless they promote hate and evil. All I ask is that you respect mine, too. 
I meant the poem rhetorically, as the teachings of someone who practices hate and bigotry and sin...then says they worship "God"...in my opinion, is a hypocrite...as no truly loving, forgiving and righteous God would promote evil beliefs or tell anyone to kill and hate other religions simply for being different than theirs. They are basically mocking their creator, using His/Her/It/ET's name in vain by saying they were told to perform such crimes or disrespect by their "God". 
So in essence their gods are "pseudo" which means "fake" or "a person who makes deceitful pretenses" or "not genuine but having the appearance of", or it's the followers who are not genuine, not true to themselves or to the loving God they are supposedly bowing down to. 
Sorry for the confusion...did any of this make sense? Think I just made everyone more confused (and madder) than they were.
Peace, love and fairy dust.
