I banged my brains on a pole
Now I can barely think at all
Dented my thick skull of mush
If only I landed on my tush

I need to look where I tread
So I don’t end up hurt or dead
Oh, why can’t I just walk
Pay attention while I talk?

Ms. Klutz is my new nickname
Falling down alot isn't my aim
What’s a clumsy girl like me to do
When she feels all black and blue?

Go get a life, hop out of bed
Put a hard hat atop her head
Stand up tall and loudly scream
“I want a piece of the American dream!”;

...Hey, watch out for that low beam!
