Sheer Poetry is one very wacky freaky girl
Streaks buff naked thru the place day and night
Upon her perverse head she carries a stuffed squirrel 
Does the Pee Pee Fairy dance as verses she writes 

She wears a greasy wig of Chinese soup noodles
Sits atop her hairy skull beside that loopy squirrel
Next to a chicken cackling at cheddar cheese Doodles
Yes, methinks Sheer is a super freaky Giggle Girl

“The kind you don’t bring home to mother”
Sonia craves and eats bags of Chili Cheese Fritos
Treats friends like a sister, brother, or significant other
Sends big care packages filled to the brim with Doritos

Her sweetheart still loves her, albeit she is wild and crazy
‘Cause she can repair computers while she streaks and flashes
Quite a young charmer, even 'tho her geeky mind’s a bit hazy
Saves the day when your new fonging piece of shit crashes

She can also read your pained thoughts; senses mine all the time
‘Tho that’s not too hard considering my brain is a stark void
She pens dark and funny poems, but never do her verses rhyme
Unless she curses in them, making some people on AP annoyed

What more can I say about my humble bardic friend Sheer Poetry?
Her endless jokes keep me laughing so hard I pee my poopy pants
No wonder folks swarm around her like a bevy of retarded bumblebees
She’s sweeter than raw honey, was our group's meeting by chance?

I don’t think I shall ever find another girl kinder than Sheer
She’s also a gifted, caring and giving person, this I do know
Of all the different human beings living on Earth far and near
Thanks to one good, worthy soul my world continues to grow

This may sound like bunghole kissing, but my fumbling words are sincere
I rarely say anything nice about dumb jerks and strangers I just met
But you can be sure my hazel eyes will shed a million blissful tears
Reading the amazing poetic works of the super freakiest chick I’ve seen yet!!
