title: "A Blessing for U.S."

May we Americans be granted safe haven,
Health, wealth, wisdom, shelter and food
May our wounds heal and lost souls be saved,
Turn bad intentions to dust, reveal only good

Let’s celebrate purity of mind, spirit, and heart,
And sow seeds of respect, honesty, faith, and trust
Put the past behind, get off to a great new start,
While we abolish hate, fear, greed, and disgust

May we laugh, sing, love, find a way to cope and care, 
Team together as one, humankind united now until eternity
Hold blessings of peace, freedom, and hope lightly upon the air,
Release our prayers into the ethers, share sweet dreams of plenty
