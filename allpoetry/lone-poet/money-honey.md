title: "Money, Honey"
writer: "Lone Poet"

Money's not funny
when you're flat broke
my pee is beehive honey
buys a liter of Coke

hey, give me lots and lots
for three late payment overdues
why, it's like little kiss-up robots
disgustingly sweet "I love you's"

alive and well
said I'm fine
gone to hell
paid my fine

had no bread to meet the Source's dues
arranged for Satan to enforce a deadly loan
sold my soul for water pills and PMS blues,
now all I do is moan, piss, and groan!
