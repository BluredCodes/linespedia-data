---
title: "~Moon Slices~"
writer: "Irish Pumpkin"
slug: "moon-slices"
source: "AllPoetry"

peering upwards

through the forested canopy

that obscures the night skye

moon winking down at us

from her perch among the stars

envious of our passion

trading laughter and heat

scoured away my sadness

replaced it with romance and gladness

time seems different when we're together

which is always, in our heart

singular since the joining

now i imagine you in sweet slumber

a smile comes to my lips

and my torment is finally at peace

gratification seeps from my pores

thank you for finding me

and beauty hath no value unappreciated

so few words to express

would but there were another word for "love"

such a common and false occurence in these times

seems cheapened and overused, a little cliche

i would say love, but it isn't true...

love is only so deep, my darling

while this is bottomless, depthless

righteous in our bonding

separate souls blended

a feeling of completion

an ode to moon slicesMoon slices...when the moon is but a silvered sliver resting upon the cloudless sky.
