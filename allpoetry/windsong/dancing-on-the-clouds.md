---
title: "Dancing On The Clouds"
writer: "Windsong"

To be in your arms forever
feels like dancing on the clouds,
looking at the stars above,
reaching out and touching my dreams
feeling your heat next to me, 
your unique scent enveloping me
in a sweet embrace,
your loving gaze
caressing my skin 
sending tremors in my body,
expecting
unspoken promises
passion burning....
we don't need words
our eyes speak out the love we share.
Dreaming......
dreams becoming reality,
High above the clouds
we float with the wind
its wings taking us from one star to another.
Mesmerized I look at you,
silver moon reflecting in your eyes,
afraid the dream will end.....
We are in love 
and dancing on the clouds
