---
title: "Remember"
writer: "Windsong"
slug: "remember"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Warm fingers 
touching,
exploring,
remembering
shapes and forms,
warmth of the other

~*~

Hands holding,
eyes looking into eyes,
memories of other times 
dawning on you 
knowing 
you made love before,
...familiar scent...

~*~

Flames of passion 
engulfing
warm bodies 
moving 
like the waves at sea,
deep breathing, 
drinking from your presence

~*~

Two hearts beating as one 
We're holding tight,
two souls becoming one 
...as I whisper your name
