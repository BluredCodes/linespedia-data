~*~

Hidden in isolation,
running from my friends,
locked myself in silence,
hiding in the night.

Ocean of tears dried
by my spirit's pain,
the never-ending searching....
Endlessly aching,
a shallow life I live,
cloaked in pretending

So many questions
and so few answers
to be found within my being,
alive but not living...

No one can hear
my silent cries for help,
the darkness pulling me deeper
to the bottom of despair.

With blind eyes I look up
to the shinning sun
deaf to the bird' wondrous song.
Can dreams and hopes,
the courage I once held in my heart
Save me now?
