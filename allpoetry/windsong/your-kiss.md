---
title: "Your Kiss"
writer: "Windsong"
slug: "your-kiss"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

I feel your kiss
 before our lips are touching,
 I could see it in your eyes,
 sweet promise of things to come,
 Time stands still
 for an eternity, 
 flashes of past lifetimes.
 Tasting
 the sweetnes of your lips
 before
 you are touching mine,
 the one and only arousing the passion
 anticipation.......
 Awakening the memories
 burried deep in our dreams,
 blood running hot,
 hearts pounding
 we're lost amongst the stars.
 Warm breath closer
 setting my senses on fire, 
 streams of passion evolving,
 bringing us together,
 opening ourselves, mixing emotions,
 flesh finally touching,
 unbearable heat,
 Burning!
 A new beginning 
 starts with just a kiss
