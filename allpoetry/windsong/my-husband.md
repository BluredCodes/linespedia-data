---
title: "My Husband"
writer: "Windsong"
slug: "my-husband"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60706-My-Husband-by-Windsong"
---

Everytime I look at you,
Years of pain and loneliness 
Wash over me,
Lost trust and lies, pretending to be happy,
Flashbacks of what has been,
Deep hurt of words unsaid,
The lost moments, deceiving ourselves.
Dreaming again about the day I found you,
When you were everything I ever wanted,
Looking into your eyes made me believe I was
In another world, where love can conquer
And accomplish everything.....
Until the day you hurt me,
My heart still bleeding  from your words,
A bold of lightening burning through my veins.
Is that the day my love died
And started looking with different eyes?
Is that the day I first saw the real you?
All there is now is emptiness and pain!
Can I ever rise from this pain 
And look at you with laughing eyes?
