---
title: "Time Passing"
writer: "Windsong"
slug: "time-passing"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56038-Time-Passing-by-Windsong"
---

As days pass, 
the silence 
cries softly inside me
like a small pond with 
dead leaves floating.

As days pass, 
your reflection fades,
hidden in the deepest 
part of my heart.

And all it takes to bring you up
is to picture the fire in your eyes.
All it takes to bring you up 
even when I ache to forget you,
is to close my eyes and 
feel your gentle hand 
touching my face.

All it takes to bring you up 
is the feel of your sweet lips on mine.
As days pass, 
the silence 
cries softly inside me
like a gently winding river.

As days pass, the silence 
cries softly inside me,
soft whispers of 
what love could be
