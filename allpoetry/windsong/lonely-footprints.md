title: "Lonely Footprints"
writer: "Windsong"

As I walk on the moonlit beach
All I see behind 
Are the lonely footprints
I left in the cold sand,
As if you were never there with me,
The shimmering stars reminding me 
It was only a dream
About longs walks and holding hands,
And kisses quenching 
The fire inside our souls.
I look behind me 
On the moonlit beach
And all I see
Are the lonely footprints 
I left in the cold sand. 
I wish I could go back again,
Dream about long walks and holding hands
And kisses quenching the fire in my soul
