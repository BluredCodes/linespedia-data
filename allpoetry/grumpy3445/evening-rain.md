Dark clouds form on the western sky
underneath, in the darkness
bright flashes and rolling thunder
expose the maelstrom within
nature senses the coming storm
but I take your hand
and walk into the downpour
we are drenched
yet the fire within burns hotter
embracing, we ignore the danger
the water it just another sensation
when I kiss your lips
arms, intertwined
hands, questing
as fast as it approached
the storm is gone
leaving us breathless
