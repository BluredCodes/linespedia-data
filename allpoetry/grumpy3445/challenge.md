Your love is my challenge
to win your heart
my heart must be big
you pull me out of my comfort zone
so that our passion is secret
yet what I wouldn't do for you
your quick mind matches mine
and your beauty is greater
than Helen of Troy
I would march to war for you
but this war is in my head
of the complex interaction
between love and lust
desire and satisfaction
as I climb higher toward the summit
the closer I get to your heart
warm and sweet is the clime
and I never want to leave
