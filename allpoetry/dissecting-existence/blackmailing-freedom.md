You are born or
Adopted into and
By me, swearing
To me your allegiance.

I am your lover, and
Your fighter, I am
The one who gives
You what you can’t buy.

I am the one you
Die for, and the one
You mourn for, within
Me you find your happiness.

Pay no attention to the
Means that I use, as I 
Choke you with a smile,
Taking your paycheck.

And as the knife pricks
Your skin, understand
That you must obey me
To keep your precious life.

You have your freedom, but
I must say what that
Is.  You are a slave to 
Freedom, and I, it’s master.

Now give me your children, I’ll
Teach them to love and grow
In me.  Teach them not to 
Question their supposed freedom.

Yes, I am the hate of your
Life, but tell the World
You love me, as I blackmail
You with your own freedom.
