Put another dead man on my wall
It's time to mourn again
This epidemic, this self-propelled disease,
Drug-inflicted desires
What are they for, what purpose but to destroy
The genius minds we've come to admire

Oh and it feels so good- the way
My mind drowned itself in ecstasy
When the flow in my blood felt like silk.
Then time after time again it entered me
Warm, loose, but fogged my brain to sleep
The day I became sick was the day I caught on
And the day I threw it away.

The sadness, the romanticism of 
The loss & need for it
The time & talent wasted for it's 
Fatal kisses, love sicknesses
Throw it all away for a day of pain
Live inside my eyes now, friend
What you've come to be...a sweet memory
A long for a kiss, a subtle swish
Of the feelings you've given me.



A*B*C 
For Layne Staley
