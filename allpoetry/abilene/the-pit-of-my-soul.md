---
title: "The Pit of My Soul"
writer: "Abilene"
slug: "the-pit-of-my-soul"
source: "AllPoetry"

I want to stop this long enough
so that I can open my arms
And fall back into a musical of tranquility
A horrorshow for them, the beautiful leaves
falling so lightly you forget that they 
are symbolic of time

I open my arms- stretch 'em out
And all of nothing happens-
Maybe a release of the last of my energies
into the suffocating air of the office
I fall into the lids of tired eyes
Fall like a stone into the pit of my soul

I find the loves of my life 
hovering there like angels
Drawing me patterns of joyful memory
I could cry from the beauty of it,
but smile instead with the dreaming
Just a moment of reverie, then into white walls I stare...
