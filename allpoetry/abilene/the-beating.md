Aaah, the love in this little body is rising
A careening wave of lava, a burning ecstasy
In my mouth I can feel the heat of my heart
A thump and a bump jarring my bones into lunacy

My eyes burn with their dibilitating glare
Fingers moving like liquid upon ivory skin
I long to expel my voice and feel
Trapped inside this little body, the beating

My heart & hips burning out like a wet wick
The beating slows and moves out like mortar
Heat pushes out of me in beads of sweat water
The flame in my eye is a black burning ember

I'm holding in my coming apart with heavy chain
Where I found it I couldn't understand
To let go, I wouldn't know where to begin
It is not my hands that hold, but my hips

My power remains in the beating of my heart
Thumping and bumping my brain into bits
Cold thoughts could it be, not really, maybe
But a heat so passionate as if to melt the devil

I feel this lava and I'm consistantly earning
The flint in my eyes, responsive to the heat
Flowing the kiss from my lips and tongue
Tranquility in numbers, come rolling in beats
