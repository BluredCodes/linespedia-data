title: "'Come, May!'"
writer: "CookieZeal"

Peek-a-boo flowers
	    from
howdy-do showers

honey-bugs prance
             the
    fertility dance

Earth is suspecting
           that
April’s expecting


full to the quiver
         and 
pregnant as ever!
