While walking, I espied
the melding senses and felt the
beauty
as I ~

viewed some melody propel
from the poised finical beak
of a swollen red robin-
well-nigh saw the colored waves
timbre launched in sparkling air-

audienced a frolicked dance
the vortex of laughing leaves
indulged by some quick spring wind
swirling in frantic circle
sun-flitt’ring fairy’s ballet-

as tasting bright and yellow stars
amidst an em’rald carpet,
my eye was fed delicious sight
by the humbled dandelion
during nature’s promenade-

smelled the perfume of the sun-
musing for His own appeal
given for our fellowship
the loving fragrance through His earth
hyssop fresh from Yahweh’s eyes.
