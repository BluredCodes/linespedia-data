What is this stuff -
icey rain turned to snow
in the middle of March,
anyone know?

Just as my tanned knees
delight to go bare
that ol'crazy North wind 
plain doesn’t care!

Nope-

it flips me off
with its whipping cold
kills the tulip 
as it starts to unfold.

Tell me dear God, 
is this only a test?
the plants are confused,
and I am a mess!

Won’t you send me
a sign that this
will end -
very soon before 
arthritus sets in?

Uh oh- 

but look!
there’s a fly on the door
and where there is one 
there must be some more

O glory-
for once 
though they come from decay, 
this least favorite insect
has just made my day!Written March 27th, 2002 ©  on Mar 27 2002 02:51 AM PST, M. Dianne Grotius Berry    humor
