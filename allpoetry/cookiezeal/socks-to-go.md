I took the chance to find the mate 
that matched the other from the crate,
but from the bunch which I had found
one was crewed, the other round;

Some were dotted from linted cling,
others threaded with kinky string-
and while I searched at midnight hour
from a brief and powdered shower,
I saw the one that I paid dear
monogrammed but single here.

So now I grope in further search
(my Nike's hungry for the perch)
 to my eyes it fell to sight 
I'll make real sure the pair is right~

Ah, never mind! forget the blend
I'm matching shades, ta' heck with trend-
So if I'm wearing socks to please
it's one of those, and part of these!
