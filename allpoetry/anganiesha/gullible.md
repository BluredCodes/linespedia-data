title: "Gullible"

I believe every time you tell me
That you are just worried about me
Every time I turn around you
It’s more numbers that I see

You tell me that I’m messing up
When you’re the one who’s doing wrong
Just when I think everything is going well
You turn around and still do wrong

You make me get my hopes all up
Thinking that you’re going to keep your word
I don’t know how many times that I forgave you
And you expect me to keep my word

I thought that we could work things out
Cause it’s been 2 ½ years
But every time I turn around
My eyes are still filled with tears
