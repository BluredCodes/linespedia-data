---
title: "Gone Forever"
writer: "Anganiesha"
slug: "gone-forever"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55937-Gone-Forever-by-Anganiesha"
---

Why do you want to make me miserable
By saying you were with a girl.
How many times do I have to tell you
I’m not worried ‘bout you and that girl

I will never allow you to come back to me
You will never be in my heart
I am so glad that we’re not together
I’m glad that we’re torn apart

My stress is leaving, I’m moving on
I now know not to be weak
I wish you some luck, I hope she does you wrong
And knocks you right off of your feet
