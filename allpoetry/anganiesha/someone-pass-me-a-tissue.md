Someone pass me a tissue
This is a serious issue
I thought I could move on in my life
I can’t because it’s a hard fight

What did I do to deserve this
I tried to be honest and perfect
But it left me in tears
Finding that no one is here

I look in the mirror
 I see the horrible terror
I’m sick of the lies,
And staying up late with cries

Why are people out to get me
I tried to be so damn friendly
But in the end it just got me
No where, and more lonely

Someone pass me a tissue
In the end I won’t miss you
When I see you I’ll diss you
I’ll be passing you the damn tissue

I didn’t deserve this pain
My strength will regain
I thought I was insane
But it is me that I blame
