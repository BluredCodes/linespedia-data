I knew that you were going out
Cause of the way you answered the phone
You said your friend had called because
He was chillen all alone

Come to find out, you were lying
He was over some other girls’ house
I thought that you loved me, and there would be no more lies
Now what is all this about

Do I really deserve this, what you’re putting me through
Was I really that bad to you
I tried to be there and give you my all
When you were down I tried to stand tall

Why can’t you be honest, and give me the respect I deserve
I never tried to do you wrong
I would stay in the house, waiting for you to call
And you still said I did you wrong
