---
title: "Never Going Back"
writer: "Anganiesha"

Every time I think of you
I want to hit you right in your face
You think that I can’t do without you
But someone already took your place

Every time I hear your name
I would think about you and I
I spit on that path, I’ll never turn back
You know not to even ask me why

I don’t know how we lasted that long
Cause all you ever did me was wrong
I thought that I would always be sad
Be sad I thought I would always be

I dried my eyes, and prayed to god
To lift this hurt off of me
It took me some time but now I realize
I have the strength now I can see
