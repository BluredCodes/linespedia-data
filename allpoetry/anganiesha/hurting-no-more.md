title: "Hurting No More"
writer: "Anganiesha"

Why do you want to hurt me,
And feed my heart with lies.
I don’t why you can’t understand.
That I will no longer be filled with cries

You try to make me sad
By saying you were with someone else
I don’t need you to stress me out
I’m glad you were with someone else

You know I love you, always will
But that doesn’t mean I’ma always be there for you.
I hate your ways, you try to play
I hate everything that you do

You need to grow up, leave me alone
I’m putting you deep in my past.
For all the hurt and confusion you caused
I’m saying you can kiss my ass
