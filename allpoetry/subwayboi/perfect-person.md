I shouldn't even touch you,
 To touch the face of perfection would be to jade it.
 This is like looking at perfection,
 Your sleeping body lying next to me. 
 What have I done to deserve this blessing?
 There isn't enough time 
 In the universe
 To examine your face
 When every single detail,
 The light bouncing off your lip,
 Should be studied and catalogued.
 People have this idea of
 The Perfect Person.
 Someone without flaws,
 Without errors, 
 Without mistakes,
 Without humanity.
 But my idea of
 The Perfect Person
 Is one who can touch me,
 Hold me,
 Make me think,
 And disagree with me.
 Tell me I'm wrong, 
 Tell me I'm an idiot,
 Make me rethink.
 You don't have to be a supermodel to be
 The Perfect Person.
 You just have to be
 All that you are.Sorry if this is too sappy for you. I was just in the mood for looooove ;)
