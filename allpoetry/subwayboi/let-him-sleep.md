they sit in the forest together, waiting
 she watches them come and heal him
 feed him, clean him, make him new again
 she will be watching them walk into the woods
 miles after they can see her not
 she is the north

 and so he recovers from the long night's journey
 resting peacefully in a womb of linens
 breathing in time to the flow of energy
 counting the stars on his head
 she waits

 the journey was arduous for them both, this is true,
 but she has recovered much sooner than he
 and looking upon him
 his lips soft as petals
 she decides to let him sleepI don't know what this is. I'm sketchy now as I write this. It's dedicated to a special guy named Greg.
