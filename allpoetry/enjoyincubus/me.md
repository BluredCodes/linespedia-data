what do you think of me?
are you scared?
do you want to flee?
just because im not like them
donsnt mean you cant be my friend
your the straight line
and im the bend
why do they do it?
i cant comprehend
just try to get used to me
cuz this is the way ill be
from now till the last day
my ways wont fray
so get used to me 
and be a nice person
because once you get to know what i am
you wont have to pretent
