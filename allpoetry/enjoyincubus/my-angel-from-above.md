i the lonely guy
wanted to walk over to say hi
but when i find the words shes gone
and i wanted to cry
its so lame 
when the girls play this game
its like i have to train
just for her to say hi
why should i waste my time?
they look at me like a comited a crime
but one day ill find someone i love 
like my angel from abovewhat do you think? is the ending a little dull?
