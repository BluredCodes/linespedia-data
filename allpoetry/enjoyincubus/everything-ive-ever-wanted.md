There she was
Beautiful as could be
She’s mine now
Her love is all I see

I'm having the best time
There’s nothing more to lose
I have the good catch
She’s she best

I’ll never let her go
My love for her will always show
I can’t wait to see her
But only once

After lunch
When I hug her good bye
The girl of my dreams
My have we bonded
Because she’s everything I’ve ever wanted
