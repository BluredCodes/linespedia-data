do you like me?
will you keep me as i be?
i saw you with one guy
you changed him 
he seemd he wanted to die
keep people as they are
dont trap them in your jar
and like me for me
not cuz of my car
if your mad
lets work things out
i dont want you to be sad
when we go out
dont scream and shout
i want things to go well
i dont want to be in hell
ill be me
and you be you
and we can go out
theres nothing to loose
