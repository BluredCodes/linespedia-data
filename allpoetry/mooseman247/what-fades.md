in a room of children's toys
everything is broken
and I hear the monster
coming out
to play

all the little girls and boys
everyone is broken
and I fear the monster
he's coming out

wake to a father's voice
with a stranger's face

staring at cameras
in a scary place

and years from now it seems
it never fades away

afraid you lost your self-esteem
everyone is broken
but you know your beauty
brings nothing
but pain

in a room of waking dream
everything is broken
and think you're sorry
for nothing

woke to a stranger's voice
with your father's face

staring at cameras
in a scary place

years from now it never
fades away

a kind of hurt that never heals
everyone is broken
you know the story
and the side
it reveals

these thoughts can spindle torture wheels
everything is broken
you know the scar
in your side

it never fades away
never fades awayNot my story... not exactly.
