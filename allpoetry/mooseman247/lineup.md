This light just seems to slow the room
a deeper gloom just breeds asphyxiation

I can't put any image through
the sound confusing everything around
it's not only me

a lineup, or anonymous zoo?
I can't get any image through
the sound

it's not only me

fortified identities
broadcasting their obscenities
from left, to right, in front of me
I feel surreal serenity

it's not only me...

---\

I know that I'd forget my head
if it were not so carefully attatched
to me

and truth is a shard of glass at 10 feet
turn your back on death, and it all makes sense
for a moment

a lineup, or anonymous zoo?
I can't get any image through
the sound

fortified identities
broadcasting their obscenities
from left, to right, ahead of me
I feel...

it's not only me
--------------who can't see.Still angry at you.
