God if you're listening
I just heard the flash
whispered in my ear
falling sideways, fast approaching
horizon disappears

a wall of metal brimstone
swirling like the sun
whispered in my ear
no horizon...

Earth and Sky
bridged by Fire
a stairway to Hell
made of plutonium

God if you're watching
I just saw the flash
screaming loud and clear
chain reaction, fast approaching
and desolate for years

a wall of cars and lamposts
the peace of everyone
preying on your fears
of cremation...

Earth and Sky
bridged by Fire
a stairway to nowhere
made of plutonium

God if you're up there
please turn off the light
praying on my fears
running backwards, fast approaching
horizon disappears

a wall of charged electrons
flying like the sun
screaming loud and clear
breif elation...

Earth and Sky
bridged by Fire
a stairway to Hell
a stairway to nowhere
made of plutoniumFlash...
