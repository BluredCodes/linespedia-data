I have many allergies
though, mostly in my nose
a list that covers head to toe
and this is how it goes

dust mites give me the sniffles
pollen stings my eyes
god knows what would happen
if I ever touched a fly

asprin makes me drowsy
pet hair makes me itch
pine oil makes me queasy
and puking is a bad... thing.

peanut butter pains me
lactose makes me sick
needles are unnerving
you'd better make this quick

the flu shot gave me measles
this cold sore isn't cold
took some penicillin
all it killed was mold

chicken gives me goosebumps
turkey gives me chills
this would all be better
if I could only swallow pills

ceramics make me carsick
plastics make me choke
used a plastic spoon once
since then, I've hardly spoke

(..at the very least,
I've had difficulty conjugating verbs,
as you can see..)

*ahem*

queasenarts are scary
ovens make me weep
I think I'm going crazy
toasters give me the creeps

alas, I've gone off subject
I think you get the gist
if someone steals my benadryl
I know that I'll be pissed.Stupid allergies... stupid dust bunnies... stupid wormholes...
