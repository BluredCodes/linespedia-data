title: "Firedamp"
writer: "Mooseman247"

started in the summer of '22
never thought it would be
the end of you

started out just shoveling coal
never thought I'd find you
at the bottom of a hole


it's just a little mining town
nothing can happen here


work long hours because the family's poor
comes home dirty, tired, and ready
for more

looking out like a brother should
sometimes looking out
isn't any good


but it's just a little mining town
nothing can happen here


kettle bottoms float overhead
the southern slope fell
leaving 20 dead

little lights go crawling in
if margins hold up,
then it's not a sin

it's just a little mining town
nothing can happen here

your bells
and your falling
your birds
and your crawling

little lights...

yellow birds in orange light
fell asleep on perch
one summer night

a brother went home with a hacking cough
see you tomorrow
you can sleep it off

just another mining town
nothing can happen here

firedamp... it filled the mine
the population noticed,
but not in time

a cutter's spark set the mix ablaze
all breath was shut out
by the traveling flames

just another mining town
just a little mining town
nothing can happen
nothing can happen

your bells
and your falling
your birds
and your crawling
little lights
little lights

go crawling in...For those of you who DON'T research old miner lingo... "FIREDAMP" is an air/methane mixture, uslually 5-15 percent. Highly explosive
