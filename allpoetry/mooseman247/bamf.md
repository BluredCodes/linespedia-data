I'd rather be stupid
and unimportant
than witness what I fear

I'd rather be crazy
and paranoid
than be sane, and sound sincere

'cuz if my dreams come true
they would be visions of the end

and if my dreams came true
then your walls are caving in

please God tell me
I'm not a prophet
but a plastic propheteer

I'd rather be crazy
not right in my head
than hitting nails up here

I'd rather dissappear

>

thank you... and for my next trick...if anyone was wondering about the title... BAMF is the noise that Nightcrawler (of the X-Men) would make when he teleported elsewhere.
