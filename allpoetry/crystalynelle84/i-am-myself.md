title: "I Am Myself"

I want a world where everyone is treated equally
If only one person contributes, they help a lot
Is it possible that every person could help?
This is our world - we need to protect it.

I need my friends more than they know
When I feel down, they embrace me
When I feel joyous, they smile and laugh with me
Where would I be without them?

I dream of a true love that wins over all
Perhaps some find it to be silly ...
But I know what I want
Without love, why would I want to live?

I know an amazing God
Some don't need Him as much as me
But He is a part of myself
Without the Father, would I be myself?This poem is a translation of a poem I originally wrote in Spanish.  To see it, read "Yo Soy Yo."
