Life is a song – and everyone singers
	We perform for a host of divine beings
		What happens if we get stage fright?

Life is a story – and everyone storytellers
	We perform for a host of divine beings
		What happens if we get stage fright?

Life is a novel – and everyone authors
	We perform for a host of divine beings
		What happens if we get writers block?

Life is a game – and everyone players
	We perform for a host of divine beings
		What happens if we refuse to play?

Life should be lived to the fullest – enjoyed by everyone
	We perform for our own edification above all else
		But what happens if we stop our life short?
