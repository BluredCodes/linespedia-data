What should I do –
	When I find myself alone?
After finding respite from myself in you,
I wish I could have you back
Although I know that you grew tired of me
	As I so often do myself

So what should I do –
	When I find myself alone?
Do I turn to family?  Friends?  God?
Who or what can fill the hole you left?
I gave you a piece of my heart
	And when you left you took it with you

So what should I do –
	When I find myself alone?
