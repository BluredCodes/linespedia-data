Why do I love you –
	after you treated me with such indifference?
Why don't you love me – 
	after I gave you all my heart?

I know I will meet many more people
And that "there are more fish in the sea"
But my emotions are winning over my reasonable mind
It's not them I want, it's you!
I love you ... still

I'm lost - but I'm not completely without hope
I know what my mind is telling me
But I can't believe it
Because (foolish as it is) I love you ... still

I pray to God that I can forget these ideas
Because I know that I have to be content
With whatever situation comes
And for now I can do it

I can find consolence in the past -
For a time, I was happy in the fact that we loved each other
Although now only I love you ... stillThis poem is a translation of a poem I originally wrote in Spanish.  If you're interested, it's "Todavia."
