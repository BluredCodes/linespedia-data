Beauty in itself is so ephemeral
	How does one find it?
Beauty within is eternal
Look into the face of a true beauty

Beauty in itself is so stressful
	How can one stand it?
Beauty within is peaceful
Look into the face of a true beauty

Beauty in itself is haughty
	How does one avoid this?
Beauty within is modest
	Look into the face of a true beauty
