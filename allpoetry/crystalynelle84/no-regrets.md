When I’m with you a million thoughts explode
Inside my head – I’m always thinking that
I ought to will myself to act … but no

I’m so afraid I’ll mess up any chance
With you, I want progression from our past –
But can I lose what we already have?

If only I could get some kind of hint
How easy would it be if you would tell
Me what is going through your mind right now?

My feelings will just have to brave the storm
I plan on seeing this out to the end,
And hopefully I’ll stay without regrets
