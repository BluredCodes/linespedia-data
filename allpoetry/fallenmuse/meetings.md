Meeting you,
was truely sublime,
a glorious Saturday.
Only to my dismay,
To find a friend wanting you.
I will not pursue you,
for I fear,
I will pay for such deeds again.
For the way I am now;
For I tore,
the heart of friends thrice,
my justice is nine,
suffer-I have,
and wish it on none do I
