I almost loved you
I felt good
You pissed me off
But never for very long
You were always so sweet
Until 6:15
What a fun, fine Chicken Shit you turned out to be
I liked others the whole time
Still I held true
You were great
Then you dropped me
You couldn't even tell me yourself
Do you like being like that?
I really hope you're happy with what-ever-her-name-is
I almost loved you
