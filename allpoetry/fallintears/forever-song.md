Silent waves of endless love
From the stars that shine above
Through my tears I can see you smile
Hold me close and stay awhile

In you eyes it’s plain to see
Of the love that you have for me
Hold my hand and take me away
Sun is shining in a brand new day

Smile don’t let me down
Pick my heart off the ground
Dust it off with you loving grace
Forever let me see your face

Silent waves of endless love
From the stars that shine above
Stay with me we can do can do it together
We are one with our love forever

In your eyes it’s plan to see
Of the love that you have for me
Smiles of joy and tears of pain
We go though it again and again

Smile don’t let me down
Pick my heart off the ground
Dust is off with your loving grace
Forever let me see your face
