Passionate desires of loveless hate come forth bringing together as one, 
In the starless night of dreams without the thought of a second breath 
To grace the melodies of joyous life of two… found out in an hour of deceit 
Left in the minute of desolation 

Warm and aware of atmospheric lies and ongoing pain 
Fed by the tears of continuous misgivings 
Growing into the hideous abnormality that mind makes me 
Unmoving in an empty space 

Succeeding rivers of crouching aches 
Bring her fourth to expel the unwanted treasure 
Unable to see the innocence of love 
A virgin to mandatory consequence 

Shoved into a world with broken legs 
A heart small and pressured 
Unable to open the eyes to dream 
Unable to close the eyes to hate 

Imprinted by the cruel intentions of society 
Tainted by the truth of abnormality 
Unloved by the blood flowing within the veins 
A heart dies in sorrow… 

A birth tainted by selfish desires 
Murdered by the gruesome hands of realityAhh..
