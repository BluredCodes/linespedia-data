Sunrise of shimmering enchantment burst their way through the
Clouds
Of forgotten memories left with an empty broken heart
Bleeding
With the heat of jealous desire found out in the time of
Deceit
Full of brilliant lies covered by sheer rivers of
Hate
In your eyes like the curtains of Hades
Empty
Like the crying pits of shattered
DesPaiR::smile::
