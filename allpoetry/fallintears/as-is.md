---
title: "*^*As Is*^*"
writer: "FallinTears"
slug: "as-is"
source: "AllPoetry"

Soaring through the sky on a feather filled cloud of luxury
Enchanting are the calls of my light merry heart
Satisfied with being just me and nothing else
Not bothering with the squabbles of a day gone by
Searching for the ears of a human
Who cares with the desire of love
Never pressuring the words out of my lips
But drawing them out with his eyes
Wonder and amazement of the simple presence
Of one so special in the heart
Of one foolish girl
She awakens and tells herself to keep dreaming
For her hero is a manifested image within her eyes
Pained are the expression she wears round her face
But light are the calls that fall upon your ears
She can handle her burdens all by herself
A striking woman of independence
But never shying of the offer of a simple
Companion to love the girl
As is::sighs and keeps looking::
