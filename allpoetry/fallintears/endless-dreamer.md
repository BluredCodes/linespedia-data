title: "~Endless Dreamer~"

Endless searching for a poetic lover
Of dreams and simple desires
Understanding the difference of abnormality
Welcoming hearts undying fire

Longing for one unknown
But who I can see within my mind
Not asking for anything else
But true love for me to find

And in the eyes of the shining sun
My quest brings forth anew
Rejuvenated spirits dance
My path shall cross with you

One day my dreams will fall
Into the strong arms of reality
For I have found one who understands
The difference of abnormality

Until then my dreams will shower
The earth in which I step
Searching for where I belong
Forever, until my deathI want... something I can't find
I want... something that I can see
I want... something I can not have
I want... something that wants me
