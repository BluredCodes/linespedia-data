Desire
To be one of
The alluring creatures
Of the summer skies spitting
The fire of beauty’s lies always inside the
Screen door of speechless passion alive and
Seen but never touch by the
Dirty hands of
DesireSpitting fire= dragon
Inside screen door= fly
