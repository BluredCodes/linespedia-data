---
title: "~Dreamless Pain~"
writer: "FallinTears"

Words are the accusations of twisted emotion
Torn from the hearts of endearing passion
For the every lying independence to use
The mind in which they have
Shallow ridicule of belief
Custom and opinionated desire
To do what they feel in their heart
Abnormality of sheer similarity
Brainwashed clones of society
Casting out the differences 
Of jealousy and fright
Lying within the decaying pit of despair
In which the gruesome reality lies also
Apologies of blood written tears
Wrote upon the invisible paper
Shunned out of the innocent mind
A virgin of consequence
For selfish yearnings
Suffocation of loveless smiles
Abortion of the world’s mysteries
Loving with their heart closed and their eyes open
Wrapped up in a blanket of false security
Forever begging to be set free
Into the carnivorous world of scavengers
Finding their thoughts to be wrong
Pounding on the walls of broken glass
To be let back inside the cycle
Of dreamless painI really... don't like people.
