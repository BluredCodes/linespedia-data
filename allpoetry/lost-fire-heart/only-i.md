---
title: "Only I"
writer: "Lost Fire Heart"
slug: "only-i"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54571-Only-I-by-Lost-Fire-Heart"
---

Cast away,
into a world of darkness and pain.
Deserving only this.

Asking all around
who else is here,
who else deserves this?

It is only I
that must be here?
Witholding my only pride left.

There must be others,
who know of the pain.
Who has been in this corner?

Looking around in circles,
no one else around.
Alone in the carkness.

It's what I deserved,
open the door on a new pain,
never felt before.

The pain of betrayal
by someone loved and lost.
Suffered by only I.
