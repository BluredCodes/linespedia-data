title: "Looking Back"
writer: "Lost Fire Heart"

Looking back through my life,
staring at my past.
Wondering here
how it changed so fast.

Playing with the thought of
you being so near.
Loosing you now
is my only fear.

Crooked ways of viewing
what has left me.
Looking back I
wish I could make you see.

All the times we fought
never changed a thing.
My feelings never left.
It wasn't a meaningless fling.

Cuz I know now
how much I love you.
Looking back at the past,
I hope you love me too.
