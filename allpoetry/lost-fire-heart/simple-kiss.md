title: "Simple Kiss"
writer: "Lost Fire Heart"

That one simple kiss,
made my heart so free.
That one simple kiss,
just won't let me be. 

That one simple kiss,
lasted throught the day.
That one simple kiss,
said it will be okay.

That one simple kiss,
lasting thought the night.
That one simple kiss,
made the stars seem so bright.

That one simple kiss,
told her to go away.
That one simple kiss,
told me to stay.

That one simple kiss,
made me feel whole.
That one simple kiss,
completed my soul.

That one simple kiss,
told me what to do.
That one simple kiss,
said you love me too.I love you Tony. Forever and ever. I will never leave you, and I'll always be here.
