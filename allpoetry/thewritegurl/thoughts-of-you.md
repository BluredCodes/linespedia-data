Sitting back in this chair reminds me of the simplicity
The simplicity of what we had back then
You and I wanted nothing more
Than to be the best of friends
Then as friends the heart grew fonder
And in love we deeply feel
But it was much more hazerdous
And hard like a dry well
Now here we sit so far apart
Time comming up to fast
It scares me when I think of how
This relationship my not last
But thoughts of you swim in my head
And likewise in my heart
From everything about you
I don’t know where to start
You’re eyes are often catching me
And stealing all my breath
At night I dream about our love
My unconsious test
You’re hands are chieseled like your jaw
You’re teeth so straight and sparkling
But the thing I love the most
Is how your words are ever startling
You’ll be here soon that’s obvious
But of what I can’t be sure
Is that I’ll be everything you dream
An everlasting torture.
