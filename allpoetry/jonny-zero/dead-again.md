Dead again-

Damn I hate these nights.
When the light fails to shine,
and the liquor forgets to heal.
And all I do is feel sorry
for myself,
and wish I had a whore
to ease the pain,
just for a bit.

Dead again, in the greyness
of late hours- Alone?
