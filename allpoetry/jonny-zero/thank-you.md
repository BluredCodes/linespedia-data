Thank you for the guilt
laid at my feet.
My cock still wet
with the simplness of you.
I'll wash you off
and drive you away.
And I'll fuck your memory
in a thousand wasted dreams.
My guilt lays thick
upon eyes and skin,
irritating,
and painful in its reflection.
