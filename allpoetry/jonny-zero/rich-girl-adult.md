She had hair of gold
and a rich daddy
drove a new car
and had three credit cards
And at eighteen
her brests were firm
and her ass was tight
I was from somwhere else
Somewhere her daddy said to stay away from
My shoes were worn
and my clothes
originally not my own
But I got to her
and I took her innocense
and she thought
she loved me
But I slept
around and she couldn't handle the fact
that she was the worst of many
I knew I would not get a thing from her
just a pair of panties she left in my car
