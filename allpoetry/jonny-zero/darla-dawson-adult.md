I woke up in a sweat
with your head sleeping
next to my fattened morning cock
Drool on my thigh
lipstick on the shaft
still clutching the bottle
of cheap wine from last night
You looked like I smelled
repulsive-
Must of been the cocain
or the pills that did us in
Fuck Tuesday morning
I need a drink
