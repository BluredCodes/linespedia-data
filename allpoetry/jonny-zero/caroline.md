She's wicked in her approach.
The way she smiles, while
giving me a hand job.
Or the way she never says
a word after we fuck.

She has the most gorgeous
ass, and the longest legs.
But they're not mine.

I'm just as temporary
as the last guy.
And that's how
she plays the game.
A masterful art which
she alone creates.

And I'll gladly spread
her legs
and take the bait.
