They both dropped by at the same time.
And I made them dinner.
After,  we talked about old friends
and all the people we ever wondered
what happened to.
Then after a short while, we talked about politics,
which of course led to the proposition of sex.

And I debated on which one to fuck first.
Both were equally desireable.

And I watched as they fooled around
just the two of them.
And I was hard and ready by the time
they got to me.

We slept all over each other till dawn.
Glorified every sin, and asked for more.
Left the lights on and fell asleep
in an ocean of sweat and spit.
And I awoke to the faint smell
of their perfumes, and the sadness
of the morning after.
