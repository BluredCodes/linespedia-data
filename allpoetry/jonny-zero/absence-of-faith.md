---
title: "Absence of Faith"
writer: "Jonny Zero"
slug: "absence-of-faith"

There is no God
and if there were
She would be a barren old woman

All the Ghettos strewn across my
vision
like a raped child

This dirty world
Is nothing less than God-Like
