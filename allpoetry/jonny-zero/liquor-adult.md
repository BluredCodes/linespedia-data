Liquor is the blood
that flows within
It's the virgin
in the bed next to me
pantyless and waiting
Liquor is the mother
I've never had
The God I pray to
every waking hour
Liquor is every slut
I've ever fucked
in cheap motels
It's all the love 
I'll ever need
Liquor is this poem
the muse to help me write
And liquor is my cock
ten inches of slow death
