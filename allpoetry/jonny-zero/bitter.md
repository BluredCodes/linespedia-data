---
title: "Bitter"
writer: "Jonny Zero"
slug: "bitter"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57104-Bitter-by-Jonny-Zero"
---

Your the cancer
in my mouth
Bitter
with black scabs

A taste
I cannot
clean
with peppermint
or whiskey

The skin
which
held me close
once
Now just a 
Testement
of dead flesh,
and of a love
so dead,
neither heaven
nor hell will
accept it

But it was beautiful
once, while
it lasted

But we both fucked
each other
One day
at a time
