title: "Ad(ult)vertisment"

I write
too many fuck words
I talk about
too many taboos
I have no fear
to say
what everyone thinks
and that is my art
But my words
are all sacrificed
for the sake
of capitolism
in a cyber word
that is less real
than a single letter
It's sad
that greed kills art
