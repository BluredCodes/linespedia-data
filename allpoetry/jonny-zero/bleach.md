---
title: "Bleach"
writer: "Jonny Zero"
slug: "bleach"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57782-Bleach-by-Jonny-Zero"
---

Piss on my leg
Ashes in my smile
Five foot eleven
and an inch left to dig
Staind sheets
need bleach
Horny housewives
on TV
Need a shower
and a beer
Get on my bike
and ride
and try
to forget
Indian girl
in my bed
bleeding
me with a hard on
I remember
the smell
