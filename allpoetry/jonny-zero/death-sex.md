---
title: "Death Sex"
writer: "Jonny Zero"

Watching you die
on sheets woven
out of our own indignity
blood red testaments
of bleeding hearts,
used rubbers,
and empty promises
the music of you
echoing off walls
as I play my games
inside of you
Receiving me
you slump and
die on my burning
chest and liquored eyes
