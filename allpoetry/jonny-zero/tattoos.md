Gloria had 22
tattoos
And one
directly
above her clit

She had Dave
tatooed on her back
Yet
She'd been Fucking me
for a month

She liked
to ride
on my bike
and thought
she
was in with
the biker
crowd

Gloria
wasen't all that pretty
but her tatoos
made her stand out
as an easy lay
