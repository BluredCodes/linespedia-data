---
title: "Your Stink (adult)"
writer: "Jonny Zero"
slug: "your-stink-adult"

The afterthought of you
is repulsive
I can still taste
your stink fresh in my mouth
Your words are as useless
as the tongue that speaks them
And I vomit the memory of you
today, tomorrow, and every day
after
