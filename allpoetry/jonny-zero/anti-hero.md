writer: "Jonny Zero"

Words are the drug
the choice is clear
I cannot candycoat
my world for you
or anyone
I feel no need
to be somthing less than I am
I cannot conform
to the cardboard cutout
anti-reality
within this world
I am the anti- hero
and I cannot be silenced
