---
title: "Jackson (adult)"
writer: "Jonny Zero"

source: "AllPoetry"

Slow beating heart
Chest soaked with sweat
Jackson in the kitchen
doing lines
A bottle of Gin
on the television
Thoughts of her
in my head
I don't want to sleep
because Jackson
is a prick
