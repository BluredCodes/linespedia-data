She was only sixteen
when I knew her
and she had pointy tits
and a big hard ass
We would spend the nights
behined her garage
with a bottle
of cheap wine
and a pack of Camel non-filters
Getting drunk
and fucking on an old rug
That was the summer
of 89
And she moved away that fall
And I fell hard
in the ditches
waiting for her to return
