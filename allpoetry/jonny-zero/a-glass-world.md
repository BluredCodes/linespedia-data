---
title: "A Glass World"

I cannot feed your egos any longer.
I'm not the team player I used to be.
I'm in a world I alone created.
But I'll let you in to share in my pain.
And I'll watch your eyes turn dead as mine.
In a glass world, I am God- I am your Secrets-
I am your fear- I am a whore
Feeble and propped up with wires.
Thank you.
