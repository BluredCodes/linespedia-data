The blonde one
is younger
has little perky titties
and I can see her
bathing suit is
indented into her crotch
Barely a teenager
Yet ready to fuck
every guy around
The brunette is taller
and has the hips
of a mother
She looks like
a girl I used to date
years back
They see me looking at them
and they like it
and they know I would like to
fuck them by nightfall
the blonde first
then the brunette
But they just
keep me wanting
with the way they move
So I pretend that I don't care
and that will get them
it always does
