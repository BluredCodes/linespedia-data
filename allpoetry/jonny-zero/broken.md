One caress and I knew
Your panties would
drop.
Your like the rest
only younger
and more impressionable.
And I'll introduce you
to the pleasure
your young mind
still cannot fathom.
And I'll leave you broken
and used,
and you'll hate me in
ten years, telling
sob tales 
to your shrink.
About all the times
I fucked you
in more ways than one.
