Listening to The Doors on the stereo 
Writing this poem 
and watching you do lines 
with a rolled up dollar bill 
Your wearing that black bra I like 
The one that shows off your 
extremely large nipples 
And I'm balancing this notebook 
on my swollen cock 
You raise your head 
and throw me a smile 
and I take another drag off my smoke 
and smile back 
Your the best friend I got, right now 
and it's not just because 
I bought the coccain
