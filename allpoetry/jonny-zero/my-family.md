---
title: "My Family"
writer: "Jonny Zero"
slug: "my-family"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58480-My-Family-by-Jonny-Zero"
---

They call me
and ask me how I am
and I wonder
if they really care
I don't want their love
or their advise
on how to make
my life better
I need no brothers
or sisters
Friends
Enemies
or lovers
I just need them
to stop calling
and pretending
that I love them
