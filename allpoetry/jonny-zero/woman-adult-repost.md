The apple alone is yours 
Plucked from the tree 
Forbidden to eat 
Delicate and naked 
breasts and cocks free 
explosion of passion 
has eaten thee 
Willows alive 
Fresh world now spent 
Curse my nakedness gone 
Woman scorned 
Now the worm is in it's place 
And new nipples are erect 
Shameful we look 
within new light 
God must now neglect 
ashes is the cure 
The price of sex is death
