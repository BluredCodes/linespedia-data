---
title: "Electric Rainbow"
writer: "Jonny Zero"

We create each other
in a moment
Your breasts are soft against mine
I feel as though I could melt into you
And a stretch
long and everlasting
Love the 
electric rainbows
we erect
in honor of
this binding
