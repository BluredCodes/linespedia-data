Another whiskey wake up call
Another blonde face down in vomit
I grab the last slice of stale pizza
eat it as I take my morning shit
Trying to remember if Charlie
got off with my fifty bucks
and left this whore knocked out
on my floor
She has a great ass but I'm
sure I didn't fuck her
As sure as I am of the whiskey
