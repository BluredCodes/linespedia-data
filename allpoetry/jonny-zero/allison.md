She has a lazy eye
and bad teeth
likes to eat sardines
straight from the can
smokes way too much
and has slept with
everyone I know
She has two kids
that she never sees
owes her dealer
a hundred bucks
and never sleeps
She comes to me
for a quick fuck
and a few smokes
and she leaves and
goes home
when I tell her to
