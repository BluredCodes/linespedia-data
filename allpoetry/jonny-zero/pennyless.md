Candels burnt down to nothing
and I'm still wide awake
aching for a drink
My stomach hurts
and my cigaretts
have lost their taste
My mother calls to tell me
that she wants the twenty bucks 
I borrowed last week
But she knows I cannot pay

I haven't seen Susan since Monday
And she left me limp-dicked
and pennyless
She'll show up on Friday and beg me
for money to get some blow
and I know
how to use her indignity
to my advantage
