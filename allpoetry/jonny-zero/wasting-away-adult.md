---
title: "Wasting Away (adult)"
writer: "Jonny Zero"
slug: "wasting-away-adult"
source: "AllPoetry"

I need some therapy
I know that
and everyone I know
does as well
I wake up
in the mornings
humping my pillow
and brush my teeth
with Jack and Coke
I listen to music
way too loud
and I think about death
way too much
I hate people who are
politically correct
and I have no respect
for anyone
I have no concept
of religion
or love
or hate
I just like being numb
and wasting away
