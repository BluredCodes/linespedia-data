---
title: "Trophy Doll"
writer: "kittycatds"

I hate the way you touch me
so rough and uncaring
expecting me to give it up
the mintue you come beckoning
I am not your toy
I am not your doll
this is not a trophy shelf
upon which I sit
You did not win me
at some circus game
the only thing you tossed
was my heart in the trash
I am tired of waiting 
for you to grow up
tired of being
your whipping boy
you can not use me
for your joy and pleasure
expecting me to smile
through all your weather
I am not your toy
I am not your doll
this is not a trophy shelf
upon which I sit
