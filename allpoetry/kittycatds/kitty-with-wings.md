I am a kitty 
so purfectly priss
but on my enemies
I shall piss
for I am the PPF Mascot
Or don't you know?
And my kitty wings
aren't just for show
For up above
I have a cat's eye view
To make it easy
to piss on you
I am not just a kitty
All priss and clean
as a faery kitty
I am twice as mean...oh my I am getting bad at writing!!
