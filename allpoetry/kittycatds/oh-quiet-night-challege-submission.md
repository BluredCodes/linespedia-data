Oh quiet night 
Cloak me in the darkness 
Surround me in the peace 
Like a blanket of mist 
In my disguise of gloom 
Sweeping the streets 
Covering the homes 
Sleeping babies at peace 
As I hide in the shadows 
Creeping toward the dark 

Oh quiet night 
Find me my home 
Tip-toeing my way to the woods 
My white gown blowing in the breeze 
Guiding me along 
Like pixies of the dusk 
Coming upon the creek 
Where the cool water runs 
Feeling the ground 
For the rock on the egde 

Oh quiet night 
Find me my peace 
In the mist of water 
Listening to the quiet of trees 
Towering guardians of the night 
My champions of the dark 
Of my quiet dreamings 
As I curl up, a tight-fisted ball 
On the damp rocks and leaves 
Their dank smell encircling me 

Oh quiet night 
This is my place 
Within it I find myself 
Alone from the world 
Watching the fairies 
Dance with the stars 
I sing to your glory 
Praise high into your soul 
Til the sun drives us away
