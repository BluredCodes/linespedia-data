title: "Could I turn you on"

I wonder if I can turn you on
The way I use to do
Would your heart still jump
If you heard my voice on the phone
If I showed up where you work
Could you ignore me
I wonder if I can make you scream
The way I use to do
Would you still sigh that little moan
If I ran my fingers through your hair
If I whispered in your ear 
Could you ignore your inner need
I wonder if I could make you mad
The way I use to do
Would you still turn red
If you saw me laughing in somelse's lap
If I ignored your worried pleas
Could you ignore your jealous rage
I just wonder...
Could I turn you on the way I use to do...
