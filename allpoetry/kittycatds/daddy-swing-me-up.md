daddy swing me up high
so I can touch the stars
reach the limits 
no one has before

daddy swing me up high
so I can taste the clouds
experience things 
no one has before

daddy swing me up high
so I can go beyond
conquer dreams
no one has before

daddy swing me up high
so I can feel free
understand freedom
no one has before

daddy swing me up high
I need it once more
show me you believe in me
like you did before...i was attempt to make this as child-like as possible, but through an adults mind...
