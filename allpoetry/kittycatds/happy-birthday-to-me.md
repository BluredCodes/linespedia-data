Happy Birthday to me
See my pretty smile
See my happy face
Happy Birthday to me
And my yearly reminder
That I am one step closer
to it all being over
and too many steps away
from making life right
Happy Birthday to me
And my newest number
that I shall forget
Like I have all the others
Happy Birthday to me
It doesn't get better
I never learn
One more year down
A lifetime left to go...Arent birthday such HAPPY HAPPY days... blah
