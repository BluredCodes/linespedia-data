Karma is a bitch
And I know I shouldn't laugh
But fate has me reeling
recent turn of events
have me giggling
I would never be cruel to you
But him, Oh I cannot say
His well planned life
Falls to pieces
Nothing in life
Has a 100% guaranteeI can't say too much about this but lets just say that something someone insulted me about is now happening to him
