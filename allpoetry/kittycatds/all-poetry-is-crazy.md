Look around at the crazy
fairy dust touches us all
witches on brooms
Death hits a brick wall
Surivor Island in the back
Test your writing know how
Jen becomes the goddess
and to her, 20 members bow
but each tell a story
my daily "soap" I like to tease
during my babies nap time
this place puts me at ease
so i am not ms. popular
(lord knows I wasn't in school)
but I could have my own whore
and he can become my tool
addicted is what I've become
to the crazy that runs around here
and for the my poetic friends
I stand and cheer...ok so it sucks... but I am complete blocked anymore... :)But all of you ARE crazy
