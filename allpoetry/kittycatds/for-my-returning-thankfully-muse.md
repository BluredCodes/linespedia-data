With the moonlight she hides
Within dreams she will dance
Never found a partner
Never given the chance
She tiptoes around
Whispers in your ear
Though you never see her
She is always near
A gentle push
A simple caress
Helps to put
My demons at rest
Even she needs moments
Of glory too
For the inspiration and love
She pass unto you
Silent and forgotten
Selfish I could be
To forget the one
Who had inspired me
So to her I write
My sorry and praise
She who spoke to me
On my best of daysApparently I wasn't nice to her and she left me, so she "helped" me write a dediction to her... And thanks to Deathy and Rodney for helping me catch her again :)
