title: "Safe behind my glass wall"

I laugh at you 
you dont know it
safe behind my glass wall
distance of miles
the only contact
a big glass screen
safely hidden
behind my keyboard
so go ahead and laugh at me
your crit doesnt harm me
you can not touch my life
I can delete your opinion
like I delete you from my life
Go ahead and laugh
you dont see me laughing too
safe behind my glass wall
overlooking you...just listening into some fights here and well... this is what I got... sue me, I find the fighting funny:)
