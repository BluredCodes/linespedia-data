As the PPF Mascot 
I have a special cheer
I do a silly PeePee dance
and wiggle my little rear
I bounce around a tad bit
while holding tight my thighs
(I think I am retaining water
you can see it in my eyes!)
I pounce around on all fours
A victim I must spy
When I get too full
I remember I can fly
Up in the air
my rear still wiggles around
and than I let it lose
to the poor suckers
On the ground!Ok am I getting any better at these or worst?
