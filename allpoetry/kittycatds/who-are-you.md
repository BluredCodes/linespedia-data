Who are you
This dream that I hold
This angel in my heart
This mystery that I cannot solve
Dreams flow from your lips
Like the moonlit over a stream
Soft and calm
And magical in itself
Your hands caress me
Your heart embraces me
I cannot pull away
Forever moving closer
Every step away 
Drives me deeper
Falling fast into your spell
Fears no longer shroud my want
Desire stronger than reason
I rush headfirst into your arms
And only in them do I find myselfThis was an old one of mine I thought I would post it :)
