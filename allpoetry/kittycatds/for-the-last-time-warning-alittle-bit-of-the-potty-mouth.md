Tired of being lied to
Tired of the same shit
by different men
Who only know how to cheat
Who have forgotten what "faithful" means
Tired of living up to standards
That I fall so short of
Tired of hearing that I am "the perfect girlfriend"
While they are FUCKING around behind my back
Tired of being walked on
And trying to be understanding
Tired of defending and supporting
And getting nothing but a knife in the back
Tired of waiting for "Prince Charming"
Or even "Mr. Right"
Tired of being a punching bag
                    a trophy
                    a sextoy
                    a willing slave
                    a subservant "wife"
Tired of being everything to everyone
And getting nothing but heartache
                        disappointment
                        knocked up and forgotten
                        used
                        left behind
I am not your whore
I am not your mother
I will not just give respect
I will not just trust
I will not be taken for granted
For the last fucking time
I WILL NOT BE USED!!I have decided that ALL men can not be trusted, at least none I seem to pick and I am DONE!!
