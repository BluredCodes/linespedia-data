---
title: "i can't hear you"
writer: "kittycatds"
slug: "i-cant-hear-you"

i cover my ears
bouncing on my bed
oblivious to life
can't hear you at all
hey look there
there's a fly on the wall
but I can't hear you
just let me be
watch the cats run
while you yell at me
but I can't hear you
i just bounce on my bed
oblivious to life
hands cover my head
I can't hear you
can't hear you at all
make them send me away
so you can shop at the mall
cuse I can't hear you
and I dont care
bounce on my bed
in my pooh underwear
I can't hear you
as you walk away
and now that you are gone
I can sleep all day....I am having one of those "wish I was a kid and could get away with being immature" days... and I really do have pooh underwear :)
