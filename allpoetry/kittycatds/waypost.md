This is my waypost
My landmark of life
The deciding moment
I had for so long envisioned
Here are my crossroads
Each way different and new
My fate lies down these road
The outcome quite unknown 
Each passage bringing hope
Each passage bringing dread
For I can not know
How the road may end
And once the choice is made
The other road is lost to me
And I stand here troubled
Apprehensive with each step
My last chance to turn around
Or run head first toward my future...
