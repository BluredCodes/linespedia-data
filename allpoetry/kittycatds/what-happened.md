What happened to the little one
I use to sing to sleep
What happened to the little one
Whom in my arms would keep
What happened to the little one
I fed from my own breast
What happened to the little one
Who could sleep upon my chest
What happened to the sleepless nights
I gave without a care
When night terrors woke her up
And we would cuddle in the chair
What happened to the constant chatter
The questions without end
Cuse mommy wasn't only smart
She was also her friend
What happened to my blue-eye beauty
With curls that frame her face
That ran around the living room
Innocently destroying the place
What happened to the little one
Some how its not the same
Now she agrues back
And wants to change her name
Oh how I miss my little one
Bright eyed, happy and free
Now I see my little one
Is turning into me!My oldest girl is growing up and each day she becomes more and more like me and as exciting as it is to see her grow, I worry when I see myself in her... and it makes me miss my baby all that much more!
