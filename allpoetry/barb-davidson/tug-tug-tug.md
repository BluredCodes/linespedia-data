title: "Tug, tug, tug"

Tug, tug, tug,

He pulls the string,

ping, ping,ping,

my heart it sings.



Doesn't want me,

Won't let me go,

His selfish needs,

No more i'll know.



I say this now,

Until next time,

Hell pulls the string,

On the heart thats mine.



Gets me going,

Teases me so well,

Next time he does it,

He can go to hell!
