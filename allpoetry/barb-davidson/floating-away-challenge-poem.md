Bubbling gently it slowly tinkles, 
Like laughter in the wind, 
Running playfully it carries a leaf, 
Tossing and turning it in the eddy's beneath. 

Nutty and fresh, it's odour carries me, 
To years gone by,as the sun glistens, 
Like rainbows moving in amongst a stone, 
As i watch the river running home. 

Cool and inviting, it makes me shiver, 
Crystal clear, i feel the taste, 
Of ice cold water upon my face, 

I feel my hair spreading out from me, 
Like fingers of water playing with my body, 
It plays a tune inside my mind, 
The rivers song is so divine.....
