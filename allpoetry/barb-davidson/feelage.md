---
title: "Feelage"
writer: "Barb Davidson"
slug: "feelage"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59975-Feelage-by-Barb-Davidson"
---

Not a word you'd know really well,
Not in the dictionary, what the hell,
Feelage a word i use with delight,
Carries my meaning, for me it's so right.

I feel the image deep in my soul,
A 'feelage' you see is complete and whole,
Total sensation, touch,taste and sound,
A wonderful word this 'feelage' i've found.
