Bubble, bubble I go, 
Not ladylike I know,
Wind in the alley,
Not feeling too pally.
Brewing in me,
A tornado you see,
I stand on my own,
In case I do blow,
The bean burger I ate,
And lentil soup late,
Are brewing inside,
This fact I can’t hide,
I must run now you see,
I don’t want a pee,
But if I fart in the street,
I’ll jump twenty feet.
Don’t light a match, 
I’m fond of my thatch,
Beans are great fun,
