Night feeder, 
God of the night,
Sucking juices,
Taking delight,
Essences required,
Longevity to give,
Vampire lover,
My master 
I’m his

Neck laid bare,
Arms entwined,
Sweat and saliva,
Reeling minds,
Amitra goddess,
Life force,
Love

No blood,
Clear fluid,
My master craves,
No open coffin,
No deep dark grave,
Life force
From within,
I spread my legs,
Head lowers,
I feed him,
No dregs.
