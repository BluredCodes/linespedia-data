I wish i could touch his hands 
i wish i could kiss his lips 
I wish i could look at his eyes 
and see his soul, see his heart, 
together with mine as just one 
it's misterious, it's intriguing 
he hook me up since the beginning... 

I can be myself around him 
no more masks, no more walls, 
I treasure every moment we share 
I dare to play silly and i don't care 
I laugh at myself 
I feel so goofy, 
I am so into him... 

I can spend all night 
just chatting about nothing 
just talking about silly things 
hanging out in front of my screen 
hearing his lovely voice on my speakers 
and doing anything to hear him cute giggling... 

It's so special 
I sometimes feel it's not true, 
but it is, and it's full of enemyes 
like time, distance, society, 
and just a friend, love... 

I bless my screen, 
i bless latinmail, 
i bless internet, 
i bless my mexican sweetheart,  
he really makes a little part of my day 
happier, brighter... 

thank you sweety, 
thank you my love 
i appreciate your candor, 
your sweetness, and all your words...
