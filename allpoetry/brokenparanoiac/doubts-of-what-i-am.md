BY: MMM

Doubts of what i am
of what i want
my fate is unreal
my destiny is fake
i have no destination
cause i don't follow my heart...
my mind guides
it's taking me nowhere
wherever i go
i have nothing
i have nothing at all...


Doubts of what i am
of what i want
the confussion sea i am in
mixes up all my being
doesn't let me live well
doesn't satisfie me 
too many mistakes
too many wrong places
too many wrong persons...


Doubts of what i am
doubts of what i want
why do i love YOU
you are not supposed to be the one
you hurt me, you insulted me
you don't deserve my love...


Doubts of what i am
doubts of what i want
i am too rational
i need to go out and take risks
but where to go? with who?
doubts, damn doubts... 
doubts of what i am
doubts of what i want...
