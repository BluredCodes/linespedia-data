Gathering Storm (survivor challenge 3)

'Neath the darkling sky
two souls meet and embrace.
Power like never before seen
surges 'round the mountaintop.
Gwynneth of the Raven Hair
imbues him with Gaea's might -
the strength of the Earth.
He gazes, stone-faced,
preparing for the looming battle.
His own fiery powers
reflect from his blood-red eyes.
Gently he holds her close,
knowing that both may not survive
the coming fray.
Edmon, Fire Wizard of Balmoria,
stands defiant with his Drow queen.
As the storm gathers
he pledges her his love
and collects the force of nature
from her eager lips.
Suddenly Soron's demonic horde
is upon them!
With one word of blasting -
"K'Panagh!" -
he shatters the mountain
beneath their feet.
The goblins are swallowed up
by the clashing rocks
as Edmon prepares for the next onslaught.
Wave after wave,
in relentless succession
goblins and orcs attack.
Fire and Earth hold their ground
and defend that last precious site.
His strength begins to falter from the strain,
and sensing this she passes to  him
of her own life force.
Without realizing the source of the power,
he instinctively channels it
into one great strike.
At last the evil minions are defeated,
but as the dust settles
he finds her lifeless at his feet.
Gently he picks her up
and places her on the great stone altar.
He calls upon all his druidic lore
and the power of the elves.
His hands glow with radiant energy.
"Ea banarok - O chelata!"
Softly she stirs, and gazes into his eyes.
They have survived
to join the fight again another day,
but her hair is now as gray as his.
