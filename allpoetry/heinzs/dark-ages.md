Dark Ages

Cycles of enlightenment
plunge back into darkness.
Man's inhumanity
or nature's wrath,
the Lord of Chaos
reigns supreme.

Blind to our neighbors' suffering,
deaf to his cries,
we try to live
our insulated lives.
Apathy is the plague
that decimates hope.

Engagement is the key,
the beginning
of the road to solution.
Commitment oils the wheels,
but it is a rocky path
strewn with pitfalls.

We ever relive
these dark ages -
doomed, it seems,
to repeatedly fail
to achieve our potential
as expressed in our dreams.

It is time to wake the sleeper,
interrupt the dream
and bring attention
and action
to the desperate needs
of this reality.

Through turmoil, stress,
and earthly strife,
we must bring peace
into this life!
