I Want to Believe

I want to believe
in man's basic goodness -
that peace on Earth
is actually possible.

I want to believe
that hunger and disease
will vanish
from man's experience.

I want to believe
that my meager existence
will leave some noticeable mark
on time's great slate.

I want to believe
that my children will have a chance
to live in a better world
than the one I inherited.

I want to believe 
we will finally waken
and learn history's lessons
and no longer repeat past mistakes.

I want to believe
in a government
dedicated to the well-being
of each and every citizen.

I want to believe
in a loving and caring God
who watches over us
and keeps us from evil.

I want to believe -
but each day of my life
my faith is assailed
by man's inhumanity.

I want to believe -
but the destitute are mute
to a deaf nation 
and a blind church.

I want to believe -
and I find solace and hope
in the hearts of poets
and artists' eyes.

I'll continue my quest
'til my own timely rest,
these issues to relieve -
'cause I want to believe!
