Distemper

What's the point,
why am I doing this?
Why do I have to be
the strong one?
I'm tired -
I don't want to
do this anymore.
I want to have fun.
I want to be carefree.
No worries,
no responsibilities,
no pressure,
no stress.
Sit and drink coffee
or toke
or guzzle beer
like you
and everyone else.
I want my life back.
No Alanon,
no therapy,
no Paxil...
just me.
But...
who am I?

4/13/2002
