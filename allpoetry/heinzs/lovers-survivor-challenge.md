Lovers  (survivor challenge)

Lovers,
sleepless, caring,
all their energies primed
for that all-consuming event,
first kiss!

Caress,
softly touching,
tongues just intertwining,
moment of passion so sublime,
embrace!

Bodies,
gently coupling,
heated fervor building,
orgasmic shudders overcome,
release!

Eyes meet
in afterglow,
sweaty smiles, still teasing,
two become one for one moment,
lovers!
