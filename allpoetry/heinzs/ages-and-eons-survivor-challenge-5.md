Ages and Eons (survivor challenge #5)

I lie here in wait...
for I know not what.
Countless seasons have passed,
leaving their marks on me.
Frost has cracked my surface,
rain has washed bits of me away.
I once moved 12 feet
during a tremendous earthquake!
Small animals scurry over me...
I particularly like the voles
and hedgehogs.
I remember the dinosaurs.

I was born in a fiery cataclysm...
my mother Gaea,
my father Vulcan.
Earth and Fire are my heritage,
yet I lie here cold and unyielding.
A moss has taken root 
in one of my many cracks.
It tickles, but I cannot laugh.
I remain humorless and stoic,
doomed to utter silence.
I am stone...
the very rock under your unwashed feet!

4/3/2002Ok... how inanimate can you get but the very rocks of the Earth?  Here is one such rock's story.
