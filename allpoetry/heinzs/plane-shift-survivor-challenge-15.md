Plane Shift

The world divides itself
twixt light and dark -
equal, yet separate realities.
Only in our dreams
does the boundary between the planes
weaken sufficiently for contact.

Yggdrasil, the tree of life,
rests its ancient roots
between the sea of sorrows
and the sea of dreams -
its branches shading both worlds
in its protective aura.

Legend has it
that beneath the tree's bole
may be found the true grail -
that holy cup used by the ancients
and lost from human sight
at the time of Christ.

Whosoever drinketh from the cup
shall experience eternal life.
Thus the legend says,
yet is this indeed a truth
or simply another metaphor
to be discovered and unraveled?

The dreamer visits this place
where planes shift
and truth blends with fantasy.
He drinks from the grail
and finds immortality
in the words put to paper.

This is the realm of the poet,
the philosopher,
the lunatic,
and the saint.
This locus transcends
the physical and the spiritual.

We have the power
to set aside strife and turmoil
and take our drink from the grail.
Come, visit this beauteous world.
Join me and the poets
and become one with the dreamer.
