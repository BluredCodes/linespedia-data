Relapse? 

You lie, 
you cheat, 
you steal -- 
all for that high -- 
that drugged detachment 
you inexorably crave. 
Anguish for your family, 
anguish for your friends, 
you endanger yourself
and those around you -- 
but you're high, 
and don't give a damn. 
You've been to treatment 
and therapy -- 
spent time in counseling 
and jail -- 
for what? 
The past years 
of struggle...
a waste of time, 
emotion, 
money, 
love?
You haven't changed. 
You don't want 
to change. 
You're a selfish, 
lying, 
destructive bastard -- 
an addict. 

3-25-2002
