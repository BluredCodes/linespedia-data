Foul Play

Ever lurking at the edge of perception
the shadows bide their time.
They have all eternity,
without substance or form -
they will have their way in the end.

Fear rises, cloys the mind,
and in that moment of unclarity
they strike!  Flesh renders,
sanguine droplets spew
and leave their bloody trail.

The next morning all that remains
is a single severed hand,
still clutching the bus transfer.
The police make the identification
from the fingerprints.

How can one explain
that your brother was killed by demons?
"He met with a horrible
and tragic accident.
It remains under investigation."

3/24/2002
