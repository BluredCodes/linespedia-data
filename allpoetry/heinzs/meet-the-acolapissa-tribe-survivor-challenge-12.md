Meet the Acolapissa Tribe (survivor challenge #12)

Should I write an ode to a butterfly
as I watch it gently flutter by,
or a poem to a small lizard,
who, himself, is quite a poetic wizard?

I'll pen a rhyming villanelle
to the 'lil one I know so well,
or an epic, long and wise
to please the one with angel eyes.

Should veelejot be the only one
to be there when the sonnet's done,
or should I keep the thoughts inside
and with the spondees ever hide?

I'll fling a couplet at therpsichore,
the goddess rescue from death's door,
and a quatrain to mothers' love
for fair slekky, what a dove!

My tetractys is alive, not dead inside,
come with me on life's great ride -
forever I'll create the sly
verses from the crawling fly.
