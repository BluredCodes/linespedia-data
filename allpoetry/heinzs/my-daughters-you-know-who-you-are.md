My Daughters

Sensitive, caring, deeply emotional -
you try to hide your soft souls
in hardened carapaces.

Each of you unique - loving -
your beauty shines outward,
though you deny its ownership.

Self-effacing, critical
of your own real and imagined faults,
you define yourselves in mediocrity.

You  are goddesses in name and fact -
faery princesses, each seeking
your one true prince.

In your eagerness and trust
you have been burned 
by blackguards and knaves.

Your pain and grief stains your cheeks
and opens deep bleeding chasms
in your aching hearts.

Your Dad cries with you -
feeling your anguish as his own -
unable to stem the raging flood.

He is powerless, impotent -
he can only tell you his love,
sending it fluttering through space.

You are not my daughters by flesh,
but my soul kin, spirit linked
in ways not many may understand.

May the might of my resolve
and the power of my resurgent will
buoy you on this sea of tears.

You each have within you
the inner strength - the survivor instinct -
release it from its fetters - set it free!

Take my hand and fly with me
to those far mystical shores
where dragons and unicorns yet roam.

For a brief moment in time
experience with me that innocent bliss
that once was yours on mother's knee.

Maybe, then, the day will not seem so dark -
nor the road ahead so long and steep -
drink of my joy and make it yours!

4/9/2002Dedicated to my cyberdaughters.
