A Split Personality Speaks - survivor challenge 4

Do you want to know a secret?
Let me take you away from this misery
on a magical mystery tour.
You and I can be flying
free as a bird - just imagine
and let it be.

The secret?
I've been crying, waiting, hoping
with lonesome tears in my eyes -
I'm down, I'm a loser
without your love in my life
eight days a week.

I need you, not another girl!
Oh, darling, I call your name -
but I just don't understand
why we said the things we said today.
It's just a rumor
and I should have known better.

No more!  It's too much monkey business!
I want to come together
to get back with you!
I'm happy just to dance with you...
I want to hold your hand.
I've got a feeling it's only love.

Across the universe you're the one!
This boy won't be a nowhere man.
Baby, it's you!
All you need is love,
so, from me to you
I can only offer all my loving.

Thank you, girl!
But, if you want to slow down,
that's all right -
I'll be on my way
and you can have your young blood!
I'll cry instead.

You won't see me,
and when I'm 64 I'll get you!
Ooh! My soul!
I'm looking through you!
Things don't ever change,
no matter how hard we try.

You'd better run for your life,
because I'll be back!
I feel fine - glad all over!
Ask me why you don't bother me...
I'm all together now.
I can act naturally!

She's a woman
and she came yesterday.
I saw her standing there
and thought... Ooh! My arms!
Here comes the sun,
and I love her!

So, hello - goodbye!
And that's a little rhyme
from both of me.
