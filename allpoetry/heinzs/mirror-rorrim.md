Mirror - rorriM

Reflections
Shadows of former selves
Haunting
Crying out to be heard
Yearning to be seen
Past perfect
Future tense
Wordplay
All part of the image fragment
Seen only with eyes shut
Futility and grief
Anger and resentment
Longing for conviction
Sitting back while life passes by
Raining on the parade
Drops of blood, still warm
Salty to the taste
Like bitter tears
Woe surpassing all
Bereft, joyless
Hopelessly depressed
I cry my own epitaph
I am the only mourner
At my funeral
