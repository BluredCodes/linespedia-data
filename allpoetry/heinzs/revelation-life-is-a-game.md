Revelation (Life is a game)

By new moon's dark
I lit a spark
to chase away the gloom.
To shed some light
throughout the night -
fend off impending doom.

In graveyard scene
I sit serene,
for demons I fear none,
I'll bide my time
and speak the rhyme
before the night is done.

Will-o-the-wisp
with voice so crisp
does bid me leave this place,
but I'll not go
'til daylight's glow
reveals to me her face.

To her I say
"Tell me, I pray,
unveil the mystery!"
The shifting sands
of distant lands
stayed silent as the sea.

"I cannot tell,"
her words did knell,
"the tale you wish to know -
turmoil and strife
must be your life -
the gods have made it so."

With that she left,
and I, bereft,
did tremble in my fear.
Her words of pain
brought tears to rain -
my shattered hopes so dear.

But wait, I thought,
this is for naught -
I will not let this be.
If I must fight
for my birthright,
I'll do it with great glee!

And then, forsooth,
I knew the truth!
thought strife may be the way -
the Gods are wise
and in its guise
will lead me day by day.

So hand in hand
I'll take a stand -
to struggle I'll commit.
Without a pause
take up the cause -
dear reader - tag, you're it!
