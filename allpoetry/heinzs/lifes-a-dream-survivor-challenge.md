Life's A Dream 

I lie alone in the torrid darkness,
Dream of places not yet seen.
My mind's eye seeking adventure,
Life passes me by.

I taste the bitterness of regret,
Live another hour, another day.
My heart yearning to fly free,
Dreams of Camelot of yore.

Reality is a bleak, featureless plain,
And I tire of its desert waste.
Fantasy beckons my soul,
Intertwine my hopes and fears.

My senses reel, spiraling -
Soul numb... the bottle is empty.
Cries pierce my ears.
Out and in, directionless and lost.

In drugged detachment I remain.
Confusion reigns o'er all
And leads me ever deeper.
Desperation has won another soul.
