Extolling the Virtues of Haiku
******************************
One: free verse

Ramblings,
Blatherings,
Valueless gossip -
I tire of all the excess wordage
To which I am daily subjected!
Give to me that which I crave -
Short, sweet, terse and to-the-point -
Haiku!
********************************

Two:  Acrostic

How wonderful you are,
Abstract in content -
Intensely formatted -
Keeping true at all times...
Upstanding Poem!
*************************************

Three:  Acrostic/ tanka

Heavenly poem
At one with Earth's harmonies
I feel the rhythm
Kaleidoscopic visions
Unfold at each syllable
