Hardwired

It starts at conception
cells programmed
genetic perfection
creating an organic machine

Birth continues the process
eye cameras capture images
skin feels mom's caress
muscles learn to move

Computer brain connects
nerve cables, tendons, fibers
mental software selects
gender, identity, persona

So much, of man is required
he forgets he is all animal
with instincts yet hardwired
he does not learn from history

Each new life born to man
must find its own and God's plan.

4/9/2002
