Jareth

Goblin King,
magic and mysticism,
hints of the Faerie might,
associated with music I like.

Romantic, tragic, makes mistakes,
unable to express clearly feelings of love,
misjudging emotions and others,
despite all his ability.

Lokison

Son of Loki,
chaos and confusion,
trickster, trouble maker,
sees things others miss.

Son of a bastard child,
despised for his heritage before
given a chance for prove himself,
so acts as others expect him to.Jareth Lokison is not my real name, maybe I'm too paranoid about internet security.  I noticed a typo or two so decided to clean things up.
