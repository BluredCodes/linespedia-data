title: "Remembering"

Couldn't see or talk to you for half a years time
and during all of that you never left my mind.

Seeing a sky of shinning stars,
remembering us on the car gazing at the Milky Way.

Seeing the full moon glowing brite,
remembering the nights and times we spent together.

Seeing a thunderstorms lightning flash,
remembering the energy and joy found behind your eyes.

Seeing a gusty day, blowing things about,
remembering you spread your arms, dancing with the wind.

Seeing children playing and run around,
remebering your four and your love for them unbound.

Couldn't see or talk to you for half a years time
and during all of that you never left my mind.
