title: "How Do You Eat A Reeses?"

peeling away the outer wrapper,

carefully remove them from their cup,

not too fast not too slow.

Gently place it on my tongue

and pull it into my mouth,

slowly sucking the chocolate off.

As the chocolate slides down my throat

I play with the filling,

between teeth and tonsils.This was posted on a dare, by the female friend who inspired it.  She gave me some minature Reeses that she had been holding between her breasts.
