---
title: "Just one Kiss"
writer: "Jareth Lokison"
slug: "just-one-kiss"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62181-Just-one-Kiss-by-Jareth-Lokison"

Lips feel so lush,
Toungues taste sweet playing together,
Leaves hearts racing fast.
