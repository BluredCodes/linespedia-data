title: "It was only a dream..."

it was only a dream
but what i see next to me
across the border from fantasy to reality
is a sleeping elderly man
with a stressed receding hairline
exhausted layering flesh-folds
and a cancerous mental cavity
this man does not have much time left
and i pity him
or do i?

for once
the obscure vale is lifted
and i can see
i am this man's dream
he is on his last few breaths
short heartbeat couplets follow
and when he dies
he can no longer dream
and so shall it be
that i too

shall die
