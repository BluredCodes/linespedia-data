title: "A Fleeced Flower"
writer: "Malveasor"
slug: "a-fleeced-flower"

Ghosts in the shadows haunt you, frighten you, and mock you
You fear them
They are pictures of a life with a preference to be forgotten
From your mind
Visualizations of times long ago that fester and burrow
Do not fear
We are here to support you and guide you to paths anew

You are loved,
Yes you are

The children giggle innocently to remind you of your pain
Faults not theirs
Motherly love presides and the children are your peers
Teach them well
Society could use a few better people than what it has
Disallow the Internet
We want icons and role models, not another weapon bearer

Even in acceptance,
Pain is inevitable

“Every time a bell rings, and angel gets its wings”
Forty, you have
A soul of torment from poisoned fangs of misunderstanding
You’re not alone
Seep deep into the crevices and minds of the worthy
Forget the past
Allow your instincts to take control of your will

Fingers of pain
Bring another pleasure

The pedals of man can be ridden to the point of ecstasy
Just be honest
Know your limitations and the limitations of those you admire and loathe
They are life
Anything outside of their existence is not worth the time; your time
I am here
Embrace the flower outside of your mind for it is in a garden of stone

Flaws are unavoidable,
Do not deny

Age is but a number, inscribed by time to categorize its peasants
Mere labels; people
Age has caused some of the most profound depression in history
Mere numbers; people
Look past it, and suffer the tragedies of having an open-mind
Do not despair
You are who you are, not for how ancient time depicts you

You are accepted,
Because you’re you


Do not let anyone take that away from you.1/5 of a series written in honor of a friend/enemy.
