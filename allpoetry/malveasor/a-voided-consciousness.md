---
title: "A Voided Consciousness"
writer: "Malveasor"
slug: "a-voided-consciousness"
source: "AllPoetry"

A voided consciousness is not to be reprimanded,
Insanity is challenging normality
Throw massive chunks of earth at those
Who freely live in glass houses

Awaken from the trance
Eyes are upon you to observe
Laughter may follow,
But from those that are uneducated

An echo reverberates through your body
“Unworthy, unworthy,” the little echo says
A bowed spine to kiss the hand of Criticism;
A set of moistened lips to brush its rough skin

An unworthy little firefly that shines a dim light;
Mentally swatted to and fro
For repelling against Satan
And falling narrowly from Heaven’s cloud

A straight line does not exist
Curved reality governs the chasms of your mind
But to voice the unknown is a crime
People were born as a collective ignoramus

Disrupt society with a legion of truth-wielding skeletons
Haunt the people with apparitions of their future—
The Grip of Death that they cannot refuse
For his will outweighs theirs, and for that, they fear your knowledge

Is it insane to perceive and accept the truth?
Or are you to condition and fill your thoughts with ‘hope’?
Dream of rainbows disembarking from clouds above
And little proud gnomes that dance around a garden of sprouting fantasies

But reality is not calm, or forgiving…
Those clouds are filled with a poisonous green hue
The rainbows are doubted thoughts of time lingering above
And the garden the gnomes dance in consists of human skulls

You are not ignorant, insane, or a threat to society
Society is a threat to itself
Hopefully there are more that share your visions of the truth
Yet so long as there are the educated, the uneducated are sure to follow

If you are gasping for air beneath the surface of water,
I will be there to pull you from the grave
But if given the chance…
Would you do the same for me?


The individual thought is often the educated cell in the collective’s prison.5/5 of a series written in honor of a friend/enemy.
