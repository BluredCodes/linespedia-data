Snicker, snicker...
They laughed behind my back
Two peas in a pod;
Two wolves in a pack

Gossip, gossip...
They continue to play
No more sanity
Through their intended disarray

"Hush, hush...
He may hear!
No more courage
When he is near!"

Click, click...
The keyboard doth show:
"As long as we hide behind the internet,
He will never know!"

Hee, hee...
They giggle some more;
But they are juvenile and careless,
For I already know--I know for sure

Cry, cry...
Someone is in pain
Because I found out
Their little game of vain

Tisk, tisk...
I really no longer care
For they were not worth my time;
Wasted time I no longer have to spareSome people will never get out of that childish phase of talking about people behind their back, and then not having the courage to tell the person or make the information public.  And they call -me- the bad guy.
