title: "Disfigured Shadows"

Disfigured shadows
Dance across the planes of glass ambitions
Too eager
Signs of “Thin Glass” are ignored

Disfigured shadows
Run rampant and molest the new day
Foolishly thinking
“Laws of gravity do not apply”

Disfigured shadows
Shame to their appearance to the population
Dancing mad
Far from the eye of any passersby

Disfigured shadows
Longing for a resolution and someone to cling
Dwarfed senses
They touch only their outer selves

Disfigured shadows,
Why will you not reach out to an attainable goal?
Sing merrily
As long as it comforts your soul

Disfigured shadows
Shrouded away from light and sight
Shrink away
Opposition is near and you will get hurt; or will you?

Disfigured shadows
Should not invest so much time into seeking fantasy
Embrace me…
And know oblivion’s eternal bliss


Embrace yourself, and find that you have had what you wanted all along.3/5 of a series written in honor of a friend/enemy.
