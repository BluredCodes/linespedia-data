---
title: "Out of my Mind?"
writer: "Malveasor"
slug: "out-of-my-mind"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57404-Out-of-my-Mind--by-Malveasor"
---

what are you looking at?
why are you staring?
hello?
hello?
not up to your standards?
a hopeless failure?

those are what I ask myself
as I observe the confused
expression on your face;
please, don't be shy
i only bite upon request
are you even listening?

bend over backwards,
is that what you want?
is that what it will take?
do you think i'm crazy?
why do you stare at me
but not word your ignorance?

flesh crawls over my bones
not by choice
not by permission
whether it appeals to you
or not;
your loss either way

patience i'm expected to possess
love i'm supposed to bestow
both are non-refundable
so do not ask,
because i shall not give
and allow my spirit to dwindle

my thoughts travel north
to intercept south,
west to intercept east...
negated norm
i'm not out of my mind
i'm out of yours
