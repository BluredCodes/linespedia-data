title: "Unfavorable Choices"

If ever a pain came to me,
Your loss is so much worse
If ever a pain came to see,
Your second to be is a curse

A pride was hurt and torn,
A friendship led to spoil
Now I have two enemies
From one friendship led to spoil

Choose he, or me...
Options of two
Choose me, or he...
It matters not who

To not choose any at all
Is in its own right a choice
For you have chosen he,
Over myself as the choice

One that disrespects your friend
In turn, disrespects you
Just because you are on the other end
Does not mean over your head it flew

Loyalty is something he does not supply
In his frienship of material things
So really you are just an object,
A part of his friendship of material things

But you refuse to see that
And so parting we must
Now the chair from whence you sat
Will be inhabited by dustSome people just refuse to see the truth.
