title: "Eye of the Rapist"

Let me take control
Let your mind be driven insane
Do not speak a fable
I care not for your name
You are not a label
Your bodies are all the same

From male to female...
From foot to lobe,
I want you either way
In a cavity but to probe,
A soul to take astray,
And an innocent mind to corrode

Shh, hush;
No one can hear
You are all mine now
Allow my body to steer,
Have your conscious bow
For each minute I grow near

Scream 'rape' if you dare
You can flail...
You can kick...
Yet your sanity will set sail
And with each and every prick
Your body cannot bail

Estrogen or testosterone; 
I do not discrimminate
No one can save you
For your body I shall desecrate
You know you want to...
Humans were meant to procreate

It's all over now...
Go back to your little life,
I have no more use
For your malice and your strife;
And your acceptance I'll refuse
As my husband or my wife
