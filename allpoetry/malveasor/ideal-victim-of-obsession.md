Your existence is irrelevant,
You have chosen it to be so
For giving unwarranted credit
To a falsified identity
For a life that was not his to give
Is truly and unconditionally
Pathetic

Error: defunct security

A heart so pure and good
Is easily led to wander,
Deceivers are strong-willed
But in the end it is those that are hurt
Who allow in the pain
Because victims are truly
Naïve

Am I naïve?

We are all a little naïve,
Some more than others
But if you think it is love
(And deep down, you know you do)
Just remember that it is a fantasy
And often a time, fantasy leads to
Delusion

Consumption is eminent

“Friends with benefits” are rarely ever friends
And never beneficial
Friendly intercourse is not bad
Unless it is under false pretenses
“Opportunity knocked,” possibly;
But predators also seize the “opportunities” of consuming
Prey


I hope your lips are not chapped from all that puckering.4/5 of a series written in honor of a friend/enemy.
