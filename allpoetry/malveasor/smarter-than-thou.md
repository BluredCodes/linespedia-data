"Mortal!  I seek information!"
Boomed the arrant Brain Tree
With undivided attention
To his Greatness I did see

"I wish to know..."
The Almighty did exclaim
"...NOW GO!"
He cared not for my name

To his will I was a slave;
Traveling over the globe,
From every sea to every cave...
Nothing pleased the Mighty Lobe
Noting the path to freedom was not easy-pave:
Others for knowledge I began to probe

"I know not what you ask,"
Said the tented Brucey B.
"You will fail your task,"
He then said to me

Everywhere I went
I got the exact same
All cower and pent...
My efforts seemed so lame

I hung my head low
And trekked back to the Brain Interrogator;
My footsteps very slow
Until I was stopped by the Esophagor

"II AMM HUNGGRRYYY,"
He mentioned without fear
I told him I failed the Brain Tree
He looked back with a sneer

"Feeed mmeee..."
He then inquired, coming fully to the surface
"What could it be?"
I asked him of his purpose

"II knooww whhaatt yoouuu sseeek;"
It is probably useless, I dared to summize...
"Gett mmeee fooodd aand yoouu'll haaavee a peeek;"
Caught my ambition by surprise

I rushed for the creature's food,
Sparing no expense
I wanted to please his mood,
Maybe he'd also raise my defense?

Purchasing what he needed,
I hurried back in a flash
To him his food I conceded,
Not realizing the quests both clash

The Esophagor munched away,
Then belched with delight
My mind set astray
For the answer I needed tonight

He told me half,
Then chuckled with joy
He gave me the shaft!
It was all a ploy!
I acted like a calf,
As he played me like a toy

"Brrriiingg mmeee mmoorre foood aandd I'lll teelll yooouu moooree,"
He said with a smile
My funds were depleted for sure...
I had fallen for his guile

There was nothing left to do
As I realized something foul,
Standing firm, I shouted in shrew...
