title: "Man of Petty"
writer: "Malveasor"

Let the children play
For the grown-up is the enemy;
Let the children say,
"We don't want authority"

Let the children run amuck
And see what happens to opposition;
Let the children pry and pluck
At the sigil of a logical vision

Let the children's pride be hurt
For they are a small people;
Let the children cry and assert
For they are only feeble

Let the children hide and scoff
No matter how sad the travesty;
Let the children turn and run off
From fear's painful honesty.
