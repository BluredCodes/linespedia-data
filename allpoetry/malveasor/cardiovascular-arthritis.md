What is this aching?
This probing deep down
In the pit of my rib cage?

As far as I can remember,
There is nothing there
But a faint echo.

Can nothingness hurt?
It must be able to,
Or else I'd feel spectacular.

Maybe one day soon,
A seed will be planted to
Allow what was there to regrow.

Is it worth it?
Only time will be able to tell
So,

Do you happen to have the time?
