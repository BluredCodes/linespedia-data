---
title: "Between You and Me"
writer: "rhinestonecwgrl2"

You make me happy
You make me laugh
You make me smile when I just want to cry

You're there if I need you
You're even there when I don't
You're always there to talk to especially when I need someone the most

I like the way you talk to me
I like the way you flirt
I like the the time we share together though we're far apart

I'm hoping some day I'll meet you
I'm hoping that we'll share some time together
I'm hoping that you'll hold me in your arms and I'll hold you in mine

Until that day though
We'll just have to wait and see
What happens will happen between you and me
