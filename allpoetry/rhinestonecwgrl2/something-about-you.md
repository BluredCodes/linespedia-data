There's just something about you, I just can't explain it
When I look in your eyes, I just can't contain it
I feel like I've known you for half of my lifetime
I was happy that day that you said that you'd be mine
Your soft gentle lips, your warm muscular arms
When I'm with you, I feel I could never meet harm
I just want to see you again and again
I don't want to be just only your friend
I hope that we will always be together
From this point on, from now until forever
