Though you weren't what I expected
I'm totally satisfied
Being shy at first 
I then came open wide
I could talk to you all night
And then some more all day
I had once given up hope
But now I hope every day
Now that we're together
I'll hold on to you forever 
And I'll never let you go
Unless you say so
