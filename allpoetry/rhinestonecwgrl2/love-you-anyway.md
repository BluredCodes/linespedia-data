I don't care what others say
I will love you anyway
I want to be with you forever
Break up we will never ever
Some will say that we won't last
And they'll say we move too fast
But I don't care what others say
I still love you anyway
You will be the love of my life
Even through the pain and strife
Some will say you'll break my heart
Then I'll tell them we'll never part
But I don't care what others say
I'll always love you anyway
