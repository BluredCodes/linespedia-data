title: "Will It Ever Happen?"

Will it ever happen?
Me with you
You with me
Will it ever happen?
Me your arms
You in mine
Will it ever happen?
Me kissing you
You kissing me
Will it ever happen?
Me wispering sweet nothings in your ear
You wispering sweet nothings in my ear
Will it ever happen?
Me hopelessly in love with you
You hopelessly in love with me
Will it ever happen?
I don't know
But I can only hope
