she loves, she loves and still 
she loves still more, 
to her utterance of love 
in the face of destruction 
a mirror's length away 

so the mirror is double sided 
with her soul stretched with 
renound beauty... can she love more...more

renound beauty wound round her soul 
from love's lament she loved and 
loved, and still 
dark eyed she loves some more, 
double sided mirror show's no grace, 
yet meets her with a lowly face

for now I see the beauty 
from within... It seems she found 
her real love within 
her own zen.. it wasnt in 
the mirror, yet it was found 
in heart and sky..... 
as she felt her own 
love and warmth, 
a feeling so... so high
