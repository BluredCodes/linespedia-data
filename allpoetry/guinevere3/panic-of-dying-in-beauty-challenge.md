As the sun began to rise . 
After a night of expectations on high... 
Blood from the necks of virgins... 
Blood from the souls of virgins... 
Involuntaryly my walk provoked  
In trances In trances In traces... 
To my chamber of slumber. 
The footprints I knew well should have 
Taken me to daytime slumbering... 
For the day's 
Light would surly kill me. 
But something from my night dreams... 
Something from those ghastly dreams 
Forced my trace-stares to take a back seat, 
Now I was following what seemed to be the 
Brightest of angels , was it a new woman... 
I fled for her golden hair, looking back 
where my chamber awaited me, wanted me 
To slumber in just his arm... 
My pale skin began to burn a bit, 
I knew that time was not on our side. 
But this journey took hold of my heart nonetheless... 
I ran after her, though she seemed 
So far from me now 
What do I do, I can not turn back and face the light 
I must reach her soon, before I die 
In her very own 
Light 
That is when I realized, my death would come soon, 
My death by following fragile heartstrings 
Moving me from what had been taught, 
what I had fought against 
For so long... 
This nightmare of sun shining high in the sky 
this nightmare is mine, the sun so very bright now... 
And the burning... 
The burning... 
The burning... and the heart 
never there laughing at me... 
Burning... I went to the piles of green flowing grass 
Burining...I had not known of beauty kept only for day in so long... 
Oh the burning... 
In between the pains and the burning 
The screams to nowhere that no one can hear... 
I enjoyed 
For one brief moment all I had forgotten before... 
Slumber of day and fight in night, I forgot the beauty I am 
now dying with ease in...  
A blessing for a vampire like me! 
who knew of only ugliness death and shame, now I die with all 
Nature has for me.
