I met him on a dark and drunken eve
we walked
we talked
We laid together...
To him I transformed into the
Most beautiful creature in his world.
But he had to leave...back to 
France he said,
as he gave me a  bunny and
A promise that he would marry me one day...
What a beautiful way
What a beautiful way
I love him, I knew we would marry
Through troubles and tribulations
I love him, and I knew we would marry...
Still gone, as am I 
I wonder what thoughts cross his mind.
for seven years we have visited
we have written
we have loved, so now is time
time to unite as one
My darling French love , he is
beautiful,
And I am in love
though one thing that frustrated me, holds me here.
California, the death, the butterflies, the 
Pickup truck. 
But I am in love and need to fall to his hands
His warm arms to comfort me when I cry out in sleep
I need that , for I feel scared and love 
Love is not so scarey to me
Love is my copius prize, be it in France or be
It in a pick up truck bound for no where in 
California
It is time...
It is time...
I want to be yours
alone 
I want to be yours
alone
I want you to be mine
I will cherish
I want you to be mine
I will cherish
