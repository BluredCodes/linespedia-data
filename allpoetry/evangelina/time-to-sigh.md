title: "Time to Sigh"

Just as you are
       I have begun to see -

        you are the lyrics
          to the vixen
             of me.

               ~*~

Drowning in the ocean 
to catch but a glimpse of you- 

My eyes sting from the seaweed 
clouding my sight. 

~*~ 

Biting my lip 
Scratching the paint- 

This mask slowly tears 
because of you 

~*~ 

Take in the hint 
of jasmine- 

the garden of mine and 
lilies. 

~*~ 

tokens gathered 
and thrown in the 
sea of the nameless- 

another heartache to mend. 

~*~  ~*~I decided to combine the two seperate works.
