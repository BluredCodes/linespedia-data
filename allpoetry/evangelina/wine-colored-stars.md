title: "Wine Colored Stars"

Bonfire of heat to rise
in the night skies.

Cold chill to breathe.

Killing the passon in me tonight.

Would you wipe tears from my eyes?

This empty stare because you weren't there.

Sweet crystal drops like perfume.

Sax solo...
      and it's lonely tune.what would you name this if the only sound in your head was a lonely alto sax solo?
