title: "take a joyride"
writer: "evangelina"

there's something i see in the eyes 
that look at me, 
staring into the depths 
of my insanity. 

can you see me breathing 
for the lark of ocean waves, 
leaping in a boundless chain 
of the silence of the lame? 

take in the scent of my skin,
and shriek with delight.
the pounding sensation might
drive you insane with me.

in a day or two-
you will be lost
in the crazy rollercoaster,
that has been the cost
of knowing my heart of passion
and crave...
you will only find the entrance
of careless treason,
lacking any logical reason
to writhe in the hunger of intense
climax

that
      never
               ends
                      with a passionate shame,
                      of a lustful game,
                       brushing the insanity
                       beneath me.another huge thanx to michael dennis rivers for the help :)  because sometimes...i do need help
