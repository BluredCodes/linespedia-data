title: "Angel Baby's Mornin'"
writer: "evangelina"
slug: "angel-babys-mornin"
source: "AllPoetry"

i see you sitting on the coffee table
with your arms crossed as you pout.
hair strung from a night's sleep
and t-shirt for bed.
you asked me if you could wear
your high heels to school.
i told you it's cold out
and you need to wear your
tenneshoes instead.
you don't want to wear your
blue jeans because you want
to wear your dress.
but the wind outside would
freeze your legs,
and you seem not to care.
your eyes roll to the side
as your mouth turn to a 
delicate frown.
even as your foot taps
on the floor in a lady-like
demeanor,
i can't help but think of 
the painting this could create.
i should take a picture of this,
oh no! it's too late.
you've already moved from
that classic sulky pose.
i saw another Rockwell as
you stood in the pose
of a little girl just waiting to
grow up like me.
darlin', you just take it easy,
okay?
'cause there's nothing better
than the days
such as this, and i know you
don't believe me.
but maybe some day, you will
finally see
that there's nothing better
than being a child...
so careless and free.trust me, it's a gorgeous painting *smile*
