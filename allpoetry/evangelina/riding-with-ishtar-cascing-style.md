Consider the possibility of the world in wonder,
Wondering that which doesn’t hold the sapling of rest,
Restless tracing to comprehend the battles you face.

Facing a masterpiece of what classic interest can be,
Being a goddess of laughter and sensual love,
Loving the womanly descent which ascends to the skies.

The sky is waiting to see your arms raised to the spiral,
Spiraling high to endless flights of your deepest flame,
Flaming the whim to be the paradise of your dreams.

Dream in the fragile state which can break without your trust,
Keep trusting the magic of the crowned moon as it glows-
Still glowing through the strength to whisper in the silence.

Silently, we will both try to complete a circle within this glass,
