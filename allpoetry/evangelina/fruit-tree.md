There was a strong voice singing
about a tree of strange fruit.  I
didn't know what it meant the first
time she sang to me. 
I was too blind to see.

A sad and haunting tale
of bodies swaying under poplars,
finally bringing chills
down my spine. 

It's hard to believe we
could such a thing-
such a cold hearted shame.
the strangest fruit is
the memory of faceless blame.inspired by Billie Holiday's "Strange Fruit"
