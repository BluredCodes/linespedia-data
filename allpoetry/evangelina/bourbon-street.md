title: "Bourbon Street"
writer: "evangelina"

All I wanted to do
was to be with you,
for one night
with no one else around.

All I wanted to do
was to talk to you,
like the friend 
I thought you would be.

And all I wanted to be
was someone you could see
as well as everyone seems
to do...
when they look at you.dedicated to a special person that I've known since I was a child.
