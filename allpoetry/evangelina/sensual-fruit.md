---
title: "Sensual Fruit"
writer: "evangelina"
slug: "sensual-fruit"

I felt your touch 
sliding slowly 
  
    
  soft touch
 of quivering fingertips
    kissing my body
      to relieve the pain
     of my heart

  your sweet words
       caress my tears
     as your mouth
     explores a world
       within me
  
   secrets unravel
           in this classic pose
          of a lust that never dies

    teasing each other
   
with a hunger of desire
   seeping through the heaviness
   of our eyes

    driving me to an astral
  island of sensual fruits to 
        feed,
    i realize that it is you
  i will always need...

    so keep your fingertips
 playing on an instrument that
     is dedicated to you,
slide
  into 
     the 
       passion
    that rests between me
  and i will do the same for you.
just don't let this moment die from
     a universe that is ours alone,
   i want you to keep tasting the harmony
 that so tenderly becomes a part of you and me.
