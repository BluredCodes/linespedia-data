title: "She Left Me"

There was no exit to find
what continuously rambled
  in her mind.
Looking back into the distance,
there is a small child at the end
 of the flight.
Racing against time,
she tries to reach the thyme
of pale colored scent,
leaving a trail which was meant
to be a lost in the child's sanity.
Reaching the end,
she can see nothing.
The child is gone
and the embrace is nowhere to be found.
She sits quietly on the silver ground
to weep for a mourning
  that never left.
The child has been soaring.written about a dream from several years ago
