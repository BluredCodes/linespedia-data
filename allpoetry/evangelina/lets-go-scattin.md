shimi dee da doo bop buh dee

 whatcha doin' to that crazy scat
bullwhatten fool?
doncha know there's a place to
go shootin' with the pool shark?
let's go walkin' in the dark.

zabba zabba zabba zuzzoo

you're such a craaaazy little stool!
battin' your flashin'
emerald eyes in a blazin' kinda way,
oh, now baby don't do me that way.

huzza huzza dazza scuzzoo

let's go scattin' baby
down the river mazoo
'cuz you know what is that 
i'm lookin' to do
for you.

find that zoot suit and 
put it on quickly too
 let's go find the reefer man
and he'll tell us what to do
to find that scattered tender face
huzza huzza scamaza doo....HAHA...my hilarious and sad attempt to compete w/ Cab Calloway :)
