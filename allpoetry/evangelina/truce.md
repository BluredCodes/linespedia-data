when you're ready to talk to me,
i'll be there for you, i swear.
but i can't always start the 
conversations with only a hope
that you'll be truthful too.
i don't like being distant
with you, i can't believe
i can't find an ounce of trust
in you.
but there will never be
any other who gets to me 
the way you do.
and so, i will always be there
for you just because it's what
i want to do.mmmm, just had to get that out of my system i guess
