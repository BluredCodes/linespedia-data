Life was the worst
Til you came along
You taught me to laugh
You gave me a song!

And still you were there
When I needed a Friend
From early morning
Until the Day ends

Someone to hold me
Someone to care for me 
Someone I know
Will Always Be There!Author:my mom, a poetess in her own right-
