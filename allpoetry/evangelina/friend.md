I let my heart fall in foolish hands
I was hungry for just one faithful friend

They took my love and threw it,
just like sand in the sea!

Then the name of love they kept 
wasting on me

Then I meet a perfect man,
who was my Friend.

This old world can never hurt me again!

The tears I cried, the hurt I felt,
the scars that are in my heart!

But this world will never touch me again.Author:my mom, a poetess in her own right-
