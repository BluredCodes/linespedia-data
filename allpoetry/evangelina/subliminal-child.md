title: "Subliminal Child"
writer: "evangelina"

subliminal
subliminal 
subliminal
sublime
to the thoughts
that rhyme
from the secrets
untold.

whispering
the whispers
of the 
demons unfurled,
because they 
won't leave me
alone.

fools
keep foolin' 
each other
for the sake
of the game.
and yet it's 
the challenge
that we'd love
to tame.

beggers
keep beggin'
to beg for 
something more.
if only to keep
breathin' in
that foolish lore.

don't ask me
but keep askin'
for the sake of 
your smile.
there's only
a little bit
of you in this
timeless style.

subliminal
sublime
messages to hear.
if only they knew
that i, too, can
fake a tear.
and the rhymes are
only from two
i hold so dear.
well, maybe three
or four.
but i'm never gonna
tell.i'm not gonna tell  (okay, to get this phrase, you must think of the sing-song beat of a child-like state.  you know, the word 'tell' lasts for 5-7 seconds longer)  *smile*
