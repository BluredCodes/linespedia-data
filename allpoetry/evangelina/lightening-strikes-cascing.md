What made you think of giving in to the childish dare,
Daring the impossible to take place of this extreme reality?
Realistically, you should be ashamed of the contradictions you face.

Facing the wings of confusion to escape from what you feel,
Have you felt the passion reach its final explosion?
Explosive rage will pour when you’ve lost the control.

Controlling the days of which you do not rule is what you crave,
What craving can I give you to replace this reckless need?
Needful wanderings have jinxed these weary eyes to blindness.

Keep blinding me with the outrageous gymnastics of your energy.
Is your energetic philosophy something to selfishly contain?
Containment is only the death of enlightenment.

So enlighten us with that spark that I have been missing,
