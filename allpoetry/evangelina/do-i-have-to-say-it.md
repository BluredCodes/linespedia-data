i try to say something
                      (anything)

                and my mouth doesn't move
                    words get caught 
                 i wish i knew how to 
                      tell you
                          
                          *
                  
              but i'd bet you already knowhe's probably gonna tell me to stop writing about him already.  but my favorite inspiration just happens to be associated with a blade.
