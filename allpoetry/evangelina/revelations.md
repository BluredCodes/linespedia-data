ah, i think i do finally see
what is becoming of me...

without him i am nothing
because he made me as i was
and without him at my side
i drown in oblivion

to fade so fast without
the creator to see me through
i would be a speck that 
would never have been

BAH! who needs that anyway?
i always said i was a loner...
he wants me to drift to show me
something i don't care to hear
about...
i'll drift in my own direction
