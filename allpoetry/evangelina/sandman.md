You lay me down on the sand below
 the water rushes around us with a slow
 crest of cream flowing for your body and mine.

 The gentleness of your touch
sends the heat through my skin as
my head turns...allowing you to dive in
to the deep turn of my throat.

   the softest kiss...
 raining the showers of your sweet lips
 as I slowly lose myself in the warmth
 of your paradise.

   with a tender hold, your arms reach 'round
  the small of my back. taking the time to
   mold the clay of passion as my
   body begins the convulsions
   of ultimate desire...

     so strange to know...
    there was never a touch below
    the shoulders to create a feeling
    such as this.  
 
    this eternal moment of the sandman's kiss.
