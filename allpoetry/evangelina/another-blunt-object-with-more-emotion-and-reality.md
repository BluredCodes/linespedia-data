Sometimes I don't understand 
  why you won't say anything at all.
If we're friends then let me know
  where we are.
I feel so isolated and you don't
  seem to care.
So why am I even here?

Is there something you want to say?
      I hear it every day
in the voices of others.  Are you a 
coward to let me know?  I've done just
about everything to show
you what goes on in my head.
The withdrawals just created more death
       inside of me.
  Now you'll never know what it meant
to know me.  Your arrogance of me will only
fuel my fire.  You will never get the chance
to see
what is really inside of me.
It will play on your mind of whether it's real
        or a game, just as you've done to me.
    Just let me be.
