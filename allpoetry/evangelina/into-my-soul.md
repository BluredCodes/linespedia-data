---
title: "Into my soul"
writer: "evangelina"
slug: "into-my-soul"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57361-Into-my-soul-by-evangelina"
---

it's like an old car wash
that has the streamers hanging
down

i'm walking through this maze
searching for something...
anyone...
and i can't seem to find 
the focus.
i hear the voices of others
calling for me.
it must be too dark for them
to see my arms reached out
to just touch the warmth
of another soul.

i see glints of silver passing
by every now and then.
but the swarm of confusion
is covering the point of view-

    an eclipse...
     lunar eclipse...
    and i can't figure out
    when it will stop.

that's what it's like to see into
my soul,
to see my emptiness...

oh people, that's the soul you're
trying to find and it's not doing
us any good.
unless you know the path to this
darkened room...

that's what it's like to see into my soul.
