(inny,minny,mieny,moe
catch a tiger by its toe...)

Decision to make
Friends to choose
Wandering mind
Destined to lose

Stillness of the early morning with the
fog settling over the fields and the
grey of the skies looking exactly
as I feel

(if it hollers make it pay
fifty dollars every day...)

Screams fill my heart
Tearing at my soul
Sheep turn to wolves
As I make myself whole

Does the cost of being alone outweigh
the cost of knowing warmth and care?
Only those who have tread both paths
will surely dare
to try to give a damn

(my mama told me to pick the very best one...)

Childhood memories
never fade
the wisdom of the mother
and playing the charade

(and you are not it.)
