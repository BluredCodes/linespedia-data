Linger with me in this
fateful night, my love, to
  taste the flesh and blood
  of those before me.
Sink your teeth and claws into
  my skin,
and partake of my riches within
this frightful flood
of jewels.
   This is what I see in the
ugliness of you...
'tis beauty of you and me,
   enveloped in the hunger of two
   seekers of the darkest love.

Grasp my stare into your
    marbled eyes
    and find a river of lust,
  quaking to a rumble of admiration.
As I rake my nails over your back,
   I see the fitful sensation
   as it graces your tremors.

We can leave them alone tonight
    as we feast on each other instead.
Streams of dead screams can fill the
  music in our heads
to ignite the flame of desire, killing
the two
who have decided to fall for
a mix of death and lust.
