There is a brave warrior inside the girl I once knew.
    She became a woman more than I could ever do,
    She laughs with a brilliant sparkle in her eyes
    and she lends a gentle shoulder when a friend dies,
    such a tiny frame to be so strong
    and yet the strength does shine through,
    this friend is much more than the little one
    I once knew.

  At times, she has been my teacher.
  And then again, she has been an annoying little sister.
  But if there is one thing that I will never deny,
  it would be the bond between she and I.
    This young woman has given me sight,
    even if only a small amount, to see
    what I would like to be.

   From the same blood, surely there is 
     a brave warrior somewhere 
     in this young woman of me.
