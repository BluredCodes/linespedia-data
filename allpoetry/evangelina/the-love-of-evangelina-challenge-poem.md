[Evangelina], I'm not sure if you know, 
but [Jimi's in the river] waiting for a show 
of that [French jazz thing] you do. 

His [devotions] to you have left me 
in a twirling spin of jealousy and spite. 
At times, I think I wouldn't mind 
to take you to [the end] of hell 
just to take you away. 

    and yet you have no idea what i'm trying to say. 

[When my head hurts], I just have to ask for you 
  and you [numb me] with the things you do. 
  [You are the one] that I want to find, 
  so just [shake me down] to the cajun rivers- 

    and [be my valentine]. 


[It's just so simple], I only [wanted you to want me]. 
And if it's too much know that [I'd do you], 
then, in the end, it's [nobody's pain but mine]. 
All of this will end in a triangular sigh -
