it seems so easy to wish for 
the emotional pain
when the physical pain 
surrounds you,
and then you'd rather feel 
the cuts and bruises
of the flesh when the emotions
seem to boil you through
the rising heat.

sometimes i think i can
handle the feelings
that torture my mind and soul,
but i think i'd rather
take the pain of my weary body
than to realize that my spirit
is not very whole
