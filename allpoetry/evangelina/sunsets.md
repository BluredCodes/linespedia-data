The sun sets in the west
so beautiful in the sky

The peace that falls over the night
sky.

For you hear the night frogs,
the crickets outside,
the wind that blows through 
the air
That makes it so beautifull at night 
fall air.
~*~

The sun sets in the west
so beautiful in the sky,
Orange and pale rose
to set the onyx stone.

The peace that falls over the night 
sky...

The eyes of the universe
to help me see through
the galaxies of poetic rhyme.

~*~

Towards the planes
of not knowing what lies
ahead of me...

I search through the
creatures of the night
and I find the peace 
that she can see.

~**~Author: my mom, a poetess in her own right; and myself
