title: "red passion"
writer: "evangelina"

sultry blues or classic rock
to set someone's soul on fire
for a melody that echoes through
the room

classy jazz or lovestruck oldies
to send a dream in someone's eyes,
or maybe gothic sorrow to relish
the gloom

ah, the sounds of the voices
in harmony blending the melody
to oblivion...

it's been a while since i've sang
with anyone who understands
what i feel with the waves
of this passion 

it's been a while since i've found
anyone who knows what it is
to lose yourself in the caves
of mindless action

dive into a mosh pit to beat
out the drums of anger and sweat
to the jumping relics of banging
with the bats

swaying to the croon of the blue 
eyes and possessed by the 
slide of the king and cocoa
of the scats

sigh...
to love the nectar of heaven
with someone who understands...
anyone who knows what it's like
to melt in the hands
of the musicians and vocals,
whether it's a legend or new...
for this, just ask me what i'd do.i've been on a quest of my musical passion lately :) maybe i'll get the ability to get lost in my music again someday
