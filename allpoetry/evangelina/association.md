title: "Association"
writer: "evangelina"

die is to live
live is to kill
kill is to razor
razor is to bleed

how long does the river flow
of burgundy streams
'til the death of us
finally ends?

calculate is to confuse
confuse is to isolate
isolate is to hunger
hunger is to feed

would you sell your dreams
and fears to settle for a bite
of the silent red river?

dream is to beg
beg is to steal
steal is to give
give is to find

find the dream that you
have stolen from us.
isolate the confusion
to stop the stream.
bleed with the razor
to kill the hunger.
