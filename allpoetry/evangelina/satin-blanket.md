(deep breath...exhale)

  Arms open wide
along the satin of the wind
 Calm trance to sense the peace
 of the purple mountains...

 my victory...

 still unleashed.

my love...
 still caged.

  In a heart 
that has become stone,
  I no longer breathe.

 Set me free...
 (whisper the satin away)
 Why won't you set me free?
