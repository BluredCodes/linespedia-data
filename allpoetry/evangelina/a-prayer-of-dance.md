---
title: "A Prayer of Dance"
writer: "evangelina"
slug: "a-prayer-of-dance"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Terpsichore! Why have you left me?
  The dance is lost
  and the music does 
  nothing to me anymore!

Oh,Terpsichore! Where is the melody?
   You gave me the insanity
   to get lost in the harmony,
   and I have lost once more.

Dearest Terpsichore! Don't take it away!
   You, dancer of the song,
   knows what I feel
   when I hear the classical score.

Oh, Terpsichore, I am on my knees.
   My pride and love will be yours.
   The sweet movement that sweeps
   the rythym under the shore
   of your song is lost once more.

Terpsichore, my muse of magical sway,
    I cannot understand the decay
    of this abandoned shelter.
    If you could only lend me a day
    of another succulent memory,
    with a kiss of your lyre.

  I have nothing more than my voice
    as an instrument to your beauty.
  My personal queen of nightly charm,
   Terpsichore, I have felt the alarm
   of living without your song.
  
  Sweet, sweet, Terpsichore! Oh, I do
   need to breathe your divine note
   into my life of death.
   Please, bright one, give me just
   one more day of your gentle breath.
