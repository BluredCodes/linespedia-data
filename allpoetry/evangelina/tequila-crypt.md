a beautiful morning to reach
  in the softness;
 cradles of sticks to offer
 a bed.

 terror escapes the silent voices
 as the moon lifts its head.
  
 gently pick up the tender ones
  when they are at their worst,
  movement too quick to keep up
  and the sunrise will find them dead.
