title: "Burn With Me"

I saw her sitting on the curb
crying. Her shoulders shook under 
the heaviness of the tears. remembering
 the final goodbye of a friend she loved,

she tried to hide the pain..with no success.
I did nothing as I felt the inflictions
of her dying tears. Another trace of my
heartless acts.  Biting the haze of cynical
words, I sat beside her.

I did nothing. She looked at me with such
sweetness, I started to cry. even the times
such as this, I still try
to understand the hurt I see
in another person's eyes.
