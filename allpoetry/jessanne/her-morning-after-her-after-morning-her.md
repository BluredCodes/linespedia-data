( phone ringing )
                 ( door slamming )

( foot steps )

           ( red shigh heel shoes next to the couch )

                                  "Puked again this morning."
                         "To many god damn beers last night."

(puts on week old dirty socks)

         "Where the hell did you run off? Where'd you going?"
                         "Didn't think I would notice right?"

( trail of beer cans leading to the fridge )

       "Don't even give me that "I had something to do" line."
                       "You know I've heard that shit before."

( fridge slams shut )

                       "Hey! where you goin now? You're mine!"
       "Don't let that hit your ass one the way out the door."

( red high heal shoes no longer by the couch )

                  "Get me another one of those before you go."
                "I've got a whole 'nother day ta get through."

( car keys jingle )

                       "And this one's just beginnin ya know."
           "Oh just quit yer shit, I don't have time for you."


            ( door slams )

    ( tires squeel )




                                         ( toilet flushes )
