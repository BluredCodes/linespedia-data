So okay, I have orange hair
And I'm not very tall
My skin is pale and fair
But I'm still to small

My eyes always cricled dark
And my wardrobe full of black
On my arm I have a mark
And I am swayed in the back

I have a hole in my bottom lip
And also in my nose
And yes I have a set of hips
And silver painted toes

My fingernails are always broke
My hair is kinda short
I have a tendency to smoke
And I'm the nervous sort
