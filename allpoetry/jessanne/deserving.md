Today I took a shower.
Am I deserving?

I meditated for an hour.
Am I deserving?

I cleaned the kitchen
I left my phone on
I made the bed
I kept myself fed
I sent out email
I stayed out of jail
I didn't do drugs
I didn't kill bugs
I washed my hands twice
I tried to be nice
I talked to my mom
I thought up a song
I turned off the lights
I stayed up all night
I had two cups of coffee
I played GTA 3

I had some inspiration.
Am I deserving?

I had gratification.
Am I deserving?
