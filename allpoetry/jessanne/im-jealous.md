---
title: "I'm Jealous"
writer: "Jessanne"

I'm jealous of your lover
I just can't face the day
Why couldn't it be another
That doesn't talk that way



I'm jealous of your lover
But I just can't look away
And if you had some other
Then she wouldn't walk that way



I'm jealous of your lover
There's not alot to say
Do you think that I could have her number
Maybe she'd swing this way
