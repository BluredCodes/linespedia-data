My favorite time of year is night
When it rains in the spring
I go out to the waterfall
Listen to it sing

Night caresses
As the rain takes in all
Above me loom the ivy tresses
And the resident bats take to flight

My favorite time of year is night
When it rains in the spring
I go out to the waterfall
Listen to it sing

I sit watching
The moon traces thin
Lines of midnight all around
And the rain beads up on my skin

My favorite time of year is night
When it rains in the spring
I go out to the waterfall
Listen to it sing

Rain and
The night silence
Fold up in and around me
Holding me in some slow suspense

My favorite time of year is night
When it rains in the spring
I go out to the waterfall
Listen to it sing

The water falls
Moves in a slow motion
In the darkness I feel more than hear
This spring night is a sweet slow potion
