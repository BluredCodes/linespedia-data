Happy Easter, Kenny

I had a dream
The dream was about you
That your family will grow and prosper
And you would get to know me too!   

I thought if I planted a seed,
And shared my business with you,
It would cultivate and grow,
And you'd be successful too!

As you succeed you get to know me;
If I ever needed your help again;
You would love me enough;
Mom, I'll come home, when?

You told me you did not know me enough;
To come and help me when I so ill.
I am sorry, I am the only Mom you have,
Emotional, loving, caring, I fit the bill.

What a Dream?  To have you near.
 How could I wish so much?
That you loved me enough,
You would be close enough to touch.

You are right to be angry 
At the Mom who created your job
Wanted you successful and happy,
Who's an emotional, loving, slob.

You see, I am sorry my son
I am the only one
I will not change my compassion
And I will always love you a ton.

I have to think very clear,
To stand up and fight or fall on my ear,
This company is my last chance 
My time is coming near.

With God I will make it!
To stand up and be part of His Army!
Fight for the health of His people
And allow Him to heal them through me.

I have God's strength, 
I have God's guidance,
I can do it and I wish you would join me.
We can unite and any division will be a passing glance.
