Thanks To A True Friend!

I am indeed blessed
To have a true friend
I am blessed to have you
I know your friendship will never end.

We have been through thick
We have been through thin
Yet we are here together
God is our source deep within.

I thank God He touches your heart
My husband invited you to my home
There we were sharing God's promises
Our friendship will not roam.

We pray, love, care, share, smile, 
God takes command.
We heal, hurt, and we touch others hearts,
Friendship united - God guides our hand.
