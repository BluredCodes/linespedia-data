To My  Unborn, Grandchild,

You are special, indeed,
God has given you a great gift!
Because of your presents 
People's spirits begin to lift.

Thanks for joining our family.
I love you, my dear one,
You bring joy to my life.
You are as bright as the sun

Thank God you joined us
I am so proud, I could bust. Birthday to be is June 2002
