Jim

I am so blessed
I have so much happiness
My brother came back to me
He showed me his humanity.
You see he loves me!
He really cares and he made me see.
He has put value in my life.
By speaking to him – away goes my strife.
The pain goes away, each and every day.
I am proud that my brother came my way.
After seventeen years we met again.
Now I have a great loving friend.
I am blessed
To have such happiness.
Before I go 
I got to show
Brother, Jim, can’t you see,
How much your love strengthens me..
I am so proud of you
I am glad you are happy too.
I see mom dancing from the roof tops
She always wanted her family together – no stops.

Thanks Bro, for taking the time,
To come to visit me - your family is so fine.
We opened up to each other,
That releases the pain, brother.


Momma look, can you see
Our family is coming together and becoming a we.
I can feel your arms come around us with pride.
You did all you could, you  were always at our side.
Momma, Jim can write songs just like you!
Your talents are coming through.

Now I can sit back and smile at life.
I can Praise God I had the strife.
I can have compassion for others you see.
Because of all that has happen to me.

Thank you brother for loving me so.
Thank you sis, mom, dad and bro.
You made a difference in my life too!
I am so proud to have a family like you!
