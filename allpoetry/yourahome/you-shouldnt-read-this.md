title: "you shouldn't read this"
writer: "yourahome"
slug: "you-shouldnt-read-this"
source: "AllPoetry"

you shouldn’t read this, 
so I wrote it 
for you. 
you shouldn’t live in 
this house, 
of anxiety 
that infects 
like dirt 
and clings 
like affection, 
but I built it for you. 
you shouldn’t hear this, 
so fucking listen 
to me. 
you shouldn’t settle 
for someone 
who respects you, 
or loves you; 
it’s not enough; 
strive for more, 
for better looking 
or less weird; 
be superficial: 
it works, 
but for you 
I don’t. 
you shouldn’t work for this, 
so I won’t 
even try. 
you shouldn’t care for this, 
so don’t pretend 
to cry. 
you shouldn’t crave this, 
at night, 
or alone, 
but please 
kiss me 
goodbye.
