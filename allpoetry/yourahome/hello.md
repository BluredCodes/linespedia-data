I walk around like I’m blind
feeling somewhat confined
or without direction though
sure do I know
where I’d like to go
so the sun I follow

but I’m lost and left behind
confusion like losing my mind
with everything so undefined
like a million different paths intertwined
and covered in the thickness of cold snow
standing still as emotions overflow

the future before me begins to unwind
and in the distance is the marvellous glow
of the place assigned
for me to dream you saying hello
