Tell me what’s wrong
Tell me what’s going on
Tell me why you keep me so far away
Why you ran away again

Is there something that you’re looking for?
Something I can’t provide?
Tell me why you keep it all inside
You’ve got so much to hide from me

Where can I find?
The source of all your pain
Don’t tell me you can’t explain!
Don’t tell me I’m not the same! (why?)

Chorus:
You think I don’t know what’s on your mind
You think I can’t find a reason why
You think that I’m blind to all you see
Why can’t you find the time for me?

Verse:
Why can’t you see?
I can satisfy all your needs (with ease)
Tell me have you had enough
Tell me am I not good enough for you?!

Is there something that you’re keeping from me?
A secret you cannot tell?
You won’ t even speak to me
You can’t even look at me! (Now)

Can you give me a reason?
Why I saw you out with him
Don’t tell me he’s just a friend!
Don’t tell me this is the end! (now?)

Chorus:
How could you lie all this time?
I know now that you are not mine
I was so blind, but now I see
I think its time for me to leave

I can’t believe I held out this long
What did I do that was so wrong?
How could you look me in the eye?
And tell me your love was just a lie?
