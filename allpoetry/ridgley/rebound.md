Can't imagine where I'd be
With out you here with me
But when I'm by your side
You don't even blink an eye for me

Don't understand, where I stand
Don't know who I am anymore
Why I play this game that I can't win
Cause you're still thinking about him

Chorus:
Yeah, I really want you
But I just can't seem to get through
He was all you could never need
Why can't you be with me
No, you don't have to lie
Cause I'm just your rebound guy
It doesn't matter how I feel
Cause you know its not for real

No I can't explain
How I deal with so much pain
With this and thay you put me through
Why do I put up with you

Cause you're all I wanted
Everything I've needed
All I've ever dreamed of
And now your here with me love

Chorus
