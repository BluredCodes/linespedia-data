---
title: "Sweet & Innocent"
writer: "Ridgley"
slug: "sweet-innocent"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59788-Sweet---Innocent-by-Ridgley"

Seems when we last met
You were with him
Now it seems he's left
And I wonder how you've, been

Have you moved so fast?
Are you still alone?
Do I have a chance?
Do you have a phone?

Chorus:
Cause I just want to talk to you
Tell me is it worth my time?
Tell me what you've been up to
I won't cross that line
Unless you want me to

Yes I see that smile
Am I getting through?
And I like your style
Hope you like mine too

Let's go for a walk
We've got all night
I just want to talk
Hold your hand tight

Chorus

Bridge:
Sweet and innocent I look at you
And you look at me too

2nd Bridge:
With my arms around you I can't see
Nothing seems to matter to me
And even if you walk away
I'm still thankful for what we had today

Chorus:
Cause I just wanted to talk to you
And I think it was worth my time
Cause now I know what we've been up to
I think we crossed that line
Cause we wanted too
Sweet and Innocent I looked at you
And you looked at me too
