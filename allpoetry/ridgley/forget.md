You never liked the way I sang
When I played you songs
And eventhough I sang them from the heart
You never sang along

I don't know why we argue
When I'm always wrong
Now I'm here all by myself
And You, You are gone

Chorus:
So I go out with my friends
And have a good time
Try to replace that space in my head
You're in my mind
Pour me another to forget
I'll be just fine
No, I don't think that I drink too much
Now I'm on my own

I don't know what you
Didn't see in me
Maybe I'm just not who
You needed my to be
Why can't you see that you
Are all I really need
Now you won't come back
And I won't beg and plead

Chorus

I don't know why I can't seem to find
A love to replace the one you've left behindAn acoustic bit that starts slow, and picks up with a punchier chorus and a sincere ending.
