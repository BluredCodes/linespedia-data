---
title: "Angelz"
writer: "PurityMx"
slug: "angelz"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59962-Angelz-by-PurityMx"
---

take a jump to the sky
keep your head up high
fly with me
to the end of the sea
cause' im right here
by your side
and i cant stand
to see those tears in your eyes

hold my hand
take a walk with me in the clouds
together we will fly
together we will fly
together we will fly

dont look back
make your thoughts mine
cause' im right here
by your side
dont give up on me
ill stitch up your wings
together well be
forever well be
flying high
together in the sky
