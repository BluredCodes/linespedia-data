---
title: "FaCE me"
writer: "PurityMx"
slug: "face-me"
source: "AllPoetry"

come on
turn around and face me
come on
turn around and face me
dont think
just breathe
open up your mind
and give in to me

dont turn - your back
dont turn - your back
dont turn - your back
dont turn - your back

and face me
look into my eyes
tell me you dont care
turn around and face me
dont think
just breathe
open up your mind
and give in to me
