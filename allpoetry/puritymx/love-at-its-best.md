title: "Love at its best"
writer: "PurityMx"

love is somthing measured 
in quality not quantity
you take for granted
what this is i feel
for you

you take advantage of what ill 
do for you
ask me a question like..
"how much do you love me?"
make a statement like
"if you love me youll....."

cut the shit
lets get down to it
why do you do this to me?
make me feel bad for my feelings, wants and needs.
make me cry every night
make me die every night
wondering why...
...why do you do this to me?
