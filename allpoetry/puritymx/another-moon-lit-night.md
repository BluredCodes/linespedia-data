take this knife and slice all i have left,
all i ever believed, 
all i ever had blessed,
cut into my dreams , 
dont pay any attention, 
just cut deep enough to release my tention, 
lick off my wound, 
taste the blood, 
probe the skin, 
watch me writhe, 
from the pleasure of this knife within,
cut away my dreams ,
cut away my life , 
once again under the stars, 
and a knife in my hand,
what could be better than this life with a knifeUH YEAH THIS IS SORT OF"LIFE WITH A KNIFE" REVISITED SO UH YEAH ENJOY....
