touch me
wanna die!?
touch me
dont fucking cry!
you little pussy bitch
go hang w/ your fucking clique
pryed at my mind
looking for answers in due time
well with friendship comes answers 
and your anything but a friend
a heratless prick
you are a pig when it
comes to my emotions
you mocked who i am
made fun of what i believed
told me i have no reason to
have the thought/feelings
that i do 
well fuck you
you heartless prick
go dwell in a pile of shit
find some of your own kind
