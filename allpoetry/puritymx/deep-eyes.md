title: "DeeP-EyEs"

glistening eyes quick to paralyze
my love
my thoughts, your thoughts
are one,
we live,
we strive,
to breathe,
to die,
together,
we live,
never apart,
the ache of this love,
felt deep in my heart,
never give up,
dont let this die,
for if you do,
.....we will too
