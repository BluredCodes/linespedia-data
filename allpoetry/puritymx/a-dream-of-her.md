title: "A Dream of Her"
writer: "PurityMx"

blood dry
dried in the sun
burning sun of your eyes
shining bright
paralyzing fright

never know
just let it flow
dont be afraid
ill take your hand
and walk you through
the desolate land
i call my head
thriving
child-like 
we'll play
