i cant believe what this is im feeling now
i cant believe what this is imbreathing now
dont ever step away
you are my only way

please just look at me 
i need to feel your eyes 
piercing through me
please dont stop 
it feels good

breaking my skin
let the blood rush
its the only way to do this
dont stop now

let it all run
let it run red
let it run down my cheek
let it run from pores
let it consume me
dont stop now

it cant end here
let your beautiful eyes pierce me deep 
dont stop til' youve found what u need
just dont give in to my desire to bleedhmmm...yummy>=}
