title: "Love"
writer: "Ravenas"

I'm afraid to say I love you
I fear those precious words will
wither and crumble in the air.
So I hold them inside
they bolster my resolve
My love for you is a salvation
Cherished
I hope you know it.
That you understand
"I wouldn't trade you for heaven
and a lifetime of italian meals."
means I love you
just like
"we can do anything together" does
and the heartfelt "I miss you's" too.
Sweet silence between us is like music
that enraptures my soul
yet when you speak
an angelic chorus could not move me more
Every tender kiss
makes my heart flutter like 10,000
butterflies wings
each one as beautiful as our love.
Life couldn't be any better.
