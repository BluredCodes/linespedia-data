title: "Sweetie (not really a poem)"

I can't tell you
to hate her
or regret the 
1 1/2 years of love.

You did nothing wrong, sweetie.
All you did was love her
and gave her all you had.

Don't beat yourself up
because she let you down.
It's her loss.
I know it is yours, too.
But it is her mistake
and her sin.
It is her shame,
but your burden to bear.

It's gonna take time to heal.
You know the cliche.
You've been here before.
And you know 
"it's gonna hurt like hell,"
sweetie.

But if she 
is blind to how
amazing and strong
you are.
And if the fight 
within you
does not make her
burn brighter...

It is better to let go
than to hold on.it has a loose poem form to it.  but it is mainly just my thoughts and support to a friend of mine who went through a break up with his chicky-poo... i won't speak ill of her... *cough*  yeah, that is hard to do... 

anyway, he is where i drew my strength from when i was on the brink, and where i am getting some now, so i wish to return the favor.  love ya, sweetie! 

and this goes for anyone who has been thrown away by a dumbass lover...  thanks for reading.  ;}
