---
title: "don't you feel it?"
writer: "donnamarie"
slug: "dont-you-feel-it"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57860-dont-you-feel-it--by-donnamarie"
---

it is all coming to an end.

New York, this is our Rome.
the Empire has blasphemed.

the basket floats upon the river's edge.
all our chemicals and propaganda and 'politics'
shall rest as Ophelia had.

the Crown of Thorns has been placed upon our tyrant's head.
we have torched the Olive Branch.
and the Dove has broken its wing.

it is all coming to an end.

don't you feel it?don't you feel it?
