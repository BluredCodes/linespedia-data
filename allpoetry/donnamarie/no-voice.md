---
title: "no voice"
writer: "donnamarie"

no voice
no sound
just thoughts
and visions

no voice
no choice
all alone
and arbitrary

no voice
no philter
lost answers
to be found

no voice
no conclusion
confusion
delusion

no voice
no noise
distraction
slows resolution

no voice
no limit
endless time
infinite.

no voice
no sound
no choice
no philter
no answers
no noise
no limit

no voice.this is a year old now, but i was just flipping through my shit and thought that this was appropriate for the current context of my life...
