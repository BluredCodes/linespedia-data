title: "manipulation of my heart"
writer: "donnamarie"

your words sting my ears,
  yet freeze my heart.

doublespeak and backtalk.

you stifle my emotions  
  cause you limit my voice
    and outlaw my words.

manipulate my heart
  and toy with my hands.
soon, they'll be cut off,
  along with my trembling voice.

i can only bite back
  my tears for so long.
then, the wrath shall flow:

drown you til your manipulative hands
  will be grasping to stay afloat,
    your head submerges.

your breath stolen,
  as you've stolen mine before.
no sweetness to choke your screams, though.

breathless screams,
  those are the worst.
but that is the only way 
  my heart can scream.
as you choke it with 
  your manipulative hands.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

twist and mold it.
but i thought it was mine.
but you can't understand "No!"

not only from my heart,
  you manipulate
  and bend
  the other parts of me 
    to your will...

i'll slice your hand instead,
  and gouge your heart.
i'll not bend yours
  to my will.
you'll not twist mine
  to hold for a ransom
    i cannot pay.

see the river flow.
