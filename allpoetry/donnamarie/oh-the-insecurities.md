it's hard to believe you're "NORMAL" when all you ever see are 5'6" chicks with long, bony legs and six-packs (beer and abs) and C's (grades and tits).  it's hard to beleive you're "NORMAL" when you don't own a tube top or a mini-mini-mini-up-your-crack-so-everyone-can-see-your-disgusting-neon-pink-butt-floss skirt or heels, or when your collection of books is larger than your collection of make-up.  it's hard to believe you're "NORMAL" when you'd rather run in the park than run for a beer and a fuck.  it's hard to believe you're "NORMAL" when your mommy doesn't own a credit card and your family has never owned a new car.  it's hard to believe you're "NORMAL" when you're 18 and financially on your own and living from pay check to miniscule, irrelevent pay check.  it's hard to believe you're "NORMAL" when you know you're not and you know you are not ever going to change to be "NORMAL" because you don't have the urge to fit yourself into a little white box and be strereotyped, categorized, or understood by small-minded pieces of shit that are only concerned with sex, drugs, booze, more sex, and MONEY!!!

as far as i am concerned, college and high school are synonymous.FUCK ALL INSECURITIES!!!  TO HELL WITH SOCIETY!!!  FUCK IT ALL TO HELL!!!   OH YES!!!
