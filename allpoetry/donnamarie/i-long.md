i long 
for the sun
in the west.

i long 
for my walk
to the edge of the universe.

i long
for the night
and her anonymity.

i long 
for the moon
and her solitude.

i long
for the warm breeze
so i can 
disspiate.

i long 
for the 
palpable obscure...hm.  am i the only one who longs for the night and the witching hour to pass the endless hours?   

am i the only who needs to escape?

i can't put into words what this really means to me, so i will stop, now.

thanks.
