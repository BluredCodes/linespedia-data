---
title: "right now"
writer: "donnamarie"

i need this
right now.
i need the contact
of the pen on paper.
i don't get contact
from anywhere else...

here i can make it 
my own.
merely concentration
to make it right,
to make it pretty.

the paper listens 
and the pen
feeds on my emotion.

the pen always 
knows i'm right.
and it convinces
the paper
to take my side.

they don't need reason.
they don't need a rhyme.
they like to be used.
they don't mind being used up.

my voice 
always sounds nice
on the paper.
on paper,
my notes resound.

the pen is my mike.
the paper, my stage.
you are my audience.
make me feel as one...
with this paper and pen.
with my own voice.
with my own mind.
with my own soul.brain fart!  ;)
