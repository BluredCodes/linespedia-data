DO YOU SEE HER?...

She walks with me
in the darkness.

ssshhh...
DO YOU HEAR HER?

She sings to me
in the night.

>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

I yearn to cry
in the sanctuary of her arms.

Her verdure delights me
by the light of the moon.

She'll show me
the avolation
provided by the forest.

She'll lead me 
through the thicket maze.

My naked feet
shall prance in her streams
and bask in the moonbeams.

The soft caresss
of the humming breeze
shall lull me to sleep.

I'll use the mist
as my blanket, 
the moss as my bed.

The frolicking
of her fingertips
shall sprinkle dreams
of amenity
and serenity.

run away with me...

who will run away with me to this place?

who will run away with me?my personification of mother nature and the comfort that she always brought to me.

who will run away with me?
