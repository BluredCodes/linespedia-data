I have a job that 

I am not happy with

I work my butt off 

and what do I get



Trying hard to be relaxed 

and not pitch a fit

No matter how difficult 

the person is I just met



The issue of money 

bothers me a lot

Knowing I should be happy 

with what I got



The more time that goes by, 

the more I want

But I stay stuck 

on the things I don't



The other part of me 

is overjoyed each and everyday

To have a loving family 

moves me in many ways



I can't wait to get home 

not to take a nap

Instead to hold my baby girl 

snuggled in my lap



My lovely wife is with me 

through thick and thin

In this game of life 

I always seem to win



Now you see there is 

different sides of me

It depends on the moment 

as to which you will see
