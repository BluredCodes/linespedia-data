title: "Starry Night"

What dreams may come
What prayers may send
What demons succomb
What powers bend
To the stars above 
To those twinkling lights
They tell of love 
And guide in the nights 
Many shapes deciphered 
Many stories untold
They existed with your diapers 
And remain as you grow old
At dawn on earth, but one shines bright
To make a day a day,
But in the night
There the rest will stay 
As I gaze up in the sky 
And think of days that passed
And all the time I let slip by
How memories do last
I wish upon the first star
And yet I don't know why
For although I know you are far
We can see the same big sky
