---
title: "Down in the dumps"
writer: "Wimbo"
slug: "down-in-the-dumps"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60664-Down-in-the-dumps-by-Wimbo"

every once in awhile I am tired
tired of life, and everything else
I just wanna end it right then
but I always have someone right by me
to guide me in the right direction
and not let me fail
but lately my friends are leaving
forgetting our friendship one by one
ive lost a few of my closest friends
and its really hitting me hard
i miss smiling with them
i miss sharing fun time with them
see my friends mean the world to me
and when they just leave me 
and forget everything good we had
it really breaks my heart
so now i feel alone
lost in the world with no place to go
so now i want to end it all
all the pain and suffering 
and all the emotional stress 
that is ova my head.
