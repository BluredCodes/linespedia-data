---
title: "i love..."
writer: "Wimbo"
slug: "i-love"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60946-i-love...-by-Wimbo"
---

i love the way you look at me
i love the way you wink at me
i love all the gifts that u give me
i love all sweet things you say to me
i love the way you hold me
i love the way you say you love me
i just love everything in this relationship between u and me
