as i lie here thinking about you
i think of all the good times we had
and how we laughed and smilied together
but now you've left me, alone
woth no one to laugh with
and no one to smile with
i feel so empty inside without you
everyday i wish you would come back
but i know that you've found another
antoher to share your unconditional love with
for one day, just one day
i wish i could have you back
back in my arms
back in my heart
but i guess my wish wont come true
