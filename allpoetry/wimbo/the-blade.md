---
title: "the blade"
writer: "Wimbo"
slug: "the-blade"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

i take the blade
its 2 inches from the skin
all my thoughts are rushing through my head
should i do this or not
will anyone miss me
do i really wanna end it all 
do i wanna leave this world of hate
so i decide its time to go
the blade goes thru one layer
then the next
its down to the bone now
and bloods going everywhere
everything is spinning around
my blood is covering the floor
i can feel my life leaving my veins
as it seeps onto the ground
my breathe is shortening
and my head is pounding
then all of sudden my heart stops
and everythings becomes motionless
i now have no more fears
no more worries
and no more pain
