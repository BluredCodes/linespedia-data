---
title: "what i did"
writer: "Wimbo"
slug: "what-i-did"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60956-what-i-did-by-Wimbo"
---

i held your hand
i held you close
i gave u a shoulder to cry on
i gave u my love
i showed you how much u mean to me
i showed you the inner me
i trusted you with all my heart
i trusted u to never tell
i let u guide me
i let u be with me
then one day you left me
i cryed for days
i cryed for for weeks
i wanted to have u back
i wanted to be with u agian
i needed your love
i needed your hugs
i am empty now
i am all alone
