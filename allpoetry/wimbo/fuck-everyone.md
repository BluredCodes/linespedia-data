title: "fuck everyone"
writer: "Wimbo"

fuck u
fuck me
fuck everyone
that i see
i hate life
and everything in it
i just wanna go away
i cut my wrists 
but it didnt work
i took a bunch of pills
but it only made me ill
why is life so terrible
why is everyone so mean
i never did anything
anything bad at least
god i just wanna die
all i do at night is cry
ive lost my friends
my family
and their trust
all that i have left
is my poetry.
