the water has gentle ripples in it
they remind me of way out in the sea
its dark outside
so the moon is reflecting on this water
i see myself staring at me
i just cant comprehend why im here
all i see is a girl
with blue eyes
and wavy hair, staring at me
i dont understand her feelings
and neither does anyone else
all she seems to do is cause pain
even when she does nothing
no one listens to her
no one cares
you can see in her eyes 
that she is hurt
her eyes blazed with fury
all she wants is to be loved
but no one is there... TBC
