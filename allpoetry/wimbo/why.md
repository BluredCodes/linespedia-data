why did u have to leave me?
why did u have to go?
why did u decide to end it all, with one quick blow?
you had alot going for you
you had people caring about you
you seemed happy at school
you smiled like a fool
now your gone
now youve laid to rest
now you can forget your past
ill always miss you
ill always remember you
ill see you soon
the world is so empty
the days drag on foreva
just because your gone
just because i miss you
i love you and..
i always will!
