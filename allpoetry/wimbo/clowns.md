title: "clowns..."
writer: "Wimbo"

im scared of clowns
they are always happy
and always smiling
they make me wanna gag
and sometimes i even do
do they ever go away
one once tryed to hug me
i cryed 
and started to scream for my life
no joke, those things are beasts
i hope that they all die
and rot in hell
die mother fuckers..die!
