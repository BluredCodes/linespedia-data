god damnit
why must people be such fucking assholes?
i never did a damn thing to any of them
never did i say a word to those cock sucking mother fuckers
they taunt me
call me names
and make fun of me until i cryed
why the fuck did they do that
it really hurt
hurt me to the point where i wanted to end it all
thanks to those fuckers ive been to therapy land
they told me that i am angery
angery with this fucking world
and how everyone in it are asses
i guess im to the point of no return
return to complete sanity
thanks to all the fuckers who made me like this
its all your fault
now i hope all of you fuck off and die
