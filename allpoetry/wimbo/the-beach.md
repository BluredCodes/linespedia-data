the beach
the beach is the place i like to be
the beach is my place to get away 
get away from all my pain
and forget about thats going on
going on in my life and in my head
the sea shells remind me of hope
becuase you can always find them
the water reminds me of love
because loved ones come and go
kind of like the tide
the sun reminds me of life
how it changes every hour
once in awhile you find a sharks tooth
and that reminds me of true friends
because they are hard to find
