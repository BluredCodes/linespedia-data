title: "my blanket"
writer: "Wimbo"

i once had a blanket
i got it when i was born
i used to bring it with me everywhere
even to the store
i used to cry when i had to leave it behind
but i would always remember it would be there
when i got back from where i was
one day i brought it with me
to the mall
and i left it in the dressing room
when i went back to get it
it was gone
i cryed and threw a tantrum
and later i saw another lil girl
and she had my blanket
but for some reason i didnt tell my mom
i guess i knew that it was time to get rid of it
so now im blanketless
and i miss it a lot
