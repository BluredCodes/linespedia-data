i feel like we are growing apart
did i do something wrong?
or was it just ment to be?
the phone calls stop,
we start hanging out less and less
i think that i am losing you 
all because i was afraid
afraid of what would happen 
of what people would think
of me and you, 
for all those stupid reasons
is this why i am losing you
