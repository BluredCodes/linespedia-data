I Love You are the three most hurtful words
you say them but do you mean it
do you know what you are doing to my emotions 
do you know that everytime that you say them 
i belive you more and more
sometimes i wish that i knew if you ment
if what you say to me is true 
i think that it is, 
but can i ever be sure?
will i ever know if you know what you are saying?
