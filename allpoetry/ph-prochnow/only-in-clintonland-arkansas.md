I love you baby girl, 
 you are so cute and precious....
but that Chihuaha puppy
    sure has YOU beat?

I figured at first you were worth
  one puppy,
then I decided you were worth
five puppies,
     because you are special.

Sure, my baby girl,
   I will keep you!!!
...'cause they are puttin' me in jail
and I couldn't get the deal done.

That's right love bunny....
  you ain't worth one puppy to your ma,
you worth five puppies
....I love you,
and
when mommy gets out of the slammy
     I'll see what I can trade you off for.


2 April 2002 - - Arkansas woman busted
trying to trade her baby daughter for a Chihauha
