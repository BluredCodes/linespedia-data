turn off the night 
turn on the day 

turn off this day 
turn on the night 

turn off today 
for this is my night
 
turnoff my night 
for soon a new day

this is my night 
I spend like the day 

send on the day 
and give me my night 

there is no light 
to see in my day 

I see no light 
when you see the day 

turn on the light 
turn on my night
