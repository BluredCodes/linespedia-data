---
title: "a lie"
writer: "despondent heart"

I live my life a lie
So whats the point 
If you can't tell your friends
Too ashamed to tell the truth
Is our love so embarrassing?
that you deny all  you feel?
So what am I to do?
Just go along with it?
Accept that you renounce what we have?

I told you they hated me
Hated the way I tried to hold onto you
Hated the way I wanted to know 
Hated the way you felt
Hated the way I loved you
But you lied…

I try to start over 
You won't let me 
I try to give them another chance
What part of them don't you want me to see?
The part of them you love?
The part of her you love?
The part you love more then me?

Maybe you were meant to be together
Maybe we were
Together forever you said
Is our love a lie?
