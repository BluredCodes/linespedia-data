---
title: "our love"
writer: "despondent heart"
slug: "our-love"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61060-our-love-by-despondent-heart"
---

i look at you 
and all i see
is beauty

oh how your eyes
burn into my soul
like fire

you love me so purely
what can i do
but return the same

when we are together
you upon me 
we make beautiful love

your touch
your sent
set me on fire

in ecstacy
we lay there
together as one

we share
but this brief moment
in time

the world 
stands still
for but a second

our love transends time
