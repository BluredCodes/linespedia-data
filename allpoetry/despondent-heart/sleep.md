writer: "despondent heart"

go to sleep
forever my darling
find that place
where the hurt stops
at night
when you dream
live in utopia
in ecstacy forever

long for the darkness
to suround you
to hold you tight
keep you safe
from the bitter cold world

go to sleep
forever my darling
you will be safe in the night
your death
in the nightthis is kinda written to myself when i was going through a bad time
