All the little children come to me with flowers
Smiling faces, litte teeth, not knowing what the futer hold
"I am here to cast a spell" I say
"My Momy says that you'r a bad man" a child says
So I take out my wand
I do a trick
I make one disapeer
The rest scream with terror and run for there lives
They cannot escape me, I am there father now
