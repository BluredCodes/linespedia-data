---
title: "My Humble Home"
writer: "Black rose"

Does my darkness hurt you'r eyes?
Welcome to my dungen of tourture
Enjoy you'r stay
Do you need more light?
Or can you handle my black heart?
Is it to cold for you'r skin?
This is the way I live
In the dark and In the cold
Welcom, please stay
I have some food in the freezer
Just wait a wile, it should be ready
Maybe you could join me
In my mind of insanity
Can you sense Satan
He's right in front of you
Close you'r eyes maybe he'll miss you
Just call my Lucifur
I'm sure we'll get along fine...
