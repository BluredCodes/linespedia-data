title: "Evolution"
writer: "Black rose"

The flower grew to be a village
The village grew to be a town
The town grew to be a city
The city grew to be a state
The state grew to be a country

The monkey grew to be a man
The man grew to be a gun
The gun grew to be a God
The God grew to be a man

Evolution is the path to destruction
Destruction is the path to extiction
Extiction is the path to nowere
Nowere is the path to my home
