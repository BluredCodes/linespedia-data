A trust I've never known.
A warm smile for once.
The light in the dark.
To feel good for once.
To know its right.
Emotions I've never known.

My known emotions,
Are hate,
Regret,
Pain,
Silence,
Death.

My emotions are always wronge,
Why fallow them?
I never do!
I cry,
And run away,
But this time I can't.
I feel good fallowing emotions.

For once they feel right.
For once they work.
For a small period of time,
I feel safe.
Don't screw it up.
I say to myself,
But I know i eventually will.
I always do!
