Life,
Is a pointless,
Turnstyle,
You go around and around,
Never to stop,
You may want to get off,
And the lucky few do,
But most stay on,
And go through horrors,
And terrors,
Some say there are good parts,
Where one can be happy,
But I'm convinced,
That happyness,
Lies beyond this world.
