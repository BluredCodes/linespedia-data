writer: "StarGrrl"

Silent place,
Where roses grow,
Where I can go,
To have silence.

Silent place,
Where I dream my dreams,
Where i can scream,
And let out my anger.

Silent place,
Where it is warm,
Where time slips away,
And life is endless.

Silent place,
Where times are good,
Where people are happy,
When life is evil.

Silent place,
Where roses grow,
Where I can go,
To get away.
