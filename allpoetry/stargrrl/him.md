---
title: "Him"
writer: "StarGrrl"
slug: "him"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

He knows me,
A way I don't know myself.
I feel good around him,
Not just a barrier for the blind.
I trust him,
And can tell him what I havn't told others.
I can't stop thinking about him.
Because he's truthful.
I know he won't lie,
I know he won't use me,
I know he won't hurt me,
I know he won't control me,
I know I'm safe with him,
Because he cares,
He knows,
He's there.
I can finally,
Let the wall come down.I love him sooooooo much!!!!!!!!!
