title: "Trust"
writer: "StarGrrl"
slug: "trust"

I learned not to trust at an early age,
To keep to myslef,
To hide away.

I learned not to love at an early age,
To have a cold heart,
And not get played.

I learned to survive at an early age,
To make no mistakes,
And hide the ones I do.

I learned to have no opinion,
No one can be hurt,
No one can get mad.

Yet,
Now I've trusted someone,
Become weak.

Now,
I've had an opinion,
Risked feeling.

Now,
I've survived,
Shoudn't let my guard down.

Now,
I love,
And I don't know what to do.
