As time passes by,
And life is lived,
Lost and loved,
He stands by my side,
And I by his.

As time passes by,
And our life grows,
He stands by my side,
And I by his.

As time passes by,
I will know him better,
And he will know me.

As time passes by,
And I grow old,
I will love him.
