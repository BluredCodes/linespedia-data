It all used to make,
Sense,
I always used to know what to,
Do,
Life was almost,
Good.
Nothing,
Was confusing.
It helped,
Everything helped.
Now,
Everythings,
Dead,
I don't know what to,
Do,
Nothing helps,
It only gets worse.
I don't know what to do,
I never know what to,
Do.
