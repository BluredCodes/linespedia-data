Baby
so beautiful
loving and unassuming
blue eyes fringed with black
golden locks frame a cherubic face
those warm, rosy cheeks smile lovingly
so where are your wings
oh bringer of joy
for you must
be an
AngelThis is the first time I felt I could write something about my 17 mth old daughter and do her justice...
She really is beautiful, you know...
