Awoken from slumber
With red puffy eyes
An argument last night
Which ended in cries.

Still hurt in the morn
You left on your wheels
You knew I was hurting
See how it feels.

The whispers of love
Have almost grown cold
The embers of passion
Are days and weeks old.

You're away for days
I miss you so much
What I need now 
Is your soft gentle touch.

The tears well up
And now roll down my face
Come home to your family
Darling, this is your place.Not quite as bad as it sounds- he planned this weekend away previously. It just feels like I'm losing him, but he'll be home on Monday. Arguments always seem worse at nighttime...
