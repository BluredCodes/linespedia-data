Fearful of the darkness
Scared of the unknown
What's lurking in the shadows?
I don't want to be alone.

Paranoia of the night
Jumping at any noise
Oh holy sh*t, what was that?
Relief, it's just the boys.

My heart is racing faster
And nerves are wearing thin
When will this be over? 
When will sleep kick in?A nervous night on my own, husband away for the w/e...
