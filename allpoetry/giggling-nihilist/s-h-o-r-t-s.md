. . . .



s. h. o. r. t. s. 

picks at the wall
like clip-on earings

nervous shake of foot

in practical  shoes,
complacent leg cross
and folded arms,

thoughts collecting moss

as the pervading city lights
smother the stars




. . . .me and mein fren was sittin' 
yeh, me and' mein fren Aroura Eden.
