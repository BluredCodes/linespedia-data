\\\



i spooned "family time" into the bowl
an' sipped for a bit--

nobody lived in a pretty jean town
and signed an apt-of-David 
to become a clown
...there were glittering Cheries growing in the forest
& sprightly colored moths fanning
lazy lazy lady bugs
floating in the seasonal winds
between the trees
where there  were dragonflies afoot
sauntering about in the sultry summer sun



\\\--me and my lovely sister cherie and her husband DAVE were having "family time" last summer --and we sketched this here pome. ain't it purdy?
