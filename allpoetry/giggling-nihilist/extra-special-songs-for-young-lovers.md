Extra Special Songs for Young Lovers

call me irresponsible
 till the end of time,
something extra special,
 bluesette, 
you are never far away from me.

Al-Di-La
it was the girl from Ipanema 
& Do you want to know a secret?
--a real live girl is the friendliest thing,
like a satin doll. 



-yeh, she wants me.
