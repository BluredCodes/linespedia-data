life
ADVANCED CORRECTIVE COMPLEX order
We fade into the crowd;
nobody knows color
as
We are "lost in the grey of it"
HOME departments Beginning of all
Ordinary Timeless Tradition. 
From Generation to Generation
Passed On, DOMESTIC BLISS
but fertile minds think identity
No good-working-relationships 
Vision. Inspired. by the ideals 
People Wonder,
Children Feel
t o u c h e s   of   c ol o r
at a threshold escape
an insipid nightmare.
 




-for a class, the assignment was to sketch out a poem to express/incorporate our feelings about The Giver and Anthem. (two dystopic novels).
