.









" compressed sound:"

written on a piece of oiled plastic

  spinning fast, tuning in, tuning in, as hysterically [iR]reverent tune out

wire to the brain!  

fluctuate swell of  minstrelsy

   in strain of diode bliss

in refrain of chorus hiss

  

turn the dial and regulate 

presssqueeze  the buttonbutton

   here, there, the ninth...



machine eye beam gaze 

  of molded frame shatter

this is the substance

 metallic crack

  of addictive  

oscillation vehicle of dramatic-ly small proportions.

little bits of beetles' wings fluttering en the aire.

   v\ 

earie at the end of a long long spiraling strand of metal,

a smoldering connection

to artistic expression... 

quick!

copyright your individuality 





     & through these annals:



put it in a book,



                see you in the papers.



hot off the presses.



         and do yourself a favor:



remember the ethic, "leave no trace"

. . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 











.bumba! o, be critical. 

i wrote this pome many, many moon ago ... 

but lookie here, i just found this quote:



"Wax discs are frangible: a lost flake is an irretrievable snippet of

   sonic memory."



   -Alan Burdick, Now Hear This, Harper's Magazine (New York), Jul 2001.
