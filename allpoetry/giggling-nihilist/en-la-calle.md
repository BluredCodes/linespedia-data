-



en la calle
man, i meet you en tha street
como el... 
     like the rain that urgently screams
 a soft whine to wash us away.

our fight concedes one to the other.

bloodied. we stand. rain pouring, pouring down.
punches and throws, they just slide off. 
 we can't help but to laugh at this induced and rainy  frustration.
hysterically slipping all over the place. 

and falling over each other. 
en la calle,
the rain steams from the ground.
cooling and hazing our differences
enough for us to touch as we are hidden.
