-



be sure to fill out the application
by the morning of the 25th
for the position of the Reeperbahn
and then George said:

"In the late 50s in England it wasn't easy to get it."
h   a    n   g   o   v   e    r       ]
"The girls would all wear brassieres and corsets which seemed like reinforced steel."
   s     u     z    i    e 
"You could never actually get anywhere."
  b    a     l      l     r     o    o     m
"You'd always be breaking your hand trying to undo everything."
      d     a     n     c     i     n     g 
quite an eye-opener
  --it could be quite
b    l   u   e      s    p   o   o   n 
	hysterical
	    . . . .



-written for fun. lonely night. beatles. 
hee... hee...

love, the giggling nihilist
