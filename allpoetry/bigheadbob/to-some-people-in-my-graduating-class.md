Graduating? We are,
Some are going away.
Some are going to KCC,
In this town they stay.

Weather Your staying or going,
I hope your life is good.
Best wishes to you I give,
I give as I know I should.Just something im signing yearbooks with.(not all just some). The few that i will actually miss.
