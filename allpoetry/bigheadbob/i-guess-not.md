---
title: "I guess not"
writer: "Bigheadbob"

If I was to write you,
Would you write me back?
I guess not.

If I was to call you,
Would you call me back?
I guess not.

If I told you I liked you,
Would you say it back?
I guess not.

If I asked you out,
Would we do anything?
I guess not.

A couple of weeks ago,
This would have bothered me alot.
Infact it still does,
But I have come to realize,
That your just fucking with my head.

So
Will I ever write you agian?
I guess not.
Will I ever call you again?
I guess not.
Will I ever like you now?
I guess not.
Will I ever ask you out again?
I guess not.

I can not say I've lost,
How it is I feel.
But what I can say is that,
If you want anything to become of us.
Its your problem now.??????
