She said she wasnt in the mood
For me to fuck around
But what did I do 
I just kept pushing 
Joking around and the such
I've never taken a single thing seriously
In my entire life
Why cant I do it?
Alot of people say im a "jackass"
And now i kind of see it
I feel like a dick
And dont know why
Please say something
Cause sitting here
Alone
Without your words
Is wearing a hole
In my soul
I know I dont diserve for you
To say anything to me
But I hope you see
that I really am sorry.well that all came out nice i think
