---
title: "Me, Myself, and Them"
writer: "Bigheadbob"
slug: "me-myself-and-them"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58142-Me--Myself--and-Them-by-Bigheadbob"

People in my life,
Are never there for me.
It seems to me, if im on land,
That they are out to sea.

Never around when I need them,
But always there when they need me.
I say to them "This is going to stop",
And they say "we'll just see".

I do not really care,
Cause they are nto my friends.
I will never have any,
Until this world it ends.

I am an utter loner,
The epitome of the word.
My life is one big toilet,
And I am one big terd.

This is how I feel,
Most of all the time.
My life will only be peacful,
When the church bells chime.

They say that they would cry for me,
But I know that is not true.
For all of them one faithful day,
They will pay there dues.

Repulse me... yes they do,
For they act like they are a friend.
But little do they know,
There life's about to end.

Suicide is not the answer,
Myself I could not kill.
But all of them out there,
Yes... Oh Yes I will.
