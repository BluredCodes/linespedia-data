Driving along,
In my car,
Stareing at the sky.

Thinking of you,
Thinking of me,
I cant get you out of my mind.

Thats not a bad thing,
I assure you of that,
I love thinking about you

Your the best thing I have,
In my life right now,
Your the only one I trust.

I dont feel like this,
About anyone else,
Your the only one.

I know you could have,
Any guy you wanted,
So why do you waste your time with me.

Im not that handsom,
Im not that built,
What do you see that I dont.

I would love to see me,
From your point of view,
If even for a moment.

So I could know for sure,
If im special to someone,
Or if im just getting led on.
