The shinnning moon,
Upon my face.
Somewhere in Mississippi,
Upon your face.

Giving light,
To all your beauty.
Oh my gosh,
Your such a cutie.

In the stars,
I see your eyes,
Deep down in side, oh yes I "sigh".

In the stars,
I see your ears,
You can always tell me your fears.

In the stars,
I see your nose,
Priettier than, a bright red rose.

No picture of you,
Could do you right.
The only way to have you,
Is in my sight.

You will come back,
In a couple of days.
To rescue me,
From within my maze.

A maze of thoughts,
Underneath the moon.
Wishing to call us, us.
Sometime soon.Yeah I know its not my best and I really dont like it. But truthfully I really dont care.
