---
title: "You Bitch"
writer: "Bigheadbob"
slug: "you-bitch"
source: "AllPoetry"

Why?
Oh Why?
Oh Why?

Did you do this shit to me.
Said "I love you" yes I did,
Down upon one knee.

Boy?
Oh Boy?
Oh Boy?

Did you start to waver fast.
Thought you would love me? Yes I did,
Up until the last.

Man?
Oh Man?
Oh Man?

Was I ever wrong.
Fucked him that night? Yes you did,
Fucked him to our song.

Shit?
Oh Shit?
Oh Shit?

What am I doing with a gun?
Thought I'd go to his house,
And have a little fun.

Fuck?
Oh Fuck?
Oh Fuck?

Whats that puddle of red?
You were both asleep,
Now your both real dead.

Yes?
Oh Yes?
Oh Yes?

I feel better now.
Finally shut you up,
You stupid sadistic cow.this didnt happent just a thought i had today.
