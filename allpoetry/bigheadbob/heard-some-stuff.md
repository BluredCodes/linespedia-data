Away
Your off
Your on the road.

Dont talk
To me
In morris code.

Be 
As plain
As night and day.

So
That I
Understand what you say.

Dont talk to soft
Dont talk to slow
Dont talk to me
Like your a hoe.

I've 
Heard some stuff
What to know if its true.

Please 
Tell me now
So I'll know what to do.

To quit
Talking to you
Forever and ever.

Or was
It a rumor
True? They are never.

No matter what I've heard,
I'll always believe you.
Cause im hoping to me,
You will stay true.
