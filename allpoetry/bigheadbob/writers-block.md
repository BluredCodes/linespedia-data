Writers block sucks
I wanna write about you
But my mind draws a blank
Nothing comes out
All day
Just sitting and thinking
My mind is gone
Never to return?
Or so it seems
An emptyness like space
Vast and horizonless
Uterly alone in itself
Empty
I cant force it
Cause it wont work
Wont sound right
Just flat out
Wont work!
Uterly alone in itslef
Empty
Now its done?
Well maybe not!
More is coming
UMMMM....
No its done
Uterly alone in itself
