---
title: "A dream"
writer: "Bigheadbob"

Granuals of sand,
Beneath our toes.
Ocean salt water,
Spraying all around.
A full moon,
Upon you face.
A slight breeze,
Blowing over our bodies.
Walking,
Talking,
Together,
I wake up.
