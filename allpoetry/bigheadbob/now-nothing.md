WHAT THE FUCK!
I thought you were different,
I thought you understood,
But your not and you dont.
You say this,
But you do that.
You said you understood,
But if you did,
Why do you do this to me?
I know when I see you next,
You'll have an excuss,
And I'll accept it.
Just because...

F U C K  T H A T

Your stuck
Im good,
Stay the fuck away.
Im through with your bullshit,
Im through with your lies.
Dont talk to me.
Dont look at me.
Dont call me.
Dont write to me.
Cause you are now nothing to me.
