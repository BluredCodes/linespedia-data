Ms.Smith says "Kyle",
I lift my head.
Day dreaminig in class,
Thought I was in bed.

Back to calculus,
I have to go.
Fighting with math,
What a foe.

I'd rather sleep,
And think of you.
But i cant,
I've got stuf to do.just a little thing that happend yestarday.
