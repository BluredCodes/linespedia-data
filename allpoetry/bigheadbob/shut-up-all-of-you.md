title: "Shut up all of you"
writer: "Bigheadbob"

If you take the words,
I say to you invain.
Then one day, I'll make sure,
That you will feel my pain.

The pain of thinking,
Someone cares.
Deep in your heart,
Oh yes it tears.

When you find out,
There just fucking around.
Your fit of anger,
Lifts from the ground.

To fly away,
In a fit of rage.
Until another,
Gets you in a cage.

And makes you think,
She's a friend so true.
And makes you think,
She's there for you.

She cuts your memory,
From her breast.
Leaves you for another,
Just like the rest.

No one's different,
Your all the same.
SHUT THE FUCK UP,
Live with your pain.

Of seeing me indifferent,
To all of you out there.
Try and fuck with me,
I really dont care.

You will fail,
Cause i have learned.
Passion from me,
Will have to be earned
