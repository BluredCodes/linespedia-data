Milk shakes, Blizards,
A banana split.
Went to DQ,
Here I sit.

In my car,
With my shake.
Just got through,
My wake and bake.

Without a DQ,
My mouth would be dry.
Cause smoking that skunk pot,
Got me high.

Every single day,
For the past five years.
Washed it down,
With a couple of beers.

Beer and DQ,
Do not mix.
Like all those porno's,
Chicks with dicks.

This is an ode,
From an ex-pothead.
With out a DQ,
I'd probably be dead.Yeah well. its the first humor poem i've ever writen. i was just sitting in phisics to day and i started writing and this is what came out.
