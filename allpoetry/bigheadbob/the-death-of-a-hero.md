The shit you've been through,
To get yourself through life.
Seems very insignificant,
Now that your put to the knife.

At your misfortune,
Now I laugh.
Cause now your drowning,
In your bath.

Was all of it worth it,
Yes or No?
Oh yeah...
You cant talk under water,
I didnt know.

You shouldent have fucked,
With me or my mind.
For you see your body,
They'll never find.

Kill you I did,
I wont deny.
Im proud of my insanity,
I can not lie.kinda stupid. but my mind isnt working right now.  not my best i know. but please tell me what you think.
