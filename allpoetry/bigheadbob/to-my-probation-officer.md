Six months of hell?
Yes and No.
Hated the drops,
Never could go.

You were cool,
We talked alot.
Treated me like an adult,
And not a tot.

You helped me through,
Some stuff in my life.
Nothing to serious,
Just a little strife.

Im glad I got caught,
Your like a new friend.
We'll probably never talk as before,
But this isnt the end.

I hope everything in your life,
Stays well and good.
Best wishes to you,
I give as I should.Just a little something i wrote to my probation officer. I got off of probation today.  and im goign to miss having somewhere to go once a month.  she was really cool.
