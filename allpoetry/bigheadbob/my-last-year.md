Time is passing slowly,
These last few weeks of school.
Watching the clock all the time,
Boy I feel like a fool.

Seninor Year sucks,
Yes, it really blows.
Stuck around all these preps,
And lots of nasty hoes.

Not a single girl in my school,
That I'd wanna date.
People say "learn to love",
I prefer to hate.Im not sure where this came from.  just being bord in class i guess.
