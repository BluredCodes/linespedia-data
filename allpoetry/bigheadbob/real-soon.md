Im all alone,
My families gone,
Theres no one there for me.

Left to fend,
For myself,
Without a penny to my name.

I've always said,
That I'm a loner,
But it hurts to see you gone.

Wanting to be,
With you right now,
But I know I cant.

Life is overrated,
And so am I,
Leave me alone you bitch.

I'll join them soon,
Just wait you'll see,
No one cares about me.

No one wants,
My companie,
So I'll see you all real soon.

No one wants,
To see my eyes,
So i'll see you all real soon.

No one wants,
To hear my voice,
So i'll see you all real soon.

Every one wants,
To see me dead,

SO...
I'LL...
SEE...
YOU...
ALL...
REAL...
SOON.Well this has nothing to do with me. i was watching a movie and the guy said "im your only family now" and that just gave me the idea. so i wrote about it.
