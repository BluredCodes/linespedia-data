title: "Sitting....?  Thinkking....?"

Sitting...?
Thinking...?

Are you so blind,
That you can not see.
Exactly what it is,
You do to me.

From the very first moment,
I saw your face.
Faster, and faster,
My heart did race.

There are times,
You make me mad.
But more times than not,
You make me glad.

Glad to be alive,
To sit and talk to you.
But no sooner have you left,
My feelings become blue.

You only wanna be friends,
That much your not sure.
You might not be, but I am,
My feelings will endure.

Not forever,
But a while.
Show me the path,
No matter the mile.

I will walk it,
To be with you.
As long as you promise,
You will stay true.

True to yourself,
And true to me.
And maybe one day,
I'll be on one knee.

Sitting...?
Thinking...?
