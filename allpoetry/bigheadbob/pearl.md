This is for a girl,
Said to her I'll stay.
Promised her a pearl,
On the other day.

I'll make this short and sweet,
To let her know I care.
On her I would never cheat,
In her eye's I'd stare.

To let her know I wuv her,
oops. I've never said that before.
But every time I talk to her,
