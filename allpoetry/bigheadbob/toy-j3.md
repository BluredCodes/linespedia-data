From the moment I met you,
I had to follow my heart.
The good and bad,
Ups and downs,
It wasnt up to me.

I just sat back, enjoyed the ride,
My feelings I did not hide.
Put them out,
In the open,
For all around to see.

Even when you do not call,
I am sitting here still waiting.
For the moment,
You'll realize,
I'm only here for you.

Most of all the time,
You make me glad to be here.
To sit and talk,
To write you notes,
Just glad to be alive.

These feelings that I have,
So very deep inside.
Are controling,
Me right now,
So if I make you mad im sorry.

Me and you are going to prom,
That much I am sure of.
Are we friends,
Or are we more,
Thats all im confused about.the last lines are not actually true now cause i know were just friends. for now at least or so i hope.
