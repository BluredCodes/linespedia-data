Im lying here in bed
Trying to think of a way
To say im sorry and be sweet
But I cant
Its not coming out
You've got my mind all boggled up.

We need to have a talk
A serious one at that
Cause theres special things we share
And they all mean the world to me
But theres other things you do
That make me wanna snap.

I write to you about how I feel
But you never write back in depth
I get a generic answer
Or no answer at all...

Why?

Are you afraid of how you might feel?
If you let me in.
Or are you just fucking with my head?

Enjoying yourself at my expence,
Leding me on until we get off of work,
When you go to your real "Twinkle toes" house.
And have fun with him
Cause I know were not doing anything.

I've never fell for a girl,
Quite like I've fallen for you
To me your perfect
In nearly every way.

I dont know how a guy like me,
Could get a girl like you.
I just dont know
What to do,
Where to go,
What to say,
How to act,
I 
Just 
Dont 
Know.the more writings that i do without rhyming the more i like it.
