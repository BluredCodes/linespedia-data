People throw around,
 The words of which I speak.
 In our hearts is where,
 These words we all should keep.
 
 I've never said the words,
 To any one in my life.
 I've never heard them either,
 From anyone in my life.
 
 Longing to have the one,
 Who will hear those words from me.
 Ask her to marry, yes I will,
 Down upon one knee.
 
 Searching for that one,
 I dont know who she is.
 I dont know her name, or her age,
 I dont know where she lives.
 
 Maybe I already know her,
 Maybe we'll meet real soon.
 Go for a long romantic walk,
 Undernieth the moon.
 
 When I finally say,
 These words to the girl I love.
 No one will ever separate us,
 No mater how hard they shove.to the girl out there i will someday love. I dont know who you are yet but hopefully soon.
