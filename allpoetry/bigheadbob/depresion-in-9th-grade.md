Poped some pill's
drank some beer's
Waiting to die
If it ever comes
Some homework on the floor
That needs to be done
If I wake up tomorrow.?, This brings back memories.  just looking through an old notebook in my closet and i found a couple of poems. not that good kinda old. like 4 years ago.
