The beatles once said
"When I find myself in times of trouble,
Mother mary comes to me,
Speaking words of wisdom,
Let it be."

Well what about me,
I dont have a mother mary,
No one to talk to,
No ont to trust,
No one to confide in,
Where am I to go.

Lost am I,
Here in this world.
No family thats there for me,
No friends that care for me,
No girl to talk to.

I've been fucked over for the last time,
No longer will I trust my secerets to anyone.
Mine is mine,
Not for you,
Do not ask for you will not recieve.

Leave me alone,
Stay the fuck away,
Back the fuck up,
Watch what your doing,
Cause I am no longer me.I know its not that good but i wrote in my present mood and right now i have quit carign about everthing.  so I really dont care. so if you dont like it fuck off.
