Mrs.Geel I always called her,
For that was her school name.
Kyle she called me,
For reasons all the same.

Taught me computer science,
How to program and the such.
How I miss her. Yes I do,
Miss her very much.

Taught me Algebra II,
Hated that class alot.
Everything she said,
Oh yes I forgot.

Her teaching style impeccable,
Always there for me.
Made everything clear,
Clear enough to see.

If she told me how to do it,
I picked it up real fast.
Wish she could have stayed,
Until this year (my last).

Loved her with all my heart,
As much as a student could.
Gave her a retierment present,
I did as I knew I should.

Havent seen her this year,
I wish I could just once.
In her class I was never,
Never once the dunce.

Best teacher I've ever had,
Never one the same.
From deep within my heart,
These feelings they do rain.

Every teacher I will every have,
Will be compaired to you.
Taught me right from wrong,
Taught me what to do.this was to my favorite teacher. i had her last year and the year before. she stil works in the school but not a teacher. i hope i see her before i graduate.
