title: "? But I'd like to know."
writer: "Bigheadbob"

I don't know why I feel,
Like this about you.

I don't know why I can't,
Get you out of my mind.

I don't know why I think,
Your the most beautiful girl I know.

I don't know why I say,
The thing's I say to you.

I don't know if you feel,
The same way about me.

I don't know if you only,
Wanna be my friend.

I don't know if you are,
Just messing with my mind.

I guess that all I can say,
Is that I really don't know.?
