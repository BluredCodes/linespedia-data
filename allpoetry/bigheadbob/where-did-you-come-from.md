title: "Where did you come from?"
writer: "Bigheadbob"

I feel like I know you,
And I dont know why.
But we barly know each other,
Just recently said "hi".

Why is this thought,
Predominit in my head.
My feelings are finally coming out,
They just gotta be said.

From two different worlds,
Or so it seems.
This kinda stuff,
Only happens in dreams.

Well maybe not,
Cause its happining right now.
If I ever met you,
In your grace I'd bow.

To make you feel special,
To let you know I care.
To run my calused fingers,
Through you soft brown hair.

This I would do,
Without bating an eye.
This I would do,
And thats not a lie.

I feel like I love you,
Deep in my heart.
If we were together,
I'd never depart.

By your side,
Is where I'd stay.
Always faithful,
Until my dieing day.

I feel like I know you,
And I dont know why.
But we barly know each other,
Just recently said "hi".To a girl. You know who you are.
