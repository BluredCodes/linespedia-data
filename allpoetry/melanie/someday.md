someday will never get here
it's always been that way
it may have been so near 
or stayed so far away

no matter how close you've gotten
no matter how far you've come
someday will always be someday
though it never occurs to some

in fact, i've wished for someday
however, it hasn't turned up yet
and even if i wish
it wont show up i bet
