i moved here from far away
i knew then life itself was over

you came to me and proved i was wrong
this was just the beginning

i was so happy i found a friend
we are alike in so many ways

we stayed together all the time
wether it was just us or the guys too

i was head over heels for the guy 
i broke it off with the other one

you showed me i needed to move on
so that is exactly what i did

one day we swore we wouldnt fuck up 
and yeah, oops we did, you got taken away 

we did wrong and you had to go
i thought why does shit like this happen to me

what i didnt realize at the time 
you were hurting more than me

i try hard to understand
as hard as i try i just cant

you moved away for 30 days
now i am without a friend again

i am here waiting 
and hoping you come home soon

i just cant wait for that day to come
i will visit and i will call

just promise to never let go of our friendship
cuz you know what i sure as hell wont!
