TOMORROW is the FUTURE
The FUTURE is TECHNOLOGY
TECHNOLOGY is KNOWLEDGE 
KNOWLEDGE is WISDOM
WISDOM is POWER
POWER is CONTROL
CONTROL is a CHOICE
A CHOICE is a JOURNEY
A JOURNEY is the FUTURE
The FUTURE is TOMORROW
