---
title: "should our love go on??"
writer: "melanie"
slug: "should-our-love-go-on"
source: "AllPoetry"

i have come so close to you
i wish now that it wasn't true
from the start we hit it off so well
everything always seemed to be swell
then that day came for me to go
to me you only let love show
you told me you loved me
that i have been able to see
now we are so far apart
we have seen it from the start
now i have met someone new
there isn't anyway i can tell you
no one can tell me who the right guy is
i am trying so hard to figure out this
i have asked a thousand times why?
it is so uneasy to figure but still i try
should i risk our love for something so
i really don't know which way to go
my head is telling me to say bye
but still my heart is telling me to try
should i give the other guy my time
or will it even be worth a dime
as i sit here in confusion
i guess i will figure out my decisionif anyone has been in my place please give me pointers on what to do.... i am opens for all ideas.
