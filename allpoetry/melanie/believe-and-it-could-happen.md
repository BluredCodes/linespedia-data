---
title: "believe and it could happen"
writer: "melanie"
slug: "believe-and-it-could-happen"
source: "AllPoetry"

when you don't have confidence in yourself,
you will not achieve
when people say you are and you think you're not,
you are not
when you think you are going to fail,
you will
when the day comes for you to reach your goal and you belive you can,
you will even if in reality you didn't
when other people put you down and you brush it off and you continue to believe in yourself even after what they said,
then you are a strong believer
if you do not belive the truth,
then you are blind
if you believe the lies,
then you are foolish
seek and you shall find!
believe and you shall conquer!
