What is death?
Where will I go? (if anywhere)
Am I even human?
Or are we all aliens?...
having swiped "earth" from the dinasaurs!
WE ARE SO CHEAP!
Oh but, back to the subject...
Will I just fade away??
straight out of existence? (Just like Elenore Rigby??)
Will I be forgotten?
melt away to nothingness?
or just simply...cease to exist?
Will my children cry?
WILL I HAVE CHILDREN?
Oh, probably not..
EEEK
Something wonderful to think about
in these wee hours of the morn.
will I be buried?
cremated?
Neither?
BOTH?!
I think the worst death is the one where you are still alive
but nobody knows it and you are forgotten...
