OOOOOOOOO!
The pain in my back grows with every creak of my bed
My eyes droop more with every tick of my electric alarm clock...
HA!!! Electric alarm clock...how foolish of me!
Electric alarm clocks don't tick, they BUZZZZ BIG DIFFERENCE!!!!

Aie me, perhaps I'll read another book
Ah yes, problem is finding a new one...
Oye, how 'bout music??
Not with out waking the OGRES!!

Coffee?? No need but with milk and sugar please.
PLEASE!!! WHERE'D YOU GO?? FINE! DISAPEAR!!
I don't need you... dum de dum...
Ambian!!! MY LOVE!!! Sleeping-pill GOD!!!

So I swallow two...
Yet sleep still hesitates to enter my night..or my day
SO WHAT SHALL I DO??? Lie here on the couch?
Nah, it smells bad...so what about the floor??
It's no better. No, but what is?! STANDING???

WHY DON'T YOU JUST SHUT UP??
Finally the yawning. Yawn Yawn...TEAR!!!
My eyes now drip with anxiety
OH MY GOSH, WON'T I JUST DIE??
