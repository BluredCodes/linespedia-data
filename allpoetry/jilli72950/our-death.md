Ever heard the saying..."you'll catch your death"? 
Well your death is my death portrayed by the same character
administering different ends. So we won't catch our own but
then you wouldn't have to catch him anyway.. he'll come when
he's called.. ;oÞthis is actually supposed to be creepy, but I dunno...
