The night before Easter
I was not all aware
Sitting on my keester
not looking for any ole hare

I painted my egges
I filled my easter basket with grass
Sprawled, strecthing my legs
anything could pass

I glanced at my dog
my basket was missing
I though:"what a hog"
but my parents were kissing..

It just goes to show that during Easter
To catch a funny bunny doesn't include ones keester ;oÞtee hee
