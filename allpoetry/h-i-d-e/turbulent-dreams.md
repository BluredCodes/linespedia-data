title: "Turbulent Dreams"
writer: "H i d e"

Feeling the pull, of the steady breeze
It calls me to you, far away

As I close my eyes
I can still see you there
Staning in the wind
blowing through your hair, waiting forever...

It was all a dream, in the back of my head
That I return to, so far away

As I close my eyes
I can still see you there
Standing in the wind
not caring where, because we're together...

Together againO_o
