title: "Burnt Photograph"

So far; I can't reach, the photos on the table
It seems so long ago, memories are unstable
It isn't what you said, that day on the phone
Not mad anymore, monotonus busy tone
We've grown so far apart, I can't see you anymore
In the crowded streets, trampled on the floor
Through the moving bodies, I stare up at the sky
Looking for an escape, someone tell me why
I let go of you, in the crowded street that day
Thought I set you free, but you just ran awayMore of my nonsense
