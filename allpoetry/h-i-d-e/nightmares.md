You are my pain
The needle that stabs my eye
Thick black blood
Running down my face
Sleepless days
Walking nightmares
of you

You haunt my memories
The ghost of my past
Days long forgotten
Remembered only
In my nightmares
of youNot my best write...but about all I'm in the mood for. ~_~
