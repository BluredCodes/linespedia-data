title: "meeting again, falling away"

ah, they meet again

walking down the street
it's late

she walks up to him
he stares in another direction

but she speaks
she says what she wants to say
she says what she needs to say

he finally looks at her

she falls into his cocaine eyes

he has nothing to say 
it's been years

she asks why

he falls away
once again, his needles call

he found a way out, but closed his way in
he thinks he fell from his world
but his world said he fell from it

a tear falls down her cheek

they part again
