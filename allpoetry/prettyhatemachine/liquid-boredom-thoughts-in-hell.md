A humble, placid quietness
Silent screams thrown into fire

In the mind, my thoughts are clear
The difference between time and space begins to tire

Liquid boredom is injected
Happiness comes in pill form

All hope is rejected
And they are all trapped in a controlled storm

Seeing the outside is literally looking through a window
And comfortableness is felt only through sleep

Consciousness plagues everyone, though
And everything we know cuts more than skin deep

We have everything together 
But we still are without
