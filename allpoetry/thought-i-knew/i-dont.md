Reflection of wat i used to be
Wat i merely represent to you
something i always wanted to be
But wishes no longer come true

I tried that night
So very hard
I tried my best
I played the cards

I looked up to the stars
I prayed
One more night
I wish you'd stayed

To hold you tight
To feel your touch
Something i always had
Now want so much

You never even said goodbye
Just left a note
I start to cry

I don't know how
I don't know why
I ask myself
Y did you die

Please no i care
Still love you dear
You will always be a part of me
i'll always be waiting here
Until the day i'm there with you
And i'll once again hold you near
