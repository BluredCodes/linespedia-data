To afraid to sleep
Can't even close my eyes
Afraid of what i might dream
Scary shadows dance across my mind

Haunting me with their evil smiles
I never really believed a smile could be evil until now
Till i saw this smile
This haunting face

Pinned down
By this dream demon
Can't even raise my arms
Dead weights at my side

No his face
Can picture it perfectly in my mind
Turn on the light
the darkness suffocates me

Try to think happy thoughts
Invision myself flying through the clouds
But the face won't stop haunting me
Y won't you let me be

Leave me alone
LET ME SLEEP!!!!
