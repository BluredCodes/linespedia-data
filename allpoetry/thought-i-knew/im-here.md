Life is a journey
To short to end
and to long to worry about every little catastrophe

When the sun has set
and the stars begein to twinkle in the night sky
Sit and stare at them in amazement
As you yern to understand their purpose
It's like all things in life we yern to no our purpose

If your cheeks are wet
from floods of tears
I will be sitting next to you
Ready to listen

Quietly waiting 
Even though u may not always see me
I will always have my hand outstretched to you
waiting for when you need it

Remember
Don't let anyone put you down
Those that make fun of you are memrly insecure about themselves
Always be happy with who you are
Because you are an individual
And a wonderful person

When ever you need someone to rely on
Or a shoulder to cry on
I'm here for you

Be happy :)
