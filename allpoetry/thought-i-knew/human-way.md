I wish for things
That can not exist
A single step
A solemn wish

A tear 
A sigh
A laugh
A cry

An unsaid word
A gentle hand
The soft sweet melody
Of a silent band

A night of sleep
A night without
There are so many times
I just wanna scream and shout

A tear
A sigh
A laugh
A cry
And all lifes other treasures and torments....
