Down on my knees
Praying so hard to something i don't even believe in
"Just watch over him
Dont' let him do it"

He is more than he appears
More than anyone assumes
He's a guardian
Sent to love
sent to care

He has yet to realise his full potential
And it looks as though he never will
So convinced he will amount to nothing
So convinced he is nothing

All he needs is someone to love him
He has so much to give
All he needs is someone to give it to
Someone to care!

"Please let him be ok"
He will be ok
Got to keep telling myself that
Got to continue to believe
Even if he won't!!
