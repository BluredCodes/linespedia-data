In a beautiful house
Along a lovely lane
Is a beautiful garden
Which never gets rain

Open the doors
And go inside
A beautiful girl
All happy with pride

No-one knows
Whats happened here
Y this girl
is suddenly riddled with fear

What changed so suddenly 
The look on her face
It's as if she's run
And just lost the race

Fear clutches her breats
As she falls to the ground
No-where to turn
And she can't turnaround

She's falling she's falling
Down a bottemless pit
Nowhere to run or hide
And nowhere to sit

She screams as she wakes
How many mistakes
Has she made.....
