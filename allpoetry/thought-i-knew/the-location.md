Feel the blade as it draws across my skin
Blood rushes to the surface and tears begin to dwell in my eyes
Done it so many times before
Still don't know y

The scars
Hidden only by the location
Wrists slit
And lifelless eyes

Who would of thought it would come to this
Curled up tightly in a ball
Bleeding and broken
Drop the knife to the ground
as tears rush down my cheeks
I am alone......
