Every time i never cried
Every night i never slept
Every thought that comes to mind
Every memory i've kept

All the times i never told you
All the secrets that i keep
All the things i want to tell you
all the nights i want to sleep

Open up my mind
Open up my heart
Tell me every memory
From every time we've been apart

Help me to discover
See what can't been 
Take me to the places
In which i've never been

Every little treasure
Every whispered word
Every time i never listened
Every sentence i never heard

Help me see the truth
Understand the lies
Every time i have a dought
Let me look into your eyes

I know you'll always watch over me
And no i still love you dear
becuase even though your not now beside me
I no you will always be near
