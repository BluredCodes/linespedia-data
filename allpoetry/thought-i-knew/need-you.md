i look over
to see u standing there
i turn back
you are gone

where u merely a figment of my worn imagination
i had u once
y did i let go?!
somthings can be oh so perfect
then turn out so terribly wrong

all i wanted was for this to last
for me to once again feel u'r hands softly embracing my body
i long for only one more chance
i need u here with me

please return your warm smile 
i need your tender kiss
return to me your hands
so many things i miss

i want u close
i want u now.
i don't just want u
i need u
