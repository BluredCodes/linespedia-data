Everday i roam around
Nothing else to do
Every morning when i wake
My first sweet thoughts are you

You've always been my courage
my strength and weakness to
For every step along the way
I'm glad it's been with you

You've opened up my eyes to sunshine
You don't have to prove a thing
Everything wonderful in my life
It is a gift u bring

I'd hope for all eternity 
For every little step
For every mile i walk alone
It is my great regret

But as i look beyond myself
to what really lies within
I want to tell you everything
but Hardly know where to begin

I love and i cherish you
forever and a day
and no matter what ever happens
It will always be that way

You are my inspiration
Every moment a dream come true
And only because every moment 
I seem to spend with you :)
