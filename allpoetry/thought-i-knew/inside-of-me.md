Someone once asked me
To draw a picture of whats going on inside
I answered them with soundless words
as a Tear drew in my eye

I closed my eyes once
I dared to dream
I see the face that haunts me
It's my own....

The face of my past
with brutal scars 
And savage words
I dare not draw wat is going on inside of me!!hey
i was just wondering how i was doing so fare?!
thanx
