Wake up
To look around in a place i don't want to be
Roll over
To see things no-one should have to see

Get up
Run away
Try to leave the place you are
Only to realise you never left the place you were

Cover your head
Tuck the blanket tight around your fear
Close your eyes
And pray for strength

Wonder away from from everything
An endless quest for nothing
Never to reach the end of forever
Never to be set free!
