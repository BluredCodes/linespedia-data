I am me
The one nobody dares to see
I thought i saw u start to cry
I looked again....was all a lie
I tried to hide wat can't be seen
Opened my eyes i dared to dream
Betray yourself and be the fool
Love the gave play not by the rules
look around the corner, wat tomorrow brings
Do not worry so much, of troublesom things
Follow the path...don't go astray
Wait your turn...you'll see a better day!
I'll b worth it!
