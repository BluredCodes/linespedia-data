Its silence is overwhelming...
         the night
enveloped by darkness
the mammals sleep
the insects prey upon their peers
  SUDDENSUDDENSUDDENLY
the horizon melts into yellow
the clouds, hiding in the night, are revealed
the aztec rises from its shroud
the sun, the colors, the feeling
~s~t~u~n~n~i~n~g~ 
the warmth hits my face
           ...ssshhhiiivvveeerrrsss...
the heat radiates, melts the cold from my skin
small hurricanes on my flesh
I close my eye...the black becomes red, then orange...
I smile, my cheeks wrinkle
as the sunrise fills me with glee
I open my eyes to the light and am stunned
it fills my head, lighting every dark corner
memories f   l   o   o   d to the surface
...chipped teeth...knee scrapes....bicycles...
....action figures....brothers and sisters...
.....
......parents....
I spread my arms to the warmth above
and the light capcapcaptures me
it fills me, surrounds me, pulls me towards the aztec
the light is infinite...and I smile....
I did not realize...
