A mountain by the sea is where you will find me
labouring under the sun until I cease to be
I lift one stone and struggle to set it into place
until the sweat pours in gallons down face
I drop the stone and fall back and rest, for finally
my labour of years has finished and I can now behold
I stare up high and marvel at what has come from this mold
ten thousand stones, four hundred trees, the work is was so much
I raise my hand to the ediface, and shiver at its cold touch
but inside is an inner warmth, a glow that I have made
for this is the goddess' castle, it contains all I have gave
I fused the stones with magic, and It never lets in cold
I cast a spell of safetty, it's future is fortold
that all ye who shall enter will be happy in its walls
and none shall pass who desire the castle walls to fall
and inside is a garden where the roses grow all year
and a stream and a pond where the water is so clear
and the birds and deer and baby ducks flock back and forth inside
for they live in this castle too, and need no place to hide
for this is the goddess' castle, and only friends may come
and I forged this place with the grandest magic, a magic know as love.
