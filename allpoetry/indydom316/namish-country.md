It's good to know
when I'm feeling down
when I'm on the edge
when I'm going to drown
when my life is pain
and my pain is life
you'll be there 
through all my strife
you will be there
to reassure
me that for my problems
there is no cure
I should take some pills 
like forty or so
then jump off a bridge
dressed like a goddamned navajo
while screaming out loud
"Seamonkeys don't exist"
I'll know I'll always be
on your support list.



For Nam, the Bastard
