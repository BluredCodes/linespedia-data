I can't stand it all
the air is so still
it's hard to breathe today
and all I feel 
is my skin peel
and all I want is to say

Is someone there, listening?
can someone please turn down the ^H^E^A^T^
can someone please hear me 
I wish that I could escape the heat

I walk my life
on a tightrope
of unkept forgotten words
and all I see is 
that they're after me
a tornado of black angry birds

Is someone there, listening?
can someone please turn down the ^H^E^A^T^
can someone please leave me be
I wish that I could escape the heat

well they can fly
but so can I
I can escape 
if I only try
but they press on
and still they chase 
I wish that they
would forget my face

my life it ends
but I must mend
for the sins committed by men
but still I flee
too much for me
(pause)
IS IT EVER GOING TO END?!

Is someone there, listening?
can someone please turn down the ^H^E^A^T^
damnit can't you all see that
I'm suffocating, this is the end of me
just let me be
I cant take it all, I cant take the heat
this is the end...
