Realm of fantasy
Finally entered...finally.....conquered?
Travel the small town
live the small life
do what you may do
be what you may be
«mage»
«summoner»
«soldier»
«guardian»
suddenly, the picture expands
the small town is just a speck
the world, the map, is huge
you sail on Clouds
through the heart of Squalls
you meet others being
«mages»
«summoners»
«soldiers»
«guardians»
you fly, together
separate lives joined finally
into one quest for the world
to fight the never ending sin
and bring peace....at any cost
but you're just a dream
and when you save the world
and it awakes...
the dream will end...
