They're so annnoying, why are they there?
They serve no purppose, they're not pretty, like hair
hair takes a lot of work, but there are benefits
but cuticles serve no purpose, they're as important as zits
all they do is tear all the time
and you bite and pick and feverishly try
to pull off that extra bit of painful skin
but no matter how hard you try they always win
then they start to bleed, and you tear at them more
then you give up but they just adore
to remind you all day that they are still there
then they bleed and get infected and theres puss everywhere
then they chop off you finger, and the biggest jest
is that you still have eighteen cuticles left!
