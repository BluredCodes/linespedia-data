He's coming for me
with his limerick rifle
my mouse won't save me

I sign off in fear
I know that I can't stop him
he'll find me somehow

I'll get a letter
"but my address is secret!"
"Dear Indy, from Monk"

NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!he's coming......*looks left, looks right*
