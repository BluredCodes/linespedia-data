title: "life"
writer: "howdieho"

life, a seemingly meaningless existence
what is it for, does anyone know
it seems all there is to life is birth living and death
but is there more to this thing
more to birth than being born
more to life than living
more to death than dieing
it seems as though no one is to know these answers
it seems that this is just a pit stop to possibly greater things
will anyone ever know?
