Young Love

Look into my eyes
my friend 
Tell me what you see
Do you see the one 
Who longs to cry?
"You were meant for me"
let me take your 
hand my friend
I'll follow you anywhere
Time with you is magical
Can't you see I care
Listento my words 
my friend 
"Please don't ever let me go,
I love you more and more each day."
Someday I will be 
brave my friend 
We'llstand face to face 
Then I'llsing the words aloud 
And melt in your embrace
