love you
Everytime Iam near you
I feal wonderful
When we are apart,it's like I
was Dead.

I want to be with you,
hold you, 
talk to you,
kiss you.

Time is presious
I want to spend 
days and nights with you.

Can we be together,
or not?
How can I tell? 
help me, 
show me, 
tell me,
how to love you.
I want to know,
Zane my beloved 
I love you so,
But if I lost you ?
I would never know.
That's my greatest fear ever.
