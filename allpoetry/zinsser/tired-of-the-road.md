Tired Of The Road


I'm stuck in a time 
Where the roads are too busy
Where everythings just an
Overpass, exit mess

I've lost my map 
Forgot my destination
All the cars are starting to pass

My gauge has been on E
For far too long
But I never quite
Run out of gas

I'm tired of going in
Endless circles
I feel like 
I'm going to crash
