A  Perfect Blend

There will come a time
when you will see
 a perfect blend of us 

This will happen sure as day
whether were together 
or apart

the warm sand beneath your feet
looking at the sun set
while the stars no longer sleep

not a word will be needed 
not a sound will be made

just the perfect blend of 
me and you
for all the world to see
                 By
Connie McDonald
