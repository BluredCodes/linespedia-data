Flower



Flower on my window sill
your beauty astounds me
I wish I could be your center
so that your beauty would surround me

Or the minerals in your soil
to promote your growth with out toil
saving moisture for your petals to sip
Sun high in the sky, ladle you can dip

Or the roots that hold you firm to the ground
So earths weather cant toss you around
But free you to sing your songs
For you know right where you belong

Or the stem that holds your head up high
beauty to shine for all the sky
Fearless and knowing your held up strong 
with love from your stem you can depend on

Springs sun would be nice, not to hot or to cold
encouraging your beauty for all to behold
All thease things I wish I could be
For their the very things you are to me
