When I Grow Up


No one ever said,
"When I grow up I want to be"
Full of dispair & desperation
Unless I have some of these

Then comes humiliation
From the depths we must sink
Degradation from the thoughts
Weve learned to think

When weve lost all humanism
Running only on base instinct
Then were stuck in defeatism
All is gone, do more 
so we can't think

I know this all sounds crazy
And it's much worse, then
this write seems
For when we were all children
We too were full of dreams

No I don't think any child said
"When I grow up I'll be a hype"
Or a crack head on the corner
Selling my body for the pipe
