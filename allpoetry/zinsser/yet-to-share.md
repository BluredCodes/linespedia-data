Yet to Share

Please dont let this
be good-bye
We have so much yet to share

All the stary nights to watch
and funny old peoples clothes to wear

Far away sandy shores calling
our name
with huts in the tropics for
our body games

Coffee houses of all differant types
to sit and ponder
what others write

but most of all I would love to share
with out worry or a care
Our minds souls and our hearts

What a shame it would be 
if we were to part     

    
    By
Connie McDonald
