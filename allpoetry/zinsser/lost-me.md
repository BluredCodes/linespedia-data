Lost Me


I made to many compromises
And in the process I lost,
You lost too

For where there once was
Spirit, Richness, Substance
Is now a shell, an empty cavity

I honestly don't place blame
I gave up, in fear of loosing
You

In my fear, the only one
Who got hurt and lost
Was Me

Now that my eyes can see
I've forgotten what
 Being me means




Connie McDonald
