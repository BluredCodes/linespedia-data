All It Could Be


I don't know how
to make you feel
the realness of me
and who I am 
what I want
Whats important to me

I dont know how 
to make you feel
secure in me
the me of us

I dont know how
because its got to come 
from you
and will if
only you want
it to

I don't know how
and wish I could
beacuse until then
The all of US
the me, the you
will only be
what would have, if it 
only could have
