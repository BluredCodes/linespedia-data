Roses
  
Roses are beautiful
Sleek and sauve
In all their colors and types
Oh what they can solve

Representing differant emotions
Depending on how their dressed
Red long stemmed or
Yellow with baby's breath

There petals alone
Strewn around the room
Can change a regular day 
Into a romantic afternoon

They stand tall regardless 
Of there surroundings, with no pardon
Roses are, the Queen of the garden
