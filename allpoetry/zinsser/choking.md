Choking


New spriing growth
Ivey up the tree
So beautiful
For those who
Pass by and see

Each new spert
Of vines clinging growth
It twists around
Intentions
To slowly choke

Seasons pass
Less limbs tend to bloom
Less beauty viewed
Unnoticed
The pending doom
