Dreamy Valentines Day

    I wake up and I'm kinda sad
because nothing special has happened
good or bad
 On this day suposed to be full of love
he left with out even so much as a nudge

As I wake up more from this little nap
I discover a note placed beside me in one of his caps
"when you wake up sleepy head,
 Get your self up out of bed."
 "Get cleaned up and meet
 me here..... Yes your clothes are perfect
 have no fear"
"I hope your nervous as hell
I plan to scare you.. Thats all I'll tell"

I jump up and make a mad dash
for the coffee pot then the bath
pouring all kinds of scents
Oh yes I caught the hints

When I arive to my surprise
Its a hotel , now a problem has arised
I know not which room you see
The lady at the desk say's,
"No worries heres your keys."

I slowly open up the door
and the first thing I see is on the floor
another note for me to read
it says " Look around I hope you will be pleased"

Slowly I take it all in 
all over the ceiling red heart baloons
wild flowers and lit candles fill the room

On the table from here I can see
A perfect feast made for you and me
I walk into the bathroom from the shower you apear
handing you a towel you draw me near

From somewhere you hand me a shoping bag
Victoria Secrets "Thats where you were at."
Something for me to wear, what a nice treat
desert for your eyes while we eat

The scare me part has come into play
because I've never seen you act this way
 "Please don't stop surprising me" I say.
On this Dreamy Valentines Day
