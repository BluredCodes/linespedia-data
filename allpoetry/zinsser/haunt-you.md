Haunt You


  Do you know how much I want 
 For you to take me, then I'll haunt
 In your mind ever more
 Powerless you'll be to shut that door


 Melt us together as one
 Show you I will, what you have won
 Complete devotion  you'll bestow
 Or none of passions fervor will you hold

 My hands and fingers  cause a flame
 That will take all night to tame
 My lips move slowly but with force
 Traveling  your bodies course

  My legs tie knots captives crave
  You'll wish forever to be my slave
   My calves continually rub up and down
  What ever body part they have found

   If you've shown only I to be
   Your mistress, goddess and queen
   Loves waters I'll let you see, 
   Come on in and test my cream
  
   Of cource one dip is not enough
   Cum again and again befor nights end you must 
    Until your body has no more
    Blissful sleep, Do Not Disturb on the door
