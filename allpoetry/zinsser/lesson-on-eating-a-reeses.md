Lesson On Eating A Reese's


Temperature gauge
Body heat rising fast
Saring the night 
Making it last

Thirsting on the 
Salty sweat
Feasting will come
But not quite yet

I want sweets
Mixed with my salt
I can't help it
One of my faults

So out comes the Reese's
Pieces placed here and there
Body heat slowly melting
Trickles running places
No thought or care

Tongue catching the mixture
Lips and teeth
Pick up the pieces
Now thats a lesson
On how to eat a Reese's
