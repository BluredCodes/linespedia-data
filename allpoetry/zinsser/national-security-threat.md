Nationial Security Threat



Its not us or them
You know, that group
That nation, that country

National Security Threat
Is everywhere on every block
Offered at a small price
With a large cost

Scared souls, scared kids
Domestic violence, Divorce
theft, crimes range to  death,

What about HIV
And full blown AIDS
Hep. C and flesh disease
Each day new cases of these


Drugs distroying this nation
One person, Family, neighborhood,
At a time
Right here in the Great U.S.A.
People are dying every day


When are we going to get real
And really do something to deal
With what is killing our countries pride
I'm sick of everyone just wanting to hide it

Its clear to me that
The war on drugs is a joke
I know the cops are doing what they can
But its got to come from a higher command

If another country was causing 
Such death as drugs, 
At least the deaths we detect,
Every man women and child 
Would sign up to Honor, Serve and Protect

Yet, its happening right now
But shamefull somehow
So lets keep it all under the rug
And only fight this war with
Hugs not Drugs
