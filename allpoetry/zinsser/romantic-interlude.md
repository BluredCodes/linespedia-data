Romantic Interlude
(ghazel)


Romantic interlude with you
Who would have thought, or knew

Gamblers never expected us to stay together
No one thought we would make it through

Best twenty four hours of my life
And babe, you know weve spent a few

Dinner, Dancing, Frank Sanatra style
Laughing, teasing, ejoyed the old people too

You made me feel so beautiful
With those piercing eyes of blue

Should I talk of the Suite
I dont know, no one would believe it true

Perfection in classic luxury
That we ruined with sweat clean through

Hey! was there even a bed in there
Who cares I was wrapped up in you

Connie McDonald
