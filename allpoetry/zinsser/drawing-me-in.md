Drawing Me In


Warm night
Crickets singing
Their song
Memories flood
Campfire flames
Dancing across
Your skin
Drawing me in
Drawing me in

Morning dawns
Frogs croaking along
Tulle fog
Memories flood
Snuggled in
Our nature-laden haven
Tousled hair
Drawing me in
Drawing me in
