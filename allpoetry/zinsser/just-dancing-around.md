Just Dancing Arouund


Just danceing around
To the tunes of Jill Scott
My girls and I
Give care to not
What yesterday was
Or today might be
Maybe switching over to Creed
To get a down and dirty beat
There must be Puddle of Mud
Or for power in the soul
The three girls you all know 
The ones that talk of the survivors sceen
The tune with Pink to get the party started
I don't dare switch over to the ones I love the most
You know the ones for all the old folks LOL
For would kill the moment, for them I dance
To show them how to free their souls
And never ever to get to old
