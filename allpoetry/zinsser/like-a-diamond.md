Like A Diamond


Your heart is like a diamond
A unique cut, made that way
And like a piece of jewelry
Should not be tucked away

So daily please place it right upon your sleeve
To hide your heart would not be fair
And cause a few to grieve

When I happen to notice
A twinkle in your eye
I Know for sure, what it is
Your diamond deep inside

Connie McDonald
