---
title: "Fear"
writer: "revengeful sinner"
slug: "fear"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55232-Fear-by-revengeful-sinner"
---

it is everywhere 
it is nowhere
in the places we least suspect
we know its there
yet we cant quite understand
what its there to do
the real question is 
does it really exist?
or is it some freak coincidence
that everyone feels its presence
yet can never tell
if it effects us
until its too late
as some believe
its not something you can hear,
taste,
see or touch
its something that is on the border of human knowledge
we dont completely get why its there
yet no one can really live without it
then again, 
maybe it just cant live without us
