It feels so warm,
yet we cannot touch it.
It is so beautiful,
yet its too far to see.
It is peaceful,
yet we cant hear a thing.

In this twilight
all is quiet
except for the soft breeze
blowing whisps of clouds 
across the glowing sky.

In this twilight
there is peace.
In this twilight,
I hear you whisper
or was it the wind?
A gentle hand on my shoulder reassures me
that in this twilight
I am yours.
