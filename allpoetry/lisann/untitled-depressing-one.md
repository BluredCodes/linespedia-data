This is a little depressing....o.k. maybe a lot depressing.  Not at all like the others I've posted,

No need for concern really, I am just having a very rough week.


A fire is cold
Compared to the fever
That rises each day in my head.
Day reaches for night
And night becomes the day,
In that manner I pray,
God grant me the power
To reach for my glory
And the key mystically moves with my will....
IF death is life,
As life is death,
I yearn that coming with all my might,
Let me see the light
That burns within me
For I cannot withstand the pain.
