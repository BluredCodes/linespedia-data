title: "Love is worth the wait"
writer: "Lisann"

Sometimes......
Love is like an algebra problem
So confusing,
That its true meaning
May never be found.
And other times...
It is like a bright shining star,
Shining through the worst of times
When it should be dim.

Love is a special bond,
It will be tried,
BUT~
If the bond is strong enough
It will pull through anything.

Love is like a balloon
With lots of ups and downs,
The ups....
Are the greatest.
The downs.....
Will pass with time.

Love is something that must happen
On its own,
Without being forced or pushed,
For when it is truly found.......
LOVE IS WORTH THE WAIT.
