title: "Confusion"
writer: "Lisann"

Too hurt to cry
I'm holding back the tears.
I want to run and hide
From all the wasted years.
I feel the confusion in my life
And it's tearing me apart,
I've got to stand up and fight
And not let it break my heart.
It's hard to hold on to heaven
When hell is knocking on your door,
The fear inside has turned to ice
And I wonder what I'm fighting for.




No need for concern.....it's just one of those days but....I ALWAYS come out on top.
