title: "Torment"
writer: "Lisann"

Did you ever realize what you did to me?
Broke my heart,
Soul and spirit?
Those scars~
I will always carry.
From my cradle to my grave.
Embedded deeply in my being.
Are your cruel tongue lashings,
Blows,
Sneers,
Neglect and torment.
I shudder with remembrance
Of loud voices,
Slaps,
Rapes,
Hair pulled
Until my scalp cried out "No more."
Trying to block it out.
Laughing, and taunting me,
When I needed comfort.

Why couldn't you look at me
NOT THROUGH ME.
Speak softly TO me,
NOT DOWN AT ME.
See me as who I was
A HUMAN BEING?
Why?
What did I ever do but be born?
The choice was yours-------
NOT MINE.
If you didn't want me 
Than why did you bring me into your world of pain?

The screams I hear from time to time......
Wake me up....
They are from ME.
Will they ever cease?
Will they ever be quiet?
Now~
You are gone.
I should have peace.....
But~
The memories are still there.
From my cradle to my grave.
From a battered child
Grown up,
Crying out for a release of the pain.
