In The Eyes  OF  A  Child

I wrote a poem for you,
I wrote it on my wrist....
I used a razor for my pen.
I signed my name in blood.
But you didn't see it.
I wanted you to see it.
You didn't ask me questions.
Even when you saw my haunted eyes.
You didn't ask because 
You were afraid of the answers.
I wanted you to feel my pain.
My feelings~
Are like the long string of attached scarves
That magicians pull out of their mouths----
They seem to go on and on and on....
Each tear that is cried,
Attaches to another.
And then another.
And the sadness
Never seems to end.
Anger pushes against the damn
That holds it back
With such force
That it crushes me and my surroundings.
Through my writing~
I discovered I have an intellect
That is NOT stupid
As you had said.
But
One that is unstretched,
A heart that can feel more than pain....
And a body that once again 
Belongs to me.
