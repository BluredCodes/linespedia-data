title: "If I were........."

If I were ice......
I'd freeze your soul
As you did mine.

If I were a knife.....
I'd slash away all the pain
You made me go through.

If I were water.......
I'd drown the memories
You've etched in my mind.

If I were a lightning bolt.....
I'dI'd strike you to the ground
As you did to me.

If I were rain.....
I'd wash away your evilness
That you cursed me with.

If I were sadness.......
I'd make you cry
As you did to me.

If I were magic.......
I'd make you disappear
Like you did to my innocence.

If I were fire......
I'd burn you
And melt away the image
I remember so often.
