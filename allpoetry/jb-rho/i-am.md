---
title: "I AM"
writer: "JB rho"
slug: "i-am"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Angry at myself for being
foolish enough to want someone
I may not be able to have.

Irritated with the fact that 
I always long to see your smile, 
hear your voice, touch your skin.

Delirious with joy that you
even give me the time of day.

Intrigued by the wonder
of you, by the sheer thought
of you.

Frightened at the prospect of 
not, and never being, good
enough for you.

Scared that this thing that
is happening between us
will end before it can even 
begin.
