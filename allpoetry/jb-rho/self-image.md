writer: "JB rho"

Secrets endear themselves to soul
Unfathomable, shameful
Hell here I come
Urges take over will without conscience 
Using body to create fire
Lost is the good heart
Never to be found
Overwhelmed by sickness
Decisions always immoral
Always destructive
Life seemingly filled with joy
Only façade
Hiding insignificant world
Insignificant man
Stumbling through 
Ignorant, dense, 
Never to learn right or wrong
Repulsed by self image
Mirrors horrify as reflection 
Proves hideous features 
Encompass entire skin
Entire surface 
Even cloth cannot hide
This beast...
from himself.
