---
title: "Instead of Me"
writer: "JB rho"
slug: "instead-of-me"
source: "AllPoetry"

Your eyes embrace the heart 

Willingly accepting all faults

That come with soul

Disruption, determination, 

Desire overwhelms any wall

That gets in your way

Relentless is your touch

That somehow can overlook

The worst a man can be

Your love engulfs 

Then protects whatever 

You choose

Longingly I look upon you

From a distance

Wondering why you choose

Him 

instead of 

Me
