---
title: "summer dreams"
writer: "moonstar"

a careless breeze
tousels my hair
and awakens my skin
sunkissed
warm

blades of grass
tickle the small of my back
making it arch
making me smile

an ocean spray
leaves my lips tingling
salty
moist

waves crash
and my heart pounds in awe
of the gentle strength
of the graceful movement

a melody plays softly
far in the distance
pulling my eyes open-
what sort of hunger 
leaves my stomach
feeling so...
~hollow~

on this first day of spring
summer paid a visit
and brought me
a memory of you
