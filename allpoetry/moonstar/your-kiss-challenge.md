eyes meet
time stills
fever burns
fever chills




a brush of fingertips
ever gentle on my neck
the subtlety of this touch
pulls me deep into you
as my heart -skips-
-and skips-

can you feel me trembling?

i close my eyes
so as not to see
but only to feel
the beauty of you
in your eyes
tongue
breath
so soft

so soft

and
time
stands
still
to
witness
this

i surrender all
let me die now
let me never wake
let me know nothing
but to live in a world of this...
of bliss...
