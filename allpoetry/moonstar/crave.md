i'm waiting for you

aching
   needing
        wanting
almost begging for you
my cravings for you
the touch of you
the smell i close my eyes to
oh, the love of you
is breaking me
driving me insane

release me
   grace me
       fill me
           encompass me
satisfy this insatiable desire
for it is true
i'll never get enough of you

to taste you
   know you
consume you, my love
make me wait no longer~

i cannot forsake the drive
to take a piece to keep
