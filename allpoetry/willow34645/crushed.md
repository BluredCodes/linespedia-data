Tears form behind my eyes, 
But I do not cry;
I curse myself, I'm too bold,
Or am I too shy?

I used to get lost
In your eyes;
My laughter used to
Make me cry.

I guess I'm not enough
To fulfill your needs;
Guess it doesn't matter
That I've lost my creed.

And it hurts to want everything 
And nothing at the same time;
I want your heart,
But I can't give mine.

But I'm done 
With the hurt and the mush;
Cause as for me,
My heart has been crushed.
