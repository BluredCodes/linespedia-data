When I'm at school, I am myself,
At home I'm someone strange.
I play the good, sweet daughter,
And act like I feel no pain.

I put up this front that i'm alright,
That everything's okay.
But when it comes down to it,
What else am I suppost to say?

"No, I constantly feel like shit,"
"Life is kicking my ass."
"I can't keep up the pace,"
"I feel like I'm falling fast."

Faster and faster and faster I fall,
Not able to slow down.
Eventually I'll hit rock bottom,
