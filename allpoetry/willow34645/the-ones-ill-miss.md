title: "the ones i'll miss"

How do I start to say,
The things within my heart?
Before I leave you here,
Before we finally part.

You have been there for me,
Through the thick and the thin,
You few, select people,
You are my close to kin.

I said I wouldn't do it,
I said I couldn't follow through,
But when it comes down to it,
Just what was I to do?

Go through life in constant pain?
Live my life clouded with anguish? 
To go on deathly silent,
Wading through waters made brackish?

I'm done with life now,
I'm far past over this.
So try to remember me,
For you're the ones I'll miss.
