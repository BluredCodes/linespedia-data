you tell me one thing
and mean another
my train of thought as derailed
why am i trying to bother?

you say that you want me
but then again "nevermind"
you're trying to move on
and telling me i need to "find"

something out there for me
the one who awaits my love
you seem to care about me
at times you seem sent from above

but at others you hate me
detest my thoughts and dreams
i can never get through to you
that not everything is as it seems

i know i'm beneath you
you deserve so much more than i
but to see you suffer more because of me
i think i'd rather die

after reading this
i hope you see the light
i want you to know the truth
that i've been hiding out of sight

i do love you, sean and i want you back
and i just can't make you see
that you don't have to keep looking
cuz all you need is me
