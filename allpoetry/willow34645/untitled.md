Here i sit
upon my chair
staring at something
that just isn't there

i wonder why everything has to be
so fucking black and white
there's never a grey area
and it just doesn't seem right

that i have to give up
the things that i love
cuz i never back down
when push comes to shove

so what's the point of going on
when i'm running on empty?
i've got nothing left
why can't you see?

you keep asking, "are you okay?"
when it's obvious that i'm not
it's really sad to think
that writing poetry's all i've got
