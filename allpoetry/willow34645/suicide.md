I have drugs down my throat, 
And cuts on my wrists, 
The room is so dark, 
In the corner I sit. 


All my friends are gone, 
As I cry a black tear, 
I soon start to wonder, 
Why I am here. 

Is it for the torture? 
Or all the pain? 
I can't find the answer, 
To keep my sane. 


I'm lost forever, 
In my scary life, 
It can't get any worse, 
Since I play my life like 2 dice. 


I walk into my kitchen, 
I see the silver knife, 
The shiny blade stabs through me, 
And it ends my life.
