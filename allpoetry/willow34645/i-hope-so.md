my eyes change from blue to grey
and i have no idea what to say
except that i can't stay away
and i'll be here for you everyday

i'd rather at the stake burn
than have to relearn
this pain i've obviously earned
i must've taken a wrong turn

when i look around, everyone's so happy
they're always laughing
have i possibly gone daffy
that i want to be that sappy

i'm sorry that i've hurt you so
and i really don't want you to go
the one thing that you have to know
is that i love you, i hope it shows
