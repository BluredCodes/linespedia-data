Illusions

She thinks that she's the love of my life,
  and I let that fantasy grow
It helps me believe I'm more than I am
  but someday the truth she will know.

She knew from the start she wasn't the one~
  my true partner is waiting for me
My wife knows I need this additional "love"
  and knows that our future is WE.
    
This lover I use just uses me back.
  Her fantasy keeps her in smiles.
But someday her tears will flow like the rain
   And I'll be crossing the miles.

Don't cry for her when it's over and done,
  She loved every lie that I told.
These lovers don't last, they're a thing of the past
  But they sure make me feel oh, so bold.

I'm on to the next, another bright smile
  will surely be waiting for me
I'll begin once again to weave and to spin
   all the love words that will set her free.

And what of the past? The ones that I've left?
  They all have such good memories.
I've done my job well, truth is to tell
  I'm only here to please.
