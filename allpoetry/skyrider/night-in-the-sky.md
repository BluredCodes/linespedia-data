Night in the Sky

~Stars~ 
Shining in competition with the moon
~Moon~
Smiling, changing personalities daily
~Constellations~
History at work in the dark night
~History~
Seeing it all through eternity
~Eternity~
The road we all take
Stars
Moon
Constellations
