Night Thoughts

Fly through the night sky
  this plane doesn't need me
  coffee too strong
  passengers anticipating
       fun
       adventures
       romance
  passengers sleeping,
       trusting me to make dreams come true
911 still frightens me
  danger lurks everywhere
  can I keep them safe?
  will this flight be my last?
Too much darkness
Not enough sun
Too many stars
stars laugh at my plight
  they know they are forever
  they know
  I'm not.I need a vacation? In the sun?
