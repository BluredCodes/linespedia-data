---
title: "Release me!"

I am stronger than you!
I am smarter than you!
Why can't I break these bars of yours?
Why can't I escape from this prison?
How did you do it?
How did I get here?
Look at you as you laugh in my face
If I could break these bars I would show you who is superior
Wait, I see now, you exposed my weakness
My will is very weak
How dare you exploit that!
You use it to your advantage!
I blame myself for being in this cage
Release me!
Release me!
