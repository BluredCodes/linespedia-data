Turn around little man
Keep your eyes on me
Stop turning your back on me
One more time and I just might snap
You don't agree with things that i do
Or things that I say
Is it wrong for me to have an opinion?
Is it justified for me to speak up?
I'm tired of your constant insults little man
I'm tired of being ignored little man
Pay attention!
For now on I will not be ignored little man!
You will respect my opinion!
Now I turn my back on you little man
