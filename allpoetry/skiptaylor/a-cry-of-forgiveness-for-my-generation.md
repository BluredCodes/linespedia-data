One day your time will come
Will you forgive my generation for the screw ups we've done?
Will you accept your fate and stand up, be a leader?
Can you change all the mistakes that we've commited?
Will you be strong and courageous in the face of adversity?
Can I have faith in you that you will take care of us?
Will you lead my generation into a great new world?
Will you please tear down the world that we live in now?
Only to build it back up and make it yours
This is your world now
The new generation
Please forgive me and my peers for the harm we've done
My generation couldn't do it
Even though our parents pleaded with us the same as I'm doing now
I trust this new generation
I trust you will conquer a new world and make up for the terrible things
we've done
This is an apology to you, the youth.
