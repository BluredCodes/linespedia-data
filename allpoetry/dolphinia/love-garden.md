So it's all too much for you,
You gonna leave this happy home?
I see the distance in your eyes as you drink that coffee.
There is a far away look, the look of desired freedom.
Do what you gotta do, but know one thing,
Without you here, there will be no love garden in this house.

Without your tenderness the blossoms won't bud in the spring.
Without your sweetness the bees won't gather the honey.
It's a sealed fate, the love garden will fade away, never to radiate sunshine.

Let's makes this easy.
Put down that cup, darling.
Here is your bag (smile bravely.)
Here is your keys (dry the tear.)
Just don't slam the door and don't look back.
