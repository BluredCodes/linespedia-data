---
title: "Eye to Eye"
writer: "dolphinia"
slug: "eye-to-eye"
source: "AllPoetry"

Sitting here, thinking about you, baby.
Watching the stars go by.
Baby, feeling my world, seeing it eye to eye.

I can't face another hurt.
This heart would never mend.
Please take your course of action easy, lay me down easy.
'Cause I love you.

Sitting here, thinking about you, baby.
Watching the stars go by.
Baby, feeling my world, seeing it eye to eye.

My love is running smooth for you.
There is no rush too hurry to the end.
I want to experience all there is to feel.
Please take the path of easy resistance, love me real.
'Cause I love you.

Sitting here, thinking about you, baby.
Watching the stars go by.
Baby, feeling my world, seeing it eye to eye.
