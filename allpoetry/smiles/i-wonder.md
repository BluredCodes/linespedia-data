---
title: "I Wonder"
writer: "Smiles"
slug: "i-wonder"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

I wonder what goes through your mind,
When you find it alright to hurt me.
Would you like to be treated this way?

I wonder if you can feel my pain,
the one you give to me. 
Would you like to feel it too?

I wonder if you can imagine to live my life,
If you could survive it.
Would you still make fun of me?

I wonder if you realize that I hate you,
And understand why.
Would you hate you too?

I guess I’ll never know,
Why you are the way you are.
My only wish is that one day,
you will wonder these things as well.
And then you will realize,
I had feelings too.
