---
title: "The crazy one"
writer: "Smiles"
slug: "the-crazy-one"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61268-The-crazy-one-by-Smiles"
---

I am the crazy one,
I make everyone smile.
People come to me
when their day needs something wild.

I am the crazy one,
I make people laugh.
I think one of these days,
My brain is going to snap.

I am the crazy one,
People don’t know me.
The only thing they know,
Is what I let them see.

I am the crazy one,
I can’t stand it anymore,
None of you know,
What I have in store.

I am the crazy one,
I take the knife from the drawer,
I hate it to end this way,
But I can’t take it anymore.

I am the crazy one,
I drive it through my heart,
And before I die I find it funny,
That no one knew me from the start.

The next day, it’s all around.
And there is no more fun.
And everyone will say,
She sure was a crazy one.
