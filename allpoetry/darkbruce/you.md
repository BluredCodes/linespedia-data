You are the dagger
Concealed beneath the cloak.
You are the queasiness inside
Making me want to choke.
You say you are honest with me
What a fruitless joke.

I gave you everything
You took it all in stride,
You never really listened,
Never knew how I felt inside,
Now I am changed forever
And through you a piece of me has died.

Behind my back you crept
Stealthily and without effort
I never suspected anything
I loved you so much it hurt
Blinded by my emotions
I was not hard for you to avert.

Whispers in the dark
Unable to be translated by ear
In soft voices you spoke
Even when I was near
Just how long has this been going on?
The answer, I’m not sure I want to hear.

I loved you Amy
This will always be true
But no more will I be abused
While you search for someone new
The 3 years we shared meant the world to me
But obviously it was worthless to you.I wrote this poem in conjunction with my other poem betrayal. They are about the same person.
