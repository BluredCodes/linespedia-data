---
title: "Alone"
writer: "DarkBruce"
slug: "alone"

Staring blankly
At the empty nothingness,
Wondering if this is my destiny
     To be heartbroken,
Alone.

  Thinking Avidly
Over the joys of the past,
Pondering how it got here
     Experiencing this intense sadness,
Alone.

  Imprisoned Invisibly
By these unresolved feelings,
love and tenderness taken for granted
     left me to rot,
Alone.

  Tortured Terribly
As endless possibilities,
Unable to grasp, slip through my fingers
     Doomed to live
Alone.

  Continuing Ceaselessly
Despite my dire plight,
Finding convoluted reasons to persevere,
     Learning to survive

Alone.
