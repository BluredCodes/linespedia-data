title: "Winter's Touch"

Cherry trees sparkle
Glistening from the new snow
Pink blossoms frozen 
Before they had a chance to bloom.

Winter and spring
In an unlikely dance
Creating a strange
And very disturbing weather.

The chill of winter
Reminds me of the bareness of my soul
Once I led a vibrant existence,
But now like the blossoms 
My heart and body,
Obliterated by the cold.

Our love so passionate
Seemed perfect, pristine,
But now only an icy fror,
Frozen in time

Like the desiccated blossoms and buds
My heart withered
You abandoned me for another
Left me behind for eternity, in my icy prison
