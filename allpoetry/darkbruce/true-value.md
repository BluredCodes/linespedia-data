Insignia:
Red, White, Blue
The “American Dream.”
Slavery Veiled
Beneath
A vibrant Façade.

Isle 50:
Washers
Nuts, Bolts
Fasteners
To fetter
Nonconformists.
Those who strayed,
Refuted the
Red and white
Striped cell
And dared mingle
With the few elite
Stars.

Too often are decisions
Clouded over
By social pollution,
A cyanide to the soul.
Will to live siphoned
By the masked succubus.
We must learn to survive
Rise above the oppressors
Live happy, liberated.
Discard the Labels
Placed upon you
we cannot apologize
For living.
Let the Ants slave away 
For the media queen.
Step beyond
Rectilinear structure.
And create
With our own hands.for those who aren't familiar with "True Value" it is a Hardware Store.
