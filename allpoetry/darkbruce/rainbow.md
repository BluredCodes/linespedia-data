Like a Storm
Life has relentlessly drenched me
With hardships, loss,
And persistent misery.

The ominous clouds
Never receded from the black horizon
My future always seemed Bleak, 
Hopeless and brazen.

A precious few times
Has the sun penetrated through this wall,
I had given up hope
And learned to survive beneath this pall.

Destined to be alone
Never did I dream the storm would subside
But today suddenly the clouds lifted
And for the first time I felt alive.

The warmth of the suns new rays
Imbued me with a new feeling of tenderness
As they created a beautiful vibrant rainbow before me
Which guided me to true happiness.
