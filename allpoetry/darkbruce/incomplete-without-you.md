---
title: "Incomplete without You"
writer: "DarkBruce"
slug: "incomplete-without-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

This inner desire
Dwelling deep inside,
Revives
Once dead veins
Now flowing full of Life

All I want
Is to be with you
Carefree,
Crazy,
Happy,
And loved

Our paths crossed
By serendipitous fortune,
So lucky am I
To have found you
When I needed you most,
In the darkest time
Of my life.

You complete me
Without you
I am nothing,
But a lifeless form
longing for
My other half.
