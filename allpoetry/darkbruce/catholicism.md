Suffocating
In a rancid pool of
Moldy bread,
Death
Becomes God.
Blood Oozes
From beneath
Red Robes.
Intestines Coil
Around oysters.

Monkeys squeeze juice
From clergy.
Nuns dance naked,
Encircling
The Pungent stench
Of burning hair,
Flesh.

Love preached
With knives held high,
Ever ready to murder
For God
Who commands evil.
Bald Halos
Demand Inquisition,
And slaughter sheep
Filled with 
Poison grapes,
Scraping bowels
In search of 
Wealth.All me in my Sleep Deprived Glory
