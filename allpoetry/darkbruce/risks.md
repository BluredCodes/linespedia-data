---
title: "Risks"
writer: "DarkBruce"
slug: "risks"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Through the corner of my eye I see you staring,
But I dare not return even the shortest glance
For fear that I would once again fall under your spell,
Be reminded of the love we shared,
Wish we were again once more.

I dare not look--I must not,
My lamenting needs to end
I need to move on,
Leave you in the past,
As you deserted me,
So long ago.

No, I dare not look into
Those infinitely deep
Black pools,
That I once gazed into
So fondly.

For I know all to well
Your eyes alone
Can sway my heart.
And daring to love you again
Is a risk I will not take.
