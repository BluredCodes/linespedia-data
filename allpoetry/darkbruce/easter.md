The death
and resurrection
of scandals,
that have festered
in faith and laity.
Priests wrestle
with celibacy and youth,
Abuse, Cover-ups
Secrecy.
The betraying of Jesus
as the horrifying acts of God
Silence the victims,
and the church
Rapes new youth,
in the shadows
