Not a month has passed
And you are at it again
Looking for a new puppet
Poisoning the mind of another man
But you cannot see him
With the clarity that I can.

But I will not tell you
The motives of this new creature
I want you to experience first hand
The pain of this sort of venture,
I want you to writhe in agony
As you experience his torture.

He is going to play you
With every inch of his pride
He is going to put on the façade
That he is a nice guy.
Then one night you will be screaming
As he plunges deep inside.

When he is finished
He will leave you crying on the floor
As he puts on his clothes
And walks out the door.
He will never look back,
A bitch like you is good for a fuck, nothing more.

And when you come back
Crawling on your hands and knees
I will be standing there
Without a shred of sympathy,
Knowing that you got what you deserved
After cheating on me.I wrote this poem in conjunction with my other poem entitled 'You', they are about the same person, I feel this one expresses more anger whereas 'You' expresses more sadness.
