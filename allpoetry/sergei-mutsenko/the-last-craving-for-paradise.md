are the mushrooms ready yet? 

sat down 
ate 
saw 

a snake stealing an egg 
in its silver teeth 

made up their minds 
made love 
slept over the wine 
woke up 

silence swishing in the dried sheets 

are the coins shining yet? 

went out 
found violins 
armchairs juke boxes gravestones 
brought home 
coudn"t make up their minds 
made love fell asleep 

the dark clambered up slippery 
over the faces 
nestled cosily by the heads 

are yet the windows open?
