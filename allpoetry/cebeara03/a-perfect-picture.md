title: "A Perfect Picture"

You stay sitting there-
A perfect picture.
Staring at nothing with those sad eyes
A truely lonely person entangeled in their regret.
You stay sitting there-
A perfect picture.
Painting images in your mind
Of how it used to be-
When everything was as you dreamed.
You stay sitting there-
A perfect picture.
Thinking of how it all happend.
How they slipped from your outstreched hands.
You stay sitting there-
A perfect picture.
Retracing memories of happiness
And waiting for something you still believe in.
You stay sitting there-
A perfect picture.
Watching the swing teeter back and forth
While you cry out your soul.
They aren't coming back.
