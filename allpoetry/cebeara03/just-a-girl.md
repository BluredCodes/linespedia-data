---
title: "Just A Girl"

She's just a girl
With slight smiles,
That passivly escape her lips.
Her eyes trying to conceal
All the truth,
All the emotion,
That runs---thrives in her veins.

She's just a girl
who's tried to protect her innocent name,
Frome the gossip,
From the meandering eyes.
She is only a girl in love,
And that's all she'll ever be.

She's just a girl,
It's not her fault,
That she fell for him.
Fell deep, without directions out,
With just one quick glance into his eyes.

She's just a girl,
Who doesn't know why,
Nothing is turning out the way she dreamed.
He's finally said he loves her,
And she turns him down.

She's just a girl in love,
Just a little lost girl.
