You look at me 
As if you have no idea
What's going on,
Or even how it happened.
Don't try to pretend you care.

No, we don't need to talk.
We finished this conversation
A long time ago,
Anything with you is over...
And it's going to stay that way.

Don't give me "that look"
The one that suggests
You can make it better...
No, you can't.
I'm perfectly happy,
Mainly because I've gotten rid of you.

Don't test my patience,
I'm known for having none of it...
So be grateful for the little bit you're getting.
Don't think I can't see you,
It's hard to see you driving by.
Just stop trying to scare me...
It's not going to work.

Stay away from me...
We're over.F'in ex boyfriends...
