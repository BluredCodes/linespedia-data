In the nighttime, as waves crash on to the shore and the moon comes through the clouds I see dolphins jumping underneath a rainbow after it has poured torrents of rain. Around them palm trees sway in the cool, swift breeze, and seagulls call to the glistening stars. The sky is as clear as day, and the ocean is filled with the echoing sounds of the dolphins. Turtles and bright colored fish swim underneath the waters where the dolphins are swimming. Below the ocean the waters are decorated with coral, sea anemones, and other sea plants. Out of the water come two mermaids and they start to play and sing with the dolphins. They are very slender with seaweed like curved tail, and long, red, wavy hair. When they jump through the air, they send glittering water flying up to the stars, and splashes back into the water like diamonds sending ripples through the still, calm waters. In a short time they are gone again.
