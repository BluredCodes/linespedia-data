The Dragon of Crystal Lake

Through willows swaying in the breeze
Splashing through rivers beneath trees
Runs the dragon of Crystal Lake
During the night he does awake
A curse of torture on him stays
Never he sees the sunshine rays
The spell on him torture and hate
The evil sorcerer knows his fate
This dragon’s soul may be rotten
But his heart has not forgotten
All of the love that dwells beneath
Through the torrents of pouring rain
Running wildly she screams in pain
This white rose is adorned in blood
Her streaming hair beneath her hood
A strewn wreath of rain and tangles
A breath of fire this dragon gives
But standing still the lady lives
The dragon then stops for her sake
And both walk off to Crystal Lake
