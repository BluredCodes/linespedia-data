Beyond the invisible

I see myself through a misty cloud
A tear drops from my eye
As it goes to the ground it freezes
Wings form on my shoulders
I spread out my wings and I 
Start to fly away
Away from this horrid life
I transform in front of everyone’s eyes
Blonde ringlets of hair fall from my head
A flowing gown of royal purple
Surround my body, in a glow
My whole figure flies upward
I have escaped from this world
Forever in a sacred place
Of jewels and glory
I have started a new path
With a new hope, a new start
To make the flowers blossom
This next teardrop is a tear
Of joy and faith
It runs down my cheek 
Down my dress and drips
Onto a new beginning
It soaks in and penetrates
A glorious light that
Glows forever into brightness
Deep inside my soul
Where never before I have seen
I stare at the dolphins in the sea
The planets in the sky
And am enchanted by this world
No more pain, no suffering
All is gone forever
I have reached beyond the invisible
And everything is possible
