The Best Teacher in the World

When all was confusing and made no sense
There you were ready to embrace
Our hard times that were so tense
Your arms were always stretched out in time of need
When all I could see were my own problems
You opened up my eyes to heed
That there were many people who needed help
You were always strong and courageous during all circumstances

One look at your face and you could see
That forever was in your eyes
Whatever we did you waited patiently
You strived to bring the best out of all of us
Even if our strengths weren’t in school
When in hard times not as much as a fuss
Came from your lips
You always put others first
Thank you Mrs. Riedel with all my heart
