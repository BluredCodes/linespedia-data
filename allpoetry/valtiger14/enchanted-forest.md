Enchanted Forest

Hear a whisper through the trees 
Hear a fluttering of the leaves
See the beauty of the wood
See the clouds all like a hood
Above the grass and the flowers
Birch trees above hang like towers
In this enchanted wood I’ll stay
I will listen and obey
Lonely wind that howls to me
The flowers that will always be
Mine alone, my joy, my love
They were designed from above
Feel the coldness of little wispy breezes 
Hear a humming of the bees
Everything as normal as possible
Only for the day at least
