title: "The Night of the Faeries"

You see them everywhere
Across the wooded forest they soar
In the starry maze of night
Through willows waving softly
To the powerful, whispering wind
Through water sparkling clear
Splashing upon the meadow grass
The faeries fly gracefully
Around trees swaying in the breeze
Above waterfalls they fly
Swooping down into the enchanted water
They are the faeries of desire and pain
Searching for hearts to make desire
For all evil and hatred
While they torment their souls
Making them living dead beings
Torturing anything they come upon
These faeries deceive even
By their charming sweet appearances 
You can hear them whispering in the night
You can see their faces
Staring at you with their evil grins
Motioning you to follow them
Through the night you follow them
To their castles in Tartarus
When the sun comes over the hills 
You wake up all is still and the faeries have disappeared 
Was it a dream or was it real?
