title: "Beams of Light"

Above the sky so bright and clear
Looms the moon without fear
Together moon and stars make one
Forming beams of light to shun
All the glooming veil of black
That stretches across the skies of lack
Forever tries to haunt this luminous light
By swallowing them bite by bite
