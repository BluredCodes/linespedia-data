Sealife

The waves roll over sleeping sand
Grabbing it in its icy hand
And letting go of it once more
Moonlight glistens and seagulls soar
Above the never ending sea
Beautiful it will always be

The ocean then becomes alive
Then all the orcas start to dive
Into the bright and glistening sea
The dolphins talk and laugh with glee
Colorful fish swim to and fro
All the babies have yet to grow

A bright star shoots across the sky
The wind gives out a mournful sigh
All the unmovable stars gleam
The lovely moon casts down a beam
Of an eerie luminescent light 
That must only happen at night

A sunken ship lies on the sea floor
Barnacles line the form of a door
The stars light up the lovely night
The sea is a beautiful sight
All of the colors come to life
In the wonderful beautiful ocean
