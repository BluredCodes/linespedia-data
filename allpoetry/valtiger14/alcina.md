There she stares forever looking at the stars
Through her window, tears streaming down her face
This life she tries to pass away
By staring up at heaven so hopelessly 
Through misted tears she screams out loud
All her pain, her grief, come to her
As she stares helplessly at the black, gloomy night
All she can think of are her past pains
They strike her like knives
And pierce her like arrows
Forever she dreams about a different life
A life full of love, joy, hope, and faith
These are the only things that keep her alive
Keep her living in this treacherous world
