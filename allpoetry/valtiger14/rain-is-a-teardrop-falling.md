Rain is a teardrop falling

Rain is like a teardrop falling
It sounds as though a bird is singing
The rain likes to pour upon me
It rains down upon the grass you see
The rain it whispers in my ear
It’s whispering something without fear


It pours upon my face
They scatter down like they’re in a race
It falls from the dark gray sky
The sun and bright blue sky say goodbye
The rain patters down on the roof
Sounds as if someone is dancing on the roof


Its sound is so soft when it falls
All creatures and birds, all their calls
Sound peaceful as the rain falls on them
The crystal raindrops are each a gem
Falling down upon the hard ground
Making a beautiful, lovely sound


Pain is beautiful and joyful
It never, ever gets mean or dull
It always laughs upon my foe
Making me look a beautiful grace
The rain disappears and sun shines
Then will come another day to rain so hard again
