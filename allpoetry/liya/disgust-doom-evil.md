title: "Disgust, Doom & Evil"

I'M EVIL
I'M DARK
I'M AN OMEN
TO THE WORLD

I HATE YOU
YES YOU!
I HATE MYSELF
BECAUSE I'M ONE OF YOU

I LOATHE THE WORLD
I LOATHE ANYTHING HOLY
I CRAVE FOR THE END
I DETEST YOUR DUMB DISGUSTING FOLLIES

I WANT TO SEE THE BLOOD
YOUR RED SENSUOUS BLOOD
REDDER THAN HELLFIRE
IMPERATIVE FOR MY PLEASURE

I AM THE DEVIL
I AM SATAN
I WILL BRING THE END
WITH MY OWN HELLISH PATTERN


beware of your evils

BECAUSE MINE WILL ENSHROUD YOURS.
