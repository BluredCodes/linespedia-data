i need a shrink

coz i'm crazy

psychiatric help

coz i'm insane



u know a doc?

prescribe me to him

coz my grey matter has become

a jigsaw thing



this way or that way

inside my head

whichever way u go

u'll see red



coz u know

whichever way i go

i see blood

very raw



gushing in front of my pupils

as i laugh

sebmerging with tears

as i cry



u know a doc?

tell me quick

wanna go to him

BEFORE I TURN INTO A GEEK!
