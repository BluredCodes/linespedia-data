---
title: "Reminded By The Moon"
writer: "debbie"
slug: "reminded-by-the-moon"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55754-Reminded-By-The-Moon-by-debbie"

The crescent longing
of the still,
bare
clay moon
taking on
season's shapes
forges vivid imagery
of time passing.
The naked,
desolate stance
it takes on
boldly proclaiming 
forever
for itself
showing no mercy,
reminding me
the limited time
that is
carefully put
upon my life.
Incessant questioning
with contained fear
the length
of my stay 
here.
Serenading me
from a world beyond;
following me 
closely
with a watchful eye,
spying
relentlessly,
always 
reminding me
the journey
of my lifetime
as it will pass
to gone
one 
day.
