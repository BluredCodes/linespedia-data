title: "Rekindled"

Across
the lavendar
summer sky
my love
dances
fervently
as I
helplessly
and mindlessly
sketch 
whispers
of secret drawings
for your eyes
to remember.
Images bounce
from cloud
to sun
displaying
my true affections
silently
screaming to you
in hopes
that you
are reminded
of me
in all things.
Can I cradle
and caress
your heart
through the distant
elements
that be?
Will you remember me
as I
always you
as our 
separate journeys
go on?
Will life steal
our breaths 
one day
with love never forgotten?
I pray so
and our love
will 
be
