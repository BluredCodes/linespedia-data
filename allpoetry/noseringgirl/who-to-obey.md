---
title: "Who to Obey?"
writer: "NoseRingGirl"
slug: "who-to-obey"
source: "AllPoetry"

Am I all alone out here?
Alone and scared
Freezing
I sit here in solitary
Wondering where everyone has gone

Why have they left me here
So alone and scared?
But yet I am not alone
I have company
The ones in my head
They at least have stayed

Instructing and commanding
Whispering and screaming
The tell me what to do
But with so many
Who to obey?

Some say to live
And some to die
Who do I listen to?
Which one is right?
Who to obey?

For ever so long
They have been here
Instructing and commanding
Whispering and screaming
Who to obey?
