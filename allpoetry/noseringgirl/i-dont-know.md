title: "I Don't Know"

I don't know what I'd do,
If I couldn't hear your voice.
I don't know what I'd do,
If I couldn't see your face.

I don't know what I'd do,
If you weren't in my life.
You keep me happy, keep me sane,
Keep my life in harmony.

All the love I have inside,
All the love of the world;
All the love I have through and through,
All of it, I give to you.

You hold my hand,
Kiss my lips.
Never have I felt a love,
Anything like this.

I need you here, here with me,
Forever in my life.
My love for you will never end,
Even through the pain and strife.

You help me through the bad times,
By loving me through the good.
I wouldn't give you up for anything,
In my life, you ARE the good.

I don't know what I'd do,
If I couldn't hear you voice.
I don't know what I'd do,
If I couldn't see your face.

All the love I have inside,
All the love of the world;
All the love I have through and through,
All of it, I give to you.

Written for Andrew Bircher
