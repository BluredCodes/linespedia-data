title: "Gone"
writer: "NoseRingGirl"

Fuck God.
Fuck the world.
FUCK IT ALL!
Maybe we shall meet again,
Or perhaps not.

Who is to know,
How things will be?
And what shall happen,
To meaningless me?

Who is to know?
I guess it's my choice.
For no one can hear
My meek little voice.

Crying out my pain,
Not wanting to hurt,
Anyone but myself.

Maybe, if I went away,
Never came back again;
Maybe then will come the end,
The end to all our pain.
