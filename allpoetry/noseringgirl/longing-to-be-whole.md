---
title: "Longing To Be Whole"
writer: "NoseRingGirl"
slug: "longing-to-be-whole"

In the dark recesses,
Of this human heart,
A soul lies dying.
Alone
And forgotten.
Separated from its other.
It cannot live alone.

*Chorus:
The pain cuts through
This heart and this soul.
And the morose feelings
Keep them from being whole.
Then, they cry out,
Longing to be whole.

The light is gone
From this life,
And forever it is alone.
In night's dark loneliness,
This life thrives alone.

*Chorus

Bridge:
The moon shines down,
Cleansing this soul.
Takes the hate,
And washes it away,
Oh, washes it all away.

*Chorus

It's different now.
This heart and this soul
Have been healed.
Softened.
Filled with love and compassion.
And once again,
After so much longing,
This heart,
And this soul,
Are whole again.
