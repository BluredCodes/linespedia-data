A happy guy on Prozac
A bastard in a fairy tale
A dead horse pulling a carriage
Images in my head
They spin around
This way and that
Twisting and writhing
Like the insides of a cat

These pictures in my head
Whispering and screaming to me
Frightening me into a state of panic
Perhaps I be a bit manic
These thoughts and these pictures
Gyrating through my mind
I just want to leave it behind
Far far away
