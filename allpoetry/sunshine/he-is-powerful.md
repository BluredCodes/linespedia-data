GOD is all around us
	Everywhere we look
	All the things tat H has touched
	Could not fill an endless book

From the tallest, tallest mountain
	To the smallest grain of sand
	Everything in nature
	Was created by HIS hand

He spoke into the darkness
	To create the Light
	HE put HIS Son upon a cross
	To make our futures bright

So when you see a flower
	Or hear a baby cry
	You’ll know that GOD created Earth
	And we’re the reason why
