Oceans
Boundless, reaching
Beyond the horizon
Nothing less than magnificient
Beauty

Rainbow
Arch of color
Blue, purple, red, yellow
After the rains end, a new hope
Magic

Flowers
Color garden
Roses, wildflowers, mums
Blossoming for our own pleasure
Fragrant

Forests
Mysterious
Green, dark, so lost within
Sequence of many unlit paths
Obscure

Snowfall
Fluffy, icy
Melting as it falls down
Silently blanketing the ground
Whitened
