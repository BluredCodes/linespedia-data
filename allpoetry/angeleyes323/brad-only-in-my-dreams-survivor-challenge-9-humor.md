Twas the night before my yoga class
I ran through the house
Chop suey I was stirring
Eeek! I saw a mouse
All my thong underwear were hung
Prints of doodie butt with care
Hoping they would dry
My date would soon be there
What to my elephant eye lashes should appear
Brat Pitt in a limo with rump roast,  a keg of beer
A bed head driver, smearing lips with chapstick
Googly eyes…what’s happening? It must be cos-mic
More rapid than thunder
My Chihuahua and his friends came
Shouting take us to McDonald’s
Taco Bell’s just not the same
So, I cleaned out my buttfuzz
Out the door we flew
A limo full of four-legged friends
Brad Pitt and me too
Down the road we drove
With a sheep and a hound
The driver with chicken lips
Not making a sound
Smelly  ol’ armpit
His belly was round
He turned into McDonald’s
But wouldn’t come in
He got out a spatula
Digging out his belly button lint
Mixing it with creamed corn
Oh my, what a jerk!
Gulping it up fast
He had to get back to work
Hand in hand with Brad I walked
My hands were trembling
I could barely even talk
We finished our meal and headed home
I knew there would be some action
I couldn’t wait very long
Grabbing grandma’s favorite button
I made a secret wish
Hoping Brad would lay one on me
Oh, how I needed his kiss
Tossing and turning I wake from my dream
Hot and sweaty
My room full of steam
I felt a lick upon my thighs
Could it really be Brad?
What a wonderful surprise
I looked under the covers
And what did I see
