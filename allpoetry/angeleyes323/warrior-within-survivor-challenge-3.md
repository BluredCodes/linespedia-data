Part man, part beast
Disengaged from reality

An involuntary fault
A haunting tormented animal

Warrior of his own soul
Embedded in portals of darkness

Excessively fatigued
Battle within himself

Terminus of a branch of evolution
Obsolete of his own kind

Circling a thousand years
Falling from the rim of a high place

Her beauty entrancing like a painting
Breathing life into his desolation

She brought to him a diverted path
Finding meaning to all the footprints

Ones that had led him to her heart
Traveling many distant miles

Finding his home, his loneliness dissolved
Yet his words tremble for this sovereign lady

Whispering to her the visions
Relieved of his burden, shackles unbind

His head lay upon her warm breast
Shielding him from his own war

Dark eyes beholding emotions of regret
In the merciless wave of time

Living with dust on his heart
His Princess, a radiance of comfort

His heart bleeds for her
A soft enlacing of love’s power

One moment in time
Solace from the beast within

The warrior’s battle is wonThis is a poem wrote for the "Survivor" challenge. We had to write what we saw in the picture. Whew!!! LOL
