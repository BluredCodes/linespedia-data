Goddess of virgin freedom
Leaving behind the tangled darkness
Of grasping, uncaring brambles
Out of her cocoon, a new birth
Walking naked into the chaste moonlight
Frightened, yet strengthened
Emerging from a premise once lost
Eyes seeking the beauty of towering trees
Searching for the vision, once seen
Hers…a story perplexed, sad memories
Souls split, an insane separation
Darkness fades into a luminous light
An empty cage, fire ablaze
Incessantly surging a lucid ecstasy
Drawing the air into her lungs
Restoring life into her body
An epiphany of cherubic proportions
Flutter of wings echo a mysterious solemn
Escaping from the indelicacies of life
Mingling with the winds of night
A saintly emotion touching her bareness
Captivating a stellar of strength
She dare ask to the sky
To cover her soul with a cosmic kiss
Clamoring harmoniously with chorus
Under Heaven’s magnificent canopy
She cries out to the morning
Rays of warmth touch her flesh
Prancing trippingly, rasping sweet rapture
Her blithe spirit caught by the gravity of light
Sublime intricate silhouette of life
Alignment with the universe, a new love
Divergence of a new dawn
Guiltless in leaving the memories behind
Nirvana’s renaissance… freedomThis is for the "Survivor" Challenge 6... Look at this picture
