[My man] you’re {on the border], brink of destruction
[Visions] of [life in the fast lane]
[Train leaves here this morning]
Jetting to [Hotel California] in a [New York minute]
Burning desire to see [the girl from yesterday]
Nothing but a [witchy woman], [in the city]
You’ll [get over it], [after the thrill is gone]
Need to [learn to be still]
Think you’re [The Disco Strangler], [King of Hollywood]
In [those shoes], a [certain kind of fool]
[Chug all night], sleep all day
Drinking [tequila sunrise] on a [Saturday night]
Letting loose at [the Sad Café]
[Twenty one] and out of control
You’re a [midnight flyer], taking it all in
[Your lyin’ eyes] [take it to the limit]
[You never cry like a lover], stone cold emotions
Line them up, [pretty maids all in a row]
[Victim of love], such [wasted time]
Gambler of dreams, gonna be a [heartache tonight]
Playing the game of [the new kid in town]
[Tryin’] [to take the devil]
An [outlaw man], a [desperado]
Living like [James Dean]
A [good day in hell]
[Too many hands] have left their scars
Your soul’s [already gone]
Gonna lose out [in the long run]
 [You need to take it easy], need to find your way
You’ve lost your [peaceful, easy, feeling]
Maybe you’ll [try and love again]
[Most of us are sad], missing the man you were
[One of these nights] you’ll wake up
Recognize you always had [the best of my love]
See that [life’s been good], [isn’t it true]?
When you quit dancing your [Hollywood waltz]
I pray that [love will keep us alive]
[I wish you peace]This poem is wrote for the "Survivor challenge". We had to choose a band and then write the poem from song titles. Not too artistic, but here it goes LOL...
