Her soft fingers excite me
Singing my joy with her presence
Dancing, gliding across my ivory body
Coaxing me to cry out
A grand chorus of song
 Along the heart of my soundboard
Titillating my bridge pins
Playing a fantasia
Dynamics of unbridled passion
Segueing into a minuet
Playfully, rhythmically
Strings of ringing steel
Echoes of sweet rapture
Tempo accelerating
Vibrations abound
As her wrists arch
Hands grasping at my pristine keys
Voicing chords in unison
A symphony of souls
Unbearable ethereal tension
Straining to reach out
Pressing her dainty feet upon my golden pedals
Making the floor beneath us tremble
Augmented cadence, a resolution of tremulous accordThis poem is for the "Survivor challenge". It is about a PIANO, coming to life, speaking about how it feels. Don't ask LOL.
