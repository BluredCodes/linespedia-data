Deranged world
Born of aesthetic desires
Adorned with protective layers
Obsessed with physical perfection
Afraid to remove the outer mask
Feelings incomplete to love oneself
Duck into the favorite alley of plastic
A vat of knives and anesthesia
Firm breasts, new face, tummy tuck
A hollow image, competition abounds
Eating disorders, depression, suicide
Reflective of societal attitudes
Creating a fantasy world 
Of erroneous proportions
Personalities thrust into mainstream media
Coerced to gain perfection
Scientific breakthroughs, medical research
Cloning babies in laboratories
Enhancement of embryos
Attaining physical attributes
A conception of deception
Robots handed to new mothers
Moral, ethical?
A revolutionary evolution?
I think NOT!This was wrote for Survivor Challenge 11. We had to write what we saw in this picture....
