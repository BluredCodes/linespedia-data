Been demoralized too many times

Ain't takin' no more shit

You're gonna pay the crime

Think you can push me 

Smash my heart, make me run

The game is over

Next time, I'll be number one

I'll have my freedom

Make my own rules

You'll never catch me

You cold-hearted fool

I'm never looking back

I'm closing the door

Ain't taking your shit

No more, no more
