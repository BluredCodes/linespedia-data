Don't try to change me
Accept me as I am
     ~~~~~~
Walk this path beside me
Together, hand in hand
     ~~~~~~
Feel my every sorrow
Share my deepest pain
     ~~~~~~
Be my words of comfort
Dance with me in the rain
     ~~~~~~
Give me reason to live
Be the air that I breathe
     ~~~~~~
I'll be your want
You'll be my need
     ~~~~~~
Be the light in my shadows
My soft place to fall
     ~~~~~~
A shoulder to cry on
Give to me your all
