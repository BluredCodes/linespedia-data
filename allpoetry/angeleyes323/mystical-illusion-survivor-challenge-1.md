There's a deep murmur

A silent stealth

Purpose made free from form

There's rise to a longer course

A safe place more bright and brave

Reason to sacredly wash

A sublime truth

Meaning behind logic

To lodge in mystical deep streams

Everything which upon my heart felt

Don't stand beneath the boughs

Cast away the heavy hours

Out from behind the clouds

The joyful sounds echoing

Truth of a sea of light

In illusions of thought

What channel my soul seeks

Lies a mansion of stately height

Ahead of the farthest limits of the horizon

Of comfort long, a voiceless muse
