title: "Zero to infinity, all in the blink of my minds eye."

As lovers they meet in a love embrace,
so shortly the sweat streaming down each ones face.

Quicker and quicker increases the pace,
as if two sprinters each in a race.

Until at last in a great mighty spasm,
they both climax in a tremendous orgasm.

That night their love outside of their now ebbing perception,
had done the wonderous thing  we know as conception.

From seemingly not much a life now created.
Who knows to what life the child will be fated.

The day of delivery, with its panicking rush.
The delivery room where the mother must push.

From warmth and and security, through the bithing canal.
Into the room kept cool and sterile.

The baby goes home with its mother to nurse.
Not knowing its life a blessing or curse.

First come the crawling and teething and such,
then walking and talking each thing new to their touch.

Then daycare, preschool and grade school and high.
This child now fully grown had learned how to fly.

Adventures undreamed of all over the world.
The days of their life ever gradually unfurled.

Then one day misfortune caused two planes to clash.
Shortly thereafter to earth did they crash.

Pain gone in instant into darkness now it seems they were led.
Not sensing, not feeling, not knowing they were dead.

Then as if off in the distance a wonderous sound.
From in the same direction a light it was found.

Seeming as a shimmering tunnel.
They were drawn as water flows though a funnel.

Ever onward and upward forever it seemed.
For this life though ended their soul was redeemed.

To find pure joy and love this one entity.
To spend the rest of time until eternity.This was inspired by one of the challenges here. Don't know if this would have made it, yet it was try ).
