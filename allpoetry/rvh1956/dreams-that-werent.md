title: "Dreams that weren't..."

Dreams that never were,
because the dreamer was unsure.

Chances never taken,
from fear of being mistaken.

The hope wasn't hoped,
the dreams were stopped.

Never dreaming them,
never knowing them.

All from one thing,
one thing so clear,
this one thing,
is fear.To be followed by a companion piece --- Category: Hope --- :o)
