Alone in the dark on a moonless night,
suddenly there comes a bright white light.

Dazzling to my mind,
I feel overwhelmed, yet I sense it is kind.

I feel an intense warmth from it,
yet I can't understand it.

As time goes by I notice more and more,
the brigthess in things I had not noticed before.

Can you tell me of this light,
coming seemingly from nowhere on a moonless night.

Can you tell me about this light?
