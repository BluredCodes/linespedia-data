Common points
Draw hearts
To join;
Differences keep separate

There is the pain
There are questions
There is guilt
There is anguish.
Is there
           a way
                    out?
Points in common,
Differences that divide;
How to reconcile?

Think 
Promote positives
Actively build
Common points

See the differences:
Can they be overcome,
Achieving acceptance?
Or changed to
Points in between:
A common ground:
A place

            {in between}

Where communion
Builds to
