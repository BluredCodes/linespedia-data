Ode to a muse


You my muse,
do not confuse,
yet amuse, bemuse, enthuse
with the words you chose.

In my aspiring to be inspired,
you have taken me higher,
like a cannon ball fired
over the high wire.

Waxing with words,
leading me in,
hearing those words,
shows me where you've been.

As I follow your words on the page,
I sense more than the words.
I sense from your words,
you are truly a sage.

You know who you are.
I have told you just such.
Now I say  here,
I love you so much.

This isn't a thing
of which you should fear.
This is the thing,
I hold you so dear.

What is here,
is yours.
Message clear,
no force.

It is for you,
yes you.
You know this of course.
You are to me a 'source'

Take this my friend.
True blue to the end.
Take it and see,
just what
you mean
to me.

((((((( ~~~~~~~ Thank you --- for being --- you ~~~~~~~ )))))))You know who you are --- Need I say more?
