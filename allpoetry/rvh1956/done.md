Is it ever over?
Is it ever done?

Seems some things are just over and over.
Seems some things have just begun.

Is there completion?
Is there an end?

Can you please tell me?
Please tell me my friend.

Is it ever over?
Is it ever done?

Is it ever over,
or has it just begun?Hey, that was a real honest to goodness poem. Maybe my muse has come back after all. :o)
