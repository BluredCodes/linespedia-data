title: "Alone, but not lonely."

Alone is sit here pondering why,
why it is I can be alone and not be lonely.

Is it that I am now complete?
Have I found something I didn't even know how to seek?

I am perplexed.
I am confused.
I even wrote this without my muse.

Something has happened that isn't clear.
Just what road did I come down to get me here?

If someone can answer,
then answer me do.

Perhaps you can tell me how I can be
alone, but not lonely.I wrote this because I am in a weird place right now. It is as if the more questions that get answered, the more questions arise. )
