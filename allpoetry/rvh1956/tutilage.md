Reading reading he is feeding.
Writing writing words inciting.

In reading he feeds,
in writing incites.

The tutor must tutor himself,
before tutoring others.

Years upon years,
hopes, dreams, fears.

Joys, pains,
losses, gains,
he faces,
life he embraces.

Searching words,
abstract, concrete.
Making his education complete.

The tutor,
thus tutored,
goes forth,
to tutor.

Students listen,
they learn.
Tutors motive
