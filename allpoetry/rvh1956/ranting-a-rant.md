title: "Ranting a Rant"

I lay right here a peeve.
Nothing hidden up my sleeve.
Not trying to jest you,
or even upset you.

All have heard the line;

'Wake up and smell the coffee.'

These words are mine,
seeing them you see me.

Why do they 'sleep',
living such bad dreams,
when the answer is much
simpler than it seems.

Be aware of things
which your life brings.
Run from them not.
End time you get caught.

I write this with care,
my heart laid bare,
showing you here,
how much I care.

So as you go now,
back to your life.
Try and go now,
avoiding such strife.
