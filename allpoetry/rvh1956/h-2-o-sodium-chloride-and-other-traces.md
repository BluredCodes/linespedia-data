Tears welling 
preparing to spill
this empty feeling
in hopes to fill.

In sensing and thinking
words and thoughts
I am drinking.

I come to the font
to feed my want.

Perhaps to be taught
getting my heart caught.

What was I thinking?
What was my thought?

Why would I?
Why should I?
How could I?

Allow
myself
to 
be 
caught.

I am sad,
so so sad.
Need to go
back to glad.Ummm, that was as far as I got. Not even sure it needs to go further. Need to adapt my old philosophy that kept me safe.   'Oh well'   :o)
