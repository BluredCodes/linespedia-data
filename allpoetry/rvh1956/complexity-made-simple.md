---
title: "Complexity made simple?"
writer: "rvh1956"
slug: "complexity-made-simple"
source: "AllPoetry"

On a journey the seeker seeking,
hears a multitude of voices,                              barely audible speaking.

All is us and we are all he in us and all in all.

Seeking the source of the voices he wanders.
Alone in his repose he thoughtfully ponders.

Where do these voices come from?
The voices come from whom?
He asks these questions,
while in thought, alone in his room.

All is us and we are all he in us and all in all.

The voices grow louder and sharper and clear,
making him feel so sure he is near.

All is us and we are all he in us and all in all.

The voices grow louder he begins to doubt,
of finding the origin if it's from within or without.

Along the way he hears the experiences of many,
yet answers he found none no answers not any.

Just hints and whispers thusly niggling his mind.
Further increasing his doubts of the answers to find.

All is us and we are all he in us and all in all.

Then one day the answer he found,
so simple yet complex and thusly profound.

The voices are all,
in all and from all.

All is us and we are all he in us and all in all.

A there having thusly the answe he found,
right there where found he plants atree in the ground.

The tree having grown bears fruit oh so sweet.
In remembrances in passing of the tree he does eat.

Remembering the voices that seemed to his very soul did call.

All is us and we are all he in us and all in all.This is one to ponder and let you see which way I have wandered. The road never ends. The journey never complete. Please join in this journey, it is more fun with friends.
