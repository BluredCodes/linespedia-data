i hate your ass
i fucking hate you
but at the same time i love you 
but you hurt me
you hurt me bad
now its your turn to die
die from emotions
from anger
die from the same pain you caused me
go now and never turn back
i still may look on
but not at you
at the road of life we traveled
the old and deserted one
how we were together down to the end
but all i can say now is 
FUCK YOU!!i dont care what you think about it its just something that was in my head and needed to come out
