are you my friend?
it dont seem like it
you hurt me
fuck dude when is this going to end?
i thought i could trust you
but i guess i was wrong
i needed you there for me
but you blew me off to go elsewhere
i guess i didnt mean that much to you
probably never did
for you to do that to me
you kissed him 
while you knew the situation
i guess i cant blame you
ill blame me
i picked a friend like you
a friend i knew would stab me in the back
didnt know it would hurt this much thoughfuck people who pretend to be your friends
