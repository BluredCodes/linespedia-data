i walked out of class today
i saw you... you were walking too
you walked to the water fountain
where you took that sip
i watched you as i walked
turning the knob and the water flowing
you bent down and took a drink
i admired it
and wished i was that water
the water that touched your lips
the water that filled your desire
i used to be that water
i did have you once
it was just like that sip
short, fulfilling and over
ohh well i may still want you
but things will change
i will grow out of you
soon i hope
until then
i want to be that sipyeah i know its out there
