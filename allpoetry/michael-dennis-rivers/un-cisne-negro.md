The cancer was beaten
But its image
Was his secret.
"Once you've been a black swan,
Not just seen one,
But lived as one,
The image will always be there
and it becomes easier to imagine,
on the horizon,
Being the black swan again.”

    Paraphrased from "Blowing Up" by 
    Malcolm Gladwell, The New Yorker, April 22I paraphrased a "swatch" out of an article that is very meaningful to me as I had a life-threatening (heart) operation.  I don't want Mr. Gladwell, one gifted writer, mad at me, I hope he understands.  There is a drastic change in our way of thinking when this kind of thing happens.
