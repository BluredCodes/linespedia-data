When I was young
I had no trouble flying.

As I got older,
I had to think about it.
And it seemed
the older I got
the harder it was for me to fly.

Now, in middle age,
I must shut my eyes tight,
concentrate hard,
squeeze
and sometimes
I get off the ground.

Once I'm up,
I go where I want,
no problem.

I worry,
when I get real old,
will I still 
be able to fly?done in 95...
