It's not the laying of hands
but the long, long wait before
that makes the flesh feel so much better.

The long walk home
with her
is what makes the good night kiss adored
coming, as it does, when it does.

The peach taste so much better 
after a painstaking peel of
its tender skin.

Women have the discipline,
they can wait,
Men can't and it is to their chagrin.

For men the joy is in
the long, long wait
before the win.Great...
