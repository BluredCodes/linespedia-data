In the woods walking,
no car, just me and my cigar.
Ahhhhhhhh....   where's my matches?
Shit, only one left... It's gotta work.
No wind, no problem,
I strike it and half the sulfur,
one whole side of the match flies off
like a little meteor.
Shit! Half-a-match, half-a-mile from my car.
How many times does half-a-match light?
Never! Shit!
I hold my breath, carefully strike the match,
cup it in my hands... it flares up...
and catches!
Yeahhhhhhhh...... I light my cigar
and walk through the woods, puffing,
happy as a pig in shit!Why are you reading this, don't you have anything better to do?
