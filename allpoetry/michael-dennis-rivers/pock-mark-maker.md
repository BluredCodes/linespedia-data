---
title: "Pock Mark Maker"
writer: "Michael Dennis Rivers"
slug: "pock-mark-maker"

Holes made by a pisser 
with a potent stream.
Pock mark maker
make your mark
you lesser man
with lesser tool in hand.
Your bland life
is traced in trails
on a mothball
in a urinal.Guess where I wrote this?
