The undoing of a man,
we say, is at the hand 
of his femme fatal.
So the undoing of a gal,
we may also say, 
would be at the hand 
of her guy lethal.
Well then, 
what happens when
femme fatal 
meets guy lethal?
Ahh, mon chere, 
maybe something 
extra especial?No question in my mind, guy lethal wouldn't stand a chance!
