---
title: "Rapture"
writer: "Michael Dennis Rivers"

I have no time for birds
and crumbs and such
I scurry about
in disarray.  A life
of focused thought,
muscles tight,
intenseness in my face
and all for naught.

Suddenly... there is a loosening,
every muscle lax, music in a 
soft landscape,
easy breathing,
sighs.

For a moment my work is done.
Not for good,
but for a while.

The eye will pass,
the wind will rise again,
but for the moment,
there is a rapture.From "Hockanum Road"
