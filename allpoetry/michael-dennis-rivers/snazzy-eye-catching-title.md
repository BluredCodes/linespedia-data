Snazzy Eye-catching Title

When I think of humankind,
impertinent bastards,
I think of the flea
floating down the river on his back,
with a hard-on
yelling "Quick, open the drawbridge!"

THE END

Frivolous Afterword

Given the size of the Universe,
we are so insignificant,
that it would be very presumptious
to consider ourselves 
much more important
than plants
or one-celled animals.

We are born,
we live and die
And... 
we'll be dead 
much longer
than we are alive;
just like the rest.Woah, get out the bongos & the bong this is heavy, heavy shit.
