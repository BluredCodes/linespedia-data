First Kiss
By Christopher Cooper

The lighting flashes making your eyes close.
You don't feel right but you're not ill.
With pressures light 
you still stiffen up
grapping on to the clocks second hand
holding time so emotions will unwind.
The sliding tip of her tongue, 
your cheek touches her icy nose
causing a chill to run up your spine.
All this leads to a screaming mind,
"Please God make this be happening 
on her side."
