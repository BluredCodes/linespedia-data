SUNSET

Slowly you
S
I
N
K

Further                          INTO     me

DEEPER
Deeper                  with                each
           
P  A   S    S    I     N     G        D       A        Y

FUSINGwithME

I FEEL YOU
Melt in me


BECOMING               Becoming                  me
                  
Merging
with
me

MORE THAN I AM
