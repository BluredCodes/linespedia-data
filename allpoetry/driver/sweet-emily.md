Six years ago
She came to me
Into my hands
Literaly

I floated above the room
Only to come down
To be the first
To kiss her

I called my mother
 I am a father now
And her name
 Emily, my sweet Emily

That mid March morning
I shall never forget

My umbrella for the rain
Since

I hate to see her grow up
But...

It cannot rain forever.
