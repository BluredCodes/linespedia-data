title: "Heart Like A Turbo Booster"

Something more
than a diesel engine
drives me
something...

I've been out here
most of my adult life

Countless state lines
stories to fill a book

The drone of eighteen wheels
and the sweet whistle of the turbo

It's hard to stop
It's something

Tattered photos of my kids
taped to the dashboard
a smile for everyday
It's really something

Last night I crossed the Pocono mountains
the weight of the load tried to hold me back
I grabbed another gear
trucked it up
now that's something

I wonder if my soul will ever rest
or if my blood like diesel fuel
will continue to pump my clutchless heart

Can you hear the sweet whistle?

I'll honk when I pass by.
