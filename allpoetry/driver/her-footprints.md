What we share
is more than a bed
It's the Saturday morning 
coffee pot and newspaper

The simple fun
of Scrabble and 
a bottle of coupon wine

The laughter of our kids
good books
funny stories
and X-Files

I enjoy her company
always will

These are mere words

If my blood were words
I would open my veins
let the crimson 
run down these Victorian streets
So that she could walk barefoot
and actually feel the warmth
And to return home
leaving her footprints, my soul.
