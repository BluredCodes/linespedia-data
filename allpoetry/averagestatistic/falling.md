---
title: "Falling"
writer: "AverageStatistic"

Letters I forgot to send,
a broken heart you forgot to mend.
A selfish mind left uncontrolled,
a love once strong,
just got so old.
Letters from a happy past,
a love I thought would actually last.
You wanted to remain away,
so you wouldn't fall for me again you say.
Perhaps another broken excuse,
tell me now, what's the use?
I need you now, by my side.
Now is not the time to hide.
It's not easy, since you've forgotten me,
you've really hurt me, I hope you see.
Petals falling from this rose,
it was your choice, I never chose.
