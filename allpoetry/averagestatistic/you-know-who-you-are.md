i see your face now everyday
hoping you feel the exact same way
like you did, once before
this is a feeling, i cant ignore
you're acting now, like you always did
in your humor i silently hid
healous of the smiles directed to her
are they in the right direction, will you make sure?
furious of the eyes, that look at her face
your gentle voice makes me the disgrace
you show off your unhappiness
with all of my ungratefullness
i thought things would somehow change
or your priorities would somehow rearrange
i try to tell you how i feel in my heart
your hurting comments pull us apart
stuck in a cloud of your disbelief
now all i feel is constant grief
a year goes by i feel the same
now who is the one to blame
why cant i get over you
maybe im just attracted to the rude things you do
dont clap your hands in my despair
you know why, because i dont care...0?/0?/0?,
