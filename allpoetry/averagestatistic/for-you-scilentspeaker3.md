I hope the best for you,
for it all to come out okay.
And if it doesn't,
I know it still will.
Somehow.
Don't worry too much.
And most of all, don't be so hard on yourself.
You're just going to make it more harder,
for you.
You should try and relax...
I know you want other people to be happy.
But what about you?
What about the way you feel?
I care about that...
If you need to vent...
Then please do so.
You know I'll be here.
...forever.
