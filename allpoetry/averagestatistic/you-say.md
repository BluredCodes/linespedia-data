title: "you say"
writer: "AverageStatistic"

if you love me so much
or atleast thats what you always say
why cant you just tell me
what did i do
to make you
so afraid of me?
if you feel so much for me
or atleast thats what you admit
why cant you just say it to me?
what did i say
to make you
feel so weird around me?I'm not expecting you guys to know how I feel... I don't care if you people think it's a good poem, or just "decent" rather then "divine" i really dont care anymore.. think how you want, but none of you know what im going through.
