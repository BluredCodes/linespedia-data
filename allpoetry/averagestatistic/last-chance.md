title: "Last Chance"
writer: "AverageStatistic"

I shouldn't have brought it up.
Now I feel like I'm loosing you.
What did I do?
I ruined everything...
Why do I always have to say those things?
Now I've lost what I deeply love...
Forever?
I had dreams for us...
You know that right?
And all of a sudden, they just came crashing down.
I'm so sorry.
I can't beleive I said that.
Forgive me please?
I don't know why...
But I feel like that was my last chance.
