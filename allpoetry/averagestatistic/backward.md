---
title: "Backward"
writer: "AverageStatistic"
slug: "backward"

---

a backward face
looks so different in regular
what is regular
you roll your eyes
at my stupidity
and i just sit here
with no movement
unable to do
or say anything
slightly clever
back to you
to hurt you back
do you even hurt at all?
