---
title: "I'm Thinking About You"
writer: "AverageStatistic"
slug: "im-thinking-about-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

If I wait until tomorrow,
I'm going to regret today.
Would it matter anyway?
It would just be another regret,
to be added to the never ending list.
It's just another wasted day...
When all I do is fill my mind with stupid dreams
and thoughts of you.
Mixed feelings collide.
I can't stop thinking about you!
Is there any hope left?
I've been searching.
Searching for so long,
for someone exactly like you.
I can't have you. ( Or can I? )
You're a liar.
A liar with my love.
