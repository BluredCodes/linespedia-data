---
title: "Missing You Some More"
writer: "AverageStatistic"
slug: "missing-you-some-more"

I tell them I forgot you.
And when they mention your name.
Or ask, I say... whom are you speaking of?
It hurts.
I'm so ruined.
I miss you.
At night, when it's just me and my pathetic mind,
I think about you.
And how hard it is to let you go.
Wondering if you'll ever want me back.
Like I want you back.
I'm missing you and I just can't stop.
Wasted times now remembered.
And now you're gone... having fun with you life.
While I'm taking mine little by little.
I'm missing you.
And I just can't stop.
