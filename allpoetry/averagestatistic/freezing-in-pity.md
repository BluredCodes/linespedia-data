---
title: "Freezing In Pity"
writer: "AverageStatistic"

i'm freezing
i shiver in my own self pity
it makes me cold
cold minded, cold hearted
staring off into space
lost track of words you said
i'm sorry, what'd you say?
i took it all for granted
as i do every other time
when will i stop?
the colors in these pictures
just wont stop fading
the letters grow easy creases
i open them too much
do you know how long
you've been gone?
i dont know what you were saying
it meant a lot
i thought i cared
now im the one talking...
... myself into not falling for you again
