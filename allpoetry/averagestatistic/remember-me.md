title: "Remember Me"

Remembering turns to forgetting
hate turns to love
doing goes to regretting
push goes to shove
truth turns to lies
happy goes to sad
smiles go to cries
laughing turns to sad
sunny goes to raining
madness goes to forgiving
beleiveing goes to blaming
and dieng goes to living
mistreating goes to respect
backwards goes to straight
caring goes to neglecting
destiny equals fate
