title: "Hope It Rains"

It's pouring...
I hope it never stops raining.
I don't know it's just that...
Rainy weather reminds me of you...
Maybe because raindrops... are like teardrops?
I hate when it's sunny.
Ms. Sunshine isn't here anymore... because of you.
The sun is so artificial.
Kind of like happieness.
Thunder, lightning.
Reminds me of your anger...
The hail that's pounding.
Reminds me of your fist in my face.
I hate loving you.
I hate all we had.
It just keeps coming back.
Everything reminds me of you...
Of us.
I hate how you hurt me,
and how I forgive you so easily.
I hate everything now that I can't have you...
I just hope it won't stop raining.
