Does it ever get any  better?
Does it?
I never said I wanted you to leave.
You just, did.
Now I have nothing to call my own.
I wanted you to stay.
But you had other plans.
... plans that didn't involve me.
... in any way.
All I wanted to do was hold you.
... and all you wanted to do was hurt me.
