title: "Golden"
writer: "AverageStatistic"

Golden stars,
cherry red skies,
moonlit faces
with morbid eyes.
The thunder bolts,
no one can cope,
silent tears,
forgotten years.
A changing past,
but never to last.
Swollen eyes,
nothing left to despise.
