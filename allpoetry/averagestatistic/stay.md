---
title: "Stay!"
writer: "AverageStatistic"
slug: "stay"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57403-Stay--by-AverageStatistic"
---

If I stayed would you go?
If I left would you show?
Would you bother if there were nothing?
Would it still mean something?
Would you leave me here to cry?
Or would you stay and just ask why?
Would we last another day?
Would you feel the exact same way?
