---
title: "I Just Can't"
writer: "AverageStatistic"

I can't go on without your smiling face.
It's just not the same,
to know you left and I'm still  here.
Who knows what to do now?
This is the last time...
Or that's what I said the time I began again.
I know how it feels to want out.
I've just never actually left.
I've heard these lines before.
I know your feelings are fading.
