---
title: "Did I"
writer: "AverageStatistic"
slug: "did-i"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54278-Did-I-by-AverageStatistic"
---

did i ever tell you
the way i really feel
did i ever tell you
i feel so unreal
did i ever tell you
that i always hide
did i ever tell you
what i really feel like inside
did i ever tell you
i watch the way you move
did i ever tell you
i dont know what to prove
did i ever tell you
i love the way you smile
did i ever tell you
you're incredibly worth while
did i ever tell you
you hurt me everyday
did i ever tell you
it hurts when you have nothing to say
did i ever tell you
i hate when things dont work out
did i ever tell you
i love you more then any amount
did i ever tell you
you mean the world to me
did i ever tell you
i love you more than anything
did i ever tell you
it hurts to say good bye
did i ever tell you
you always make me cry
did i ever tell you
that im just not strong
did i ever tell you
that i just cant move on
did i ever tell you
i cant waste any time
did i ever ask you
why cant we be fine
did i ever tell you
that you're always right
did i ever tell you
i dont want to fight
did i ever tell you
that you'll never change
did i ever tell you
that im just not the same.
