---
title: "Stupid Gary"
writer: "AverageStatistic"

Stupid Gary.
You just don't get him.
He makes no sense...
all the time.
You wish things were how they used to be...
but you know that could never happen.
You're so pathetic.
Stupid Gary.
You just don't get him.
You know he led you on.
Do you like being hurt?
You just can't take it.
You just let it slide...
Stupid Gary.
