---
title: "What to do?"
writer: "AverageStatistic"

Blinking away the rolling tears,
can you avoid them?
Look up and pretend they're going away.
Yet they're still there... you can't ignore them.
Try to look away,
make sure no one sees you...
You're so transparent.
We can see right through you...
And your tears.
Did I make you cry?
Oh, I'm sorry...
Now you know how I feel.
ALL the time.
You tell me no...
I leave...
I just don't understand you.
