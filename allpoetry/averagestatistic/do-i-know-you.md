---
title: "Do I Know You?"
writer: "AverageStatistic"
slug: "do-i-know-you"

Hello stranger.
What's your name?
Do you want to talk?
About love, feelings, movies, music?
You have a sweet way of talking...
I like the way you think.
I admire the way you feel...
... about certain things.

Hello stranger.
What do you look like?
I know it wouldn't matter.
But I just need a hint of what the perfect guy looks like.
Thanks for being my friend.
It seems like I know you...
Somehow, from somewhere.
Isn't this strange?

Hello stranger.
I'm in love with you...
Did I tell you that already?
You seem so beautiful to me.
I doubt this would work...
Lack of feelings?
Never meeting?
Not knowing eachother?

... I think I'm in love with a stranger.
