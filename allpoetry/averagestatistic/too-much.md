---
title: "Too Much"
writer: "AverageStatistic"
slug: "too-much"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62820-Too-Much-by-AverageStatistic"
---

You brag way too much.
It hurts me.
And most of all, it gets so annoying.
I'm going here,
I'm going there.
I'm going to her house,
I'm going to his house.
I'm going to this concert, with these people.
Well I'm not!
I don't care okay?
Do what you want and don't tell me,
unless I ask.
Or unless I actually go.
I hate when you brag too much.
