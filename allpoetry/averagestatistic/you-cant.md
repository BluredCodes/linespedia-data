---
title: "You Can't"
writer: "AverageStatistic"
slug: "you-cant"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62815-You-Cant-by-AverageStatistic"
---

You can't keep faking this love.
The love you show so often,
is fake and dead,
... and broken.
Can't last any longer.
With lies of romance and love.
You're just so needy.
And so am I.
It's hidden.
The way you really feel.
It's so deep.
... and I can't stop digging.
