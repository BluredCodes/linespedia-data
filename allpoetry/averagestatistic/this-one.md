..this one goes out to..
she looks at her wrist
devoting her scars and cuts
to her most previous
..you..
how does it make you feel?
does it make you want to run from her?
well guess what?
she's been running all her life.
are you scared now?
do you wish you never loved her?
all because you're responsible...
for her pain.
it's your fault you know.
you made her feel this way.
