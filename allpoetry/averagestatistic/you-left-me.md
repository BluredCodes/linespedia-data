---
title: "You Left Me"
writer: "AverageStatistic"
slug: "you-left-me"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

You left me with a broken heart,
I guess I should have known.
You left me with these broken dreams,
I guess they should have shown.
You left me with these quiet lies,
I guess I should have cared,
But I guess it was a lack of time,
in which no-one was prepared.
I cannot take these mistakes,
they will never again be right,
and every time they're attempted to be fixed,
I start another fight.
