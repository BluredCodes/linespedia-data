everytime i look at the scar
it reminds me of you
you call me dumb
and you tell me its my fault
not yours
who else would have done it
you were the one
stretching the wound
helping me do it to myself
shouldnt you feel responsible
i wouldnt have started
if it wasnt for you
i know its my fault too i guess
but the sad this is
you helped me make it worse
you didnt tell me you didnt want to see me hurt
you let me keep going
you wanted me hurting
it made you feel good to know
i hurt myself over yousorry if there are any spelling errors, i dont care
