---
title: "Temptation"
writer: "AverageStatistic"

you tempt me to fall
and follow close behind you
you drag me around
like a beat up doll
you were so sincere
i thought you meant well
you hit me
hard
you pushed me down
and you tempted me
to just keep going
i trail behind you
with my broken parts
you've dragged me along
for way too long
i'm beat and tattered
i dont want to  be your doll anymore
