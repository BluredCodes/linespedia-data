---
title: "Road Trip?"
writer: "AverageStatistic"
slug: "road-trip"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

I'm just so fed up.
Angry, sick, tired, and confused.
So, get off my ass.
All you do is brag.
It's so god-damned annoying.
I just want to punch you in your mouth.
Knock out some teeth, make you bleed a little.
When I'm upset, don't come in,
talking to me,
thinking you're making me feel better,
when all you're doing is making me feel like I want to hang myself.
Just leave me the fuck alone.
