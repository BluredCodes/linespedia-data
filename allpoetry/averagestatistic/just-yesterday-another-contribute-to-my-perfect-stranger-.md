Just a few days ago,
we just started talking.
If I gave you my heart,
would you accept?
I know you've handed yours out to many...
what makes me different? ( right? )
I feel like I know you so well.
Although I don't know much.
If I told you I wanted to be with you.
You'd think I was crazy...
So I'll just keep my mouth shut...
and pretend I feel nothing for you.
I think I love you.
