---
title: "love you so deep"
writer: "scarlas29"
slug: "love-you-so-deep"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55026-love-you-so-deep-by-scarlas29"

I know I’ve told you that I love you,
But how do I make you realize, 
What I’m feeling down so deep?
Do you know the feelings of how I yearn,
And how I tell you, I love you deep?
Do you know of how I wonder,
How did you step unto my path?
Do you understand when I tell you,
Do you fully comprehend,
That I love you,
And that I am not going-
anywhere?
Cos so deeply I’ve fallen,
And I’m loving every minute,
That I spend with you,
In this rapture of whimsical love,
That you’ve coated me in. 
I know you love me,
But do I know how much you feel for me?
Do you want me forever,
Do you want me now?
I have a mind and I can think,
Of all the things I love of you,
Of how I dream and you surround me,
Cos I love you so deep
