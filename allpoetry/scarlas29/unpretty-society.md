Ain’t it funny,
How poorly you can feel.
By one small remark society-
Makes you feel…
Loathed,
Undedicated,
Imperfect.
I watch and I learn as man makes you feel-
Completely incomplete,
Unable to match perfections of the imagination…
I could easily loose my mind,
As I see myself disintegrate into timeless wonder,
Of how I could be.
I’m in mental pain as I feel so damn unpretty,
Cut up and bleeding,
I sense my disbelief.
Never told how wonderful I can be-
Or curiosity can kill me…
So many beautiful faces,
But constantly they make me feel-
Guilty as my ugliness creeps in…
Rearing it’s head to my esteem.
Loathsome thoughts envelope my crazy head,
As women dance through society-
Cutting corners to be pretty,
Why don’t you take one aside-
And just make her feel pretty,
Special,
Loved…
Instead of cutting her esteem?
Comparison to no one,
Is what she needs to hear,
Beautiful as a rose,
The rose in an evil society.
Make her know it,
Make her see…
Cos so many women sink each day,
Tell her you love her and that she's perfect-
As her imperfect self needs to be.i was having an upretty day.
