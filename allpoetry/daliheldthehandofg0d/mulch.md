the simplest way to take it in

is to stop questioning what the papers said

the simplest way to turn back time

is to throw your life’s earnings into the fire



the simplest way to break the spell

is to keep your faith in my innocence

the simplest way to swallow whole

is to sweep the skeletons under the rug



there’s no real cause for panic

keep your seat, sir

keep your seat



the simplest to way remain calm

is to ignore all of that whispering from the vents

the simplest way to save yourself

is to walk into the woods and never return



there’s no real cause for panic

keep your seat, sir
