No, you’re right.
You were somehow
always right,
weren’t you?

Our discussions left me
thinking about God.

Do you remember
that time I tried
to kiss you
under the overpass?

It should have, and could have
been, but just wasn’t.

I traced the lines
in your palm
while you talked
about spirits.

You used to tell me how
I remind you of Holden.

Things never seem
to stay the same.
Are you ever
coming home?

GBE
