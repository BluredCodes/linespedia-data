---
title: "Tonight Tastes Eternal*"
writer: "daliheldthehandofg0d"
slug: "tonight-tastes-eternal"

we lie together
two bodies
separate and impermanent 
isolated by the inevitable
bound by the possible

save your tears
my only love
tonight tastes eternal

we have been here before
appearing much different
but feeling the same
oceans afar and ages ago
even there we were love

take my hand
simply believe
tonight tastes eternal

someday we will depart
as ashes to black ashes
and dust to settling dust
though for now we have each other
and for each other we have now

feel my lips
to remember
tonight tastes eternal

we lie together
one spirit
stretching behind and ahead
out of time and out of place
this love is everlasting

here before
and here again
tonight tastes eternal


GBEi tend to believe that everlasting love rings infinite throughout time, and that all lovers that have met in the past will reunite once again.  this is about differing bodies with coinciding  souls, being together forever- as it should be.
