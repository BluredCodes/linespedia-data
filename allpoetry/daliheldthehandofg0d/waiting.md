I never minded waiting
maybe I should have
of course this waiting
is a strange kind of business
waiting is an assumption
waiting requires faith
waiting slows time 
waiting breaks my heart

one cup of coffee
two unanswered phone calls
three, or four sighs
and a sad soliloquy 

I never minded waiting
perhaps I didn’t notice
the denial in my blood
I wasn’t sleeping, just a daydream
waiting is pseudo-impulsion 
waiting skins my knees
waiting ticks and tocks
waiting dies when we do

GBE
