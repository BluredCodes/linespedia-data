---
title: "Do You Remember..."
writer: "ronna"
slug: "do-you-remember"

Do you remember,
The moments we shared?
Do you remember,
How you used to care?

Do you remember,
When we were never apart?
Do you remember,
When you somehow stole my heart?

Do you remember,
The love that once was.
Do you remember,
Well, who does?

Do you remember,
All of the late nights?
Do you remember,
Having all of our fights?

Do you remember,
The days that are now gone?
Do you remember,
The love that you won?

Do you remember, 
The ring I used to wear?
Do you remember,
Saying you’ll always be there?

Do you remember,
The day you said goodbye?
Do you remember,
The times that I would cry?

Do you remember,
How I wanted to be with you?
Do you remember,
Well if you don’t, I do.
