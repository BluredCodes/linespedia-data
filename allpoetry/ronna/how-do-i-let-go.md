---
title: "How Do I Let Go..."
writer: "ronna"

How do I let go of the past,
When that’s all I know?
My love is so vast,
It’s hard to show.

How do I let go of you,
The person I care so much for?
What can I do,
To keep you from walking out the door?

How do I let go of your touch,
That blessed me everyday?
How can I miss you so much,
And somehow have nothing to say?

How do I let go of the thought,
Of us together one day?
Love is what I’ve sought,
In many different ways.

How do I let go of the memory,
Of the love that we shared?
How can I make you see,
How much I really cared?
