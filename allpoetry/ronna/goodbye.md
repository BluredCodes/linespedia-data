---
title: "Goodbye"
writer: "ronna"
slug: "goodbye"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62349-Goodbye-by-ronna"
---

When I was born,
You were there.
Now it’s time to mourn,
About someone for whom I care.

I didn’t have a chance,
To say my goodbye.
In the back I stood in a stance,
And slowly began to cry.

The tears soon flooded my eyes,
And I quickly wiped them away.
I know sooner or later everyone dies,
But too soon came that day.

In the end you were really weak,
And you felt the pain so badly.
I wish that today with you I could speak,
But you’re gone now, sadly.

You are now in a better place,
No more pain can you feel.
But with me you left a trace,
Of a part of you in your will.

I will see you again one day,
Till then you’ll watch over me.
There’s one more thing I have to say,
In my heart you’ll always be.
