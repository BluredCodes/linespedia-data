---
title: "I Could Never Forget..."
writer: "ronna"
slug: "i-could-never-forget"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62352-I-Could-Never-Forget...-by-ronna"
---

I could never forget,
The love that we shared.
I could never forget,
The times I was scared.

I could never forget,
The look in your eyes.
I could never forget,
Your words that were wise.

I could never forget,
The sweetness in your smile.
I could never forget,
Not even for a while.

I could never forget,
The taste of your kiss.
I could never forget,
The love that I now miss.

I could never forget, 
The warmth of your touch.
I could never forget,
How I miss you so much.

I could never forget,
How close we were.
I could never forget,
The love that did occur.
