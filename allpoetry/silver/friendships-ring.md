---
title: "Friendships Ring"
writer: "silver"

We dance around the sacred prye,
Alone and yet through some force together.
Fire laps at our feet,
Arms to sky our words meet,
We dance around,
Around,
And around we go,
Love burning in the middle,
Fellow humans take my hand,
Raven cry not,
For in this barren land,
An alliance of power will create anew,
What seems to always be lost,
But found in hearts that number many and few.
Friendship binds us,
Our humanity seperates,
We are one with many forms.
Identity is but a word,
Our lives are not entirely lost this time,
For we shared our hopes with another.
Though I may not know you,
Or maybe in fact I do,
Friendships ring has bound us,
Love is not it's only name.

We are alone and together,
Near and yet so far away,
We let ourselves be one with each other,
And yet seperate we stay.
Friendships ring has bound us,
And yet it does not confine,
We are alone and together,
Friends and seperate people,
With diffrent dreams and goals,
Diffrent things we all desire,
And yet fate has bound us,
We burn together with lifes fire.
