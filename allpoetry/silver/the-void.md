There is a void that was once a perosn,
I no longer care...
Once I tried to fill it,
It's like its become all that was ever there.
I just realized when I woke up this morning,
The void was all that was left to me,
But I no longer care.
Lights are always there,
Floating in the darkness,
Would be saviors in an over dramatized fairy tale...
I am the void,
I devour what comes close,
I take my pleasure by watching others fail,
I am vain,
Condensending,
I put it together so I can watch it break down.
I don't hate,
But I will never love,
Nothing personal,
After all I can't seem to care about you...or you...or you...
You saw the destruction and redemption,
You were there...and you...and you...and you...
I am the void,
Monster that haunts your dreams...
Kill me...before I am entirly lost to me...
Take the knife...I like blood...it's pretty...
I'm a monster...face t you named me...and you...and you..and you...
The void has a voice and it tells you to fill it,
But the price I fear is high.
