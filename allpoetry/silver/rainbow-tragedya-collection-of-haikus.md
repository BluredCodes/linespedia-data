The flow of water stops,
As virgin legs tremble...fear,
It's to cold for her here.

Shatter proof glass breaks,
In the scream of our silence,
We masked our anguish.

Mortality shrugs,
Its shoulders broadened by time,
Nothing stops denile.

Broken flowers fade,
They don't bloom or fly away,
Picking up pieces.

Rainbow tragedy,
Painted smile on white canvas,
Petal on the floor.
