Don’t want to be attached
No commitment, but I got your cash
Gee whiz, I’m sorry
Gotta wash my hair, it’s stinky
By the way, gotta boyfriend too
And guess what?  He’s not you!
Give a kiss and run away
That’s how these ladies like to play
Squeeze your heart until it pops
Clean up your feelings with a mop

[REFRAIN]
She’s my 30-second girlfriend
She just can’t seem to find her place for me
She’s my 30-second girlfriend
It just goes to prove that these girls are mean

Friday night, we had a blast
She was smiling, I moved in fast
So mushy it would’ve made you sick
Next day my butt received a kick
“I’m sorry, don’t love you that way”
I said “Wait!  Why can’t you stay?”
She said “’Cuz I’m a stinky girl”
“Just can’t be the center of your world”
And that’s when my heart went splat
I was no dreamboat, and that was that

[REFRAIN]

But at least one thing does stand true:
My Lord Jesus is a single guy tooJust a little punk diddy for ya'll . . . Not that all girls are mean -- But you guys know what I'm sayin'!
