title: "MAN, GOD, and SATAN"

Death swipes its trophy as another child dies
Gunshots drowned out by a broken mother’s cries
Lifeless innocence proving the twisted nature of reality
A heart’s passionate faith dies in the arms of brutality
How can this be?

They say You’re behind it, a divinity of hypocrisy
You turn Your blind holy eye to bloodlust animosity
Why don’t You save them, why don’t You bring peace?
The world has chosen to hate You, and they’ve laid out their fleece
How can You be?

Is faith blindness to reality?

Death breathes upon My face with these nails ripping My skin
But you only smile, a reflection of how your black heart’s so thin
I tried to save you, tried to bring you peace in your world of disease
Why won’t you love Me, just take My bloodied hand please
How can this be?

Demons say you’re nothing but mindless toys for the taking
You turn your mortal eye to all this death that’s been reaping
Why don’t you save each other, why don’t you seek peace?
I have chosen to love you, I’ve laid out My Son on lease
How can you be?

Is mortality blindness to reality?

You know, I love your deep philosophical theological atrocity
Your arrogant self-assurance makes the kill so very sweet to me
You’re far beyond saving, though salvation’s right in front of you
But you won’t see that, my loving little puppets, for I’m the master fool
I know how this can be, how you can be, how I am

You won’t be blind in my reality, but then it’ll be too late.Just some personal thoughts of mine, in response to some stuff I've read here lately. I hope you caught on to there being three speakers . . .
