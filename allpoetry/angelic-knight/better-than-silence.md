You have your preconceptions but I
Cannot stand on the sidelines this time
I’m tired of taking the easy way out
No, I’m not ducking now, no matter what they say
Call me crazy, but this heart’s crying for truth
It’s screaming, it’s bleeding, and it just won’t sway

My one weak voice is better than silence
Silenced masses screaming there’s something better than this
Comin’ outta the woodwork, so get outta my way
Stay cowered in your corner, but I’m having my say

Can’t you see they’re hiding freedom from us
It’s trapped in the box of societal say
Why are we smiling when we should be crying?
We’re choked by the standard, it’s invading our minds
Their thrones are built upon our lack of defiance
Will you bow to their way or will you stand up and say:

My one weak voice is better than silence
Silenced masses screaming there’s something better than this
Comin’ outta the woodwork, so get outta my way
