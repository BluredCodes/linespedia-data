What makes a great day?

Is it the weather?
Sunshine falling upon you,
Those pulchritudinous skies of blue,
Would that do?

Or is it the people?
Amative friends,
Who entertain you to no end,
Who help your feelings mend.

Or maybe it's food,
Scrumptious refreshments, all of them free,
With a spot of tea,
That sounds good to me.

Whatever a great day may be,
You can place them in your destiny.
Live life to the fullest and  you will see,
there is a great day waiting for you and me.I was bored and wrote a quick poem.  Hee, hee.
