Full of secrets,
no lies to be seen,
is captured in a diary,
pages unseen.

It stays in a hutch away from society,
With a lock on the hinge,
It hides words unacceptable to propriety.I'm really not sure if the line with propriety make sense... but no one would help so... if it doesn't please tell me how I can fix it... thanks!
