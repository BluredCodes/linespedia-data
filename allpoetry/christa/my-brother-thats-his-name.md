My brother, that's his name
He hollers, and screams, and shouts out my name.

He's driving me crazy,
He's driving me batty,
He's leading me to insanity.

I love him bunches but he gets on my nerves,
Good gosh.

My brother, that's his name
I hate it when he plays his stupid games.My brother was drivin me crazy yesterday so I wrote this.  Hee,hee.
