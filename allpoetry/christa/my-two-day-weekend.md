It's Sunday night,
Another week to go by,
Until my two day weekend,
A bit of joy in my life.

School is way overrated,
You could say I hate it,
But I guess it's ok,
I would just rather stay away.

Go to some secluded beach,
Watch the vibrant sun rise over the tranquilizing waters,
Doesn't that sound so much better?

So, it's Sunday night,
Another five days to go,
Until my two day weekend,
That's where I want to go.Hee, hee.  Weekends are so much better than weekdays... do we not all agree?  Although weekends can be just as stressful.
