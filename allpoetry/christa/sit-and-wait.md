---
title: "Sit and Wait"
writer: "Christa"
slug: "sit-and-wait"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

the days go by
i sit and wait
for you to come and sweep me off my feet
but are you here now
when i need you the most
no, you don't come
i sit here alone

so, i sit here and wait
for i know when the time is right
you will come with all your strength and might
and i will be lighthearted
glad that you were worth the wait
but all i can do now is sit and wait.
