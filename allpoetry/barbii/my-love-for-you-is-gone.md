---
title: "My love for you is gone"
writer: "Barbii"
slug: "my-love-for-you-is-gone"
source: "AllPoetry"

Did you see the love I felt?
Or the times I showed affection?
Did you notice anything but yourself?
These games we played made me hurt.
I felt so alone 
my face shoved in dirt.
Damn, 
once I loved you.
Needed you like oxygen.
But now I'd rather watch myself bleed
then to start a conversation with you.
We once had something beautiful.
But you stomped on it with boots.
