---
title: "Out below"
writer: "Barbii"
slug: "out-below"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61867-Out-below-by-Barbii"
---

Look out below,
because 
I will jump.
I hate my life,
and that's 
all too much.
Don't you see,
how mean 
you were?
I hate the way of living,
because of you 
assholes
below.
Damn,
the way you hurt me.
I hate the way,
I allowed 
you to play,
with my heart 
and soul.
So,
please look out below.
