---
title: "Greed"
writer: "Barbii"
slug: "greed"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

It is a sickness
that each one of us 
carries.
A greed 
of money.
A lust 
for beauty.
But,
what becomes of the rest?

Does, 
this mean 
that everything in between
are not even to be looked upon.
Because,
if so.
The world sure blows.
And people truly are insecure.
And I damn you all to hell.
