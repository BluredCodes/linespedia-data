---
title: "So Sorry"
writer: "Barbii"
slug: "so-sorry"

---

Little kisses,
for your grave.
The ones I 
gave 
were so 
lame.
I wrote 
for you.
The crazy little things that you do.
And,
now, 
to share my love,
the one I would never show.
Now,
you are dead,
and what good are you for me now,
now, 
I hate life.
For it was too short,
and god stole you away.

I never even said goodbye.
All we shared were complete lies.
And, I am sorry.
