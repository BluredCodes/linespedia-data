---
title: "I love you more than oxygen"
writer: "Barbii"
slug: "i-love-you-more-than-oxygen"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61570-I-love-you-more-than-oxygen-by-Barbii"

You are my

breath.

The

fresh 

air I 

lack.

I love you because,

you are funny,

and I feel

so crazy

for you.

We like the same things,

games and books.

Fantasies,

are our touch,

without reality.

Too bad,

you will never see,

mine involved you.

But inside,

to you,

I will always be that friend.
