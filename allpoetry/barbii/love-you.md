---
title: "Love you"
writer: "Barbii"
slug: "love-you"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61563-Love-you-by-Barbii"
---

Your eyes 
are so vivid.
So nice and 
prettiful.
I like them so 
very much.
Would you mind if I touch?

Your smile
is so good.
Its crookedness
fits your personality.
Matches your funniness.

Oh, 
boy.
I think 
I just 
love you.
