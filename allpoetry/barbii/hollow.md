---
title: "Hollow"
writer: "Barbii"

He yells.
I hate the screams
oh,
boy is he mean.
I love him.
Always have,
yet,
the way
he screams 
at me
makes me feel alone.

Hollow,
is my nature.
I was born to be harassed.
Otherwise God would not have made me so fat.
Can you,
be born
for the entertainment of others.
To clinch your father's
need to yell and scream?

What if I was
not,
in the family
I am today.

Would,
I feel
so hollow like clay?
