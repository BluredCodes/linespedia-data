---
title: "Living to die?"
writer: "Barbii"
slug: "living-to-die"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61565-Living-to-die--by-Barbii"
---

This is silly,
can't you see?
Why should we 
have to live,
when living is only
to die.
In one way, 
everyone's already
dead. 
So live this life of lie?
They say we live 
and living can be happy.
But would you be,
if you knew your fate?
Your fate of death, 
and death alone.
Alone, 
you'll die. 
And to die 
is to live.
Living is dieing.
Shit,
I think
I would rather,
not do 
either.
