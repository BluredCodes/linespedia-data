---
title: "Mangled Heart"
writer: "Barbii"
slug: "mangled-heart"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

I wrap my arms upon death,
squeeze it 
and welcome it.
To taste the blood
the flesh
of death.
I calmly expose my veins
and ready myself 
for the blade.
I cut my wrist 
and feel the death,
the death I loved 
and couldn't wait to feel.
Now, I am dead,
and death is real.
