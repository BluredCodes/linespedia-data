title: "His Prey"
writer: "Barbii"

Don't hurt me!
I beg you,
leave me alone.
Please
No! No!
She screamed as he cut her throat.
Nothing left made a sound,
but her gurggling yell.

Sitting back, 
he watched her die.
Fell down in her blood,
that puddled like water,
at the banks of the mud.
He felt so happily satisfied,
but for how long.
Ever so silently he stood.
Picking up his blade he went away.
To find another victim.
