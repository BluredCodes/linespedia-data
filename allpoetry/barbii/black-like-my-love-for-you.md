---
title: "Black, like my love for you"
writer: "Barbii"
slug: "black-like-my-love-for-you"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61569-Black--like-my-love-for-you-by-Barbii"
---

Did you know,
that I loved
you?
For you.
Not like the others.
They used you.
Never even liked you.
But 
because you were nice,
there
and offered attention,
they attacked
like dogs.
If only you saw.

I am different.
I always was.
Just because,
I am not pretty,
doesn't mean, 
I never had 
that much to offer.
Because
for you,
I 
would live 
the world
out 
forever more.
