---
title: "Beautiful death, my sweet lover"
writer: "Barbii"
slug: "beautiful-death-my-sweet-lover"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61560-Beautiful-death--my-sweet-lover-by-Barbii"
---

He was my sweet lover.
He made me feel the death,
because he opened my eyes 
and took my hand,
helped me see,
fore, I did not understand.

He showed me a beautiful 
death,
how wonderful it could be,
and in one swift motion,
He opened my veins for me.
I was dead and no one would
ever have to see.
