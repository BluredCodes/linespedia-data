Did we laugh?
Was it a game?
Sure, 
I know what this means.
We lied didn't we?

These stupid,
sad things
made me cry.
Oh!
Please don't ask why!

For,
if you know,
I might kill you.
Rip off your head,
and swallow it whole.
Spit you back up 
into a bowl.

Yet, 
for now,
we stay the same.
Playing this lame 
game.
Hurting my empty 
soul.
