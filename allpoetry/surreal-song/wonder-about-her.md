title: "wonder about her"

she eye'd me , stare so vicious

 my ear drums vibrated, her cry so harsh

 and started to grow dizzy whilst she pulled at my ears

 and so i let her go

 upon the floor

where she laid, and i stared

 and i pondered how i should think about her

 and i wondered and i wonder

 til she pulled at my toe

 and i thought of her as fun
