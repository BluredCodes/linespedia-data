~ u no ~

one day i was leaving a room
 and i came upon the door, the ones with a metal grill
 so u see light from outside flowing in
and i saw a shadow i think 
 and i opened the door, and it opens out wards
 and i turn the knob and push it forward
 and there's a girl on the otherside who was trying to get in thru the door
but i was there pushing the door out 
 while she was trying to grab the door knob and enter the room
 so she jumped back
 where upon i looked upon her face
 and i thought,  "i caused her to jump back"
and i say "hi!"
and she smiles and laughs and says "hi!"
 and i walk pass and so does she 
and i walk upon the mud and try to lightly step across
...........and walk under the trees.........should read while listening to the grand finale of gladitor, or... better yet, listen to the last 40 secs of the battle track,  

i no, i change tenses like nothing at all, but its a poem
