---
title: "i fall"
writer: "surreal song"
slug: "i-fall"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

i look ahead and not behind

though you twitch, turn, and live bored

i pace myself at a slow tempo

though you stare and hope for others' eyes

i look about with wonder at the sun

though you find your evanescent love

i lose myself in utter confusion - and joy



and though you stumble upon your quick, steady, strong feet


.................I fall up..........................
