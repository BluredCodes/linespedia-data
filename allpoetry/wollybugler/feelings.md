This is the way to my heart. 
If you keep on doing,
What you are doing.

For me, 
I will have to be 
A very good person 
From now on…

And the thought of me 
Being a very good person 
From now on, 
Seems to be impossible 
At this time…  

But, what you do to me, 
Is you make me feel very special, 
Even when I don’t want to feel
Anything at all…  

Like I said, 
I hope you will keep doing 
Whatever you’re doing for me, 
And my life of feelings…
