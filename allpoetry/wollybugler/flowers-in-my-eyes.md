---
title: "Flowers in My Eyes"
writer: "wollybugler"
slug: "flowers-in-my-eyes"

Flowers are some 
Of the most beautiful things
I have ever seen, 
In my life.  

They are all so different.  
They all come 
In different shapes and sizes.  
Each one has its own beauty.  
They have a certain natural beauty 
to them.  

There are not many flowers
That I don’t like, 
But, there are a few.  

The ones that I do like 
Are very beautiful.  
The one I like the most 
Is the white and purple rose.  
They are the most beautiful 
I have ever seen!!!
