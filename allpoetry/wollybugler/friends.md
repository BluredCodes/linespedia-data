---
title: "Friends"
writer: "wollybugler"
slug: "friends"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Friends are people 
Who are there 
For each other 
Through thick and thin.  

No matter how good or bad
The problems may get.  
They will be there for you, 
no matter what.  
 
If you ever need to talk 
Or just want someone to be there 
With you, they will be there
To help you through
No matter what it may be…

That is what 
A true friend is,
Someone who will be there,
For you, 
No matter what…
