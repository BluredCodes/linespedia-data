Love…
 
Is something that is shared 
between two people 
who really care for one another,
They don’t want to have any pain, 
Or suffering or to be left 
In the dark 
About anything…

When two people care enough
About each other, even if it hurts
To tell the truth, 
They will be straight forward,
Even if it hurts…

But, 
They will always have respect 
For each other 
Forever and ever!

And that is what true love really is…
