Scream in silence
And the delusional
Deep-red anger lines
Etched with outraged
Catharsis on an imperfect
Canvas could be your
Echo.

Straight slices of an irate essence
Streak straight over yourself:
Red-rose thunderbolts gracing
A grey organic sky.
This is disgust, release and
Imprisonment
Made real by metallic lover;
Self-loather.

A voice can be possessed yet
Not dared to be heard.

Sting.

Scream.
