Know me when.

Show yourself.

Did you hear the broken man scream?
Shattered bottles spill 
Their liquid broken promises
Across the broken concrete
Of the defeatist's alley.

Defeatist?
Repeatist.

A repeating motif
Of Hellish faces held fast
By bricks in the Strip 
Club wall.
Club us with hate,
Bludgeon us with
Degradation.

Becomes
Segregation.

Don't isolate me then,
Don't forget me when
The sins of a
Population become etched
In my eyes.
When I am transformed into rose-petal
Tears:

Know me then.
