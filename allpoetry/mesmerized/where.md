Where? 
Where did you go? 
Why? 
Why did you go? 
How? 
How could you just leave like that? 

I came back and you were gone. 
I looked for you. 
I looked in all the places you used to go. 
But you weren't there. 

When I finally found out. 
Found out you were gone 
I cried. 

I hate to cry 
but when I looked into the mirror 
salt streams were running down my face. 
The unfamiliar sting burned my eyes 
and blurred my vision. 
My head hurt. 

Why? 
I asked you if you would ever leave me. 
You didn't answer. 
Now I know why. 

Was it something I did? 
I would never hurt you on purpose. 
Were you fighting your own demons? 
Why didn't you come to me? 

I miss you and I want you back 
but I know 
it's not my decision. 
You have to came back in your own time. 
If you come back at all. 
I love you. 

Why? 
Why did you go? 
Where? 
Where did you go? 
How? 
How could you just leave like that?
