title: "Just Before Death"

Footsteps in the distance
And headed my way
They're not turning back it seems
I cannot run away

So I turn to all the wrong places
So I find love in all the wrong places

At leastI can hear when death is at my door
And stare it down 'til it walks out my door

It's coming back, I hear it now
It's pace is seamingly increasing
And I can't find my faith this time
Cuz it's got this hate it is releasing

I gotta pray for my life this time
I've hid it all, so I can't find it
Have to find myslef so deep inside
I think I hit my heart beside it

I pray to God to help me live
In the name of the father and the holy spirit
God will help me find my lost life
An angel is singing, now I can hear it!
