I need this
I know I do
This empty bottle
I think I do
Breathe in, breathe out
Sniff it all in
Inhale it all
So long, it's been
This music 
Is over taking me
This smell
Is undertaking me
I cannot hide
I chose to not run
but it's everyday
That I run from the sun
It's a shame
I know this
It's my life
But what is all this?!
One more hit
And I'll be done
Can not breathe fresh air
Until my life is done.
