I'm hot, I'm melting
I'm pot, I'm bleeding

The voices in my head tell me I'm dead
it itches, it breathes
I scrach it, it's bleeding

I'm too fucking hot
This weather must go away
I'm too fucking hot
This weather must go away

I'm bored, I'm tired
I'm feed, I'm dead

DEAD
DEAD
DEAD

I'm too fucking hot 
This weather must go away
I'm too fucking hot
This weather must go away

AWAY
