Youth!

I've only hit the tip of the iceberg!
-(there's a whole mass of relentless ice below me)

I'm in my prime!
-(no wonder I feel so out-of-place with my indivisibility)

I've only just begun to live!
-(I have my whole, difficult life ahead of me)

I'm at the dawn of my life!
-(The sun beats hard and hot against my back)

I can do anything I want to do!
-(Too bad I don't know enough to decide)

I can learn from my mistakes!
-(Do I have to make mistakes?!)

I'm in the spring of my life!
-(and I have allergies...)
