Thinking about kissing you,
Thinking about touching you,
Holding you, held by you,
Loving you, and Wanting you,
Holding you,
Touching you,
Feeling you
Touching me,
Kissing me,
Feeling me,
Holding me within your gaze
Within your arms,
Feeling you on top of me,
Within my legs,
Within my grasp,
Thinking of you grabbing me,
Pulling down
Thrusting
Writhing
Twisting
Pushing
Pounding,
Thinking of you loving me,
Holding me,
Pulling me
Onto you,
Surrounding you,
Holding you
In my arms,
In my arms and
Inside me,
Feeling you
Feeling me.

And with you and only you
Can I feel the truth of Love
and Making Love.

(Heated cold,
Chilling heat,
Feeling you
Feeling me,
Feeling you
Everywhere,
Don't stop there...)

Hearing nothing but your Breathing, 
Heavy with our sinful act

(Wetting you with my tongue,
Savoring the taste of you,
Quaking, quivering under your touch)

Feeling nothing but loving you,
I cannot speak.

(Kiss the silence from my 
Gaping mouth,
Let me speak yet
I cannot,
Instead expressing
Through fierce looks,
Gentle touches)

Words rush my brain and flush my face,
Yet words I cannot say.
I can only breathe and feel you

Feeling me.
