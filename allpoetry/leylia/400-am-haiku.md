Nothing else to do;
maybe I should visit Nick.
Sleep is for the weak.yes, i realize that sleep being for the weak is a rather recurring theme in my haikus, but let's face it: it's a rather recurring theme in my LIFE.  backstory:  Nick is one of my best friends, who just got a job as a night-stocker at Target.  his hours are ten pm to six am.
