---
title: "I Still"
writer: "Leylia"
slug: "i-still"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/53933-I-Still-by-Leylia"
---

but there are still times when i feel
that pulse, that dizzying
stream of consciousness from which flows
every word of every poem written for love
and yet to be composed for happiness in it,
which forgets to remind me 
to remind myself to remember
to breathe;
which stops my heart for one,
two, three beats at a time 
and i feel it will burst from the
intense heat and pressure of your voice,
your touch, your lips, your own heart;
which detaches my head from my spine
and lets it hover there above my
body, writhing and rolling there in
the anguish of our physical distance
yet intellectual and emotional closeness;
it smiles in mild bemusement at this
chaotic, torturous scene below it,
and silently and calmly reminds me
that we will be together soon.
