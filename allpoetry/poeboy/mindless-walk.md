I walk alone,
Cause I have no one,
Some come and some go,
Some laugh and some cry,
Some live and some die,

The world is a bitch,
And has room for others,
Cause it is a really lonly place,
Even down to the lace,
It is vacant,
Empity and vast,

No one can last long,
They just keep singing this song,
It is a song for the broken hearted,
Or for the ones who are lost,
Lonlyness isnt free,
It has a cost,  
Which is your soul,

Lasting is hard,
When you cant make it,
Sometimes you cant take it,
So you have to fake it.the cursing
