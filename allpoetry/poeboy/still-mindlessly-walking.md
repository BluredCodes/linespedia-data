I still walk alone,
This broken home dont know,
I climb a million mountians and scream,
your name is all that i dream,
I sleep soundly in your corona,
Watching your body move,

I know i cant get you,
I know you arent mine,
I wish that you were,
but your his,

It isnt that I love,
for I dont,
I just love your voice,
And all that surrounds it,

I enter your boundry,
You reject,
I see your feeling on the walls,
I can get the picture and leave,
But at that moment I feel it again,
I remember all that i was before,
All that i stood for,
Before i met you.once again i think im posessed because i dont know where the emmotions come from cause im single. o well dont care it was interesting to me.
