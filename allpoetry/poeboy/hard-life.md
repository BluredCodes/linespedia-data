Here I sit staring at the wall,
It makes me feel as my life is in stall,
I cant find the way out,
I dont know how I got in,
It must have been something I did,
They always say it is because im a kid,

I dont know what your saying,
But i always see it,
I know what it isnt,
yet i dont know what it is,
It seems to be darkness,
Darkness comsuming my soal,

It consumes all that i belive in,
It makes me feel as my life is a sin,
I get to where i cant make it through the day,
And I hate all that everyone does,
I dont care who know,
Nobody gives a fuck,
Or knows where to go,

As I look closer I find it was all my fault,
I dont know what I did,
Or how i go,
All I know is that i must go.do you feel the same way i do
