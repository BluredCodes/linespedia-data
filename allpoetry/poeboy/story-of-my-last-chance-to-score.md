We were gathered here today in celebration,
But the festivitys drew flat,
I stand here in the main chamber,
I notice the way you look at me,
You dont know what i see,

I see your heart,
The look on your face arouses me,
As I approach you I get that feeling,
I can see you moaning,
And feel the heat of your breath,

Your voice makes me quiver,
I dont know what it is,
But i just want to look into your eyes,
And say I love you,

As we begain to talk I get a call,
I know that it cant be more important,
So I give you my number, 
But the worst part is you never called.hey what can i say Im a teenager i get horney.
