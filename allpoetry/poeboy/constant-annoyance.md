Your constantant buzzing drives me mad,
And I try to slap you and my girl thinks im crazy,
So she leaves me and now i am so sad,
And when i see a daisy,
I begain to cry,

All because of your buzzing wont go away,
I try to kill you with all that i can think of,
But I cant seem suceed,
Your tactics of war are insane,
I look for the time of your leaving, 
And your eturnal buzzing is gone,

Now you are gone and i am alone,
Just because you wouldnt go home,
I did what I had to do,
And I did it all to you.
