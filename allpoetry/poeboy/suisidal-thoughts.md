Staring into the blankness of my eyelids,
Relising that I am hopeless,
I find the gun and load it,
I cock the hammer and fire,

I began to float towards the heavens,
I look around and see all my friends and family,
See them crying and missing me,
knowing that I dont need romance to live,
I open my eyes and I am back to normal,i am crazy ill do it dont push me
