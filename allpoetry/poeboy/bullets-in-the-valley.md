As I walk through the valley of no return,
Unaccompanied and flaccid,
The waters often churn,
Bringing memorys unwanted,
Death Sickness and Relationships lost,
They come with out cost,

Bringing the tide of glee,
It seems to avoid me,
Watching wondering,
Confusion engulps me,
Burns you down to the cree,

The constant murmering of loves lost,
Distorted and made wrong,
The effects of the digital age show,
For time has lost its track and flow,
