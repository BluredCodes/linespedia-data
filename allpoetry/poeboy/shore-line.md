As I walk down the shore line, 
I notice all that is washed up,
Drift wood,
Shells,
Even some animals,

As I walk down the shore line,
I see all that is good,
I feel clean yet dirty,

As I walk down the shore line,
I see all that is meaning full and important,
I relize i am just dreaming,
I leave the shore line.dunno
never been to the beach that had drift wood and stuff
