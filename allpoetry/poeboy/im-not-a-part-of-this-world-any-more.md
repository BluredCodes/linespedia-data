The screen is fading,

I wonder if i made it,

Watching in stupidity,

The screen seems to take my soul,



It was cold and icy,

The touch hurt,

I get pulled in deeper,

There now is a bluish glow,



As i fade off the face of the planet,

No one seems to notice,

The planets seems to repel me,
