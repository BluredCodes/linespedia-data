I stand here on a cliff,
Thinking and reflecting,
Should i do it,
Should i not,
The desision is in my hands,

Your distance kills me,
I walk to find hope,
Standing here I see,
A fragment of a memory,
A gental kiss,
A heartfull hug,
I soon remember all that i miss,

I take a step back with out looking,
And I trip and fall,
All because i wasnt carefull,
All for you.depression bad
