Stupid home
I live alone in my stupid home,
It is a rikedy old house on a hill,
Metal roof and kreeky stairs,

Freaky pictures with freaky stares,
Loyal dog hate full cat,
Ugly wife and nasty rat,

Smelly rooms,
good protection,
