Life 
My life is not great,
I dont know why,
It is just that i live by fate,
Not that i hate,
cause i dont,
but i wont,
i wont go to the place,
the place you know, 
the place you go,
especially when you want to hurt,
make me feel like dirt,
it is life,
that is the reason for what life is,
if you go then you lose,
if you dont you lose,i forgot to take my zoloft today cant you tell
