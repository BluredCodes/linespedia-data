April twentith is coming soon,
Ill be passed out about noon,
Getting up early will be hard,
And so will be eating pigs lard,

Im going to get stoned,
Im going to walk around,
Im going to stumble right into april 21,

Then the fun is over,
I go home from my spot under the bridge,
And i make it to the haven of my soft warm bed.im a stoner so see what i do
