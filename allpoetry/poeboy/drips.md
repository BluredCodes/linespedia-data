I sing a song with out the words,
You look as though you dont love,
I look for the ending ,
Or the bright light at the end of the tunnel,
It is athough my head is a funnel,
Draining all that enters,
Keeping none for its own but all for its none,judge this one harshly try to find the meaning because there is one deep in my mess of letters and words.
