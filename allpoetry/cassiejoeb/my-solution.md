I can fix my problems with the blade anytime
I wear long sleeves to hide my solution

I can do my own work
I can heal myself

Your distain for my arms
I don’t think you understand
Your tears for me 
I wish you only knew

I can fix my problems with the blade anytime
I wear long sleeves to hide my solution

I shouldn’t have to hide
I don’t want to hide
She told me if I never hide
They will take me away
