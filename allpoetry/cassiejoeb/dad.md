Dad, I don’t want to hate you
Every time I hate you I hurt myself inside
I wanted you to know but you ignored me
So I just kept it all inside

Im sorry I wasn’t enough to keep you around
I’m sorry that I wasn’t as good as your new life
That I can’t measure up to everything you have
With your two step-sons and new wife

You hurt my mom, Dad,
You raped her when you came home at night
I don’t know why I believed you,
When you lied and said she was alright

I was little, Dad,
You were my life
Things changed when you left
You made us all cry

So, Dad, how is your life?
Are you doing all right?
You never needed me 
So I never needed me
And you can live without me
I had to live without you
Well, later Dad,
I’ll take your advice
I’ll try to live without myself
In turn ending my life
