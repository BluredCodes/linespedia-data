Something I do without wanting to be done
If you meet me, don't dare a glance into my eyes 
for I will hypnotize you, in a small second of fire
then feast upon your blood leaving you dry.

Stranded somewhere with your life sucked away
The last vision you see is burned in your mind
And then you slip away
I wish I could slip away

By the morning I am here, so shaky
And they tell me and it makes me
They convince me that-
It’s so nice to see fear

Creeping through this case of me
And I cry, staring at what we did
With him inside
I eat what lives
