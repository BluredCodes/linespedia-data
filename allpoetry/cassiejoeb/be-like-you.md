---
title: "Be Like You"
writer: "CassieJoeB"

Sometimes I’d like to be that way
The way you are on your facade
Where all the troubles glide away
And you’re at ease with it everyday

You’re that something
That I never knew I’ve always dreamed
And you’re that someone
That I always thought I’d never be

And all this time, here you were
Being all of my short dreams
I hated you being so fake,
But I always wanted to be like you
I dont know why I want to be like you
