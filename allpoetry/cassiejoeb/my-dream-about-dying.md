title: "My Dream About Dying"

I dreamt about dying
It was the best dream I’ve had
Since I’ve died inside
I had a dream about dying
It wasn’t even sad
I didn’t see anybody cry

A few people gathered my grave
They said I couldn’t run anymore
Said I’d died so young
I never felt so old before

My father looked very brave
There to ease the public eye
I really didn’t care
That we never said goodbye

Yeah well, they always thought I was insecure
At first they said God made me pure
Played with my fire and I got burnt
I did wrong, they called me dirt

They all talked about the life I had
Things I already knew
That I was a just child gone bad
And may God bless my soul

When they left I was alone
More alone than I ever was
And it felt so good
I hurt inside from broken trust
More hurt than I ever was
And it felt so good

When they left I was alone
More alone than I ever was
And it felt so good
