Even when Im standing still
The entire world is tilted down
Sliding into chaos heard-
Me screaming out that sacred sound

I didn’t even hear my words
I danced on tables in my head
Almost crashed a thousand times
But never fell down like they said

My mind doesn’t work so easy
It takes a little time to cry
I live inside those big closed doors
And kissed my liveliness goodbye

Slow and steady wins the race
Give the turtle fucking speed
As fast as life can take me through
Im game to pay the travel fee

Around we go on my last spin
Lets take a walk through the wall
Unlikely doesn’t make any sense
And I cant wait until I fall

Goodbye to all your fucking eyes
Watch what Im about to do
Helped me all you ever could
So I died because of you
