.
.
.
*
Oh! the persistence 
of this "unspoken desire"
which pesters me like a recurring dream
Oh! the "eternal torment" of a heart on fire
with whispers exploding 
in flames of screams ...

I shall surrender 
to  "enchanted" Silence 
to the fragrance of "jasmine" and hyaline streams
I shall follow the "journey of a raindrop"
of the "Crystal Dragon" 
with softer gleam ...

serene

z

l

g
*
.
.
