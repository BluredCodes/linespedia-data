It could have been East
my love
West; North; South
my love
It could have been Heaven
It could have been Hell
It is hard to tell
my love

It could have been yesterday
my love
today; tomorrow; never
Love 
It could have been Real
It could have been Dream
it is not what it seems
my love

But in a split second it cobbled
Love
expanse ripped from an atom
Love
the Ocean caught within rock and tide
the Earth folded in valleys; rivers;
in magnificant mountains 

and from this avalanche 
my love
three-dimensionally shaped
from Destruction and from Life
we are summoned to survive
my love

to be Sun Moon Stars
sounding a holy cornet -
fusing 
Love:

returning Home

myraOh! Going Home ...
