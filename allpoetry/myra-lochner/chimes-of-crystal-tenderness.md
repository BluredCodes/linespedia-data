Oh Love!  
I do not want to hurt you
by using the riches of your words
stitching colourful garments to hide 
my sorrowful poverty 
and deprivations …

No: 
I want to harmonize 
the deepest murmur of rivers 
crossing the heart; synchronizing stars’  
Silence in accordance with joyous 
constellations;

Sending 
the four winds 
our musical messengers
chiming crystal tenderness
in unreachable domes 
of glass;

Tuning   
fragile trembling of souls 
to the crescendo of  nebulae 
sounding passion’s perfect 
pyrosphere 

Beloved
do not leave me with lanterns
by taking away the sun of the Supreme;
you are more than Ideal or Poetry – 
you are strumming stars
you are the Axis
of Love

myra
