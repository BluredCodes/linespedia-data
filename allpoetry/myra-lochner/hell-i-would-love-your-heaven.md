"Hell, I would love your Heaven", 
he winked with a wry smile,
"and as you are a Christian
walk with me that extra mile ..."

I took his fragile fingers
all that was still untouched;
how catabolic was his burden
convictions far too much ...

"Please pray to God for mercy",
he mocked with weary words;
but in his feverish focus
I saw ... the Lord!

myraHe died ... but was forgiven ...
