You've zipped me in a file 
one day
thought I would then
forever stay
within the harddrive of your mind
muted 
to mere rooted rememberance
purified from humanity 
and sensorial sin

You've zipped me in a file
one day
thought that should keep me
from decay
within the zone far from alarm
but silenced 
under sharp surveillance
programmed for crash
and recycle bin

You've zipped me in a file
one day
But I've selected all routed
decoded and replayed
restarted and rebuilt

and with a smile
swopped characters:
converted file to Life

Day 
saved to a Disk

myraAgainst the odds ...
