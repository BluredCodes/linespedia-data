I am forever Music

the laughter of the Sun
illuminating this darkened pathway
for flowers not to stray
in their transition from seed to bloom

I am forever Music

the dewdrops of the Moon
saturating secret growth at night
to sprout evergreen sight
turning dimness to Light

I am forever Music

the glossal glide of the Star
harmonizing the cosmic voice
the heavenly Homebound
of angelic sound

Write me

myra
