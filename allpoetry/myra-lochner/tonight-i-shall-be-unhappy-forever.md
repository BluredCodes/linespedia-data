Tonight I need nothing to be happy again
nothing more than tears blotting
transparent blood on the tablecloth 
Tonight I need nothing to be happy again

Tonight I shall forever cry
for the canary which stopped its fluid sun
filling the corners of its cage
Tonight I shall forever cry

Tonight I do not want new happiness
but the sad loss of bygone hours
the silenced stride of the family clock
Tonight I do not want new happiness

Tonight I shall be unhappy forever
Tonight I need nothing to be happy again
Tonight I shall forever cry
Tonight I shall be unhappy forever
