What’s in a Name?

Who are you: 
you who dare to search my name
With acuity and agile fingertips?

Mira, it says in Spanish, when prompting  all to see
Myra, insists  the Word,  for Paul to sail with glee
Myrrh is it when it sooths the pain
Or balm temples before humification
For all mythologists I am  “my sun”

In my own mind I am carrying my Ra:
Christ the Purest Light
Who guides me on his Homeward Flight

How dare you think then you may feel me
With fallible fingertips?

I am but simply 
and insignificantly ...
me

myra
