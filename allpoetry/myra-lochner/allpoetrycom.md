home

welcome
favourite fun:
www.allpoetry.com

disseminated dreams of hope
decoded into songs and poems:
is that a diamond ... or a tear?
is that a smile; merely a sneer?

sculptured light to reform homes
castles; shacks or digit domes
added authors - new and best
im search and then forget
 
brb Soul, can't be late:
Real Life's role call
cannot wait  

myra
Place we call Home ...
