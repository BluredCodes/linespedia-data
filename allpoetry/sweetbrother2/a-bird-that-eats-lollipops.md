She complains
          it's a lovely Saturday
                   yet she's trapped behind
                                       this glowing screen;
she wants

                 F R E E D O M

she thinks
           i wish i could be 

                               a bird
                                     soaring over
rooftops,
       majestic
               sequoias
                        skyscrapers
                           jagged mountains

She would not be
              a  common household sparrow,
                graceless city pigeon, or
                 screaming seagull

feasting on worms, raw fish, or breadcrumbs

                                   She wants to be
                 a bird that eats lollipops
                                      carefree & joyful
                               like a laughing child
