They called her
               
greeneyed redheaded freak
                    sent her running home from school
                                                spilling tears

Hm, that was many long distant years ago


She is on my screen
at this moment
             emerald eyes drawing my own eyes into
                                       steamy embrace

her rolling curves 
           leer & tease
                       packed tightly in teal leather


Her crimson lips
              (curled into a perfect bow)
                                     pout at me, playfully
                                   daring me to drown in them


This is
      the greeneyed redheaded freak
                        of rainy gray Edinburgh
                                  in 1983
                        tormented by fresh-faced blond girls


                               she sings to me
                              as I gaze at her pale beauty
                             her voice is moist & heavy
                            an invitation to the lonely
                           I crawl through her swampland
                          seeking to feed on her luscious fruit
>
This is
      the gangly girl
                  who'd sit home crying
                           during school dances
                      in rainy gray Edinburgh
                                   in 1983
                       a brazen flower had yet to bloom
                           today I sit here dumbstuck
                    drunk on
                        her heady fragrance.
