Relative merits
of palm-pilots
& DVD players
are heatedly discussed
over chicken & collards

The latest products
of Hewlett-Packard, Sony & Microsoft
are earnestly debated;
the mother at my elbow
wonders why I am so quiet

Breakfast gossip I heard this morning
revolved around
parole & warrants,
petty crimes,
small acts of revenge,
the wind-chill factor
and last night's game

My dinner companions
in their cozy kitchen
discuss retail discounts on
state-of-the-art
hardware

Breakfast conmrades
chat about 
software for the mind

which I hunger for at every meal

The evening meal
concludes
with a dessert of sublime content,
full bellies and boundless TV choices

But as this morning's breakfast throng departed
the only abundance noted
was a teeming ocean
of need.
