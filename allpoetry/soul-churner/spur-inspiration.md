title: "Spur Inspiration"

Brainchild elate me,
Infect me,
Inflame me.
Install me for insight.
Institution,
Animate me.
All that I am,
Fills me.
An ordination,
I illustrate me.
Writing to what wills me,
I create,
I reveal me.
