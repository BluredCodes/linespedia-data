title: "Does negativity have a smell?"
writer: "Soul Churner"

Lingering as an odor

At odds like some disease,

Passed down from one hand to another,

Interupting all decrees.

I,

Myself,

Am often guilty

As a skeptic in my own right.

Refusing to be unenthusiastic,

An asshole who's impolite.

May this leopard change her spottage

For a brand new shiny cloak.

I'll cover this imbecile side of me

And not smell like the butt of this joke.Hey guys! I'm trying to change. If it had a fragrance, then I've been one smelly person.
