I rise up into me and out from darkness.

Peering through the shadows that talk to me,

Surprisingly,

I am not passive.

I am the strength from which I came,

Brought out of calignosity.

An apparition appearing to not be the same,

I come back to myself

As a stone once thrown from the flame.

Like water I am changeable,

I am mutable,

Needing a little nuturing to be healed.

Chaotic times are turned circumstances

Wrapped up in forces to be revealed.

Blown off course to its opportunities,

I am freewill where fate is not sealed.I was meditating while writing this. Changing my karma perhaps?
