Trying not to view you 
As another piece of ass,
I doze off to your world
For seconds of mass confusion.
Reality floats me to the surface.
I gasp for another breath,
Forsaking you behind
To your dark abyss.
Rummaging your deep waters,
Delving you for depth,
I swim up empty-handed,
As you drown in the shallowness.
