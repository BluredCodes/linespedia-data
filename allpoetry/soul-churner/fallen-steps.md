title: "Fallen Steps"

Leading to nowhere,
They climb like an endless mountain
Becoming the outcome of consequence and chance.
Interpreted like a dance 
Denouncing whose flaw it was,
Being impartial but narrow-minded;
Failure has many faces and is certain to be unkind.It is unkind, but at least we learn from it. There is a light in the darkness. We sometimes have to look hard to find it.
