The kingdoms rising all around the flowers are blooming
a beautiful sight even throught the night
the fog is nice a clear blue mist the smell of sweetness
through the air loving the serenity oooh and we all know why

The flowers are rising as the sun rises, The Faery's are waking
yawning their small arms the elves are gathering 
around to watch them them with smiles an warm hugs, 
greets of respect the drows are hiding in the darkness of the kingdom

Unseen by the light but their still serene keeping to themselves
trying to be unseen, their smiling thou through the darkness
the elves go and see them and they just turn and run, there she is
out there a lonely elf waiting for a friend

the faerys come and sit by her side but shes twice their height
they don't know whats wrong but they smile at her warmly the smiles
they go on through the day even through the night when shes not lonely
anymore they invite her to their home and friendship is made

so so nice it's so grand the master plan of her she chuckled with her
new friends she has made they don't care about anything just because
their different they don't have to run away races and colors no
discrimination ooooh this might be the end then they go to bedDon't ask I wrote it for a friend thats an elf in an RP heheeh criticize if yah want
