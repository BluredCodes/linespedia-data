Watch out walking down that path, deaths in the air notes on the rocks
better watch out before you see the cops, their coming down the path
coming for you, Asking their questions like stupid people ignorance with my luck.


They think I'm 'causin trouble, running a muck all I can do is say what the fuck, flip that damned switch set that sonic perfection ringing throughout
those ears, the ears that are attached attached to the head jesus god, 
I'm going insaneee...

The police ask me what the hell to do cuz they got lost, they say "It's up to you" what the hell I can't pass inspection I'm a soceital deseise. A Stereotypical infection cuz I look suspicious they gotta pull me over.Don't ask what it's about cuz I don't know!
