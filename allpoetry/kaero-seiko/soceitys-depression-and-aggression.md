Soceity thinks it has us in it's grasp theres no
telling how long I shall last
With this damn mediokre world I don't know I'm not a girl
Not that smart not that right I'm just putting up with this fight
I know the hell I see the soul the pain I love the shit we behold
the trauma we pose every day the newspaper media's sharades it will grab us
just wait and see it's all about different mentality,
if you can't be right if you can't fight then your just part of this world
your nothing special nothing kewl wait and survive soceity will find you.
Take you out shard your soul with your pain and all you hold. The things you long and things you see, 
just wait for the shit with this mentality, 
watch out for soceity the angers coming inline it will get you every time. No matter how long you aggress it will hit you in the chest, Watch out for media schemes the safety belt the opprutunity save your life we'll save your soul.Hmm what should I say I wonder where I went wrong if anywhere tell me criticize thats how we get better is it not?
