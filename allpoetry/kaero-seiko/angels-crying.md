The angels are crying in my mind I
 see them all around me all of them are dying their all already dead I cannot do a thing all 
I do all is watch it hurts me till 
my ears begin to bleed with ringing my ears are ringing I see the 
blood spurt oh my this hurts 
my head is hurting badly everything is hurting my skin 
my flesh my Soul all of it is 
burning my Soul my Soul is churning burning with 
desire the desire not to hate I 
see this life as Hell but you see it as Fate you see the 
nasty places you see the 
ally ways there’s more behind my mind hidden all in places 
if I decide to share be very 
flattered because your almost the only one since everything was 
shattered you see my soul right 
now you see it all come down you see it all and all you can 
do is frown nothing here 
is stolen its all from my mind but if you read it remember 
every time this is why rai 
chose me chose me to be trained then she gave up because 
I would’ant share my knowledge 
everything I do nothing, it all seems to make a collage you call 
fate so see it as your 
soul and see everything around you see it as you want see it 
as you be only thou knows the 
answer and no it isn’t me I’m very sorry you thought so 
but it really isn’t true once you 
see the light why does it turn to darkness maybe you 
should be asking the questions and 
I should be giving answersCan you hear them too? hehe just criticize it a bit don't mind What we know whats wrong makes us better.
