Together now, we mourn the loss of yet another friend. 
We drink our beers and remember all of those who have passed before.

Through the years we lost them. One by one they fell.
Now there's only us. The few, the hard, the cold. The ones that made it through. 

So, one for all and all for one. Say your premature goodbye's.
Hold their memories close to heart and never let the fallen go.
