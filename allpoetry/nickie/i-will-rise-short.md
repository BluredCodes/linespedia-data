---
title: "I will rise (short)"
writer: "Nickie"
slug: "i-will-rise-short"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56325-I-will-rise--short--by-Nickie"

You may shoot me with your words
You may cut my with your eyes
You may kill me with your hatefulness
But still, like air, I will rise.

3-31-02
