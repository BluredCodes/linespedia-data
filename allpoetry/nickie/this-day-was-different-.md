Different
I knew this day was different
There was something
It had saddness
Written all over it.

I knew this day was different
It was raining
And then hot.
Something wasn't right
But i couldn't put my finger on it
Untill i talked to you.
"Are you okay?" you said
And i knew something was up.

I knew this day was different
You came in the door
Said you were tired
But i knew that you weren't.
About to pop in a movie
And then you ask me to sit down.

I knew this day was different
"Are you okay?"
You said once more.
I look at you and say
"You're breaking up with me aren't you?"

I knew this day was different
i had it all figured out in my head
I knew that you were going to say waht you did
And i knew how i would respond
But it's a lot different when it happens.

I knew this day was different
And we sit there in tears
An hour has gone by
And you tell me why
Why your breaking up with me.

I knew this day was different
You say you don't want to hurt me
And make me promise not to hurt myself
And to that i say "i don't know"
I wish you were still here

I knew this day was different
And we can still be friends
And you say you still love me
But i still don't quite understand...Nic just broke up with me...about 3 hours ago. and this is just letting it all out so please don't be mean! *Sigh* :'(
