title: "Soarin'"
writer: "Nickie"

I can't hold on 
To what i feel inside
I feel i should be
Flyin' and soarin' high.

I have my feet down low
But my wings are up
I can reach my new height
With just a little luck.

The sun is out
I got the wind on my back
And now i'm gonna show you
The things i had lacked.

I can't hold on 
To what i feel inside
I feel i should be
Flyin' and soarin' high.

I'm ready to fly
So here i go
I see you down there waving
As i look down below.

With a twinkle in your eye
And a smile on your face
I look into the sky
And my heart begins to race.

I can't hold on 
To what i feel inside
I feel i should be
Flyin' and soarin' high.

So i couldn't hold on
To what i felt inside
I let myself go
And now i'm soarin' high.this is my FIRST song that i've EVER made! and i'm proud of it, but i have to figure out how play my guitar with it...
