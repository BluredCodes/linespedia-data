title: "For you my friend"
writer: "Nickie"

I'd give you my life
If it would make you happy again
I care about you so much
And will always till the end.

Your Beautiful,
Inside and out
Don't argue with me 
Cuz i'll win without a doubt.

Your the best friend 
I've ever had.
And for you my friend
I'd give you anything.I care about you so much miranny and you know i do!
