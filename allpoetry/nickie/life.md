---
title: "Life?"
writer: "Nickie"
slug: "life"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54670-Life--by-Nickie"
---

Life
Lust
Pain
Love
Death
Is this not what we all go through?
Sometimes pain comes after love also
But if you look that the fifth word
Death
You will see that we all die
And all the lust, pain, and love
Doesn't matter.
Death does not care
How great or bad of a person you were.
And even life does not care.
But life is what gives us our chance
To prove our love for others
And to prove that we know pain
And how to cope with it.
But no of it matters when you die.
Life
Lust
Pain
Love
DEATH!
