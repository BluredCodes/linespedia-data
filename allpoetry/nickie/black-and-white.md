Colors decieve the eyes
Colors take your attention away from life
And truth.
Colors blend in
Showing mistakes
And the mistake creates a new color
And with that new color 
Another color will be born. 
Black and white
Defined in their contrast
Like you and I.
Without all the confusion
And colors of your soul
I see you in black and white
And I see where mistakes
Have been made
Because I see the grey.
But with the grey
No color can be formed
And I can see your truth
And true form.i was bored, and i looked at a black and white picture next to me so i wrote this...I don't understand it, but this is what came out, so oh well.
