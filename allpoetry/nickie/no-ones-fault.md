It's no ones fault
I wish you could see
Things just didn't work out
And this is how it's going to be.

I know there is pain
And it's too much to bare
But there's still no reason
For the red on your skin so fair.

I wish you would listen
Both of you please
You are both GREAT people
And i say that with ease.

I wish that you could see
That what i say is true
It's time to start over
All fresh and new.

You both will get over it
This i promise to you 
Your thoughts will be of laughter
And nothing blue.

My words mean nothing
But they will over time
Your heart is mending
And so is your mind.

You both are great people
Beautiful inside and out
And i'll be there for you
No matter what it's about.

You've both helped me 
In my time of need
Now i want to return the favor
That you once so kindly did for me.

Please remember it's no one's fault!*sigh* ::sadness::
