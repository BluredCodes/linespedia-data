---
title: "Only You"
writer: "Nickie"

You are the only one
That has treated me like this.
Like I am everything to you
And that we were meant to be.
At first I was decieved,
 Thinking that you only liked me for my looks
But later discovered that you like me for me.
I saw those wonderful words
And melted.
You still don't know
How wonderful i felt
And still feel.
I wish I could say something
To make you feel the way I felt
When I saw those breath taking words.
It's been one of the BEST feelings I've ever had!
I'm sorry if i haven't made this clear,
But i feel so luck to have you!
One day you called
Just at the right time.
I was very depressed
And you did everything in your power
To stop me from hurting
And i did stop hurting.
I'm so happy that you didn't stop liking me
Even when everyone said you didn't have a chance.
And I am so happy, 
Not because you got me,
But because I have you now.
