Blue spirits come again
But maybe they never left.
My aunt sees these
And draws them on special paper.
Not to be done to loved one's
Afraid of what is there
But still I aruge with her
And she sits down to draw.
She's done mine twice now
Years in between the two
And yet they are the same
Blue spirits.
Sad and nevrous
Is the meaning.
They say the past comes back to haunt you
But my blue spirits have never left me.
After seeing them for the second time
My aunt knew they were there to stay.
And she is right
For they have never left me.
Sad and nervous
Blue spirits.my aunt claims that she sees these and she's knows the colors and the faces and she knows what they mean. so this is all true.
