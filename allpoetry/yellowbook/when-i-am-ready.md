I need something in my life that is solid and secure.
Someplace that I can escape to.
Where I can close out any worries and open up to more peaceful things.

To where my heart is always happy,
to where I can find myself.

I know now that nothing is in my hands.
I used to think I had it figured out and planned on my dreams coming true.
But it took 1 day to change my life and a memory I can never erase.

So when I'm ready to smile.
When I can enjoy things I once loved again,
my petals will open and soak in the sun for my soul to breathe.

I am counting, praying, and hoping that the day will come,
and I will be ready.Writer's Block!!!!
