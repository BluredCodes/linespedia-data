In swelling muddy water
I peek into what looks like my future.
With a simple left turn
I lost everything.

Conversations seem to bore me.
Reality is a joke.

Blue fountains of hope run livid through my mind.
Maybe today is the day I return.
But the simplicity of my being is wrecked and disguised.

Your sneering face reminds me why I am in this freakshow.
By destroying my reasons and, 
capturing my emotions invites myself to be misplaced.

I want to stetch out in sand and grasp seashells as the waves swarm over my toes.
