title: "Saw you last night..."

I am walking towards you 
I can't believe you're here
Chills from morning dew
or maybe my fear

Familiar emotion
Haven't been here in awile
Fills me inside with your deadly potion
Memories thrown in a pile

Of never having occured
Feelings blurry
Flutter like wings of a bird
Whispers of absurd

Unreal things I see
Why do you look at me so
Why don't you speak to me
Intensity running low

Stirring from down under I realize
Once again you are out of sight
Wicked part of lives
Misconstrue the light

Of you...

Why do I think of you at such times
You give me that feeling I love to hate
These thoughts are crimes
Giving me the trait

To believe it is Okay
To long for you 
I never hear you say 
Anything that is true..

I never hear you say anything anymore
I don't feel that morning dew
Walking through that door
I know it was a dream of you...
