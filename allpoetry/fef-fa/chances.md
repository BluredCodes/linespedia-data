title: "Chances"
writer: "fef fa"

Future days are so tempting to follow
Think they will fill empty time
Crushing to see they are hollow
Wasting today on the chase is a crime

Forgetting yesterday like it was never here
Not realizing it took you to tomorrow
Too busy creating fear
Too occupied with faking sorrow

All through these hours
Throwing to the wind thoughts of the end
Some invisible power towers...
...over us except we don't care what's around that bend

Curve around it while holding our breath
For the hand of time to drag us up those flights
and hope it does not bring us to the hour of our death
Instead letting us slip through that plight

After all tomorrow is another day
Another chance to tempt what fate has in store
My words will always say
"I need one day more"
