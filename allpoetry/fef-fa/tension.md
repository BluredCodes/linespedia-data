Feeling wrong
Self pity
Knowing I really have been wronged
Feeling like this 
Makes me long for worse times
At least I know now 
There would be resolution
Restitiution
Now who knows
...could go on forever 
This silence
Why should I cry
When he is the one who is silent
Feeling like the outsider 
In my own space 
It's my time
But he takes it away
I don't even try to take it back
Why...it belongs to me...
Falling deeper into this clinical classification
Sometimes you just have to give in to the dark
To get to the light
I wish he would speak
The only thing that can make this better 
Is the inevitable fight
