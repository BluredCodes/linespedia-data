Your gentle touch, so light like
Flowers floating on the breeze,
Makes my senses reel...
Your lips upon my skin, tender,
Dance across my flesh as
Fire, burning, branding, claiming...
Your hands drive doubt from my mind,
Caressing, embracing, a summer wind,
Whispering soundless desires...
I am helpless to resist, so overwhelmed,
Drunk on the heady exhiliration of
Your body pressed against mine,
Melting against me, completing me,
Filling me where I was once empty...
Mindless, delightfully inebriated by 
The feel of you, the smell of you, the taste of you
The whole universe
Spirals
In one wonderful, beautiful swirl of
Passion, emotion, sensation
Where the line between "you" and "me"
Is blurred, diminished in sharing,
As we collapse into each other's arms...
