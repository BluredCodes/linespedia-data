---
title: "Abscond"
writer: "silverael"
slug: "abscond"

You are a whispering wind that beguiles,
The coaxing devil that smiles,
The charming prince with all his wiles,
As you run away with my heart.

And I give it to you without thought,
So blissfully unaware of how I am caught,
For there is no help to be sought,
As you run away with my heart.

But you hold it so gently as you carry it away,
Opening it to the glories of a bright new day,
Protecting it from harm, come what may,
As you run away with my heart.

So you have the essence of me and all my trust,
With no walls between us, no shadows of lust --
I'll stay beside you for love, not because I must,
As you run away with my heart.
