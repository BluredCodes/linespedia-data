title: "Call Me"
writer: "silverael"

Call me 'sweetheart' one last time,
Let me take your hand and hold it in mine,
Let me kiss your lips and whisper softly
But call me, call me, one last time.

Call me 'darling' like you once would,
Don't tell me I can't love you, or if I should,
Or that it's right or wrong - just hush,
Until you call me, call me, one last time.

Call me to your side and hold me near,
Let me listen to your heart beat, so dear,
Until you send me away, away to cry,
Never again to call me, call me, one last time.
