---
title: "Fade Away"
writer: "silverael"
slug: "fade-away"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59914-Fade-Away-by-silverael"
---

I just want to fade
Do nothing more than
Become one of those
Distant shadows,
A slip of a memory
In a sea of aimless dreams.
I want to belong,
To look the same
As him, as her,
As that man on the corner,
The women in their
Designer clothes.
I want to blend in
With that high crowd
And not be asked
For my name...
Only to seem like
One of them.
I don't want to be
Recognized or even
Spoken of in
The closeness of
Others' homes...
I want to be distant,
Pull away from
Your frame of reality.
I want to go
Where your world
Overlaps hers
And her life
Meets my dreams
In the flutter of
A single heartbeat.
So tired of this game
Where I disguise myself,
Not knowing --
Never knowing --
If the looks in their eyes
Are simple disguist
Or complete indifference.
Can they see beneath
Or do they only
Skim the surface
And judge by what
They can't see?
So tired...
Hiding within,
Crying my tears
Into a single pillow
Beneath the lonely stars...
So I'll hide
Within them all,
Surrender my soul
To the pulsing
Stream of life...
Become part of everyone,
Yet completely solitaire,
And bathe in 
False serenity.
And you'll mistake my smile
For a sign of
Simple pleasure
Even as it conceals
The aching in my soul
While I fade into
The loneliness
Of the night...
