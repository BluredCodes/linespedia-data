I gave you this gift --
Which you opened and soon tossed aside.
What was it that displeased you,
That made you run away and hide?
Was the shape of my gift
Not pleasing enough to your eye?
Did its song or rhythm make you
Turn your face and sigh?
So I put the gift away,
Took and hid it out of sight.
And there it stayed throughout 
The years, the days, the lonely nights.
Perhaps it was not a great enough 
Treasure, perhaps you will learn to see,
The thing you took and tossed away
Was the very heart of me.
