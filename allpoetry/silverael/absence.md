---
title: "Absence"
writer: "silverael"
slug: "absence"

oh love, oh love, i weep for your passing,
the loss i feel so deeply in my soul,
like waves upon the shore ever crashing,
whose pain at breaking forever must grow;
and as each tear slowly slips to the ground,
makes a solemn path to join with the sea,
your voice taunts, echoes with barely a sound,
reminding of a time when it spoke to me,
and whispered softly of devotion true;
but now it has taken its warmth afar,
away from the heart and the love it knew --
i can do but dream on a single star,
       of a fire burned to ashes and left,
       as only an ember, cold and bereft.
