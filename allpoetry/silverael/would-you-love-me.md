---
title: "Would You Love Me"

If I was 
A flower
Glistening in the sun

Would you love me?

If I was
A moonbeam
Dancing in the clouds

Would you love me?

If I said
I want you
In the softest whisper

Would you love me?

If I said
I need you
In the lightest touch

Would you love me?

If I said
My soul is yours
In the warmest kiss...

Would you love me?

(Would you love me?)
