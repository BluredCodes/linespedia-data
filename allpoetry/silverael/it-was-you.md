Didn't you know that I love you?
I said it every time I smiled.
Didn't you know that I adore you?
Dear heart, it was you all the while.
It was you when you asked why
I looked as if I were in a dream.
It was you when I was dancing in your arms
And nothing was what it seemed.
It was you and so it remains...
A wish, a song, a heartbeat so true.
Each day perfection by your side,
A cloud of joy simply because, darling,
It was you.
