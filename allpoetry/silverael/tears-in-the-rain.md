title: "Tears in the Rain"
writer: "silverael"

Can't hide my tears in the rain
Can't let it soothe or wash away my pain
Or mend the sorrow that shatters my heart,
Slowly tears my mind apart --
I left you standing under the sky,
I left you standing, asking me why,
But I left you standing all the same,
Left you standing, whispering my name --
And I whisper yours, but you cannot hear
When it falls from my lips like that crystal tear
That travels down and over my cheek,
When it is caught in my throat; I cannot speak
Without the words rending my soul
Into a thousand pieces, taking their toll
As I lift my face up to the rain,
Letting it pour down my misery and pain...
