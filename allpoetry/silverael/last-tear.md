title: "Last Tear"

Say your last goodbyes,
Whisper your last endearments,
For the sun has cast
Its last warmth down on me.

Do not sigh or worry,
Just look up to the skies,
For the sun has cast
Its last warmth down on me.

Dream of me tomorrow,
Sleep peacefully, as I ever will,
For the sun has cast
Its last warmth down on me.

Hold me one last time,
Wipe away my last tear,
For the sun has cast
Its last warmth down on me.
