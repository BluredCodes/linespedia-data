Like a shadow
You were here 
You were here
and touched my face.
You felt my tears
and kissed my lips,
then you were gone 
ever so swiftly gone
gone without a trace.

I looked for you
I cried your name
I cried your name
into the darkest nights.
I waited here
and dreamt your dreams,
but you were gone
ever so swiftly gone
gone into the morning light.

You never called
You never spoke
You never spoke 
never again spoke my name,
but for in my mind
where you ever remain
though you are gone, 
ever so swiftly gone back
from whence you came.
