---
title: "I Should Hate You"
writer: "silverael"
slug: "i-should-hate-you"

I should hate you.
You shouldn't make me
Feel this way --
Shouldn't make my
Heart stop
Beating
When I think of you,
Or my world
Spin into oblivion
When you touch 
My face.
I should hate you
For taking away my
Control
My defenses
The threads that held me
Fast to reality
For taking me to
This place
Where I am duality
Trying to run
To hide
But wanting -
Needing -
To stay here in your arms
Where I feel --
Am? -- 
Safe and warm.
I should hate you
For doing this
For making me
Love you
So completely
So trustingly...
I should hate you.
But I can't.
