title: "Angel"

I let you gaze upon my face
When in shadows it was once concealed
And against revulsion I did brace
As what darkness hid, light revealed
But you did not flinch, my heart,
Nor did you cry out in disdain
But touched your hand upon my cheek
Which flushed for being so vain
The gentleness erased my fears,
Traced away the scars from long ago
And what I had once only dared to dream
Was finally upon me to know.
A chance to live without the cloak
With no need to flee or hide,
To allow life's patterns the pain surcease,
And let the doubt subside.
For this I owe my very soul for
The release from its prison strong,
And the knowledge that angels do exist,
Your touch proof of their gentle song
