Humm this was written before "Until Then I Shall Wait" and I found it so I decided to post it.
~~~

There was much waiting
For the end to come
Graduiton
Parties
The words goodbye 
Yet here at the beginning 
I already ask, how will it end?
Where then shall I go?
Perhaps I should shut up and live,
But when will that come to end?
The friendships
The youth
The joy
Will I too grow old?
Humm…..
Time will tell
So for now
Party on!!!

~~~
The poem was getting too touchy-feely  so what the heck I just gave it a cliché ending and got it over with. So there you have it.
