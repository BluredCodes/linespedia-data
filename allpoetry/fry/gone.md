---
title: "Gone"
writer: "Fry"
slug: "gone"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61074-Gone-by-Fry"
---

I look up to the sky
But not thinking you're there
I know
I realize 
That you are gone

I wish it wasn't true
I want you back here
With me
With us
But I know your are gone

I need to see you again
I need to feel you again
Your hair
Your smile
But I know you are gone

But this will never happen
And the thought slowly kills
My heart
My soul
Until I will be gone
