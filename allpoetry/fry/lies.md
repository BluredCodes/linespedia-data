Lies
We've all said them
We've all heard them
They may have hurt us
They may have been told with good intentions
But how can a lie be said and meant with care?
Love?
It can't
They may mean well
But i'ts still a lie
It is still ugly
It is still evil
And it always, usually, hurts

So why lie?
Out of embarassment?
Out of pity?
Out of fear?
Whatever the reason it's still wrong
It is still evil
Ugly
And always casts a shadow over your heart
