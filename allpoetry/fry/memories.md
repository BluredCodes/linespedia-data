I woke up this morning
With a memory in mind
A memory with me and you
Standing side by side

Then I looked into the mirror
Again in memory
Of the way your eyes filled with tears
When you fell and scraped your knee

Walking up the stairs
A memory flashed before my eyes
Of how no matter what happened
You would always manage to smile

Everyday I live my life
So full in memories
Some are good and some are bad
But at least they're of you and me

Deticated to my little brother who passed away just more than a month ago
