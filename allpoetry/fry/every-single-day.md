Every Single Day
It's like I'm living undercover
Telling lies
To watchful eyes
So the truth they won't discover

Every Single Day 
I listen to others sin
But I smile
All the while
I bury mine deep within

Every Single Day
I never speak my mind
With the fear 
That what they'll hear
Is what I feel inside

Every Single Day
I pray that death will come
And so I wait
In silent hate
