---
title: "I Once Believed"
writer: "Fry"
slug: "i-once-believed"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62456-I-Once-Believed-by-Fry"
---

I once believed in
A higher power
But I lost my faith
And I lost my way

I once belived in
Love and Peace
But I lost my brother
And I lost my way

I once believed that
I was pretty
But I lost my confidence
And lost my way

I once believed I
Would make it in life
But I lost everything
And lost my way
