Sin
It's all around us
In the ground we walk on
In the air we breath
In everyone we meet

But what do we count as Sin?
A sexsist comment?
A pair of eyes running up and down your body?
Wanting something with a great passion?

We seem to be unable to escape Sin
It is in our homes
It is in our stores
And it is in our church

Where do we have to run?
People say "Pray to God and you will be saved"
But praying gives no feedback
Your mind just makes a feeling up so you can sleep at night

We have no where to run
No where to go
It wraps around you like a snake
Sufforcating
Squeezing until you snap
Until you lose control
And lose your way
