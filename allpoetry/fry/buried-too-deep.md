---
title: "Buried Too Deep"
writer: "Fry"
slug: "buried-too-deep"
source: "AllPoetry"

I wish I could tell you
The way I feel
But my feelings are 
Buried Too Deep

I want to ask you
For your help
But my hurting is
Buried too Deep

I need to tell you
What is happening
But my courage is
Buried Too Deep

I need to open
And expand my mind
But my thoughts are
Buried Too Deep

I need to fight the battle
Within myself
But my strength is
Buried Too Deep

I hope some day 
I could ask for help
But my voice is
Buried Too Deep

Buried Too Deep 
In sorrow
Buried Too Deep
In regret
Buried To Deep 
In the memories
Of the things 
I will never forget
