title: "Lost In Reality"

Lost in reality with you,
That's where I want to be.
With one glimpse into your eyes,
Love is all I see.

When I look into your eyes,
I am blinded by your love.
I know that you were truly meant for me,
WHen I feel your gentle touch.

With one glimpse into your soul,
I can see all the good that's there.
When your body's close to me,
There's nothing that I fear.

When I'm taken into your arms,
I am guided into safety.
Nothing could ever harm me there,
Because I have you to protect me.

With one kiss of your lips,
I melt by your touch.
Lost inside the moment,
Our hearts beat as one.

Lost in reality with you,
That's all I eer dream.
To live inside your loving arms,
That's all I ever need.
