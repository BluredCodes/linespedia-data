---
title: "Close to Your Heart"
writer: "meganmb2004"
slug: "close-to-your-heart"

I've never had this feeling,
The feeling that you give to me.
When I'm with you I know
That as long as we're like this I'm happy.

I want to live in your arms
Where I can forever feel safe.
I want to stay there for eternity,
Where there's nothing to make my heart ache.

I want to listen to your voice
Always telling me of the love you feel.
I never thought in my greatest dreams
That a love like this could be real.

Look into my eyes and smile,
So I know you feel the same way too.
I pray that you'll always love me
Just as I will always love you.

And know that whatever obstacle comes along
We'll get through it together.
We can get through anything that comes,
As long as we have eachother.

As long as I have you
I have everything I'll ever need.
Just hold me closely to your heart,
And promise to never let go of me.
