title: "I am Not a Child"

I am not a child.
I've felt the pain of loss.
The feeling of one-hundred knives piercing my hert.
The feeling of loosing someone who meant so much.
I've felt the same pain you've felt.
I am not a child.

I am not a child.
I know the difference between right and wrong.
I've made decisions on my own.
I have began to for the rest of my life.
Just as you have done.
I am not a child.

I am not a child.
So let me experience life.
I no longer need your restraints.
I need to enter the real world.
The same world you once entered.
I am not a child.

I am not a child.
So allow me to grow up now.
There's nothing more you could do for me
besides push me farther away
while you try and keep me close.
I am not a child.
