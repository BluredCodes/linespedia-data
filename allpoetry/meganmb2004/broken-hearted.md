Has it been a mistake?
All the I love you's I've said,
All the time I've used on you,
All the tears I've cried for you,
Has it been for nothing?
For nothing but a broken heart,
And a hundred sorrowful thoughts?

Has it been a mistake?
Saying you want to be with me,
Saying you never want to let me go,
Saying I'm your everything,
Have they all been lies?
And will I be left with nothing,
Nothing but pain and tears?

Has it been a mistake?
Agreeing to be with you,
Wasting all this time with you,
Even writing these words for you?
Please tell me no,
So I can go back to being happy,
And continue to feel the love I feel for you.
