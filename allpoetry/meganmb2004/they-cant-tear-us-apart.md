---
title: "They Can't Tear Us Apart"
writer: "meganmb2004"
slug: "they-cant-tear-us-apart"
source: "AllPoetry"

Who cares what they say?
They don't feel the love we feel,
And they don't know the pain I have
When you're not around.
They can't tear us apart.

As long as the stars twinkle,
And as long as I can see them in your eyes
I am not going to let you go.
They can't stop us from loving eachother.
They can't tear us apart.

TheY don't know that the love we share
is deeper than the ocean,
And that our thoughts of eachother
are never far away.
They can't tear us apart.

They can't see that I'm not leaving you,
And that what they think
Can't change the way we feel.
You're going to be mine forever.
They can't tear us apart.
