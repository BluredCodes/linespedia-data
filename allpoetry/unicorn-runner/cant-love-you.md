---
title: "Can't Love You"
writer: "Unicorn Runner"
slug: "cant-love-you"

I can't love you anymore
for I have found someone new.
I'm not going to let you mess it up, 
I'm not going to let myself fall for you.

You've had your chance for the longest time,
months and months at that.
I've given up on us being together
and I'm taking my heart back.

You are one of the closest people
that I hold in my heart.
But I'm keeping your love locked away
and I'm looking for a new start.

I know you loved me, as I loved you
but it just wasn't enough.
So I'm going to get over you
and I won't fall for you again.

I can't love you anymore
because I have found someone new.
So this is my last good-bye
as I lie and tell you I don't love you anymore.
