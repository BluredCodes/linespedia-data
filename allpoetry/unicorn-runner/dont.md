Please don't tell me you love me. 
Please don't tell me you care.
All those things you say to me
are just meaningless breathes of air.

Don't say the words that you don't know.
Don't mess with my mind.
Don't play with my heart.
Because all you will find
is our friendship falling apart.

You had to chance the chance to lie to me
You had the chance to make it work.
But now you and me can't be,
for I have someone else.

PLease don't tell me you love me
please don't tell me you care.
Please don't tell me lies,
because it's just not fair.

You can't me when you can't have me,
love me when I don't need it.
But I'll always love you.
so don't tell me you love me
and that you truly care.
BEcause I'll believe you and fall again,
and I can't do back there.
