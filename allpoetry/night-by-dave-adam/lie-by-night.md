A long time ago, (about 30 years)
	when Christmas was like on the cards,
	a child grew up in a hot southern town,
	and had never seen snow in his yard.
	The children from there were told every year
	that Santa would land on the beach
	touch down past the dunes on the north end of town
	so distant and so out of reach.
	But, now back to the boy, that lived in the south,
	that this song is really about,
	he had in his mind that this all was a lie, and
	"This Christmas I'm gonna' find out!"
	It didn't seem real that some Santa concealed
	Would make these rounds every year,
	this boy was not buying, he knew they were lying,
	and tomorrow it all would be clear.
	An ambush of sorts thought up by a child
	in an attempt to get to the truth,
	while some content to buy this lie
	He had his plan for the roof.
	His flashlight was ready and so was he
	as he waited the hall light out
	He had in his mind that this all was a lie, and
	"This Christmas I'm gonna' find out!"
	He was so nimble, he was so brave
	as he made his way on the roof,
	while some content to buy this lie,
	This child was out for "The Truth"
	I guess he found out before he died
	there is no Santa Claus.
	He hit the ground in his pajamas
	his light fixed on the stars.
