title: "Aftermath (clabo with kerri)"

In the middle, 
Frightening souls awakened in the darkness,
To lash the endless curves of spirling meltdown,
Disturbed by deviltry lacking emotions.
They crave the taste of blood and 
create poisonous potions.
Awaken for one reason to show their wrath,
not a living thing will be left in the aftermath
