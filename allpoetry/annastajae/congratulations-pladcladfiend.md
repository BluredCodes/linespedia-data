Congratulations,
i am happy that you have been 
chosen to still the souls of 
hundreds of high school students!

make me proud, fingers
but don't still my soul with your beautiful shots!

My friend you have a talent..that she obviously saw in you,and not in me! 
i obviously did not have what she was looking for.
i am not disappointed that i did not make it!
i am happy that you did!plad, just made the yearbook staff as a photographer for next year...i am proud of him!
