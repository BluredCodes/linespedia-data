"Let me get this strait you like each other enough
to share in the magic of making love,
but you don't like each other enough to make
any attachments"

Oh, but you don't understand my friend
I would give anything to have that attachment.

I have longed for that attachment,
See i just don't have the guts to talk to him about it.

I have spilled my soul out to him,
but this seem to be the only thing
I can't share with him this fear of telling him 
what i feel is unbarable!

It is crule torcher on my heart!*sigh* this is kind of a conversation i had with on of my friends (pladcladfiend..go read his stuff) only i sat there not saying a word feeling stupid as i always do>>>> i just wish i had the guts to say this to the guy...i just pray he doesn't read it!
