All alone in my car
so far from home
I gaze at the tree's 
as I take every sharp turn (there's alot).
I watch the rain hit
the leaves and drip off
onto the ground
like small waterfalls 

I begin to drift into 
my own world...

My hand clinches the wheel...
as I lose control of all
My mind,
My heart,
and
My car

My car goes down the side of a small cliff.

and I am now in pain,
Pain of losing my mind,
Pain of losing my heart,
Pain of losing my life!

But I wouldn't be in this pain
if I would have just told you how I felt.

I wouldn't have felt that the only thing that
would take this pain was for me to
