Character is what you
are in the dark

soul,
spirt,
courage,
resolution,
intellect,
intelligence,
and
mind

NONE of which you have
you lack all,
you egocentric bastard!

Don't say a word
for once listen to what i have to say
listen to what i feel
listen to my fears.

Why won't you just listen?
damn you,listen to me!*sigh* yes i can be evil and mean, too
