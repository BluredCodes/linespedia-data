Think about it genus,
If you made me so happy,
Then why was i so sad?

I didn't really love you,
I was just wanting you to think i did,
So i could have someone to say that i loved, 
Even though i truely didn't!

What a little manipulating bitch i am!

Yes that's correct i NEVER loved you and i NEVER will, 
You were just someone to have sex with,
Someone to use!

What a little manipulating bitch i am!

I let you get attached and, 
Then i ripped out your heart and tore it to shreds!

What a little MANIPULATING BITCH iam!i never did this...this is not ture...i am not that mean...it was just the kind of mood i was in
