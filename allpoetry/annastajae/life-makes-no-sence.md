"Life makes no sence!
Can you make the confusion go away? 
If you can't, then who can..."

Oh my dear little sister, 
I am good, but i am not that good!
The confusion of life will always be there,
Even if i could solve your problems.

Oh my dear little sister,
Only if i were a magician, 
I would make all things easier for you,
and the world,
but i am not a magician,
i am just a girl who wants the confusion
for you to vanish!this was written for my sis!
