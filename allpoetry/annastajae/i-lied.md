I lied
I am sorry, that i lied

I've always sucked at lying 
to you (acutally to everyone)

I lied when i said i wasn't disappointed
when i didn't make it.
If i wasn't disappointed i wouldn't be 
sitting here crying and writing this!

See this was the one thing i acutally tought
I was some what good at and i was rejected!!!

I wish i had the talent that you do,
but I guess my talent will never hold up to yours!this is to pladcladfiend...i am sorry i suck at lying!!!
