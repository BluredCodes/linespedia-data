Must you watch over me like
you would a 2 year-old?
Why do you watch my everymove?

Mom, i am not 2 anymore.
I am 16 I know what 
I am doing (most of the time)
You don't have to protect me 
from eveything...you can't, 
You can't protect me all the time
I will make my own desions 
mistakes or not...
let me make it ON MY OWN!!!

I've been through alot in my life 
with deaths, and health problem, but
you can not continue to treat me
like I am 2.ERRRRRRR...GO AWAY LADY!!!! *sigh* that felt good to get out!
