title: "Die zany die!!!"
writer: "annastajae"

DIE ZANY DIE
you wicked evil clown,
go away,
get out of my mind.
DIE ZANY DIE
leave my thoughts,
you stupid clown,
get out of my mind
DIE ZANY DIEANYONE who knows me knows i am scared to death of tradionaly masked clowns
