---
title: "what do you want?"
writer: "annastajae"
slug: "what-do-you-want"

i am going to ask you right here right now, 
what do you want to do?
where do you want to go with our relationship? 
we are at a stand still, 
and i hate it 

i hate the pain of not know...
not knowing what's next..
not knowing when i am going to see you next
or even when you are going to call next!

i hate to say it (because i am so stubern),
but i care...
i care alot!
about you
about our relationship and what's going to happen with it!

i care about what you want out of this all!
do you want a real relationship or 
do you just want someone to "have sex" with!
i couldn't begin to even imagine what you will say
when you read this that is if you even do which i hope and 
pray you DON'T because i am to scared to hear what you have to say!yeah yeah this is crappy i know i just had a thought and i just typed it!
