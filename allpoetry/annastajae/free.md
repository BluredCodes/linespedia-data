Standing all alone
In a meadow
Surrounded by daisies
Feeling free

Free to love,
Free to hate,
Free to CRY, 
and to let my emotions
RUNFREE!
unlike I've ever been able to do.

What brought about this need to be free?

Could it be the meadow?
Could it be the love in my life?
OR 
Could it be my love for life?hmmmm..don't ask!
