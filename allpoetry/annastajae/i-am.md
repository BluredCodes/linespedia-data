The words "i am.." are potent words; 
be careful what you hitch them to.
The thing you are claming has away of 
reaching back and claming you....

I AM just a scared heart!

I AM scared of love
I AM scared of hate
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
I AM scared of life
I AM scared of death
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
I AM scared of heart
I AM scred of mind
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
I AM scared of normal
I AM scared of insane

I AM just a scared heart!
