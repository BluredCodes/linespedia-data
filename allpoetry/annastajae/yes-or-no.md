---
title: "Yes or No"
writer: "annastajae"
slug: "yes-or-no"
source: "AllPoetry"

Why can't you just ask God what you are suppose to do, 

and have him give you a simple yes or no answer?

I know in his own way he does, 

but you still have to figure it out on your own...in a way!

Why can't it be simple?

Why does everything have to be so complicated?

YES or NO...why can' it be that simple?the idea for this was from a friend (my sis) who asked me "why can't god answer yes or no?"
