When you fell in love with her
you left me and everyone behined...
why is that?

When you call to talk to everyone...
why don't you ever talk to me?

When you come home to 
spend time with everyone...
why don't you ever spend
time with me?

You know i am your family, too
I am your baby sister,
the lost forgotten one!*sighhhhhhhhhhhhhhh*
