i gotta pee...someone help what do i do?
i gotta pee so bad,
but if i get up i will pee on myself!
and that will make a big icky mess,
that i would have to clean up! (ewwww)

DEATHY!!!! help me! CWM?? REPO?? 
ANYONE! 

fine i will just sit here and dance around in my chair,
until i figure out what to do,
and i will sing the lucky charms commercial to get my mind off of it!

HEARTS, STARS, HORSESHOES (i gotta pee), CLOVER AND BLUE MOONS, (i really gotta pee), POTS OF GOLDEN RAINBOW, AND ME RED BALLOON...i really hope that's not a water balloon!!! oh i gotta pee!!!grrrrrr!!!!!
