"She won't live past the hour"
~*~
"She won't live past the day"
~*~
"She won't live past the week"
~*~
"She won't live past the month"
~*~
"She won't live past the year"
~*~
"She won't live to be 16"
~*~*~*~
HA that shows how much you truely know!

I am a fighter!
I have been fighting my whole life!
an NOW i can finally say you are wrong

I am 16 and I AM STILL LIVING!!wooowooowooo i don't give up that easy!
