title: "Videotape Lover"

As i sit on my bed all alone,
With my camera in hand,
I repeatedly play that split second shot of us.
That shot we tried so hard to hide from, is the only thing keeping me saine

When you said you were mad and left without saying goodbye.
I knew i would have to resort to my camera once more playing
"NOW SHE'S TAPING"
Over and over again, just to see your face and to hear your laugh!

"NOW SHE'S TAPING"
"NOW SHE'S TAPING"
"NOW SHE'S TAPING"
