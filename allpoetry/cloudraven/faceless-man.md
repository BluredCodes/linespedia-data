Am I human I scream? 
Your no longer the faceless man, 
but I am still the peasant under your feet. 
I stand with arms wide open and you push me away. 
My anxiety grows inside as I prays for some signs. 
I have made my own prison. 
I have chosen my sacrifices. 
I ask you who's got my back? 
You said you would stand here with me, but were are you? 
You cannot just wash away all those years. 
You think you can never die, I fear my immortality. 
Was it just an illusion that you loved me? 
What's life for if you are torn from me? 
You cannot hide love, it is inside us all. 
Am I not a human to you any more, 
now that you are no longer the faceless man
