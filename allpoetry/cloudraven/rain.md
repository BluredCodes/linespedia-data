title: "Rain"
writer: "CloudRaven"

The roof top glistened with silver drops
of water as the rain tumbled.

The bare trees turned a dark brown
and the ground drank as fast as it could.

The wind pushed the tress up and back,
as the cars went down the wet street.

The window pane was covered with
little drops of water.

When I look out at this majestic scene
my own tears plummet as I think of you.

No one could hear me cry
over the cry of the wind.

No one could see my tears
over the rain as it falls.

From out of my window I could
see all and hear all.

My love seemed to burn in my heart,
as I thought of our parting.

The rain fell harder, and so did my tears,
my love was gone and so was my sunshine.
