---
title: "Chained"
writer: "CloudRaven"

I’m chained to the wall of remembrance.
Forced to live each day
recalling the evils of yesteryears.
The blood of the innocence and
the tears of children mingle together.
As the woes of the world rest on my shoulders.
Sometimes I pray for this day to be my last.
But ever morning I awake to the pictures of
horrors flashing through my mind.
I know that I can not forget,
the tattooed numbers will not let me.
I must retell the stories.
I will not let all the suffering
that happen go unheard.
I will not allow the horrors to happen again.
I will remember all the faces,
all the names of the people who no longer
have a voice to retell the unimaginable events.
I am chained to the wall of remembrance
Because I can not and will not break the chains.
It will not happen again!
