---
title: "Stone The Crow"
writer: "CloudRaven"

The road stretches in front 
the sun beats down and the crow laughs 
the corn fields surround me 
the sun burns my skin 
and the crow laughs, mocks me 
every step I take he follows 
hopping from fence post to fence post 
starring at me, laughing at me 
I ignore him and keep walking 
hoping for civilizations around the next bend 
but there he is again,laughing at me 
each step I take he mocks me 
my feet are so heavy as he 
gracefully fly around me head 
laughing at me, teasing me of my misery 
I ignore him as his laughing echoes in my head 
he sits on the post as if he is king of the world 
well your going down bird I scream 
as I chuck the stone at him, 
I miss and hit some corn 
he just laughs so more 
that's it, I can't take no more as 
I throw stone after stone at this devil of a crow 
I hit him and he is down for the count, 
teach him to laugh at me 
as I continue down this deserted road.....
