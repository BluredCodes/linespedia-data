title: "Ink blots"
writer: "CloudRaven"

Look deep at the card and tell me what you see.
I stare and continplate the question,
but try as I may all I see is spilled ink on paper.
Do you not see a butterfly?
Well, sorry no.
Can you not see a face?
I look again, but all I see is ink spilled on paper.
Concentrate he screams at me.
I think the vein on his neck will pop.
I look again, get a little closer, close one eye, squint, still nothing.
Just spilled ink on paper.
I look at him, see the vein on his neck, decide to lie.
Oh, I say, now I see, It's a butterfly.
Ah, he says, vein deflates, jots in his pad
and the shows me the next ink spilled on paper.
