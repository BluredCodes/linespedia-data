title: "Giant Purple Flying Bunny"

Wake up in a tree, 
wonder how I got here, oh well. 
Trip and fall, guess I had one too many last night. 
Really hate being a freak. 
I mean not a bird, not a bunny, 
just a giant purple flying bunny. 
Tried the circus for a while, 
it was fun, just hated being laughed at. 
Don't they think I have feelings. 
Not just fur with wings. 
So now I just wonder, 
walk for miles with no wear to go. 
Wonder if there are more like me, doubt it though. 
Never seen a flying bunny before. 
Never seen a 6 foot bunny before. 
Never seen a purple bunny before. 
People don't know what to do when I hop through town. 
Flying I am not too good out, 
well good at the flying, bad at the landing. 
So I just wonder and wonder. 
Hoping to find someone else like me, 
another freak to share this miserable life with.challenge poem
