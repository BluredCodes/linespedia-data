title: "Judgment Day"
writer: "CloudRaven"

A star twinkles in the dark sky of despair.
My shattered life lays in front of me like a morbid jigsaw puzzle,
of broken relationships and fragmented memories.
I look for the silver lining and only find tin foil.
The fog thickens in my mind as I try to focus.
The smell of the baby powder tickles my nose as I remember the event,
the thing that changed it all for me.
As I cry bloody tears for the pain is real and the reality scares me.
As the cry in the dark frightens me and
I am face to face with my mistake.
And now my judgment day has arrived as the star fades and
then disappear into the despair.Just read a news article and the poem came to me.....
