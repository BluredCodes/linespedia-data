Were the hell did it go?
It was here a minute ago.
Did I hit the delete button?
Man, no way, shit!
Can't be gone....
lost in the web of my computer.
Spit it back out, damn you!
I need that file,
I didn't mean to hit the delate button.
Shit I need it back.
I hate this machine,
always losing my files,
eating them up and never giving them back.
Don't remember hitting the delete button,
why can't you find the file.
It was here a minute ago.
Shit I really need it now!
Hate this machine!
