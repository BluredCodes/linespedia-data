Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow

Yesterday, I saw in a dream.
A misty realization of life,
of time gone by,
of lost loves and forgotten memories.

Today, hit me in the face,
with pressures and stress,
with due dates and bills.

As I sit quietly tomorrow comes,
with it’s wish of a future,
of newly discovered things,
of new loves and new dreams.

I lived through Yesterday.
I made it through Today,
and I dream of Tomorrow.1998,
