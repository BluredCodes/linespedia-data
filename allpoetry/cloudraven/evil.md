What do you call something that is pure evil.
Something with no conscience or soul.

The kind of evil that chills you to the bone.
Ice cold eyes and a heart of stone.

What do you name the kind of evil that
Rips your heart out from your still warm body?

I know what I would name it.
I would name it you!
