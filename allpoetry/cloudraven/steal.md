I could feel the cold steal
              against my head.

I could taste the sweat as it
               ran down my face.

I want to scream, but know
               I can't.

I want to run, but I have no 
              place to go.

I close my eyes tightly.

I can taste the blood as I 
              bite my lip.

My finger tightens as I pull
               the trigger as
                  I pray for forgiveness.....
