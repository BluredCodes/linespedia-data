OK, here is the deal 
the other day I walking home 
had a drink or two, maybe three 

anyway I'm walking home and see this dog 
cute little dog by the side of the road 
I mean it is a beagle, not a pit bull 

so I go over to the dog 
thinking he is lost or hurt 
don't know what I was thinking really 
he was a cute little dog, 

so I go to pet it and all of a sudden BAM 
DR Jekyll into Mr. Hyde 
This beagle has a set of choppers 

Dracula had nothing on this dog 
he comes tear at me, 
I fall on my ass, 
scramble to me feet and make a mad dash 

the little beagle looks more like a pit bull now 
he is catching up and all I can see is his enormous teeth 
thinking he is going to rip me apart 

so I keep running and he keeps chasing 
Cujo comes to mind as I turn the corner 
slam into the cop and look dazed 
he grabs me by the collar 

he wants to know the problem 
I tell him abut the beagle from Hell, 
but he doesn't believe me, 
guess is the alcohol breath 

so he gives me a stern warning 
and sends me on my way 
but I know for sure that there was a 
mad beagle with chomps from hell 
chasing me last nightThis was a challenge poem, it actually one, can you belive that. Wait don't answer that, LOL
