---
title: "Earth and Heaven"
writer: "CloudRaven"

---

Earth is filled with sorrow
that mortal men must face.
Hearts are wounded and mended.
Lives ruined and found.
Earth is the battleground
for hate and despair.
Everywhere you turn you see misery.
Pain on the faces of the old and
fear on the faces of the youth.
And yet there is hope.
Hope when all else fails.
Hope grows in the grayest of days.
As we look up and pray,
we ask for solutions to our problems
and answers to our wishes.
For nothing is so horrible
that Heaven can not heal.
