---
title: "The Light"
writer: "CloudRaven"
slug: "the-light"

---

I'm glad you have found the light
just don't shine it in my eyes.

It's nice that you found religion,
but don't shove it down my throat.

Don't push your beliefs on me.
Live your life your way
and I'll live mine my way.

Stop preaching the sins of the world
and the evil of my destruction.
Because I don't care!

I'll do what I want and you can't
make me do what you want me to do.

So get the light out of my eyes 
and get on your way.

Go save someone else because this
soul doesn't need saving.
