title: "On Bended Knee"

I see a room full of people
on knees begging to a 
G*d they have never seen.

They believe stories so
unreasonable and irrational.

They drink of the blood of their
so called savior and eat of the body.

I watch in silence as they pray for
forgiveness to a faceless, voiceless,
image of their imagination.

I laugh to myself for I knew better.

I am alone in this world and no force
out there can save my soul.I wrote this because of all the horror stories going on in the churches today. Not anti-G*D, just sick of religion.
