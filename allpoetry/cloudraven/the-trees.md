The trees surround me.
The envelope me in darkness.
They encircle me in fear.
I am trapped, no way out.
I run for freedom.
I see no escape.
I shout for help.
If you cry and no one hears you,
Did you cry at all?
I hear a sound in the distance.
I run to it.
I see a light.
I yell to it.
I trip and fall.
I jump with a deafening buzzing sound.
I’m awake I scream.
I open my eyes to see the trees.
This wasn’t a dream after all.
I scream, but no one hears me.
