They say your not real, are you? 
They say that this is reality, could it be? 
They say they are not after me, 
but I heard the whispers. 
They say to take the drugs, I'll feel better. 
You say they are trying to kill me. 
You scream at me to watch out, 
they say to ignore you. 
I want you all to leave me alone. 
GO AWAY NOW! 
I can't think, 
I can't breath. 
I need space. 
You say, they say. 
I saw an alien once, 
that is what I saw, you believe me. 
They said it was a hallucination. 
I saw the men in the black car try to kill me. 
You told me to run. 
They said it was my mind playing tricks. 
They say I am paranoia, 
I am NOT paranoia. 
I am not insane. 
I want to be alone. 
GO AWAY NOW!!! 
Leave me alone! 
I don't want to talk to you, 
I don't want to talk to them. 
I want silence. 
SILENCE I said. 
STOP....talking. 
I am not insane. 
I am not paranoia. 
I don't need drugs. 
I just need silence. 
Silence, silence, silence 
I just need a little peace...... 

I am not insane.
