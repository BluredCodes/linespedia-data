Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
      Time rushes on
Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
  Minutes fly by.
Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
  Hours pass.
Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
  Someone is being born.
Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
  Someone is dying.
Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
  Someone is falling out of love.
Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
  Someone is falling in love.
Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
  Someone is having sex for the first time.
Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
  Someone is having unprotected sex.
Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
  Someone is dying they just don’t know it yet.
Tick, tock
      Tick, tock
  Time passes on.Think it needs work, any help would be appreciated.
