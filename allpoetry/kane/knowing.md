---
title: "KnoWinG"
writer: "Kane"
slug: "knowing"
source: "AllPoetry"

KnoWinG THe TiMe iS  NeaR,
KnoWinG We'LL hAvE sO MucH To FeaR,
We KnoW,
AnD StiLL We WanT WaR,
We Know,
We stiLL HaVe So MucH tO RePenT FoR,
We KnoW,
No Better,
It WaS ALL iN thE ProMiSe
KnoWinG tHe TiMe Is At HanD
