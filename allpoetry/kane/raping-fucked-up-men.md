The thought of Raping a MaN,
Who Has Done Me Wrong,
Turns My Cock from Short And Thin,
To thick And LonG,
To rip his Asshole and Then Laugh in his Face,
Will give him an over whelming feeling of Disgrace,
To violate him, hurt Him, humiliate Him,
His Pain Will never be over,
Especially if you catch him when he's sober,
Rape him with the Barrel of Shot Gun,
Letting Him know your not doing it out of anger,
But simply cause it's Fun,
Make a Video tape,
Send It to Family and friends,
And I promise the Humiliation Never Ends,
What's He to Do But Kill Himself,
What's He to Do But Cry,
Any Man unless he's a Fag,
Would Much Rather Die,
A Painful Death For All the Fucked Up things He's Done,
There's Nothing worse than being penatrated, 
with a loaded Gun,
But only Fucked Up Men Should Go Out This Way,
Some Rapest, 
All Child Molesters,
Should Pay
