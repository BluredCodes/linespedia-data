title: "My Sickness"

I Have A sickness,
That causes me to look at life 
in a fucked up Veiw,
I've Tried to Cure Myself,
But cureing myself is something 
I just can not Do,
So I'll Just Have to live with it,
Try My Hardest To Deal with this Shit,
And Live My Life Day To Day,
Trying To Find Time,
To Dream, To Pray,
I can't Get rid of the Voices,
Or the Confusion in My Head,
It seems like the Only Way I can Ease My Mind,
Is If I make Myself DeaD,
But it's gonna be some time before I die,
Until then I'm Going to Keep Bitching to The Lord,
Oh Why? Oh Why? Oh Why?
