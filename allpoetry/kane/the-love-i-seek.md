---
title: "The Love I Seek"
writer: "Kane"
slug: "the-love-i-seek"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55365-The-Love-I-Seek-by-Kane"
---

The Love I Seek,
Is the Kind That Will Inspire Me,
Motivate Me,
Give Me Reason to live on,
But I Quit,
I'm Finished,
Almost All My Hope Is Gone,
I Make Love,
but Never recieving it,
I lose Money,
But Never retrieving it,
Now The Love I Seek,
Is the kind from the Creator Above,
But I'm A BAD MaN,
I may Never Recieve His Love,
I make Righteous Choices in some cases,
and In other cases I Commit Wicked Deeds,
My flesh rots
and my soul bleeds,
The Love I seek,
Will Mend My wounded Soul,
A woman to heal me,
To Love Me Whole,
But I could Just be Crazy,
In believing that Kind of Love really exist,
Could I be Wasting My time in hopeing,
Should I Just Slit My Wrist,
No Not Yet,
I'm Going to hold off on that
and Instead Smoke A Bowl,
Maybe forget for a little while,
That I have a Wounded Soul
