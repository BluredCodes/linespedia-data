It's 4-20,
And I It Feels Like It's the 4th of July,
Today All I'm gonna do is just Get High,
It's 4-20,
Today Just Grab a Joint And Smoke Your Stresses away,
That's My Plan For the entire Day,
It's 4-20,
And I'm anticipating something bad to Occur,
And if shit Goes Down, My Mind will be in a blur,
It's 4-20,
And Nothing Will Fuck this Day Up For Me,
I'm Gonna Go Downtown and be another busy Bee,
It's 4-20,
And I'm Gonna Be Busy Smoking WeeD,
An nobody can stop me,
Because I and Others Have the NeeD,
