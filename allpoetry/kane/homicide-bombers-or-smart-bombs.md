Are they Suicide Bombers, 
Homicide Bombers,
Or are they Smart Bombs,
Well they would be Smart bombs,
If they Didn't seekout innocent Prey,
If they actually had a stradigy,
In a time of war they could,
Save the Day,
We Drop Bombs From Miles Away,
And We miss Creating Many innocent Casualties,
So if you ask Me I think Smart Bombs Should Be here to Stay,
Just develope new tactics,
Take out Groups of enemy soldiers in a single blow,
As more and More Smart bombs are Produced,
I think The terror Will Shrink instead of Grow,
Imagine a Bomb that is Dedicated into hunting you Down,


I see the Idea you have, I know where your comming from, 
But I think you need to think about this more then revise this shit.
