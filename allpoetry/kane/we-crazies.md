The Crazy FeeD off the other Crazies,
Craziness is just as contagious as anger or hate,
Dellusions from being Crazy,
convince me to think my craziness is my Fate,
When Voices,Visions, miraculous events and coincidences take place,
I find myself seeking some of GoDs Good Grace,
Being crazy helped me find the Lord,
Being crazy kept me from becoming bored,
I once thought religion was all about mass control,
Now to I know to Love the Lord is my only Goal,
But will I be Excepted by him?
I'm a sinner, Not a Saint,
I continue to confuse myself until I feel faint,
I've excepted the fact that I'm crazy,
I've excepted the fact that I like to Sin,
But likeing to sin will never become loving to sin,
Because I hold Jesus the Lord Deep With in,
I'm a crazy man who loves Jesus,
Really Crazy I've been told,
Now I sit back and listen to the other Crazies,
who like speaking of the BiBle and the Days of Old,
But I Could Never Become Like them, A preacher,
That's a Kind of Crazy way too advanced for me to comprehend,
but no matter how crazy we are we'll all will know Jesus in the End,
I'm Crazy for the Lord
