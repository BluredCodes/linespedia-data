A Terrorist Attack on 4-20
Would Really Fuck up Our Nations Youth,
Osama Bin Laden,
Would Be just as Forgotten,
As John Wilks Booth,
4-20 is a Day that's already know all too well,
Weed, Colubine,
And Hitlers Arrival to Earth, From Hell,
A Terrorist attack on 4-20,
I think Would Create Chaos,
Like We've Never Seen,
But I won't Give two shits about it,
Because I'm A weed Fean,
And on That Day,
I'm Gonna Be Sure to Be High,
And I'll Sit back while I'm stoned 
and watch the Day, Fly By
