Everyone thinks their so Smart,
Everyone thinks they know it all,
And Because of these two things, 
Were Bound to Fall,
Fall straight to the Ground,
And our Children will have to build up their lives,
From the Nonsense We Pass Down,
Let's just Face the Facts,
We Don't Really Know Shit,
As Much as we try to UnderStand,
We just Don't Get It,
Only when it's Over,
Will GoD then Help us Understand,
Only when the Promise is fofilled,
Will GoD lend Us a helping Hand,
Until then we Need to Have Faith,
That the SoN of MaN,
will Come Once Again,
And save our pathetic asses,
From Our Fucking SiN,
Everyone Gots Something to Say,
So Say it,
Just don't Fool yourselves,
By thinking you really Know,
Just remember one day you will have to reap,
Everything you sow
