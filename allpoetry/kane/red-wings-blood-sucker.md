We Who Have Earned Our ReD Wings,
Are With Out A doubt the True Kings,
Of knowing what a Woman Wants,
I must admit that at first I shy,
About Pulling the Bloody Tampons From Their Cunts,
The Acidic Taste of Blood Trickleing Down My Chin,
The Thoughts that in the Days Of Old,
This Was A Forbidden SiN,
Leave Me,
I develope A craving, I develope A thrist,
It Makes Me Feel Like A Vampire, 
Forever Cursed,
Earning My Red Wings,
Has Turned me into a Blood Sucking Beast,
Waiting For that time of the month,
Is Like Prepareing to Feast,
But I don't Think about It as Being Just Hungry,
I see It as taking a Womens Problem Head On,
We who Have Earned our Redwings,
Are the True Kings,
From Dusk til Dawn.
