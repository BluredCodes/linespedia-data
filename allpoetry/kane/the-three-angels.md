Three Angels Were Called Before Before GoD,
Their entire time on earth they had Never commited SiN,
GoD only Made Jesus Perfect,
So he Had To send the Angels Out In the World To Commit SiN,
The First Angel Comes Back and tells Him,
I've Killed A ChilD,
GoD Tells Him "I Forgive you"
"Go Get A Drink Of the Holy Water,"
Then The SeconD AnGel Followed,
Telling GoD,
I burnt Down A Building Full Of People,
GoD Tells Him "I Forgive You"
"Go get A Drink Of the Holy Water,"
Then The Third Angel Comes Back and Says,
Lord GoD I have Pissed In the Holy Water,
And GoD tells Him,
"You Know They Will Never Forgive You"
