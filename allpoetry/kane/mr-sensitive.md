I Am Sensitive,
And I'm not just talking about,
The area under the head of my Dick,
I'm the type of Guy who will compfort you 
when your Hurt or sick,
I want to Know Whats On your Mind,
I Don't Just listen to Get A piece of Ass,
I Am Mr. Sensitive
The Problem is I have No Class,
I may blurt out some Crazy shit,
But When it comes Down to it,
When You have a problem,
Your problems Become My Own,
And Not Because I don't want to be the one 
to have to hear you Bitch and Moan,
But Because I Care, 
I really Do,
And If You Really Know Me,
You Already Knew,
I Am Mr. Sensitive
