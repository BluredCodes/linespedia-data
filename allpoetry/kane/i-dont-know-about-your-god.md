I Don't Know About Your GoD,
But My GoD WoulD Never Keep Me From My Loved ones 
For All Eternity,
I Don't Know About Your GoD,
But My GoD Will Forgive Me for My SinS,
And Reward me According to My deeds,
I Don't Know About Your GoD,
But My God Is Great,

My GoD Is Righteous!

I Don't Know About Your GoD,
But My GoD loves Me,
He will keep his promises,
I Don't Know About Your GoD,
But My GoD is Waiting for Me,
_To Come Home_
