After Thinking LonG ANd HarD,
I've Decided,
That if My Nation Would Ever want Me,
To preform a Suicide Bombing,
I Would Do It,
That Is if My Nation Would Ever want me,
To preform A Suicide Bombing,
After all 
Life Is But A Breath,
What is a Breath,
Compared to an eternity?
After thinking LonG And HarD,
I've aknowledged that Suicidal thoughts 
Make Me Happy for a reason,
Why Not Make GooD of it,
A friend once told me,
That we need to Learn to Distinguish
Between the worst of The Two Evils,
Killing People Slowly And Painfully,
Having them Suffer,
Or
SenD their Souls to the LorD,
In a Blink of an EyE,
leaving a Mess,
For the Flesh,
To FeaR
After Having some time with My Voices,
I dwell on the thought that GoD will Forgive Me,
And Reward Me according to My deeds,
According to My Intentions
