title: "Suicidal Fantasies"
writer: "Kane"

I fear Death No more,
I look forward to it,
I fantisize on how I want to Die,
Where I want to Die,
Who I'll Have Watching,
The fashion in which I take my own life,
I No longer Fear
The suicidal thoughts 
I could never Control,
NightMares Have Become Daydreams,
And I feel closer to being able
To make Dreams come true
