My Fucking Voices Won't Leave Me alone,
It's Sunday 3:22am
And they Have me watching Wolf Blitzer On CNN,
They Have Me Waiting for the Outcome Of the Peace talks,
Peace Talks On Gods Day,
What a WorlD,
I can't Help But think Something Really Bad Is Going to Happen,
The Meeting is in progress right Now!
And Let me tell you,
My Schitzophrenia is really fucking me up right Now,
I can't help But Pray For Genral Powell,
And All.
