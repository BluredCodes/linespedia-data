Only The Anti-Christ Will Bring Us the iLLusioN,
We So Desire,
But For This Peace,
Will we send Our Souls,
To Face the Fire,
The WaRs Were Promised,
by Our GoD,
Our LorD,
He Dosen't First Bring Us Peace,
He first Brings Us A SworD,
True Peace Is In the Promise,
but first we must Suffer the Times,
Then is When We will aknowledge the LorD,
Then is When We will repent for Our Crimes,
After 7 years of an iLLusioN Of PeacE,
Many Will See the Signs of The Lord in the Heavens Above,
AnD The Holyspirit will Give us a Glimpse Of the Perfect Love,
Many will see the Begining of the EnD,
Of the Anti-Christs Reign,
Then the MANY will Know true suffering,
And Know true Pain
The Anti-Christ and His Armies,
Will Persecute and Kill those who love the Lord, 
But Our souls They Can Never Take,
We will be those who will Stand Against the Fake,
WaRs And Rumors of WaRs,
all promised to happen,
So what's One to Do,
No sense in sitting around,
Making yourSelf Feel Blue,
Just Know The LorD,
KnoW that He Loves You,
If you Don't Know Now,
Soon You too will See the Signs,
You Will See them Attempt to Destroy Our Souls,
But They'll Only be able to Put Some Bodies,
In Some Holes,
The Souls who follow The Cross,
Will Be Redeemed,
144,000,
will be left to sing for forgiveness,
and after 1000 years,
Satan will be Creamed
