title: "When A Man Dies A Woman"

You are not a whore
Not even before
You can still win the war
Just forget all of the lore
Passed down generations before

When a man dies a woman

You’ve been around too many nights
The lies radiate with might
Incomplete too many fights
While wishing for your name in lights
Only wishing that for once you were ordained right

When a man dies a woman

Everything you may have heard
You hope to deem this untrue
Remember that I heard everything from you
The world finally gets the truth from you
Even if it is a askew view

When a man dies a womanlovers are killers
