title: "Fated"
writer: "Autotomy"

I need to see your eyes only
All I ever wanted was to know that you cared
This hurts me sometimes I know
Did we have to be together? 
No

Friendship turns to hate
Is this how it is going to be?
Will you wait around for me?

It’s hard sometimes I know
Had to cheat to be together
Promise you’ll frown when I’m gone
Did I have to do this?
No

Friendship turned to hate
Can we come together as friends?
Will you wait around for me?

I want to you to know that I can deal with the new you
Even if we can’t be around each other
Why did you let me get away with those fucked up games
Can we be friends forever?
No
