Weaker than you
Pale as the color blue
I lay beside you
Dreaming of having nothing to do
As I inhale your sweet perfume
I wait for you to dream of me too
Waiting feels like forever
Just like you 
I am beneath the stars
Wishing for things to be
Hoping that we’ll still see 
The inner beauty that lies
