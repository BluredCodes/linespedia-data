Your falling back
Your fading out
And I’m not there
To break your fall
When you’re around you kill the sound
Enjoy the silence when I’m around
Cause I’ll be taking the final bow

Walk into the guiding light
Only to find out
In the end
That you were right
I spiral out
I wander down
Yet your not there 
To catch my fall


We both fail at this misery
When will we realize we don’t need to be free?
We’ll stay right here
Just you and me
Until harmony screams out tyrannysomething old..something new.. put it together.. Our time will come
