title: "Insecurity brings new options"

Push back the deadline
This is not that important
It’s only your life passing you by
There goes another missed opportunity
You exchanged it for staring at infinity
Like the slave you truly are
Put your trust inside you
Take control
Seize power away from them
Give it back to you
What does this have to do with me?
Absolutely nothing
The question you should be asking is…
Why have I wasted all of this much energy?
On something that has given me nothing in return
I’ve done this now
It has been a struggle
A new level has been achieved
The level of self-awareness
Now I leave this place
To go test out my gift
