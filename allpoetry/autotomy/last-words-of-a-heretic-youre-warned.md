This is revolution

Suppression
Boredom
Then comes the fun

Crucify the junkie
For another useless piece of shit

This is evolution

Freedom
Seclusion
Then the change shall come

Revelations bring hallucinations 
The curious wonder if they could try some

This is an instigation

Rejection
Infiltration
Then comes the one
