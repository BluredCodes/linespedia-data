You’re the worst thing to ever happen to me
I never wanted you
All of my dreams were unfulfilled because of you
I could not go to school
I couldn’t go to prom because I had to care for you
The best years of my life were spent raising you
When I should have been being raised myself
Life as I knew it ceased to exist

I wouldn’t change my life even if I could
You became my world
Everything else became unimportant
I did everything for your well-being
And for that I thank you
One day I hope you cans see that all of my screw ups
Were in reality victory’s
I love you my child
