What could I have done?
Everything became undone
Like a sweater caught on rusty nails
I unravel at deceiving glares leaving me feeling frail

Liberating self esteem
Walking amongst broken dreams
Wishing for thoughts of anarchy
Some days even you fall prey to fantasy

Over thinking what went wrong
Living in denial for far too long
Once again I feel alone
Thoughts often drift of home

Friends and family once seemed dear
Their faces seem to disappear
Drunken times seem to remind
