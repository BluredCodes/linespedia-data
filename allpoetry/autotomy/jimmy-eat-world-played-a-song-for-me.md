Jimmy Eat World
Was the place to be
On a Wedsday night
If you were me!
There I was 
In the middle of their pit
Being happy
Becoming free
Then I heard a few other fiends
Screaming requests at the band known as Jimmy!
I thought to myself 
I have to join in
I shouted out ROCKSTAR!!!
Then Jimmy said a matter of fact.
“Jimmy Eat World doesn’t take requests. So here’s rockstar”
The crowd erupted into cheers of glee
Especially me!
Because Jimmy Eat World played a song
Just for little old meWed April 10, 2002 
Jimmy Eat World @ The Koolhaus, Toronto Ontario 
they took my request! rockstar.. played it just for ME!!

Autotomy is still happy
