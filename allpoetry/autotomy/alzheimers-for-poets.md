I awake to become a has been
My time is done 
I have nothing more to contribute
You will read my previous work
Marvel at its originality
It could be the last brilliant writing I ever write
Not everyone is on top of his or her game all the time
This applies to me and sadly to you as well…

Does that mean we should stay up late?
Hardly go! Rest rejuvenate your selves
Do not worry about petty words
Every time you speak words flow do they not?
Even as you write complete nonsense like this
You are still making something very unique
Something very artistic

Tomorrow is today
It comes that soon
In a blink of the eye
Your world begins anew
One day you might even wake up to find
The words did not leave
They were here all along
Words will wait for you
People will not
I wake again to resume my place
As a has beenstick to the plan
the fall of man
