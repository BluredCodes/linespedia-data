title: "Ballad of the young offenders"
writer: "Autotomy"

Feel you feel me
Will you return infinity?
I wish that this could be
Just like the day you and me 
Became we

I wish for things just like they were
Before the stars in the system seemed to fade
You never made me laugh as much as you
Then again you weren’t mine
I was merely pretending all the time
