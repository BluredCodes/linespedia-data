Words only make me stronger
I cannot forget what you’ve given 
I want to watch you burn for good

Seeing you makes me unholy
I don’t want your genocide in my lifetime
Feelings beneath make me homicidal
You can't live this way forever
BURN
I 
Need you inside me
FEEL 
The 
Pain slowing injecting
Into my core
I need no one no more

FEEL me inside this interstellar planetary
Fucking waste of space

I wanted you to fell cleansed
But all I got was dirty in the end
Why won’t you feel something for me?
All I cannot be is everything you 
Want me to be!

Fucking cleansing
No new love comes in-between
