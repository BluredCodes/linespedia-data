Be weary of those like you
For they have a tendency to snap
React
They dislike just like you
They fight just like you
But in the end
They’re merely carbon copies of you

Now what the fuck do you do?
Fought your whole life to be you
Then when you get your way
Your trend is now followed every day
Identity lost amongst the fray
And watch those ideals go down the drain
Now ask yourself if you should stay?

Or stray?At work yesterday some unknown assailant Possibly gecko asked me where I got my cardigan.. This didn’t frighten me at first since it was an honest question.. Everyone asks someone where he or she got something at one point or another.. Yet while sipping my tea today I pondered if next week they’ll be thousands of teenagers running around in cardigan sweaters in Newmarket and Crapford..

My out of style is coming back I’m bored but I’m excited..
