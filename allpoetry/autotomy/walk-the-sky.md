Bitterness
Life seems hopeless
 Overhead in heart wrenched skies 
I walk the sky

Goodbye
Regression is acception of thoughts unfulfilled
Walking the skyline 
Only makes you 
Defined

Walk the sky
You’ll feel fine
Take certain care
Altitude descends
Just like our love
 
Walk towards dying light
Things bought for me 
Bring no pleasure
In motionless night

Clouds break through raven skies
They take you
Yet don’t come back for me
Running in hindsight
Reveals nothing but
Mistakes

 Walk the sky 
I’ll be fine
Take certain care
Relationships end
In due time everything diesyepp its a song alright...

we'll dance off-time to the songs we've never liked
