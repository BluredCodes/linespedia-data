Take this as a token
To refresh your memory of me
Keep it close to you always
As a reminder of what we could be

It’s not very big
It didn’t cost much at all
But the memories held with in it
Shall quench my spirit whole

Metallic and shiny
With a touch of black paint
Four letters on the top of it
All capitals inscribed in white

The edges now stained and tarnished
Along with friendships long ago
All that remains is a bottle cap
To remind me of what I once hadOnce long ago during a very drunken bush party when I was a young and was allowed to leave my tower.. I received a bottle cap as a sort of a promise ring from my 'state of grace'.. of course we 'fell from grace' some years later.. yet when I was spring cleaning I found the bottle cap .. so I decided to write about it.. thusly VIBE
