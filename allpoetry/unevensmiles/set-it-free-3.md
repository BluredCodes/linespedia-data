SET IT FREE

Im as far as you can see                                         
but your no where, closer to me
Because Im stuck in these chains of fear
With no signs of freedom, anywhere 

I can't change the way I feel
Believe me ive tried, to keep myself from
Going insane and losing my mind
And nothing seems to help my will
So I'll just take the chance deep inside

I've done all i can do
(So,) the rest I leave(up) to you
To tell me how you feel, or give me a sign
Because, I won't overrun your Competence
And irratate your mind

I have doubts and dreams
but please tell me if its not what it seems
But understand, i dont want to lose are frienship
And this is all just to get it out of me
But if you ever deside that theres something inside
Please dont be afraid to set it free
