title: "Crystal Hearts Broken"

Was there ever a reason
To put me through this pain
But what would not have happened
If we hadn’t played this game

Juggling fragile crystals
Which are our hearts
But you laughed when you dropped one
And when it broke apart

You broke my heart and burnt my soul
But you never had enough
You always wanted more
With complaints returned with a scoff.

Why do you treat me like I’m nothing
And forget that I do feel
I loved you and still do
Wasn't that in the deal?

I can't believe that in those days
I couldn’t see through your hurtful lies
Or that snicker here and there
All you were was the devil in disguise

There isn’t an us any more
But nothing to separate us
Just two people going down the same road
