You remind me of someone I once knew
Someone who was just like you
They had all these ideas you think are new
They led the revolution you hope to lead
Yeah There is no new blood in your veins
You think that you're so special
God must have stiched you by hand
I figure rather he has an idiot facotry
And you're just the lasted design with the same interior
You're not even new and you're not improved
