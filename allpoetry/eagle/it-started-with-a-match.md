And She watched
And she saw it burn
                         to the ground
        She knew he was inside
 Knew all the things she'd never said

And there were ashes in her hair
And the dust blew in her eyes
                         and set them ablaze
        She knew that he dying
 Knew that she couldn't save him this time

It started with a match
That simply lit a candle
That just slid right off the table
Then a rug began to burn
And soon so did the walls
Shortly he had waken
And soonder he had died

And she looked at the pile of rubble left over
and her shoes were now black
                         with dust and ashes
        He wasn't ever coming back, She knew
 And she had never said "I love you"
