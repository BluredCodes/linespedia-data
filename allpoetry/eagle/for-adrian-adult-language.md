Little Adrian grew up when I wasn't watching
Now he's wearing black and shouting "Fuck!"
Filled with rage and teenage angst
And he has yet to run into any good luck

When life handed him lemons he had no use for lemonade
Looks like you pulled the short straw today, little boy
You have to be the one with the slashed up wrists
You have to be the one who will never know joy

We ask ourselves if we're alone in the end
But Adrian's been so alone all along
I guess he never even was the happy little boy I thought he was
He can end it soon, this doesn't have to go on for long

Poor boy breathing his own poisoned air
I should have know that Adrian was never a little boy
Yeah Adrian was always a grown up man
Who hated waking up because it meant he still had to live
