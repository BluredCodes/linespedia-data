---
title: "Kitty won't die"
writer: "Eagle"
slug: "kitty-wont-die"
source: "AllPoetry"

Sitting in the middle of nowhere
Waiting for a car to pass by
Screaming just to see if it makes a noise
Yeah and curiosity killed that damned kitty
Killed it nine times through
But Clichés don't affect this kitty
Yeah he's back for the 10th time
Back for the 10th time
No he wouldn't let them keep him down
So small and frail
Wanted to try everything once
And just maybe he's gonna get to
Becuase this little kitty
Just wouldn't let clichés keep him down
