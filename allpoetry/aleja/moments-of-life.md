---
title: "***Moments of life***"
writer: "aleja"
slug: "moments-of-life"

There are always this moments in life,
were you just want to die,
and everyone is asking you, whats wrong?
and you just fill like  hypocrisy going on
you just say, everything is ok
but you really just wanna go away
and get rid of that bunch of hipocrites.

this is when you open your eyes,
and you realize
how wrong is life
and you fill u want to die

its just this moments when u see how wrong is everyone,
and u fill so bad 4 them 
but u cannot do anything...
and the worst
they dont happen 4 once
they'll happen 4 life

but what can we do?
maybe they are for some good.

this moments of life
we cannot let by
so lets all just try, 
just even at once 
to not think of that
moments of life.
