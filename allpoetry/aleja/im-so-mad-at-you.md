Im so mad at you
why you had to go and pick me?
i belived in you
u betray me.

Im so mad at you
i loved you with all mi heart
i thought u did too,
you hurt me, and the worst...
you heard me and u didnt care.

Im so mad at you
this is not fair
why did you play with me?,
with me and my feelings...
now i know you dont mean anything to me 
you have taught me to stop trusting in people.

im still mad at you,
i realize life goes on
and the fact that you are gone doesnt mean anything to me 
im really leaving now,
i dont need you anymore...
and even worst i dont want you,
why would someone want a mother fucking ass?
and now that you are all alone looking for someone to love you,
im one of the hapiest persons in the world...
and u know what?

IM STILL MAD AT YOU
