Happy Birthday i say to you my  special friend.
Happy birthday ill say to you till the end
Happy Birthday i sing to you on a special day.
Happy Birthday i write to you on a special card.
Happy Birthday i read to you from my special card.
Happy Birthday i wish to you from the bottom of my heart.
A Happy Birthday i really want you to have.
Happy Birthday my super friend Charles...

(I wish you the best...)
