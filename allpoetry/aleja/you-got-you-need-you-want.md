---
title: "YoU gOT, yOu NeEd, YoU wAnT."
writer: "aleja"
slug: "you-got-you-need-you-want"

You got the moon
you got the stars
you got my heart
you got my mind 
you got the sun
you got the clouds
you got a gun 
you got a house... 

You need some money
and also honey
you need a son
to give the sun 
you need a wife
to be in your life
you need to run 
to have some fun...

You want to go 
you want to stay
you want to pray
you want to lay 
you want to live
you want to die
you want to cry
you want to lie

You got
You need
You want...
