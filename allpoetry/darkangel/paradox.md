title: "Paradox"
writer: "darkangel"

I can not speak
for words are hollow
i can not dream
for disappointment follows

innocence gone
love betrayed
realization made
love is fleeting

no longer can i be so foolish
to fall in love
just because i want too

My mind refuses 
to give in
to whisper sweet nothings
to believe that anything
could last longer
than a moment

My heart has grown cold
scarred by wars
of love and rejection
yet i hope to find someone new
but the faith is gone
that it could be true.
