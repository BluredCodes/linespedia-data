---
title: "On being human"
writer: "darkangel"

As i sit here reading
i am struck 
by the universality
of our emotions
we all feel the same 
with slightly different words
the repetition 
of love and suffering
an old story
endlessly retold

Different faces
and different lives
but truth be told
we all want the same thing
someone to hold

there is no pain
that has not been felt
or any words
that have not been spoken
we are not unique
merely players
in the human history
of the world
