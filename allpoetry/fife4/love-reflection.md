Love?
                           In Love?
                           Out of Love?
                           In Love yet sad?
                           Out of love yet happy?
                           We cannot help but to Love!
                           Try to Love those who need Love.
                           The tender embrase is warm to the touch.
                           But out of love is entirly different.
                           There's no one left to Love.
                           You wish for devine truth.
                           Do you need truth?
                           Love not Love.
                           Love Life.
                           Live!Just a fun little ditty.
