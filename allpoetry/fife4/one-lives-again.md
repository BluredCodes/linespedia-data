---
title: "One Lives Again"
writer: "Fife4"

One
                                     Dead. Another
                                     Gone.
                                     How many 
                                     More?
                                     Left to
                                     Die.
                                     May God
                                     Forgive.
                                     Anointed with
                                     Graciousness.
                                     Deceived in
                                     Hope.
                                     Recoil all
                                     Forgotten.
                                     One Lives
                                     Again.
