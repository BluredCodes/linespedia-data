---
title: "How Do I Loathe Thee?"
writer: "rogue24"
slug: "how-do-i-loathe-thee"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60376-How-Do-I-Loathe-Thee--by-rogue24"

You've never around when I need you
It seems you stay away forever
When I reach for you 
All I have in my hands is darkness
Pain in my heart
And tormenting demons in my soul
I hate the way you never hold me
And the way you're always right
But it seems I never hated you to begin with
Especially when I see you smile
But what I hate the most is...
How Much I Love You
