Vengence
Anger
Pain
Torture
Suffering
Torment
All the things I feel when you turn away
All the hate I feel when you dont listen
When you dont appreciate the love I give
All the pain I endure for you
And you're selfish ways!!!!!
And the worst part is 
I cant hate you!
