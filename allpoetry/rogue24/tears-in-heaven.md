title: "Tears in Heaven"

The tears in heaven
Fall upon me today
As the sadness of the world taunts my existence
The sky questions my being
As the tears roll steadily from the sky
Heaven and Hell ask "why?"
I can see no answer which would satisfy them
So turn away
Instead of giving my soul
I return to the depressing world
I feel satisified with my broken soul
In which one can heal
No one in heaven or hell could match you
You are the soul that rips me apart
And heals my wounds
The one who blisters my heart
And cools the flames of anger
Erupting a battle of sanity
Drawing a thin line between falling off the edge
Or drowning in my fears
You have become my angel
And my hell
I love you none the less
