Taunting Sun
Disappear!
Leave the stage
With all your glory

Depressing moon 
Gaze upon me
With darkness of night

Bestow upon me
You means of evil
As I crawl to escape my night
And one day I will find the dawn
I will one day find myself
In your comforting arms
And so much love in your poisoned grasp

For once you cant hide
The truth has found you
And someday
So will I
In all my sadness and solitude
I will find a way to remove the blindness from your eyes
You will once again find
I am the one
And I am all you ever need
I am the taunting sun
And you are the depressing moon
