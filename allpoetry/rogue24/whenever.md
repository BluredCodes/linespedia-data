When I cant see you
I feel lost and alone
The world has no meaning
The chaos that surrounds me
Drags me into the lonely hands of death

I cant touch you
And the universe falls apart
A star falls for each tear I cry
The moon in all it's solitude
Falls from the sky
And the sun would just refuse to shine

When you leave me alone to find myself
I feel like my whole life is crumbling
Through my fingers like the sands of time
That challenge our love

When I hold you close
Your arms become a barrier to the outside world
I can only hear your voice
Everything else seems like a blurr 
Your eyes gazing into my soul

But whenever I cant hold you
The world falls apart
And my soul dies in the poison
That my heart can no longer stand

And whenever we share moments like these
When we lay under the stars
I know everything is perfect
And my eyes I know
I will always love you
Even after the world endsJust a poem about being seperated from the one you love...and its strange how you really feel this way
