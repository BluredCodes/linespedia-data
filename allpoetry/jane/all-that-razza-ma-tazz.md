---
title: "All That Razza-Ma-Tazz"
writer: "Jane"

I remember ragtime music
under the stars, boater
hats, and vintage cars,
and all that razza-ma-tazz.

A tall handsome mister,
a cute ardent miss, lips
close enough to kiss,
and all that razza-ma-tazz.

Racoon coats, flasks
for the hips,soft
pressing of eager lips,
and all that razza-ma-tazz.

Wedding bells pealed
to ragtime's beat,two
hearts joined and complete,
and all that razza-ma-tazz.

Now, all that remains
through the years-two hearts
separated, and funeral tears,
and all that razza-ma-tazz.
