title: "Anticipation"
writer: "Smeagol"

heartbeats
faster and faster
hands sweat
cold and clammy
nervous pace
back and forth

heartbeat 
faster and faster
hands sweat
cold and clammy
nervous pace
back and forth

heartbeat
hands sweat
nervous pace

nervous pace 

nervous pace


DING!!!!!


microwave's done
