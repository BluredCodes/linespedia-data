Once there was a young boy who lived in the forest
Spent his time hunting for his next meal
He lived in the shadows for he was lonely
Often he would wonder if indeed love was real


As Fate would have it one day when he was hunting
He came across a Fairy sleeping in a flower bed
She was the most beautiful creature he had ever seen
Slowly she awoke, smiled at him and said


Dance once more around the fire
Feel your spirit flying higher
Find your heart's true desire
We must live for love
Dance with me and take my hand
We'll travel off to mystic lands
Open your heart and you'll understand
We must live for love


When the boy touched the Fairy he felt his soul awaken
Together they danced all through the night
He lived his life for love and he was rewarded
The shadows were banished, he was bathed in the light

Dance once more around the fire
Feel your spirit flying higher
Find your heart's true desire
We must live for love
Dance with me and take my hand
We'll travel off to mystic lands
Open your heart and understand
We must live for love
We must live for love

We must Live for Love
