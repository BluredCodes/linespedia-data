Good gentlemen I feel it is my duty
To tell you the news of a wonderous beauty
Who came unto me this early morning
Without a sign and without a warning

Her face was like porclin her hair was like gold
An angel from Heaven close to twenty years old
Her skin it was milky and pure like good cream
Truely a vision that came from a dream

But then this wonderous creature did open her mouth
And I was shocked and alarmed at the sound that came out
For it was deep and raspy almost like a man
Then to my horror I realized it was my own brother Dan

So dissapointed my heart fell to the floor
Crushed and betrayed it would rise nevermore
So if a vision of beauty comes to you in the night
Ask her to speak before you turn off the light
