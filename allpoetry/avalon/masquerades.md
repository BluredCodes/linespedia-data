---
title: "Masquerades"
writer: "Avalon"
slug: "masquerades"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61097-Masquerades-by-Avalon"
---

To you I hold a different mask
Masquerading what I am
Telling nothing but that I'll let you know
Hiding what I can.

To me, you hold a different mask
Masquerading what you are
Hiding from me whatever you can conceal
Telling the world nothing by far.

To someone else, I hold a mask different
From the one I hold to you
Restricting what they know about me
Telling not what I told you.

We show ourselves to people
But different selves they are
To one person you may be shy and meek
The next, your a burning star.
No one ever acts themselves
The real inner person pure
Why are we always masquerading?
Why can't we be who we are?
