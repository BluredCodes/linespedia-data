Arrogance fills the air
emissions of self proclaimed royalty
Possessing audacity and gall
expecting the people's loyalty
An attitude expected
from such a selfish breed
Not sure what they want
so I bring them what they need
An "Idiot's Companion"
is what they call my trade
Here's an orientation
of how the game is played
You hire me
to stay by your side
Not to polish shoes
or make sure they're tied
To protect you from yourself
and the stupid things you say
Make sure things you do
don't get in your way
At the first sign of ineptitude
I slap the crap out of you once
Lowering the probability
of your becoming a dunce
We'll make bad habits good
at least those we can't quit
Our slap awake therapy
eliminates stupid shit
What've you got to lose?
You poor miserable slob
You need the shit slapped out of you
and I need a freakin' job
