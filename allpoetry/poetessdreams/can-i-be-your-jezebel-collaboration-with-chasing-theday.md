Can I Be You Jezebel
By Shaela M. Phillips and Ian Sawicki

Can I be your Jezebel?
In the lapse of time
the stir of frappucino
the scattered ashes after we complete
our rendez vous
in, intervals
are shameless immorality - 
i shall breath you inside,
all touches sacred
burnt offering in disguise,
come to this meeting of minds
were we recollect souls
pinched in moment, squeezed

Can I be you Jezebel
wanted on tips of skin
limbs in harmony
memorized lives 
that comfort in cushioned bruises of the heart
follow heart-raced sigh -
smile at the questions oh yes!
blemish me - strike me,
appendage locked stirs

You know me well
in silence swept in the sixteenth chapel
adorned  by you, 
people without faces
silent passion speaks
ceilings explode -
clothed in branches of words
in seal lips tasting spice
this obsession thriving us, MAD
into the wee hours of the night
falling into a slumber where the leaves glow 
(red)
rare goods in this life -
painted strokes blush
as tremors divine explore
times elaborate plan,
dreaming the question of sleep
and wander syllables bright 
(red)
our message inaudible loving in beauty
