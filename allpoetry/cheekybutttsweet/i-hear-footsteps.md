title: "I hear footsteps"

I hear a sound 
Little footsteps
sneaking around

"shhh" I hear voices say
"You'll wake up mum"

I hear rustling of dishes
and something just broke
I hear giggles  
I smell smoke

"shhh" I hear voices say
"You'll wake up mum"


I smell fresh coffee
I hear footsteps on the stairs
I pull the covers up over me


"shhh" I hear voices say
"You'll wake up mum"

 I hear the tray rest  on the table
I try not to smile
Hmmm my carpet. PLEASE let it be stable

"shhh" I hear voices say
"You'll wake up mum"

I feel  the  covers lift up
and two bodies get under
ever so quiet 
till one farts like thunder 

"shhh" I hear voices say
"You'll wake up mum"

 
I feel two kisses upon my cheek
Slowly open my eyes
 just a little peek

Stretch out my arms
Give an almight yawn
Look to the sky 
and realise it is only dawn

" Happy Easter Mummy"
I hear them say
"we made you breakfast"
