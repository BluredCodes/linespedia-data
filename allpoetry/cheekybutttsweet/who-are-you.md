Oh my god 

Am I dreaming or is it happening again? 
please leave me alone 
go away 
I am but just 12 in a day 

Too scared to open my eyes 
Hoping this is just a nightmare 
oh no! 
please dont touch me there 


If I just make a sound 
you will leave then, I am sure 
Just like you did before 


Too scared to open my eyes 
and see who you are 
I hope it is not my brother 
and please dont let it be my pa


Stop touching my breasts
get your fingers the hell out of me
please someone stop this
you are scaring me
carn't you see?

you prick
I hate you 
You make me sick

Leave me the hell alone 
get out of my bed 
You are scum
I wish you were dead
