title: "Unseen Bruises"
writer: "cheekybutttsweet"

Go ahead
Hit me again
Please leave a bruise this time
for me to show you in the morning
then you can see what you have done
no more pretending you dont remember
no more telling me I'm imagining things
no more calling me a liar
then I will have proof
a mark to show all those that say they care
all those that are never there
Go ahead hit me again

Please make a bruise where people can see
without a bruise they don't trust me
Please leave a bruise in a place I can show
I just need to let everyone know
Go ahead hit me again

My own brother you think I could trust
The evil in his drugged up eyes
That once sparkled, once shone so bright
Even now as an adult
I back away from those eyes
My body and soul in pure fright
Go ahead hit me again

Oh as an adult They now all see the bruise
For my daughter now has not one but three brothers
Oh yes, now they see the bruise
The way I protect her from them
The way I shake when they raise a temper at her
The way I take her away from them
Go ahead hit me again

You left your mark on me afterall
Have your drugs
Have your sorryfull life
But if you ever hit me again
I am not a little girl anymore
I will fight back
and remove this god damn bruise
The unseen bruise
So go ahead 
Please hit me again



Go ahead hit me again
