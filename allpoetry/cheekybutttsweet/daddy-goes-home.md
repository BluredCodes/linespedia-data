title: "Daddy goes home"

I love you daddy
I know it hurts you to leave
One day I will be bigger
Then I can come stay with you
I love you Daddy

Mummy says it is ok to cry
I have two daddies now
But it is not the same
I want to be with the daddy
That gave me my name
Mummy says it is ok to cry

I love you daddy
Mummy says I am just like you
In so many little things that I do
I will be bigger soon
Then I can come home with you
I love you daddy

Mummy says it is ok to cry
I will be big and strong soon
You will see
Although you are not here
I know you love me
Mummy says it is ok to cry

I love you Daddy
I wish you were not all alone
You can call on me anytime daddy
On the telephone
I love you Daddy

Mummy says it is ok to cry
I wish mummy did not take me so far away
But soon I’ll be bigger
I can come home to stay
Mummy says it is ok to cry

I love you Daddy 
I know it hurts you when you leave
But I will be bigger soon
And I know you love me
