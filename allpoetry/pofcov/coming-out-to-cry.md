title: "Coming out to cry"

I am here Lord, I don't know what to do. 
You have save me, and you have feed me. 
You have no so much for me, yet I have nothing in return.
Thank you Lord for saving this poor and dirty self, from the gutters. 
Thank you so much to make me your own.

Oh Lord! I ask you that you help me find myself.
I have fallen down, and i need your help
