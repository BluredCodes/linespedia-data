---
title: "I am Ready"

Oh Lord why have you chosen me?
This filty sinner.
You have sent me Jesus Christ for me.
And you have done so much.

Lord I am ready. 
To be used by you lord. 
Lord let me be right for your will to be done.
You have saved my life, so hence i am yours.
