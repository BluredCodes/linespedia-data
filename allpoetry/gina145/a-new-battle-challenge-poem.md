Once I lived
In “Total Bliss,”
At “Peace Within Your Embrace.”
I thought myself
“The Perfect Woman”
’Til life revealed
That you were not
The Perfect Man.
Seeking release, I wrote
My “Bittersweet Memories”
Until all were gone.
“Give me Strength” now,
As I head into a new battle
Against the “Eternal Torment”
