Fire up your soul
tasting the flavor of intensity
washing over your body
your eyes rolling backwards
tongue dangling for the wild beasts

lost in the vivid darkness
the desperation drips
from my begging lips
tossing, turning under the truth

wrap your fingers around blackness
gnawing and pouring into your veins
smokey sirens billowing into the stars
beckoning you into blind happiness
you forget the warmth of me
captured inside of your soul

kiss the spiderwebs from my eyes
collecting me into your hands
promising me forever
while death whispers
under your breath
