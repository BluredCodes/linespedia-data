What did I say?  What did I do?
Forced into a cage, not being understood.
The words oozing out of his mouth
Are fake!  
They hurt like the sting of a thousand hornets.
How dare you make me feel that way:
So lost and alone,
Not knowing where to turn.
In her smile I find solace
Except when she agrees with his words
And then I'm truly alone.
