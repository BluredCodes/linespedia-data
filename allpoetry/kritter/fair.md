---
title: "Fair"
writer: "Kritter"
slug: "fair"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59147-Fair-by-Kritter"
---

Fair is bullshit,
And fair doesn't exist.
Trapped in a continual downward spiral
Of guilt, fear, and hatred for their words
And the thoughts that torture and taunt our innocence.
It wasn't our fault,
But who fu*king cares?
It happened,
It's done,
Just not over yet.
Living on inside our mind,
Constantly reminded
Of what we could have, should have, and would have done
If only life was fair.
