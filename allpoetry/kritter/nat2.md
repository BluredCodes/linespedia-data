Feelings flow like water,
Rushing in from an ocean,
Too cluttered too often to know how to feel.
Some things wash in crystal clear,
Like the blue-green tide on the beach.
Beautiful and pure,
Like you,
Your gaze and caring, understanding touch
Forces me into a dream-like state
Of non-real images of the future.
Unsure of what to come,
Or even the present
You have been there through it all.
A soft pillow to lay my crowded head on,
A warm blanket to hold, to feel safe and loved in,
A cool breeze to soothe the red-hot fever in my heart.
Complete and secure in my love for you,
It's all I'll ever want or need.
