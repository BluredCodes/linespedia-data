I'm not the girl you think I am,
You think I always do right.
Wake up and see the light!

I'm not miss goody-goody anymore.
If you don't like it there's the door.
There's so many things,
That you don't know about me.

I'm a bitch,
and a witch.
Sure I'm nice a lot,
But there's so much more to me.

I'm not going to let people run my life,
Because it's my life,
And I'll live it the way I want to.

Why do people try to walk all over me?
Why can't they leave me be?
Yeah I'll take a lot of shit,
And go through hell.

I'm not perfect!
I have problems too,
No matter what I do.
You always expect way to much for me!

Can't you just accept me for me,
and everything I do?
And not try to change,
The person I am?
