This world is so full of shit,
Sometimes i feel like i don't even fit.
Friends stab you in the back,
And tell everyone everything you "lack."

What the hell?!
Sometimes I just want to yell.
People hurt you, 
And try to tell you what to do.
And don't even care about you.

This is my life,
And I'll live it the way I want to.
No matter what you do,
No one can make me do things I don't want to.

Why the hell won't people let me go?!
This is all I know,
I'll do what I want to,
Whenever and however i want.this is just a few feelings i had to get out.....
