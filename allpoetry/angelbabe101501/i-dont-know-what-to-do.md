We were getting so close again,
I thought for sure we would be.
So why couldn't I see?
Why couldn't I see that
What I did would hurt you?

I try to talk to you,
But I just can't think of what to say.
So what should I do?
I guess this is what I gotta pay.

I hate that I'm hurting you,
But I don't know what to do.
I can write so much,
But I can't seem to talk to you.

Why is that?
I want to be close to you,
But whatever I do,
I just seem to hurt you.Hey Jesse if you ever read this poem. I'm really sorry about everything. i know i'm sucking at everything. but i'm trying. i'm just a little...insecure...right now. please don't leave me alone. i need you right now. i really do like you. :(
