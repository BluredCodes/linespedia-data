---
title: "What Should I Do?"

My head is telling me to let go,
But my heart is telling me no.
Or maybe it's the other way around.
I just don't know,
Do I need to let go?

If I hang on to you,
What's that going to do?
If I let go of you,
What's that going to do?

It's like that song,
"It's tearin' up my heart when I'm with you,
But when we are apart I feel it too."
What should I do?
Should I just let you go?

Will I regret my decision?
What's a person to do in my situation?

I never want to say I love you again.
Cuz when I do,
I get hurt.
Hurt by you,
Hurt by everyone.

We either need to just be,
Or we need to just be apart,
Cuz I have feelings too,
It's not all about you.

If I make a choice,
Will I regret my decision?
