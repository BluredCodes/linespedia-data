Why can't I do anything right?
It seems we are always in a fight.
I always screw this up for us.

I just want to be with you,
But with everything I do,
I don't know how it can ever be.

You gave me everything you could,
And I know that,
But I took it for granted.

I am so sorry.
I totally messed up.
And because of me,
I don't know if we can be.hmmmm....i mess things up so bad for us...:(
