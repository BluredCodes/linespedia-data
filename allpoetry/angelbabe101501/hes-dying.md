Why? That's all I want to know,
Why is this happening to me,
To my family?
Why does this have to be?

He's getting worse,
It's coming back.
It's getting worse and worse.
What's going to happen?

Will he die?
Will he leave me here to cry?
This...This cancer,
It's killing him!

All i can think of is,
"No! No! Don't Die!
You can't leave me here to cry!
You were supposed to live forever!"

I love you,
So very much...
You can't do this to me!
Oh why does this have to be?!This is soooo hard. He is my grandpa and i always thought he would live forever and my grandma has serious heart problems...they are all going to leave me....
