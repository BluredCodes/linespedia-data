title: "For Michael"
writer: "ab"

From the moment we met and you said "Hi,I'm Mike,"
everything about you I really did like.

I think God put us together right from the start.
Your sweet, glowing smile almost stops my heart.

Michael, my love, you're such a gentle soul.
I feel like this half that's been left is suddenly whole.

We play music together, in perfect rhyme.
Our hearts are now beating in four-four time.
