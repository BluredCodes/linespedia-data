At the end of the day, everyday 
I sit in my room, alone 
And I take it all in, one at a time 

I see the picture of us on my shelf
I see your faces, and we look so happy

I see the roses from you
I show them off with such pride

I feel the necklace hanging from my neck, that says simply " I Love you"
I wear it so close to my heart

I see the blue box of notes, filled with so many words of life
I read them and they take me back 

I can still smell you, I love that scent
As if your right next to me

I see the stuffed animals next to my bed
They comfort me at night 

I take in the pictures, roses, necklace, letters, the smell, and amimals
These are perks and signs of you all over
It makes me remember, it makes me realize how much I miss it all

I take it all in at the end of the day every day
