Tell me what do you see?

A harlot
A woman
A girl 
A slut


Beautiful
Sexy
Bitchy
Hott

Weak
Stupid
Demised
Mean

Wonderful
Wild
Ugly
Keen

Special
One of a kind
Shy
An own mind?
