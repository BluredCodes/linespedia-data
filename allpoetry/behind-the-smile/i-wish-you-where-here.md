My god, it's beautiful
I wish you where here
crisp, cool air around me
I know how much you love the cold 

My god I wish I could share this with you

My god, it's amazing
I wish you where here
slience is all I hear 
I  know how loud it is over there

My god I wish I could share this with you

My god, it's breath taking 
I wish you where here
The sky is a thousand diamonds on the oceans sand
I know much you love the outside 

My god I wish I could share this with you 

My god, it's perfict 
I wish you where here
The only person near is the wind playing with my hair 
i know how you like to feel the wind in your face 

My god I wish I could share this with you
