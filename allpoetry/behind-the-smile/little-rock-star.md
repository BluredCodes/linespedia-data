you are a little rock star 
you with your spikey hair
you with your blue eye stare

your the knewlest person ive seen 
and the kewlest person im getting to know

you are a little rock star 
you with your rock star band 
you with your clothes taking a stand

your the sweetest  person ive met here 
u befriended be, no questions asked

you are a little rock star
you with ur checkerd pants 
you with ur funny rock star dance

you are MY little rock star
