---
title: "Was she worth it?"
writer: "Behind the Smile"

When I found out 
It felt like had swallowed shards of glass

Was she prettier?

When I found out
I fell into a thousand pieces 

Was she skinner?

When I found out 
My eyes cryed an ocean 

Was she funnier?

When I found out
My heart died a million deaths

Was she captivating?

When I found out
I was swept by you under the rug

Was she a better kisser?

When I found out 
I was hidden by you behind the door

Was she worth it?

When I found out
I became a little more stronger

So I ask myself,
Was he worth all this pain?
