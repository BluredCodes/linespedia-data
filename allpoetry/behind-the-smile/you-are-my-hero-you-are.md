Your heart stolen
your face swollen

Your life scattered
Your things shattered

your soul dying 
Your daughter crying

Yelling is all you used to hear,
Oh he half fixed that problem
Made you deaf in one ear

Kocked over the crib she lay in
Being with him you knew was a sin

You where so young and beautiful
He praid on girls like you, so young and gullable

From family you stole
For him to spare your soul

He cheated and lied
you, he denied

He hit you anyway 
He knew you where carrying 
How did you live like this every single day?


Your heart breaking
Your body aching

You are so strong 
How did you last that long


You are my hero
You are my savor
You are my  creater

Heavin and earth 
couldnt have made you better

You are all I want to be
You are my half other
You are my mother
