title: "Any day now"
writer: "Behind the Smile"

Any day now I will be hurt 
Any day now it will start to burn
Any day now it will all fall apart 
Any day now it won't be so hard
Any day now it wil settle in
Any day now life will begin
Any day now I will be heard 
Any day now I will learn
Any day now I'll get over you
Any day now a lie will be true
Any day now it will get good
Any day now life will be as it should
Any day now I'll feel my sin
Any day now I'll win
Any day now I'll understand
Any day now my plane will land
Any day now it will be bright
Any day now wrong will be right 
Any day now it won't feel so numb
Any day now I won't feel so dumb
Any day now the end draws near 
Any day now I will think clear 
Any day now he'll answer my prayers
Any day now I won't feel their stares
Any day now I won't be ashamed
Any day now I will be saved
Any day now I won't be abused 
Any day now I won't be used
Any day now I won't be hurt
