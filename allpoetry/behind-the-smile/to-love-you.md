title: "To love you"
writer: "Behind the Smile"

To love you meant giving up me
You would let me get away with kickin my own ass
I would say the more tragic the better
Its such a same we could have had much more pain
I loved you muchly
What was wrong with me?

I was a deer 
Caught in the head lights

To love you meant hating me
You said I was beautiful one day 
The next I was to ugly to be seen with 
One day I made you happy 
The other you hated my guts
What did I do wrong?

I was a butterfly 
Caught in a spider web

To love you meant hurting me 
You said you loved me 
But soon I knew
All you did was cut my throat
And steal my broken heart
What could I do?

I was a child 
Crying in a strangers body
