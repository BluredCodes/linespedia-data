I was afraid you'd lie to my face
I was afraid I'd be replaced
I was afraid I'd feel that slap in the face 
I was afraid I'd be erased 
I was afraid I'd be chased 

I was afraid you'd force me to 
I was afraid you wouldn't be true
I was afraid of what you'd do 
I was afraid to be persued
I was afraid to lose you
