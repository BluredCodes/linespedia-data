---
title: "I hate"
writer: "Behind the Smile"
slug: "i-hate"
source: "AllPoetry"

I hate the way you look at me 
And I hate it when you stare
I hate it when your mean and I 
Hate it when your never there

I hate te way you look down on me 
And I hate it when you yell
I hate the things you never say and
The things you never tell

i hate it when you don't call
And I hate it when you call to much 
I hate it when I'm not first and 
I hate to feel your touch 

I hate when you tell the truth 
And I hate it when you lie
i hate the way you make me upset
I hate when you make me cry
