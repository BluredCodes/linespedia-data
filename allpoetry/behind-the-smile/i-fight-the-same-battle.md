I fight the same battle
I fight the same urge

Do I give in? 
Do I walk away?
Do I go?
Do I stay?

Your smile lights up my face
Your very touch give me such a rush
Your cheek next to mine
You've given me every sign

Only three hearts are involved in my battle
His, mine, yours
Do I give in to what I wan't?
Or do I give up what I have?

Your so soft 
So funny
So sweet
So what I need


I'm stuck in the middle
Between my heart
And my mind 
I am blind

I still fight the same battle
I still fight the same battle
