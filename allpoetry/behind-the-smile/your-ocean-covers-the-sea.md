Your ocean covers the sea
Your weight covers me 

I put my hand in yours
And your lips on mine 

It's so dark in here
As if i where blind

A simple little crush 
Could make me love you so much 

A simple little kiss
Could lead up to this

Sighs fill up the air
My mouth can't move 

Your hand gently on the side of my face 
The touch of your warm embrace

I never knew love could be shown this way 
Not a word needed to say 

Your eyes stare back at me 
With love and tendercy 

Our hearts beating heavier
Our chests pressed together

Your ocean covers the sea
Your weight covers me
