---
title: "At the door"
writer: "Behind the Smile"
slug: "at-the-door"
source: "AllPoetry"

An angel sits in the corner of my room
And the devils knocking at my door
A canle lit in the middle of my room
And I'm on my knees with a prayer
MY eyes wide shut
My mind openly closed
A cross in one had 
A sin in the other 
Tears in my eyes
Lies in my throat 
I read his words
I listen for his voice 
The devil sits in the corner of my room 
An angel knocks at my door
