If I where born a boy?

Then I wouldn't get picked on
Then I would be taken more seriously
Then I would have strength 
Then I wouldn't have the stares
Then I could achieve my goals
Then I wouldn't be abused at all
Then I could get what I want 
Then I could get away with being a whore
Then I would be able to walk down the streets at night
Then I would be respected
Then I could be trusted
Then I wouldn't be afrade of the opposite sex
Then I wouldn't be weak 

If I where born a boy
