---
title: "Between Heaven and Hell"
writer: "Behind the Smile"
slug: "between-heaven-and-hell"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58252-Between-Heaven-and-Hell-by-Behind-the-Smile"

Heaven is a fantasy while alive 
In death heaven is reality 

Hell is here on earth 
The cruel reality it's here to stay

Our life line
Is followed by a string of pain 

Between good and evil
Heaven and hell

Their swords clash over and over
again

I can feel the lords pain
Thru the rain

We will rise and fall
But we will meet again

Thru the gates of heaven 
And the fire in hell
