To my best friend, YOU have corruppted ME

Your funny has hell
Your fearless in the face of evil
That's kind of ironic
Cuz you such the little she devil 

I like the fact that your honist
That you won't lie
I'm gonna beat up the "wee wee's" that hurt you 
And make you want to cry 

I remember the days 
You humping the trees
Me complaning
well... cuz that's just me 

The way you make me laugh 
And the way you make me smile 
When the day seems like a bore
You make it all worth while 

I love your attitude 
Of "Why the F*CK not!"
Remember your hangover
Hah you got caught

You may not like overly nice people
But I know you love me
Your a sweetheart
Could that even be ?

Your the koolest chick I know
Your way with words is amazing
AHH the cheese people and the sinep
Your a crazy lil rock star, but lay off the blazin

LOVE U!
