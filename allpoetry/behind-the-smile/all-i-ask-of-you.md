title: "All I ask of you"
writer: "Behind the Smile"

Please all I ask of you
Cherish my name 
Love me for me 
Tell me it's true
Before you leave 

Remember who I was 
Tell me I am beautiful
Pack up your bags
With a picture of me 
Before you go

Call me your love
Hold me in public 
Take my hand in yous
Let them know I'm yours
Before your gone

I'm so pathetic and sad
That's okay so are you
I wish upon a star
But it burns no more

I can't see you anymore
This is meant to be left alone
We know it's a lost cause
I hate me so much more

Can I kiss you once more?
