What happenes when your real life exceeds your dreams
What happenes when your secret wishes are answerd

But I wish you where here
To see how Im growing
I wish you where here
To come where Im going 

And I thank you

I may not be able to see the things you see
Or hear the things you heard
I'll follow you into the ocean
I'll meet you at the other side of the sea

Your crazy
But not quitley
Your pradictable
But you still amaze me 

And I thank you

Im out of breath while your speaking
I hang on to your every word
I can fee my knees weaking
I want to breath in your every breath

Your the life that wakes up the night 
Your the life that rests the day 
Your my inspiration
You keep me focused

And I thank you

To define you would be an impossiable task
You give me to much credit
You dont tell me what I want to hear 
You tell me what I need to hear 

And I thank you, for it all
