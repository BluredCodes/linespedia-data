I cant change it
I cant help it 

Pushing me around 
Or pushing me away 
Im unhappy without you
Im miserable with you

Rude enough being alive 
When no one wants me
Im such an inconvience to be with 
Im convinced theres no need of me 

Forgive me for starling you
Because you shattered me 
Im trying not to feel so rejected
Im trying not to be so disconnected

I can see where Im coming from
But I dont know where Im going
The more I change 
The less I feel 

Dont let me know the truth 
Cuz I might disapprove
I broke down Because I was tired
I simply dont feel good

I hate the fact that I always wanted to be yours
And that you never wanted me 
I force a smile to please you
You do nothing to ease me 

I cant help it
I cant change it
