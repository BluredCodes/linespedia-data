Why do you like me?
What could it be?
It is some thing i can't see

My eyes arn't crystal blue 
My hair isn't beautiful blonde
My chest isn't a double D
I'm not a perfict size 3

My skin isn't silky soft
My body isn't firm and strong
I'm 5'6 slender and tall
My hips arn't perfictly tiny small

My thys's arn't modle like slender
My feet arn't polished and danty
My legs arn't long and smooth
My voice doesn't sound sexy or sothe

I wish I had your eyes
I wish i could see what it is you see
I wish I loved myself the way you love me
