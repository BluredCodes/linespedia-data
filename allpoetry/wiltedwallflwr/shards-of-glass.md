can you love?

the shards of glass that i am?

painful britle memory cuts into my daily life
like the person i am

the shards of glass in my blood
goes through my heart 
as painfull as the sight of you

the place i hold
is the place with you
that is not obtainable

protected.

from humans
ill hide in my fragile cage

dont take me out

suffer.
suffer.

my skin of glittered cut blood
would shred the hand that touches it

no, my shards of glass reality
is broken
and there is no mirror
to see behind me.
