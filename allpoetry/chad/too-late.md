Dry candy covered dreams
and words, unspoken.
(Break this chain which sits unbroken)
A cage of wire 
high enough to feel free.
Hauled through the honesty
but where am I?
Pushing through possibilities
locked doors and reasons why.
Lost the words
somewhere between you and me;
lost reason somewhere down the line.
(somewhere you say you're doing fine)
The very thing which makes the body be
irritates to the bone, a histamine plea.

And I'll speak from time to time,
loud words too late to matter,
when it's too late
to make it better.lacks structure.......... but give a f*ck
