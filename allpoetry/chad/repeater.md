Words losing weight.
Maybe earlier,
maybe a day or two ago,
but somewhere, words became noise.

Slip into an engine of automation,
smiling into the same sweet spot
which sits between minutes repeated.
Somewhere these words had motion.

Running on automatic,
or is that neutral?
stalled into a freeze frame
of blank faces.

Mid-week malais
Sunday, Monday "Happy days"
Needle stuck on repeater,
as the words float through the ether.Two questions: Is freeze frame one word? And do you spell malais like that?... OK three questions: Is malais even a word?
