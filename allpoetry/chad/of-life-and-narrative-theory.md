Spark ignites
and narrative begins.
The story is always the same,
only the plot points differ.
Equilibrium becomes disruption
in a frenzy of awakenings and sprints,
which burn into our growing pains.
When will recognition occur?
Some one always said it would,
but not today... and not this way.
And so my expectations and enigmas rest
lazily on my back,
the pace slows, as always happens
in the second act.

This is open but restricted,
Linear but full of flashbacks
and the new equilibrium awaits.hmmmmmmmmm, narrative theory
