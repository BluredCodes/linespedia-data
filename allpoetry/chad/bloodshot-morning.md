These are things I do without you.
Take a steady breath and step
into the jaded moments and broken words,
which hang around like spies.

Broken hearts and messages
are wasted in the air.
Take a rain check,
make a moment last.

Listen for free,
but don't listen to me.An oh so vague way of re-telling an oh so simple moment. *shrugs* I just do it to keep Birchwood guessing =)
