---
title: "My friend the bottle of wine"
writer: "Chad"
slug: "my-friend-the-bottle-of-wine"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60378-My-friend-the-bottle-of-wine-by-Chad"

I wonder by this bottle of wine,
what thoughts should spill into my mind.
Of broken hearts and words unspoke,
of seeing the world and getting the joke.

But now I reach the final drip,
The world continues on its merry trip.
And leaves me here alone and pissed,
and lets me ponder on moments missed.They just keep coming!!!!!!!!!!
