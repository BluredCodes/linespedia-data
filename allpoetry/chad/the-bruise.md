---
title: "The bruise"
writer: "Chad"
slug: "the-bruise"

A question mark
sits on the cheek
but the answer, too high.
Through the eyes,
I can see it.
Through your voice 
I can hear it.
Slip under the colours,
to where the reasons lie (try)
in wait for retribution.
