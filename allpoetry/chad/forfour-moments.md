title: "For(four) moments"
writer: "Chad"

Cold velvet summer skin,
pressing down, holding you in

(She makes me stumble
with arms outstretched)

When you're where ever
As long as I'm there
to breath you in

and the pictures hold you in
Just enough for me to hold onrandom thoughts put down on a page.
