"LIFE" the movie
with crappy music,
cinematography is weak too.
It all just seems to be point of view.
I think the director must be crazy
and there's too many writers
which makes the plot seem hazy.
It seems like all the characters
are a little over complicated.
To be honest I find this whole
genre a little over-rated.
And i didn't even get a front row seat,
And my popcorns stale, what a damn cheek




... never mind I hear the sequel is gonna be great.
