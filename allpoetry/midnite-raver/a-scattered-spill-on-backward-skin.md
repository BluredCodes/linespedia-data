---
title: "a scattered spill on backward skin"
writer: "midnite raver"

i tire of my eyes,
my burnt out bulbs are baby lies,
i drift to touch these shades i see,
the truth's infecting me,

in-valid forms of soft-brained skin,
to taste, to feel, to dream, to sin,
the shining sun, my only friend,
it helps me to begin,

in afternoons alone i drown,
my scattered thoughts destroy the ground,
the farther out i feel i go,
i find the world one claims to know,

waiting for the fall of time,
wandering between the lines,
discarding skin for what it buys,
i tire of my eyes.
