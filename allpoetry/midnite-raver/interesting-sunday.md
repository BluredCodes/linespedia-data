if however,
that strain to consistency breaks down,
a situation of crisis ensues,
to exist,
"no one should fear what they cannot see,"
shade and shadow,
the way people think they should behave,
the way in which they think they do behave,
and the way in which they actually behave may be distinctly different,
fiction,
my most significant point of departure from the [intention],
the fascination with theory,
disregarding acceptable discarding,
unwrapping,
the nature of the institution is determined by its' function,
electric sunday,
"there are doors that open by themselves."
