~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

For Sorcha Conway, Good Friend, Poet, You’ll Be Sadly Missed

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

There is a brand new angel
Watching from on high
A good friend and a poet
With whom to laugh and cry
I look towards the heavens
At all the shining stars
Knowing that her journey
Would heal her earthly scars
Though we are left her legacy
Of thoughts in written word
Left down here for all of us
So she can still be heard
A story of a lifetime
Tales you told of Jim
The Haiku and the Tanka
Where does one begin?
So as you look upon us
Just to let you know
Your many friends still down here
Have let their sorrow show
In life you were treasure
A friend to one and all
Many of the poets
Have stories to recall
My friend, I’ll sorely miss you
As will so many more
Hoping that we get to meet
When we reach Heaven’s door
