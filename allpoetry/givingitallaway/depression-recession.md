title: "Depression Recession?"

Millions of voices screaming for change
A country ran dry and emaciated
Like the carcass of an eagle
Under an apathetic sun

They tried to bring the eagle
Back to where it was
Tried to feed it
Tried to sustain it
But one must ask himself:
"How does a dead thing live once more?"

Perhaps all the eagle is good for
Is to stuff it with God-knows-what
And put it on display
For the whole world to see
So that they can carry on
With trying to bring the dead to life

And while most of the world gazes 
At a marvelous bird with wings spread wide
There are some who know
What's really inside...
