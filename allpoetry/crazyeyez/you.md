title: "You"
writer: "Crazyeyez"
slug: "you"

Suffer my desire for you
Rest, if you must
But don't tire out of my love
You fill my heart with sunshine
Everytime I'm with you
Never leave this love
You drew a circle of love around me,
Took my heart and squeezed
It from its pain
I thank whatever gods there may be
For blessing me with such a 
Wonderful soul
I see you and no one else
You whsiper softly in my ear
All the words I long to hear
We will always be together
You and I
