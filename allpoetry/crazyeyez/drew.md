Drew,
I cannot say that your eyes
Brought such surprise to my
Wounded heart
The sweetness of watching you sleep
Brings glistening tears to my eyes
I'm so glad you are here
To change these tears of pain
To the tears of happiness
When you run your hands through my hair
I am drawn to your face that is so heavenly fair
I never thought that i could 
Love like this
I'm so in love with you
I can't deny that
You are hard to resist
Especially your kiss
You're driving me crazy
I want your love
And I need your help
I found my love true at last
You are such a sensitive mind
You are my lullaby
At night when I am scared
From the dreams of my past
Everytime I see your
Beautiful face,
My heart does begin to race
Never again shall I be cursed
With the pain of love
Your love is so infinite
UndyingDrew is my fiance
