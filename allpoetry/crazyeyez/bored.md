Amuse me
Please, somehow
Bored sitting here
In this damn computer Lab
1 1/2 more months left
Till I'm outta this 
Shithole School
Where gang members
Are bused up to the 
Richest school in Vegas
You would think that there was dignity
In this school, that its full
Of prepsters, 
No, its not what you think
There aren't any prepsters here
Just another drug infested, gang related
Hypocritical asshole gathering
Just like every other school
But people still like to call us the rich bitches
Its true but not all of us
Are what we seem
