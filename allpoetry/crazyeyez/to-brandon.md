My shadow's digging looking for a clue
Over thinking, always causing migraines
Mention this to me
Before I pine away
Something has to change
And I feel this
Slipping and fading slowly away
You slipped away from me
I hope this feeling sucks you down
But who am I to judge?
Put me where I don't want to be 
I'm heading back
Home
Fuck your dysfunctional mind
I'm so glad its over
No one is innocent...Don't worry
I just need one sweet moment
Without you
When I am whole again
Trust me
Hatred and selfishness is what keeps me alive
Alive
I guess its worth it
If you're not here.
