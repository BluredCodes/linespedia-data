I love you
If you but knew
I will give to you my love
Always and forever
I love you
Because of who I am
When I am with you
we fly higher than birds 
When we are face to face
You love not me for my face
But what's inside
My love for you is of a mystery
I cannot solve
I miss you when you are gone
Missing your fragrant scent
Desire always lngering on my lips
After your beautiful kisses
I need you to stay
For life is not worth
Living if its not with you
