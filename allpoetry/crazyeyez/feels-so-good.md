---
title: "Feels so good"
writer: "Crazyeyez"
slug: "feels-so-good"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61524-Feels-so-good-by-Crazyeyez"
---

To hear you speak
makes me so weak
Make this last forever
Your touch is so wonderful
It feels so good
Nothing can compare to what we have
Thank God I found you
Kiss and caress me
It just feels so good
My heart is high again
You are all my world
Your love knows no labor
You resued me 
From me
You let your love abound
It brings me to tears
Tears of happiness
To have someone like 
you loving me
