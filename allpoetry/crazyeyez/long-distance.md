Mention this to me
Within the emptiness I may find peace
I don't want it
Someone who seemed to feel the same
And constantly feel this
Coming like some distant storm
But there was nothing I could say
Nothing I could do
To make you stay
I guess my warning
Meant nothing to you
I told you it would end
Once you left
Just give it time
You hold the light
The light that leads me
To a better place
I can't find it anymore
You're gone
To the shithole called Tucson
I'm always praying for you
Always...
I'm dreaming of your face
Once again
And its killing me
I don't want to be dismal
It just took so long 
For me to figure out
That you are gone
I just want to start things over
But I have already swallowed
This poison of long distance that you have fed me
We are choosing to be like this, here
Its the cold silence
That I feel 
Without you here.Long distance relationships suck!!!!!!!!!!!!!
