Thought I was high and free
Get up and free yourself from yourself
I've gone under two times already
Both were a bit messy
What you need is someone
Someone who strong
Strong to guide you
I doubt you'll ever find her
Since she was right there
In front of you all along
But did you notice?
No...
But I'm tired of
Waiting for you
Waiting for you to change
Right...Like that will ever happen
Your head was almost empty
Must've been all those drugs
I am trembling
At the thought of ever
Being with you again
It makes me sick 
Just thinking of
You and me
Breathing the same air
I can never say anything that I want
When I am with you
Well you know what!
Fuck you
I'm throughIt was a nasty break up :(
