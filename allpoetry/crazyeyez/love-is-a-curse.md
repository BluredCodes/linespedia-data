---
title: "Love is A curse"
writer: "Crazyeyez"

I'm happy
I'm better off without him
Time will heal this bruised heart
Sometimes I think love is a curse
For the unlucky people
especially me
He maywrite me down in history
But I will always rise above him
In my pride
He shattered my heart
I'm a woman
But did he realize that?
No
When I try to show him
He is oblivious
I still get treated like shit
With disrespect
The one thing I know I'll be better off without...
LOVE
This is what I know
They say that love is blind
Well, they got that right
On your part
Now I feel no remorse 
For leaving you
None...no shame, nor guilt
