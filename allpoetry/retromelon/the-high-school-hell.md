Everything seemed right
I thought I had adjusted
Little things became to unweave
And the new friends I made seem to
Get less out of joking and more cruel
The school has screwed me over
And the teachers have no
Care,respect, or cofindence in the students
Except for a chosen few
Why in the bleeding hell get into this business
If you can't handle it
We are teengagers we screw up 
We learn
You don't need to send fat old petifiles to look up our skirts
Just hoping to get us in trouble Cause it gives some sense of meaning to their sad pitful lives
I don't know what the moral is
But its just another moment that I 
have to handle right
With bloody tears not a knife
I just want it to end in some way, but hey
Tommrow is only a day away
But I wanna go back
To where I belong
to where the ones who held my past and 
are with me in the present roam
I want to feel 
Not targeted, and hated by teachers and so called friends
Old have the key to my heart
And only a few new actually do
The schools rules are screwy
They don't have any clue
they hire drug addicts
and peverts to run our school
Teachers who know less than we do
And could be helped by a class where they are a student
So called 'securtiy guards' 
who target any one and everyone looking for atleast one thing wrong
but never cheerleaders,
two minutes late for a detention i didn't derserve
even though i ran until i though i would pass out
And i ask is there a reason why I don't try for another place
What is keeping me here
in the god forsaken place
It is hell, spare a good few
so i tell you now i will leave you
