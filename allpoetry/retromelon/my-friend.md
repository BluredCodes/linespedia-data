He just left
No body save a few knew
It felt as,
Well it felt like a brother left while I was sleeping
I always knew how he felt
For one of my friends
come to find
she felt the same way
a question like what if
wanders my mind
He was my one best guy friend
The one of two guys(the other being no other than daddy)
I thought I could trust
And to her
And her to him
He never felt like he was anything
but left a imprint
and apparition of himself
in everyone he met
touched so many people
so many loved
he was talented in arts
with a cooky funny, yet beutiful smile
His personality
Can pick me up
When I'm in the lowest state of mind
I keep expecting him to pop up
Scare the shit out me behind the stair well
Hug me in the court yard
Every time I walk by a certain place
I feel liking breaking down and crying
Yet feeled with rage
No he's not dead
I dont think I would be pissed off at him if he was
But I never really can stay mad at him
No one hated him like he though
Screw the damn preps what the fuck do they know
Some how it feels still
Like it wont get better
my friend
where are the promised e-mails
My friend
how can i bare this hell without you
my friend call HER
you'd be suprosed
my friend
if you read this call me
cause I love you, I always will
Its against the nature for anyone not to
my friend call all you touched
so my friend after all this
I dont know
if you are hurt, or sad or more mentally broken
My friend 
How could you leave
AND not say anything
Not a damn thing
I know we talked about it, but
my friend
where are you now?
