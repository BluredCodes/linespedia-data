title: "I Need A Reason"

I need a reason to go on.
I am all alone.
The one that I love truly.
Just ignores the fact cooly.
I have no friends left in life.
In my eyes you can see only strife.
On my face you never see a smile.
To all who know me, I seem vile.
I see nothing in this world but pain.
They all think tha I should be slain.
I have become worthless to all.
The only thing I have left is to fall.
I only asked for one reason for this.
I need this reason or I will never have bliss.
