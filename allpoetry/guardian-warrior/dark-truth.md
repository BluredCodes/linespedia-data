title: "Dark Truth"
writer: "guardian warrior"

No matter how hard we try.
We will never know why.
No matter how far we look.
We will never find that which was took.
No matter how much we pray.
No one will ever hear what we say.
No matter what we want.
We won't ever get anything but taunts.
No matter what we dream of.
We will never find true love.
No matter how much we don't want to believe it.
That is the silent truth of it.
