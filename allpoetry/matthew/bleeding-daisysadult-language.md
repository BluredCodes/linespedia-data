Pastiness, pastiness is what you want from me
Look hard, look long and say what you see
I’m not beautiful to you?
I’m not good enough; I’m not strong enough, 
Look at me, look at you 
Now shut the fuck up

Smile dry, smile sad 
Smile big, smile high
Don’t stop, don’t think, don’t seek the reason why

The world only wants happy people for their happy lives
With their happy shit and their apple pie
Don’t fall, don’t hurt, don’t cut don’t bleed
Don’t ask me because I don’t care and you don’t want what you need

Lick me; split me, any way you hurt me
Take it all, all for free
Take me; hate me, any way you want me
Take it all, all for free

I’m tired of being nice
I’m tired of thinking twice
I’m tired of the day
I’m tired of your way

In Short:

Smile dry, smile sad
Smile big, smile high
Don’t stop, don’t think, don’t seek the reason why

I hope you smile so big that your face cracks baby
Don’t worry too much because you’ll be bleeding daisy’s
I want, you want, smile wide maybe
It’s not my fault if you’re bleeding daisy’s

I can’t cry and I hide my pain
I can’t love and it seems in vain
I can’t see to find my way
And I don’t want your bleeding daisy’s

So fuck you and your bleeding daisy’s
