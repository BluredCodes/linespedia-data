title: "Shadow Chaser"
writer: "Matthew"

How could I not feel for you?
How could I not do all that I can do?
How could you feel for me?
How could you set my love free?

I am the Dark
You are the light
With all the pain in my heart
Only you can set it right

I feel as a beast does
Crouched in the corner
Hiding in the shadows

You are as a white dove
Pure in your nature
Chasing away the shadows

Your joy, your kindness
Hope for life living
Please take away my numbness
And let me feel your endless giving

Light up my life as the sun lights the window
Come into my heart and chase away my shadows
