title: "Bat in the Hat"
writer: "Matthew"

Bat in the hat
Bat in the hat
Rats in the closet
Eating the cat

Badness, badness all around
Move yourself to the creepy sound
Black hat, black hat have you any soul?
Sold it all for a sack of gold

Bat in the hat
Bat in the hat
Cat in the closet
Skinning the rats

There’s the skeleton man with the bony finger
Watch out little girl you better not linger
If the bad man gets you in his icy grip
He’s going to take little chicky for a nasty trip

Bat in the hat
Bat in the hat
The cat went crazy
Now where’s the rats?

Silver and gold, silver and gold
Black hat’s gone and the bad man’s old
Black to green to black to green
The crazy cat went and got real mean

Bat in the hat
Bat in the hat
The cat is cool
And he bagged the rats
