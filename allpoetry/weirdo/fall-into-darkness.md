My life is meaningless and going nowhere
But strangely enough I really don't care
Everything is mediocre
All is lost, its all over
No inspirations have struck
Everything is fucked
I'll put this simply 
The whole world is empty
I try to find something to hold onto 
But find instead theres nothing left 
My priorities are all askew
As I breath what could be my last breath 
I'm standing on life's last ledge 
This wild ride has taken me to the edge
It's a downward spiral I can't slow down
As darkness falls all around...hmmm... I think it is finished...
