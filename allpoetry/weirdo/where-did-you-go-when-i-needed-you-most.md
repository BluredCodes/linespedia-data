---
title: "Where Did You Go When I Needed You Most?"
writer: "weirdo"
slug: "where-did-you-go-when-i-needed-you-most"

I taught you everything you are
But now I'm lost and I need help
Away I have fallen so far
Please don't shove old emotions on the shelf
Too late...I reach out and you are not there
When I fell I expected you to care
But you were quick to condemn 
And point out all of my sin
Instead of help I got insults and curses
My broken heart you could have nursed
All I needed was your love 
That turned suddenly so cold
Where did it go?
Underwater and drowning I couldn't breathe
Yet you stil didn't listen to my screams
You say that I've fallen away
Yet I still remember the day
You promised you'd be there forever
And I never thought our relationship severed
As I fall still deeper into this hole
I realize...you could have saved my soul
But you're as empty and hollow 
As all your oaths
Where did you go when I needed you most?
