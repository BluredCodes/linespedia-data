baby im sorry 
we’re falling apart
theres something inside me
thats breaking my heart

‘cause each time that you hold me
and kiss me goodnight
i cry in the darkness
‘cause somethings not right

but the way that i love
each time that your gone
i can’t help but wonder
where’d i go  wrong

had i loved you too much
had i loved you just right
should i hold on forever
or let go tonight

(chorus) ‘cause i loved you forever
but its time to let go
i can’t keep on faking
the love you won’t show

i’d hate to forget you
to leave you behind
but i can’t keep on wishing
the years will rewind

I can’t hope for something
thats never been there
my dreams have been shattered
‘cause you’ve never cared.

you took me for granted
but begged me to stay
i can’t keep on hoping
you’ll love me one day

(chorus)so baby, my baby
we’ve fallen apart
theres something inside me
thats breaking my heart

i know that you’re sorry
as much as i am
i have to be honest
you’re not the right man

but theres nothing to change it
i just want you to know
good-bye my sweet darling its time  that i go....I wrote this the day before i broke up with my boyfriend. And it's weird because now everything reminds me of him, and i miss him... :(
