I'm past 180 today,
not going to make it,
and that thought makes
me smile, doesn't matter
if I'm stopped short,
because I already know,
and what I know, doesn't
scare me.
I guess it's just something
you learn, to recognise the times
when you won't make it,
a life skill whose path is
paved with many pains,
or a road full of holes
you can't avoid.
I'm past 180 today, but
still I'll try to get
there, that's just part of me.This deserves a little explanation:
The sad part of me has worked out that at a certain set of traffic lights on the way to work, if you are past house number 180, than you won't make it.
