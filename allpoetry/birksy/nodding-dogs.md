It's just my luck to see
the dog not nodding his head at me,
instead it shakes indiscriminately.

I wondered what it meant,
perhaps just his neck was bent,
perhaps the shelf where it sat, leant.

Of course I'll never know,
nodding dogs are always on the go,
so I turned, walked slowly home.

If nodding dogs shake their head,
best I go straight back to bed,
not another word had to be said.
