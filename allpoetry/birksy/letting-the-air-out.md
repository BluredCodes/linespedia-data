title: "Letting the Air Out"

In my hands a cushion, blown
up before we did, full of air
that I breathed when we were
still together. So now I
release the valve, squeeze it
tight, hear the whistle, let
it mix with the stale stuff
now we're apart.
And when it's finished, when
the plastic is creased into
itself, I drop it in the bin,
and hold my breath until I leave.
