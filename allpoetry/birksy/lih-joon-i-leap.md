I leap, fall down, 
pretend I banged my knee, 
ask for help, look into your 
eyes, will you treat me, 
will you believe that 
I want you near, I will 
strain myself to get you, 
and when muscles tear, I 
will crawl, eyes plead 
with my despair, don't 
leave me down here, 
without you.Dedicated to mystysaint
