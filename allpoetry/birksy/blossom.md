There's petals on my lap,
didn't know I was in blossom,
but then I've been so barren,
I guess it was time for me.
I like this new found flourish,
it's a while since I felt okay,
never thought anything wrong,
I guess that's how life fails.
Wonder if there'll be some fruit,
oh, dear, I don't like bees and wasps,
perhaps I'll shake these petals down,
go back to being buried in the ground.
But I'll sway like the breeze,
attract... who really knows,
but that's not a problem when
my seeds are all I care to grow.
