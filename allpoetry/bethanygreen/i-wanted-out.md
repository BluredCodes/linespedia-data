title: "I Wanted Out"

I wish I would of known
I had always trusted you
I thought I was alone
And then I turned around to see you

You grabbed me by the arms
And tried to force me down
I tried forever to pull away
But you just kept your ground

You started touching me
I wanted it to stop
But you wouldn’t let me free
Dead, I wished you would drop

You looked at me and grinned
In a sly and scary way
I thought you were my friend
When I went in your room that day.

I told you I didn’t like it
And you looked at me once more
I tried to move away
As I reached for the door

You wouldn’t let go of me
And I started to get scared
That wasn’t the place I wanted to be
Cause I knew you didn’t really care

You looked into my eyes
“He won’t know,” you said.
He didn’t hear my cries
So my clothes you tried to shed

Why didn’t you leave me alone?
When you knew it wasn’t right
Your trueself you had shown
As you held me down that night

             
                           --- Bethany Green
