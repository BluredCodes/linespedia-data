title: "Mine"
writer: "Cinderella"

And I smile to myself, 
because I realize that it dosn't matter.

I smile, 
because I know that for me, 
there is a "One."

I grin, 
because I know that whoever and whatever I encounter now,
is just making me want Him more.

No, he's not here now,
and No, he dosn't need to be.

I still have years to mature,
and years to know myself.

But when I look into his eyes,
I know this past will fade,
and our new future, will come.

So I smile,
on all the love I share now, 
all the questions and plans,
and I know that I do have that "someone" out there.

And when he comes, 
I'll smile at the past I learned from
and the maturity I gained.
