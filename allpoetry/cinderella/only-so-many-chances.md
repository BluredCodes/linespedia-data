title: "Only So Many Chances"

You have only one life to live,
you only have so many chances to take.

You have so many mistakes to make,
so try to make them happy ones.

Live for right now,
and let even the bad times be bittersweet.

Don't let go now,
Dive right in, dive in head first.

Don't worry about their silly little heads,
and ignore their jealous stares.

It's all the same in the end,
So make the ride a good one.

Be yourself,
