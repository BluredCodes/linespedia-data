You
 
I could be with you forever. 
If I were to choose one,
it would be you.

You make my heart soar,
you make me go weak.

When I am lonely, or sad,
I turn to you.
 
When I am happy, 
I share it with you.

When someone needs comfort, 
I take you there.

When a soul needs mending,
you are the healer.
