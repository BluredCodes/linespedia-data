If I had a million days,
I couldn't tell you.

If I had a million pages to write on,
I couldn't tell our story.

If I had  ship,
I couldn't sail it over the oceans of our love.

If I had  power of light,
I couldn't share the light of our connection.

If I had a million jewels,
none could compare to the feeling you give to me.

Don't you know I lay my heart at the foot of your essence.

I know for a million jewels, the power of light, and a million days, I would give up none for you.
