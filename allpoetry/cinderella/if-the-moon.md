---
title: "If the Moon"
writer: "Cinderella"
slug: "if-the-moon"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57840-If-the-Moon-by-Cinderella"
---

If the moon was never again to shine,
I would tell you 10,000 more times.

If the stars all fell right now,
I wouldn't regret what we shared.

If the sun was never to rise,
I wouldn't take back our words.

If I was to go blind,
I would not forget that face.

If the ground were to swallow me whole,
I would think about you the long way down.

And if the moon was never again to shine,
I would tell you 10,000 more times.
