Is it just me, or is alot of this crap?

People are so fast to judge,
not slow to react.

Does cursing in the face of wasted talent,
make you feel better about yourself?

Does wasting your time on unimportant words that won't make a difference,
make you think you're a better person?

If feeding the hungry is so important to you,
stop being fake, and maybe try a little harder.

Stop writing false sonetts, get your ass off you're couch,
and go do something rather than being a hypocritical half-wit.Im sorry about this one...it's less of a poem than me venting about self-rightous, dolt, foolish people who are such hypocrits...does anyone else see that this world is dying but me?>?? and here we are wasting our time on our nice expensive shiny laptops.....i feel like im wasting my time as well.
