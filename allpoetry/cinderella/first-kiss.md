There he is now,
sitting here across from me.

We don't have alot to say,
but there's something about him.

It dosn't take alot to make me blush,
but one thing for sure, is his touch.

This is why I have to kiss him.

This is our moment.  We have to take it.

We can't ignore.  I can't look at him the same.
This is it.

What's going on in my stomach?? What's going on in my chest??
