THE MAN SLOUCHES, 
                  POSSES AN ACCENT 
                     SIPS A SHORT BLACK. 

MECHANICAL THINGS ROLL BY, THERE SILVER GEOMETRIC SHAPES REFLECT A CITY SCAPE. 

WE LOOK AT THE OBJECT WITH OUR EYES CLOSED 
OUR MOUTHS OPEN ANTICIPATING 
PENITRATION. 

OUR BODY PARTS EXPOSED TO THE NOTE TAKING AUDIENCE OF PESSIMISTIC FREAKS. 

NO TIME FOR DYING ANYMORE 
FORGOT HOW TO FLY. 

BANG WOOD WITH CLENTCHED FIST 
AND BUY ANOTHER SHORT TRIP TO THE ALYSSUM.
