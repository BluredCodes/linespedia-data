NOTHING LEFT TO GIVE OR TAKE 

WAIT, BREAK 

LIGHTENING SHATTERED SKIES TELL ME, THAT THE BREAKABLE CAN BE MENDED 

WITH GENTLE HANDS AND THOUGHTS THAT TASTE OF CANDY 
SOUL FOOD 
RIPPLES AS SKIES PART AND WHITE LIGHT BEAMS. 

STANDING AT THE EDGE OF THE MOUNTAIN 
RISKING ALL 
THROWING EVERYTHING AWAY SO YOU CAN KEEP YOURSELF.
