WHERE DID THIS BLACK DAY COME FROM? 
THIS DAY THAT LAYS BEFORE ME, 
STRETCHED AND UGLY IN FORM. 
WRINKLED OLD SKIN, BIG ROUGH HANDS. 

BLACKNESS AND EVIL OZZING FROM EVERY PORE AND EVERY OPENING.
