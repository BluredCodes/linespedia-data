As the dragon begins to awaken during lands globally shaken,
We see this sign seen of a time long ago as dragons are awaken,
For great pressure is now applied to release such great fury of fire and fear,
As these few who surround this dragon named Israel sets this great stage so clear,

During this time for it shall quicken each scene as shocking and unbelievable to see,
With these changes in both mind and soul of many during now and after is meant to be,
Read these words as inspiration as they come from a simple site off cnn.com so be strong,
No mistake is to be made of a future unwritten filled with wrong days and nights so long,

Even with that said this dragon who is awakening now has been trapped for many years,
During such a struggle of blood that washed over land is washed away by great tears,
Still this dragon being enraged could not hold back after such times of great pains,
As enraged as a dragon could be again a great power shall restrain this fury in its veins,

As each giant step of the dragon crumble all in its path to its main course at seven,
This second dragon enraged during nine eleven will bring great fires from heaven,
With this cleansing of blood from a united stand to bring again fire upon Iraqi sand,
Then will come his next meal an area north above a morning calm to reclaim this land.Dedicated to Isreal.
