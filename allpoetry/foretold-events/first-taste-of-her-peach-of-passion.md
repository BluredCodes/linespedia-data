My first taste of her peach of passion comes,
With many years waited till my fire of desire begun,
As I hear her voice my heart beats one hundred fold,
I slowly lick her sweet juicy fruit she shakes as if cold,
With her first great cries of pure hot and lustful pleasure,
I cherish each spicy and tasteful drop of her luscious treasure,
As I deeply indulge another long taste of her peach of passion,
I finally hear her scream of infinite climax to a fore played fashion.
