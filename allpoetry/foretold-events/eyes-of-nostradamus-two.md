Eyes of Nostradamus (Part Two)
By: D. Clark

Begins this first challenge of the first year new age, 
From the eastern sands engulf a great city, 
Anguish bleeding hearts united from seven serpents, 
Furious fire falls back upon these sands, blood thirst quenched.

When it’s the second year of the new age,
Beasts will awake to fiercely shake united lands forsaken,
When this will bring three wounds to understand to divide a land,
Blood red moon he shall see soon around or before a time of June.

Wickedness of a leader in tongues binds such bitter taste,
Those of youth with a mind beyond our time become,
Upside down will be the mother during her painful labor,
Her children will be bare the pains as well of such until seen.

Each flock of the raven will prance around the fear of still,
Break the latch without the key open his cell barks hounds of hell,
Lust at dusk bleeds your heart with his breath of poison onto many,
Cast his torch to the sand onto holy land mercy no bloody hand.
