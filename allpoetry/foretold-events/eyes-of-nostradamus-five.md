By: D. Clark


Date comes in play during this transformational delay,
For the date is the one-hundredth year new age time’s dismay,
Her first steps of each child reborn from death after long times,
For this land has changed, with all life recycled and cleansed minds.

Rise of this leader as with the towers of light during this changed night,
His kingdom has expanded from mother to other brothers insight,
With the luminous flight took on instant our momentous steps across our nine,
 So long a trip that beauty and age its self was left behind as well as time.

Return of disciples from journeys end for he will know of not his foe,
Radiant sight of blinding light of a spectacular night of sacred sands flow,
This clue will become true as pyramids of not only earth massively show,
Each stone placed if known over four thousand score, as this land will glow.
 
Unites this tribe past our ninth brother to his long departed mother,
Sudden inclined speak this peace of mind of distant brother to another,
Shall he free passage of mind acceptance becomes magnificence of wonder,
Never again will question any friend for each trial earned after awaited thunder.one-hundredth year new age -> 2100 A.D.
