By: Virgil K. Reed 

written: 12-03-00 


Celestial bodies collide, then asteroids abound. 
At lands end, bottomless oceans come to surround. 
Aged Anunnaki, giant, ancient men- 
cryptic beginning, oblivious end? 
Travelling onward, one day to return- 
to judge man & all he has not learned? 
It is said they will arrive from the Southern sky; 
perhaps they'll separate man's truth from all of man's lie. 
Upward we'll look, point, and then proclaim: 
“What is that, who are they, what is their name?” 
Alas, its merely those who from heaven to earth came.By: Virgil K. Reed, written: 12-03-00
