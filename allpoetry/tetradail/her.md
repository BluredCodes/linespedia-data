Not only those who know
     showing those ways
     ways too expose the truth 
     to life, like confronting faces
     of the masses making makers make masks
     Have you found things to be true 
     if you only Knew the things you might know
     Patterns that matter 
     in dreams that seem
     obscure by noise in muse 
     they use these things
     You cannot let a lier lie to you  
     For the you won't know whats true
     And if everybody told you that
     Would you say what they say too?
     Everybodies open mind is brought up
     on others lives
     The fabrication of a fibbers fib
     just thrives on lives fed lies
