Once there lived a maze
   that puzzled the minds who gazed
   at the sights they saw before
   confused they asked for more
   Where to know to go the 
   the lives we chase they 
   seem to show 
   the things we seem to be 
   the way we are today
   too chase away 
   the things they say
   with what we think to be
   Lost in a thought
   you've finally found yourself
   going in circles on time you've delt
   post potential energy for pondering
   energy stored for endless wandering
   and not knowing what had become
   lifes labrynth unscrolled a sum
