Object: one person starts out with saying " one fat hen"
         then the next person and so on
          then you go to two ( as in 1, then 1,2 then 1,2,3 and so on)
          until you get to ten
           If you mess up on the saying 
             you drink until you get it right
             WARNING this is a tounge twister/memory game
              the difficulty increases the more you mess up



             1.One fat hen
             2.Two skinny ducks
             3.Three brown bear
             4.Four runing hare
             5.Five simple simon sitting on a fence post smilling
             6.Six sinfull maidens swiming in a brook
             7.Seven I slit a sheet, a sheet I slit upon a 
             slitted sheet I sit
             8.Eight fiddlers fiddling on a fiddlers fiddle
             9.Nine  One smart man felt smart, two smart men felt
             smart, three smart men felt smart they all felt smart
             10.Ten  I'm not a fig plucker, nor a fig pluckers son
             but i'll pluck figs till the fig pluckins done
