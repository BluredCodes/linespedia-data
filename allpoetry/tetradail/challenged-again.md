Focusing on a vibrating tone 
       alone and unbalanced
       a frustrating challenge
       to hold upon whom
       which would think it was good

       Needless to say 
       the feedback drove it away
       to the palace 
       of reflected fears
       a reality much to strong
       for specific sound
       in open noise

       The silence
       built to last 
       grows smoothly in
       the palace of reflected fears
       roots deep underground
       the noise of rain
       sings to the 
       sane minds sound
