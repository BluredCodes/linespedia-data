I have to write an American song
I wouldn't have to think for long
So, many things to sing about
When, I look up sitting in class
I see the flag outside waving
Singing it's own song

Everything you could sing about
Is out there whistling its own tune
So, it's not objects I sing about
It's our thoughts that really matter
It's our feelings that urge us
To hang that flag up high

It's not in the rules
That would should hang the flag at school
It's not written down
That the flags should be flown
It's our choice
We do it on our own!!!This was written by a friend of mine. She is in the 11th grade. Please give any comments that you feel nessessary :) Thank you all for reading. Lee
