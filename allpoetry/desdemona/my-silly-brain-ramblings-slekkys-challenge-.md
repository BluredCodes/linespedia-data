As I sit here 
Writing a "A Letter to Jaiquai" 
Eating "crackers and cheese" 
Stroking "my pet turtle" 
I wonder to myself if "I'm crazy" 
I live in my own "total bliss" of insanity 
Seeking "sweet revenge" on those who "love me" 

"Dost thou" love me turtle? 
"thou shall not judge" you silly mammal 
*SMACK* 

"Way down deep"" a piece of my heart" unfolds 
"Where hope abides" within my time 
"Give me strength" heart to see through my mind 
"I know peace within your embrace" will enlighten it 
Then I will become "the perfect woman" 
Which is "my greatest fear" of all 

Until I can find what I was looking for 
I think I will call up a friend 
It is her Birthday today
