---
title: "I Can't Wait"
writer: "The Natalya Project"

If I had had the nerve I would have held your hand,
I would have kissed you until you melted.
Nonetheless, tonight was beautiful.
Almost as beautiful as the girl in my head.
Almost as beautiful as you.

Did you notice that I drove all the way home in the slow lane?
I did not want tonight to end.  I did not want to say goodnight.
Now I'm home and I'm wishing that I had told you how I felt.
But I think you know.

The next time that I see you, I will hold your hand.
I will kiss your lips until they tingle.
I will fall asleep with your head on my chest.
I will wake up and see you smiling in your sleep.
I can't wait.I don't even care about the poetry.  Tonight was poetry.  My life is poetry.
