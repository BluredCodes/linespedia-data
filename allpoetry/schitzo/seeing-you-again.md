---
title: "Seeing you again"
writer: "Schitzo"
slug: "seeing-you-again"
source: "AllPoetry"

Waiting to see you again,
my mind goes through anguished states.
Seeing you again will help me
through hard times, settle my mind
answer my questions.

I know is hasn’t been a long time
but it feels like an eternity.
Failure is never an exception
(when you love someone), failure can 
never be found in the heart of a loved one.

I will never fail, I will always 
love you, with all my heart.

Seeing you again will help me
remember all the love that we have shared
for so long.origionaly written 1-7-02
