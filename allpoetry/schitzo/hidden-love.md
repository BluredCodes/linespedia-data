---
title: "Hidden love"
writer: "Schitzo"
slug: "hidden-love"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59570-Hidden-love-by-Schitzo"
---

The hidden love that you have for him,
far surpasses the possibilty
of love for someone else.

These things are hard to make it through,
but it is easier than you think.

Just because he might have loved you,
his heart may never heal.

You may be justified,
for what you did it for,
but one day your hidden love 
just may come out.this is about one of my friends but it was just a feeling not a factuality
