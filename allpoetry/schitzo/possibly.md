I don't think we could be further apart,
because of me,
You don't trust my heart.
What goes through your mind,
when you just blow me off?

You think of him,
ensted of me, now.
How come all this efects me so?
It's because, I love you,
and I always will.

Spears through my heart,
pearce through me like a bullet,
shot from a shotgun,
at point blank range.
My heart is in shreds,
It can never find settlement.

You have made up your mind,
to be with him, and not with me,
I am in this situation,
this situation of knowing,
but not being known.
How come?

You said you would always support me.
well now more than ever,
I NEED your support.
I never thought I would go through this,
you choosing someone over me.
you use to love me so much,
so much that you couldn't say.

There was a time that you were happy,
with me.
But I believe that time has passed,
so I will try to move on,
as you have with no trouble.
It doesn't bother you to change your mind,
but it bothers me to change mine.wrote on 2/8/02
after final break up
