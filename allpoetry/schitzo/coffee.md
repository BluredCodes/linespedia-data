guzzling coffee
burning taste buds off
wating for your intriguing call

sugar settles at the bottom
with the aroma of hazlenut
flots through the air

you talk of matrimonial blizz
when you finaly call
still not saying what you are painting

one day you will 
and i will see the great masterpiece

i may just be babbling
but oh well you can live with itthis is more or less a babble
