---
title: "Was  Love Wasn't"
writer: "Piper"
slug: "was-love-wasnt"

~**~


Love, giving and kind
Creating soft reflections
Eye's image divine.

~**~

Surface love suffers
Rubbed roughly with disregard
No gloss, no shine...gone.

~**~

Sadly curled alone
Head drooped down in its sorrow
Years of hope destroyed

~**~

No yesterdays and no tomorrows: (
