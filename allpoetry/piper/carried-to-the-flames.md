******

I was split 
like a cord of wood 
dried from too much sun, 
carried to the flames,
and then thrown in.

Burning fast 
from your vengeance,
just because
my imperfections
brought on
that wicked grin,
chilling thoughts 
ran off with my brain.

I knew that I was
no longer good enough 
to adorn your mantel
and sure enough,
you discarded me, 
and sacrificed my love
to the junkyard
of your heart.

Now, as I burn 
I wonder if 
the burst of light 
you've made with me, 
will appease your gnawing
hunger to destroy,

Or if you will stalk 
and punish others 
throwing more souls 
into your tantrum mind,
dwelling in the caverns 
of your despair.
