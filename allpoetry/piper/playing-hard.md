---
title: "Playing Hard"
writer: "Piper"
slug: "playing-hard"

Spank me.
Make my cheeks burn.
I have been very bad.
Daddy, please do what you must,
I understand.

While you're there near my rear,
Could you do a little something
Just because I'm dear?

Give me a pinch
Where I'm round and firm.
Add a little caressing 
For my open nerve.

Give me everything you have.
Assert your dominant position.
Have your way, because 
I am yours for play.

Wow! That feels great, 
But maybe that's bad.
Maybe you should spank me
Just for being good,
Instead....so....

What do you want to play next?
Doctor and nurse?
