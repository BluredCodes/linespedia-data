---
title: "Dark v.s. Light"
writer: "badgirl0731"
slug: "dark-vs-light"
source: "AllPoetry"

The darkness engulfs us all
making the sunlight weaken and fall
it changes your personality
could this be reality?
maybe it's true the world isn't good
I mean look at our society today!
Maybe the sunlight is just a mirage
A dream, the way people want the world to be
Everyday the darkness grows
filling the heart of every soul

Maybe the world was really light
But people chose wrong over right
They stopped caring
And their children did too
Could this be how the darkness grew?

No one knows for sure but if we give up hope
The darkness will take over light
And the world will be full of violence and hatred
Not even a little bit of hope will make it
I'm not asking for a light blinding gleam
I just want to live in a world somewhere in between
