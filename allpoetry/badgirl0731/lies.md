There's no escaping
instead of seeing the world i see you
giving false hopes without reality
crushing hopes and my future life
don't you see what you've done to me?
oh but i think you know you're killing
not only me but others too
how could i believe in you?
I thought you'd bring me energy and life
but now I'm miserable and full of strife
I lost friends nd losing family members
because of you
what i thought was "innocent fun"
took away people's lives
you hid the truth 
so i would believe you
"everyone does it"
"it will make you cool"
the truth is 
not everyone does it
and because I believed you
I'm doing it too
