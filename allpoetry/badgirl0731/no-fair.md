it's not fair that you make up two categories for girls-
slut and prude
you use one for your own 
desire but behind her back you don't approve
you tease the other and call her names
just because she won't give into your games
Is it so hard to see
that most girls are in between
but to you that's not true
because you think once she crosses first base
she's through
no longer is she prude
but considered something profane and rude
but you could do exactly that
and you would get a pat on the back
you would be considered "so hot"
but boy you're really not
maybe if you think about what you do
you'd realize girls get reputations too
bad ones...because of guys like you
