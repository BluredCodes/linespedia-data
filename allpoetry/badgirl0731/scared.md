I'm sorry I don't say it to you
But I love you, with all my heart-
That's the truth
I'm just a little scared
Those words are just so hard to say
It's easy to write
even easier to type
"I love you-I love you"
It's fun to do our "143" game
But somehow it's not the same
but don't give up on me
just wait you'll see
I'll say "I love you", nice and loud
...and I'll say it proud
