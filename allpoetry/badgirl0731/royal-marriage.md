Even though our wedding is one day away
i'm afraid to say
those two little words that matter so much
i touch my eyes, they start to tear
I know this is going to be one special year
My life is going to change forever
but at least i'll have you by my side
helping me be strong,my love and my guide

I fall into sleep dreaming of the past
we were best friends but that was changing fast
soon we were in love and together at last
but then you pulled away
and you broke my heart
we were now seperated, apart
i moved on
but i guess you didn't 
you looked at me with regret in your eyes
i looked away,i was tired of your lies
then a few days later you told me you missed me
and you were just afraid of where we might lead
i forgave you and you made me queen

As i awake
for this glorious day
I remember to thank God
so i pray
walking down the aisle with my daddy
he's so proud
I pass mom and give her a smile
she smiles back with happy tears flowing down....
Now i'm facing you 
I can see the love in your eyes
and i'm glad i chose you
"I do"
now i'm finally married 
to my one love...
My King
