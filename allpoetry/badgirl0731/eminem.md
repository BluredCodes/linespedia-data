i don't care how everyone else views you
i'm seeing you with my eyes open 
you may seem mean and rude
and hey i admit some stuff you say
it's not okay
but it's the truth
and no one tells it like you
no matter how many times you're banned
or how much you curse-damn
you are my idol and i'm your fan
maybe not as big as stan
but i know what's real and 
what's not
i'm loving the way you just don't care
and you don't care how people see you
let them stare!
i want to be as strong as you are
you might think i'm crazy
probably thinking "girl don't you dare"
maybe i can't rap like you 
but when i'm older i'm gonna be just like you
my parents think it's crazy
some of my friends dislike you
my boyfriend thinks you're cool
but not as big as Tupac or Ja Rule
but me i'm gonna be loyal to the end
you just wait Eminem
i'll catch up to you soon!Eminem deserves some respect!
