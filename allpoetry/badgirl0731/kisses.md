Remember last night?
right after our fight...
I was upset and mad
because you're friends were talking about me
...talking trash
I ran off, you ran after me
and i saw that it hurt you
just like it hurt me
you apologized
oh so many times
you gave me a kiss on the lips too
so I forgave you
we went inside and you pulled me close to you
and you told me your love  for me will always be true
after that your lips found mine
and the kiss was divine
we both needed each other
and there was one kiss after another
I didn't want to leave you, not ever
I wanted to be with you forever
maybe that's how a kiss is supposed to make you feel
It also makes you realize that love is real
