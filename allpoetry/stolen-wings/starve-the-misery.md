---
title: "Starve the misery"
writer: "Stolen Wings"
slug: "starve-the-misery"
source: "AllPoetry"

Damn the silence that overwhelms me when I sleep
Crack it open with sobs and screams
And yet forget to wake.

Damn the ever following darkness
Stabbing and burning at my flesh
Break down and start to shake.

Damn the people who pretend to care
They strangle with their cold hard stares
I have no tears left to give.

Damn the hollow they call a soul
Starve the misery, drowning and cold
Damning the will to live.Hmmm..Eating disorders anyone?
