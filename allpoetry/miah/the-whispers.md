title: "The Whispers"

A hint of love in every word,
Sometimes soft, but always heard.
A subtle grin with my mistake,
Yet, an open heart for you to take.
A gesture you will see again,
As love springs out from deep within.
Something you can't turn around;
It never leaves once it is found.
A hug given as you walk by.
A whisper that can never lie.
Each moment that is used to speak,
The same messages of which we seek.
In the shadow there comes a light
To guide us through our darkest night.
And when we think all hope is gone,
That special someone comes along.
To bring us up from the dark and deep,
A soft voice that we strive to keep;
Those whispers throughout our entire life,
That heals with love and rids the strife.

May the "Whispers" be with you . . .07-31-01,
