title: "Flying Low"
writer: "Brittany"

stars are lying low tonight
I feel I can reach to touch
stars are flying low again
how long till they fall?

I'm down and out
can't help to pick back up
I'm down for the count
confused and beaten

stars are flying low tonight
looking at me with their solemn eyes
staring at me once again
please just let me upi don't know.. it's a depressing day
this is all that came out
