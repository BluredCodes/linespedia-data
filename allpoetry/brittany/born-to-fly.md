don't listen to a word I say
you wouldn't understand me anyway
don't try to understand what I say
you wouldn't hear me anyway
I'm over your head
and under your toes
I'm too good for you now
so leave and let me go

*born to fly
born to glide
over your head
and under your toes
you see me now
OOPS...
where'd I go?*

OOPS I did it again
I think of it now and then
don't let it bother me
no one would ever agree
I'm over your head
and under your toes
you'll never understand me
so leave and let me go

*born to fly
born to glide
over your head
and under your toes
you see me now
OOPS...
where'd I go?*

invisible to humanity
dying to be free as me
how can you all live
and still never give
I'm over your head
and under your toes
I was always born to fly
so leave and let me go

*born to fly
born to glide
over your head
and under your toes
you see me now
OOPS...
where'd you go?*meh.. i don't know what this is..
it's something.. but i'm not exactly sure of what that is!
