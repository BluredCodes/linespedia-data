Chinese Rug

A tapestry teasing
to the eye and mind
colors blended
weaving worlds together

Empires embroidered 
red squares, golden eyes
and dragon fire
stitched together
for feet to feel

are embers embedded in the coal-like
black fibers?
etchings that are burnt to memory
by historical teachings
rolled onto parchment, then fabric 

aged linens
laying upon floors

how many nights of practiced
Kama-Sutra
are absorbed here

if these lips traced in red thread
could talk
what stories would unravel
from ancient times traveled
