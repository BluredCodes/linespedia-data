Can I?

In singleness I found you
sitting, listening to the music
watching couples dancing, smiling
dreaming yourself on tripping the light fantastic
with a man to match your moves
and I asked
Can I?

The night passed by quickly
we shared conversation
and smiles
I wanted to see you again, to hear your voice
I presented the question again
Can I?

as we spent time together
learning of each other
our dreams and fears
laughter and tears that were parts of us
I knew I wanted to know everything about you
Can I?

you too have been hurt
and trust has been broken
we look for the utopia of a relationship
that feels good, natural
and share in life and living
Can I?

as time passes
and we grow older
daylight sharing walks and talks
and keeping you warm in the cold of the night
being there with you
I ask
Can I?
