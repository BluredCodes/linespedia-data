I am Thinking

I am thinking
no more 
on serious things
for life is going to move forward
no matter what desires I may have

Love may come
yet, may not
shall I try to stop the day!
to have a moment of happiness,
Or let ships take courses
perhaps to berth together?

I am thinking
what is happiness?
Is it wealth
Is it health
is it passion unbridled between lovers
or is it knowing that this moment I live
and have gifts

Gifts of the knowledge obtained
by living each day
Gifts of love shared
and also of the hurts healed from
when things did not go the way
planned

and that caused sorrow
So why plan what may never come
I am thinking I shall just let be what is
and if I shall find this moment wondrous
than I shall be content.
