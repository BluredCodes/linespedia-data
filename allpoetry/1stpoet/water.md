Water

I looked out upon the lake
to the fountain
a tornado shaped volume of water
sending a volcano of whispers into the air
As I saw each bead cascade to the surface
a bullet sending splashes sprawling towards space

this vortex of spray
held back by gravity from reaching the clouds
rained a harmonious echo to my ears
I pictured each diamond-like droplet
as miniature rainbows I could wish upon
and the lake a treasure to swim in

it shimmers as gold with the sun lapping
upon its surface
It resembles a dance as the fountain unfurls 
waves as sails are satiated by wind

I rest.
