Think

Think no more of the past
the water under the bridge
but focus on what lies ahead
the future viewed from the ridge

there is the unknown that is just showing
like the sun upon the rise
we know not what tomorrow brings
it shall be a surprise

will one find love within
or search forever more
one knows not of these answers
and what does wait in store

we hope upon what is not seen
and ponder on what was
can fate be made to change a course
I do hope so because

I have been waiting, within my mind
for completeness in my life
and I hope that I may find rewards
reaped from all the toils and strife

in conversation and in kisses
I think on where I am
for I know that with thee woman
I am a better man.

for I think no more of  the past
the water under the bridge
I am thinking on what may lie ahead
as I stand upon this ridge

a mountain I have climbed
and reached these bluest skies
and I see the life of springtime
in the green glow of your eyes.
