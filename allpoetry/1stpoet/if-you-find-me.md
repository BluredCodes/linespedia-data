If You Find Me

If you find me naked
I ask only for warmth
perhaps a blanket
a shroud to protect me
from elements
fear, and pain
Can bare skin and souls
be kept from that which will burn
or cut, freeze?

If you find me hungry
what will stop the pangs 
of desire
and emptiness within a body
and mind
searching for sustenance both physical
and emotional
Will I be satiated by sensuality?

If you find me thirsting
shed tears of joy to my parched lips
the entrance to a desert
waiting again for growth
I blow like sand, drifting
alone
will I form a dune
for you to climb
claiming me as your own

If you find me sleeping
ask
am I dreaming of you
for if I am
let me sleep, contented
in my bliss
Or wake me with a kiss
then clothe me, feed me, and quench my every desire.
