Alabaster Beauty

In your skin
of alabaster tone
I follow your formings
texturing muscle and bone

from your foot up your leg
my fingers do trace
and your moanings cry and beg
as I tenderly stroke your face

in palming of your breasts
and teasing nipples of pink
your mind does leave its refuge
for of nothing do you think

I kiss your lips slightly parted
and find the wetness of your tongue
we could grow together older
yet feel forever young

my alabaster beauty
statuesque and carved for me
I see the love within your heart
you shine with purity

skin that glows within the moonlight
star fire shines within your eye
I need no wings for lift my dear
for your presence makes me fly

breathing, touching, and so much more
a doll that truly lives
to treasure you forever
all I have I would surely give

William S. Dawes
