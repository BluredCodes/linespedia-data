Barstool Barbie

There she was looking fantastic
living and breathing
yet still seeming plastic

an object of envy of every guy
could they wine her and dine her
to make their spirits fly

porcelain skin, with shimmering blonde hair
with lust they craved her
wanting her legs in the air

she was a perfect woman with breasts firm and round
and those press-formed lips
ne’er uttered a sound

but she sat there, almost as if looking fake
no one dared approach her
for with her sat Ken, with a body by Jake

molded and sculpted with man’s eye in mind
girls would mirror and try to become
an illusion that was transcendence, one of a kind

barstool Barbies are seen everywhere
when alcohol blinds
and there is smoke in the air

an image or dream to be put on a shelf
most have searched for this person
I know I have myself

yet reality came and woke me to see
that I do not want plastic, or something not live
I desire love and warmth returned back to me
