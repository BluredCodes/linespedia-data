Never Did it

I like went to the computer
and thought, like hey
I can write a poem that someone might like
but, like, I can’t like get my thoughts together

if writing is like painting
my brush like missed the canvas
and the words just dripped
from my pen to the floor
pooling and looking like blood
from a dying brain

like
hey, I could finger paint on the floor
and draw out the demons
that like transfigure into
figures fermenting in a ruptured thought

but since I like did this
it never happened.

William S. Dawesjust being valley, and a bit of wine tonight.
go figure.
