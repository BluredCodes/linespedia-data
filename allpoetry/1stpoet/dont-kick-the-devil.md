Don’t Kick the Devil

Tempting fate
tempting is the thought
to control
to form one’s own world
and rule it

how does one
truly
transform that which is around
and conform normalcy into insanity

the world is a crazy place
no rhyme or reason
except in poetry
where there is some structure
to the chaos we paint with words

in pain we ponder
we swim in fear
we float on dreams
and crash when we awaken to the sounds
of voices within our minds

our own little demon
tempting us
teasing us to write
we are vexed to venture to the unknown
of what words we will write

I do not kick the devil
but I do thank my muse
for these stirrings that never stop
and the tasty torture of screaming
when the words again speak
