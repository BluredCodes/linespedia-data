title: "Gettin Jiggy"

I am watching the awards
and seeing dreads
on heads
beads swaying
as the hip-hop beat
moves hips and heads
in rhythm

Rappa gangsta
with the beebop
slang, hangin
words on the tongue

clappin people
thinkin along
with the hood story
kinda gory
about a bro
hit when a cap
popped

or lovin
huggin and making sweet
sweat
rolling upon satin
sheets

William S. Daweswatching the awards and shaggy is singing
