Raindrops in the Lake

Sitting watching the rings
dance upon the lake
I wonder of the magic
that brings things full circle

level and calm
storm tossed or frozen
the lake remains a lake
bounded by shoreline

in summer heat
volumes invisible
ascend
yet one does not see the change

then in the coolness
of the shower
the waters return
refreshing

Love is like that
when evaporating,  one is left 
lower in spirit

when it is showered upon
(one)
the rebirth
brings flood.

William S. Dawes
