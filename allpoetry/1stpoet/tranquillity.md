Tranquillity

Seven AM
the fog is lifting
bodies from slumber
now start shifting

in the night
the mist was in your eyes
as we kissed
and I heard your soft sighs

we melted like chocolate
into each other
hands touching skin
light as a feather

on the moon
is a place
called tranquillity base

last night I went there
no longer earth bound
I orbited you
and landed softly

I felt weightless
time stopped
for those brief moments

two heavenly bodies
I, your sun
You, my moon

I give you light
you reflect it
I give you warmth
you absorb it

and you forever
will be 
pulled
by my gravity.

William S. Dawes
1st Poet
