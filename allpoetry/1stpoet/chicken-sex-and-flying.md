Chicken Sex and Flying

I may be in a funk
sorta on a wine drunk
so I am thinking perhaps like a nerd
about beans and being a bird

when chickens have sex
there is no foreplay
roosters would peck a breast to death
if it had a nipple

then the other thought I had
was if I lost a lot of weight
I could eat bean burritos
and fly
