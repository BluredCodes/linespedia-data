title: "Undying Blood"
writer: "Lylorn"

coarse, dried blood,
tasteless and undying,
flowing thru collapsed veins
to agonizing flesh,
tortured upon death's door
for refusal to surrender
to this horrid pain,
called life.yawn...girls are evil.
comments however are welcome.
