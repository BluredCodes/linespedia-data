Sky Harbor Airport.
Here i am.
M'lady's head upon my shoulder.
Mine, upon hers.
No words, just sitting,
holding hands,
leaning on each other.
My gaze out the window on the runway,
but my focus on m'lady's reflection.
infinite stuff left to say
so damned much to do
and so desperatly little time...
but neither of us let the moment die.
so we sat in silence
wondrous silence
the silence that said it all.

a boarding call
one last embrace
a goodbye
and a great moments end.
a tear in m'lady's eye as we part,
to match my single tear from the heart.
