delicate purple rose,
broken upon the ground.

euphoric scent lingers,
amongst the scattered petals.

thorns revealed,
beneath lost blossom.

a rose stained of blood,
and three tears shed.i'm far too much of a perfectionist to consider this one done, but i wouldnt be surprized if i never work with it again, or just end up deleting it.  Whatever.
