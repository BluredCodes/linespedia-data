title: "The fast forward button"
writer: "Enraptured"

I wait and wonder, wonder and wait.
Why then can I not know? 
Can I press fast forward, just see a glimpse of what is to come?
Details are not important, 
Just the comfort of knowing.
Having the chance to see. 

And would it ruin everything?
All enjoyment, all surprise.
What happens then?

If we will know while we embrace, that we create a story...

The story may change, 
The times my fold.
Twist and lose form.
