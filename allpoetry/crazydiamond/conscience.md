title: "Conscience"
writer: "CRaZyDiAmOnD"

within my hand, i hold the cure
i have a way to make all that is uncertain 
become all too sure
i know the answer 
to every question you may ask
i know the quickest way 
to complete every task
i know your darkest secrets,
and all your worst fears
your thoughts and words unspoken,
and all your wasted years
you're not that hard to read,
i know all you try to hide
i know about your sins,
and all the times you've lied 
i'll always be steady aside 
as you try to run away
i was with you at your birth 
and i'll be there til the last day
if you learn to listen, 
i could be your best friend
but if you don't acknowledge me
i will be your end
