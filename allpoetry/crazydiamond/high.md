title: "high"

misty illusions storm around my brain
the smoke leaves me with emptiness 
and nothing left to gain
but for now it is my only love,
my only love. . .
peace and joy consume me
and bless this very hand
to take away the pain
and give me strength to stand
everything united,
every soul as one
darkness takes control
i'm beginning to feel numb
death has now become me
but my buzz has just begun
