---
title: "in vain"
writer: "CRaZyDiAmOnD"
slug: "in-vain"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60718-in-vain-by-CRaZyDiAmOnD"
---

sleepy souls and weary eyes
these transparent people don't hear the lies
accepting the fear they cradle us with 
treating life as a mission
not as a gift
rising to the top, not on a dream
but on society's expectations
of what they should be
ultimate submission, to what they know is wrong
they see this as a race,
that's beginning to seem long
into the night, their demons prevail
they can no longer fight it 
they know they have failed 
their days go by slowly now,
with nothing to do
they are left all alone,
with nothing to prove
