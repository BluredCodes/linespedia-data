title: "baggage claim"
writer: "CRaZyDiAmOnD"
slug: "baggage-claim"

don't forget your suitcase, love
your soul is neatly packed inside
all your fears and hopes and dreams
all the feelings that you hide
once you board the plane,
you cannot turn around
once you're in the sky, 
you cannot touch the ground
no matter how far you travel
no matter what you find
don't forget your suitcase,
and what's neatly pack inside
