you can't read my complex mind
you can't feel my soul
you think my thoughts are just delusions,
aspirations growing cold
there is no place for me in your world
it's a product of your fear
running away from your conscience 
but it's quickly growing near
insecurity feeds your dreams
emptiness fuels your cries
your quest has been in vain,
searching for truth inside your lies
