title: "dirty money"
writer: "CRaZyDiAmOnD"

where'd you get that money 
that you're holding in your hand?
how'd you buy this beach house
sitting right here on the sand?
what about your mother, who's living in a shack
she pleaded for help, and you just turned your back
why can't you face your loved ones
are you ashamed of who you are?
why can't you tell your daughter 
how you got that beautiful car?
she's a growing girl, 
living on maybe a meal a day
her mother's begging for child support
but you don't have time to pay
you musht go out and make your fortune grow
and make sure that people recognize you
everywhere you go
keep swimming in your money
but when the pool runs dry
you'll take a look around
and see you're left with nothing but your lies
