i wish there was some software
to save us from our pain
a hard drive that could free us
from this hell that we maintain
a cell phone that could call on jesus
and ask him where he's been
or dial up our friend the devil
and thank him for our sin
we have the comfort of the machine,
and behind it we hide;
our technological revolution
is spiritual suicide
