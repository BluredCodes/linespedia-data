---
title: "disconnected"
writer: "CRaZyDiAmOnD"

ice cutting through my heart
bleeding the notes of this song
i'm here at the end but where did i start?
when did life become so long?
friction links together the thoughts inside my mind
scattered fears on lost delusions,
conscience of another kind
longing for careful diffusion
