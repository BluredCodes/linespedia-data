i'm flying through a sky of infinite lies
refusing to be cradled by soft clouds of deception
searching for a fierce bolt of lightning
listening for the impetuous and beautiful sound of thunder
i prefer chaos over this painful silence
a vaccum, void of all feeling 
it's magnetic- trying to pull me in
tempting it is, to some, the false security they share. . .
sharing! simply united in their surfaced souls, 
tightly their hands are connected,
but the greatest form of seperation exists in their hearts
