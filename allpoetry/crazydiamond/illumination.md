title: "illumination"

turn off that fucking lamp,
that's sitting by your bed
it's blinding your mind
and the thoughts that it's been fed
you can't go on forever 
you have to stop and rest
the more you hide the more it hurts
as your worst becomes your best
your hate becomes your santuary
your deviance, your home
when the light goes out tonight
you'll see your pain has grown
you'll be stuck with nothing 
but the fear inside your head
all because of that fucking lamp
laying shattered by your bed
