chaos slowly creeping into my soul
searching for a way to escape
i reach for the empty friendship 
that lies in its hand
momentary peace in a cloud of placid smoke
clarity of mind by blinding my vision 
worries flee, along with my reality
fear subsides to laughter
and laughter to numbness
once a recreation,
now an existence
a constant companion to end the pain
