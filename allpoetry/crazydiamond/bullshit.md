---
title: "Bullshit"
writer: "CRaZyDiAmOnD"
slug: "bullshit"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58530-Bull-by-CRaZyDiAmOnD"

do not falter
do not rest
forget your own dreams
strive to be HIS best
perfection is the key 
in this game that we call life
just give up your soul
and you will see the light
don't look for answers 
inside your simple mind
your kind and loyal savior 
wants you to be blind
