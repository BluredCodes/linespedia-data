clostrophobia 
is setting in
here with my loved ones 
i'm stuck once again
all i can do is shut my mouth
and shift my eyes,
freeing myself 
of their judgement and lies
fiercely disguising
my fractured soul
leaving behind me 
the paths i have roamed
