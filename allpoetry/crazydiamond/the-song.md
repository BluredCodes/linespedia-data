---
title: "the song"
writer: "CRaZyDiAmOnD"
slug: "the-song"
source: "AllPoetry"

the music playing in my head
is taking me away
waves of tranquil energy 
pulling me from the fray
through the lonely crevaces
my soul will gently bend
these perfect notes and melodies
are now my only friends
then, awakening my reality 
is the look inside your eyes
but i still hear the song above your startled cries
friend, you took a picture 
of the thoughts inside my mind
a musical mirror to my soul
you're reading all my lies
my heart was broken down 
as the notes became too long
the song becomes my eulogy
now that i am gone
