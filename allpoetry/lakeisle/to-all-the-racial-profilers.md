Do you call yourself Nazi's?
Because this is surely a Holocaust of Arabs.
The terrorists came and hate filled our country
(not that hate wasn't already there!)
Islam is bad! you cried
Kill the Arabs! you thought
why?
Because of extremists you would hurt innocent
people?
Rip them off of airplanes and buses...
Because they have olive skin, dark hair
and eyes, and maybe an accent?
Drive by gas stations and grocery stores
shoot them with sawed-off shotguns
until their blood spilled to the ground
Harass us in school, where everyone is
supossed to feel protected, because we
have a funny name? (Abdullah, Ahmed)
Stand up in class and express your opinion
that "ALL ARABS SHOULD BE BANNED FROM
THE UNITED STATES BECAUSE THEY CAN'T
BE TRUSTED!"
Do you call yourselves ignorant mother-fuckers?
(it's what you are)
But it's ok to profile us isn't it all you
black people?
[or should i say African American]
in a recent survey over half of you
think it's ok to profile Arabs...
hey, as long as it's not you...
and to the rich white lady who's article
was in Newsweek, who said
"i don't regret reporting him"; kicking an
innocent man off the plane b/c he
had olive skin...i hope you feel
good about ruining someone's day.

And to all the Arab kids
my people, my culture
who have had stones thrown
at them in school, in the streets

I feel for you
I cry for you

And to all the rednecks
Before you venture out with guns blazing
I ask of you one thing.
Take off your blinders
And see through someone else's eyes
To all the racial profilers out there
Please remember that sometimes,
words hurt just as bad as stones.
