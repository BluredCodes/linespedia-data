---
title: "Vespa"
writer: "LakeIsle"

i sting
with sharp words and hot looks
floating, flying
i locate my prey
tongue like a razor as i lash out
cut them until they bleed
why? you may ask
it's from my own hurt
my own self-pity and loathing
years of torment from my mind
that no one will venture in or love
doomed to think differently
like no one else
this is why i sting
i am vespa.
