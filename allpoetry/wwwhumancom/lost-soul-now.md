no one got ignored but i felt futile
i read just one thing
and fall away
here comes that one last moment
the breath
the means to take me to an end
a fucked-up grin
a call to line
what so simply we call
an ambiant sound
a body and mind
divide

a message, a cry
so many inside
to peace, to joy, to justice
it's mine
all mine
can't keep me away anymore
so call it safe
but broken and torn
to derive from hate
but not anymore
no more
no more 
no more

peace inside shattered
like glass apon stone
a solitary meaning
gone
all gone
crawl inside the basement
and flee from the heat
to bind me my statment
not me
i'm coming down
be with me when we meet
so untold anymoreso afraid anymore
i'm wishing someone with me
i can't see a thing
a hold of the hand is all i need
maybe
help is a long way off
and just where to begin when it arives?
i'm a lost soul now
just a lost soul now

a message, a cry
so many inside
to peace, to joy, to justice
it's mine
all mine
can't keep me away anymore
so call it safe
but broken and torn
to derive from hate
but not anymore
no more
no more
