Reminiscence...
Thoughts of the way you used to kiss me so softly, and look at me with that shine in your eyes
Or how I could wake up to you with your arms around me
Your touch sent waves of warmth through my body and chills down my spine to the tips of my fingers and toes
The only word to describe the feeling is indescribable
Every moment that I spent with you brought me so much happiness
Every night I went to bed with you on my mind...smelling you on my clothes, imagining that you were still lying next to me and anticipating the next morning when I would wake from my dreaming to something much better than any dream I could ever have...
Your love
