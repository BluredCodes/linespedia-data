Rebecca Azul
Most popular girl in school
Class president, prom queen
Face like in a magazine
Friends with the "cool crowd"
Always makes her parents proud
But every night, under dark skies
In bed, she lies alone and cries
Picks up the phone, for a friend to hear her bawl
And finds she has no one to call
