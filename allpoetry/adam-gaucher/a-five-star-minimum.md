title: "A Five Star Minimum"
writer: "Adam Gaucher"

It seems required, a five star minimum.  Where
songs simply written amplify through junk-
yards housing half-turned gears whose teeth
need flossing.  In laughter beauty waving smiles
crumbles stone wonders.  Their pasts broken down
against the grain splinters fly.
Hair hangs down to hide a human's ugly
face, to here we find the end relinquish
by means of jagged masses.
