---
title: "You As Music"
writer: "Adam Gaucher"
slug: "you-as-music"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58693-You-As-Music-by-Adam-Gaucher"
---

Have I told you
about my love
of music? I'd
give anything to
be a misplayed
note on the worst
song ever written.
