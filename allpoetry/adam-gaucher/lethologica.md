---
title: "Lethologica"
writer: "Adam Gaucher"
slug: "lethologica"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

is a state where inspiration runs
dry.  Your head lies peacefully cloudy
and severed like a jar of
vapid tuesday-night diners.  Your
ears leak a corpsed mind's
waste with a thud of helplessness and
deserted photographs.  You
stub your toes but not before
they're frost bitten anyway, and it's
like climbing an Egyptian pyramid
with only your left nostril.

The state of lethologica pulls you into
a path of dulled influence for the
fear of impatiants and stupidity.
You try to shake hands with a goat
in September; you attempt to reach
the speed of light with a single
bar of soap.  The ego you've created
crumbles inward to a single plane rapidly,
where your mind floats away to observe
the second dimension as an
intruder from the third.
