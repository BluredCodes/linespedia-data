---
title: "Alas Fabulous"
writer: "Adam Gaucher"

Fabulous! I'm alive...start the stop watch.
Put my clothes on, feed me genetically-
altered food, teach me about
God and Jesus and Columbus and
Washington and Rimbaud...Oh
wait, not him, he's not a human like
us.

Great! I'm ready for "life"
in the American dream.
Exactly that, a dream. A game to
play, and entertain our weak minds
until we die.
