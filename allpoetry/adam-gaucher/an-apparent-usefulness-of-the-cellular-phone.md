I thought she was calling someone.
I thought she was going to call someone.
I was wishing she'd be calling someone
saying "Guess who's here!", and the other would
say, "who?", and she'd reply, "that guy with
long hair who writes in that book all
the time", (trying to convince the other to
join her), and she'd reply, "wow, I'll be right
there!", and by the time the other
arrives, I'd be gone, off to some distant
existence alone in contentment.  When the
other arrives, I am gone, and the two
would go to the bathroom and
enjoy their lesbian orgasms.
