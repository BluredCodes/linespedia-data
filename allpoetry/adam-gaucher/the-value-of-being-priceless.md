title: "The Value of Being Priceless"

I have never chosen someone
with flaws such as it
is the definition of perfection.
The shattered gravel I've traveled,
in fact for many years,
scraping soulless, eyes
fixed out the window, searching
lazy for a time of the life,
just a minute or two of that truth
and reality, the love of loving, and
the feeling of being loved at all.
Being one mind the world can share, but a
heart that only one can steal,
mine is true and unique,
mine is priceless but not valued.
