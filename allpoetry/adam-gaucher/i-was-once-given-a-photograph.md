title: "I Was Once Given a Photograph"
writer: "Adam Gaucher"
slug: "i-was-once-given-a-photograph"

I was once given a photograph
of a basement that I was
sitting in.  I happened to be in the exact
place where the image had been
captured many weeks prior.  I
decided I should put this picture somewhere
as to not lose it though out the night.
I thought about this, looked
around for a place, and noticed my
coat lying in the chair across the
room from me.  It seemed like the
prime space to rest the item, so I
proceeded to chart my course of action:
Walk forward dodging my crazed friend to
the left, while acknowledging the existence
of two other friends on my right with
a healthy nod of the head, arriving finally at the
jacket placing the image snugly into the
right hand pocket along side my spare
pack of Turkish Gold.
It all seemed simple enough, so I
readied myself and was just about to
charge when I looked up and noticed that my
coat had disappeared.  I turned to my
friends who had, by then, shifted position,
and noticed how one of them had been
lost as well.  My brain stumbled briefly, so
I could only smile at the jacket frozen in my hand.
I placed the photograph securely onto the arm
of the couch, and I sat comfortably back
into the warm touch of reality.
