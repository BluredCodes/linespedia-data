South America has been elevated.  That is a
fact, we live with it.  We live on
it.  We climb it and then, live on
it.  One hundred mutants absent
of structure and clothing selected
naturally by magnetic pull, living
here, on it.

There are people
bellow, down there, who tend to
point and laugh at us, not realizing
that we're the ones in position
to relieve our bodies of
liquid metabolic waste over the sides
and down on top of them.  (Not to say
that we actually will, although it would make
for one hell of a revolution).
