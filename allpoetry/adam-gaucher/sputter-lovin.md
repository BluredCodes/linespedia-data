title: "Sputter Lovin'"
writer: "Adam Gaucher"

There's a deer in you back yard
so you bring your Jazz
band.  You proceed out the sliding
glass door and you begin to dance
the charleston.  The band is accompanying
you and you're having a good time
when the deer begins to dance as well.
The two of you on hind legs dance
together for a while and the
circle of grass you inhabit rises
into the air and starts to spin
rhythmically.  Out from the hole
beneath you rises studio 54 in
all its glory while in the center
stands Andy Warhol.  He smiles
as all physical matter is
swallowed into his dark dark
glasses like a black hole
falling fast
to slumber with a most
beautiful cheese sandwich
digesting in its stomach.
