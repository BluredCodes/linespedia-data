---
title: "Another $19.95"
writer: "Adam Gaucher"

Production feeds off the ignorance of
people. Not knowing the average temperature
of thirty-three human beings. Not
knowing Ph balanced from a hangman's
death-knot. The screen emits negative
energy:
I want it, oh
I need it, oh
I have it! $19.95.
And not knowing that all soap is the
same, while soap in ultra is more but in
smaller bottles. Not knowing that "easy
living" is "working your ass off," and that
two out of three labcoat actors agree
is equal to terrible odds:
I want it, oh
I need it, oh
I have it! $19.95.
And not knowing Bob Dole's cock is as
hard as anyone's when it comes to
selling bikini models, I mean, selling greasy car
parts, and greasy beer. Not knowing
50% more cotten for all these years,
compared to nothing, couldn't fit in your
ear anymore. Not knowing
by the time you've hit the bullseye,
it's moved another foot (to the
right) and you pay another $19.95
to take a shot, never in the
dark when aim is always provided.
