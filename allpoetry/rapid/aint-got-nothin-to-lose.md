---
title: "Ain't Got Nothin to Lose"
writer: "Rapid"
slug: "aint-got-nothin-to-lose"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55664-Aint-Got-Nothin-to-Lose-by-Rapid"

Yo lies are so venemous
they just eat ya away
Yo ahead of me
in my life's game,
And I'm never gonna be the same.
I kicked you outta my life once
and I'll do it again.
'Cause by the days end,
I just cant put up with these sins.
I cant take it anymore
and it rips me apart.
Every time I get it right
you make me start,
all over again.
But this time
its the end.
And yo gonna lose,
everything I lost and more.
Yo dignity and yo pride.
You aint never gonna be able to hide.
From everything you did and will do,
and yo the one thats gonna be through.
Yo loss, not mine.
