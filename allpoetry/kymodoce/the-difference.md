was this u were talking about 
when u mentioned in that hot summer day
was this u were talking about
when we were so very close as if we were one 

I remember you had this feeling
for being being yourself and still not sure about things taking shaping 
was it this u had imagined that day
when the sun was shining bright and the stars never felt that way before

baby wasnt it was me who always beleived 
what was there that time 
we would always cherish and talk about
cause i knew it form the very begining it would take that unroamed road

U tremed it as long distance relations and i termed it as Distance- Love relation
u thought its difficult for the LDRs
i believed and still believe there's always a beauty in DLR's

But am still sure for have u not once said 
that i would not be you if you would be so forgetful
as i still carry along myself and ur toughts 
on those very words which is still so ture

but i feel this pain of longingness and distance
which had i never imagined taking this turn to the unroamed road
but if this is it 
i still can go on, on to the road which has never been travelled before 
the road of uncertainity but faith
the road of emptyness but hope

As it was you whom i imagine
would difinately come cross and be across my arms
at this turn or may be the next coming one.
