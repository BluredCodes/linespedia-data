I'm sorry 
I took one look and started to cry
your face so pale so week so tired 
you speak the sound of your voice so sweet
music to my ears, over and over a broken 
reccord thinking of you. hopeing preying 
that your ok. water strikes down my face 
with saddness once more,i love you with all my heart.
get well soon......
