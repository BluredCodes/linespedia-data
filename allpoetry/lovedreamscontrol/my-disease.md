My sadness is a fire
it grows with swiftness
in every moment of each passing day.
This fever of my sickness
it dances freely in my soul.
This very moment is drowning
on the tears plentiful
I spill so freely.
The regrets that burn my brain out 
make me feel sorry for myself.
When all around me, 
the earth is calling 
showing me things I'll never see.
I'd give forever to feel hunger,
so spoiled and selfish
fat and laughing.
I'd be happy to experience
some greater tragedy.
