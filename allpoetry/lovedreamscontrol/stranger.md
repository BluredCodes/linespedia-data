I sit and sip my plastic glass of water.
I look over at the stranger who is not so strange.
I feel moutains might tip themselves over,
but I know that will never happen.
I imagine what I look like from the veiw
across the table. A stranger, sill so strange.Wild things happen and I cannot explain.
