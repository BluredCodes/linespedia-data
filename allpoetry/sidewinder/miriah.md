Miriah....
                     Miriah.....
They call the wind.... Miriah,
Somehow that song sticks in my mind,
Never letting go....
                        Touching a special place in my soul,
I dreamed...
                    that I had a daughter by that name,
Maybe one day...
                        Who knows?
You never can tell what fate holds,
But somehow you wonder...
And dream...
                           I can see her now,
Warm....          safe in my arms.....
Looking at me with eyes
that a child holds,
                      Touching that place which holds only love,
Could it be...
                just my imagination?
But...
        one never knows,
Still one can always hope.

March 28, 2002
