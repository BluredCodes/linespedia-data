title: "A Shadow In The Wind"

The first glimpse of a shadow in the wind
descends with blessings of the elements,
Circles of light
shelters what you perceive as nature,
Draw upon the wisdom of instinct,
Challenge the hidden world within,
Find truth in a crystal splendor
whispered by a raven
touched in flight,
In misty dreams & shadowed memories
hear the clash of brave souls
who rage in battle
from a forgotten time,
Destiny's first touch,
a prophecy in the making,
seeds of magic
rise to touch the morning sun
with the blessings of The Lady
focused in the gold dawn.

Nov. 10, 1997
