title: "My Shining Light"

Trust is something that is given to us
by those who love us,
And as we show that love to others
it becomes a strength,
No more,
It becomes a beacon of light
for those who are lost,
Who shall be first in that loving?
That I know not,
But through my love
those who come are welcome,
May love in my home
be a shining example.
