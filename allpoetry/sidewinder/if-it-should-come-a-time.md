title: "If It Should Come A Time"
writer: "sidewinder"

Caught past the dreams
forever held in the memories
unwarranted in time,
How is it that you touch me
where it matters most?
I must confess that the blindness
in sonnets overwhelms my heart,
And you my dear are a necessity
urgent within thy grasp,
I watch you and need you,
Disregarding my own dependency,
If it should come a time
that we should part,
Then the horizons
that we shared shall disappear into the shadows,
And I will be a poorer man lost in the summer.
