title: "When It Comes To Matters"

You say that you know me,
But when it comes to matters
I am but a stranger in your eyes,
Fools only profess
and try to touch 
what they can only imagine,
Once you told me
that when I needed one to talk to
I could come,
But when the need is there
I only find shadows,
Where is it
that a man needs more
than love songs shared in intimacy?
And here you are
claiming friendship,
But all I find
is emptiness in the wind,
The reasons don't really matter,
But the truth is there
for all to see,
Need I say more?
Yet in celebrations
the rage is met

May 17, 1999
