title: "Silent Twilights"

Ascending twilight
held in freefall,
Silent within,
Hidden beyond the circle,
Touch the harmonium
if only for an instant,
Delicate imperfections
emerge in radiant time,
Transformations in shapeshifting
which affirm the magic inside,
Poetic in the influence
fulfilled in the rise of a horizon.

Feb. 14, 1999
