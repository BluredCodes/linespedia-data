---
title: "Out Of The Black"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "out-of-the-black"

Out of the black
touching where once it mattered,
Forgetting the pain
wasted on those regrets
long forgotten,
Today is favored
by those who celebrate
honor and patience,
And in the dreams of yesteryear
accomplishments are met
by more than a hope,
But it's that hope
that allows us to step out of the dark.

Dec. 26, 1999
