---
title: "Does It Sound That Bad?"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "does-it-sound-that-bad"
source: "AllPoetry"

If dreams become reality
and fantasies become a step away,
If reality disappeared into the faded past,
And all the pain just simply gone,
Babe,
What would you say?
If love became a common thing,
But still was held so precious,
And the world became one within itself,
Hate just simply didn't exist anymore,
Babe,
What would you say?
Think about what I'm saying,
It doesn't sound half bad,
Think about the consequences
and what really happens,
Seems quite mad,
So take as you will,
But as you give me a thought,
yes,
Think about what I'm saying,
You know really,
Does it sound that bad?

July 4, 1982
