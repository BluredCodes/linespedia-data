---
title: "Where's The Justice?"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "wheres-the-justice"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54862-Wheres-The-Justice--by-sidewinder"

As I see things now, I see a world before me,
But I'm unable to touch it,
I'm unable to explore it,
it's sad,
So sad,
Why?
Because I'm here surrounded by bars,
Confined by four walls
prescribed by society
in which laws are set
by so called justice,
But where is the justice?
I can't see it,
I guess
maybe...
I never will.

Jan. 2, 1981
