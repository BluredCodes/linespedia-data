Here I am sitting in the silence,
Not even the coyotes are here to keep me company,
Every once in a while
I hear a car traveling by,
And I wonder...
Is that you?
You'd be surprised by
how many times I've wondered that,
My mind keeps
coming back to the last time we were together,
The smile on your face
brings a smile back to mine,
I do want to hear your voice again,
And even though we made no promises
my heart jumps at the thought
of you coming by,
Well...
a man can dream can't he?

March 28, 2002
