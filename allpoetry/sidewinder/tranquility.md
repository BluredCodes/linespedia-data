Tranquility,
Spirits touch in emotion,
Hearts rise into the sunset,
Remembered by those where wisdom
awakes,
Paths await in eyes
where infinity shapes
those ways through teachings
experienced in realms
unique in a key
unforgotten,
Patterned in time,
Mystical within perceptional
traditions,
Knowledge surpasses splendor
within a destiny.

Feb. 14, 1999
