title: "Escape"
writer: "sidewinder"

Let's go,
Leave your work behind,
We're gonna drop everything
and find something new,
It doesn't matter where we go,
As long as we're together,
What do you say?

Let's escape
and find each other again,
Sweet love is waiting for us,
All we have to do
is escape,

I know,
It is kind of sudden,
But that's the name of the game,
You see,
Life shouldn't be all work and no play,
So loosen up,
Relax,
And take it as it comes,

Let's escape,
And find each other again,
Sweet love is waiting for us,
All we have to do
is escape,

Maybe if we did this more often,
We could take life's ups and downs,
And thing wouldn't be so routine,
So kick back and enjoy,
You know,
We've only got one life to live,
So let's go for the gusto,
What do you say, Babe?

Let's escape,
And find each other again,
Sweet love is waiting for us,
All we have to do
is escape.
