title: "A New Beginning"

When silence strikes
and time revolves,
True hearts know that nature
gives its blessings of life,
For a merging takes place,
In the shadow there is light,
In the brightness there is dark,
For each has a balance
and one can not exist without the other,
In this turning point
each revolves a magic
and conjures a moment in the universe
when nothing matters,
But everything exists,
simply the epic arrives
at a moment of reality,
For the influence that those empower
transforms life itself,
And in that revelation
creation & chaos
opens a pathway
to wisdom in the otherworld,
It's this that starts a new beginning,
Let our quest begin!

Feb. 28, 1997
