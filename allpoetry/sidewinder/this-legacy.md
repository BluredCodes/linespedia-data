There will be many who will question my life,
Who was he?
How did he live his life?
What type of person was he?
Those who know me can answer those questions better than I,
Even still..
All you have to do is read my words,
And you may find your answers,
Only time will tell,
Long after when I'm dead & gone,
But I hope...
that I'll have left something behind
to fill in some of the blanks,
And I hope...
some of the love that I share
will go forward
as I continue with my dreams
that somehow find their reality,
Those of you
who find pleasure with my poetry.
Share it with those who know not of me,
So that this legacy
that I leave behind
will someday simply
make someone smile,
And if that happens
them my friend
I've served my purpose
which is the best that I can do.
