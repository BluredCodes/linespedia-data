---
title: "These Pages"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "these-pages"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58175-These-Pages-by-sidewinder"
---

Some of my old friends
& some of my new,
We sing of the good times,
Sometimes we sing the blues,
These pages written in music
are written of my heart,
The tunes somehow follow me
wherever I go,
Those pages are written of my soul,
Somehow they make me whole,
Some are written of memories,
Some are written about the lessons in life I've learned,
Some are written about stories
that were told to me a long time ago,
Now...
I keep then here
in the poems that I write for you,
I pass on...
            what was passed on to me
and probably...
               you'll pass them on
to the next generation
                      who'll remember & celebrate
what we've chosen to share.

April 6, 2002
