And the hero dies,
	Why?
	No one knows,
	He just gives up his life,
	Does anyone cry?
	No!
	It's all for nothing,
	The pain,
	The love,
	It's insane,
	But it's true,
	Some call him a fool,
	Some say he's crazy,
	But he just lives in a dream world,
	Yes...
	a special fantasy,
	Strange
	but true,
	Bizarre and freaky,
	But that's him,
	The stranger
	through all the end. 

	Feb. 3, 1981
