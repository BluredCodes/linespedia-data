title: "Harpist Of the Wind"
writer: "sidewinder"

Listen...
Yes,
Very carefully.
And maybe...
just maybe you can hear him.
yes...
He's the harpist of the wind.
Some doubt that he exists.
For those he doesn't.
Only for those who believe.
And keep their fantasies in their hearts.
For the harpist is a part of a world
that only elves,
gnomes and wizards abide.
And it's not for everyone.
Because you have to believe in magic.
Can you?
It's not hard for me.
Because I've seen him.
No...
It's not just my imagination.
yes...
I've heard his music.
And yes,
I've sat by his side.
And he's shared his great wisdom.
From him I've learned much.
From whom?
But of coarse who else?
The harpist of the wind.
So if you keep listening
maybe he'll appear.
But you've got to keep listening
for the harpist of the wind.
