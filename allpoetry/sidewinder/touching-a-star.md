---
title: "Touching A Star"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "touching-a-star"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61862-Touching-A-Star-by-sidewinder"
---

Passions in a heart
	that takes life a step within the moment
	forever releasing those frustrations
	that grips your soul,
	Silent awareness
	which slips into the shadows
	and there one reaches for
	a private moment,
	Where the quiet fulfills a promise,
	And in that promise
	life celebrates a quiet truth
	touching a star of hope.

	March 22, 1999
