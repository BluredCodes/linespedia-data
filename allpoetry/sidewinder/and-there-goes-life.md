title: "And There Goes Life"

I remember the first time I went karaoking
with my friends at Coco's,
I was scared shiftless,
I sang The House Of The Rising Sun,
And I was surprised that everyone loved it,
By & by...
I got used to the crowd,
Somehow I loved the attention,
Now...
You can't get me off the stage,
Though...
I need to learn a few new songs,
Oh well...
Life is funny isn't it?
What you thought you'd never do,
You wind up doing,
And there goes life...
throwing all the surprises at you
that it can,
You never know about some things
until you try them.
So...
     try them!

April 6, 2002
