title: "In The Silence Of Stone"
writer: "sidewinder"

To stay in touch
with choices that one makes
in one's life
can be chaotic,
For the self prophecy
makes some cynical,
For over time those faces
reach into places that one
keeps private,
And the silence overwhelms
one's heart,
For the soul cries for mercy
in an never ending legacy
that rises in unexpected places
mired in the darkness of eternity,
rage completes itself
ancient in its own making
forever held in the silence
of stone
which remembers all that has
been seen
touching those whispers
that were never meant to be heard.

Feb. 20, 1998
