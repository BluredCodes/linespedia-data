---
title: "A World With New Eyes"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "a-world-with-new-eyes"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59087-A-World-With-New-Eyes-by-sidewinder"
---

Emotions touch
in rhythms
wherein the magic is met,
Gracefulness completes
the silence
varied only
beyond the shadow
of what we know before,
Confident in the presence
of the nimbus
that encloses us
and makes us remember
the dance,
Listen to the muse within,
She'll show a world
that some only dream of
endured through those ecstasies
and pools of dreams
in the passion of a heart,
Wherein a tapestry rich & dark
plays the music of the soul,
Crescendo & plummet the voice of the song,
The cadence of water
rippling through until all is spotless,
A passion renewed
under an azure sky
and the smile that glows within us
reveal more
then the truth hidden behind the eyes
waken by the touch of the heart
and we see the world....
                          with new eyes.

May 8, 2001
