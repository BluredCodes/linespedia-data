---
title: "Envision The Dreams"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "envision-the-dreams"
source: "AllPoetry"

Envision the dreams
that one mirrors in one's own eyes,
Seek the path & the journey
fulfilling one's own destiny,
Link yourself spiritually
and touch the magic
which inspires,
Harness the power
in rituals expanding the potential
in one's own life,
Endeavor in the insight & wisdom
expanding doors that were once closed,
Revealing the answers
that were once only questions
everchanging one's own life,
Listen to the pulse,
Forever remember
that life is only a circle.

Sept. 22, 1998
