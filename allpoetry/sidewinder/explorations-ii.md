As subtle as a breeze
there's a yearning felt
that drives my soul
crazy....
In your eyes
I can see that you
feel the same,
So what are we going to do about it?
Let's reach into the rivers of lost souls,
Take what we need
and go from there,
Hear those whispers
that call you?
What remains?
Does the desire
rage within your heart?
meet me at the place of your choice
and let's explore those wonders
to set our souls free.
