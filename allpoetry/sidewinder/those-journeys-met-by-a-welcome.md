title: "Those Journey's Met By A Welcome"

Self assurance,
Those corridors
that get lost behind the love,
Those new encounters
met by a single night,
Horizons that touch at both ends,
Secrets forgotten accidentally through time,
A need for the summer
where someone can enjoy the warmth,
Those journeys
met by a welcome
from your eyes,
A first breath
freeing my lonely heart,
Finally someone
who can touch my soul.

June 7, 2000
