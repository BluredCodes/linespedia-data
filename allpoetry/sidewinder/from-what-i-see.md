title: "From What I See"

Today I'm here
and everything to you seems a dream,
Maybe it's not reality,
But I can't help how I feel,
They say that love is blind,
The example from my point of view,
It's true,
Why?
Because when you're not with me,
all I can do
is think, eat, breathe, remember, dream & love...
you,
For you're in my mind,
Yes constantly,
But what can I say?
You are my life,
So your love lives within my heart,
And my fantasy becomes real.
