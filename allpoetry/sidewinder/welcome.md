In her eyes
there came a look,
In that there was an eagerness
to love and be loved
as none known before,
To touch
as if one could reach into a heart
and satisfy that which can not be satisfied,
To share
those few seconds
in a lifetime forever remembered,
Welcome my love,
It is and always will be
more.

Sept. 26, 1984
