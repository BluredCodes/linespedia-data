title: "Reaching For The Next Sunrise"
writer: "sidewinder"

Touching the sunshine
	deep into the day
	only a heartbeat at a time,
	I'm waiting for the warmth of your eyes
	to bring a smile back to my face,
	The shadows matter not
	as long as you're around,
	And somehow we find our company
	is what we need,
	Abiding past that long cursed loneliness,
	If I fall
	you'll help me back to my feet,
	And the same goes for you,
	As we discover what we both can share,
	And when the nights comes
	our sighs touch
	even beyond the quiet,
	Reaching for the next sunrise
	April 7, 2002
