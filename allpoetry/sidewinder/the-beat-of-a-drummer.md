title: "The Beat Of A Drummer"
writer: "sidewinder"

The beat of a drummer,
	Do you hear what I hear?
	Feel the rhythm of the beat,
	The soul of everything,
	Do you know what I mean?
	Play the drum my friend,
	I'll always hear you
	if only in my dreams,
	keep playing the drum
	though I'm not sure of what it means
	to you,
	I hear the beat & the roll,
	All the way to my soul,
	Never let me go,
	And yet..
	when I hear it,
	It always makes me whole,
	I never tire of hearing you play,
	It's become apart of me,
	My hope & my cheer,
	It's strength
	grows inside of me,
	And love grows so dear.
	Play the drum my friend,
	I'll always hear you
	if only in my dreams,
	Keep playing the drum,
	Though I'm not sure of what it means
	to you.

	Sept. 2, 1984
