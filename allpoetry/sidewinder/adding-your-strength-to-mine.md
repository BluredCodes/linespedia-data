---
title: "Adding Your Strength To Mine"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "adding-your-strength-to-mine"

I can see from the look you give,
You give from the best of your heart,
that means alot to me,
For the dream lives on
and a solid start begins,
You're showing the best you can do
and that's all that matters,
You're leaving a little love,
something that someone can hold on to,

I can see that you've been hurt a lot,
the lines on your face, 
The curve of your mouth,
so sad...
and yet you're here,
reaching for me as I for you,
Hoping I'll make it better,
And I hope that I don't disappoint you,

Where in those times
can a heart surrender
forever reaching
a step at a time?
Reawaken those moments
only met...
by your smile,

The process is slow,
Tedious & cloudy is the right path,
Always just beyond tomorrow,
And it's enough to know that when I'm lost,
You'll be there,
Adding your strength to mine,
Understand this...
and we'll join together in harmony
forever reaching
a sheltered place
within our hearts.

June 13, 2000
