Somehow there were thoughts
that I never said out loud,
They were arranged in the back of my head,
Maybe I should have said them,
But I didn't,
What was I thinking?
I don't know...
somehow I guess they weren't very important,
Or were they?
If they come back up
I guess I'll write them down
so the next time you're around
I'll remember them
like old friends
that we talk about
from time to time,
Who knows...
maybe they'll become part of a
important decision 
that we'll make,
You just never can tell.
