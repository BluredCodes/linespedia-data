title: "Sweet Smiles"

Sweet smiles touch our hearts,
Music playing loudly in the background,
But it matters not,
For I have you in my company,
And I have your smile to keep me warm,
We create our own conversation,
So minutes turn to hours
& the night passes by,
I hear the laughter over my shoulder,
But you whisper to me
to keep me enticed,
What secrets will we hold?
Only the moon & the stars will tell,
For romance fills the air,
Where will we go from here?
I think...
I'll leave that up to you,
Yes...
I have a few pleasures in mind,
But...
only to take it slowly
& let the mystery
take its lead.
