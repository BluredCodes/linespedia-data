title: "Windworn Clouds & Dusty Roses"

Windworn clouds
	smile on their own & blossom with time
	Highlighting things we take for granted,
	Starlight visited by the ancient,
	Forgotten by the trail of tears,
	Enchanted by hope,
	Magically touched,
	Embodied by beauty,
	gateways held by a blessing,
	And summer invites you to explore your destiny,
	Frequent rain welcomes the land
	where dusty roses reveal their fragrances,
	Witness the summer solstice
	and return to a time forgotten by most.
	May 4, 1999
