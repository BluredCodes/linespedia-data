---
title: "Something Hidden"
writer: "sidewinder"

On the right side of heaven
an inner reflection
shared by an open heart
shadows reside
in the deepest fate,
Renewal of a spirit,
Paths of those who would rise
in the flight of an eagle,
Sacred in its own truth,
Forever held in a simplicity,
Its these limitations
that harmony must rise above,
Fulfilled in its own truth,
Tested by time,
A lesson that is learned
by the circles of life,
You now are free,
Touch that part of your life
which makes you whole.

Sept. 22, 1997
