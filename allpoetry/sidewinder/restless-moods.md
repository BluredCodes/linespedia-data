I guess...
          I'm just tired of being alone,
Don't get me wrong,
                    I love my solace
But there are times I'd love some company,
Maybe a friend or two dropping by,
Or even a lover,
Yes...
       that would be nice,
I get tired of having the same dreams every night,
I want something new to dream about,
Don't you?
It's beautiful here where I live,
But it's ten miles away from my friends,
I guess....
            they have their lives too
But every once in a while
                          I just get restless,
I know...
          I need a social life,
But in this little town
                        all you find is the gossip,
So here I am
               in this restless mood
waiting for something to happen,
                 Could it be
that I need to make the first move?

April 6, 2002
