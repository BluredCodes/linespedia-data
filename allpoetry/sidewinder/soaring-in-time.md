Soaring in time
where a face smiles
touching the shadow in the wind,
Whispered in rhythms,
A quiet memory
that seems to hold forever,
Reflecting a mood that
touches an essence of rebirth,
Glimpses of dreams
that reveal answers to ourselves,
Silhouettes that rise a step at a time,
Seeking the twilight of a lost vision
where a heart mirrors within itself
and the battle continues
sustain the melody
of awareness.

March 14, 1999
