---
title: "Writhe In Time"

Temptations writhe in time
forever touching souls,
Celebrating its meager touch,
Where in this life
blood and ashes fulfill what more can dream,
Rage on and consider what you see,
There's a world where the wind
touches the freedom in the age of legends
enchanted by a heartbeat at a time,
Those realms reveal planescapes
uncaged in veils,
Infinitely focused,
Expanding horizons
a step at a time.

March 31, 1999
