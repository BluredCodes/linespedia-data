---
title: "Facing Memories"
writer: "sidewinder"

Those dreams that seem out of touch
are just moments away,
Though shrouded and hidden,
Completely out of reach,
Mere time matters not,
For the illusion successfully completes it,
And in the scheme of things
life holds on,
Reaching for reality,
Though hard and cold
memories move on.

April 17, 1994
