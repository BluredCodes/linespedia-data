title: "The Way Of The Unicorn"

The way of the unicorn
is seen through magical sight & sound,
Silent passages which awakens
mystical revelations harkening
memories fulfilling achievements,
An epic begins,
There a soul will be tested
finding a destiny,
A legacy within truth,
To face a passage in the dawn
where the innocent of heart
fulfills a rising of the moon,
A page forgotten
immortal to the masquerade,
Shapeshifting between imagination & reality,
Variations in the wind after time
where the age of innocence is over,
Conjured in a path to victory
wherein sight reveals magic forever held.

Feb. 6, 1998
