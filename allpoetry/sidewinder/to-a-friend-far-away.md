title: "To A Friend Far away"

Where are you my friend?
	far away
	lying in the sand,
	The dreams that we kept are here at home,
	Though someday we'll make them come true,
	Each day that you are gone,
	A part of my heart is there for you to hang on to,
	Our friendship grows stronger,
	Though the day grow longer,
	But someday we'll be back together fishing in Colorado,
	Or hiking in mountains
	that hold some secret cave
	in the corner of our minds,
	We're playing a game
	of lost treasures
	and forgotten times
	though the elves and dwarves don't mind,
	We'll be back having the time of our lives,
	I know it can't be easy
	being away from home and family,
	But in the blink of an eye
	I can see your smile,
	Safe at home with friends and family,
	Here you are...
	ready for another game
	just between you and me?
	Jan. 28, 1991
	This was written for a friend of mine...Randy
