---
title: "Touched By A Memory"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "touched-by-a-memory"

And dreams of life in miracles
forever held in a moment of time
magically mesmerized
by a single touch,
To whom it may concern...
Thy words take to heart
in this declaration,
For its through the hope
countless moments
silently move forever,
Like a single cloud in the sky,
Only to disappear from the heat of the sun,
Touched by a memory.

April 10, 1996
