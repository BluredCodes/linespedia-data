title: "Moment Where Moment Meets"

Serenades touch
in increments of time,
Hear the chance
of the challenge within,
Catch a second breath
and conceptions which intercedes
in a blind crossroad
between
those rights & wrongs,
Yes, excursions
which resolve
those secrets held glistening in the wind,
Hear the voice that sings,
Spirits rise
& celebrate time,
Risen in four-four harmony,
Precise in the silence,
Moment where moment meets.
