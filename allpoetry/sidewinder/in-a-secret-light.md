In a secret light
pictured by your shadow
ingratiated through time itself,
Heartbeats rise and fall
like a breath
sacrilegious
without freedom
soaring gently,
An outpost in its
final moment,
One simple hope
beneficial  to those
who belong,
New directions wonder in
the spirit of what can be,
Primal in its newness.
