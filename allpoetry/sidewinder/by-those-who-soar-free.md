---
title: "By Those Who Soar Free"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "by-those-who-soar-free"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58846-By-Those-Who-Soar-Free-by-sidewinder"
---

Immeasured by cages drawn in the back of a mind
never taken seriously,
Created by the pages of a past
that is held in the light or blocked by a shadow,
Imagination that flows free,
Rich in the scope of things to come,
Planescapes seen in truth,
A tranquil focus seen in the face of time,
Turn to a center,
An accessory masqueraded in a tale
motivated by emotion unforgettable,
A secret revealed,
Cut in passion,
Woven together
by a touch
related to the intrigue that rises eloquently
beyond mysteries,
Initiations concerned in a renewal
of the spirit
reflected by the inner,
Counseled by those who soar free
in worlds of self-transformation,
Nourished by a well spring of life itself.

March 16, 1997
