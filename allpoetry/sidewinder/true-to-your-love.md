---
title: "True To Your Love"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "true-to-your-love"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58761-True-To-Your-Love-by-sidewinder"
---

Through the eyes of yours
I'm tasting of love,
And the deepness that we share
will hold a memory forever,
Tenderness...
is something we both need,
For the touch of our hearts
holds a bond
that would hold forever,
Could it be...
that the dreams is meant
for you & I,
Soothe the savage beast in me,
And I'll hold the world in my hands
just for you.

April 20, 1985
