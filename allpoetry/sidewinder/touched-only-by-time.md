Legends are full of heroes & heroines
touched only by time,
Yet those miracles
rise in the promise
where chivalry & traditions
are chronicled by the song of the bard,

And etched in the songs
are rhythms of sound
that are endlessly echoed throughout time
portraying those miracles 
that forever last
recalling the pride
of the visions of the past,

Therefore a celebration is met
awakened in passages
wherein the lore connects
in secret understanding
gently touching the soul,

Arousing those feelings
only our hearts can know
remaining a promise
arising the shattered past
mesmerizing your soul
into your inner being.

May 18, 2000
