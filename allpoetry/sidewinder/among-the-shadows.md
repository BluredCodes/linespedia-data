---
title: "Among The Shadows"
writer: "sidewinder"

source: "AllPoetry"

A beacon to light a focus
which arrives in a consciousness
fulfilling those dreams
that are shared among the shadows
presented in a time of chaos,
Reaching for the power
which is envisioned by the achievements,
Determined by goals,
Set by our souls,
Those energies channeled through meanings
harassed in spiritual potentials,
creating a quest
that lives within ourselves,
Linking those dream images
and pathways,
fulfilling a book of shadows
to guide oneself through life
and its tribulations.

Feb. 1, 1998
