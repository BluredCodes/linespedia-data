title: "A Rage Within A Whisper"

Shattered dreams
long ago weaved in the pattern of life,
Touch the sense of balance,
Feel the flow,
Let emotions & logic guide
as twin roads,
Each in their own destiny,
Separate...
But yet also they entwine
more than some suspect
to some's dismay,
Creating both chaos & harmony
to a universe both old & new,
Vibrating like Adrenalin,
Fresh & alive,
Precious to all,
Discover that medium
kept secret though
yet rages like a whisper
in a forgotten song.

Nov. 4, 1996
