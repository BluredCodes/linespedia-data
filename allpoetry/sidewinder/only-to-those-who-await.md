title: "Only To Those Who Await"

Faces touch
wherein the magic rages,
Promises within a miracle,
Hope celebrates those
that keep it in their heart,
And laughter keeps life in perspective,
Hopes arise,
Wherein the thought is lost,
Dreams within a reality,
Certainty lies beyond those
that fail to see two sides,
And aspirations keep running,
And so....
emotions touch within a completeness,
Varied in time within time,
The question remains
only to those who await
for a beginning
and forever see's it as it is.

Sept. 24, 1999
