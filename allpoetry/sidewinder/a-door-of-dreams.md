---
title: "A Door Of Dreams"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "a-door-of-dreams"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61456-A-Door-Of-Dreams-by-sidewinder"

Fragmentation's sheltered and tattered,
Merged through revelations,
Spiritual growth
fulfilling a magical need,
Mired in truths,
held in circles
that reveal those secrets and shadows
which awaken memories and changes
forever kept,
Understanding and wisdom
revolving in a circle of harmony,
Violet clouds shimmering in the twilight,
A door of dreams,
Visualizations magically touched,
Insights constant in awareness,
Trances vital to an awakening of the heart,
Lessons forgotten,
Mirrored in pages,
Oblivious to the original meaning,
Conscious to the effort
of freedom,
Inspired by the rhythm of life.

Jan. 1, 1998
