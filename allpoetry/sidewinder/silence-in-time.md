Silence in time
touches a heart
where the night rises in the full moon,
The day brings about change,
Yet fails to shine the light it knows,
Reflections reveal what some hide
wherein the shadows speak to your soul,
Thoughts reveal their true meaning
while the mind only thinks....
Challenges and changes
which focus on the images
wherein time reveals the spiral dance,
The dance is too beautiful for words
and constitutes many angles and lines
that only seem to flow when worked upon.

April 8, 1999
