title: "Upon Through The Day"

Dream upon through the day,
As if the reality came crystal clear,
For the memory haunts you,
And in every waking moment
you need her,
You feel her touch,
You smell her perfume,
You can almost taste her tears,
With that it becomes unbearable
for the wanting in you
grips you,
Never letting go,
Without her nothing else matters,
And the days become endless
like a void in time.

Aug. 22, 1985
