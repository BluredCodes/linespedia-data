title: "Within That Silent Melody"

Within the silent melody
a harmony exists
touching a completeness in my soul,
Reaching where
I need that drink just to satisfy,
In that awareness
a calm
touches that need
just to reveal
& center,
I'll find a solitude
that holds a peace
in this world of contradictions,
And the clatter and
upheaval seems not to matter,
Touching that smile
that some need
while walking in the dark.

Oct. 10, 2001
