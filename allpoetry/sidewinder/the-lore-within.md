title: "The Lore Within"

And now it haunts you
like silence in the night,
Forever touched in mystery,
Faded behind the shadows,
A masquerade,
To touch the balance point
between morality & immortality,
Can the shelter of the dawn
or a rage of a sunset forever
hold the promise of humanity?
And the vagabond sings a song
that touches a heartstring,
To roam the world
and touch the gypsy in all of us,
Listen...
Hear the call,
Let the smile honor
and foresight reveal the
lore within your soul.

April 18, 1999
