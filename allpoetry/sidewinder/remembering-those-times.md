---
title: "Remembering Those Times"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "remembering-those-times"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61803-Remembering-Those-Times-by-sidewinder"

To touch within the rage
a heart forever lost
hidden behind the shadows,
To reach where one
can find time
only in those flames
kindled in a heart,
Can those illusions reveal
a truth,
And in the hour where the waning of time
mires beyond the heavens,
Can the melancholy touch a soul 
only to find the wind whispering,
I told you so,
But in that telling 
all of the muses
smile,
Remembering those times
remaining the same of those pasts
held in perfect harmony.

Feb. 8, 2000
