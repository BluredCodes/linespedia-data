It's 4 am..
	Another restless evening,
	I'm alone...
	bored out of my freaking mind,
	Waiting for the sunrise to some,
	Silence in the night,
	In the distance I can hear a dog barking at the moon
	or maybe its coyotes that he's hearing,
	It doesn't really matter,
	It's a matter of symatics,
	I hear a car
	passing on the highway,
	I wonder where he's going to?
	Probably to work
	& start their day off,
	I remember doing that myself,
	I'd get myself started early in the morning
	just before daybreak,
	Drive 40 miles to work
	and watch the smiling faces,
	As I held up the flagging sign
	some were in a hurry,
	But because of the equipment we were protecting,
	We were there...
	And protecting the people from the equipment,
	Still....
	           some didn't appreciate what we were doing,
	But that's alright...
	                      cause what come around
	goes around,
	And in the foresight they'll get what's coming due.
	April 3, 2002
