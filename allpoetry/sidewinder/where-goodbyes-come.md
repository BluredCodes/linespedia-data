---
title: "Where Goodbyes Come"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "where-goodbyes-come"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61857-Where-Goodbyes-Come-by-sidewinder"

Beneath the silvery moon
unclad within a miracle
forsworn in the pentacle
of time,
Paradise not withstanding,
A sheltered hope
lives within the steps
just beyond the horizon,
Free of the constructs
twisted in the fabric of space,
Touched in the darkness,
Whethered in the prime
where goodbyes come unfettered,
Wherein reflections of the moment
rise like a stain-glassed
road.

March 5, 1999
