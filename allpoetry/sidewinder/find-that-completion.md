I always wondered what she would look like,
That picture of loveliness,
What her hair would look like,
That smell of her skin,
The color of the sparkle in her eyes,
The simpleness of her smile.
The feel of the warmth of hands in mine,
The sound of her laughter,
The smell of her perfume
enticed by the romance of the day,
How she would charm me,
What she would say,
How it would feel
just to have her by my side
and find that beginning of two souls entwine,
What we would learn from each other,
What dreams we could share & make them a reality,
How life would change
even beyond our wildest dreams,
Find that completion
that needed to be made,
I looked & I looked,
Finally I found...
you.

March 28, 2002
