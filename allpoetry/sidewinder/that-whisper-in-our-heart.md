title: "That Whisper In Our Heart"

Tenderly touching those pleasures
held only in the silence,
reflections climb in the shadows
forever reaching a time
where those echoes
ripple in perspectives,
And those who listen
meet beyond the veils,
Where a rainbow of dreams
is held just beyond the horizon
of a romantic shadow
touched in secrets
that whisper in our hearts
verily.

Oct. 25, 1999
