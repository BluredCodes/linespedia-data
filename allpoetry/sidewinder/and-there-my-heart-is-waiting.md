---
title: "And There My Heart Is Waiting"

Forever remembering
those days in the rains
where you once reached out
and touched my soul,
Promises made, 
Dreams fulfilled,
Velvet embraces remembered intimately
imminent in those times
where a heart recalls emotion
that is felt without consequence,
Sensual in splendor endearment,
Passion meant by an ocean smile,
Deep longing for those days
where you see in eyes
a voluptuous sensual love,
Ravenous & untamed,
And there my heart is 
waiting.

Oct. 23, 2000
