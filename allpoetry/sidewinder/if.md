If I were the ocean,
I'd hold you forever in my arms,
If I were the sky,
I'd set you in the stars
and never let you down,
If I were a shellfish,
I'd build you a home
and make my world around you,
If I were a bird,
I'd let you fly with me
and see the world by the air,
If you were mine,
You'd have anything,
Anything at all.

March 10, 1982
