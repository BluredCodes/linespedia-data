title: "The Cutting Edge"

Laughter in life
is the cutting edge
when things become to much to bear.
Sometimes we have to
step back
and take a look at ourselves,
There are times we take to much for granted,
Responsibilities revolve in a bitter end
resulting in a tangled web that we weave,
Forgetting the child like voice
that we all have,
Telling us that the simple things in life
have a purpose,
And sometimes we have to imagine,
dream, play...
just to satisfy the child in all of us,
But society insists on logic
and becoming worldly,
Let me ask this...
Where is the balance
between the two?

Dec. 26, 1998
