---
title: "Perceptions In Prophecy"
writer: "sidewinder"

source: "AllPoetry"

To touch a dream
forever held in the promise,
Destiny rises where
in the wind
time emerges as shelter
magical in the light
manipulating reality
authentic in a precept
wherein insight touches
perceptions in prophecy.

Feb 9, 1999
