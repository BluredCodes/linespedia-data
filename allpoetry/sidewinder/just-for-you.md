title: "Just For You"

The heat is rising,
Feelings I've never known
are somehow making themselves true,
Love....
It's what you do
that makes me want to
just hold you,
Sweets sounds on the radio
is making it so smooth,
I can look in your eyes
and tell you want it too,
so why don't you?
Love isn't wrong
as long as you take it slowly,
Baby,
who love's you?
I do!
And when you need a smile,
I'll be there...
just for you.
