---
title: "Care Of Time"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "care-of-time"
source: "AllPoetry"

Covet thy ways
in justice,
Trace the lines among hearts,
Swift to cut fear & darkness
against a moonless light,
Rich in the forsaking of words that tear away
emotions forever,
Purge thy hate
in the remembrance of a dream,
Hold in ecstasy
an in exuberance of illusion
to this day forward,
And hear the muses twisting in the shores
of a sea drenched memory,
In the plains of passages
caressed by smoke & steel,
Pampered in the dawn,
Destined by the care of time.
