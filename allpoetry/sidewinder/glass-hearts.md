title: "Glass Hearts"

Glass hearts break very easily,
So when you touch them,
You have to be very easy,
Otherwise they'll break in a thousand pieces,
So when you're with me
handle with care,
I know,,
I'm not a china doll,
But karma has a way of evening things out,
Especially when you least expect it,
It's those subtle surprises
sheltered in the imagination of the young,
And through the night
I'll be waiting
for a voice
soft as yours,
The prickling of hair of the back of my neck
suggests your arrival,
And I can hear you whispering
to the cat
shii...ush!
In my mind I let you have your fun,
For where is the romance
if there's no spontaneity?
