Those that come my way
	touching my soul,
	Do you realize what you've left?
	Marks that strengthen me,
	Sometimes making me a little wiser,
	Though some may wonder,
	Yes..
	I stumble,
	But somehow you help me up
	just be you being there,
	Tears & laughter we've both shared,
	And through the friendship
	I hope we share some more,
	Do I...
	do the same for you?
	If so...
	Then maybe life is worth living,
	And together...
	we'll help each other along.
	April 1, 2002
