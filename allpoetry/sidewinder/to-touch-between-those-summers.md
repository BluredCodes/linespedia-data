title: "To Touch Between Those Summers"

To touch in celebration
your eyes
forever met in those seasons
where a heart reveals those passages
that time echoes joined in the silence,
And only I...
can find a hidden shadow
where a heart beats
moment by moment,
Hard intervals
between those summers
returned in thought,
Wondering....
when will I see you again ?

Dec. 26, 1999
