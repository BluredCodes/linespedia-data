Do you want me to
tell you how I feel ?
To touch upon the 30 years of rage
built up inside of me,
No....
you don't,
Tears can make a soul
grow old,
Why must a person act
as if there's nothing wrong
when someone treats them
with no respect?
Time rolls with the tide,
And the world rolls round & round,
Life does have its ups & downs,
But lately it has more downs
than ups,
Why?
Sometimes...
I feel I must...
just get away
for my peace of mind,
They say that time heals all wounds,
Will it heal a wound of the heart?
Make me smile...
if you can.

May 14, 2001
