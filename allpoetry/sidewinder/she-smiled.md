As the sky darkens
the twilight of the clouds
is seen above the horizon,
And here I am
looking across the water,
Feeling the breeze cool against my neck,
Wondering will I see her again?
Who knows?
Still...
as I remember yesterday,
She smiled,
And in her eyes that glowed
there was a feeling,
A feeling of warmth
which will stay with me.
