Somewhere through time
we shall meet,
If only for a little time,
Could it be....
we were meant for each other?
Two hearts beating in the night,
Life is full of surprises,
The truth be known
there's nothing I'd rather do
that to spend some time with you,
There's something in the air
and I think its love,
So my turtle dove
come to me,
Then all of the dreams we've dreamed
will come true
with me and you.
