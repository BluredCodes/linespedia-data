---
title: "Only Learned"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "only-learned"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

With a syncopated rhythm
a style touched in imagery,
Celebrated only within the season,
Forever met in the warmth of time,
Could it be what I'm listening for 
is beyond the echo
where those illusions touch?
Follow me
And I'll show you
more than what some can see,
Feel from the heart,
It will never lead you astray,
Somehow it answers those questions
only learned at life's expense.

Dec. 18, 1999
