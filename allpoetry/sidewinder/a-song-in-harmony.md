---
title: "A Song In Harmony"
writer: "sidewinder"

Red warrior,
battle rage,
A sheltered pain,
A page in history
touched in song of a soothing bard,
Revelations hinge in promises
made to those with an open heart
heralded with visions
of magic
promised in the bright blue dawn, 
Celebrated by the touch of rain,
Centered in rainbows,
Fielded in a field of gold,
Harmony to those
who live with balance in their lives,
A gift from the Goddess
who smiles & love all,
Can you hear her song?
It resonates within my heart
and touches everything within my life.
