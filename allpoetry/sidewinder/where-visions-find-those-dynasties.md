With the light of the stars and the moon
time speaks of those journeys and quests
met only in a revelation where enlightenment touches
the gaze of the innocent,

Where travelers find earth's passage through an ocean of heaven's blood
time tells of the furies born from his destiny,
Water holds the beauty,

Collected only through those memories
a heart finds a soul
and in the echo
shadows touch
where only once
a miracle is found,

Reach and find that magic
forever met in a destiny,
provide the belief
and the world is yours
to take command,
Spirits in time
shall unite
extraordinary
realms
where visions find those dynasties
acclaimed in tales.

By: B.E. Whitehorn & Lori Breed
