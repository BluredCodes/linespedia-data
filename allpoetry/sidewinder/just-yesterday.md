Just yesterday
my dreams re-surged an urgency
and touched a smile,
Casting a prayer and a need,
Is there a lesson to be learned?

Somehow I'm forgetting those resolutions
that we all make
in the dark
where promises become
fragmented just by a touch.

Can the seeker
find the answer that he needs?
Or is it a quagmire
where time struggles?
