title: "Greetings Remembered"

Midnight dreams held in a summer's night
where promises touch on a golden truth,
Where goodbye's seldom shine,
But then never say goodbye,
Say until we meet again,
And then  when those roads
that are traveled touch
on the crossroads of life,
We'll share many more memories,
Greetings rise,
Welcome to those hearts satisfied,
Recognized beyond the shadows,
And if time or fate parts us
then our next meeting
will be well remembered.
