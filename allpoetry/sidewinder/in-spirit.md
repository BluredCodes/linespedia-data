To benefit
a charity from the heart,
A giving that is most cherished,
sanctifying
that
which is held in honor
in spirit,
And in fact
a heartbeat shared by a memory
tranquil to those who give,
Reach into a soul
that soars into the clouds,
Reaches beyond the stars
and gives without expectations
freely
with an open hand.

Oct. 5, 1996
