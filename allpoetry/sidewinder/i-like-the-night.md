---
title: "I Like The Night"

I like the night,
Stars that shine bright,
A full moon that rages,
Sometimes you can meet
the most interesting people,
Conversations that hold a promise
intense beyond understanding,
Footprints in passing,
Moonlight faces
which reach heart to heart,
Inward searching,
Journeys met
only in a caress,
Transitions echo
wherein the nectar touches in the horizon.

Aug. 16, 2000
