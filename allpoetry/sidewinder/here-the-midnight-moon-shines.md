title: "Here The Midnight Moon Shines"

Here the midnight shines
and I wish you were to share it with me,
Am I asking too much?
Lately I'm needing some company,
Yes...
I'm missing you,
I need your smile,
Our conversations,
The laughter that I find
when you're around,
The romance that touches
now & then,
Who knows...
Maybe a surprise or two,
I'm sitting here in the silence
and I'm thinking of you,
I wish I knew your phone number.
I'd call you.
And then maybe we'd pick up
where we left off,
Back into each other's loving arms,
Or at least find solace in each other's company,
Wouldn't you agree?
