title: "A Window To One's Soul"

A window to one's soul,
A clue to their reality,
Feeling memories
of an ever changing past,
Printed by hopes of forever
in a calliope of moments,
Touching a spirit,
At the same time bringing an aspect
of one's personality,
Forever changing reality,
Forever bringing to mind
a secret lost thought.

May 9, 1996
