Over shadows
sheltered by hope
in dreams as a traveler through life
a presence is found,
In that,
Images reveal themselves,
Speaking truths that guide me
in ways I thought never possible,
New paths that are journeys
which reveal new worlds
in mysteries which reflect
breakthroughs and growth,
Some suspect as a rune of destiny,
A place which my heart flows
in its own joy,
A gateway forever held.

July 18, 1997
