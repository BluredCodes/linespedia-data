title: "Rebel For A Cause"

Rebel, rebel,
from the south,
 What cause have you found?
Is it justice that you fight for?
Could you live with society & all of its greed?
Or would you live on...
without those needs?
To suffer with purpose...
Is that what you want?
Or are you caught in the middle
but still won't change?
It's that pain that you flaunt,
the tears in your eyes I can see,
But time has passed,
Still on you dream,
Is it all worth it?
Could be,
And then maybe...
Not!

March 23, 1985
