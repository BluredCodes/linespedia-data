Blessed it be,
The words from thy heart
flow from the rites of assumption,
Trust come with time,
Wisdom rises within the mind,
Images pour like the rising sun,
Fear disappears in the illusion where
once ignorance reigned,
The world rises in new beginnings
and truth forever remains
in journeys we each travel,
A new dawn separates the song
held in each heart,
healing & growing new horizons,
a dream flowing like the waters
of a new world.

April 13, 1997
