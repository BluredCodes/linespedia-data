title: "Whisper Of A Child"

Child of innocence
touch my heart,
Fill me with joy
wherein the harmony
is a song within and without,
Look for the twilight
in the promise of the moon,
Starseeker touch my soul
forever seeking the breath of life,
Watch for the wind,
Its secrets reveal the hidden,
And the silence
whispers its truth.

April 9, 1999
