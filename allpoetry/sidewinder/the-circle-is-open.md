title: "The Circle Is Open"

The circle is open,
Forever touching upon a world of vision,
 Perhaps...
some may call it fantasy,
But it allows you to dream,
Speak to the faerie folk,
They show you wisdom in your life,
Without change
something sleeps inside us,
Forever forgotten,
Like a mystery never solved,
Reach into your spirit
and let the child in you live again,
Seeking play & curiosity,
Forever keeping you young.

Oct. 3, 1998
