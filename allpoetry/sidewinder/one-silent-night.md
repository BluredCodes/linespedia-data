title: "One Silent Night"

One silent night you dropped by,
We talked for a while
catching up on conversations
we thought we had lost in the silence,

I was so excited about what I was involved in,
I had to tell you all the details
about the projects I had in mind,
And in your kindness
you simply listened,

Later when we were both relaxed,
conversation turned to intimacy,
Somehow we both needed it
and sanctified the loneliness
shared in our hearts,

We savored the warmth
in our touch
and even though we hadn't planned it that way
our bodies found a way
to complete each other,

Later we parted company,
You had to work the next day,
Though I did ask you to stay,
And you smiled with a reply...
I'll see you soon!

And now I'm sitting here
anxiously awaiting your next visit,
Writing these memories
that I collect,
Hoping for more
than what I should expect,
Still..
It's true what they say,
Absence does make the heart grow fonder.
