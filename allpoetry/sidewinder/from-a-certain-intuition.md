From a certain intuition,
Perhaps...
a dream or two,
Wonder touches
within a smile,
Ascended in memories,
Otherwise
within the magic,
A promise is fulfilled
and celebrations of the heart
unique in a design
returns to those
in a realm of time,
Harmony centers within
the spirit,
Visions sacred in the belief
oblivious to the
perceptional experience & insight.

April 29, 1999
