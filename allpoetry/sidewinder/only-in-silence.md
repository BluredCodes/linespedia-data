title: "Only In Silence"

Listen to the music of the rain
falling,
Where time dances
and life reveals is secrets,
Afternoon showers chase us through the summer,
Magic rages in the twinkling of an eye,
And before you know it,
A heart touches one in places
where only the gods know.

Follow in time
where wisdom speaks
only in silence
and nature teaches those who listen,
Revelations and memories
which are held in dimensions and realms
connected to a past
divined by a sacred tune.

And in the shaman's eyes
visions come and go
to those otherworlds
where a dragon smiles
and sees into a heart
where truth and kindness
remove the veils of darkness.

Nov. 4, 1999
