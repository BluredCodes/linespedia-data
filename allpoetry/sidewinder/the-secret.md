---
title: "The Secret"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "the-secret"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

There it is our freedom,
	And our love will be there
	sharing dreams and fantasies
	at a rate that no one but us will know,
	You're my comrade
	and many travels will we know,
	But yet there is something of you
	I do not know,
	It's a specialness about you,
	A secret I long to bare,
	But maybe it's something you'd rather not share,
	You have your right to it,
	But still it lures me,
	What I can not say,
	There is curiosities of a thousand men,
	But this you will not speak of today,
	As the days grow on
	we grow even closer,
	Yes,
	The love we have,
	You can see it in our eyes,
	So as we grow older
	this thing we might even share,
	But until then
	I'll be patient and wait,
	Maybe it's just as well.
	Jan.1, 1981
