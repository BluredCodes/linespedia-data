---
title: "Recent Hours"
writer: "sidewinder"
slug: "recent-hours"
source: "AllPoetry"

To touch your warmth,
The sparkle that remains in your eyes,
The taste of your kiss,
My heart trembles at these thoughts,
Do you think of me
when the nights are cold?
Does my smile touch your soul?
When I say hello...
Does it complete thy need?
These recent hours touch in ways
that can't be found in a lifetime,
yet...
in the sand of time
a measurement is somehow lost,
Is it we the lovers
when found in our shadow
sigh only in ecstasy?
Or when the loneliness becomes too unbearable
we together find a way to pass the time?
I know I have found a thousand questions
that seem to have no answers
except when we find the silence
aroused by our body movement
which speak in volumes.
