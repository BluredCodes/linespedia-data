Through sandcastles that cross my mind,
Under the clouds
held beyond my imagination,
The quiet drifts into completion,
And yet the child like voice
whispers illumination,
Never knowing those revelations
that pierce the eyes
which echo like ripples in the sand,
Allowing time to mire in an offering to the past,
Private in a sigh,
Yet through the cries that touch,
Familiarity runs in simplicity,
Quickened in truth,
Personified by those roads
ironic in meaning.

May 16, 1999
