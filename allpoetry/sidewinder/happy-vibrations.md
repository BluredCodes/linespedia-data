Calling carousels,
Happy vibrations,
Distant melodies
hold forever in my mind,
Connecting to those dreams
that reveal simpler times
wherein the heart matters most,
And I smile
in just touching them,
Could it be
that to some
those reflections
brings harmony
and warmth
forever in joy?

June 18, 2000
