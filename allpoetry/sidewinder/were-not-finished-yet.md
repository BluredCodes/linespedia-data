title: "We're Not Finished Yet"

My amigo,
You've touched my soul,
When I was in pain,
You relieved it,
You understood
where some refused to,
We've both had...
Good times
and bad times,
But those were the moments that counted,
In your eyes
I am held true,
In time we may part,
But who knows?
Destiny as a road
may bring us back together again,
And if not...
then those memories shall always touch me,
Still...
there's a reason that we've met,
And somehow...
I don't think we're finished yet,
Because we have too many plans
uncompleted....
                 Unresolved.

Nov. 2, 2000this is for Carl ...a very good friend
