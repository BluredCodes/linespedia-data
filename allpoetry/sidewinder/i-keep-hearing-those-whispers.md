title: "I Keep Hearing Those Whispers"

I keep hearing those whispers
that reach into the wind
forever keeping those secrets
soaring into the night,
Never realizing those souls
that touch a heart are
forever remembered
only within the silence,
Could it be that the dream
echoes beyond the illusion?
And each time that it is heard
a miracle remains to those
who understand,
Forever whispering into the darkness
where fears resolve themselves
and touch a completeness
where a universe smiles in understanding.

Feb. 9, 2000
