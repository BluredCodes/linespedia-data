title: "Two That Touch"

Two that touch
in ways forgotten by most,
Embraced by the heart,
Emotions celebrated in time,
Could it be that memories
forever reach...
only to consume & entwine
with the other half?
And when the twin shadows meet
the extraordinary happens,
Forever remembered,
Forever celebrated,
Smiling where expressions need a moment
to cling.
