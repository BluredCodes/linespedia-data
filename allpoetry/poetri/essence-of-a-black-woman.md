Essence of a Black Woman

 I am a black woman loud and proud I stand
 Brown skin, fair skin, and dark skin are the shades of my lovely face
 Big brown eyes, full beautiful lips, and soft round breast
 All of which are gifts my African mother gave to me

 Mysterious and complicated
 That's what people say
 Hard to understand 
 Some feel that way

 I just don't know what to say about black women today
 I'm not complicated or hard to understand
 They try so hard to unriddle my mystery 
 But they can't seem to figure it out
 It's not a mystery about a black woman 
 It's the ESSENCE OF A QUEEN
