Pain is an over-used word,
i admit.
But this must be the real thing,
true and raw.

How does it feel?

It sucks.

But deeper than that,
Your simple request for departure
meant more to me
than you'll ever have the privilage of knowing.

Who do I call at night?
Who will give me The Feeling?
I don't know, 
and when you left

I didn't care.

All I know is you're gone
and that I still love you,
and I don't know myself.
I used to, why don't I know myself?
You misled me, and I loved every minute.

I guess the feeling wasn't mutual.
