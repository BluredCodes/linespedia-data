Oh no, no honey, you have not yet seen
The red hot anger flowing to every part of my body.
You'll never see it;
The blind love I possess for ALL things.

You'll speak empty words
And I'll spit spiky replies.
They will surprise you,
And you will call me "bitch",
And I will not give a fuck.

Cuz baby, that's who I am:
Too deep,
Too serious,
Too loud,
Too understanding

For you...

So keep your distance, little man.

I'll bite,
And I will not write
Some phony note of apology.

Honey, you're gonna hafta get over yourself
And accept me.

This is who I am.
Deal with it.
