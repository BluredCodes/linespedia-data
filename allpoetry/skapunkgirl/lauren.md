A beautiful symphony
plays all around you.
You're caught up in the music,
letting it take you in.

Then suddenly, it is cut off.

Is this analogy even close 
to a young life being taken away?
I don't think so.

I've tried many times to write about this.
It always ends with tears.
Hot, sincere tears
streaming down my cheek.

There is a point in a deep-thinker's life
Where the answer is simply right on the shallow surface:

You're gone, and I don't know the reason.
There may not be one.
I just need to accept that.

I can no longer talk to you, and hear your sweet voice reply.
I can no longer see your face pull into that smile.
It's hard, but I'm here anyway.
