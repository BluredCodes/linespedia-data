title: "Just Young Love"

My heart skips a beat
whenever you call.
You've let me down once,
Please don't let me fall.

No one approves
But all through the night,
You tell me you love me,
It makes it alright.

Don't you just wish
We could make a big bubble?
Then no one can tell us
That together we're trouble.

This time is it true?
There's been so much shit.
Your love clarifies things,   
I'm good when you give it.

No one sees what I see,
And when we're alone,
Oh, it's so sweet,
My heart's simply flown.

This is just a love poem
From a girl to a boy.
Although it's pretty average,
Your love I enjoy.  :-)
