title: "Sometimes a Broken Heart"
writer: "Apparition"

Sometimes a broken heart
can't be cured in
... a day
... a week
... a month
... a lifetime

Sometimes a broken heart
can't be cured with
... chocolate cake
... chocolate pudding
... chocolate ice cream
... lasagna

But it can start to mend
with
... time
... talking
... friends
... and eventually, forgiving.
