title: "Mouth full of poison"

Blood spilled heart-felt head filled, play dead
Consider yourself now my friend enemy number
One you are bound and gagged, sacred circle broken trust now the plague has been cast in your town, acid tongue venom word why bother to lie? Leave the lamp behind your mouth full of head and lies. Don’t walk in fire, you might find that you’ll get burned what I find dirty face Guilt at last, is this a lesson that’s learned? Can you hear your blood in your veins, calling out your demise? Silence a word to the wise I cant hear your scream drowning so it seems your mouth is full of poison.
