Little kids starting out
Starting out at school
Some are excited
Some are sad
Sad about leaving mum
Guess you have seen the news
Some little kids are picked on
We all wonder why don't we
Guess there isn't a balance
Of love and repect there
So its not the child who is picked on
Who needs the extra care
Its the child who is doing the picking
It could be have been a little problem
When the child was only small
But because it hurt so much inside
A little anger inside them
That might have made the roots
From their little tree go wrong
Because we are part of nature
Because we all need to be nurtured
Because its all inside us all
We all need to be nurtured
Within our little tree
If things get sorted 
When they first start out
First start school
There there would be a balance
That we could aim for
As we watch them grow
As every little thought
Adds to thought to thought
And if kids have thoughts
Thoughts turn to joy
The Good Aussie "GIDA MATE"
Would be still strong and clearThinking back on my school days.
