I'm trying to understand them
I'm trying very Hard
Come to think of It
Are you a Bus Man
Or are you a Bum
Doesn't Matter You're a Man
Not being Disrespectful
Just Playing with Words
Men like the Body
Women like the Words
I would send you "Today"
If You would hear, Tomorrow
Yesterday has gone Now,
Yet Tomorrow is still to Come
Don't Mean to be Disrespectful 
But I'm trying to Learn
To Understand Everybody
Will you learn my Words
I Respect my Body 
Will you Respect Her's
When Tomorrow becomes Today
I'm thinking about
It thinking all the Time
It effects My Heart
It effects My Mind
No One is to Blame
Can take a Life Time 
To Understand 
HUMANS !
