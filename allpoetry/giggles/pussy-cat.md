Pussy Cat, Pussy Cat
Sitting over there
How I love you so..
And the love that you give me
When you curl your tail around me
Giving Love So Tenderly

Knowing me better than Me
As you know my Heart
As you don't Speak
But show your love for Me

Poor Pussy Cat
The World is trying
To tame you, stopping you 
From killing your Prey
If they only knew

It was part of Nature
To keep the Birds Alive
To give them Reason 
To keep them Alive
So they won't loose 
Their want to Fly

As birds know we,
Humans take their homes
But humans don't feel
Deep enough for the cat
And blame the Cat
For the mess in life
For the birds plight

But forget so Quickly
We Human's sent the Cat
Outside into the Wild
And Just like all Animals
Are Just trying 
TO STAY ALIVE
