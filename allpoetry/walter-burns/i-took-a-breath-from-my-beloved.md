I took a breath from my beloved  
and carried it away  
for that is what she did for me 
one blesséd yesterday 

I took the sight from my beloved 
because she had so much 
I breathed her in my memory 
and know of what she's touched 

But when I went to see her thoughts
you know what I should find? 
Through all the layers of her mask 
inside, she pays no mind!
