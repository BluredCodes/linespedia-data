---
title: "Runaway"
writer: "Walter Burns"
slug: "runaway"
source: "AllPoetry"

I'm not coming back this time 
You can't make me see the light 
You may think that you are right 
But I've stepped into the night 

Cannot reach me in your bed 
Cannot teach me, I am dead 
I have found the friends I seek 
Cold and dark and I am weak 

Saw myself inside a star 
Wonder Wonder where you are 
Saw myself I'm falling fast 
Someone wished it wouldn't last 

Maybe I could turn back now 
But I will not milk that cow 
Now I've left and I am gone 
Do not know the road I'm on 

Someone Someone what to do 
Someone Someone be like you 
Someone Someone save my soul 
Someone Someone make me whole 

The record spins in this street 
Connecting veins in my feet 
Doubt is clouding in my mind 
Now it’s raining on my shine 

But its dark and I can't see 
Wishing you were more like me 

No one likes to show you how 
Do the things you do yourself 
Begin...
I do not know how to go 
It’s too late to go back home  
Within...
Saw myself I'm falling fast 
Someone wished it wouldn't last 
Who wins?
Who wins?
Who wins?
