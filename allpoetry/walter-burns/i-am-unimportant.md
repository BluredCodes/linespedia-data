---
title: "I am unimportant"
writer: "Walter Burns"
slug: "i-am-unimportant"

I am unimportant
existence bleeds
into misery's 
drain
now gone and left
with picket fences
and bombed
out houses-
memories-
how can I matter
I'm a name on a page
and before anyone will care
I'll be dead
and they'll all say,
"What a queer look
they had back then."
