Inferior,
Hurled from a broken cast
Everyone was there in line
But I guess that I was last

Inferior,
Not as good as you
Even though we all were born
When the world was
Something new

Inferior,
On the edge of something great
I look into the chasm, yawning
And deem that it’s my fate

Inferior,
Fades in the shallow pit
Just enough to bury me
Yet not enough to quit
