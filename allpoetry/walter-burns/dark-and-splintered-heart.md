title: "Dark and Splintered Heart"

Is love a dark and tendered heart 
A paradoxical support 
That holds the plain and splintered world
In lieu, in elder, timeless court 

If love is filled in tankard’s breadth
And mixed with measured cups of pain
To add an ounce or maybe two
Would make it sweeter, slow to drain

Is love a thing to banter ‘bout
As if it’s folly, man should seek
To craft a world that’s steady thus
A marriage formed of bold to meek
