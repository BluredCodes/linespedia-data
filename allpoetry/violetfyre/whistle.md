---
title: "Whistle"
writer: "violetfyre"

---

The train whistle tells me it's going home,
But all that's home is the face
That went through $150 of cocaine in one night.
But it's the face that holds me
In the smoke-filtered light
Long enough to fall in love.

The clock tells me it's going to be a long night.
Your tear-tinged voice tells me it'll be a long life
Of battling you and battling drugs for your attention,
While your family and friends are too stoned to attend your intervention.

Your lips tell me it's a one-time thing,
But your eyes tell me you're unsure.
My memory tells me you've lied before.
I'm not sure of anything.

This paper tells me it's getting late.
Tear-smeared ink stains the page,
And words are blurred through these brown eyes
That saw your smiles, your apologies, your lies.

You wear the face that screams regression,
And I'm screaming at the top of my lungs.
Everything tells me I should hate you.
I know I'll never leave.
