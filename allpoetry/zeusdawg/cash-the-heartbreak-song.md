Cruisin’ with the top down, know what it’s like
Chillin’ out no doubt no yellow stripe
Down my back even though you be hurtin’ me so
Damn right I’m still mad cuz you jerkin’ me though
You got the nerve to ask if friendship is an issue
Guaranteed, you ain’t even there to hand me a tissue
To wipe my fool tears so I just don’t cry
Just as well cuz I think that I would rather die
Than to feel the way you make me feel
It was good but it was you with the final seal
You know what I’m sayin’?  You shoulda been lookin’
Too late now, cuz you can’t smell what Zeus is cookin’
For you it’s a dish that is best served cold
It ain’t revenge even though revenge is sweet I’m told
Or maybe it is either way you know I can’t tell
They say the best revenge is living well
Doing swell but look at what you did toss
Into the lake let me tell you that it’s your loss
Such a shame it didn’t work
If it’d been me such a jerk

Hook:
You know you broke my heart, but that’s okay
Cuz it’s good I learned to feel this way
Got no love for you nothing left to say
Cuz you threw my love away
2x

You think you’re the cream of the crop, the Tigger Doll
Started swallowing so much, now you’re the bigger doll
You’ll take the quicker fall than most that fight me
Your hands will end up around your neck like Spike Lee
Getting into battle with the original master
You can’t dodge my bullets unless you move faster
The rapid fire blaster making me number one
And as Zeus done wrote it, so shall it be done
You can’t run, used to laying on your back
In the back of a two door, or some college student’s mat
Needing relief from the truth? Look to the skies
Another reminder of getting laid by five guys
Don’t make excuses I ain’t paying any mind
You didn’t really care when they were spankin’ your behind
Seek and ye shall find why I rhyme with such emotion
My lyrics’ll make you feel so loose you’ll need some lotion
Beggin’ for potions but the motions that you multiply
With so many others your double standards surely fly
Such a great friend always missing the alert
And when I really needed you, you left me in the dirt

Hook 2x

You know my love is like cash, an ATM card swipe
It’s moolah, or wampum when we smokum peace pipe
My skills make you sing, do-re-miin’
Yo it’s like cha-ching, when you be seein’
Dough that be turnin’ into bread
Greenbacks, without ‘em you’d already be dead
You got the Washingtons they be buying you things
But if you want bigger things, it’s the new Benjamins
Currency, the francs, for wedding bells
Drachma, the Euro, or some lady’s shells
Mexico with the peso, London with the pound
Maybe in a barter, you could use a cow
Then Mongolia, they got the Togrog
Got so much money maybe you should go for a jog
See what you would have if you would have been willing?
Your own secret stash of all my schillings
