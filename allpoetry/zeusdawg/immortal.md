From two years ago to now in the making
The Z-Dawg lyrics are done, and earth-quaking
Comin’ out of nowhere, now you got the sound
Leave it to the cream of the crop to throw down
Verses so heavy you know you’ll feel lost
And if you battle Zeus you can’t afford the cost
Tired of the games so I’m gonna pull your card
Before physically feathered, you’ll get lyrically tarred
And abused, after the wrong fuse
Was lit up, run down, washed and reused
Now it’s too short and the fuse is past raw
The camel’s broke down you reached your last straw
I can’t ignore your wack rhymes anymore
Now it’s too late to change your new fate
The weather locks of change are taking you under
Zeus Dawg, The Immortal, has come to drop thunder

The Immortal, Zeus Dawg is the one in the house
Whether you a chick babe spouse or two bit louse
The Immortal, Zeus Dawg is the one in the place
Your lyrical talent can’t even look him in the face

Sixteen lines to match my clean rhymes
While fake-ass cats brag about supreme crimes
Ignoring Olympus that blasts like turbines
You wanna mess with Zeus?  Well you’re mine
The only advice I can give is remain idle
Cuz jumpin’ in the ring with me, is suicidal
Better make the guys in your section stand
And put ‘em all in the Lyrical Protection Plan
I made your selection, and it went boom
Zeus and DL is here, so make room
We’re comin’ through better watch where you’re steppin’
Cuz in this game of chance I’m the ultimate weapon
I’ll never come in second, and right now I’m pissed
Don’t ask for specifics I’ve got a whole list
I’m coming with verses so heavy, it’s like lumber
My I. Q. is more than three times your house number

Hook 4x

You wonder why these lyrics I’ve written that I’m now spittin’
Are getting in your face like Bash Brothers that are hard hittin’
Well, you see me in the street and I’ll watch your pulse quicken
And that’s when you’ll meet your defeat, like a false chicken
Cuz I mean every word I say and you best listen
Any way you like mess with me you’ll end up missin’
I entertain’ when I step in the ring
So just excuse me while I do my Zeus Dawg thingmore lyrics... Hmmm... This has been a good morning...
