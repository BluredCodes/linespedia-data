In rap I'm what you call a mimic
I'll spit your rhymes, four times, before you ever get to breathe a lyric
You got no speed or spirit, plus you have no freakin' heart
I won't rest until I find you trapped inside a cuisinart
Put your knees in park, no sudden moves, or flash photos
I'm brash, loco, paranoid, and I'll see cash solo
My fast flow goes to the speed of beating snare drums
If I go slow, you'll end up broke and eating chair crumbs
With rats-a-crawlin', while I relax a mat to sprawl it
Yo, my name is Zeus and I'm a friggin' rapaholic
There's no cure for this type of virus, the mic's inside us
Nights define us more than all our different likes divide us
You can't outrhyme us, with lollipops and cauliflower
Act a fool and we'll just call the cops and go for hours
I'm everlasting, never fasting, forever maxing
Cheddar blasting, with a knack for the better casting

Chorus:
Who am I? Only the guy, who wouldn't try
To stand idly by while you got drunk and got high (4x)

My friggin' match was sparked before the psychiatric ward
When all my cash went into choosing guys for my theatric board
Shakespeare, who sips wine, but never takes beer
Stays clear, then observes what he creates here
I relate fear to keep your system and your brain together
Got you chasing shadows while you run away from chain letters
My name's better than anything that you can't imagine
In a land of fashion I'll make fun of how your pants are saggin'
Don't cross the borderline, you'll order yours, I'll order mine
If you wanna shack up with an expensive whore that's fine
I know you're mortified to be facing your lord inside
Of the beat, when the immortal might bust you up with his .45
I'm from a city of lights, where the witty and bright
Had better never leave their hits in sight
Cuz they’ll get jacked up tight, before they close their eyes
They’ll steal your jokes and lies and eat your coke and fries
Who am I? Only the guy, who wouldn’t try
To stand idly by while you got drunk and got high
Got punked to the sky, got sunk in denial
Got convicted lyrically as your defense stunk in a trial
All the while my rhymes become legendary
Abuse the mic like a machine gun mercenary
No time for punks to try to write a verse and barely rhyme
The visionary climbs more than the stage can carry...

Chorus

If you catch me in the street I might be shy, probably won’t try
To end your life but once I get on stage and on a mic
My rage triples, I’ll leave you mad cripple
Wishin’ you were back in time and sucking on your dad’s nipple
And I did it from the ground up
From knowing nothing bout this game to breaking the whole town up
One thing or another I did got made fun of
But I kept going until I got the verbal gun up
Then I became an instant traitor
The instigators, wannabe litigators, went from men to haters
Now I’ll see you later, time to finish with my story
Become the best for years and then fulfill my glory
Instill parental tears, and make my foes yield
To the one true Immortal that rules the whole field
Keeps the door sealed, reaps the sowed meals
Relates to you and tries to answer the question...
