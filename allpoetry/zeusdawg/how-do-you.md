How do you tell your best friend
When they just came out of the closet
That you're homophobic
And don't want to be friends anymore?

How do you let the poor clique you belong to
Know that you're rich now
And won't be around them?

How do you tell the great girl who asked you out
That she's too ugly for you
And you're looking for a calendar girl?

Chorus:
How do you stomp on someone's heart
And throw it out the window
Without giving it another thought?

How do you tell the retarded kid
That he's too stupid
To play on your stickball team?

How do you let your mother know
You don't want her to come to school night
Because she has two jobs?

How do you make fun of the fat kid
Who you were just joking with
Because it would make some girl laugh?

Chorus

How do you try to make your friend
Of many years leave a treasure behind
Just so you can claim it?

How do you invite the depressed girl out
Even though you don't like her
You just like her big tits?

How do you try to shatter someone's dreams
Because you're supremely jealous
That they surpass your own?
