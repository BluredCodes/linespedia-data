Can he like just me?
I know he has a girlfriend
Do I have a shot?

I don't have money
But I want a brand new car
Where can I get one?

I can't date this girl
Keep her in my back pocket
While I shop around

Do you think he cheats?
He's always cheated before
I think I'm special

Can't remember me
Just because he smokes a lot
And he's always high

I have the same job
And I drink every weekend
But I'm still matureGod... I hate stupid people...always saying stupid things... stupid... what a dumb word
