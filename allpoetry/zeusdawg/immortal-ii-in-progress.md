I treat the microphone like an action figure
Pose and make it fight the bad guys with the skill my rap delivers
Zeus on the attack you shiver while you're falling to your knees
Crawling through your screams, cold, foes are dropping like degrees
Beg for mercy or your life, never worthy of the fight
Running is a bad idea, better hurt me or the sight
Of immortal on your tail firing from his urgent pen
Zeus’ll catch you now and he’s coming faster than virgin men
Surging when haters shout in the fray
Electricity ability move out of the way
It's the judge jury execute announcing delay
Better budge hurry next to you's an ounce of decay
You wish you hadn't said what you said, but now it's too late
About your escape you'll power shouts as loud as you may
