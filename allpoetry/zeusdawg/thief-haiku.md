Wallet sticking out
Grab and pull out his pocket
Now I have money

He notices me
Yelling for police to chase
So I start to run

Into the subway
Over turnstile I throw
Token at driver

Reach in for my prize
Oh no! Must have dropped the cash
At least I'm not caught
