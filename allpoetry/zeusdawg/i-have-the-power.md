It ain’t even a thing, all you other cats can’t even sing
About what Zeus brings to this here left wing
The immortal, on tour with the world’s best engine
It’s no wonder I score when I get my revenge in
Silly punks, lookin’ like a sideshow
In they mama’s car braggin’ bout how they ride low
Ridin’ right into a Zeus Dawg trap
All I need is one smack and I’ll put ‘em on a road map
Wishin’ they could rhyme like Zeus, the rap footloose
If you battle me, son it’s like vamoose
Whether truce or a treaty, rich, famous, or needy
Run into my crew and we’ll hit you with the BB
Trust me, I know from experience, I’m serious
At the end of this you’ll recognize the rhyming genius
Olympian, allegiance never moved
And I’m kid tested, and mother approved
