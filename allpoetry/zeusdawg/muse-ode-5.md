She sits there
Waiting for me, I imagine
The most beautiful woman I've never seen
Her hair waves in the wind
Skin sparkles in the sunlight
Eyes so deep I could fall in them for days
She only gives me one thing
And that ain't sexual
It ain't physical material
With one look
Just one look
She can make the gears in my brain and my heart
Turn as one
So I can sculpt the greatest works the world has ever seen
She's my inspiration
I'm nothing without her
If I was good before
I'd be perfect when I've found her
