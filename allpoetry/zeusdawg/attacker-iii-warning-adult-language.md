Going to school I was the triple "R" master
Arts and crafts or charts and graphs I always passed
Top of my class a teacher's pet with an attitude
Swung without having to, twice when I'm mad at you
And never caught, a million crimes and never served time
Calmed down and moved on to a better word rhyme
Brain divided by a mental apartheid
Where genius would hide the dark side, you see
I was surrounded by these assholes always living up the glass life
Claiming they hard when they can't even wipe they ass right
So last night I got myself a little dark
And let my anger get the best of me, the temper shit'll spark
First guy didn't even see it coming, the second ducked to swing
Missed and I hit him in the jaw, he saw his luck begin
To fall, Zeus Dawg just hit the wall and made the neck snap
Came back from the attack in time to write the next rap
