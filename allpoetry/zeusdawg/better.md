Whether to start or to end, better can't even agree to describe me
While you get mad because the friggin' beat is inside me
Jealous because I don't even have to try to outrhyme you
And upset because to take my check is taking yours times two
Don't get upset, I'm just better than most
Debtors and hosts, cheddar can roast and forget I'm a ghost
Since the beginning I've been overshadowed and overjoyed
At the thought of being praised up by my cuorduroys
I beat this guy over the head last night
Just cuz I caught him holding a glass pipe
Monday morning while you're asleep
I just did more than you could do in your whole week
Defeating me is a tall enough task
Trying to ask me to submit makes you fall twice as fast
Nice at last with the spirit of the song in me
While language freezes when I step to it so wrongfully

Lyrically I, play with juxtaposition
You mess with me, you'll get sent on an impossible mission
My quarters always jump inside the well wishin'
They could sit a spell, so they would know to tell, kiss and listen
The one man in the world that can lease britches for a week
Just to go on a date with Denise Richards
And come back with a hop in my step, limpin' a while
From the footrace that she won putting a crimp in my style
Dirty minds follow over-flirty times
While I skip town counting down from thirty rhymes
Just to get my verbal hex settled, do it again to get my check doubled
And take my rhyming to the next level
Now let's wrestle from the seat of your pants
You'd better quit, and admit, your defeat or your chance
Of survival in the rap world, land of the "Z"
Will get cut by sixty-seven down to thirty-three

[I'm better than you] From thunderbolts to trips
I devote my lips to proving I wrote the script
And folks'll flip, when they see the ropes and whips
Cuz their hopes'll dip after all their jokes get ripped
Forever clever betting less in degrees
And getting blessed with the keys to mixing guesses with pleas
It seems incessant to me, at least unstoppable when
I beat you every day and your attacks don't hop at the trend
My songs pop to empty the pawn shop
And you can hear it from a bus when you visit the wrong stop
It's a long shot, even if you're a fairweather friend
We have a massacre here, so would you care to attend?
Come on, I think it's scary to send, your very best men
Into battle with the genius who won't dare to pretend
So now let's tear it again, you think you're better than me
Wanna prove it? Then we should get together and seeOne more song
