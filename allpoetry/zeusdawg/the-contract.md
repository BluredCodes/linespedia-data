Zeus, the chaser, hereafter referred to as Jeff
Existing as legal matter and since my last breath
Was to come up with a way to let you know that he’s here
Immortal and invincible describes the disease clear
You, the friend, hereafter referred to as Bree
Has love from Jeff, through the crazy and insanity
This is a promissory note that no matter what happens
Jeff won’t stop lovin’ like he won’t stop rappin’
Bree has proved to be a true ally, a great friend
In existing in the do-or-die, the late end
Of this friendship will make up for its late beginning
Jeff and Bree will collaborate and create the winnings
Bree should know she’s one half of Jeff’s friend spectrum
Now that he’s cut ties with all the rest and left them
So it’s official Bree can use this to defend her rights
This will hold up in the court of Zeus, friends for lifeJust in case you were worried...
