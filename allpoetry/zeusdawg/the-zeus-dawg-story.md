Misunderstood from the age of one, never packed a street gun
Didn’t need it, back at apartment forty-one
Fought my way from the projects to all things suburban
Helped my parents out so they could have their own curb and
Claim success, but the rest I can’t condone
Life full of stress had to try and make it on my own
Then I ran into this Wuss Side thing followed by the rap game
Started to rhyme now Zeus Dawg’z the name
Helpin’ my fam be my point
A family kid I’ll make it big, but I never rolled a single joint
Never pulled a weapon just to take somebody’s wallet
And I don’t care about what you wanna call it
Been called every name in the book
I recognize when lies and envy got everybody shook
That’s all it took for a great to be born
Creative linguistic steady with my form

Stop, look, and listen to the cream of the crop
That pops, hooks and hot lyrics meet at the top
And hops, skips, and jumps only farther away
I say, I’d take your order but the bar’s in the way
So stay, right where you are and don’t move an inch
My Super Soaker’s set to spray and if you even flinch
Zeus Dawg’ll pinch the trigger and you’ll be all wet
Cuz What you see is what you get
And placing bets on Zeus Roulette
Is a hot experience that you won’t forget
So stand right next to me and you’ll be all set
We’ll catch every batch with my fishing net
Steady leave you torn, I say just what I’m provin’
Ready with the hip-hop thing to keep you movin’
Seventy seven, make that seventy eight, no wait
Check the tempo so my beat’s never late

Out looking at nature? When birds split, I hit ‘em
Rhyming with my crew, the words fit, my rhythm
Z-Dawg, with the DLWG
Jumpin’ into battle with the high and mighty
Watch your back, cuz my allies are restless
Trust me, you’ll hate yourself if you test us
Always ready, Ever steady, never letting up
On the grip in the musical gun clip
Thunderbolts ain’t no joke
Electricity goes from me to the Hayters causin’ smoke
Hair standing on end gets so you can’t pretend
That all miracle trends release with what I send
Everybody bow down to the Zeus Dawg sound
Especially those thinkin’ that I’m playin around
Fools givin’ me crap bout how they pound for pound
Turn around, and you’ll see how I just ran you downYet another song...
