---
title: "Denial is Better"
writer: "dottie"
slug: "denial-is-better"

No there is not hope!
Mankind will never be kind.
Mankind will never free itself
from filth and muck and mire.
For man creates himself as much
as trash and wind and fire.
A carless child who never grows
and learns to never help.
But whineswhen trapped in his own room
unable to cross the wire.
To stomping feet and useless strife
another man will beat his wife.
I cried when I was young and strong
"These pigs will never win!!"
And so with cause stoked vim
I jumped into the fray.
War torn now and veteran of
party politics
The world of Thomas Jefferson
is just a bunch of sticks.
Oh, yes the three:
Faith and hope and charity.
God ego id.
Denial is better.
Somewhere over the rainbow.
But one CAN call it hope.
