title: "Sole(Soul)Hope"
writer: "dottie"
slug: "solesoulhope"

I So
don't want to get in your head
I can't find the way out of mine yet.
I So
can't see your spirit
My own is in my way.
There is nothing like the quietude
of sleepy man machines.
Nothing like the ages
Iceless and evergreen.
The cycle falls and rises
rise and fall the tides
I So
don't want to walk on you
with words or boots or guile.
Can it be there is NO agenda
no threat to: "can't we idle just a while?"
Life and death is just a crap shoot.
Let my children bury me
not the other way around.
For in the long and short of it
that's the sole hope to be found.
