title: "heartaches and such"

If it weren't for old heart aches and such,
No one would talk.
For what would there be to say?
If not for love gone wrong
"Shoot, she done went and hurt me fellers"
It would add up to a very long day.
I daresay what? on tv, movie, book and song
what? would fill the pages of our life book,
God willing they be long.
There would not spring from hearts all broken
Poems filled by dreams unspoken.
So here's to heartache:
Live long and prosper
for you are the beer of life.
