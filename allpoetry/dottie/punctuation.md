I love punctuation,
 it tells me what to do.
 To stop.
 To think?
 To pause,
 To list:
 to add a list; into a list-
 it tells me" what to say",
 it helps me find my way(go here not there),
 and, it helps me say ,hooray!
 I love punctuation with all its'
 many flaws-
 and mistakes that people make with it
 Brings me great Guffaws!!!
