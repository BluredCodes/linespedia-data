I didn't keep you.
I gave you up to the
clinic and the sucking
force of my long lost
self-esteem and my
fear and so I became
a haunted human
a ghost.

I didn't stay with you.
I gave you up to the numbness
of cocaine, booze and the
search for self-esteem and
easy money and so I became
a hunted human
a dealer.

I didn't walk away from you.
I gave myself up to the insane
play of some drama written long
ago and so became
a shadow human
an actor.

And I am acting still.
