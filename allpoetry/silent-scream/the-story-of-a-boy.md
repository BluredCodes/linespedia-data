---
title: "The story of  a boy"
writer: "silent scream"
slug: "the-story-of-a-boy"

Run your fingers through my hair
hold me tightly to what you love
I just want to be with this 
and nothing else
in the dark
i don't want shadows
in the light
to be alone
it's been here too long
and no longer is it
avoidable.
I can't help but wonder 
what it's like 
to hold you with compassion
to love you not a crime
I want this to be the beginning of something I'll never forget.
