title: "Don't live it collecting dust"
writer: "silent scream"

I can't break through
the door I don't own
with shattered blue flowers
and orange popcicle lips.
The angels need to die
black inside
faults as no other.
Make them feel better
because I can't do it alone.
Stick a key in the socket
learn to fly for a new beginning
love lost suicide
speak no more.
Everyday is a new day.
The bright black sun shines in
nothing i can do
I feel so alive it hurts.
You can only crawl in the dark
for so long
It's time to stop dwelling 
time to live a new life.
These problems are holding me down
down to a chain.
Too hard to build wings
when the skin won't break.
boil new blood
red not blue.
When I perfect the recipe
I'll give you a call.
