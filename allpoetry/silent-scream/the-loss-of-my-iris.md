So they stand.
blue, green, red and purple.
the blue being annoying.
the green being subtle.
the red being cute.
the purple being rebellious.

         ***

Blue sees the cloud
turning more grey every second.
Green sees the fog 
slowly cleary as moving.
Red sees the flower
standing scarlet in between.
Purple sees the blanket
being pulled over her eyes.

         ***

And together they stand
Blue wallows in her puddle.
Green hides behind the teeth.
Red closes the transparent door.
Purple freezes in the snow.

         ***

And behind their own closed curtains
they wonder
will I ever be free?
Four girls 
Four lives
Four minds
Four hearts
Four friends
