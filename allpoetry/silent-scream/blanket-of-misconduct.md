---
title: "blanket of misconduct"
writer: "silent scream"
slug: "blanket-of-misconduct"
source: "AllPoetry"

It physically hurts
to be
the raindrop 
that lands in the lake of these 
They are so much more than me
bulemic, anorexic and all.
They are so much more useful than me
fucking until you fall.
I am a blue raindrop
and they are all black
black like rubber and tar
that sticks to kill
and suffocates you.
I want a blanket of misconduct
following over my head
When i need your warmth
I'm able
When I need your touch 
you're there
Oblivious to what it is i'm thinking
and the same part for me
I want to know
my blanket of misconduct
want to know 
like I thought I used to.
i want what's right infront of my face
yet it's blind behind his eyes.
i will not cry though it kills
i will not scream though it tortures
i will silently crawl in a hole and wait for the scary part of the movie where i close my eyes until it's over 
wait 'til the rejection's over.
