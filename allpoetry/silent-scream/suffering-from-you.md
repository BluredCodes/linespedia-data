---
title: "Suffering from you"
writer: "silent scream"

it's a black bug crawling up my spine
with eyes shimmering red 
and claws digging bright.
it was searching for a place to scrape the bone
and it found a couple, lucky.
so I don't know how to get it out
but it keeps digging farther in
it's nearing the bone marrow
the pain is leaving a screaching wretched sound
flailing about in my ears.
my teeth are clenched all day
my spine is turning black and purple
each vertabrae screams for me to kill
will someone please give me a knife and a shovel
to dig this fucking ugly asshole out?!
