the moon is an
open throat
offering melody 
to the percussion
of fingertips
and rain
tapping  
nakedness.

vanilla silk
slipped,
fallen, 
tangled round 
frenzied limbs.

a glissade
of droplets
tracing, teasing, 
collecting like
a sea of 
eager tongues
speaking and tasting
till they fall
lazy and numb
in a hush.

open your eyes in 
this darkness,
there is still much 
to be seen when
the lights fall
to sleep.for my friend with the big imagination...hehehe!
