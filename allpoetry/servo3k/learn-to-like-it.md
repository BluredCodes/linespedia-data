you spit at me with you firery words of "wisdom"
your advice is so insulting

"you are stupid"
"stop waiting on cupid"
"you cant handle dealing with yourself"
"learn to live on your own"

i see what you are trying to say

LEARN TO LIKE BEING ALONE

you say its better for me
well it probably is

you say im to young to be this sad
yeah i probably am

i suppose i am weak


but i dont like to be alone
i didnt think that was a weakness.......

UNTILL NOWi wrote this about a conversation with a friend i had
