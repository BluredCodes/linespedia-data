the world is ending
(drink)(burp)
are yall reading the news any?
i would not want to be the president right now
south korea is begging for their lives
and america has got to big for its britches
(drink)(gulp)
ha
were getting rid of all the drugs
we'll never do that

blah blah blah

he rambles to my mom

she yawns and changes the channel on the t.v.

and he still rambles on

she leaves the room

he still rambles on

and on and on

(belch)

there are too many people on earth

im surprised this has never happened before

my mom returns to the room

"michael im trying to watch this" she says

do you want to watch something that happened ten years ago or do you want to hear about something happening now?

(does he expect an answer?)

i sit at my computer and record almost everything he says

(belch)

and america aint got timei just sat here and typed everything that went on in my house for about twenty minutes or so, then sat back and laughed. is every house hold this sad?
