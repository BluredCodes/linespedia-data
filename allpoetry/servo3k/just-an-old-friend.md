---
title: "just an old friend"
writer: "servo3k"

left with a longing pain
a void
riped apart
lost in the dark
looking for you
you are no where in reach

has it been to long?
where did you go?
things are blurred
what feelings are left?
are there any at all
I suppose not

have you changed any my dear
its been so long

I feel like i should be asking simple questions like:
how is the family?
how is the job?
oh thats good
things could be better for me

but you probably figured that
but you probably didnt care

did you ever?
