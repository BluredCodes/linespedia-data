your love is rough on the heart
first it captures my hearts attention
then your love procedes to completely invade me 
and not allow me to focus on anything else
you fill me up with your ficticious words of happiness and "our future"
but then you start to change and with draw your love
you begin to hold back
you start to with draw yourself from me
then you tease me with your love
you take it away for short periods at a time
then return in such sweet ways
as if im  fortunate to have you
you even lie and say i will never be blessed with having you again
but once again you return your love in a grand package
i accept it with such joy and appreciation
(i play along with your game, not knowing what you were planning the whole time)
but then your love plays its final trick 
an evil trick it is
it leaves one last time
never to return to me
and now you act as if none of it happened
