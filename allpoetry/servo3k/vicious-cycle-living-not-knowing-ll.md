damn this vicious cycle

i have no one to blame but myself
(or maybe you)

im once again "living not knowing"
no one to blame but myself
(or maybe you)

i feel so "used and broken"
all i can do is wait
and fret for my future

stress for my future
stress for my family
stress about myself

such a state to be in living not knowing
"that girl was so out-going"

well not anymore
