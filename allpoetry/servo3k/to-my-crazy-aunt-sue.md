sue,
you crazy old woman. i never realized how much you ment to me untill you were gone. ive always heard that you dont realize what you have untill its gone, well now i know the true meaning and feeling of that phrase. i remember the holidays with you, you always made me laugh. you were good to my grandmother, she misses you so much. i never realized how great of a person you were untill you were gone. i feel horrible, i wish i could have absorbed every wonderful thing about you before you left. you left your husband, but dont worry your children will take care of him. he will be okay. youre funeral was so touching. everyone had such wonderful memories of you. and we laughed as if you were still there. please dont worry about you grandchildren, they will be well taken care of. they are beutiful and they have very loving parents. my grandmother misses you calling her every night and tieing up her line for two hours. she always gripd about it, but now she would give anything to hear the phone ring, answer it, and hear your voice. the holidays will be a little more dull now that your gone. but we will all share our memories of you together. sue, we will all miss you and we cant wait to join you.

                                 keeping your memory alive,

                                         shellythis a letter to my aunt sue who passed away about a month ago
