shut up
shut up
just shut your god-damn mouth!
im so sick of your never ending pointless bitching
nothing you say will make a difference
deal with it
you are not important to me
not your place
not your right
please....i dont want to fight
just shut-up!i was realy angry with my brother when i wrote this.
