title: "jelousy?"

jelousy is the feeling
not sure why

but when i hear your name i want to scream
i cant stand the thought of you enjoying your self
why cant you sit in your room and cry for once

why cant you feel anything?

i dont understand my jelousy
it seems as though i have nothing to be jelous of
so i dont understand why i have this feeling

are you having more fun than i?
surely not
do you have more friends?
maybe
but not true friends (they all talk about you behind your back, calling a punk and a dork)

so i why am i jelous
no one has taken my place with you
at least not yet

ill never understand any feeling ive ever had for you
