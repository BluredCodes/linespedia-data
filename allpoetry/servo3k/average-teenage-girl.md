so this is the life of a teenage girl
you go to school and try your hardest (and of course its not good enough)
you come home to find your mother and brother drunk off their asses
but its okay, you expected it

then your brother starts to cut in you
he disects every aspect of you and decides you are shit
you dress like a slut
you make bad grades
you sneak around and lie
you hides things
you are disrescpectful 

and you think to yourself.....

hmmm thats not what i thought of myself


dropped out of school
couldnt keep a job
mooched off both his family
his mother
his father
stole from all of his family

and he wants to bitch about what you wore to school today?!!

who the hell does he think he is?!!

no one i care about

he is no one to me

and my mother just sits back and sips on her cheap blush wine
and when our voices get to loud she will tell us to be quiet
she never cares about the abuse

oh and he is such an angel

he drinks water instead of soft drink!
he cleans his room once a month!
he cleaned the kitchen....once

oh and never forget all the things he has done for you
remember the time he stole your cds and pawned them?
remember the time he took your vcr and ruined it?
he cuts you down

oh and daddy is even better
he doesnt talk to you
but he loves your brother
(whom he is not in the least bit related to)
he has a vice
one impossible to get rid of
and has no desire to quit

i remember when i was little and i would go visit him
there would always be a lot of people around
and they all had the same vice


and of course there is high school its self
tons of rumors a float
people with two faces
and the ones who seem to feed on the humility and sadness of others
its a sad enviroment
especially for its purpose
no one pays any attention to what school is realy for
its a social parade
not the learning experience it was built for

and they say these are the best years of our life

if so , SHOOT ME NOW!
