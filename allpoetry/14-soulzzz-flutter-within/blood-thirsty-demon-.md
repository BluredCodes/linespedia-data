~ } ~ } YoU BlooD SucKinG DemoN { ~ { ~

What is this desecrated path you've lead me on??
~~> My decrepit destination is ever so hopelessly unknown.

Isn't there a purpose for such a slimey trail of BlooDieD GorE??
~~> This UnBarable inner pain BlinDingLY CoverSS such a HideouSS
RealiTY of TruTH for mE tO OwN.

Why would you ever isolate me, keeping My mind captive in this DarKeneD
DungeoN oF a ScatteReD DesolaTE ExistanCE??
~~> I SquaLLingLY ScreaM-OuT for those worthy, but way UnForeSeeN, EmotionSS
Of MysteriouSS GenuinE UnconditioNaL LOVE to TenderLY CareSS and SoothE
This frequent NightMare that does StrngLY EmbraCE My WorLD and its
EmpTiNeSS of a ReMorseFuLLy ScathED ThrowN.

Can you NOT need an EssencE of FaithFullY-frEE SereNE SpiriTedNeSS to
InFluenCE DreaMinG oF a SurVivinG StriFE??
~~> I am ConTinuaLLY diSSipaTinG iN your PLightSS ViciouSneSS as youR
KepT BlinDinG RagE SLowLY kiLLS oFF my VitaLitY'S JourneY thrU FeeLinG mY
UniQuE SpirituaLaliTY DesiRinG itS GrowTH.

Now LisTeN HerE, tO mE, you DemoN !  WiLL you PleaSE AdHere TighTly onto My 
LoneSomE HanD as I InTentLY show you MoTherEarTH's PreciousLY CreaTivE GiftS Of a FateFuLL LifE-RepLeniSHinG LiveLiHooD???
~~> I shaLL GatheR-uP your TonSS oF PainFiLLeD BaGagE and Use such a
MesMeriZinG TaLLenT of LuLLinGly LurE YouR ScorNeD THoughtSS into a weLL
DeSerVeD SauNA of aN inSpiraTionaL BatHinG "SOUL".
