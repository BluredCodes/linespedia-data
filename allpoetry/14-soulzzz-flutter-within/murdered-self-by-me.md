MurdeReD ->SeLF ->bY ->mE.....

--->> How could this bE?

I've walked these NightMares, I Stand AlonE, I calmly
Feel Your battered tears pounding a scattered spree.

SaoKinG-uP BlooD DrencHeD EdgeS oF ThirstY LeecheSS 
UpoN a SilenceD HangMaN'S trEE.

A Crimson's tide pours ever so SteadFast 
cluttering a shirt's stained sleeve.

I sought out Your RageLessNess thru such Hollowed
Death as I finished to fill in silhouettes of a
Shadow's Utterly Murdered Me.

You've witnessed My inner child's slow drowning underneath
That puddled moment of a grim PitchBlackened Grandiosity.

There wasn't even a 2nd glimpse after I linched My head
Up as High as this MoonLit Night reaches over Your Sun
Beamed Glee.

Forever more I do stand to hastely become an ornament of
That barren skeliton's tree.

I didn't need Your rope to throw My Life far away from a
World's beseeching Plea.

It was the sole Reality of a Deadened Want to feel any
GreyMatter throughout My EndLess HeartFelt BleeD.

You trampled down My every wish thru Your Tainted 
Abusive Tastes of hopelessly stabbing into an
Innocence of No One else but Me.

And now I've learned the Clear Perfections of Murdering
This inner Child's Freedomsss within a single tear shed
As being Raped twice over by Your hatreds into My each
And every Blackened Abyss of a continuous 
NightMare Drenched Dream.
