title: "Life's Guarantee"
writer: "paint me a wish"

Will I ever find Life’s guarantee?
Am I scared? 
Scared of what the answer might be? 
Is it, that I need someone to be Happy? 
What am I to do?
But if so who?

An angel from a cloud,
A lonely lady in a crowd
If it happened I would shout out loud!!

But this will never happen to me
These sort of things happen to people you can’t see
Good things only happen to them
I mustn’t be one of the lucky people 
They go out and buy another Gem
Love always seems to find them.

Is there someone for me on the other channel?
Someone to take care of me.
Do I get a vote from loves panel?
Or will they just disagree?

That I don’t deserve someone to love
I have to watch, and just observe 

There must be someone out there for me 
Maybe on a branch of Love’s Cinnamon tree,
A love that is sweet and tastes just right
I want to be hooked after just one bite

But this wont ever happen to me
Stick around and you will see
This sort of thing doesn’t happen to me
I wont ever find Life’s guarantee.
