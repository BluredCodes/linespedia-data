---
title: "Haven of colliding planets"
writer: "royalorange"
slug: "haven-of-colliding-planets"

perfume smoke of burnt orange peels              
simmer over coals sealed of gold                
	incandescent sky
the moon never asking why
	hatching the evening star like an egg
sunset in a crystal ball
	imperial blue
in the kingdom true
	under navy eyes
keeper of wise
	stellar reflections
gaze endless photos
	that mark the start
never the end
	of our evening tonight
	this love demarks
the intensity of all creation
	both our skies
become the ground that holds us 
	like galaxies
and new suns
	of stars far and long
colliding to finally have the touch
	dividing into our lives
combine our souls 
	like night
and mystery of consuming paradise
	but its your star
	i follow
and yours that leads the way
	every night to get home
inside under between and near
	to hope for something
forgiving
	luminous
something to shelter my failing 
	in this cold lonely life
