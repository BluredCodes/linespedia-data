Antique gloss set astounding of rock bed 
inside the hidden bowl from swerve of rivers
to bend of bay skin flows like white water foam 
with every crash of the churning thrust

It will take with it the only touch 
that reaches over rocks 
of purple flesh and bone 

Roots divide the incredible oak woods 
extraordinary flower blooms birth
lilac petals of perfect violet
one by one petal by petal 

Animals curse the beauty unbelievable
each moment that ever was had come for now
this tide flows of lavender blossoms

When the rain cries for fragrance to blend
and the rapids howl for lost hearts to mend
swirl in eddies of passion violence 

Carried like gifts from this coast to every end
land over violet waterfalls 
falling to drift and pollinate these shores
after burning through the hottest suns  

Remind us that everything must grow and die 
fears kiss my mortal bones to ensure that
the seeds that float to the soil of love pollinates
our flower to tallest trees made of forever
