ode to a lonely label-less can 

o lonely label-less can 
what art thou inside? 
be thy beef stew? 
be thy cream of celery? 
be thy green beans? 
(be thy anything?) 
i shan't not know until i open thine head and gut thee 
but i  wouldst  not do that to thee,  can, 
for thou be'est my only friend, 
the only one i talk to 
for thou art the only one that will listen 
and to gut you would  simply  be rude 
nay,  my lonely label-less can, 
i could not hurt you 
for,  i love you 
i think'est that we should be married 
perhaps the 15th of july 
but since thou hath an expiration date, 
we shall have to make everyday count 
o, what shall our children look like can? 
shall  they have your gentle smile? 
shall  they have your noble brow? 
or shall they have my goofy smile and large pachidermian ears? 
(can i give myself such airs?) 


  "...how many bottles and cans piled up in our garbage pales?..."-

THE WELL-KNOWN giggling nihilist, MY AUNT, AND I WROTE THIS 

POEM..WELL, MOSTLY ME. I DICKTATE AND SHE "CLICKETY-CLACKS" 

(TYPES). SHE STOLE THIS POEM LIKE THE  TREACHEROUS  HAIRY-LEGGED 

 DYKE  THAT SHE IS!! --THE WHORE-ER!! --THAT CUNT!!

--THAT  BUSHY  ARMPITTED,  CRUSTY TOE-NAILED  LECHEROUS  SKANK!!

--I SHALL HAVE MY REVENGE!!
