title: "Passionate Kisses!"

Eyes closed, great anticipation, 
lips trembling, feeling his breath, 
then his lips, on my brow, 
my cheeks, my chin...

His tongue meets mine.
Soft lips 
gently tug on my lips... 
sucking, releasing, 
again and again… 

Tongues engaged in 
a tug of love... 
probing, darting, 
deeper now, 
more daring, 
wet, 
sliding in and out, 
finding their own rhythm, 
the rhythm of love. 

As our lips meld together, 
our hearts beat 
faster and faster… 
pulsation begins
in my loins,
aware of
the frenzy
above. 

Passionate kisses, 
perfect kisses, 
set my body on fire 
and fill me with desire… 
desire that will only end 
when our bodies become one 
again.
