When someone you love is hurting,

How can you not be hurting, too?



When someone you love is crying,

How can you not be crying, too?



When someone you love is fearful,

How can you not be fearful, too?



When someone you love is dying,

How can you not be dying, too?The way to love anything

is to realize it might be lost."


G. K. Chesterton
