It's so easy to be forgotten

In this busy world of ours…



So many thoughts

Battle for dominance 

In overworked, overtaxed brains



Small wonder that so many people

Fall by the wayside…



Never thought of,

Nor remembered,

Nor missed!



What becomes of these

Pitiful, wretched souls?



It is as if they don't exist…

Living their invisible lives

Day after lonely day.



Who will save them

From obscurity?



Will it be you?

Will it be me?



Wait and see…



The only notoriety

They may ever receive…



Their names listed

In an obituary.
