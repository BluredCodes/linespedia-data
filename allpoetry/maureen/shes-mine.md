title: "She's Mine!"

She's a charmer!
Beautiful face and eyes,
Soft fur, cute bushy tail,
Just the right size.

A magnificent creature!
When she prances around, 
Her silky gown
Almost touches the ground.

She has so much fun
When she goes on a tear,
I laugh as I watch her
Leap in the air.

Her contented purr
Tells me all is well.
She makes my heart swell,
I love her, can you tell?

She's one of a kind,
A very fine feline,
Just what I had in mind,
And she's mine!


Adopted her recently at an animal shelter.  
I had almost given up finding the perfect
cat for me when I spied her.
It was love at first sight!
