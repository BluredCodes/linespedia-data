Im am the night.The one in the dark corners waiting for you, my love.To devourer you, to kiss your sweet, soft, succulent neck. To rip your flesh and suck your warm, sweet blood. Make your blood mine. To warm my flesh and make me whole again. I hold you in my arms and lower you softly on the bed. As i leave you my love, I shall only remember you only by your soft neck. And you, my holes. Now I shall go and sleep in my death bed.
                                      ~orignal date~March 20,2002~
