There once was a lil dude that lived in a shoe.He had so many socks he didn't know what to do.Sometimes they where bad, sometimes they where good.When they were bad he would have to make them get washed.When they where good he let them stay hidden so big people can't find them.Then he also had a sock that keeped on losing her stink,everyday.Her name was Lil Bo Stink.When all the lil socks graduated and moved away, lil dude had party everyday intill 5 in the morning.He had partys intill one party he died from a crack overdose.
                         The End!
