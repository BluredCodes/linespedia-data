I breath to move you closer 
in a paradox of sleeplessness, 
I find the strange appealing. 
On a runway paved with champagne, 
the ocean meets the sand 
and we applause the settling of a day. 
We fear not love, 
We fear not eachother, 
We fear not ourselves. 
In this climb towards grace 
We are benevolant. 
And time, 
Time has no heart here, 
and no strength. 
We do not let fear seperate us from happiness. 
Because we are who we are, 
No more, 
No less.This one is dedicated to Chris (strife), don't lose hope...-Zion
