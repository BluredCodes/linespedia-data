title: "It doesn't Matter"

Has saddness claimed victory over us again?
Have you turned we into I?
Have we died?

Did you know that you are my Hero?

This bottle of Whiskey is stealing the hours,
Lending me time away from pain,
Giving me a reason not to call.

Do you play back the days?
Are the nights reserved in salty pillars?

The vampires are out tonight,
Watching the lonely,
And hunting the heartless.

I got you figured out,
You can't lie much longer,
Cause you're suffocating.

I can't save you this time,
I can't even save myself.

I guess I'll see you in my dreams.
Where I always have.
Where you are always smiling,
And I am always sober.
