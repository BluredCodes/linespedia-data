title: "Useless time"
writer: "Beyond Zion"

We've laid to waste,
and had a prayer.
I hung on your words
Lived on your taste
And starved for a moment.

He's not so angelic.

This color has worn,
this love has passed.
Now,
Our souls are lost.
We are lost.

I'm not so angelic.

We stopped looking for absolution,
in tragic afternoons.
We came to no better conclusion,
just useless time.
