---
title: "Me and Corey"
writer: "Beyond Zion"

You're making my head hurt,
I can't believe you,
You actually made me cry.
Fuck, 
Oh now you want me to forget it,
how can I?

What do you mean it's in the past!?
Oh so if I went off and screwed some one else,
you'd forget it?
That's a load of crap!

No I am not being immature
I am being realistic sweetie.

You lied to me!
No, I will not forgive you!

Yes I love you.
But you don't love me.
No, it is true.
You don't lie to people you love.

Yeah it was a mistake,
but I can't forgive you.
Why!?
Because every time I kiss you I will taste him.
Every time we make love,
He'll be there.

I can't hate you.
No!
I will not say it!
But I will say this:
Goodbye.

Yes, this is the end.
We can't walk together on this road anymore.
No, love isn't always about forgiveness.
There are some things
that have no repentance.

I will always love you,
but from a distance.
Goodbye.
