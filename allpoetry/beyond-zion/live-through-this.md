Replacement,
That one great placebo
for a perfect thought.
Like when you want to speak
but there are no words.

In life we are the challenge.

Like manaquins,
we stand by idle
with potenial energy.

Yet, we find reasons for failure.

Under-developed by choice,
we examine our loss
of intention,
And follow each other over a cliff.

The sword has fallen, and the pen remains true.

Steady to the brink of sadness,
we adjust our televisions
to promote our happiness in sorrow.

Capture this moment, for it will fail.
