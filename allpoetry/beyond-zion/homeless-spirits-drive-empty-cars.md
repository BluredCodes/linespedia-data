title: "Homeless Spirits Drive Empty Cars"
writer: "Beyond Zion"

If you could only see what I see,
I seem to hide behind smiles.
I foreshadow the truth with devotion,
But only to lonliness am I faithful.
If the air could drown me,
I would hold my breath.
I feel so alone
When we lie together.
I am human,
So where is love?
Where is devotion?
Where is truth?
In between you and me.
