when you pulled away
and promised eternity
I didn't believe you
if only I had known

I didn't want your promises
fake glances and smiles
all i ever wanted
was your happiness

when you gave it all up
your unkowing sacrifice
i knew then you didn't understand
i didn't need your goodness

i only wanted your happiness
