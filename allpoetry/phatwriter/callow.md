callow wither shadow me
stalking poems truffled trees
evil kittens lovers lost
moose and bug
hearts and hugs
starving poems of sadness
wasted words of withered callow
heed this warning all
espcially me
i know my fualts and feathered ways
haunting souls and weathered days
theres more to life than being grim
death and love, love and sin
hearts and hats, forkes and knifes
callowing these brainwashed lifes
look around us here and there
all we have stop and stare
peek and glare, seek and flare
blessed here i know my way
heir the cool break of day
so rejioce my freinds
my truffled trees with moose about
fleas on there backs
kitty cats and lovers lust
brandeshing a brainwashed trust
stop and think 
about how great life is........
