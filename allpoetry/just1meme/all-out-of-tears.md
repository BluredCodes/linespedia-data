I am all out of tears
I am finished with the crying
While inside
My soul is still dying.

I am all out of tears
I miss you so much
I ache to have you
Just one small touch.

I am all out of tears
I am remembering it all
The good and the bad
And our emotional fall.

I am all out of tears
I have wiped them all away
Pushing forward 
Trying to have a better day.
