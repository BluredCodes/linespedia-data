The air so lonely and cold where I lay
not a drop of light, and never again to see day.
The tears upon my eyes began to fall
everything stood still along with the call

I heard my name spoken
and then it started again
my soul took place in to a leap of death and faith of sin.

To think everyone I once knew, did not notice
I was there, nor gone in to the night
of the fine misting airs.

My thoughts came together
I was only gone for a week,
no one choose to message me, or even
ask me to speak.

I was alive
but so far away,
even the ones I know on allpoetry.com were on delay.
they never sent me an e-mail..
or wrote me a letter..

slowly its starting to fade in..
slowly its all coming together...

I was forgotten,
and never to be found.
Placed in a room of darkness with no one to come around.

I hoped to be missed, 
I was hopping to be sought for..

longing for the friends.. on allpoetry.com that needed me no more.I was gone for about two weeks, and I realized that no one has checkedo n me or anything... and I thought i would write a poem about it, and express the way I feel.. Please understand that... I am not holding anything against anyone.. But felt like.. opening up.. on my thoughts....
