Inside I'll have you hide,
Ill show you the dark side.

In your lips ill taste your blood
they will find your body weeks later lying
in the dark cluttered mud,
face down... your throat slit.

all things you've learned you'll forget.
You will be tempted in the magic of love
gifted and enchanted with hells, and heaven above.
before you let go of my hands, and climb down to the
forever land... Look me in the eyes,
I will take great pride.

Not long after your body will swell
you'll learn the tragic bliss of love and hate.

I will be your fear and fate, as each tear falls.
I will cure your pain when you call.
Ill stand beside you, 
I'll close the gate to the things that haunt you.

you're terrified, do not fear me.
I am the one that could see,
the pain and misery you've carried so long.

Think of it as being free...
free from pain, and insanity.

I promise to preserve your body
inside a special place..

I'll wrap you in the whitest sheets and dry your skin
And you will forever be mine.
I'll kiss your lips when I want.
I'll touch your face when I dare
You will never leave, and you will always be here.

your last dying look will be my beautiful face.
I am not trying to scare you,
Do not fear the weary blade.
She comes in, and lets you cave....

I'll whisper sweet words to you,
I'll make love all night to you...
forever mine you'll be.
close your eyes and you will see.
