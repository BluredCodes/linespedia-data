title: "Remember Me This Way"

Remember the way we touched palm to palm
and all feelings melted in to songs of joy.

Remember my eyes that glare from the skies.
think not of the good byes.

Think of the way we were,
All the beautiful moments we shared...
Not heaven or hell could take that away..

Once we pass, we lose our destiny
but not our memory..
I'll never forget your lips, or your finger tips..
or how you would make me laugh..
and the only thing I ask, is that you dwell on the good times rather then the bad.

We will meet again,
And I'll offer my hand..
And once again we will find the lost moments
and regain our past.

My last words for now till we meet face to face,
Is I love you,
and I care deeply.. nothing can shatter the heart
when its beating.......
