Through these eyes you do not see the pain,
They are hidden behind the pouring rain.

Inside the coffin is where I'm safe.
Inside the soul that's torn and aches.

In my dreams and in passionate meanings,
I feel my self falling.

I'm an allusion of the world,
and we are all stalling.

The mirrors trapped inside our hearts,
never to spoil or over lap the broken parts.
in the gazing dreams, pieces of me.
where screaming and loud voices come to me.

As I stand upon the very ledge in which
I fell, Below me is heaven and hell.
There is a god, and there is a devil..
allusion or true?

there are ghosts that walk upon the grounds,
and creeps upon your frowns,
Do you see them? Illusion or true?

does vampires, and demons exist to?
Does your skin cover your organs, Illusion?
a foundation......  of a false creation..
things the pupils, do not see....
reasons, and excuses.. cover the light
and keep us in darkness, darker then night.....

We are Illusions of this world...
far away, before we fade..
and die, and live in panic of our ways.
