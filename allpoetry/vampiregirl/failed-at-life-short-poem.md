As I sit upon this chair of lead 
and with my empty heart and my mind in dread. 
I count the lonely midnight stars. 
I see planets like Jupiter and mars, so separated yet so close, 
Only if I knew how that felt. 

To wish, and have this wish come true. 
For once in my life, feel something real and new. 
And Never again will I mistake heaven with you. 

When my body and heart will lye at rest 
I have failed at this grieving test, 
for fate, for love, and for destiny does not exist.. 
And for this I have failed at life..
