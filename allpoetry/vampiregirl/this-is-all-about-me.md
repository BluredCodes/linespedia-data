Inside the face that hides behind my eyes
A tragic memory of wasted time.
In my heart I feel it changing..
But my mind is just a creation..
a creation of grace..
a feeling of my past and future being erased.

Inside the harp plays..
words of many people that seem to fade.
Darkness turns to night,
I'm filled with things I can't describe.

To believe, would be simple
to not believe would be hard
I feel my self falling apart.

The tide began to rise,
The sun settled for the last time...

All things left with out even saying farewell.
I was stuck in a lonely house, a cottage outside of hell.
The rapids were slowly pressing against the rocks..
the stars lost their glow, and no one seemed to notice but me.

If only I were not blessed with eyes to see
such things.
If only I were not blessed with a heart of feeling,
Then I would leave this trigger behind.

if only I were not blessed..
with all the things that will one day lye at rest, I would feel alive.
If only this world was never made.. and all things created began to fade..
I would be okay.

In the misty fog, I search far away.
Learning to live, only day by day.
To forget his face that passes me,
the hunger of my destiny.

Such cruelty...
such tragedy..
This is all me.
