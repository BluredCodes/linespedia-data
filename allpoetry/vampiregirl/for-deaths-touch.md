title: "For Deaths Touch"

The words became clear
as the seas began to stand still
all things I've learned of life and love
had became real.

The shores and rocks were tumbling from the sky
wishes of death, and angels glanced in
to these eyes.

I felt the touch upon my pale skin
the earth rattled while the snakes were let in,
they slowly wrapped around my neck,
and caused a passion for fear.

Those that I knew were no longer here.

Wishes and demons all stood in the pitch black night
angels stood beside waiting for the sun to rise.

Inside my heart the falling of my pride
the gentle touch upon my wounds.
and falling tears I could not bare to hide.

the stench and torment I've been through
caused sunny weather to turn untrue,
down the rain blasted as the wind drifted through
out came the bad, and in came the new.

I felt saved
loved, and in harmony unlike before
I felt deaths touch and was cursed
with wanting and needing him more.

The passion grew deep
and the sensation unclear
like an earthquake falling with many of my tears
along side the river the unblessed tarnished cares.
