“Brand new addiction”
She was tragically hip, with a over sized chip on her shoulder
She was only 18, but she looked so much older
Wasted her mind, trying to find unattainable happiness
Got into a few rides, she lived through them all
Oh but sooner or later, sooner or later she has to fall
(Chorus)
She’s got a, Brand new addiction
Just wants to go down in history
Rather than autobiography fiction
Looking for a perfect grain of sand
Getting out of hand, Brand new addiction

She said life was full of ups and downs
Yellow smiley faces, and unhappy clowns
She made bigger than it was, Cause she can’t remember
Through all the fun, all the fog and all the fuzz
She’s mellow dramatic in a stereo and static type of way,
But sooner or later, sooner or later she has to pay
(Chorus)
She’s got a, Brand new addiction
Just wants to go down in history
Rather than autobiography fiction
Looking for a perfect grain of sand
Getting out of hand, Brand new addiction

She’s got to find a fix, for the glitch in her matrix
It doesn’t matter where it’s coming from
As long as you’re getting some from every pot
The best in the bunch goes sour in the lot
Sooner or later, one of you is going to hate her
And have to find another, Brand new addictionThis is kind of based on several life experiences... But the "her" in the story isn't me. It's actually several people, that just kind of- fit together. It's poppy, I know. But what can I say? Sometimes I'm just like that. It's really better with the music.
