“Staying here”
Turn on the TV, it’s Jerry Springer
She was a dead pan ringer for Sally Jesse
Fought with her mother’s sister’s cousin
It sure was messy, that’s life in Hollywood
That’s life in your neighborhood
(Chorus)
Girl next door makes you beg for more
What are you staying here for?
Hollywood calls it, a perfect screenplay
Mumble the words, don’t care about them anyway
What are we staying here for?

Sitting on your couch, watching WWF double team
Being in the big ring, it’s your pro time dream
Barbie can’t do it with Ken, even in Dollywood
There’s problems with inept men
That’s life in your neighborhood 
(Chorus)
Girl next door makes you beg for more
What are you staying here for?
Hollywood calls it, a perfect screenplay
Mumble the words, don’t care about them anyway
What are we staying here for?

You’re staying here for the action
Life being the freakish main attraction
What are you pretending to be?
Blind men don’t care what you look like
It’s what they feel they can see
What are we staying here for?
