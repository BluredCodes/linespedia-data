Shake me, Break me
I am your China Doll, with that southern drawl
Beat me, Hate me
Batter me until I crawl upon my knees
And I ask you "please stop, please listen"
You do not, it's all part of your plot
To mold me into something you can hold
Something you can call your own
When I'm no one to be had
Shake me, Break me
Until tears fall upon my stained face
Hurt me, Mistreat me
Til I fade into something to replace your void
I am your China Doll, With the southern drawl
I am your toy you toyed with
Until I broke, and now I choke
Upon your words of bitterness
Shake me, Break me
When I melt from emotions felt
You will miss me, you will cry
But until that happens, you just whisper
"I hope you die"I wrote this in a few minutes. It's another one of my ramblings that isn't thought out. ...Raw emotion, all I can say.
