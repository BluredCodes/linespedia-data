I gave you, all I could give
I would have given my life, if it meant you could live
Live in happiness you so deserve
So close to our goal, so near the end
So far from the start, almost touched the heaven send
And now you write me a letter, that I don't understand
That I really don't want to
I'm at the cross road, I love you
I'm at the cross road, I don't know what to do

I know where you're coming from
I know what you're feeling
Even though it leaves me reeling
It's the past that makes you wonder
It's the future, that I'm afraid of
We both understood the risks
We never thought they would threaten us
But they did, but they have, but they do
We're at the cross road, the road of safety
We're at the cross road, the road of unknown

My tears fall, even though I try to stop them
I saw our lives together, I saw children
I saw love, I saw two people that could withstand
The test of time, now the line is drawn
Are you still mine? Are you still my only?
I'm at the cross road, Don't let me go I don't want to be lonely
I'm at the cross road, It wouldn't hurt so much if I didn't miss your touch

I would be there tomorrow, if it would
Stop this pain, this tension, this sorrow
I would beg, I would steal, I would borrow
Just to hold you near, just to stop the wondering fear
%98 sure of me, %2 sure of her
Why am I so afraid, we can never again be?
Here is the cross road, will you journey down it with your hand in mine?
Here is the cross road, Will this be the end? Have we drawn a line?To the only one I'll ever love.. Where do we stand?
