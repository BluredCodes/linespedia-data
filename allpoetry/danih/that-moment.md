That moment,
Those few seconds,
Standing there,
Palms sweating,
Chest heaving,
Heart pounding.

It was time,
I had to get on stage.
I was thirsty.
I was scared.
For the first time in my life, I was scared.

I couldn't remember the words.
I had forgotten them.
They were lost.
I was lost.
Lost in my thoughts.
I was losing.

Losing hope,
Losing courage,
All I gained in that moment was more anxiety.
I'm going to trip.
I'm going to fall.
They words are lost.
I am lost.I wrote this as an expanded moment in english class, and it just came out as poetry.
