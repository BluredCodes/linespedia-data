They say hindsight is 20/20. 
I wish foresight were 20/10. 
Because all I have is hindsight-bias, 
And it's keeping me in a downward trend. 

If I could change the mistakes I've made, I would. 
If I could avoid all mistakes I'm going to make I would as well. 
If I were able to accomplish both I would be charging money for my gift of foresight. 
But I can't. 
I'm not psychic. 

I know I could have avoided mistakes if I had only looked closer. 
But I didn't. 

My hindsight is 20/20, 
And all I have left is hindsight-bias. 
As well as the ability to learn from the mistakes. 

The mistakes I have made don't really matter. 
I've learned from them in the end. 
All I have is hindsight-bias. 
If only foresight were 20/10.
