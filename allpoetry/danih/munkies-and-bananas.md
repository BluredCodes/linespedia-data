No one would imagine that you and I could be friends.
All I can fathom is that our friendship doesn't follow trends.

It's hard to see all of the differences between us with as close as we've become.
When I think of us I think of us as a lump sum.

You the bannana, I the munky.
You the lock, I the key.
You the mango, and I the tango.
I'll quit this, it's corny.

I'm corny,
You're blunt.
You're smart,
I'm smarter. (J/K)
But all that really matter is that you're the better farter.

They can love or hate us we will never, ever change.
You the puppy and I your tangled mange.All right, this isn't the greatest poem, but I wrote it to make a friend laugh.  It worked!  There are a lot of inside joke that only her and I would get.  And yes, I realize munky is spelled monkey.
