I have it
I want it
I feine it
I crave it
I lost it
I got it
I need it
I found it
I love it
I like it
I posess it

IT?

What is it?

Is it talent?
Life?
Love?
Ambition?
Friendship?
Fun?
God?

IT

A pronoun.
A two letter word.
Quite possibly the deepest word in the english language.Do you now understand why you are not supposed to use prepositions at the end of sentences?
