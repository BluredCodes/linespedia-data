As I walk through my life
and stumble through each day
I wonder who am I?
where shall I be one day?
Shall i live a good life?
shall i be a good man?
will i live by the ocean?
and be buried in the sand?
will i die quickly?
or die making a stand?
shall my family love me?
or will i have none to command?
shall i fight with some rebels?
or will i just follow the band?

So answer these questions, you cellular phone calling, computer chatting, faxing, satellite using, jimmy hat wearing, comet watching, love making, sex having, chair using, disease fearing, Godless wonders of Generation X...

You, you, you cable watching, stale twinkie eating, Mikey likes it, email using, air conditioned, saved by the Bell watching, microwave owning, recycling, internet meeting, energy savers of the scientific world, wonders of Generation X....

Answer these and convince me... you holders of knowledge.... answer these...just a lost thought with no where to go....
