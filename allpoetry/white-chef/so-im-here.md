So Im here.
Glancing through the past.
Looking to the future.
What life could hold at last.
I am here,
are you still there
I wonder will life takes me
As I continue to take the stares.

I wished to be 
more than you could see
A nobody
who would listen to only me.
I want to move on
and take my bow
And the only question I continue to ask is
How?
When? 

Why cant it be? 
Who moves forward,
for all to follow
into the horizon,
with the light as my guide.
But Im still here. 
Looking for the answer to the question
which I still havent asked. 
Where are you?
