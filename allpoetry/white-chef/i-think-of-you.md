---
title: "I think of you"
writer: "white chef"

And this metamorphsis in front of my eyes
Which brings a flame to the iced concrete of the harsh world
All that brick by brick has built up
into an inpenetrable fortress with a door left open
for you to wander inside and destroy my fears.
The weight of my mind has tipped the scales
as all I fear flows drip by drip
drip by drip it disperses.
as you filter the feinds which haunt me
leaving me feeling proud.

Yet still nobody knows
You.
I search but I never find
the beauty you posess
as on the inside your radiance shines through.
I try to try
only to fail to try
and open my soul
let it flow
and meet with the one
who will spare my sanity.
