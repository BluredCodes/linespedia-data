It seems no coincidence but certainly a plan
To underteach even those who reach beyond the common man

It is time for revolution, not platitudinous change
A Kafkaesque situation in person, very strange

If a white man is born in Africa and to America he came
Would he not be African American to the liberal's shame

Do not seem me haughty, don't defend personal destruction
Stop the liars from spreading fires with race and evil unction

So many persons fear the truth, thinking they shall be judged
Polluting the ground that makes the road that honest men have trudged

With everything behind me, am I history?
Or is it that life gives us constant mystery
