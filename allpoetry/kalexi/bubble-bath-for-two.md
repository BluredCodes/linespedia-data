~~Bubble bath for two~~

Warm, fragrant bubbly water
Flowing over our skin
Washing away the days cares
Your arms surround me,
Carressing my shoulders,
While kissing my neck and
Sending tingles up and down my spine....

As we maneuver our bodies to
Aid our growing desire, feeling the
Sensation our love has inspired
In our hearts which cannot
Be denied.....


After the water has almost lost its warmth,
We start to climb out of the tub, 
Taking turns rubbing the soft, warm towels 
Over each other.........


When we reach the haven of our bedroom,
I start to massage lotion into your
Smooth skin, ever so slowly, taking my  time
While you are chanting for me  not to stop..............

I realize that your almost asleep, we agree to
continue this tomorrow night,
Falling into a peaceful sleep, 
Dreaming of our next bubble bath
