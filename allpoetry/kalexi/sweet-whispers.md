~~Sweet Whispers~~

Softly, gently, say my name,
Gaze at me with unspoken promises
Take me in your arms, caressing my skin
All over, as I tenderly kiss your neck
While you whisper sweetly in my ear.

Our passion continues to grow as
We slowly ride on the waves of ecstasy
Created by our everlasting love.

Lying in your arms after the waves
Have subsided, leaving us both breathless
Our bodies still entertwined
Giving sweet, slow kisses to each other
As you whisper sweetly in my ear.
