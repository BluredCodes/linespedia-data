Struggles

Sometimes life can be very hard
Not allowing us to let down our guard
Some, live with struggles everyday,
Hopeing they will just go away.

Dealing with struggles isn't easy to do,
Trying your best not to feel blue
Strive to take life one day at a time,
Always live life to the fullest
To the best of your ability.

Struggles are something I live with daily
Staying positive seems to help the situation,
However staying positive can be a very difficult
position to possess throughout your life.
