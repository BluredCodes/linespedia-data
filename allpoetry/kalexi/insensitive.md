Insensitive......


Some do not realize how much their words can hurt
Speaking before thinking
Usually has heart-breaking affects.

Only thinking of yourself
Is not a nice habit to have 
Maybe you think your a better person,
But, what it really proves
Is what a small-minded person you are.

Being an insensitive and selfish person
Has negative effects on people around you
Giving them a pessimistic view of their life.
