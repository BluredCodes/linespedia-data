I Miss............


Riding a bike, feeling the wind blow my hair
Diving into the water on a dare
Driving my car, wherever I wanna go
Holding my son, as a baby, and watching him grow
Pushing a buggy in a crowded store
Taking my dog for a walk
Going for a walk along the beach,
Feeling the sand between my toes
Dancing in your arms, to our favorite song
Most of all, my independance........
