Butterflies

They have beautiful and colorful wings  
Always making your heart sing
Flying high up in the sky
Giving beauty without a try
One of nature's most delicate creatures
With so many wondrous features
They are a symbol of freedom and innocence
Seeming the world could actually make sense
God must have put them here for a reason
I think Spring is his favorite season


2-26-01
