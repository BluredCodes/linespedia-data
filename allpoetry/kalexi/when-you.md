When You.....

Say certain things to me
You hurt me,more than you know
One simple gesture can make me forget
What you said to hurt me, my heart easily forgets

Look at me in a way only you can
I can see disapproval and pain in your eyes
When I realize the mistake I made,which caused pain
I apologize and try to make us both forget,and start anew

Tell me how much you love me
I feel like a queen, on top of the world
Whisper promises of what our future will bring
Tears of happiness form in my trusting eyes
 
See more in me than anyone ever has
I know I've found my one and only,my heart
Has been searching for as long as I can remember
Only you have the gift that gives my heart and soul a smile
