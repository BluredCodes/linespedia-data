Rainy Days

Dark, dreary, cloudy and grey
Cold, messy, no fun to play in
Depressing me, trapping me indoors.
Longing to see the sunlight
Change my mood, allowing me
To wander outside feeling the
Sun kiss my face and warm
My entire body, as I smile
Forgetting all about the rainy day.

3-30-02
