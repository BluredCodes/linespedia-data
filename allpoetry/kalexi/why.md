Why

Why does everything confuse us so much
Everyone seems to have lost their touch
The world keeps spinning around
But,you always make me feel safe and sound.

Why do people act the way they do
Pretending to like you, but it isn't true
Some never learn right from wrong
Standing in line, waiting to learn, all their life long.

One day we will all understand why
When we are flying high up in the sky
Holding our fathers hand
Granting us the knowledge to understand why.
