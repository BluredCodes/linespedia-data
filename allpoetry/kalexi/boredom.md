Boredom

Boredom must be my best friend
Always hanging around me,
Just like uninvited company
Showing up out of the blue....

Now that it has found me
It refuses to go away
Wanting it to leave me
Picking on someone else....

I cannot help the fact that
I seem to get bored easily
That is the way God made me
A trait that I've had to accept....

That is why I have decided
Writing, reading and learning
More, is turning out to be
My saving grace....
