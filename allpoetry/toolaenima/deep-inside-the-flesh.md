Things feel so cold
This Knife brings the force 
Feel this dagger go in my flesh 
Taring me deep inside 
Feeling lost without the pain 
Feeling crossed by all thats holy 
Wanting to slice myself 
Without any warning 
No Feelings left inside 
The need to die festers within the night 
Putting this knife inside my heart 
Left with pain feeling obsessed 
with this blade and handle 
bringing me so many sensations 
Have u seen a man 
without a reason 
Look into my eyes and u'll know the feeling 
Dreaming of being punkcured 
Feeling hopeless and empty 
THe only way to die 
is threw the knife 
being sliced to death 
oh so nice 
leaving this behind  
dying painfully ..
