With many schooling fish swimming to and fro 
Over a shimmering oasis of parrot's feather 
As dancing air bubbles burst up from below. 

A Japanese fighting fish swims past real slow 
Over swaying water hornwort and heather 
With many schooling fish swimming to and fro 

A fragile Angelfish glides through the airflow 
Looking for her mate and swimming together 
As dancing air bubbles burst up from below. 

The colourful Neons with their stripes aglow 
Dance amidst the plants swaying altogether 
With many schooling fish swimming to and fro 

Yellow and blue stripped Danio's bestow. 
Gifts of bubble nests in mating weather. 
As dancing air bubbles burst up from below. 

Swimming just inches above coloured gravel 
Algae-eaters and armoured catfish do travel 
With many schooling fish swimming to and fro
