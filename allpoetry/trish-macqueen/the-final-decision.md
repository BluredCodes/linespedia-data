title: "The Final Decision...."
writer: "Trish MacQueen"

As the sun sets behind a snowy mountain top,
I stand on the cabin's front porch and watch.
Whether I'm ready, or whether I'm not…
The time has come and it's too late to stop.
My breathing changes with a quick little hop,
And I know in the cabin is my perfect match.
As the sun sets behind a snowy mountain top,
I stand on the cabin's front porch and watch.

As I watch, I recall special times from the past,
And how he has always begged me to play.
But tonight, I'm to make a decision, at last,
While I feel he's pushing…pushing too fast.
I look within me, but the decision is so vast.
I want to run, but my heart keeps saying stay.
As I watch, I recall special times from the past,
And how he has always begged me to play.

With my heart throbbing, I ache to my core,
Knowing, this decision is one I hate keeping.
He looks up and thumps a spot on the floor,
Beside where he lays, awaiting some more.
Tomorrow a needle will settle the score,
And my dog will be permanently sleeping.
With my heart throbbing, I ache to my core,
Knowing, this decision is one I hate keeping.
