title: "Broken Promises..."

Night after night, you come home drunk,
and smelling like a perfumed skunk.
You tell me that, it's all my fault,
and when I disagree, you commit assault.

With two black eyes and a busted lip,
a broken arm, and a fractured hip.
I tell my family that I had a fall,
and lie to my friends, at every call.

I've lived with you, for over ten years,
yet every night, I still hide my fears.
Now, you've become the man I despise,
and every day, I plan for your demise.

One night, you'll be sound asleep in bed,
and I will place a gun, to your fat head.
With memories of broken promises, since we wed,
I'll pull the trigger, and BANG....you're dead.Poet's Note:  I wrote this one night after watching a documentary on abuse on the tele.
