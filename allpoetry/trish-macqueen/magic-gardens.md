title: "Magic Gardens..."

Large Shasta Daisies growing new,
Amongst Canterbury Bells, so blue,
And Hollyhocks that bloom true,
With Foxglove and Fever Few,
Creating magic gardens for you.

Colourful Sweet Williams, I view,
Amongst Coreopsis covered in dew,
And a Sunflower or two,
With Yarrow and Giant Yew,
Creating magic gardens for you.

Imperial Lillie's facing front, too
Amongst Sweet Laurel, I knew,
And Petunias sticky like glue,
With Pansies given their lieu,
Creating magic gardens for you.
