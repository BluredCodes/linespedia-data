---
title: "Velvet Butterfly..."
writer: "Trish MacQueen"
slug: "velvet-butterfly"
source: "AllPoetry"

Delicate butterfly floating in the sky,
with soft, filmy wings coloured with dye.
Drifting silently upward into the night,
as twinkle stars guide your mystic flight.

Rainbows of clouds like soft white pillows,
enhance your trip with sailboat billows.
Over the moon and past the planet's rings,
travelling smoothing on what the wind brings.
