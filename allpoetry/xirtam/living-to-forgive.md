---
title: "Living to forgive"
writer: "Xirtam"

We want, and want, 
and just can't wait.
We try to say sorry,
but its too late.
We hope were forgiven 
for what we did wrong.
For what we're singing,
is the sad quiet song.
We're being tossed left and right,
light turns to dark, and dark turns to light.
We need to help. We need to try.
Or the cold damp dirt is where we'll lie.
You will not see it coming your way.
You won't even know its your dark day.
It will be nice, and it will be fair,
but turn your back, and it won't care.
You tossed it out but need it in, 
because all you're filled with is deep dark sin.
You need to say sorry, you must forgive.
If you dwell in hate, then forget to live.Part 2 to Repent.
