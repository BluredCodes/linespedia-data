---
title: "Love Fear"
writer: "Xirtam"
slug: "love-fear"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55868-Love-Fear-by-Xirtam"
---

I want you more than I need the day.
I need you more than words can say.
I heard your voice through the softest song.
I'm so confused, what's right? What's wrong?
I need you now and I need you here.
For life without you, is life's worst fear.
