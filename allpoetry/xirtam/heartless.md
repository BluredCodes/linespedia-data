I long for love
the kind to weep.
But hate is looking 
for the benefits to reap.
Taking heat from all love's fire.
Locking away light,
and all love's desire.
Never again to see 
love's fate.
And all you can do, 
is wait for the hate.
You try to shed light on
all the dark.
But hatred holds tighter and 
leaves it's mark.
Sitting there swimming through 
your own brain,
You hit the abscence,
and go insane.
You now know there is 
no turning back.
You're forced to stay there,
and stare at the black.Creepy and weird. I don't know how this poem came to me.
