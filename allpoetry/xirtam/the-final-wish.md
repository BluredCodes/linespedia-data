---
title: "The Final Wish"
writer: "Xirtam"
slug: "the-final-wish"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55864-The-Final-Wish-by-Xirtam"
---

Oh god's angels, your wings on high,
send my peace from the sky.
The blood I've shed and my many tears,
took away all pain from all my years.
They may not hear me, not a sound.
But let them know, I'll be around.
As a final wish sent from above,
let my loved ones know that they are loved.Dedicated to the families who have lost somebody special.
