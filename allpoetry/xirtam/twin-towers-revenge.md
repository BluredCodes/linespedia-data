title: "Twin Towers Revenge"

In the morning, they stood so tall.
They could hear the New York call.
People are working, and moving about,
but then the Devil started to shout.
The demons were flying with a happy frown,
and then the towers came right down. 
Then the earth cried for such a loss.
It was time to show who is the boss.
Our country fights hand in hand,
together we're strong, united we stand.This is for all of the U.S.A. We will be succesful. We will have justice.
