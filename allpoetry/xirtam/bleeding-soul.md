---
title: "Bleeding Soul"
writer: "Xirtam"
slug: "bleeding-soul"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62053-Bleeding-Soul-by-Xirtam"
---

Bleeding tears through one's own mind,
hate and frustration is all you'll find.
The lack of effort that you put in,
just takes your life and digs right in.
"Speak up" they say like they know whats right,
the options are there, but no answer in sight.
To find yourself you must learn to love.
All the answers will come from above.
Its always dark before its light.
Whatever happens, it will turn out right.A compass for the emotionally lost.
