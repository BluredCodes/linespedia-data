Laid down softly on the floor,
not one sound, and my lifes no more.
Is this the end, is that death I hear?
Haunting the living is what I fear.
The dark day hovers around your life.
Your life is hunted with the grim reaper's knife.
I walk in living, and not one clue.
Was I alone? I wish I knew.
The lights were out, and not one sound.
But wait! A hint of breath was found. 
I turn to find me face to face, 
with an unknown figure that sparked the chase.
I turn to run, but theres no where to go.
I'm left to try and fight the foe.
I gave up. My energy is a wreck.
And then I felt the grip on the neck.
I slowly lost all the air.
I lost life without one care.
Laid down softly on the floor,
not one sound, and my lifes no more.Another one of my tragic poems. I hope you're not scared, because she is behind you right now.
