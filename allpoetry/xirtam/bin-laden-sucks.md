---
title: "Bin Laden Sucks"
writer: "Xirtam"
slug: "bin-laden-sucks"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56751-Bin-Laden-Sucks-by-Xirtam"

I hate him,
you hate him.
We all hate him.
Lets kill him.
Go U.S.A. all the way.
I hope you find him.
Lets hang him today.

Bin Laden Sucks.
The EndI hate him. Need I say more?
