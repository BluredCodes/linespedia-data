---
title: "So as long as I live"
writer: "angel of darkness"
slug: "so-as-long-as-i-live"

There is a world outside of my window, 
And the sprits are calling for me.
My heart may be dead,
But my soul will live on,
In the light of the moon,
And forever, 
So as long as you love me. 
I’ll be here for you 
And protect you the best that I can. 
So never let your heart 
Or love die.
I want you to feel the wind in your hair,
And the scent of a rose,
Through out your life.
So hold the memories of the past,
In your hand and hold it tight,
So as long as you live you’ll remember me,
And how much our friendship meant,
To each other.
I’ll love you so as long as I shall live,
And even in death our friendship shall survive.I wrote this in the memory of a good friend of mine. May her soul be at rest now.
