title: "Lonely old house"

A lonely old house,
Sits on an lonely old hill
Peering down on a lonely old town.
No people dare to go up there,
No one can stand to think of anyone being up there
Everyone refuses to exist anywhere near it.
So the lonely old house,
Just sits on its lonely old hill,
Alone and deserted,
Till the end, 
Of it’s lonely old days
