Now I, 
Lay me,
Down to sleep,
I pray to you my soul to keep.
And if I die before I wake,
I pay to you my soul to take.

Hey,
I don’t know who’s hearing this,
But it really doesn’t matter you see,
I'll make a deal with you,
I’ll never ask you for something for myself,
And since I've given you my soul,
I was just wondering if,
If I happen live through the night,
Whether you may consider 
Giving the world some peace,
Just for one day,
Not forever.
I haven’t asked for anything that silly like
Happiness,
Popularity,
Beauty,
Love,
My loved ones to be back with me,
I’ve never asked for anything really.
All I have ever asked for was 
Protection on the few pieces of family I have left,
And world peace for one day.
I don’t really care what happens to me 
But just grant me those two things 
And I’ll be ever so grateful for it.
There are very few things I want,
But what I do want is for everyone to be happy,
Even if I’m not.
Their happiness is all,
I’ll ever need,
It’s all anyone should ever need.
Thank you!
