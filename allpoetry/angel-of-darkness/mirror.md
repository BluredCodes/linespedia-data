title: "Mirror"
writer: "angel of darkness"

How can you look in the mirror at night,
To see the horror that you provide,
You hurt me cause I'm not like you,
So you think that I must be weaker than you,
Cause I'm not evil inside.
You attack me when I'm too weak to fight,
I wish that someday you might understand how
horrible you really are.
I can't understand how you can be so spiteful all the
time.
I'm not afraid of you or your so-called friends,
I pity you for being so stupid to think that you can
bring me down,
I still don't understand how you can look yourself in
the mirror at night,
To see the horror in which you provide.
