---
title: "When will we listen"
writer: "angel of darkness"

Can you believe 
All the anger and hatred there is in the world? 
If you can 
Then why do you allow it to continue? 
"LET THERE BE PEACE" 
A young child once screamed, 
Only to be ignored. 
But why 
And for what? 
“We should listen to the children they are our future” 
Bellowed the child’s mother 
With a tear in her eyes 
And holding her baby in her arms. 
And yet still no one listens. 
Why? 
When will we learn 
To listen the wise, 
And ignore the evil and feeble. 
When will we learn to 
Stand against conformity 
And exist as individuals? 
When will we listen to that child 
And it’s mother? 
When will we listen to the cries of humanity? 
When will we exist outside of the egg shell’s 
We call a community? 
When will we exist? 
When will we live? 
When?
