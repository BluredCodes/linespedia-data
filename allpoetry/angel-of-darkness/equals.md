title: "Equals"

Each and everyone of us 
Has a mission,
Has reason,
To live theirs lives
In a way that will make them feel more human,
And every mission begins with hurt someone you really love.
No reason ends with a death,
But begins with a betrayal of trust.
The world is full of lies,
And people who want nothing 
But the chance to deceive another.
Their hearts may be dead,
But souls still live on in the atmosphere,
All around us.
You can hate them for what they do,
But we all must learn to love them for what they cannot do.
We are all equals in this world,
Equal hatred,
Equal pain,
And equal stupidity.
So why can’t we treat one another equally?
