title: "The reflection in the mirror"

I see a reflection 
In the mirror,
A reflection 
That I don’t even recognize,
A reflection of a girl so hurt and destroyed 
That she can no longer cry,
And refuses to feel.
The reflection girl is staring straight at me,
And says,
“Why are you fooling yourself?
You’re not like the others.
You have something inside that no one else does,
You have me.
So be real,
Stop lying to yourself,
Just be yourself,
Take back your life
And get rid of all this superficial shit.
It’s time to show the world the real you,
And stop lying.Just think about the person who I look at each morning in the mirror and what she is seeing.
