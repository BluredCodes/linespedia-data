---
title: "Those Moments"
writer: "angel of darkness"

When gentle touches,
And precious moments 
Cease to exist.
Or when the so-called 
Love of your life no longer wants you,
And you feel your compassion starting to disappear,
What will do to stay strong during such times?
Do you stand tall 
And show the enemy that nothing?
How can one live in a world that refuses to feel?
When all those little moments 
That makes life worth living
Are being forgotten 
And changed through and through.
When will those little moments last forever just as they are?
When will love last?
