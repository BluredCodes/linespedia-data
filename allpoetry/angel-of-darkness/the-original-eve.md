title: "The original Eve"

The Holly mother 
Of all mothers,
She who is the first mother of all mankind.
A woman who stand alone,
Who is like no other,
She who stood by 
And loved of her children the same.
Who never judged,
But loved.
A woman who wouldn't judge you on your mistakes
Because she had made worst.
I woman who loved all mankind the same,
And still does to this day.
A woman who binds 
All of us together,
And makes us all family.
The best woman who ever existed,
The holiest of all that is holly,
The mother of all mothers,
A woman like no other,
Our original Mother
The Original 
Eve.
