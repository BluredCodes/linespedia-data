With every trickle,
There is a drop.
With every drop 
There is a dream.
With every dream,
There’s a wish,
And every wish must rot.
With every bit of pain,
There is sorrow,
With all sorrow,
There is death,
With every death,
There is love,
And every love must end.
Nothing ever stays the same,
Though nothing ever seems to change.
Everything is complicated 
Yet it is very simple all at once.
When will the very down poor of emotions come 
And change the worldview 
That everything is different,
Because if you look carefully enough 
You can see everything is like one another.
They are all connected
Nothing is truly different,
Everything is like everything else.
Everything is something different
