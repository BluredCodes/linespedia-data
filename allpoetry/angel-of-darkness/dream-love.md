---
title: "Dream love"
writer: "angel of darkness"
slug: "dream-love"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59055-Dream-love-by-angel-of-darkness"
---

Allow me touch your beauty.
Create me so I shall feel no shame,
No anger,
Feel only you.
Control me so I may be your reality
And become my reality,
Become mine,
Become my unreal reality.
Let me have you 
To touch and play with you all the days of my life.
Empower me,
Love me,
Sex me,
Be with me.
Become a real life day dream,
Become what I can’t have 
Nor ever really want when I’m awake.
Be my dream love.
