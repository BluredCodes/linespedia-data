Friends who were once so close 
Are now separated by miles.
Anger consumed one,
And a loveless heart controls the other.
The lies that once made them such good friends,
Now keeps them apart.
Not a word is spoken between them,
But both know what the other is thinking.
Such a waste of a friendship.
Neither will ever apologise,
Both are being controlled by their selfish pride.
Why do we allow evil tendencies to destroy our lives?
What is there out there that can stop it?
When will we lean?
Why does bad things happen to good friendships?
When will it end?just something I was thinking about.
