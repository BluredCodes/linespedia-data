---
title: "A bit of Truth"
writer: "angel of darkness"
slug: "a-bit-of-truth"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55042-A-bit-of-Truth-by-angel-of-darkness"
---

The world is full of people,
Who believe that they can handle the truth,
But the actuality is that,
They really can't,
They’re no better than anyone else.
We are all alike,
One another,
No one is really all that different,
And no one can really change.
We are the dead!
None of us really exist,
Or really have a purpose.
We believe whatever we want to.
But the thing is that we never,
Can truly understand or accept the truth 
When we finally get it.
We are all just playing a part in 
This joke of a life.
We really don't mean anything,
We are the joke.
Don't believe me look deep
Into a person's eyes,
And if you don't see anything,
Then nothing is there.
The world is full of people like me,
Pretenders,
People who play others as fools.
We never learn,
We only study those 
Who actually have such things 
As emotions and feelings.
But never actually ever experience them ourselves.
We are the unfeeling and unemotional 
Beings who play all others as fools 
So to get what others have,
But will never achieve.
We are the living dead!
Can you handle that bit of truth?
