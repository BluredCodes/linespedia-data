Wash the laundry,
Wash the car,
Clean the windows,
Clean the bar,
Lick the bands,
Lick the tan,
Doing the dishes,
Doing the pans,
Do Dan,
Do my bo,
Do somthing else but I can't remember what
Putting the fire out,
Putting my shoes on,
Putting my clothes on,
Phone my house,
Phone my boss,
Phone my best friends,
Kiss my puppy,
Kiss my boy toy,
Kiss my ass good bye.
Hope to heaven no one ever finds out what I did today.I don't really have a point to this one.
