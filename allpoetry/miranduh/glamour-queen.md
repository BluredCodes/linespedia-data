title: "Glamour Queen"

The usual parade of gowns, 
Vera Wang, Chanel, who else? 
The silenced cries of cramped feet 
forced to fit into mile-high steeps. 
A megawatt smile is flashed, 
exposing several whitened teeth; 
She poses daintily in her dress, 
bending slightly just to reveal some flesh. 

Tell me how you got that figure. 
I want it too. 
Tell me how much money you've saved. 
In other words, I want your bank book. 

Realistically set in her glamourous flat, 
She pops a champagne and downs it just, like, that.
