sometimes i feel like i'm changing
i am cold i am hot
i am happy i am not
my waters thrash
the current sways
to keep the thought
of pleasant days
but still every piece of me
reaches for common ground
to exist on
hear the sound
of my true self
wish to be
like no one else
and when i try
to be this way
i cannot stay
i cannot stay
