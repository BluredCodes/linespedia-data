---
title: "things that had no use at all"
writer: "miranduh"
slug: "things-that-had-no-use-at-all"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/53958-things-that-had-no-use-at-all-by-miranduh"

i am trying to be fair
but when i think back on it
i cannot remember
one
single
stupid
thing
to write about you
and that is something in itself.
