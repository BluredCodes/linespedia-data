why am i beautiful?
why i'm beautiful?
because i have no idea which sentence is the correct
one. 

imperfect one. 

i'm so pretty.
oh so pretty.
because i know that song.

i remember salute your shorts.
that's gotta count for something. 

why am i beautiful?
why i'm beautiful? 

because my mom said so.
