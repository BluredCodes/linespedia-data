There's nothing I can do to make you see,
My life revolves around you,
Your's doesn't around me.

You talk about selfish desire,
and what makes up your life,
70/30, I figure in neither
Where am I in the fire?

I cling towards the vain hope, that you'll open up
As I push my life forwards
Wondering where we'll both end up

I push, you drift, but what do I push for
Not sure it's what I want, just a goal,
Waiting for fate to open a door.

So I take a deep breath and hold on tight,
I'll push as hard as I can muster
Because I know that you're right.hmmmmmmmmm
