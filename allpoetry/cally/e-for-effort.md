Evolve in time and space
Watch your mind crease and curve
Or stupify in sopherific silence.
Just don't 'Miss' me.

Poor your heart out on paper or screen
Get high on intelectual nicotine,
Drown yourself in tides of paper,
Just don't 'Miss' me.

Use your mind, find your way
The question's there, you need to reply,
Put it down, back it up, make it right,
Don't just shout out without a fight.
There are no reasons you can't do this
So why is it always 'Miss' 'Miss' 'Miss' 'Miss'?Bit of a double meaning this one :) Born through frustration in the classroom and the depenacy some people.
