title: "What makes us happy"

What's in the future, who can tell;
I'm not bothered, it can go to hell;
I'm sick of worrying what's round the corner,
I'll stick with fate and what ever adornes her.

I think we all worry far too much;
About pain and anguish and money and such;
If it hasn't yet happened it might not,
Lets just be happy with our lot.

As you can tell, I'm quite cheary,
I don't think eyes look good all bleary;
I'm not a big one for a sad old frown,
I'm not letting things get me down.

So as I ponder
Sitting here
You know, I think 
It might be the beer.smile smile smile
