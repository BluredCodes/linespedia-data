I ask you what you want,
You answer, You don't know
Sometimes a bit less vigerouse, 
sometimes a bit too slow.

I sit here confused and wanting
An answer that is clear
Just tell me that you want me
And that you need me here.

I know I speak in riddles
But the truth is not hard to see,
You know you talk in tounges,
Always foriegn to me.

Talk to me in tounges
In any way you can
Talk to me with touches
And make me understand

You are my life and future
You are my soul and  heart
You are the thing in space and time
That makes the doubt depart.hmmmmmmmmmmmmmm
