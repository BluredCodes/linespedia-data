Petrified, putrified, purified, 
Pointless pityable pain;
Annoyance, agrivation, anger
Absolute afflicted aim;
Naked nameless nurosis,
Nearing numbing necessity;
Inevitable, inumerable, inordinable
Insatiable, intriguing intoxication;
Catastrophic, callous, cataplexy;
Cruel catatonic caringAn experiment :)
