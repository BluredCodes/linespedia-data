Waiting for the weekend 
To tick down to terminal
Sitting silently, 
Bored but knowing it won't last,
Getting angry 
At the time that's passed.

Watching work pile up 
To a predetermined perminance,
Knowing the hours 
Of wasted moments, sleeping
Anticipating days 
Of endless termoil, weeping.

There's no-one else to blame,
Each weekend it's the same,
Lounging in the sun
While work just sits not done,
Late Sunday night, I'm angry
Overwraught and tired

I'd better get off my fat ass
Before I get myself fired.Hmmmmmmmm, one trick pony is me :)
