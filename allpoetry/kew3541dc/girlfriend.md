Girlfriend
I gotta know
Gotta why you care
He doesn't know your there
Girlfriend
Answer me this
Why be one man's stepping stone?
When you can be another man's Queen

Girlfriend
You ask for advice
The only advice I can give
Love yourself Trust yourself
Only be true to you
I know its hard for you
or you to understand me
Girlfriend, I been there done that 
From a friend to a friend
You deserve a man that would love you till the end

What is it that makes you deal with the heartache?
Is it his promises?
Is it his money?
Is it his so-call love?

Girlfriend 
You are everything
Girlfriend
Answer me this
Why be one man's stepping stone?
When you can be another's Queen
