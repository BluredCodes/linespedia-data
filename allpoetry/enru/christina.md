I know this girl whose name is Christina,
Nothing rhymes with her name cept Argentina,
But thats ok, cause she is fun to talk to,
I like it when she laughs, cause its kewl,
She likes chick rock and thats ok,
I think shes kewl anyway (:-P),
She's one cute girl,
She makes my mind swirl,
Hehehe, now she thinks im hitting on her,
But maybe she just needs to know what compliments are for,
'Cause if she take them graciously they'll keep coming,
I will try to be completely forthcoming.....
She is hot as the inside of the sun,
And plus, she alotta fun!!I wrote this one for Christina 'cause she was feeling bad about not being able to go with her friends to the beach and swing-dancing, its not serious. she thought it was funny, it cheered her up.
