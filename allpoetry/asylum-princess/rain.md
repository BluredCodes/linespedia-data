The rain is falling all around
Onto the leaves
Dripping to the ground
Cleansing all it touches
Washing away the past
Renewing purity

Some see the rain
As merely dark and dreary
Inconvenient wetness
Dampness unwelcome

To my eyes
The rain brings beauty
To my mind-
A new beginning
To my life-
A peaceful freshness
To my soul
The rain brings
A new start
