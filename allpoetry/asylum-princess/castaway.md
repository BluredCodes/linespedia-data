Reaching out through space and time

My senses tingle

I feel your presence

It’s as if your palm is outstretched

Luring me to you

Hypnotically sedated

Drifting in the sea of all that is you

Soothing

Serene

Slowly exhaling

Captured by your spell

Yes captive, but summoned

Contentedly free yet swallowed by you

Slowly

Gently

Navigating my soul through unchartered waters

Anchored only by your hands in the small of my spine

Sheltered in your embrace

Your lips against my temple

So that I may hear your breath as you guide me

Entwined

Tangled

Waves of bliss splashing

Crashing against my skin

In the harbor of your arms

I am your willing castaway
