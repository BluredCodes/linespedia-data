If you love someone... let them go.  Isnt that what they say?

Do they have a rule for this?   I never had you anyway.

In you I saw more than my future.  You were a way to forget my past.

Years of pain and misery upon my life were cast.

The more we talked I shared with you.  My peace with you was real.

There was overwhelming intensity I never thought I could feel.

You brought color to my world where I saw just black and white.

As I approached the end of the tunnel, it was you who caused the light.

Each moment draws me  closer as in love with you I fall.

I opened up my world for you, to you I gave it all.

This is as far as I go with you, for you have set a limit.

If you cant surrender your world to me, my love I cant be in it.

You took me to the very edge and then you slammed the door.

If you loved me like I need you to, you'd want to give me more..

People in your life have hurt you; so you refuse to let me in.

If you cant stop distrusting me, we never can begin.

We built our castle in the sky, but we will never share it.

I cant settle for part of of you, I know I couldnt bear it.

Just know my love is endless and you'd never have regret.

Im sorry for your pains of past, too bad you cant forget.

My love I can not set you free, for you were never mine.

I wish you endless happiness until the end of time.
