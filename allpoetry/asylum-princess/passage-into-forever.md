title: "Passage Into Forever"
writer: "Asylum Princess"

Come to me
Place your hand in mine
I want to love and lead you
To another place in time
Relax and follow quietly
Let me show you  the way
I promise  once we get there
Forever we will stay
There are things I want to give you
To nourish your sanctity
Centered on your pleasure
Is where my mind will be
Walking hand in hand down a forest path
Or under a moonlit sky
Lying quietly together on the cool grass
The satisfied look in your eyes
Rushing out into a thunderstorm
To make love with the pace of the rain
Intense while the lightning is flashing
Taking me again and again
Encouraging all your ideas
Let your imagination run wild
Surrender and give your all to me
And have faith like a trusting  child
I love you and want you to feel it
Never question and never doubt
My life...I would forever devote to you
The man who will bring "me" out........
