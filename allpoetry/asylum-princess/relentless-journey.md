Will I ever escape this abyss of calamity?
It appears that an immense barricade has been placed in my path.
Towering endlessly over me-
I see no way to transcend.
I ponder the times in my past  when I was truly happy.
Carefree days of childhood;
Making mudpies-
Waiting patiently for them to dry in the sun.
Flighty days of being a teenage girl;
Sneaking kisses in my highschool corridors.
Twice the joyous discovery that within my body grew a beautiful child 
Waiting to be born.
I long for the tranquil winds of quietude
To breeze peacefully across my existance.
Desperately I seek solitude and refuge-
Doubtful that they exist for me.
I hunger for the touch of my soulmate
To share the sensual passion that dwells within me.
Im saddened as I wonder how I arrived as such depths of monotony.
Still the question remains...
Will I ever escape this abyss of calamity?
