Wading through life
No meaning 
No desire
No aspiration
Every monotonous day became a replica of the one past
I saw you there standing in the distance
My storm seemed to suspend
As I entered a land where emotions flourished
Total surrender of my soul is what I give to you
Not as simple as it may sound
An inner release of all my existence
A transformation of my total essence
A mental ritual of trust and communication
Our love reaching deeper and deeper
Our journey reaching higher and higher
Everlasting nurturing and joy
Stronger as one than we ever were as two
If not for you I would have remained lifeless
No meaning
No desire
No aspiration
You have resurrected me with your words
I gain encouragement from your strength
I rest well, knowing you love me
It is your love that brought me to where I am
I am...because of you
I can not give back nearly what you have given to me
I am blessed
You will always hold the key 
To my heart, my life, my pleasure, my world and my spirit
Only you can open the doors to the mysteries of my future
I look forward with visions of things to come
I rest secure in the knowledge that I am never alone
The shadows can not hurt me
I bow to you like the wands of a willow
For never again will I wake with
No meaning
No desire
No aspiration
Everything I treasure lies within your being
You have reached out your hand to me
For this I thank you
You are truly my life's breath
Come to me.......
