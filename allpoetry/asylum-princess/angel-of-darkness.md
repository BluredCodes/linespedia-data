title: "Angel Of Darkness"

The hour past midnight
He comes to me
Deep dark eyes
Wings of black
He dwells in the shadows
Pressing his soul into mine
He gently takes what is his
Our bodies entwine in rapture
Exposing in me what was hidden
Breath against flesh
Currents of desire race through me
His visits feed all I hunger for
I wish away the day
Craving the darkness because he is in it
I close my eyes and sleep
For in my slumber we meet again
I dream of death so I may dwell with him
But it is not to be
Destiny is the cruelest
When hearts it separates
The morning light sweeps away the moon
Everlasting our distance remains
For I am his Mistress of Light
And he my Angel of Darkness
