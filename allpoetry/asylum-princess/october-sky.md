As I walk alone 
Through this moonlit field
I feel the bittersweet caress
Of the darkness upon my skin
A savage wind moves the branches of the trees.
Against the sky they appear as skeletal hands
Clawing at the moon

Falling onto the cold damp ground
My gaze fixed upwards
The blanket of stars in the night sky
Surrounds me
I am lost in my thoughts
As the beauty of the autumn night
Takes me in

My emotions are spun like a spiders web
As I dangle by a single thread
This sacred place is where I was meant to be
Arising from my earthy bed 
Onward I will tarry 
Broken yet set free
Under this October sky
