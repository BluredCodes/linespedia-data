Alone I greet the world
Crying in the void
My visions destroyed
Slowly I lower myself to the cool grass
Lying silently still, I stare up at the moon
Realizing the similarities that we two share
The moon so distant and rarely full 
And I am incomplete as well
So beautifully it shines through the darkness 
Yet both of us remain seperate and secluded
Someday from deep in my silence
Out of time and out of space
My life will come alive
Love will be my music
Like a leaf blowing in the wind 
I will find a place to land
Like a candle bringing lumination in the darkness
Love will light my way
Drowsy eyelids will awaken to make love in the morning
No longer will I want to sleep away the day
Together my love... we will greet the world
My void destroyed
