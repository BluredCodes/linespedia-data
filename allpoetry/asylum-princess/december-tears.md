As the sun came up this morning
It didnt seem so bright
Still she taught me to see in the darkness
Things most cant see in the light
She'd slay the monsters that lived in my closet
Just to ease my fears
The bravest person I ever knew
As I grew throughout the years
I heard her voice in the wind today
As I turned to see her face
The cold winter wind caressed me
As I stood silently in place
Now these flower petals on the ground
Mark the place where my precious one lies
They will blend with the rain and become of the earth
As these tears fall from my eyes
Kneeling in the damp grass
With my cheek against the stone
As long as it rains and the wind blows 
I will never be alone
She is gone but not forgotten
Her love remains behind
She will live in me forever
Love knows no space or timeI wrote this for my grandmother a few years ago....she was and is the light of my life....the contributor of all good in me....
