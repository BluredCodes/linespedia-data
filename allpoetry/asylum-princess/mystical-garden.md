title: "Mystical Garden"

Through the shadows and foggy mists of my mind 
I was searching 
Hopeful at first 
Optimistic that I could find One so rare 
Lost and alone 
Seemingly abandoned 
Carelessly wandering to and fro 
I stumbled upon a garden 
Hesitant to enter its gates 
Whispers called me to venture in 
Once inside I was certain this garden was different 
Like no other I had ever walked 
Peace filled the air 
I drank it in 
Like it was a gift to welcome me 
There were walls all around 
Yet they were not there to capture or hide 
Silent and strong 
They protected all that was within 
Existing only as a shelter 
Never a prison 
Down every path 
Everywhere I turned 
There was an essence of peace 
I found myself soothed beyond my wildest imagination 
Had this place been created for me? 
Was this wonderful place a dream? 
A hypnotic sound of music played 
The melody felt immortal and timeless 
My soul felt restored 
From the moment of entrance into this gorgeous refuge 
I felt whole…real…and free 
The garden was my source of all that I envisioned 
All things encircled within one place 
I must wander its paths of discovery forever 
Eternally I will nurture all that grows within 
I am home
