My life is in pieces around me

I retreat within myself

It is a feeling of being at a crossroads

Standing at the edge of a jagged ravine

Afraid to jump

Terrified to reach for a hand

Ive lost so much of my soul here

Some I willingly gave

The rest ripped violently from me

I curse the day I allowed him entrance

My world was safe without his shadow

Through my pain and tears

Will I ever see the light?
