title: "The Maestro and His Temptress"
writer: "Asylum Princess"

Existing from a spark...
Two beings exploring their passion
Two hearts journeying to their inward desires
Two souls traveling toward their destiny
One eternal flame
He is her captor, her savior, and her master
She yearns for his strength and his love
She is his peace, his happiness, and his life
He yearns for her gentle, innocent flesh
Together lost in a world of beautiful harmony
A symphony of love is played
He directs her with his precise skill
Each time together they play a different song
Powerful..strong..drumbeats
Serene..melodic..overtures
Notes driving deep into their spirits
The atmosphere around them is electric
When at last the music ends...they pause
Two bodies forever strong as one
Bonded...yet set free
