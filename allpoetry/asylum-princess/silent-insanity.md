title: "Silent Insanity"

Between the edge of sleep and wakefulness, I feel him. 
I am merely half conscious, yet I am certain he is there. 
The silence screams out to me. 
How it terrifies my soul to be this lonely. 
No restraints, yet I seem unable to move. 
I wait. 
The night is long. 
Sensing him so vividly, yet obviously I remain alone. 
All that I hear is the haunting sound of my own heartbeat. 
My mind cries out in ghostly moans that only I can hear. 
I reason myself back to sanity and......... 
I wait.
