From the dark side of my dreams
He calls to me with twisted screams
The time to feel Him has now drawn near
My hunger grows and I have no fear
Transcending from me in my slumber
The beast He comes to drag me under
Precious creature with wings of black
He comes to me to take me back
My body longs for things unknown
He knows my pain and I’m not alone
My soul craves passions yet untold
On wings of lust my will unfolds
Chained at His side where I belong
Shackles of love that make me strong
My tears were silent He heard my cries
The clouds fold back on open skies
Reigning down to claim His prey
He comes to make me His today
Oh my darling entity
Needs no restraints to capture me
I’ve waited for Him patiently
I knew He’d come to set me free…
