For a moment in time…I knew elation.

Now plagued with misery, I am lost.

For a moment in time…I felt completion.

Crushed with the pain of betrayal, I am broken.

For a moment in time…I found my place.

To a user of my trust, I gave my all.

For a moment in time…I offered my soul.

Pierced and impaled, it came back to me.

For a moment in time…I had peace.

My heart lies lifeless, bleeding at my feet.

For a moment in time…I was sheltered.

My being is twisted, broken and cold.

For a moment in time…I was safe.
