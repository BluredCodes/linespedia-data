R-uin of
I-mmortal
P-erception

Pounding on the walls of a dark prison
Screaming to be free
Secluded and alone in a vast nothingness 
Wandering through this elaborately dark place
Unable to see into the future
Life seems to be fading away 
Not wishing to die with it
But only to bleed away the pain
Hard to grasp the reality
That once life's breath, so precious,
Has given the chest it's final rise
Kneeling at the upturned earth
Heartbroken and tears cried
The footprints have stopped in the sand
The only thing to do is say farewell
Staring blindly into the underworld of death
Eternal sleep has once again prevailed......The pic is Dorian Cleavenger titled "Life".
