---
title: "Dreaming"
writer: "Asylum Princess"
slug: "dreaming"

Strong are your hands that gently cradle my face
   Our eyes like open windows lock in fixed embrace
   Your strong arms firm around my waist
   Craving your kiss but resist moving in haste
   Your body gently lays me down Im burning with desire
   Moving with me perfectly; we share consuming fire
   You slowly reach to feel my breast
   My fingernails across your chest
   Anticipation surges in waves of calm and bliss
   My body quivers endlessly from just your tiny kiss
   Lowering my mouth on you, I want to take you in.
   Passions rise and heated cries bring sweat against my skin
   My legs embrace your body pressing down upon my soul
   I starve for you no longer love for you have made me whole
   No longer will there be a place where I end and you begin
   Take my hand and lead me where you know Ive never been
   Cries of passions fill the room as we two become as one
   Our circle forever closes and our love has just begun
   We drift to sleep so peacefully, and as real as it was seeming
   The morning sun shines on my face and I was only dreaming
   Some paths crossed are not by chance
   Bound more by fate than circumstance
   A time will come when this dream is real
   I will taste the shadow of your soul and your passion feel
