title: "[The dismal beauty]"

The dismal beauty
of death's pale, looming invitation stands.
So much time have I spent
crying my fears into the palms
of my tired worn hands
expecting reassurance,
comfort
in the darkest hour of pain.

Maybe I'll sleep,
But what if from my slumber 
I awake
and find myself alone.
'til the crying is done
and the tears are dry
'til the rain has stopped
and no more clouds in the sky.
I'll wait for my captor.
