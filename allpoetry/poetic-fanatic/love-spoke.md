~Love Spoke~

Love spoke and the great creation began,
beginning the harmony of all things then.
Love said, "I'll show exactly what I am".

Love spoke saying, I will give unto you,
something of me and you'll see it is true
for true love prevails all trails of time.

Love spoke offering a wonderful gift to me,
to renew my heart after I tried to break free-
Freely Love opened, great gates for humanity.

Love spoke at the moment that I saw her face,
facing of my heart, falling... out of it's place,
but Love gave me so much of a greater space.

Love spoke softly- this is what I have for you
whatever comes your way, you'll make it through
and through the portal, I'm waiting for you.

Love spoke to my soul and I heeded the call-
Love waits on our hearts, to embrace them all.
