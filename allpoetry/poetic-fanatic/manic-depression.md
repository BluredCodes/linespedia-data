Manic Depression

Apathy is a relentless nemesis,
To always strive for excitement,
another thrill for another day,
cocaine highs or heroine lows...
In the mind, briliantly bizarre
thinking commences, no fences. 
Is this genuis, is it insanity?
Fear everything, fear nothing!
Norepenephrine, Epinephrine, 
Seratonin surge brain and blood
and would you feel like a god?
Paranoia is ever, always present,
other demons, too many to mention.
Delusions, illusions, confusions,
what the heck is real anymore
or is it just a neverending dream,
not waking up, only getting deeper.
Floating, sinking and thinking,
supreme powers, war in your head.
Feels like Heaven, feels like hell
and tomorrow, whoever can tell?
Normalcy, non-existent, unobtainable.
The world can crush every once of
breath as if, squeezed to death.
Only God can help you survive now.
There are those who see death as
the only possible route out and 
you might think... crazy huh?
