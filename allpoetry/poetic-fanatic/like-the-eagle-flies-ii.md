* A Christian Write *

~Like The Eagle Flies II~

Like the eagle flies, He's chosen to lift,
lifting all, our burdens off of our backs,
backing us up, the end of our days and beyond.

Like the eagle flies, He releases the bondage,
binds that kept hearts chained to the earth,
earthly concerns mean nothing to the spirit.

Like the eagle flies, we are now forever free,
freely to be freed of debt, guilt and penalty,
for like the eagle, we have new wings to soar.

Like the eagle flies unto light of heaven,
heaven has now become obtainable to us all
and we all can show gratitude for the gift.

Like the eagle flies, nothing holds us down,
down fall enemies one by one till there's none
and nothing can seperate us, the Love of God.

Like the eagle flies, we'll sail on His wings,
a promise He brings, all new Heavenly things.
