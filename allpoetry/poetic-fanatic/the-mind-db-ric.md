The Mind...
enigma's key-
Door to reality, 
our future is hidden in there,
as is, the passage to our destiny.
Power, perception and planning,
all spark in corners, dark...
roots grow form in
the mind.

The Mind...
window to peace,
walkway to destruction.
Of fears, faith, hopes, doubts and despair,
all of these and what we are, reside there-
As life swiftly follows,
Ev'ry person chooses,
what we keep in
the mind.
I just hope certain friendS understand my message that with our minds, we must be careful. PF
