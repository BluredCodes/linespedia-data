~As The Eagle Flies~

As the eagle flies, through, heavens soaring,
our love soars with each moment becoming closer
and magic closes in to interlock our souls.

As the eagle flies, through the skies freely,
I'm freed, released totally, inhibitions with you,
for you and all you are, you're perfect for me.

As the eagle flies, love's wings take me higher,
higher, the flame in my heart for your love burns,
burning our boulders into nothing but smoulders.

As the eagle flies, our souls have met many times,
through time we will build stronger as days past,
passing our limits of dreams, how it could have been.

For with you, my heart flutters, wings of the eagle
when he prepares to enter into the great heavens.
