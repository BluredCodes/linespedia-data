* A song I just wrote that felt inspiring. :o)

Seek the Lord,
kindred souls,
quickly whilst He's found.

The time will come
when evils whirl,
what else is there around?

Oh great Lord,
our Lord above,
How awesome is thy name!

Your mercy
and great love,
came and took our blame.

Seek the Lord,
when storms blow
and fly upon his wings-

Over all,
the world below,
see the peace it brings.

Oh our Lord,
with sweet accord,
we sing this song to You-

Show the world,
You are the Lord,
so all can see it's true.

Seek the Lord,
Kindred souls,
when times are even good-

Don't forget,
He holds control,
give thanks, for we should.

Oh our Lord,
hear, Thee please,
give strength to us all-

To stand for You,
our bended knees
and answer when you call.

Seek the Lord,
when you're down,
comfort will abound,

Blessings, rich
will come around,
have faith, don't look down.

Seek the Lord,
Brothers all,
the time is drawing near.

He will come,
ready or not,
don't face Him in your fear.

Seek the Lord,
kindred souls,
quickly whilst He's found.

The time will come
when evils whirl,
what else is there around?
