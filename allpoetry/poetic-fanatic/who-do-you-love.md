~Who Do You Love~


Who is it that you would love?
Have you given it much thought?
Some people are just so obvious
and then the others... are not.

What about that good neighbor,
one, who lives down your street?
He's the guy, that's always waving,
every time you happen to meet-

Always smiling, pleasantly nice
and yet, you would never think,
one day he may be, forever gone,
crossing over finally, the brink.

Maybe, it's someone on the net,
a faceless friend, but a true one.
Would you happen to have regret,
God forbid, when they are gone?

It's something so simple to ponder,
who do you love, do you even know?
For those of which you are certain,
it would not hurt to tell them so.

If by chance you may be confused,
here's a simple solution for you,
take heart and put this to use,
and see others that you love too-

If their departure of this earth,
hung a dark cloud over your eyes,
you'd definitely know their worth,
don't be caught by such a surprise.

Time can be an awful, cruel nemesis,
it always seems, we chase the wind.
But some people need to hear this,
someone loves them... just around the bend.
