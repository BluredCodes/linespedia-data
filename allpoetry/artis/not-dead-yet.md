---
title: "Not dead yet."
writer: "artis"
slug: "not-dead-yet"
source: "AllPoetry"

Flies are circling all around my head
just out of reach... for now, they hover
in hopes of soon regurgitating 
the many flavours of my skin
but they've still failed to discover
this meal shirks participation
for as of now...I'm not yet dead
so let their useless wait go on
`
\./~~~~~~~\./~~~~~~\./~~~~~~~~~~~~~~\./
`
I'm just a poet who's reposing
in a hammock near my willow
watching clouds do abstract paintings
with my eyes relaxed...half closed
head perched on a rain-stained pillow
while from hard work I'm abstaining
`
\./~~~~~~~~~~~~~\./~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~\./
`
Flit away you maggots, boasting wings
find some excrement for your next fling
lest I take a shine to swatting things
I've got much more time then you I'll bet
so "Buzz off please...I'm not dead yet!"
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~\./--Artis
