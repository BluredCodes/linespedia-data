You are glass, 
shiny and reflecting all of the colours,
and me,
me?
I am breaking you, 
I swear I didn't mean to
I swear I didn't mean to.
I'm stupid, and out of glue.
I can't feel you, 
But you are jagged, 
And I made you sharp, 
I made you sharp.

Will you let down your guard, honey?
