---
title: "nothing ever changes"
writer: "neha bhat"
slug: "nothing-ever-changes"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55043-nothing-ever-changes-by-neha-bhat"
---

our fortunes
our misgivings

a look ahead
and a look behind

in future there is nothing
to find
hazy is what lies behind...

pain, torture, grief
and accusations,
moments of peace and quiet
razed in hue and cry
in diatribes...

unsung songs
muted voices
silent eyes
all tear filled
but gone dry...

voices unheard--
decisions taken
arson, misery, stoning
and death.

barriers and divides
unsketched yet drawn lines
walls abound
separation seeds
implanted.

safety lies here untouched.

undone and done
victims victimised
manipulators win.

peace disrupted
quiet disrupted
anger and pain raging 
the roads.

tolerance down
barricades up
hands wield arms
instead of flowers.

nothing can set right
an 'accident' that went wrong.

until today
nothing changes
people come
people go
time comes and
times goes...

yet
nothing is there here
that ever changes.
ever.
