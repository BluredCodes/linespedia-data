title: "sing song traveler (AP ASSIGNMENT)"
writer: "neha bhat"

I am nothing, 
But a sing song traveler.
Moving and flitting 
From town to town.
Hitting the road each
dawn,
And spending each night
in a different shelter.

I am nothing but a 
Sing song traveler
Regaling my newly met friends
With something unknown to them.

As a butterfly I jump,
As a squirrel I scamper;
Leaving behind but my
traces. On some faces
And in some hearts
As the words of my songs.

I am nothing but a
Sing song traveler
As I move, I come across 
towns. Towns all different from one 
another.
On some lips I leave
Behind a happy song
And on some, a sad tale.

As a sing song traveler
I start this dawn
And reach a town
All happy faces,
Smiles and smirks
Abound in here.
And I begin my song
‘Life is happiness with
sprinkled grains of
sorrow…’
I tell the tale of an
Unknown friend,
About his sadness and loss
And when I am done, 
I can see—
Not an eye remains dry.  

The night has
fallen and
Day has come to rest.
I sleep waiting 
For morrow. 

As dawn breaks,
I move again
To a town of pain,
Death and sorrow.
Tears abound
Streaming down bright eyes.
And when someone asks,
‘Who art thou?’
I begin my traveler’s song
‘I art thy friend
And come from a land of
happiness
As misery isn’t what all life is
made of.
Its got beauty and joy too.’

And when I am done with
I know that
Happiness has found yet
Another home here.

As the sun sets and 
Sky turns a pale crimson,
I arrange my bed
And think to myself
My experiences 
as a sing song traveler.

Each town becomes home for
a day.
All people my kith…
Some need happiness
And some a little sorrow.

Moving across cities and climes
Spreading the message,
I am just a sing song traveler.
My job remains
To tell stories
And let people
Regale in them.

When I left my clime?
I don’t remember
When I would reach back?
I can’t pre-decide.

I can just as a sing song traveler
Flit from town to another
Spreading a little peace 
And love;
Making the place a 
Little more better.

Yes I am nothing
But a sing song traveler.

I came as one
And will go as one
Just a sing song traveler.
