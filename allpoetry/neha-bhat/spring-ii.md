a plant grows
little by little
with sprouting of its
each leaf

creepers rise
with their tender shoots
engulfing the parent plant...

red, blue, green, white
burgeoning boquets
of flowers
abound around...

water sprinkled 
forming dew drops
on the surface
its coolness touching
the bare feet...
as we walk along...

bees drone, birds chirp
sun shines in magnanimous beauty
a lazy dog rests under the water tap...
trying to rid himself
of the heat...
the guzzling water cooling him off.

wafting smell of freshly grown mint,
abound in our human periphery...

squirrels run--
helter skelter...
one tree to another,
one plant to the other;
their race continues.
jumping, falling, scurrying away.

and in my house,
on my table i sit
writing this.
and welcoming spring in my own way.well it is titled spring II as i already have written poem on spring earlier....

neha
