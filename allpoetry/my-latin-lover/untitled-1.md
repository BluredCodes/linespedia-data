the way you make me feel inside
theres no words to describe
your the best thing i've ever had
theres no way i want anything else!
you make me so incredibly happy
i all i want is you





i know this poem is short, but when you like someone soooo much and you can't find the words to explain your feelings, the littlest bit could mean the most!!!!
