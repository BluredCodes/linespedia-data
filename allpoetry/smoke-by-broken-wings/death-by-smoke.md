Lying there in your bed
Wrapped up in your arms of lies
Just kill me, I want to die
My head is clouded with your whispers
My mouth tastes of ashes
Skin thin like paper, tears in my lashes
I want to vanish into the smokey air
Geez life just isn't fair
You hurt me to much for words
But why oh why would I do it all over again?
