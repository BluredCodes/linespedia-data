She sits on the edge of your bed
Looking into her glass eyes
They seem to have a glitter of life among them
Her hair dark and in curls
Dressed all in black
The only color lies among her hair {A little red ribbon}
and her eyes{Green grey gold red and brown}
Her hands soft and cool
Her face smooth and young
Her lips a crimson red
She sits and waits for you to arrive
For you to hold her 
For you to aid her as a guide
She wont talk back
Never make you cry
She wont hurt you
She will always listen
Always lay by your side through sickness and in health
Brush her hair 
Dress her up
She wont ever give you up....Let me be your Doll...
