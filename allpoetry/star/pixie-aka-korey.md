Your just like a pixie
Tickeled pink with laughter
Sent into this world to save these pathetic lives
But they run you down
They hurt your soul... your glow
I wont let that happen anymore
I'll give you my wings 
So you can fly
Give you my blood so you wont die
Give you my eyes so you can see
Heal your wounds so you wont bleed
You are worth so much more than you think
You are alive again
You are free again
Follow me into the clouds
Watch the rain fall
Bathe in the sun
You can feel again
Y can breathe again
I wont let them hurt you pixie
My friend...Yea Korey you're a pixie...........to me :)
