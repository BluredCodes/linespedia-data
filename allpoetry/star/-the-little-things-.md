I bleach the sky
Scratch away the pain
tearing at my skin
Its the little things that kill
Its the little things we feel
Going up then coming down
I burn the stars
Watch them glow
Hold you close
Watch the show
So much we can do
So much we have been through
Im addicted to life
Addicted to fools
Addicted to you
Sleep the day
Let me lay here with you
Hold me close
God I love you
Lay your sweet lips on me
I walk to you blindly
Feel warm-Center of gravity
Thick skinned-Falling away
Pretty words could never say
Set them on fire they're all the same
Im riddled by you
We give eachother all we know
In silence we can still talk
Believe me when I say Im not letting go
Together with our hands in eachothers back pockets we walk...{away}::sigh:: Im not going to let you go...
