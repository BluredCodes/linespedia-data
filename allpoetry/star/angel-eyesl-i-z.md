Her eyes so pure so true
Dont make them cry because of you
Hold her close save her soul
Dont let her fall
Dont lose control
She's so innocent
So gentle
Dont hate her
Dont let those hateful people contain her
She needs to be free
Needs to Be loved
She is something sent from above
Dont let those angel eyes see the pain
Dont let those angel eyes cry
Hold her close shes your only escape from reality
Hold her tight
She will keep you safe
But in return you need to love her to
