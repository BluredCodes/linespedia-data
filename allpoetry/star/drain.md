~*~

Something cold is forced inside her
Blood beneath her finger nails
Dirty needles break the skin
The dead things crawl from deep within
Her throat is soft, Her lips are red
Her thighs are white, Her heart is dead
She likes it where she gets it
But it never felt the same
With blood on her hands
Street lights whisper pain

Sliced her tongue
Caked in mud
On smiling lips is innocent blood
The warmest eyes so quickly growing cold
It stings inside
Poisons her with time
Mutate her and breed yourselves a savior
Drain her completley 
And do yourself a favor
The shine lost in her eyes
She stands alone in the rain
So this is how it feels to die
This is how it feels to recieve pain...
