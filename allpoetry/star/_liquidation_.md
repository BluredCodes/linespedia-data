My blood just wants to say hello to you
My fear is warm to get inside you
My soul is so afraid to realize
How every bit is left of me
And I want you...

I can take it sideways
Sometimes somethings feel like I'm on the other side
Waves of every feeling ever felt screaming
Hold it close
So I cant taste it

Steal the flesh back from the starving
Static minds
Deadly impulse seems so natural
We'll bring the real rain

Cut it Cut it Cut it
The sex
Cut it Cut it Cut it
The love
Cut it Cut it Cut it
To feel you inside me

What if I go to sleep for days
Will you count the hours
The last kiss hung in your room
I die in day dreams
I want to feel your power
Take more than enough away

I'll trace you body with my fingers
Comedown and waste away with me
Always closer to the flame 
Ever closer to the blade
Open up 
Open Wide
Climb inside you
Fit inside you
See inside me

Cut it Cut it Cut it
The sex
Cut it Cut it Cut it
The love
Cut it Cut it Cut it
To feel you inside me

I cause the need
Make me bleed
No need to worry about the time
Your lips are so inticing
Cover them with mine
Your liquidation is a siren song

Teach me
Just show me
I'll seep under your skin
I will hold onto your heart
Attraction
Frustration
Come to me
Your sanctuary...
