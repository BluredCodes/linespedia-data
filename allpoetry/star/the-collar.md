Its quiet in this room
The glass walls surround us
I want to be consumed 
I want to feel breathless
A kiss is shared 
Deep and true
I want you inside me
I want to feel you through and through
Your hands pull off the shirt
I had bought for you 
My hands glide across your chest tracing the cuts I had given you
Your eyes so deep and piercing my heart
You push me to the floor and hold my hands apart
Your eyes keep watching me as you slide the blade across my skin
Feels like electricity burning from within
It hurts for a little while and a small moan escapes my lips
"Dont stop I just need a kiss"
Like you need my lips to cover yours when I cut you
The taste of your blood so intense so amazing
I claw your back theres nothing else I can do
Your hands close on my throat 
Im am barely breathing
But no you wont stop
I dont want you to
The rest of our clothes slide off
and you kiss my neck...my spot
my breath sharpens and I feel your piercing teeth
I love you on top of me like this
I want to be beneath you
A collar placed around my neck
You pull me closer
Pull me to your lips
your lips on my ankles 
Your tongue up my thighs
I feel you inside me
Im so surprised 
You stop for a moment 
My chain clenched in your hands
I think to myself what is he waiting for and I rememeber what I told you before.
I sit up 
my lips near your ear
I whisper to you 
"Dont worry about hurting me, Give in to all of me, give me all of you.."Imagine us in that glass cube we can see the people pass us by, but the people cant see you...
