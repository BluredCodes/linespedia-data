~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~

  Not a word passes your lips
Just a golden kiss
As I whispered silently...
Help me through this

I can see the sky from here
I can feel my soul down here
Life becomes unreal
Take me in your arms
Show me how to feel again

You know me better than I know myself
Take me home with you
I wake to you next to me
Your MudVayNe shirt rests near our bed
I slip it on
So much love I have been feed

Can you feel me?
Knee deep inside you
No where else to be
Longstanding....
Everlasting....
{Breathless)

You're a wild flower in the starlit heaven
Turn off the sun
Pull the stars from the sky
You make me hard when I'm all soft inside
I fly free tonight
The fire burns in the night
Together in darkness we ignite

Still I dream and there you are
I call you Flame
You call me star
I've got your picture in my mind
It's as if you are the last of your kind

No one knows except the both of us
No one I'd rather trust
You're all I have 
You're all I got
But I don't have to hear your words
Cause I already feel your thoughts

In our coffe shop I stood 
I've never felt so good
A life I never knew
Lucky for me I got you

When you are with me..I feel...I care...I am
Lucky for me you understand
Through our blood we are one
From all we have been shunned
But we've found a new home
The nights we shared talking on the phone
We never tired
You are my fire

How quickly life can turn around
We've seen our share of devils in this angel town
Under neath your clothes
Lies a heart of gold

I never dreamed I'd find somebody like you
So forgive me if I feel like I don't deserve you
Together we can rest
Rest my head against your chest
Keep in mind I am always with you
Because my love is all for you........8........
