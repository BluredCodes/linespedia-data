Lusting face of green and red
Ive got the solution I've got the grip
These lives falling undead
Sink the needle and lets begin
Nothing to bleed in
Because of you Im alive
For you I wake
Swallowing you
Sedate the innocent
Turn your back on me
You're never alive
I hope you're satisfied
Because of you I stay
Held on the outside
Bleed in through your smile
Two faced
I feel you
Be glad you finally walked away
Hard to find how I feel
Controlling me every step of the way
Sufforcating me
Now I'm here for all to see
Bleed in me
Looking at the world through dying eyes
If it feels so good then why do you stop?
If it feels so bad then why do you take it?
Am I really that good or do you fake it?
Swallowing you
Doesn't make much sense to you
Doing all those things you do
Cant seem to get away 
Blow it away
You keep shooting those glances
You've taken your chances
You trip into a world that never seems too far away
How can you be this way
Just another vision...This is for those people who treat my Kelly..my love like dirt.Which he is not. You see he is better than those people. He actually has a heart. You can push him down and break him, but im gonna be there to pick him up... pick up the pieces. You're gonna have to go through me to get to him and believe me you wont win....Im much stronger
