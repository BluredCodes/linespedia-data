Will you be my destruction
Will you help me get through
To taste the life flow
Sinking into soft skin
Ingest rejuvanation
I offer blood
I offer everything
Do you want to feel the warmth
To taste the life
As I go under please take me in
Believe-Relieve I'll end the world tonight
Show me how to shine
You were there all night 
Gonna taste some blood before I close my eyes
Turning the knife
Suck me , Fuck me, resurrect me
Cut me and infect me
Slice me I want to die screaming
The blood let drain
Look past the skin
Taking you in
Bleed me, Feel me, and inject me
Feed me, kill me then dissect me
Mouth full of disease
Orgasmic death
So warm
No where else I wnt to be
Why do you like to lick my wounds?
Swallow Razor Blades
Suck hard as I drain
Suck the world inside of you
Splitting forth the night
Alien love: angel light
Pull my chains tight
I like what you put me through
Cause I almost feel alive
I want you to feel this too
Take the plunge
Take the dive
Slow motion:Liquid Universe
What do you have to believe in 
Sinking into underground
My New Death Sensation
