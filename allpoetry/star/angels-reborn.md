title: "Angels reborn"
writer: "...Star..."

He was her angel her gaurdian angel
Always there to help her 
Always there to save her from the sickness
He feels he has lost her now
Feels she has a weakness
Just because she lost her grip
Just tear out her heart then
Watch her bleed into your hands
She doesn't feel no more
She drowns in blood on the floor
Where is her gaurdian angel now?
Is she being punished?
Is she being hated?
Hate her for what she is now
She cant erase the past
She undertands her faults now
She now finally starts to laugh
She looks back down that path
Where she once loved that angel
But she is lost to him now
She feels so torn
So lost 
But now she sees the light again
Her angel still watches, but he says nothing
Why does he hate her so?
Angel live on; see through the crowd
See she still knows you're near
and she doesn't fear anyone anymore
She is once again reborn an angel
She is clean and whole again
She still will be there for you Gaurdian angel
She still cares
She can still feel
Now they are both reborn angels
looking into new light
Seeing where they were to blind so see
Feeling what they never felt before
Maybe this time just this time 
Angels can learn from eachother once more.....
