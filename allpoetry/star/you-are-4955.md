I see and understand now.....
So I am a whore then to you.....
Some love I've recieved huh 
Funny 
You seem just like the rest.
Do you even know what a whore is?
So much anger and so much hate
your blinded by it 
You can't even see the love others have tried to give you
and you just threw him away and treated him like shit
He still stayed by you
and tried to talk to you, tried to understand you
but he doesnt need to understand 
WE know you all too well

You let the hate overwhelm you 
and all you possess in your eyes is anger and cold
you think you were a gaurdian angel to me
How have you helped me
lol
By calling me a whore!?
Hating me for what I've done
Treating me like I was diseased
Never wanting to ever again touch me
Well fuck it
I dont need this 
Im too strong for this now
Believe what you want and feel what you want
Hate me, Love me, Kill me, it doesn't matter
We can't and will never be the same..ever

You write your poems... 
You think its funny!?
but its no joke... when you are the one running
You're too weak to face this
You see these eyes of mine they still possess life
These lips still express love
These arms still hold tight to the light
This mind is even greater now
This body was once yours 
This heart was once yours 
These lips kissed yours
These eyes looked into yours
 
But its funny how when I gave my body up
I felt I lost a piece of me
You took me greedily
You treated me different
and I felt alone
Was I not good enough for you!?
Am I truly a heartless bitch
I think not

I have love again
and I'm holding on to it
and I'm fighting for it
Maybe its not I who needs the gaurdian angel
Maybe its you
Look inside yourself
Let the real you come back out again
You'll understand I know you will 
but your other half I hate
it sickens me
It hurts everyone in its path
I know now that side is almost to an end
It will no longer last
You didn't love me
Well maybe you did
but only for a short while
I dont say what you say about me
I didnt hate you like you hate me

The truth... 
I know what the truth is
And I didn't always lie to you
I held back most things
because I did love you
I loved the real you
but not the you now
It hurts to see you hurt yourself like this 
but in time I hope you learn
In time the real you will return......Its sad how you keep hurting yourself like this
I feel so sorry 
because you are so blind and so stubborn
This will be the last poem i will ever right about you
