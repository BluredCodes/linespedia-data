If I had a razorblade

Or if I had a gun

I would cut or shoot myself

Wouldn't that be fun?



Killing yourself don't make

It better, but it sure don't make it worse

So maybe I should take the gun

And aim it at my head

And pull the trigger, once for all...



Finally I'm DEAD!
