Raindrops on a black branch;
               Upside-down through the looking-glass
Peoples reflections 
               In the water
Silently screaming, 
               Darkness
Darkness
               In the raindrops
It's your turn to die
               Disappear
Sleep well, little child
               You don't understand
HATE 
HATE 
HATE
