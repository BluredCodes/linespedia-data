---
title: "A thousand miles away"
writer: "Aixerona"

you are miles and miles away
and i can hear you sigh
since i am innoncent
i am serene
and pretend to have wrapped my arms
around you.
from a million miles away.
if anything is missing
i have it on a wishlist
in case my star ever 
meets yours,
i wish i was with you
i wish i could touch you
and i wish i could watch you sleep.
