title: "Boys for Vicky"

Boys for Vic were baby dolls
To play with, collect, then set aside,
Break by accident, try to fix, give up
Because she found a new one to play with
Oh-so-much-better, and prettier, too.

Friends for the girl were 
Treasures within treasures
To guard with her life
Til the end of time
Lest she got hurt
A thousand ways.

Vicky lost a hundred
And lost herself ten thousand times

None of us can now recall
How many times Victoria died.
