title: "Lost years of love over a second’s worth of words."

I cried, Kissed my fingers.
I breathed, blew the kiss.
I called, no one answered.
I bled, waiting on death. 

Never had I thought, not once
That I’d lose years of love
Over a second’s worth of words.

Never had  I thought, not once
That you’d be the one
To say goodbye.

I fall, guilt stricken,
I hurt, lost in despair.
I crumble, crushed by memories
I’ve died, still loving you.
