---
title: "Sweet Misery"
writer: "Aixerona"
slug: "sweet-misery"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62142-Sweet-Misery-by-Aixerona"

Break me again
And set me on fire
Burn me with something
To write about

Turn chaos on
In my mind
Pain me with something
To paint in blood

Set your guilt
All over me
Cry me a sweet something
To sing the blues

Be my sweet misery
Make me cry
To dry my tears
