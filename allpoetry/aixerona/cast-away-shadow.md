---
title: "cast away shadow"
writer: "Aixerona"
slug: "cast-away-shadow"
source: "AllPoetry"

a shadow behind me
to scream my goodbyes to

(maybe this was unfair
but that's the way
a woman works)

scream as i hate the way
it expected me 
to melt within it's
cold, plain darkness

send the winds it's way
to push it away
and it never even noticed.
