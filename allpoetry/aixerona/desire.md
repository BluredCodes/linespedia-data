desire rode us
like a beast in the wind
as pleasure pierced
the midnight silence

desire, in flames
rushed through our veins 
to melt our flesh
and steal passion from the sun

desire was my body
his skin and mine
wild flesh ruled the night
every breath screaming passion.i made a picture and this just popped up. :l
