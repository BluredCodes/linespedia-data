---
title: "after goodbye"
writer: "Aixerona"
slug: "after-goodbye"
source: "AllPoetry"

everything is flashing by
(so fast, so fast)
like a slap

all those times
you called me yours
all those promises

childish cliché's
that meant the world

(i'll take you away 
from it all) 

seems like it's been forever

(i'll never leave) 
you turned away

(i love you)
and you were the one
to say goodbye.

wasted words
wasted smiles
friendship. 
lost in a second's worth of words.

precious tears
precious blood
my scars
are yours for free. 

felt like forever
then i saw you again
nothing changed
but the love we shared.
