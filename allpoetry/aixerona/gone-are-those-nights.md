---
title: "Gone are those nights"
writer: "Aixerona"
slug: "gone-are-those-nights"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62626-Gone-are-those-nights-by-Aixerona"
---

i wanted to see my hair
brush your lap 
where i would fall asleep
to have you in my dreams

but i had to see it all
get blown away
a storm in a desert
despair in disarray
bloodshed in my tears
a scream in a sigh.

i tried to piece us back together
but it kept falling apart
i know now
that gone are the nights
where i would tell you everything
when i only wanted to listen.
