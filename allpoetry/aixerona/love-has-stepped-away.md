title: "Love has stepped away"
writer: "Aixerona"

it has been long 
since love stepped away

(though he might not understand)

i have grown
to turn away

(though i still eye the rear-view mirror)

ever so slowly
i can feel again

(though i wonder if this is worth it)

i would hate to have to 
love again

(though i wouldn't mind another try.)i am forsaken :l
