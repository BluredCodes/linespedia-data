---
title: "love it seems ( can fade away )"
writer: "Aixerona"
slug: "love-it-seems-can-fade-away-"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57296-love-it-seems---can-fade-away---by-Aixerona"

i never knew
love could fade
                     so fast
                     so cold
                     so brutally
i forget how to love
as ever so slowly 
as he forgets about me
                     again i go unnoticed
                     even as i say goodbye.
