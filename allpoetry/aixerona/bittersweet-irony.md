---
title: "bittersweet irony"
writer: "Aixerona"

my man got up and left
didn't bother saying goodbye. 
and it's so funny, it hurts.

my best friend turned away
said goodbye, left me here,
and it hurts so much it's funny.and no one wants to believe me when i say i suck. pfft.
