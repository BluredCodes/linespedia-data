title: "all of your pretty lies, oh how i should have known."

so i was just another conquest
just another kiss
just another girl 
who owns oh but another
fragment of your face

oh but a piece of a portrait
in a reflection of your heart
a busy second in your schedule

and to think i fell 
for those gorgeous lies
copied plastic i-love-you
while i was oh but
another face
( waiting in line )
