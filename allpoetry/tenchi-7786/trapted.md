Help me!
I’m trapped!
Held hostage!

I’m locked Behind bars.
At the very bottom of the deepest, darkest ocean.
Chained to the dark, cold ground.
Five boulders are chained to my legs.
The key is hanging two inches from my reach.

They watch me non stop!
Two sets of red eyes glow in the dark in front of me.
They press in.
No room to breath.
No breath to speak.

All self confidence,
has been erased
All hope has been deleted
like an old computer file.

Sometimes when they turn around
 I grab for the key!
But I let go when they turn back around.

Help me Can anyone hear me?
Can anyone understand? 

Help me!
Before my life melts away.
Before my pimples turn int wrinkles.

Help me
Please
Help me
