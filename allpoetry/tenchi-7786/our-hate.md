Is their a cure for life?
Life is so painful.

Is their a meaning for life?
Why do we make life, pain?
We don’t even know the rare daimon inside of one another.

We make each others life a living hell.
We cause pain.
Its our fault.

Our racism,
Judgement,
and hate,
kills innocent people
