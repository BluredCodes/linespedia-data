---
title: "I would like to fly."
writer: "Tenchi 7786"
slug: "i-would-like-to-fly"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54891-I-would-like-to-fly.-by-Tenchi-7786"
---

I would like to be a bird.
Free to roam the sky.
To fly is my dream.
To be free free free.
To fly to the North.
and to fly to the South.

To fly over lakes,
to soar above trees,
to fly above the clouds.
To sore like a hawk
to glide like a falcon
to dive like a eagle.
I would like to fly.
To fly is my dream.
To be free free free.
