---
title: "What Would I do with out you?"
writer: "Tenchi 7786"
slug: "what-would-i-do-with-out-you"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54885-What-Would-I-do-with-out-you--by-Tenchi-7786"

My love is given to you.
My hope is within you.
My life depends on you

Without you I would have no reason to live.
Without you life would be just a nightmare full of pain and sorrow.
Without you disease would have no cure.

So don’t you understand?
“You” are you
 my friends
