title: "Would You Still Care?"
writer: "Tenchi 7786"

Would you cry if I died?
Would you care if I was not there?                          Would you ever think of me again?
Would you still care?

Would you long to hear my voice once I am gone?
Would you want to touch me one more time?
Would you want me to hold you one last time befor I die?
Would you still care?

Would you take back all the things you said because I am gone?
Do you regret the times you wern't there now that I
am gone?
Now do you care,because I am not there?Great Poem Mike I really like it.
