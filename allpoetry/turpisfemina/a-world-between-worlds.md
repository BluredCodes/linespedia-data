---
title: "A World Between Worlds"
writer: "turpisfemina"

In a world between worlds.
In a time between times.
In a place beyond reality.

There are no boundaries,
No consequences,
and no limits.

This passion cannot be dulled,
This love cannot be extinguished,
This hope cannot be destroyed.

Your strong arms surround me,
So gentle, I feel as if I’m floating.
So protective, I know that I always will be safe.

Your warm hands caress me,
So soft, they feel like silk.
So stimulating, I feel as if my soul has turned to flame.

Your sweet body presses against me.
So warm, I never want this to end.
So strong, it takes my quickened breath away.

Together forever in this world of hope,
Forever in this moment of passion,
Forever in this moment of truth.

There are no boundaries,
No consequences,
and no limits.

For we are in a world between worlds.
In a time between times.
In a place beyond reality.
