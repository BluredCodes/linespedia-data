---
title: "Alone"
writer: "turpisfemina"

Close your eyes and count to ten
Open them to pain again.
The endless pain of this horrid life.
The tears and blood of endless strife.
Take my trembeling hand, and lead me through.
I would give my life, my soul for you.
Touch me and take away this pain.
Kiss me and stop this endless rain.
Please, I beg you, hold me tight.
Don't leave me alone on this cold dark night.
Touch me with your warm, strong hands.
Hold me close, because I can't stand
being alone again.
