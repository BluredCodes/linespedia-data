Cold.

So cold.

I can't breathe.

Needles 

so sharp

stabbing my heart.

Where are you?

I can't see.

Please.

Hello?

Is anyone there?

So cold.

Can't move.

Can't run.

Help me.

Please.

Anyone.

Can you see my tears?

Can you hear my screams?

I'm sorry.

I didn't mean to bother you.

I'm leaving now.

Goodbye.

Have a wonderful life.

Just know this:

Someday

you will see

those who live

 in the frigid dark.

Those like me.
