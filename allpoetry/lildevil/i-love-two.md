title: "I Love Two"

I'm so confused
I dont' know what to do
I say that I love you
But I love him too

I don't know him
As I do you
Hell, all I know is his name
But it's everything I don't know
That makes me want him
And get caught up in his game

I'm not saying we're over
I'm not saying we're through
All I'm saying is I need some space
Til I decide what I'm gonna do
Cuz I don't find it fair
To tell you I love you
When I love someone else too.
