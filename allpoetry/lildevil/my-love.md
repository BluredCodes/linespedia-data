---
title: "My Love"
writer: "lildevil"
slug: "my-love"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55663-My-Love-by-lildevil"
---

I don't know how i feel
no words can describe 
you're the one i think about
before i sleep at night

i wait for the day to see you
the day i taste your kiss
i love you with all my heart
i hope that day comes quick

i am longing to hold you
but are you for me?
i know you felt the same once
i wish you were happy with me

you are my sky
you are my day 
you are my only love
to be with you would be heaven
so i'm willing to wait above

i'd wait a life time to have you
maybe one or two
if only your heart could beat
as mine does for you

i know that i've lost you
and i only want you back
i want to replay the past
and this time not lose track

all i want is for you to love me
equally as i do you
so there's just one question...
what am i to do?

my days are so gloomy
but my nights are so clear
for that is when i see you
when i feel your soul near

i wish to have your love back
but most importantly to have you
my heart longs to sense you
and my body even more does too
so most importantly, i still love you!
