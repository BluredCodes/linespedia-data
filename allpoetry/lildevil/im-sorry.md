Daddy I'm sorry
I never became a star
Daddy I'm sorry
I'm not as good as you are
Daddy I'm sorry
I quit everything you enjoyed
Daddy I'm sorry
I wasn't born a boy
Daddy I'm sorry
You have to hear me breathe
Daddy I'm sorry 
You're not happy with me
But Daddy now I'm tired 
Of trying to fit in your shoes,
For I don't wear the same size
And I don't enjoy the things you do
Daddy I'm sorry
I hope you'll understand
Daddy I'm sorry
I won't grow up to be everything you think I can
Daddy I'm sorry
I'll never wear the clothes you wear
Daddy I'm sorry
I'll never have your eyes to give that evil stare
Daddy I'm sorry
I'm not what you want me to be
And Daddy I'm sorry
But I'm happy with being fucking me!
