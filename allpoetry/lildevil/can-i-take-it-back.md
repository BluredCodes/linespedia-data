title: "Can i take it back"
writer: "lildevil"

o i wish i could tell you
this secret that i need to say
but if you find out this information
ur sure to stay away
so now i sit her talking
repeating lie after lie
for i'm trying to protect our friendship
i'm trying to stop you from saying good bye
i know that i should tell you
but i don't want you to be upset
and what i did with him
i did only for a bet
i know that you will hate me
cuz i messed with your man
but please understand i know i messed up
and i will take it back if i can
if there's someway to make it up to you
tell me what to do
but dont' blame me for everything
cuz ur man was there too
