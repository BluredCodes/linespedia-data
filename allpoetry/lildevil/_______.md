I've been through shit
I've been through hell
I'm only 14
And my life isn't turning out so well

I was the one hit 
I was the one hurt
I was the one abused
The one treated like dirt

I'm always on the bottom
I'm the one who doesn't fit
I'm the one picked on
The one treated like shit

You better not hit me
You better be nice
You better not pick on me
You better think twice

For one day I'll rise
One day I'll be a star
One day I'll do great things
And be greater then what you are

I'm gonna watch you fall
I'm gonna watch you swell
I'm gonna watch you scream
As you burn in hell...

... Now I'm on top
Now I'm the start
I laugh at you down there burning
Oh crap, I'm no better then what you are
