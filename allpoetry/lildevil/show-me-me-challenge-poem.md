Look at me closely
Tell me what you see
I know you don't see me
For I can't be seen

Hiding under my clothing
Telling only about the good
If you knew the real me, you'd cry
I just know you would

I don't show my feelings
I don't tell when I'm hurt
I hace to stay strong
So people won't treat me like dirt

I smile when I'm angry
I try to hide the pain
I wear lots of makeup
So people won't see my life is full of rain

Please notice my signs
Please read between the lines
Force me to show the real me
Before I become even harder to findI hope you like it.
