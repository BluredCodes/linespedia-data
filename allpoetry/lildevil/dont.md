Don't show me sympathy
Don't show me your love
All you did was lie to me
And now I've had enough
Don't smile at me
Don't show me you care
Cuz when I needed you most
You were never ever there
Don't call me your lover
Don't call me your friend
Cuz a friend and a lover 
Give support til the end
Don't call me at my house
Don't come atound my door
You messed up for the last time
And I don't love you anymore
Don't give me presents 
Don't give me time
I'm forgetting your existance
So please, forget mine
