---
title: "My friend"
writer: "lildevil"

come to me when you need to
when you need any thing at all
i'll be the one to save you
to pick u up after ur fall

i'll give u the world
if that is what you want
i'll protect you from all evil
ur the one no one will get to haunt

call me with a problem
call me just to chat
stay with me forever
never wonder off my track

i'll give u my hand
i'll give u my heart
for u've stayed by me
u've helped me from the start

if you need an extra inch
to help you reach the stars
just come and call my
i'll boost you up o so far

i'll give you strength
i'll give you courage
i'll give you anything you need
just make me this one promise...
you'll always stand by me
