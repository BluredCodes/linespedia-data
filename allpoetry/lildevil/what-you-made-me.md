I can't stand to see you
You're the being on which I place my hate
Please turn around and leave
My sould has nothing left to bleed
You've taken all I have
No more happiness is left
All your hatred has been molded into me
You've finally had a success
I show no love or compassion
Especially for you, my creator
I hate you the most
You've ruined my life
You raised  me to hate
Yet you want respect
I've been shown no repsect
So none can I give
No sorrow I feel 
No love I show to you
