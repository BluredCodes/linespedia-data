After all the hurt I've felt
I can't let you go
After all the truth I've learned
I can't forget what you told me
I know they were lies
I know you never cared
You only said you loved me
To try to fill your heart which was bare
You cheated on me
I forgave you
You left my life
You pushed me away
I'm only more determined to get you back
I know nothing about you
For all you told me were lies
So why can't I forget you
Why can't I let you go
What am I fighting for
You were never truly mine
