title: "Beauty brings boredom"
writer: "lildevil"

Give me your heart
Give me your soul
Give me everything you have
So I know I've got you under control
Buy me the world
Give me all happiness
Take me to the stars
Give me all courage to be fearless
Give me all love
Give me your keys
Show me the world
Provide me with everything i ask please
I'll try to love you
But it's only my beauty that makes you stay
And if I get bored
I'll do as I did before, and just walk awayumm, it's about a girl who goes through so many guys and forgets them when they fail to give her what she wants...it reminds me of some friends of mine!!!
