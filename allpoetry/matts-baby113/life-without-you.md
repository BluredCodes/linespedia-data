(Life Without You)
Life without you would be terriable 
Life withou tyou would not be great 
Life without you would be bitter
Nothing in my life would be sweet 
Then I would be forced to find somebody else
But life without you would not be worth living 
So, why live at all
I ask this question because one day we might part
Or we might make it to the end
