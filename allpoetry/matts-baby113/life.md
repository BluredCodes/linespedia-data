(Life)
What is the meaning of life
What is the meaning of life to you
Is there even a meaning 
Are we just put here to live and die 
Or to actually become somebody
Most of us become somebody 
Some of us don't
What is the meaning of life for a nobody
That's the question everybody asks
But, the meaning of life is what you make it
So, make it the bestDO you like this poem? I think it is kind of iffy.
