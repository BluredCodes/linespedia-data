(Untitled)
My love is like a flower
It grows everyday for you
And you grow more special to me too
Everyday I grow stronger
Just because of you I am not blue 
Without you my days would be gloomy 
And my nights would be sad
That is why I am so glad
To have you here with me
To eternitywhat do you think?
