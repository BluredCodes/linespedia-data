---
title: "Part of You"
writer: "Minds Eye"

I am the darkness that shadows your mind
Some feel I am a burden
Maybe I am

But I can be used to your advantage
You just don't choose to
None of you do

I don't care!
Why should I?
You don't seem to

There is a catch to my existence
You must believe
Believe in me and all I am capable of

Are you afraid?
Does curiosity have you within it's grasp?
Then look for me
Look deep within yourself

Sometimes, late in the evening
When your mind can't help but wander
You look deep into that corner of your room
And see me staring back at you

The more you believe in me, the stronger I become
Let me be what I am destined to be
That dark shadow in the corner of your mind.
