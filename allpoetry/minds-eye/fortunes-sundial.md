title: "Fortunes Sundial"

Carpe- Diem
dangled playfully on his chest
Can you tell time
from my eye of glass?
Green moonlight beams
shot into the eye
Directing me to a
new horizon
Traveling down moonlight drive
looking for my existence
I found you.
Now I am happy.
