I feel so pure, so gentile.

		I want to run the softness of my tongue
			over your fingertips
		I want yours to feel the tenderness
			Of my pubescence

I am innocence even though I do not act it
I am fragile but only you know how to break me

A feeling of intrigue rushes over my body
Mmmm, it tickles my senses

I let you admire my femininity
I let you kiss me as you smile

Now, I feel embraced
And it is now that I feel special.
