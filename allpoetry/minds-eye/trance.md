---
title: "Trance"
writer: "Minds Eye"

I close my eyes
    Shooting stars dance on my eye lids
Kissing them so gently
    So that I may dream

SLEEP...

I can't let go of the days tensions
    Methods that push me towards slumber
Writing, struggling to release the thoughts that race around those that stands still.

BREATHE...

Take in the nights still breath
    I taste the evenings nectar so to induce peaceful slumber
Wrap in a blanket of moonlight, almost as warm as the dawn.

DREAM...

My minds endless wander as I fall to ease
    You bring sweet Wonderlands, full of adventure
Let it be you that brings me the new day
    So that I may feel the warmth on my face.
