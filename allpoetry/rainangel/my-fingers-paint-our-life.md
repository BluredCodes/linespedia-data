title: "My Fingers Paint Our Life"

If my fingers were attached to my heart,
I would paint.
A beautiful scenery with an ocean,
Trees,
Wind blowing straight through me.

It would be bright,
Like I hoped my life would be.
A portrait untainted,
Full of perfection,
Like the love we share.

If my fingers were attached to my heart,
I would paint a portrait,
Of a perfect sea-side life,
For us to live in.
