It's not meant to be understood,
Or touched,
Or scrutinized.

But to be recognized,
And beautiful,
Symplistic.

The feeling,
Of being in a large room,
Looking at one painting.
The feeling of sharing the artist's thoughts,
His brush.
All alone.

It is Pier at Blue Point,
or Torso of Venus.
So sweet.

I could live in that moment of perfect art.
