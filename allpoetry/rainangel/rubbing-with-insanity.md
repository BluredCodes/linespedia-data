---
title: "Rubbing With Insanity"
writer: "RaInAnGeL"
slug: "rubbing-with-insanity"

There is something inside me,
That abuses me,
Kills me while I sleep.

I feel it's hands on my heart,
Rubbing it just the right way,
While I lie still,
Too scared to move away.

There is something inside me,
I feel it break when you touch me.
Your hand like a hammer,
My soul is glass.

I feel your gentle caress
When I am awake all by myself.
When I look at you lying next to me,
I cannot help the anger felt.

There is something inside of me,
I feel it moving like an alien wanting out of my pain.
It rubs against my anguish,
Driving me insane.
