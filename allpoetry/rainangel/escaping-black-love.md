title: "Escaping Black Love"

Sometimes,
The night stretches too far into the day,
And it beckons me with it.
I feel like I am swallowed,
Into the whirlpool of love,
That I have for you.
I breathe it,
Sleep in it,
And I cannot escape it.
But when the sun rises,
With the early morning dew,
There is a pain in my chest,
When I awake and don't see you.
So I live in the night.
I love breathing it,
Sleeping in it,
And never escpaing it.
It pulls you towards me.
