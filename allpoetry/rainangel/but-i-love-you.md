---
title: "But I Love You"
writer: "RaInAnGeL"
slug: "but-i-love-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

Like yesterday,
When we walked together in the rain,
And I could feel your heart beat,
When I laid my head on your chest,
And I didn't hear that old pain.

Have I told you recently how I feel?
I sometimes think you just know.
Like you should know I love you,
Why should I say it,
If it shows?

But I love you.
And I wanted to say it.
I want you to know I feel it...
In every pore of my body,
In my smile, my tears, 
You'd have to be blind not to see it in my eyes.

But you know I love you...
Right?
