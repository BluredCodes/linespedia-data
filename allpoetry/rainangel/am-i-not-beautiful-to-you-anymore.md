Am I not beautiful to you anymore?
Just a woman on the street,
Wearing a nice perfume,
Bur ordinary.

I want to shine,
Like the star you once gazed at,
Claiming your undying love for me,
And blind you with my beauty.

So many times,
I begged for you to love me as I loved you,
And you touched me,
Not my heart---
My pussy.

Am I not beautiful to you anymore?
Just a woman passing you on the street,
Wearing a nice perfume,
But ordinary,
No longer your lover.
