Faint whispers on my neck...
I hear your breathing gently in your sleep.
Now I lie in bed alone,
Remembering too much to weep.

The smell of your clothes,
When I snuggled against your chest.
I remember when you made love to me,
How your hands caressed my breasts.

And now forever seemed so short,
Merely a day in my entire life.
But for the short time we were married,
I loved being your wife.
