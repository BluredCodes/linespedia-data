title: "Before I Met You"

I don't remember who I was before I met you,
Tasted your lips,
Fell in love,
Or smiled from my soul for the very first time.

I don't want to remember who I was,
Before I held your hand,
Or you held me,
And I made love to you for the very first time.

I don't want to remember who I was before I heard your voice,
The sweet soothing sound of your laugh,
The crack when you smile,
I was never me until I felt it, heard it.

Now I'm complete.

I don't want to remember who I was,
Before the sun touched my face.
Showed me love before me,
Your soul,
Your body,
Your grace.

I love you.
