Fire... oh the desire...
To burn my skin...
To see it melt my flesh.
To see it all fall away...
Feeling that agonizing pain...
As it invades my mind.

Vila dolor est..
Life is pain...

When it starts to rip my skin..
Eating its way deeper within...
Healing all my sins...
Burning my life away.

Vila dolor est....yea it needs some work but this is just for starters~*~Jess
