title: "Pitied by the Pitiful"
writer: "the Pitiful by Malveasor"

Pitied by the pitiful,
Such an irony of the Fates;
Or is it?

A mask not worn to hide from hurt
But to feed like a parasite
Why should time be invested
In one that would lie to his own mother
And deny the evidence?

Refusing to believe
Does not make it untrue
For the reality of it was born
And therefore,
It is not only true
But undeniable as well;
Until truth meets you

Sanctified by actions took,
Scorn a friend at leisure
Your time is wasted at not caring,
When in truth,
You lie the most to yourself

Do not treat me as a fool
I do not fear you
My masks are nonexistent

Your words flow freely,
Like intoxicated floods from rivers ashore
To grace the ears of a believer
And make the carelessness known
As they are for your benefit alone

What is friendship?
Research words you use with ignorance
For perhaps conflict can be avoided
And you would not be perceived
As a pompous burro
To those that trust and invest
In those like you

Mistake it not
For feelings to you only present themselves
In pouches of good luck charms
To be used when you want them to be used,
Emphasis on “used”

Blinded eyes can often see
Truth is an aura,
Not a negotiable ideal

Nations built astray;
Buildings constructed of repentance
In a city of sin
With you the mayor
And I the asphalt 

Corruption is evident
For an apple that was thrown
By the tree of knowledge
Into a pit of despair
That is merely circulating reflections
Of that person you claim to be
And not of whom you are

Fraudulent heaps poison the mouth
Deception is in the air
With a presence I wished not be found
For I never befriended you;
I befriended what you pretended to be

Your time is up
Go rot in someone else’s grave
I have enough maggots in mine


In the end, both the king and pawn go back into the same box.2/5 of a series written in honor of a friend/enemy.
