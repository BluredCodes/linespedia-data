I never realized how much you cared
the thing that seemed like a dare
i never gave you the chance
to show how much we had enhanced.
I never looked at what was there
for you and me,
But I think now we were meant 
to be.
You were always the one,
but I never let you show me
that you were that special 
someone.
i just hope now that I have
that someone
there that will care for me
the way he did back then
si I can show him thta he 
can care for 
me that way again.

~Kimmy
