title: "black sheep"
writer: "Kimmy77"

So left out ,
so alone.
you could only hurt me so much,
if you only knew how much.
Why did I feel so alone?
was i felt out to be throw?
in your eyes i never amounted to anything you liked
yet I have so much with nothing to hide.
I have not failed you yet you still look at me different!
my mind thinks about it more and more everyday 
yet im older now and on my own way.
I feel so lost in this world with noone to feel my pride
yet im hear and i can not hide.
so i say to you:
"Enjoy your life cuz your not apart of mine."

~Kimmy
