Something to soon in my life
what is there to feel what is tere to hide?
Lost emotions inside
something there hard to find.
you are now apart of me 
we now have become one
and you are in my life for now to come.
the feelings i feel inside of me when you alive
seems so real but yet your so far behind.
the more i feel you 
the more i want you here
but most of the time i sit back and fear.
i cant handle a third right now
but your coming no matter the time.
i feel you so alive inside.
yet I long for you to become part of the family
so the day will come when you are in this world
for things may become worse then they are
i long for you to see what will become!
GOD BLESS MY CHILD!
