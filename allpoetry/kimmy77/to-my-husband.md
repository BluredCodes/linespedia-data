---
title: "To my Husband"
writer: "Kimmy77"
slug: "to-my-husband"

its so hard to look you in the eyes


is there something to hide?


can you see me


or is that just for me to see?


i love you, more then you know,


but i cant help what seems to flow.


we sit here in silence


we dont experience the violence.


we have so many problems to solve


cant we sit down and let things evolve?


you seem so far from me now


we look at each other and wonder how?


so far from here we cant see


the things that make us be.


your the one i love so dear


can you ever hold me near?


i love the look in your eyes


but right now that seems to subside.


i cant find you in this world some how 


i want to find you now!


you look at me and think i dont care no more


but you dont know what i feel inside thats so sore.


i cant describe the feelings i have for you anymore


thats why we have to find out what we are here for?


do you love me ,

do you feel me,

do you see me,


all i know is some how you will find me.


and in the end we will look back


and know we were in love for a fact.


~Kimmy
