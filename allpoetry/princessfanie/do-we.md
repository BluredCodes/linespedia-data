---
title: "~Do We~"
writer: "princessfanie"
slug: "do-we"
source: "AllPoetry"

Do we take a chance to exist here

Do we walk separately into this daylight

Do we transform the beauty of our love only to the night

Do we dare to dance in this make believe universe

Where stars collide and we watch them explode

Do we taste the existence of our living beings 

Do we tempt to make this real

Do we live for fear or pain

Constant thoughts we contemplate

Do we take this chance to exist....
