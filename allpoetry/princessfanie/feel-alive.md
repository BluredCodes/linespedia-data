Feel Alive
Run through a sandy ocean shore
Relax in the sun
Walk patiently through the pouring rain
Don't rush
Take your time to study the world and feel love
Grasp each new moment and treasure all new sunsets
Study your dreams
Listen to a childs questions and answers
Slow your pace
Don't feel too much anger
Don't criticize race
Learn to let go
Always say thank you
Lend a helping hand
Don't be afraid to create a dream
Take a deep breath
AND
~FEEL ALIVE~
