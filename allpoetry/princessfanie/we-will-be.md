---
title: "We Will Be"
writer: "princessfanie"
slug: "we-will-be"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55967-We-Will-Be-by-princessfanie"

You take me back into this heart of yours
You fill my life to soaring heights
You love me for who I am 

This is where I want to be

So with each deep breath and sighs of relief we will be

We will walk into these memories of today and all tomorrow's together

Forever we will be
