The river flows sometimes faster than I dream

So I coast a bit waiting for the rain to fall

I hear sprinkles lightly touch the river water

I love each new rapid that takes me your way

Taking us back and forth
Studying this new distance we discovered 
I've begun to feel when I thought it was impossible

The rapids take us faster into this calm river where we feel what we need to drift together

The feeling of contentness in the silent waters we find you and I

Holding on through tough rapids that may pass
 
Hoping for our waterfall down river to bring all we need to hold on
