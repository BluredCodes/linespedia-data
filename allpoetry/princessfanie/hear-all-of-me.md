---
title: "~Hear ALL of Me~"
writer: "princessfanie"

It is your voice I hear every time I wake

I run into sounds of our voices colliding

I step back to resist you

I take deep breaths to gain the strength to move on

I never want to feel this pain again

I have no heart left

I am empty

I am fighting a loosing battle 

I want so much to be

But can't control my destiny

Please speak to me when I need your voice

Please don't stray too far from this defeat

Love will come our way again

Love will be outside our door once more

I will listen and wake for your voice to call me back again
