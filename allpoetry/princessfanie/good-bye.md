You called tonight to say "Good Bye" to a life of us

A life of love
A life filled with so many moments of you & I

Holding each other until the sun filled our room
Leaving one another for the day and not wanting to let go

Now you are saying "Good Bye"

"Good Bye" to us

A life of dreams
A life of butterflies in flight

A life without your touches
A life without me tracing your smooth lips
A life without your hands tracing my hips and loving me through the night

You are my everything
AND
My world is lost without your love

I will miss the laughs that come just thinking of you
I will miss the endless freedom I felt when loving all of you

You make me complete
You make the one and only me

You made us and now your setting me free....
