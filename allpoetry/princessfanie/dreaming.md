It seems I'm in  dream world even as I wake

I ache for your precence
I long for your smile
I wait for your sweet carresses to touch all of me inside & out

I close my eyes and drift away to a world where I wake with you

I will forever carry you through every dream and every sunrise... because you are mine to keep
