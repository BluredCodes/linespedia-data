With every new sunrise I hope this pain subsides 

Life sends us such beauty to delight our eyes

It brings happy memories to last through infinity....

We take so many things for granted
We hold all we can in our hands 

Until it's time for the beauty we have to grow and blossom
Into wonderous places we can't reach anymore
