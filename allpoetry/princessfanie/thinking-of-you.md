---
title: "~Thinking of You~"
writer: "princessfanie"

No matter where the sun may be nor the stars or the clouds in the sky
No matter how dark it may be 

I'm always thinking of you

I long for your touches

I'm mesmerized in your arms
I'm so free in your eyes
I fall deep inside these eyes that look at me with such intense yearning to savor all my taste

Sounds from your lips that make my heart stop
That make these butterflies swarm inside me forever

I'm always thinking of you and for always I hope these treasures consume us and guide us through every sunrise and moonlit night to come our way no matter the distance
