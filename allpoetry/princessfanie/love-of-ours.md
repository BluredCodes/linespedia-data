---
title: "~Love of Ours~"
writer: "princessfanie"
slug: "love-of-ours"
source: "AllPoetry"

Come to me every morning

Speak to me with only your eyes

Show me of our destiny as the sun seeps through our souls

Keep this heart warm
And
I will forever love this man I hold

For I honor all you have given me
For I am all you will ever need

I tremble at the sight that stares into me

For it is a love so pure....so delicate

Who knows my heart so preciously

I step into your life to guide you
And
You hold your hand out for me to guide this love of ours through infinity
