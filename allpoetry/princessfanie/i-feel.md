---
title: "I Feel"
writer: "princessfanie"

I step into this cold grass with my bare feet 
I feel the coolness
I run to you with open arms

I feel this freedom all around us

I step inside with you
We lay our bare bodies on these cold sheets
Both entwined in each others arms

I feel this love all around the two of us to be one

The feeling to have all we need in each other

These feelings are sublime
