---
title: "*All Alone for Now*"
writer: "princessfanie"
slug: "all-alone-for-now"
source: "AllPoetry"

I close my tear filled eyes and wake to an ocean
Trying not to sink from the pain that carries me far away
I drift to a slient place
Where I feel nothing

I'm lost
I'm alone
I'm far from any reality in my head

I just want to disappear in these open waters for awhile
I want the sun to soak up all these tears that surround the raft I continually float on

Will these stars guide me thru the night as I float...
Will anyone ever take this pain away or fill this empty heart that cries out to the heavens as I ask "WHY"...

I only hope in the near future my raft floats ashore and I step onto these sandy shores to feel life again

To feel this love somehow, somewhere again

I open my arms and I reach for life again
