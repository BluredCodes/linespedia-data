Cascading flowers of Daffodils & Daises
Where will I find your love again....

Under the leaves of Tulips and Pansies 
Our memories are quickly fading....

So run past the sadness
Breathe new life 

Pick this rose right in front of your eyes

For every thorn I will brush aside to give you new life 

So with this sunlight that sees so much power of our love not letting go
Let it's warmth protect us 
And
The rain fall upon us

To feed this love back into our stems....
