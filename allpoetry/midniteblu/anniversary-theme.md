I've watched seasons change and years go by.
Though time has passed, my love for you won't die.
More than friend or spouse but soulmate for life.
A deeper meaning than just husband and wife.
Twenty passed, two hundred more.
Knock, knock, who is it? Eternity is at our door.
I'll take your hand and walk you right through.
A nice stroll into forever, just me and you.Happy anniversay Tim and Monique
