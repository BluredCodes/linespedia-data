The sun rises endlessly...
but tomorrow is promised to no one.
Night will fall continuously...
but tomorrow is promised to no one.
You can fight nature but you'll never win.
You can plead with God but he won't hear you.
So live now and love as long as you can...
Because tomorrow is promised to no one.
