This girl,
Quick to anger- quick to love
Looks forward to a better yesterday
Because she's afraid of tomorrow.

This girl,
She's always thinking of someone
Whom she wishes she were good enough for,
She never is. 

This girl,
Defies the norm and ends up average
Tells the truth to everyone else,
But lies to herself.

This girl,
Thrives on chaos- wishes for peace
Cannot look people in the eyes,
She might see her own reflection.

And this girl,
She's surrounded, but all alone
And she's forgotten herself
In her own oblivion.
