title: "How."
writer: "Irb"

how is it
I'm so pretty if I have such an ugly soul
how is it 
no one loves me like they've loved no one before
and how is it
nobody has ever thought to sit and ask me why
just let me sit here in this pool of blood until it's good and dry

sit and stare sit and stare sit and bleed sit and stare

she's so independant when she's got somebody else

sit and bleed sit and bleed sit and stare sit and bleed
