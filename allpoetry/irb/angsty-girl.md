---
title: "Angsty girl."
writer: "Irb"
slug: "angsty-girl"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56134-Angsty-girl.-by-Irb"
---

I get so tired of calendars
and one-way streets
and kisses

Of being just some girl
who's never loved
an' no one misses

I get so tired of patience
and hope and love
and drinking...

Of never getting peace
and always fuck
ing thinking. 

I'm so damn tired of love and hate
and how I'm
so alone...

But most of all, I'm tired of life-
it's bled me to
the bone.
