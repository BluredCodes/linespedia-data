I can't takle this no more,
you are too young
you mom is never there it is like she doesn't care 
you live ina house with no one there 
it even echoes when you whisper
you scream "IS ANYONE THERE????" 
no answer .
you are running down the hall 
but it keeps getting longer 
where is every one ? where did they go ?
why can't i find any one? you think while you run 
i wonder why do you tolerate all this?
sitting home all alone and even whe she is home she lloks at you with discust.
why do you tolerate all this bull shit they put on you?
you are too youngyou know i will always be there for you.
