cyclic progression of an unnatural sort
(short tumble de dumble down the stairs...
you shoulda seen his back contort),
lackadaisical drunken stare
and where, O where 
were his angels, then?


tragic consumption of unrealized potential
(detrimental behavior, of course 
paramount and instrumental)
tongues are lolling and tones are coarse
forcing remorse
is a mortal sin

(dead, un-dead compete
circling complete)

transgressions transcending the brand new horizon
(contrived respiration, dead-blank mind
 blind: a between worlds liason)
"my turn to see, those eyes are mine"
fine is the line
[-when-]
angels are waitin'.

critical (human) condition
