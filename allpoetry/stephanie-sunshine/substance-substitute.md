cover me in shades 
of truth: 
impressive instant(aneous) 
charade, 
no substance 
beyond the collage 
of societal imposition 

serve the ego 
a disposition: 
substitute perversion; 
possibly, maybe 
take it back, 
jealousy on tap 
(for this quiet revolution) 

don quijote quit 
while the gettin' 
was good: 
s l i d e(slip)s h o w, 
sowing the seeds 
of utopia 
(sleep to dream)>>awake 
and 
the stakes are 
H 
I 
G 
H(eaven is a fantasy for the misinformed) 
      ::a comforting lie:: 
            [semi-formed, 
                fragmented 
                  figment 
                      of an 
                        imag- 
                            inat- 
                              ion] 

cataclysmic deviation 
constitutes reason, 
(rendered 
irrelevant): 
i'm slowly growing deaf 
to the sound 
of a shock-wave intent. 

surrender: 
personality projection 
will be the death of me... 
                        ..synchronized swimming is for the birds 
                        ..and this cat's on a hot tin roof 
                          (under the influence) 
                        dancing for you and your respectability
