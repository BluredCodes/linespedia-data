always
     (i mean)
A
 L
  W
   A
    Y
     S
     interrupting my patterns
with
   your
     un-time-ly
               dot(.)s
we clash and mesh and breathe
            in unison
            always
            forever1997, er... yeah.  another example of my "older" stuff.
