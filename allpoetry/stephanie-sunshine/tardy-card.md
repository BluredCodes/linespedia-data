a literary progress promise
has been broken, badly
two days have transpired;
but i'm wired
in positive, productive intent.
(forgive me for tardiness, lovah?)
man alive!
i've analyzed "us" into demise,
simplicity has been stricken
from my list 
of experience, residual.
romantically idealized
logical conclusion
persists
and i am a walking contradiction....
TRYING to express
my 
total devotion
(a notion abstract)i think i'll add onto this at some point.  any comments would be much appreciated.
