Challenge Description: I want you to take at least five titles from MY author page and incorporate those titles into a poem. It can be any five titles you choose. You may choose to use more than five but you must use at least five. Please put quotation marks around each title so I know where to locate them :o) Have fun!! (75 points)



--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

"enchanted" reverence reveals 
internal inadequacy 
(inaccurate) 
immaculate conceptual sight; 
seize the dream and see 
its frailty 
(release)... 
relief is brief in the face of disaster 

"eternal torment" 
perpetual present tense: 
trapped in a cage 
of self-imposed-restraint 
("way down deep") 
keep your secrets to yourself 

claptrap sentiment sedates 
emotion moguls: 
altered perception 
creates a trait (love of love) 
via validated assurance 
"urgency" to consummate 
relations (falsely founded) 

[these are the days/ways of our lives/loves] 

"Adona S'ais--Sweet Dreams"
