urg gurgle
grumble rumble...
trickled tumble
(cafe karuba)
caresses esophagus,
softening bowels:
cappuccino cleanses the colon!

molten 'nilla
negates constipation
(conflated congelation)

um...
¿donde.. está.. el.. baño?crap.
i'm lame.
