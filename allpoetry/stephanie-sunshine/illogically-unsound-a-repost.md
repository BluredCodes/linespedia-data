inherent mystique of supreme affectation
generates intellectualized manifestation
of reason...
            (treason)
...stripping mystic, magnetic attraction
contracting an affliction of conflicting interest:
testing...
            (test)
            (test)
                  caressing caustic thought.

crop the dreams and fill the seams
with emotional validation.
            
            (integration)

placing
logistical
integrity
          A
          B
          O
          V
          E
instinctive divinity.
            identify: nomenclature tendencies.
            (unnamed beauty remains: 
                                    stained by futile identification/classification theories)
                   
Believe in love as an entity. 
            
            [illogically (un)sound]well, i'm VERY open to suggestions.  feel free to be as harsh OR kind as you are inclined
