title: "passed the clouds"
writer: "FireChild"

missunderstood smiles never seeming to dissapear
from a mouth that knows so much

wishes held in the heart of malevolence
never reaching their goal

yet allways knowing
knowing
never stopping
only pausing to smile
can you touch it? 
do i have to ask?

words spoken out of frustration
moments passing painting a memory

wanting to reveal the truth
never following through

out of cowardice
selfishness
never wanting
only taking
you can touch it
touch it
