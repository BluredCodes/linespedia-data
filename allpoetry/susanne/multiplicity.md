title: "Multiplicity"
writer: "susanne"

Ludicrous lamentations



Loiter in Latavia's mind



Litany of elaborations



the enriched kind



neologism and perseveration find



Out of sync with time



Blood brethren



berate and bemoan their fate



Mercury spills



Beaded bubbles slip and slide



In separation a need to hide



Feel the boiling brew inside



Sniveling snarling sac of seepage



Errant erumpent



Hail the halls of Hell's passage



Where primal fear awaits



Animal instincts delegate



Screams sewn shut salivates



Soul is torn



Spirits mourn



Seek thee child to be reborn?



Insatiable need



for nearing normalacy



Holy hearts bid entrance



Vested vigilant soul takes care



Allows no knowlege of the carnage there



After six weeks lost in space



Dimensions reshape



No peace for the needy



No rest for thy weary shell on this earth plane



Until the truth regains and remains in one



Iron will of wills



Until mercury spills



Soon beaded bubbles will slip and slide



and slither into Latavia's mind



Metallic taste



Smell the waste



Hear the wails



Eyes burned blind



Touch your fate



Hell awaits



Blood brethren bemoan their fate
