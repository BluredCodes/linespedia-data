Warning: Material may be offensive and or triggering - deals with child abuse.

Fly paper stick in front of my nose
hands and ankles tied in an offeratory pose
Sour semen smell from the hanky in my mouth
Father time ticking in the sweat beneath my blouse
Garden snakes slither within my earthen tomb
Humpty Dumpty's falling while the dish runs away with the spoon
And if Mary had a little lamb whose fleece was white as snow
It died away that blackened day and up the chimney rose
The King was too strong
She couldn't get away
So little Miss Muffet was raped today
While little Jack Horner stood mute in the corner
Vomiting curds and whey
Where was the old woman who lived in the shoe
When crimes were committed against me and you?
She was with Mother Hubbard
Head stuck in the cupboard
Scrubbing the wood through and through
