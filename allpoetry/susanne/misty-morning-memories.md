Misty morning memories



meander in my mind



Gently lift and carry me



to another place in time



I sail upon the wings of desire



Across a velvet blue sky



Lightly touch down



to a billowing cloud



soft as a new babes behind



Soon I'm delivered



to the place I should be



Where my soul feels alive



and my spirits set free



A little piece of heaven



on earth for me



in Wild, Wonderful, West virginia





Stumbled upon by happenchance



Fortney Mills is nestled



in the back woods of a rural ghost town



Tis here my true love for nature was found



In an untamed wilderness where beauty abounds



I marvel at the sight and cherish the sounds





Hear the rush of the waterfall?



Ah...nature calls...



In unabashed delight I strip down



lie on a smooth flat rock (form fitted it seems)



the warmth of the sun passes through me



In peaceful contemplation I hear myself breathe



Then the most majestic sight I have ever seen



A swarm of silver tipped butterflys



in every shade and hue



flock around my body



and in awe I cannot move...



They flutter round about me



unlock the child within



and as they leave



in hot pursuit



I try to follow them



to no avail



Goodbye my friends!



I frolick to the water's edge



mesmerized by the cascade's flow



feel the flush on my skin



sweet smell of jessamine



and without hesitation jump in!



My senses diffuse



Somehow in utero again



As I rise to the surface



and begin to swim



I laugh, I giggle, I'm a child again!



Misty morning memories



meander in my mind



Gently lift and carry me



to another place in time



Soon I'm delivered



to the place I should be



Where my soul feels alive



and my spirits set free



A little piece of heaven



on earth for me



In Wild, Wonderful, West Virginia
