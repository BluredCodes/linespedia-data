title: "Somber"
writer: "Pandemonium"

I sit in stillness in my room
 Exchanging gazes with the wall
Surrounded in my tepid tomb
 Finding solace in nothing at all

I seek a poem or a song
 A comforting word or touch
Wondering where I went wrong
 How, so hurriedly, could I lose so much?

My questions are greeted by silence
 I'm lost beyond belief
I'd gladly give my worldly possessions
 To grant my heart some small relief.
