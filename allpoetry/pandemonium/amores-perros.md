Beauty
Caring
Kindness 
Sharing

All that we are
All that we're not
All of our soul
All that we've got

Passion
Protection
Purity
Possession

Two hands joined
Two bodies entwined
Two spirits mingle
Two hearts aligned

Love is gained
Then is lost
Lived and died
That's the cost

Love goes....

Nothing 
Something
Everything
