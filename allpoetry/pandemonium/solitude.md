Cursed to walk alone
Meandering slowly ahead
He steps forth on the cold, hard stone
Living and breathing, but emotionally dead.

More than once he has felt love
But everytime he lowers his guard
With a horrible force, pain rains from above
How much more can he take? How much more can he care?

Perhaps he is doomed to his personal abyss
Meant to be adamant, yielding to none...so shrewd
Forever plagued by lonliness
He strides the path of solitude...5 December 2001,
