Blind

No thoughts in my head,
The words have all left.
The secret whisper of voice,
I once held so dear.
The visual perception,
I once saw them as,
Has now left my eyes,
Leaving my blind.
Voices from beyond,
Now calling my name.
They want me to leave,
And join them,
There day.

17-04-2002
