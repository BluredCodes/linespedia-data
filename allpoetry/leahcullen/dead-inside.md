Dead Inside

I’m dead inside,
I’m all alone.
I’m dead inside,
I’ve turned to stone.
No pain,
No sorrow,
No more tomorrow.
I’m dead inside,
And I’m all alone.
This prayer that I pray,
Is the last I will say.

18-04-2002
