Try

Standing on the edge of a cliff,
Wanting to fall,
Scared to fall,
Hoping to fall.

You must reach deep onside,
Find such overwhelming peace,
You feel you can fly,
This peace is your ultimate,
It's your innerself.

Rely only on your innerself;
Stand before the universe;
Show all of creation;
Sore like a eagle,
Fly for all eternity.....
