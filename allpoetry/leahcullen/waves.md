“Waves”

Green eyes of the sea,
Once so pure and true.
The gentle waves of time,
I once saw in you.
Now like black caves,
Trapped in a cell,
I wish I could help you,
Out of this hell,
Your tidal waves,
Now drowning me.
I wish I could help you,
Just to see the gentle tide dance.
I wish I could help you,
Out of this trance.

-Leah Cullen-
