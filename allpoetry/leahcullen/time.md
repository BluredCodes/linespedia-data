“Time”

Sometimes you look out the window,
Wondering of times long past.
The glint of light,
That shone so bright,
Has now just turned to dark.
The moon is frail,
And so is life,
But why can’t I live,
The way I want?
Minutes pass,
And they always will,
But why can’t time just stay still

-Leah Cullen-
