“What’s The Point”

Sometimes you think,
Just what’s the point,
Of going on this way.
You live your life every day,
Yet things all stay the same.
Nothing changed,
No more light,
My world is full of black.
What is the chance of turning back,
Once you’ve crossed the line.
Sometimes you think,
Just what’s the point,
It’s just a different judgment day.
    
-Leah Cullen-
