“Dragon’s”

The dragon’s eye,
That stares at night.
The dragon’s call,
Your dangerous plight.
The  flames of time,
Are burning quick.
The dragon’s claw,
Will make you think.
You can hide and cry,
But at night it’s true.
The dragon’s call,
Is here for you.

-Leah Cullen-
