Live For Tomorrow

The world waits for no one.
The world isn't going to wait for you.
If you keep living in yesterday,
You will have no chance
In living for tomorrow.

Living for today is hard,
I know.
But if you live for yesterday
You'll see the world race past you,
And change without you,
And leave you behinde,
With all the forgotten souls of yesterday.

Live for tomorrow,
Always live for tomorrow.
Tomorrow is change,
Tomorrow is a new world,
A new begining.
Tomorrow can change the world,
While yesterday only kills it.Another Oldey!!! :-)
