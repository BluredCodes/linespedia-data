“Drop”

Birds of Pray,
That stalk’s the night,
Darkness glooms,
Inside my tomb.
Walls of pain,
Inside my head,
Secret words,
I think I’m dead.
Take my pain,
And make it stop,
This life of mine, 
I think I’ll drop.

-Leah Cullen-
