“Think Of Me”

When times are past
And some things new.
You think of me,
And I’ll think of you.
You’ll always be,
In this heart of mine.
It’s just untill the times are right.
Thing’s will change,
And times come and go.
And all I do,
Is think of you.
You are my heart,
You are my soul.
I just can’t wait,
For old times told.

- Leah Cullen -
