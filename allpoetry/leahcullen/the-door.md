“The Door”

I wrote a poem,
Just for you,
This life of mine,
I think it’s through.
The pain inside,
I just can’t take,
I need to be, 
In a different place.
I don’t know why,
I feel this way.
My time has come,
My day is through.
I wish I could,
Say some more.
But my time has come,
I see the door.

-Leah Cullen-
