“Can’t Go On”

I don’t know what it is,
That’s trapped so deep inside.
I don’t know why,
I feel this way,
I wish it all came clear to me.
I can’t go on this way much more,
I don’t know what to do.
I cant stop the tears that fall,
Every time I am alone.
No one knows what’s wrong with me,
They haven’t got a clue.
I change for them to keep them happy,
I can’t go on this way.
At night I lie awake and stare,
Yet nothing comes clear to me.
The sun brakes the dark once more,
And it starts all over again.

-Leah Cullen-
