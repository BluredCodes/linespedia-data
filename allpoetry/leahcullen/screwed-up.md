“Screwed Up”

I just don’t know
What’s going on.
I’m so screwed up inside.
I just don’t want 
To live this life.
Is it all just in my mind?
My life has no theme,
No aim,
No fun.
Why am I living this life?
I’m so screwed up inside.

-Leah Cullen-
