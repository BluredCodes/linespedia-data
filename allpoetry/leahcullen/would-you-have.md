“Would You Have”

The pain I have,
Trapped inside.
If I’d have come,
And said goodbye.
Would you have left?
Would you have died?
I just don’t know,
Why I didn’t go,
The night you left forever.
I’ve never spoken
A word of this.
I need to know,
Would you have left
This world for so long?
would you have left,
To never sing our song?

-Leah Cullen-
