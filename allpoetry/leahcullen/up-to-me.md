“Up To Me”

I’m going to crack,
I’m going to break,
I can’t go on this way.
I need to breathe,
I need to talk,
It’s the same every day.
I’m going to cry,
I’m going to die,
I just can’t see the way.
I need some help,
Why can’t you see,
Please don’t leave it up to me.

-Leah Cullen-
