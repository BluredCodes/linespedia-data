Angels or Paths

I sit and wait,
does angels contemplate my faith?
And do they know the place where we go,
When we reach the end of our lifes journey?

Are they the stars in the moonlight sky?
Watching our lifes journey twist and turn,
in so many misleading directions.

Are those so many distant
flickers in the sky actually,
in some misconceived way,
the path of life your meant to take?
The way your meant to look...... UP!!!

Or does the reflections of those angles
in the sky,
on the lakes, seas and rivers,
actually mean that yo are meant 
to look..... DOWN???

Angels are meant to be some sort of 
peacfull hallucinations;
But, if the are so peacefull,
why can't we choose?
Why do they contribute to the
pain and confusion of life?
That's one of the many mysteries of life

Which ever path you choose,
It will change you life for ever,
The path you choose is yours,
UP or Down???
