“Hollow Soul”

You close your eyes,
And think a thought,
But not know what’s going on.
You feel alone,
A Hollow Soul,
This time you’ve got to cry.
You always hide,
The pain you feel,
Your completely numb inside.
You feel alone,
A Hollow Soul,
This time you’ve got to try.

-Leah Cullen-
