Another Day
 
 Another day over,
 And I leave work behind.
 I go on home,
 And close the door.
 I close my eyes,
 And want to cry.
 I walk upstairs,
 I want to die.
 I light a joint,
 And take a breath,
 Now everything, I shall forget.
 I think some thoughts,
 All crazy inside.
 I don’t know why,
 I wish I was blind.
 
 17-04-2002
