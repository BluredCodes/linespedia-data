“Wall’s”

I felt a weight,
Has lifted my mind.
All I do now,
Is wait for it’s grind.
It’ll drop so hard,
And crush down my wall.
And all I do now, 
Is wait for it’s call.
I’ll feel it inside,
Untill the day
I am claimed.
Then I’ll hear nothing,
And re-build my wall.

-Leah Cullen-
