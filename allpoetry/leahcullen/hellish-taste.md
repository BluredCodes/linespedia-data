“Hellish Taste”

If you find me dead,
Don’t act surprised.
It was always there,
It was in my mind.
I just can’t face,
This life much more.
I need the key,
To open the door.
I can’t survive,
This is not my place.
I can’t survive,
This Hellish taste.

-Leah Cullen-
