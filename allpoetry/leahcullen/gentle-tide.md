“Gentle Tide”

You are that secret
Part of me,
I never let you see.
You are so deep,
And dear to me,
I just can’t let you see.
You are the one
Who helped me up
When times were hard,
And the sea was rough.
You are the one,
I love the most.
You are the one,
To whom I toast.
You are my light,
So deep inside,
You are my oceans,
Gentle Tide.
You are the one,
I love the most.
I had to let you know.

- Leah Cullen -
- 24th of March 2002 -



Dedicated to Jean (Mum) Cullen - For all the hard times I've put her through, I wanted to let her know I love her
