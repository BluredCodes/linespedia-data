“Joke”

I’m thinking clear,
But still can see,
Just what’s so wrong with me.
I just can’t seem,
To break this spell.
I’m trapped inside,
My living Hell.
My mind is blocked,
And can not be broke.
I just don’t know why,
It’s all a great joke.
The pain that I feel,
Have a great laugh,
Who’s to know,
When it all turns on you.

-Leah Cullen-
