Fear

Flames glowing,
like stars at night.
Devils vains run through yours,
Burning at your very soul.

Destruction....
Burning, feeding on every target
to hit his path.

Sparks flying,
like mysterious nights.
Planets exploading.
Total anialation.
The Devils destruction,
Feeding on the power of fear.

A life of Death.......
A night of chaos......
A dream of 'Reality'.
Like an existance of bounderys.
Trapped by fires walls.
Total elimanation.
