“Dark Of Night”

The dark of night
But moon shines down,
The fairies dance
On secret grounds.
The night,
So cold,
And full of fear.
Not knowing what’s in store.
The winds blow loud,
You hear a sound.
And wonder if you’ll live,
The night’s unsure
But this is true,
At the end of the day,
