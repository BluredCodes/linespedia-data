Dead

You walk around,
Your head held high.
I don’t know why,
I look to the ground.
You go out at night,
And do what you do.
I don’t know why,
I wish I was you.
This life that I live,
So sad to comprehend.
This life that I live,
I wish I was dead.

18-04-2002
