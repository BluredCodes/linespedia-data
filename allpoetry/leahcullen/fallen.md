“Fallen”

Flittering and gliding,
Right down to the ground.
Once was strong,
And held on tight.
Flowing and dancing,
The leaves prancing through,
The person I knew,
I thought it was you.
You’ve changed,
And you’ve fallen,
Let go of your branch.
You’ve hit the ground now,
No where your found.

-Leah Cullen-
