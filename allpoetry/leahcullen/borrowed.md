“Borrowed”

You think a lot,
Yet speak no words.
Time is borrowed,
It is not yours.
You can’t control
Who opens doors.
Each room is filled,
With different times,
Different laughter,
Inside your ears.
Nothings going to change,
It’s just another year,
Time is borrowed,
