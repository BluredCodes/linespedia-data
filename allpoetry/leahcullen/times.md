“Times”
Of times to come,
And shadows past.
Our lives tomorrow,
A distant glance.
Today’s the truth,
And tomorrows doubt,
Who knows what this life is all about.
When things go wrong,
And troubled times,
Sit down and think,
Open your mind.
Times are hard,
And that’s the truth.
But think of times,
You once knew.

- Leah Cullen -
- 24th of March 2002 -
