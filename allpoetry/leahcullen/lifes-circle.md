Lifes Circle


Egg shell time.
As graned sand falls.
Like lifes seconds
passing by.

Just like the start.
Can turn side down.
Crush your life,
to granes of sand.

Regrowth of life.
We once knew.
Not to know again.

Life will Die.
Death will Live.
Never,
Seen,
Again.
