Fairy Tale

The monumental fairy Dream
Can be changed like a storm
With the twitch of an eye
A curse of the lip
A flicker of a hair,
The Death of Cinderella
Killed by the Dream
Of her life long fantasy
The prince of Hell
The torture of 'Happiness'.
Beauty and the Beast
The Beast shall destroy
Claws like spears
Shredding the Beauty,
Shall be no more.
The fairy tale Dreams
Shall be destroyed
Forever and never
