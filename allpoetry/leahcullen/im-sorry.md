“I’m Sorry”

I said some things,
I didn’t mean,
I didn’t want to hurt you.
You are the one,
I care for most,
But all I did was desert you.
I care for you,
So much inside,
I’ve got to make you see.
I didn’t mean,
Those words I said,
I don’t know
What came over me.
If I could take it back,
I would.
And never would I have said,
Those things I said,
To break your heart.
Deep inside now,
I feel dead. 
Please take me back,
And forget those words,
I’ll never say again.
I didn’t mean a piece of it.
I’m sorry for my sin.
