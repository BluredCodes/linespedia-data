Smoke

Swimming through smoke rings,
Inside of my head.
Not knowing what’s going on
Outside this great time.
Not knowing who’s there,
Not wanting to care.
Smoke rings of time,
Are trapped in my head.
Hazy but light,
Spirit entrapped,
Not knowing the time,
The ash will last fall.

-Leah Cullen-
