“Charged”

Do you think,
The same way I do.
Are the colours the same?
Is this all true?
Can you see the blue tide?
The same way as I do?
why are you trapped?
Is this all true?
Dancing and twirling,
And shining like stars,
Is this all true?
Or can you be charged?

-Leah Cullen-
