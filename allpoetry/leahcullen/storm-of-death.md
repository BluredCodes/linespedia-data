Storm of Death

Death rides a gentle Storm
Enchanting and devine
Not knowing where it's bring you
Or if your coming back.
This Hell on Earth
I just can't take,
I'm killing my mind
And scaring my skin,
Crawling with desire just to feel
The Storm of Death.
Loose this feeling deep inside
Hiding truth of who I am.
I've lost it now
My life is sold.
The Storm of Death,
So sweet to taste,
I'm losing now
I've lost my place,
Lost forever,
No disgrace.
