“Some People”

Some people say,
“Life’s to short”
The way I’m experiencing it,
I think it’s too long.
Too much time to think,
And get depressed.
Some people say,
“Money doesn’t buy you happiness”
In my life it would.
No more struggle,
To make ends meat.
No more struggle,
To pay for heat.
No more struggle,
To pay the bills.
Some people say.
“From riches to rags in three generations”.
I guess were the third,
And labelled.

- Leah Cullen -
