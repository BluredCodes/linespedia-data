“Hide”

Sitting alone,
With my blinking eyes.
I look at you,
And I just don’t know.
I close my eyes,
With one quick glance.
I’ve walked away now,
It’s my solo dance.
I am alone,
And this is true.
There’s nothing else,
For me to do.
I’ve lost the will,
I can’t survive,
There’s too much pain,
I’m too tired to hide.

-Leah Cullen-
