Bated Breath

With bated breath,
I sit and wait,
Longing for your voice.
With bated breath,
I sit and stare,
Wondering when you’ll come through the door.
With bated breath,
I sit and cry,
Knowing I’ll see you no more.
With bated breath,
I sit so still,
Waiting for my chance to leave.
With bated breath,
I sit and glare,
Wondering why, I am still here.
