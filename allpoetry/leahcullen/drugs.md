Drugs

The drugs I take,
To ease my pain,
I don’t know why,
Its so insane.
The times are hard,
But why can’t I live,
Without these scars?
The drugs I take,
To ease my pain,
I don’t know why,
It’s so insane.

-Leah Cullen-
