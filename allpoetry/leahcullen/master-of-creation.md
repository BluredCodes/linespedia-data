“Mater Of Creation”

When he calls,
He calls for you.
The Master Of Creation.
Your time is short,
So make the best,
Of what you had,
On this messed up place.
Time is short,
And this is true.
He knows what in store,
For me and you.
So don’t be scared,
And tell no lies.
Maybe next time,
It’s your turn to die.

-Leah Cullen-
