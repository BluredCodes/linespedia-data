“Found”

Clouds moving fast,
While birds fly around.
The life that I had,
It just can’t be found.
A mirrored reflection,
In the ponds of time.
A secret distinction,
Of life, could be mine.
Winds blowing through,
The seconds of sand.
This life of mine,
Shall one day be found.

-Leah Cullen-
