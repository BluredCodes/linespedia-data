Windows

Looking at the window,
Who reflection do I see?
Who is that person?
Is that person me?
I’ve change so much,
How can this be?
Who is that person?
Are you sure it is me?
Look at the window,
Who’s reflection do I see?
Who is this person?
Knocking on the door.

17-04-2002
