Noise 

The noise in my head.
The whistle of wind.
The glare of the moon.
Rain falling hard.
I’m trapped inside,
And just can’t escape.
When will I leave
This hellish place.
I want to go back,
To where I once was.
The path that I took,
Lead to the wrong door.

17-04-2002
