Confused

I have no inspiration.
I have no muse.
This life that I live,
Is so confused.
I wish at night,
When I close my eyes,
This world would leave,
That I would die.
The pain I feel,
Deep down inside.
I don’t know why,
I want to be in the sky.

18-04-2002
