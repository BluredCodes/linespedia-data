“On My Mind”

Standing proud,
And laughing loud.
You’ve left this world,
You had to die.
You are so missed,
In these deepest times.
You are the one,
That’s on my mind.
You are the one,
I loved so true,
You are in every glint of snow.
You are the one that light’s the stars,
You are the one,
In distant fires.
Your always here,
But never seen,
When will you appear to me?

-Leah Cullen-
