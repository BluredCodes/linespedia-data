“A Book”

I have a book,
My thoughts inside.
This life of mine,
I’ve got to hide.
I don’t know why,
I just can’t show,
What’s in my head,
I just don’t know.
I have a book,
My thoughts inside.
The books now closed,
It’s time to die

-Leah Cullen-
