“Come and Go”

Some words inside,
That make no sense.
I guess it’s just another,
Messed up place.
The life I had,
Is now long gone.
I just don’t know,
What’s in my head.
Nights come and go,
When the sun breaks through.
The life I had,
I wish I knew.

-Leah Cullen-
