The Confrontation


Can you feel the Grey?
Stalking yo in the silent Breeze,
Death Strikes,
It's time to confront your existants,
It's time to face your fear.
The path of life
Faith marked out
Is staring you straight in the eye.
The eye of faith.
The eye is silvery Grey
Peering at you in a pool of faith.
Is this the life
Faith marked out?
Wandering in a mist of unknown,
Dancing inthe hidden forest of Death.
You know, with another role of the dice,
This life could be yours.
Who knows when your numbers up?
Who can tell when the Grey's coming
