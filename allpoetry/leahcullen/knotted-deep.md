“Knotted Deep”

I wish I knew,
What’s in my head.
The thoughts are knotted deep.
I can’t decipher what’s going on.
In this life I live.
What way to turn,
When seconds pass,
What life I choose to live.
This life on earth,
The same each day.
Or a different path to live,
Never to turn back again.
I’d leave the ones I love the most,
But watch them from afar.
I never want to hurt them,
But what’s best for me.
I wish I knew,
Just what to do,
But the thoughts are knotted deep.

-Leah Cullen-
