“Sitting High”

Sitting high,
On branches low,
Just contemplating which way to go,
A distant glance,
A light that flickers,
A different twisted leaf that blows.
Dancing, twirling,
Falling fast.
This life on earth,
Is going to pass.

-Leah Cullen-
