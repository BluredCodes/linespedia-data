“I Don’t Want To”

I don’t actually 
Want to Die,
It’s one of my biggest fears.
I don’t actually 
Want to Die,
I just don’t want to be here.
I don’t actually
Want to Die,
I’m all alone inside.
I don’t actually 
Want to Die,
But there’s nowhere left to hide.
I don’t actually
Want to Die,
It’s one of my biggest fears.
I don’t actually 
Want to Die,
But I’m running out of time.


- Leah Cullen -
- 24th of March 2002 -
