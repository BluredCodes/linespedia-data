“I Just Don’t Know”

Smoke rings floating,
Through seconds of time.
Flying high,
And gliding low.
What happens next,
I just don’t know.
They break and fall,
And turn to dust.
I suppose it’s just like life for us.
The seconds come,
And the seconds go,
What happens next,
I just don’t know.

-Leah Cullen-
