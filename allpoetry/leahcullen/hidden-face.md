Hidden Face

You think you know me.
My hidden face.
You know my mask
I've hidden my face.
What's that you say,
'I know your mind.'
You know the roll in play
In this life of mine.
You are blind,
You can't see me,
My mind you know,
Is just a script.
It's not my thought,
It's just my fort
You can't get past
My Dragon's flame,
It's there for me,
You can not see,
You don't know me;
I can't explaine.
