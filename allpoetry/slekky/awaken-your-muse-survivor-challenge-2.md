Arise
in the morning
and turn your dreams into
reality with golden pen
Breathe In

Amuse 
my senses with 
your poetic muses
and bring dead emotions to life
Breathe Out

Create
your poetry
and offer me comfort
as you spill magic on paper
Breathe In

Lie down
as the sun sets 
let fantasies arise
invite possibilities in
Breathe Out
