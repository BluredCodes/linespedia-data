I enter the safe haven
Of the dimly lit diner
A welcoming warmth
Strokes my chilled skin
As my body quakes
In disobedient shivers

My bones ache
With icy pangs
And my muscles are rigid
From my long and bitter journey
As I trickle puddles onto the floor
In frozen pools of water

The odor of greasy breakfast
Singes my nostrils
And the clangs of busy dishes
Sing a consoling melody
As I traipse to the corner
And claim my seat

With an hour to waste
Before help comes 
I order a breakfast
Of runny pancakes and charred sausage
And I absorb my surroundings
For the first time

In the corner sits an antique jukebox
Belting out sad country tunes
As the lights flash on and off
And keep timed beat
With the rhythm of the music
As if it’s revealing the story of it’s own life

Two truck drivers 
Are engaged in cordial debate
As they smoke their cigarettes
And drink their coffee
I feel reassured 
By their relaxed camaraderie 

Then she caught my eye
A mysterious beauty
With a tear stained face
Her pouty lips curved down
As they quivered with the effort
Of holding back her wails

Her cryptic eyes
Flashed with glistening tears
Like opulent emerald jewels
Caught in the moonlight
And they betrayed her strong reserve
As she cried silently in the shadows

Oblivious to my vigilant stares
She began to say a prayer
Parched lips barely moving
With hands gingerly clasped atop the table
And a torrent of tears
Accompanied her insistent whispers

She captivated me
Kept me spellbound in her sorrow
As I became wedged between
Her world and mine
And her suffering swathed me
In a blanket of anguish

Her arms moved and I followed their lead
Shocked to see her encompassing a young child
As he rested his head upon her breast
And smiled in the comfort of the muffled echo
Of mother’s heartbeat

His eyes were a lifeless blue
Edged by dark shades of gray
And I noticed he had his mother’s lips
As I imagined what his youthful smile
Would look like
Pasted upon her conspicuous face

Her trembling fingers 
Ran through his thick hair
As she placed an affectionate kiss
Upon his forehead and whispered in his ear
A chuckle escaped his lips
In feeble and broken gasps

She embraced him snugly
As the tears came with more vigor
And she was unable to smother her sobs
Another prayer was shaped from her lips
And I tried to decipher her words
To concede only one …….”Amen”

I felt the urgency in her prayer
As I recited one myself
For this stranger, this woman
Who innocently held me confined
In her prison of heartache
As she pleaded for something I knew not

Then her tragic story 
Unfolded before me
In twisted waves of misery
Shoving me to my knees
As I watched in horror
The calamity unfurl


The young child
Her precious son
Gazed tenderly at his mother
And in a diluted voice, he spoke

“The Angels are here Mama”

And she gathered him in her arms
Her tears tumbled on his limp body
As he fell silently to his everlasting slumberMy fear of losing my children surfacing once again.
