The frosted moonlight 
Beckons this Lady of the night 
The voice of the breeze
Calls to her in faint whispers

“Set your spirit free”

As the velvet zephyrs
Besiege her supple body
And blows icy kisses within her 
The night strokes her sleek skin 
With its sinuous, liquid fingers

She becomes consumed with yearning
And obsession runs through her
In waves of cerulean electricity
And she succumbs to the summons of the hour

“Set your spirit free”

Unwilling to defy the passion within
She steps into the shadows
And allows nature to enfold her
In its vast embrace

She is drawn to a place
Etched within her memory
A place only visited in daydreams
Or within her feverish fantasies

She stares in awe
As she recognizes her surroundings
A place she deemed
Only dwelled within her own mind

Whispers upon the wind
Implore her in melodious tones

“Set your spirit free”

She strips the clothing 
That binds her body
And invites the Moon God 
To dress her in the suede fabric of star dust

She begins her release
As she bares her flesh 
To her savior of the night
And she treads closer to him
Climbing the branches of the tallest tree

Again she is greeted
This time in the songs of the lost souls

“Set your spirit free”


Lady midnight cries in sorrow
As her frozen teardrops 
Form diamonds upon the forest floor
And her body shakes 
With the sobs of ten thousand nights
Kept locked within her until this moment in time

She begs for restitution
Asks forgiveness for sins of the heart
She pleads to the moon for absolution
And she welcomes the beams to drench her
In fervent, emotional hunger

Her soul flies in liberation
As she releases the torture
That once scorched her scarred and bleeding heart
And kept it locked in chains of despair

She begins to shed self-inflicted blame
And open her eyes to the splendor
That saturates her essence

She hears the voices
Rejoicing in her emancipation
As she sends her unwavering love
Upon the wings of the wind
To the youngest angel
Her child who was given wings too early

She remembers his smile
Shooting sunshine to her soul
She remembers his laughter
Singing sweet songs to her heart
And she remembers his death
When desolation became her worst enemy
And her closest friend

She finally bids her son goodbye
As euphoria infiltrates her soul
At the delivery of her own guilt

One last tear runs down her cheek
As it engraves itself upon her face
To forever remain as a constant reminder
Of her tiny angel
And the day 
Of her joyous enlightenment
