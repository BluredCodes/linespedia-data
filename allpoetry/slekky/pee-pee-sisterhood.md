Hop from one foot to the other 
do the pee pee dance
wiggle your butt
so you don't go in your pants

Squeeze your legs close together
and sing the pee pee song
I'm a PPF and I'm glad
I don't have a ding a long

Hop up and down
till you can hold it no more
then pee on a meanie's head
be sure not to twinkle on the floor

you must always respect
your sisters in pee pee hood
as we all band together 
and uphold all that's good

Squeeze your legs together 
and sing the pee pee song
I'm a PPF and I'm glad 
I don't have a ding a long

We must let all the people know 
Never to insult our booty
or instead of a peed on head
they may just get the doodie

We are sisters of pee pee
one sticks by the other
and you never know 
our Queen might except a Pee Pee brother

So with these lessons learned
perfect your pee pee dance
So that your Pee defense
will surely be enhanced!:o)
