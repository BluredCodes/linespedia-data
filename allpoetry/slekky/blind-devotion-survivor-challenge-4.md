{Do you want to know a secret}?
{I’ve got a feeling} 
that {you really got a hold on me}
but {you won’t see me}

I want to see you 
But {you’ve got to hide your love away}
{Help} me see {within you without you}
All of you
When {all I’ve got to do}
Is see that {I’m looking through you}
That {I’ve just seen a face}

I offer {all my loving} {from me to you}
{because} {all you need is love}
{any time at all} just {come and get it}
and {I will} {rain} {real love}
upon your broken soul
and kill the {misery}
that dwells within your {sea of monsters}
deep within your soul’s {sea of holes}

{Step inside love} 
and emerge into {strawberry fields forever}
{Here comes the sun} to give you {a taste of honey}
{and your bird can sing} another happy song

Don’t dwell on {yesterday} or {the night before}
Or the {things we said today}
because {tomorrow never knows}
which {magical mystery tour} we will take
{across the universe} on the {long and winding road}
within the {sea of time}

{Can you take me back} to {a beginning}
{a day in the life} of {another girl}
the girl I used to be
who was {free as a bird}
when {golden slumbers} would silence {a hard day’s night}
of {crying, waiting, hoping}
while you were {fixing a hole} in my heart
and I was {flying} {for no one} but you

But {all things must pass}
And I can’t {get back} that {girl}
The old me, the younger me
{Her Majesty} has taken leave {in my life}
and bid the {Sun King} {good night}

{Good day sunshine}
{Mr. Moonlight} has offered {a shot of rhythm and blues}
to dim {the inner light} on {another hard day’s night}
as I {cry for a shadow}
with {lonesome tears in my eyes}


{Baby it’s you} who can {carry that weight}
and break the {chains} that bind my heart
Let us {come together} {Oh! Darling}
Please {don’t pass me by}

{Bad Boy} {Blackbird}
go the {Blue Jay way} {like dreamers do}
on the {Moonlight Bay}
where {only a Northern song} with {words of love}
will you {shout} from your lungs

{There’s a place} called {Penny Lane}
where the {two of us} can {slow down}
and do nothing
but still be doing {something}

{Don’t bother me} with {every little thing}
that is amiss when {I’m down}
but {hold me tight} {Honey don’t} let me go
say {Cry Baby Cry} as I lean into your embrace
{that means a lot} more than you’ll ever know

{I want you}
{I need you}
but you’re so far away
you have {run for your life}
leaving me looking like {the fool on the hill}
while I’m {searchin’} for my {Soldier of Love}
in a world so full of evil

But I’ll continue  {the chase} to {the end}
{I’ll be on my way} until I’ve released your soul
{eight days a week}  I’ll search
{I’ll follow the sun} until {you’ll be mine}
Even if the journey is {long, long, long}
{I’ll get you} and make you feel {glad all over}

{It’s all too much}
{It’s only love}
maybe {I’ll cry instead}
and {wait} for you to rescue me
from the {little child} within me
Until that day I’ll {let it be}
And {I’m gonna sit right down and cry over you}
And tell you {I feel fine}

{If I needed someone} I will only choose you
to catch me {if I fell}
{I want to tell you} all of my fears
I want to share all of my dreams with you
{I just don’t understand} what went wrong

{I call your name} but you turn your back on me
{I want to hold your hand} but you push me away
{tell me why} you have stolen my heart
and hidden it from me
where it slowly begins to die

{why}do you deny that {we can work it out}
when {what goes on} in my heart
is only true compassion toward you
and honest devotion to your happiness

{So how come} you have abolished our love
how come  you {ask me why} I love you
I just do, because you’re you
{Don’t ever change} for me or anyone
Because I will love you just the same

You say you’re not loveable
And that you’re {nobody’s child}
But I see the stars in your eyes
As I lose myself within the depths of your soul
{Ooh! My soul} weeps with yours

I just want to make you happy
But {I’ll be on my way} {it won’t be long}
{I’ll be back} again to offer myself once more
but {not a second time}
for I too, have limits

Just say {the word} and I’ll be right there
You are everything to me
I’ll do anything for you
