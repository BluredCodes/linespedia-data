Night falls again
And I find myself immersed
And buried within the images of my mind
As I endeavor to focus upon a cohesive semblance
Of the radiant light that serves the Dream Goddess

Downy clouds of emerald haze
Sway around her 
And frame her outline in satin linen
Illuminating her striking form
In a majestic silhouette

The opulent mist languidly dissolves
To unveil the pewter chains
That keep her so brutally confined
And tears of woe fall from her sorrowful eyes
As they tumble to the ground  
In crimson puddles

I look into her timorous eyes
As she reaches out her arms to me
Beseeching me to enter the safety of her consoling embrace
But an imposing inferno blazes fiercely between us
And I cannot find assuage in her defense

I gaze upon her majesty
As trepidation strikes my soul
In enormous waves of piercing torture
And the Mistress of Nightmares appears before me
Dressed in a tattered robe of blunted gray velvet

Her daunting eyes laugh tauntingly at me
As she begets her mighty sword
Gripped in her bony, ivory fingers
And the sky billows in thunderous screams
As charcoal clouds roll, forming macabre apparitions

My body quakes in unrelenting terror
As my enervated legs thrust forward
And I trudge through the lacerating gauntlet
Of raven colored rose bushes
Adorned in spiked thorns with scarlet blood upon them

The anguished cries of tortured souls
Ring powerfully through my ears
And I hear the heavy footsteps
Of the baneful mistress following close behind
As flaming tears of dismay escape my eyes

Invisible fingers grope me
As the blackened sky spits acid rain
Dawn-tinted blades of ice
Slice my skin and scorch my flesh
As the eerie sounds of this phantasm fracture my vitality


I am consumed by my own imagination
And I know not how to escape this purgatory
Yet this  sovereignty was contrived by my own distorted mind
And insanity threatens to drown my rationality
As I strive to break free from the frigid grasp of this hellish world

But the gates to reality have been locked
And I comprehend that with each new night
I shall become a spawn of wicked illusions
And a prisoner within my own trances
Until the day I set the Dream Goddess free

Sounds from afar call me into existence
And just before I awake
I look into the eyes of my Dream Goddess
As she sheds a solitary tear of vexation
And vanishes into the nefarious grasp of the
