Speak to me with a silver tongue
Not with scarlet words
In this world of hopes and dreams
Broken crowns can be mended and
Promises can be sealed
But bleeding hearts will
Permeate the wounded soul
My eyes can see your torment, your
Essence is trapped within your silence
With virtuous design, I will present you with
Truth and breath fire into your frozen soul
And with your own power
Destroy the shackles that bind you
The hunger is within you
Inner strength will prevail
Demons that invade nocturnal dreams
That scream torture into your sanity
Threaten to demolish your courage
My devotion will break your chains, the
Demise of the beast within youIf you read DOWN, you will see.......Speak Not In Broken Promises But Permeate My Essence With Truth And Destroy The Inner Demons That Threaten My Demise     :o)
