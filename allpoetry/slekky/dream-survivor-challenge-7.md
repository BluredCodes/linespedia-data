The sun sets as night befalls 
Yielding to the power of fantasy
The stars behind my closed eyelids
Become translucent figures
Melting together in fluid waltzes
As my subconscious gives birth to dreams

Droplets of crystals weave throughout
In an enchanted dance of the stars
While sapphire lightening bolts 
Shoot daggers of brilliance behind my eyes
Giving way to lavender imagination
Within the deepest contours of my mind

I am light as a feather
As I float within this alternate reality
Drifting into the gentle grip of my vision
As it appears before me 
Cloaked in the silver garments of my reverie
Motioning for me to enter

I submerge myself into this paradise
This UNreality I have so lovingly designed
As I bow before the Dream Goddess
And she unravels striking hallucinations 
Draped in golden fancies
Gifted unto me, the creator of this world

She spins glorious tales of my aspiration
Into her silken web of dreams
So that I may find solace in slumber
And heed the hidden messages 
Invoked by my own intuition
And summoned to call upon me

I glide with faultless ease
Within this city of untainted sensations
Tasting the rainbows’ arches
As it drips saccharine juices upon my parched soul
And moistens the lips of my discontented spirit
So that I may emerge fresh into reality

Distant sounds ring out from the world beyond
As I struggle to remain in this realm
Pleading to the Dream Goddess to bequeath one more gift
Upon my weary and beleaguered mind
As I watch with immense regret
Her stature vanishes into the opaque fog

I watch my world swiftly contract
As the colors give way to dark shades of nothing
And I begin my descent to the truth of actuality
As the sun rises and day has arrived
My eyelids flutter open
My awakening becomes
