In this box
This place of unity and closeness
so cramped with all the hues of the sunset
I feel nothing but lonliness
void of love from another

Others are called
to create life on canvas
to create art coming from passioned hearts

I sit in despair waiting my turn
hoping for my day of glory
hoping I'll find peace in unity
and then you were placed beside me

You didn't look to me
as a white crayon void of color
you looked to me as if I was filled with light
and your blackness 
filled the gap within me

Now I have purpose
to bring the brightness to your darkness
as I soak you up and you fill me with midnight loveWhite Crayon/Black Crayon
