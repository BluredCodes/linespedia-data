Crawl into the depths of your secret fantasies
Let your soul descend to the kingdom
of boundless possibilities within your padlocked mind

You possess the key
Open the door of victory and allow yourself
To be immersed into your illusions

Climb the tree of wisdom
Look upon your world with open eyes
Let the mystical scenes
Of honeydew sweetness and indigo lust
Bathe you in foams of silken desire

Two worlds within one
Your duality is donned before you
An orb in platinum sunshine
The other marinated in azure moonlight

As you turn toward the light
Your world of hopes and dreams
Bask in the radiance of its fervent glow
And dance in the realm within the shadows
Where mystery and chance abide

Watch as the two spheres collide
Painting portraits of golden mirages
Let your visions enlighten you
As they cloak you in satin robes of illustrious cognition

Look not unto the provinces as sequestered domains
But mesh them together
And weave a wondrous web Of fantasy and reality

Hear the melody of the cherubs 
As they sing with lustrous voices
And watch your splintered worlds 
Dance in provocative pulses
As they meet in a tangled embrace

Observe as your dreams come to life in swirls of pastel mists
Watch them mingle with the enigma of imagination

Take hold the hands of optimism
And grasp your dreams
As you pull them with sanguine arms
Into your world of reality
Fortify your mind with the endowment
That resides in both worlds
Bring them together and indulge in the strength
That you have contrived
In the sovereignty of your edified proclivity

Celebrate your enhanced knowledge
And magnify your majestic powerI hope you like it  :o)
