The purity of innocence
Gripped within the icy clutch of society
Held tight in the sweaty palms
Of corrupt minds
And greedy hearts
Who wish to slaughter the virtue
That thrives within the unsullied child

The child screams for the food of knowledge
But he is fed with lies and half truths instead
Force fed until he is sick with it
And he regurgitates those lies from his own lips
As he becomes mal nourished of truth
Dehydrated of integrity

Born without a chance
The program has long been writ
The babe is ripped from mother’s protection
And thrown into the vortex of the cold world
That is filled with the frozen hearts of lost children
Who have already breathed the program in

The child’s veracity is viciously torn from him
And replaced with avarice
As the internal bleeding
Goes unnoticed
And untreated
Until he is drained of all he was born with
Until he is deprived of himself

He begins to crave the program
Like a drug
Hungering for the high

He begins to live the program
Like a disease
Until the deceit becomes his truth

Mother strives to break the code
To keep him nourished with honest dignity
She tries to re-write the program
That has been forced upon her son
But the task seems unfeasible
As so many others promote the program
That she slaved so hard to break free from

The blood that she shed
Is still dried upon her hands
And now she vows
She will shed the sordid blood of her own son
And pervade him with the crimson richness
That is the blood of hope




Mother will fight for her child
As she disables the program
Piece by piece

She garnishes the abominations with
Devotion
Tenderness
And self worth
And the child savors the flavor
Tasting life for the first time
Until he begins to yearn for it


He slowly arises from within himself
As he pries the bitter grip of society
From his throat
And he escapes their clammy clutches

He seizes
