I just got promoted to PPFN
that means
Pee Pee Fairy Newbie!
Woo Hoo

So now, I need another promotion

What should I do? I ask myself

Well, I went to the store
and I bought ALOT of water **snickers**
and I drank, and drank, and drank
until I became VERY full.

Then I started doing my pee pee dance
Every fairy has their own special pee pee dance

Mine goes like this....feel free to join along  :o)

First you squeeze your legs together
and squeeze your butt muscles too
then you start to jump from one foot to the other
**make sure that pee gets mixed up very well**
as the mixture gets more urgent to be "poured" out
you must begin jumping up and down
instead of shifting from side to side
**Are we having fun yet**
Then you must squeeze your legs together
even harder than before
and make sure to stick your booty out
as you walk to your next target

Today, I decided to do "target" practice
on my son's father, we'll just call him "J" 
with a CAPITAL J for JERK!!!
Anyway, I walked up to the bed
where he was sleeping like a baby
**Awww, how cute**
**but not THAT cute**
Anyway, back to my mission
I quiety assume the position, 
right above his head
and I proceed to pour my well mixed pee  
over his head and I fly away
as he chases me, 
but he can't fly so **na na na na NA na, you can't catch me**

Wow, that was fun, 
and now that I have my wings
HE CAN'T CATCH ME

So that's the story 
of how I have been training 
to better myself as a PPF
and I'm hoping to be promoted 
very soon to
PPFSOD -- Pee Pee Fairy Secretary of Defense

Thank you for considering my application for a promotion,
I will be awaiting your early reply.

Signed

Slakky PPFN  :o)See, I've been training, very hard!
