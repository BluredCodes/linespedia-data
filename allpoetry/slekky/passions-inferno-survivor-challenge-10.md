Lucid dreams entrance
My wandering soul
With silvery lust
And thoughts of your touch

Feverish passion
Slithers between us
In a sinful dance
Of flesh against flesh

Shower me with your
Velveteen kisses
Saturate me in
Shameless Obsession

Shock waves of pleasure
Erupt from me in
Electric tremors
Of pure ecstasy

Scorching fire ablaze
Writhe within the flames
Dance with me in bliss
As our bodies fuse

Adorn me in your
Steamy embraces
Excite me with your
Tantalizing touch

Our bodies entwined
In rapturous heat
Melting soul with soul
