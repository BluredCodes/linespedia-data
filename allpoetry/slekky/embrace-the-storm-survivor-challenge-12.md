I stand naked in the storm
Stripped of dignity
Innocence was lost 
Somewhere between
Molestation
And 
Rape

Let the thunder howl
Drown my thoughts
Stifle my cries
Make me 
Numb

Let the rain fall down
Pelt my soiled skin
Cleanse the scars
That haunt 
My 
Soul

I raise my arms to the sky
Look in the eyes 
Of devastation
Tempting 
Fate

Let lightning strike me down
Slay this depleted life
Let me find
Serenity 
In 
SilencePersonal, but force itself out of me......just want to mention it was NEVER family that did this to me.
