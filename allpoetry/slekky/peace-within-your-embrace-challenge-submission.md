Cover Me with your moonlit sheild 
Protect Me in your shadows

Blow a kiss throughout my body 
as you whisper upon your breeze 

Welcome Me with your liquid embraces 
Sing to Me within your silken winds 

Surround me in darkness 
as my soul ascends into your depths 
and offer me your stars as guidance 
while I gaze upon them 
and imagine each sparkle is a joyous soul 
as they relay my wishes to the Heavens 

Carry Peace upon your spine 
and deliver this humble gift unto me 
packaged in the sounds of the night 

Let your languid zephyrs 
allow sanction to the melody 
within your dwelling 
and present to me harmony 
with each gust of wind 
as you create your quiet music 
within the serene stillness 
of your silent essence 

I sigh into your faint light 
finding synchronization 
within my own voice 
and you gently carry my sighs 
within your suede whispers 

Surround me in moonlit protection 
Carry your song upon your downy breeze 
and lend me your TranquilityThe challenge was to write a dedication to the night.
