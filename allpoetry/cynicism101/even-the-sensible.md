---
title: "Even the Sensible"
writer: "Cynicism101"
slug: "even-the-sensible"
source: "AllPoetry"

You're diffusing into my soul, and whether
you're filling me out
or filling me up
or filling me in
I like the weightlessness

It's spring again,
and even the sensible have
some growing to do.
