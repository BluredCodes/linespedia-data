So I suppose that you agree with me that there is probably no justification for anyone's actions. We've all sacrificed someone else's peace of mind; we all realize that's terrible. And for what?

For a measure of happiness, you say. For a measure of control.

Well, I, for one, have known the kind of happiness that encompasses the whole body, not just the smile. And this wasn't it.When spring hormones go bad...
