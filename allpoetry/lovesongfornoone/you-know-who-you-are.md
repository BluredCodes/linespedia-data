---
title: "You Know Who You Are"
writer: "LoveSongForNoOne"
slug: "you-know-who-you-are"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61550-You-Know-Who-You-Are-by-LoveSongForNoOne"
---

No one else makes me feel this way
Just thinking of you brightens my day
When you're around my knees get weak, 
I freeze in place, and just can't speak
I wonder if we'd ever have a chance 
But I know that we wouldn't, not for romance
Soon you will be going away
I wish SO much that you could stay
Maybe someday you will see
Just how special you are to me
You bring me up when I am down
You make me smile, never frown
You take the time to listen when needed
And you never really act conceited
I hope that we will stay in touch
Because you mean so very much
I'd like to thank you for all you've done
And let you know you're the only one
Cuz its only you who makes me feel this way
Thinking of you brightens my day
