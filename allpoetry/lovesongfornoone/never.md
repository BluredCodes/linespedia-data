Never will you understand what it is I feel for you
Never will you be a man and admit all you have put me through
Never will you try to fix whats made my life so bad
Never will you stop and think how much fun we could have had
Never will you wake up from a wonderful dream of me
Never will you ponder what we could but just won't be
Never will you see the good you know that I possess
Never will you talk to me, you're just like all the rest
Never will you look at me the way you look at her
Never will you decide that it is I whom you prefer
Never will you see these tears that really show my heart
Never will you know it kills me that we are apart
Never will you know how much truth there is to this
Never will you see because you don't know I existblah
