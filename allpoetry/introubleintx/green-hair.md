Oh my Josh,
what did you do?
Your hair is green
through and through.
It sparkles in
the shining light, 
wonder if it glows
under blacklight?
It doesn't match
the clothes you wear
what made you want
to have green hair?
You know you can't 
wear it to school
where others might
think you're cool.
Well, cool you'll be
on your backside
if I find out 
it's permanently dyed.
And you'll end up
with no hair
after I get the 
razor and some nair! 
And then what will
your friends think
when all they see
is bald head pink????LOL...I did not harm him I swear...It was hairspray... LMAO
