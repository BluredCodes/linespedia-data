---
title: "~~Never Part~~"
writer: "introubleintx"
slug: "never-part"
source: "AllPoetry"

When you are near
please hold me close
when you are gone
think of me the most.

Remember my love,
remember me dear,
So when we're apart
it will seem like I'm near.

Let us make the most,
when we have the time
so while we're apart
It will be on our mind.

I love you forever,
you're a piece of my heart
I know with our love
we shall never part..The ramblings of me to my husband..Over 10 years ago..My how things change... :)
