What lies behind this screen I see
envisioned within closed eyes
someone out there watching me
hidden, somewhere in the midst of lies

Personality felt through words
while hiding face within a mask
putting me to this final test
closing eyes, with questions ask.

Setting out to create a challenge
within the keys it must come true
the image in the darkness breaks
and falls further from my view.

The blackness closing in on me
like depressions curtain call
I reach out and find you close
in your arms, my resting fall

As your mask shatters on the 
ground, your face I finally see
realizing that as I was watching
