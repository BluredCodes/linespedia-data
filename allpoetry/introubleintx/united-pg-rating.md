Do you know, 
in the times we have spent
you make me weak with desire?
Your body heat
intensifies the flames,
fueling them to burn higher.

A casual glance
sends me over the edge
wanting and longing for more.
More than what
is now the given pace
embers burning me to the core.

Feelings inside me
that can no longer be denied
the fever drowns all coherent thinking,
Until such time
that we meet, the fire fueled
from each others touch, like drinking.

Finally face to face,
skin to skin, lips tasting lips
tasting tongues, exploring fingers, excited,
By the thoughts
and the actions, taken from 
fantasies leading to the bodies finally united.......
