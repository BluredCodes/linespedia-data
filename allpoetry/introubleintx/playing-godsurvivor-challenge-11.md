We're playing God,
can't you see
messing with
mortality.

Cloning animals
what is next?
Babies born,
a certain sex.

A chip implanted
under our skin
to make life easier
where will it end?

We're more than
numbers on a screen,
our privacy no longer
what it seems.

Where are we going?
Where have we been?
They'll know it all,
enemies and friends.

All because we let
them control,
handing them the
keys to our soul.

Not stopping them
before it starts,
telling them no
and showing some heart!

Faith in ourselves,
and our way of life,
not in a doctor
weilding a knife.

Let's take from them
this power abused,
and go back in time
before we are used....Hmmm...
