The line now drawn 
between good and evil,
which will you choose
when the task is yours?
Will you choose the light,
and power forever, or
will the darkness be what
you seek? Will you 
travel the line, between
the two, until you fall,
landing where fate 
has decreed you belong?
Will you drink from the 
chalise, where knowledge 
abounds, then make your 
choice? Or will it be done 
blindly? As all things have
been done in your life? 
Will you look at what has
happened? Or will you let
the future be the same
as a toss of the coin? Just
leave it all to chance, and 
hope that when fate steps
in, the hand you are dealt
is what you would have
chosen, had you made a 
choice, instead of once 
again, tempting fate.....Since I was officially voted off, this doesn't count, but I wanted to write my view of it anyway! :)
