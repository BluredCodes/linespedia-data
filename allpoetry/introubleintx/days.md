title: "~*~Days~*~"
writer: "introubleintx"

As the night
draws to a close
the dawns light
a dusty rose.
I cannot wait
to look upon
all the colors
that go on and on.
The daylight brings
a brand new start
one more chance
to ease my heart.
And yet I long
for night to fall
the chance of hope
that did not call.
The things I missed
what could have been
as the night closes
a new day begins.
