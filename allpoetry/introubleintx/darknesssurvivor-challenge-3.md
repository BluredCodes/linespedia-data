As the sun sets
and darkness falls
I turn to you,
for you are the only
one to alleviate my
fears, although you
are to be feared yourself.
Your eyes look to my soul
and in you I find the 
safety I have searched
so long for. Hiding from
the storm that is brewing
inside me, as well as in
the world around.
Your cold touch 
warms me, as no other 
has done, your love a beacon
in the darkness you bring.
I wish for that darkness
to engulf me. There is 
safety in the night, as 
long as you are there.
Saving me from the 
terror, yet bringing
a certain fear, in 
belonging to you.  I will
welcome the fear you 
bring, as I welcome the
comfort I get from your
embrace. For you are the
future I have been 
running to, while escaping
from the past that 
threatens to erase all
that is left of me, without 
you.Don't know...But that is what I got... :)
