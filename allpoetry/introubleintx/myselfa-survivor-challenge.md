In my life there have been
times that I have felt alone
like no one on this earth shares
these feelings I have known.
I take a look around and 
stop, before it is too late
and I've gone  too far to
turn back.  Knowing that
to keep on this route I will
see nothing more than
where I have ended up.
I long to know where I
have made a wrong turn, 
left or right, along the way.
that which is precious to me,
part of me knowing it was
needed by you, more than 
by me. And yet, you ask 
me to leave it with you, 
to live no more, and 
save myself from none other than
myself.Here it is... "In times like these I stop and turn to see where I left that part needed by me to save myself."
