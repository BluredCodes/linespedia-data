Words...
that paint
a picture
to share
with others
who share
with you...
Feelings...
of hopes
and dreams
future plans
broken hearts
loves found
friends lost...
Knowing...
somewhere someone
out there
is sharing
their words
as you
share yours...
Believing...
kindred spirits
seek others
that choose
to live
and die
on paper...
Poetically...
living,
loving,
sharing,
believing,
knowing,
and dying...
Becoming....Well, my work...Don't know...But there it is... :)
