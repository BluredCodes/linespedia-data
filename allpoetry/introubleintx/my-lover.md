---
title: "~~~My Lover~~~"
writer: "introubleintx"
slug: "my-lover"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61985----My-Lover----by-introubleintx"

He has hands that are warm
used to gently please,
A smile that makes
me weak in the knees.

Eyes that are tender
and look to my soul,
while filling me with
desires I can't control.

His voice speaks with passion
in soft subtle tones,
and I know what it takes
to make him want to moan.

His body a temple
known only to few,
Tall, tan, and muscled
an attractive view.

But then there's the inside
those parts I can't see,
his great sense of humour
and personality.

And let's not forget
this most important part,
he's kind and he's gentle,
and has a big heart,

He's the perfect lover,
and creator of schemes
to bad he exists
at night in my dreams...
