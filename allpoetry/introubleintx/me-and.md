---
title: "Me and...."
writer: "introubleintx"

As I climb upon you
for what I know
will be the ride of my 
life. A sensual feeling
comes over me, you
between my legs, and
as I ride, you make me
sweat. I know you can
feel it to, as we take
this journey together.
Along the city streets.
Not worrying if people
will stop and stare,
because we are alone
in our own world,
making each other 
happy on this trip we
have taken. You are so 
ready for the challenge,
full of pride, the lust we
share evident in the 
shine of your paint. I 
have taken care of you,
and now you are so 
taking care of me, as no
other has ever been 
able to do before you.
And as we finish our
ride together, you have
given me another memory
to think about on those
nights when you and I
cannot be together. 
Leaving me looking 
forward to our next 
journey taken together.
Just me and my bicycle.....Okay..I tried...Don't know if it is right or not...But I gave it a shot...
