Weeping dreams
of crimson tears
of hopeless loves
and silent fears.

Lingering thoughts
randomly shared
wandering minds
familiar, cared.

Waking moments
etched in time
forever lived
once seemed devine.

Now unnoticed
no words to speak
to dream these dreams
the mind too weak.

Until the time
the tears don't flow
dried up inside
no pain to show...
