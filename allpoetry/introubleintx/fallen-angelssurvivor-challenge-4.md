As we {Walk This Way}
so that we may 
{Come Together} as one
we look to the sky and 
enjoy the {Pink} hues
that we see in the 
dusky night sky. 
We {Dream On} about
that {Somebody} we know 
that has made an impression,
as we {Remember} to 
{Draw the Line} between
fantasy and reality. As
{Darkness} falls, and the moon
shines upon us we decide to
{Let the Music Do the 
Talking} to our wounded 
spirit. As an {Angel} takes 
us on a {Permanent Vacation}
with a {Rag Doll} that has
been granted a {Magic Touch}
we take heed that our chosen
path is {Nobody's Fault} but
our own. And it's {No Surpirze} 
that we have ended up like 
{Rats in the Cellar}. I with a 
{Monkey on my Back} and 
{My Girl} on {The Other Side}
working with the likes of a 
{Voodoo Medicine Man} to bring
me back to her. While I am 
going {Crazy} she is {Cryin}
and wondering why {Young Lust}
brought her here. And as the {Fever}
breaks, and an {Attitude Adjustment}
is made we see that we have
come {Full Circle} and are now
more {Jaded} than before. As we
walk away, no longer {Fallen Angels}
we look for the {Sunshine} that is
{Beyond Beautiful}, letting the 
{Light Inside} us shine on the 
surface, as we have never done 
before. And we know we have
{What it Takes} to make it 
even if we end up {Livin on the 
Edge} once again....There ya go...My tribute to a great band..LOL..Sort of...
