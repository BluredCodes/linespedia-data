As you two begin your lives together
in each day, look for a moment you see,
to take a time out, sharing forever
this gift in time savored, your love and thee.

Entice each other, through all that you will
reliving each minute, spent not alone
but taken in time, you know how you feel
the love that speaks softly over the phone.

The end of lifetime, your final embrace
moments you've captured, taken back in time
show plainly so, in the smile on your face
to leave this love grieving, is such a crime.

Your final  meeting place, heavens above
walking the streets, with the angels' and doves...Okay..Another attempt at a sonnet... :)
