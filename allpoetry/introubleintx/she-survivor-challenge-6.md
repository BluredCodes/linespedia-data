Naked and alone
she stares out 
from her tree top
haven, knowing she
can no longer go home.
Waiting for the one,
her true love, that has
beckoned for her 
to meet him, in this
place, new life begun.
And as the mist rises,
and the sun sets, a 
chill enters the air, 
a shiver to the bone,
the night sounds cry.
She shouts out his name
beckoning for him to
come forth, and take 
her away from this place,
away from where she came.
And she listens, for sounds
that will let her know,
he has heard and is 
on his way, her lonely
heart, breaking, now pounds.
As she realizes that the love
she thought they shared
was nothing more than a
game he played, to trap her
in the hell she's dreamed of.
Now forever alone, chained
to the life she chose, so 
that she could be with him
she begins anew, and she
swears it wasn't in vain.
For she will grow strong,
and someday they will 
meet, he will remember the
love that could have been, and
she will have carried on....
