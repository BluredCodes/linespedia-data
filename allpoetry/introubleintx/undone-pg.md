title: "~~Undone~~ (PG)"

...A rocky road traveled, our life has been
...our hearts becoming entangled as one
...part of me starts, is where part of you ends
...We lived through the past, our future begun.

...Holding hands, sharing dreams, bodies that touch
...Kisses, softly sighing, flesh against flesh
...Your body and mine, I'm missing so much
...Your memory lingers, in my mind fresh.

...Soon we shall be free, to touch once again
...Memories fade, into fantasies new
...Bodies will start, where the fantasy ends
...Forever love stays, between me and you.

...For we started as two, becoming one
...Our heart and soul, neverending, undone.....
