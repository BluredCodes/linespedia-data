Sweet enchantment drips
from half moistened lips
excitement that grips
Now blowing my mind.

Two bodies as one,
Fantasy begun,
As clothing undone,
A treasure to find.

Lie naked with me
touch places, you see
hands roaming, feel free
in pleasure resigned.

When passion is spent
in heated moment
air smells of sex scent
bodies now entwined
this moment defined.....I don't know...Just another one of those Moments.. :)
