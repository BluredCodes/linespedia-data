We made
a moment in
time, shared only by us
forever trapped, a memory
savored
by those
that remember
words spoken, igniting
fires that can only be put out
by the
meeting
of our bodies
sharing thoughts unspoken
yet known, actions speaking, no need
for voice
and yet,
the need grows as
each day passes, without the
release, sought from each other, time
fighting
against
us and our quest
for companionship, just
out of reach, yet hope lingers,
for now
until
we find the time
to share, or forget that
the need exists, leaving hungry
un-fed
forever 
longing for what
will never be, the chance
not taken, the fire now embers,
dying
like the
yearnings of what could
have been, yet will never 
be more than a fantasy shared,
with words...You tell me...Cuz I am not sure... :)
