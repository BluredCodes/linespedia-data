Locked inside a box
for days on end.
I thought you would
remember your friend.
I remember the days
when you would visit me
and now those days 
come infrequently.
Maybe your busy,
or you have someone else
the kids are at home,
so I'm left by myself.
My batteries going dead
but you'd never know
you've forgotten me,
I'm trying to let go.
But I miss all the fun
of making you moan
and the times when you
let me talk on the phone.
Do you remember those
days, when I was new
and you wanted to share
when I made love to you?
But, now I'm forgotten
your toy in the box
oh wait, I hear something
am I being unlocked?
The light after darkness,
your taking me out,
maybe there is someone
your thinking about?
And I am the one 
you turn to instead,
as I lay with you
on your kingsize bed.
You grab my control,
wanting to turn me on
but my batteries are dead
chances of fun are now gone.
Maybe next time
you go to the store
you'll remember your friend
that you threw in the drawer!Okay...I redid it...After reading some of the other ideas..I decided that my first one was completely wrong! :)
