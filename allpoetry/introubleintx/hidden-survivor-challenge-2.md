As she
sits alone and
thinks about him and the 
plans they made, she wonders if it
will be.

or if
he will not show
like before, when they have 
talked about getting together
that day.

Their time
running out fast.
The desires turning 
to embers, from what was once flames
burning.

And yet,
she knows that he
still wants what she offers
and she needs the release he will
give her

Only
when they can find
the time to meet, in a 
place together, for them hidden
away.
