The day the Twin Towers fell to their rest
forever etched in the minds of us all
put our Country's liberty to the test
for now we have seen , that we too can fall.

What once was deemed sacred, has been now lost
our land that houses those you turned away
your terrorist scheme, many lives did cost
in the end, tis you and your's that will pay.

You may think you have changed our way of life,
yet together we have conquered the fear
for onward through the struggles and the strife,
United we'll stand, forever rings clear.

Our flag will fly, and our differences fade
