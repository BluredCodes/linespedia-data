We are far apart
wishing to be close,
Holding to our thoughts
dreams of tomorrow.

Longing for a touch,
just a smile would do
impossible now,
but not forever.

Paper and pen seem
all we have to share
our future rests here
in our written word.

Until we can be 
reunited as
one, never to part
