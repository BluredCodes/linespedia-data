title: "*Forever Known*"

A modern tale of young lovers are we
Though this is a future we did not see
 
We battled and fought to get where we are
from those battles, we're forever scarred

The wounds are healed, though cut deep
Been through depression, wanting forever sleep

Suicide thoughts have entered the minds
Yet not realized, through acts devine

The love that once was, seemed forever strong
Has changed in this time, now seeming wrong

The picture has changed, we have both grown
You in a cell, times you've left me alone

Now I have told you, that this has to change
I no longer want to play fantasy deranged

I want love, and trust, monogamy from you
Or I want let out, to seek a life new.

I know that this hurts, not sure if it ends
You've been my lover, and longest time friend

I want you to know, that I love you true
I don't want to keep fighting just to keep you.

For we are still young, and there is still time
to finally be happy, it can't be a crime.

We can part as friends, the kids are grown
and seek out to find a love, forever known....Just I don't know...Something...
