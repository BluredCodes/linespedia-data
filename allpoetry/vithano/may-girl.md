Over the oceans where America lies,
The people gather on the first day of May
To feast the first light of the sudden-sprung spring,
To fete the first flower, to dance in the day,
And to choose a fresh maiden
For the new queen of May.

Here where the sun never heaves from the shore, 
 And Spring has a permanent home, 
The maid has been chosen, 
Nor need she be new
For my thoughts still gather 
Round the first light of you.
