Come quietly, come at once, 
But come at ease, 
For the water is warm
And the season ready 
To embrace the child 
Now risen suddenly. 

The air remembers
What it was that left,
And the winds lament
No more that sudden
Schism. Come quietly.

Come at once, come
At ease, for the days 
Are short, the hours
Heady, and the journey
For the taking. Come
At ease, come quietly, 
And come at once.
