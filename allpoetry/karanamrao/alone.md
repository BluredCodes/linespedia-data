title: "Alone"

I'm all alone
amidst myriad
brimming stars,
and the languishing moon
at gelid water's edge,
beleaguered by my past
sins of concupiscence,
and the segue of cause
and effect.
I wish I knew the scallop-
edged difference
between illusion
and reality,
and walked into
the freedom of the night,
without being caught
in the semiotic
jungle of delusions.
