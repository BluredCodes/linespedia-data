---
title: "Dream lover"
writer: "Kei lynn"
slug: "dream-lover"
source: "AllPoetry"

I look his way,
Wishing he would look mine,
wondering, dreaming,
what it would be like,
If I stared deep into his eyes,

I seem to fall
yet no pain will come
when I hit the bottom of this,
endless pit his eyes wandering,
barely missing my own,

Is he avoiding me?
Or does he just not see,
The love I hold for him?
And only him, I see his smile,
I hear his laughter,
Like the pealing of silver bells,

I smile with him,
yet I am alone..

Finally he looks my way,
He smiles,
I return the greeting,
and he walks away,
my heart is weighed,

So this is what broken heart feels lie,
I wish for him to be mine,
Always but know it is not possible

Yet I cannot help but wodner,
What could be if just once,
He would look my way
