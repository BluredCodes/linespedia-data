---
title: "I hate"
writer: "Kei lynn"
slug: "i-hate"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

I hate the way you laugh at me,
I hate the way you whisper,
I hate that there's no privacy,
I hate it when you listen,

I hate the way you mock me,
I hate the way you tease,
I hate the way you look at me,
And how you always plead,

I hate the way you talk to me,
I hate how you're always cruel,
I hate the way you touch my side,
And even when you drool,

I hate it when you're mad at me,
And the way you do your hair,
I hate it when you turn you're head,
And pretend that I'm not there,

I hate the way you look away,
I hate the way you dance,
I hate it when you walk away,
And mostly when you chance,

I hate the many things you do,
And even the reasons why,
I hate the way you run away,
And even when you cry,

I hate the way you scream at nite,
And the way you call me friend,
I hate they way you taunt me,
And the way you always mend,

I hate they way you brag to me,
I hate the way you argue,
I hate the way you howl with friends,
I hate the way you are you,

I hate the way you sing to me,
And the way you kiss,
I hate the way you bang your head,
And even when you miss,

I hate the way you write me notes,
Becuase they're so mean,
I hate the way you hold me tight,
Even though you're clean

I hate they way you giggle,
I hate they way you laugh,
I hate the way I love you,
Even when you're mad.
