fell threw a cloud,
I was flying,
I Hit the ground,
Now I'm crying,

I Saw the Stars,
As I was falling,
I heard a voice,
Who were you calling?

Silently,
here I lay,
Tears falling down,
nothing to say.

Burnt and limp,
on the ground,
Breathing calmly,
With hell around,

My eyes faintly close,
As I loose my touch,
Tears stream down as,
I'm loosing much.
