---
title: "Only to Dance"
writer: "Kei lynn"
slug: "only-to-dance"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56882-Only-to-Dance-by-Kei-lynn"
---

I dream the sweetest dreams,
But only to dance,
I feel the stupidest things,
Only a chance,

I hear the sweetest voice,
I want to dance,
I miss a life of hope,
Only to inhance,

I wish to dream the sweetest dream
I wish only to dance,
I want to live an adventure,
Of hopeless romance.
