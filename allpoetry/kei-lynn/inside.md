title: "Inside"
writer: "Kei lynn"

What I see in you,
Is more than I confide,
What you are to me is,
My smile and strength,
What you give me
Is peace of mind,
What I want to express,
Isn't easy to say,
Do you feel as I do
So confused at times,
Do you realy want to open,
Up and show what you hide,
Doyou trust that you
and I can confide all that
I feel inside
