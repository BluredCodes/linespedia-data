Have Real Faith in Yourself

Have Real Faith in Yourself
Like an angel in the sky
Have Real Faith in Yourself
Like angels above your head
Oh Have Faith in Yourself
Like an angel
Oh Have Faith in Yourself
Oh Have Faith in Yourself
Like an angel 
Have Real Faith in Yourself
Like the angels in the sky
