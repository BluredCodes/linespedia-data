title: "A New Life, a Purpose for Life"

A life starts as a baby,
A baby has a purpose to be
On the earth.

Who knows what 
Your purpose is,
But we will find out
The answer someday!

Everyone has a purpose in life, 
From the day we are born,
To the day we die.

There is a purpose
That we should find,
In the days that we live.

When we die,
We will find the answer
To the purpose of why
We were here…
