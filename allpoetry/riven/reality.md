Help me if I'm falling
Don't notice me
If I'm crawling
I have a Monet
As to what this world should be
But in reality, 
Night is more real than day

During the day you drive a Benz
Successful at runing lives
Viewing the world through smoke filled lens
A lawyer protecting the law
Clients are just sources of dough
Strive for the top because you crave control

As day turns to sun down
The residents of Perfect go to sleep
To dream of world
Truer than theirs
It comes out at night from your mind
Because in this world we are confined
A cage we cannot see or taste
The only thing holding us back is our fears
Or ourselves

Free
Your
Mind

Reality is nothing but a dream
We keep trying to wake up or so it seems
Working at a desk in a cube
And growing babies in test tubes
A piece of Americana pie
Served steamin hot
Reality--is just a mere lieWritten sometime last week in my Spanish class during some lame Spanish movie I forget.
