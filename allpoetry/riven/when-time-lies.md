We live and die by it
And yet we have never grasped it's concept
Couldn't nurture it's cause
And now we are attempting to defy its laws?

Why are we trying to find 
The answers
To where time
Lies
It's just mere lies
The need to soar above the skies

Wwwwwwwwwwhhhhhhhhyyyyy

Have we not learned from Icarus
That if you fly into the sun
You will be burned
Same result from time
Only the damage is not physically
But emotionally

There will never be "the right time"
No better time than now
Tell me, why would tomorrow be so fine?
How is tomorrow's hours 
Different from todays?

Do not seek where time lies
Or else you will not be true
To your heart and it will not be true to you
Take the time
Creed for life
And everything will workout rightI've found taking the time helps.  To not rush things.
