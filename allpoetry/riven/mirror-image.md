She steps out and reaches for the towel

Wipes away the water that made her clean


Wipes away on the mirror the steam


What she sees in the mirror


Is an image that she has feared


In her heart she is not as she seems


A book that has been judged by it's cover


Read one too many times and coming apart at the seams


She says to herself she is not fine


To herself she is not of our kind


She does not realize beauty is only skin deep


It does not start off at your


But what's inside you


In the eye of the beholder


Someone holds her beauty that she has not realized


Deep within her eyes


Eyes are the doors to the soul


Someone has looked into them and seen it


She ponders for a second


And suddenly likes her mirror image

--7/19/2001
