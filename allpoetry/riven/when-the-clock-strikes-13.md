Alcohol, you are my friend
You have stuck with me until the end
In a matter of weeks--
My mother died
Wife said I lied
I couldn't find peace, but I tried
I seek solitude in a personal hell
I crawled inside a booze filled shell
Plotting the end of my life
On the table I see the knife
But I have so much to live for
Why can't I see it anymore
The clock is tolling the hour

Thirteen?!
I may be drunk, but my senses are still keen
The hell is wrong with the clock
I get up to check and see
Because what I hear, I don't believe
The clock face says Thirteen O Clock
And in my brain something twitched
My thinking pattern switched
Pour the booze down the drain
Do you question me if I'm still insane?Inspired by "The Right Hand of Evil" by John Saul.  Awesome book.  This is an actual part of the story....if I remember it correctly, I read it a couple years ago.
