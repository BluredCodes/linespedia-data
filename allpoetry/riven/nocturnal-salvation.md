John stepped out into the cold midnight air

Insomnia plagued him like a string of the flu

Wandering up and down the streets at night

Did he hallucinate that they had turned blue.




Walking down skid row

Music came from a building

That could topple over at the slightest blow

John went up the unwelcoming gray stairs

And looked inside

Blackness



A staircase infront of him

That seemed to go on for miles

He ascended the staircase

And descended into insanity

He stopped at the top and walked down the hallway

The music growing ever so louder

An early style 

Reminiscent of Medieval times

He looked through the door and saw th band

Playin well and playin fine

He took a seat in the back and looked at the band

And what few people were there



He began to drift off to sleep

His subconscious taking hold of the reins

He could not get comfortable in that chair

He stepped outside and fell asleep to the sound of rain

He awoke to even harder rain but was it the same day?

He went into the room and did not see anybody

He glimpsed upon skeletons carved in stone

He looked down at his hands and saw the effects of time

John left the room

And made the journey back to his bed

As he left the room one of the skeletons turned and looked at him and began to follow

As did the rest
