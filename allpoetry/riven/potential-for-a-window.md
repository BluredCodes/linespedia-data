Opportunity
Is at my door
But it has the potential
To be so much more

If I open it what will I find
The key to unlock my mind?
It has potential for a window of danger
A doorway to a Netherworld
An abyss of fire and pain
A surreal world, yet the same

Opening it is a risk
Is it one I am willing to take
What if I find
That this world is fake

Stepping into the vast void of space
Being one amongst the stars
And yet it is so cold
My dreams reach so far
Filling a space once empty

How do I begin
Dreams that could be considered a sin

Eyes
Have potential to be windows to the soul
yet they do not fufill their role
The one that does or so it seems
Is your nightmares and dreams
They open the door for opportunity
And you make take a risk
Pay attention to themSeriously do pay attention to the dreams you have.  It's usually your brain telling you something.
