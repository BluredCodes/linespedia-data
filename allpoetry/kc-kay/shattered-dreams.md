---
title: "Shattered Dreams"
writer: "KC Kay"
slug: "shattered-dreams"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

How do you expect me to look at you
With all the lies you've sold
All the times you've promised
Every single lie that you've told
How do you expect me to forgive you
When you've broke every peice of my heart
When you promised us forever
But instead are breaking my heart apart
I had dreams that I looked forward to
Of spending so much of my life with you
But instead you repay me like this
Telling me you promise we can work this through
The thing is you've said it before
And I agreed to try and work it out
It didn't work then
But you say I have nothing to doubt
But why should I believe you
When you've told me nothing but lies
When everytime I want to be with you
I end up with tears running from my eyes
So I guess it's time to give in
I have nothing left to give
There will be nothing more but shattered dreams
And a better life to live
