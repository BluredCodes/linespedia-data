---
title: "~*Would You*~"
writer: "PaSsIoNaTe BaYbEe"
slug: "would-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

I sit here thinking back 
On all the time we've spent together
And all the first's I've shared with you,
Praying to the god I hope is up above
That all this pain will just go away
Wishing you would just grow up
And stop playing all these games.
What if tomorrow I woke up
And out of the blue
I was no longer in love with you?
Would you regret 
All that you've done?
Would you feel bad
For always making me so sad?
Would you cry
From the pain like I always do?
Would you hate 
Yourself for not being true?
Would it make you feel 
Like your whole life just ended?
I sit here thinking 
About all the pain you've caused 
And the hell you've put me through
Yet no matter how hard I sometimes try
I can't help but love you still.
I only wish I knew 
What you would do
If you were me 
And I was you.
I honestly don't think 
You could ever handle 
Being in my shoes
With all that you have
And do
Put me through.
Some day 
I'm gonna do it all to you
And really find out
What it is 
That you would do.
