title: "I realized last night"

I realized last night the pain I must cause
Being like I am
Needing what I do…

I realized last night the loss I feel
Knowing you are gone
Simply need to walk through that door

I realized last night my love for you
How I wish I could hold you to me
Through distance and space

I realized last night how much we have been through
The darkness around us holds us in
Preparing us for yet another round of war

I realized the monster I have become
The fears inside of me bringing forth in life
Memories imitated and executed from horror’s first life

I realized last night...
Many things…
That I need you…
That you bring me peace…
That lying in your arms I am finally happy…
And yet in lying in your arms…

You are sad…
Tears reached my head…
We both suffer so much…
And yet for what?
