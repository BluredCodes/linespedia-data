You know i never finished...
What I wrote, What I felt
I couldn't write more then a few words
What I felt, What I saw
I could only wait for tohse immutable Dozens
What I saw, what I struggled
Minutes drawn upon like a lost lovers blade
What I struggled, What I Bled
Sitting there, dressed in my finest armor
What I bled, what I shared
King's Corps and Pauper's Thrones adorned the hall
What I shared what I waited
No comfort reached my soul, sitting on patted benches
What I waited, what I pained
Though I knew the worse was yet to arrive
What I pained, what we Suffered
For lying in there is a shell, a ghost
What We suffered, What we Knew
Of once of life, our brother now lost
What we knew, what we remember
And now, later, weeks had crossed paths
What we remember, what we gathered.
Forgotten tapes and forsaken times remind us still
What we gathered, what we shared
Nor Rumtuguger nor Grizzabella dared brings back
What we shared,what we could
And though time has past, joy has come
What we could, what we had
I'd give up the world for a few more hearteats
What we had, What I lost...Rumtugtuger and Grizzabella are references from Weber's Cats.
