I don’t like answering machines
For being so far, touching is nar close
For being alone, it doesn’t fill the gap
For being lonely, it nary compensates

I don’t like answering machines
It is a simple message
Played over and over to many
Yet never changing to trained ears

I don’t like answering machines
Not at this particular moment
For my longing for you grows
Untill I lay awake with tears on my toes

I don’t like answering machines
It lacks your inflections
Those little notes you make that drive me wild
Those small tones within your throat that bring me to peace

I don’t like answering machines
It fails to hold your mind
It relays naught your heart
For in it, I nary can touch your soul

I don’t like answering machines
For to speak to it is to gain no replay
To whisper to it is redundant in peace
For to question it is to gain nary an answer

I don’t’ like answering machines
For a hoping heart, it does no justice
For a lonely soul it holds no joy
For a troubled mind it holds no peace

I don’t like answering machines
You may argue, but I will still believe this to be true
To simply speak onto a machine
I don’t’ like them, simply because it’s not you…
