title: "To you, My Dear"

To you, my dear
Take it
Take it all
Come into my chest and rip out my spine
Move down my body and cut out my spleen
Just leave me here to rot

Take my soul and burn it upon the pier
Take my heart and stew it with your feast
Take my love and throw it towards the wind
Take my passion and turn it to pain

To you, my lovely dear
To you, My evil queen
Take everything that I am
Take my sanity and revel in it
Roll about it like pigs in mud
Leave me violated and scared

Take what you will

You can have him, which ever that is
You can have them both
You can pray and fight
You can cry upon your pillow at night
You can let them abuse you
You can let them torture you
For you know I won’t
You know I can’t

My love for you was simple
My love for you was pure
I only want to bring you joy
And you seem only to want to bring misery

So take it all, My Dark Love
For I need it no further
You promised not to hurt me
Then twisted you blades into my scull

Have him.
Have them…
And allow me to die
Quivering and Alone!
