title: "Just let me Lay here"
writer: "wanderingpoet"

Just let me lay here
Lay my head upon the floor
No pillows, nor rug
Just the concrete and me

Just let me lay here
Broken and alone
While fleeting thoughts lay 
Tattered and Torn around me

Just let me stay for a while
Let me curl up into a ball and sleep
Tears will come later
I just want to lay and stare

Just let me forget for a time
Allow me just this once
Put our pains aside
And just let me lay

Let me curl up my blanket
Naked and alone
Pretending its soft surface
Was simply you…

Just let me imagine my head
Nestled up within your lap
Just let me imagine your hand
Gently stroking my groomed hair

Just let me stay for awhile
Lie to me and tell me I’m all
Tell me your love beats back others
Allow me to believe for a few minutes

Just let me lay here
Lay my head upon the floor
No pillows, nor rug
Just the concrete and me
