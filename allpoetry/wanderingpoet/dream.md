I dreamt of you this morn
Sitting there by your computer
And I was just watching…
Just watching…

We were sitting outside the sunlight
Simply staring out into the distance
What were we seeing?
What were we seeing?

I turned to gaze into your eyes
Only to seem them turned away at the pivotal moment
And I just have to wonder
To wonder…

We were walking along the empty highway
Two different sides along blackened rivers of char
Why were we so far?
 	Why were we so far?

Seemingly lovers by trade
And yet it felt as if the skies could pick you up
And the darkness carried me home
Carried me home…

I hear sweet tunes in my ears
And look around to see you…
But you were not there…
You were not there…

So I slowly stirred from my slumber
Looked over to the plastic and silicon demon next to me…
Yet the simple fact remains…
You are still not here…Just a dream I had this morning...
