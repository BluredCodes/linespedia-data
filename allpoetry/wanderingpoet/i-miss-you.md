title: "I miss you..."

I miss sleeping next to you at night
The phone molded into my skin as before
Hearing your soft breaths against my skin
Feeling the peace surround us

I miss laughing with you
The joyous mixture of irony and crash
Smiling and staring at blank walls
Wishing I could see you again…

I miss talking to you
Debating this and that
Popularity verses fantasy
In the end turning to playful mocking…

I miss hearing your soul
Sharing our life stories
Hopes, dreams, passions and nightmares
With the freedom of young lovers discovering each other…

I miss just being safe with you
You pulling and inspiring emotions in me never fathomed
Lifting me up to heights and depths I could never understand
And the soothing you gave me…

I miss being with you…
Hearing your moans against my body
Feeling the heat of your passion
The strength of your need

I miss the magic between us…
One minute in conversation, the next in passion
Exploring our fantasies as our blood boils
Searing us with frenzied touches and words

I miss what is so far away now
I miss exciting you
Entrancing you
I miss you needing me
I miss you loving me…

I miss you…
