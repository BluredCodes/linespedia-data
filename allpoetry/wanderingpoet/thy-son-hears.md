title: "Thy Son Hears"

Words expressed in anger
Words expressed in hate
Words that know no feeling
Yet so heard to fate

Screams uttered in silence
Tears shed in the past
Pain flow overwhelming
Pain compounded untill the last

Last of ages
Spread bare to bone
Life's Pains buried
One feels utterly alone

You only feel the disgrace
I only feel the burn
You say I disgust you
I say the world will yet turn

Whose place is it to hate?
For thine words strike tears
Can't you see your child crying
