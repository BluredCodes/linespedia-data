We were once like a song
Flowing, floating, it’s power intact
Our words flying through the wind
With twinges of chords
And the drums of our hearts

We were once an original
Placing the track in the player
Hearing the fullness of our passion
Feeling the entirety of our love

We were once an opera
Unknown words un-understood 
Yet music flowed through our veins
Telling the tale of our magic

We were once like a song...
Direct from the recorder
And now…

Why do we feel like a radio?
All the words understood
Yet cut and torn…
The main message is there… 
But it just… lacks…

We switched on the stereo
Cut down our music as to fit the time frame
And now we lost our souls to the conformity
Moved our passions from the endless night
To the pacing day…

We were once a golden record
Now turned into a common song…

And I just miss singing with you…
