I witnessed…
Upon a dreary day
Long ago within the pavements
A duel of dismay…

Two women,
Same and yet condemned
Standing side by side
Face to face, winds racing down onto them…

They stared at each other
Glaring at the other on that shelf…
Awaiting the other
First to move against herself

I didn’t see who struck first
Lady in black, nor Woman in White
Though faster did the blows impact
Along those darkened cold sights…

Black tunics ravaged around white dresses
As powerful pricks scoured their faces
Talons teeth and dull edges
Brought about wicked desired laments

The fight ravaged on
White woman valiant while Lady did attack
Holding down, pinned to once safe soil
Only to be fought back with pins and jack

I watched this from afar, you understand
I could not rush to see
To witness these two from standing so far
Could it mirror a fight within of thee?

The fight ravaged on
The white striking back with hordes feared
The black impaling with sabers of pain
And somewhere in the middle, a cry appeared

Both fell to the granite
Bloodied and Surprised
Gazing at each other
With fury in their eyes

Jumping to their feet
They continued their plight
Fighting far and wide
Into seemingly endless nights

When the dust settled
When the pavement dried
All I could hear
Was a single Child’s Cry…Just a warning, This is not about two people...
