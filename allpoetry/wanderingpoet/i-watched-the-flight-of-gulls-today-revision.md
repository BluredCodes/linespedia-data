I watched the flight of gulls today 
Floating high above my car 
Painted wings soaring slowly in the sky 
Staring down at knolls unclean 

I watched the flight of gulls today 
Racing towards their ever quest 
A journey of love and meat beheld 
Swooping slowly through the tattered hills 

I watched the flight of gulls today 
As they dove into oceans clear and blue 
As they rose above flying gentle in the beyond 
Slowly gliding towards relief’s past due… 

I watched the flight of gulls today 
Smiling at simply freedoms 
Watching as they raced over to the ground 
To share in their newest catch 

I watched the flight of gulls today 
Bringing pleasure within my soul 
Until a solitary beauty flew over my ride 
And simply floated above all rights 

I watched the flight of gulls today 
That single one of no great import 
And then as if to get my attention 
Sang in rapture above my scull 

I watched the flight of gulls today 
Hearing that harsh, yet simple song 
How it raced over through the brave sand waves 
As the solitary beauty sailed away…Heheheh... This is the revision of the original poem, a little better fitting my mood right now. :)
