The poisoned little pill slips down your throat
Digesting slowly within acid confines…
With no rhyme
Very little reason

Oh, lover, can’t you see the rage?
Oh, lover, can’t you see the pain?
Oh, lover, can’t you see the anger?
Oh, lover, can’t you see the rain?

The burning liquid rushes down your mind
Brining infected blood through your pores
One little shot…
One less moment…

Oh, lover, can’t you see the core?
Oh, lover, can’t you see the tears?
Oh, lover, can’t you see the frustration?
Oh, lover, can’t you see the fears?

The leaves burn into cinders
Filling lungs with piercing smoke
One more hit…
One more shot…

Oh, lover, Can’t you feel the darkness?
Oh, lover, Can’t you see the cult?
Oh, lover, can’t you look within yourself?
Oh, lover, can’t you see the fault?

The anger permeates through your bones
Held in place with unbreakable doors
No keys in sight
No hinges within…

Oh, lover can’t you see the worry?
Oh, lover can’t you see the anguish?
Oh, lover can’t you see the crimson?
Oh, lover, can’t you see him languish?


The hammer clicks into place
Bringing finality within Her soul…
Death of flesh is naught…
Death of hope rings eternal…

Oh, lover, can’t you see the ruin?
Oh, lover, can’t you see you’re Seth?
Oh, lover can’t you see the running?
