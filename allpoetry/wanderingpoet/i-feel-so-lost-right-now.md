I feel so lost right now
	With the heat of our battle still upon us
	The intensity of the words searing our flesh

I feel so lost right now
	Lying next to you after sufferings false stop	
	Only to recount soon our torturous pleasures

I feel so lost right now
	Mistakes befallen and pained treasures to behold
	Often taking their place among our clouds

I feel so lost right now
	Not knowing what to think
	Not knowing what to say
	Not knowing if I should wait
	Not knowing if I should lay

I feel so lost right now
	For sometimes it feels like bliss
	Sometimes the nightmare looms
	And we both scream in our pain

I feel so lost right now
	My love for you burns strong
	Yet trust long since faded
	For steps skipped and knowledge forsaken
	Betrayals loom far in distance

I feel so lost right now
Sitting alone in front of this machine
Speaking to a ghost of the love we both share
Only waiting for the next spark to spring…
