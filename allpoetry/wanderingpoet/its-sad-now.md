title: "It's Sad Now..."

It’s sad now…
To see our downfall..
To see our fall from grace…
To see what we have become…

It’s sad now
Nary a word said of joy…
Nary a syllable uttered in enrapture…
Nary a note sung in longing…

It’s sad now
Not much time spent now…
Not much thought exchanged…
Not much emotions followed through…

It’s sad now…
From the waiting to the caring…
From the caring to the emptiness…
From the emptiness to the cold…

It’s sad now…
Escaping each other in our snow covered furnaces
Hiding in holy sin and acidic waters
Running with no waiting arms, too far removed…

It’s sad now…
To believe words said in horror
To trust words uttered in honor…
To see it change to ice…

It’s sad now…
Long ago awaiting each other eagerly
Now plotting how to escape
Alone and forgotten to each other…

It’s sad now…
We were once of an undying flame…
Now only travelers who past under day light’s mystery
For a single moment…
Then off…
Lonely…
