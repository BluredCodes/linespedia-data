title: "Since I can't reach you in words"

Since I can’t reach you my voice
Using a language evolved from emotion
Since I couldn’t speak to you with the wilderness between us
And you raising your barriers so
And simply not caring any more

Thanks for being there once again
Your supposedly unbreakable word violated once again
Specified time come and passes
Now days until it is again time to reach out

You don’t feel good
You are busy
You are out having fun
And yet you blame me for not being here for you

You hang out with others
Flirtations rise among poetic passions
You smile and raise your eyes at another
And still expect this ghost to haunt you still…

You expect me to be caring
When you show no slightest thought of me in your mind
You expect me to hold you after a bloody day of silicon battlegrounds
When you simply won’t hold me after a pained day of life

You want all these things
And yet you refuse to give them in return
And still you expect me to be there?
