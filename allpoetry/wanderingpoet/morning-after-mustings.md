---
title: "Morning After Mustings"
writer: "wanderingpoet"
slug: "morning-after-mustings"

Reading the words you speak to me
Feeling the excitement in your voice
The passion, the challenge
Hearing your happiness in your letters
And knowing yours smiling brings me light

And yet a dark shadow sweeps over my soul
As denied knowledge suddenly comes to light
Through the words and hidden thoughts
Differences in tones through poem after rhyme
I still have to face the facts

I can’t touch you like they can.
I can’t inspire you like they do…
I can’t challenge you like they will…
I can’t fulfill you like you want…

Depressing realizations flood in my mind
As I realize the pain I have caused
Stumbling through hidden forests dark
Somehow I pushed us into the thorns.

We don’t talk much anymore
We only scream and fight
We only make ourselves more miserable
We can only find ourselves away from the other

I guess what I’m trying to say is this…
It feels like it’s over…
I’m not what you need…
So I pack my things and go…
And allow you to live…
