You wanted it?
You wanted to behold the rage?
You begged and pleaded
You cursed and screamed
And still I took it

You pushed and ravaged
You pained and speared
You ignored and balled
And still I took it

Now you want to see it?
You want to see the key to my soul, ehy?
You want to see the rage that fumes inside of me?
You want to see the darkness that you are so accustomed to?
Fine, HAVE IT!

You withdraw when I shout, fine
You numb when I scream, fine
Now what?
Now what do I do?
Do I have to do the same to you
To show you what the fuck I put up with?

Do you want me to go and play with your mind?
or worse.. Play with your heart?
Do you want me to go and fuck with others?
Do you want me to show you the depths of what I am capable of?

Do you want me to show you the evil that I can behold?
Do you want me to abuse you
To torture you
To leave you to die like all of the others?

Do you need me to be like everyone else?
Drag you into a hole and cum and piss all over you?
Do you need me to slam you into a wall
Tie you to the bed?

Do you need me to fuck you?
Fuck you in every way there is?
Do you want to see the way I can be?
You wanted it…
Just remember that…
You wanted to see me in my entirety
And pain is the only thing you understand…Sorry about the words...
