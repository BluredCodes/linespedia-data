my star hung up in the sky obscure in smog
and twilight of the city doesn't fade
and perpetual in half darkness we've shone
for the day the sun rises my star will fall away
and drown in fragments to mortality
and when the moon rises she'll take me away
so i've found my life at dusk rising freelast line is from my other poem "dusk" so i guess its a bit of a continuation. its not done either.
