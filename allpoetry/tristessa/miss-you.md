beyond death, through the darkness
in the shadows and in the moon's glow
through the life of my mother's mothers
and man's countless terrible retributions
and the years and the blood and the kiss
through the times of tears and sadness
the joy and anger, melancholy and fear
and the winter to span epochs of time
through the dances of children to songs yet unsung
and the tears that we have yet to shed
i'll wait for you above the clouds
for my heart is steadfast as my lovehey you
