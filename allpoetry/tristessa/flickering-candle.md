title: "flickering candle"

and they say that god is smiling
               and that we are always loved
               and that he gave his son for us
               and we nailed him against the sky

kneeling like a sinner
in this musty pew
with my fading flame casting
dancing shadows all around me
and i can't help but wish
that i could be dancing too

now silent but for breathing
i've got my head bowed
and i took that cracker
cuz it's what i'm supposed to do
but somewhere deep inside me
i'm crying for the truth

standing still and quiet 
in this crowded church
with my flickering candle
and a picture of Jesus 
with light in his eyes
and thorns upon his brow

               but i don't see his puncture wounds
               no i don't see his cross
               and i don't see much beyone the glow
               of my candle burning out
