what if i crash from my current precipice
and fall backwards into no one's arms
and all i can hear is the sound of her voice
not saying i love you

what if i scream everything that i can't say
and jump right into my warm abyss again
and all i can't see is the end of my pain
what if i crash tonighta splurge. i'm not sure what to make of this.
