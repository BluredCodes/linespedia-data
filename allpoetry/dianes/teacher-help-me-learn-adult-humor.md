Teach me teacher
Help me learn
I'm more of a hands on student
books don't help me learn!

Show me how
Guide me I say
Sometimes it's easier
if you show me in play!

Does X+Z
=US
If i pass this test
do I get an
A+!

What's that you say
stay after school
Private lessons
you'll give me the tools!

I need an education
I need to learn
So many things to remember
the wheels how they turn!

What do you mean 
sit on your lap
What is that bump
I feel against my back!

You say its a ruler
You call it your tool
Momma always taught me
don't be no fool!

But then again 
she also said
Do unto others 
as they do unto you!

Up goes my skirt
time to use your rule
Oh yes you are right
Its harder in high school!

Do you hear bells ringing
I thought I did
Time for another lesson
I think I learned something,
I am sure that I did!

What's that you say
I've been bad
You smack my butt with your ruler
Oh yes teacher that smarts!!!

Bend over the desk
How many licks
I never had lessons 
before like this!

Keep saying my vowels
A-E-I-0-U
Each time I get to O
you smile
yes you do!!!

Maybe someday
I'll be a teacher like you
Educating can be fun
If you know what to do!!!!

I'll go for my Masters
in S&M or 
major in Domineering
there's a subject
to have interest in!!!!

When's my next lesson
I promise you this
I don't mind coming again
I'll do as you wish!!!"just some fun and laughs"
