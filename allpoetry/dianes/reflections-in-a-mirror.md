---
title: "Reflections in a Mirror"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "reflections-in-a-mirror"

Reflections of this person
shine back into my eyes
Who is this person
am I to surmise...

The face looks familiar
but she looks so lost
I can see it in her eyes
Love made her pay the cost...

I see only shadows
of the love she thought she shared
Her heart has been broken
no feelings were spared...

She has paid the price
to be in loves game
She has lost in love
She will never be the same...

Reflections of this person
I don't like what I see
I can't give into her pity
because the reflections are of me...

Tomorrow when I wake up
and look into that mirror
I will be stronger
my mind will be clearer...

Lessons learned the hard way
seems to be the only ones I learn
Reflections in a mirror
eyes pleading, this time learn...
