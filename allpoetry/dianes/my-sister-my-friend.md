---
title: "My 'Sister', My 'Friend'"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "my-sister-my-friend"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58101-My-Sister--My-Friend-by-dianes"

One day you will remember
a friendship I gave to you
It was a special friendship
like a sisterhood offered true.

I thought of you
like such a special friend
Told you of my hope and dreams
discussions without end.

Oh how you brought joy to me
smiles and laughter to my lips
Now I feel only hurt
with that knife within your grip.

I only wanted your happiness
never wanting ever to inflict pain
Not knowing that your happiness
would only be gained by my pain.

You listened to each confidence
fully knowing well
by not telling me of your feelings
you sent me straight to Hell.

Don't tell me that you're sorry
you know I don't believe you
Cuz, if you could do this to me
A true friend and sister never were you.

Don't you ever call me sister
Don't you ever call me friend
Cuz, you don't know their meaning
you've proven that to the end."for all the people who've had a friend who they love fk em over in the name of love"
