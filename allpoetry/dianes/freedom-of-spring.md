title: "Freedom of Spring"

Freedom of the Spring
has finally sprung
away from this house
and my boredom.

Out to the woods
to commune with mother nature
Camping and hiking
jokes and storytelling by the night fire.

It's my sanity
my release
my little safe haven
it makes me complete.

Just to sit back and relax
listen to nature sing
heal my past
my heart sighs relax....relax."camping time is finally here"
