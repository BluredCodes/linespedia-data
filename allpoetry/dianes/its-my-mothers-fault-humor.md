title: "It's my Mother's Fault (humor)"

My mother always taught me
if you have nothing good to say
keep your mouth quiet 
so you don't regret it one day

She taught me to believe the best
in people that I meet
I tried to remember this
even in defeat

It is a fault to trust too much
one I can't seem to change
I believe too much in people
not knowing when they play

So if I trust too much
If I believe people are good
It's all my mother's fault
I listened like I should.
