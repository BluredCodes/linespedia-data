---
title: "For Once a Trust is Broken"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "for-once-a-trust-is-broken"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57183-For-Once-a-Trust-is-Broken-by-dianes"

Once a trust is broken
you start to question why
You start to wonder if everyone 
is filling your head with lies...

Even if you want to believe
even if you want to trust
once that has been broken
you protect your heart...you must...

Knowing you must go on
knowing you will heal
a trust that is broken
leaves you so much which to deal...

You start to become suspicious
wonder if you should believe
Because once a trust is broken
it just leaves you to grieve...

So forgive me if I can't believe you
because right now I can't trust anyone
because a trust was broken
and it can't be undone...

For those of you who know me
who are really friends
You will understand this
and give me time to mend..."sorry, just can't believe anyone right now...forgive me"
