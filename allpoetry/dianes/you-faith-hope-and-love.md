---
title: "You, Faith, Hope and Love"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "you-faith-hope-and-love"

Take me to that place
the one you've told me about
and love, happiness and health
is all there is about...

Take me in your arms
Cuz, I know your love is true
You were always there for me
It's just I never knew...

I was taught to believe
that you were always there
to show me your mercy and comfort
knowing that you will always care...

You designed me in your image
or so that's what they say
You know everything I do
but you love me anyway...

So even if you are just a image
and even if you are not real
Should I break my belief
let you lose that appeal...

Even if you are a spirit
You give me Unconditional Love..
What harm does it do me
to believe in you...Faith, Hope and Love...
