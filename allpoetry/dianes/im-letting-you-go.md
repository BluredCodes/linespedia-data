---
title: "I'm Letting You Go"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "im-letting-you-go"
source: "AllPoetry"

I'm letting you go...
not because I want to
but because I have no choice
your words said it all
thank God I couldn't hear your voice...

Your happiness matters most of all
that's what loving you means to me
and if I can't provide that
then you're better off without me...

I'm letting you go...
not because I want to
but because I have no choice
your words said it all
thank God I couldn't hear your voice..

I loved you like no other
but I know I will go on
I can't pretend you didn't hurt me
and I will feel the pain  for so long...

I'm letting you go...
not because I want to
but because I have no choice
your words said it all
thank God I couldn't hear your voice..

I want you to be happy
I want you to have love
apparently I wasn't the one
even though I thought I was...

I'm letting you go...
not because I want to
but because I have no choice
your words said it all
thank God I couldn't hear your voice.."be happy"
