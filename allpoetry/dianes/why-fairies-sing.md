---
title: "Why Fairies Sing"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "why-fairies-sing"
source: "AllPoetry"

A fairy sits by the falls
tears falling once again
Knowing she has lost a love
and heartbroken she sings her song

It is a sad melody
one that touches you to the core
Soft and sweet the melody
you stay for the oncore

The song does put you in a trance
eyes fixed upon her in your stance
Listen to her little tune
Sung from her broken heart to the moon

It's so sad but beautiful
the lilt of her voice
makes your heart full
Her wings encase her body so still

Then as her song is about to end
She stands and stretches
wings and arms outspend
Her song spews forth

Notes so glorious without end
Her lungs filled with mournful breaths
Tears stream down
Fall unto her tiny breasts

Now the crescendo
is about to end
You can feel her heart
as it tries to mend

Her fairy song
has touched your soul
Reminding you
of loves of old

So hold those memories
in your heart deep
Remember how the fairies weep
for each love lost is their pain to keep

So when you hear that melody
it's a fairy crying for lost love 
Her song sings out
to the moon above
 
The way she heals
the heart is to sing
that melody to make you feel
so that once again you will heal"been awhile since I did a fairy write"
