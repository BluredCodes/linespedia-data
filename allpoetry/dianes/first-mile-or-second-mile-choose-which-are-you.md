I always remember a parable
I heard when I was small
I have never forgotten it
and it applies to all...

The story was about friendship
how friends will walk with you
Oh they will walk the first mile
when it's sunny and so blue...

But when the storms come
and darkness is all around
They pull their coats around themselves
and they are nowhere to be found....

It's so easy to be a first mile friend
there for all the easy things
Walking on the beach is easy
when laughter and good times ring...

But when that second mile comes
with storms and shadows deep
It's only our true friends
Who hold you when you weep..."the only reason I put personal is cuz this parable has always stuck w/me...I always try to think of myself as the second mile friend holding onto my friend through the storms and high tides and rejoicing with them when the sun shines through...."
