Emotional rollercoaster that's what I"m on
one day I'm singing
next day depression dawns...

Up the hill
holdin on tight
heart beatin fast
now down the other side...

Emotional rollercoaster that's what I"m on
one day I'm singing
next day depression dawns...

Down in the valley
heart sinks so low
catching my breath
Does my fear show...

Emotional rollercoaster that's what I"m on
one day I'm singing
next day depression dawns...

I want to get off
will it ever end
my stomach is churning
crying begins...

Emotional rollercoaster that's what I"m on
one day I'm singing
next day depression dawns...

Stop the ride now
I need to get off
Stop it now
My heads spinnin off...

Emotional rollercoaster that's what I"m on
one day I'm singing
next day depression dawns...

Push the stop button
Push it now
I would but
I DON'T KNOW HOW....."Stop the ride I wanna get off!!!!!!!!!!"
