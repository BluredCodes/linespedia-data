---
title: "Look at Me"
writer: "dianes"

Look at me
What do you see...

Am I not worthy of your glance
maybe a word of hello per chance...

Look at me
What do you see...

Don't judge me by what your eyes see
take a chance get to know me...

Look at me
What do you see...

I look back at you with questioning eyes
Stop hiding behind your disguise...

Look at me
What do you see...

You finish brushing your hair
turn around without a second glance...

Look at me
What do you see...

Come back, look again
See me for who and what I am...

Look at me
What do you see...

I'm just me
not someone's fantasy...

Looking at me
I like what I see...

Took me so long
just to accept the who I am in me..

Look at me
What do you see..."just a little self examination"
