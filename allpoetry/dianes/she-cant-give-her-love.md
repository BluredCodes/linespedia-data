title: "She can't give her love"
writer: "dianes"

She can't give her love
even though she has so much deep inside

Please don't ask her
because all she will do is cry

Too many times she gave her love
to only have it handed back

Now she's too afraid to give it
will do anything not to attract

She's protecting what she has
for she only has one heart

It's battered and it's weary
Beaten from the start

Oh yes, she can love
but she'll only love so much

That's the way she protects her broken heart
she handles it with kid gloves

So let her love you
let her hold friendships dear

But don't push her into emotions
on that she's very clear

For no one will hold her heart
for many years to come

And it will have to be a special man
for him to receive her love
