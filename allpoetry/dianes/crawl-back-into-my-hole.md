Let me crawl back in my hole
from whence I came
Let your lips never more
call my name
Forget that I existed
forget that I feel
Forget my emotions
for once they were real
Let me crawl back into my hole
never feeling but safe to steal...."inspired by gecko and his coffin write" ty me friend.....
