Play me
I'm used to being second violin 
in the orchestra of life

Rosin your bow
slide it across my strings
listen to the melody it ensues

Place your fingers
on my chords
you know all the right notes

I carry the harmony
waiting in the background
to let my strings sing their song

Listen to my song
it was there all along
sometimes second violin plays it best."something different"
