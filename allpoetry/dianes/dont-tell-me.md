---
title: "Don't  Tell Me"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "dont-tell-me"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55794-Dont--Tell-Me-by-dianes"

Don't tell me that you love me
then just walk away
I would rather you not tell me
that way pain won't come my way...

Don't tell me all the little words
that I long to hear
Especially those three
Please, don't even call me dear...

My heart has been broken
one to many times
I seem destined for heartbreak
I fall for the same old lines...

So don't tell me that you love me
lets not even pretend
For if you say you love me
It will just hurt more in the end..."ok i lied i can't help what i feel"
