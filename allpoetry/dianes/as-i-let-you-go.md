---
title: "As I let you go..."
writer: "dianes"
slug: "as-i-let-you-go"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56417-As-I-let-you-go...-by-dianes"
---

Do you remember me
I'm the person you said you loved
the person you said you only dreamed of

Do you think of me
Like I think of you
remembering what I said to you

When you close your eyes
is it her you see
or is it me floating back in memories 

Do you reach out to touch
and not find me there
forgetting you left me standing here

Can you feel the love
that I had for you
you always knew my love was true

But you left me here
now I'm all alone
my heart is heavier than any stone

Will you remember me
Can you feel my pain
My tears fall down like pouring rain

Will you remember me
as I remember you
I loved you so...now I have to let you go..."goodbye"
