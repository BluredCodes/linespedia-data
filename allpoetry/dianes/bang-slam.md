---
title: "Bang, Slam"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "bang-slam"
source: "AllPoetry"

Bang. slam
Shut the door
to my heart
forever...
That  is exactly 
how I felt
the day
I got your letter.

You shared your  thoughts and feelings
then eventually your love
I opened the door
to let you make a home in my heart
like you were a gift from above.

Bang. slam
Shut the door
to my heart
forever...
That  is exactly 
how I felt
the day
I got your letter...

Before I had it padlocked
to keep out the hurt and pain
You opened the door 
to let me love again
But now the tears do rain...

Bang. slam
Shut the door
to my heart
forever...
That  is exactly 
how I felt
the day
I got your letter.

Breaking a solemn promise
I made to myself
Knowing my promise was in vain
I let you settle in
got too comfortable I see
Now I was too involved
Love is blind they told me...

Bang. slam
Shut the door
to my heart
forever...
That  is exactly 
how I felt
the day
I got your letter.

I guess I made the mistake
of letting the door open
Knowing I couldn't keep you in
my love was just a token
I can't hold you to promises of the past
I only had one wish for you
Your Happiness
and that still lasts...

Bang...
Slam...
Shut the door
to my heart
forever...
That  is exactly 
how I felt
the day
I got your letter...

Bang...
Slam...
Shut the door
to my heart forever...
This heart can't mend anymore
It's padlocked 
No more will ever enter...
