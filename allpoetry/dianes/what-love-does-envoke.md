---
title: "What Love does Envoke"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "what-love-does-envoke"

To feel your touch
and hear the whisper of my name
whether love claims me
in the early hours of morning
or deep into the night
my heart is ready to take this flight
feeling the blood rush
through every artery and vein
it starts out when you whisper my name
knowing the feel of your touch
as it tenderly caresses me
making me want you so much
Spooning and moving like snakes in the grass
moaning and groaning
let's make this last
Lets explore
till we can't explore no more
if someone comes round 
we'll just shut the door
Fingertips tracing
the lips that they kiss
tongues and legs entwining
into sweet bliss
Hands roaming
pulling each close
so tightly together
our hearts beat in sync
eyelashes touching cheeks as we blink
each pore screaming come back
I want more
there is so much
we've yet to explore
Hands running through
each others hair
pulling back heads
to kiss deeply in care
Move with me
groove with me
Enter within
Hips rise
lips lock
Passion and desire win
We make each other feel
like no one else can
faster and deeper
God, your my man
Hold me
scold me
oh yes...
I've been bad
spank me baby
do what only you can
Heat and friction
the passion flames
Suddenly the crescendo
calling out names....
Collapsing and kissing
snuggling close
how much we enjoy
what love does envoke....
