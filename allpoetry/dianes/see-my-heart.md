---
title: "See My Heart"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "see-my-heart"

See my heart,
it's upon the floor,
That's where it fell,
when I read what you had to tell,
See my tears,
they fall too,
For a love,
that I thought was true,
See my pain,
it's not your fault,
I set myself up,
made a fool of myself,
See me smile,
as I lie and say I'm okay,
I'll give you hugs and well wishes,
and you will be on your way,
See your happiness,
It's all I ever wanted,
I knew this would happen,
but that isn't any comfort.
