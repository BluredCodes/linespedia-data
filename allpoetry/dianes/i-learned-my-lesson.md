title: "I Learned My Lesson"

I learned my lesson
so hard and true
No more writes 
from my heart so true...

I swore I wouldn't
put on display
my life like a diary 
What can I say...

I tried to keep it
not personal
but i write what i feel
and i feel what i must...

How was I 
suppose to know
who to and who 
not trust...

So no more writes
from my heart to your eyes
My heart is closed
Forever in disguise..."sigh"
