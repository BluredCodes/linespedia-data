---
title: "Let Me Find, Love, and Learn"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "let-me-find-love-and-learn"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60014-Let-Me-Find--Love--and-Learn-by-dianes"

Let me find
PEACE
deep within
the recesses
of my 
SOUL.
Let me fall in
LOVE
But let it be with
ME,
Because only once I
LEARN
to
LOVE
MYSELF,
Can I
LET
OTHERS
LOVE
ME 
TOO..
