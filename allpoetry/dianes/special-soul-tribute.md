Did you know you are a special soul
who makes so many smile

Even though your miles away
your warmth spreads among us like the sun's rays

How many times you made me laugh
turned my grey skies blue

Gave me little friendship hugs
till the sun came shining through

You are such a special soul
and a friend to many

I'm so glad you are my friend
May our friendship be never ending.ty for being u....just u.....*smiles*
