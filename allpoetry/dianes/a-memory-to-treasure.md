---
title: "A Memory to Treasure"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "a-memory-to-treasure"
source: "AllPoetry"

I still remember the first time we met
how you smiled even through your tiredness
My plane was late and I didn't know
that you were waiting as the day did go.

When I got the message 
that you were there
I dropped my suitcases
All I could do was stare.

The clerk said I guess you're surprised
she could see the shock in my eyes
I was so nervous to place that call
Never even thought to look at the clock on the wall.

Three AM
but I couldn't sleep
till I finally met you
and our eyes would meet.

I placed my finger over the peephole on the door
Wanting us to see each other
the first time face to face
It was a memory time will never erase.

The smile that came across your face
as you opened the door and said I was late
Of course I never knew you would be there
You drove so far to show your friendship and that you cared.

Such a short time 
that we had together
But it's a memory
I will always treasure.
