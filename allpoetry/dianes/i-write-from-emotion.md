---
title: "I Write from Emotion"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "i-write-from-emotion"
source: "AllPoetry"

I write from emotion
it's the only way I can...

I don't have the rhyme and metre
it's just how I am...

So when you read my writes
it's just emotion coming through...

I don't expect everyone to understand that
it's just my feelings in my writings...

my emotions real and true
these writes I share with you..."just me"
