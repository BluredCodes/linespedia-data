Don't ask for my forgiveness
for it's not for me to give,

Don't tell me I will be okay
it's not my life you live,

Don't tell me how much you care
Cuz, I'm the one you hurt,

Don't give me platitudes and hugs
while I sit here imagining you being smug,

Don't tell me I will find someone
Cuz, I wasn't looking before,

Don't tell me it was a coincidence
you don't know how this ocurred,

Don't expect me to heal and to forget
the pain which I feel,

You both have to remember
I'm a person, I feel, I'm real...
