I will never understand
how a woman or a man
can go from one to the other
no feelings do they utter...

They just go jump in bed
satifying carnal needs
Getting satisfaction
from mere attractions...

I guess I must be
old fashioned you see
Cuz I need much more
then just to say I scored...

To me it's something special
to share with one you love
Cuz only then 
Do you understand the meaning of...

If I share myself
they get not only my body
but my heart, it's special can't you see
I give myself completely...

I feel it is a gift
you don't just blindly give
Cuz when the morning comes
you have to live with what you've done...

So I'll never understand
how a woman and a man
can be promiscuous
and do it without love...
