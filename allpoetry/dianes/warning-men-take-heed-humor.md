Right now if I had a man to fk
I'd do him good I'd mess him up
I'd make him take away my pain
probably inflict some on him
cuz im insane......

So take my warning
and run for cover
or with my breasts
you i might smother...

You might enjoy
how I ravage you
but you will be sore later
and black and blue...

I really am not promiscuous
but i am hurt and ticked off
so please don't anyman come to close
cuz i will use you then go have a smoke......lol"little levity"
