Where do I go from here
directions aren't very clear
I've thought hard
and I've prayed...

Please Lord
Show me the way...

I'm lost 
please come find me
I can't see
my tears do blind me...

Please Lord
Show me the way...

I look for answers
that I seek
you say the world
will be inherited by the meek...

Please Lord
Show me the way...

I'm here Lord
over here
sometimes I cower
in fear...

Please Lord
Show me the way...

Give me your
unconditional love
your sweet blessings 
from above...

Please Lord
Show me the way...

When I die Lord
take me there
to you for
eternal care...

Please Lord
Show me the way...sorta like my personal prayer song i wrote
