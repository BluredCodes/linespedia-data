---
title: "Listen do you Hear"
writer: "dianes"

Listen do you hear it,
the song of the whipperwill,
and the chirps of the crickets,
in the thickets upon the hill.

Listen do you hear my heart beat,
as in beats in time with yours,
Both are calls of nature,
Singing to our ears.

Listen do you hear the doves coo,
in their songs of love,
Listen do you hear my whisper,
as the moon shines above.

Listen to my soul cry,
searching out for yours,
Can you hear me sighing,
Listening for sure...
