---
title: "Closure for Me and Maybe You"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "closure-for-me-and-maybe-you"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56934-Closure-for-Me-and-Maybe-You-by-dianes"

One day you will remember
how i loved you true
how each and every moment
i only thought of you

One day I will remember you
and not be filled with pain
I will keep only happy thoughts
try to forget regret and disdain

You brought me happiness
and light into my life
and whether your words were true or not
I felt more then just a spark

People come into your life
for oh so many reasons
some come for a moment
some come for a season

You were but a season
not meant for you to stay
I'll never know the reason
Why life's path took you away

So go on and be happy
bring joy to someone new
but just always remember
I loved you...it was never a secret how i fell for you
i didn't try to hide it
and everyone knew
so now i need closure
i'm sorry that i had to post
my heart upon the internet
no more will i be a dope...

"be happy and i will try to be happy for you"
