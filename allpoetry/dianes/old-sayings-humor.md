I keep thinking of old sayings 
that I used to hear
From the time I was a child
and remembering from year to year.

Step on a crack
break your mothers back (why is mom on the sidewalk)

See a penny pick it up
all the day you'll have good luck (found money, couldn't it have been a brinks money bag that fell of truck)

Liar Liar
Pants on Fire (hmmmm, that one i never understood, is liar a pyro too)

When I was your age (zzzzzzzz, always fell asleep after that phrase)

As long as you live under my roof (yah well since I was a kid and no money like I have a choice)

Eat your spinach 
there are starving children all over the world (envelope please, address)

Someday when you have kids
I hope they grow up to be just like you (THIS CURSE WORKS)

Honey, I'm sure it happens to all men (first lie a woman learns and has to be convincing)

Where you born in a barn
close the door (no but maybe i was conceived in one)

Who do you think you are talkin to
one of your friends (no i like my friends)

But the day you hear your mother or father's words
come out of your mouth 
You know
YOU ARE GETTIN OLD!!!!!Please feel free to comment or add any of your old ones that you remember hearing as you grew up and what you thought when you heard them said to you.....thanks
