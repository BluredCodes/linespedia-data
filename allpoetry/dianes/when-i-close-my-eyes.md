title: "When I Close my Eyes"
writer: "dianes"

When I close my eyes
I see you
Colors of light
Still shining through
Your light
Has brought me hope
Along with love
The hope I thought was remote...

When I close my eyes
I think of you
Wondering what
Are we going to do
I feel your love
Coming shining through
In your words
I know your love is true...

When I close my eyes
I think of you
My love I send to you
When I close my eyes
I dream of you
Hopefully one day
I will open them
And be with you...
