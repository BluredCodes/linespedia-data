title: "Turtle"

Do you wonder why
I hide within my shell
Only peeking my head out
To grab a bite or smell.

The world is very dangerous
with preditors galore
So I'm very lucky I have
This armor shell I adore.

I can pull myself inside it
to keep everyone at bay
Only to come out
when it's safe and you are away.

I admit it does get lonely
this little shell for one
But it protects me 
and lets no harm become.

So I think I'll keep my armor
stay inside my little shell
Cuz, then you can't gobble me up
and make soup out of me as well.
