Words Words Words
that's all they really are...

I read the letters upon the page
once it was blank
now stained with age...

Words Words Words
that's all they really are...

They take me places
I've never been
Even places deep within...

Words Words Words
that's all they really are...

They make me feel 
I can't explain why
Others don't feel them, why do I...

Words Words Words
that's all they really are...

For if they are only mere words
does feeling them
make me sound absurd...

Words Words Words
that's all they really are...
