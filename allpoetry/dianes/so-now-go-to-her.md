---
title: "So Now Go to Her"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "so-now-go-to-her"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58117-So-Now-Go-to-Her-by-dianes"

I don't want you anymore
don't come knockin on my door
I don't need your pain
I have nothing left to offer
you have nothing left to gain

Even though I love you
and you know it's true
I will walk away 
You have left me blue

I will try to be
the one you see
who can put aside
the pain deep inside

So now go to her
be free to love
No feelings for me
none do i see

You have moved on
now I need to carry on
My tears of pain
are but in vain

So now go to her
which is where you are
Go by plane
go by car

But I will remember you
through my pain
my tears still fall 
like pouring rain

So now go to her
Happy she will be
she revels in
your company

You say you've found
that special love
like a gift came down
from up above

So now go to her
for me it will be rough
what will always hurt
is my love wasn't enough...
