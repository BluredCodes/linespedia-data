title: "My Heart Floats in the Clouds"

I lie upon a grassy knoll, and look up in the sky,
watching each passing cloud, as each one floats by,
I pick and choose the animals, out of which each cloud  has made
wondering do you do the same thing, while you lie so far away...

I close my eyes and wonder,
do you see the same clouds too,
I wonder if you know how high up my heart floats
like the clouds when I'm with you...

I fall off to sleep there in the summer sun,
knowing in my heart, our love that has just begun
I wonder do you feel the heat, of passion at it's best,
do you know how many times, you put my love to the test...

My dreams bring you closer,
a smiles plays across my face,
In my dreams you're lying next to me,
my head, genlty, on your chest, I place...

I know that distance seperates us,
each in a different place,
But a connection between us had been made,
one which no one can erase...

So when you see the clouds pass by,
find a cloud, that looks like a heart,
It's one that I send to you, in the breeze,
to carry my love to you, while we are apart...
