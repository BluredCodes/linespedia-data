I'm not a number
don't care what society says
I'm a person
with a heart
I do my part
From the very start
it seems
that all I'm good for
is to 
work
cook
and clean
raise some kids
Do the wash
Carpool
Fundraisers cost
Work full time
now be a nurse
and banker too
what more can I do
When do I 
begin to count for more
then a number
at work and home
When do people
stop keeping score
of what I did
and continue to want more
Yes, I'm whining
I do it well
If you don't like it
Go to Hell
I'm not a number
do you hear
Come back when
you find a person dear
Ok I'm done
Venting over
What's that 
Why didn't I
pick up the cleaning
you must be dreaming
Get it yourself
or get an elf
To do your bidding
and I'm not kidding
I'm not a number
but get mine
Work and You
are out of line
I'm not a number
do you hear
have I made that
perfectly clear...."rant and vent lol"
