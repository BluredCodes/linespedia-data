He had not only her heart
but her soul
Not freely given
it was out of control
She read his words
upon the page
Touched her heart
longing of age
Opened her trust
gave it her all
Only to find
it was words
after all..."generic" for all those who've experienced what they thought was "real" only to find out it wasn't.
