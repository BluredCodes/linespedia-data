title: "I Speak for All the People"
writer: "dianes"

I speak for all the people
who have loved in vain
You know who you all are
basically we are all the same

You give all you have
to the person you hold dear
Hoping that they will return
your love without tears

Your relationship may last
a few weeks or months
or if you're lucky many years
You never really know
which direction it will veer

You try to hold onto
all the memories that you have
Remembering the special times
of the love you had

You try to forget the pain
and the faults you both had
You blame yourself for things
of which control you never had

You try to move past the pain
and to start life anew
But you never do forget the love
of the person your love once knew..."forgive me if I speak for the broken hearted"
