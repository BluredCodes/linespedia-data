Damn, I need a punching bag
to slam my fists into
I'm not  a violent person
So a dummies gotta do!

Vent all my frustrations
sweat my anger out
Ball up my fists and punch
Gonna scream and shout!

Whammo! Punch! Slam! Bang!
Curse and carry on
Punch this damn bag
So I can just move on!

Can't explain my feelings
doesn't do no good
You are not in my shoes
I'm just misunderstood!

Only problem with punching
and venting out all the crap
is sometimes this damn dummy
send me sailing back with it's slap!

But I get up and I fight back
Gotta get strong once more
and when I'm done with my punching bag
I will leave it till I need to vent once more!"To my friend, I'll hold it first then you can hold it for me!
just so the bag doesn't knock us on our asses!"
