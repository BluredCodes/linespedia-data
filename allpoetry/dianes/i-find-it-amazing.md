---
title: "I Find it Amazing"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "i-find-it-amazing"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59578-I-Find-it-Amazing-by-dianes"
---

I find it simply amazing
to read what others write
How they feel the same feelings
and pen them with all their might

Each writing is unique
each in it's own way
But basically the emotion in them
are the way we feel, just different days

They all tell a story
whether fictional or true
Some make you feel happy
Some make you feel blue

You read the words upon the page
and no matter what they say
its how you interpret them
and the visuals that they play

So tell me your stories
pen them as you may
and I will continue reading them
feeling them, visualizing as your words say"sometimes you read other people's writes and go I felt that, I thought that or I coulda wrote that"
