---
title: "Speak to Me"
writer: "dianes"
slug: "speak-to-me"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54705-Speak-to-Me-by-dianes"
---

Speak to me of your dreams and wishes,
I want to  make them all come true,

Speak to me of your past pain,
I'll dry your tears and closely hold you,

Speak to me of your fears,
And what they mean to you,

Let me give you the strength to conquer them,
Let me help you muddle through,

Let me be there when you are lonely,
Hold you when you are sad,

Let me watch you while you are sleeping,
Falling asleep holding my hand,

Let me be there when you are happy,
To see the warmth of your smile,

Speak to me of your wants and needs,
I will offer whatever  I can,

You know I want you to be happy,
After all you are my man..."sigh"
