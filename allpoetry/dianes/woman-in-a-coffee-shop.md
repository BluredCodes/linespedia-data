title: "Woman in a Coffee Shop"

The coffee shop is filled each day
and each day she sits by the same window
at the same time sipping caufe'ole
you wonder is she longing for her yesterdays
the ones filled with her laughter and loving ways...

From looking at this woman you would never guess
all of her feelings and how life put her to the test
She's not as old as she appears
but trials and tribulations
have left scars and tears...

No one seems to notice her
People pass by and do not acknowledge her
It seems she is a ghost
An apparition of someone who sat and waited
for maybe someone who never kept their date...

So did her spirit never leave
and did her heart stay
and when he didn't arrive did her heart get so heavily laden
that she just decided to leave it here with her memories
Remembering her last words to him...

I have waited forever for you and will wait for you forever...
