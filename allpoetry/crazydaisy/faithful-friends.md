title: "Faithful Friends"

Friends are honest, friends are good

Friends always love, even when misunderstood.

Friends are faithful, friends are kind

Friends are loyal, to our faults are blind.

Friends are true through good and bad

Friends are listeners when you are sad.

Friends are fun, friends make you laugh

They will always lead you down the right path.
