---
title: "Daisies"
writer: "crazydaisy"
slug: "daisies"
source: "AllPoetry"

Daisies make me happy

They smell like laughy taffy

I'm only kidding but for real

Daisies make me really feel

Like Laughing, jumping, singing songs,

Daisies make me happy!
