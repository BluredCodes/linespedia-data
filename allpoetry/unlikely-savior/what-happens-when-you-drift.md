the water is clear because
the grass is green in the sky though
the trees are sprouting from my
raspberry pie.
this illogic makes more sense
than the mess the world is in
because....
the water  isn't really there
its everywhere. it's....
a quarter to two
sleep is coming, coming for me now.
you can't stop it
neither can i because
i drank the water.
it became clear.another old one. hope you like it.
