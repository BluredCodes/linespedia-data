it's 3 A.M.
all fear has left
night still, but day
approaches.
moving ever slowly
yet swiftly.
waiting patiently,
yet wasting no time.
daylight to drive away
my darkness:
rouse me from my sleep
peaceful and full of dreams,
to cold, hard reality.
my darkness is my friend,
lover, protector.this is something that i actually wrote a few years back, and there's a lot more that i plan to post now that i have a window to the world that i can share thruogh.
