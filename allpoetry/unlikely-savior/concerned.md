confront,
decline,
reject,
deject.
full of
contradictions.
never rid of 
addictions.
riddled with 
conflictions;
concerned,
learned,
burned,
hurt.
addictions stem from
rejections,eased by
contradictions fed by 
conflictions.
be you never 
concerned.
hurt.the second in the alcoholic haze poems.
