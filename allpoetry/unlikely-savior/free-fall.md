keeping you up
breaking you down
why am i here

keeps me up
breaks me down
why am i here

i'm in free fall
somedays it works
most it doesn't 
wish it would
wish it could

keeps me up
breaks me down
why am i here
keps me up
breaks me down
free fall

wish life were good
just a pile of shit
can't figuire it out
even in and out of doubt

keeps me up
breaks me down
why am i here
my life is like a free falli wrote this when drunk on halfa bottle of jim beam on the last night i drank. there were more from that night and they will follow.
