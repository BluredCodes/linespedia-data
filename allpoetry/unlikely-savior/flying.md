title: "flying?"
writer: "unlikely savior"

fly to the angels
on imposed wings
these are the banes 
my addictions bring.
life hurts,
day to day
carrying on, hefting this weight
forging on, no time to wait.
alcoholic haze,
gives comfort to my mind.
numbs my pain,
hides me from the world.
safety in the bottle
never to face reality again.
the pain is too much,
too much to bear.
my heart is weighted down 
with pain and fear;
maybe the alcohol helps it float...
fly to the angels;
i want to be home.third in the alcoholic haze series.
