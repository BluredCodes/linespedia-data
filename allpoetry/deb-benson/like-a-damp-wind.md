This is written for my brother who I spent the weekend with. We hashed over some old memories that neither one of us wanted to explore, but we did.

She walks thru empty doorways
Down the narrow halls of memory
Drenched in anxiety
She sits and explores the memory
It tumbles over itself
The memory
Like a damp wind
Rises
Sifts thru her hair 
And moves on
Leaving only the fondest ones intact
