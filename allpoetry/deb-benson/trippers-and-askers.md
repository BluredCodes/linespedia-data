Hi All:  when I saw this line of Walt Whitmans for a challenge I took it
"Trippers and askers surround me."  It made me think of Woodstock and the 60's

Surround me
Trippers and askers
Fools and beggars
Drinkers and dancers
Poets and music-makers

Fields filled
People unknown
United by music
Peace and love
Thousands became one

Thru rain and mud
A pot-filled haze
Thousands became one

Brothers and sisters
Not of blood
But of soul
Thousands became one

Today my heart remembers
Exquisite peace and joy
Wanting the world to become one

Trippers and askers
Fools and beggars
Drinkers and dancers
Poets and music-makers
Dreamers
Surround me again
