title: "Daily Rum"
writer: "Deb Benson"

The light from the lantern grew dim
The shadows began to shrink
I watched him lean against the wall
Sneaking from his flask a drink
How sad to think he needed
To drink his daily rum
His family was waiting
As passersby look and say
"What a bum"
A functional alcoholic
Able to work each day
Never making it home sober
Having to drink along the way
He finally trudges up the stairs
Dinner waits for him
He sups a bit, then goes to sit
Until the light from the lantern grows dim.
