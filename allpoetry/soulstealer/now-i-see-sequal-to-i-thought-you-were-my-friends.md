You used to
share those jokes
with me.

But now I see.

You wanna talk about me,
Joke about me
And not give a shit about me

Now i see*explodes with anger* AHHHHHHHH i dont care they pretend to be my friends ill pretend to be their friends I DONT NEED FRIENDS ANYWAY I AM PERFECLY FINE ALL BY MY LONESOME* go hides in a corner*
