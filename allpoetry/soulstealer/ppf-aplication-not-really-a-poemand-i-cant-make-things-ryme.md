I'll drink 10 gallons of water
Then pee on brothers head
i'll do my speshul pee pee dance
That goes like this

drink 10 glasses of water
nothing other 
Kick up your right leg 
And twirl in a circle

Then pout your lips and shake your booty
Pick your target
And SPLASH

Thats my dance 
Wonderful isnt it
I could make cars crash
but i wontsniffles i probably wont get in cuz wahh*runs around screaming* hehe thank you foryour time
