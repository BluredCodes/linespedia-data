STOP STARING AT ME!
Your eyes penatrate through my skin
To the center of my soul

Your wide eyes staring
Staring
Staring

STOP THE STARING
i cant stand it
it hurts inside
i dont know why
it just does
it makes me want to hurt you
Badly.
Worse then i ever have

go to hell
