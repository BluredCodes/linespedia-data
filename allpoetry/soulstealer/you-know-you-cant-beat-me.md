You know you cant beat me.
Why do you even try.
I'm not saying im better then you.
But you think your better then me.

But your not.
You cant beat me.
Thats obvious.
Im smarter.
I'm taller.
And i'm MUCH stronger.

So can you please
Tell me why you think.
You can beat me.go away
