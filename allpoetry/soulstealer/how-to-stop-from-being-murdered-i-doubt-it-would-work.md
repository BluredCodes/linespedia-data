I scream when you enter my room
I look at your face
Your sunken eyes and ratty long black hair
Yous scare me

I look at your hand
I see a knife
The other hand i see a gun

What do you want from me
You come closer with the knife hand outstretched
I wont your blood you say
I look at you take the knife and use the handle and open up a scab
It bleeds
Here you are i say
You look at me and laugh
Then you leavethis is like a story i guess but its crappy really crappy hehehehehhehehehhehehhehehehhehehehhehehehhehehheehehhe mabey i should leave this website for awhile and gather myself*laughs hystericlly and leaves teh room*
