Somtimes i wish i could trade places with you
I see you at school
Surronded by friends
You have perfect hair
The best clothes
And your popular.

I love my life i do
But i wish i could just trade it with you
For a little while at least

I could be you and you could be me
Everyone likes me for me
But people like you because if they do theyll be liked to
People like me because they no me
But if im you
Id have more friends
