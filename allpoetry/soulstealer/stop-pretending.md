Stop pretending!

You know your not that perfect
Cheerful
Jolly girl 
That you pretend to be

You try and play off your feelings
But people can tell

Your not that perfect angel 
That everyone thinks you are
Even at home
no one knows who you really are

It's amazing you have any friends at all
With that false act
You know you hate them
You hate them all

So stop pretendingstopp preteending
