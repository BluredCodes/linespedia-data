Soulstealers of America.
We are brave
We are strong
We fight for what is right
Somtimes fight for wut is wrong.

We steal from the mean
And keep to ourselves
Somtimes we stack boring souls
On our huge shelves

We steal souls from everybody
Not just the mean.
The more pain your in
The better the soul.
If its to happy we throw
it in the garbage bin

I personally
Like souls
From the tortured and
Insane.
And of course souls from someone
who has felt much pain.soulstealers of america
