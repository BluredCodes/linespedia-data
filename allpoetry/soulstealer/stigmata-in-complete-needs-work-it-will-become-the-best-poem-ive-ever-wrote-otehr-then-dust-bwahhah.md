im lying in a pool of my own blood
there are nail marks on my wrist

Impossible jesus was nailed by the palm of his hand
No it was his wrist

My feet are also nailed 
or pinned whichever one u choose

There are slashes on my back
am i being crucified?

I rise in the air
taking the form 
of jes us on teh crossmhmm
