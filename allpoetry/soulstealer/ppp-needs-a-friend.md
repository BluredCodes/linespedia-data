I wanted a friend 
Ever so badly
Like Nam has a blue cow
Named moo.

Im gonna find me a friend
And name her Riley
Or mabey Taliya.

I set of for adventure in the deep 
Woodlends of evil pixy town.
Looking for a black unicorn
With a golden crown.

I stumbles across an acorn
How weird.
When that unicorn suddenly appeared.

She neighed and made
Sounds unicorns make.
She smelt like double decker
Chocolate cake.

I'll be your friend till the very end
I said
And she responded
With a neigh and a toss of her head

She said climb on and shed bring me to my bed

So now i have a friend
I decided on the name Taliya.
Shes the most magnificant thing
Since nams
Blue cowyay taliya woo hoo
