I'll be your friend
Before you know me
I'll have your trust
Before you earn it

But I wont be around for long
I'm like a cloud.
Being there for awwhile.
Thne i'm gone.

Where did i go?
Why did i lie
Why did i leave withought saying
Goodbye

Because i figured it wouldnt be 
As painful
As staring at you eye to eye
Shaking your hands
And saying good bye.i got the topic from a song i heard on the radio or tv i dunt remember
