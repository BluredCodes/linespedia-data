I lug around
To bring this jug around
Thats filled with water.

For the other
PPfs
Who need there water.

Well who brings me mine?
Who makes me a shrine?
I just want some cool refreshing water.

I have to go get it myself
Because i have no apprentice
To bring me my divine water.

Well poo
I'll just hop on a kangaroo
And it'll find me my water.i want my water
