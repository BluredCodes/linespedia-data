Dear Mr Osama,
Why would you to that to us mr osama
Why would you kill your own people to do that mr osama?
What have we done to you

We dont like everything you do 
But we dont go and destroy 
Your country

We never did anything to you
(not that i can remember)
But you get some people
And knock down the twin towers

We let our women wear nail polish
And even let them wear belly shirts
And short shorts

Is that why you did that to us
But what you dint know
Was doing that
Brought us together
Not tear us apartblech i wrote this out of no where and no disrespect  to anyone in afghanistan becuz i feel very sorry that they dont have as much cures for sickness liek we do and they dont have as much food as we do
