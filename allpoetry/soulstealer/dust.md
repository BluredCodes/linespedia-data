Dust
The dry dust
Suffocates me,
As if a pillow has
Been placed over my face

It's so warm out here,
So dry,
The dust hurts my eyes.

It flies into my mouth,
As if it's a piece of candy.
The taste is horrible.
I choke on it.
The wind blows so hard im lifted
Off my feet,
And after all this
