title: "Best Friends"
writer: "Stereotype"

I will be here through pain, through hurt,
I will stand by you when you make mistakes,
For you are more than just a people,
You are my best friends.

No Matter what anyone says, whatever they do,
Remember that i will always be there for you
For you are my best friends.

I will not doubt you, i will stand by you,
For i always know you would do the same too
For you are my best friends.
