Oh in the days of the pumpkin child a finding we did go,
to find our good friend Roland at the 2nd Hurst Horse show.
We were not aware then that Reading held our fate
to meet Roland Ramsdale again out good old Christian mate.
To Hell he told us we would go unless our thinking changed
a christian BBQ on sunday was soon to be arranged!
He visited our houses to try and save our souls
gave us handy leaflets on "how to reach our goals"
But we were destined for fame with Obnoxious Child in mind
We will never 4get u Roland, u were one of a kind!!!
