title: "Still"

I wait
Silence
I look
Stillness
Will it ever end?
The ache inside my body as i remember you
It makes me sick
Where was god?
So great and almighty, yet he took your life.
You. So kind so sweet - Gone.
And here i am over a year later
Still writing, still crying...still remembering
Holding on to the memories
that can never be taken away.
I would swap them for you back n e day.
