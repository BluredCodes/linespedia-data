title: "You"
writer: "Stereotype"
slug: "you"
source: "AllPoetry"

You were the man.
The man who changed so many lives,
Who touched us without knowing,
You were him.

You were the man.
The man who loved by day,
Who loved by night,
You were him.

You were the man.
The man who laughed and smiled,
frowned and winked,
You were him.

You were the man.
