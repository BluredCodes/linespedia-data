You've been gone for a while now 
yet still i ask myself why? and how?
would it have made a difference that one more day
all i needed 4 me 2 say
Goodbye, Au Revoir, Auf Wiedersehen,
No more suffering no more pain
Auf Wiedersehen, goodbye, Au revoir
damn those cigarettes, nicotine and tar
Au revoir, Auf Wiedersehen, goodbye
to u i dare not tell a lie
when i raise my eyes and say 'I love you'
and wish i heard 'I love you too'I kno i may have spelt the german and french wrong, i don't learn german so i guessed the spelling, but i spose i dont; have an excuse 4 french if its spelt wrong, ah well, u get wot i mean!
