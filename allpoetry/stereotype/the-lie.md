You think you know me - you don't
You say you'll do things - you won't
you only see wot u want 2 see
look deeper inside, theres another me
I think i know you - I don't
I want to do things - I won't
I try to look beneath the skin
but with you i just can't win
We could fite 4eva and a day
but then wot would there b left to say
we're both to stubborn 4 our own good
u give in, i never would.
Sometimes i think you would b better off without me
Im not that great im sure u'll soon see
I could never tell you this
that You and i will never live in bliss
Maybe we are living a lie
and if so then why?
