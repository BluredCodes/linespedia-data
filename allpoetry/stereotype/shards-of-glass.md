title: "Shards of Glass"

Shards of glass lay all around,
her heavy breathing the only sound.
Pain.Pain is all she can think,
with each breath she feels her heart sink.
The jolting pain of the sharp edge through her skin
she covers her mouth to hush the din,
no energy left to put up a fite
her face drained of colour turning slowly white.
Sad moonbeams in her innocent eyes
but no one hears her innocent cries
she did what she thought she had to do
no way back now and nobody knew
what she had been thinking, what had entered her mind
she knew no one should have thoughts of this kind.
and now all that remains are shards of glass
no one knows as the hours pass
but she is happier where she lies alone
silent and lonely, completely unknown

The way she felt all her life.
