writer: "Stereotype"

Lifes a cruel world with so much to lose,
given as a gift of which we abuse.
Taken for granted seen no other way
but each one of us shall have a debt to pay.
Through greed and hurt we will feel the pain
spread through our bodies, through every vein.
In this "perfect" world we're all alone
to survive and brave the great unknown.
We may think others know us well
but they only know what we will tell.
Judgements passed on people without care
material feelings in a world that aint fair
and still we go on to hurt eachother again and again
trying to protect ourselves from any pain
Impossible tasks yet still we try 
and continue too untill we die
Life life, fill life, love life
for no one knows how long we will have life.
