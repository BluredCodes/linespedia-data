We've moved on
Hadn't realised
Only looking back do you see a future.
We've changed so much
without knowing
Yet i see now how different we've become.

So little time passes, so much happens
Unable to predict the future, we live each day as it comes.
In the future we will look back at now,
How different will we be then?
If the things that changed us were absent from the past
would we be this way?
and as everyone changes and grows around me i notice how i am growing too
good or bad? i don't know.

Something will never change though.
The Past.
What we had
No one can take that away.
