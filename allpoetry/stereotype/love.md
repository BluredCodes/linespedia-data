---
title: "Love?"
writer: "Stereotype"
slug: "love"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60388-Love--by-Stereotype"
---

You feel me fall yet make no effort to catch me,
I feel myself fall right through your arms,
but there is nothing there to break my fall

You were there to break my heart.
