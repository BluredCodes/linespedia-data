Since that first meeting,
fleeting emotions passed,
shrouded in promise,
and I still love you.

Minutes, days, months, fly away,
and yet your memory keeps fresh
awakening me every morning 
with that same foolish grin 
and kissing me goodnight
with your crystalline eyes.

It took me a fall,
a winter, 
a summer
before I realized that your eyes
reflected my desperate feelings.

Back then, I pretended 
and looked past you,
to only my best friend,
missing out on a love
I'll never experience. 

You walked uphill and down for me
You waited for me to need you
You loved me when I collapsed into myself
And now I love you more than you know 
And you love her.

You moved on, rapidly and without remorse
casting your intense glances, your sweet smiles 
upon new territory, forgetting the unforgettable
And I'm alright, I'm okay.

You're only in my thoughts
About every other minute.
I look at you, baring my soul,
and you sit at the table behind me,
smiling and laughing with her.

Rip me apart, steal my heart, tear my soul
It's not fair; I never wished this to happen,
I wish you would disappear in the mists of the past,
