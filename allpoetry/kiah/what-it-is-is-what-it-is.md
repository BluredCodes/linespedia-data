---
title: "What it is, is what it is."
writer: "Kiah"
slug: "what-it-is-is-what-it-is"
source: "AllPoetry"

I need
and
I want
and
I have

I bleed
and
I hunt
and
I break
and
I am

dead inside
half-alive
full of pride

Because I am
I am
I am
in full stride
on this wild ride

I am
so very fucking alive.I wrote this here at my crappy temp job.  It is very rough and as such sort of a choppy read.
