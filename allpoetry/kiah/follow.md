title: "Follow"
writer: "Kiah"
slug: "follow"
source: "AllPoetry"

Follow.
The flourescent paper,
glowing neon wood way.

Follow
Painted square mountians,
electric firelignt beacons.

Follow,
in yeilding dissidence.

Follow. 
Stone paths,
leading to plate glass tombs.

Follow,
your need to cosume.

Follow,
in compulsory non-conformism.

The vivid invasions of direct insiuations.

Perfect blistering numbness through constricting release.
