I'm gonna get you fucker.
I'm gonna spit on you,
and laugh in distain.

I gonna fuck you up.
I'm gonna make your life hell,
and laugh in distain.

Big tall man,
with a slurring hand.
Raise your wit to my sword.

I'm gonna show you hate.
Now you will know love.
Love so strong it destroys fear.
Hate so real, I can taste it.

I'm gonna love it,
and savor it.
Do you want your savior yet?

Think...
and
blink.
Contemplate...
and sink,
into the hell you've stirred up.

You didn't know who he was,
you thought he was weak.
You never thought about me,
the younger, psychotic freak.

FUCNIKGGODDAMNEDFOOL!
You rang the bell,
and now it's time for school.

Are you ready?
Are you?
Too bad. 
Neither was he.
It's a coffin,
or it's me.

This is your big chance.
Big man, in slovenly pants.
My head is big,
and so is my heart.
Let's see which will break,
here alone in the dark.

Sacrifice is the ultimate devotion.
Show me your resolve,
your going to feel my emotion."Don't you know God hates?"  - Delta 9

Lean on me,
when your not strong.
