First of all you lied to me
And told me twasn't true
Second of all you told me the truth
Which increased my hate for you
Third of all you go behind my back
You took my heart; You stole my love
No, its not normal, Nor is it alright
For this betrayal you have bestown upon me
Has turned my bright days into cold, dark nightsHow is it??
