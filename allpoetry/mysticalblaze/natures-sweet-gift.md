Natures Sweet Gift

The calm breeze of a beatiful spring day 
The sounds of bird singing their joyful hymn
The flower blooming in the month of may 
As cute squirrels jump from limb to limb

The water ripples are never ending
The glow of the moonlight on a calm lake
The moon says good night and stats descending 
As the sun rises and begins to wake


The way the snow covers the freezing ground 
It falls like someone's gently crying tears
The racing pace of the shivering sound
As it storms downward captivating my fears

Nature's sweet gifts play music for my heart
