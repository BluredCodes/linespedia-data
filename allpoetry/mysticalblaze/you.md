Today I saw you
For the very first time
My heart lit up
When your gaze met mine
The gentle expression on your face
The way those beautiful brown eyes shine
Causes my heart to race
I glance away to see if your eyes are still watching me 
I look quickly back to see
And in fact you are still staring at me
Your eyes calling my name 
There is a desperate look on your face
You reach for me and take my hand
You walk me down an enchanted path
You turn towards me and just stare
I know what you want from me, but do you care?
I told you didnt't want any heartache
Or any pain from you
So you look me straight in the eye and say...
