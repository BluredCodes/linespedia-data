Today I walked out the door without saying goodbye
I ran outside and let out a huge sigh 

I was so glad to be away from there
I ran away so quickly without a care

I walked down the road in the pourning rain
As I try to rid myself from all the pain

Some tears gently fall down my cheek
Beacause my sadness and anger have finally reached their peak

I could no longer control this rage anymore
It all erupted from me at once like a flesh being tore

I collapsed on the ground laying on the cold wet road
As my body gives up an goes into dieing mode

I just lay there and look up at the sky
I sit and wander as I watch the stars fly by

I close my eyes and doze into sleep
Now the time has come for me to no longer weep

My thoughts washed my anger deminished
Finally the day has come that my life is finishedComments???
