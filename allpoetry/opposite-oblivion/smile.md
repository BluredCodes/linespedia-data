title: "smile"
writer: "opposite oblivion"

your smile intrigues me into a world of pure enjoyment never before have I seen such beauty, like a beautiful orchestra of melodic melody Enters the mind of the loveless as i gaze upon heaven here on earth, if only I can see that smile , never a sweeter symphony. do I have the right to feel this way?
should I let you slip through my fingers like sunlight through the clouds just as the sun finds it's summit. tis the question of love much like a message in a bottle, can be tossed to and fro in the violent undercurrent of life or can be found and the words are the same to a person as your smile FIND ME!
