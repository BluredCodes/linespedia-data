---
title: "beauty unlikely"
writer: "opposite oblivion"
slug: "beauty-unlikely"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60032-beauty-unlikely-by-opposite-oblivion"
---

sweet and soft,
lovely but scared oblivious?
does she really comprehend
when I see when I look
I gaze to be surrounded in everything perfect
to be at last found, or lost more thann ever before

I long to hold and embrace with goddess
of my heart. sweeter than soul food 
my heart beats, does she know
