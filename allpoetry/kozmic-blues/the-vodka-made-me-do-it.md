I'm sorry.
I didn't meant it.
Really.
It didn't mean anything, I don't love her,
I love you.
It was just a kiss, I swear, it meant nothing.
It's you I want, it's you I love.
It's always been you.
We just got to talking, and then she fell over,
She was just a stupid drunk, I was only picking her up,
And then she kissed me.
She,
Kissed ME.
I'm so sorry.
She meant nothing to me.
And you mean everything.
You are my everything,
I love you,
Please forgive me.
I love you,
I want you,
Don't leave me, please don't leave me!
I NEED you.
The vodka made me do it....This didn't really happen, I'm not actually a typical cheating male bastard :)
