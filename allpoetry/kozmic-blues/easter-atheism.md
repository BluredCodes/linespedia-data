I'm sorry friendly Christian, but the Easter train has sailed.
You've closed the stores to pray to God, to pray to He who failed.
Off you run to worship Jesus, your saviour and His son.
But when you ask for help from him, prepare, for you'll get none.
To ransom we are held, the disbelievers of your Lord.
We aren't allowed to shop today, we sit at home, ignored.
Is it too much to ask, that you keep it to yourself?
You believe in what you will, and we'll cherish our health.
For nothing else is there, this is my opinion only.
But distant in the stars of night, a friend of mine is lonely.
Just like us he searches, and just like us he breathes.
But will he ever find us? Our lonesome friend, ET.
There is no God above is, and there's noone down below.
The only thing to find us, will be life from the unknown.
From the bowels of space and darkness, a light shines down on us.
