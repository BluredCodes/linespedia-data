title: "From Heaven Sent"
writer: "KozMic BluEs"

When the mountains fall, and the world but dies,
Will you love me when, the seas are skies.
The blue of sky, and blue of sea,
Together come, like you and me.
Distant sun, who burns so bright,
Sets upon the world, this night.
Tomorrows sun shall rise again,
But not the same, not now, not yet.
The misty morn, and hidden heat,
The sun is shunned into retreat.
Clouds so grey, like rhino skin,
A light so bright, lies deep within.
As rain beats down, the day so dark,
I see a light within, a spark.
Not in heaven, not in sky,
But inside you, you burn so bright.
You are my sun, my guiding light,
You are the one I love tonight.When one listens to Stairway To Heaven, Sweet Child O' Mine and Imagine, this is what one comes up with...
