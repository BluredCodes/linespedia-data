Jesus said that I had sinned,
I took a toke and I just grinned.
And Jesus laughed a laugh of kings,
And we rejoiced and smoked our thing.

Jesus said that he had died,
I laughed so hard I almost cried.
And Jesus frowned and Jesus sighed,
And Jesus said "It's true, I died".

Jesus said he died for sins,
I lit a pipe and drew it in.
And Jesus said "Now hold it in",
"For those who hold are those who win".

Jesus said that I was bad,
I should have loved and not been mad.
He showed me this and now I'm glad,
With Jesus' weed I'm no more sad.

Jesus said I had been good,
He said I'd done the things I should.
My life had now begun again.
So Jesus said "Goodbye, My Friend".:)
