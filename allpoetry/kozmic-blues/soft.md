Not a word,
Only breath,
On her neck,
On her body.
Warm beads of sweat,
Rapidly appearing over her soft, smooth skin.
My hands come alive with a mind of their own,
Rubbing here, sliding there.
Hers the same,
Only smoother.
Her skin so soft, so smooth.
And the passion burns, deep into the night.
Not a word,
Only Breath,
Not a sound,
All except...
Pleasure,
Passion,
Ecstacy,
Love.
