And together we shall march,
We'll march towards the light.
Together we will live our lives,
Together we shall fight.

We'll board the ship to neverland,
We'll sail off out to sea.
Towards the setting sun we'll sail,
Together, you and me.

I'll hold you in my arms,
As the sea swallows the sun.
I'll brush the hair out from your eyes,
Just lie there my dear one.

The sun has gone and all is dark,
The night air crisp but warm.
I'll wrap you in a blanket and,
I'll hold you till the dawn.

A kiss upon your forehead,
As the sun rises once more.
A lonely tear escapes my eye,
As I lay you on the floor.

The cancer has become you,
The drugs eased all the pain.
I want you to be with me,
But I know you've gone away.

At least you were so happy,
At least it didn't hurt.
At least you knew I loved you,
At least you weren't inert.

But what 'at least' can I give me?
What words will help me mend?
The only words to get me through,
At least we'll meet again....Don't worry, i didn't lose anyone to cancer... yet, i'm quite sure i will lose a lot of people to it in the future though.... *sigh*
