I stared deep, deep in her eyes.
And I saw something. 
Something so beautiful, it was as if God had reached out from those eyes,
Those deep, deep blue eyes,
And he had touched me, and I knew then, that it would be alright.
That everything, would be alright.
I saw a war.
A violent and bloody war.
And on one side, between a mortar and a gunman, I saw myself standing.
Staring straight ahead.
No weapon in hand.
No hate in my heart.
I was looking at the enemy.
And yet, I was looking at an allie.
For she was standing there, just like me.
No weapon in hand.
No hate in her heart.
Just love.You could not possibly begin to imagine my thoughts at this moment....
