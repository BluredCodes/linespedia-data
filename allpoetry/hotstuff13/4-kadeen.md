I thought you were cute
I thought you were fine.
You were what i thought about
All the time.

But now I know deep inside.
Your a jerk.
You made fun of me.
Tormented me.
And for that I hate you.

I was your genie 
You were my master..

But now i know.
Your a dirty little bastard.sorry i swore this is for my ex kadeen he can go kiss a green cow
