---
title: "Into the Flames"
writer: "shedevilrkg"
slug: "into-the-flames"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61368-Into-the-Flames-by-shedevilrkg"

The seemingly gentle arms of wrath
surround me
grab me and pull me
towards the enveloping furnace
where dreams are destroyed
and silent fantasies burned
in the ever-consuming
flames of hell.
As I am pulled over the threshold
into the very center
of the patiently waiting fire
I give into that peaceful darkness
and let my dreams flee
from those flames
slowly encompassing 
the whole of my body.
I let them drift
among the peaceful clouds
hoping that the comforting breeze
will carry them throughout the ages
to a place of peace
where they can forever reside
inside the mind
of another young hopeful
where they hopefully
will not follow
into the flames 
that I now embrace.
