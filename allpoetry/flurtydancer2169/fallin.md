I am fallin in love
With your handsome face
With your sweet embrace
I am fallin
Into your words of comfort
Into your hands of safety
I am fallin in love
With your voice of passion
With your eyes of pain
I am fallin
Out of lifes worries
Out of the worlds disgrace
I am fallin in love
Unto your heart of compassion
Unto your soul of purity
I am fallin
Will you fall with me?
