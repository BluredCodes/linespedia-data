but to pull the mask
will sever my soul
its so used to pretending
it has nothing else to know
to pull of my mask 
will show my tru being
but i dont know what that is
if i pull it off i will be naked
of all the 
memories and emotions
tears and smiles
fears and trials
from my mask
i will be "free"
my mask knows who ive become 
not who i am
a mask...
"my freedom"i wrote this because someone commented to take my mask off and it will show my true being and i got inspired
