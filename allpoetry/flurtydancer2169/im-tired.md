Im tired of...
falling in love
feeling your breath on my neck
looking into your hallow eyes
holding your hand
being wrapped in your arms
laying with you
kissing you
waiting for you
wanting you
being scared of you
you yelling at me
you hitting me
you saying sorry
you looking through me
forgiving you
still lovin you
caring about you
sharing my feelings without you listening
feeling the burn across my face
crying over you
im tired of...
being tired
