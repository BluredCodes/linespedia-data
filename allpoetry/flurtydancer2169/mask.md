a mask...
the sugar coated cover of pain
so much pretending has tired my heart
so many smiles have ached my cheeks
the anguish and angst is covered with my smile
the crying and cuttin hidden by my bright eyes
my whole life is fake and my emotions are veiled
the chains and bearings of pain are tightning and piercing my body
my cold blood is revealed as my skin is severed
a mask...
my "freedom"
