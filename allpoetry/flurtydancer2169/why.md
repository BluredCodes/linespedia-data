why am i afraid to die?
why do i always cry?
why does your touch make my world diasappear?
why am i afraid to lose everyone?
Why am i afraid to lose Him when Hes already lost?
why dont i look in your eyes whe my eyes sting with tears?
why do i look in the sun when it only shuts my eyes?
why do i pray?
why do i dream?
why doesnt anyone care?
why do i wake up in the morning when no ones around?
why do i scream when i know no one can hear me?
why do they hear me when i want to be unheard?
