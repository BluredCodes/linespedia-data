im scared to be alone
im always alone
no attention
alone
no affection
im scared to be alone
someone might just push me
over the edge
im so close to the edge...
alone
im so close...
push me and ill cut deeper
deep into my element
where my life lives
ill bleed
all the pain will release
with my distant blood
you can try to stop me
but then youll just wound me again
youll make me 
incise deeper
bleed faster
my eyes are distended
alone
my heart is feeble
all this..
alone
you tell me to commit
my life to God
you say live your life for Him
I say live eternal life
with Him
Ive cut deeper
felt my blood
like Jesus
I feel the scorn
like Jesus
I feel alone...
dont leave me
