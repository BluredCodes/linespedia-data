dont watch me die
dont just stand there and cry
help me!!
save my soul
save my heart
help me!!
save my being
save my breath
help me!!
save my touch
save my kiss
hold me close
kiss my sour lips
push life into me
breathe your years into me
DONT WACTH ME DIE
DONT JUST STAND THERE AND CRY
you can help me
please help me
i need you
i know you need me
or tears wouldnt be fallin
from your beautiful eyes
my breath is shorter
i feel His grace
too tender to miss
save me
from death
catch me
from death
pull me
from death
stop me
from death
