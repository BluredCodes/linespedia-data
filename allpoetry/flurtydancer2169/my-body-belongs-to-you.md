take my clothes and ill take yours

moans of your pleasure inside me
my blood is cold but my skin burns with ecstasy

keep going till i feel the peak of desire
your eyes are like the sun burning with fire
my desire is reached as i moan with sexual bliss

as i lay beside you, my legs entwined with yours
i stare into your eyes and promise myself forever yours
my body belongs to you even though youve broken my heart
you said you'd love me and that wed never part
now my body and soul impure
to my husband i will not be fully his no more
a part of me is missing and it belongs to you
