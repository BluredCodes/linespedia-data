---
title: "carry me"
writer: "FlurtyDancer2169"
slug: "carry-me"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59868-carry-me-by-FlurtyDancer2169"
---

carry me out to sea
where the wind carries messages of past lovers
carry me to the sky
where our minds and hearts can find a way
carry me to the moon
where our worries can float away
carry me to the sun
where it burns with our desire
carry me to the stars
where they are countless of our love
carry me through the rain
where it holds evey lovers tear
carry me through the heavens
where God can bless our beings
carry me to your heart
where i can take away your pain
carry me to your mind
where i can change your thoughts of death
carry me to your soul
where we can become one
