cupid uses an arrow to make people fall in love
but instead the devils bow has just hit me
right in my soul where i try to feel
i wish God would have pity on me and my tainted soul
the devil has frozen my heart 
where im so numb i cant feel the sun
does the struggle ever end?
to try to drag the bow out of my skin
but every day it falls deeper
until it takes over my body
soon i will vanish into the demons home
where my heart will burn to ashes
ill never see the sun
or Gods many blessings
so can you help me?
help me remove the devils engravement
all i need is love
thats the remedy
that is whats missingI dont even know if engravement is a word but i used it n e way!!!
