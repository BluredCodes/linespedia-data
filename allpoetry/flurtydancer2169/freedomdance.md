freedom of the world
my emotions knitted into every grace
freedom from their lies
the worlds hypocracy
in my own world
everytin else fazed
from my tears of darkness
power and emotion
shoot through my broken body
expell like light
through my beauty
floating...
above the tears
above 
the fears
the scars
above
all cares 
freedom...
to dance
