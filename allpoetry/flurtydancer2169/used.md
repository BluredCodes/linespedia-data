sometimes i feel their heated stare
upon my throbbing heart
their eyes pierce it
paranoia through my head
despondancy through my used body
too many hands have touched my skin
too many tongues have sperated these lips
too many have felt my temperature rise
too many have whispered "sweet nothings" in my ear
too many have thrown that all away
just feel my heart literally sinking to my feet
just feel my body shake of fatigue
every minute i cry
every minute another whisper falls into the wind
i wanna feel my blood trickle down my hand
i wanna feel...
i wanna feel reality
no more dreams or stupid little prayers
no more anguish for things undoneI feel that this is unfinished i was never satisfied with this ending
