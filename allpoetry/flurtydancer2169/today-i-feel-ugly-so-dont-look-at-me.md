today i feel ugly 
so dont look at me
dont look in my eyes
they are filled with pain
so dont look at me
cause today i feel ugly
in a hole of depression
the darkness feels like light
the sadness clouds my smile
theres no way ill tell you why
cause today i feel ugly
so dont look at me
i just cant make up my mind
and i dont want  to hear your problems
i have too many of my own
so just leave me the fuc* alone!
cause today i feel ugly
so dont look at me
i dont want to speak
theres no point in sayin a word
cause i dont really care
my clothes dont fit right
and my hairs a mess
my bodys in pain
and im full of stress
cause today i feel ugly
so dont look at me
the clouds are coverin the sun
and thats what i feel like in my head
give me a break
i dont want to be mean but
today i feel ugly
so dont look at meIt seems like i feel like this almost everyday, im just a big 'ol mess!!he he
