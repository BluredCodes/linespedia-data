You see a figure in the corner
She is crying for the thunder
She is weeping for the lightning
For the world for it is dying
Her tears are falling for the rain
For all the people and their pain
She sits in the darkness for the world
In this darkness she is only a girl
With no light to guide her way
Theres nothing out there, she must stay
She cries for those in pain 
Those who wont smile again
But who will cry for her?
For she feels the most hurt
She sits in the darkness for those who feel anguish
But she knows deep down inside that someone will change this
Someone will join her in her obscurity and save her from her cry
They will hold her hand and guide her to the light
