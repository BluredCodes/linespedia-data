you never say i love you too
you never give me a hug
you never look at me
except for detest
i need you to love me
for i love you
and i dont want to die
and i dont want to lose you
i want to be accepted by you
i want you to like me
i want you to love me
i need your attention
dont yell at me
dont hit me
*
*
*
*
*
*
brother...

please love me
