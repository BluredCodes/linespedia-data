I dont want to stab you in your back
but youve already twisted and pulled out the one in mine
youve taken that knife and plunged it into my heart and twisted it
Im so numb from your lies
I dont even feel the blood trickle down my breast
Im so cold from your icy stare
My tears have frozen on my cheeks
everyday i cry
everyday i get that piercing and twisting in my back and heart
every time i breathe in 
my heart stops for a second to try to remember how to love
every time i look at you
i remember that chilling wind that opened my eyes
when you told me you loved me and...
every time i look at the sun
i remember that same wind and have to close my eyesTHANX FOR THE TITLE ANDREA
