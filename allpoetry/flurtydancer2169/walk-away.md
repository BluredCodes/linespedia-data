as i lay in a sea of my own pity
where no one else will swim with me
the darkness crumbles my soul
so many wishes have never come true
and so many dreams have ceased
i want to walk away
leave it all behind
i want to walk away
and never look back
at your haunted face
at your hallow eyes
at your purged inner child
dont follow me
