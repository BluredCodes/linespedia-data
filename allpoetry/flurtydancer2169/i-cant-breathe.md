title: "i cant breathe"
writer: "FlurtyDancer2169"

i cant breathe
he doesnt even care 
i cant breathe
he never listens
i cant breathe
he hits me...
:gasp:
he hits me
i cant breathe
i want him
i cant breathe
i need him to invite me
to his heart, soul and mind
i cant breathe
i need to find
my heart, soul and mind
i cant breathe
kiss me
i cant breathe
touch me
i cant breathe
hold me tighter
i cant breathe
breathe for me
