pain doesnt go away
it stays like any obedient child
pain is a pleasure
a pleasure inside your scarred soul of inner demons
the devil finds pleasure in our pain
thats why he taunts and teases
but dont we do the same?
we trick and cheat
to others their pain is our pleasure
so cold hearted and relentless
we find it so amusing to see someone cry
their tears, our giggle
their laugh, our pain
our pain is his pleasure
its an endless circle
so why do we give in?
to mortal, immortal sin?This is very short but last night i woke up in the middle of the night and was thinkin but my shit and the firt couple lines came to my mind and i had to write it down or i would forget and as i was writin all of a sudden these other feelings came through my soul on paper
