the chain of your death is wrapped around me
dont you wonder what it does to me?
your lips your eyes, so perfect in every way
like dewdrops in the heavens on a rainy day
angel wings have carried you to where you belong
ill be with you someday, it wont be long
ill miss your sweet smile and how you laugh was like a lullaby
i miss everything about you, i cant bare to cry
the pain is too enormous
and im too ashamed to show
a tear fall down my cheek
the pain will have to grow
it grows with thorns to pierce my heart
i dont want to be touched now that were apart
youre the only one that loved me
my "angel eyes" you said made you free
well now your angel wings have done the same thing
im blinded by your love 
i cant see anyone else
your spirit is within me
carried with wings of a dove
i remember your face and the way you used to stare
but every throbbing day that passes by
your picture begins to fade
i want you to hold me tight 
i need your doting to shade
the way you held my body
caressed me as if id brake
well now id like my soul for you to take
i still cant cry
no matter how hard i try
my body is tired from the scars that ive engraved
i want to go deeper im just not brave
give me the strength so ill be by your side
then there'd be no reason for anyone to cry
