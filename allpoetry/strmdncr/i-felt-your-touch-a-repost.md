I felt your touch 
in the electric ripple of pleasure 
that surged through me 

I felt your touch 
in the building glow that warms 
my body, mind and soul 

I felt your touch 
in the thousand stars that exploded 
deep within my heart 

I felt your touch 
as you sent me spiraling 
high into the clouds of earthly delight 

I felt your touch 
when I looked into your eyes 
to find your drowning pool of passion 

I felt your touch…. 

I want... 

more….
