I’ve known love
And 
Affection

I’ve had dreams
As we all do…

I’ve lived a life 
Of
Joy and sorrow
And of
Great happiness too…

I’ve seen places
And 
Wondrous sights

I’ve done things
Most
Never do…

But all those things
No matter how great
Or
Exciting

None compare
With
The wonders
Of 
You….*Smile*
