Lush is the path she walks
Though the garden of her dreams
Each day she tends the flowers
Growing by a lazy stream

Daisies, marigolds, hollyhocks
Primrose, and tiger lilies too
All nurtured with loving hands 
In colors of every hue

Strolling pass each flower bed
She adds to a growing bouquet
Nestled in her warm embrace
As she goes on her merry way

In the corner of her garden
Beneath an old willow tree
Stands an antique wooden bench
Where she takes ease in her reverie

All though she cannot see him
His presents is felt just the same
She knows everything about him
She even knows his name

She prays someday he'll be there
Whispering his hearts desire
Holding her in his arms
Setting her heart on fire

But for now she'll be content
To leave her love's display
Lain gently on this lovers bench
A beautiful flower bouquet

Dreams of love will sustain her
As she moves through each passing day
Knowing someday he will find her
Walking her garden way

Looking back onto her garden
Amazed from her repose
For on her bench she finds 
A single yellow roseFlowers for my love...
