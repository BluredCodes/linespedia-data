In the deepest recesses of night
In the corners of our minds
Float the pieces of our dreams
Desires yet to find

Searching through the forest
Where lust and passions hide
The hunter and the huntress
Seeking where they might reside

Moving through web like shadows
Taking slow deliberate steps
In pursuit of passions prey
As through the night we slept

But the master holds the strings
That blend and twist with delight
Magic woven into our dreams
Till morning brings the light

Pleasures spun from moonbeams
Sparkling, crystal threads
Fairies dancing like fireflies
Through the web forming in your heads

Love, lust, and passion
All the same in our deepest dreams
For the weaver is a wizard
Who rules the night supreme
