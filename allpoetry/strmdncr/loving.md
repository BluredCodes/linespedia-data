Lover, 
turn the lights down low 
Come dance, 
dance with me slow 
Let me feel your arms, 
holding me tight 
Sway to the music, 
all through the night 
Let your hands slide gently along my skin 
Kiss me long and sweet, 
do it again 
Remove my clothes, 
one piece at a time 
Take me down, 
your body on mine 
Delight me with your body and soul 
Make me surrender, 
lose control 
Seduce me with your tender touch 
Do all the things I love so much 
Savor the nectar that comes from within 
Give me your love again and again 
Hold me close as my passions rise 
Look deep, 
see the fire in my eyes 
Love me stronger, 
harder then before 
Make me want you, 
more and more 
Bring me down slowly, 
gently, 
and then 
Start it all over, 
take me back up again 
Enfold me in your arms, 
hold me tight 
Stay with me, 
love me, 
till morning light
