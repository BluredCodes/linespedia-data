title: "~~~ Let My Spirit Soar~~~"

Through a forest of pine and oak I wander
On this peace-seeking journey I advance
In quest of a place of solitude
Where my soul and spirit will dance

This trail is overgrown with bramble 
Fallen timbers now block the way
Foliage green and lush shield the sunlight 
Casing shadows on the beaten clay

Before me the forest opens
Panoramic is this magnificent view
Tranquil waters lay before me  
My hopes now kindled anew

Wavelets lap at the shoreline
Playing tag with the feeding birds
They dance to the rhythm of nature
To a song only they have heard

I kneel at the edge of the water
Arms spread in devoted esteem
Offering my prayer of thanksgiving
For the beauteous things I have seen

As I pray my soul is nourished
And my heart feels light once more
For the Gods of the lake have blessed me
They Let My Spirit Soar….
