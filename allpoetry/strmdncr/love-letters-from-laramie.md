At precisely three o’clock each day
The postman makes his merry way
Through the town and pass the glen
Then down and back, around the bend

In his bag he carried treasures
Books, and cards, and special letters
Birthday greeting from Auntie Jen
Vacation postcards from Uncle Ben

Letters from someone very dear
Wishing he was really here
Sealed with a kiss and something more
Words of love to the girl he adores

She reads it fast then she reads it slow
He says all the things she wants to know
Then in her pocket she tucks it away
Saving it to read later again that day

There’s a happiness dancing in her eyes
As anticipation begins to rise
For she’ll send love letters from Laramie when
