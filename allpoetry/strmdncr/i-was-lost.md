When you kissed my cheek 
I felt your breath upon my skin

And when your fingers touched my body 
There was a stirring deep within

But when you whispered words of love
So softly in my ear

When you said my name loud enough
That only I could hear

I was lost

In the velvet of your voice

I was lost

I simply had no choice

I was lost 

When your lips went exploring
You sent shivers down my spine

And when our bodies came together
I thought I’d nearly lost my mind

But when you roared my name 
In passion

And when you said, “I love you”
Late at night

I was lost

In the velvet of your voice

I was lost

I simply had no choice

I was lostWhen I hear his voice I fall in love all over again...
