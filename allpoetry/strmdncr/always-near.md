Stars explode and galaxies collide
When ever I think of you
Your whispers, your touch
All the wonderful things you do

How very lucky we are to feel 
This love we hold inside
And I treasure every moment
Here by your side

When you make love to me
I feel like I could fly
And when you hold me in your arms
I’m so happy I want to cry

I don’t know how all this came about
Don’t know what led me here
All I know is that I love you
And need you always near…
