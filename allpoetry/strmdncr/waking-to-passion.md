Waking to Passion

Early morning and your essence permeates the room
I breathe deeply, taking you in.
Cool morning air washes over me
And I come alive with blissful memories of passion

Moments of fervent lover’s battle linger in thought
No quarter given, nor expected
Submission simultaneous  
Every nuance of rapture incendiary

Moisture saturated arousal begins
Thus my tender ministrations on male flesh
To wake a sleeping giant from dreamy repose
Reluctant, yet acquiescent  

Questing lips elicit languid response
Growing bold in their southward trek 
Mapping hill and dale on hardening terrain 
Investigating the path to heavenly bliss

Discovering that which I seek, I indulge
Consuming my prey in ardent oblivion
A ready banquet to indulge my senses
And quench my deepest desires

You come alive with blissful memories of passion
Cool morning air washes over you
I breathe deeply, taking you in
Early morning and your essence permeates the room
