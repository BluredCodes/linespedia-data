Let me touch you 
Whisper softly in your ear 

Let me touch you 
Say the words you long to hear 

Let me touch you 
Tease your body and your mind 

Let me touch you 
Bring you pleasure so divine 

Let me touch you 
Explore the secrets of your soul 

Let me touch you 
Love you, 

Let me make you whole
