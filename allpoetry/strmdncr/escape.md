Escape
Caught in a dark gaze 
Unable to look away 
All hard angles and plains 
Knees weak, feel the sway 

Iron hard sinew 
Embrace, a steel cage 
Lost in text rapture 
As I turn the page 

Stop this addiction 
No more romance please! 
Maybe Tom Clancy 
Will give me some ease 


P.M.Williams
