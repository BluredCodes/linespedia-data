---
title: "The Consequence of Greed"
writer: "Iris"
slug: "the-consequence-of-greed"
source: "AllPoetry"

A long time ago
In a very distant land
Lived peace and trust
And many a helping hand

A place of great beauty
Nature and fresh air
No sign of misfortune
Not a hint of dispair

But nothing lasts
Innocence is lost
Greed controls the mind
Who cares about the cost

Along came possessions
Forgotten was trust
Purity was gone
Merely ashes and dust

Pretentious became the soul
Closed minded and cruel
One who did not conform
Was peresecuted as a fool

Empires were built
Great fortresses of greed
Never content
Forever in need

But there is always a cost
When such mistakes are made
Conseqences of gluttony
Never shall fade

Though slowly at first
But harsh all the same
Reality struck
And the consequence came

No fresh air remained
No beauty in sight
Darkness surrounded 
A great loss of light

Was this a fair price?
For such empty need
To please the desire
To satisfy the greed

Now nothing remains
Of this once peaceful land
Ruin throughout
Not one helpful hand

Is this a warning?
A prediction too true?
Will this be our future?
It?s all up to you
