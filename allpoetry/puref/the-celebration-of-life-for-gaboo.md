title: "The Celebration of Life (for Gaboo)"

One day at a time, let me walk closer to thee.
I trust you with my loved, do not let me forget.
Life is a celebration, each day a new guest.
Although the sun may leave, and shine upon others,
remember it's warmth and tell of it's return.

I didn't walk upon water, or part the red sea.
Not even heal lepers, much to my dismay.
But, I did sit and listen, truthfully and intent,
that is my gift that I lay upon your hand.

When I am gone, return me to the ground,
so that I may be re-born, and see you again.
Time is finite, but love sure isn't,
I will remember your face, and great you at the gates.

One day at a time, let me walk closer to thee.
I trust you with my loved, do not let me forget.
Life is a celebration, each day a new guest.
Although the sun may leave, and shine upon others,
remember it's warmth and tell of it's return.For my Grandmother, Gaboo. I will try to live up to your life, and honour your name.
