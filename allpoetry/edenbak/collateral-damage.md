[]A faceless child bleeds in the street.[]   

His~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~arm 
Flung fifteen feet from his body 
He watches flies drink the sugary blood. 
The~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~shit 
In his pants the last warmth 
Ere he takes the final breath. 

[]A faceless child bleeds in the street.[]   

As salacious cheers soar 
As politicians point perfunctory fingers 
As TV news reaches sedentary viewers. 
He is the evil execrable enemy 
Menace to the plastic bubble 
The crown jewel of the “Free World”.

[]A faceless child bleeds in the street.[]  

B~~r~~e~~a~~t~~h~~s~~~~~~~~~~~~~t~~u~~g 
As he whispers winsome wishes for mother 
Who somewhere lies lifeless in rubble 
Blood~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~d~r~i~b~b~l~e~s
From his quivering lips
Staining his teeth the colour of victory.

[]A faceless child bleeds in the street.[]  

Dying in the name of democracy 
Necessary means to necessary end 
Our boys scored another victory. 
So wear that flag proudly, soldier 
Drop that bomb for your country, soldier 
Divest the world of dishonorable devilry, soldier.

 A faceless child bleeds in the street.This is probably the harshest poem I've posted to date. This is not the usual kind of highly polished poem I would post. This is one of those poems written in the heat of the moment - in a time of strong emotion. It is posted in its origional crude form with very little revision ... I think my feelings at the time show through best this way. This poem refers to the tendancy to dehumanize our "enemies". We never refer to the human souls we kill, just those faceless and inhuman foes. "Collateral Damage". The sad thing is, people fall for this rehtoric all over the world. Killing should not be so easy. Devalueing human life should not be so easy!
