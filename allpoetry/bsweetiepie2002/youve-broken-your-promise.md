You promise to love me till death do us part
You've broken Your pomise, now there goes my heart
You promise to never lie
You've broken Your promise, now there goes my trust
You promise to never beat me
You've broken Your promise,now there goes my thought
You promise to never make me cry
You've broken Your promise, now there goes my life
You've promised so much, and each has been broken
No more lies, No more trust, No more love
Now please leave before I make you drop to your knees....


To every women and man who's ever had to put up with this.
