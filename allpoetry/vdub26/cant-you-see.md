---
title: "Can't You See"
writer: "VDub26"
slug: "cant-you-see"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59550-Cant-You-See-by-VDub26"

Can't you see how much you hurt me
More then you can really know
Your words stick in me like daggers
You say how much I disappoint you
Not considering how much it hurts
Not knowing how much I try
To be good enough for your standards
But I am never good enough
And you let me know that I'm not
You break me down until I can't stand it
But you can't see that that is what you do
So I go on, with your words piercing me
And knowing that you can't see it
But you must know how much you hurt me
Even if you can't see it
