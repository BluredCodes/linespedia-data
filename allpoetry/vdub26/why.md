Why is there so much hate in the world?
Why do people kill each other?
Why are people forced to live on the streets?
Why do we treat each other badly?
Why can't we make things better?
Why do people make others' affairs their own?
Why does it hurt when I see someone in pain?
Why doesn't God fix everything?
Why do I feel unimportant?
Why do people worry about what others think?
Why is there so much destruction?
Why can't we all put our differences aside,
And love each other unconditionally?
Why don't we try it and see what happens?
