This is my get away
My pen, my paper, and my mind
I sit and I write, to empty my thoughts
To get out all my emotion
It's my paradise, my deserted island
Where I can be all I want to be
I write with my pen, I could all night
Poetry, songs, whatever comes out
It calms me, more then anything can
Takes away all of my stresses
Lets me get it all out of my system
I can be myself, and not be judged
And I can write about whatever I want
I always have a place to go
When the world feels like too much
Here, on this page, is my get away
