---
title: "Why Not Me"
writer: "VDub26"
slug: "why-not-me"
source: "AllPoetry"

I want to be your everything
Your shelter, your protection, your love
But you can't see that's what I want
And I can't find the words to say it
But why not me?
I can be everything you need
I can help you in so many ways
But you don't know how much I care
And I don't know how to show you
But I will find a way
To show you that it's me
That wants to be your everything
