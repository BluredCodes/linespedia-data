Look, do you see the destruction?
If you can't see it, you must be blind
Just look around, at all the corruption
There is jealousy, greed, and envy
It's what runs our nation today
But you refuse to believe me
Look Around!
It is everywhere, from east to west
Hate, sadness, death, despair
It has filled the earth today
But you will not accept it
How can I make you see?
I just tell you to open your eyes...
Look Around.
