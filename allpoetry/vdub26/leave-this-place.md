---
title: "Leave This Place"
writer: "VDub26"

I want to get away from here
The yelling, the fighting, the hate
But no matter where I go, it's there
Every place seems to be filled with torment
But there has to be somewhere
I can go to get away from it all
Hopefully I will find that place soon
So I can live peacefully, and
Without all of the suffering
But until I find that place,
I stay here, in the midst of turmoil
And all the aggression that is present
But someday, I will leave this place
