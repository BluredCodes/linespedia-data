I just can't take it anymore
The screaming, yelling and arguing
It's almost non-stop, unless we're asleep
I can't take this, it's just too much
How can I make it stop?
I try to keep it from happening
Keep the yelling from even starting
So that I don't have to listen to it
It fills my head, makes me feel bad
But it doesn't stop, it keeps going
I wish it would stop, but it never will
There is no way to prevent it
Not even if I try to be perfect,
It will still go on, forever
