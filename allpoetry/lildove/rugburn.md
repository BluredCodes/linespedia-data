Messing around in a pretty hardcore way
Now I don’t know what to say
My ass, it feels like I’m sitting over a flame
And I have no one but myself to blame
Everyone’s getting sick of hearing me complain
Told my mom I was walking in a towel and I slid
I’m so glad she believes me, although I am her most sneaky kid
I never realized how much you use your ass in a day!
Now I know you need it in every way
Stupid carpet
Stupid me
I guess this is what I get
But the definition of HOT would be he
Why did I squirm so much?
*Grins* I was going insane with his touch
Stop laughing at me!
I don’t care if I look funny
Just let me sit in my ice cold H2O
Man this blows
My god, I am so dumb!
It was great yesterday, but all I have to show for it today is
MY POOR, ACHING, RUGBURNED BUM!!!
