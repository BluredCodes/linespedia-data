This is going to be my prayer 
To whom it may concern
I don’t know if anyone is even there
But it’s okay Kath, live and learn
I know you are sad
You always seem to be
Don’t feel bad
There are so many things I know you don’t remember to see
Boys may have hurt you, yes that’s true
But what’s the point in always feeling blue?
Stay happy, smile!
You may just feel better for a while
Don’t think about the bad things
Think about the good
Think about the happiness, not the sadness that Alex brings
Remember his smile? His warm embrace?
His cute little laugh, and adorable face?
Don’t be sad, be happy
Remember the good times you had together
Don’t get all depressed and sappy
Think how happy he is with her
Remember when you saw him, how it made your whole being shine?
Remember how when he was around, you had no reason to whine?
Remember his cute little stomach, his developing six-pack?
Remember how he told you he loved your rack?
Now you smile, be happy, forget all that sad shit
Don’t mind it a bit
You have so much to live for
Don’t be a depressing bore
Jarrod is cute, Kyle is even better
You’re a teenager! You and a guy don’t always have to be together!
You’re going to work out soon, get nice and thin
Think about that and grin
Soon, all those guys who never wanted you before
Will see you walk by, and want to look some more
So smile!!! Just smile
That’s all that I want for you
You have so much to be happy about
So don’t be so blue!This is the prayer i wrote to myself...i keep it in my purse, and happen upon it every once in a while.  It makes me smile, and that was the point...so it helps a lot.
