---
title: "Whisper My Name"
writer: "LiLDove"
slug: "whisper-my-name"

I’m tired of restraining all this pain
In loving you I have absolutely nothing to gain
I want to scream at you-tell you exactly how I feel right now
I need you so badly-I can change, just tell me how!
But now I don’t care, I’m so tired of you
And how whenever you’re around I feel so blue
Because of how you act towards me
I feel like I’ll hurt endlessly
You play so many games with my heart
Even if you don’t realize it, you’re tearing me apart
Damn you!
What exactly do I have to do
To prove to you I’m worthy of your love?
I could be your angel fallen from above
But you don’t know it
And even if you did you wouldn’t care
I’m so pissed and hurt-I’m about to have a fit 
But when you’re around I just can’t say it-I have no more tears to spare
My god I wish that you could see
That the person standing there when everyone leaves, is me
I act so angry, but really I’m just hurt
Because now when I’m around you, I feel like a piece dirt
I’m tired of restraining this pain
All I wanted was for you to hold me close at night
And hear you whisper my name
