title: "Why Him?"
writer: "fantasy13"

You could have any guy you wanted but you had to choose mine
I told you how much I loved him, how the thought of him warmed my heart
I told you how when I looked into his eyes my world would disappear
I told you, when he smiled all my worst thoughts would float away
So why'd you go and take him, pulled him away from me with your charm
I thought I could trust you to understand, that I could love no other
I can still remeber the day when you pulled me to the side
You told me he was yours, I thought I died
My heart had split in two, my eyes got dark as the night sky
When I think about this day, a silent tear falls from my eyes
And I can only think of one small question, please dont take this the wrong way 
But I have to know the answer, and I have to know it now
Im hanging on your every whim, so tell me Why Him?
