This is the cure for common boredom...makes sure you rhyme

i wanna be a porn star
or hang out at a trashy bar
i really want a guy named josh
but he acts extreemly posh
so i listen to the spice girls
and imagine them in white pearls
i gotta see brett tonight
he should be afraid because I bite

i went to camp kateri
and i got stung by a huge bee
josh might come to paxon
i think im gonna have him
although id rather have jc
because he is a celebrity
i read harry potter
by the way i am his mother

when people wear pink scarfs
it really makes me wanna barf
i know that my song really sucks
to tell ya the truth,it sucks like a duck
but i dont care what you think 
cuz, dude, i gotta tell ya, you stink!
