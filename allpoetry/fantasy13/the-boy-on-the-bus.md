title: "The Boy On The Bus"
writer: "fantasy13"

Hes really cute
he seems so shy
but damn, why wont he meet my eyes!?
i look at him
i stare at him
but every time he looks away
one smile, laugh, giggle, HELL
he could call me names
just say my name, just say one word

i talk real loud, just so he'll hear
but his damn cd player, it covers his ears
"take 'em off, BOBBY PLEASE!"
do i hafta get on my knees?
i wont do that, but boy im inclined
i'll do anything for you, just gimme some time
just tell me, i'll do it, if you look in my eyes
just 5 seconds of contact thats all that i ask
by the way did i tell you I've seen you in class?
you just sit there and stare at the teacher, you look bored
i got yelled at by mr. hart cuz i was staring at the door
the boy that sits behind you, ohmigosh, he's so short!
but moving on, i swear i dont mean to be a dork
i just want you to like me
don't make me stalk you
cuz you're the boy on the bus, can i sit next to you?
