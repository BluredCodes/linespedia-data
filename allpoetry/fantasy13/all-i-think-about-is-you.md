All i think about is you
the way your eyes shine in the light
All i think about is you
all bright days and my dark nights
All i think about is you
how i want you in my arms
All i think about is you
and your sweet loving charm
All i think about is you
your silent stare and toothy grin
All i think about is you
and how you always love to win
All i think about is you
how much i wanna kiss your lips
All i think about is you
i swear, i think im whipped
