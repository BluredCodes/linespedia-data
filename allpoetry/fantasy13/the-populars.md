The damn "populars" they piss me off so much. 
I truely feel bad for them. 
They have no real friends. 
They stab eachothers backs, every chance that they get. 
They say "I love you sarah!", they're just sayin that to your face.
But when you leave, when you're not there...you wouldnt even believe.
You flirt with the male teachers, to get a better grade. 
It's sad, you think you're cool and shit but you're not, you have no friends!
