Some day i will hold you
some day i will cry
some day i will look deep in your eyes
some day i will speak to you with no fear on my tongue
some day i will wish this day had sooner come
some day you will know my true, yes real, name
some day you'll recognize me and i'll feel no shame
some day i will feel you, in ways i never knew
some day i will realize i'd biten off more than i could chew
and all of this is because of you
