I have no faith
people ask me why i dont believe
they wonder why i dont go to church
why i dont pray before i eat
why i refuse to let people talk to me about god
i dont think i have anything ot have faith in
nothing great has ever happened to me
and whenever i did pray, why didnt i get some kind of sign?
if there is something up there, a higher being, why havent i seen him?
why hasnt he come sit next to me and tell me everything is going to be okay?
why did he let my nana die? why?
there is nothing to have faith in
thats why i have no faith
