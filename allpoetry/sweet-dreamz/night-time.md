Night Time

After the sun subsides
behind the horizon,
the weary day closes.

The heat drifts away,
breezy cool, night winds 
blow, like a whisper.

Traffic dies down,
people settle in homes
and the quiet prevails.

The moon glows in peace,
all alone she rules,
a queen of the night.

Then, the stars make
another wonderful debut,
with music of their own-

Like Christmas tree lights,
their patterns mesmerize
me into a serene moment.

When all frustrations
of the day's trouble,
float away into oblivion.

A time good in life,
to re-discover who you are,
to re-envision your dreams,
look deeply in your own heart
and understand what life means,

To bask in creation's wonderment,
enlightenment for your soul...
finding, harmony to be the key,
by which all things are controlled,
and peace will truly follow...

All of these things can be found,
in the quiet time,
of night time.
