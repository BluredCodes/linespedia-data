title: "I Slowly Drift Away"

I will run and I will hide
It's me you'll never find
Watch as I jump into the sea
You don't know where I'll be
Off into the deep blue
I have nothing to loose
As I slowly drift away

I will jump and I will fall
I have already made the call
Look at me just disappear
Now I'm no longer here
Lost in the moonless night
No reason for me to fight
As I slowly fade away

Once in a while it's all okay
But not now, not today
I just want to go
White stripe on the black floor
I've left through the door

Find me my divine place
I wish it was in outer space
Floating off into the dark
I've already left my mark
Let me drift, let me fade
