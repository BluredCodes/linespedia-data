Born on a Tuesday afternoon
Die on a Tuesday evening
I don't know why
I want to die
I just think it's time
For me to die

I was born on a Tuesday afternoon
Now I die on a Tuesday evening
It's only right to do what is wrong
But I don't know, it's been happening so long

The time has come around
To lay myself in the ground
Back to the earth I go
Now I leave this how
I don't want to tell you
That no one ever knew
That tuesday was my last
The day that came so fast
And everything I was before
Has come to be no more
Because I want to leave this place
But I can't tell this to your face

I was born on a Tuesday afternoon
Now I die on a Tuesday evening
It's only right to do what is wrong
But I don't know, it's been happening so long

And I say 'Goodbye' to you now
And I don't know why or how
I thought I could go this way
But I know that I can't stay
It's time for me to go
Somewhere I have yet to know
But I must tell you this
I never felt true bliss
All I've ever seen is pain
Nothing is to remain
There'll be no more of me
You just wait and see

I was born on a Tuesday afternoon
Now I die on a Tuesday evening
It's only right to do what is wrong
But I don't know, it's been happening so long

Born on a Tuesday afternoon
Die on a Tuesday evening
I don't know why
I want to die
I just think it's time
For me to dieI wrote this poem almost two years ago.  This was a time in my life when things weren't going too well.  I was very suicidal, and I wrote this to let my feeling out.
Plus, this poem was included in an English project last year, and it was my teacher's favourite poem.
