Paths entwinedNot by that of Eros' bowBut by the pure trust of Phoebus' lightFound beneath a white roseGrass green thornsStained with both bloodsPure white petals dipped in the blue frostThe rusted empty balladPlays stillFrom the broken music boxHidden beneath your fingersThat once touched an angelWho rose back up the stairwayUntainted But droppedTwo Identical feathersOf whiteWithin your palm And mineMingling our pathsAs oneThe torn balladStrewn together againBy melodies Carved from the blue diamondsIn your eyesAnd the brown ambers in mine
