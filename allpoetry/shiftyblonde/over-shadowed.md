Over shadowed by troubles and doubts
No conclusion to my melancholy life
Incoherent thoughts reiterate;disarray
Seemingly endless perpetual strife.

Harrowing numbness transcends feeling
beneath this influential tyranny
I become defiant and begin to rebel
As I journey to break free: to liberty.
