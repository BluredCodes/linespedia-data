In the dimension we live in
Verbal assault goes hand and hand with treason
Constantly exploiting our children
A four-year-old boy lies on the other side of barbwire
With an AK-47 loaded and ready to fire
He pits himself against elders and self-desire
Who are we to monopolize
On the tragedies seen by our eyes
Contemplating this “marketing” in disguise
Imperialism is our weapon
Indignation is our platform
Defeating militant minds
Behind friendly lines
Claiming that we mistreat the very people
Who staged the destruction of the steeple
Anti-government citizens
Blind to their own ignorance
Rage on with war
Fighting the constitution
With cries of absolution
The CIA’s immunity to the system
Adds to the fists raised
Eight months of torture hidden behind facades
Exposed only to find a court with no laws
And this is what we pledge allegiance to
Imperialists professing to be pacifists
When we found a war with the Spanish
When we robbed Panama for a canal
With slogans that read “He kept us out of war”
And yellow journalism of propaganda
As faith in the government staggers on a frail veranda
Here it is written in vernacular
One of the system
But not the victim
Everyone claims anti-government
Third world countries begging
To have what we so eloquently bad-talk
If you don’t like it than walk
I know what it means to be American
While a perfect government is yet to be seen
I make my home herebefore you start a revoution against the U.S. think about how good we have it. other countries would die to be under the government we have. and to those of you who do not question our government, think about doing so. without the pointing out of corrupt government there is no way to see a problem like this fixed.
