She had to be perfect,
Blonde hair, Blue eyes.
Full of intellect,
But its just a disguise.

Her body was numb, 
She wished she thought twice.
Because all this has come,
At such a high price.

Her mind was rushing,
She didnt know what to do.
The blood was gushing,
She wished she knew.

When she looked at the wall,
The air felt scarce.
She started to fall,
Because of the vein she had peirced.

When she heard her moms scream,
She knew what it was from.
She wished it was a dream,
That she wasnt that dumb.

When she looked at the floor,
She saw the knife.
Covered in blood,
That would soon take her life.

She guessed it was fate,
She was going to die.
But it was too late,
When she whispered goodbye.
