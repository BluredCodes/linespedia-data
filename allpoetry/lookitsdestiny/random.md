Hey theres a duck on the floor,
Im sitting by my door.
My matress is pink,
I swear M&M's think.
That cup is crusty,
My trophy is rusty.
AH!My mom yells,
I hear the church bells.
It says it seven,
That means im closer to "heaven".
I want to die,
But theres other fish to fry.
I live in this room,
Away from the doom.
One brother is crazy, 
The other is lazy.
The cops are my friends,
Says books the library lends.
Drugs are fun,
Like Big Pun.
I have a knife,
It helps me in life.
Shit this is random,
Like a bomb.
I guess its cool,
Just like school.
My face is white,
Like a light.
Van Gogh was good,
He lived in the hood.
I need food now,
So I can turn into a cow.
Goodbye,
Hey sum1 just walked by..
He is cute.
Like my boot.
MMMM....Pie,
Goodbye!
