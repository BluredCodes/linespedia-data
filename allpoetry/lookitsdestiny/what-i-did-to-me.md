---
title: "What I did to me"
writer: "LookItsDestiny"

I was afraid
Of what you would say
That the bruises and scars
Would scare you away
You would wonder why I did it
And you wouldnt understand
So I had to leave
Before I started to care
You still call me all the time
It's like you wont leave me alone 
But I didnt want to show you 
Because I was afraid
When I finally tried to show you
What I did to me
In hopes that you would understand
And that you wouldnt leave
I showed you all the scars
And you tightly close your eyes
Then you ran away
I knew you never loved me
I knew you never cared
As I sat alone in agony
I wished  I never showed you
What I did to me
