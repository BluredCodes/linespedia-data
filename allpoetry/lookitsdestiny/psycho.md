Psycho.
Whats wrong?
Is there something on your mind?
Do you want to talk about it,
Or just sit there and cry?

Psycho.
Tell me whats up,
Whats making you sad.
I want to help,
Instead of watching in tears.

Psycho.
You need help,
We've been thru this before.
Its not your fault,
That your mother was a whore.

Psycho.
Put the knife down,
You don't really want to die.
Please dont do it.
No--Goodbye.
