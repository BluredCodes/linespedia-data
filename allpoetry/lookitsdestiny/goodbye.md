I sit here,
Silently.
Waiting for death. 
The blood is draining,
From my wrists.
My arms are numb.
They will not move.
I feel like a cloud.
I am not me anymore.
I look down at my lifeless body.
And start to cry,
Why did I do it?
I could have survived.
If i had just waited.
Only 4 more years.
It would have been done then,
Over, Finally.
But I did it.
I had to do it.
I just did.
