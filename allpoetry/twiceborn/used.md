---
title: "Used"
writer: "Twiceborn"
slug: "used"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

You shred my heart

Tear it in two

Throw it to the ground

But I love you



You stomp my soul

To a bloody mess

Ground it to pulp

But you, I bless



You use me like

A timeworn ragdoll

Drag me around

But I answer your call



You divide me up

Cause so much strife

You complicate me

But I share my life



Your gaze wanders

Attentions shift

I'm left all alone

But you, I lift



I was so loved

So it did seem

Until this moment

But of you, I dream



I weep bitter tears

Upon our grave

Our love I thought

That I could save



I thought you cared

I thought you knew

I thought you understood

That I loved you
