---
title: "Queen of the Damned"
writer: "Twiceborn"
slug: "queen-of-the-damned"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61773-Queen-of-the-Damned-by-Twiceborn"
---

Your words ring hollow in my ears

They're full of bitterness and fear

I see through your facade so clear

Your empty words, I will not hear



You whine and moan and yell and scream

Throw temper tantrums quite obscene

And if your own way, I don't lean

Your break my heart and crush my dreams



You say, "I'll love you for all time"

Really, you mean, "I'll make you mine"

Your lies are such a heinous crime

Your love for me, only a mime



With your false actions and pretend

Your righteousness,  you can't defend

Your selfish pride, you can't rescind

With your own will, you can't contend



You hold me fast, while rancor breeds

Then spurn me, when it suits your needs

Say, "I want to follow your lead"

But make me, your fickle whims, heed



You dictate choices, overbear

And tear me down most ev'rywhere

So you remain beyond compare

Never, the limelight, have to share



You speak of her as though she stays

In forefront of my mind all day

While all, to you, I want to say

Is that you are my life, my way



Visions of you controlled my thoughts

Affections of my heart, you caught

A future for us, I had wrought

But you traded them all for naught



Will ever you be satisfied?

Until my will to yours has died

My ev'ry wish has been denied

And ev'ry truth has been belied



I ponder these things in my mind

As you continue, me, to bind

And with your machinations grind

So that you may, on me, be dined



I'll fill you till your soul expands

So on my ev'ry word you stand

And with my tongue, I will, you brand

A harlot, the Queen of the Damned
