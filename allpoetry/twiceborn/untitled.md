A commitment of convenience

   Love propped on excitement

   Becoming secondary to misplaced exuberance

   Lost to a lust for attention



   A foundational loyalty

   Love built on stability

   Growing in spite of continuing repression

   Hanging on by a thread



   A struggle for prominence

   Love waiting to be true

   Thrashing wildly for the light overhead

   Before being pulled into the depths again
