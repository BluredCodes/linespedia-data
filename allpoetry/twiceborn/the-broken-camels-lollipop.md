title: "The Broken Camel's Lollipop"

The broken camel's lollipop
shattered sweets of
a desert sun
rising
scorching sand burns
my feet
trod upon the graves of
life eternal
grasped only in the hands of
angels
sing loudly the praises of 
I AM
reborn in the image of
a broken camel's lollipop
