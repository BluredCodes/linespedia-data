Another bright

   Cryonic darkness

   Exit fair golden haloes

   Indentured, just King

   Laments

   Moaning noiselessly

   Over precious Queen

   Resisting sentence

   to

   Underearthen vaults

   Wherein xenophobic youths

   Zealously aquire

   Base causes

   Deciding eternally fates

   Great, humble

   Icons jeopardize known lifeways

   Men never offering

   Profound qualms

   Regarding

   Sacred trusts

   Undermining values with

   X

   Your zeal
