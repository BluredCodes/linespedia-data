title: "Secrets Revealed"
writer: "Twiceborn"

I died tonight
No corpse rotting
No flesh decomposing
But I died tonight
My heart rent into nothingness
I no longer feel
My every hope
My every dream
The very reason for my life
Shown to be a meaningless pile of crap
Wasting away as it leads me astray
Betraying me every moment of the day
Stuffing empty, deceitful promises down my mangled throat
Bartering love for whoremongering
Chronometric gifts exchanged for wasted words
My mind dissected and violated
Its secrets laid bare
Misused to torture and abuse me
Uncontrolled lusts rage rampant
Bypassing true devotion for mistakes of yesteryear
Hypocritical demands reveal detachment from the union
And a separation draws near
