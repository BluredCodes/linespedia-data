---
title: "Redemption"
writer: "Twiceborn"
slug: "redemption"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

You know me for who I am

A lost soul in search of truth

It is only myself that I damn

With the foolish follies of youth



You know me for what I am

A fallen creature of clay

Gone is the innocence of a lamb

And now there's hell to pay



You know me for what I will be

A child reborn in the image of Thee

It is Your death that sets us free

And the Blood you shed that holds the key
