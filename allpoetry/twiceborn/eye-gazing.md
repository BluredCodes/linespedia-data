---
title: "Eye Gazing"
writer: "Twiceborn"
slug: "eye-gazing"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58019-Eye-Gazing-by-Twiceborn"
---

Imagine my eyes

And what they would be

If they were all you could see

If they were all of me

Spherical forms, swirls of color

Floating right here beside one another

Searching your soul, receiving the same

Searching for hope, for someone to blame

Searching for peace, searching for love

Searching for someone below or above

Who would take them in, plumb their depths

Know their strengths and why they have wept

Understand their weaknesses, understand their fears

Understand why they fell and wipe away the tears

What would you do, if you looked in my eyes

And the life that you've lived, you realized

Was empty and useless, wasteful and through

As if every moment you've lived was untrue

Would you wallow in misery, dead to this earth

Or would you die to yourself and give way to rebirth

This is the question that begs to be answered

When your eyes from mine are so very sequestered

So open your eyes and look into mine

And see what they'd be beyond space and time
