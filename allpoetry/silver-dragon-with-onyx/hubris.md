Do birds not sing?
 Do flowers not bloom?
 When you did everything
 You ensured your own doom
 
 Don't try to run
 Don't try to hide
 Ignore it and have fun
 Your last day on this side
 
 You thought it was right
 You thought it would aid
 But you got in a fight
 And you started to fade
 
 Don't disobey
 It will make it more difficult for you
 Just do not stray
 Or your chance of happiness is through
 
 There is no sin
 For we all make mistakes
 Except for one you can never win
 One deed of hubris is all it takes
 
 It's one thing not stood for
 You simply can not practice
 Need I say more?
 
 How about eternity pushed into a cactus?
 
 So heed my warning
 Mortals of blood
 Mistakes are made night to morning
 But do not provoke the sleeping bud
