Words, music, and visions
Fill my head
All my knowledge
Wasted
My afflatus sits
I am one of the 9
One of the special and caring
Talented and sharing
But no one reaches to me anymore
I can not provide what will not be accepted
I have all necessary for the next masterpiece
In my mind, at my fingertips..
The words to decribe are on my lips
W a i t i n g
and
W a i t i n g
But in the end, wasted on no one
No one to accept, appreciate, or even just listen

I am an empty muse
With no one to inspire
