---
title: "Hidden Truth"
writer: "Silver Dragon with Onyx"

So many masks to hide one soul 
So many emotions that lie 
Don't judge a book by its cover 
Don't judge a person by expressions 

Have you heard the song Reflection? 

"When will my reflection show... who I am inside?" 

I've taught my reflection so well 
That it hides the real me, 
From others and ME 

So many masks to hide one soul 
So many emotions that lie 
Don't judge a book by its cover 
Don't judge a person by expressions 

When will the masks be torn away? 
When will I be exposed? 
Raw personality and real emotion 

So many masks to hide one soul 
So many emotions that lie 
Don't judge a book by its cover 
Don't judge a person by expressions 

In the mirror I see a stranger 
A body with no truth 
Time will reveal what life does not know 

So many masks to hide one soul 
So many emotions that lie 
Don't judge a book by its cover 
Don't judge a person by expressions
