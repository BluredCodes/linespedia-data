The Anger Inside
by confusedlover


There's so many things that bottle up-
inside
that i just cant say
If i let the anger and hurt get me,
it will make people feel sad, mad and full of pity
so many feelings -
inside
they just cant come out
people will cry, people will rebel
all because of...
the anger inside
friends, family, school, work, myself-
cause emotional torment inside
filing me up with so many feelings 
that i just cant handle.
If i let them out i will...
scare all, hurt all and 
cause mor problems with my so called "friends"
they betray, they deny
they deny the things that they do to hurt me;
dont they know i never forget?
im called a friend,
yet they hold things away from me, 
keep me away frm their true selves
i never forget, so i guess i never forgive
but all i know is that i will eventually fall-
Out of myself.
