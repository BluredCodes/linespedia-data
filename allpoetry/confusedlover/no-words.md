No Words
by confusedlover, Valerie

There are no words to describe 
what i feel for you.
I care about you,
more than you will ever know.
I know i hurt you, but
It hurts me not to be with you.
I know i should be
Im scared
Terrified of loving someone more than life itself.
Im sorry i lead you on, 
im sorry im not ready for what you want.
just know
Time is the answer.
Im being selfish, 
but you deserve the best,
and I cant give that to you right now.
But until we can be together,
my heart belongs to you.this poem is about someone that means alot to me, all of my friends on this website should know who im talking about, but im not mentioning any names.
