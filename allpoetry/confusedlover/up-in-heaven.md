Up In Heaven
by confused lover


Grandmothers-
their full of love,
Love and joy, but 
why do you loose them before you get to know them?
I know shes there
I know shes watching me, wondering just when,
when, we will be together
forever,
Up in Heaven
I don't know when i'll die
and I dont know what is in store for my life
or exactly when God wants me to be there in that place,
Up in Heaven.
Grandparens are great people
but why do you loose them?
I never met my grandmother,
we do share something unique in my family,
a name,
we share a name, maybe that is why
I feel like i know her, like i am close to her
I feel as if i am living life under her eyes
I cant wait until the day  i see her,
be with her
in that place,
there 
Up in heaveni wrote this poem a really long time ago, and gave a copy to my grandfather.. he framed it and hung it up in his home... he recently passed away in october of 2004 (may he rest in peace) and my aunt and my father decided to make copies, and send it to all of my family, and to bury a copy with him.. my father has the original.. and that means so much to me.. this poem meant so much to him and now he has it for eternity.. so i wrote this poem as a tribute to my grandmother and it now is a family poem.. and will live on forever..
