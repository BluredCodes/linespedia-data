(a spoken word song)
been thinkin about this day
for the past few months
how i,d tell you
and make you feel for me
though i knew i wouldn,t
so i sit 
and i wait
and i think
i imagine you being here
i put my arms around you
a warm embrace
watchin vintage tapes
the state - RAD - empire records
and somehow i feel this isn,t right
-------------------------------------------
                (chorus)
and i know you,re not here
and i know i,m alone
but i just can,t help myself
you,ve gotta realise
my thought pattern
my motive
sporadic at best
i,m all about you
dig?
                 (Chorus)
--------------------------------------
i crack open another bottle
that green rock
yea its my 4th
but i,m not counting
and what if i was?
i know everything about longing
not that you don,t know everything about longing
do you know anything about longing?
its been a long day
its been a long week
its been a long life
a hard life 
don,t i owe it to myself
yea i suppose
---------------------------------------
                 (chorus)
---------------------------------------
and the phone rings
i said hello
and he said hello
how are you
and i said oh i,m alright
and you?
but there,s no reply
it must be you
i,ll pretend its you
and i ask how your day was
and i tell you i love you
and you,re only response:
      [dialtone]
and you,d tell me i,m right
and you,d tell me its right
and you,d tell me 
it,ll all be alright
-------------------------------'
           (chorus)
--------------------------------
and i went out to get a coffee
grande capuccino
no lie - i,m predictable
sheepish grrl at the counter
she questions me
and yes i,ve seen the movie
and i suppose i do look like him
and i thank you for the compliment
and i remember it was cold
oh so cold
just plain dismal
and i walked 
on and on
searching
but i know you,re not out here
and i see a friend
and i tell him 
that there,s this grrl
and he says its good for me
and i laugh with him
cos i know i,m too timid
too shy
to do what this day 
was to bring me
------------------------------------
           (chorus)
------------------------------------
and i,m sorry
and i,m sorry
and i,m sorry
and i,m sorry
and what am i sorry for
i,m pretty sure you don,t even know
know what you mean
who you are
cos this IS about you baby
you don,t know who YOU are
and that,s the problem
------------------------------------
            (chorus)
