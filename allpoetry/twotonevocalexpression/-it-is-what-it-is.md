you just do it
grrl you do it to me
consistently
i can,t help but glow
every shining time you arrive
and if i could tell you
i would
oh baby how i wanna show you
i could
just give me your affection
you should
this silence could thwart
a lesser plan
your lack of expression could crush
a lesser man
but trust me
i,ve seen it all before
think i,m just gonna 
wait you out
and if i could tell you
i would
oh baby how i wanna show you
i could
just give me your affection
you should
and when my time comes
i,ll be the first in line
the first in line to tell you
you,re wonderful
    you,re great
       you,re everything
and i want you baby
and if i could tell you
i would
oh baby how i wanna show you
i could
just give me your affection
you should
been diggin on you lately
been thinkin about you
take your hand
and show you what 
my world,s all about
it,s all on you
just you
i,m all about you
all you gotta do is
reply
and if i could tell you
i would
oh baby how i wanna show you
i could
just give me your affection
you should
