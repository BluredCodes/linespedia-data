and the day begins
long before i awake
the scents and the warmth
such excitement
and contentment
people organize and converse
w/o a moment,s hesitation
or care
and yet everytime i close my eyes
i can,t help but see you smile
so comforting 
and sad
all in the same
just a thought to relay to you
thought you should know
that you will never escape my thoughts
for better or for worse
i must thank you
for this
day broke early this morning
true
but i,ve been broken for weeks on end
