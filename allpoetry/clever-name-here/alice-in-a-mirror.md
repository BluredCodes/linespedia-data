Alice in a broken dream
You cling to shattered hope
Your face on a broken mirror
A mirror with glass
Alice I know you to call me
Each night as i dream
Everyday I search for you
I search for your face
Every time I find you
You run away from me


Alice let me hold you
Please don't be afraid
Come out from the darkness
Come be with me
The face in my broken dreams
Oh don't you know?
Don't you know you have my heart
My mind and my soul


My face was on the other side
The other side of you
If I ever find you again
I won't let you go
Alice of my broken dreams
We are two sages
It was meant to be

Alice let me hold you
Please don't be afraid
Come out from the darkness
Come be with me
The face in my broken dreams
Oh don't you know?
Don't you know you have my heart
My mind and my soul

Alice I know you see me 
You see me in your dreams
As every night I see your face
And you next to me
Your face was on a mirror
A mirror with no end
Don't you understand my love?
It was meant to be
