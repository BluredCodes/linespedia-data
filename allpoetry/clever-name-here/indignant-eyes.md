Stealthily, I am walking down the avenue

The talisman around my neck clinging to my skin

As the sun covered the earth like hot lava

Penetrating my skin

I wanted to run away

Run away from all indignant eyes that glared

The dissipation of hope became obvious

I can not use my past to escape my reality

I know I will drown in its cold depths

As its' black tide chases me down

No I cannot use my past to escape reality

My past won't escape me

But I will escape my future
