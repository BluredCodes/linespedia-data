~~~~ 



Unheard 

Silent moaning, 

Hating, 

Grieving... 

Splashes of scarlet 



 D r 

      a 

       p 

     in 

       g 



Over 

A mind bent 

To destruction, 

Hope washed away with tears. 



Pretty sheets soiled 

By the stains of despair, 

Glisten 

In the midnight air. 



Constant flow of water 

Sloshing 

Throwing water everywhere 

Scarlet 

Swirling in a river, 

Mixing in the flow. 



Eyes turned opaque 

Staring, 

Hands pleading, 

Wrists wide open, 

Dangling severed skin. 

Blue tipped fingers 

Shriveled, 

Floating 



Satin hair hanging, 

Hiding all the fear. 

Scarlet lipstick 

Message on the mirror, 

Under heat's fog: 





please don't forget 

to feed the dog.
