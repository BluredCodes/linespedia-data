~~*~~*~~*~~

Starchy dreams of pale moon days
Crimped into an apple pie crust,
Like carelessly crushed crackers,
Swept away and tossed in the trash.

Made-up facial surface adornments 
Belie the turmoil inside my mind;
Lies I tell to make things peaceful,
I won't share the terror that's mine.

Tangled twisted images of daylight
Melt into a masterful creation of
Frankenstein stomping on my brain, a
Flesh toned agitation compilation, drained.

Stirred to act upon life's broad stage,
Spastic movements of yesterday's finds,
Considered worthy spectacle for the masses,
I present puppet dances of my wooden atrophied mind.Don't worry. I am fine. Just an exercise poem. My psych training is showing again.
