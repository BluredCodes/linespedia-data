title: "Easily Torn"

Written on parchment
a fragile presence
delicately created 
with care,
formed to fit 
each natal need,
enhancing advancement 
of every breed.
Silent seedlings 
lined and waiting 
to be discovered 
in their infancy,
touched by the 
mighty hand of wisdom,
gently urged to 
enter each life-form, 
thrown down to join 
those on earth, 
then the trauma 
of release, 
held for seconds 
corporeally warm, 
too soon caught in
life's intricate web 
rocked by each storm,
parchment, easily torn.Human evolutionary outgrowth from the spirits' home down to earth
