title: "Outside the Universe"
writer: "Sprite"

Under the deepening orange glow
Of wild fires on the moon,
The solemn image is cast upon
This beautiful earthen home.

Above moon and clouds crowning,
Higher than a man can see,
A visual melody of noted stars
Shine on precious invisibility.

Outside the ether of senses' reach,
In the air-starved atmosphere,
Energy's stunning filtered light
Transcends matter death found here.

And inside milky waves
Of galaxies outside our scope,
Praying mantis natives sleep
Waiting for us to awake...

We soul mates universally twined,
Connected by the golden rope from 
Which we both have sprung, whirligigs 
Yearning to reach and touch the wind.

We stretch our mental eyes to perceive
The enigmatic sights of the eternal,
As we beg His mercy and reprieve
From the depth of our own failures.

Looking up into the purple span of sky,
We dream of sights above us while,
Playing with our math and science,
We attempt to find the unseen mystery divine.

But those unscientific truths
Resides outside of the known universe,
Outside our earthly senses' reach,
Beyond physical detection.

Past seeing
