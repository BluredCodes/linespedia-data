*****


Rocking, 
I am only a shadow 
on a ledge, questioning
fate, and wondering if 
there is one for me or 
is it all decided on 
some whim of our 
history

inside the mist of 
obscurity the passageways 
are steep, but as I groped 
around each corner, up the 
trails that lead to other paths, 
I was assured that I thought I 
knew where it was that I 
intended to go. 

So I 
continued to
climb as fast as 
I could to find it, 
my magic place of
peaceful star 
studded

 *    *
*Happiness *
  * *   *

But the climbing 
left me here uncertain,
darkness in descent before 
I was aware, and now I 
find myself, I do not 
even know where. 

The 
landscape 
is unfamiliar, 
the rocky walls 
lean in on me, and 
all I can tell is that 
light is spiraling 
down into a pin, 
below.

I ask that 
Godly spirits fly 
into the lavender pall, 
to find me if they will, 
and decide if I should or
shouldn't continue up 
this craggy wall.

I am a 
witness to my own 
disappointing journey, and
tenuously disturbed by my 
travail, my difficulty is
that, I wish to get 
away from 
here. 

Please 
hurry to my 
precarious perch and 
tell me now...Should I 
continue to trudge up this 
hill or give myself up to the
ever present will imposed on 
me to stay unexposed and safe 
inside the womb of this sky, 
frightened to move on and 
climb,sitting as a shadow 
of my dreams spinning
in a cricket's eye.
