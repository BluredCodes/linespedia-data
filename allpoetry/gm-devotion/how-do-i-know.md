How do I know
How do I know that she is the one
The only one there ever will be
Is she the one

Do I move on
Or do I stay addicted
Three years have pasted
Never once did I see her face

Yet she stays in my heart
Never a day have I forgotten
The smile on her face
So warm so tender

I don’t know if I should let go
It seems like she was always a part of me
A part of me never forgotten
The part that stays closest to my heart

So how do I know
So many questions unanswered
The years have gone by with out me knowing
How do I know if she is the one

Will there ever be anyone else
Is she the one
Is this love or is it just a crush
Too many questions and not enough answers
