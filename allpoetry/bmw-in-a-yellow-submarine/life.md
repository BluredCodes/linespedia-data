Life whats the point?
I mean you die sooner or later
Whats the point in living when your just going to die in the end?
I sit and wonder why we are put on the earth
Whats our purpose?
Are we put here just to be sad and not happy?
Or are we put here to accomplish something in life? 
I really want to know the reason 
I don't see the reason in living 
I try and figure out why i was put on the earth 
But all I can do is think and i never come up with a reason
When i think i figured it out i am wrong 
When i think i am accually happy i'm accually sad
I thought i was going to be a happy normal person but i guess i was living in a fantisy world 
There is no normal no matter what we think there is no normal 
Whats the point if were never happy and everything in our life is going wrong
So what is the point in life???????????
