Your Little Sister
Thats who I am.
Your little sister
Happy to be.
You are the best brother anyone could ask for!!!!!
I'm glad I knew you.
I'm thankful for all the time we spent together....
I just wish it was more!!!
You mean soooo much to me you don't even know!
Now I know I've ment so much to you.
I look back at all my baby pictures, and the ones of you and me together are the best of them all!!!
I've always looked up to you and I always will!!!
You are the most important person in my life, and nothing or anyone will ever change that!!
You mean so much to me that durning any free time i had off of school i wanted to come and spend it with you!
I always saw us growing up together...
I don't know what I am going to do with out you I Love You sooo much!!!
People would ask me "Who's the most important person in our life or Who do you look up to?", and I have always and will always say my Big Brother!!!!!



Rest In Peace 
Matthew Sean Minogue
November 21, 1972 - April 9, 2002
