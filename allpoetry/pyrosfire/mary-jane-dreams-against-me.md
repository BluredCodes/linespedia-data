Aging with time 
Inhale
The brain is divine
Sleep
To wake in cold sweats
Bare feet
Joint to heat
Heat to pull
Smoke surrounds
Decadence to shadow this waste
That is me

Fateful ways 
To handle bad days
Joint to heat 
Heat to pull
Five’s and ten's
Will delete this day’s horrid end
As I explain through pen

Tangible with Mary Jane
Loneliness makes no sense
Mary Jane seen what its up against
Easy to conquer
So it seems 
When I am held under...
Mary JaneGuess you would know what I mean by mary jane
