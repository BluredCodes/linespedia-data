how many times have you invited me 
and told me not to leave
then again you left me
in anger and rage
because of one day of pain
you gave your life away
he could never see...
see this..
your my sister
the sister you left me..
left me without

this love exist
from the blood 
that constricts 
when we meet through fist
from our birth 
and of our first breaths risk
he didnt cry when those cuffs were on your wrist
guess who did

so yes you taught me in early age
later to be taught by me
not to allow senseless heart ache
but to no avail
to prove your love
he put you in jail

for all i have tried to prevent
by letting you vent
and when i had finally tired 
of hearing you cry
you went out and fired 
your last breath in the free world
for him
two bullets
and your life expired

sister my best blister
for you...
my heart will never weitherthis is very personal,  just tell me if you would feel the same for a sibling you have
