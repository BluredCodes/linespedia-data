Sun was shining 
Sky was blue
8.30 and I was awake
But I didn’t mind

Drove to work with the window down
The wind in my hair
Radio played my favourite song
I sang to the traffic lights

No hassles at work
Makes a change
Got to leave early
3 o clock and I’m home free

Best friend left a message
Grub round mine
Back in the car
Window down

Country air
Country roads
No road kill
Nice change

Barbecue weather
Barbecue food
Nothing burnt
Nicely full

Off out to the club
Supposed to meet the girls
Took my best mate
He offered to wear a dress

Club wasn’t too full
Plenty of room to move
Drank my 2 pints of shandy too quick 
Had to go on the lemonade

Moshed my ass off
Saw some old friends
Met some new ones
Sucked my lolly pop

Stay for one more?
There was no more
It was 2 in the morning
Time to drive home

Put on some banging tunes
Danced in my car
The policeman smiled
And waved me by

Stayed up till 3
Fell asleep at 4
Perfect end
