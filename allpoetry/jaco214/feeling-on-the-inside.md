i've got this feeling
deep down inside 
i felt it when i was with you
i felt it when i was on the phone with you
i'm feeling just thinking about you
i don't know what it is
but it feels great

soon we will be together
i know it
my feelings for you
and your feelings for me
they are too strong to let anything go

i'll never let you down
and i trust that you won't let me down
i can't stand not being with you
soon the time will come
when it is you and me
togetherA little more than 2 weeks later after writing this poem, she became my girlfriend.  :)
