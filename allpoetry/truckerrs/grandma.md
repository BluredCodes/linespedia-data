Grandma

My mind goes there a bit more than often
I never wanted to see the time
The pain just never seems to soften
From when she left, one day, one time

How could I have grown-up without her
It was her special love that made the difference
In me, who I am, and the things that I prefer
Always there, she never treated me with indifference

Asking God one dark night for her never to die
It came to me, I was asking out of selfishness for me
Be thankful for her time, don't waste by asking why
Live and enjoy the most special person, I would ever see

God gave her to me, I thank Him for that
She was my life, she made my life
He loaned her to me,and I thank Him for that
Oh I miss her, she was my grandfather's wife
