Yellow Sneakers

Looking out into the crowd
People and more people constantly moving
As if honoring, the settling dust seems to enshroud
Its victims in a sandy colored covering

Finding a pint size, pales
All duties performed in my lifetime
Eyes are weary checking for details
Oh me, I turned my head just one-time

On the far side a yellow pair of sneakers
Zig zagging through the crowd, pigtails bouncing
Checking around perched high, many more seekers
Through the noise I barely hear the man announcing

My eyes are glued on a yellow sneaker
How can they be so swift?
Guess it's hard to admit their stronger, we're weaker
New policy, next year tie me up for my birthday gift

A plan well done
Yellow sneakers went to the cotton candy stand
And there in her brown sandals having fun
My little rug rat hangin' out, just as I planned

Those yellow sneakers saved the day
So bright and easy to spot
I knew they would show the way
