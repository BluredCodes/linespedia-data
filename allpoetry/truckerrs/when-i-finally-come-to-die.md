When I Finally Come To Die

When I finally come to die
If I'm allowed one last look
At the place where I lie 
Will I see loved ones around a prayer book?

If I see you praying, then for sure
Our good-bys have been expressed
For I want my love to endure
Long after they have laid me to rest

It is you my darling, I worry about
Think of me only, in your eyes
For you must never take my love in doubt
For reassurance look to the heavens and skies

It is my death not yours
So don't take my glory away
I'll be angry if your mourning underscores
The fact this is mine and the Lord's day.

On my last look if no one's around
Then it came about a different way
I'll still go to the same burial ground
Because of the way, I'll need more to pray.

Either way Jesus died so I might
Be forgiven of all my sins
So when they give me my last rite
His love doesn't end for me it just begins

Patiently I will wait until we reunite
Because the day you step into heaven
Even the Lord will be in awe of the bright
White light, brightening as you do any haven.

My dream can come true
Spending all eternity with you
Once maybe, but I no longer fear death
Your love will be with me long after my last breath
