Rain

The clouds were darkening
Then the skies opened up
And everywhere it was raining
As the earth tried to drain the cup

The grass seemed to grow
As it was kissed by the rain
Trees were busy with their dumb show
The culverts looked like city water mains

Nature was saying thank-you
For this first spring rain
In the spring came life anew
The rain now performing legerdemain

Air smells of a freshness
Seldom of equal scent
Everything is alive never-the-less
Earth seeming to breathe in contentment

Number one story on local news
Rain went across the road
Took a truck for a cruise
A man died while waters overflowed

A rain that made flowers grow
And trees blooming look so nice
Took a life someone loved so
Seems unfair for pretty flowers to pay that price.
