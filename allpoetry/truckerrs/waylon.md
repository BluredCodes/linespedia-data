Waylon

Part of country music's soul died today
Not just Nashville, but all of us will pay
He was a rock for us who liked real country
Got started in Texas then went to Tennessee

Nasheville said you'd better do it our way
He said, yeah that'll be the day
If there's gonna be music, it's gonna be mine
So Nashville asked him if he would please sign

Through the years there were 16 number one hits
Done by a man who lived by his wits
Oh he'd do it any way they would say
Just as long as it was his way.

Country music and all its fans are crying
Because he's gone guitar, songs, and everything,
His music will live on, hearing it will ease the pain
But we worry most about who's left to entertain

Who's going to fight the suits to keep our music
Whose going to be strong enough not to politic
Will someone stand up and fight with all their might
Maybe Alan will show them the light

No matter what we do, it will never be the same
Thank God for keeping him off that airplane
All we can say to this man is well done
He did it his way, we will miss you Waylon.
