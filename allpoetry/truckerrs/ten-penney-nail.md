Ten Penney Nail

I used to watch TV
Wondering what I'd be
Maybe Roy, Gene or Bowie
Didn't know who,
But a cowboy was for me.

Every Saturday morning,
They were there to teach me
How to rope n' ride n' sing
But most of all they taught me
The value of truth n' honesty

Now in my dreams they ride
Beside me everyday
Yes Stoney, Wild Bill, n' Kit
They lead me on the trail
These ol' boys, are straight as a ten penney nail.

Where will those behind
Find their hero
Nothing measures up to
The guys from the big screen
So sad to be caught in between

Now in my dreams they ride
Beside me everyday
Yes, Hoppy, Rex, n' Cisco
They lead me on the trail
These ol' boys are straight as a ten penney nail.
