Her

My lungs inhaled deeply
The pungent odor of her flesch
Caressing the softness of the body
I longed to be on again.

Moving up from behind her
My hand gently touched her rear
The beautiful shape and fullness
That aroused me to a new height.

Sliding my hand along her back
In a slow but smooth fashion
Life was at it's very best
Two of us together in harmony.

Reaching her neck I gasped
As I felt it's length, my lips
Pressed against her in wonder
If she could feel the depth of my love.

My hand reached for her chest
My heart pounding like native drums
Spreading word throughout the land
Of my feelings each time I move my hand.

Soon my hips will be undulating
With pleasure in rhythmic unison with her
As we ride around the arena
The way my favorite horse and I like to.
