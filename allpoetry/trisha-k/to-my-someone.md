To My Someone...
   I hope I'm not asking too much, all I really want is a friend.  Someone who is always there for me, someone who will hold me and tell me everything will be alright.  Someone who calls just to say hi.  Someone I can talk to all night about nothing at all.  Someone who will come over just to see me smile, and will have fun even if we do nothing, but just because we're together, it's all worth it.  Someone I can fight with, just to make up. Someone I can tell everything to, without the fear of anyone else finding out.  For once, I want someone to take me seriously, someone who will listen to my problems, someone who cares about me.  Someone who will hug me when I cry, someone who will laugh with me, even if my laughter isn't real, someone who will smile just to try and brighten up my day.  Someone I can share all of my hopes with, someone who understands my dreams someone who will just show up at my door step, with a hug and a smile.  Someone who knows my ambition for life, someone who knows my fears, my every wish, my every desire my every failier, and loves me just the same.  Or maybe thats just it, maybe, I just want someone to love me! 
   So, to my special someone, I hope I'm not asking too much!

...Always...
