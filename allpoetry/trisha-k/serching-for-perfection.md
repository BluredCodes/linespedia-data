---
title: "Serching for Perfection"
writer: "Trisha K."

I thought love had given up on me
now I realize I was the one
who had given up on love
I guess I always thought 
that if I didn't fall in love
I couldn't fall out
I set my mind on trying to find 
the perfect guy
the one I knew didn't exsist
I looked everywhere
for a love like this!
They always say that fate is made
How come mine always seems to run away?
I didn't recognize love
even when I had it
but now that I've lost it
I miss it more then ever
I serched the world,
looking for prince charming
but I was too blind to see,
my perfect guy...
was standing right in front of me!

Trish12-28-00, I had him, I lost him, and I can't get him back.  Just a little bit of advise...Don't ever take anything for granted, once it's gone you'll realize what I mean!
