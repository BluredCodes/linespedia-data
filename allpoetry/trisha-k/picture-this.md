Picture this... I wanna know exactly what you see
Whatever it is, I know it really isn't me
Picture this... and take a good look at me
one more time tell me what it is you see.

A tear stained face? Trembling hand?
Shaky knees, but trying to stand?
Picture this... a little girl with broken dreams
Dark brown eyes and bursting seams.

Picture this...surrounded by people, yet standing alone
Screaming your loudest, yet nobody knows.
Picture this...whatever it is you think you see
I'd just like to know, do you understand my story?

Please...Picture this...look past my smile
for it's not really true,
if anyone can see who I really am
I hope it would be you.

Picture this...a smile that glows
but is not really true
A laugh that shines but it is fake too.

Picture the face, happiness a mask
yes I've tried to leave it behind
but the courage I need I can't seem to find.
Just picture this,
whatever you think you see
I'd just like to know...
Do you enderstand my story?Kinda confusing but thats how it is!
