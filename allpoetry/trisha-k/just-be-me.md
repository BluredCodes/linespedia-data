As I sit alone on my bed
I cry and I think
of what's been said
I try to imagine 
what they think of me
I try to change
to be who they want me to be
Why can't I just be me?
Is all I want to know.
Why can't I be me?
and let the real me show?
Everyday I wake up
look in the mirror and think,
"What a disgrace! 
Why'd God waste his time,
on such an ugly face?"
but today it has all changed,
for today I made a friend
He didn't like me for just my smile
he told me I was perfect,
with the heart of a child.
He told me I was kind and pretty too
and he gave me the courage to do
what he said must be done
He told me that yesterday is gone,
and tomorrow may never be
so today is the perfect day,
to start being ME!And to this special someone, I want to say thank you!  I luv ya!  and good-bye! :)  8 monthes isn't that long!
