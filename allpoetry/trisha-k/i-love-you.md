---
title: "I love you"
writer: "Trisha K."

I could never really say this,
 so I'm gonna write it down.
 I want you to know the truth
 I don't know what I'd do without you!
 My words just never came out right
 but I knew you could always tell just how I felt inside.
 No matter what you could always wipe away my tears
 hold me in your arms, and kiss away my fears.
 
 We've grown up together
 and the years have gone so fast.
 You've always been my hero
 and I'm glad I can't change my past.
 Most of all you've been my friend
 and the best one you could be
 I know I never told you
 but you mean the world to me!
 
 The years we've spent together are memories heaven blessed
 moments I know I'll treasure forever
 because with you they were the best!
 you could always find some hope through my darkest fears
 you could always find some joy through my river of tears
 you filled my world with hopes and dreams
 I never would have found.
 You gave me wings and taught me how to soar
 you gave me hope I never had before.
 
 Now all that is left to say
 is something I know I won't regret
 and if you remember nothing else
 I hope this you won't forget...
 *All that you are, is who you have become.
 all that you are is the one I've some to love.
 What I'me telling you is true
 I promise, today...tomorrow...and always...
 I will forever love you!*Aaron, I don't know what I ever would have done without you!  I love you and you will always be my bestest best friend! Thanx!
