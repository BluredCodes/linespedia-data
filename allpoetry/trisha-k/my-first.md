***Just so y'all know, I didn't write this poem but it was written for me by my daddy!  It's my favorite!  I hope you enjoy!***

My First

She was my first
I love her with a fathers heart
for years she was the only one
but that was just the start

There are times when we should talk
but I don't know what to say
I feel that I am loosing her
in a very old fashioned way

It's just because I must be old
but she never says a word
she doesn't know she means the world to me
she thinks I'm just absurd

I wish I could tell her how I feel
But I'm not a friend, just old
she will always be my very first
that's why we broke the mold

Since then there have been some more
one plus three that makes four
She's the one I just can't reach
Little does she know, I have to preach

She thinks I'm just an old fashioned bully
she never gets her way
she needs to understand me fully
I love her more then words can say.

I only want whats best for her
but she cannot see my pain
I try the very best I can
and it seems sometimes in vain

She keeps herself hidden away
far from me, it would be safe to say
She comes out to talk, but never to me
I'll beg for her love, that was meant to be

The woman in her has come out so fast
I wish her childhood could only last
She's the friend I need to quinch my thirst
After all, she was my Fat Butt First!FYI-My father and  I got into a huge fight and didn't talk for 3 monthes, this stopped our fight!  Before this, I never knew he could write so wonderfully, it brought tears to my eyes!  I love you Daddy!
