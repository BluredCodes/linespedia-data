Wouldn't it be great
to live a fairy tale life?
To be Cinderella, or Belle,
To find Mr. Right!
But what if you were on the other side?
The Wicked Witch, the Evil Queen
Treated like an Ugly Step Sister
What if Prince Charming
never really was as perfect as he says?
What if Ursula really won the fight?
What if the apple truly killed Snow White?
What if Sleeping Beauty had been laid to rest?
A night in shinning armor no where to be found?
My life is not a fairy tale,
and it will never be!
So for now I guess I'm happy
with just being me.
And since Prince Charming's already taken
and Mr. Right is gone too,
I guess I'll have to settle for you!Pure Boredum!
