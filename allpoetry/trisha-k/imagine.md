Imagine

I imagine your hand in mine,
and I feel the butterflies.
I imagine your arms around me
and I feel my knees get weak.
I imagine your soft lips,
giving me a sweet kiss.
I imagine hearing you say you love me,
and I feel my heart skip a beat.
I imagine being with you,
and words fail me,
I don't know what to do.
When you walked into sight,
it was everything I imagined.
But, instead of me, 
you were with her tonight.
Then I realized that my dream,
would always be just a dream,
that I knew would never come true,
'Cause, I can't live my dreams without you!I wish it was me, but she is a very lucky girl and I hope she realizes what she has.
