I am from the sun kissed islands
and the turquoise blue seas
with dark brown eyes and wavy hair
where *Aloha* is always friendly
and the trade winds blow

I am from the spotlight
the center of attention
the music plays
my grass skirt adds to my story
the one I tell with my hands
as I show my island traditions

I am from the mountains
The changing leaves, the rushing rivers
the fireside chats with friends and family
marshmallow roasting till dawn
and fireworks on the forth of July

I am from the all night slumber parties
with all of my best friends
Christmas parties with cousins
and pictures on the wall

I am from the back yard bar-b-ques
and sunday morning church
Family lunch at Bama's
From green chili and tamales
Gramma Tina's rice, and Grampy's one dollar bills

I am from the tears of pain
The smiles and laughter of joy
From mamma's eyes where I learned to trust
From Daddy's hands where I learned to love
and from those who cares for me
I learned, I am who I was meant to be!

Trisha Kealoha*\O/*
Hawaiian
