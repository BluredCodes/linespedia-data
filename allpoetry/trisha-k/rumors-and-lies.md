title: "Rumors and Lies..."

How come you never told me,
about rumors and lies?
You told me,
How alcohal and drugs,
could ruin my life.
But the things they say,
hurt so bad inside.
So I isolate myself
and I try to hide.
The truth will find me
If I only give it time.
I want you to wrap me in your love
and wipe my tears away.
I want you to hug me,
and say *Everything will be OK*
Mom, why do they all hate me?
What did I do wrong?
everyday I see people getting hurt
and I know I have to be strong,
cuz I don't want it to be me.
How do I make them believe
that what their doing isn't right.
They're robbing innocent souls,
of the happiness they deserve.
Don't they know, that sometimes,
the most dangerous weapons,
are only spoken words?
