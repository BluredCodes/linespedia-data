I thought that is I ever heard you say good-bye
It would tear me apart
But when you didn't say anything at all
your silence is what broke my heart
How could I let you walk away
When "I'm Sorry!" was all I had to say
All I had to do was look you in the eyes
and tell you the truth
Instead I let you say good-bye
I guess I was stupid
for playing a game I knew I couldn't win
After all, I was the one
who made the same mistake again
A mistake I know I'll never forget
and I myself will never forgive
Ive tried so many times
but not once could I swallow my pride
enough to admit how I felt inside
and now that we are done
I realized that saying "I'm Sorry"
would never have been enough!

TrishI want you to know that even though there is no longer an *us* I am sorry for hurting you, but I am glad that we can still be friends!
