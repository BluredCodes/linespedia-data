Whenever you look at me
tell me what it is you see.
The color so brown?
The shape so round?
or do you see the light,
reflecting off my eyes?
Do you look further,
and see what's going through my mind?

A soft stare, a gentle look, a short glance
and you've got me hooked.
As I stare into your eyes,
I can see what you're thinking inside,
and it seems as though the same thoughts
are going through my mind.

But then something happens,
to steal you away.
Together again, we'll be
I pray.

But for now I won't cry about loosing you
I'll celebrate the fact of once having you
Then I turn and run away
because I didn't want to admit,
I'd fallen for you!

The truth I never thought you knew,
you held onto all along.
But then you said you needed some time
to figure out where you belong.
I can't figure out, 
why it's so hard for you to see
Babe, you belong with me!

TrishFor my Bright Eyes...You'll always be my wish come true!!!  
I miss you!
