title: "Always a Friend"
writer: "Trisha K."

I'm not going to cry,
when you walk out that door.
I'll say good-bye,
but nothing more.
It's your choice,
I can't change your mind.
I just hope,
happiness you find.
It's obvious to you and me,
that we just weren't meant to be.
You have a new love in your world,
and I honestly hope,
she is the perfect girl.
But no matter what,
through thick and through thin,
I'll always be here,
and I'll always be a friend!Forever...Foralways...and no matter what!
