---
title: "Only Mine"
writer: "Trisha K."
slug: "only-mine"
source: "AllPoetry"

If I run away from love
I can't get hurt
No one has a chance to leave me
No one has a chance to break my heart

I won't let anyone get close to me
because if no one could love me
then I can never disappoint them

I can't let anyone see who I really am
the one longing for love
but unwilling to fall for it
If I don't fall in love, I can't fall out!

No broken hearts, no shattered dreams
no spoken lies,
as long as my heart is only mine.

The fear of losing love over took me
Love had not given up on me
I had given up on love.

Not giving anyone a chance
in fear they might hurt me
might turn and walk away
when really, I needed him to stay.

So if I keep my heart hidden away,
I think that it is safe to say,
I'll never have a broken heart
or shattered dreams
and I'll never hear a spoken lie,
as long as my heart is only mine!
