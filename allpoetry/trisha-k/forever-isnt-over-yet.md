Forever Isn’t Over Yet

Graduation day,
I watched you walk across that stage
And tears came to my eyes
When I saw the smile on your face
You were moving on in life
Going far and following your dreams
And as you left your past behind
You were also leaving me
Summer flew by quickly
Before I knew it, you had to go
All the feelings I had locked inside
I had to let you know
Standing on my front porch
Trying to sum up our relationship
In those few minutes we had left
I held in my hand a letter
Filled with secrets I had kept
I didn’t know what to say
Or how to make you see
So I handed you the letter
Hoping it would tell you
How much you meant to me
I was so afraid of loosing you
Afraid to say good-bye
But I kept the promise to myself
And didn’t let you see me cry
But the words you said to me
Are ones I’ll never forget
“Our friendship is forever,
and forever isn’t over yet.”
With those words you smiled
And I knew it was for me
I hoped that you would catch me
Cause I realized I was falling
Just by the look in your eyes
I know you could tell how I felt inside
That was when our lips finally met
For the very first time
We said goodbye and I was still
Trapped inside your embrace
That’s when I felt a tear roll down my face
“I’ll miss you,” I said as you turned to leave,
“Behave, go far, and follow your dreams.”
You smiled that smile, and I swallowed my pride
With that one last hug,
I let you see me cry.
But anytime I miss you
I think back to that summer night
The one, right before you left
Then I remind myself,
~Forever isn’t over yet~
