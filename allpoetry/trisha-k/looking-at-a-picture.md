Looking at a picture
We look so happy together
Frozen in that moment
Where I want to be forever

I'm holding on to you
and your hand's upon my knee
sitting there together
that's my favorite place to be

A picture is worth a thousand words
But ours is worth much more
Yet I can't think of one thing to say
That could even out the score

Yesterday we started out
as just the best of friends
But what happens in the future
I guess it all depends

I want there to be a me and you
I wish there was an *us*
You're the one I dream about
and you're the one I trust

So as I look at this picture
I see what the future might bring
but no matter what happens
We will always share this memory!
