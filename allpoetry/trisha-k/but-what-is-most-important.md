But What Is Most Important

We were all created equal
yet no one is the same
If it was up to me
I'd let everyone play the game

No one would have to sit the bench
Or worry about rude remarks
No one would have to go through life
walking in the dark

No more standing in the shadows
No more standing all alone
Everyone would have a time to shine
and a place they like to call home

Life would no longer seem
like falling to your knees
and reaching for a star
Everyone would be able
To be just who they are

But what is most important
For everyone to see
Is I wish we could all say
*I love me, just for being me!*
