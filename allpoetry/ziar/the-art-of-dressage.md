Friday’s here it’s time to go
For our weekend Dressage Show
Load the trailer pack the truck
WHAT? 
There’s no room for all my stuff

Here we are 
dressed in black in white
In ninety degrees? 
You think we’re ‘all right’?
You must be joking who thought of that?
Some masochist who loved 
black hats

I warm up 
my horse begins to snicker 
I can tell right now 
he’s no friend Flicka
Galloping in
to a screeching halt 
He passes gas, an after thought?

Turn left at C or was that right?
My caller sneezed oh what a plight
Lets go left the whistle blows
Up to the judge’s stand we go

That’s okay I turn and smile
As Savoir Faire leaves a pile
Cantering past the letter E
That was suppose to be 
a simple change of lead

I think we are up to minus six
Savvy’s brakes need to be fixed
Ten-meter circle, it’s nice and round
He snags the chain the ring comes down

Patiently we stand and wait
As the TD drives in the stakes
Final movement down centerline
X, halt, Salute we did just fine

Who’s the masochist? 
You may say
I am 
All the way
