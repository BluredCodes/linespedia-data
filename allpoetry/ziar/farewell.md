title: "Farewell"
writer: "Ziar"

Say farewell and lay to rest,
The tears, the joy, the pain, the bliss
Unpack my dusty filled hope chest
My wants, my needs, I have missed

The wind blows hardest near the end
Ravishing a thread that binds us still
Always wishful as a willow I do bend
Disappointed to down another pill

A leopard bears his claws and fangs
Hunger satisfied, he saunters away
Free without the slightest of pang
Off to his den, where he will lay

Fiery red consumes my soul
Release the beast let it go
Lest remains an ebony coal
Shattering hope was foreknown
