At rush hour and holidays 
It’s just a real slow cruise
Welcome to the Northern Virginia
Traffic Jam Car Blues

Sitting here watching them
Acting like total loons
As cars weave in and out 
It must be the toxic fumes

Screeching tires honking horns
I’m surprised they’re not all worn
Interesting hand jesters
Plenty full of scorn

The Cardinal’s out
Have you heard?
The finger will be
The new state bird

Out of Towner’s crashing barriers
Wanting to be in the H O V
Laughing for they didn’t see
Five lines will merge to three

Over the median they swerve back
I slam on breaks to give some slack
The driver has a screaming fit
Like I was the freaking nitwit.

Oh my gosh we pick up speed
I head out to take the lead
On the side a car is stuck
Cripes! Just my freaking luck

What the heck?
Why all the rubberneck?

As you know people begin slow
About this time I got to go
Thinking I need to squat and park
I’m not much on making my mark

Okay Okay so that’s not so
I’ve done it once or twice before
It was freezing cold, in two feet snow
As I hid behind my coat and door

Today was a gorgeous day
Who knows what message 
I would send
Letting my derrière
Hang out in the wind

So long for now I gotta go
But to show you I am not slow
I’m writing to the Cracker Jack guys
“Please be wise
Remove those licenses 
As a prize”This is frivilous and those who read I hope you get a laugh. 
My enjoyment was in the writing and certainly not sitting in traffic today. hehe I am so glad I don't live in the DC area anymore.
