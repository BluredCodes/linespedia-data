A clock has stopped a bell now tolls
Your choices made now unfold
Spiraling into the dark unknown
Leaving us here all alone

Grey Skies
Shimmering Eyes
Trampled Grass
Shards of Glass 

Lights, procession, dressed in black
A cherry chest dust, bones and ash
Shroud within these granite walls
What will come has come to pass

Grey Skies
Shimmering Eyes
Trampled Grass
Shards of Glass

Triangle fragmented, two remain
Hindered by our earthly chains
One day two will rejoin with thee
