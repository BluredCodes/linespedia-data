The Cloak of Burden

On the horizon ominous clouds appear
A bitter wind whips and blows 
Casting confusing thoughts of forlorn
Wary to breathe of aching painful woes

Drowning in a whirlpool of darkness 
Slipping further into an abyss     
A peacock mask to wear and display
Brilliant colors, a depiction of bliss

Hands pressing against the emptiness
An invisible barrier for those confined
Secure within familiar surroundings
Visions harbored in ones own mind

Somewhere within lays a beating heart
Hiding from the truth, lost in turmoil’s plight 
Buried deep to shield the pain
Bracing, no longer ready for flight

Whispering softly hoping to be heard
The familiar words forever lost 
A fridge breeze comes callously by
Chilling my heart, a hardened frost  

A lone rose wilting
Beneath a harsh star
Rainbow on a horizon
Wait, wait it is not far 

In darkness a light appears
Deep within the pit of despair
Gathering strength, I climb my way out
Shedding this cloak of burden I bear07-06-00,
