A rose
by any other name
would still be
blood
and silk,
but love
by another name
would be you.
My heart is
frozen in time
in your hands,
though I always
thought mine
were colder.
You left
silk petals
and I gave the
blood,
but these roses
are all thorns.this was longer, but my computer crashed before I could upload it and this is all I could remember...it is pretty though.
