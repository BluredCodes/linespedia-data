You were so ugly,
now you are beautiful.

You are so great,
you used to be pitiful.

You used to be weak,
now you are strong,

You now do right, 
what you used to do wrong.

You are bright yellow,
you once were blue.

You were old,
now you are new.

*aliThis poem is about the transformation and change one can go through emtionally and mentally in such a short time.
