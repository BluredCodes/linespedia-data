The rain makes me fell all careless and free.
It piter-paters around me like the beating of a drum.
People say it is God shedding his sorrowful tears,
but what if he is just sweating his pitiful fears?
It is like the end to a new beginning.
the metamorphosis into a beautiful new morning.
To many people the rain is a symbol of deression or saddness,
then why does it project a beautiful rainbow over my cloudy sky?

*aliI obviously wrote this free verse out of my love and passion for rain and contrasting what i think to what others think about it.
