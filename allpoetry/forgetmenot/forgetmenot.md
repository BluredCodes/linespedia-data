Dear someone who will listen:

I feel forgotten, no hope
no pride.

I feel rotted out and decayed,
and all eaten up inside.

Like no one cares,
whether i am dead or alive.

Whether i weep in a corner,
 or whether I crumble or survive.

whose shoulder do i cry on?
who will reach out a hand?

Who is willing to not only hear,
but litsen and understand. 

You dont have to save me,
or give me every penny youve got.

Just do me one favor,
forget me not.
