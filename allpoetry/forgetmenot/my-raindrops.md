These raindrops drip down my rosy cheek,
These little beads of emotion speak.

All this spirit contained in one single drop,
All I have to do is smile and this rain will stop.

These salty drops of anger, sadness, and fears,
are none other than my very own tears.

*aliI have a deep love for rain, and i think the art of crying is beautiful and inspirational also. I compared the 2 beautys in an extended metaphor.
