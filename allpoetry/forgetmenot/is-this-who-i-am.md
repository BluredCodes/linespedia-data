title: "[Is this who I am,]"

Is this who I am,
An empty piece of soul?

Just another lie told,
A substance of below.

A serious blowing fuel,
With nothing left inside.

With flaming anger,
With no decisions to decide.

I’m a failure to my life,
And the all the possible choices,

I should try and start over,
And listen to those voices.

Make amends for my mistakes,
And the damage in my past.

I am going to start off with the time,
That went by too fast.

See me in a year,
You’ll be sure and recognize

That glowing bit of soul,
That took off that disguise.
