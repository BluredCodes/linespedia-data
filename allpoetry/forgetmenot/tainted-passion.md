title: "Tainted passion"

I see you look at her,
the same way you look at me.

I see you treat her the same way,
and I know you know I see.

You tell me not to wait up,
cause things wont work out now.

You basically say,  "forget about me"
but i just dont know how.

There are so many reasons
that we cant be together that I know.

Im just worried that if I redirect my affections,
I will turn around regretting I let you go.

You say "maybe later..."
but do you think our feelings wont grow stale?

Do you think that if we are together
my and her friendship will fail?

I know you like her,
but do you love her so?

Please tell me the answer now,
I really think I should know....

Please dont stoop to sharing beautiful complements
and shower me with compassion.

Because I know that all you are doing
is tainting this beautiful passion.


*alithis is a poem about a guy i am dear friends with... we both admitted we cared for eachother, but we didnt do anything about it. my best friend has felt the same way about him for a while, and he admitted he cared for her to, though not as much as me.....(?)He told me maybe in a while but i dont know......I am working on redirecting my feelings on someone else and things are going well, so maybe things will change, but he and i both know i cant hurt my friend like that.
