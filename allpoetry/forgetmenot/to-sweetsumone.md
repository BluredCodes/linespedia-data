Dear sweet some one:
I know you are out there, some where.
Where are you so i can find you fast.

Ive been searching all my life for something
that is meaningful and will last.

I dont know who you are, what you look like,
or even if you care.

All i kno is Ive got to find you,
and you are out there, some where.

ali*This is a rather superficial sounding one i am not that proud of, Its just to that special guy out there i havent found my happiness with yet.
