Don't quit on nme now,
not after we came this far.
farther than any ship can sail,
or any shooting star.

We've been through everything,
wrong and right, 
we fought everything to be fought,
but we won the fight.

I know all the pain has burned,
even left us branded,
I dont know about you,
but my hopes have landed.

Before i was drowning deep
 in a sea of sorrow,
I was living in the past, 
no today or tomorrow.

My dreams drowned with me,
deep in hte big gray sea,
Then came the draught,
and sprawled on the ground was me.

I wanted to rebegin,
I wanted to start a clean slate,
I wanted a second helping,
but first clean off my plate.

I didnt want anything the same,
didnt want to leave behind a clue.
the only thing I wouldnt do diffrent,
Is be aquainted with you.

........Love you JessThis poem was written about the friendship of me and my friend Jessica Hoey ( averagestatistic).  We went to hell and back and came out and showed the world no one or anything could change us being friends, i dedicate this to her.
