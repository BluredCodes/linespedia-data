---
title: "Mother Earth’s Secret"
writer: "Netsubo"
slug: "mother-earths-secret"
source: "AllPoetry"

Enter into a cave
Study the columns,
Of a passed Queen..
Her name.. Mother earth.

Stalagmites and Stalactites,
Making a labyrinth..
More perplexing
Than the reflection within a diamond.

People say..
To look at the light
Near the end of the tunnel.
But who says it goes to the other end..
Of a mountain?

But who am I to say..
That those people are wrong?
Enter the labyrinth,
At your own risk.

I wonder how many will find this...
Note..  I’ve lain upon the ground.
At a crossroads
With the top pointing my direction.

Follow if you will..
Maybe we’ll meet on our travels.
I don’t doubt,
That others have chosen a different path.

Follow your hearts desire
Know..
If you follow your emotion
They will act as the emotion to your reason.

Knock on the door..
Of opportunities home.
Or wait..
Until you’re ready.

Inspiration,
Something..
Nice to have.
But you dont need it.

Follow yourself..
Live for what you need
Nod your head in acceptance
And don’t forget..
Smile at those you pass in this labyrinth

We’re all here..
For the same reason.
Find what you’re looking for.
You will.

But can I promise you how long..
It will take?
All the secrets..
Of your heart..
Can’t be revealed for you.

Open your eyes
You’ll see..
Open your eyes..
We’re all here..
