title: "Keeps Me Focused"

There's some static off in the distance...
And every day,
A new skill is learned.

My thanks...
Will be to do something...
With these abilities,
That I have come across.

A few tricks...
Nothing big.
Nothing yet...

But check by,
Every once and awhile.

This helps me keep focused,
Fills these drums,
With my anger.

Fills them...
Instead of my heart.
Leaves that evil,
For a black hole to deal with.

You give me mine...
I'll give you yours.

I Hope it all doesnt change...
Too much.
'Cause even though a lot is bad...
A hell of a lot is good too.

Thank you for giving me love.
Thank you for giving me feeling.
Thank you for holding me close...
Thank you.this is just the beginning of a very big thank you to the people who have kept me here and the ones who make it all worthwhile.  of course, you know who you are.
