---
title: "I'd Love To"
writer: "Netsubo"
slug: "id-love-to"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56118-Id-Love-To-by-Netsubo"
---

I’d love to tell you..
I Love You.
But you’re nowhere to be found..
And I don’t even know your name.

I’d love to tell you..
You make me Right.
But your embrace is missing
And the beating of your heart can’t be heard.

I’d love to tell you..
You put the Smile on my face
But you’re just another person in a crowd..
And I’m too blind to see..

I’d love to tell you..
All of my Hearts Secrets.

I’d love to tell you..
Every Desire I’ve ever felt..

I’d love to tell you..
How Cold it is..
Without You

I’d love to tell you..
But..
Your face is gone
And my heart is still beating..
Waiting to find you..
