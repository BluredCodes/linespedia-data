---
title: "Too Much To Lose"
writer: "Netsubo"
slug: "too-much-to-lose"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Watch your tears fall..
And feel a fraction of the sorrow in your heart
I’d catch them..
But these tears..  are on the inside.

You make it easy to see your emotions..
Sometimes.
And I wonder..
If thats on purpose.

Cause sometimes..
It’s hidden
For none to see..
Except for you,
Through the mirror of your eyes.

Seems like my words..
Aren’t doing the same as before.
And what I’ve got to give..
Isn’t enough.

Tell me..
Have I changed..
That much?

Wanting to forget it all..
Isn’t that much of a sin..
Is it?

Tell me..
If I’ve changed..
So much..
For the worse.

Cause your actions are speaking to me
And saying there isn’t much left to grab onto.
It’s hard to see your eyes drop when you see me now..
Cause it seems like im not good enough for your company..

Guess I better forget about myself,
Cause this means too much..
To let go.
It means too much..
