---
title: "Lost in your river"
writer: "BlueAngel"
slug: "lost-in-your-river"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

You came into my life like a warm breeze
Just touching me, so gently
Arousing all my senses
Waking parts of me that where sleeping
Letting the sunshine in my heart
You blew my dark clouds away
I am lost in your love
Swept away in your river
Wanting to get to know you in every way
Wanting to share your thoughts, dreams, and secrets you have
Spend time with you from beginning to end
Because there might not be a tomorrow
I've realized how blessed
I am that you share my soul 
My life
For without these pieces of you
My life is just not whole.
