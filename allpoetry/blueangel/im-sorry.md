~I'm Sorry~ 

Wipe the tears from my cheek
I'm sorry that I can't protect you all the time 
When things go wrong I try my hardess 
Try to do my best  
But I some times fail 
I am so sorry that I'm not very good at this
I wish I could make every thing go away
We will rise about this soon 
This will all fade into the back ground of our lives....
