~~Friendship~~
 
Friendship comes to me as you 
You have touched my heart.
You are a breath of fresh air
You are you..
We may live on the other ends of the earth
But we know and feel each other
We share our lives and love
We have our ups and our downs
We can go to the ends of the earth and return
And you my friend are so special to me
We become stronger and wiser
And we shall share life together
What ever it bring
As I take your hand
We shall be one ...
