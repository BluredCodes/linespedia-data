---
title: "~Touch~"
writer: "BlueAngel"
slug: "touch"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57766--Touch--by-BlueAngel"
---

The touch of your body as we meet 
To feel the passion in our hearts
Feeling your lips as they touch mine
                    Kissing you gently
Working down to your chest
Feeling you as you breath
Stimulating all of our senses
Entwined as one
Longing to be
