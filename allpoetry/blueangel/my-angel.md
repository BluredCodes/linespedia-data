Over the years my pain will lessen
But my thoughts of you will always be fresh in my mind
Feeling you moving as you grow
The excitement you bring as you play
Smiles on our faces
Knowing longing to touch you
To play with your toes kiss your fingers
You bring so much joy…
..Then
Having my heart ripped out as they tell me
Tears, Pain, Anger…
..Why
Screaming inside
No one hearing
No one knowing my pain
I start to question every thing I know
Wondering why you?
Now my thoughts are fill with
Where are you?
Wanting to hug you to see you smile
To look in your eyes, hoping you know that I miss you
I love you with all my heart and one day we will be together
My love for you is so great, loving you always…
But you already know ……

Love Mummy
