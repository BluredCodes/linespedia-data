~Machine~
They say you are just a machine 
Your noises you make 
The buzzing 
The shaking 
I hear you scanning 
You are the key to my future 
You are the one that will change my path in life 
You see the future for me 
I lay there in your small space 
Knowing this is going to change my life 
You scare me 
You are not just a machine .......
