---
title: "Can you see me?"
writer: "radha"

Can you see this carcass of mine
As I sit here looking at you
Can you smell me
Can you hunt me
Catch me as I scuttle
Sewer to sewer
Bone to bone
Like a rodent
Rip me to pieces when i'm alone


Unless I am eaten by my own heart
Unless my liver turns to dust
And I fall into a pit of darkness
Disintegrate into what I most fear to be
Unhappy, trapped and full of misery.
