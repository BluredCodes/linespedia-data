---
title: "Skin"
writer: "radha"

source: "AllPoetry"

I often wondered how it would feel
To be in your skin
To see the world as you do
Become free again
To look into a mirror
Of a thousand faces
And see you in me

I wonder how it would feel
To see a world of heaven
And not a universe of hell
To see every door open 
And feel the heat of an eternal light shining down upon me
To believe that tomorrow will come
And simplicity is the key

How good it would feel to sleep silently 
Free of evil
Of sin
And guilt
If only I had your skin
