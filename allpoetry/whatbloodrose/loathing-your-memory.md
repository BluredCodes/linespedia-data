title: "Loathing Your Memory"

These are the things that I hate about you
These are the things that taunt my withered mind
I hate your eyese
Your black soul shines right through them
I can't stand your scent
For your colone carries from blocks away
And constantly lingers apon me
I loathe your touch
So much it makes me squeemish
So much I can't stand to take into account what you've done
Until it's too late

These are the things that I hate about you
These are the things thate raise the screams from my soul
I hate the way you look at me
Staring with your lustful eyes
I can't stand your forceful ways
Locking me on the ground under your heavy body
I simply loathe your degrating tone 
Reminding me that I am nothing
And this is all I will ever amount to be

These are the things that I hate about you
These are the things that haunt me at my every waking hour

....why can't you leave me alone?....
