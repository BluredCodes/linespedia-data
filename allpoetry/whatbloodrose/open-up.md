"Open Up!"
You said you're comeing inside
Do you have a warent for this search
I don't understand what'd I've done to deserve this
I'm afraid to know what's going on inside

"Open Up!"
I said "No" buy you're still coming
Taring me apart from the depth of my soul
Stealing everything I've known to be real
And leaving me with nothig more than a mess

"Open Up!"
I cry as I pray for someone to stand up for me
But only darkness has been left there to save me

You ruffel through and destroy my innocence
So now, I've become your toy
