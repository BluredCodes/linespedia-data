My face, torn and weary
My heart, broken and burning
My body, shameful and naked
My soul, unknown

Smeared wet paint
Dance ritualy apon my face
The colors of rich blue and nothing

Messages imprinted
Flare accross my body
To let those know of the suffering
That goes on within

These scarlet teardrops
Fall from my pail wrist
A message to those whom forgot to care

These scarlet teardrops
Have left me cold
Colder than the winter days

These scarlet teardrops
Have stained my skin
Lik the ink in the bible of destruction
Like my soul is staned from within

These scarlet teardrops
