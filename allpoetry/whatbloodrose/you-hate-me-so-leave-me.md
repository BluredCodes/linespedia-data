You had me yet you hate me
You're preciousl little boy dropped out of highschool
Yet you love him above all else
You praise him and you don't care what he does
But things are diffrent for me
Appearently I'm an enormous dissapointment
With everything I've done
You don't care; and complain about how I'm a mother's worse nightmare
You bitch about your husband's race and how it differs from yours
You blame me for being the result of the man you married
You relate me to everything evil when I'm trying to love you
You say repetivly how you wish you'd ever begun to enduce my waistful presence
Well fuck mom!!
Why the hell did you have sex with a man you hate
Why the hell don't you abort me before my existance shadowed any others
Why don't you just give me up for adoption so I'd nevr know your face
Why do you put all the blame in me
Can't you be your scapegoat

You know, it's funny that you ask what I will ever amount to be
Because I'll be more than you ever were or ever will be
You know why
Because I'm not mking excuses
I'm not hiding behind false sussesses
And I'm not denying everything realistic, just to suit my own ego
BITCH!!
Why the hell can't I speak with you calmly for once
Even when I try to get advice from, you end up yelling at an have no idea what I said
I'm stuborn?
Have you ever listened to yourself
Or is that another one of this qualities you hate because of me
Can't you pick on anyone else
Isn't ther another why to relieve yourself
If you hate me so much mother then why can't you just leave me hear as I cry
Instead of mockig me until I die
