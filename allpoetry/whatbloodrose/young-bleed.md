title: "Young Bleed"
writer: "WhatBloodRose"

Scattered scarlet
Tearful faces
The draft still follows
Each families' not so cheerful dinner
The young fight
Fiercful and ignorantly
For a cause that is unknown
The yong have so much energy
Ther foolish games
Who's got the upper hand
A desparate nation
Sends our young to bleed
Is that alright?
Dear god
I hope it's not alright
