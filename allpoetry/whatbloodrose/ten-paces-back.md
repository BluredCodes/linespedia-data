Ten paces back
Behinde the other two
Ten paces back
So they've forgoten my presence
And don't ress apon me the feeling of longing

Ten paces back
Behind the other two
Ten paces back
So they can't see the tears that are falling
And can't pretend as if my presance isn't an inconvienence

Ten paces back
Behind the other two
Ten paces back
So I they cannot be bothered with my presence

Ten paces back
