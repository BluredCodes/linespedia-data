---
title: "Things She Says"
writer: "WhatBloodRose"

The careless things she says
Break my heart
And tear my soul in two
The things she says
Pushed me into maddness
An drove me ino weakness
The things she says
Show she doesn't love me
And make known her selfishness
The things she says
Aren't supposed to leave a mother's mouth
And are hurtful but true
Things she says
How if I was never born her life would be so easy
And she wouldn't have such trouble
The things she says
However cruel she may be
If my mother says them to me
Then what but the truth could it be?
