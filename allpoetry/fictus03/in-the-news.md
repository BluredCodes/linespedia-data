title: "in the news"

i’m tired of hearing about corrupt officials
about death and parents who need a box of tissues
of listening to “another shooting today”
and how our youth died in a street race
i’m tired of seeing that yellow tape
of hearing the stories of rape
and listening to the siren
while our children sit cryin’
“another robbery at the corner of. . .”
“today another parent misplaced their love”
i’m tired of witnessing the proof that our society provides
of how everyday we walk but we can’t measure our strides
is this what we’ve become
all the hard work undone
“world peace” shouts the people
is that even feasible
peace begins in our hearts
the vision dies before it starts
these tragic times 
have me crafting rhymes
because that’s how i deal with my emotional pain
the way things go i’ll be writing ‘til I’m slain
for reasons unknown we don’t love our neighbor
always fighting for things to better savor
crimes against humanity
and it’s left for us to see it on tv
our news is always filled with violence
oh when the time comes where the anchors remain silent
