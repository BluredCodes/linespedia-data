a brief soliloquy if i may
the ever driving essence
that of which sends us to the very edge of. . .
well, depression
simultaneously  brings us the joy of life
for in a single moment in the grace of 
a soul can be set free
a world of knowledge slow dances with four letters
its this that i now speak of
it contains the power to
desolate
enrage
brighten
control
it is our obsession
our want
our need
temples built with full frontal endearment
nothing more nothing less
than the everything
that of which defines our actions
severed earlobes as a post mark of love
and all its power
who are we to deny it
who are we to praise
it embeds itself in the heart of emotion
and leaves only to return
i speak of
bliss
depression
elation
sorrow
delight
grief
and of lovelove not only another but yourself as well.
