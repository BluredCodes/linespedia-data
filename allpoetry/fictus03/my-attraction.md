my attraction lies not only with your physical beauty
but with the beauty in your eyes
with the sweet sound of your voice
it lies with your gentle touch
and how to speak of love
but with the most fragile words
for even in vein it can be shattered
and while we haven’t met on the same level
as to join the heart with the soul
i pray that some day…
you see the dilemma i face
makes not my stance seem unbalanced
it merely presents the ill-timed emotion
of that which you bring to me
let it be said
all that needs to be said
unbreakable riddles of emotions
and my attention surrenders to your grasp
breaths are taken with care
as not to forget one
i pray that some day…
with your breathtaking smile
in the way that you make me feel
in all that is you
this is where my attraction lies
