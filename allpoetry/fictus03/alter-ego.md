title: "alter ego"
writer: "fictus03"

betrayed by mind set
diluted by instinct
deprived of light
the entity that finds shelter beneath our conscience
lurking in shadow and evading the right
a comforting blanket of pretext
behold all that lets us be
without regret no less
and clears our thoughts of improper
hides the essence in which we were taught
yet it creates imagination
and allows for wonder
it sits just below reality
only to be exposed when needed most
“the duality of man”
so to speak
and let it be
for devoid of our life mate
to function is closer to inconceivable
than ever it was before
deny that which “makes” us
then and only then
do we become gratuitous
