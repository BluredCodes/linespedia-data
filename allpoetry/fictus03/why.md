if a subject so fragile can be discussed than let it be discussed
the subject of why
why do we do things like that
why must you say it that way
why this and not that
it is the very question that brings upon madness
fearlessly seeping through thought
and embedding itself in mindset
full battles with this wretched enemy
and it lies unscathed
it brings us knowledge and frustration
it temps our curiosity and tires our tongue
powerless to the immortal entity
as it mocks us for all we’re worth
and taunts our need to know
while it effortlessly pulls deception over our eyes
and causes paralysis of vision
to set better terms with reason
why discuss why
to which extent
before it is driven to the brink of exhaustion
