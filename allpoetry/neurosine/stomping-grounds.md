title: "Stomping Grounds"

Victimize me,
opened them,
watched them,
carry me away across tumultuous currents,
into the unknown,
and back to myself again.
Crying Bleeding,
thin blood, thin skin.
My ancestors quarrel within me,
sending their every wish,
out from within,
my all writhing skin,
flowing in unseen currents,
adrift in warm and strange water,
comfort me...
they pretend,
I am greater than them.
Yet I'm only a strange,
perhaps maladapt,
compilation,
only a meager reflection,
of all those sould within.
