stark blaNK BLACK,
heArt Attack,
they won't give it up,
I can't take it back,
braining birdz,
stuffing their sacks,
ill tempered undulations,
broke me good.
as they should....
their job is done,
send the rest of me into the hood.
gone for good,
out of sight,
with no mind.
All alight,
undone and untwined,
this soap bubble popped.
I could have stopped,
but religious authorities,
tore me into shreads,
gently opened my lid,
and put things in my head,
shook it up,
and did it again.
They said it was because i sinned.
I'll fucking do it now,
again and again and again.
Quite the monster,
they have created.
I won't let them soothe me,
I won't be sedated.
They told me they loved me,
and loved him,
but hated....the truth after all.
I'll just fall and fall,
my head full of lies. 
To much to try to regain,
some semblance of a mortal soul...
while I'm eating...
while I'm eating them all.
