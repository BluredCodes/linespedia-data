Too lovely and pale for this punishment,
undeserving the exquisite decline,
into poignant meaningless nothing,
everything screams out its pleasure,
tasting every repetative,
application of pain.
   Again...again...once again.
Just until the blood and the cuts get to be a bit too much,
too much to touch,
to tender to clutch with this love so peculiar.
It's not wrought of hatred as such....
only an intense need to completely experience the moment,
while getting your rocks off,
hunt...hunt for that glimpse of the greater,
speachless thing....waiting there in the wonderful pain.
