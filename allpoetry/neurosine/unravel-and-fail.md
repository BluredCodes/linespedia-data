---
title: "Unravel and Fail"
writer: "Neurosine"
slug: "unravel-and-fail"
source: "AllPoetry"

Dread is an old faithful enemy,
always there when I'm losing my mind.
Steps over and right into me,
salting wounds, and disclosing,
every bitter lie.
Impending doom I know so well,
almost an old friend of mine.
There to fill the hollow place,
that used to hold space and time.
I want only freedom and solitude,
time to see who I am,
and all of the things I must do.
Fear tries to kill me outright,
then persuade,
persistently tries,
to make me destroy myself,
my body, then my mind.
