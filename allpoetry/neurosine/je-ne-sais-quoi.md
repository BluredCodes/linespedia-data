---
title: "je ne sais quoi"
writer: "Neurosine"
slug: "je-ne-sais-quoi"
source: "AllPoetry"

Indefinite laughter,
down long shallow halls,
enigmatic remasters,
of nothing at all,
seize the mind,
clench the heart with emotion.
We grasp towards that sense of wonder,
a longing undefined.
Unforfilled by god, or state, or anything made of the mind.
Always that something important,
and completely undefined.
