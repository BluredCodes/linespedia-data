Dont worry I tasted the pain,
I didn't know,
I could go insane.
Dont ever leave me and tell me it's real,
don't ever lie again,
or say how it heals.
as it hurts you.
know for a moment,
how i feel.
You don't have a clue,
or a glimpse of what's real.
Just alone in your shallow delusion,
wanting others to join,
in the massive confusion,
of a goth girl,
in a lost baptist world.
You're not,
I'd say,
an evil bitch,
just a fucked up little girl.
