title: "One Step Ahead"

Further forever,
further whatever, 
pretended endeavor,
whatever, we taste it,
or just simply fake it...,
our mouths speak of rating,
energy wasted hating,
berating our distant reflection,
only delaying that critical moment,
when you'll fly or fall....
that moment when all depends on some weary decision,
some perception many suppose lies within us all.
As if there were a clear voice to call,
and beckon us into ourselves,
and the moment.....
we become as we fall...
into it...
into us....
into we,
becoming....be.
