---
title: "Dread and Sentiment"
writer: "Neurosine"
slug: "dread-and-sentiment"
source: "AllPoetry"

Dread is never the answer,
hope is a faith and denial,
abstraction, distraction, reaction to these straw men,
leave us guilty without trial.
Relying on nothing,
sentimental drivel,
past disgust,
in anguished memories,
haunt me,
naive dreams wishing,
my soul can't tell the difference,
between a promise,
and its deliverance,
the hammer and it's swing.
