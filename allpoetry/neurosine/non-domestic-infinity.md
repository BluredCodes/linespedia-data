---
title: "Non-Domestic Infinity"
writer: "Neurosine"
slug: "non-domestic-infinity"
source: "AllPoetry"

Indebted to definably nothing,
obscenely profound laughter,
continues to escape me.
I sip at the occasional,
Pools;
that go down to infinity.
Nerd extraordinaire.
I pretend I have no time for my cares.
everything is where it should be,
Just like me.
An existence defined by denial.
Leaves nothing that can ever be fair.
Ambiguity is truth,
contradiction of every stated fact,
A sign of higher wisdom,
And another level for sorrow,
And another for despair.
No blame, vengence, or trial.
No fear, guilt, or vile,
Consensus.
We can not decide,
The future,
In predetermined whiles,
Passing by,
While we try to grasp on,
Hold it to us, while we stand,
We should know now,
Get ready to ride.
Hell, we’re already inside
