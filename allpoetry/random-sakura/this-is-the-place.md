---
title: "This is the Place"
writer: "Random Sakura"
slug: "this-is-the-place"
source: "AllPoetry"

This is the place where dreams don't die
Where apples grow and it is common to fly
Where a rainbow is brighter than any hope
Where people playing have no need to cope
This is the place where dreams don't die

This is the place where the sun never sets
Where love is free and never forgets
Where a child's face is only too pleasant
Where everyone is king and not lowly peasant
This is the place where the sun never sets

This is the place where happiness exceeds
Where thoughts and questions tend to be
Where the saddest person loves everything
Where caring is normal, and birds always sing
This is the place where happiness exceeds
