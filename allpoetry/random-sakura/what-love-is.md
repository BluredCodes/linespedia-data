---
title: "What Love Is..."
writer: "Random Sakura"
slug: "what-love-is"

Her eyes spoke wonders to my soul
I could feel the breeze slowly passing by
I, walking forward like an inorganic fate
Knowing that my complex feelings would collapse
Reaching her I slowly turned into a dark shadow
As a small glimmer of audacious hope rose… and fell
I stumble back out of sight, away yet again
They say true love is but a hallucination in our minds
I think they are fools to believe in such lunacy
What, without love, would we aspire to have in life?
Would dreams be the same without the passion of love?
Guiding our thoughts to believe in something more
I don’t imagine that possible, not in the least
The reassurance of love’s tender kiss is all I want
It is a desire, nay, a necessity in life, that is what love is
So I walk forward, darkness lifted from my path
Ready to reach my dream, my need, my love
Fate would either be cruel or compassionate
But love would always remain the same
