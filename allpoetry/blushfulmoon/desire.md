One look in your face,
Into the depths
Of your haunting eyes,
A passion ignites me
An unquenchable flame,
Deep inside my soul
An uncontrollable desire
Of which i have no right,
To ask for
Passions of emotions,
Fill my soul
With looks only for you
No words are spoken between us,
Only want and desire
It feels like electricty,
I long for my arms to feel your embrace
Two tender hearts,
Liking to be one
Yet not meant to be
