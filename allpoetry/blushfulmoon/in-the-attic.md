While cleaning out the attic
Of an old abandoned home
A old chest was found
In the corner all alone,

As i wiped off the dust and cobwebs
I lifted up the squeaking lid
Underneath lace, and tattered clothes,
Old love letters were neatly hid

Words of love had been written
And told of love that was lost,
They needed back together
No matter what the cost

Letters spoke of stolen moments
Sharing memories that were  wrong,
For they to one another
Did not belong

Just an old yellow stack of letters
Were all that remained,
Hidden in the old chest
Lost lover's without a name
