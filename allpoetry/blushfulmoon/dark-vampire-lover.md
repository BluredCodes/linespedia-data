title: "~~Dark Vampire Lover~~"
writer: "Blushfulmoon"

I and the night are one,
	As I silently stalk the shadows
	I hunger,
	To drink of thee
	An oath to darkness I have sworn
	Evil shadows cover me,
	Unholy works to be done
	Before the rising sun,
	No peace, no forgiveness,
	For my sin
	My world is black and gray,
	Don't cry for me
	Nor pray for me
	My father lives below,
	My only friends the wolf and night,
	Dark paths I must go
	No more sunlight upon my head
	Moonlight fills my sight
	No heavens angelic destiny
	Just an eternal blood thirst night
