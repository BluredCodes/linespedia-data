You came to me,
In a time of need
You gave me meaning
For something i had to search and seek,
You didn't ask for forgiveness
Yet you prayed with me,
And my sins you forgave
With a prayer,
From heaven above
In the blink of an eye,
My old life became new
So lucky to be
One of your chosen few
Now forever i will bloom,
With your soul in my heart
Never alone,
Never again in the dark
