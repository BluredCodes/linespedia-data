writer: "Pityful Me"

TELL ME
For I am needy
What do you see
When your eyes
Fall upon me

TELL ME
only the truth
good or bad
never fear that
I'll get mad

TELL ME
do not lie
For I doubt I have
The umph to say goodbye!
