Do I push the blame
Upon the Milage checker?
How much do I say
If only he'd hadn't
He wouldn't, He would
Than I would have not?

If words hadn't been spoken
Blame hadn't been hurled
Where have? Who have?
When there was... 
No where, No who
Than would there have been a who?

So, now there is a who
to deal with
A who that happened when
I was not looking or seeking
So, who do I blame?
The checker?, The Who?
Or the I?

But perhaps sometime's
Maybe 
Just maybe
"WHO" just happens!!!!
