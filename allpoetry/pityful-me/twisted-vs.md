Twisted V's
Valium and Vicadon
They 
   Slide
      Down
With the greatest of ease
The head gets fuzzy
And the ears are buzzy
And then my prose flows
Little dittys about affairs
Semi rhymes about my times

Twisted V's
Hide a lot of fears
When followed by a few more beers
Also drys dripping tears
Quells the need
To cut and bleed
Quiets the shame
And the need for self blame!!
