What is it like to be in love?
The true stuff
The Real McCoy

Does it come from 
The heart 
Or is it from the loins?

Is real love financial?
Support
Or not giving a care about funds?

Is it home together every night 
Without fights
Or moments stolen here and there?

Is it shared laughter
Tears
Or is it shared offspring?

Which voice to choose
The one that brings warmth?
Or the one that quizzes and questions?

Tell me dear reads
Just what is the REAL MCCOY????????
