The poems I write
Are as shitty as my life
Yet, you give me pretty stars
I love you all for that

Kudos to you
And like my life
I lap it all up
(GREEDY) 
All the pretty praise you give me

The poems I write
My free therapy
And the pretty red stars
Gives me a high I've never had!!

Kudos again to you
And my life
I soak it all up
(SELFISHLY)
All the sweet support you given me

I see the stars
And walk among the clouds
With my head up high
Higher than ever before in my life

THANK YOU SO MUCH !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
