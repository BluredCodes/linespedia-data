TUNA SMELLS
TUNA SMELLS

But I bet my Tunarita will sell

TUNA SMELLS 
TUNA SMELLS

Eat your tuna with Lobster tails

It's full of Tuna
Drenched in Vodka
came from Aruba
served with maracas

TUNA SMELLS 
TUNARITAS SELL

If you drink it, I won't tell


Pink,

Stick this drink 
right up your A$$
I think you're smoking 
too much grass!For Pink Fluffy Bunny in response to her poem "Lobsterita"
