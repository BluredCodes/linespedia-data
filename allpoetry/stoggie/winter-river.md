Excuse me
I’ve forgotten how to breathe
I’ve forgotten how to live
I’ve forgotten how to be everything that was me

My apologies now
Before I ruin you
Her eyes
Her breath

Heal my heart
…Then drive the knife into it
Sweet poison hurts me
Yet I love…

After all you’ve done
I’m sorry
But poison loves me

I need a change
But poison loves me

Still life
With no need to breathe
I’m wishing to stay here
Winter like waters
With an icy flow

I’ll stay here awhile
 …Heal the wounds in my chest
I’d ask you to stay
But her poison loves me

…oh my poisonous love.
