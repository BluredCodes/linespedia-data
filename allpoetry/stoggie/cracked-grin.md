My view is through a cracked smile
Oh so happy
Vibrant colours embrace a tickling grin
What makes me smile?
What makes me laugh?
Positive look, a gradual nod
Be careful not to sin

My mind is on a cracked grin
Oh so naughty
Distilled shades eating within
Why me rhyme?
Why me worry?
Negative dreams, slowly lie down
Smile me a cracked grin

My time runs on a cracked watch
Oh so slowly
Second hand moves like the hour
Kill it dying
Kill it dead
Straight posture, heighten that chin
Fret me love to die within

Smile me dying
Smile me love
Die my grin
Die my friend

Love it so happy
Like it pretend
Forget to be content
Through your soul within

Do what you need
Do what you please
As long as you promise
To smile me a cracked grin
