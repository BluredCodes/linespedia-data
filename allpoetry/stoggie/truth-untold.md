---
title: "Truth Untold"
writer: "stoggie"

For ages without days
Seemed as if time was spent waiting
In the dark of night
My life dictated by one of no intentions
Spineless actions you've taken
Turned my happiness in misery
To an everlasting slavery
Mouth of fables
To leave us with demise
Anything to bid me far from peace

A war to be raged
Throughout this mind
Clouded with memories
Internal thoughts of bliss
Ravished by your dismiss
Fucking you in the thoughts of my pain

The loss of the presence I carry
Will forever leave you in guilt
Cry forever following your heart
His fake shade of self seems to have
Taken you from me
I cannot die without one to die for
So I will eternally stand in your way
Eating at the back of your neck
Fucking you through an undying lie
