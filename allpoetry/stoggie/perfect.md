Gleaming eyes
The sound of children’s laughter
Beautiful life we lead
Bright smiles
The sight of little glaciers
Captivated by the icy shores
Satan’s dust
Distorts my already quiet voice
Ignorance becomes my source of happiness
Shady thoughts
You’ve forgotten what’s real
Kill the danger of truth

Every lie
Every child
Everyone rejecting what’s real
But they can smile
But they can pretend
And no one can tell the difference
You’ve eliminated all truth
And I’ve given in…
Forget what’s real…
It only distorts my fake smile…
