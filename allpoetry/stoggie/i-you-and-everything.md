---
title: "I, You, And Everything"
writer: "stoggie"
slug: "i-you-and-everything"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55831-I--You--And-Everything-by-stoggie"
---

I’m in the place
That is less visited
By the loved boy.
And I’m crazed by a substance
Of a natural cause.
The pain my heart is weaving
Enters me,
Dying.
Foolish mind…dead unkind,
The sane I’m grieving.

Oh, now we’ve met the end,
So long time unlived,
So long my friend.
Give me ways of release,
Anything at all.
I can no longer walk the road
Of my destroyer.
Drug endues me,
Smile me your detest.

Insane
In space
Everything here counts for less
A melody for me to breathe
Everything near comes undressed.

So, to lift off seems impossible
My legs numb, with anesthetics
You might as well hold me down
…might as well kill me now.

Deaf noise
Blind beings
I’m everything you’re ever seeing.

Even with toxins in my blood,
You’re my most unforgotten history
I’m in debt to your every memory
I’m dying for everything so graciously
And accepting the outcome so contagiously.

With the end of my life, I end your memory.

Looking for the quick cure
To this naked fantacy
With my spirit in a bottle
I’m looking to kill my heart
With my spirit in a bottle
I can pretend to end you.
Forever.
