---
title: "For Unknown..."
writer: "stoggie"

The vibrant colour of your skin…
Your presence is of a strange utopia.
My feelings are inexpressible,
Touring the ambers of my body,
As your touch soothes like a baby's formula.

You've outlined this beautiful holiness in sin.

Desire is what I have for you,
Intoxicating me even when our day is through.
Your voice echoes within my body,
Pushing me back, 
Afraid of what you're feeling...

Happiness is something I’ve longed for,
And you've filled me up to the brim...
After all those months I now feel like I can sore,
You're like a goddess, but warm blooded,
Your beauty stretches from the surface of your skin to the core,

...You’re everything I’ve been looking for...
