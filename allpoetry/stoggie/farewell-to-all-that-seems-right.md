---
title: "Farewell, to all that seems right."
writer: "stoggie"
slug: "farewell-to-all-that-seems-right"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58390-Farewell--to-all-that-seems-right.-by-stoggie"
---

On the day of the year’s final frost
A diverse beauty ended tearfully
This winter’s cold heart dies, living up to its name
In so little time I feel your love slip away
I’ll care for you always
For this is our final departure
And because I know this
And because I care
I start to slip into a hell I’ve met before
One I swore to keep my distance from

Look into this puddle of sorrow
Can your angelic eyes see our reflection?
Our dead souls lie there in discomfort
As if they had something more to say

I’ll be strong
On the outside I’ll flex my emotions
But mourn your loss within the core of my heart
I find myself silently screaming in our loss
Our death
But you raise your spirit high
Ignorant to the feelings to come
This I know
Always
This I feel
Forever
You’ll cry for the arms that once bled your name
The winter is almost through
You will wake from this benighted nightmare soon.
