Faith
In my former definition of self
Seems to have slipped
Stand with my conscience
I'll destroy the thought of happiness
And choke you with guilt

Smile
You know they're all watching you
Run away from these hands
They offer bitter love
The cheerfulness you throw up
Cannot last
It's gone
Along with these bitter loving hands

Destroy everything
But your smile
The defiant mind you posses
Will fade
The tears you've refused
Will drowned you
They will kill you

Embrace your misconception of me
Your loneliness will not end
Bathe in the pool of sadness you've created
Tears will run the course of your face
Reminding you of the mistakes you've made
