title: "The doors of Heaven opened."
writer: "New life path"

The future beacons 
With bountiful grace
Of showered gifts
And sheltered embrace.

Your dependence removed
You now stand alone,
The Master of fate,
Your soul has always known.

You'll see all the sights
Adventures threatening soon
You'll be a glittering star
Far far beyond the moon

Your spirits are rising
You fate calling you
Your name harks heralds,
Trumpeted.

In the lottery of life
You drew aces from the start
Life's handed you the frame 
Your past life of pure art.

And when the light of heaven
Dawned and touched your soul
And the memory of its' history 
Back right to start of time
Swept you through the fleeting universe 
To the gift that you now find
Searching for the answer,
But a total different kind.
