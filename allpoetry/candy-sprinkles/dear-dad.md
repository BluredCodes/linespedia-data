Dear Dad:
Mothers says, but mother doesnot.
I am here with out you.
And you didnt show again.
She takes my fragile mind
and does sommersaults inside
"He doesnt love you nor want you near"
I take the wind at my back 
like you used to say
Mommy hates me
she tells me everyday
I made you leave
because I am brat
I didnt brush my teeth
or pick up after the cat
I promise Dad ill be good from now on
I dont care about those other girls
Please daddy hurry, get on the ball
Mommy isnt dearest at all.........
