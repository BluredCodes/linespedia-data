title: "Shanghai Easy"
writer: "Candy Sprinkles"

When you come to the realization,
of being a tourtured soul.
Its more than opening your eyes,
for you body pays the toll.
Some regret, some live in fear
Some become heartless,
and never shed a tear.
For having a scar,
placed upon your heart,
living the rest of your life
but living it apart.
Not saying to look for the next,
but because of him dont blame the rest.
Look at it as if, 
another chapter in your book.
But always remember when he left,
its your heart he took.
