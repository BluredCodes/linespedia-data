---
title: "Your Mouth"
writer: "fantastix"
slug: "your-mouth"
source: "AllPoetry"

Crimson lips, soft crimson lips part ways
Swallow my sweetness inside you
Into your mouth where my tongue once lived
Down your stomach where my kisses roamed
Inside of you...inside of me
Infinity
