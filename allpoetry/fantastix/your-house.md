---
title: "Your House"
writer: "fantastix"
slug: "your-house"
source: "AllPoetry"

Waiting out on a limb can I come in?
I bet you’re ignoring my knocking
I won’t go away this time you’ll pay it is you who has stolen
My heart away
I want it back if you don’t like its design
After all my love you will always be mine
