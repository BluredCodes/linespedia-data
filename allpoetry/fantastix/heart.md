When tomorrow looks as though it’s ten million miles away
And yesterday lingers in the bitter shadows 
I’ve learned you have to hold on to your heart
If no one will hold you now and emptiness is filling your eyes with tears
Love will come fast and furious
When you promise to follow your heartThis is the weakest thing I've written lately. My apologies to all who read it.
