---
title: "A Love Like Breathing"
writer: "fantastix"

The first time I saw your face, I waited for the next glance
I longed to feel your eyes burn their way deep into my soul
Slowly, when I felt your touch time stood still
You became the sun and the moon with a gentle trace of your fingers
As feelings washed over me I couln't move past any part of your beautiful mystery
Taking me to a house of light that alluminates so bright, leading me to the coldest truths
Your voice a silent serenity, my lovely oasis, my way home
So soft and flowing, lulling me into dream
Falling into your arms, inhaling all that you are, breathing out all the lies that bound
The embers of my heart burn of the flame you ignited with insatiable hunger
With every fiber of myself weaving into every part of you
I found a love like breathing to be a dream come true
