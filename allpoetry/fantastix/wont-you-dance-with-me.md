---
title: "Won't you Dance with me?"
writer: "fantastix"

Don’t be such a playa
Watch me spin around and around 
Don’t take your eyes off me or I might fall
Dance with me baby
With your eyes No No don’t touch!
Keep your eyes locked with mine
Burn deep fuel the fire in my soul
Taste it with your tongue but don’t touch me
Just 
Watch me 
Intensely
Dance with me baby
Keep the tempo with your stare 
Guide the rhythm
Oh won’t you dance with me?
