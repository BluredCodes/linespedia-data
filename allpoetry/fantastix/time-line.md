Look past the mystery 
The truth you cannot see
Forget about the history
I want you to look at me

Life and love are allusions
darkness and light come together as one
Hearts and souls a dillusion
Nothing more can be done

Smiles there like masks--
On the faces of ones we claim to know
I try to fulfill my task
As their secrets and mine harbor and grow

To the future 
From the past
Link the torture 
Break it fast

Look past the mystery 
The truth you cannot see
Forget about the history
I want you to look at me
