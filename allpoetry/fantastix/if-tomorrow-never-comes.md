If tomorrow never comes
My last thought tonight would be of you
Not of your angelic face, nor your gentle voice
Just your words and promise that kept me safe
Your heart opened up and let my sorrows live inside

If clouds forever loom over my sunshine
May your smile be my rainbow
If forever ended yesterday
May your shadow watch over me in peaceful slumber

If my voice echos endlessly
Singing towards the gates of God
May he remember your name
Watching over you as I'd hoped to 

If tomorrow never comesI wrote this for Nick, he had gone away for a while and I hoped he would come back. However there is something ironic about the date of this poem.  September 11, 2001 was an earth shattering, life changing event. It made everyone thin about today and tomorrow and forever. This poem is dedicated  to all those who never got to say goodbye.
