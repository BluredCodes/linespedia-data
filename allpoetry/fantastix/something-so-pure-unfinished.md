Something so pure when you walked in the door
You changed my life and made everything right
That night 
That night
The sky opened wide as heaven parted its clouds, stars exploded colors of red and blue 
Turning the happy moon a shade of deep purple, bidding the fiery sun adieu
Shaken to the core of my soul, the earth quaked beneath my feet
Your breath swirled around so warm and so free, swiftly in time with my tender heartbeat
