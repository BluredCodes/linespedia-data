---
title: "I Could"
writer: "fantastix"
slug: "i-could"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62457-I-Could-by-fantastix"
---

I could fly around the world on a carpet woven of dreams
I could stay in a palace with golden stairs, where it rains gems of all sorts
Diamonds, rubies, emeralds, and amethyst 
I could eat  on the finest china, with the most glorious silver 
Dine on the most succulent of delicacies
And still be lonely 
I could live each day like a goddess smeared in fragrant oils
I could think exotic thoughts and live out my countless fantasies
Floating on a cloud, held close and cherished
I could whisper all my secrets to the endless blowing wind
Weeping time after time
And it still won’t mean a thing
I could feel the love of the entire universe rest in my hands
I could bathe in a sea of silk, nourishing my soul
Soft, smooth, milky, and pure
I could sail across the seven seas on a boat fueled by desire
I could see the orange blazes of the setting sun 
Deep, passionate, hot, and bold
I could sleep for the rest of my days on a magical journey to my heart
Blissfully imagining the way it all might be
Still I wonder if anyone will ever be beside me
