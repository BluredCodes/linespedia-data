---
title: "I Loved You"
writer: "fantastix"
slug: "i-loved-you"

You will always be a phantom kiss
A daylight dream
And I have the sense to know I don't know how to 
Let you go
As smoothly as you came
So casually you turned away 
Eager to fly
Love gone before it came to be
Eyes blind before they had a chance to see
You will always be an empty promise
An endless apparition
And I have the sense to know
I'll never understand why I loved you so
