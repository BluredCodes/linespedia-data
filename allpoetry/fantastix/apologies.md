Don’t you get it? 
Believe me when I say I’m sorry, cause I’ve never felt this way before
Sometimes I don’t say the right thing or say the right thing the wrong way
God only knows that I mean well and I never want to hurt you
But somehow it’s out of my hands
I babble and f*ck up so many times
It’s a wonder you’re still by my side
