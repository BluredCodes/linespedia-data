Dear, Soul mate,
     These are a few things you should know : In our life we have a mirror spirt that exsiste in the world. When you find one you may feel you know the person and you have meet before,  because they remind you of  your own soul. If you find this person hold then with the the power of God, never let them go you will find but one. Seize the day, Never have regrets. And most importantly, never give up hope. Nothing can stop a love that is true, not even death. Love always finds a way. When you are afraid and alone, know that there is one that loves you and waits for you.  Love will find us together, but if you would my love, find your way to me today for what better time to start? 
                                       Your true loveThis is to my soul mate the only one I love.
