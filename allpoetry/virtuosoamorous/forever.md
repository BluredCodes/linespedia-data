Forever, the grass will grow

Forever, the river will flow

Forever, Love will I know

Forever, the flowers will bloom 

Forever, the Birds will fly

Forever, little children will ask why

Forever, my love will be held in your shy

Forever, time will move on 

Forever, I will live

 Like these things Love is life and for as long as one holds it in there heart  for me I will see forever,

I will grow,

flow,

bloom,

fly and be held in your loving shy.Note to those that read this, Love is immortal and like it's maker will never die or be taken from you. For as long as one person holds love in there heart for you, YOU will never die.
