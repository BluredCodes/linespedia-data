SOULMATES are made in heaven
for you and me.

When I look at you I see me.

Today I thought how it would
be when GOD brings my SOULMATE
to me.

Tomorrow is a new day full of dreams,
waiting for GOD to give you to me.

SOULMATES are made in heaven you see.
SOULMATES are made for you and me.

Yvonne J. Griffin
