Tears of joy, laughter
and happiness.

Tears of depression, anger
and sadness.

God gave us all these tears
to use each and everyday.

When we cry he hears us.
When we laugh he feels us.

When we cry our tears of 
depression he is right 
with us.

Show God that you 
know how to use your
tears each and everyday.

Yvonne J. Griffin
