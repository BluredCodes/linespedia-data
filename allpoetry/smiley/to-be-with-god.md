TO BE  WITH GOD
by Yvonne Griffin  7/23/99

1) TO BE WITH GOD
 is to have blind faith

2) TO BE WITH GOD
 is to allow him to strip
 you of your pride.

3) TO BE WITH GOD
 is to allow him to  give you
 strength when you need
 it.

4) TO BE WITH GOD
 is to claim your joy.

5) TO BE WITH GOD
 is to claim your healing

6) TO BE WITH GOD
 is to give up your sins.

7) TO BE WITH GOD
 is to ask for forgiveness.

8) TO BE WITH GOD
 is to share his word.

9) TO BE WITH GOD
  is to love one another.

10) TO BE WITH GOD
   is to believe that he can
   conquer all.
-AMENThis was my first poem.
