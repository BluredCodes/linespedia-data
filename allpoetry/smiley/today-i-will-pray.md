title: "Today I  Will Pray"

Today I will pray 
for the lost souls 
of the day.

Today I will pray
for the children
that can't play.

Today I will pray
for the families
that have no place
to stay.

Today I will pray
for every girl and
boy who's father
passed away.

Today I will pray
for every woman 
who longs to be
a mother.

Today I will pray
for the lovers who
were told to stay
away.


TODAY AND EVERYDAY I WILL PRAY.

Yvonne J. Griffin
