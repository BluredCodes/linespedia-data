title: "CLUELESS (so I thought)"

All alone, all by myself.
All I can do now is think
of how you left me.....
Left me without a clue.
Now I sit here all alone
with all these thoughts
I have in my head.

Going 'round and 'round
wishing that I were dead.
No answers from you.
No signs that your
return is soon.

I was clueless the 
whole time until the
very end. All I 
wanted to do is curl 
up and waste the rest
of my life away.

On that uneventual night...
I washed down many aspirins
with vodka and orange juice,
wishing that the pain would 
just go away.

By God's good grace I woke
up the next morning. That 
was then I realized that
I had to choose between 
life and death. This time
I could not be clueless on
this decision.

Then I realized that
someone had to be with 
our children(it wasn't 
going to be you).

Loving them, nuturing them
and to guide them in the
right direction.


As you see, I choose life 
over death. After all I 
am the one raising our
children.

Yvonne Griffin
