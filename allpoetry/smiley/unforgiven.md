I woke up this morning,
        and
you were not by my side.

OOPS I forgot that you 
are unforgiven, for the
things you said last night.

I came home from work today,
         and
you weren't there.

OOPS I forgot that I kicked
you out, because you were
unforgiven for seeing that
young thing today.

Yvonne J. Griffin
