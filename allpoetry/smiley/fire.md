Flames of love.

Flames of passion.

Flames of anger.

Flames of disgust.

THIS IS WHAT FIRE REPRESENTS IN US.

Flames of destruction.

Flames of sorrow.

Flames of beauty.

THIS IS WHAT FIRE REPRESENTS IN THE WORLD.

Fire dances.

Fire weeps.

FIRE IS THE ONLY BEAUTY THAT WE CAN'T BEAT.

Next time when you look at a flame, let the 
FIRE speak to you.

Yvonne J. Griffin
