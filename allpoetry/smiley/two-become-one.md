Longing to feel your embrace,
gentle touch to my face.

Our legs connecting as our
bodies ever so gently touch.

I look up at your soft luscious lips
waiting for our kiss to commence.

You look deep into my eyes as you
softly whisper those three words.

Our lips finally meet and the thought
of our saliva exchanging is just
the ultimate goal.

Your lips are so tender to the touch.
Your tongue is doing things
that is making my body 
go into a rush.

A rush of shivers that is.

You pull me in so that
our bodies truly meet.
I hold on tight with
much delight.

This feels like eternity
and I wish that it could
last forever.

BUT..........

Eventually we will have 
to come back to 
REALITY.

YVONNE J. GRIFFIN
