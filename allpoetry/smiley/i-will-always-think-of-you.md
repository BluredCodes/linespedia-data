I thought about you last night.

I thought about you today.

I remember when I want to think of
you, all I have to do is pray.

I will continue to pray each day
and night.

To keep your love in my heart
(I feel that is right).

Through my prayers, you let me
know that you are always near.

Thank you GOD, for always being
there for me.

Thank you GOD, for showing your
love to me.

With this poem GOD, I'm asking you
today. Please continue to show me
your love each and everyday..

Yvonne J. Griffin
