title: "Places of Wonder"

Let us go to the places of wonder
The places that bring us to our feet
That seems to make us do the impossible
But impossible, no, a journey.
Let us go to the place where water runs through the streets
Where a ride with a pole, lantern, and a stranger
Doesn't seem strange at all.
Let us go to the jungle
Where wildlife runs
And there are no cages to hold back their freedom
The comfort of security will not be at hand.
Let us go to the great water
Where the boats sail with the river north
And the greatest of the rulers ruled.
Let us go to seemingly unending stone
Where battles were fought and a country protected
But when does the stone end?
When does it all end?
Let us go to the places of wonder.Anything that could be changed, that you think would help.
