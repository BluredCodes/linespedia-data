Tunes For Baboons



                   Anything you do
                   they'll misconstrue

                   Anything you say
                   they'll raise a nay

                   For anything you'd think
                   your life they'd try to sink

                   So toss em a bananna Hana!
                   because maybe they'll
                   slip on the peel?
                   and theres no tellin how good
                   that could feel!
