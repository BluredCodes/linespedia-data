What's This Feeling.

Whats This Feeling Way Inside
Why Can't I Get You Off My Mind
The Answers I Seek I Can Not Find
The Answers Arn't Out There Or Am I Blind

It's Hard To Act,Or Be Just Friends
It's Not My Heart Twist And Bends
I'll love You More Than You'll Ever Know
But All You Do Is Turn And Go.

Whats This Feeling Way Inside
Why Can't I Get You Off My Mind
The Answers I Seek I Can Not Find
The Answers Arn't Out There Or Am I Blind

Where Have You Gone Why Did You Leave
Why Can't You Stay And Try And Belive
That Loves Right In Front Of You
Just Give It A try
Before I Just Give Up And Let My Love Die

Whats This Feeling Way Inside
Why Can't I Get You Off My Mind
The Answers I Seek I Can Not Find
The Answers Arn't Out There Or Am I Blind

Just Give Me A Chance
Look In My Eyes
Im Not Worried If Tears Fall From Your Eyes
Im Trying To Prove My Love To You
I Will Do,What Ever I Have Too
Just Cause I'm Trying To Prove
My Love Too.....You.....
