You Where My Angel 
 My Voice Of Reason.
YOU Where The One To Make Me See
 That The world Was Not Just For Me
You Showed That Theres Beauty In Every way..
And My One Wish Was For You To Stay
 Here With Me From
 From The Start To The End


Never Before has Anyone Touched My Life
The Way You Did
Never Have I Thought I Was Born For A Reason
 You Change My Life Though And Though
For The One Simple Reason
" I Love You"
The Things I Did Where Never Enough
Not MAtter What I Did I Still Broke in Half
When You Left Me Here With A Broken Heart
I thought We'd Be Together and Never Apart


Your Heart Like Gold..
The Only Treasure I Want
Your Eyes Like Dimonds
Sparkling Like The Stars.
The Way You Smiled
The Way You Laughed
Makeing Me Happy Just To Be Right there With You

How You Touched My Life You'll Never Know
Listening TO My Problems ..
Holding My Secrets.Never Told Before
You Know My Life Better Than Me
And As I ThinK How Can That Be
I Found The Answer 
And Thats The Part..
You Where The Only One With The Key To My Heart

You Showed Your Love From The Start.
Saying We'd Never Be Apart.
What Happened To That Saying You Said To Start
Its Ok That Where Apart but why did you steal my heart?

YOu Make Me Smile..
From The Gift Of Your Heart..
YOur Kind Words 
Your Life Like A Story
