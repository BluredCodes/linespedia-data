HOW OUR LOVE IS SHOWN

How Our Love Is Shown You Have Never Seen
but there something I have to say
Girl I love you and every singel Way..
I love your smile and looking in your eyes
the jewels of love that can never be despiced

Stareing In The Sky And I Wounder
 Why I'm Not By You Side
I Close my eyes And see you there
every moment i can spare
just seeing you is all i need to brigthen my day
Its Not Magic Girl its Just Your Way

I Miss You Girl I Miss you more and more
So I took Your Picture Off Of The wall 
Holding It Close to my heart i watched the
first Sunset Of the year
Hoping You Where Seeing It Too
Feeling your love through and through

I Sit By The River Thinking Of You
Woundering How To Prove My Love To You
The Only Thing That Came In My Mind
Was My Missing You

Girl I Know I Dont Show You My Love That Well
But If You Look In My Heart
theres Not One Part Thats No Defoted
To "YOU"
So Please Know From me To you
I LOVE YOU
