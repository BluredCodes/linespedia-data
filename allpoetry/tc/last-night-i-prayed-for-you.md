LAST NIGHT I SAID A PRAYER FOR YOU, THIS IS WHAT I ASKED THE LORD TO DO:
TO PROTECT AND WATCH OVER YOU
EVERY WAKING DAY, UNTIL THE NIGHTS’ END.
TO HELP YOU DISCERN YOUR ENEMIES FROM YOUR FRIENDS.
TO BLESS YOU WITH UNBOUNDED PEACE.
TO GIVE YOU STRENGTH WHEN YOU ARE WEAK.
TO GIVE YOU A CARING HEART, SO YOU MAY FORGIVE THOSE WHO DO YOU WRONG.
TO GIVE YOU COURAGE TO FACE THE OBSTACLES THAT MAY COME YOUR WAY.
TO BLESS YOU WITH AN ABUNDANCE OF FAITH AND PATIENT, TO HELP YOU WIN THIS GAME WE PLAY CALLED LIFE.
TO GIVE YOU WISDOM TO KNOW THAT THINGS HAPPEN FOR A REASON AND TO UNDERSTAND IT’S IN THE MASTER’S PLAN.
SOME COME INTO OUR LIVES ONLY FOR A SEASON, SOME LONGER.
TO HELP YOU REMEMBER THAT WHAT DOESN’T KILL YOU ONLY MAKES YOU STRONGER
FINALLY I ASKED THE LORD TO NEVER FORSAKE YOU.
