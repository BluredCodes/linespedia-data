---
title: "My dying heart"
writer: "Tc"
slug: "my-dying-heart"
source: "AllPoetry"

I VOWED TO LOVE YOU, YOU VOWED TO LOVE ME.
WHAT HAS HAPPENED REMAINS A MYSTERY.
FOR WHAT WAS, APPEARS TO NO LONGER BE.
WE'VE LOST SIGHT, DON'T DO ANYTHING BUT FUSS AND FIGHT.
SEEMS LIKE WE CAN'T FIND SOLID GROUND.
THERE'S NOTHING BUT DARKNESS ALL AROUND.
NEITHER OF US WITH A THING TO SAY.
WE SIT BACK AND WATCH LOVE SLOWLY SLIP AWAY.
I'VE TRIED ALL. 
DON'T KNOW WHAT ELSE TO DO, TO STOP THIS PAIN FROM RIPPING ME APART.
TELL ME. . . CAN YOU HEAR THE SOUND OF MY DYING HEART?

ONCE I MADE PLANS FOR A FUTURE FOR YOU AND ME.
NOW A FUTURE I CAN'T FORESEE.
WE'VE REACHED A NEW PLATEAU AND THE MAN I LOVED,
IS NO LONGER THE MAN I KNOW.
FEELINGS OF EMPTINESS TEAR THROUGH MY SOUL.
WHILE I STRUGGLE TO STAY IN CONTROL.
DON'T KNOW WHAT ELSE TO DO, TO STOP THIS PAIN FROM RIPPING ME APART.
TELL ME. . . CAN YOU HEAR THE SOUND OF MY DYING HEART.
