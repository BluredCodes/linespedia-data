Last night I had a dream.
It was such a beautiful scene.
The grass was the prettiest shade ~ bluish-green.
It had to be an autumn day.
For the leaves on the trees had started to change.
To beautiful hues of burnt orange and gold.
Oh! what a lovely sight to behold.
The wind was playing a gentle melody,
While blowing softly through the fallen leaves.
There was a pond about a mile or so.
With images of you and me sitting underneath a huge oak tree.
Your hands caressed my thigh, your lips found my breast,
and slowly we undressed.
Our souls intertwined, we were suspended in time.
My emotions, I couldn't contain, breathlessly I called out your name.
As we reached our peak, our bodies shake fervently releasing sweet ecstasy.
Oh! it was such a lovely scene, even if it was just A Dream.
