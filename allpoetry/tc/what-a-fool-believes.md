STILL WATERS RUN DEEP, BUT EVEN A FOOL KNOWS 
STILL WATERS RUN COLD.
SOME SAY WHAT GOES AROUND, COMES AROUND
…AND
EVERY DOG HAS ITS DAY.  WELL MY FRIEND YOURS IS ON THE WAY.

I’LL WEAR A SMILE AS MY DISGUISE AND WIPE THE TEARS FROM MY EYES.
I’LL NO LONGER WONDER WHY. I’LL HOLD MY HEAD UP HIGH.
FOOLISH IS A PERSON THAT DOESN’T KNOW . . .
THAT ONE PERSON’S TRASH IS ANOTHER PERSONS GOLD.

NO PAIN, NO GAIN IS WHAT I’VE BEEN TOLD.
SO WHAT LESSON HAVE I LEARNED FROM LOVE’S LITTLE GAME?
WHEN SOMEONE MAKES A MOCKERY OF LOVE IT’S REALLY A SHAME
…AND
WHAT ONE CAN’T BEGIN TO CONCEIVE.
IS REALLY WHAT A FOOL BELIEVES.
