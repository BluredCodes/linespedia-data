title: "Don't cry for me"

WHEN I DIE, DON’T CRY FOR ME
REJOICE.
FOR MY SPIRIT WILL BE FREE AND MY MIND WILL KNOW PEACE
MY TORMENTED SOUL WILL MOVE ON TO A PLACE, WHERE TIME HAS NO VALUE
AND EVERYONE IS COLOR-BLIND.
ALL THAT WILL REMAIN BEHIND IS MY WORN AND BATTERED BODY,
THAT WAS ALWAYS AT WAR WITH ITS SELF.
BUT EVEN IN DEATH MY BODY TOO WILL FIND REST.
SO YOU SEE, WHEN I DIE DON’T YOU DARE CRY FOR ME.
REJOICE.
BECAUSE I’M FINALLY FREE.
