title: "All I  wanna do"

I WANNA HOLD YOU IN MY ARMS TO KEEP YOU SAFE FROM HARM.
LAY YOUR HEAD ON MY CHEST,SO YOUR WEARY MIND CAN FIND SOME REST.
BE YOUR LOVER.  YOUR BEST FRIEND.
BE THERE FOR YOU THRU THICK 'N' THIN.
TURN YOUR FROWN INTO A SMILE.
WHEN SOMEONE OR SOMETHING GETS YOU DOWN.
WRITE A LOVE SONG TO THE BEAT OF YOUR HEART,
JUST TO LET YOU KNOW HOW VERY SPECIAL YOU ARE
THERE ARE A MILLION THINGS I CAN THINK TO DO
BUT ALL I WANNA DO IS SHARE MY LIFE WITH YOU.
