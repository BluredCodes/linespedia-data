MR B.

MR B IS AS SEXY AS CAN BE.
HE’S FIVE FEET- NINE WITH DREAMY BEDROOM EYES
AND KISSES THAT TASTE LIKE WINE.
HE’S AS BOW-LEGGED AS THE DAY IS LONG.
HIS NOSE IS ALWAYS COLD, BUT THAT JUST MEANS HE HAS A WARM HEART.  
SO I’VE BEEN TOLD.
FOR HIM THE SKY’S THE LIMIT.
IN MY LIFE, I’D DO ANYTHING TO HAVE HIM IN IT.
HE’S THE COMPLEXION OF CARAMEL
FROM FIRST SIGHT, HEAD OVER HEELS I FELL 
MORE THAN A SCHOOL GIRL WITH A CRUSH
JUST THE MENTION OF HIS NAME WOULD MAKE ME BLUSH.
BEING WITH HIM WAS MY CLAIM TO FAME
SECRETLY, MR.B & ME
SO I CHANGED HIS NAME TO MAINTAIN ANONYMITY
LOVING HIM WAS HEAVENLY.
I SHOULD HAVE KNOWN MR.B WAS TO FINE
TO BE MINE, ALL MINE,
WITH HIS KISSES THAT TASTE LIKE WINE 
AND THOSE HAZEL BROWN EYES
LOVING HIM DAMN NEAR DROVE ME OUT OF MY MIND
BUT MR.B WILL FOREVER BE MY FANTASY.
