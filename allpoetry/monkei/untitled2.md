My mind is empty...
Blank...
I can't think...
But I'm not sure I want to.

I've told you everything,
Almost...
And now I'm tired...
and I'm going to sleep.

I've explained my self fully,
Or as best I know how...
Now you know me...
Not just my mask...

But, Now,
I'm tired...
And I'm going to sleep.My poem "Now You Know" (definitely better) came from this one.  Just thought I'd go ahead and post it.  The two are pretty different, but the ending is similar.
