---
title: "Always There"
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "always-there"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55064-Always-There-by-Monkei"
---

You're always there,
When I'm hurting.
You're right there for me,
When I'm in pain.

The times when I need to talk to you most,
The times when you promise a word,
You're there.
You're always there.

So last night,
When I needed you
to tell me everything was ok...
You were there.

Last night, I needed you
To hold me
And make everything all right...
And you were there.

I always know,
What I'm going to do
When I need you,
Because you're always there.

I know what I am supposed to do,
When I need your shoulder
To rest my head on,
And cry my tears.

I figured out how to do this,
Because you're always there
When I need you to be
There for me.

But can I make this work?
When I'm finally ready to open,
Ready to tell you all...
Will you be there?

I don't know what I will do,
If I am looking
For comfort from you,
And you're not there.

I don't know
If I can stand to shed tears,
Cry by my self,
If you're not there.

So I'm telling you now,
Just as you wished
I won't know what to do
When you're not there.

You said you'd be there last night,
You said we'd talk.
And when I called on you,
You were there.

So now I'm sitting here,
Writing a poem
Dedicated to you
So you will know.

I don't know
If I can hold on.
Can I depend on you
To be there?

Because,
I can depend on you
On you for anything...
Right?

Because, 
You know the pain I feel,
And you'd never leave me alone...
Right?

I know,
You're not like them all,
And I can put my trust in you...
Right?

What I'm wondering now,
Is that,
If that's the case
What happened tonight?

Tonight I needed you
And you,
My dependable buddy,
Weren't there...This poem is "Never There" reworded to fit my situation...I think I like this one better.
