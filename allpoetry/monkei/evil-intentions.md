They're closing in around me,
Suffocating me,
Blocking out the light.
The noise surrounds me,
Drowning me out.

They keep coming closer,
Make them stop!
It feels like the sound,
Is reverbrating,
Off the inside of my head.

I don't know what to do,
how to protect myself,
from the barage of the people around me.
I see evil intentions,
In all of their eyes.

I wish you could see,
How they affect me.
I wish you could save me,
Take the people away.
But you can't.

I wish you knew,
The feeling inside me,
When they surround me like this.
I wish I could explain it,
But I can't.

So, for now,
I'll hold my breath,
And close my eyes,
And use all of my strength,
To keep it from showing.

There is nothing else I can do,
I am afraid.
All I know, is that
I have to control it,
