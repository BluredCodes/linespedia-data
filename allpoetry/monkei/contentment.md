I don't know,
What it is,
I'm feeling right now.

I don't understand,
The emotion,
Rushing through me right now.

I didn't but just realize,
That I only feel it,
When I'm with you.

I think I know,
I think I see,
What is happening.

I think, finally,
You've gotten to me.
You have been trying.

Did you see,
Did you feel,
The way we connected tonight?

Do you realize,
What happened tonight?
Do you know?

We talked,
We disscussed,
And we shared.

Like old friends,
We pulled down our guards,
And let our hearts out.

I think,
In those last few minutes,
I leared more about myself and you,
Than I ever knew before.

I think this feeling,
That I'm finally feeling,
Is called contentment.
One like I've never felt before.
