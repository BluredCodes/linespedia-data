title: "I'm Leaving"

I'm leaving...
I can't stay here,
And suffer the abuse,
You don't know your giving..

I'm leaving...
I'm tired of the drunken nights,
And the nightmares I wake up to,
That you don't know your causing..

I'm leaving...
I can't take any more pain,
And I can't take any more of you,
You don't realize what your doing to me do you?

I'm leaving...
I'm hurting...
And you can't tell.
You think that I am yours.

I'm leaving you here
To deal with the problems
That you can't share with me
That I'll never be able to fix.

I'm leaving me here
To start over
So you can't find me
When you find yourself...alone

I'm leaving...
I tell myself,
But then...
I wake up.For those of you who know me, the inspiration for this was simply a comment I read...I'm not going anywhere.
