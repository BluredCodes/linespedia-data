The more I am around you,
The fuller I feel.
The more of a person
I see in me.
And less of the coldness,
Remains.

The more I see you,
The more I catch myself smiling.
The farther you get into my mind,
The lighter my heart gets.
And now, when I close my eyes at night.
I know you'll be there tomorrow.

The more I put my trust in you,
The more you put hy heart as ease.
Because the more I trust you,
The more you give me reason to.
I never, in my life,
Been able to trust someone like I can you.

So forgive me,
If I hang on the subject.
You can't immagine,
How nice it can be,
To have a person,
That you can trust completely.

But I wish,
You would hang around more,
And the smile would light on my face,
Every time I saw you at the door.
'Cause I know when I see you,
That my day just got better.The last stanza is not great, but then this one isn't one of my best, I just need to get it out.
