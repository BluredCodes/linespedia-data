---
title: "You Saved Me..."
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "you-saved-me"

The stillness surrounds me,
Envelopes me,
Releases me.

The quiet is suffocating,
Peaceful,
Rejuvenating....

I am no longer alone,
The pain has finally given way,
I am no longer afraid.

You've done it, you know,
Released me,
Set my heart free.

You've made it ok,
Set my life straight again,
Turned it back around.

I'm headed down the right track now,
Picking up speed,
You saved me.

I just thought I'd thank you,
For everything you've done,
Everything you've given me.

I just want you to know,
I appreciate you,
You saved me.

And that means more to me than you know,
More than you can comprehend,
You saved me.I'm not very sure about the ending, but I think generally it came out ok.
