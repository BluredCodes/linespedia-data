---
title: "I'll Always Be Here"
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "ill-always-be-here"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57730-Ill-Always-Be-Here-by-Monkei"
---

When I look into your eyes,
I see you looking back.
And I know you'll always be there.

When I reach for you,
I feel you reaching back,
And I know you'll always be there.

When I smile at you,
I see you smiling back,
And I know you'll always be there.

You may not understand,
Why I feel the way I do.
But I just though you might want to know,
That I do.

Because I know...
You'll always be there.
Know...
I'll always be here.
