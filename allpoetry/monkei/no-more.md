---
title: "No More"
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "no-more"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57731-No-More-by-Monkei"
---

You have felt so much pain,
And been through so much,
That it hurts.
It hurts me to know,
How much you've felt.
I want to take it all away.
I want to purify your heart,
And clear your mind.
So the pain you once knew,
Can be no more.
