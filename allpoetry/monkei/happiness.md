What did you do?
How did you do it?
Tell me!
You made it all better,
And you didn't even do in conciously.
I don't know how you achieve things like that.
I don't know what it is that makes me so happy.
But apparently you do.
How do you do it?
If only I knew....
It makes me want to be around you all the time.
Even when the conflicting emotions are running through my brain.
Even when those "Tears" are running down my cheeks.
You make me happy.
And I don't even know how.
For so long I was so unsure.
And for some reason,
Tonight...
All of the clouds were swept away.
And reasurance took its place.
This is a joy I've never known,
A love without fear.
A care without doubt.
A happiness without tears.
This time...
No tears tonight,
Only smiles.
I only hope...
you feel this to.
This increadible happiness.
This wonderful release.
I want you to always be as happy as I am tonight.
I want you to always feel this wonderful.
No matter what.
Ok?
Always....
Be happy.Not really a poem, but then my last few haven't REALLY been poems.  I feel like they say a lot though.  There has been a lot of emotion lately, and I'm just sorting it all out.
