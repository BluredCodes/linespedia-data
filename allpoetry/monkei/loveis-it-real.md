Love...
How would you define the word?
How can one word,
One four letter word,
Mean so much?

How do you know,
When your in love?
Is there a difference,
Between being IN love,
And loving someone?

Can someone tell me,
How to tell?
Can someone tell me,
What it is?
Can someone show me,
A picture of it.

Is it real, 
If you can't see it,
Can't touch it,
Can't taste it?
How can something so real,
Be so abstract?

How can something,
That hurts so many,
Be so wonderful?
How can something,
That causes so much pain,
Be the focus of everyone's life?

Love...
