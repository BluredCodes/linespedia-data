The feelings you provoke in me,
ones I've never felt before,
are indescribable.
I want to be with you,
every second, of every day.
You are indescribable.
You ask me...
Why?
But I can't tell you,
You're indescribable.
I know you can't immagine why,
I care so much for you,
But I can't tell you why,
I'd love to,
But you're indescribable.
You're wonderful.
You walk into the room,
And my heart skips a beat.
You hold me in your arms,
and I've never felt safer.
But you...
You are indescribable.
I don't know how to explain,
What it is I feel for you,
it, is indescribable.
I just hope you know,
and never doubt,
that I care about you,
even if I can't tell you why.
You...
are indescribable.Got this poem from a title suggestion on another one from Hollywood, (thank you) But it does relate to my life, and is dedicated to someone. (A)
