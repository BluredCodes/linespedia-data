title: "If I Could Paint"

If I could paint,
What I feel.
If I could put it on a canvas.
It would be a mixture,
Of calming blues,
Invigorating greens,
And blood reds.

If I could paint,
What I feel.
If I could put it on a canvas
For the whole world to see.
I would paint of laughter,
And sorrow.
I would paint of smiles,
And tears.

If I could paint,
What I felt today.
If I could put it on a canvas.
I would paint fields of flowers,
And a little summer shower.
I would paint, a bright shining sun,
And a sunset to remember.

If I could paint,
What I felt today.
If I could put it on a canvas.
I would paint a beautiful dawn,
That would fade into a beautiful day,
That would fade into a beautiful night.
Today, 
My friend,
Has been beautiful.
Thanks, to only,
you.

But I can't paint.
So I'll write you a poem,
Telling you,
What I would paint,
If I could paint,
