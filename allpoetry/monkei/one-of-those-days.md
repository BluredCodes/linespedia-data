---
title: "One of Those Days"
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "one-of-those-days"
source: "AllPoetry"

It has been,
One of those days.
One of those days,
I've decided to hate.

Today is one of those days,
That I've needed to talk to you,
Needed to vent.
Needed to get some stuff out.

But you weren't home,
Weren't there,
You were gone...
Busy.

You said you'd always be there,
And I know you always will,
Today has just been,
One of those days.

I know you don't mean to,
And I know you didn't know.
But sometimes when you're gone like this,
The pain gets almost unbearable.

I realize it's not your fault,
And I've come to know you can't prevent
Those days when,
I feel so alone.

But the more I've looked around,
The more I've come to see,
That the closer the people come,
The more differences they have.

That's probably just me though,
Trying to draw you close, 
To prevent the pain that comes,
When I am alone.not great, none of mine have been lately.  They sound "forced" and I don't like the way they 'flow'.
