---
title: "Believe Me, I Would..."
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "believe-me-i-would"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58656-Believe-Me--I-Would...-by-Monkei"

If I could explain to you,
What you make me feel.
If I could say in words,
The emotions you provoke.
If I could write the millions ways,
That you make me happy.
Believe me,
I would.

If I could paint you a picture,
Showing all the days we've shared.
And all the things I've felt.
If I could write you a poem,
Telling of the happiness in my heart,
And the gladness in my soul.
If I could tell you a story,
About the things you make me feel,
And why you make me feel it.
Believe me,
I would.

If I knew how,
To make you understand me.
If I knew how,
To explain myself to you.
If I knew how,
To tell you all about me.
Believe me,
I would.

But...
I guess,
That is one thing,
I don't know how to do.
So I guess this poem,
Will just have to suffice,
Because I don't how to say it all to you.
If I did...
Believe me,
I would...I am not sure about the last two stanzas, (the last one in particular, but I don't know how to make what I said there fit into the style I was using.)
