One day, after reading one of a friends poems I said to her;
"You are a wonderful writer."  To this she replied, "I do what
I can." and I repeated. "No, You are a wonderful writer." This
time she said. "I do my best."  So again I said, "No! You ARE
a wonderful writer."  So she replied. "There is no such thing."
I looked up at her in suprise and said. "Then you are no such thing."
She smiled at me. "You are correct, I am not."  I studied her
for a moment and replied. "But you are here, so you are." She shook her
head at me. "Am I?"It's an intresting thought, if you really think about it.
