---
title: "My Heart is Crying"
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "my-heart-is-crying"

I smile,
My eyes sparkle,
My body laughs.

But my heart is crying.

It hurts,
'cause I know,
You're in pain.

It hurts,
'cause I know,
I can't make it go away.

It hurts,
'cause I know,
You do.

I just want,
for you,
To be happy.

I just want,
for you,
To feel free.

I just want,
for you,
To rid yourself of the pain.

My heart is crying,
Because...
Your's is.
