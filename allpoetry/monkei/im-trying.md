---
title: "I'm Trying"
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "im-trying"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

You have to understand,
That I've told you a lot.
And you have to understand,
That it's not everything.
But, I'm trying.

You seem to understand,
Or at least accept the fact,
That I can't show you everything,
It just hurts too much.
But, I'm trying.

You know a lot,
More than anyone,
It's all coming out now,
Faster than I would like to permit.
But, I'm trying.

I can't make you see,
I can't get you to feel,
What I've felt,
And what I've gone through.
But, I'm trying.

I know you think that you will understand completely,
But I know, that isn't possible.
You didn't live those memories,
I did, and all I can do is share them.
And, I'm trying.

All I can do 
Is tell you what I can
But when it gets to be too much,
You have to let me stop.
Realize, I'm trying.

I know you don't want me to hurt,
And I know you want to make it go away,
But just like you, I scar.
And some of the pain will always remain.
Know, that I'm trying.

There are going to be 
Rough days ahead.
And there are going to be days
That all I need is to be held.
And I'm trusting you here,
Putting my faith in the fact,
That you'll be there to hold me.
'Cause I don't know,
how much longer I can hold the pain off.
And the day is fast approaching,
That I'll need your two strong arms,
To help me ward it away.

But, all I can do now
Is tell you what I can
But when it gets to be too painful,
You have to let me stop...
I just hope you know, I'm trying.I'm trying, A, and you're helping.  I tried not to push you, and now I have to ask that of you, in return.  I wrote this one for you, to get it off my mind, so maybe I'll sleep a little better tonight, knowing that at least you can know, I'm trying.
