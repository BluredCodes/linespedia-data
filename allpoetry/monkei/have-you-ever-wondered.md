---
title: "Have You Ever Wondered?"
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "have-you-ever-wondered"
source: "AllPoetry"

Have you ever wondered,
What it would be like,
To wonder through the stars,
In the dead of night?

Have you ever wondered,
How it would feel,
To slip into an ocean,
Full of orange peel?

Have you ever wondered,
What someone might think,
If you appeared,
Up out of their sink?

Have you ever wondered,
How someone might react,
If you showed up,
In their grocery sack?

No...!?!
..Neither have I.I wrote this one, just now, for the heck of it.  There isn't much to it, and it doesn't make much sense, but maybe it made you laugh, 'cause I think that was the idea.  I am trying to write some lighthearted poems, but the 'happy' feeling is harder to catch and describe in words than most others.  So I guess I'll just make you laugh, until I can figure out how to write a laughing poem.
