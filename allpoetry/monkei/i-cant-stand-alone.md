title: "I Can't Stand Alone"
writer: "Monkei"

I can't stand alone...
I can't hold myself up anymore.
I am not strong enough,
To support the weight,
of the pain I carry.

I can't stand alone...
I need you now,
I need you to hold me up.
I can't do it anymore,
Now is the time,
I need your support.

I can't stand alone...
I am crumpling under the weight,
Of the memories,
That are flooding back.
I need you to help me.

I can't stand alone...
