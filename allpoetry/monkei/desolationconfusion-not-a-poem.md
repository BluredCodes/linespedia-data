Desolation is when you are all alone in a crowded room.
Desolation is being lost in the desert surrounded by the nothingness
of the people who don't care.
Desolation is having all the friends you ever dreamed of, yet having
none when you heart needs one most to confide in.

Confusion is liking what you are, yet longing to be something else.
Confusion is being taught to know something, yet believing another.
Confusion is being stuck in a crowd of familiar strangers.
Confusion is understanding the people around you, yet wishing you didn't.It's my interpretations of the words. Something I wrote last year for english, just thought I'd post it.
