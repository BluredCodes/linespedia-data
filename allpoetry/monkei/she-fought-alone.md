---
title: "She Fought Alone"
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "she-fought-alone"
source: "AllPoetry"

She fought alone,
when no one was there to protect her.
She stood tall,
when everyone turned against her.
She held her head high,
When he abused her.
She offered a smile,
when the pain racked her.
She held back the tears,
When they tore away at her.
She built walls,
When fear came too close.

And when the walls crumbled,
and the fear came,
He was there,
Waiting...
To prey on her,
and her battered body.
Yet...
She fought alone.
