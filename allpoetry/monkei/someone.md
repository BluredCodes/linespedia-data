---
title: "'Someone..'"
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "someone"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

I hear my feet,
pounding heavily on the cement.
My breath comes in gasps now,
My chest is on fire.

I can hear him,
He's coming closer,
Catching up,
Soon he will have me.

I know I can't run forever,
And I know he'll never stop.
I'm going to fall soon,
"Someone help me!"

I stumble 'round a corner,
And he grabs at my flying hair.
He comes out with a fist full,
And I topple to the ground.

He stops,
Looking down at me,
He laughs bitterly,
And I know, that it's over.

I've lost,
I finally fell.
Now he knows he has me,
And I can't get away.

"Someone help me!"
But there is no one there,
It is just us,
I am defenseless.

Tired, and hurting,
I cannot fight back.
There is nothing I can do now.
But scream, "Someone help me!"

There is a pain 
In my side..
His foot,
Buried in my ribs.

Frantic now,
Knowing not what to do,
I yell,
To the people who are not there.

There is nothing.
"Someone help me!"
"Please! Someone help me!"
But there is no one.
