---
title: "Just to be Near You"
writer: "Monkei"
slug: "just-to-be-near-you"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60794-Just-to-be-Near-You-by-Monkei"
---

I miss you.
When you're away for so long.
When I can't hear your voice.

I need you.
To talk to you.
To let my heart out, like you taught me.

To see you.
To see your smile.
And hear you laugh.

To hold you.
Wraped up in my arms,
Like I'll never let you go.

To touch you.
To feel your warm skin,
Pressed close to mine.

To be with you.
At any time at all.
Just to be near you.
