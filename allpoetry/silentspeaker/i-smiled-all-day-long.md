I had a great day
I smiled all day long
Nothing could bring me down.

I learned what I got in two of my classes,
And yet I was still smiling. 
I smiled all the time...
I couldn't help it.

Some of the chosen few know why,
And some of them will learn shortly.

But all I can say is to the person, I think he knows who he is, THANK YOU SO MUCH. 

YOU MADE ME SMILE ALL DAY LONG...IT HAS BEEN SO LONG SINCE THAT HAPPENED... I COULDN'T REMEMBER HOW IT FELT TO SMILE, MY CHEEKS STILL HURT FROM SMILING SO MUCH!!!if you cant tell......im really REALLY REALLY HAPPY!!!
