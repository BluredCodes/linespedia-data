Little angels I tell you.
They don't cry or ask questions.
The just simply except the fact that daddy is gone.
Never to return.
Helping mommy through the tough times ahead,
Just like they had done before.
Being as good as they can,
So mommy can get through one more day.
Talking to people,
About their daddy's death,
Because mommy said it would be good for them.
Not running around,
Not making noises,
Not being kids.
Instead being adults,
In a very hard situation.
Acting like mommy can't even act,
Like an adult woman, 
Who can handle this very hard predicament.
Like an adult who has to worry, not just about herself,
But about the two little kids she brought into this world.
The two little angels.
