im sick of being the stong one...
im sick of being the one to make everything all better
im sick of being the back bone...
the one you can trust to be there
the one who doesnt care if you cry on her sholder
the one who holds back her tears,
because her little cousin is looking to her for support
that she cant find any where else,
because everyone else has looked to her for support too.

im sick of being the fucking strong one...


IM FUCKING SICK OF IT!!!!!!!!!!he was my uncle too...people dont seem to get that...they dont seem to get that the almighty and powerful Vicky can be hurt or sad too. they dont seem to get that i can be in pain too...that im not a completly heartless bitch like they think i am.
