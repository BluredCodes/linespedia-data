There is only one way out.
Only one way out of this whole mess,
One way to make things all better.

Through that space,
Let me go.
Don't try to stop me.
I'll be fine. 
I'll live with out you.

Will you live with out me?
Will your daily life go on with out me there by your side?
Or will you sit there, 
Realize your faults,
And find a way out too?

The door is open,
I can take those few steps.
I will take those steps,
Not knowing where they will lead me.
But I will put more faith in those few steps,
Than I ever had or would put in you.

Will you find your world closing in upon you too?
Just as I did.
Or will you find that with out me, life is easier?
No one in your way,
No shadows in your sunny day,
No clouds in your blue sky,
No shit in your front yard.

I'll find my way.
I'll find someone else.

Will you?
Will you take some of your own steps?
And climb out that space at the end of the way,
