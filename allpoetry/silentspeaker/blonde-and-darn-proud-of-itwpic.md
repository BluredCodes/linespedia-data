Yeah...sure I was born blonde, 
And yeah, I have really blue eyes.

But does that automatically make me stupid,
Or a flake, or 'dumb'...?

What did blondes do to deserve to be called dumb?
Why must they be picked on and called names,
Just because of their hair color?

Show me the woman (person) who gave blondes a bad name,
And I'll beat the crap out of her.
Because I don't need to be put down, mislabeled, or bashed, because of my hair color.

My hair is NATURALLY blonde, and naturally getting darker.
But still every summer, I get the blonde jokes, comments, and jests, just because my hair NATURALLY highlights. 

And the naturally means I cant help it...
I have nothing to do with it, if it were up to me...I would be a brunettei hate my hair
