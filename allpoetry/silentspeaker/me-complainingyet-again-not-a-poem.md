its not easy to be me.


I know it looks like it is,
seems like it should be.
I have everything i need.
everything is right in front of me.

And I know its my fault for not taking what i have,
and running with it.



But my life is not what it should be.



"the truth is what it is, what it should be, is a dirty lie."what else is new...besides me complaining...
