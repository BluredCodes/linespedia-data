i dont know what to think any more


I'm so confused about everything

friends, family, enimies, and people that are around me.


i try talking, 
but i get to shy.
i try to listen as best i can,
but sometimes i need someone to listen to me.
i try watching,
but there is so much pain around me.
i try to fix as much as i can,
but im not someone who can snap her fingers,
and make everything all better.
 
I wish i could be...but im not perfect.i dont know...lots of things have me confused lately...

espically my friends and family.
