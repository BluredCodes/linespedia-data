Love,
What a mysterious thing.
A mystical feeling it is.
To be in love.

Love brings lots of different emotions.
Love brings sight to the blind,
Hearing to the deaf,
Comfort to the suffering,
And pain to the painless.

Love makes the parents of this world happy when they first see their children.

Love tears their hearts when the parents have to bury their children.

Love brings a couple together when they marry.
and brakes them apart when he dies.

Love makes the children run to the door everyday to welcome home daddy,
and makes it harder for mommy to tell them that daddy is with the angles.

Love makes people come when called,
Even if it is to the hospital to watch your uncle die.



Love, and love alone, takes, brakes, makes, and mends many things. 



Do I still believe in love???


Yes I do.

But now I have more of a respect for what love does for one, or many people. 


Do I believe that Love will show its ugly head around my corner of the world???
