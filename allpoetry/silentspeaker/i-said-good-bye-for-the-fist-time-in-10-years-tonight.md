I said good bye for the first time in alomost ten years today. 

No one knew this was going to happen, he was helthy.
He just got a new job, this wasnt suposed to happen.

He was a great uncle, father, brother, son, and husband. 
I don't know what we are going to do with out him.

Even if he did come out of the coma, more than half of his brain is litteraly dead, so he would be mostly a vegitable. 


So, no matter how mean this sounds, we are hoping he dies. 

So I prepared myself, and said good bye...



GOOD BYE UNCLE BILL.....I LOVE YOU!
