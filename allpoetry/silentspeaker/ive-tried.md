(Him)

I've tried to not like him...


It doesn't seem to work.


I was on a cruise with tons of people, (hot guys are what I'm going for) and still...I couldn't help but think of him. And it killed me, I would go to parties, see a really hot guy (CHRIS) and think of him. I went to the beach, played beach volleyball, and swim, and think of how much he doesn't like swimming. I would go to the arcade and play a racing game, and think how he would kill me because I wasn't doing it stick shift. 



So what should I do....?


I don't know...I need advice... Or someone to make me not like him any more.





Help me.



LUV,
