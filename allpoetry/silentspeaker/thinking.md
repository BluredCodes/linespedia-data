"Today I caught myself smiling for no reason...then I realized...I was thinking of you"

Thinking is one of the worst things for me.
I think, and my brain tells me, to ignore what my heart says.
But then I see you, and my heart starts to beat so loud,
I'm afraid you will hear it. And hate me for it. 

So the question is...Should I ignore what my heart tells me, and never know if you felt the same way. Or tell you how I feel, and have you tell me that you hate me....hmmm...yeah....
