title: "She Stands Tall"

She stands tall for all to see.
People entering our country see her.
Standing tall, proud of what we have accomplished.
People come from all around to visit her, and get in her head.
Holding her head up high, smiling.
People see her on TV and in movies, being celebrated for her glory. 
Holding us all up in our time of need.

She stands tall, proud of US.
