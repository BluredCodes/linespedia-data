The perfect couple


The perfect couple,
I saw it happen right before my eyes.
The perfect couple,
I spent many a Friday/Saturday night with them.
The perfect couple,
So alike in feelings, so different in ages.
The perfect couple,
Kept apart, because people just don't understand.
The perfect couple,
Going through the same thoughts and feelings.
The perfect couple,
21 and 15, in love.
The perfect couple,
Two of the greatest people in the world, and two of the best friends I could ask for.
