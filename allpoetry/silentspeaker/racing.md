"Live Fast.... Die Young"


Hell yeah...

Racing down the street at 130 MPH.
Flying off the pavement, 
Tires squealing,
Gears switching.
Heads bobbing,
Seatbelts tightening.
Eyes squinting,
Minds racing,

Racing down the street at 130 MPH.stupid i know...
but ya know...
cant sleep...
and we just won a race against a better car than the one we were in...so hahaha!
