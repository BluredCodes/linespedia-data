---
title: "My Lover's Death"
writer: "Elvra"
slug: "my-lovers-death"

I see him there dead
on top of my cold, dark bed
I had hit his head 

he never a care 
I tryed but he hated me 
I loved another 

he then found it out
then he started to hit me 
I screamed for some help 

he was not stopping
I got away and ran fast 
In my room I rest 

I hear him coming 
I look for a phone to use 
The phone wire was cut 

I see my red lamp
started to think in my head 
I must do the worst 

he walks in my room 
he comes at me with a knife 
hit him with the lamp 

now he must be gone
motionless on the still bed
what must happen now 

police at my door 
someone must have heard me scream 
I fall to the floor 

I sit there thinking 
bad things are going to come 
a knock on my door
