To me she was old
as old as you ever could be
but the lines on her face 
gave her character
and a dignity
beyond anyone else I knew
always in a dress
and stockings
even on wash day
up to her elbows 
in a old wringer washer
she ironed everything
including her under garments
they say I look like her
the biggest compliment 
I've ever received
but when she was 
no longer herself
living in the past
when she was aware enough
to have a past 
I could no longer go to her
for fear of seeing myselfFor my favorite grandma.
