title: "A Summer Evening in Indiana"
writer: "Pandora"

Fireflys twinkle in the night,
like earth bound stars,
flying though air thick and heavy
with the smell of green and
the promise of rain.
Foretold by thunder that rumbles 
from dark and bloated clouds 
spreading fingers of lightening across the sky.
The fireflys chased into hiding by wind
that wrestles with the trees,
trying to bend them to it's will.
The rain comes driving across the fields,
so hard you can hear it's progress.
A summer storm that departs 
as quickly as it arrives,
to let the fireflys twinkle again
