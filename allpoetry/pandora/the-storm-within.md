---
title: "The Storm Within"
writer: "Pandora"

The sky darkens, mirroring the storm of my soul.
Thoughts boil like the clouds,
Spilling into puddles of ideas.
Lightening flashes of inspiration appear,
Only to fade in a rumble of discord.
After it passes a storm leaves the world clean.
Not so the storm of the mind, the soul.
But inside the gale is a brightness of spirit,
Buried beneath the inclement psyche for so long
The tumult may extinguish it.
The storms of nature pass.
The storm with in lingers.
And unlike the vivid diplays above
It has no visual manifestation.
