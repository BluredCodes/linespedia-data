---
title: "Inside I cry"
writer: "Pandora"
slug: "inside-i-cry"

We talk of mundane things,
If we talk at all
The kids, the house, your job
And inside I cry

We lay in bed at night in silence
When we are both there
Seperated by a vast world of differences
And inside I cry

We live in the same house
Like boarders who must encounter each other
But with out the smiles and nods of recognition
And inside I cry
