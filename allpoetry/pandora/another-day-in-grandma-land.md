We do the Teletubbies dance
then repeatly sing the chorus of Dalton's favorite song
hey baby hey baby hey baby hey
Bailey wants to see the dog-dogs
so Animal planet it is
we watch nakes and piders and elphanants
I feel each new animals appearance
a slap here, a poke there
and look it's (a nake or pider or elphanant)
I threaten to change the channel
Bailey takes up a traffic cop stance in front of the TV
one hand on her hip, a palm thrust toward me
NOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!
they fight over who gets to sit in Papa's chair
but end up on the floor amid every toy in the house
I'm hungy, where's my cuppy?
and we're off and whining
No you can't go in Aunt Taryn's room
and for the tenth time Papa went bye-bye (lucky him)
Yes your mommy will be back soon (just not soon enough)
I look at the clock, they've only been here an hour
another day in grandma land
