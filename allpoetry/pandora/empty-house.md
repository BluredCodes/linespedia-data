---
title: "Empty House"
writer: "Pandora"
slug: "empty-house"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

My parents owned 
an empty house
with a window 
that wouldn't lock.
I led him there 
one night.
In the autumn chill,
we made 
our own heat.
The quiet
of abandonment
made our noises
seem foreign.
We stayed there
through out 
the night.
Watching 
the shades 
turn from
black to dirty beige
In the light
the mystery
was gone
and so was he.
