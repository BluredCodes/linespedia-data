Just eighteen
waiting to go bad
biding his time at KFC
choking the chicken
night after night
cruising the city
on a mission
with no godly connection
the burden of his virginity
weighing heavy on more than his mind
I was the older woman
experienced in all he was not
He came knocking on my door
A jehovah's Witness ready
for another kind of religous experience
willing teacher, eager student
class is in session
for sex ed 101
no prerequisite required
he excelled in oral....recitation
and was a wonder at anatomy
he still called on god at times
only in a different vein
after nightly summer sessions 
and a few all nighters
I pronounce him proficient enough
to strike out on his own
He quit is job at KFC
and the chicken choking too
I waved goodbye
and shed a tear or two
cause now I'd have 
to start all over
with the next
cock....sure male
that showed up at my dooruh huh....I swear on all 4 of my grandparents grave
this is true...he did work at KFC....his name was Chuck
and they did call him Chuckie the chicken choker
