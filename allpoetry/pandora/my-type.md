---
title: "My Type"
writer: "Pandora"
slug: "my-type"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60240-My-Type-by-Pandora"
---

I want a tall man
one I can climb like a tree
sit in the branches

I want a dark man
contrast to my white skin
the attraction of opposites

I want a short man
look into his eyes on my level
toe to toe, face to face

I want a pale man
one who avoids the sun 
like I must

I want a man
any type will do

as long as he wants me
