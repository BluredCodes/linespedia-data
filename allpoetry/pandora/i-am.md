title: "I am......."
writer: "Pandora"

I am a dreamer
obsessed with possibilities,
castles in the mind,
hearts that never bleed

I am a drifter
wandering through life
a muddle of time and place
snapshots of circumstance

I am a woman
receptacle of life
nurturing by necessity
a mother of mankind

I am a poet
words paint my pictures
scences from out of mind
truth a matter of vision

I am a failure
just here to take up space
holding out while holding on
ever on the brink

I am anything
I am everything
I am nothing
