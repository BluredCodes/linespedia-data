title: "Shadows of the Night"

He lives in the shadows of the night
where the moonlight never reaches
with all the possibilities of obsessions
and dreams of a light beyond
a saving warmth he has not known
a healing balm to sooth his scars
but he dares not venture 
from his protective cocoon 
it was his salvation
when he learned that
the darkness of his soul
was no match for 
the darkness of the world
