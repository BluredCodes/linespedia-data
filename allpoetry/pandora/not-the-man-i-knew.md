I caught a glimpse of him 
a thin gray shadow
down a long corridor
he was not the man I knew
who carried me to bed at night
and taught me how to use a hammer
to help him roof the house
was that so long ago
so frail now
with labored breathing
and sluggish gait
in his face the ravages
of time and drink
his independence lessens
