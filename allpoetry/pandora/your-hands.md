---
title: "Your hands"
writer: "Pandora"
slug: "your-hands"
source: "AllPoetry"

I close my eyes and see a pair of grease stained hands.
The only part of you I can still picture.
I forgot your face because I didn't want to remember
the last words you spoke.
but your hands, they never did me any harm
rough, hardworking mans hands
they were gentler than they looked
I still see them, still feel them
I'd want them back 
if the rest of you didn't come with them
