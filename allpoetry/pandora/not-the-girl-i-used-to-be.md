title: "Not the Girl I Used to Be"
writer: "Pandora"

When you look at me you see 
the girl I used to be
wild and carefree

But years have changed my face
I walk at a slower pace
I've put away my ribbons and lace

Now the time has come for you
to give me that which is my due
and change your outdated view

I'm not the girl I used to be
I'm the woman that I've become
