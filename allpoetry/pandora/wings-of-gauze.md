---
title: "Wings of Gauze"
writer: "Pandora"

I dreamt of you again last night
on sheets of silk with wings of gauze
a creature meant to fly

I spoke to you in dawns pale light
heard no answer to give me pause
only the winds deep sigh

I sensed you just out of my sight
so I cursed nature and it's laws
with God I cannot vie
