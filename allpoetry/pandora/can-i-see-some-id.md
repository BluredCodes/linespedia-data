I stand in line
waiting my turn
behind a string of youth
making purchases requiring ID
they grumble and moan
about the hassle of it all
my turn comes around
I ask for my favorite brand
a harassed counter boy
gives me that look
"Can I see some ID?"
I smile and hand it over
he looks at the card that says
you must be this old to purchase
then at my license
oh boy here it comes
"I'm sorry Ma'am"
(I liked it better before
he thought of me that way)
"I have to ask
I grin like a fool
he just made my dayLOL....almost 40 and this stil happens to me
