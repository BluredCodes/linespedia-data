I'm not a possesive type person
generally what's mine is yours
( and vice versa)
borrow my car
take my last few dollars
would you like a bite of my sandwich?
you can have anything you want
if you give me back
my side of the bedThis is for my husband who says i leave him no room in bed
and he's always sleeping on the edge....maybe in more ways than he knows lol :)
