I walk into the room
first time at his place
dingy trailer
no lights
no heat
it didn't matter
we were here for one thing
beeline to the bedroom
the only decoration
a gunrack over the bed
holding an array of whips
half undressed I wondered
would he use them
make them sing through the air
blur the line
between pain and pleasure
on my back I stared above
mesmerized by the possibility
anticipation vibrating through me
seeing me looking
he reaches for one
fondling it
answering my unasked question
I've never used it for sex
I wouldn't hurt you
unless you wanted me to
and now I can't tell him 
the truthyes it's a true story
