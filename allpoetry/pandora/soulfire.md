title: "Soulfire"

Whispers in the night reveal 
A depth concealed inside.
A myriad of emotions transverse the distance,
Words a window to the past
And a doorway to the future.
Looking, searching, hoping,
For one deserving of trust
Among the perverse ramblings 
Of those whose narrow outlook
Blind them to the possibilities
Of a world beyond the known reality.
Created out of a desire to share
That which would be misunderstood anywhere else.
A lusting, A longing for what feeds the soul fire
And fuels the wondrous heart
That seeps it feelings like smoke.
Drifting upwards to the heavens
Lingering among the the pinpoints of stars
Seeking in the constellations a truth.
