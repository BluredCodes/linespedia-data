Mirror mirror, on the shelf
why did I marry a wicked elf
he rants, he raves
comes at me in waves
Where's the old evil crone
wonder if she has a phone
poison apple's what I need
to help me do this foul deed
one bite my dear
please don't fear
I'll tuck you in snugly
my sleeping ugly
