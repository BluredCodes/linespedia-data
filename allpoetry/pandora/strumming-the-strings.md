I watch him play the guitar,
strumming the strings.
The music flows around me and
I remember this morning.
When those fingers played me
pulling forth a tone and rythmn
that built to a crescendo.
Even now, as I watch him
it still pulses within me,
this duet of desire.
A composition in O major.
It's the most beautiful music 
he will ever play.
