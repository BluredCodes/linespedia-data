---
title: "Who I am"
writer: "Pandora"

I remember being young and hopeful
Before I learned that the promise of youth
Does not always fufill itself
I dreamed many dreams of what my life would be
My current reality is not one of them
Enmeshed and entwined in events I did not foresee
My path has meandered, lacking direction
Would I change it?
It has brought me here, to this place
It may not be the one I would have chosen
If I could have seen beyond a day, an hour, a minute
But it is who I am
