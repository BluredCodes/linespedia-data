Skin
Yours pressed against mine
Wondrous

Touch
The length and breadth of me
Sensation

See
The blush of passion rise
Revelation

Taste
The dew of love's exertion
Luscious

Smell
The mingled scent of desire
Perfume

Hear 
The strangled sighs of pleasure
Ecstasy
