---
title: "She Sits"
writer: "Pandora"

She sits among the pansies 
under the slippery elm 
sunlight dancing through the leaves 
like nymphs and fairies at her feet 
a flower in her own right 
in the spring of her becoming 
roots not so deep to hold 
waiting for the wind to carry her 
to distant fertile soil 
where she can be 
exotic among the ordinary 
admired for her beauty 
til autumn dims her bloom 
and no new seeds are sown 
then she'll long for the pansies 
and her place under the elm
