---
title: "Stolen Identity"
writer: "Pandora"
slug: "stolen-identity"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Who I used to be no longer exsists
you saw to that
you tried to mold me to fit your ideal
what you got is just a facade
a covering for what I have really become
bitter, restless, angry
cracks appear,  threatening
to bring it all down around us
my true self to become apparent
can you live with the real me?
can I?
