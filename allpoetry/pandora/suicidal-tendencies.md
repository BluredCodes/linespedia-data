I have counted pretty pink pills
one by one
Wondering if there were enough

I have caressed cold gun metal
loaded and reloaded
knowing one is all it would take

I have stood above icy waters
deep or shallow?
would i break my neck or just drown?

I have held razor blade to skin
cold steel, warm blood
Trickle of red on pale white skin

Why am I still here?
