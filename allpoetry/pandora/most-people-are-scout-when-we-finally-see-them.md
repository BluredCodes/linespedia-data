Do you really see
who you are looking at?
Not the overlay
but the underworld
they wholly populate.
Or do you project
upon them the you 
that you want them to be?
Remove the reflective covering
and unveil the truth
of the heart.the title was the inspiration for this piece
from "To Kill a Mockingbird"
