title: "I Saw the Face of a Nation"
writer: "Pandora"

I saw the face of a nation
tear stained in grief
grimy from dust and debris
It cried out in it's pain

I saw the face of a nation
red from anger
white in horror
blue with sadness

I saw the face of a nation
with a childs bewilderment
weary of death
stern with resolution

I saw the face of a nation
it was mine
