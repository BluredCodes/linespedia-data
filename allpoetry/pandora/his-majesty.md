You have traveled from your kingdom
through the valley of the self-righteous
bearing your contempt as a gift.
Oh prince of the perfect
am I to fall on my knees
and beg of you a pardon,
for in your eyes being human is a fault
and your arduos journey to my door requires a tithing.
Well sound the harolds for I have news.
I am not your subject to be ruled.
