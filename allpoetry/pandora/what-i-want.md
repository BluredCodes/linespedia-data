title: "What I want....."

What I want is....
....to view the sunset from within your arms and
      still be there to witness it rise
....to watch you approach from a distance and know
      that I am you destination
....to taste upon my lips the salt of your skin after
      the physical release of passion
....to laugh together uproariously at somethin no 
      one else understands
....to know your history as well as I now my own
....to span the distance with more than my heart
....to give you more than just my words

What I need is....
....your assurance that all is well
....your presence in more than just my thoughts 
      and dreams
....to know that my heart has a home with you
....to hear your voice just once before it's too late

What I know is....
....only how I feel and nothing more
