we were cruising down a blacktop road
listening to Freebird and watching
the yellow lines appear out the of the black
in the middle of nowhere and nowhere to go
love and liquor the only things on our mind
one wasted..the other wasting us
the blacktop turns to gravel and everything turns to dust
this is our spot, any spot on a deserted dirt road
where we cavort like pagans in rituals
unknowingly passed down through the ages
we thought we were free like the bird 
we thought we had all the time in the world
we didn't know the world was not on our time
