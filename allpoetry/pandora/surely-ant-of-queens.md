In a castle on a  verdant green hill
behind a door with many locks
on a sunny breezy window sill
sits a lovely little box

In the box resides a pretty little bug
who’s heart is full of joy and gladness
for she saw saved from being squished on a rug
and has no clue she is surrounded by madness
	
Because imprisoned in this room
are three beautiful little queens
praying silently not to meet their doom
at the hands of dastardly fiends

The little bug knows not their pain
or what their praying means
all she cares is how’ll she’ll reign
for she’s surely ant of queens
