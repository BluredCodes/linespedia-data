---
title: "Devour me"
writer: "Pandora"
slug: "devour-me"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61726-Devour-me-by-Pandora"
---

Dine on my words
Consume my touch
Absorb my voice
into your flesh
Sip from my soul
Consume my reality
Inhale of my heart
Devour me as you will
