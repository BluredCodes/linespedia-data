---
title: "It haunts me still"
writer: "Pandora"
slug: "it-haunts-me-still"
source: "AllPoetry"

He rode his Harley 
into my life
the spring I turned 18
long black hair
wild in the wind
just like his spirit
it haunts me still

He took me for his
to show me I belonged 
to no one
free like the wind 
in our hair
and the woman he found inside
She's with me still

I haven't seen him
in 22 years
since that long ago year
when my innocence
was given freely
He was the first
and he haunts me still
