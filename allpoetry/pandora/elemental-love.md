The suns heat spreads over me
Warming my skin
Filling me with fire
You are my sun

A cool wind blows around me
Tenderly caressing my body
Heightening my senses
You are my wind

Raindrops fall upon me
Like soft kisses
Leaving trails of moisture on my skin
You are my rain

The earth lays before me
In all it's beauty
A feast for my eyes
