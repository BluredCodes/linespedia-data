He cruises down the strip
wearing midwestern conservative
outdated five years ago clothes
that scream out of towner.
He does a few circuits.
"Checking out the merchandise."
he says to himself.
Something he heard 
on a cop show.
Was that the one 
about the vice squad?
Momentarily nervous,
he checks the rearview mirror.
Pushing those thoughts 
from his mind
he steadies his nerve.
I am going to do this.
Twenty-three years of marriage
and not once.
Not once did she offer.
I am going to do this.
His resolve back,
he pulls over at the next corner.
A worn out looking woman
in short skirt 
and red wig
approaches his window.
She leans in
giving him a view
of doughy breasts.
"Hey baby."
"You lost?"
Something I can do for you?"
For all his preparation
he is speechless.
"Come on sugar."
"Talk to mama."
She smiles
and he notices
she has no teeth.
