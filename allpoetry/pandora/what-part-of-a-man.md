title: "What part of a man?"
writer: "Pandora"

What part of a man
do you look at first?
What part of a man 
is the best not the worst?

What part of a man
is a feast for your eyes?
What part of a man
gives you more sighs?

What part of a man
gets you from the start?
What part of a man
sets him apart?

What part of a man
gives you the shivers?
What part of a man
makes you all quivers?

What part of a man
sets your heart racing?
What part of a man?
Depends which way he's facing.
