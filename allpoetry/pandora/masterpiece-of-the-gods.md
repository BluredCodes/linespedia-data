title: "Masterpiece of the Gods"
writer: "Pandora"

There is a vibration in the air 
it calls me to you
male form in repose
nude reclining on a bed
a masterpiece of the gods
arms reach to receive me
pulling me into your sensuality
your scent assails me
as I lean foward to taste the moment
my hair falls around us in a curtain
shutting out the world
and all I am is lost to you
