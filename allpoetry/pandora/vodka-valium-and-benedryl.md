foggy, hazy, murky brain
but somehow I remember rain
all the rest I know to be
is what spectators told to me
none of it can be real
except the vodka, valium and benedryl

at the bottom of a pool
I danced like a watery fool
from that emerged a naked mermaid
and preformed acts that should have been paid
none of it can be real
except the vodka, valium and benedryl

Memeory can be fickle
sometimes it gives us a trickle
of three days in hell
under a self induce spell
none of it can be real
except the vodka, valium and benedryl
