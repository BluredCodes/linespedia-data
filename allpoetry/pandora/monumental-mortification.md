The monumental magnitude of mortification
is magnified meteorically by malingering malice
manufacturing miasmic meanderings of the mind
and a macabre malady of malevolence
that maintains a maelstrom of mactation
it manifests a malicious malaise
from a mendacious mendicant mentality
and  a mercurial melodramatic memory
