I am going berserk serk serk
Cause all i do is work work work
and my boss is a jerk jerk jerk
in the shadows he lurks lurks lurks
because he thinks everyone shirks shirks shirks
this to me really irks irks irks
so I am going berserk serk serkok I admit it...it's lack of sleep and I will delete this later....but I keep singing this in my head....probably hoping I will sing myself to sleep....so everyone sing
