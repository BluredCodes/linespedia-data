---
title: "Smoking and Thinking"
writer: "Pandora"

I sit here, smoking
the one vice I have left
the one that offends the most people
my head circled in a cloud of smoke
Why is it I think better when I smoke?
It's like I am drawing in words an ideas as I inhale
I once heard someone say they smoked because 
they needed something to do with their hands
Could I say I smoked because 
it stimulates my thought processses?
Tell that to the surgeon general.
Warning: May cause you to think!
