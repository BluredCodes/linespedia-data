title: "God Help His Fallen Angels"
writer: "Madriar"

God help his fallen angels as they 
lay nearly dead on the street. 
The world is a cold cruel place.
Their kind just can’t survive, so full 
of love and patience and kindness and 
we watch.
We watch them plummet to their death.
Yet we do nothing about it. 
God help his fallen angels because 
no one else will.
