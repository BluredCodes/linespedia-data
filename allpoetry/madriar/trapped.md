Trapped

Help! Let me out! 
The walls are closing in on me,
the sky is as black as the midnight.
This is my own prison,  have I been
sent here to die?
Bang Bang against the door of my 
prison cell but no one hears. 
Pacing, Pacing trying to make the
time go by.
Am I going out of my mind?
Someone free me, let me out, 
I don’t deserve to die this way!
