Its Not Fair!
Why can't I get this right?
I've tried and I've tried 
and gosh darn it I've tired
again. 
But still I fail and still 
I feel like I don't belong. 
Why am I here? 
Why was I put here to suffer
this unthinkable fate? 
Why doesn't this make sense
in my brain?
When the kid in the next chair 
over tries to explain I get 
lost even more. 
Frustration, confusion ya, 
I know those things but
this is pushing it to the max.
Somebody zap me out of here!I really am not good at math.
