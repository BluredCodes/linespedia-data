I have a secret that is 
burning inside of me. 
My soul screams to me
the secret, that I can’t 
reveal. 
I am sworn to 
silence but my heart 
just burns to tell 
someone.
I must fight the power 
and keep the secret to 
the very end.Secrets are one of the best things friends share. The best part about it is, the trust you give your friend to keep it forever.
