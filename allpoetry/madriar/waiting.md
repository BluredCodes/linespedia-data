Why can’t time just fly by now?
Pacing pacing across the floor. 
I’ve walked my steps one hundred 
times before. 
You told me to be patient, but I can’t 
wait anymore. 
Tick tock tick tock goes the clock on 
the wall. 
I watch the clock with anticipation but
time just seems to slow.
Drip drip goes the sink, tap tap goes 
the tennis ball bouncing across the 
floor.
These are things, I’ve really not noticed
before.  
Pace and pace again, but the time will
not end.I hate waiting
