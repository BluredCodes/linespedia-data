The Dance

Dancing with my shadow
In the light of day
Leaning back and twirling
Losing myself in our play.

Dancing with my shadow
Under a full moon
Moving softer now, seductive
Humming a little tune.

I cannot see my shadow
His bulk is smothering me
I struggle for the next breath
But cannot fight my way free.

Shadows loom and melt away
My shadow does the same
Sometimes he entraps my soul
Other times he is my friend.
