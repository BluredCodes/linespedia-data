Where did the innocence go?
So many lives have grown up too fast
Too soon...
Life claims the ones we love most
And leave the ones we don't
We cause pain to our selves
Though we feel it constantly
We make wishes on meaningless stars
But belive in our hearts that they are heard
Some refuge from the storm
While others frolic in it
And in the essence of death
We find a comforting repose
But through all of this
We stay for a reason
We still find meaning
And within our self
Lies the future...My first non ryming poem...?
