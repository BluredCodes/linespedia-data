Have I had enough
Though the story is the same
I've grown tough
There is no point in hiding
For whats in store 
Is not worth fighting
We've become close
Right from the start
And in the depths of us both
There is a wish in our hearts
I live the way I do
In hopes 
That I might find you
I am once again  
In the same state
Beliving that one day 
I can defy my fate"Sometimes I become afraid...I don't want to belive that I will be alone.I welcome each relationship with open arms.Because you never know who might be in them.I'm just tired of the hurting.Please let this be ok..."
