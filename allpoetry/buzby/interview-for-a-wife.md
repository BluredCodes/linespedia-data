The ideal woman for the appointment
Of wife and one day mother too
Should be aware of your favourite meal
And once a week cook it for you

The ideal person for the job
Of growing old and grey with you
Must love you for your good points
And forgive all your bad points too

The ideally suitable female
Of this job that’s much desired
Should always try to tend your needs
And never claim to be too tired

The most  ideal aspirant
Of  spouse and companion for life
Should be both loving and forgiving 
As is befitting for a wife

More importantly, the successful applicant
Of this most prestigious post
Need not be the most attractive
But be the one that you love mostInspired by Sophie Hannah's Person Specification.
