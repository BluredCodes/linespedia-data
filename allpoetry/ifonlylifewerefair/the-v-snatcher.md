Oh why do I feel like this? You won me over with a kiss.
    You took me to your special spot and that's when things started
    getting hot. I felt your hands upon me and your touch was so soft.
    I couldn't stop myself I wanted it so bad I just wish that I 
    never had. You took from me, my virginity. You used me like a whore
    and just dropped me off at my door. You said goodbye and made me cry 
    but now you'll get yours. You took from me, my virginity. You made me
    want you but now I hate you. I cannot go on like this. I fell for
    you with just one kiss and now I am really pissed. Don't act like
    nothing happened just keep on laughin'. Cause I'll get my revenge and
    that'll be the end.

    You took from me, my virginity. You made me want you but now I hate you
    I want you to die. Because you made me cry and I wonder why I let you
    touch me like that. I cannot believe what I have done. Because you
    took from me my virginity---FADEI love writing songs and this is my first one that I have posted up here. I hope you enjoy it!!
