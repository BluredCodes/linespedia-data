title: "Satan's Son"

Come with me my child into the dark forest. Take my hand in yours. Follow me into the sea of dead souls and don't fear their cries for help. They were foolish mortals who sold their souls for one night of passion. Don't even listen to them they are fools. Come me with and join me on my throne. You shall rule all that is evil  and destroy all that is kind and beautiful in the world. I will  make you my prince. My little prince of darkness.  You will be my child. Follow me onto the paths of hell and feel the white hot flames burn holes in flesh skin for the last time. These flames shall bring you comfort as you become my immortal son. I kiss your lips little dark haired one. If you take my hand you shall never feel pain again. You will live forever in my palace. No human shall harm you and you will know love. I will take you in and teach you how to be like me. Don't run away f I beg. You have the power to destroy all that gets in your way. You can steal souls and play with them like paper dolls. These are your puppets my son. These are your toys to play with. Come with me and you shall know true happiness. Come wallow in the blood of the innocent for theirs is bitter sweet.
