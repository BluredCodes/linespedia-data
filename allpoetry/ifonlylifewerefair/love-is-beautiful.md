Love is the most beautiful thing in the world. It sends you soaring to greater heights and makes you do crazy things. It leaves you with an empty, longing feeling whenever the one you fancy is away. You feel that you cannnot live without them and you want to hold them and never let go. You love the smell of them and the feel of their kiss. You love the hugs that they give and the feeling you get when they hold you. You love when they call you when you are sick and try to comfort you. This beautiful feeling is like no other. It also hurts too. when the one you love plays with your emotions and says "I love you" but doesn't mean it. Or when you care for them greatly but all they want is sex. It hurts so bad that you feel like killing or cutting. It feels like a dagger is being jammed into your heart over and over. It hurts but only for a long while.This is for my dear friend i just want to know that everything is gonna be ok from now on!!!!! Love Ya Jewels
