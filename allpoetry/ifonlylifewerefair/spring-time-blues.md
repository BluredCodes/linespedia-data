title: "Spring Time Blues"
writer: "IfOnlyLifeWereFair"

The beautiful spring air is evident as I lay indolently in the soft green grass watching the fluffy white clouds pass by in the baby blue sky. The butterflies flutter in the distance showing their vibrant colors. I breathe in the fresh cool air and wonder if their is anything that is better than this. The small yellow flowers billow in the cool breeze as the bees buzz busily among the purple flowers of the garden that was planted so carefully by the huge willow tree. I feel the sun upon my face. It is pleasantly warm. Oh how I wish this day could last forever. Suddenly the sky turns a dark grey and I hear thunder in the distance. Oh dammit I mutter to myself as I feel the first raindrops of the storm fall on my face leaving me with a rather angry feeling. Slowly I pull myself up from the grass and run inside. I go to a window and watch the  lightning strike the ground violently where I was just laying. "Oh well April showers bring May flowers don't they?" I thought feeling disapointed.
