"Beauty is only skin deep." many people say but will happen if all beauty fades from the world The beautiful woman will turn into to dogs and the ugly will stay the same the earth will be morose and brown with no sunshine every day for the dried up flower all withered and dead and the great minds of the world won't have a brain. There won't be sexy legs or even a cute rear to be seen and all the selfish preps will be choking down pills and voka and be drunk and ugly just like the rest of us they will be called names just like the rest of us. The dirt will cover the world and not a blade of grass shall be seen. And the world will tumble into darkness. The movie stars won't be skinny but fat and hairy and this is when you must learn that beauty is only skin deep. You would have to let go of all the vanity that you had and look deep inside to find the inner you. Don't be so shallow and only look at their ass. See where their heart is and love them for their personality. Beauty fades but love is forever. Ugly is only skin deep too. I am ugly and why can't you see how much it hurts to look in the mirror and see a monster. How can you make me feel so bad? Take me for an ugly woman and nothing more. BEAUTY IS ONLY SKIN DEEP
