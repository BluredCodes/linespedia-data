---
title: "Betrayal of a friend"
writer: "IfOnlyLifeWereFair"
slug: "betrayal-of-a-friend"

You told me that you were my friend but why have you betrayed me? You left without a word or a sign of compassion. Why do you feel that you can just treat me like a rag doll or drag me through hot coals. My feelings smolder and burn as if they were pieces of wood for the fire. I gave you everything and loved you like a sister and this is the thanks that I recieve?? What kind of monster have you become dear Elena? Am I just a pawn on a chess board to you? Did I ever matter? How come you don't even want to know me any more? Are you embarassed in front of your new friends when I wave hello. Why have you let these new people come between us? They will only throw you onto their pile of meaningless junk after they lose interest with you. They are just trying to gain popularity. Why do you go to them instead of me if you need help I am your true friend.
