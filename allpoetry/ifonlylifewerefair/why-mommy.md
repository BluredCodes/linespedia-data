---
title: "Why Mommy??"
writer: "IfOnlyLifeWereFair"
slug: "why-mommy"

I was just a tiny egg growing in your tummy then you came and messed me up I hope you feel real crummy. All I wanted to do was have a normal life but no you wouldn't let me grow and let them cut me with a knife. You didn't even know me but wanted me to die, and I just have one question Why Mommy, WHY? Why didn't you want me? What did I do to you? You're the one who got me so why not love me too? I could not see and did not talk I couldn't eat or even walk. You killed an innocent life I just wanted you to know how many times teens have just let me go. I have been killed many times but this is just wrong. I trusted you Mommy! What have you done!?? and now I  am really gone. You killed a little baby that would never know its name or know the outside world or ever rise to fame. Why MOMMY!?? What crime did I commit? Did I do something so wrong that I wasn't worth your spit!? I hope you read this now and see how I feel. You made me die I hope you cry until you are ill.
