title: "The Beauty of Marriage"

The wedding bells ring high up in the tower. Listen to the beautiful melody as they proclaim the marriage of the young dark haired lovers. Today is a joyous day indeed. The fragrant smell of roses fill the nostrils of all who are present. The white doves fly above the cathedral and they make no sound as they soar high in the blue sky. The beautiful church is showered in the warm sunlight of the sweet afternoon. Their love has overcome all obstacles and today is the day that they vow to love each other until death breaks them apart. Marriage is a very sacred thing to them and they will live in harmony and much happiness forever and ever. The last kiss seals their promise to love and cherish each other. The rings are on their fingers like the worlds smallest handcuffs. They are so much in love on this day. This day of beauty and love.
