title: "Vampire's Lust"

I lay your fragile body against mine feeling your heart beat and the blood flow through you. How soft you are  and how beautiful. I run my fingers through your auburn hair and kiss your neck again and again tasting the skin. The passion burns inside me as I hold you tight. My hunger is awakened and I lay you on the large bed that is smothered in silk. I look into your emerald eyes and see lust. I lift you up and push my mouth against your neck once more and sink my fangs into it tasting your blood. It  tastes warm and erotically sweet. I watch you become limp in my arms. You are no more my love.
