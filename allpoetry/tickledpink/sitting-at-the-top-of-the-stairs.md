title: "[sitting at the top of the stairs]"

sitting at the top of the stairs
listening to the knives you spit out
fly towards each other
you pour boiling water
on already burned skin
you dig deeper
in a wound thats been alive for so long
you knew what it held
when you signed that contract years ago
you knew the terms 
it set for the rest of your life
and you signed it
live with the consequences of a hastened decision
but dont make me miserable while you bear your situation
you're not even trying 
but you signed
for life
