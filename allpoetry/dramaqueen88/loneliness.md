It has been eons,
Since you whispered a syllable to me.
Have you forgotten,
That I exist in the same universe as you?

Perhaps I have become invisible.
Over time, that happens to some people.
Could I possibly have become,
Only a slight figment of your imagination?

To you, I'm transparent:
That girl who's always around,
A shimmering pool of clear water.
I am a window when I long to be a door.

You don't know the pain you cause me.
