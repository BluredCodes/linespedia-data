Under the moonlight, I am the goddess
In the rain I dance with grace
Moonlight in my hair
Other than snowwhite I am fair
I am as free as a bird without a cage
No one can guess my age
And if you dare,fair sir, to guess my age
You might find your life without even one page
So touch me if you dare
All your doing now is a stare
We will see how much you know
Hurry before I call my lepoard of snow
The rain now stops, as I gently kiss your lips
I touch your face with my finger tips
You have reseved this kiss because I know who you are
I have searched for you near and far
Wher have you been
I have been worried sick, snice you have been gone I have committed much a sin
Yes making the gods angry was not right for me to do
But I was trying to find my way back to you
I could not look for any signs of you
I was not allowed to
Without you I've gone mad,crazy with fear and pain
So here I sit, and dance in the rain
My life has been full of tears
and many more than one fear
I have hope now, now I can feel your touch
Oh, how I have messed you so much
