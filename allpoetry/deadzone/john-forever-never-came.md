title: "John, forever never came."

We talked about eachother
How once we graduated we would stay together
But why didn't you tell me
That you where going to take a life?
Why didn't you let me see
That you took a knife?
You could have told...
Now there's no one to hold
When saddness is near
I have no one to call dear
Oh,god, please let him live and breath agian
I can not stand alone,I can't take a breath without grief
His life with me had not even began
Now I must stand alone on the reef
Dear John, life will never be the same
Because forever never came.
