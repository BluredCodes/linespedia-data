Dad tuck me into bed
Make my heart glow
Kiss me on the head
Let your love show


When my real folks got deforsed
My heart was broken and smashed
I though things couldn't get worse
And my heart turned to ash


But when I heard my mom found another
It made my heart glow
And my heart began to grow


When I frist seen you
I knew you were the one for my mom
My heart put another piece together like glue
It felt like a time bomb


But when I heard you were staying
And you started giving me hugs
I knew that was the answer to my praying
I,once agian,felt snug
