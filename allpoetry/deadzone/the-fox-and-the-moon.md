The fox and the moon
Want a lovely sight
At the Blue Lagoon
On this very night


The fox stares
At the moon
The moon glares
And says "We'll be together soon"


On the following month of June
The fox goes back to the hill
But there is no moon
And the fox felt ill


For his darling moon
For the way she understood
And for there talk at the Blue Lagoon
He says "I would do anything for her if I could"


Every night she is not there
The stars were twinkling without her
He began to think she didn't care
At frist he did not think this could acure


The following June
He never went back to the hill
He began to forget about the moon
For he had found anouther fox named Jilllast year,
