title: "Love will never find me"

My heart will never grow wings
it will never fly
As I watch people find their happy endings
I know I will never find the right guy
maybe in another lifetime or in ten years
intil I do, I feel like hiding my heart
And I know no one will ever call me dear
or that they will ever start
because love will never find me
no matter were my heart is hidden
no matter where I am looking I will never see
Because to me love is forbiddenlast year,
