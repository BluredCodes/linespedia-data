so of course i'm scared
locked in a little box like this

for once its not about a boy
but a Man
and inspiration

when I was 15 I was so straight-edge I put the teachers to shame
but things catch up on you
and I cant even picture 1997 anymore
except for you telling me it'd get better

here's to the Future, 
how about a trip?
show me where you ran to
in the beaches and montreal and miami
teach me how to rollerskate and drive and bury things within

i bet i'm more like you than i thought
