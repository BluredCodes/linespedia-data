this is about me
but much more about you

pretty boys are everywhere
but i'd rather sleep with you--across phone lines masquerading as distance

those days
when i'd clean my room and blow dry my hair
and you'd pry me from my latest essay
for movies and popcorn
and sex in dark corners

I snapped
You lost something (I think most called it a soul)

I'm sorry to admit
but it wasn't all me
I tried
and was more beautiful
than the sum of your parts

I Still Believe In You
and if you want me I'm there
you don't
and that's okay
let me turn my mic up

and say, yeah, I loved you,
so whatcha gonna do about it? 

/////so its not a poem. but its from the heart
and its here to be dissected
