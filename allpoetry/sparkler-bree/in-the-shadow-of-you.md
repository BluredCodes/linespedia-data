---
title: "In the Shadow of You"
writer: "Sparkler Bree"

I guess I'll always be in the shadow of you
Everything I do, you do ten times better.
I want my spotlight,
But you always seem to steal it.
This isn't supposed to be a competition
I thought it was a relationship
But you make me feel bad at every turn.
For once I want to do something and have you not there.
Not there to take the spotlight from me and put it on you.
You say I have no confidence, 
But how can I when you are always there
Ready to say something funnier than me
wittier than me
smarter than me
And you wonder why I put you down whenever I can.
Because you don't ever let me out of your shadow.I feel like sh*t. You tell me to join this thing, but while you get 15 comments, I can't even get 2.
