Cat with no bones
lying limp on my lap.
Comfortably dozing
in silent cat-nap.
Head tilted back,
chin towards the sky,
She's sniffed too much cat-nip
and on a calm sort of high.
Are her dreams filled with fishes
or birds in the trees?
Or blazing colors 
and death to all fleas?Yeah, that last line was a stretch.  I had to type this thing with one hand, because my "darling" pussy has fallen asleep on the other, which in turn has caused my arm to fall asleep.  I'm bored, and it's amazing the things that one comes up with to write about in such a state....:-P
