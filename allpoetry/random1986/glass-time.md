Ignorance dissolves understanding
broken by glass time
fratchered by bliss as your eyes
your eyes stare.
they creep behind you
dissolve the truth
breeding the weak

bleeding into this situation
they multiply they confuse
i think of an idea then creat
hideous mistakes as i break
as i heal my starvation
as i nurse this filth

fearing this faded acception im still afraid of the dark
splinters minds as it crumbles
submerged in pity and whispersim not confident about this poem so i wanted some more opinions, comments
