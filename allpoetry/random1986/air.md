Floating on air
riding on sweat and rouge
sticky hands rub your tired neck
take deep breathes
count to ten, count it in

moving closer
guided by confused eyes
insiding agrevates
irratates
motivates to stop

i walked for a while
looking around with expecting eyes
blunt knives lead to parasuicide
not an illness but a state of mind
dancing on hot ash
feeling fever...burning rashhmmm.
