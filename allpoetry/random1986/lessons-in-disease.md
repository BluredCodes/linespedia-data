shes a perfect disease
a pretty little thing
lost in despiration
lost in pity

pretentious little hore
dipped in insecurity
lost in despiration
lost in insecurity
lost in self esteem

hostility and judgement
your so full of shame
never knew hate and pity were so similar
shes a perfect disease..
