Give me some faith
to stop me tearing my mouth apart
give me some security
to stop me fearing my life

fearing a long silence
still hiding from myself
ashamed of my inner weakness
still ashamed of myself

you survived my lover
all these drunken years
pausing for days
i dont connect the way you do
broken wire

the most sour person 
i'll ever hope to meet
