long empty corridors where secrets lied
dusty sweaty rooms where children once cried
ive seen their ghosts- they were so tired
red swollen eyes, behind lived their lives

they lived under daddies outstretched fists
mummies tightened grip
they had had enough anyway...

grey eyes, black tears
weary and aching
so bored you could kill
you lost your fear
when you lost your father
your so cold in bed
as if you had died
