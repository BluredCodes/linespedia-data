They watched
as he twisted and circled
they rioted 
when he fell to the ground
It keeps me weak
it snaps my neck

too young 
to pay painful respects
a legend to mystery
is that what you expect?
it keeps me dead
it hurts my chest

another page, another faded mark, smudging, blurring into dark
lost time, lost direction
he hangs up your son
he crys with me
he sleeps with me
he watches me as i twist and circlejust want to find people's general opinions.its based on a suicide.
