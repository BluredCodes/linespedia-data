i remember being too scared to open my eyes
everything seems so insignificant
pictures painted to match faces
narrow white rooms all look the same
and all i want to do is live

everything is ridiculously numb
when your so bored
all you want to do is die
all these words make you feel awkward
all these smiles make you isolated
when you feel old inside
sometimes its an effort to live
