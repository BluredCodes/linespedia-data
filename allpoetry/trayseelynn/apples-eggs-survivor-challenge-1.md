Apple Fritters warm and sugary
Tree Top Apple Juice is very dreamy
Blossoms of spring time's scent fill the air
Turn frowns into smiles without such a care
Into the yard, children sing and play
Delicious chocolate candy makes the Easter Bunny's day
Green grass secretly hides all kinds of surprises
And some won't be found, till the new sun rises
Red rose buds, homes for bumble bees
And grass stains won't come out of little boys knees
Yellow tulips make the day such a blessing
Apples; candied carmeled and even dressing

Baskets and baskets of candy galore
Full of bunnies, eggs, jelly beans and more
Of all of the things about Easter I see; is
Chocolate bunnies; all just for me
Covered in egg shells from head to toe
Confetti is scattered all over, don'tcha know?!
Eggs that someone mysteriously forgot to cook
Makes a big mess, I'm so afraid to look!
Me and my nephews fight for the prize egg
Laugh and Scream hysterically, it's under my leg!
Because now the fun's over, all the eggs have been found.
They are all accounted for, not one left on the ground.
Are hard boiled eggs good to eat when thier hot?
Inedible and nasty? Naaa! I think not!I know this is silly, but DARN HARD to make it read both ways!!! LOL! Worth a shot though, huh?!
