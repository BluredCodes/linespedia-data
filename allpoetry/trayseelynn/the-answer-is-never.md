---
title: "The Answer Is..... Never!"
writer: "trayseelynn"
slug: "the-answer-is-never"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54445-The-Answer-Is.....-Never--by-trayseelynn"
---

when do you get  to the point when enough is enough
when you fight and argue over petty little stuff
when you say that you love eachother more than anything
when he promises you forever and buys you a ring

when issues arise that have to be resolved
when the more time you spend, the more you're involved
when does it get to the point that you settle the score
when the love that you're building, and it's you he adores

when do you draw the line, or who call the shots
when he says that you're perfect, and you swear that you're not
when do you tell him that you'll be there forever
when you're in love with him, your lives, one, together

when do you stop trying, when it's all be said and done
when love overcomes all, and both of you have won
when do you stop loving him when he has broken your heart
when each day without him, the more it makes you fall apart

when do you stop caring and just let him go
when someone else has stolen him, taken over the show
when do you go on without him, stop feeling empty
when the past year with him, is just a memory!

when do you get to the point that enough is enough?

if you are truly and completly in love, the answer is.... never!this poem was taken off a piece of dialog from the movie, "THE MEXICAN" when "Leroy" asks "Samantha" ... "When do you get to the point when enough is enough?"
