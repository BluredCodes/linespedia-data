~^Entrancing Flame^~

A lonely flame entrances me,
Tells me how lonely a flame can be.
How hot it is here in this room.
A wick and wax, this flame entombed -

Solitary; sitting here by itself waiting,
For a new flame: anticipating.
A match to ignite it; bring it to life -
Knowing that it is, its only light.

Oh, to be alone like he?
Such pain, heartache, and misery -
I don't think I could handle the dark.
Although asleep, I'm lit by a single spark.

To be a flame on this candle's head -
Blow me out; kill me dead!
I don't want to be alone anymore!
What is the reason I would be here for?

To light up the darkness; set the shadows free.
Maybe being a flame isn't as bad as it seems.
So instead of being here all by myself,
Bring me a new candle: a mate; on my shelf.

Bring us to life; we'll make you smile.
Sit here and be entranced for a while.

~^April 10, 2002^~
~^Tracy Lynn Richardson^~
