flawlessness perfection in a dream that she casts
you can not believe  the images you're seeing
with eyes closed and sleeping in this dreamworld
illusions take over your soul and your being
this fantasy happiness you can lose yourself in
the desires of she has chosen takes over your exsistance
concieved of a smile when no one was looking
you can not fight her, you have no resistance
she has her way with each thought and each memory
a victim of visions unimagined until now
you recognize each color, each smell, and each touch
realizing you had been there before; somehow
by chance or by fate you do not know you are there
she has you in her grasp and won't let you go
you obey her every word following her every wish
you realize that you will soon lose control
each surreal image plays like a movie in your mind
frame by frame she portrays every desire of your heart
asleep; though awake everything is so complete
your imagination soars on her wings; a new start
her hands glide over your eyes and you scream in delight
your hopes have found thier way into this alternate reality
this Goddess of Dreams shows you things; a moment of tears
suddenly a peace falls over you; clouds of serenity
a voice so calming; familiar; tells you everything is fine
you remember the soft voice belonging to your heart
listening to each syllable flowing from each of its beats
realizing that it was once you're heart that made you believe
to let go of yourself; let go of your fears; just let go; release
awakening; you find that it wasn't a dream
it was your once destroyed soul trying to heal the hurt
making you realize it would all be ok; you would be fine
opening your eyes you see that night turned into day
