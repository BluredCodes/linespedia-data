knowing that {it's my life} that i'm {living on a prayer}
doped up with {bad medicine} i'm alive without a care
i'm {damned} if I do and {damned} if I don't
because if {that's what it takes} i know that i won't
falling {in and out of love} i'm {wanted dead or alive}
{living in sin} takes a {miracle} to know i'll survive
{never say goodbye} for the {next 100 years}
{now and forever} {older} i'm fighting the {fear}
{one wild night} i was the {queen of new orleans}
full of crazy ideas, living out crazy dreams
in {real life} there are a lot of {sad song night}s
but i {thank you for loving me} through each of our fights
in this {two story town} i'll {sleep when i'm dead}
when {all i want is everything} on every billboard read
i'll {always run to you} and drink your {bitter wine}
paid for your love with {blood money} it will all work out fine
you were {born to be my baby} as fate would have me say
from a {destination anywhere} i know we'll find our way
{every word was a piece of my heart} to you
this {game of the heart} is tried, tested and true
{i believe} {i can love} you the best that i can
{i thank you} {i want you} beecause you are my man
{if god was a woman} i know what she'd say
{if i could make a living out of loving you} i'd always live this way
{is there love} that is unending cuz i think {it's just me}
{i'll be there for you} if you could only see
i'll {keep the faith} forever, please don't {lie to me}
{life's too short for days like this} i wanna let you know
that i don't have any {love for sale} at least not anymore
this is {more than we bargained for}; this love; you and i
{naked} as we lay together and stare up in the sky
this {mystery train} we're riding on, takes us everywhere
don't {runaway} and give up without a care
these {secret dreams} i dream of us, make me want to sleep
cuz i'm {shot through the heart} on a {silent night} in these dreams i chose to keep
please give me {something for the pain} it hurts me oh so bad
cuz i'm {staring at you window with a suitcase in my hand}
i want to be with you forever; and move in with you {tonight}
i don't know what the {price of love is} but the {hardest part is the night}
{i don't want to fall to the fire} baby, embrace the love we made
and know that together we're very hot cuz it's {99 in the shade}
{wild is the wind} that blows though shadows on the wall
{you really got me now} catch me love, before i trip and fall
although i know that it will be hard {letting you go}
it {ain't no cure for love} when it all spins out of contol
you'll {always} have me to yourself it is the {american dream}
and it's {all that really matters} no matter how it seems
this {bed of roses} you layed me in, is starting to wilt and die
{in these arms} you lay with me, wipe my tears from my eyes
{come back} to my heart where you belong i'm begging you please
i wonder {does anybody really fall in love} the answer is NO it seems
this {diamond ring} on my finger, is starting to lose its shine
and the taste of our love reminds me of {bitter wine}
you {drive} me to the ends of emotion, with all your words and ways
i'll be one the {edge of a broken heart} until my dying days!
{good ain't good enough} and {good guys don't always wear white}
for your arms, i'll be in forever, love, for them; i'll always fight
{heaven help us} {i believe} i can keep you here with me
and {i can love} you forever, this dream can set us free
soon i'll awake from this dream of mine, and you will be long gone
{the hardest part is the night} for me; i know i'll have to be strong
[hey god} please bring him back to me, i promise i won't screw up
i'll make him see, believe in me, and i'll always be enough
{maybe tomorrow} will be a better; and it won't hurt so bad
{now and forever} love, you're the best dream i ever had
