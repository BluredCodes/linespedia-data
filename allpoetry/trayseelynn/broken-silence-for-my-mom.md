---
title: "Broken Silence (for my Mom)"
writer: "trayseelynn"
slug: "broken-silence-for-my-mom"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62049-Broken-Silence--for-my-Mom--by-trayseelynn"

the silence around her is deafening 
no one hears her cries
she sits all alone
as each part of her dies

she thinks to herself
why am i here?
her heart on a shelf
wiping away each dusty tear.

she loved her family completely
her children are all grown
cries to herself discreetly
a prisoner in this home

the four walls that surround her
keep her bound in chains
memories seem a blur
in the shadows they remain

a life of hurt and sorrow
the burdens of the past she bares
she lives for only tomorrow
where again, at the walls she stares

but she is loved without her knowing
by someone within arms reach
although it isn't showing
all these lessons she had to teach

a mother who's arms surrounded
this child she tried to shield
have bloomed inside her; astounded
like new born flowers in a field.

she was molded from her compassion
her love, her prayers, her dreams
complete from a mother's passion
no matter what it seems

my mother is an eagle
from God's hands she has flown
one of his most regal
the love to me; she's shown

I wouldn't trade one moment
of my life I've spent with her
and if I've never shown it
I thank God each day for her

My rock, my heart, my strength
she's my world
No matter what she thinks
I'm still her little girlI love you Mommy, and I'm always here for you NO MATTER WHAT or WHO comes along!!!!! And I would never change one single thing in my life if it meant I would have to be without you!
