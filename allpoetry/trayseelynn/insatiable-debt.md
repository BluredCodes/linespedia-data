---
title: "Insatiable Debt"
writer: "trayseelynn"
slug: "insatiable-debt"

encompassing me with this insatiable desire
each kiss; lingers, takes me in
your love; my heart unknowingly aquired
to feel your hands upon my skin

levitating; our bodies as one; soar
against all odds we make it through
our love so high, never touching the floor
we speak no words, only truth

my heart beat syncronized with yours
time stops; suspended in this moment we're in
breathing in; breathing out; there are no cures
for this disease that feeds within

taking over my body, my mind, my soul
no explaintion sought, has been found
did you mean to make me lose control
around your finger, i'm tighly wound

our commitment lives between these sheets
those vows we spoke a year ago
together; whole, as one, complete
our lives to eachother, we now owe.

this debt to you, i will pay in full
with each breath i breathe to speak your name
why is it that i feel like the fool?
was this commitment we made; spoken in vain?

this ring on my finger burns my skin
the words you spoke are still in this band
unending; these circles, unbroken, though thin
will never be removed from my hand

those words; my promise; they are all true
wherever you are tonight; don't forget
even if they didn't mean the same to you
my heart is forever & eternally in your debt

~* Saturday - April 13, 2002 *~
~* Tracy Lynn Richardson *~
