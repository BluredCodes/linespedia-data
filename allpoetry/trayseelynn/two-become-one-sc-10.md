title: "Two Become One [sc 10]"

a voice in the night
brings darkness to light
my heart and my soul
lose all self control

in your hands i've found
my heart's tightly wound
in your eyes i've seen
what love really means

i see us together
lasting to forever
through any storm faced
our hearts interlaced

as one we shall live
equal love to give
our life intertwined
until the end of time

when you pledge your heart
two lives; brand new start
two shall be made one
a new life: just begun
