Hidden with in her is a beast in captivity
A silent and solace driven serenity
She cannot hide this lover’s demise
Truly herself, she wears no disguise

Moonlight cast shadows on parts of her soul
A ponderable feeling she can't lose control
Freedom is bound to her by chains and walls
A safety net below her incase she falls

A balance of desire and passionate tales
Something her inner child knows very well
Frightened by loneliness she can't be alone
The world that she serves here on her own

Trying to please everyone and herself
She realizes she can't answer to anyone else
Standing on nothing, no hope or goals sought
Her solitary independence she's faithfully fought

No one let her choose the path she now follows
Its her pride and her heart that she quickly swallows
There’s no one to tell her which road she should take
It’s her only decision to what her life makes

Mistakes, lessons learned of things she's done wrong
This is her creed, her broken heart's song
They can't bring her down by hateful thing said
Pain isn't found in any book she's read

She dreams as she stands there staring into the night
It’s for her own life she'll stand up and fight
She just wants to be free from the walls and the chains
Only the scars from life still remains

Don’t judge her by looks; it's her beauty inside
It’s behind her outer shell that her heart truly hides
Look into her eyes, ice blue, frozen in time
Loving someone too much is her only crime

Arching her back she will finally stand tall
Naked, untainted, nothing hidden at all
Nothing is left, imagination gone; still confused
You can't see the images, heart battered and bruised

Proudly she stands through all the hurt and the pain
Her feelings from tears fall from her like rain
Yet, you'll never know this, she's hidden it well
Only time will tell how much she'll reveal

A fragile piece of art; her body and mind
The purest of treasures you had the pleasure to find
Her dreams keep her wanting, craving, in need
Arms reaching, outstretched, as her heart desperately bleeds

Lovers of the past have sliced it thinly into shreds
And sometimes she thinks she'd be better off dead
It’s her passion, her willingness to go on, to live
She has so much more of her self left to give

Lifeless and broke, she’ll settle the score
Remembering exactly what she’s here for
Someday she'll stand before someone else new
You never know, maybe, that someone is you
