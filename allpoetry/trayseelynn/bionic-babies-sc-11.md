now a days you have your computer science baby specialists
who concieve each notion of a perfect baby and run millions of tests
you can pick the eye color, hair color, skin color, and even the baby's sex
want one baby? maybe two? have a twin now and one later? what's next?

i can have your baby for you or you can have mine
we can have each other's babies at the very same time
don't ya think that if we did; would it be a crime?
some people would think that if we did, it would be devine!

I could have a baby to look exactly like Brad Pitt
and I would have it before Jen; she would have a fit
Wait! Maybe Jon Bon Jovi's baby; come to think of it
I would have Jon and Brad's babies if the laws permit!

But what if I wanted him to be smart and make a lot of money
what if I wanted him to be a her and have hair the color of honey?
eyes would be the bluest of  blue; wouldn't that be funny?
it's hair would be the blondest of blonde; the color of golden honey

Heck, I could pick to have the child be the president or even a queen
I could have my child be perfect or so they make it seem
people have so many choices now a days, it makes me want to scream
a mother can even have 8 babies; her own base ball team

but now i have to think about what i would want of my child's life
i wouldn't want it to be a product of someone's surgical knife
to protect my baby and keep it from heartbreak and strife
I would keep it under my wing and guard it with my life

if i ever have a baby, the old fashioned way is the best
i don't want to have it undergo unneccisary tests
and if God gave me a baby that way, i would surley be blessed
my baby would be my life, my love, my heart, my soul, i think you know the rest!
