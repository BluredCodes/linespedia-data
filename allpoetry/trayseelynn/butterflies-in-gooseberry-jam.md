Butterflies in gooseberry jam
Utterly silly girl that I am
To think of a thing that makes me giggle
To watch the butterfly in the jam wiggle
Each flutter of his wings makes a mess
Really yucky I must confess
For he is stuck and can not get out
Licking his wings; his tongue flapping about
I realize this poor butterfly is really stuck
Easing him out of the jam is real tough
So I gently pick him up by his wings

I clean off the jam off his legs and things
Never thought he would get in over his head

Golly I hope his family is fed
Only if I could find them and see
Only if I could make them come to me
So I could make sure they weren't worried 
Each mintues that past; I tried to hurry
Butterflies are a Fairy's best friend
Every one flies on the love that they send
Reach carefully to set this one free
Ready to fly, he looked back at me
Yet I thought I saw him say something as he flew

Just a flitter of wings in a silent "thank you"
Am I the only one that could understand?
My little friend the butterfly that fell in the gooseberry jam?This poem reads across and up and down... if you look at the first letter of each line it says:
