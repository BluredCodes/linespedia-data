Alone;
you make me whole
my heart, soul, mind, spirit
only you can win me over
Complete

Complete;
my life with you
is the way it should be
'cuz nothing else matters to me
Always

Always;
is how love is
feelings for each other
through trials we face together
Promise

Promise;
our word; our oath
undeniably strong
able to hold us in its arms
Our Vows

Our Vows;
sacred and true
forever unbroken
to last throughout eternity
Lifetime

Lifetime;
without an end
desire and passion
never again spending my life
Alone
