She;
finds herself lost in him and mezmorized
lead through the darkness by the fire in his eyes
behind them she sees a hero, a knight
heart piercing darkness, gives way to the light


He;
knows that with him, she's hypnotized
hears her soul drown in tears as she cries
longing for freedom, his spirit in chains
a heart's broken pieces is all that remains

She;
Runs to his arms, so strong and so calm
shadows overwhelm her, something has to be wrong
suddenly cold winds sweep right through his cloak
disappearing before her in a cloud of dark smoke

He;
Knowingly leaves her alone, he was scared
Not believing for him, someone actually cared
Once a black heart filled with evil and death
turned warm, slowly beating, inside her caress

She;
Screamed out his name, echoing through the mist
Voice piercing right through the evil darkness
On wings of a fairy, her love found it's way
To him, meaning everything, in his heart she will stay

He;
Roams aimlessly through each battle each fight
the promise of her love leads his through the night
Never again, will he push her away
Remembering her arms around him that day

She;
Saved him from death, darkness, and hurt
Picking his heart from his grave, right out of the dirt
No longer the damsel he hid in his heart
Nothing on earth will ever keep them apart

He;
Finds himself lost in her, and mezmorized
Lead through the darkness by desire in her eyes
Behind them he sees her heart for him, true
