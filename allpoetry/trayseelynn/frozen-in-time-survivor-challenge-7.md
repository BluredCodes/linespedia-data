conceived by an image, a look in each other’s eyes 
blinded; not realizing that it was born to die 
a rapid pace beats straight from their chest 
sacred; not knowing it's soon laid to rest 

i bring you a different look into their hearts 
together; not knowing it will be torn apart 
illusions of happiness were born from a dream 
a story book love; or so it shall seem 

he walked gallantly into her life one April eve 
her hero came to save her; his heart on his sleeve 
she quickly recognized she would soon see 
that this was the love that she had so dreamed 

it was born on the night that he looked in her eyes 
the bluest of windows; from two separate lives 
each day they would spent together grew long 
her heart was his heart and both would be strong 

trying to reveal truest emotions they felt 
soon to play out the cards they were dealt 
her life wasn't normal; complicated by health 
she wanted to be with him; not anyone else 

over and over he swore her his heart 
through hospital stays; they were never apart 
he held her next to him, the two became one 
their life spent together had only begun 

each minute spent with him; days turned to nights 
talking for hours until darkness was light 
memories and secrets unfolded before them 
love quickly formed; he knew she adored him 

things were so perfect; but too perfect it seemed 
co-dependant on him; she constantly leaned 
expecting him to understand that she was so scared 
to be alone with at all was what she had feared 

never had she lived alone by herself 
depending on him and everyone else 
not sure she could handle being alone 
while she sat there and he wasn't home 

he couldn't give her time to deal with her life 
it caused her stress, it cut like a knife 
to hide what he was feeling they fought all the time 
although neither one of them committed this crime 

he swore to his death he would be there forever 
and that they would always be one; be together 
but communication turned cold; neither one would speak 
their heart's so exhausted from fighting; so weak 

it got to the point neither one was willing to try 
feelings of passion and love had gone dry 
though she loved him still with all of her heart 
their love that was unending was soon torn apart 

he called her and she wanted so bad for him to say 
that he loved her forever; till his dying day 
but things weren't working out for them anymore 
they wondered what they had fallen in love for 

he had given up on their love and their dreams 
and she would too or to him; so it seemed 
though her heart beat cried out his name 
nothing they had would ever be the same 

the death of their love grew dark and grew cold 
buried with dreams, the future; their goals 
they both were alone, days turned to weeks 
in someone else's arms is now where he sleeps 

while all alone overwhelmed with the love they once shared 
it's hard to believe that he ever truly cared 
she mourns for the love that they shared; one of a kind
