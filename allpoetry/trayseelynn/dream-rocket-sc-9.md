One day on the way to yoga class
I lost Grandma's Favorite Button somewhere
I back tracked all my steps real fast
But I couldn't find it anywhere

I remember waking up that day with bed head 
looking in the mirror at my elephant eyelashes
I ran outa mascara and used a magic marker instead
because it would have worked the fastest

My doodie butt brother ate creamed corn for breakfast
and I had some left over chopped suey
I left it in the microwave way too long
and it kinda ended up all gooey

I searched my purse for some chapstick later
and checked myself for belly button lint
I remembered my Chihuahua ate mashed taters
I thought maybe that's where the Grandma's button went

But wait, I think I remember the rump roast
that we had the night before 
naaa that wouldn't be where the button was lost
cuz I had checked all over the floor

Please Cosmic God's give me a sign
Of where this button could be
I've retraced my steps nine hundred times
and it's no where that I've seen

The spatula I threw at my Doodie Head brother
was still laying there on the ground
Maybe I can replace Grandma's button with another
or hire and detective and a blood hound

I've looked so much I've got googly eyes
and my armpits are dripping sweat
not finding the button is no surprise
it's where it's at that I just don't get

I sat in the park and day dreamed a while
hoping it would come back to me
all of the sudden Brad Pitt walked by; flashed a smile
and i knew the Cosmic God's where looking out for me

Brad Pitt? In the Park? How lucky could I be?
I was wearing my favorite thong underwear
In hopes he would take them off of me!
but please don't let there be buttfuzz in there.

I followed him down to his car; oh my god
will he realize I'm drooling on myself
Brad Pitt? In the Park? How fonging odd?
he was looking at me and not anyone else

I licked my thin little chicken lips
and closed in with my hands on his face
i was hoping i wouldn't be a clutz and trip
i'm not one for style and grace

THEN IT DAWNED ON ME WHERE THE BUTTON WAS
How could I forget that I stuck it in pocket! 
I woke up from that dream; I musta been on drugs
