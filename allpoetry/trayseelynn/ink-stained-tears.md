---
title: "Ink Stained Tears"
writer: "trayseelynn"
slug: "ink-stained-tears"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54534-Ink-Stained-Tears-by-trayseelynn"
---

each word that is written
by the ink of my tears
wearing my heart on my sleeve
as i'm  facing my fears
it makes me no difference
what people may say
they can't stop me from feeling this way
i know in my heart
i'll love you forever
and only time will tell
if we'll be together
my heart is my pen
each word is unending
with meanings so true
it's my heart i'm defending
but you knew all of this
when you told me you loved me
and forever with me is where you would be
forever has now come
to a bittersweet end
and now as i try
my hearts on the mend
but the band-aides won't fix it
there's only one choice
to make my heart whole again
just to hear your voice
my thoughts dance gracefully
memories written by heart
played over like a movie
because now we're apart
in color i can see you
the look in your eyes
telling me you love me
why can't you realize?
you can't just forget me
you can't and you know it
but the more that you try
you're starting to blow it
because when i look
into you're eyes, all i see
is pictures of you
staring back at me
why can't you stand
to look into mine
are you afraid when you do
that i'll come to mind
you know in your heart
exactly how you feel
this feeling that your tossing aside
are still very real
someday you will wake up
and realize i was your heart
the one you tore out of my chest
and ripped it all apart
but, of course, i am still here
as i always will be
i never stopped loving you
and you never stopped loving me
