---
title: "Just A Façade"
writer: "trayseelynn"
slug: "just-a-faade"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56831-Just-A-Façade-by-trayseelynn"
---

her eyes are a endless mirror to her eternal soul
yet fire engulfs you and you lose control
piercing right through you; bleeding you fall
her glance from across the room says it all

her lips; moistly wet with encompassing desire
words spoken from them take you much higher
her tongue calls you silently drawing you in
it's her emotional goal; your heart she must win

her smile entrances you; it's all you can see
gleaming; controlling you; setting you free
glowing embers of fire radiate from her face
hypnotized it takes you into its embrace

your body realizes it can't fight her stare
it gives up completely without such a care
she has you extremely lost under her spell
addicted to her; you just tripped and fell

you reach out your hand to touch her cheek
fingers outstretched, but you just can not reach
just a little farther, with all of your heart
you know if she denies you; you'll just fall apart

yet her eyes, her lips, her smile returns the thought
you can't live without her; it's her heart you have sought
she closes her eyes for a brief moment in time
and you realize the spell was broken; what a crime

she had stolen your heart, threw it there on the floor
walked out on you with the slamming of the door
you thought she loved you with every fiber of her being
and it was her truest feelings you thought you were seeing

she raped you, she robbed you, stole every part of you; gone
you swore that she loved you but boy you were wrong
deceivingly threw away all of the memories you made
even the ones you swore you'd take to your grave

you were warned by the tell tale signs before you began
you knew you should have tucked tail and ran
but you thought she was different and your love would not die
turns out the time you spent with her were lies

how does it feel to have it all happen to you
"what goes around comes around", the saying is true
next time you'll know not to believe in her; all the lies
so watch out next time you decide to look in her eyes

you might just think her love for you is true
but remember all the pain that you had to go through
each night you spent alone crying out her name
unfortunately; she wasn't doing the same

she may not have been the person you wanted
but it's her eyes that you see, your life, she has haunted
when you close your eyes at night to sleep
and she visits you secretly in the dreams that you keep

remember the words i have spoken to you
no matter what you think, every single one's true
she'll take you completely into her web of lies
manipulation rules, it's your hearts final demise

your heart's on the floor collecting dust everyday
you let her win; and she got her way
do you think that you're the first victim she has
you won't be the first and surely not the last

shut the door behind her, throw away the key
i warned you she'd do this, you didn't listen to me
the black widow spider; she wouldn't let you breathe
when you started to suffocate; you should have believed

i'm not sorry she did this, i'm not sorry you're hurt
because it was once my heart you tossed in the dirt
i believed that you loved me, when it was all lies
i was lost in the passion, the fire in your eyes

when it was all over, you told me i was dead
i was left with your image and voice in my head
memories, dreams, shattered and broken
my love meant nothing; worthlessly spoken

when you come back and beg me to return
i hope the words you said will eternally burn
since when do you physically speak to the dead
you should have thought about the things you said

the love and the trust i had for you is gone
because i know it wasn't me who was totally wrong
you can't beg me enough to take you back once again
because the game that you played, i was bound to win!when she throws your heart into a meat grinder and serves it to you for dinner, and you realize it was all a façade  then eat it baby! you will never ever be forgiven again!
