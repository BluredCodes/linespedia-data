Sparkly beams dance jubliantly around me like fireflies, like
Jewels of God's creation lighting the darkness around me
Covering the world with tiny little candle lights
The beauty that I behold infront of me makes me speechless
Bottom to top they fly around me like little fairies
Of awesome wonder my eyes beheld their magic
The fireflies bring happiness to all living things around them
Ocean; sea; river; so many of them surround me, engulfing the dark
With each flicker of light from thier tiny little bodies makes me see that
The spectacular wonderment God has made from the simplest creature
Sunlight does not compair to these candles in the night
Reflecting
Off the dew gathered on rose pedals; entrancing
Them in their own illumination
Making the lighted beauties multiply
Prisms; beams of light dance around me like spotlights
Of wind and rain and snow they rule and command
Rainbows of colors surround me; spectacular wonderful colors
