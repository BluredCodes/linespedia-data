---
title: "Cherished (a rictameter)"
writer: "TinasHeart"
slug: "cherished-a-rictameter"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57816-Cherished--a-rictameter--by-TinasHeart"

Cherished
are the moments
I am given with you.
Each day with you, a gift so rare,
take my hand, let's not let the moment pass,
making memories that will last,
sharing my life with you,
a miracle
cherished.
