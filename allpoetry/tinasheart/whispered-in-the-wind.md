Whispered In The Wind

Each new morning, I awaken to the sun's glorious glow,
glowing in my heart, the sweet splendor of your love,
splendid thoughts of you stream through the windows of my soul.

In the sound of the flowing winds, sweetly whispering,
whispers of your love, soft as an angel's breath,
breathing in love's heavenly scent wafting through my thoughts.

Like music in the winds, my soul lifts in singing,
You are the song in my heart, the joy of my soul,
a soulful tune that streams endlessly on the chords of my heart.

I pray that the angels keep you safe and content,
contented by the love that faithfully flows from me,
may my love wrap around your heart, bathing you in the warmth.

Until I'm in your arms again, when body and soul we are one,
hear the melody of my love whispered in the winds.
