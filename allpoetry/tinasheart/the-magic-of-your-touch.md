The Magic Of Your Touch

Each night I'm mesmerised by your charm,
charming me with those beautiful eyes,
eyes that pierce through to my soul.

There is something magical in your touch,
touching me with the power of your love,
loving me so completely, by you I'm captivated.

You touch my heart like no one ever could.
Can you feel the magic that our love holds?
Holding us with a power we can not fight.

The loving intimacy we share is always spiritual,
my spirt spirals and turns, drifting Heavenward,
the windows of Heaven open up to bless our union.

Magic surrounds us as we soar on wings of love to ecstacy,
forever I will be, captured by the magic of your touch.
