Magical Journey By The Sea (a Sonnet)

Come with me on a magical journey,
where your childlike fantasies come alive.
Cast aside all cares, let your troubles flee
in this mystical land, no need to strive.

Enchantment arises beneath the moonlight,
when the creatures of the sea come out to play.
Let it fill your youthful soul with delight,
laughter of a child, to last all your days.

Listen to the melody of your heart,
see the mermaid ride on a dolphin,
changing your life, wonders it will impart,
as you capture this enchantment within.

Resurrect those dead, childlike dreams again,
within your heart, may the magic remain.
