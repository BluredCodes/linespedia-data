I Would Do Anything For You

Is there a way I could show to you how deep my love runs?
Running with all my might, I would go the distance for you.
Just to hear you say you know I love you would be my prize.

I would capture all the stars in the sky, if only I could,
so you can see the beauty they shine, I'd bring them down to you,
yet, the light in your eyes is brighter by far than any star.

I would give you the sun, so you would never know a cold day,
troubles would melt away and all your days would be warm.
The sun's warmth can't compare to the warmth of your embrace.

I'd pull down the moon for you, so you would never be lonely.
Alone with the man in the moon, surely you two could be friends.
Yet, you'll never know a truer friendship, then the one I offer.

There is nothing I would not do to show you how much I love you.
I'd do all these things to give you the happiness you've given to me.
