Reach Beyond The Stars

Glimpse the sky where dreams lie magical,
the magic, a treasure of all you believe,
for believing makes our dreams come true.

The stars, patienly awaiting to be captured.
Capture those stars sparkling in your eyes,
keeping your eyes fixed on the prize ahead.

Our dreams await just beyond the stars' reach,
reaching high, touch a falling star, make a wish,
wishing and praying for your dreams to come true.

Never loose sight of your dreams, never let go,
going the distance, make things happen for you.
Believe in yourself for you're someone special.

Follow your dreams wherever they may take you,
set your goals and aim just beyond the stars.
