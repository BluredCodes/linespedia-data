Tell Me You Love Me

Look deep into my eyes, can you see my love?
Loving you is the magic of all my days
a day without your love would break my heart.

Don't you know you're what makes my soul shine?
Shining through the night to bring you pleasure
pleasing you is what I long to do each day I live.

If I held all the world's magic within my hands,
I'd hand it to you so you'd see how much I love you,
your heart would be filled with all this knowledge.

But I hold no magic in my hands, it lies within my eyes,
eyes that are fathomless oceans, swimming with adoration
I will be adoring only you untill time is no more.

So please take me within your arms, tell me you know my love,
Please don't break my fragil heart again, tell me you love me.
