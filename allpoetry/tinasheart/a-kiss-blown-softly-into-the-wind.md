A Kiss Blown Softly Into The Wind

On my heart, you whisper ever so softly,
soft, gentle winds blow, touching my heart,
heartfelt words of love spoken into the wind.

No matter where you go, by your love, I'm surrounded
in the surrounding winds, I feel your soft touch,
touching my cheek with the gentleness of your love.

Whenever you are gone, I long for you to embrace me,
my heart lifts as I feel your loving prescense nearby,
I embrace the winds near to me, for I know you're there.

Your gentle spirit breathes life into my lonesome soul,
soulfully, it emcompasses my heart, I'm filled with peace.
The peaceful winds blowing, whisper of your love to me.

Wherever you are, blow a kiss to me into the gentle winds,
as the wind caresses my cheeks, I'll know I've been kissed by you.
