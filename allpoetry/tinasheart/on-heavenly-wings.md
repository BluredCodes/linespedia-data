On Heavenly Wings

My heart awakens in cherished renewal,
eyes opening, I see beauty all around.
Suddenly, my world seems more vivid-
Life is beautiful when love abounds.

My world's scented sweetly as the rose,
bathed in the pureness, your love holds;
dew drops from Heaven fall on my heart
as the splendor of rapture... unfolds.

Surely we've savored Heaven's sweetness.
On celestial wings, we've soared so high,
intimately, we touched the gates of ecstacy.
Eternally I'll drift in love's lifted skies.

I, your angel will fly lovingly unto you,
so just call my name when you're in dismay.
Anything within my power, I will do for you,
all of your burdens, together we can slay.

Splashes of sunlight stream freely
through the open windows of my soul,
my heart soars on the wings of joy.
Knowing your love, has made me whole.
