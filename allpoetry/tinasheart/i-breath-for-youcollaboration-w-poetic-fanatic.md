~I Breath For You~

Father in Heaven, my heart flows with thankfulness,
thanking you each new day for Your unfailing love,
love that surpasses all my heart can comprehend.

You made the earth, the sky, wonders of creation,
creating a resting place, that I may praise You,
for You are my life, love and lasting happiness.

Breathing in Your love, my spirit drifts Heavenward.
Heaven enters my soul, when Your glory overshadows.
Shadows threaten, yet I'm refreshed beneath your wings.

Grant me wings that I may soar to higher ground,
forever grounded in the light Your love has shown,
shining radiantly throughout all of the earth.

And for those who may be swallowed in the darkness,
let me guide them, swaying each of them your way.
