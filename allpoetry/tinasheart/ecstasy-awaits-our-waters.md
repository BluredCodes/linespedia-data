This poem is written only for my husband with all my love to him. 

Ecstasy Awaits Our Waters 

I wait for your return at the end of a long day. 
As another day ends, once again I long for you. 
Come now with me, let me ease your weariness. 
Making each moment last, ecstasy we'll renew. 

From the troubles of the world, we'll now escape 
into the fathomless ocean of our endless love. 
Let all your troubles drift out into the current 
as we submerge into the waters of erected love. 

Like lovers lost to the depths of the sea, 
we're lost in infinite love, held therein. 
Our souls colide in the still of the night, 
like the waves crash as the tide rolls in. 

You fervently kiss my lips, I long for more, 
I desire to be filled, only you can satisfy, 
fill this aching longing of my thirsty soul. 
I'll take my fill of you, never running dry. 

Passion streams, endless as the deep seas, 
hearts beating loudly as the ocean's roar. 
Your touch more fervent than the summer heat, 
as richest treasures of love we now explore.
