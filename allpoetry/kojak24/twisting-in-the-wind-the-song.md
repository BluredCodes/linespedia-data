Our love was over - before it began, 
So many problems - it had to end, 
Happiness walked out - and lonliness walked in. 
Leaving our hearts - twisting in the wind. 

.......Chorus ..... 

Twisting in the wind - we couldn't survive,
Rumors and lies - hurt us inside, 
Defeating - everything we tried, 
Leaving our hearts - twisting in the wind. 

II 

We knew - it was time to forget 
But we didn't want - to end it yet, 
So we placed our vows - on an altar of regret 
Leaving our hearts - twisting in the wind, 

.......Chorus ..... 

Twisting in the wind - we couldn't survive,
Rumors and lies - hurt us inside, 
Defeating - everything we tried, 
Leaving our hearts - twisting in the wind.

Twisting in the wind - nothing we can do, 
Knowing inside - our dreams can't come true, 
Trying so hard - to understand, 
But there's too much pain - our hearts won't mend.
