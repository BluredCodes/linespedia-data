title: "Untruth ... Or (Nutured Delusions)"

Someone cries out
in longing every night,
For the one hungered for
just to hold her tight.

All the lonely  years 
made her come undone,
Till she found a friend
who was the only one..

Many years a couple 
have come and gone,
But now her sad heart 
wants to sing a new song.

She worries about
giving friend the boot,
What the hell girl
If he moves just shoot.

Another love in her heart
she knows will never be,
The dreams she feels inside
are only fantasies.

Poor thing keeps yearning
for what can never happen,
All she does all day
is keep her mouth a flappin.'

The one she wants
is not part of her plan,
He turned and walked away
and she didn't understand.

She knows the sad truth
of what I've spoken,
And she needs to realize
that I'm not jokin'. 

Her love for him
isn't worth a token,
Now she'll spend 
many many years
