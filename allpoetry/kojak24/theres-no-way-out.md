1st Verse ..... 

There's no way out ... I've tried everything, 
To escape the hurt ... avoid the pain, 
But everywhere I turn ... I see her there, 
There's no way out ... and she don't care. 

2nd Verse ..... 

My hurt keeps growing ... deep inside, 
And there's no place ... to run and hide, 
No way out ... no where to turn,
In my heart ... the flame still burns.

Chorus ..... 

Oh there's no way out ... from a broken heart 
when the one you love ... tears your world apart, 
You knew your love ... didn't stand a chance, 
So you set her free ... to find a new romance. 

3rd Verse ..... 

Now she's gone ... and I'm alone, 
I know she'll never ... come back home, 
A bitter lesson ... I have had to learn, 
In my heart ... the flame still burns.

Musical Break .....

In my heart ... the flame still burns.
