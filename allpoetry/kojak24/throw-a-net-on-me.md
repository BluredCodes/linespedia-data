Throw a net on me boys! 
Throw a net on me! 
Run me naked down the street 
and throw a net on me! 

Smack me up side my head! 
Really ring my bell! 
Maybe you will break my neck 
and break my evil spell. 

Oh throw a net on me boys! 
Throw a net on me! 
Put me on a sailing ship 
and set me out to sea. 

Walk me up and down the plank, 
Play me some heavy metal, 
Put a rope around my neck 
and choke me till I'm gentle. 

Throw my corpse in the water 
I'll turn my soul into an otter, 
Letting the sharks munch on me 
until an eternal nymph I be 
swimming on and on 
in an eternal sea 
on and on endlessly. 
Oh throw a net on me boys! 
Throw a net on me!
