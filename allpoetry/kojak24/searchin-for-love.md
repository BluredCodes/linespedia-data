I

My foolish heart's - 
gotta lot to learn,
When it comes to lovin' -
I keep makin' wrong turns.

Chorus .....

Searchin' for love - in all the wrong places
Trying to choose - from a million faces,
Searchin' the bars - and the rendezvous,
Lookin' for someone - to make my dreams come true.

II

Yeah, it's booze and women -
wherever I go,
To the honky tonks -
or the girly shows.

Repeat Chorus .....

Yeah, searchin' for love - in all the wrong places
Trying to choose - from a million faces,
Searchin' the bars - and the rendezvous,
Lookin' for someone - to make my dreams come true.

Bridge .....

But I know - it will never be,
Cause one night stands - got the best of me,
And the only time - I ever take a chance,
Is to make out - in a back seat romance.
  
Musical Break ....

Repeat Chorus .....

Yeah, searchin' for love - in all the wrong places
Trying to choose - from a million faces,
Searchin' the bars - and the rendezvous,
Lookin' for someone - to make my dreams come true.

Tag .....

Searching for love
in all he wrong places,
Searching for love.
