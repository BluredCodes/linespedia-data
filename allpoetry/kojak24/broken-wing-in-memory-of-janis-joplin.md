I'm like a little bird with a broken wing
sitting in a tree longing to be free. But
no one will fly with me! My Poppa bird
left me and our babies in a lonely nest. Oh
God! .. please give my heart some rest from
this aching in my chest.

Oh, where did Poppa bird go? Did he go 
South for winter? He left one day and 
didn't come home for dinner. I'm so alone
with no one to fly with me, no one to fly
with me.

The babies cry and ask when Pappa bird is
coming home. What can I tell them? It's
goodby to Poppa bird. He won't be home
again. Yes, I'm like a little bird with a
broken wing sitting in a tree. There's ...
no one to fly with me
no one to fly with me
no one to fly with me
no one to fly with me
