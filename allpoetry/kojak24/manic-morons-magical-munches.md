It's revelation constipation en-sensation oval
upper rump under skirts... where you goin'
Oedipus Rex? Good queen Mommie is coming to get
you to get you ... I'm coming to get you again
and again...arise the spirit calls ... hear the
knocking in the head ... fool fool fool fool ...
smart heart or bart fart lives in the hole and
grows and grows until the smell goes up the nose
... we legs open wide with eyes entering the
canals journey ... wild ticket for sale ... where
we go the hornets are hot ... Who knows backward
bent? Un-zipped the President cries wide open as
Monica blossoms before his eyes I didn't have sex
with that ...but her mother. Help me out fools
the Oedipus is RISEN from the ashes ... where
there is gashes there is tittyredyelloworange -
nipples - lesssoftorhard ... living on the come
in the bummer San Francisco L.A. Hollywood
Hollywood? Hollywood!! I love you! I loved it!
I smoked it! You want some? Come and get it no
money needed... go on asshole..asshole..soft lap
dance with Jenny Atalia ...AustraliaEngland
USAMexico...everywhere... solidlovers munching
hair... ah you caught me didn't you? Monster of
hate//vomitspitcominghomeicecream//. Gaseous
oasis everywhere! Here comes the maker and the
shaker...see him there ... who he is is me we?
HE FELL FROM THE SKY ... Now say goodbyebyebye
byebyebye ... Don't cry!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
