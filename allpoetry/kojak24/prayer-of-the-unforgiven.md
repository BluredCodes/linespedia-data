---
title: "Prayer Of The Unforgiven"
writer: "kojak24"
slug: "prayer-of-the-unforgiven"

I've been alone for so long can your love do me
any harm? I know you don't really mean it and
your heart and soul aren't in it but after so
long alone-I'll take anything I can get-wretched
fool that I am I hope you understand my
situation. I'm still waiting at the station
reciting the prayer of the UNFORGIVEN. Our
tongues are long and hard. Can your love do me
any harm? I'm just a Honky Tonk Man do you
understand? Whiskey and women dressed in linen
no underwear to cover their hair. Carry me away
into a world of dreams and fields of play to run
and flush upon my face sweet nectar's grace.
Hooray! Angels surround my bed and urge me to
drink the wine of insanity. The prisons are full
of those with no love given - the ignorant - the
wretched faces staring from their cells ...Is
Momma coming to get her baby boy? Our insanity
means profanity to empty ears and blank faces for
the disgraced factions without traction to bind
them to the world. We belong to the underlings.
The love you can give me may mean just a little
to you but it's everything to me. Please keep me
going..I feel I'm drowning in an ocean of dung
filling my nostrils with lips sucking my mind and
Angels dancing around in my brain...Everything
seems so strange. This is so incoherent but so
apparent to those that breed in the night
moonlight cells madness howling inside their
souls...Where the hell is the train? Will I be
here forever? Wherever I am? Save a little love
for the unforgiven. Never to awake from these
nightly floods of semen uncertain emotions
grinding my soul into the ashes wrapping arms and
legs around my chest .. rubbing honey on my
breast? Where is the tree of life? Where can it
be tonight? Is it the source of my desire? Just
let me see it once before I'm left alone in this
quagmire of the dammed. Vinegar for my lips and
my crown of thorns. Can Jesus save the
UNFORGIVEN? Can he raise them from the DEAD?
