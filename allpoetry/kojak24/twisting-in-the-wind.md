..Twisting in the wind
.....how did it begin?
..twisting in the wind
.........was it a sin?

..The Devil walked in!
.I could see you there
....like his lady fair
.a ribbon in your hair

.................... I'm twisting in the wind
.................... I'm twisting in the wind

.......How did it end?
.....How did it begin?
....Winners never lose
......Losers never win

.................... I'm twisting in the wind
.................... I'm twisting in the wind

...how did you survive
..all his fucking lies
....a heart like yours
.....so sweet and pure

.................... I'm twisting in the wind
.................... I'm twisting in the wind

....so sadly, insecure
...unsure, lost, alone
..Marilyn can't you see
.........anyone but me

.................... I'm twisting in the wind
.................... I'm twisting in the wind

...let it be let it be
..........let it be me
.....harmony with thee
.....Oh can't you see?

.................... I'm twisting in the wind
.................... I'm twisting in the wind

.....if you let me go
..while I love you so
....I will die I know
.twisting in the wind

.................... I'm twisting in the wind
.................... I'm twisting in the wind

H E A R T S ........ twisting in the wind

My crazy - jealous heart loves you 
...........Don't lock me our of your life
and leave me ...... twisting in the wind.

...... and swinging  to the tune
..........of everything ventured
................ nothing gained,
..No more sunshine no more rain,
no more nights filled with pain. 
......... I die ......... NOW!!!
