Love letters from my heart
written on the sands of time,
Fill me with longing for you
and haunt my mind.

Flooding my soul with passion
as I whisper to the winds,
Oh darling, my darling
please come back again. 

Oh you'll never know
the tears I've cried,
Or the nights of torment
without you by my side.

For I cannot face
what life may bring,
Without you in my arms
to make my heart sing.

Your love is everything to me
eternal bliss, 
The greatest passion
I've ever known.

A forever orchid of love
the sweetest kiss,
You're my princess on a throne
please never leave me alone.

Oh darling, my darling
will you marry  me?
And sail our ship of love
across the seven seas?

Oh please make me happy
and fill me with pride,
Please tell me dear 
that you will be my bride.To my darling Sandie Angel. She is always in my heart and I love her so. May our love last throughtout eternity and our flame of love burn brightly forever. From her Guy Bill ....... love always. xoxoxoxox hugs and kisses.
