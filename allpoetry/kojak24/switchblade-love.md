I

I'm a pretty kitten can't you see? 
But something evil got hold of me! 
My switchblade love wants to set you free 
and send you off to your destiny. 

II

I'm a kitten a sweet little thing 
I will jump around and prance and sing, 
And then slit your throat when you giggle 
so I can watch you spin and wiggle. 

Chorus ..... 

Oh sweet little thing sweet little thing 
It's switchblade love in the early spring, 
If the cuts I make can make you sing 
will you buy me a wedding ring? 

III

Oh my switchblade love my switchblade love 
did you descend from Heaven above? 
Oh switchblade love little furry thing 
Can you make all of the birdies sing? 

Tag ..... 

Oh switchblade love switchblade love 
Is the Devil the one you love? 
You're a little kitten oh so sweet 
Will you  share your switchblade love? 
Your switchblade love 
Your switchblade love
