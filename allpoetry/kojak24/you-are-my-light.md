---
title: "You Are My Light"
writer: "kojak24"
slug: "you-are-my-light"
source: "AllPoetry"

Oh the light of your love 
shines brightly before me, 
I would follow you to Hell 
I would follow you unto death. 

My heart sings in your presence, 
Your world envelopes my soul, 
Nothing matters to me - BUT YOU! 
I would slay the world for your love! 

No challenge too great, 
No journey too far, 
No ocean too deep, 
You are my pathway to the stars, 

You are my guide, my essence, 
My heart's desire, you light my fire, 
My love's a burning flame, 
An Angel in flight, a song in the night. 

Every time you call my name 
I swoon in your presence, 
I have never known such love before, 
such joy, such need, such passion. 

My life is worthless without you, 
Nothing can change what I feel, 
Nothing can destroy my love, 
I am yours until the day I die. 

I will always be here for you, 
All things that I am or will be 
depend on your love, 
Oh my darling ..... 

!!! .. I LOVE YOU SO MUCH .. !!!
