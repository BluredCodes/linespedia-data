Puppet man..............
Just wanna be your puppet man...
manipulated/masticated/regurgitated/refrigerated
agitated/constipated..
pounding ... Devils hounding interaction
surrounding ... Defeating all evil factions
forging ... bursts of soulful traction
wrapping chains of lust between our legs
squeezing/hurting/arching/sucking
/attacking/biting/interacting
Puppet man ............. Puppet man
Puppet man ............. Puppet man 
Just gotta be your puppet man. 
Do you understand? rhythm in mind? 
river in time?::::::::::::sweet erectus?
nectar unexpected?:::::::::entry injected?
juices flowing?::::::::::::lustful infinity?
Sucking tongues? ...... Wild and rampant!!!
Rigidity? Proclivity? Afterbirth? Aftermath?
Halitosis? Spermatosis? Coagulation? Masturbation? Generation? Expectation?
Expectorations licking rejuvenations?
...........Just want to be 
your puppet man........................
Do you understand?understand?understand?understand? Alpha .. Omega .. to the end!!!
