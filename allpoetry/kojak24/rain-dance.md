Rain-man, rain-man do your dance,
Make us laugh take a chance,
Show us all your insides out,
Let your tears flow all about.

Reveal the secrets you possess,
Rub our shit upon your chest,
Matted crap between our ears,
Makes us laugh at you and jeer.

We so smart watch you writhe,
Your gyrations hypnotize,
Arms akimble in the air,
Jerk around everywhere,
No rain will fall -
you stupid fool,
Why don't you stop -
this silly dance?

Fuck off you jack-offs - 
its raining now,
Deep within this heart of mine,
Rivers are flowing into time,
As Satan sips on cherry wine,
while you asshole crap upon,
your dry righteousness.

Go to hell you motherfuckers,
You will be the ultimate suckers,
I'll keep doing my rain dance,
I'll be so wet -
and pure and cleansed,
While you assholes die,
From the surge of sludge,
That rushes down your throats,
And fills you up until you
Burst into a billion -
aeons of shit!
Dry, arid criticism is dead.
PURITY WILL SURVIVE!
RAIN-MAN IS ALIVE!
