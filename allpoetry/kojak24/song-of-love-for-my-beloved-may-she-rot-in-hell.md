Are you there my love?
(you stupid creature)
high in the air somewhere
(high on rope-a-dope}
trapped in space and time
(drinking cheap wine)
by the loneliness in my mind?
(I'll be fine)
Swirling round and round
(get off my table)
with another force
(or a horse of course)
a warrior like me
(bullshit is free)
or a gentle poet
(slice of his head)
and passive seas of dreams
and emotions haunting
my world are you there my love?
(floating out to sea)
Can you reach across endless
eternity to grasp these loving hands of mine?
(a crazy fool like me)
Whore of all creatures! Are you in season? 
Have I lost my reason? Do you really love me more?
Bitch of indecision are you talking treason?
Mount your steed and flee silver tongue orators
praise your seed! The madman cometh with
a backward step am I hip? Or have I slipped my chains.
..... I buried her beneath the floor .....
No nonsense here at my candy store. 
ALL HER CANDY IS MINE! 
I praise her name each morning
while I lick the floor and spin like a clown .....
around and around till I fly in the air to 
join her there somewhere in the sky on high
racing with time. Oh how I love her! 
She's always in this heart of mine! 
She's always unfaithful and one of a kind.
Oh how I love the dead! ...
buried beneath and under my bed.
My world is RED! Yes Sarah ...
I am VERY VERY weird!!
