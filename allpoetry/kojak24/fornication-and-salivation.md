title: "Fornication And Salivation"

Manic monks in deep contemplation
pray aloud for their own castration,
Libidinous grinds throughout the night
fill their minds with earthly delights,
Nubile nuns from mother superior
glide them into their magic interiors.

These injections feel so strange
but they can't escape the delicious pain,
Hands on their tools of intervention
They're ready for a nuns convention
Squeezed between virgin thighs so tight
rhyme and reason takes its flight.

Sounds of joy fill the air
lust abounds everywhere
They shout and holler 
and stomp their feet
just like unicorns in heat,
Screaming with magical precision
they expose their condition,
Until climaxing in unison
they fall upon the ground.

Now all is quite, all is still
satiation fills their souls with gloom,
It's so very quite in their lonely rooms,
Failure floods their souls 
They've broken a vow and the Spirit knows,
Daylight breaks ... a new day begins with
the chant of ............................
DOMINUS VOBISCUM ... DOMINUS VOBISCUM
DOMINUS VOBISCUM ... DOMINUS VOBISCUM
