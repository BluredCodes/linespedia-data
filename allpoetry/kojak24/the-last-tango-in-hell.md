Destroy my world! Ravage my heart! 

"Oh the light of your love shines brightly before me, 
I would follow you to Hell - I would follow you unto death." 

Destroy my world! 
.....Deny my lust! 
..........Wreck my dreams! 
.............Wash away my sand castles! 
...................Lady Patricia choose the method of my destruction! 

"My heart sings in your presence ... Your world envelopes my soul! 
Nothing matters to me - BUT YOU! I would slay the world for your love!" 

.....Whatever you do I will succumb! 
..........Your choice decides my fate! 
.............Name your poison! 
...............Forbid me access to your heart and I will die! 

"I will flee!" 

No challenge too great, 
No journey too far, 
No ocean too deep, 
You are my pathway to the stars. 

"I will hide!" 


You are my guide, my essence, 
My heart's desire, you light my fire, 
My love's a burning flame, 
An Angel in flight, a song in the night. 

"I will run! I will cry! I will fly! I will write songs of hate!" 

Every time you call my name 
I swoon in your presence, 
I have never known such love before, 
Such joy, such need, such passion. 

"Satan be my guide! Virgin blood surrounds me! 
Red roses on the cross! Devils live inside my mind 
and evil rots my brain! I am undone 
and fallen into a pit Will you pull me from 
my disaster? Or do you serve 
another master?" 

.....Did I love in vain? My life is worthless without you! 
..........Nothing can change what I feel, 
.............Nothing can destroy my love, 
...................I am yours until the day I die. 

Are you dancing in the rain? 

Oh Rainy day woman 
my leaves are brown 
and falling down 
my trunk is knotted and old 
the rain freezes my face 
Hope for me is gone 
Can I carry on? 
I couldn't be right 
for being wrong. 

All I wanted to do was shout my love to the stars 
and dance with you around the moon and howl my insanity! 
What else can a poor child do? 

I will always be here for you, 
All things that I am or will be 
depend on your love, 
Oh my darling Patricia ..... 

!!! .. I LOVE YOU SO MUCH .. !!! 

But I love in vain!!! I love insane! 
Storm Dancer overwhelmed me! 
I cannot bear this pain! 
Motherfuckemall

HAIL SATAN! MY LOVE WILL SURVIVE?
