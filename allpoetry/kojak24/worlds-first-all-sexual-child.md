Burning lips ... aching hips ... painted finger tips 
scratches so deep ... my blood flows down 'Morpheus' 
rows of transfiguration searching hard for my 
identification ... a trip so obvious ... because I 
know who I am ... a full-blooded woman and a natural 
man! Broad shouldered ... hairy chest ... a triple 'c' 
bra covers my breasts. So long, awkward and tall ... 
I bang my 'dingdong' on the wall. Purple eyes and 
painted cheeks stare at me from my mirror in the hall. 
A rose in my hair ... arms open wide ... I'm both bride 
and groom - man and woman - a changeless soul with mixed 
identity ... what the hell happened to my virginity? 
Hocus pocus ... I'm a 'crocus' (a magical flower so 
beautiful to see) my mouth is open ... my tongue is split 
and slit and both sides of my mouth are talkin' shit ... 
glamor queen and jelly bean I be ... I'm big and hard ... 
and mean, mean, mean ... but sometimes I dance and prance 
for everyone to see // my friends call me Imogene. Gosh, 
as Rick O'Leary ... I lost my cherry long ago to a guy 
called Joe and a girl named Mary ... a triple decker for 
my big pecker ... with both woman and man. Oh it felt so 
great having ALL ENDS TAKEN! ... I never got over those 
attractions .... such satisfaction for the man and woman 
that I be - a prick with masculine femininity. Yes, there's 
a sweet girlish side of me cause Mamma's a he and Daddy's a 
she - how they produced a happy freak like me is no mystery. 
Anyway, does it make a difference? Or you envious or 
delirious? Do you think all this bullshit is serious? 
Painted nails and ruby lips ... dressed in sandals on the 
Venice beach I walk among the squall and screech ... 
preacher man on roller skates shouting while he masturbates 
for me. How great the thrill for the both I be. Does He 
love me as she or me as he or both? Can I do HIS will? 
"Good golly Miss Molly" and Hallelujah, I'd love to do it 
to ya! Cause I'm the worlds first ALL SEXUAL child! Love 
both of me or maybe three like the Holy Trinity? Who can 
tell? Anyway it goes, I'm goin' mad ... the sacrificial 
lamb is dead, my transformation is complete. The blood 
flows freely along a river of love. King Oedipus Rex inside 
of me shares Mother's fulfillment ... as I resurrect Father 
with a RISE-UP magnificent to behold ... holding his pride 
in my hand ... I feel so grand ... I'm a metamorphosed woman 
and man. I'm all that I can be and I hope and pray this 
discourse hasn't been (margaritas ante porcos). Go with 
God brother and sister or brother/sister. So be it, for 
it is spoken ... you can claim my love for a token. 
And I ain't jokin'!
