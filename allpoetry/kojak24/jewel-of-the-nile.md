You are the jewel of the Nile, 
Magically intermingled with 
the voice of a thousand Pharoahs. 

The current of love flowing through you 
is cleansed by the starlight in your eyes. 
Lucky is the God ... 
who possesses your charms! 
Fortunate is he indeeed ... 
who flows inside of you, 
Building up to the roar ... 
of a thousand waterfalls, 
as he sinks into the ... 
pleasure of your arms. 

You are VENUS GODDESS OF LOVE 
APHRODITE IN EXTREME 

And he is CUPID 
Your ENCHANTER OF HEARTS 

May you grow and ... 
multipy in intensity, 
May the stars ... 
burst forth in your mind, 

As you and he spin in wild dizzy pleasure 
of anticipation and longing, 
And he drinks ... 
from your fountain of love, 
Fully satiating his lust,

While life forms together in one FINAL GIGANTIC BURST OF 
ENERGY *** and you become ONE! *** 

How lucky you two 
to be so much in love. 
May you and he prosper 
forever and share

A M A T O R Y   D A N C E  (Time To Tango My Love)
 
Murmuring incantations to the rising sun, they 
shared the wakeful moments ... Excitation 
Heightened by stars bursting forth in their minds ... 
Passion anointing expectant hearts with desire ... 
Mouth on breast nurtured self with atoms of 
vitality ... Fingers explored life's inner cavern ... 
They reeled in unison, drinking of each other ... 
Until spent by mutual intoxication, paused to 
regroup their senses ... Then, thighs trembling 
with rhythmic bursts of energy generated moans 
and cries ... While hunger's devouring tongue 
searched out secret wounds of of rejuvenation ... 
And bodies taut with spring like rigidity fused 
together ... As the sun settled beyond the 
horizon ... And the moon rose blood red before 
their eyes. ( amantium irae amoris integratio est) 
Angel Eyes!
