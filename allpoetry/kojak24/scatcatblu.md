title: "Scatcatblu"

Were I young 
and strong again
Starlight
of my Heart,
I would be your
forever Knight
and you the
Magic Princess
of my dreams.

We would dwell
in an enchanted
crystal forest,
Where music
would play to 
our hearts desire,
Until the noise of
a million violins
sounding in
perfect harmony,
Would ovewhelm our
enchanted world
destroying our
crystal forest
forever.

As we melded 
together,
Two rivers
of love,
Flowing as one
into a sea of
tranquility
on an endless
journey far away,
Where our love
would be
unending
and last
