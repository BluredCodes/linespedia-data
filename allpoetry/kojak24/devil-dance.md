Oh starlight starlight shining bright
within the devil's lair of light,
Luminous souls entranced by fright
We dance with the devil tonight.

Rhythmic twitching - breasts exposed
Satan trembles from this sight,
As we praise him with delight
and dance with the devil tonight.

BRIDGE

Oh devil dance! Oh devil dance!
Let's do the devil dance
Until we fall into a trance
As Satan eats our burning flesh
while we do the devil dance!

Shadows fall the light grows weaker,
Now darkness shrouds our evil keeper,
We shout his name and scream and cry,
But we're alone and want to die.

It's to late now there's no answer,
We turned our backs on the MASTER.
We cast our lot with the grim reaper,
Now he'll always be our evil keeper.

REPEAT BRIDGE 

Oh devil dance! Oh devil dance!
Let's do the devil dance
Until we fall into a trance
As Satan eats our burning flesh
while we do the devil dance!
