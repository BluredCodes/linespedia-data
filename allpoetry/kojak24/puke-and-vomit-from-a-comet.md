Puke and vomit from a comet
spills upon the heads 
of all those who crap 
their beds with enjoyment
from their latest criticism.
Criticize, criticize, criticize
better to masturbate
than munch on tiny
little fairy criticisms.

Look for content and meaning
not for bad construction.
Be sure you bring them up
..no matter how weak the poem..
before you tear them down.
A poem that breathes can
be corrected why discourage
with negative interjections
because you think
that you're perfection?
For those with a bombastic
education .. or mode of expression
.. confounding and confusing
others .. does not signify great
mental possession if meaning fails
with your regurgitations
you fail yourself
and your expectations.

So beware o' cynic your mind is
not photogenic but walks backward
drinking from the puke and vomit
from the speeding comet
to fast for you to understand
you born without
the necessary rhyme or reason
to be a poet for all seasons.
Better to drop your pretense
and get a little f****** common sense. 
Puke and vomit
puke and vomit
puke and vomit
drink this cup of puke and vomit.
My it cleanse your insides
and melt your awkward interpretations
If there is ability deep within
your chest .. you got to show it!
Right now your grandiose erudition
is making you a faker .. not a FAKIR.
You'd be better off as an undertaker.

But I know you can do better if you'll
just get your s*** together.
Even my little mind knows this.

I'm just waiting for the train
