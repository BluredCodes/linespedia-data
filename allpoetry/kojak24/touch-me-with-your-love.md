I

Touch me with your love, 
Make me whole inside, 
Touch me with your love, 
Please forget I lied. 

Chorus .....

Hold me close to you -  never let me go.
Touch me with your love - say you love me so,
Kiss me on my lips - oh so tenderly,
Wrap me in your dreams - and stay with me.
 
II

Please forgive what I've done,
By cheating with another one,
Thought I'd have a little fun,
And  made your teardrops run.

Chorus .....

Hold me close to you -  never let me go.
Touch me with your love - say you love me so,
Kiss me on my lips - oh so tenderly,
Wrap me in your dreams - and stay with me.
 
Bridge .....

I'm sorry for being unkind,
Hurting you time after time,
And how lucky I am to find,
You in these arms of mine.
