Trembling colors 
waving in the air 
fill my world
with wonder,
As I search
among the thorns
to find a
rose to love.

.....Moonlight shadows
.....follow me
.....everywhere I go,
.....Rivers flowing
.....in my heart
.....cleanse my soul.

. Swiftly moving
.. streams of light
...course through
....my veins
.....washing away
......impurities
.......in my mind.

And I'm free 
to love again
with passion
unknown before.

... In memory of dreams cast aside yesterday,
... Please play loves song upon the strings
....inside my heart.

........ Moonlight shadows
....... pass over me
...... and in
..... full passions
.... swoon
... I drink the
.. elixir of love
. from your lips.

While the ever changing tempo and rhythm
of our hearts beating in unison becomes
musical enchantment for our souls ...

and ... then ... we ... are ...  O N E !
