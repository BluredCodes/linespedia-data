title: "There's Hunger In Your Eyes!"

There's hunger in your eyes I've never seen before, 
And you're looking at me in a way I adore, 
My hearts an open book and I tremble with each look, 
With a passion so deep I want to run and hide. 

When you look at me the way you do, 
I'm aflame inside with desire for you, 
When it comes to your love I have no pride 
I just want to be there by your side. 


I want you with a need I've never known before, 
Every part of me aches to be explored, 
Though young in years I'm full of emotion, 
My desire for you is deeper than the ocean. 

I writhe in ecstasy at the stroke of your hands 
Then I reach for you and I feel you expand, 
Tears of joy flow freely - for now I am a woman - 
promise me you will always be my man.
