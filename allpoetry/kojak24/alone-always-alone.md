title: "Alone - Always Alone"

The path we take -
never ends or begins 
the first step is the last 
never ends or begins .......... 
can you understand? 
never ends or begins .......... 
together every day 
never ends or begins .......... 
yet ever alone /
ever alone /ever alone 
never ends or begins .......... 
someone always there 
never ends or begins .....,.... 
to interfere and cause us woe 
always the same .......... 
what we know now is nothing 
always the same .......... 
same things every day
/every day /every day 
always the same .......... 
your love was new and 
always the same .......... 
now it's old 
where did it go ?????????? 
and how did it change 
where did it go ?????????? 
was it the rain
/the stars /the oceans 
where did it go ?????????? 
what caused the outward flow 
where did it go ?????????? 
where everything is endless 
yet the same .......... 
can you explain 
yet the same .......... 
the rain /moon /sun 
/stars /hearts /flowers /trees? 
yet the same .......... 
what answer do you bear 
yet the same .......... 
standing there alone 
always alone - always alone
- always alone no underwear
/hair /lips /eyes /teeth 
always alone - always
alone - always alone 
all gone! never really there 
always alone - always
alone - always alone 
Only love /death 
/illusion /confusion 
once here and now there 
once close and now gone 
what song do you sing 
is it sad /joyful 
/woeful /aching /hurting? 
help I drown in a
sea of nowhere to be seen 
emotions linger -
yet nothing is felt 
dead /lost /cold
/empty /alone 
lust /hunger /pain /rain
/Spain /Egypt /mummies
/Nile /passion 
all the same!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 
alone-alone-alone 
where you ever here 
really here???????? 
retro me satana
