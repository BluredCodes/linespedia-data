Cotton candy, ribbons in my hair, riding the pony 
at the fair, torture me and wrap me in a cryptic 
snare, drag me to your lair and screw me in my ear 
children dance around the carousel Angels sing and 
wish them well, the Devil laughs at all this 
simple crap...he don't have to take this rap...oh 
golly I see Jesus there in control a thousand 
fishes for all to feed water into wine the lame 
walk and the blind see oh sweet Jesus saving me 
... twist and dangle for many moons passion maker 
don't hesitate sit on my face till the crotch is 
eaten away...sacrifice and blood sacrifice flesh 
and blood twist my arm and break my leg make me 
feel like a foxy hag ... what a drag for a hag 
in drag..sack-cloth and ashes sunshine and shadows 
freight train to Seattle hot black shinola maggots 
squirming under glasses vogons show vibrant asses 
virgins with suction applied fulfill the promises 
of the bride..."Yeah, your Momma don't dance and 
your Daddy don't rock and roll," Mother lied and 
Father cried cause Mother humped Reverend McBride. 
All the babies are so happy cause the mothers take 
care..little cherubs all ... so well protected 
from evil et-Al..Give me some Hadacol! run to 
the sun there's when the come has run drained 
into my anus red upset by Captain Kidd ... uga 
uga boga loga talkin' the dozens with the 
crippled cruisers ridin' their crutches and 
frozen satellites out of sight and I should cry 
bye and bye oh hell is it going well? who knows 
true love comes into play everyday come what may 
I'm here for you and whatever you choose will be 
my ruse align me straight ahead send an army to 
my bed let them twist my head free will I be 
eternity fraternity mixed audience and everything 
else blood on the moon phantoms cry with banshee 
finality what matters if atoms scatter bodies in 
the air everywhere the penitent penis named Enis 
stood at rigid attention at the whores convention 
satiation filled the air! I'm in Heaven tonight!
