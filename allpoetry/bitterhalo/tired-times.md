Dreamt of a time where walls were sewn to bodies 
Left the light on in the hallway to see where i was going
The times i died, the times i cried, the times you sighed...

Sleep with your hope held tight
Time will only tell 
Why you were left here all alone...
The future holds no bounds...

You crept up the sidewalk with your head held low
The sun knew no words to speak...
it's the crash, it's the cry, it's the will to survive...

I slept with the hope you gave me
I left open the door to let you in
Time will tell who you really are
but for now i'll...
