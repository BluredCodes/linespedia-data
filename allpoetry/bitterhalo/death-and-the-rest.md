title: "Death and The Rest"
writer: "bitterhalo"

I remember the days you were so good to me
I remember those times you let me be
I remember when you walked away
Turned around and said to me

"Death, the rest is history
Love, the center of pain
Fear, the only way to live
Life, what's the point?"

I remember you wanted to leave
I remember you couldn't take anymore
I remember the sidewalk that you fell upon
On your knees you said to me

"Lie, you always did
Truth, you'll never know
Sin, the only thing you showed
Shun, that which is life..."
