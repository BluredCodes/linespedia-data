Moonlight shines down the path we used to walk
you left me here, cold and wilted
i was hoping to amend the mistakes i made
but when i looked up it was too late...

i've had a hard time with you

The sun didn't shine when you called my name
we could pretend it was alright
we'll be alright...

i've had a hard time with you...
we could pretend
that everything is alright
