title: "And I Stand Rejected"

I read all the books
I looked through all the pamphlets
Nothing looked pleasing so i stood here waiting
Left at 2 on the bus 
Time flew as the wheels turned
Lost in memories of what you could be
Lost in forever, the meaning was gone...
Just one more time
Around the block
The hand on my watch clicks 1
My body starts to ache
Staring at the sky
Time stood by
And still i tried
It's getting harder now
Each time i cry
The tears of misery have left my eye...
