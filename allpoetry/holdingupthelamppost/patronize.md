Yes, thank you.  Shut up.
We really don't need to hear it.
If everybody has a voice,
And something important to say,
Then may your God raise his hand,
And wave his finger defiantly,
Removing the filthy remains of your
Absurd reason,
And destroying that very voice
You can't seem to stop using.
Patience is quickly fading, pal.
I don't have much left.
I only patronize you because
You fucking ask for it.
