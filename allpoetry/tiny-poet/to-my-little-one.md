---
title: "to my little one"
writer: "tiny poet"
slug: "to-my-little-one"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56308-to-my-little-one-by-tiny-poet"
---

for five short weeks
you grew inside
im going to be a mommy
i told everyone with pride
i felt nothing but happiness
each and every day
until the day came 
when you just couldnt stay
your life on this earth 
isnt going to be
you just werent meant 
to grow inside me
but i have to find hope
that there will be another
and i will be so happy 
to be a new babys mother
but you my little one
i wont ever forget
the happiness you brought me
is something i dont regret
may your little soul find peace,
and may you be glad
to know its you
i wish i could've had
