I'm on the outside
unable to scream
watching your choices
like a slow-motion car crash
wondering will you swerve
or will you fly
through the windshield
and smear our future
across his cruel white-line smile
I wish I was in the car 
to grab the wheel
shout a warning
but you are alone
driving your own life
all I can do is watch
open mouthed with horror
as time slows and slows
until I see you through a jello haze
praying that you liveurgh.  who knows.
