Down at the dock
I said to Jock,
who was drinking some hock,
please dont mock,
but I think I`m in shock
for in my sock
I`ve  found a rock.
So I gave it a knock
but it bounced off the clock
right into his cock!

(and now he is in shock)


A girl called Jan
had baked a flan
inside a pan
to give to a man
whose name was Stan
as she wanted his van.
But along came Dan
and scuppered her plan
and stole it and ran.my brain hurts now.
