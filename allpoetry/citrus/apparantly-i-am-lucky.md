---
title: "Apparantly, I am lucky"
writer: "Citrus"

Apparantly, I`m lucky,
he chose me to make his tea,
As only I will know the way,
a nutricious meal should be

Apparantly, I`m  lucky,
he needs me to tidy his room.
As only I know how to clean
and dust this teenage tomb.

Apparantly I`m lucky,
he wants me to wash his clothes,
And put them all away again
as I know where it goes.

Apparantly I`m lucky,
that his mates all come around,
that I am not allowed to speak
or even make a sound

But, yes I did feel lucky
when I heard him tell his date
Come on and in and meet my mum
You will love her, she`s just great.yea I know, I know......hook line and sinker.
