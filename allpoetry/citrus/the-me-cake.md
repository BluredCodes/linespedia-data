writer: "Citrus"

Take a pinch of a whole load of nonsence,
Add a touch of the mystic and spice,
Mix a handful of laughter and giggles
with some caring and truthful advice.

Now a spoonful of childish and silly
a mind thats occasionally bright
and whisk it together with friendship 
with a helping of sexual delight

Now beat all together with vagueness
set in a bad example all night
Then sprinkle the top with some mix ups
and you`ll find you have made me just right.thats me in a nutshell.....oh I mean a cake.
