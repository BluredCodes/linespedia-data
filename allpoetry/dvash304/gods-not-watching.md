Quickly, while the sun sets, make your drastic change
Time to let the world know you're done with all the pain
Never wanted anything more precious than your mind
But when that's the tortured part, you tend to miss the signs
Never want to be in this position ever again
Sometimes it's hard to understand just what it is He sends
Maybe it wont matter when you aren't in control
But maybe that's when decisions take their hardest toll

     Oh...
     Quickly, while God's not watching
     Make it, change yourself
     Oh...
     Quickly! While God's not watching
     Do it, you've been waiting for so long

Quickly while your mind sets, change you face inside
Decide where it is that you always put your pride
Never wanted anything to do with all those lies
Even still you know that you will keep all your ties...

***

Went down to my various friends' places
Watched the shocked looks on their faces
Told them where i wanted my life to go
They all agreed that they would show (me)

     Quickly!
     Quickly!
