I felt safe.

But that was back then



Let me get to the point i'm trying to make now.

Let me get to where i can point out exactly how

You've alienated me this time and why

I don't ever again want to even try

To be you friend

No, never again



Let me get to when you decided to be God

Let me explain why i've been living a facade

You've never even listened to anyone but you

And now you can't understand how all the hatred grew

Against you now

You before the plow



     I don't even need to wind it up that far

     Everyone sees the pretty little box without scar

     But noone sees what it holds inside

     The ugly face that you try to hide



Let me show you why everyone claims no association

Let me explain exactly how you recieved revokation

Maybe if you would try not to be so controlling

Maybe if you would try to listen when the bell's tolling

For your social demise

See why she cries



     I don't even need to wind it up that far

     Everyone sees the pretty little box without scar

     But noone sees what it holds inside

     The ugly face that you try to hide



Wind and wind and wind and wind and wind and wind

Until the ugly face is all that we see.
