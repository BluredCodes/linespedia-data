I'm sick
Been a dick
Been kicked in the face more times than i'm alotted
Been tattooed with and then cut along the lines that were dotted
They never respected me as even just a living being
Thier sick love for something more obscene...
What, i'm just supposed to sit and take it in the ass?
You've raped everything and destroyed who i was in the past

I'm sick of it
Been a dick toward it
I'm not going to sit by while my so-called "friends" attack
They expect me to do nothing as they force me to their track
They tried to break me with their twisted "morals" and their style
I persisted. They'll never capture me, i'll never be just another tile
In the floor, a uniformally sound work of "art"
I'm not going to be just another part

I'm sick of it changing me
Been a dick toward it "saving" me
I'm not going to just sit here and accept your "i'm sorries"
Digging into my soul like miners in the rock quarries
Why's it ok to kick me in the head but i can't do it to you?
Who the fuck died and made you God? Why do you control the zoo?
If i could snap your neck you know that i'd do it
If i could have my revenge you know you'd never live through it

I'm sick of it changing me and commanding me
Been a dick toward it "saving" me and commending me
You're gonna get fucked one of these days
Eventually it's gonna bite you in the ass but will it even faze
You? Will you even notice when the world backs up against you?
Will you even notice when you can't get through
The pain, the torture, the insidious acidic seeping inside?
How will i become your karma? Hmm...i still have to decide

I'm sick.
Been a dick.
You called me gay
How could you say
That, since between the two of us
You're the guy that likes boys, trust
Me on this: everybody already knows.
Now i wonder what this shows?

Go fuck yourselfi'm so sick of it.
