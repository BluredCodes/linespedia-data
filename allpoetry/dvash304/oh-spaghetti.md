Ever lie bleeding in the bed of an ex-lover?
Did you ever lose your mind when you discover
The true nature of your own shadowy mentality?
Have you felt the urge to plead insanity
After the murder of a former friend and traitor?
Did you want to be the first to piss on the ashes later?
Have you ever tried to run, bur forgot not to look back?
Do you still feel like you're mind's not intact?

     I do. Yeah. I do.
     I could never dig out
     I did. Yeah. I have.
     I can't find my way back.

Has it ever all built up so high the pile would shake?
Then it all comes down one day as you awake
Like your problems stack them selves the worst way
So that everything an confront you on the very same day

It makes me crazy.
Oh spaghetti!
Oh spaghetti!
