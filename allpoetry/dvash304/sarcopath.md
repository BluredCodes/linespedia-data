My body aches!

     I feel evil

     My heart is raw!

     I feel evil

     My world is gone!

     I feel evil



Call to action the dread zombies of war

This time we'll show them the floor

The zombie master invades us again

There is no peace in this world of sin

Now that we're off to the battle of hate

I guess we didn't ever clear that slate

Everybody needs some comfort these times

There is no comfort, only hate that binds

It's in full swing this time, you will die

Come pitch in and fight, no time to be shy

Let's show these demons what we have in store

Call to action the dread zombies of war



     My body aches!

     I feel evil

     My heart is raw!

     I feel evil

     My world is gone!

     I feel evil



This always comes off no more than a gamble

Our world is destroyed and all lays in shambles

How can i look you in the eye again, it's my fault

I can't believe this horrible relentless assault

It can't ever be the same after all of this

Lost articles of a life we may never miss

It's an era where crime kills crime kills crime

What has happened this horrible time?

We're already turning to evil, what's to fight for?

Call to action the dread zombies of war

The weak and stupid lose to the ample

This always comes off no more than a gamble



My heart is raw!

I am evil.



I am evil

I am evil
