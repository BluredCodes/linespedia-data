Denser than anyone else i know.
Just keep on going on and on with the flow
You lost touch with your honor far in the past
I can't even remember the last
Time i felt like i could trust you with the littlest secret
I know that you would always just repeat it
To think you used to be the best i knew
But somewhere along the line you threw
Everything away.
Threw it away.

When you abandoned your friends to get some ass
To find anything with a hole you could get in fast
It became the only thing you even cared about
No matter what it could amount (to)
You've lost your friends and you don't care how
So you've made some new relationships now.
Good for you. Hey man, how's your boyfriend?
It's too bad we all hate him too, it's such a fitting end
For you.
For...you.when i wrote this, i didnt really get the point across that i was trying to in the first place. its about this guy who used to be just about my best friend, but then he ended up betraying all of his friends just to get some ass. then he ended up losing his virginity to a guy, and he's not even gay. in fact, he's fucked 3 different guys and no girls, and he still says he's straight. but now he's been elected into a national honor society, and i think i may have to shoot him.
