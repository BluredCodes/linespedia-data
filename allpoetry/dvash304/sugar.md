Sweet like the rose, but hardened from life
Out in the world, on the edge of the knife
Sweet like the sun, and just as on fire
Awakened by violence, which we never tire
Sweet little girl, do you like what you see here?
Do you see life and love, or constant fear?

     Blackened feelings always growing faster
     You're never freed from the chain of greed
     A man is dead and there is only laughter
     But who will save us from our planted seed?

Sweet like the rose, but hating the thought
Never understanding just what you've sought
Sweet like the sun, burning for so many years
But what can make us realize our own fears?
Sweet little girl, do you like what we're doing?
Blow up the world and see who now's suing

     Blackened feelings always growing faster
     You're never freed from the chain of greed
     A man is dead and there is only laughter
     But who will save us from our planted seed?

Sweet like the rose which brings us hope
But what is hope in the noose of rope?
Sweet like the sun which lets us live
Live?...Ha! You may be the next to give
My sweet little girl, will you ever be happy
Now that everybody wants to come and stab me?

     Blackened feelings always growing faster
     You're never freed from the chain of greed
     A man is dead and there is only laughter
     But who will save us from our planted seed?

          Sweet like the rose
          Strike that deathly pose
          Sweet like the sun
          Now you'd better run
          Sweet little girl
          Killed for her pearlsway back in the day (2000 or so),
