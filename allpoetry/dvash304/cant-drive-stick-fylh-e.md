...And now you think you're happy.

You think that your new sappy

Pathetic life is so perfect

And you don't care what it infects

Or inflicts upon the rest of us

But, i'm so sorry to bust

Your fucking little bubble

Or maybe i'm not, you're not worth the trouble



You're such a hypocrite

You cloud yourself with bullshit

You blame everyone else

And say we brought it on ourselves

When you're the only one

Who even cares what's done

You two talk big about running away

But all you do is just stay.



Why do you think anyone would care?

Everyone knows that you wouldn't dare

But we all want you to go

Come on, by now you must know

If you think you're so mature

Why can't you even endure?

You tell us all we're little kids

But just look at what you did.



I believe it's time for you to go.

What ever will staying show?
