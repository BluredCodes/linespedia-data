Dear me:



It's you again. Just wanted to remind you that you're fucked up.

You've got everything you ever wanted and never even sucked up

It didn't take anything to get to where you find yourself now

But you want to throw it all away, you're ready for the final bow

Ready to make a quick exit and leave all those you've loved behind

Come on, man, what are you, fucking blind?!

How can't you see what's laid out in front of you as good?

Don't you know how sacred the ground is where you've stood?



You never even gave  yourself a chance at this.

All you could see is the cloudy evils that got you so pissed

I never knew that you would turn out this way

What happened to me? Where, now, can i stay?

I'm that happy little kid inside of you, been here all along

I was here when you wrote your very first song

I'm all that you were ever supposed to become

But you've decided that you're better off being dumb.



You knew that only you could erase me, and you did it

You arose when someone was embarrased of me and hid it

Just swept me under the fucking rug, now didn't you?

Everything you stood for just fucking blew.

Now you've attained what you were created to become

You don't realize how your brain has become numb.

All you think about is what you could've done differently

Your brain just seems to battle you incessantly.



Now i want to come back.



Sincerely,



     -You
