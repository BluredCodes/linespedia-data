How do i love you?
Let me count the ways
What a cliche beginning
To such an important display.
But, i suppose
I'll make you a list anyway
Just so that you remember
That i'm thinking of you today.

For one thing,
I love your integrity
Even though
It led to all you said to me
I admire your resolve
And you complete versitility
Although it messed me up
And meant ignoring your sensibility

I love how you love me
And how you show it by causing pain
I love how your torment me
When i never tried to do the same
It's always great
When you intentionally make me seem lame
And how you were concerned
About how leaving me would make me insane.

Yeah, right.January 2002,
