Could've been better but you threw it away
She loved you but knew she couldn't stay
Shooting your mouth off like it's a gun
When this has all dried up and it's no fun
Thinking you can do what you want to her
But now she's gone, you're no longer together

Drawing the leash where it should not be
What she can't do is what you can't see
Snapping at her like a dog you control
Now she'll always regret that kiss she stole
Now the filter's gone bad and you're the dead one
