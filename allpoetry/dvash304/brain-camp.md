The wonderful people who run the farm
Keep it in check with their own long arm
Killing my person with their single style
Making me be like them, rotting on a pile
Serving me crap like their twisted ways
Assimilating every one who stays
Making us be just one single being
Making us be the same and agreeing

The wonderful people who run the farm
They hit me in the face with their long arm
Conformity keeping us with their style
Making everything into a big poopy pile
Everyone be the same as their ways
Everyone needs to understand what stays
All of us need to be one single being
Surely you must all be agreeing...?

          They washed it up
          And took it for theirs
          They wouldn't let me be
          Kuz everyone stops and stares....

               Individual.

The wonderful people who screw with my head
They make me be them so i wish i was dead
But i don't want to conform and be one of them
I want to be me, i am my own man
They washed my brain and sent it to camp
Shine through both ears the freaking lamp
My head is a vaccuum of space and time
They want me to look the same in a line

          They washed it up
          And took it for theirs
          They wouldn't let me be
          Kuz everyone stops and stares....

               Individual.

                         The wonderful people who screwed me up.
                         The wonderful people who screwed me up
                         The wonderful people who screwed me up.
                         The wonderful people who screwed me up!!

          They washed it up
          And took it for theirs
          They wouldn't let me be
          Kuz everyone stops and stares....

               Individual.

The wonderful people who run the farm
And want to lock me up in the barn
Funny farm for all us freaks
Funny farm where nobody speaks
I'm not like you in any way at all
You can't control me i'm an individual
I'm not your slave i'm not working for you
I'm just myself and i'll be like that all the way through

          They washed it up
          And took it for theirs
          They wouldn't let me be
          Kuz everyone stops and stares....

               Individual.

                         The wonderful people.
                         The wonderful people.
                         The wonderful people.
                         The wonderful people.

The wonderful people who run from the farm
And managed to escape from their huge arm.

               Individual.
