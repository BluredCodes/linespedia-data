U234

Silently gliding
The pregnant Behemoth moves
Where no light penetrates

Carrying Japan’s final hope
In her belly
Fathered from the shores of Germany

Bringing

The genetic code
For a monstrous offspring
To unleash on the world

And
The fire of the sun

Far across the Atlantic
Enigma hears the words
Watches and waits

As the hope of Germany perishes
With a bullet through his brain
The call goes forth across the seas

Surrender all submarines….
Long live Germany
Then silence

U234 surfaces
To meet the USS Sutton
And labors forth her precious cargo

In the deserts of New Mexico
Critical mass is reached
And Armageddon is born

Swaddled in death’s shroud
She finally reaches her mother Japan
By the wings of the Enola Gay

And drops
Into the unsuspecting arms
Of Hiroshima and NagasakiScary how close WE came to being the ones to receive her!  It made me think.
