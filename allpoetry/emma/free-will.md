Free Will

If Sin were a beautiful woman
With hell hounds from her womb
And scaly coils that circle around
Like a cold reptilian tomb

Would you partake of the ashes
From her fork-tongued mouth that fall
And rape her against the gates of hell
In the guise of Satan’s pall?

If Death were the offspring created
From the nefarious tryst you engaged
Causing hell’s gates to swing open
And vengeance the battle now waged

Would you still find her so tempting
If you knew that your debt would be paid
By creating a bridge to your brothers
And spreading Death’s touch like a plague?

If Satan should whisper so softly
To your darkest desires and laud
The rebellion that burns in you deeply
To forge yourself into a god

Would you take of the fruit that he offers
And believe that the power is yours
In spite that the apple is rotted
Like flesh covered in gangrenous sores?

Free will is a gift from the heavens
And Satan permitted to lie
By God’s Holy providence only
And the omniscient gaze of His eye

The unholy trinity’s power
Was conquered with blood as the cost
Sin still is a beautiful woman
But Christ regained paradise lostThoughts after reading Milton's "Paradise Lost."  It was just running through my head.
