Staring...
Reaching...
Grasping...
for something just out of my reach

I know you're there
I can't see your face
but you haunt my dreams
unceasingly...

Two bright lights
stare out from the abyss
give light to a world
otherwise too dark.

Stumbling...
Shaking...
Crying...
For that glimmer in the dark.Imagine yourself in a dark room...you keep sighting this flickering light in the distance, and keep reaching for it...but just as you get there, it disapeers.
