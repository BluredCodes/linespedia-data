---
title: "Unwillful transformation"
writer: "Tegs"

She would be transformed into someone else
for there were those who would never except her
as she was.
She hides herself from those she is closest to
Hiding behind a spell and a mask, to be accepted
not sure that it's worth it.
Her smiles are pretense, her words are not meant
but if she every truely devulged who she was
she would be shot down.
Forever doomed to grace the earth alone; only her true love,
to accept her reality, for if he rejects her,
she will turn to foam. 
And so this girl, not only just a girl
is doomed to dance on shards of glass forever
until she again discovers,
the forgiving ocean.
