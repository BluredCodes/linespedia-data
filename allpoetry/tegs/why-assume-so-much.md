---
title: "Why Assume So Much?"
writer: "Tegs"
slug: "why-assume-so-much"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60594-Why-Assume-So-Much--by-Tegs"
---

Why do you assume 
that we all wear red white and blue?
why do you assume
that we all love your god?
why do you assume
that it snows here at Christmas?
why do you assume
that we have a summer break?
why do you assume
that I know where CA TX or AK is?
why do you assume
that I will rearrange for you?
why do you assume
that you're any better than I?
