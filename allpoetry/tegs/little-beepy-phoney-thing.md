---
title: "Little beepy phoney thing"
writer: "Tegs"

The innocently beeping
starts my heart crazily leaping
for doubts apon me had been heaping
But now I know it's all ok.

It's funny how an object so tiny
with buttons on it so damn shiny
can stop me from being so whiney
And make me smile such a big smile.

But then it's not really this thing here
that kills off all of that fe-ar
I wish you were a bit more near
Cos right now I'm going insane!
