title: "Crushed"
writer: "Tegs"

Cold foamy breeze brushing my cheek
freezing the tears
and smothering the scream.
A wave of darkness crashing down
pushes me to the bottom,
crushing out all breath.
A flicker in the distance
too far to reach,
taunts and teases, and just makes me sure
that there's no way
I'll ever get out.
That I'm doomed to lay on this sea bed
and suffocate 
agonisingly
slowly, 
alone.
