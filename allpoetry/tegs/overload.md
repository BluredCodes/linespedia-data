---
title: "Overload"
writer: "Tegs"

Too many sounds coming out of one 
mouth
Too many tears falling from those
eyes
Too many strange smells for one single
nose
Too much screaming for my poor virgin
ears

The senses on overload
the feeling too much
Brought out all my emotions
with one single touch.

Burning out of control
down a road way too long
when these others shut up
then you might hear my song.

Softly
Floating
Distantly

Further and further
away from my heart
Driving and twisting
with sadistic art

Bleeding and crying
and stabbing within
It's me thats inflicting
the pain that I'm in

Neverending
no sense making
tearing
agonising
stupidly
falling.Strange I know, with many differing styles, but hey, why not experiment!
