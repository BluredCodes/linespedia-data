title: "If I had wings..."

If I had wings
I would fly to you
surround you in my arms
and let you know I love you.

I may tell you this
via some electronic annoyance
but it's never as good
as being followed my a kiss.

I want to be able
to fly to you anytime
when I am so alone
and need your comfort

Or if I just want to share
a few moments with you
to enjoy your presence
and feel loved again.

But I surpose if I had wings
they would soon become useless
because i'd end up never leaving
once I'd found you again.
