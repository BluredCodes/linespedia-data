title: "Howling"

The wind from your breath
is eating away at my face
the constant screaming and taunting
is building instensity,
and ferocity.
It's peeling back my skin
like the sand from the beach
after too many tides.
Please turn down the volume,
the amps or the content.
Because my face is peeling
my mask is about to come off.
And you won't be able to handle, 
what's underneath.
