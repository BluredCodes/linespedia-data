---
title: "The Music"
writer: "Tegs"
slug: "the-music"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60495-The-Music-by-Tegs"
---

Hypocritical melodies
the sweet voices sting and repulse me
Do they believe the shit they say?
Or is it just for the money?
That one, four letter word
repeated over and over
offends me deeply.
And I wonder, if they mean it?
Are the hard-rockers really so tough
Or just trying to hide their fragility?
Are the teeny-boppers so shallow
or so they hate their songs, as much as I?
Do they dress up like sluts because they want to
or is it just to attract their disinterested auidence?
Are the dance-music writers as boring as the music,
or just running out of inspiration?
The music puzzles me.
The computerised clips irritate me.
The lack of individuality,
stretches my patience.
And yet I just have to keep listening...
