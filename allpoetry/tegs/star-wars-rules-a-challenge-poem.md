Zoom through hyperspace 
in a rusty pile of junk 
shoot some stormtroopers 
there's ewoks in that trunk! 

Fight some bad sith lords 
Shoot them troopers down 
bring gifts to old Jabba 
Fett looks like a clown 

Oh, 
Star Wars rules 
star wars rules 
no better show is there 
Cos; 
Han Solos hot 
and Lukie rocks 
and Leias got mad hair 
Oh, 
Star Wars rules 
star wars rules 
no better show is there 
Cos; 
Yoda's cool 
and Chewies huge 
and vaders quite a scare. 

But don't forget the past 
Amidala's quite a chick 
Annie was so small 
And Jar Jar was so sick 

ObiWan used to be hot 
And Qui Gon he got snapped 
Palpatine was almost good 
And R2 he got zapped 

Oh, 
Star wars rules 
star wars rules 
no better show is there 
Cos; 
Han Solos hot 
and Lukie rocks 
and leias got mad hair 
Oh, 
Star Wars rules 
Star wars rules 
no better show is there 
Cos; 
yodas cool 
and chewies huge 
and vaders quite a scare!Think jingle bells...
