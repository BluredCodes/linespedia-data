---
title: "Lucky Me"
writer: "Tegs"
slug: "lucky-me"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55891-Lucky-Me-by-Tegs"
---

Floating high on a cloud of strawberry frosting
raspberry kisses and chocolate tears,
I find myself in your arms 
and find myself smiling.

This is the home I have dreamed of long
absent of painful rejections, misunderstandings
like a knife through my skull
but these things never happen with you.

You are my rainbow, that shines across the oilspill
The twinkelling light in this suffocating dark
You are the only thing that holds me to sanity
my dream of a future, full of love.

So the smile you see, that tugs at my cheek
Is not because I'm laughing at you,
but I am laughing at the rest of the world
because they have no idea, how lucky I am.
