Hi! Guess what I'm Australian.
(No, I'm sorry that's not near Germany)
Hey guess what? It was 30 degrees here today
(No actually thats quite warm)
I'm quite an eloquant speaker actually
(No I don't talk like crocodile-dundee)
I have a pet that I'm quite fond of
(No, he's not a kangaroo that I ride on)
I've just started my autumn break at school
(Yes I go, and no it's not called fall)
It doesn't snow here at Christmas
(So I suppose there's no Santa in a sleigh)
So because I don't live with the bright lights
of hollywood shining out of my ass,
You think I'm inferior...
hmm...
