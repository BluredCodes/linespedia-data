title: "All the Senses (Kiss)"

Caramel Sweet, liquor smooth
sliding down my throat
Playing with the senses
softly caressing the insides of my veins
feather light, desending apon me
covering my body slowly
silken tickling within my ears
soft laughter, clear and sweet.
Warm and rich, satisfyingly full
Comforting darkness, safe and secure
nothing to see, nothing to be scared of
all-emcompassing, all-enveloping
running down my spine like water droplets
to my fingertips and beyond.
