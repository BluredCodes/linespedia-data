---
title: "White Page"
writer: "Tegs"
slug: "white-page"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54403-White-Page-by-Tegs"
---

You scrawl your words in pink and gold
to try and add some life to your misery
To try and find a way to glean some wisdom
or see the truth that you have blinded yourself from.
You scribble like mad trying to fill up the page
to hide the white-hole, the emptiness
that is nothing but a mirror of your mind.
A sinkhole of nothing, nothing coming in,
or going out.
The blank page mocks you, it's truth confounds you
no matter how many pretty swirls you try and cover it with
some white will always show through, 
And remind you of your empty core.
The words, numbers and random scribbles do nothing
to hide the futility of your situation
or the lack of substance in your thoughts
because they are just as empty,
as that long white page.
