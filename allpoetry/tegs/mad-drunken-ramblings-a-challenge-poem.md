Larsh Gnight wash long 
larsh gnight wash tuff 
I wash testesd an-d tolied 
and lotso odder shtuff 

Ii-started in da pub 
Eiyes had abit ta dwink 
but den me wife she come and say 
ya godda fix da sink 

So oof home Eiyes stumbled 
Ta twy and fiz da pipes 
but den some space men came from space! 
Wid guns oof diff-rent types 

Day took me up indo da ship 
and asked 'where is da childin' 
We only go 2 kids round ere 
fat bob and skinny lin 

So oof dey went dey took da kids 
my wife woz preety mad 
But byes zis time the boozy had gotten 
bit much fors zis young lad 

Zo run I did to da loo 
di only one in town 
but di kew, it woz too lomg 
zeer 12 peeple all a-wound 

Ii woz gedding werrse 
I really nneeded to go do dat damn loo 
zo I shoulder barged em all 
and got da do me poo. 

Wha was de point oh dis story 
I is not reawwy shooore. 
But enyway I hopes zis let you know 
sumting dat not make yoo snore.
