title: "Love's  Sweet  Melody"

Loves sweet melody
Sung from the heart
As souls blend as one
Vowing never to part.

Eyes shine with love
As you gaze into mine
Pulses race with passion
As hearts combine.

A touch a kiss
Magic unleashed
Loves known before
From this moment cease.

Love ever eternal
This vow we take
Untill we cease to be
This promise we make.
