You bailed out when they needed you
Leaving the broken pieces to me
To try to put together
How is it you couldn't see?
Surly you knew the distruction
Your death would leave down here
Didn't the broken hearts worry you
Or was you just didn't care?
Can you see them now from where you are
Their lives one by one in tatters
Each living with guilt and blame
Though to you would it rally matter?
I've tried my best to no avail
For damage irriversable you did that day
You had the choice to stay or go
You alone had the final say,
When oneday you meet with them again
Please have the courage not to hide
The reasons for your actions
Why over them you chose suicide.

For I can't give them the answers.
