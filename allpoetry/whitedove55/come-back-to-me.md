title: "Come  Back  To  Me"

Remember my love as you soar
Through the quite star filled night
Remember I will be waiting
Until once more you come into sight.

Although the miles may separate
Vast oceans come between
Feel my love surround you
Protecting you from harm unseen.

Days so long and lonely
Nights empty without your embrace
This soul so full of longing
For a glimpse of your loving face.

Until your journeys over
And your back home here with me
My love will travel with you
Only then will my heart be free.
