---
title: "Sins  Of  The  Fathers"

She looks to the heavens
Pleading for answers
To questions 
So many times asked
Why does the Son? 
Follow the father
On the spiral road to hell.
Surrounded by darkness
Destroying the mind
Wasting the body
Future non-existent
Must sins of the father
Follow the son
Will the son follow the father?
