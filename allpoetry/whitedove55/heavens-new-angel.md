Heaven has a new Angel today
She flies on gossamer wings
This gentle soul once full of sorrow
Now how her heart must sing.

The void you left in so many lives
Impossible to fill
The compassion and care you showed
Will be remembered, I know I will.

Your poetry a gift from god
Read by many far and wide
Each reflecting the beauty of you
As your own pain you tried to hide.

My friend we mourne your passing
You, our Angel filled with love
Be happy my friend be safe
As you meet with loved ones up above.

I will miss you my Friend.
