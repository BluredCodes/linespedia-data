Well here I am in the city
With not a cow in sight
No Kangaroo's or Wombats
No Fox's or Owl's at night.

I met up with those traffic light
That scare the life out of me
Oh how I longed to go home
To sit under the old willow tree.

Terrible thoughts I had
Of three days computer free
That even worse than the lights
I know quite mad I then would be.

Joy oh joy guess what I found
That beaut Daughter in law of mine
Surprised me with a computer
This girl to me is so very kind.

Now Son your Mum won't be to long 
We still have lots and lots of time
Just let me have a play here
I realy need to write some rhyme.

I guess three days of traffic lights
Won't be so terribly bad at all
With my Darling son and Daughter in law 
And a computer at my beck and call.
