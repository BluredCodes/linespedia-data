title: "Bent  On  Revenge"
writer: "whitedove55"

Spiralling
Out of control
On a collision cause
With disaster.
Bent on revenge
To cause biting pain
To hurt, distroy 
Bring each to their knees.
Dragging others down
As they seek distruction
In their private 
World of hate.
Will we all fall
Or can we survive
Have we the strength
To stand firm?
