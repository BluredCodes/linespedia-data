Little one
Come fly with me
With wings of gossamer
Let your mind run free.

On fluffy white clouds
We’ll play hide and seek
I’m sure if we look
A little Robin we’ll meet.

I’ll find you a rainbow
Of colours bright
As we slid little one
No more sadness in sight.

Your heartache and pain
Will be left far behind
Only laughter and smiles
On our travels we’ll find.

Remember little one
How ever sad you may be
My arms are wide open
I’m always near you see.

Nanny loves you Taylor.
