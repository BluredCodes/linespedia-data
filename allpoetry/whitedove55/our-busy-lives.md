Such busy lives we lead
Always on the go
Forgetting things that matter
Not letting compassion show.

We fail to see a friend in need
Of a loving comforting word
We fail to hear them ask for help
Is it really that we haven't heard?

Or is it we can't spare the time
With so many other things to do 
Family, work our social lives
This friend could just need you.

Acquittances are all around
True friends are hard to find
True friends are but a few
True friends are one of a kind.

So remember when a friend is down
Show them that you care
For one day you may be the one
To need that friend right there.
