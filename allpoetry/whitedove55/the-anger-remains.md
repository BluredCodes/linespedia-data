title: "The  Anger  Remains"

I smile I laugh
As you expect me to do
I give comfort encouragement
Stand by your side.
You don’t see the rage
Churning in my mind
Do you realise what it takes
To be there for you.
Your thoughtless actions
Destroyed my world
Volatile unpredictable
Your temper let loose.
You don’t see the pain
You brought that day
To you just a game
A nightmare to me.
Your foolish actions
Tore my heart in two
Thanks to you
Those I love have gone.
I’ll be here for you
As a mother I must be
For the babies I must be
But the anger will remain.
