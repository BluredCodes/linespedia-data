title: "All  I  Ask"

Corroding
Distroying
All that was good.
Anger 
Pain 
Left in your wake.
Screaming
Cursing
Words burn like fire.
Childish
Immature 
So called Adults.
Leave
Depart
Fight your fight alone
Peace
Contentment
Is all I ask.
