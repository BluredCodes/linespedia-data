Eyes of sadness
As you gaze into mine
Asking questions
Answers your hoping to find.

To young to understand
Their anger and fights
I’ve watched you toss in your sleep 
Night after night.

You asked me why
Do they go on this way?
How do I answer?
What can I say?

You ask is it your fault
Are you to blame?
Did they love one another?
Before you came.

Your torn apart Baby
By those you love most
How can they do this to you?
When for you love they boast.

It breaks my heart 
To know this sadness you feel
When will they see what their doing
Why can’t a pact they seal?

Remember little one
I’m not so far away
My loves always with you
Through out every day.

Love you .T.
