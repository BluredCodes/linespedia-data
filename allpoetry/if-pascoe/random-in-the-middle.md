title: "random in the middle"

she drops by dressed in plastic and stinking sweetly;
says 
without you i can’t wait, the edges jade, and everything is too damn straight.
so i crush her in my fingers and light her on fire.

she turns the air into atmosphere;
says 
these crazy days flow like waves and can you kiss me one more time like mary jane?
i smile and throw her over the balcony.
