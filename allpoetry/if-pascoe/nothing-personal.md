i do not belong to you;
there is no marriage left, 
your hypocrisy has cost what you cannot pay,
and someday
tears will burn your lonely eyes without me.

like an untrained dog
you follow each bitch in heat,
beg for scraps,
raise a leg on every hydrant, 
then run when you are called to come home.

you are an invertebrate
who cannot push aside a tiny vice
to spend some time 
with his son and (former) wife;
two victims of your diseased and selfish mind.

you piss in our bed,
stab my heart with your lies, 
deny, breed misery,
give me nothing personal,
and i fucking hate you.
