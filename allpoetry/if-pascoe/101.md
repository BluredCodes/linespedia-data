and we as gods mold little dreams;
pop culture fantasies,
snakes and vessels
that fire burns into rigid forms
like we are.

take the clay in front of us;
this dirt between our fingertips is
adam and eve, 
still red earth 
waiting for birth into silent life. 

and imperfection is our legacy;
too clumsy hands that twist and squish, 
impress in mud our empty eyes, 
our compromised, 
mediocre, sculpted lines,

not art but tragedy.
