I fall into you and breathe relief in your shoulder
boulders roll down my arms
ashes plunge from my tongue 
and we’re strung from the stars
making sparks in our eyes

and the walls between us
are London Bridge

falling down

falling down

London Bridge is....





(nothing like us)
