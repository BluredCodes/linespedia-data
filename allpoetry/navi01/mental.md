Spiders  of  the  past
Are  crawling  up  my  back
Hissing  dark  predictions;
Staining  my  world  black.

Suppressed  uncertainty :
Back  for  retribution ,
Sealing  my  eyes
With  emotional  pollution .

A  cyclone  of  confusion
crumbling  the  whole . .
rejecting  rationality ;
shredding  all  control .

I am 
an exhausted soul.
