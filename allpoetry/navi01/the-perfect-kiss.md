You were a piece of mystery; 
A new enchanting song 
That tossed my soul afar 
To a place where I belong. 

Hid in a corner of the world 
We found ourselves alone, 
And in the midst of laughter 
We encountered the unknown. 

We were thrown among the stars 
In each other’s gleaming eyes; 
Our hands discovered each other 
And our hearts shed their disguise. 

Our smiles were sufficient 
To convey what we desired.. 
And, in slow-motion, we moved in 
To taste what was aspired. 

As my lonely lips met yours 
Two worlds fused into one, 
And I knew at that moment 
Something splendid had begun. 

Now it’s been eight months-- 
Eight months of swelling bliss, 
‘Cause every kiss since then
