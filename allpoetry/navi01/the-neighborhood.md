title: "The Neighborhood"

A sparkling web of incidents 
Intertwined with dreams, 
Knotted winding avenues 
Linked by frisking streams... 

Magic talking briar trees 
Warding off my fears, 
Scarlet velvet butterflies 
Mopping up my tears... 

Peeping between branches: 
Cerulean dragon eyes; 
Flying horses hurtling 
Above clouds in the skies... 

Pixies between petal walls 
Of blooming daffodils, 
Clover charms and daisy chains 
And rolling down the hills... 

Flocks of friendly cycle sprites, 
My pink banana seat, 
Wheelies popped on old cement, 
Running in bare feet... 

Swaying from the monkey bars, 
Flying on the swings, 
Singing with the melodies 
Strung on faerie wings...
