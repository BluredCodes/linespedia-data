(And there he was
Tall, mismatched, with a goofy smile
And a warm, open hand.

We passed time, we passed laughter, 
We passed words, we passed grass.

We raided the kitchen like tigers,
We raided each other like kittens.

He told me about his band and fed me Oreos;
I made him try pickle juice (which he loved)
And taught him to open the microwave with his elbow
In a single backwards karate thrust.

Swirled words and absurd worlds
Stirred and spurred between us;
Between us whirled fables
(Told wrong)
Sending puffs of laughter
To the twisting, tinted ceiling.

We shared bits.
We shared bites.
We shared bowls.
We shared buds.

Then, last Friday morning,
He didn’t wake up.
