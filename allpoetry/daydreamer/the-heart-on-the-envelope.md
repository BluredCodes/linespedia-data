The heart on the envelope could be easily seen.
It lace, and cloth, and red in between.
But would she notice the trouble I went,
To bring her this piece of love that I sent?
Could she think that it’s a message above?
Could she be honest, and think that it’s love?
The heart on the envelope is my work, yes indeed.
For that heart symbolizes the love that I need.
The heart sent to her is only one hope. 
The slightest of tugging of Heart-wrenching rope. 
Would she know it’s me, no… of course she would not. 
I dropped it and hid – I ran from the spot.
On the other hand, yes. She could realize the fiend,
And have him arrested for such a terrible deed.
But then again, I was only a kid.
A kid with a crush, and that’s what I did.
In school yes it was, on Valentines Day.
I gave her a card, and then I ran away.
On the heart, yet she missed, a distinct mark of joy…
That someone had loved her.. That someone.. This boy.
Yet she failed to consume the love that I handed,
And dismissed the card, like a bad flu expanded. 
What could have I done, to keep us from part…
Maybe next time I’ll put more than one heart.Story Time! Yes, there is a story behind this poem. When I was in kindergarten, all those years ago, I had a crush on this girl. So, it's Valentine's Day, and being only in Kindergarten, we were handing out Valentines, and I put a heart on the envelope to my crush... She never noticed. This is the first poem I ever remember writing, that I actually liked.... Kind of juvenile, but still, to me it has meaning.
