title: "Wonder..."
writer: "DayDreamer"

I don't know where I left it...
Cuz I didn't leave it behind...
I don't know how to look for it...
Cuz I don't know what to find...

I don't know if I did it right...
Cuz I didn't do it...
How do I look at it so close...
When I can see right through it...

I wonder if my tears are seen...
Or if my crys are heard...
I wonder if that's possible,
Since I've never said a word...

I don't know how to pray at night,
Cuz I don't know what to believe...
I don't know if my pain is gone...
Cuz I've never had pain to relieve...

I don't know if the water's cold, 
Or if it's me that's hot...
I don't know if the tide is in,
Or if it's me that's not...

How do I climb a mountain...
When I only have a hill...
How do I make the glass half full,
When there's no glass to fill...

How do I ask the question, 
When there is none to ask...
How do I drink the vodka, 
When empty is the flask...

How do I capture lighting...
When there is none to see?
How do I start a fire...
When there is none in me?

I don't know if I'm dying...
Cuz I've never been undead...
How do I make the clear true sense...
Of these thoughts in my head???Spurr of the moment poem. And yet... one of my favorites.
