title: "Tomorrow is My Birthday"

Tomorrow is my birthday,
But I don’t really care.
Tomorrow is my birthday,
But I will not be there.

Tomorrow is my birthday,
A year has come and past…
Tomorrow is my birthday, 
My newest… and my last…
