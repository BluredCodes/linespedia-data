---
title: "everyone knows better than me"
writer: "audrina"
slug: "everyone-knows-better-than-me"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57291-everyone-knows-better-than-me-by-audrina"
---

The whole world decides,
this is the way,
we've lived it we know
the trap alluded me again and now the control angers me 
I become afraid of my actions
Desperate
no more decisions, easy life
almost better
for who?
