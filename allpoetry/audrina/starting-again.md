I find myself back to the womb, knowing nothing
I wish this pain would subside, our love be gone
I do care and it angers me
This place is not mine
The streets scream monotony and I just arrived
the plan unknown I fight for her
If I suceed her life may inspire
If I fail the dispointment of losing both of you will kill me
