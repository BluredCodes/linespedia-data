---
title: "I know you"
writer: "audrina"
slug: "i-know-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

The old sofa became comfortable one day
the stain where I ate eggs and toasts hungover
the smell of your feet in the morning
my bath robe torn yet you looked in lust 
the plan was unknown to me
I announced my discomfort daily
the depression I called it
you bought gifts and looked the other way
I envy that oblivion you call life
I know you
inside and out
you are not who I want or expected you to be
this is not your fault 
I call it fate
we have sex one last time before I announce my departure
the disbelief brings tears to my eyes
but that is just a show
Im happy some would say estatic to be rid of you
although I dont tell anyone this I just play the part
