I went to the house looked around without emotion
It doesnt appear wholesome, not yet
The stairs cleansing but not matrimonial
The walls need paint, refreshing the old arguments that might linger
The drawers empty, the cupboards bare but holding great potential of midnight depression
Too much to take in, especially the first viewing
Maybe some wall coverings will coat our seperation
In time it will be my home
