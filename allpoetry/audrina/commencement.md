The remote marsh warms my soul
It has been too long since I allowed the breeze to envelope me
Now waking to a new beginning, a realization of my inner passion
Walking the wooded path to remember each tree like a good friend
Embracing the knowledge 
You protect me from the dark dangers
You give me peace
If I had known beforehand I would have never left
I was driven headlong by a force
Now I am reminded of how wonderful of a gift I have
Commencement
