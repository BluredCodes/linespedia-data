---
title: "I wanted..."
writer: "Pyli"
slug: "i-wanted"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

I wanted to see your eyes
looking deep inside mine
I wanted to hold your arms
And feel they were part of mines
I wanted to see your smile
As sweet as an innocent child
I wanted to read your mind 
And see you were reading mine
I wanted to feel your heart
Really close to mine
I wanted you to be part of my life
But there was not enough time
sorry you couldn't stay my love
i guess i'll see you in heaven my sweetheart...
