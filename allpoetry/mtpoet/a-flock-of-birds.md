A flock of birds

A flock of birds flew thru blue haze today.
I watched them as they winged thru April sky
near Jack's Knob moving north upon their way
toward a destiny beyond my eye.

Perhaps they've come from whence my heart still yearns
& go someplace where poetry awaits
or to a beach where sand in sunlight burns
before the tide returns as is its fate--

before the day turns black & hides the sea--
before the words I seek, too, flee to night--
before the wind that chills, chills all of me.
I pause to watch then later I shall write.

Then may the words, that I will write tho few,
rise up to sky on wings & fly past you...
