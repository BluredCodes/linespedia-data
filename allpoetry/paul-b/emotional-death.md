title: "Emotional Death"

I am cold to this world around me
Useless death and shocking theme
Leaves me safe in my core
No feeling but felt once
And freed from confusing compassion
I am rooted in deepest sleep

I wait for the clash of ringing doom
A sign that maybe means
Freedom from this deadened core
To love, to feel, to hurt once more
To touch, be felt, make my mark
And awake to the pain of  sorry man

No rhyme around me 
To salve harsh words
Just hard rhythm to pound me
To pulse the blood once again
Through hardened veins
And break this self made wall

I will awake!

And wake I did to fallen grace
With Death’s own angel as mentor
Gripped in spite by its’ close embrace 
But alive! With hot blood abound
I live still my humanity found
Life.It was written after losing an aquaintance and realising how little death affected me.....  Emotional Death!
