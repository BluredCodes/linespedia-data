File into classrooms
File out of classrooms
March into meetings
March out of meeting
'Till you stop telling your feet
Where to go
Constant routine
Void of all reason
Hearded from one place
To another place
In a constant cycle
Being force fed
Into national obesity
Until we are slautered
And fed to our own kind
To start the cycle again
So file in
