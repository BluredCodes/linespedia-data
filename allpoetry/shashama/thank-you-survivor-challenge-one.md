You taught me what love really is
Brought me back into sanity, you helped
Me grieve when I lost my friends, you gave me
Hope when I needed it most
And guarded me from my fears, you
Gave me so many lessons, and taught
Me how to live and live my own
Life without restraint
You held me when I cried and
Saved me from myself, you showed
Me what I needed to see
And when I needed to see you most, you
Were there for me, you were always
There to hold me and wipe my tears
When I was crying
I needed you then, I still do now. I
Cried on your shoulder
I laughed at your jokes
Would you come back for
Just one more day so we could live
Like we used to again
To run and laugh and be happy again. But
Thank you for being there
You helped me through my life
For without you, I would be dead
That is the truth, and thank you.
