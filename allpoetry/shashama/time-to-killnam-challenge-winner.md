Challenge Description: write me a poem with the first stanza's each last line rhyming, i.e.: i went to the park it was dark i went with my friend Mark then i want the first words of the second stanza to rhyme with the last lines of the first, continue for 10 stanza's. you can add more if you want. :0) Nam (100 points)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Will I get my happy ending?
Still, I’m stuck on patent pending
Kill real and fake’s constant blending
Until it’s myself I am defending

Sending letters to the sea
Pretending that I’m really free
Unending wounds won’t let me be
Befriending people just like me

Degree rising into the sky
Plea with you to let me fly
Disagree and make me cry
Flee; just know that I’ll still try

My tears fall down to the floor
Sigh ‘cause I can cry no more
Dry my eyes, still feeling poor
Why? I’m empty to the core

Or maybe I’m stronger yet
For my destiny’s not set
Nor a reason I should fret
Soar through clouds so newly met

Let you mind control the plot
Bet you leave, it’s there to rot
Regret your actions; forget not
Forget you will, the whole lot

Shots are fired, high into air
Hot passion’s love is not always there
Blot it out, ‘cause it’s not fair
Knot in stomach, it’s real I swear

Err is human, so I despise
Affairs are always simple lies
Flares go up, reflect in eyes
Aware to children’s lonesome cries

Advise me how you cannot hate
Supplies are low; it must be fate
Wise are those who were not late
Goodbyes are needed in this state

Wait for me, stay for the thrill
Date and time grows closer still
Berate my words, in voice so shrill
Await the time when I will kill
