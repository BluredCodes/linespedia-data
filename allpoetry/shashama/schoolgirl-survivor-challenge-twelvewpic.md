While skipping through the park one day
A little schoolgirl stopped to play
She found some rocks and sticks and stones
And set out to build structures unknown

She built all day, from dawn to dark
While sitting there, inside this park
Once in a while, she’d stop and say
“I am making good use of this day!”

Minute by minute, hour by hour
Telling the time off the tallest clock tower
She worked without grievance; she worked without care
‘Till few minutes was all she had to spare

And so she went home, quite pleased with her work
A spring in her step, her hair held a perk
She had labored until she was cold and red
A picture of lovers, asleep on their bed

They held one another with much care and grace
He reached over and brushed the hair off her face
This couple, somehow, was quite alive
Perhaps because with love, this girl did strive

Each knew the other, would cause them no harm
So they slept safely, in each other’s arms
The lover slept there, all through the night
And melted away, with mornings first light
