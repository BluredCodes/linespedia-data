Pee Pee Faeries are the best
They love to play and love to jest
Outfits of health food do them good
But sometimes they’re misunderstood

Tayseelynn, she is the Queen
With wings that shine with Faery gleem
She rules over all that she can see
Knows all to know about poop and pee

Sheery Sheer, she’s on the right
She loves fun, but hates to fight
She’s a princess, through and through
Just watch out, she’ll pee on you

Now Lone Poet’s on the left
She loves truth, and hates theft
She’ll make sure that you are good
Like a modern Robin Hood

There is a mascot too you see
Her name is Kittycatdz
She’s a cat, the only one
She’ll teach you how to have some fun

The Pee Pee Faeries earn their wings
By doing good and noble things
Like making outfits of tofu
And giving them to me and you

Humor is their main game
Through AP, they have endless fame
To become one is a goal
They must like this poem, as a whole

So come and dance the Pee Pee Dance
You’ll find fun, perhaps romance
And if you take it all in stride
They will stay right by your side
