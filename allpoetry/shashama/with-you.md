Walking beside the one she loves
Feeling his colors all around her
Happy to just be by his side
He offers to take her for a ride
"I warn you," he says "It's my first
Time with a passenger."
And from his look she knows
That he rides to taunt death
He rides without any fear at all
And she looks up at him
With tears in her eyes
And she tells him with honesty
"I don't care if I die, because
I spent my last few minutes
With you."Inspired by the manga: "Mars"
