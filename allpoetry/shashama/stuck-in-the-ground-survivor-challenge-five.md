I can see you all
Witness your rage
As I tell you
To slow down
And warn you
Of danger ahead
I save little children
And old ladies too
And standing here
I watch you
I watch you go by
Sometimes
You pay me no heed
Only to be caught
For ignoring me
But sometimes
You watch
And obey
My orders
And follow
My directions
As you sit
Comfy cozy
And I stand
Straight and erect
Cold metal
Stuck in the ground
Wanting to get out
But I know
I must stay here
And save children
And little old ladies
I am a traffic sign
