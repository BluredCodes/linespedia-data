Spinning
Around again
Still trying to hold on
But I always lose hold of things
But still

I try
To make things right
Somehow, I always fail
But I will keep trying always
Each time

Sometimes
I stop to think
I don’t know where I am
And don’t know where I am going
Funny

I wish
That I just knew
What I’m supposed to do
So that I don’t end up spinning
My wheels
