The Crow is one amazing bird -- threatening in the menacing way the bird can pose and in the loud cawing proclaiming it's territory.  The Crow doesn't like folks to know it, but it is good natured and sociable.

I am the crow
Can you hear me
CAWING?
Proclaiming
What's mine
I am the crow
Can you see me
FLYING?
Soaring
Through the air
I am the crow
Can you feel me
PASSING?
Drifting
High above your heads
I am the crow
Can you smell me
KILLING?
Slaying
The poor little mouse
I am the crow
Can you sense me
TRYING?
Attempting
To better myself
I am the crow
Watch me soarIf you don't know, don't ask....
