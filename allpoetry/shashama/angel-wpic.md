Once
I met
An angel
She told me then
To never fear her
To this day, I do not
She looked at me with sad eyes
Those eyes drove deep into my soul
Crouching there in fetal position
As if wondering why I chose to stay

But
Inside
She knows why
She is lovely
But that is not it
I stayed because I saw
The pain thats hidden in her eyes
And I know the feeling too well
I stayed here because she is my friend
I stayed with her because she needed me

I
Needed
Her as well
For I was lost
As she seemed to be
And we cried together
And after we were finished
She flew off wearing a smile
And I walked home alone, once again
And I knew she was with me, watching meIt's a structured poem...on syllable, then two, then three, up to ten..then start over
