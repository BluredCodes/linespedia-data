Another question
Another answer
Monotonous days
Watching the clock’s hands

Breathe for me now dear
It’s too much effort
Watch the heat rise up
From the dark pavement

Lazy days drift by
No need to wake up
There’s no need to move
Just stay there in bed

Sleep the whole day through
Don’t confront your fears
This is how we live
Here in Lazyville

Never get things done
Never live your life
Just sit, and don’t move
Here in Lazyville
