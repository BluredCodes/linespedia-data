When the dark dream of night
Becomes a catalyst for terror
And all hope feels gone for good
When the slimy feeling of loss
Creep crawls to control
Slip slides to the center
And becoming completely free
Is no longer an option
When fickle feuds
Over a fickle feudal God
Become the only reality
When lust love fades
Into a foggy background
And silent screams of tourture
Eco in broken craniums
You take my breath away
And bring me back to sanity
From the sleeplessness of my world
And tear me back to my senses
With a slap in my face
When I feel like I am lost
You find me once again
You lift me up onto my feet
And dust me off, and clean me up
You take my breath away
You save me
