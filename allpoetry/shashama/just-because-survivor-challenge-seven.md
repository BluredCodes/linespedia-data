A friendship
Was born
With the shake
Of a hand

And it flowered
And grew
With the love
Of these friends

It blossomed
Out more
‘Till the two
Were in love

And it grew
As if it
Was sent
From above

Now we know
‘Tis so sad
But good things
Will not last

It was fun
While it lasted
They each had
A blast

But they
Had to
Move so
Far away

Though they begged
And they cried
They just
Couldn’t stay

So they drifted
And lost touch
Almost like
It never was

And the friendship
Went and died
There’s no reason
Just because
