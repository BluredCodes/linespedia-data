No more shall I bow to her
No more shall I bend
At her every little whim
I refuse to be dictated
I refuse to curtsie
Because she told me to
I won't comply
They say: "pick your battles"
Well I chose this one
I won't be her maid
I won't give in
I will tell her how I feel
And I won't let her
Smash me down
I'm not her toy
I am sick of this struggle
This power battle
She seems to feel
The need to control me
Well, not anymore
I won't let her
I'm too sick of it
I'm too tired to comply
I refuse to let her take over
Not anymore
So let this be
A silient warning


Watch your backDedicated to my father's girlfriend, Lindsay
