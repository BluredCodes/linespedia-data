They all wanted
For him to be
PERFECT
They all pushed
And prodded
To make him
That way
They all tried
To conform him
To their ways
And they tried
To live through him
And make him
Just like them
And make him believe
What they wanted
Him to believe
They tried to mold him
Maybe they went to far
They should have
Let him live
His own life
Not the perfect
HUMAN
Nor the perfect
MACHINE
For he was human
But they tried
To make him
Something else
Than what he was
