She ran away
From all her fears
From all her cares
She returned to nature
Where she was not judged
Where no one cared
What she wore
How she acted
She went to a place
Where she could be free
And not worry about people
And criticisms
She returned to nature
And finally
For once
In her life
She felt free
And completely comfortable
With herself
And who she was
And she did not miss
The civilization
That she left
At all
For the first time
She was truly
Happy
