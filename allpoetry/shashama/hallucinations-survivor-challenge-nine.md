Hallucinations
Run through my mind
Crazy things
Brad Pitt
In thong underwear
Taking
A yoga class
Cosmic buttfuzz
Mixed with belly button lint
Stuffed inside a rump roast
And fed
To the Chihuahua
A few chicken lips
And some
Elephant eyelashes
Goes good
With creamed corn
But only
At McDonalds
Grandma’s favorite button
Called me a doodie butt
While waving it’s spatula
Incredulously
Chapstick used as deodorant
To stop the armpits
From smelling
And googly eyes
Found in my Cop Suey
Eternal bed head
Nearly scares me to death
Frog jump down my throat
Tear me from the inside
What drug
Should I use
Tomorrow?It's best not to ask....
