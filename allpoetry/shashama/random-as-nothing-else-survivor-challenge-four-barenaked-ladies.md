{I'll be that girl}
Who will never say
{I told you so}
For I think
That {never is enough}
{Am I the only one}
Who will never
{Break your heart}?
I'll {never do anything}
To try and hurt you
But if all else fails
{Blame it on me}
I'll take it

{When you dream}
It's the {same thing}
"Just {pinch me
So I can {go home}"

{Wrap your arms around me}
So I know
That I'm not {just a toy}
You {light up my room}
So please don't {leave}
Me in the dark
Look out my window
With a "{hello city}"
We'll stay up all night
{{Who needs sleep?}
Listen to the {helicopters}
Fly past my window
And suddenly
{Everything old is new again}

It's {stomach vs. heart}
And you don't see
{The humor of the situation}
{It's all been done}
{One week} after another
And everything I do
Is {too little too late}

I know what I did
{I live with it every day}
The {same thing}
Repeats in my mind
But maybe one day
{You'll be waiting} for me
But not now
That's {life, in a nutshell}

{Tonights the night
I fell asleep at the wheel}
Crashed and died, I did

All my life
I've been the {new kid}
The {alternative girlfriend}
I'm sick of it
But you aren't like that

So if you call me
{Call me calmly}
And you'll be
{Off the hook}

And {in the car}
We'll listen to
My {box set}
{Some fantastic} things
Will be said
We'll reminince
About {grade 9}
And we'll go back
To {the old apartment}
We'll visit
{Straw Hat and
Old Dirty Hank},
Our old neighbors
And they'll say
"{What a good boy},
Take {these apples}

But you always were
A {great provider}
At least you get
An "{A}" for effort
But don't forget
I'll {call and answer}
Your calls

{Intermittently},
I think
I should just raise
{The flag}
Surrenduring
But just like
{Brian Wilson}
{I know}
I'll survive

Maybe
{This is where
It ends}
But somehow
I don't think so
For when I see you
I feel like I'm
{Falling for the 1st time}

When the
{Conventioneers}
Start to
{Sell sell sell}
Thats {when I fall}

{If I had $1000000}
Could I prove
That {the wrong man
Was convicted}?

Maybe I'm {crazy}
Maybe it's the {alcohol}
But I think
That {I love you}
