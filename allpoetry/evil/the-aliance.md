They told us it was a new type of biological bullshit that'll save mankind
A chemical used in warfare for us to use to smell, remembered for all time


But the chemical acted up
Now the world is fucked


There are too many choices for me to make
There are too many lives here that are at stake


What's a hero like me to do
When the aliance here is screwed


Just remember at Armageddon the president and his friends aren't gonna stay
Just remember all you have is eachother to hold your back, eternally


It's just a claim staked material possession for this country to claim
They're higher than the rest of the world, my country's famed
When all is over and done with the only thing I remember is my name


Just the bullshit that happened earlier doesn't seem to matter anymore
And the world keeps turning just without the governments whores
The earth is still burning, with everyone running around doing their chores


Where is the aliance when you needed them the most?
Well, the only thing I can say is they're toastSomething to think about when the time comes...
