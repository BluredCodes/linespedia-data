Foundation Crack, break 
pedestal lift awake 
from slumber, Edges blaze, reveal 
As eclipse halocrowned. 

Entwine vine reach Imbibe devour
Upright being inside heating 
Bivalve gasping Red Sea, Quake reverant:

ticker-struck as by hand of lifegiver 
Wrung waves ignite rays streaming 
Forward from central core 
To be intangible no more!it's about inspiration.
