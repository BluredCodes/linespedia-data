Hey look at that 10 dollar bill.
It's mine.
Sitting waiting for me to pick it up.
Waiting for me to slide it into my pocket.
It will soon discover the soft material of my jacket
it will join the colony of other bills 
it will have a place and will not be lonely.
I will use it to buy the most amazing things.
Things that will eventually be the end of me,
Not that I have a problem.
Not that I need the 10, or any other of it's cousins:
the 5 the 2, or even the 1.
Besides it didn't just fall from that little girls pocket
It's mine.
Now i've got it. 
I'll be fine just as soon as i find another.
