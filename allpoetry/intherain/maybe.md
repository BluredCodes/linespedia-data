maybe 
some other time, 
in a different place. 

we'll find, hopefully 
that in our deepest rivers 
the currents never change 
and we have not been carried apart 

we just closed our eyes, 
because below the surface 
these waters are cold, 
and stole our breath. 

can we reclaim our vision
see through murky darkness, 
though our senses are numb, 
that you still touch me.I know i haven't put any capitals in...  i'm not sure if this one is vivid enough... i wanted to distil an ocean
