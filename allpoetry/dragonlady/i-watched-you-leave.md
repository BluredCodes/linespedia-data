title: "I Watched You Leave"
writer: "Dragonlady"

I watched you leave…
Walk away…
From me…
From us…
From our future.
You left…
Like you always do…
When something
Is BIGGER then you.
When you have no control.
You gave me everything…
But a fight…
Respect…
What I wanted.
Everything was a mere trinket…
NOT what I wanted…
Not love…
Not you.
You left so nonchalantly.
Never looked back…
At me
Holding my broken heart
Out to you.
Waiting for something…
For anything.
You gave me everything…
You left…
I watched you leave…
I’m better now.An experience that made me sad, that made me strong!
