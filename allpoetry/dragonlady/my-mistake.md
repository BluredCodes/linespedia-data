I believed in you,
For so long.
You made it so easy.
You offered me things, 
That I thought were real.
For years,
Whether you were here or not,
I had such good thoughts of you.
You gave me things...
              I thought.
Now I know, 
You are not real.
This whole thing,
One huge DECEPTION.
What I thought you were,
A farce,
Backed up by years upon years
Of lies.
You are fake...
         My real life,
                 Santa Clause.Do you ever get so upset about something, that you can't even write about it (at least not well).  This is a perfect example.
