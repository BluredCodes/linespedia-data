I long for…
My own family
~ Rust colored carpeting ~
Metallic wallpaper
* Ceramics class *
Christmas and Easter morning
Thanksgiving dinner
~ Tucking someone into 
Strawberry Shortcake sheets~
Deviled eggs &
Jell-O molds 
* Mystical stars made up of ~
Unicorns and rainbows *
A past
That will never be my future
But leaves me…
Forever longingI'm not sure.  But you know how it goes... it was just there.
