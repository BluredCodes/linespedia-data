Grab the mirror and razor,
Chop up another line.
Suppress the pain and memories,
Proceed with your decline.

Wipe up whatever is leftover,
Apply finger to gums.
Push away what is real,
Because you’re in love with the one thing that numbs.

For once consider the outcome,
The destruction for which you’re clearly bound.
And remember the words, a wise man once said,
“What goes up, must surely come down.”Yeah...
