title: "GO AWAY  ~ A Rant"
writer: "Dragonlady"

You irritate me, 
With your negative vibes
And your holier then thou attitude.
You should do all of us –
Worthless people a favor…
GO AWAY!!!!!
Dig a hole,
And curl up in it,
DISAPEAR!!!!
You have nothing worthy to offer.
I hate the way you make me feel…
The effect you have on me.
Go ahead and think your angry thoughts.
Just do it somewhere far away from me! 
I hope someday you realize…
But I know you never will.Just this person that iritates me.  He thinks that he's better then everybody, and it makes me so mad.  Stuborn jerk!!!!   GGGGRRRRRR!!!!   ~by the way, it's nobody here.
