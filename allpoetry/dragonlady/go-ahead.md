Go ahead and take me,
Do with me what you dare.
Go ahead and hold me close, 
Run your fingers through my hair.

Go ahead and tell me,
All the things I want to hear.
Go ahead and say kind words,
Of a love so true and dear.

Go ahead and stand by me,
Be patient and be true.
Go ahead and do for me,
All the things I’d do for you.

Go ahead and treat me,
With kindness and with love.
Go ahead and give me,
All the things I’m dreaming of.

Go ahead and leave me,
In anguish and distress.
Go ahead, do all these things,
