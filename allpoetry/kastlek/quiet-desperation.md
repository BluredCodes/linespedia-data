Another day has risen with the sun
One more chance to make it right
Every day the producer of new hope
Burning in many a soul
The new sun a promise
A life slowly unfolding
The petals of the newly budded rose unfurling
Exposing the fresh beauty of promise and hope
Promise and hope yet unfulfilled

***
The petals turn brown and wither
They drop off and flutter to the ground one by one
Some hastened by the biting wind and cruel storm
The cold ground claiming their unfulfilled promise
Can you stop and listen quietly?
Quiet desperation can be neither seen nor heard
By the undiscerning eye 
Or the deaf ear of busy lives
Fulfilling their own promise
Stop and listen
Profound silence
Hush
Hear the quiet desperation
In your wife
Your husband
Your co-worker greeting you good morning
The store clerk ringing your purchases
Your best friend
Your child
Your lover
Your brother or sister
The despair of a soul
Wilting, withering, dying
Lack of care and tending
Hastening their departure
A casket filled
The promise and hope permanently gone
Buried in the cold ground
A casket fulfilled
A life unfulfilled
Their quiet desperation forever unheard
No one ever knewWhat can I say?  Just something I noticed...
