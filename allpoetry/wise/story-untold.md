Story untold
Of searchers for fame
But don’t find it
Open harts 
But closed minded
Gaze on documentaries of grates
to find likeness
or adopt qualities
give an open hand 
to the closed fist of 
frustration and bothery
add another ingredient of life
to the cake of success
to be eaten after the 
hard work main course of 
rock glass and sticks
speak an efforted scripture
over drum bass and kicks
homes to be built 
out of blood sweat and bricks
make you tough
being raised on ham eggs and grits
don’t mess with hoes or tricks
you think that’s your girl
but that’s not your chick
unforgettable
if I write it down
then the words will stick
