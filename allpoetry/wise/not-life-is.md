title: "Not, Life is"
writer: "wise"

life
I can't stand it
when everyone on the planet
thinks I take them for granted
life 
is it planed
I don't understand
how can there be someone for everyone
when loves in such high demand
life
is it real
one mans dream 
can be the next mans reality
even if we all work together
like a faculty
sooner or later 
we all end up as casultys
life
is it worth it 
yes but remember
god didn't give you this life
because you diserve it
he gave it because 
it was worth it
