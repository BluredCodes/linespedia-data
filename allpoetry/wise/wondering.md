What makes the world turn
Gravity pull
Or greed, money
Gluttony
With despise, envy
Stomachs are full
Jealousy juice
In big gulps
Bathe in hot happiness 
Dry of to a 
Cold calypso of catastrophe and chaos
Lost love
Long passion filled kisses 
But fake hugs 
so prolongs the misery
