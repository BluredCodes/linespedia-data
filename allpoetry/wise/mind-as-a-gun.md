time tickin
rhyme spittin
race against the clock
live fast 
study hard
don't let the living stop
throw the clock out the window
watch time fly
with norespect for living
there's no respect for dieing
driving imaginary things
it's just metal
people think happyness is found in machiens
so what happens to happyness 
when the metal is dissasembled
crash, burn
it's your turn
to meet your maker
sinner
cover up your natural beauty
with the worlds makeup
ghetto, hip-hop, poetry, beatnic
who am I
black boy with grammer
mind as a gun
one in the chamber
finger on the pen like a trigger
stylo frapper papier   ( pen hits paper )
bangs the hammerplease excuse the spelling.
