I was blind 
and now I’m deft
you lied to me
and acted of innocence
now all that’s true is
touch and breath
no longer can I look into eyes that lie
this is why I’ve become blind
you spoke of words that were not true
making me regret 
my non neglect of love for you
so when you speak 
your words stay suspended in air
for now I am deft in both my ears
but thank you very much
cause now I’m aware
how to avoid a real life true fear
