I never felt so old
I never been so bold
As to put my warm hand
On a shoulder that’s cold
It’s not 
Sex, money, drugs, or rock and roll
It’s self control
World please see
Originality is key
And we need to be free
Don’t try just be
Slow down and feel the rush 
Sometimes we think to much
Wanna know what life’s about
Ok
Breath in
Breath outI see a lot of my friend rushing and worrying about life. Sometimes I have to tell them hey breath in breath out.
