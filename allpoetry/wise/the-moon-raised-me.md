title: "The Moon Raised Me"

Never quit
Stick to it
I’m the white on rice
The speed of light
Live dreams 
Look down on kites
But not the things they’re connected to 
Clime the mountain of knowledge
To the peak
Bathe in the clouds
Come down
Ingrave scripture in the cold stone
Which is life
Which is life lived well
Pounding my existence
Into a life supported globe
Surrounded by black light
Absorbing my florescent thought
Converse with consciousness until mad
The sun stole my childhood
The moon raised me
Night is no place for a baby
My future belongs to the stars 
And is dedicated to death before my timeI was trying to better understand the life that I'm living.
