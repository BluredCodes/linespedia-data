title: "Thoughts are people To"

On the outside looking in
Standing in the middle 
Of a crowd of no people
The thoughts push and shove
On their heads
A genius drizzle 
It is dark were they live
No faces all are equal 
So strong is the color of my people
Some come first
Even though rushed 
Know to wait their turn
To serve purpose 
Number 10,000,000,923
It is your turn to surface
SHOULD I BE ME TODAY?
