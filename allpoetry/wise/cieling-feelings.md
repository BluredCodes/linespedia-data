Pray hard 
For good days that lie ahead
While I lie in bed
Thinking about
Past blunt conversations
And sex in a keg
Don’t wanna eat the government’s cheese 
And spend the government’s bread
Hard living and sacrifice
Makes future life 
A cushiony bed
Upon which I can rest my head
And think deep
Love, me 
Words said by
He, she
Thoughts thought
By enemies
I’m not concerned
It’s only words
And I brush the haters of like this
