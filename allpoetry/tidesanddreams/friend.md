I called you a "friend"
What the hell is the definition of a "friend"
Friends don't hold shit from another "friend"
Everyone played me like I was a fucking fool
So now my question is, Who is my fucking "friend?"
In this entire world I think I only have one friend
And there is no need for the quotation marks
So now because of my "friends" I am looked at as stupid
I knew one person was playing games
But every other person too!
I feel angry and ashamed
Lifeless, ill, and pitiful
So now where do I find a "friend"If this poem pertains to you, sorry, but then again I can't say you didn't warn me! But maybe I was being blind, deaf, dumb, and stupid!
