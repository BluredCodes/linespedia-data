---
title: "Child"
writer: "TidesAndDreams"

You call yourself a man
That's something I can't understand

I see you only as a boy
Still searching for childhood toys

Never knowing when you've found a gift
That's something you'll be sure to miss

This isn't a game of tic-tac-toe
Or just searching for an easy hoe

You're not ready to be adult
You just see everyone's faults

But you are the one with the problem
You could be a beautiful rose, but right now you are just a thorny stem

You are afraid to grow up
Not seeing the wine in the cup

Stop being a child
Get rid of all that's wild

You need to try to become a man
Stop playing in the childhood sand
