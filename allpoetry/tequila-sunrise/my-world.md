welcome to my world
its where i fell in love
the real me is blurred
its one of my many flaws

its where everthing
revolves around one girl
she gives reason to live
energy to wake up in the morning
instead of dying on sleeping pills
will to get up and go to school
so sometime in the future
ill return the favor to her


welcome to my world
explained with 3 words
drugs
sex
music
my world has changed
those 3 words seem strange
who needs them?
when all you need is love
well i have that
and im better off
