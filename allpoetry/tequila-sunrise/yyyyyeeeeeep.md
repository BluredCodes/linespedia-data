take antoher shot of courage as they say
courage for me to move on
and not let this bother me
its my way of dealing with tihngs
you have yours
you look at me like ive done sometihng wrong
but its how i deal with things
because my life wasnt perfect
ive been left out in the rain
nothing has gone right for me
except you, so please take away this pain
so you see now 
why...
if you want me ill stop
ive already done everything in my power for you
this shouldnt be hard
i do regret
wake up in the morning
realize everything
i know why youre afraid, but
that wont happen
yea youll hate me for this
and ill hate myself for it
and if you hate me just say so
just say so
and stop giving me those little hints
that make me wonder
