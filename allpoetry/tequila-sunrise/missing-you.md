writer: "tequila sunrise"

missing you everyday
its the hardest thing to do
i know its a little over the edge
but thats how much i love you

i would spend every second with you
and this paranoia
is a side effect
to caring too much

sometimes i think
what you see in me
because i dont see nothing

would you show me?
so i could feel better
would you help me see
just what i did to you?
what you did to me is easy
beauty, sweetness, talkative
only one who i can talk about anything
i dont know what else to say
although theres more to you
i just love you so much in every way
