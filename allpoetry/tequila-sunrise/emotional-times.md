times like this...
its when i blur the line between life n death
when i hope i take my last breath
how could i be this stupid
how could i have said what i said?

i never knew i would regret it
i was mad
i realized everything after i said it
it was fuckin stupid
but before i though about it
every word seemed to fit

and im afraid that
this would change what we have between us
i hope its strong enough
cuz times do get rough
and when they come
i will be standing to face it
would you?
because love this strong couldnt be broken by an argument
