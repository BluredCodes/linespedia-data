Monday
get up in the morning
wait for the night..
get in a fight
drink till im drunk enough
to take these drugs
seeing what isnt real
feeling what i shouldnt feel

tuesday
wake up..
effects of yesterday 
remain in my brain
take advantage of this
drop some more
trip till the night 
drink to get in a fight
even though i know
none of this is right

wednesday
im feeling guilty
to myself
every problem comin back
sickened by it
i need something new
anything would do
one more day left
anything to get me through
today is the end
no limits
its my way to vent
well after today i wont
be here
clock comes to twelve
i consulve in your lap
solving my problems
was never this easy
even when its the hardest thing i have to do
