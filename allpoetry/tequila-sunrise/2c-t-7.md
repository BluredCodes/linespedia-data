If ive survived it
didnt mean you could
i never told you how it felt
if ive talked to you prior
maybe you would still be here

but by the time i got there
it was too late
ive never seen you this way
your small body
had a lot to say
like your soul
trying to escape from your body
to leave this misery
inhumanely strong 
i guess thats what pain does to you
adrenaline...
i found you in the car
U shape neck craned back
eyes rolled up far
taking you to the emergency room..
our whole friendship flashing before my eyes
you always trying to catch up to me
made me realize
its stupidity
now tell me was it worth it
i mean who would want their last words to be
"I DONT WANT TO DIE!!, THIS IS STUPID!"?
Never listen to me again
             Too Late
