now everything changed
like your filtering your emotions
your additude changes and you say notihngs wrong
well if that was true
i wouldnt be here writing this sad song

well things change,
i hoped for the better
but it changes for the worse
i said im sorry
but that wont satisfy you
if you want me to suffer greatly 
for the mistakes i did
then say so
instead making me wonder
making me fall down to the floor
crying
because something has changed within you
and im afraid its gonna change everytihng within us
