you know that no matter what you do
again, im sitting here writing a love song to you
i dont know what it is with you 
but whatever it is 
i just cant resist
im always afraid of someone stealing you from me
my precious one
my everything
everyday i think about you
you are to die for
youre everything i want and more
so why am i this way
why do i worry too much
its like everytime 
i try to stay calm
something comes up
i end up being harmed
but thats love i guess
so heres one to you
to a love so true
as i wonder about what you do
to make me love you so muchanother one i just started writing, what is this called a progression?

i just.... open up this page n start writing
