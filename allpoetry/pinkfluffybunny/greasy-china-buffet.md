I love the flowers 

I love the dafordils

I love the mountains

and all the rolling hills.

I love the fire flies

when all the lights are low.

Boom de ba

Boom de ba

Boom de ba

Boom de ba


Oh like my I almost forgot my sunscreen and bug spray.  I don't want like dirt and branches on my pink sweater.
