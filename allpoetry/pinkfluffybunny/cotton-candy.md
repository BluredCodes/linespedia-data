Cotton candy comes in handy,
when you sleep with doodle dandy.

His noodle is small
and looks like a doll.
But other than that he is quite manly.

You need cotton candy to melt in his crack.
So that he doesn't talk back.
He resembles a spider monkey now.

Rotten crunchy crust in the corner of his eye
makes you want to puke and die.
But you tell him a lie that he is handy! 

Doodle dandy is a slut
with cotton candy in his butt.
And lives with his monkey in a hutt.

Like this was a book report or something come on people....
