A pee pee fairy I wanna be
I have the wings I am an Angel you see.
But you can trim and cut and stitch
Just don’t let me end up a witch

Fairy Queen Oh wondrous one 
I fall at your feet ….. 
(I tripped on a bun)
sprinkling would be really neat
think of all that pee pee fun

Trainers and tutu and tiara too
Please can I be one of you
I’d love a wand to wave about
I promise never to scream and shout

Can I dip and dive with thee
Promise I’ll always pee pee
I’d never wet my knickers I’m sure
Cause I’d crash into the floor

I promise to respect and obey
And live to pee pee another day
I’ll play real nice around the room 
Down the halls I’ll pee pee zoom

I’ll sprinkle and tinkle just as you do
Bringing fun and laughter to………umm who?
Oh to pee pee and  be a P P F I T
other pee pee’s I’d never splash or hit*Oh Please My gracious Pee Pee Fairy Queen and the council of great ones..........pllleeeeeeeaaaaaaaaassssssssseeeee*
Practicing her pee pee pirouette
