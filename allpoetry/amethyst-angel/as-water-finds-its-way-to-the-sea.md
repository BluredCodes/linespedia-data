As a flower opens to the sun
So my heart has opened to yours

As the stars shine silver in the night
So my eyes shine only for yours

As the sun rises in the east
So my passion rises to meet yours

As the planets revolve around the sun
So my world revolves around you

As surely as Spring means new life
So surely my love for you will abide

As your heart sings it’s song
So mine will harmonize in joy

As your soul flies constant and true
So mine will fly contentedly beside

As water finds it’s way to the sea
So I have found my way to you

As peace settles after sunset
So your peace I do secure
