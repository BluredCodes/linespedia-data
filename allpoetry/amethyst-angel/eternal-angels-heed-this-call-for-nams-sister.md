Gather close and listen well
Only once shall I cast this spell
Lift your hands unto the sky
Release your thoughts that they may fly

Eternal Angels heed this call
Help me break misfortunes wall
Grant her peace that she may live
Her heart and soul has much to give

Lend your strength to make her free
Mother moon hear my plea
Shine your softness in her mind
Clear her eyes so she is not blind

All in tune with universal ways
Lend and listen as your body sways
Take this fear and cast it wide
Allow her family in her to abidePeace and light Nam stay strong in your love for her.
