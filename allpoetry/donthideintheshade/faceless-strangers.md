---
title: "faceless strangers"
writer: "donthideintheshade"
slug: "faceless-strangers"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54779-faceless-strangers-by-donthideintheshade"
---

you are nothing to me
just faceless strangers
so why do i care?
why would i offer my body
as a human sacrifice
if it would make you all happy?

i dont know you, i dont know what you feel
you are just faceless strangers
but i can see some of you are sad
and some of you are hurting
i want to stop it
i want you to be so damn happy

ill offer my body
a human sacrifice
make them happy
make the faceless strangers happy

you are nothing to me 
just faceless strangers 

ill offer my body
a human sacrifice
let the faceless strangers be happy
