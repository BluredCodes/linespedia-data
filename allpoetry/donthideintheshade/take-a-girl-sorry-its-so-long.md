take a girl
make her cry
wish her no love
only hate
watch her struggle
thourgh the years
make her hurt
cause her pain
make her afraid
give her no advice
just a single blade
make her stay here
although she isnt happy
laugh at her tears
give her nasty dreams
allow no-one near her
they wont hear her screams
teach her that she
will always be alone
dont tell her she
is capable of anything
turn her heart to stone
tell her of the pain
the truth may bring
tell her shes insane
she hurts inside
tell her shes to blame
let no-on by her side
tell her youre helping 
protecting her from life
by offering blood
through her knife
teach her about suicide
tell her it helps
she is terrified
tell her she never
would need to cry again
it would all go
one cut through the vein
youve offered her
her wish to die
shes never questioned you
shes never asked why
why you did this to her
why shes locked up
kept from life
other people cope with it
she tells you youre wrong
not everything can be so shit
she wants to see it all
she optimistic
you say no-one will care
never care for her
shes evil, theyll just stare
you make her hurt
outside and inside
youve always kicked her 
deep in the dirt
shes had enough of the times
youve seen her cry
you made her lose the battle
you watched her fall apart
you said everyone would hate her
you tore out her heart.dunno......bit crap really.....
