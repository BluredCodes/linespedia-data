ring of fire
turn to ashes
ring of fire
turn to stone
ring of fire
burn out
fade out

tree of leaves
bear all
tree of leaves
ask for wind
tree of leaves
blow away
fade away

angel of death
take me now
angel of death
look after him
angel of death
u know who i mean
angel of death 
fade

ring of fire 
turn to ashes
ring of fire
turn to stone
ring of fire
burn out
fade out
fadeu cant expect much, i wrote this just now......
