Yesterday
You made me feel less than you
You frustrated me
You turned my day upside down
You hurt my self-confidence
You made me feel tired and worn out

Today
I became myself again
I felt good to be who I was
I was happy with my accomplishments
I felt important
I had excitement not often found
I helped someone

Tomorrow
It brings mystery
It reveals the unknown
It adds the results of yesterday and today
It makes a person wonder
It brings a new hope, yet
It is never guaranteed…havent written in awhile...just some thoughts that came out...
