Looking
I can see for miles
Now that the dust is gone
I feel the calm wind tingle my neck
The breeze is wonderful
I am at peace
My fears are relinquished

Reminiscing
Standing over a valley of ashes
Memories lost in the heaps
Pain is still felt looking on
Malevolence is unforgotten
Souls tarnished
Physical remains wither away
Once a center of life
Has no more to offer more than rubble

Fear
Hell embarking on Earth
Evil sweeps over integrity
A master disguises in coward fashion
Amassing casualties with destructiveness
Radicals reaping the high of brutality

Unity
Ultimate tragedy still breeds new hope
A statue remains tall in the harbor
Abstract unison chants in harmony
America’s brethren join hands
Frozen hearts thaw
A nation’s morale is heated
Calling out evil with the power of love
Refusal to fall in helplessness
Instead we rise
High enough to kiss the heavens

Crying
For fallen soldiers and heroes
I wish you were still here to see
A mass entity stimulated
Applauding your actions
Standing for our beliefs
Falling in an act of bravery
Alone defies cowardice


Respiration
Smile young woman
Uncover your eyes
Rejoice at blue skies
Clouds lifted by angels
Vintage eyes see new light
Smell the fresh air of vivacity
Sing a new song
Today is a brighter dayI had to write this poem for a school project based on the events of September 11
