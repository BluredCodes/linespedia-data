I                                                                          I
You                                                                      uoY
We Become                                                          emoceB eW
Together as one                                              eno sa rehtegoT
Love I cannot explain                                  nialpxe tonnac I evoL
Two halves making a whole                          elohw a gnikam sevlah owT
I want all of you, always                          syawla ,uoy fo lla tnaw I
Eternally inseperable                                  elbarepesni yllanretE
You drive me wild                                          dliw em evird uoY
Everything you                                                uoy gnihtyrevE
Physical                                                            lacisyhP
Mental                                                                latneM
