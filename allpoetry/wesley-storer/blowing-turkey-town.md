(On The Mountain)

The stars are beautiful tonight
Brilliant ebulliences of light
Over the living born to die
Against their will not knowing why
Over the ocean's tidal roar
The rose tinged scarlets soar
The sunsets will subside
The drenching rains will hide
Their heavy shrouds will fall
Like tombs of gathered gloom
In the forest darkened gloom
While on the mountain high
Celestial sequined sprites
Sparkling swirling spheres
Blink and twink in speedy flight
Against their will not knowing why
Upon the living born to die
Let their distant beams deify
No hopeless dismal remorse
Vex the Lord's true course
The stars are beautiful tonight
In beacon mustard seed skies
God's faith traveling light
