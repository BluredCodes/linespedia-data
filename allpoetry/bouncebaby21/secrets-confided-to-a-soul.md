Secrets confided to a soul
who can not compare

Hopes swept away on the gilded wings of an angel
wounded beyond repair

Plummeting toward the earth
crashing in despair


Weeping eyes dowsed with tears of pain
as the final breath is lost into the abyss


A heart beating slower more
love lost with an everlasting kiss

Peering into the clear reflection
with a face that was nearly missed



No mind with a clue
to what it could be

A life long spent wishing
for a ghost no one could see

And all for the sake of what
no man can ever flee
