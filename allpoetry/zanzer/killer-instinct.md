I took the knife in my right hand and
I took the blade
Placed the sharp edge against the skin
Of my left wrist
That was the first.

I bought a bunch of pills
Not sure what would happen
Subconsiously I knew (I guess) 
that was number two.

I made a noose out of hemp
And slipped it around my neck
Thank you for stopping me...
