Listen

Along fenced-line-split realities
Pacing trampled bare-earthed perplexities
On the edge, unfairness stoking angers fires
He confronts surrendered freedoms and hope
Please don’t talk; Walk with me – listen.

Razor sharpened stupidity
Keeps Australia from reaching in for me. So
From roof-topped impossibility, He shouts “Freedom”
And four disinterested desert winds whisper
To a country misled, not inclined to listen.

Across a dust-blown and a stone-peppered wasteland-compound
Daily, He drags ebbing hope
Passed sad tear-tired and emptied eyes
That stare beyond the gate
And wait …. And wait …. And wait …. 

Is anyone listening?
