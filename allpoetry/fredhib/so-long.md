SO LONG

Touch ground 
Walking
Sounds echo behind - past neat
See air 
Blowing
Swirling memories - dissolve keep
Hear dance
Pounding 
Red desert dust feet - trample deep 

Beat rhythm
Clocking
Timeless rocks – clocked hand
Waken waken up 
Commanding
Sun-king burns – unchanging sand
Blind girl
Bleeding
Stands dispossessed - her land.
Her home - Her dreaming

Too short to know
Too long to forget

Wanderer respect

Souls touch
Meeting
Hearts-stone 
Melting
Wishes Warmth
Generously 
Sharing

Too short to know
Too long to forget

Wanderer respect

Cold red-soiled-sand 
Dealing 
Ace-of-hearts and kneeling
 Feelings felt
Dance beating
Dry dust powdered bone 
And dead dingo-dogs moan 
Go home 
Leave us alone

Too short to know
Too long to forget

Wanderer respect
