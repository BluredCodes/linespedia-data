The kid cries and lies with his tears


The kid cries and lies with his tears
Delighted to perform
And sob for the world shrunk-to-fit pin-hole-camera-lens mediocrity
In tinselled-truth street-dance-theatre media-feeding frenzy
Told to be what they expect to see
Not the person you aspire to be
And to shock,  (But - just a little), their sensitivity 
While TV dinner-time family shakes prefabricated taste from HP bottled sauce  
And that evening’s news is reduced to a whisper and a flicker of a picture 
Of a kid who cries and lies with his tears.


Fred Hibbert
Saturday, 23 March 2002
