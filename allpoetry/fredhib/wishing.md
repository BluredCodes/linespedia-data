WISHING

Fly, drift, sail,
Across indifferent waves.
Pushed on ageless tides,
Frustrated by indifferent currents,
Wind; always testing; trying; teasing.
Becalmed.
Stormbound.
Set sail, take the helm and pray.
 Guide, or be guided?

Fly; thoughts,
 Shatter surface-tension.
Unfurl the Spinnaker,
Rise upwards, break free,
Breathe!
Rocket to celestial shores.
Triumph!

Drift; feelings,
Untroubled by restless waves.
Float on timeless rhythms of currents and tides.
Pray.
Be guided to golden sands, and,
Rest in Eden’s shade.
Paradise.

Sail; desires, passions,
Grasp the steerboard.
 Command.
 Certain of yourself,
Sure.
 Plough a furrow to windward,
Course by instinct.
 Fulfilled!
