Freedom – Faith’s Hope

Freedom is 
A drop of rain that falls with kindness and grace 
Through a scarlet sun-seared sky
Driven hard on a hot wind
And must fall or it will die as
A meaningless unfulfilled promise


Hope is 
A seed planted in the ground covered with earth warming love
And watered and weeded and 
Watched and smiled on from above
While deciding life or existence as
A meaningless unfulfilled promise

Freedom is 
A willingness to choose choices and grow in faith
Protected by hope’s prayer that befriends in times of despair
And whispers better times to come
When morning’s light will warm your soul 
Opening hearts door to welcome the new day or you may stay as
A meaningless unfulfilled promise 

Hope is
What You will say
When children cry and mothers scream
And your world becomes a sea tuned green
And the sky is dyed by brother’s blood torn rage
And buildings fall and legs fly off 
And terror rules the day in a
Meaningless unfulfilled promise


What hope then?
What freedom there
Share with me all thinking men
The only prayer
Faith and Freedom’s Hope.
