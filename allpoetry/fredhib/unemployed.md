Unemployed 

		Some people
		Puff on ventolin.
		And others sip their friendly gin.
		I chase dogs on Saturday
		And keep-me fit and slim.

		Some people
		Count-on their money.
		And others the TV.
		I meditate on Sunday,
		And sum-up the Holy See.

		Some people
		Ride the iron road.
		And others motor bikes.
		I stop home on Monday
		And fly Grandma’s secret kite. 

		Some people 
		Don’t like Mondays.
		And always count the minute
		I live each moment as a gift
		And Tuesday’s for fun. In it?

		Some people
		Sit in armchairs.
		In offices, others are revolved.
		I sit down on Wednesday 
		And the world’s problems - solved!

		Some people
		Are paid for their sweat.
		Others for their brain
		I get paid on Thursdays 
		For signing my name.

		Some people
		Get drunk on Friday.
		Others are never sober.
		I get drunk whenever I like
		And I love my life, moreover! 

						Fred Hibbert
