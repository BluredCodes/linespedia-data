Not Another Love Song

Trampled trust
Punished promises
Requited and unrequited 
Given and taken
Used and abused
We’ve all been there 
Whats new?

Eyes full of moon
Waves on crystal shores
Beat time to hearts entwined
As tender touches explore and whisper
Forevers and evers
Just us two – Do me a favour
Whats new?

Argument and tears
Sultry suspicion 
Feeding jealous fears
Goodbye coition 
Slam doors
Children scream
Lovers no more

Don’t sing me another love song
I’m not impressed by romance
It lies with sighs from sultry eyes
And tempts us to pretend it cannot end
And daddy slides 
Out the front door, when
Lovers are never no more.

Whats new?
