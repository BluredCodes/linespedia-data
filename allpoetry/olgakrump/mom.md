JUST LISTEN MOM, PLEASE DON'T CRY
EVERYONE IS TELLING LIES
I'VE BEEN THE GOOD GIRL ALL MY LIFE
ALL THESE LIES CAUSE PAIN AND STRIFE
I DIDN;T DO IT, NEVER WOULD
I'VE TRIED MY BEST, DID ALL I COULD
I DID NOT DO THE THINGS THEY SAY
I'M JUST NOT LIKE THAT IN THAT WAY
I KNOW I'VE LOST YOUR TRUST AND GAINED YOUR HATE
AND NOW I'M SCARED TO EVEN DATE
YOU THINK THAT SEX IS ON MY MIND
SCARED TO THINK OF WHAT YOU'LL FIND
YOU'LL FIND NOTHING THERES NO REAL DIRT
IT'S MISUNDERSTANDINGS THAT MAKE OYU HURT
I'M AS SCARED AS YOU ARE AND OFFENDED TOO
WHY DO PEOPLE SAY THE THINGS THEY DO
