i'd hate to say that i'm in lust
i know you're spitting in disgust
it's not my fault i can not lie
 it's not me you want that's why i cry
you talk about her day and night
i try not hold in my tears with all my might
when i'm alone i cry and wail
i tried to get you and i've failed
a sudden shock a stab of pain
i'm drownign in a pool of shame
i'm unwanted you enevr cared
cough, gasp, i'm out of air
