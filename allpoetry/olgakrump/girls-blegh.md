---
title: "girls.. blegh"
writer: "olgakrump"
slug: "girls-blegh"

i don;t know what to do
when everyones gone
when i;m all alone
at the break of dawn
why are girls so mean
and truly not there
how can they say that thay're friends
when they really don;t care
when i can sit here and cry
and they really don;t see
how the things they say
have broken me
how they can talk about me
but not to my face
how they can't understand
that i need my space
each tear that falls 
is a step away
from these stupid girls 
and the shit they say
i don;t need a tissue
i'm letting them fall
hoping that the faster they do
i'll be rid of it all
