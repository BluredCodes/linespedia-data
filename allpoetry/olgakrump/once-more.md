---
title: "once more"
writer: "olgakrump"
slug: "once-more"
source: "AllPoetry"

Are you ever gonna call me-will you ever let me know
Do you know how I feel? Can I let my feelings show?
Do I tell you how I feel or watch you slip a way?
I wanna tell you everything but I don’t know what to say
Will I seem too desperate if I tell you I care?
If I wait too long will you still be there
I don’t want us to just stop talking like we did before
It was too hard to live through and I couldn’t handle it once more
