---
title: "piss em off n' die"
writer: "olgakrump"
slug: "piss-em-off-n-die"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/61966-piss-em-off-n-die-by-olgakrump"

i'll make everyone mad
till they dont wanna be friends
i'll cancel out everything
make sure everythhing ends
i'll start to disappear
until i'm barely even there
so by the time it's over
nobody'll care
by the time i leave
ill have been gone for years
no one will know me
there will be no tears
no on will notice
no one will cry
there will be no one to mourn me
i'm just a stranger who died
