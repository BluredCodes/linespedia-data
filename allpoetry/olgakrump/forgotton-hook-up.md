do u know that you hurt me
do u even care
why are so quick
to forget i was there
are u ashamed what we did
did it not mean squat
wil u even remember 
or have u already forgot
have you forgotton i'm here
have i faded away
when you're flirting with her
just don;t look this way
i can't face you anymore
you don't care at all
you've chosen your girl
and i'll take the fall
no one cares how i feel
that i'm slipping away
maybe sometime they'll notice
they've made me this way
