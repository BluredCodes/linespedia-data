title: "dying alone"
writer: "olgakrump"

what do i do
when nobody cares
where do i turn
when nobody's there
who do i run to
when i've pushed them away
when nobody'll listen
what do i say
if i were to go
how'd i know if i'm missed
if i didnt say goodbye
would anyone be pissed
if i told off all my friends
till i had no more
if i left a note
who'd it be for
once i am gone
how long will it be
who's gonna find me
hanging from a tree
