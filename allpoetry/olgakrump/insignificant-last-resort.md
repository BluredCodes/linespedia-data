title: "insignificant last resort"

it's how everytime i'm near you
you always walk away
how even when i'm not hurting
you always find a way
how everything is more important 
then to waste your time with me
how you're always looking for an excuse
or somewhere else to be
how you make me feel like crap
and still i don't know why
how everytime you do it
i still get back in line
you come to me when you have no one
and disappear as fast you came
though you never really care for me
my feelings stay the same
even though i've been replaced
and even worse by my best friend
i know when she's gone you'll come running
i know its not the end
