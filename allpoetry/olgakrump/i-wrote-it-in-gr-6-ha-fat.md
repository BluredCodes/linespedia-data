when you step on hte scale
you big fat whale
you see a large number 
you huge cucumber
now think of all the food you like
that's everything right?
remember the last time you sat
your chair was pouring over with fat
didn;t you know its a sin
to haev so many chins
face it your a fat kid
you're not small nor mid
you're the fattest thing i've ever seen
you big jellybean
do u notice how nothing fits
that's b/c of allthose timbits
don't tell me you're gonna go eat now
ugh! you fat cow
have you ever heard of a diet?
well i really think you should try it
if you want you can go cry
i understand since your colesteral level is so high
