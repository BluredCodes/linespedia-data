title: "fake"
writer: "olgakrump"

Hidden are the secrets, spoken are the lies
The truth is never spoken, no one knows I cry
My tears are shed in private; I smile in a crowd
Even though I’m ashamed- I let you think I’m proud
To hide things is wrong, I know it’s a mistake
But I know you wouldn’t accept me if I let you know I’m fake
