there's so many things i wanna know
maybe thats a reason i cant let go
i can't understand. don't kwow why
you say you don't want tobut make me cry
are you two-faced? are you fake?
are you even sorry for your mistake?
will i ever know? will i always care?
will i find out why life's not fair? 
will my questions be answered?
will my answers be questioned?
will i ever stop asking so many questions?
