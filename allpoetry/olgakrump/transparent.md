i'm melting away to invisable
Surrounded by no one who cares
they're trampling over my existance
They're swallowing up my air
They're tearing apart my life
and throwing it away
scattering all the pieces
until i just fade away
i'm encaptured by my failures
Emprisoned by my hate
It's hard to tell if i'm still here
and by now it's past too late
