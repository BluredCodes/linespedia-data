Sex is  a trend
So join our cult
If you lose a friend
Its not our fault

AIDs is an illness
That spreads like a plague
There are some you’ll miss
Cause their knowledge was vague

A cure is uncertain
When you deal with this stuff
They do what they can
But it’s just not enough
