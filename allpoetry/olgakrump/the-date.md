He nervously approaches
The perfect gem
Sweaty palms
Fidgeting hands
Eyes searching desperately 
  for anything
Trying to grasp the key
  to making this simpler
Only to be erased by her
 stunning beauty
Effortlessly she awakens 
 the butterflies in his stomach
He wipes his palms
Takes a deep breath
Temporarily calming the jumpy nerves
He swallows to wet his dry mouth
 and finds the words in her piercing blue eyes
The confidence in her kiss
 to ask her 
To be His.
