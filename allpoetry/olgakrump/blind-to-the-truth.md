---
title: "blind to the truth"
writer: "olgakrump"
slug: "blind-to-the-truth"
source: "AllPoetry"

i was happy to see you
glad you were there
the more we talked
the more i thought you cared
i bit the line
i got your hook
even opened my eyes to take a look
only saw what i wanted
a you and a me
blind to my challenge
i was where i wanted to be
lost sight of my goal
lost track on my own
lost you in the meantime
i'm left all alone
