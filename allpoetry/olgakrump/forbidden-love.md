Forbidden love, the sweetest fate
Secret love opens up the gate
The highest land, the brightest light
Ever burning star, an amazing sight
Confusing thoughts, a jumbled brain
Good or evil, sun or rain
A weeping flower, a silenced voice
A sniffling sound and cheeks so moist
The last petal falls the sun goes down
A blinded love, molded frown
