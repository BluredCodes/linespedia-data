DON'T
Don't ask me to meet you
If you know you won't show
Don't ask me to stay
If you want me to go

Don't ask me to follow
If you know you won't lead
Don't sak me to come 
If you're just gonna leave

Don't tell me you love me
If it's not how you feel
Don't say your love's true
If you know it's not real

Don't ask for my number 
If you know you won't call
Don't say that you love me
If you don't care at all

I don't want you to tell me
You'll always be there
Don't want you to say it
When you know it's not fair

So don't ask me for anything
Now we're apart
All i have are memories
but you've got my heart
