i have nothing to say
i'm not even confused
even after what's happened
i still don;t feel used
i don;t know what's happened
it's like there's nothing there
whenever something ends
i don;t seem to care
everything used to hurt me
till i broke down and cried
but now i dont; feel anything
i guess that part of me died
i don;t know if thats a good thing
now i'm so quick to move on
although i'm never satisfied
like a part of mes gone
