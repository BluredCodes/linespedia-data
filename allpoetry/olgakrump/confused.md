I watch confused, so uninformed and trapped
Between two lead doors bolted shut
No where to go
No room to breathe
The hard silence seeps into my jumbled thoughts
Infecting my already bewildered mind
Each theory competing with the other like Roman gladiators
Fighting for their lives
To discover the reasoning behind all the
Crap
As to why he tramples the beautiful garden of my soul in his
Heavy combat boots
Without a second thought
Or even a trace of regret.
