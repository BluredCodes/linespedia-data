title: "phony bastard"

It’s the way I look forward to seeing him
And how he brightens up my day
It’s the way he makes me feel beautiful
And knows exactly what to say
It’s al those things on the outside that everybody knows
But it’s what isn’t said or done all the hidden things unshown
Like the way he always leaves me
Whenever I’m in need
His selfishness, uncaring
And I little touch of greed
The way he somehow disappeared
With out a trace- but left his mark
How the only thing I’m left with
Are shattered pieces of my heart
