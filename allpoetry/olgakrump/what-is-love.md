Have you ever had the bestest friend
Then suddenly wished it’d never end?
For you have found yourself so deep in love
But then I ask myself what is love?
Is it when you look into each other’s eyes
As if they are the deepest skies
They took your hand and took you high
Into the clouds you both did fly
But then the clouds fell through
For it’s never in you
That you’ll find their love
So I ask again, what is love?
Was it sent to us to be again?
Or just a way to inflict pain
Is it an unfinished puzzle to be solved?
Or just a way to be dissolved
 Is it how we’re punished for the wrong?
That nothing good will last for long
Has someone not yet figured out?
That we are only left to cry and pout
Is there ever a happy ending in life?
Or does it all end in pain and strife
Will anything good happen while I’m here?
Or do I just stay this way, filled with fear?
And with each day that’s passing by
I realize I won’t ever meet the perfect guy
And so I will die the way I was born
No one will care no one will mourn
Soon I’ll be in my own sweet home
