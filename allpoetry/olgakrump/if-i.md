if i gave you a penny
would u throw it away
would you class it as petty
and give it away
if i sang you a song
would you say its off key
would you pick at its meaning
as you did to me
if i gave you a smile
would you just look around
would you bother to notice
when it turned to a frown
if i shed a tear
would you wipe it away
would you leave it unnoticed
and me while this way
if i gave you my hand
would you let it go
would you leave it empty
and let it alone
if i gave you my heart
would you hold it tight
or will you continue to break it
like im afraid you might
