"Cool Water"

On the beach.   Sand between my toes.
Cool sand, every grain distinct.   Soft sand.
Light gray sky.   Solid sky hiding the sun.
Cool breeze, fresh and clean.   Refreshing.
Blue-green ocean.   Waves flow over my feet.
Cool waves, gentle and friendly.   Feels good.
My sweet love.   Walking beside me.
Warm heart, smiling and caring.   Together.

1992

--------------------


"Emily Dead"

With a face so sweet,
she happily skips around.
Touches the people on the street,
and smiles when they fall to the ground.

1992

--------------------


"Good Night"

May ye rest this night in shadowed peace
beneath the active leaping fleece
and if in early hunger you wake
a truly delicious feast they'll make

1995

--------------------


"Fear"

My thoughts return to you
but I've been there before
It happened with another
whom I now somewhat deplore

1996.
.
When my niece was younger, she said the name "Emily Dead".  I don't recall why she was compelled to say it.  At any rate, the poem is what came to my mind when I heard it.
.
