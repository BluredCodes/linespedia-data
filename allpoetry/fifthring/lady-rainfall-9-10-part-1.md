"Lady Rainfall IX"

Her blood,
the will.
Her flood,
the thrill.
The rush,
so fast.
She would,
to fill.
She sings,
life to.
She brings,
the new.
The lush,
to last.
She rings,
the true.





"Lady Rainfall   X (part 1)"                    (04/17/1993)

Your heat,
inside.
Her touch,
the guide.
The stroke,
that teases.
The touch,
that pleases.
She comes,
to fill.
With her,
sensual chill.
You feel,
love make.
She shouts,
her wake.
