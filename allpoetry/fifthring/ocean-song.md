---
title: "Ocean Song"
writer: "FifthRing"
slug: "ocean-song"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55293-Ocean-Song-by-FifthRing"
---

In the waters of the ocean,
flow the waves of desire.
In the soul of a creature,
burns a warm and treasured fire.

He was alone, he saw her swimming,
he felt his heart growing inside.
He could never have imagined,
a thing of more beauty on the tide.

All his ventures round the coral,
all the beauty he did see.
All the magic of the ocean,
had fallen from sight completely.

She moved with grace through the currents,
a body created by design.
Meant to dance to the song of the ocean,
an existence which none could refine.

He felt her presence in his soul,
his soul reached out a gentle hand.
A chance but to touch such beauty,
would truly be most grand.

She glided upward to the surface,
filled her lungs with fresh sea air.
Did not wipe the water from her face,
or comb her fingers through her hair.

She felt the drops roll down her cheeks,
let the water caress her skin.
Let the spirit of the ocean,
touch her soul deep within.

She wished that it could be her home,
she takes comfort in its hold.
A freedom within the constant grasp,
a sense of security untold.

She filled her lungs again and down,
she moved through the water that she held dear.
Her eyes closed but still she noticed,
she felt a peaceful presence near.

Moving forward she felt a touch,
against her flesh sliding along.
She felt as she had never before,
the beauty of the ocean's song.

She felt a heat inside her grow,
her body could not move.
She felt a sense of untold freedom,
and her soul had filled with love.

As the glide along her body ended,
her eyes opened wide.
Her senses returned as her body tingled,
but still she felt inside.

The soul of another joined with hers,
a single, pleasant, one.
She broke the surface to breath again,
as the feelings warmed her more than the sun.

He opened his eyes and felt her inside,
an energy beyond compare.
He raced to the surface to fill his lungs,
and the power moved him into the air.

He felt the sweetness he had touched,
as two souls merged.
The ecstasy of the pure sensation,
as a comforting whole emerged.

She saw his body return to the grasp,
the sleekness of his form.
The very skin that touched her own,
that made her feel so warm.

She felt the force drawing him to her,
she dropped, her arms in empty embrace.
He swam to her open arms,
and filled the welcoming space.

She held tightly to his body,
as they moved they felt the water's rush.
The love held her close to him,
their bodies almost flush.

They broke the surface and she released,
at once they both dove in.
Suspended in the depths they looked,
they saw a new life would begin.

She saw him clearly through the currents,
love had changed her eyes.
She saw the beauty of the ocean,
all that beneath lies.

He saw her, the greatest treasure,
ever held by the ocean.
He saw her moving toward him,
in her graceful motion.

She pressed her face to his,
her hand caressed his skin.
Together they felt the warmth,
that they shared within.

He smiles at his happiness,
a smile that will remain.
Any sadness they once felt,
they never will again.

In the waters of the ocean,
flow the waves of desire.
And in the soul of two creatures,
burns a warm and treasured fire..
.
.
This is a similar concept to my poems, "Woman Without Whiskers" and "Pony", only slightly less so.
