Lady Rainfall  V

She falls,
at night.
She sings,
her plight.
Her promise,
to touch.
She brings,
delight.
She calls,
to bless.
To meet,
softness.
Her love is,
her clutch.
Her sweet,
caress.




Lady Rainfall   VI                   (03/27/93)

She comes,
so cool.
So deep,
the pool.
Her touch,
her soul.
She knows,
no rule.
You feel,
you hold.
Pleasure,
untold.
Touches,
your soul.
You join,
so bold..
.
.
  It was after number four that the rhyme scheme had run its course.

  I had more to say, but it was no longer coming out in the rhyme scheme that I was trying to maintain.

  Hence, a new rhyme scheme was born.
.
.
