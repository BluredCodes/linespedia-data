"Lady Rainfall   III"

Fall to me,
from the sky.
Let me hear,
your pleasure sigh.
You will see,
my emotions high.
As you near,
a lustful cry.
As you begin,
your fingers touch.
I'm held within,
your loving clutch.
Pull me in,
a warning sign.
Feel your skin,
next to mine.





"Lady Rainfall   IV"                    (12/06/92)

Anguished call,
sweet reply.
Moist delight,
from the sky.
Quickly fall,
to pacify.
Drops alight,
flowing by.
Ecstasy,
with one touch.
Eternity,
in the clutch.
Always be,
never less.
Than the sea's,
light caress..
