"Lady Rainfall"

Born of water,
flesh of fire.
Blood of earth,
breath of desire.
Ocean's daughter,
taken higher.
Above the mirth,
out of the mire.
Air of sweetness,
honey touch.
Burning plealess,
sultry clutch.
Soft caress,
flowing skin.
Sensual bliss,
from within.




"Lady Rainfall   II"

Free of spirit,
refreshing soul.
Taker of,
polite control.
Waiting you sit,
collecting whole.
High above,
sweetly roll.
Gentle fingers,
giving touch.
Moistness lingers,
lustful clutch.
Enchanting singers,
voice of body.
Know the lover's,
beautiful melody.1992, .
