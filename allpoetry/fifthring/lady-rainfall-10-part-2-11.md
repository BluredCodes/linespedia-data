"Lady Rainfall X   Part II"

The cry,
you hear.
The high,
you near.
She feels,
your rush.
As muscles,
crush.
The chill,
does burn.
As sounds,
do turn.
Inside,
the fold.
A world,
untold.





"Lady Rainfall   XI"                    (07/04/93)

From the ocean,
she does rise.
From the deep,
into the skies.
With the notion,
to revitalize.
Life to keep,
within her eyes.
Fall she must,
and in turn.
In the dust,
heal the burn.
Like a lust,
to live again.
And to just,
let life regain..
.
.
Number 11 was done a few months after the 10 pair, and it is also a third new rhyme scheme.  Thing is, though, that I never wrote any more after 11.

  Well, that is to say that I didn't write any more in the shown series.  I do have at least a couple more poems of the Lady Rainfall concept.
