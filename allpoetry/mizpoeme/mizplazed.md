~*~*Mizplazed*~*~





Missed your call

Like your smile

A little lonely

It's been a while

Sleepless tonight

One horse town

Streets are quiet

No one around

The waves roll in

Crash upon the beach

Stars are beautiful

But too far to reach

So much to say

No one to tell

Feeling "Mizplazed"

But hiding it well



MJMP
