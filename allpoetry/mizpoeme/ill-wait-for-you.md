Waiting For You



Late at night

As I lay in my bed

Thoughts of you

Creep into my head

Are you aware

Can you sense

Is this fate 

Or Just circumstance

Am I dreaming 

Reading too deep

Thoughts of you 

Keep me from sleep

The warmth of your eyes

The fullness of your lips

Imagining your arms

Around my hips

I want to touch you

Taste the salt on your skin 

But I just walked away 

Rather than let you in 

Do you think of me

Am I keeping you awake

If given a second chance

Is it one you would take

Just say the word

Give me a clue

If it is me you want 

I will wait for you
