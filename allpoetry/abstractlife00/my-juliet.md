The temptation,
The love,
It's you,
High above.

My life,
Your sting,
It's us,
Forever being.

Taste me,
Taste you,
Everlasting sex,
Never through.

You live,
I pine,
Eternal "us,"
Worlds design.

Lifting high,
Freely fall,
Float away,
I dissolve.

Eyes together,
Morning songs,
Secrets shared,
Eternal long.This piece is for my Juliet.
