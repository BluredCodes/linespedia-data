To no distance I will not tread
For many moons to follow and see
No darkness in thy day or night exist
Only illuminated eyes are afar
White stallions swiftly ride against undying wind
Purest of heart daggers through so softly
No force nor act shall penetrate this love
For it be bitter to thou and sweet to thy lips
Only a thousand nights may break thy subtle heart
And a thousand tears will soon come before that night
Till the moons set and winds no longer carry
Hearts deaden in hollow ground while darkness overturns09-06-1999,
