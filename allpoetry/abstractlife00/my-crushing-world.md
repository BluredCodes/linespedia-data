Days go on with endless music
While my body twirls around under blue skies and rapid rains
Puddles become drums which beat with the sound of my heart
I just image myself galloping on a beautiful arabian
Its tail flutters in the brisk air
Its body is so pure and unharmed
Skin shines in the glimpse of the moon like new porcelin
Above is a full moon in a freckled sky
I feel as if I am floating over clouds
This deap trance has silenced my every nerve into ecstacy
Emotional drugs that have burried me in a bath of fallen leaves
Body so still knowing that if I move silence would be broken by the golden crisp
Fingers tickled by points and stems
Squandering the already dead into shattered pieces
The inevitable shredding of life
Fragile nature surrounds my world
Unwantingly I move in hopes that I would not crush organic miracles
Divine miracles that brighten days and more subtle nights
Harmony is what I desire to unite
Freedom of body and enlightenment
Only I weep clear falls and crush earth beneath
For my tears glacier the world11-16-2001,
