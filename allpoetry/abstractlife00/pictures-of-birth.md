Its the neverending journey
Searching for the means to the end
Gravitational resistence between emotion and reality
Spinning around the circle of love with giddy
As fertility of life becomes a gouge for the eyes
Birth...remembrance of the fatal attempt to be recognized by a truly silent world
The evolution kept in stillness by time
Telling no tales the hands just spin
Its the screaming that turns senility 
Almost sounding like hundreds of keys and pitches
Maybe from the growth of hollowness
Hollowness so deep it would put astronomist in awe
Dancing to the sonata another voice appears...
REVELY!!!
