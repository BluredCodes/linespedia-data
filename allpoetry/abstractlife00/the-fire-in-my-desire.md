Your eyes burn an an endless fire in my body,
So radiant with beauty and passion;
It torches the night sky with a brilliant red.
A color that defines every curve of your lips;
Subtle in every move of your body,
And screaming emotion with every word.
Sincere you are genuine...
Your emotion describes even the hardest of things,
Good in heart to the worst of bodies.
Appreciation is deserved of you, 
Happiness could hold you.
Desire I feel;
An emotion so raw- so real.12-28-1998,
