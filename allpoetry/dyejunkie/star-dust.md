title: "Star Dust"

A million stars in a daydream sky,
A million thoughts when you don't know why,
I ran to you to ease my pain,
To hold you close and keep from rain

My tears fall down a glistening light
Like star dust falling deep in the night
You kiss my face to remove the dew
And you hold me close till the night is through...
Until Morning.

With Love to whom this is for [You know who you are]
