A life could be foretold
In a twinkle from a star
But you cannot judge the way I am
Until you've come this far...

Looks alone can be decieving
As you only see this Porcelain Shell
We all lack the strengh and vision to see
The inner side as well

Inside she protects a heart of glass
So fragile and precious to hold
A wall around to keep you out
But would you ever be so bold?

Some percieve she's just a ditzy blonde!
Devoid of intelligent thought
But a purple dream within the stars
Has no price to be sold or bought

You stare unto those glistening pools
Of blues and greys, and wonder...
Can one person really stand alone
In this world of rainand thunder?

This Pisces doesn't swim too fast
Too many nets to get caught upon
She'll catch the tides and ride the currents
To find a soul worthy of her love to put on

So she'll carry through and ride her waves
Taking life's little bumps as she goes
Her path is blind, but still she shines
