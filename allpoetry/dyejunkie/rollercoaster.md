Nervous anticipation
Wanting...awaiting
Holding out for natures course to kick-start
A rollercoaster ride created at birth
How long will it last?
What ever happened to that tunnel of love?
That tipsy-turvy ride known as lifeWritten in Burger King waiting for friends to turn up :) It was a spur of the moment thing!
