i miss my head 

i miss my face

i miss my home

i miss my place

intuative informity



i miss my blue

i miss my sky

i miss my veil

i miss my bride

constuctive conformity



i miss my blade 

i miss my start

i miss my blood

i miss my heart

can i overcome



i miss my ink

i miss my pen

i miss it all

and i want to start over again



look at the sky, look at this place

look at me not deserving this face

look at me burning the back the hole

look at me as i lose control.



some say i've died

some say i'm zombified

some say i'm without pride

some say i'm instructified.





just following my instructions
