---
title: "Surviving the Storm"
writer: "Jdr"
slug: "surviving-the-storm"
source: "AllPoetry"

Morning reflects the fiction
of last nights storm.
And bluer eyes look long into the sky.
Thinking of you,
and the slammed door you left behined.
Right before the first crack of thunder
shook the soul.
Daylight brings the closure
we've desperately avoided.
And this day is the debris
of words not meant.
Walking toward the car
in the pouring rain.
You looked back and smiled
one last time.
I cried and yelled, and all the pain
leaked out like a salve.
Cursed the storm that embraced you
within its flashing glory.
Now I sit in the window.
Hallow in the shadowless morn'
Broken and lonely, but I survived the storm.
