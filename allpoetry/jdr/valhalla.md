Alas, time has come
Like a coiled serpent
kissing with
black forked tongue

Swords held high
Every eye facing
toward the infinity
of Odin's red sky

We march on
through the gates
of war-

As the screams
of life penetrate
distant shore

Alas, it comes
Like fire racing 
across my bones

And into
great halls
of Valhalla
My spirit goes
