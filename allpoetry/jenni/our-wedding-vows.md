The wedding vows we made
So many years ago,
Are still so very vivid,
They still warm my heart so.

Every ounce of me still yearns
For your touch and loving care.
My body, it still tingles
Whenever you are near.

Although we don't do now,
All the things we did before,
You still mean the world to me-
That and so much more.

I know you still love me too,
You show me all the time,
By the tender, loving things you do,
And by your patience, so sublime.

So on this, our twenty-third anniversary,
I simply want to say,
Thank you for being all that you are to me, 
Each and every day!



To Jeff,
With All My Love.
