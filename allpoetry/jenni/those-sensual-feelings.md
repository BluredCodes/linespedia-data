THOSE SENSUAL FEELINGS
Erotic Emotion...

Ah!! Once again this feeling of bliss,
I'd almost forgotten how badly I needed this!
Feeling my naked body being caressed,
Feeling my entire soul being possessed.

It's been a while since I've experienced this sensation,
My wet and warm body yielding to the sensation.
Riding those waves of ecstasy,
Living again my wildest fantasy.

While I lay waiting for those waves of passion,
With foam now covering my skin so ashen, 
I dream of things you always do to me,
And the sensations I experience while I'm alone with thee.

You thrill me so much, it is a shame,
To waste our time together playing this game.
I'd much rather be here riding you,
Until my body is black and blue.

So I guess I'll stay here a while longer,
And enjoy it as you get faster and stronger.
Anticipating the things you'll do to me,
While I'm alone skinny-dipping in the sea.
