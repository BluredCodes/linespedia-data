The silver chain and teardrop pendant

That encircles the femininity of your waist

And brings to me the most vivid of fantasies

Of you and me and the smooth connection

	of our flesh as one



The rocking motion- slow, then faster

An expression I can't describe on your face



Your body and mine, sliding- racing

Toward the pinnacle of our emotions



No, don't stop- it isn't over yet



The crystal refracts the light

Swinging in rhythm to our movements



The swell of your breast under my palm,

A chip of coral presses- I taste it



The scent, the feel, the words you say,

My mouth meets yours, tongues play



Hands and fingers explore- searching



The soft but firm roundness of your thighs

Pressed to mine- a tremble, a gasp of breath,



You in my arms, no need for words

To express what we feel in our soulssuch an emotion... I feel sorry for anyone who can't feel it fully
