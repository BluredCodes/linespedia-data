...and the silence deepens

        where even a whisper

        sends ripples

across the body of time.

my spirit flows with the sound

        leaving a trail

        of emotion

        and thought-

a never-ending path to follow.

will the sounds of nothing persist?

will the winds never change?

the voice of the night 

        speaks softly

        sending chills

        through the shadows

        leaving invisible tracks

        staining the void

        reaping

the desert harvest.

never will the sound of

        nothing

        be the same.
