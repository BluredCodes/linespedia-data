---
title: "Swept Away"
writer: "kyew"
slug: "swept-away"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58539-Swept-Away-by-kyew"
---

Our souls entwined-
misty moments of
smooth, rounded curves
and warm connections.
Like two autumn leaves,
drifting on the wind
within life itself.

Your little ways...
a touch here,
a word there,
draw me closer
every day.

Press your fingers
to my lips-
I taste your need.
Drowning in your eyes
your desires I fulfill.
You need but ask...

Swept away
in a whirlwind
of emotional bliss.
You write the songs
that make
my heart dance-
bestow your
fevered blessings
upon this
chilled brow
and I am whole.

Question not
why we love,
only that we do.
Zephyrs of 
moments sweet;
killing me gently-
now reviving.

Swept away-
returning with less.
Ascending notes struck
soundlessly
upon the
xylophone of captured
seconds
unwind-
loosing the bonds
holding me earthbound.

I soar above the clouds
holding you close-
just you and I
embedded in
velvet foam,
graceful cushions
of yearning and
I am
swept away.
