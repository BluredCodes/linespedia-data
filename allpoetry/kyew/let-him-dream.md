---
title: "Let Him Dream"
writer: "kyew"
slug: "let-him-dream"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

A child's wonder enraptures him
Dimming his sight with fantasies

His eyes unfocused
	seeing other worlds

These visions held dear
Cherished as valuable treasure

A memory fades with time
But the dreams grow
	with a life of their own

Wishes for a reality that could be
	has already been
And the world drains their colors

Constant wear of water on stone
The treasure is revealed
	'baubles in pewter laid'
Sensibility cures the cataracts
	of imagination and musings

He holds to his dreams
	no matter how unreal
        in danger of being discarded
To follow them into oblivion

Adulthood- the killer of dreams
It strikes as the hidden viper
	suddenly deadly

What harm can come of an open mind?

And when the world shuns
	this man of vision
Stand by him-

Let him dream.


who can consciously destroy a child's dreams? there are some that can
