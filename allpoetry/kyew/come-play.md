Come play with me.
A gentle touch have I
and tender lips
to sip as much
of you as I
can take within.

~

Come play with me.
My hands are soft
and even strong.
Here they long
as so oft
they do
for a touch
of you.

~

Come play with me.
Curl up in my lap-
lay your head down
while I stroke your hair
listen to your sighs
feel your breath
on my skin.

~

Come play with me.
Enjoy the day without
ever leaving the bed-
the sun lying warmly
across the sill,
draped upon
your smooth flesh.

~

Come play-
A game we both know-
a lover’s dance
to entrance
and bestow
what we feel
in our souls.
