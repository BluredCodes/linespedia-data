title: "A Peace"

I dream of you all day and night

	and still I wonder

	what would become

	of me

	without you



My thoughts still wander

	in and out

	beneath the shade,

	meandering slowly

	between the trees,

Of a love for which I've searched my soul



Among the rocks

	the darkened corners

	and even the light,

I seek what I have never found,

	knowing one day

	I'll end my journey

	with success

Or with such a pain too great to bear



And I will live...

	or I will die...

	but I will find

A peace for my jaded and callused heartwill I?
