How to paint this river's rush
	what color do I use to make it
	warm when the sun shines and
	cool in the cloud's shadow?

What kind of brush would cause
	the sound of rapids
	washed across rocks
	like an unchanging wind?

There is no canvas that splashes
	cold mountain water
	onto your shocked skin

Or pushes sand between
	your toes.

I have never seen paint
	that smells of pine
	and earth.

What mortal artist could capture
	the endlessness
	of outdoor nights?

Who could portray the slipperiness
	of a salamander
	in your hand?

And who would think to add
	that small, lost dog
	so free and unhurried
	that graciously accepted
	a gift of food
	and companionship
Then, as easily, said goodbye.

written while I was camping... I just had to 'paint' it
