Mother, can you see me now?

	The wall is gone

	you need but turn-

But turn slowly and carefully.



Mother, have you grown wiser?

	Please, not more clever

	with a deeper bag of tricks-

Let the games be over.



Mother, I have learned to love.

	Love so selfish

	and demanding-

Demanding selflessness from you.



Mother, I can show you love.

	No peaks or valleys

	to obscure the broadness-

The larger view of One.



Mother, have you learned to accept?

	If I give to you

	what you took from me-

Take me for granted.



Mother, I wish you could know.

	I would tell you

	but I can't-

Force the words, SEE my words.



Mother, why the tears?

	Life is joyful

	pain-

Feel the pain in happiness.



Mother, I will hold you.

	When the night is cold

	and dark, do not fear-

We are never alone.



Mother, this is yours.

	This barrier of

	ego-self-

I trade you me for Us.



Mother, am I really dying?
