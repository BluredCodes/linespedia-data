---
title: "She Moves"
writer: "kyew"
slug: "she-moves"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

With a gracefulness that speaks volumes
        of erotic poetry,

She moves

The smile on her face lifts my spirit
        to new heights when

She moves

I anticipate her stopping point
        but I'm miles away when
                again

She moves

While the sun and the moon
        and the stars stand still

She moves

I turn away but for a second
        -one second too long-

She moves


see me standing here, please...
