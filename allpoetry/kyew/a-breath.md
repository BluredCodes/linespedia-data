Anticipation boils my blood,

I watch you come to me.

The shape of your body;

the curve of breast and hip.



I want to taste you-

to explore with my tongue.

You leave me with no desire-

no will but to please you.

And in that I am satisfied.



A gentle touch to make you tremble

a caress to hear you breathe

of passion.



Say you want me to,

Tell me what you need

and it is yours- anything.



My body pressed to yours

a thin film of sweat.

Salt from your lips to mine.

More pressure- a grind and slide,

a smooth machine.



Don't stop now,

make the moment last.



I cool you from the heat

of lovemaking with
