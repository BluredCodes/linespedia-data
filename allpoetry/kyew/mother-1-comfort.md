Mother, should I build the wall?

It's not difficult

very simple-

To forget all I've learned.



Mother, can I sleep with you?

I need to feel

your inside-ness-

To know you from without.



Mother, will I ever know?

I wish to understand

what you see in me-

What makes you call me 'pretty boy'.



Mother, where will I go?

The search is endless

and ever so tedious-

Wearing the inside out.



Mother, do you know me yet?

You have seen

my dark side-

The turmoil in my soul.



Mother, why do you run away?

I have no place to hide

from the watchers-

The ones who would tear me apart.



Mother, can I play you a song?

I know your favorite

well enough-

Enough to know you hate it.



Mother, will I be alone?

When the nights are cold

and dark, I fear-

Afraid to be the One.



Mother, may I write some words?

I can build the wall

with the stones-

Bricks of spoken language.



Mother, does it have to be so high?
