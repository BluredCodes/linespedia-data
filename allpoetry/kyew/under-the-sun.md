...and in the warm light
of the summer sun
that masked the frivolity
of the night-play

We wandered the fields
with spirits soaring

In the heat of the day
we would lie beneath
that old oak
and watch as the light
spattered on the leaves

Sun-dappled shadows
spun and twisted
upon our faces

We would run
to fall in the grass
clinging to each other
laughing from the depths
of our souls

How we found
that warm summer pond
and played
so carefree as only
two creatures of the earth can be
loving life
and living love
under the sun
