---
title: "Kitten"
writer: "kyew"
slug: "kitten"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Purr for me, Kitten- 
that purple vibration 
sifts my thoughts. 
The chaff rises to be 
blown away by 
the easiness of 
intertwined moments. 

Play with me, Kitten- 
losing ourselves 
in the leaf-pile 
of frivolous time. 
The days will pass 
and with them 
the tears and 
the smiles. 

Feel with me, Kitten- 
memories to store 
and sample later 
like old love letters. 
The fermented images 
making the sweet wine, 
nostalgia. 

Be with me, Kitten- 
curl up on my chest 
while I stroke 
your hair. 
Intimacy so innocent 
as the warm spring sun 
lying comfortably 
in a window nook. 

Climb my tree, Kitten- 
the strong limbs and 
thick trunk and 
rough bark. 
Climb to the highest boughs 
where I cradle you 
in dangerous safety 
while the wind rocks me. 

Find me, Kitten- 
an adventure inward 
where no borders 
interrupt. 
See the vast wildness of 
diversity where 
everyone is strange. 

Come to me, Kitten- 
meet me wherever 
the wind 
does not follow. 
Search me out-
inside and out-
satisfying 
your curiosity. 



written about an ex-g/f... she was playful and the name stuck in my mind for her
