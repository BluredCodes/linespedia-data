---
title: "Away She Flies"
writer: "kyew"
slug: "away-she-flies"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56357-Away-She-Flies-by-kyew"
---

As the purple shade drops
	from my eyes, still
	I cannot see her

I wander through this
	distorted reality thinking
But never knowing exactly
	which way to turn

The earth drops away
	in front of me
And I am left standing
	on a pinnacle of unbelief

A step backward into nothing-
	the past is gone

Away she flies without
	the briefest farewell
But the end is yet to come

A ceaseless wind cradles
	her memory to mine
And within I find a means;
	a path to resistance

Oh, but it is a dark road
	this that I travel

No warmth to be found
	and I wonder
Who will be the next to fall?

Away she flies and I know
	it will not be her...

But could it? Yes, it could
	it could easily be meabout suicide... nothing more to say
