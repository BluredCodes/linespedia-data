title: "Summer Rain"
writer: "kyew"

A peal of thunder
in the distance;
The turbid sky roils
a stiffening
breeze-
A harbinger of rain

A fresh moist scent
	fills the air
	with damp
	anticipation

Colors take on
	a darkened vibrance -
	a fundamental yearning

Warm drops fall-
	the thick petals
	of some hybrid rose
Enveloping the body
	(and the mind)
Spattering the pages
	of an often visited
	story of love
	never found

The cocoon of this feeling
	the silken web
	that holds
	emotion within
Parts ever so slightly
	to release
	the power
	of the storm


storms can give a bit of their power to you if you know how to ask...
