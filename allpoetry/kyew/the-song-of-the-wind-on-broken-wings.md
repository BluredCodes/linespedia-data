---
title: "The Song of the Wind on Broken Wings"
writer: "kyew"
slug: "the-song-of-the-wind-on-broken-wings"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56204-The-Song-of-the-Wind-on-Broken-Wings-by-kyew"
---

When I am afraid I speak your name
in awe of the strength in this talisman.
Would you hold me on a cold, dark night
when the only sound is 
the song of the wind on broken wings?

Would you comfort me in your embrace 
whispering the meaning to
the song of the wind on broken wings?

When I am alone I forget your name
captured by the cruelty of the gods.
Could I forgive when the storms
lash and howl at me of
the song of the wind on broken wings?

Could I forget when I awake in the night
hearing your name in
the song of the wind on broken wings?

When I have escaped, I listen for your name but
it doesn't come to mind and I die.
Could I relive those few simple moments
of blissful peace before
the song of the wind on broken wings?

Could I deny the chill grip of death
while my soul screams the chorus to 
the song of the wind on broken wings?

But life would not be as sweet because
it is I who sings
the Song of the Wind on Broken Wings.
