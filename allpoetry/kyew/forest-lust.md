title: "Forest Lust"
writer: "kyew"

Oh, the density from afar;
	the forest looms
	spreading its quiet
	solitary presence
Far beyond its boundaries

On silent feet, I approach,
	cautiously and with
	great wonder

The individual icons of age-
	the trees that
	harbor its essence-
Seem so stolid, almost watching

I feel the weight of my
	intellect pressing

Do I belong?
Am I welcome?
As I shed the trappings
	of humanity,
	the mystery of it all
	enraptures me

A primal fear engulfs my mind
	leaving me with
	a bronze taste
Of feral survival

The air becomes a mode
	of communication

My senses have expanded
	and multiplied...

Surrogate Mother, I have come
Brothers and Sisters, I have come
Heed my call and answer
This creature that I am
	knows you

I pass through, disturbing nothing
	and leaving only
	my message of musk
To review the day with
	small breaths,
	casually sorting
	(precursor to the hunt)
Fills me with longing-
	carnal desire
A need to devour the weak
	the injured
	the sick
	the dying
	the slow
	the unwise
It courses through my veins
The strength of the predator
	permeates my body,
	crushing my pseudo-will
Giving me a blood obsession
The path is clear; I follow

Night grows and I greet it
	passingly with respect
It is my ally- a trusted friend
A companion to share my lust

I can never return
	to that world of light
	where monstrous noises
	Invade my tranquility;
Where noxious odors
	drive my consciousness
	within to hide

I can no longer ignore
	primal instinct-
	baser urges
	of being alive

I have come home and nothing
	can tempt me away.
