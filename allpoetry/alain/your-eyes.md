---
title: "Your eyes"
writer: "Alain"
slug: "your-eyes"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Like golden pebbles, phosphorus branches, blazing stars, 
are your hungry and ravenous eyes. 
Like spirals, and climbing ivy 
they unravel me. 
You have scattered, uprooted me from the wooden wharves, 
where I stood and wrangled me in your tempestous sea. 

I saw twilights forming in your Eyes 
Whispering comets encircle them. 
I swear I saw shimmering stars aligning forming your name. 
The moon, shooting wandering stars,
were dissolving in them my frenzied thoughts. 
Constellations, Galaxies, Universes converged 
into one to keep me eager for thee. 

Now, I want to clasp you in my arms, 
look into your eyes, and hold you tight. 
I need to have your undivided attention 
and fill you with my love.
