Abruptly, 
My heart unfastens with beautiful words and songs. 
For my lovely radiant mistress 
Underneath the glowing sun. 

Abruptly, 
My heart swells at the sight of my loved one. 
My heart swoons, leaps, and dances. 
As a frivolous firefly lost at night. 
. 

Abruptly, 
My heart fills with humble tender devotion. 
Like a mother hen with her chicks. 
My being loves, serves, and pampers 
At the one who pierced my heart, mind, and soul. 

I hear honeysuckles composing music for you. 
The butterfly’s dance, gyrate, around them. 
Bees and birds participate hypnotized, 
In orbital acrobatic mating trances. 
I hear their music in quiet nature stanzas 

Abruptly, 
As you walk by. 
Your beauty obliges gardens, orchards, and groves. 
To dress in wardrobes of multi-colored flowers and roses. 
I follow you, strolling among them with open arms and hands. 
Exuberant with face upwards towards the sky and sun. 
Breathing in traces of perfume, fragrance and scents. 
You left behind. 
. 

Abruptly 
I follow you with blind stupor senses, 
Like a lonely tango 
Playing alone 
To be heard. 
By two lovers 
In a vesper tine shaded patio courtyard.
