---
title: "And when I die"
writer: "Alain"
slug: "and-when-i-die"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

And when I die, all you will have left are my words. 
Let it be these. 
I never loved anyone as 
Intensely, 
profoundly, 
Spiritually, 
physically, 
as you. 

You are 
Lovely 
Tender 
gentle 
Pure 
soft 
luscious. 

You are aromatic, with ocean-smell, sea sand fragrance, terrestial flower. 

You are delightfull, bountiful, feminine, simple, enchanting. 

Your love empowered my life with kindness and care. 

With you by my side I lived!, I cried, I loved. 

And when I sleep. 
Earth, Sun, and sky will envelop you. 
In its warmth you will feel my touch.
