title: "Near to me, Near to you"

Near to me 
The glow that shines on your face is keeping me
Near to you
I keep you smiling underneath your eyes
Your lips are keeping me inside your arms
touching you
touching me
Oh tell me what you've done to me?

Sunflowers sitting at your feet
Your gaze is like golden wheatfields
in open spaces.

You see you are my harvest and
kindling fire.
Near to me!
Thats where I want to be
near to you!

I will only stare at walls, to 
have you near me

I can't stand growing one day older
without you
Near to me
Near to you

I know what they meant
when poets of old said
Love is eternally!
