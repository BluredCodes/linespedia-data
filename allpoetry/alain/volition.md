---
title: "Volition"
writer: "Alain"
slug: "volition"
source: "AllPoetry"

I am writing without seeing what I'm writing.
My heart dictates what I need to see.
My soul hides what it blemishes.
I want to prepare a thousand roads
for my steps and a thousand lives to walk upon them.
