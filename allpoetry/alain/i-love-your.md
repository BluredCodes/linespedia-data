title: "I love your"

I love your laughter
your heart
your feet,
your body,
your thighs,
your eyes,
your soul,
your kisses,
your sweet whispers and llulabys

I love and adore your womanhood,
the languid repose of my spent love.

I love your white breasts and lithe arms
I love you completely and endlessly
like the world loves spring.

I give myself to you freely
without fear or shame
like wintersnow on pine trees.
