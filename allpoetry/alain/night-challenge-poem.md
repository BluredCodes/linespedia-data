The night hearkens unto me, 
as it speaks to my heart 
" I bring pleasure and delight 
becuase I am night your song 
queen and maiden of love, 
where lovers meet and sweet surrender to me". 

"Come with me 
where the bells chime 
and you will see, 
I am made to serve you in quiet demeanor." 

I watch the moon and the stars 
announce your glorious entrance 
through my life like a key 
your love! 
oh darkness 
conquers me. 

to your light 
my face 
to my sight 
your eyes 
walking alone through a courtyard 
I looked up at the red moon with its crescent flowers 
The lonely towers 
The silent phosperous clouds pass me 
the ice filled air numb my cheeks 
and I was contented least 
with my voice and my prayer 
I hear you whisper 
"I am night 
your rest 
your beauty 
and your life"
