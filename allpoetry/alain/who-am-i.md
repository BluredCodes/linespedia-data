---
title: "Who am I?"
writer: "Alain"

My name is who am I?
because I am whatever you want me to be.
One moment I'm your brave and noble knight
and the next I'm your trouble, pain, 
and a Sean Penn wannabe.

So I have decided that I will be whoever 
you want me to be Fabio, Pierce, or Brad Pitt
and whenever you need me just ring the bell 
and call on me.


Just want to tell you that whoever I am
I will always love you for all you are,
My bastion, My guiding light, and my parakeet
But wait I hear it coming "I don't listen to you
and I don't put down the toilet lid"
Who am I? I don't know but I'll stay clear away from 
you the pots, pans, and "ouch" the bread roller, and me.
