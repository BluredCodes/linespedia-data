title: "The sea shell"
writer: "Alain"

I've been living inside a sea shell. 
Venturing out only when no one can see. 
This is how I've seen the most beautiful things 
like golden tan thighs and lovely puffed rumps, laying open to the sun. 

Let me tell you what else I have seen. 
I observed sirens in mourning, because they 
are not without tails. 
I've tiptoad around grumpy Poseidon when he's asleep. 
Blackbeard was just a legend in his own mind! 
I know where all the treasures chests are hidden! 
I have cruised in deep remote oceanic dwellings. 
I have seen the secrets and glory of Atlantis. 
I've slept in Neptune's emerald bossom. 

So next time when your strolling by the sea-sand 
just be careful don't step on a seashell 
it might just be me!
