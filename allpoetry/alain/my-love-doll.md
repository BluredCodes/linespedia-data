title: "My love doll"
writer: "Alain"

My precocious and stray love doll. 
The one with the hidden smile, timid eyes, 
and furtive glances. 

My love-doll is like a new-born star, 
radiant and sparkling. 
You illuminate my eyes like an incandescent diamond, 
whirling and voluminous. 

I am your priest my love doll, drunk and apathetic 
Ignorant and foolish in need of understanding. 
My love-doll, I adore your light-brown hair, spiraled, 
and pastoral. 
Where were you conceived? and when did you appear germinated, 
flowered, sweet and perfumed? 

You are like Eve to me. Pure and translucent 
delicate and sensitive as a lily. 
Forgive me, If I ever should forget your innate beauty. 
take my life, my love doll if you will 
but never! never! stop loving me.
