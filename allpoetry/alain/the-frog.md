At dusk,
Amidst the shadowy misty fog.
A green frog rests peacefully
sitting on a waterlily.

A flail, a swagger,and a flutter,
is all the unsuspecting dragonfly
perceives
as its caught and swallowed
in midflight terror.

The frog lies still
wallowing in midnight tales.
Wondering what his next meal will entail.
