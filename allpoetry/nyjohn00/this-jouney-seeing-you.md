This Journey, Seeing You
Days go on
See into you more
The depths of your feelings
The warmth of your hearts embrace
The ways you show you care to others 
The beauty of your smile
I picture you bathed in moonlight
Your skin and hair a glow
Sitting so close
The essence of you surrounding me
Our faces almost touching
Looking deep into your eyes
Our lips reach to caress
Feeling you love flow to me
As our linked hearts touch and hold
Their embrace is one of love
A hearts caress of oneness
A craving to see only more
