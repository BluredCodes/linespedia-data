Until We Kiss Again

The feeling will not leave
Though I do not want it to
Asking, we both received
And that, I do not regret

Now, we are apart
Each in their own life
A part but still as one
Yet drawn together

Apart, yet closer still
And the kisses that we shared
Still linger, my lips remember
They hunger for you so

Until we shall kiss again
