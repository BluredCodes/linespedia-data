A question I've heard in a song
Asked in lyrics so innocently
The music plays on
The song talked about the feelings felt
The thrill of love, newly felt
The romance of the summer meadow
Walking on the shore
Listening to the sound of the waves
Hear them whispering, "I love you"
Music speaks of laughter and smiles
Of yearning and wanting you by my side
But then the question is asked
What happens when the music stops
Will you still love me so
Want me there wherever you go
Yearn for my hand in yours
And be my love forever more
I answer the question from my point of view
With you,,, and you with me
The music will never stop
