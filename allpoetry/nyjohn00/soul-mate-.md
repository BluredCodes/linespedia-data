Soul Mate :)

You are my Soul Mate,
The one that I adore,
Every day I thank God
For allowing us to be

There is a place deep,
In the depth of my heart
A place just for you
Safe and secure, surrounded in love

It is so plain
Others always see
The magic when we're together
You were made for me.

You are my Soul Mate,
The only one for me.
Our  passion and love,
Sets our souls free

I know that you see
All the things we share
And I know we will always
Always be together
