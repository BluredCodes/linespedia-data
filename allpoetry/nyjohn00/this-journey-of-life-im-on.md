This journey of life I'm on
Learning you, and all you are
Seeing your smiles
Making them mine
Feeling your sadness
Making it mine 
If I could really see
All that is you
I would hold it, embrace it 
Cherish it always
There is a touching heart to heart
It is more intimate than loves encompassing kiss
It brings the tears of joy, of sadness
More dear than a life of talking
It allows us to feel what a loved one feels
To see with their eyes, just what is inside
Your heart has touched mine
In ways I never dreamed
You have become a part of my life
Mistakes have been made
Thoughts conveyed, Emotions felt
At times I feel, I want you too much
Wanting your time, Wanting you near
Knowing it is a dream, a muse of my mind
I'll continue to listen,,,,
Learning your mind
