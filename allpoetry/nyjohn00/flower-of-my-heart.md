Flower Of My Heart

A plant raises its head above the soil
Pushing up into the air above
Millions just like it, as far as you see
All look the same
Nothing to tell them apart
Bathed in sunlight
Nurtured from below
Drinking in the rain
Basking in the sun
Breathing the sweet air of spring
But then something happens
It senses the time is right
A flower comes forth
Beautiful petals
Soft, delicate and fragile
Exposed to all around
Open to the world
Dangers on all sides
Open for you to enjoy
This delicate flower
Is so much like my heart
My heart sensed you there
It felt the time was right
It opened up to you
Gave you all its love
It is there for you alone
Petals of my heart
For you to savor deeply
Flower of my heart
Blooming forth
All because of you
