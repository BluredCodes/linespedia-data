Their once was a pair
A couple of ladybugs you see
And all they wanted to do
Was dance this dance of life
They danced all over the place
Always close as could be
And in there hearts 
They understood
Just how they were meant to be
Together they were
Each day was treasured
They needed the love of the other
So on with life
They will dance some more
Always dancing together
