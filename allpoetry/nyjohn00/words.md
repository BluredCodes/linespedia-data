Words can be words,,, 
Words can be thoughts,,,,
Feelings straight from the heart
Words can paint a picture of a dream
They can lighten your heart
Make you walk on air
Making you whole
They can tell of your love
Describing just how you feel
I choose my words with my heart
They are just for my loved one
I try to give her a glimpse
Into the depths of my heart
Needing her to see
She is from heaven above
