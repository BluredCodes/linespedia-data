Feeling like a very old knight
Armour scared and dented
Its' been through so many battles
I spend my time polishing it
To make it shiny for you
For you my fairest lady of all
Deserve nothing less than my best
For if I can't be your shiny knight
On my steed I will ride away
And let you be for a deserving knight
Who will be the one of your dreams
For as I said, my beauty sweet
Only the best for you
