I awake to the soft sounds of waves
They hiss so softly as they roll in
I picture each one I hear
As they kiss this sandy beach
As my senses return from slumbers repose
I feel you gently stir
Moving so softly in my arms
Your head upon my chest
Feeling your breath on my skin
I kiss your hair and whisper your name
Thanking you for your love
From where I lay on the pillow
Holding my love so tight
I see the tip of the sun
Escaping darkness's clutch
It edges over the rim of the world
The start of a new day for us
But in this moment I see us
A life with you my love
How each day is to be fresh and new
As it rises higher
I see its light sparkle on the waves
Each one tipped with a diamond
They glitter and dance toward shore
Each one oh so bright
But dull when compared to your smile
And then the sun clears the rim
It takes its place in the sky
Banishing darkness, lighting our day
Just as you bring light to my life
And take my darkness away
Then looking down at you
I see you looking up at me
Without a word I see your eyes
Telling me you love me so
With the sweetest smile you pull my lips to yours
And we share our loves caress
And as you hold me
I look at the sun
A tear flows from my eye
Thinking of how
Much I love you
You my precious true love
