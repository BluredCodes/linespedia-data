title: "A Gift To See"

You are the person 
With a gift  
You see into my heart 
Seeing things I never knew were there
I look into your heart 
Melting at the vision so stunning 
I begin to cry, 
Cry at the wonder of the beauty inside 
Two souls so close together 
Two souls bound together
Sharing the same gift of love 
.
