I'm afraid you'll never know 
How much you mean to me                                                                                                                           I could list a thousand reasons 
Using words from A to Z 
But I don't know how to write a hug 
Or how to read a kiss 
I don't know how to touch a soul                                                                                                              And even more than this 
Words can't love or comfort you 
When the world is closing in 
And when you're hurt or ailing 
They can't caress your tender skin 
When all these things you are to me 
Then how can I convey 
My life would end, my dreams would die 
If you ever walked away 
To tell you how I feel 
A million words would fail 
So would a million more 
They would be to no avail 
You are my very life's blood 
You have touched me to the core 
Once a ship lost on the oceans 
In you I've found the shore 
You are the sunshine and the rain 
To the flowers of my soul 
I felt a part of me was missing 
But with you my life feels whole 
So read between these picture full words
and see the image that is real
It's the only chance I have 
To conveying what I feel 
Still I'm afraid you'll never know 
How much you mean to me 
You're all I can conceive 
You are my infinity
