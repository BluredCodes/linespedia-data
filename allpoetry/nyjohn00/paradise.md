Come with me to an island in the sea
Where the warm air blows its lovers caress
Where the sea is clear and oh so blue
Its color matching the sky
Walk these beaches with me
Hand 'n hand in the warmth of love

Lay with me in the moonlight
The warm sand beneath us
The moonbeams shimmering in your hair
The sand sparkles like diamonds
Looking into your eyes
I melt into your soul

Kiss me and make love to me
Tell me I'm your love
I want your heart held close to mine
As we make love 
Laying on a bed of diamonds
In a paradise of our own
