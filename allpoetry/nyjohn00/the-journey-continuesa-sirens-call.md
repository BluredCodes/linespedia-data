This journey of your mind
The endless depth of your feelings
The more I learn 
The more I see
The more I need to explore
I've seen you paint my life
With the colors of your rainbow
Taking it from drabs of gray
Giving it the colors of life
Though you don't really know it
You touch my heart with each word
I listen to your words
To the ways they twist and flow
They tease and beckon
A sirens call from old
These visions in your mind
Capture my very soul
