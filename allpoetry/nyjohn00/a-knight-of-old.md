A knight of old
A warrior it is told
Found his maiden
In a deep forest glen
He fell immediately under her spell
His heart felt like it was hanging over a well
He opened his heart
He knew right from the start
To open was to risk pain
He knew it was not in vain
For in her eyes he saw
A love that held him in awe
