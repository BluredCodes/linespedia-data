The Sunsets

We have spent our day together
Never have I felt so close to anyone
How you fill my thoughts
You are all my loves, wants, desires
Your smiles fill my heart
So as today's sun sets 
As we sit on this beach of life
Sitting with our backs against a log
Feeling the warmth of the sand
It is nothing compared to the warmth we have inside
We sit, arms around each other
And watch the sun defeated by nights darkness
Slowly descend into its slumber
There will be no darkness for us
The brightness of our love
Brightness to light all the dark corners 
Will illuminate our way, and keep us safe, together
Think of all the days to come
 Filled with the wonder of us
Think of our love growing stronger
Love that will never know any bounds
Think of the understanding of souls
So linked whether together or apart
That they just understand the others
Thoughts, needs and wants
Think of us and all the sunrises and sunsets to come
