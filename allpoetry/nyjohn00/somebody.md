Somebody


There is a person who misses your laughter
To him it is the sound of angel bells ringing in heaven
The touch of your hands, the sound of your voice
Is all I desire, day in and day out

There is a person, who misses the wonderful, 
The love in all you do
The secrets of your heart, the dreams you expose
The way you make wishes come true

There is a person who waits for tomorrow
A day when we can be together
No more longing, no more yearning
This person will hold you forever
