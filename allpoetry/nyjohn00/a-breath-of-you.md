A breath of you
Breathing you so deeply
Wanting the essence of you
Drawing it deep into me
I want this essence of you
In each and every cell
When I hold you in my arms
I feel myself tremble inside
In awe of you
Desire for you
Need to breathe in your thoughts
To make all you want mine
I will know your desires
Give you all you need
As I breathe all you are
And give my love to you
