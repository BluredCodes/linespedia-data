---
title: "Needing You Near"

Tears in my eyes as I think of you
Wanting you near me
My heartache grows
The times keep us apart
Time that means so much
Holidays apart, days on end
The yearning grows
This love so pure
When will it end
I need you next to me
This I hold true
For without you near
My life is empty 
Like a shell on the beach
Only a memory
