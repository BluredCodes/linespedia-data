I saw you today
You were precious 
So beautiful in my eyes
If I were to describe
The woman of my dreams
Describe down to the last detail
The list would describe you
If I were to make a list of all the things I love
The list would go on and on
On all I see in you
Thank you sweetheart
For all the love you give
I pray always
To hold your love as mine
