The feel of our kiss
Is breathtaking to me
It starts with the sweetest lips pressed to mine
The hint of your tongue searching to entwine
It deepens in passion as the seconds tick by
It feels like we are drawing each other inside
Our tongues play a game of tag to be sure
Arms embrace as we pull into lovers embrace
We try to tell with only our sighs
Just how much love is held inside
This love it flows from lip to lip
A kiss of love, as only ones in love can feel
