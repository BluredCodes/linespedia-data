The scent of you

Walking on the beach with you
The warm ocean breeze on our faces
It carries the scent of your perfume
A fragrance so pure, nothing can compare
It addicts it fills my senses
The essence of you
An aroma so clean 
It is of your heart
I breathe it so deeply
I want it part of me
It reminds me of your presence
In all of my thoughts
I feel our first kiss
The first I love you
I breathe it so deeply
Never to let go
