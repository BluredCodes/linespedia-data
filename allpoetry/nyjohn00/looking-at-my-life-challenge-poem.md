Looking at my life
So many things I've done
I see now more clearly
All the paths gone by
So many things done right
Others well what if
My life would have been much better
If I had just looked inside
For in my heart I've know for so long
It has yearned for a love so strong
A love like no other
A love of hearts linked as one
A love where warmth abides
Where it is given with nothing wanted in return
No manipulation, no hidden agendas
A love that is so pure
Wanting nothing for itself
But given to each other
If I had looked inside
I would have know it then
I would have looked and looked
I would have found you my love
