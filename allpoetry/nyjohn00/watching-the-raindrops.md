Watching the raindrops
Running down the window pane
Falling so far
As if from heaven above
The angels brought us together
They opened our eyes
Letting us see
And as I ponder
Wishing for you
Listening to the music
The soulful lament of a Celtic flute
It is as if the angel above 
They listen to this too
And the raindrops are really
Their tears at wanting us together
They saw us, flounder about
They saw us going about life
But not really feeling the love that was due
They let us feel, let us see
We might see in each other
What other never will
We were made to be together
Never to part
And so the angels cry
Feeling our want
Want for each other
Together
