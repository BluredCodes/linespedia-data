I wish I could see
All you see in me
You tell me I'm this wonderful guy
A dream to you I seem
But when I think of all she said
And you know who she was
All the pain those words caused
A lot of doubts inside
Wounds that fester
Never to heal again
And looking into the mirror
All the Insecurities
Rush into my head
I see a man
Nothing special
Not as young as before
Hair that has a touch of gray
And no smile when you're not here
A face with little wrinkles at the eyes
They have seen so much
But have seen nothing
Till they looked and saw you
