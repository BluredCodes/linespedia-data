title: "I want to be the light"

I want to be the sun
Filling your day with color
Blue sky and sun
Should be your daily companion
A pleasant breeze
Flowers in bloom
Birds full of song

I want to be the moon at night
Lighting your pathways in dreamy light
The magic of the night
And my thoughts will be the stars
Twinkling for your delight
Looking at the sky you will see eternity
