This Journey of discovering you
Days and months go on their ways
Moments filled with passion
The glimpse of your heart
You capture a moment in a single sigh
A smile in your eye
An embrace were we melt together
The passion that transcends 
All distance without end
The yearning for the touch
Of your lips on mine
Where time will stop
And we will whirl into a universe of our own
As our lips explore
The love that is felt
The feelings grow with each new view of you
More and more the wonder of you grows
The yearning for more                                                                 
Desire to be filled
With all that you are
All that you will be
To know you completely
And still want you more
