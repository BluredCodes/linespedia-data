Him next to you

I breathe you deeply
Wanting you to be mine
Yet when we are apart
I see you next to him
I know how we are
And how you say you are

But my heart still pictures
Him next to you

I hear your laughter 
The excitement in your voice
The way we walk hand in hand
Joking and teasing
Seeing this and that
The smile in your eyes

But my heart still pictures
Him next to you

Closing my eyes
I can hear your voice
Telling me you love me
Love with all of your heart
Feel your lips touching mine
Heart as one

But my heart still pictures
Him next to you
