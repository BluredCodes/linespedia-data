Waking from a dream
Struggling to hold
The images so clear a moment ago
We were wrapped together
Impossible to be closer
I still felt your lips as slumber ended
My heart yearns for that moment 
To hold you like that
Your arms so tight around me
The passion in your eyes
Your urgent sighs
The feel of your lips as nibble with delight
Legs intertwined 
Pressing, pulling so tight
Together making love
For hours on end
The need never slackens
The fever complete
Then the moment is gone
And I'm left with the yearns
Of you my sweet love
