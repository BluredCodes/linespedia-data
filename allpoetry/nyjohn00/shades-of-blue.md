My day began to show its shades of blue 
As thoughts came falling back to you
And precious times pass without a reason                                                                                         
The soft air brings the spring of season

Long before these days of warmth delight 
I wished upon the brightest star one night 
And as quick as night to day                                                                                
My blessed wish soon came my way

Like the rain upon the fields in drought 
Or the ending of my longing doubt 
My greatest dream did manifest 
As I lay within your arms to rest

In my desolate and darkest hour 
I found you my dearest flower 
Felt your kisses of passion, thoughts blend as the tide 
felt your love burn me deep inside

For your smile awake my sleeping spring
 And love into my life you bring
if As the sun falls behind the sea                                                                                                                           
Your Shades of Blue I can still see
