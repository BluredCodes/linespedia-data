Wings of a Dove


Dreaming at night
Flying across a black starry sky
A sense of freedom
You never feel when awake

Touching the unknown
Seeing what you've never seen
Soaking it all in
"Off to Never-Never Land"

Flying on the back of the wings of a dove
Getting a sensation of heaven
All pain is gone
Kissing the sweet dew of the morning

Waking from a fantastic dream
Looking over to see your sun-kissed face
Still flying on the wings of a dove
I kiss you, thankful that you are near me still
Knowing I will never fly alone again
