A nightmare without end
On and on
Over and over
I see the cycle
Again and again
A spouse who is manic
A child who they say
 Has the illness only more
Like his mother but with
Effective you see
Mania and schizophrenia 
Normal on his med
It's the alcohol and drugs
Watching him spiral
Going down and down
Was difference when younger
Was able to help
Now all I can do is pray and hope
For the peace of his soul
He is destroying his life
Hurting the ones around him
Seeing the love of his life broken
Crying in my arms
Asking me why, again and again
I wish I could take
All their pain away
I'd gladly take it
I can't even cry
The terror I feel
Seeing my child
God, I ask why
