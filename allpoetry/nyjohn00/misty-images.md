Misty images
Something moving
Shadows blend 
Shadows touching and then move apart
I see the image of us together
It is there right in front of me
A look of happiness on our faces
Laughter is heard in the breeze
I see images of being one
Of spending our days together
Of days to months
Months to years
A life full of you
It looks so real
This image I see
It drifts in the mist 
Surrounding me
My heart longs
God it wants
It feels that it is close
I reach to hold this sight
But as I reach 
It disappears
And I'm left to my thoughts
Thoughts filled with love
Longing for you
Trying to make it so
