I have a friend a princess you see
She confides in me
So many things
Her hopes and her dreams
A love that she found
She kept her hopes
For love to be all around
Today she told me her love is astray
Her heart feels like breaking
My heart ached for her
She has touched me with all that she says
I felt helpless and useless
I could not mend this rend
I want her to know
That I will always be there
My shoulders are hers
And I’ll take her painHug to you my friend,,,Princess
