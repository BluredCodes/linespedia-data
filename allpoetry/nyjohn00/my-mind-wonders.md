My mind wonders
Do you love me the way I love you?
Do you miss me the way I miss you?
Do you burn for the touch of my hand in yours?
Do you burn for my embrace?
Do you yearn for the smile that I freely give to you?
Do you feel the love I offer unconditionally? 
When I look into your eyes,,,
I know the answer
