Life in chaos
Feel like I'm alone
Too many problems
God I'm so tired
Wish they would listen
If only so easy
In the state they are
All I can do
Is try to guide
Feel it is all on my shoulders
No where else to turn
Who else will try
To help them
To hold them
To love them still
I cannot give up
I will not allow myself too
I will not let it have my son
