You take my bluest blues
My midnights of thoughts
Where everything feels 
Feels like it is impossible
Where my strength gone
No more will to fight
When I feel like I fail
Have no idea of what to do next
You take all these feelings from me
You take them with your smile
With your faith
The love that flows from you
Tonight you took me
Took my soul
Held my heart in your hands
As you held it
You gave it a kiss
A kiss of untainted love
A love you give to me in all you do
With your kiss 
You breathed new strength
You made me feel I can do anything
Precious what I am saying is
Your love makes me whole
Gives me all I will ever need
I will love you forever and for always
