Standing on the hillock
Seeing the distant horizon
I call the wind to answer me
To tell me of my love
Let me hear the words she says

Raising my arms above
I beseech God to use the wind
The wind that covers us all
It sees and caresses us
Carries our voice

Closing my eyes
I offer God my prayer
To hear her words on the wind
Feel her breath upon my skin
The caress of her voice all around

I hear her voice
Carried on the wind
It beckons, caresses
Calling me near
"I Love You"
