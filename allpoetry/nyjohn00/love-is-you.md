Love is


Love is who...
Love is who it is that always puts a smile on your face
Sweetheart that is you!
Love is what...
Love is what strives you to go through life
Honey I want my life with you
Love is when...
Love is when you're with that special someone
And you feel that nothing is wrong
Darling all our days together are perfection
Love is where...
Love is where there's a place in your heart
That can't be filled by anything but that special someone
My love you are that person
Love is why...
Love is why human beings strive for perfection
Precious you are that perfection

I have found all I could ever want in YOU
