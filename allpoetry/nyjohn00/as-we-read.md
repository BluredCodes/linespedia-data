Looking into the heart
As we read and write
Moved we are
By what we read
We say it touched us
Moved us, made us feel
We tend to take it to the heart
We feel the pain
Feel the hope
Long for the love displayed
Wanting it for ourselves
Responding, saying too much
Not wanting to offend as often we do
The response is a compliment
Saying all that was felt
And at times all that was wished for
