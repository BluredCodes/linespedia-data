Blue is the Essence of You
                                                                    

 My thoughts sail through your calm waters
 And my heart soars in the heavens above,
 Or a pristine snow simply left to the shade
 There is no calmer peace than your love.

An eclipse casts a light of mystery to the land
conjuring my wildest dreams set in a shade surreal,
If somehow I could capture that wordless sight
Only then could I explain how I feel.

Perchance your feelings fall to a cobalt hue
And your heart wanders aimlessly in despair,
Know that I will always stand by your side
And fill you with love, because I will be there.
