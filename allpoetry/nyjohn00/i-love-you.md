I love you
God I really do
You always are asking me why
Wishing I had the words to describe
Just how I feel as I look at you
My revelation when ever I deeply think
Is to see someone wondrous
In all her splendor
She doesn't need fancy clothes
Gobs of makeup, jewelry galore
I've even seen her without her makeup
Embarrassed though she be
The beauty I saw melts my heart
This beauty is sweet and pure
It is there for me
This love so true
I repay, with a love for you
