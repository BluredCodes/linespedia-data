Time with you 
Is always a dream
You don't realize 
Even your voice, oh I could listen
Your laughter is musical
Is like a nightingale's sweet song
Soothing, refreshing, reviving my spirit
Like a cold drink on a hot summers day
The freshness of the air after a rainstorm
Just you by my side
You are my sun in the daytime
You are my moon at night
Lighting my way, basking in your light
Your rays give me strength
Health like the warm sunshine
The moon beams you cast
Illuminate my darkest hours
Filling me with the need of you
Your touch is like the dedicated gardener
Loving tending seedlings
Watering, nurturing
Helping me grow inside
Time with you, God it means so much
It is my life
