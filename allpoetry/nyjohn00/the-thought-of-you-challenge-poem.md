Just the thought of you
God how you own my soul
We had a friendship that grew
We talked, laughed, shared, and sighed
The beauty that is you enthralled
The more I learned of you 
The more I needed to know
With each passing thought and sigh
I found myself just yearning for the touch
The touch of your hand in mine
To walk along these many paths
Taking in all the wonders in this world
Making memories of sharing
Feeling our hearts embrace
To wrap you in my arms
Feel your warm flow over me
I can sit and picture you
The twinkle of your eyes
The curve of your lips
Looking deep in your eyes
Seeing the reflection of our love
As our lips caress and tongues seek
To let each one know the yearning 
This yearning so deep
Enflaming our heart
Needing you near
Just to feel all your warmth
