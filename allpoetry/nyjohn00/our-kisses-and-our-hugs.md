title: "Our Kisses and our hugs"

Our kisses and hugs
Thoughts and feelings
Love expressed
Yearnings felt
Thoughts that match
In oh so many ways
A thought of compatible
In every way imaginable
Talking and sharing
Discussing and debating
Have you ever seen
People discuss religion
And not get angry
So much on common
And the things where in we differ
There is never an argument
Respect of each other's thoughts
There is such love felt here
A mutual commitment
That even when we disagree
We protect each other's feelings
I want to tell you all that is in my mind
And I will sit for hours
And listen to you as well
As time goes on
I see more that it is meant to be
You and I
Were destined to find this love
If one were to describe a soul mate
It definitely would be us
We seem to know each others thoughts
Even before they are thought
What I'm trying to tell you
In this bumbling way
Is that I love you sweetheart
In each and every way
