Standing on a bluff
Looking out to sea
I feel the salty wind
Stirring the sea
The breakers rush toward me
Crashing on the beach
The ocean has so much power
But it is all due to the wind
Gods' breath, blowing on the sea
His power is great
No limits at all
He allowed two hearts
To become forever one
He allowed us to meet
As the wind does the wave
And rushed us together
To merge as one
