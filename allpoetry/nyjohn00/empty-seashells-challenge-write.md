Empty Seashells

Walking along the beach one day
Seashells polished and shining
Rolling this way and that
Some still alive
Others just a memory
Each one lived 
The life it was given
Was it empty of love
No caring to be found
Just moving with the tide
Till only the shell 
Marked its time
I know my time is limited
As all of ours is
I want you to know
How you have made my life whole
You took me from the endless moving on the tide
My life has a purpose with you at my side
So when my time comes
And I'm just a shell
Where ever I am
I'll know my life was worthwhile
