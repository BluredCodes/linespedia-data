Alone on a rainy day

Sitting alone on this rainy day
My thoughts of you
Needing you near
A quiet little dark coffee shop
Soft classical music plays
Laptop on the table
My only joy was our briefest messages
The sound of your smile
Yes I hear it when you talk to me
It reaches through the phone
Grips my heart in its embrace
I talk to you, tell you all
Trying to make you feel, you are there
Wanting my words to tell you
Missing you sweetheart
Sitting here writing my thoughts
Wanting you
Needing you
Loving you
The longing grows
It I were to spend all my minutes with you
All I have left in this world
I'd still be longing for more
More of you
