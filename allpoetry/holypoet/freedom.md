freedom

             fly , fly! up the sky,  talk to the sun in all its might
             kiss its face and try to radiate scanty rays...

            sit on the tower of the chruch, temple or mosque
               it being no concern...see can symbolize humanity..

            if thou, meet god , ask him to bless us...
             and preach u lesson of love...
            u be the angel , try to drop love
            from that yonder sky..

            there if u meet my mother, tell her  i still remember her...
             dad has married  to the girl he love...
  
            can anyone stop u from reaching that yonder sky....
            u are free o bird! u are free
