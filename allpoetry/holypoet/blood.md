blood
  humanity never ugly was it as  godhra found it ,
blood to blood was  the order of city,

 a cry sounded so pitifully , 
it was the woman breaking her bangles,
somewhere a child cries, while a man shivers
blood to blood was the order of city,

news flash, killings, lootings , and all
but no reason in season found.
some fanatic raise , and others follow him.
is our conscience so weak my dear?

while some suffer , some make mayhem,
wake up my dear..for the country needs you
lets be compose, resolve and not say
blood to blood was the order of city

rajdeep gupta
