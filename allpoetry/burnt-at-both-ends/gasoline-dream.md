Boxes need boxes
The shit you don't need ever
Needs a box that fits

Books, tools, parts, fools. Cringe!
Find, pack, lift, carry, sweat. Burst!
Kill me or kill it!

Pouring Gasoline
Over the entire mess
Joyous Inferno

Dancing now naked
Dancing, singing, Arrested
I plead sanity... yeah... moving soon....

driving me nutz
