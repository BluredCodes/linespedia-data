I guess nerdiness
is finding too much 
in too little
too often
and denying the truth of the
relative insignificance of
any one single thing in this world...
delving into it
to the exclusion of all else...
stuck..
obsession...
in my head 
this madness 
like...a brain without a body
like...
lips without a kiss.I cut out the first 75% of this poem... it made me gag to reread it..
this part I like...
