In this Haiku, I wait.

.................... Kissing the air ...  I
The space where you used to   .......    Miss
Look back from.................     the kiss.


Drop from .................... the waiting
my eyes ........ wet blinking wet thoughts
Tears ................. drip perfect rain.In this Haiku, I wait....
