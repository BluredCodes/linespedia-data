Some patient observation
clears a jungle of thoughts.

They wait now like crickets
for the full bloom of nightime,
to sing their chorus of dreams.

On a high leaf
a musing lizard sighs...
Ripples in a warm bathful,
Murmurs in this forest stillness,
Rise to soak lips and skin.

This grateful green humid mist
Reflect in the eye pool
of the Gecko._____=8-]_____
