Birchwood my love

You write me such sweetness
and burn me with sweet heat
and surprise.

Swinging slow the easy
time rolls 
we sojourn
each crescendo
eclipsing expectations
basted
wasted happy glorious
simplicity struck like a match
mellow moments undulate in the surf
delicately shading
poetic sensual phantasms
that flush my face
with luckylove of life
...an oriental happylucky 
humor sensual
I am coated
soaked
ablated with gratitude
and it's gifts...yes
