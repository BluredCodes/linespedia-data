love

love is uncontrolable and irresistable,
love is passion love is lust ,
love is mad love is beautiful,
love is patient love is blind,
love will make you lose your mind, 
love is sexy love is rough,
love has uncontrolable urges, 
love is wealth ,
love keeps good health,
love is sad and love is used,
love has been brudaly abused,
love is good love is bad, 
love is hard and love is sad, 
love is soft love is silky,
love is red and love is milky,
love is darkness love is light,
love is shadowed love is bright,
love will blow your mind,
love is like red red wine,
love is like a snow white dove, 
love is love.
