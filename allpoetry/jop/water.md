Water 
I love water and this is true, 
 my favrote thing to do,
 is go where water is like a lake or a  slough,
 it relaxes me to lay there and watch the sky, 
i'm afraid to close my eyes for might die,
 laying there as the water rises almost to my chin,
 i'm afraid to fall asleep for it might be a sin, 
to be so scared of gods creation,
 what?You say,
 the nation is what scares me so,
 and makes me want to go,
back to my heaven in peace together,
 by the water forever!
