---
title: "Please tell me that I love you"
writer: "Rosanne"
slug: "please-tell-me-that-i-love-you"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59383-Please-tell-me-that-I-love-you-by-Rosanne"
---

Look into my eyes 
and tell me what you see
Is there love, is there love
for you in me

You're so sure
In all the things you do
Because you know
That there is love for me in you

Look into my eyes 
and tell me what you see
Is there love, is there love
for you in me

You're so beautiful
I can't believe it's reel
But please, please help me
And tell me what I feel

Look into my eyes
and tell me what you see
Is there love, is there love
for you in me

Look into my eyes
and tell me what you see
O please, please, let there be love
for you in me...
