title: "Nasty Little Devils!!!"

There I see it, 
The last cigarette,
I want to light it,
But can’t handle the regret.

It sits there lifeless,
Waiting to produce its sweet smoke.
I really should light it,
Just give it a little toke.

I can smell its sweet aroma,
The comfort as it rest upon my lips.
However, if I do it I will again be lashed,
By nicotine’s whips.

So what do I do,
To fill such undying boredom?
This craving I have,
Will I ever overcome?I HATE CIGARETTES!!!!!!!  (like to smoke 'em though!)
