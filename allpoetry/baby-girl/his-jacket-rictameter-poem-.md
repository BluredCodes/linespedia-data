~ JACKET ~
The one I want
You wear it when its cold
It smells of your scent so sweetly
You wear it so proudly when you work late
It keeps you warm when i'm not there
It is blue like your eyes
Can I borrow
~ JACKET ~Rictameter ~ a poem that consists of the same word at the beginning and end of your poem. Contains nine lines. Each line goes 2,4,6,8,10,8,6,4,2
