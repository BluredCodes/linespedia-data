---
title: "You're The Only One"
writer: "Treas"

You're the only one
Who always brings a smile to my face
You're the only one
That gets my frowns erased
You're the only one
That can take all my fears
You're the only one
That can wipe away my tears
You're the only one
That makes me giggle and laugh like I do
You're the only one
That understands me, when I understand you
You're the only one
Who can make my heart skip a beat
You're the only one
Who all my standards meet
You're the only one
That I have confidence in
You're the only one
That I love through my soul deep within
You're the only one
That's ever on my mind
You're the only one
That's always been so kind
You're the only one
That's never been mad at me
You're the only one
That, in front of me, I can see
You're the only one
That I love, and always will
You're the only one
That I've loved. .always and still.hehe
