title: "Words"
writer: "Treas"

What are these words
I hear so faint
Drawn on my eyes
With sliver paint
Not golden
Do they shine
But enhance
Eyes not mine
Forcefully can they blind
Hearts of millions
Of all kinds
Words unheard
By the weak
Entrancing
They can not speak
The weak use them wrong
That's why there are the strong
All the things you think you mean
Are illusions
Still, real they seem
Words aren't toys
Used for fun
Words..
Can blacken out the sun
When you say... "I love you"
Do you mean it
Are you true?
If you say "You're going to die"
Please, give me a reason why..
Words can mean the world to me
Use them wrong. .
    And you might see. .
