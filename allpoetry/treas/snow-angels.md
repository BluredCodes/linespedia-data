Snow angels
Are my fantasys
Endulgent kisses
So heavenly
Finger tips
Smooth as silk
Pale faces
White as milk
Flowing hair
Glowing brown
Longing past
Angelic gown
Deepest eyes
Pool in pool
It mysifies
Stale smiles
Brighest teeth
Life worth while
In beneath
Loving heart
Beating prayers
Our life
