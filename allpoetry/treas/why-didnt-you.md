---
title: "Why Didn't You"
writer: "Treas"

With no meaning was there pain
Honorly consuming
My unconscious brain

A part engolfed in winter seas
Fufilling me not
A soul that grieves

A lost, part was once mine
Accidently stabbed
My clock of time

The hour glass still waitingly ticks
With perscision and grace
Sand drops click and click

Whispering words haunt my mind
Destroying, devistating
My only one kind

Spiraling pictures creep in my head
Dizzying, mystifying
Wrong I am led

No pain. .did this cause
Here and now
Discover my flaws

Soon enough to find my way
Somewhere else
