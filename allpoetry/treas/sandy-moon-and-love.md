---
title: "Sandy Moon and Love"
writer: "Treas"

The sandy moon
Reflects off the ocean
The ocean of tears
Love--it fears

Each drop of sand
That falls in the ocean
Makes it glow brighter
Sets it on fire

Sparks that set the moon
Up in flames
Screaming out death 
Until it rains

The sun is the only hope
Only needing one drop
So watery and pure
Flowing nonstop

No where can it be found
What an influence. .
So. .so profound
Not in the sky, nor on the ground. .

So bury me now
In the ocean of death
Grieve me no sorrow
'Cause there's tomorrow

Swallow me now
Each grain of sand
So small. .
Engulfs me how. .

Please spare not a drop
That will a second stop
Leave me a day
Less not throw me away

My trash can be. .
Someones treasure
Amongst many
A smile, causes pleasure

A simple touch
A simple word
Something so simple
Drifting unheard

A flaming ocean
A sandy light
A crying sun
Leaving me dusty
Emphasising done

I see a light
I see it's bright
Leave me now
Forever and a day
But trust me
You'll be happy somedayno clue
