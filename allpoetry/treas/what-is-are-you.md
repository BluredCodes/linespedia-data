What is salt
What is water
Salt water of course

What is an eye
Why do I cry
These unsalty tears?

What is a thought
What is a feeling
Whats fear and can it be caught?

What is a memory
Why do they stay
Bad or good they never go away

Are you a fantasy
Are you a myth
How can someone be
So fun to be with

Are you a rainbow
Are you a circle
I've never seen anything
Just so perfect
I swear
If I can't believe it's real
Until I get a chance
To feel

What is a dream
What is a wish
Do they came true
Mine sure did
Well I wished for you.

Can someone really reach out
And grab a star
Seeing as how
They are so far

Can someone reach out and
Touch they're soul
I believe you have
From what I know
And have been told

You are just one wish of mine
And you answered the others
And of course that's more than fine
You seek the inbetween
You're perfect....
From what I know and see
And you'll always be.dono just ramblings
