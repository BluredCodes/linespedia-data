Nam, you're so sexy
In that pink tu-tu
I would say. .
Man. .it's really you
I've never seen anyone
Wear something like that before
Could you. .perhaps. .wear it more?
You look so good
And it amazes me
Just you and the pink tu tu
Is a sight to see
I don't know how much more I can take
Is that. .tu-tu. .FAKE???
Nam. .I thought. .
That. .thats the tu-tu i bought!
I bought it for Nell!
You just could have asked
And I would have gotten you one too
But. .please remove the tu-tu
And give it back to Nell
If you don't. .well
Hmm. .you DO look sexy
In that pink tu-tu
With that fairy wand
And that diamond tiera
But I want to wear it
WAIT. .Is that Nells TOO?
How dare you. .
You sick freak! stealing things
From poor children like that
You deserve *smack*
Awh. .I had to touch it somehow
That tutu is something. .WOW. .
Ok enough with the tu tu
It's making me mad
I don't like it anymore
Because it's been bad
Throw it away and come over here
Oh hehe. .sorry dear. .lol i tried
