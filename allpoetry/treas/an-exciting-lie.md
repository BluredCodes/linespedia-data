title: "An Exciting Lie"

Midnight summer circus shows
Work concessions
. .I decompose
Filter out tempting cries
As I sit
In envious lies
Scavenging for things I'd never had
Is the practice
I've founded bad
Yearning self-contained thoughts
What's the point 
Of things not faught
Scary ends and unstarting days
In search of things 
That money pays
Filling Earth with evil ways
Is why we do
The things we have
Not me, not you
Conjoined in equality
We stand for nationality
And put on shows 
Of unusual things
Talents, Traits
Wild out fates
Meanings, and not
Which we do a lot
No one can now 
For it's one big show
That never ends, never dies
Everything we live
Is. .an exciting lie.i dont know. .just something i thought about. .about the world today
