---
title: "I See Only You"
writer: "Treas"

When I look at you
All I see is myself
I see your oceans
Filled with my face
I see your branches
Holding my embrace
I see your smile 
Smiling at me
Me smiling back
It's you that I see

I see a sunset
I see a love
I see something meant
I see something 
I won't regret
I see you
I see me
I see something 
That sets me free
I see the future
I see good things
I love you
And everything you bring

Through good and bad times
You're the one
Always on my mind
It's the love, it's meant to be
Forever, 
You're the one I'm going to see

I can't ask for anything more perfect
Than you, yourself
It's you, not your wealth
Not your family, Not your car
It's. .Just you. .By far

No one has caught their eyes with mine
The way that you have
You're the one that looks that fine
You're the only one that knows me for me
And you're the only one..That I can see.awh love *giggles*
