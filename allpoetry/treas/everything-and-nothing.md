---
title: "Everything and Nothing"
writer: "Treas"

The Earth
Such a simple place
With many difficulties
From day to day
Round
Two half cresent shapes
Distorting us
It's a crazy race
Billions of problems
On one surface flat
Each of us worrying
For this and that
Billions of laughs
Billions of tears
It's crazy how 
There's billions of fears
Billions of deaths
Billions of screams
Weird what billions means
One place
With billions of things
And no one knows everything
On our little dream machine
Will we ever?
Can we capture the thought of everything?
What is everything. .
We will never know. .
Has it been in our grasp
And yet we let go?
Everything. .is like nothing
Nothing we don't know
But yet. .everything it seems to grow
We say we have found the cure!
Cure to what. .the thought to endure?
Nothing ..that which we will not find out
Nothing is hard to imagine
Can you imagine someone else's life
If you weren't there?
What about if the world never existed
What would everything look like?
White..black..don't know what is nothing?
Everything. .with nothing to have
And nothing. .never having anything
The answers we might never find
But it still stimulates the mind
To creativly think. .of life
With a drop of nothing
And a drop of rain
Minus everything
Even pain
The Earth
Is such a simple place
Where lies
Everything
and
Nothing
In a never ending race
Right in front of us
Lying in one single space.
