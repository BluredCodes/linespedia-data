title: "Life's Answer"
writer: "Treas"

What's the point of life
When it's all a mystery
What's the point of living
Without a history
What's it mean to walk away
When you never stay?
What's it mean to stay
When you're always walking away
Life has a meaning
And few find it out
Everyone lives in secrets
Living in shame and doubt
Reach out and touch the sun
Pull back
Laugh and Run
Spoil the moon
With death and age
Read a book
Turn the page
Saught out answers
That aren't there
Search in silence
..Everywhere
Ansers lie in the dark
Deep within the mist
Life's lesson will never come
Until you're lifeless, dying, and done.
