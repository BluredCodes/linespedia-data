title: "What's love"

What is love
And is it real
Because I want
To know why I feel
Like I can't talk
Like I can't see
Is it you
Or is it me
I can't seem to concentrate
I can't seem to think
All my thoughts consist of you
Some shape and form
There you are, born
This rock is you
This tree is too
I can't relate to anything
Because it all revolves
Not with them, not with me
Just one thought
Comes to mind
What is love
And will I find
The answer to the question
Or will I seek forever
Never finding
The truth
Behind
All the
Lies.
