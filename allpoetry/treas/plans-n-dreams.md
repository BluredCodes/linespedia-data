---
title: "Plans N' Dreams"
writer: "Treas"
slug: "plans-n-dreams"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54347-Plans-N-Dreams-by-Treas"
---

Someone so special
Someone so true
I can't help
Being in love with you

It can be hard
Being the one for you
Since you're always on my mind
Thinking is hard to do

I didn't plan it
But no one ever does
Love is unpredictable
And it always was

They say I can't see past you
They might be right
But what else do I need
Besides you in my sight?

I don't care what anyone says
Because, they don't know what I feel
They don't know what's inside me
They don't know that this love is real

They can think anything they want
They can say I'm making a big mistake
But. .what are chances for
At least, them, I take. .

Plans can be made
And dreams can come true
My plans and dreams
Are Only Of You.
