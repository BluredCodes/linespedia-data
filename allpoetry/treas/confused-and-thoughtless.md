What is a mountain without it's height
What is a snake without its bite
What is the rain without being wet
What is a race without someone's bet
What is the sun without being warm
What is a life without being born
What is a kiss without having love
What is a push if it's not a shove
What is an animal without being prey
What is obedience, not learning to stay
What is a childhood, without one memory
What is a teenager, if they aren't who they will be
What is an adult, if they don't go to work each day
What is a grandparent, if they don't give candy away
What is she, dying on that bed
What is death, and why are we afraid to be dead
What is a feeling, without a simple cause
What if they spoke, and stabbed all our flaws
Would they cry out, and lose their jagged claws
Would we respect a nation of our own
Would we throw our sticks and our ragged stones
Would the world smile and grin
For all those who live in deadly sin
Or stand up for those who speak their mind
More than all the human kind
We live as a nation
We live for who we are
We live to others standards
How can we get so far
We die for our nation
We die for our sake
We die to help those in need
Why are we so fake
Dying for three causes
But only dying once
Living for three things
And only having one life
We seek for our depression
We seek for a happy knife
We die giving up so much
Ripping and crying
Pushing and stabbing
Living not a life of freedom
Becuase it all gave up
When really you went blind
And it's yourself you can't fid
You got preoccupied
Looking at the guilt and hate
You missed your own safe fate
You won the prize but never owned it
You gave it all away 
Because you didn't feel good that day
Your life is a home
You live for your life
Home is where you live at
Whether it be day or it be night
A home in bloody tears
Is not a home at all
Glace out the window
And see a smiling face
The smile is the sun
The sun smiles on you
And you frown on the world
Just because you are unhappy
You. .ruin their lives too
They still love you. .
