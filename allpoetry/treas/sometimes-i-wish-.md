Sometimes I wish I was a cloud
Floating about, thinking out loud

Sometimes I wish I was a train
Chugging along, through sunshine or rain

Sometimes I wish I was a cat
Sleeping all day in someones lap

Sometimes I wish I was the moon or sun
Shining down, bringing smiles and fun

Sometimes I wish I was a star
Gazing at someone lonely, looking afar

Sometimes I wish I was my mirror
Reflecting back my tear

Sometimes I wish I never cried
But that means. .I never tried. .
