How many shadows does it take
To realize my life is fake?
How long does it take to absent my lies?
How long does the world have to spin
Before I realize I can't win?
How long until I learn something new?
Is lifes lesson never through?
What is a lie. .
It's everything I've ever known
Once again. .I find myself. .alone.
