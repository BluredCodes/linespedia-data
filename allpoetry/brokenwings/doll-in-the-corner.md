title: "Doll In The Corner"
writer: "BrokenWings"

Doll in a corner
Thrown away for something better
Never was a pretty one
But she did well enough to suffice
For a while

Doll in the corner
Dirty porcelain broken
Never was a perfect one
But the comfort was there
Until something better caught your eye

Doll in the corner
Shattered head to toe
Never was a speakable topic
But she knows 
And her pieces are too small to put together again

Doll in the corner
With feeling glass eyes
Never was one or the other
But she's stuck on the flypaper
To those feelings she can't deny

Doll in the corner
Forgot her, didn't you?
Never was worth it
So light the fire to her dress
And burn her pain away

Doll in the corner
She was once clean
Never too holy for you to hurt
Her heart was fragments on the floor
After four months had passed

Doll in the corner
Did her best for you
Never could make you happy
Maybe you'll forget her pain
But it's always hers to bear

Doll in the corner
Gave all she could
Never was good enough
So turn your head away from her tears
And maybe her screams won't echo

Doll in the corner
I can't make myself move
Never could ask you why
So in my segments I will lay
Forgotten, but unable to forget

Doll in the corner
Will you pick me up?
Never looked to see me here
No one comes to piece the pieces
That love has strewn upon the floorI think it speaks for itself.
