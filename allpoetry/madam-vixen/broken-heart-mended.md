title: "Broken Heart Mended"
writer: "Madam Vixen"

Broken heart falls to the floor
All the pain it just couldnt carry anymore
The truth revealed as it shattered
Apon the floor where it layed scattered
The tears poured from her eyes  
Off came the mask and her disguise
She walked out of the darkness into the light
She grabbed His hand and held it tight
He healed her hurts and her sorrow
And gracefully led her into tomorrow
The pieces of her heart he skillfully mended
Then back to the heavens He miraculously ascended
