title: "Strangled"

The life in you has been sucked out,
Your heart beats no more,
The pulse you once had no longer exists,
We all saw it coming and so did you,
A hand reached out but you did not take hold,
You let your life get strangled away,
The very breath of life diceased,
I can see the hand prints around your neck,
Try and hide it with make-up and such,
But the marks are to dark to cover up,
You let him over power you,
The strongest of all was on your side,
But you pushed Him away and tried to defend yourself,
Death and distruction is what you've gained,
I hope it was worth it,
Your strangled.
