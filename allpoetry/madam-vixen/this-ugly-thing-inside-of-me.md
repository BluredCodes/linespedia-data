title: "This Ugly Thing Inside of Me"

This ugly thing inside of me is seeping through my skin,
This ugly thing inside of me is called sin.

Every waking moment it's lurking right behind me,
It's trying to overcome me and it won't let me be.

I can feel it's nasty hand tapping on my back,
I do not turn around in fear of an attack.

It's so near yet to far away,
Keep me in Your hands, this is what I pray.

This ugly thing inside of me is seeping through my skin,
This ugly thing inside of me is called sin.
