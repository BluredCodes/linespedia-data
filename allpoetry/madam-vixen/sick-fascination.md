I have a sick fascination of people being strangled
Severed limbs and bodies that are mangled
Razors dragging across the skin
As i watch the blood draw from deep within
Humans being buried alive
Scratching at the coffin, trying to survive
Jumping off a cliff hitting sharp rocks below
As someone watches intensly, the whole while screaming "NO!"
A knife in the chest
Not the most creative, but will always be the best
A hammer to the head
also leaves many lives dead
This is all very disturbing and perhaps even sick
