---
title: "Cry Out in the Night"
writer: "Madam Vixen"
slug: "cry-out-in-the-night"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62371-Cry-Out-in-the-Night-by-Madam-Vixen"

Let your tears flow like a river
Pour out your heart like water
Lift up your hands to him now
For the lives of the lost people

Arise, cry out in the night
Someones life is at stake
Let your eyes flow unceasingly with tears
With passion for their souls
