Sarah, with her hair so bright
and bracelets galore
She tells a joke and she knows 
just how to make me hit the floor.

Renee, with her snort and infectious laugh
and shining bright eyes
What's with her
and stoner guys?

I can talk all day with these girls
and I never feel alone
We can talk about just anything
Even guy's cologne.

These gals are here for me
Through thick and thin
"When she'll be back, 
we don't know when."

All I can say about these two
is that I love them so
Some will probably say
"They stick together like glue."

Thanks to them for what they've done.
They are so very sweet.
I can't think of more rhyming words,
so this poem is incomplete.Dedicated to my two best homegirls, Sarah and Ren. Love you guys... this poem came to me at 7:30AM this morning. Hehe.
