title: "Happily Ever After..."
writer: "Jet"

The nights have never seemed as dark
As they do when we're apart.
And the days have never seemed as long
As they do to a lovesick heart.
Every minute spent without you.
Each day without your touch.
Every single waking moment.
I miss you oh so much.

Come rescue me from memories.
Come save me from this place.
Wisk me away from loneliness,
Wrapped up safe in your embrace.
I would be your princess.
And you would be my knight.
To hold me and defend me
On your steed so white.

Let me have my fairy tale,
If only for one night.
Leave me with a memory
Of when the world was right.Exams are really a bad time to write poetry...I seem to get sentimental...
