---
title: "Awaiting You, Still..."
writer: "Jet"
slug: "awaiting-you-still"

Standing in the pouring rain
Just to feel the chill.
How can I even begin to explain
What this means to me?
Lightning splits the swollen sky.
Mirror of my broken heart.
My soul struggles to deny
All this pain I feel.

Waiting in the summer heat.
Golden sun upon my face.
Feeling anything but complete,
In this portrait of perfection...
Time's passage cannot erase
This pain of loneliness.
Echoes of a lost embrace
Whisper in the breeze.

Surrounded by the falling leaves
That dance in the chill winds.
Funny how everything one believes
Can come crashing down again.
I want to run and hide,
And save me from myself.
All these silent tears I've cried
Will strangle me tonight.

Alone in the driving snow,
I miss you, love.
More than you could ever know.
Be quick my love.
Empty shell of frozen pain
Waiting to be thawed.
Light the fire of my love again
Oh please return to me.

Standing in the pouring rain
With my heart in my hands.
We've come full circle once again.
