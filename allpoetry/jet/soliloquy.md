And so I am alone
Always and forever.
With teasing glimpses
Of the outside world
To leave me wanting more.
But always just...
just barely out of reach.

Am I outside looking in
Or inside looking out?
Does it really matter,
If you can't even find the door?
Only a wall...always a wall.
Invisible to everyone but me.

One day...some day...
Someone will find a way.
To wrest me from my solitude
And bring me back with them
To walk among the living...
One day I will learn to love again.

If all the worlds a stage...
Then this is my soliloquy.....
