Life goes on, even as I stand
oblivious
To all that surrounds me.
In the crowded airport you take my hand
so gently
And tell me it will be alright.

Oh love, if only it were true.
if only
My world weren't crashing down.
I can't face the world without you.
Too scared
To face the days alone.

Like a prayer I say your name.
And you
Whisper mine in return.
Trying so hard to tame
the feelings
Rising quickly within me.

Your lips press against mine
and suddenly.
Everything makes sense again.
How on earth can I resign
myself
To a summer without this?

The hardest part is letting go.
Oh, how I
want to beg you not to leave.
More than you can ever know,
I need you.
I will never be complete without you.

I hear you whisper as you turn
to leave.
I love you.

Everything will be alright.
