Your fragile heart is open
To the cold and bitter world.
Stirling dreams were lost before
Stolen in the night.
Scars of love have marked your soul
But you still you dare to hope.
Yet everytime you fall from grace 
You break a little more.

Live today, let it be
Dare to dream for tomorrow.
Take your heart, set it free
Take a chance on this moment.

Starlight caught upon your hair,
And the moon shines in your eyes.
Two hearts once lost then found again,
On this twisted road of life.
To have, to hold, to love once more,
To catch a shooting star.
The dream is ours to dream again
With the world sprawled at our feet.

Live today, let it be
Dare to dream for tomorrow.
Take your heart, set it free
Trust in love tonight.

Live for love, live for me
Dare to dream for tomorrow
Take your heart, set it free.
Take a chance on this moment.

Take my hand and walk with me.
