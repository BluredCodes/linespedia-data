The bugle calls to them,
The brave and valiant men.
Who once defended England
And would die for her again.

Like a siren song they call to you
To leave the pain behind.
To go and have a pint with them
And let yourself unwind.

She is waiting for you, also
Ever patient in the night.
Waiting until the time comes
When you let your soul take flight.

The good fight has been fought,
You've already said goodbye.
It's time for you to join them
In that brewpub in the sky.

Over a pint of lager
With Nanny by your side.
Remember everything you've done
And watch over us with pride.In memory of my Grandad, Harry. 1924-April 22, 2001. Hope you and Nan are tearing it up together.
