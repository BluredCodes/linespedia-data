_








Scream louder ... ask the shadow whom you call Jehovah to fuck your pity ... cower inside your desolate figure... 

that shudders to the sound of it's own voice...



Dont cry ... wipe away your tears of shit and accept

my breasts across your back ... feel me press my softness into you as I drain your vanity with mine... 

Define ... and Confine ... Time after Time




Take this entrance into my shame ... excruciating movements of sex ... follow the curves to the downward motion ... of these ... broken notes ... discouraging words ... of hate spit from the mouth that licked your courage away...
