__________
felo-de-se


butterscotch marvels and wonderlands

searching deep under emerald green sands

find to discover flowing of molten crust

not sure but certain in the myths we trust

impale me under your cold dead sea

searching for you attached of navy blue

finding us~ belonging to lust

love dripping ~from all above

~like murdered gods

cut with goldenrods~

burn me dont bury me 

~alive~
