^



Notes by our distance made more sweet...

missing you .... kissing you
the waltz began

offering us the hands to hold while your away
im away

but will not stray 
full score 
concerto

notes of a sharp scale brace us off-line

semitone
falls flat

and the natural high
note
falls low

second forth
missing the 3rd
melody

rhythm, rhyme, pitch bend to the point of 
broken \/beat
her into critical submission

the secret e  y  e s
of harmony 
leaves us 

sweet soft and silver toned

start again
Me(m)Lo(r)Dies
