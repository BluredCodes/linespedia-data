bomber in the sky of bright forecast send us yoooour

stash ed

secret concealed weapon you keep adjusting

zipp ed

up until forgotten but never out of his mind

heat ed

for combustion can able to force through any material that attempts to say 

no 

blow me  

i cant stay away from you

i want to hear you ScreeeeeeeeeeeaaaaaM for me

just once

then gone

its a day you wont forget

fire at will and Ill hit you with all i got to keep you inside
