~Shaitan~



15 seconds to 5 million

ringintomeetonguescratching

scratching so fast your hand doesnt move

::mixer elixir

Buttons of equator make me hallucinate and become::


serpent of the telephone pole rising to stretch gravity over her eyes aneat me

stick her lips while the split goes to my clit

opened then separated 

now become 2

won when we became 1

we run our insides to the floor

Serpent of defiance through the smog over clouds into the universe of stomachs

split for emus
amuse her with your tenderloins
shed out of your skin and into mine

mind of the watcher come to me in cards
possess what i thought i knew in these shadows
playwithher andmyeye givinto I
no else can slither like myown madness
blend this release into all ofher sadness



demoniac 
immersed
