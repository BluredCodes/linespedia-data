___






executioner

a tangled web I weave ... conceiving so much punishment ... purgatory in amounts of ecstasy ... for draining ... straining muscles warming the blood ... Slaughter ... enmity ... lost her cherry to your face ... when your eyes said I do... I do love you ... come to me blue ... with coolness in your feline hide ... dog stride ... leash me to our time unkindest time of all ... finishing stroke with our death blow...

(punishment
