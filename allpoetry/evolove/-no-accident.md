street light flash stop red now
brisk as the tires your wearing
slip in the black ice and no
stopping backwards spinning
rubber to side up down
while they run for cover 
creature of dust storms
scouring eyes cant see in 
blind attempts to be the 
one who can put a green
to that fucking sign










That was no accident
