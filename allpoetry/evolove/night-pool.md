Blood flour on the hour
Nachtschwimmbecken

noughts and crosses
on your green pool
pretty paint 
[pink mink]
my dear child
how you do stink

bloodloss 
costs

more then my song
belongtoolong
below the hotel

vertigo too slow
me down
yellowallscorched 

expel
smell raw
blistering painful swell

Nostocaceae

blue-green algae
sticking for the honor 
of murals
Vacant body 
sleeps 
so soundly 
bubbling 
in her swirls
