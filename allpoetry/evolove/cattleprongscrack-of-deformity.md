\/



cattleprongs


with folds of back/\wards and split your ribs ... head in your thighs ... cover your eyes... 

Arms of umbrella limbs ... hard as sticks to break ... belly of a button catches your smears ... cover your ears...

Up against the wall ... your palms force the flat in ... out and around your shrinking head ... separation knows no limits ... and your eyes are holes ... blacker then your forfeit shadows holding the wholes of your hands
slip the sands...

 cant hear ... wont speak ... nothing to see ... deafening in the crack of deformity
