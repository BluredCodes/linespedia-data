Grimstone whispers in a snake charm
virus
ill give her to you
after i steal her for myself
violent stings
leather screams

never intended to let this go

shes POs Secede for me
obsess for me
heresyblasp h e m y

nothing will be okwill you control me too
when i come to youwalking right past through

vultures for the sickness queen
ineedhercreamcorruptiondream
shaking and losing heat

Inside of me ... finally

teeth clatter make it chatter
continuous
these women are insane

suicidal canem i s s i o n s

rip these sheets
with my teeth

absorb your scars
needle bliss
only one who could 
make her beg like this

we are one
to eat ourselves 
into manythings

Not h u m a n
