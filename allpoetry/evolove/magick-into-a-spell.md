she came from under something not worth rising until the time when something could lift such a being into his heart ... and grow this eternal magick into a spell that is placed on the world ... and they will see for the first time ... nothing is impossible

Nothing is impossible he said ... rising faith to her dread ... bow our head ... and feel once ..... for the first time.... a new beginning reaches a middle ... and smiles wicked to greet the sins reaching heaven


Soaked
in thoughts..................... like my foam to drool
im bubbling
