~ 
~In the backyard aging dying to seal up the house for war ... loading my guns.. i new these people.. they were old friends or people we knew... i had some others with me inside... i think i felt you ... but your face wasn't apparant 

load them in one at a time and go to the attic.... where there is no walls.. but dont wait.. leave again to walk along the fences.... make sure they cant come into the opening of weakness 

On the couch I hear them entering through the front ... now I see them in the hallway... im loaded and ready to kill ... buy my mind ... say what if ... what if I cant because I love these people...the ones trying to get in and take over... 

and when he enters ... the one with me tries to shoot.... but is the one that accepts the bullet.... gracefully he falls ... and i approach.... to point and smile...my barrel has its own eyes...but i melt my weapon.... and bend over to receive punishment ... kiss his chest ... bend over again ... and take swats across my back... i beg lower.. lower.... to hit the tender of my ass ... and then i kissed his lips.... i dont think he wanted to kill me anymore..... 
~
