Ven om
demoralization masochistic ways
compose me

like a piano playing begging notes

backsliding looseness of morals
in a vice pressed
all the way
to crease

evil courses immortality
spill it over and over

soften this weakness
of the flesh

crying sin 
opium den

belonging to her
she belongs to you
