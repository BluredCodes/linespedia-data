!




madness
suffocation inside lungs of moreair
thats your title ... no

thats my thoughts

rectum dream
cuming queen

acid fever
licking clean

fuck myself for you
bound

pound me 
a little harder

tasteless vulgar 
harsh
nipples colder

to cut your knives
from your skin
force it down 
further in

spin ... angles fill 
angels kill

end with driversdown
not around

and the trees all sit 
behind our green sky
~goodbye
