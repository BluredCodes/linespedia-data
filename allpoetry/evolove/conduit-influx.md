cleansing to be.. enema esophagus
canal of gullet ripens for juicing
figure it out.. a matter of course
Smile your neck from rivers to wands

nuzzle in the eye of the needle
cataract scud of white water rain
Passage outbreak of motion

egress vomitary pores
of all escape
Invasion of another destiny

pour into another way in 
Of your opening mouth

vascular chasm of hiatus
