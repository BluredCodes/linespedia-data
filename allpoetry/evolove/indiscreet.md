Indiscreet...

oooh yes sweet meat tease me need me

your the one i am hittin for 
full body bass banging armor

closed down the gigolos 
your the one gettin blows

all night  inside so tight
closed eyes  out of sight

feed me cream me on your knees im begging please me

im taken and not returned
your bend hits that extra curve

no more glisten only shine
planted inside me your growing vine

dine outlined in our wine
vibrates inner sunshine
outshine any lifeline

one day before this Valentine
his feline said to him
"youll be mine"for the one that drives me crazy with love.. passion... insanity... heart//ache ... and then comes back to give me a peach... and makes my wounds beautiful ... you know who you are.... oXXXo
