The puppy goes around where does he doze

the crap hits the ground and splats his nose

bound around the bloodhound grows

surround profound the bleeding bitch chose

dispose of your chores on the table wellfound

expose your stink for the stares of astound

compose great softness for feelings confound

close the scents surround fecal lost and found
