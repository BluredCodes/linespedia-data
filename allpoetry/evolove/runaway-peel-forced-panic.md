:



Open field made of iron vertical amazement with horizontal glares

single filed by churning wheels getting to go with minds paralyzed fornication

all aboard~ no place to get off 

except for vials and bunkers holding this fluid tasting of black tar tracks

loose of steam escaping a million miles an hour

into our power out of control

rock me back and forth into the runaway leading abandoned
                                     crooked under madness

lair of running now ~ trying to leak out
                                     silk inside

divide 

                                     parts made of steel hiding 
addiction peel over bones forced panic

turn me on and you just might get it
