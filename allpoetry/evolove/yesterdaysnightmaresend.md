::



dont go please stay
swaying in gray
drifting astray

nothing could stop it
nothing could stop us
now
then
when 
did it become too late

i dont remember ever seeing you leave
so suddenly
coldly 
bodily
boldy

i just remember being lost in your face
for more then many nights
days come to me running

gunning down this hole
slipping in the stroll 
of my mind

we lost time we lost time

affection is abuse
bruising
misusing
noosing

pursue and undue
i havent got a clue

cruel ... feels so true
