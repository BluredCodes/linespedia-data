~Opening scars ... not the only way in ... part me in the middle for romancing ... kingdom come ... ill be undone. And the growl of your mouth drops 2 feet under...


marking the trails  ... tonguing her tail ... scratches the mattress for remembering ... nerves remorse ... ribbed in course ... pounding and gouging to dig an ending ... deeper, my love

catastrophic intrusions make the falls come flowing ... owning every ounce of her voice ... beat me against the shape of your front ... back ... again ever after


~flip me strip me come surround me you've found me.... and keep this to undue in your ...sealed pockets~
~~consistancy
~
~
