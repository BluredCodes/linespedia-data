conundrum



I found out why...

nothing is the same      coming together never becoming anything

right there ... give it to me
       
                   having all sharing none           sharing all having one

and he loves me       and she loves me      and they love me

    so much they fucking hate me   disgrace me  contemplate you see

and    then         there it was done                felt so much

became my own          wondered about       all i have ever known

so i offered inside      slide into me wide      and stood there beside myself

all in patterns                   riddles to fondle every place i ever hid          

Just as his teeth entered...... i came
