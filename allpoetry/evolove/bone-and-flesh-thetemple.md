Ritual Fire scorching higher
Virgin is damned sweet ma'am
and the children lay within her
Cyclone 
blender a blur

Mend the myths of cracked monoliths
covenant of the wicked
born addicted
Dick sweet prick
filling the sick

My temple took me in
lets start again
Flesh covered wall
light of nightfall
Im Unstoppable
unbreakable
Indefeasible
Inconceivable

Rattlesnake overtake
earthquake of daybreak

make us now bleed us then
baptized in blasphemy
Amen
