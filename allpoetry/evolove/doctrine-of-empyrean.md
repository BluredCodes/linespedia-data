static ... static

         panic button
swishes of your kisses
           lifts this nemesis
abyss my orifice
         plunge our paralysis

doctrine of empyrean
love me to live
ill teach you to die
fiery bodies
cry like wax
mold for the shape of you

stigma ... stigma

            enigma switches
scars of rapture
      captured our moment
for more then a lifetime

   lover of fate
   keep me safe
 eternity from hate
held away from myself
into your dreams 
to 
     emanate








::For the one that dreams of me::
