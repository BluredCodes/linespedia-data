---
title: "visions of the west"
writer: "thepawn"
slug: "visions-of-the-west"
source: "AllPoetry"

concrete replaces cobblestones
nuclear plants funded
the extinction of the windmill
vacant faces all heading somewhere
just as long as we’re leaving home
drunk on driving for countless nights
of staring at the yellow lines and passing lights
green signs give us names and numbers pass us by
between the static and the 8-track
classic country ballads of simple cowboys
fill our hearts traded our saddles for bucket seats
midnight advertisements full of
dazzling escapades just a few miles
down the line frontier is dead
all its babies circumcised
baptized in blood of chinese spanish mexican indian
and of course the well honored anglo saxon
casualties first communion black hills gold
designate the god father las vegas
designate the god mother hollywood
if we ever get to a big city
we'll chase around the ambulance all night
trade in the 2002 model porsche
for a 1960’s t-bird for 1900’s model-t
for any old jalopy that will take us there
this is the economy of transportation
need all the oil we can get to
tour this feed this sustain this
great big wondrous nation
right through the arch the gateway to the west
fast food for your hunger collect each
and every friendship medal of louis & clark
50¢ for a happy meal in a paper bag to go
let's see how far this flat tire will take us
break down at mount rushmore 4 good ole faces
slave owners political pugilists honored in
sacrosanct standardized texts
siesta at noon in the dusty old motel room
with the fake bull horns hanging on the wall
salt lake city san francisco phoenix omaha
topeka albuquerque houston denver austin
grand canyon yellow stone
all recorded on kodak camera
red faced tourists with straw hat & shades
standing under each welcome sign
for the sake of bragging to the neighbors
if we ever go back but for now
there’s always the hope of blowing
eastern life into some tumbleweed town
founding a cantina building a great oasis
in the southwest corner of s dakota
renovate and revitalize and capitalize
on the bones of some quaint culture
sell a few tickets let folks come watch
a blue blooded actor play custer
erect a monument to big paul bunyan
straddling the city limits
send babe to packing town
make some fodder to feed the tourists
tempted to visit by brochures and telemarketers
bringing more of our own kin into the fold
in the only place we dare to dream
manufacturing a postcard of the west
the way we imagined it would be
send it to each and every lost friend & relation
we’ll never see
