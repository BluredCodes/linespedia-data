trying to assemble the fragments
because of my jealousy
because of my curiosity
because of my ignorance about
what lies between polar whims
always pulling, pushing
tantalizing mystery beckoning to me
from so far away
tried to look into the moon
to somehow grasp the radiance of the sun
only found myself unappeased
only found myself discontented
with the way you look at me
always calling from far away
but never taking one step near
