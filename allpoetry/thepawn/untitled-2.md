chasing a dusty paper trail whence
two tatter-winged doves fell into a pit
and stoked by their own zealous fires emerged
two char-black ravens
fancying themselves the phoenix
died as two, reborn as one
a crimson eye held in the other's esteem
refused to see the indifference of the world
one wing apiece they clung to one another
talons would tear out each other's heart
dare they choose to fall apart
and fall back to the earth
drink the blood and sap the strength
rob each other to make a gift
a paper valentine
take it to show the sun
watch it fade
cherish it until it falls apart
