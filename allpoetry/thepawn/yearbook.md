time to liberate can't hold back can't stand to see what this cage has turned me into a dandy fool but i guess we should let go let all those crazy thoughts out and hit record before it's too late you know i can't stand it when you watch over me like that but you'll just have to see what's in store no promises of a happy ending not this time not yet you may let your body decompose muscles feces eyes bed sores urine sweat and your mind may wander once again to the shores of cuba to see the lions on the beach tear them open and dissect them one by one if you please getting sick of this place getting sick of you getting sick of you all and i'm sure you all feel the same so let's take a break and get better lsd benzedrine morphine saccharine tablets and caffeine whatever it takes to revert to a comfortable state love those whom you wish and fuck with patience and wisdom like the rabbit sadomasochism for those who dare gentle kindness for those who dare not life's a game try not to shit your pants in the middle of the struggle but if you do don't be ashamed it's happened to the best of us if you must bow out please return sometime soon or at least spread the word throw discretion to the wind try to tap people on the shoulder lightly before you collapse their skull with a big fucking hammer just don't be surprised if we meet for the duration and i shy away for you see who we are now they're both taking a vacation to god knows where and i hope they have fun
