You must be so ashamed
Of all the nasty things I've said in your name
Guilty words preclude guilty actions
I wish I could take them all back
And give you a better impression
There's an embarassing beginning
To every story that's worthwhile
It's brought me this far so why go back now
I'd rather refine what I have left
Than turn away and relapse into
A persona I've come to regret
Sometimes I wish I could forget
But I'm living it every day
And every day it lets me learn something new
So I wish you could put it behind you
Learn to forgive learn to be patient
While I learn learn how to apologize
