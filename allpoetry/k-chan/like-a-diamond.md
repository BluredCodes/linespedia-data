---
title: "Like a Diamond"
writer: "K chan"
slug: "like-a-diamond"

On my shirt
The word "Angel."
That is what he calls me.
In my heart
The word "Angel."
Because that is what he calls me.

Fragile,
My dream.
Fragile my trust.
It can be shattered.
Tears might make it rust.
Does he know of my fragility?
Yes, for he attempts to soothe me.
He has not hidden secrets from me yet.
I'm watching; this is what I shalln't forget.
Dare I believe his sweet words and let him near?
Dare I sing to him my own true melody
The one he can join for a harmony?
His words go on to win over
Is he my four leafed clover?
Rare but b'loved and still
Play no more games!
As you will
Name me
Myself.
Inside him
It is illuminated
After all the times
I thought that it faded.
But even with that rich gleam
Still I fear for my awakened dream.
He’s like a crystal, I imagine,
A thing I might shatter
Should I touch him 
Should I believe
Should I 
Trust?

Glittering 
Something true
The light remains
Yet he is
Real

In my heart
He is like a crystal
That is what it seems to me
In my eyes
He is like a diamond
That is what he must beFor someone. -cc- *Blushie*
