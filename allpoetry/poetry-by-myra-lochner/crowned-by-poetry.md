Why sad, my Love, give me a kiss ...

 ** I need no kisses to be happy

Refusing a kiss? What does that mean?
I thought  all women need kiss and candy 
to be happy
 
 ** No ... I need roses and reason 
perhaps poetry ...

If love is the reason
roses pale into to a bloody bribe
what good is love, if love is bribed
by bloodied rose to do so?
(Pour me wine and pleasant music;
build me a small fire and beside
bring me the body of my beloved)
For if the way of love is bribe by reason,
then my desire is good reason for you to love 
lay your heart beside ...

 ** I want to walk on yonder hills
On connubial softly clouded paths 
I want to seek Soul's poetry
Written by angels, not by beastial skills
Let's seek then purity in poetry!


My beautiful lady gets her way:
poetry offers on a  plate ...
But it has to come  from the spring of spontaneity
and not from yesterday’s emotions ...
Let wine be aged;  let bread be fresh -
start now ... promise: no cheating.
Put away all the books of poems around you
close those poetry sites ... 
when you have done that let me know

 ** Aaaah, listen: who is master and who is slave?
I’ve done as you requested, Sire -
What shall I write, and dream about, dear Beast?
Shall I say what lies heavily on my mind
or unchain my heart … unburden breast ...
set free the birds of love  to rest upon your chest?

Great first line: great! I will follow now

 ** Before you do,  accept this gift
of fragrance ... yes, this  magic gift
of romance and of secrecy

Oh  Lady
That is not a line - that is a rose: you’ve cheated...
That is  not a line; it is a woe
a frightening delightful place to go ...

 ** ...

Once there was a man who dreamt he was a  prince
but he was nothing more than a poor beggar
 and he upon journeying to a foreign land
found the woman of his dreams
though in a dream he travelled so and found her ...

 ** My dearest Beast ... listen ...

He laid upon the grass - this man who would be king 
and gazed up at the azure skies ...
Yes Love?

 ** ... Poetry drips  from your fingertips
What calls it forth?

...only because you bring the love from me
...only because you call it forth

 ** This is a wonderous poem; it must be read aloud: 
with action and with music and with lights
What am I going to do with all your words?
I may not paste it under your own name
nor under mine, because it is ours ...

My words are for you...

 ** But what am I going to do with all these thoughts?
I am going to write a poem ...

(For me or for the world?)

 ** with all these words
(is it original,  or is it flawed)
 a poem for you ...

Is it mine?

 ** Yes …yours and also mine, to display before all eyes
please give me a line ...

Do you need a starting line, my love

 ** No: that I have ...

What kind of line must I give you ?

 ** Write me a haiku ...

Ok: i'll give you a haiku:

   the sound of autumn
   just like the fitful lovers 
   cicadas screaming
 
 ** Beautiful!
 ...oh to make beauty out of the thing hated
like God made light out of infernal darkness  …

   why do crickets chirp?
   because they are hungry for love
   or char'table crumbs

   I'm busy she says
   but bees ignore the acacia
   until it opens

   (Oh I hate haiku
   but my love makes me write them
   one does what love wants

   a beast of burden
   carries the world on his back
   always with a smile)

 ** Oh Sadness is where all 
the beauty of Poetry originates ...


Birds,cicades, crickets, monkeys ...
all cry and some make the most
melodious noise
I don't write from sadness -
I have that in the past
 ...so now I write from the next best thing:
longing

 **No wait! This is enough for one day, Beast ...
Your Majesty did well; true royal touch!
Go now and cool your heated blood:
I set you free ... from Poetry

 **(And when the sun at last touches 
them with tender fingers
it is the knowledge that she made him king that lingers ...)*wink*
