B e loved, i've forseen 

L ove, Divine...our own, innocent and pure 

U tterly in heavenly bliss 

S o happy together through eternity 

H appy as an imbreiated pair...imbreiated with the wine of love 

I nnocent and pure I give my heart to you, my virtue brand new 

N o woman has ever been so faithful than I 

G lowing with a rosy radience 

B eautiful! My soul eminates with a brilliant, Holy aura 

R adient and bright and spotless as my gown 

I bask in pure joy on this, my Wedding Day.. 

D ancing on air as I do a reverse moon walk up the Aisle 

E ternally pledging my love to the man I chose to love
