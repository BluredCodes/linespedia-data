I’ve come to realize how blessed I’ve been,
How fortunate, how lucky fate has treated me…
For I tell you no queen
Has ever been crowned so regally
With only half the wealth 
As I've inherited to enjoy and treasure
Nor any woman on this Earth
Has been graced with the privilege and honor
Of finding this gem of value and of great worth..
A gem, which sparkles in great splendor
And compliments my style, grace and character….
Bringing out the very best in me,
Yes, Indeed, I am the richest woman in this land….
I have been given this gift…. this perfect jewel of love....
The gift of a perfect husband!
