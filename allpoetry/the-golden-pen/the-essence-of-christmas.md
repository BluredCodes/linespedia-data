---
title: "The Essence of Christmas"
writer: "The Golden Pen"

Christmas is the radiant glow
That comes suddenly
Upon pale cheeks, tainted by the frigid winter's snow,
Christmas is the peals of laughter of joy and glee
And the carols sung by every youngster, young and old
Around the Christmas tree;

Christmas is the love
That is cherished forever
Inside a human treasure chest, it is the beams descending from above
From the light of the Christmas Star.
Christmas generates within the core of the soul
Transforming humanity into something extra-ordinary and beautiful.

Christmas is the Gift bestowed upon
A wicked world by God above...
A true gift of joy and adoration,
A metamorphis of the heart and mind..
The splendor of the Christ Child
Who on that first Christmas morn,
Enchanted this Earth with His Presence
Of transcendental love and true divinity...

Christmas is a mixture of all above..
Joy, cadence, laughter, wonder and Divine Peagentry...
An eternal, inner paradise!
God's Gift of unconditional love!
