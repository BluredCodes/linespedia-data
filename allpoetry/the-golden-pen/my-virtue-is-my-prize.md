My virtue is a prize
To be earned by a kingly man.
It cannot be bought, sold, or negociated in a compromise....
My virtue can only be won in a contest by a true gentleman
 
 
My virtue is above everything else in this existance
The knight who pursues my heart and soul
Must be an expert huntsman, willing to take a chance
And set his mark upon the goal.
 
 
This contest is not easy.
It takes great skills to obtain success.
But great joy and victory
Far outweighs the battle and endless stress.
Only those brave knights that can pass these pre-requisites
Get a chance to woo my heart and soul,
And it is only the best man of all these numberous suitors
Who walks off with my Virtue lifted high upon a pedestal!
