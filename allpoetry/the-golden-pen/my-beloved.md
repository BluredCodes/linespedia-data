---
title: "My Beloved"
writer: "The Golden Pen"

Sunshine on a silver platter
My beloved brings to me,
Music, sounds of birds twitter tatter
A symphony of nature
Around my bed brings he;

A mantle of warmth his arms engulf my soul,
A bath of kisses shower me with a love so pure and true,
My body tingles with a refreshing splash of his cologne...
with towels of carresses he dries my body, all clean and beautiful,
my skin soft and in tone
My Beloved lavishes me with his tender loving care.

And I am blessed be,
To have this perfect love that makes me feel alive and new.
My spirit soars higher than a cardinal
Over a sky of blue
My heart pounds with delight and a passion
That burns eternally
My Beloved rejoices and delights in me..
What more in this earth can I hope for
Than what my Beloved brings..I could not ask for more!
