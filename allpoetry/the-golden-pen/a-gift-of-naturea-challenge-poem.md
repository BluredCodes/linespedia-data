Eyes have never seen the living splendour, 
Nor human beings ever could explain, 
The beauty, grandeur and vivid colour 
That comes alive on a warm spring morning after rain, 
The verdant shiny blades of grass all shiny, tickling your feet, 
The chirpin' of the brown robins twitering from tree to tree, 
Your nose breathes in the aroma of  daffodils so sweet. 

Yes, nothing can compare to this picturesque display, 
Sun, warm and golden and smiling gaily 
As crickets in brown petticoats play the clarinet 
while birds sing in tune, and children play 
On golden blades of grass, as men sit by playing chess, 
In the park on this lovely Spring morning,and I sip lemondade, 
Praising a generous Heaven for giving us this Gift. 


Mary Gonzalez (c)2002
