---
title: "Love Tokens"
writer: "The Golden Pen"

I'll be sweet to you as the purest honey,
If this you promise me,
To be my love forever, faithfully
I promise to be as gentle as a lamb in spring,
And my love everflowing as the sea
Should you happiness and joy you'll bring
Like a smogasboard of deep affection unto me
I will give you the golden keys to my private paradise
Should you sweetly yeild your soul
And every day together as you look into my eyes,
You'll discover there a love and passion so real and beautiful...
Lover, swear that you'll be ever mine
And I will give myself to you,
My very soul, my heart, my mind is thine..
With a love so innocent and true.
