---
title: "A Love Divine"
writer: "The Golden Pen"
slug: "a-love-divine"

You make my heart and soul
Laugh with joy and glee,
I have been shown my inner beauty..I am beautiful!
Once divided and clouded in misery,
Now I am blinded by a healing light that shines,
Your presence has filled my life with rays of sunshine
I am made whole by a Love Divine.

A true miracle,
My life's been touched by a gentle, loving hand
As if by magic or the realization of an ancient oracle
I have found heaven
In the form of the perfect man!
