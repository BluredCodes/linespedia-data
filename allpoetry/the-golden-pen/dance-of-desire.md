Two souls embraced amongst a strawberry field, 
Bodies clasped around each other, 
Soul to soul, concealed 
Within mountains of red strawberries, dancing with one another 
In the morning sunlight.
Twriling from dusk till dawn in sheer delight. 
Mouth-watering desire mingled with the taste of passion fruit 
As the couple danced the Waltz of love together
cheek to cheek, Tongues in pursuit 
Tasting the effervescent fruit of exctacy and desire, 
Satisfying their carnal cravings with love's sweet kiss, 
Thus lighting passion's fire, 
As flesh burns against flesh in a moment of exstacy and bliss.. 
That consumes them both in an instant of deep passion and pleasure!Dedicated to my boyfriend, Alex Aris
