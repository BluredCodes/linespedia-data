Two silhouettes mirrored upon a golden sunset, 
Wrapped up in nothing but their own ecstasy, 
Two souls who have never met 
Suddenly unite, at last...alive and free. 

Snowflakes falling from their eyes, 
Mouths mingling together, dining in luxury! 
Hearts pounding, melting the ice 
A marriage of joy, rapture, pleasure and love divine, 
Sharing their nuptials around this December's Rendezvous! 



Mary Gonzalez (c)2002I wrote this poem in homage to the future meeting of myself and my beloved this coming Christmas 2002!
