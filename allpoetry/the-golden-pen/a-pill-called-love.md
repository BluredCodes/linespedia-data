title: "A pill called 'LOVE'"
writer: "The Golden Pen"

I never knew the full measure and weight of love until
It hit me like a brick wall
Since then I have come to realize 
The full extent and meaning of existance and the reason of Man's Fall
Is not sin nor work nor the sum of all its parts
But the truth of life lies
Within the particles of this tiny little pill
Giving you a mighty natural high
That lasts you forever, inflaming hearts
With the aphrodisiac of passion
A pill of this magnitude can only be manufactured in Heaven
by tiny angelic hands with the right ingredients on hand
Producing a drug so safe it's natural.
