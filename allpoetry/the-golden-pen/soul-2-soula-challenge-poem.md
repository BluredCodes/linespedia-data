Our souls have met before,
In heaven I suppose,
Now on Earth they chanced to meet once more
a Magnetic rendezvous 
Brought together by fate and love,
Our worlds collided once again,
Merging two seperate bodies that from above
Were born as ONE and yet mistakenly seperated up in Heaven,
By Destiny's hand, these two have met at last...
Heart talking to Heart, soul dancing with soul
Our souls now celebrate this nuptial of desire in celebration of our past!

I knew there was something familiar in your eyes,
Something of mine deep down in your soul
Your body reflected images of me in masculine disquise,
The minute we touched we knew
We were cut from the same material
Sharing the same essence and cosmic design,
Breathing the same breath as our two hearts beat in unison.
It was Destiny's Miracle
That brought us face to face
Soul 2 Soul 
Merging in a loving embrace,
Never to part again, but embark upon its journey through Eternity
Ying and Yang have finally found each other through time and space.
