Two souls growing and living far apart,
The one from the other reaching out past the distance..
Heart calling heart
At fate's insistance.
Joined by an inner connection non could explain,
As blood is thicker than water
The truth remains,
Spirit is much bolder and richer..
Soul seeks its own counterpart
With the hope of uniting once and for all
Via a telepathic wavelength and the power of Love..
Two kindered spirits that were once split in twain
Finally Merge once again


Mary Gonzalez (c)2002Dedicated to my Soul Mate: Alexander Frederick Anthony Aris who beckoned my soul beyond distance and space and became my best friend, my boyfriend, my cofindant and now my fiance.
