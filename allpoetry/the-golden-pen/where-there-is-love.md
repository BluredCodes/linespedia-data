---
title: "Where There is Love"
writer: "The Golden Pen"
slug: "where-there-is-love"

Where there is love,
The world is a better place.
Souls who immerse from the Holy showers from above,
Are drenched in a pure mystic grace.
Where love dwells and reigns,
No corruption or destruction seeps in.
Where there is love, hope remains.
The heart where the seeds of love are sowed within
A fertile soil, blossoms in full array,
Adorning nature with vesitage colorful and gay;
Where there is love the human race
Breathes the air of joy and peace
Where there is love God governs the soul
And greets His children face to face..
Where there is love
Life is full and sweet..
Love's the very lifelihood that pumps life within the soul's veins
When Love generates, the soul's nourishment is complete..
When Love's the strategy instead of hate everyone wins...
Faith, hope and charity and a Holy peace remain
Yes, Love makes the difference
And proves a strong deffense against violence..
Love is the boundary between war and peace
With love man finds a holy innocence
A state of brotherhood and joy that will never cease.
