.c.o.|.d.

warmth drained from my very fingertips.
hope everlasting chains me to the mortar of the wall.
my lonliness has become my only salvation.
my pity - weakness.
my heart beats fitfully
with no reason left to live.
all i can feel -
all thats left

is the .c.o.|.d.

my temptation draws me nearer;
closer to the flame
humid air shallows my lungs
the fumes make it hard to breathe
i turn away 
shocked at what i see.
my body - ringed in blood
still spilling from my wrists
back to my past i run

feeling the pureness of  the .c.o.|.d. that surrounds me

Gray sky
as far as my eye can see.
bleak wind
filling my ears with the song of a forlorn past
and a future with no hope or love
i spread my arms
and feel its chill caresses raise my skin.
i breath deeply and fall forward over the cliff -
the place where i once held my percarious perch.
its all gone

now i feel only .c.o.|.d.

I open my blank eyes
and stare at the ground as it rapidly approaches.
my heart was content with this
and all i want has been fufilled
but i feel empty inside
as i listlessly plummet to the ground like an inatimate china doll
i never hoped it would come to this
now the ground nears

and an everlasting .c.o.|.d. overtakes me

i thought this was what i wanted
