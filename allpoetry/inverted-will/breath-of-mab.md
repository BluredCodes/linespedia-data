title: "breath of mab"
writer: "Inverted Will"

I hear whispers
on the broken path of what once was.
Whispers of Mab's realm,
where all our nightmares lie within the cruel stare of her hard, cold, unloving eyes.
"We are totally alone"
she whispers to me.
Her musical voice, beautiful to the ears,
a horrid scene to the soul.
"You merely have me."
Her whispers echo throughout the cold room.
"Come to me my beautiful darling,
become my prey."

And I stand strong, but once again fall hopeless under the call of her manevolent voice.

Pulled into my dream world again.
