title: "my mind merely wanders"

My mind wanders

on the teathered, tedious, restraints of the wind.

Each night a new idea.  Each passing moment contains a wonder to my eyes that had previously lay hidden.

I hope the storms help me to think...

I have regained my joy of it.

me
