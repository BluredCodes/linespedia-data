To you right is Death
To your left is Joy
Ahead of you is Prosperity
Behind you is Fate,
I dare ou to move...
Which path do you choose?

To your right is Destination 
To your left is Playfulness
Ahead of you is Youth 
Behind you is Life,
I dare you to move...
Which path do you choose?

To your right is Aggravation
To your left is Pleasure
Ahead of you is Peace 
Behind you is Maturity,
I dare you to move...
Which path do you choose?

To your right is Love 
To your left is Desolation
Ahead of you is Forgiveness
Behind you is Blame,
I dare you to move...
Which path do you choose?

To your right is a Road
To your left is a Map
Ahead of you is a Way
Behind you is a Trap,
I dare you to move...
Which path do you choose?

To your right is Knowledge
To your left is Teaching
Ahead of you is Friendship
Behind you is Loneliness,
I dare you to move...
Which path do you choose?I thought that this was a good way to show how the good comes with the bad... and even though you may think you would want to choose the good in every segament, the bad (that comes with that good) may be worse than simply choosing the bad to start off with.
