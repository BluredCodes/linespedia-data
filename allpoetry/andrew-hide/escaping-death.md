And as I find calm,

at the end of the universe,

last resting place

from infinity,

the calm, washes over me,

mist clears my thoughts

of the fear of death, I've had,

being, as I am

I can now rest,

have a home,

found outside of time,

hope is now in me,

in this space

a non-existant life time away,

new dreams can now be realised,

place content in my heart

where before, only fear existed,

time can no longer end my days

can't bring my death,

exist I shall forever, outside of time.This was originally a Survivor challenge.

To write a poem, the first word of each line when put together should make a sentence, related to the poem.
