title: "Celtic Story Teller."

Way back in celtic England,
so the stories say,
that the druids in their folklore,
had a magic kind of way.

One of these ways I'll tell you,
and time I hope your find,
to try something different,
and open up your mind.

When you're feeling weary,
and wish to take a rest,
go down to the woods,
and try this simple test.

Find an ancient tree,
a comfortable spot within,
relax and close your eyes,
and let the dreams begin.

The spirit of the tree
will tell you stories, old,
of wonders that it's seen,
and other trees have told.

In dreams you'll see the visions,
of the tales that are told,
from a celtic tree spirit,
the story teller, from days of olde.
