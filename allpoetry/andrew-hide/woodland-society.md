A group of trees, a wooded land,
all that we see, do we understand?


The ivy climbs the oak,
stangling its sole provider,
the bramble, like a falling cloak,
forever growing wider.
The popular reaches up for heaven,
still looking for more light,
the silver birch, with ghostly glow,
ready to fight the night.
The holly creates a shelter,
but to prickly for us to live.
High in the apple, mistletoe,
with berries, poisonous, white,
sucking from its hosts boughs,
lifes blood, a parasite.
