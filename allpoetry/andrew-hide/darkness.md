              A 
              R
              K

The sun beats down, burning,
hot against my skin,
but it's brightness doesn't penetrate
to the darkened soul within.
              
              D
              A
              R
              K
              N
              E
              S
              S

The halogen lamps, hurt my eyes,
image of a blinding light,
but it doesn't illuminate the walls
of my soul in endless night.
              
              D
              A
              R
              K
              E
              R
              N
              E
              S
              S

The candle-light flickers,
dancing across the ceiling,
but this beautiful, twisting light,
can not sooth the darkened feeling.

              B
              L
              A
              C
              K
