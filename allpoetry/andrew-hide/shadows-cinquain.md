title: "Shadows   (cinquain)"
writer: "Andrew Hide"

Shadows,
dark, haunted sight,
images, from nothing,
runs ice over my soul, inside,
shivers.

Creeping,
forever there,
on the path I have chose,
to find enlightenment and truth,
in light.

Only
with perception,
and with understanding,
will I be able to see through
shadows.
