title: "The Touch of Frost.      (Cinquain)"

The frost
blankets over,
all left standing by morn,
caressing, both alive and dead,
so cold.

The grass
becomes as glass,
brittle under foot fall,
webs, dress glazed blades in chandelier,
ghost white.

Mirrored,
solid water,
ice covers silent pond,
making liquid firm to the touch,
dead still.

Covered,
is my bare skin,
ice fingers, penetrate,
deep into my flesh, awaiting
deaths warmth.A survivor challenge.
