{If I was} a {sinnerman},
{lay my body down} in my {trail of tears),
for I would fear to {live forever},
in my {wastelands},
these {fields of fire}.

{Dear god},{breathe} 
the {call of the wild} into me,
and set this {fallen angel} {free},
{take me home} to my {sister and brother},
my {homeland}, I want to see {that certain smile},
{the gift}, so {I see hope} in the morning light.

{May your good lord}, {the maker},
turn my {hell into heaven}
bring warmth to my {cold, cold heart},
for {when the wind blows},
I shall lay down my {guns and arrows},
before we {all fall down}.

Place your {hands around my heart},
show me the {light in your eyes},
the {textures} of {pure love},
stop this sinnerman from
{tumbling down}.All song titles from Midge Ure.
