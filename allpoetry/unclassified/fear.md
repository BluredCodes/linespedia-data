In the boots of an old man
we watch swallow after swallow
tasting the bitter juice squeezed regrets 
watching him
gazing slowly upon his wrinkled skin
the accustomed frown on yesterdays back door step
fear in his eyes just looking up
the mountains, a face
blank as he stares
wondering from where
this man even comes from
karma killed the beauty of life
and thats just a lesson learned
because nature has her way with you
always
and when you don't know it
its only sweet revenge
did you hang the women of western Europe?
did you slaughter the Jews in the Holocaust?
did you demand the black servants 
and rape their daughters 
and kill their children?
Fear.
He doesn't know why
he cant tell you himself
to even play the part
so we watch
send our regards to the gods, mother earth
strong and powerful she gets her way
always
