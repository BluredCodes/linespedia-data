whether it be you or I
that measures the moments
one of us constantly -breaks
the motion is ticking

the pendulum sweeping
parabolas arching
extensions through time
by which we connect

i mention quietly
you rain down the heavens
i cannot imagine 
reaching to your absence

[eloquent stutterings of a segment of space
which reverberates with a meaning----------lostamemory
long distorted      copper coins tarnished to pay the fare:::temptations
{to destroy
all but the truth}spake
Hamelin attracting the rats
sickening spiral of the lies intruding everysecondminuteofwaking_____
a grip which slips upon your wrists whenever
the
cards
fall.
           The skies are revolving under my feet.
           Unhinged and suspended in spatial constellations equals ecstatic transcendence.
           Movement revolts; my cochlea cracks.
           All too self-referential.]

....It's all a shocking process of self-elimination, please remain seated, it's secretly glorification....
