What God Has Done For Us!

Do You know what You did when You died on the cross,
And how You brought all of us life.

Do You know the joy that You have given to me,
All the love that covers the strife.

I know how much better my life is with You,
And i will share Your love with all.

I know how much I mean to You,
How You always come when I call.
