A Dream Come True

The perfect guy,
The perfect night.

The perfect meal,
The perfect light.

The perfect scene,
The perfect kiss.

The perfect shoes,
The perfect dress.

A dream come true,
A gleam in my eye.

A wonderful day,
And loves goodnight.
