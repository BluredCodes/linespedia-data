Where ever I am
I am  an unknown face
In between  the mob.
A dark shadow 
Swinging on my mind
As an aloner
The wordsless  dialogue 
 with an artificial Relations 
When ever a smile smiles
It glows as a glittering spark
Expressions and feelings 
Doesn’t explain to any one
Doesn’t share any one
everyone are...
In their selfish motive
They are in their war
They are in their dreams
Hours of time melting 
They are in their despair
Castles may be figured
on white paper as civil plan
As a fantasy ,as a question
smile doesn't bloom
and a unsatisfied dialogue
They says that-
You wander in your dreams 
We are busy in our work
We are busy in our dreams
Tears may distrub
time may distrub 
Where ever I am
I am  an unknown face.
