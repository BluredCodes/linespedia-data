It is sad to think that life flies.
The word "drifts" would be better.
Airplanes don't drift,
And things can drift, yet stay.
Death drifts, no...
It looms, hangs and stands.
It waits
For a sign that you and I will never see,
And it falls
With the likeness of a sharp needle
Aimed at bursting our bubbles.we are all driftwood...
