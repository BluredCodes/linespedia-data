title: "Diamond"
writer: "atia"

Consider yourself a diamond in the rough
Every gash the world throws at you
Will only make you brighter
Will only fill you up with light,
Will only make you shine,
Will only define you,
Make you into the person 
You were meant to be.
So what if it doesn’t work?
Know you, have you, love you
Love life, 
Love the width and breadth of it
But love. 
Love every scratch that leaps
Upon your skin
Every look that rests
Upon your mind.
Did you think it would be simple?
Welcome to the world of fools!
Can you feel yourself falling?
Just as I did
And do everyday…
Perhaps someday the fatalism,
The idealism,
Will all come to mean a thing or two,
And we shall be cut
To fit in…His name is Diamond but this is not for him. This is for me... This is everything that I have learnt because I know it can never be.
