I've been up an extra hour
Getting my life in order,
Working on the part of me
You don't care to know about.
If you do, 
It never shows
And I can not think of it
Anymore.

The day saw me rise,
Higher and happier,
Then sad again.
But I have sworn off caffeine.
I have no wish to hide
Anything I feel
From me..

The night shall see me still
Hoping to hibernate,
As I reach out once again
For my bottle of 
"Extra Healing" 
Body lotion.I would like to dedicate this to my little stuffed winnie the pooh and friends who are my instant doses of happiness :)
