Do you know that its liberating
To know that you are trapped?
Or that self-conflict
Is actually resolving
When you know that's where you are?
Today I learnt why I hide
Why I crouch away in a corner,
Why I've travelled thus far
And locked me up this tight...
I'm too afraid to feel
When I step out,
To hear things I shouldn't be.

Do you know if you reached out
I would take your hand?
And that scares me to bits...
