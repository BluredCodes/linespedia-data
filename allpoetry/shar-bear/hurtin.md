---
title: "hurtin'"
writer: "shar bear"
slug: "hurtin"
source: "AllPoetry"

this one goes out to the one i love and miss
and everyday i wish, for just one more kiss
for you to hold me again,in ure arms so tight
looks like it might be another lonely night
you told me you loved me, and you left me
told me no more fears and no more tears
i need you in my life so these black clouds clear
baby i cant explain just how you made me feel
had the power to heal, so tell me whats the deal
i need you now, so baby come home fast
and we can make it last,more future less past
i cant tell you how much i miss you, and how bad i wanna kiss you
for you i would wait a lifetime, you ma pride and joy
