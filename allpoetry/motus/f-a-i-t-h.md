FAITH,
SOMETIMES I SIT DOWN AND THINK IF WHAT I DID THIS MORNING WAS MY DOING 
OR JUST A ACT OF FAITH,
SET OUT THIS DAY IN THE MORNING LIKE EVERY OTHER DAY I DREAM ON MY WAY TO WORK OF 
ALL THE THINGS THAT I WANT TO BE,
THE THINGS I WANT LIFE TO GIVE ME,
THE THINGS THAT I`VE ALWAYS DREAM`T OF!!!!!
HOW MANY OF US GET THESE DREAMS OF US  TO COME TRUE?
LIFE FROM MY OWN THINKING IS JUST A PLAIN ACT OF FAITH!!!!
I STRONGLY BELIEVE THAT IF ONE COMPLETES A JOB, A ACT, WELL DONE A  MARRIAGE ITS NOT HIMSELF 
DOING THIS ITS FAITH (GOD) HE CAN DO ANY THING HE BELIEVES IN!!!!
SET YOUSELF OUT LOOK IN TO THE MIRROR WHAT DO U SEE?
JUST A BARE HUMAN FACE WITH A REFLECTION OF FAITH, IF YOU LET SOMEONE CLOSE TO
YOU LOOK IN TO YOUR EYES ASK THEM WHAT THE SEE DEEP INSIDE?
ALL THEY SEE IS DARKNESS, THIS IS FAITH,
EVERY HUMAN GETS HIS OR HER FAITH WRITTEN BEFORE THEY ENTER THIS WORLD,
THEN ITS JUST A FEW STEPS TILL WE GET THERE, DEPENDS SOME WALK UP QUICKLY,
SOME TAKE THE LIFT, SOME USE THE EXPRESS ELEVATOR, BUT LET ME REMIND YOU THEY ALL REACH 
THE SAME POINT .......
ITS JUST SOME REACH FAST AND SOME REACH THERE SLOWLY, ALL DEPENDS ON THE WAY, THE PATH OF
LIFE WE CHOOSE, IN ALL THIS FAITH PLAYS A VERY IMPORTANT ROLE,
ITS ALSO ONES OWN BELIEF!
SO LETS FIRST START BY HAVING FAITH IN ONES SELF THEN SET OUT TO CONQUER THE WORLD!!!!!
IN ALL THIS I FORGOT TO TELL YOU IAM STILL DREAMING AND - GOSH ITS OVER IVE` REACHED WORK
JUST ANOTHER DAY  STARTS ALL OVER AGAIN,
DILIP/MOTUS
