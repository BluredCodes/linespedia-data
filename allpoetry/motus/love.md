Love,

What is love?
We all can define love in various different ways.
Love is something which grows with time its like old`wine
The older it grows the more muture it gets,
The taste of old wine is splendid the taste of old love is not so,
In life we all are on the look out for new love or to be loved by someone!
When that someone comes by,we never understand him/her we never take them seriously untill that someone leaves,
This brings out the love in us which we never saw when that someone special was with us,
Love is just a four letter word` but in real it means a lot.
Telling someone I love you ! can change the world for that some one who really is waiting to hear that.
And for someone who is used to hearing that everyday it means just nothing ….
Yet love is a common word used on a daily basis by us all,
Lets love someone and let someone love us.


Dilip
