IN LIFE,WE SOMETIMES SIT AND WONDER!
WHAT IS THIS ALL ABOUT?
IS IT WHAT WE MAKE OF  IT OR IS IT SOMETHING THAT HAS BEEN WRITTEN FOR US,
TIME PASSES WE GROW FROM OUR TEENS IN TO YOUTH AND YOUTH IN TO THE OLD AGE,
THEN ONE DAY WHEN ALL THIS TIME HAS PASSED 
WE SIT AND THINK ,DID WE EVER GET TO DO ALL THE
THINGS WE EVER WANTED?
AND THE ANSWER IS NO !!!!
THIS IS BECAUSE THE SPACE BETWEEN EACH GROWTH OF OUR LIFE WE NEVVER UNDERSTOOD 
WHAT WE DID WRONG AND HOW IN THE NEXT WE SHOULD CORRECT IT , 
UNTILL THAT ONE DAY COMES ,IT CREEPS UP ON YOU AND YOU ARE  OLD!!!!!
ITS ONLY HERE WE FINALLY LET OUR MIND TAKE OVER THE HEART AND TELL US ALL THE MISTAKES
WE MADE AND COULD HAVE AVOIDED`BUT GUESS WHAT ITS NEVER TOO LATE,
TEACH THE YOUNG ONES GUIDE THEM SO THE DONT MAKE THE SAME MISTAKES
IN LIFE AND REACH WHERE WE HAVE!! JUST SITING IN A CORNER WONDERING WHAT WENT WRONG AND WHERE?




DILIP
