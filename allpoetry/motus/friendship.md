TO A VERY DEAR FRIEND,
WHEN WE FIRST MET I NEVER THOUGHT WE WOULD END UP BEING GOOD FRIENDS OR EVEN FRIENDS,
TIME HAS PASSED SINCE WE FIRST MET,OUR FRIENDSHIP HAS GROWN,MY LOVE FOR YOU AS A FRIEND 
HAS GROWN,
ITS VERY EASY TO MAKE FRIENDS BUT REAL HARD TO KEEP UP TO THAT FRIENDSHIP FOR AS LONG AS
WE HAVE BEEN PALS,I HAVE HEARD YOUR HAPPY AND SAD TIMES,AND IVE SHARED MOST OF MINE
TO YOU AS WELL,YOU HAVE ALWAYS BEEN THERE AS A FRIEND WHEN I NEEDED A SHOULDER,
I HOPE WE REMAIN FRIENDS FOR LIFE AND NOTHING COMES IN OUR WAY.
BUT I ADMIT TODAY THAT I KNOW THIS IS NOT FOREVER ONE DAY YOU WILL BE MARRIED AND
GO AWAY ,THEN ALL I WILL HAVE IS THE GOOD MEMORIES OF YOU AND THE TIMES WE SPOKE
AND LAUGHED TOGETHER,
AND MOST OF I WILL MISS HAVING A GOOD FRIEND,MAY BE I HAVEN`T TOLD YOU THIS BEFORE
CAUSE I WAITED FOR A SPECIAL TIME TO LET YOU KNOW THAT I ALWAYS CARED FOR YOU FROM 
THE BOTTOM OF MY HEART AND WILL ALWAYS DO,THE SPACE YOU HAVE I DONT THINK I WILL BE ABLE TO
REPLACE,BUT YES I WISH YOU ALL THE BEST IN LIFE AND HOPE THAT THIS SOME ONE YOU FIND AS
YOUR LIFE PARTNER CARES,LOVES AND MOST OF ALL UNDERSTANDS YOU FOR WHAT YOU ARE 
A WONDERFULL AND A SWEET PERSON IN REAL.
OUT OF EVERY 1000000000000000000000000000000000000000
 
MOTUS
THIS ONES PERSONAL, WRITTEN FOR A GOOD FRIEND OF MINE AND WHOSE A WONDERFULL PERSON
ONLY SHE DOSENT KNOW THAT HERSELF...........
GUYS PLS ALL WRITE BACK AND LET HER KNOW SHES IMPORTANT TO MOTUS.......THE PART TIME
AUTHOR:)
LOVES
DILIP
