---
title: "Your eyes (love)"
writer: "motus"
slug: "your-eyes-love"
source: "AllPoetry"

IN EVERY CROWDED ROOM I SEARCH FOR THOSE FAMILIAR EYES,
FOR WHEN I SEE THEM,
 I TURN AWAY AS THE WORLD MAY SEE THE LOVE BETWEEN US WHICH I NEVER END HIDE


DILIP.S.
