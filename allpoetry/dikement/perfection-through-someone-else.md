title: "Perfection Through Someone Else"

Your skin
My heart
Let's bleed.
Let's resurrect these days that passed us by.
For love
For hate,
Just empty into me your thoughts on everything.
I'll be your book,
Write some chapters and bury your stories knee-deep.
Consolidate all your trust in me,
Just trust in me and love me.
I beg
I plead
I'll harbor all these feelings in my mind,
I won't let go,
Until I meet you at the right time.
Then I'll envelope you in all my ways
And teach you what I learned today.
We will be together,
We will be a constant force.
