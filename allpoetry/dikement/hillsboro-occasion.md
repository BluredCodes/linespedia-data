If you stalk me, hold me gently.
If you rob me, take the TV and turn the lights off.
Indignation, self-mutilation.
Its all the same.
Rhyme these kids to sleep.
Drive a car into a ditch.
Seed's are sewn every quarter-hour.
I passed out yesterday, and woke up shameless.
I need a free path.

My friend walked a freeway exit,
Lined with glittering stars.
How close I am, I'd have killed,
"To be where you are."

Turn the lights off,
And strip search the battery wires.
Turn the lights off,
Hold me gently,
Use a magnifying glass,
But don't start fires.
Branch out.
