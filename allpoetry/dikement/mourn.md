Sing to me,
I'm too self-centered, 
And selfish
And self-demanding.
Give me a chance (there I go again).

With a trouble so deep,
Would you be the same as I am?
Crowded with glances of wrongly accused curses,
And filled to the brim with screams of contempt?
Well, here I am...

Sing to me,
I deserve it.
I feel so sick,
And so sad, 
I need comfort.
Give me a chance (there I go again).

With a burden so broken,
Would you act the same as I do now?
Struck down by abusive mental capacities,
Ones that can't keep to themselves?
Well, here I am.

There I am.
And there I go...
