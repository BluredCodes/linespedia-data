title: "Irresistibly Something..."

In the meantime, 
The other side's asleep at the wheel.
You look content in your daze.
Our eyes meet in my sleep,
I hold my heart,
Pretending to be unaware.

I still hold this useless feeling,
No one hears me anymore.
I still have this hoping season,
No one wants it anymore.

I am a born inconvenience.
I walk in turbulent pathways,
The lighting is unfocused and unclear.
Where do you want me to sit?
I'll stand...

I still grip this hurting feeling,
No one hears me anymore.
I still fuck myself, searching for meaning,
No one gives a shit anymore.

You can cry all you want,
If you start to bleed you've been cursed.
Don't look my way...
I only speak in words...
Don't look my way...
I only speak through hurt...
