If we sit here long enough,
Will our seats melt?
Will our chairs burn and turn black?
The time that I have spent here,
Has added up and is staring me in the face,
Its expression's blank.

We are eroding,
I just know it.
We each swore in blood that this would never happen.
Well, just look...

If I stare at the wall long enough,
Will a picture of you appear?
Probably so, this dimension's bending,
As my dilemma's building.
My expression's blank.

We are eroding,
I just know it.
We each signed on the dotted line that this would never happen.
Just fucking look...
And cry your eyes out just for me,
We'll never have these times again,
We'll never hold these hands again,
It's all just boiling down...
