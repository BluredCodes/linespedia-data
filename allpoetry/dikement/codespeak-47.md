I want resurrection.
Rip the skin from my bones.
Take away all my hate.
Open wounds are beautiful.
Shattered glass cut the roses neat,
And with a silent breath I began to overheat.
You hear me, don't you?

Risk the chance that you wish to take,
Blame whoever you need to blame,
Just walk the skyline's gaze.
I need to look away...
Soared through cataclismic feats,
And as I pause to reaffirm myself, I overheat.
You hear me, don't you?

SCREAM...
All I want to hear is your voice caught in a tangle.
Pledge to me you'll do everything to save me.
Praise me.
Praise me.
Rip the grin off my face.
It was murky to begin with,
It was sad and full of shame.

Shattered time revealed to me through me,
And with a shortened headstrong glance, I began to overheat.
You hear me, don't you?

'Cause now I scream...
All I wanna know is your skin softly grazing my neck.
Pledge to me you'll save this sordid soul before there's no turning back.
Praise me.
Praise me.
