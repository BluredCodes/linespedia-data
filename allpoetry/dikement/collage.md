---
title: "Collage"
writer: "DikeMent"

Paint me a pretty picture,
Enveloped in colors and shades.
Inspired and beautiful,
Everything in touch with today.

I'll take your hand,
And cut off your fingers.
I'll take your painting,
And piss all over it just to please me.
'Cause I hate the world today.
Nowadays...

Ask me a question without want or need of answer.
Build me a bridge,
Specifically for me to burn to the ground.
Go on your way, pretend that I'm secure...
No wait, just...

I'll take your hand,
And cut off your fingers.
I'll take your question,
And piss on its personification.
It's all in tact, just to please me.
'Cause I hate the world today.
Nowadays...
