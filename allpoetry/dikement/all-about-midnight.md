Gone out tonight
Stars said they'd shine
I whisper to the wind and ask it gently,
"What can I do?"

'Cause I feel stuck here,
Daytime's nagging and the clock just keeps on ticking.

Parallel to all my decisions,
I tremble as the doctor makes his first incision.
You wonder, don't you?
Just as I do,
"What can I do?"

I can't help it,
I feel so stuck here,
You are wasting my time, or I'm wasting mine.
