You're fucking in the next room.
Scream his name, whore.

You salivate and wail every detail.
You tell me with the best description you can give.
I only asked how your day was.

We're close...
So why do I separate you?
I love you...
So why do I try to hate you?

I awake to the sound of you.
You're with him, I'm against the wall in tears.

I tried, but he caught the star as it fell.
While I was truly reaching,
He strolled in and stole your heart
(Coincidentally breaking mine).

We're close...
So why I separate you?
I love you...
So why do I try to hate you?

Fuck him 'cause you "love" him.
Fuck him 'cause you "love" him.
You know in 9 weeks it'll all crash and burn.
I pray to God it meets the fate that it deserves.
