title: "To: A Fallen Angle"
writer: "Isz"

.               As the sky opens with tears
.             The winds bring foul news
.           For the angle has started crying
.
.         Your smile has faded
.           Like the fleeting sunset
.             Leaving me speechless
.
.               I travel to you with hope
.             Filling my mind with troubles
.           Even only how to give you flight
.
.         For you have fallen from heaven
.       And to put you back there
.         Is what must be done
.
.           Leave this world to me
.             With my mind I will heal
.               A hug will bring a smile
.
.                 Enjoy your life as you may
.                   Leave the problems for me
.                     I will fix them as I do
.
.                       When it is time
.                         I will join you
.                           Till then smile for me
.
.                         The light will be found
.                       With time to heal
.                     And a path to follow
.
.                   I will wipe your tears
.                 Fix your broken wings
.                   Fill you with joy
.
.                     Let me help you
.                       Before you help me
.                     It is what has to be
.
.                   For from the heavens above
.                     You can give me the warm
.                       Everything needed for me to smile
.
.                     Take flight dear angle
.                   To the clouds above
.                     And be happy forever
.
.                            By IszIt was something needed to be said... thou truly I don't know if it works out well in my poetic form.
