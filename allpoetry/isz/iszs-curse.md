Tis the time of the awakening
For the fingers have been pointed
And my curse has come into the light
The pain around me has found it's root
The others are having the pain
Even if it is between themselfs
Not envolving me in the slitest
It always happens while I am around
So the fingers point to me
For if I was not there
The pain would not have been
Those are words of wisdom to all
I will always be there when you have time
But beware of the pain I bring
For all around are touched by my curseNo word checker
