---
title: "Never to the End"
writer: "Isz"
slug: "never-to-the-end"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/60151-Never-to-the-End-by-Isz"
---

You have never been there
To see the world in my light
The tears of my soul
Dripping free from my veins
Ever is it flowing
Pain so deep it scars my mind

Never will you see me
For no one understands
My world is mine to shape
But it's a craft I have lost
So I hold the pain closer
Waiting for the never ending day

At my side you faltered
Never to stand again
I will never be there
For I must stand alone
The scars are taking hold
And I cry out loud

You have never been here
In this world of mine
No one can see through my eyes
My curse is mine a loan
I dream the night away
And hope to die by morning

By Isz
