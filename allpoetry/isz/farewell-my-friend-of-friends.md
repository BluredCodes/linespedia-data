The days come to a close
You are always on my mind
Back as far as I can remember
You where always there
When I was sick you would know
The warmth of your body kept me sain
Your call I waited for every morning

Even though you could not be there
I told you all my problems
You where my closest friend
And you where my son

Now even thou you are sick
I wish you where close
Miles apart yet steps in my heart
I will run to your side
Help you through the pain
Even now I know you must go
You take with you most of my soul

Dear Meara I will always miss you
May your days here have been full of happieness
The memorie of everything you are will never fade
My son I hope you will never forget me
I will never forget youThis is a poem for my late cat... I will miss him forever and always.
