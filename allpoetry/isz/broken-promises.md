---
title: "Broken Promises"
writer: "Isz"
slug: "broken-promises"
source: "AllPoetry"

As time slips away
I remember all that we said
You’re empty promises
Everything has been broken
You can't call
Reasons unknown
Just to be there
But a back is all I see
Don't say words you can't keep
Might as well walk to the wind
For that’s all your words are to me now

By Isz
