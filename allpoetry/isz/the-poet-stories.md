title: "The Poet Stories"

As the mind runs with memories and tales 
The heart is set free to play 
Thou the body is stuck here 
The soul flows endlessly through out time 
With all senses unleashed onto the world 
A piece of paper becomes a world 
With a pen, it is shaped 
By the end of it all 
When everything returns to a whole 
A story has be told 
For this is the way 
The way of the poets 
And the stories they love to tell 

By IszMay your stories never have an end.
And your worlds been endless to the imagination of all.
