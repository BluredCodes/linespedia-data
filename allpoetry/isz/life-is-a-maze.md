title: "Life is a Maze"

I walked the paths of live
Only to see the walls around me
It is true that we do walk a loan
Always trapped in a maze of life
Thou some find the maps that are hidden
Others just find their own way
From the paths I have taken
The countless dead ends I have found
I could map it out for you
But would you believe me
Or just continue traveling
I have found no map
And have taken no help
For this I am lost
Forced to forever travel
This maze of life

By Isz
