How long is forever to you?
Is it to the end of time?
Lifetimes till its end
Just a few years
Days that sail on by
Could it be hours?
More like minutes
Maybe even seconds
But the world has shown me this
That forever is only
What you MAY have thought
For that blink of an eye
So when you say
I will love you forever
Remember that forever
Is a life time to me
I'm sorry I believed you

By Isz
