All around me the battle rages
I seek the inner calm that I know so well
it was easy to reach in the school room but so differant 
here as the battle rages and men die for a king
I slip into the calm that is in me

I focas 
wave my hands in intricate motions
mutter arcane words
Heat explodes from my hands
men scream and die

I hide from my eyes from there suffering
but no I can not take
all the while the battle rages

I run
the blood of others om my hands
why am I weak?
