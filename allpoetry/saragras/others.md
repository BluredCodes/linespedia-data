I sit there and stare at the powder
feel the needle in my hand
the knife in the kitchen
it would be so easy to end this painfull
existince

a voice calls
no dont I care
it calls it sounds far
I ignore it

I sit there and stare at the powder
feel the needle in my hand
the knife in the kitchen
it would be so easy to end this painfull
existince

no dont we care 
the voice calls again
joined by another
i fight the urge to look and see 
who
its nothing a joke
they dont care says 
a sinistar voice

I place the powder in my mouth
place the needle in my vain
grab the sharp knife
now end it the the sinistar voice says
"no don't" the voices say "we care"
they sound closeJust letting out some things from a bad time in my life.
