Hiddin 
unable to face them
there voices sound near
or prehaps they are far 
there voices carried by the dark

I crawl on
unable to walk 
Why?
I am unable to remember why

Someone whispers 
give in 
end your torment
death whispers
in the dark

Shouts are near
these sounds are 
are at hand

I feel uplifted
I am flying upwards
I am leaving the voice 
in the dark

Then I sadly relise 
Im dead and my soul
is floating up to 
the pearly gates 
of the afterlife
