I live life with no hope
no ray of light to lift
the darkness i am forever 
trapped in this hellish
life.........
with no hope

I live in the dark
with no friends or allies
only enemies
for they despise me
forever putting me down
i live with no hope

once a ray of light
shown down upone me
but...............
the darkness repulsed it
for i am not allowed to
live in the light
for i have no hope
and therefore doomed
to live in the darka poem about my life
