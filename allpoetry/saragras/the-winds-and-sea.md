the wind is strong
the waves high
we fill our sails
with the winds bounty
we plow through the waves
the wind picks up and we 
fly accross the sea

the sky darkens
black clouds roll up
the sea foams and boils
waves crash acros the 
ship

still we hold the ship
on course
we laugh at the waves and 
the black clouds
the waves rise up
and crash upon the ship 
and still we laugh

the clouds fall back
and we laugh somemore
then we sail onwards
into the unkownjust some crazy thing i wrote
