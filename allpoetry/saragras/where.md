---
title: "Where?"
writer: "Saragras"
slug: "where"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56139-Where--by-Saragras"
---

I fear they are near

where are they?

near or far?

Are they upon or lost behind?

Is it real or my mind?

I fear they are near

steps upon the path

I hear 

To late for they are upon me
