I lash out
I feel the battle rage 
build in me
I lose my mind to the horrors that are me
My blade finds flesh again
I scream an inhuman roar
I succumb to the rage
again and again I lash out
my blade is fed blood again and again
I am a monster

The cry for quater is shouted
but regardless I kill again and agian
I have succumbed to the rage
I am a monster

All around me I see the horros of battle
But I still kill
My Blade strikes out again and again
the enenmy retreats 
we give chase for the battle rage is still in us
I am a monster
