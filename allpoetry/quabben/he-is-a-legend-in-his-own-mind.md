title: "He Is A Legend In His Own Mind"

He stands in front …. Dominant
The air of a superior mind …. Stubborn
A dream gift for any venture …. Nightmare
Open to all that need to speak …. Deaf
One of the team he does proclaim …. Dictator
He alone sees the big picture …. Blind
The route is clear he knows the way …. Lost
With all his talents we cannot fail …. Hopeless
I know this man and so do you …. Tyrant
He is a legend in his own mind …. StupidI think we all have met this guy!
