Calm beat of warmth-
Stray please from my breast
Leave my lips
Take your light from my womb
The sun’s fading embers drench you in it’s silk
The ending world’s last breath breaks your smile
But please if you believe that love is true
Leave this place you live
My heart expands too much to soon
My arms grow cold as we exhale
I can’t live so loved
I’m not used to such heat
Leave me
Let my veins feels the chill again
I’m scared you’ll leave
So leave.If i did not make it clear, when i say leave I am really thinking- stay.
Jen
