I venture 
      It seems
Into the wide yellow green haze of your arms
Each day i pray to
        Melt into you
To forever be assured
        That we will stay
	          Together- last
Everyday i see you, hear you
         Everyday i feel love bend my heart
         Into the shape of your eyes
I miss you-and miss you-and miss you
It never stops
        This constant craving-
I want to steal you away
      No friends
      No family
      No life
               But me, and you and our four hands
           Grabbing at the air
Our future together
So close- yet it fades into the
              Background
You smile and i cave inside
             Break me- sweet lover
                     Break meFor Bryan- a muse i find my self obbsessed with.
Jen
