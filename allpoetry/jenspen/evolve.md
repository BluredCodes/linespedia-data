Touch me
Watch me -taste my fury
See my rapture
	Falling
	A thousand dirty stones clatter
I rebell and hate
I am your lust
	Gently sweetly slowly 
	I will fuck you
But don't look at the nails
	Tearing scraping
	Below my thin beige skin
Don't lick up my sadness with pity
	It is bitter and too deep

I am a caccon i tell myself
And tomorrow i will awake
	Seperating from the yoke of old scars
And you will behold my beauty

It can be real i say-
	It can be realWouldn't that just be great- if what we were at our highest was what we would someday become-
Jen
