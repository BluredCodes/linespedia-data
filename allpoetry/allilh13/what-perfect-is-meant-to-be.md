If I could be someone like you,
That would be the happiest day
My eyes would ever see.
If I could love as much as you.
You don't know what that would be for me.

If I had know how much you cared,
I would have spent my time
So differently.
Because of you I understand
What perfect is meant to be.What do ya think.  Give me insight please.
