Wanna see me ride my bike?
Look, Ma - no hands!
I got a new car with a spoiler;
See how fast I can go!

I take risks,
I make risks.
And if I lose, it's fate.
You've no control
Over keeping me whole -
And neither do I, I think.

We guys admire
Evel Kinevel
And think we are immune.
Nothing can touch me -
I'm Superman!
Look, Ma - no hands.

We will go out and take
The same pig-headed risks
Because, Goddamnit,
No one's gonna tell us what to do!
Besides, we've always done it this way.

Sad about Charile, though.
I guess he wan't lucky but
These things happen.
I hear he's in the hospital.
'Cause he took the guard off his saw...

Look, Ma. No hands.
