Im shy,
If theres people I dont know,
I'll sit in the corner,
And wait there till I have to go,
I cant talk to people,
Some just freak me out,
If they talk to me first,
That I can do,
But I cant start a converstaion,
Thats just nothing I could do,
I dont like to talk in front of a lot of people,
I turn red and I blank out,
I worry what people will think of me,
I dont want them to think I dont like them,
Just because I dont talk much,
And walk away,
Im not werid,
Im just shy,
I dont make friends very eaisly,
I dont talk to many people,
And I dont know why.
