---
title: "I wanted you"
writer: "iTsOnLyMe123"

We both knew deep inside our hearts,
We wanted each other from the start.
I saw you, you saw me.
You wanted me... and I wanted you.
It would be just us two.
Now were together just like I knew we would be,
Together forever just you and me.
I love you more then words could ever say.
The words you say take my breath away.
And I'll love you forever and always.
No one can break us apart,
Becuase you hold the special key to my heart.
So whatever you do I just want you to know,
My love for you will only continue to grow.
