I started counting the stars in the sky,
Then I realized,
I love you more then all those stars,
Theres more then a billion stars out there,
And I have more reasons then that,
I have more reasons then all of the grains of sand on a beach,
I love you more then all the smiles weve shared,
I have more reasons then the hugs weve shared,
I love you more then all the kisses,
More then all the memories,
More then all the times weve cried together,
I love you more then all these things,
And I will love you forever.
