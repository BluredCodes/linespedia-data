Im lost in a world I dont really know,
Wandering around not knowing where to go,
People all look the same,
They only know me by my name,
They stare me down with their dark brown eyes,
Saying why is she with that guy?
Their dark skin makes it turn to night,
I cant really help it if Im white,
Im different for all of you,
I dress like a white girl too,
Just because your spanish and black,
You think you can talk behind my back,
Im always left out from your "popular" things,
Just becuse your one of those Latin kings,
Im a minority and Im white,
I know it doesnt sound right,
Your all darker then me,
But the color isnt all you should see.
