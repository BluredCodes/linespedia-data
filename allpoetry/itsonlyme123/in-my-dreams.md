title: "In my dreams"
writer: "iTsOnLyMe123"

Everynight I dream of you...
I hope you dream of me too.
Everyday with you...
For me its like a dream come true.
Just thinking for you makes me smile...
And seeing you makes my day worth while.
With you and me together its like a whole new start.
But Im sad when were apart...
I think its because you have that special something in your heart.
I never thought Id love you like this...And just by the way you kiss.
It must be because of all your specialness.
You have something no one else has...
No one else is like you.
Because of each little thing you do.
Just for me to think... you all mine...
Makes me smile all the time.
With me and you together...
I hope that we last forever and ever.
