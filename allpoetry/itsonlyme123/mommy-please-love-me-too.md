Its not fair,
Mommy you chose him over me.
And you push me around like Im nobody.
Everytime I need you you say your never there,
Most of the time you act like you never care.

You act like your the only one that was hurt,
You chose him over me mom,
How could you do that?
Im lonely,
Scared everynight,
Waiting for you to come home,
I cant do anything,
Because Im always left alone.

We fight a lot,
Since its only been me and you,
I dont know what to do,
I cant put up with this anymore,
You make it seem like what happened was something I did,
You say you dont,
But you treat me like a little kid.

You say you love me,
But mom is that really true?
It feels like your doing everything just for you.
I need you to love me too.
I need help mom,
I dont know what to do,
I cant sleep at night,
Because daddy scares me,
You cant even see,
Im scared to get close to people,
Because I dont know how they'll be.

Mom I thought I could trust you,
You had one last chance,
You and dad blew it,
Because you guys are stuck in your little romance.
I love you guys with all my heart,
You say you guys love me too,
But I hope some day I'll belive it,
And maybe it will come true.
