Wrap me up in your dreams,
I want to be in your dreams all night,
With me in your mind,
I'll make you feel alright.

Your in my dreams tonight.

Wrap me up in your dreams,
I wanna be with you tonight,
With your body against mine,
You'll hold me really tight.

Your in my dreams tonight.

Wrap me up in your dreams,
I wanna hold you right now,
With you hand on my face,
I'll run across the ocean somehow.

Your in my dreams tonight.

Wrap me up in your dreams,
I wanna be with you,
Touch me with your lips,
I want you to kiss me too.

Your in my dreams tonight.

Wrap me up in your dreams,
I want you to kiss me all time,
You'll tell me "I love you",
And that you'll always be mine.

Your in my dreams tonight.


Wrap me up in your dreams,
I wanna dream with you at night,
With your face right next to mine,
I'll dream with you till daylight.

You in my dreams tonight.

Wrap me up in your dreams,
I want to wake up next to you,
With your body so close to mine,
I'll just keep on holding you.

Your in my dreams tonight.

Wrap me up in your dreams,
Im laying next to you,
I whisper in your ear,
That I really love you too.

Im in your dreams tonight.
