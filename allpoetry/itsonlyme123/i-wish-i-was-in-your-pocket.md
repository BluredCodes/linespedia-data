Sometimes I just wish I was in your pocket,
So you could take me every where,
And I wouldnt ever have to miss you,
Because youd always be there.

I wish I was in your pocket,
So I could pop out and kiss you all the time,
I wish I was in your pocket,
So you could say youd always be mine.

Sometimes I just wish I was in your pocket,
So you could take me every where,
And I wouldnt ever have to miss you,
Because youd always be there.

I wish I was in your pocket,
So I could see you all the time,
I wish I was in your pocket,
So you could hold me all the time.

Sometimes I just wish I was in your pocket,
So you could take me every where,
And I wouldnt ever have to miss you,
Because youd always be there.

I wish I was in your pocket,
So you could keep me warm when it gets cold,
I wish I was in your pocket,
So I could stay with you until were old.

Sometimes I just wish I was in your pocket,
So you could take me every where,
And I wouldnt ever have to miss you,
Because you would always be there.

I wish I was in your pocket,
So you would know Id never leave you ever,
I wish I was in you pocket,
Just so you would know that Id love you forever.
