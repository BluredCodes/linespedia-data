I am only one person I can not do any thing,
I say to myself.

Everyday there is something new,
Another struggle I go through,
Another test I have to pass,
Another person I have to please.

I am only one person I can not do everything,
I say to myself.

Everyday people say,
Do this for me,
Or why cant you stay?
Why cant you do it this way?

I am only one person I can do somethings,
I say to myself.

Everday I have to make people happy,
Why cant you come?
We never hang out,
First you have to help me then you can go out.

I am only one person I can do alot of things,
I say to myself.

Everyday I dont have enough time to do things I want,
Are the parents going to be home?
What time will you be back?
Oh.. no wait you cant go out its to late.

I am only one person..
Trying to make other people happy,
Even if Im not happy myself.
