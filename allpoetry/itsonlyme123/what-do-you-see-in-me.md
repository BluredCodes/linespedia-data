title: "What do you see in me?"
writer: "iTsOnLyMe123"

I sit here and look at you,
And I start to wonder why,
Why are you with me?
What do you see?
How could you love someone like me?

I dont deserve someone like you,
You say I do,
I dont get it,
Why are you with me?
What do you see?
Why am I so special to you?
What did I do?
Why do you love me the way you do?
Is it the same thing that I have found in you?

I look away and say to myself,
Your perfect to me in every way...
I love each little word you say,
I love the way you make me feel...
In my heart its a feeling too real.

I look at you again,
Why are you with me?
I say to myself,
Why arent you with someone else?
Why am I so special to you?
What did I do,
To deserve someone like you?

I look away and say to myself,
Your perfect to me in every way...
I love each little word you say,
I love the way you make me feel...
In my heart its a feeling too real.
I cant believe I have someone like you,
But I do,
And I love you.
