title: "What scares me"
writer: "iTsOnLyMe123"

A lot things scare me,
To a point where I'll just scream,
Muppets are one of them,
I'll tell you what I mean,
They look so real,
The way they move,
The little voices they make,
If I see one my hands start to shake,
I think they'll all attack me,
Some how some way,
They'll come alive and eat me,
Watch it'll happen to you one day.
Another thing that scares me is goats in a way,
I got knocked over by one when I was little,
If I see one I'll run away.
Those things will eat you too,
If you dont watch out,
They eat you alive,
Then they'll dig you a grave,
And the priest at your funeral,
Will be a muppet im telling you,
After your gone,
They'll eat your family too,
I warned you now,
But it will still happen some how.
