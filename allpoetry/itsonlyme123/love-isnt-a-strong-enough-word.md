title: "love isnt a strong enough word"

I love you,
Those three words arent strong enough to tell you how I feel for you.
No words can explain what you mean to me,
There arent enough words that can explain how much I love you,
Love isnt a strong enough word.
You mean more to me then any thing I have,
Or ever will,
You mean more to me then any thing in this world,
And outside of it,
I love you more then words could say,
I love you more then any thing in the whole world,
And everything outside of it,
Put all together.
I love you so much,
I would die for you in less then a second,
Just so that you could still be alive even if Im not.
I would feel all your pain for you,
Just so that you would always be happy,
I would cry for you,
Just so that you would never feel any sadness,
I would fall for you,
Just so that you could never get hurt,
I would walk in front of you,
Just so that I would hit all the bad things before you would.
When I try to tell you more then I love you,
No words come out,
Nothing that makes sense any way,
So until the day I can find the words to tell you,
How much you mean to me,
Or how much I love you,
I guess the words I love you,
Will just have to be good enough for now.
