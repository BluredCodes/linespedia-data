You are my best friend,
Your always there in the end,
Through all the broken toys,
The mean little boys,
The heart breaks,
The moments that time takes,
The family problems in the middle of the night,
Through every little fight,
When you moved,
Even that proved,
No matter what I went through,
You came out of the blue,
Just to help me,
Everyone could see,
Your my little bounty,
My quicker picker upper,
Were sisters from the heart,
And no one could ever break us apart.
