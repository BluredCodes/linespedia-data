Im losing my grip,
Your only at my finger tips,
Your drifting away,
I cant reach you any more,
And I cant find another way.

You say I choose him over you,
I felt that a long time ago too,
You choose him over me,
And I cant help it,
Thats the way it will always be.

Im losing my grip,
Your only at my finger tips,
Your drifting away,
I cant reach you any more,
And I cant find another way.

Im second to you,
Your too busy with him,
Cant even have one day just to see me.
Without him.

Im losing my grip,
Your only at my finger tips,
Your drifting away,
I cant reach you any more,
And I cant find another way.

You say you lost me,
And cant hold on much longer,
Your to far away now,
I cant reach much further.

Im losing my grip,
Your only at my finger tips,
Your drifting away,
I cant reach you any more,
And I cant find another way.

Its always my fault everything that happens,
I get caught in the middle,
I cant choose over you too,
I feel like Im going to hurt both of you,
No matter what I do.

Im losing my grip,
Your only at my finger tips,
Your drifting away,
I cant reach you any more,
And I cant find another way.

I love you both just the same,
Saying he hurt me,
Acting like you never hurt me too,
Were drifting apart,
Saying you want it to stop.

Im losing my grip,
Your only at my finger tips,
Your drifting away,
I cant reach you any more,
And I cant find another way.

Blaming everything on me isnt going to help,
You say you dont like what he does,
And what he says,
Talk to him about it not me.

Im losing my grip,
Your only at my finger tips,
Your drifting away,
I cant reach you any more,
And I cant find another way.

I just wish we were the way we used to be,
Without worrying if we'll see each other again,
I'll never give up,
It will never be the end.

Im losing my grip,
Your only at my finger tips,
Your drifting away,
I cant reach you any more,
And I cant find another way,
Your gone forever starting today.
