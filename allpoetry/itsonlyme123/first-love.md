I miss you so much when your not around.
I dont know what to do, I wanna fall to the ground.
When im with you, you cant see...
Just how happy you make me.

Everything feels so right when were together.
I hope we last forever and ever.
I know I love you...
It's so true...
Cuz I would do most anything for you.

All the memories that we have will last a long time.
Cuz I hope that you'll always be mine.
No matter what happens I'll always love you...
I'll never forget any thing you've helped me get through.

You are my first love and my everything...
For you I would do almost anything...

I think you have to be...
The best thing thats ever happened to me.
Thanks for making me life better.
I love you always... and I will forever.
