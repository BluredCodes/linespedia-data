Your the sweetest,
Your the best,
My adorable one,
And all the rest.

Your the greatest, your cute face I adore,
Your everything I wanted and even more.

I love you with all my heart,
I hope what we have never falls apart.

You make me happy with everything you do,
All I want in my life right now is to be with you.

When Im with you I get this feeling I love,
I just dont know how to thank God above.

For giving me your heart and soul,
Making me feel on top instead of low.

When you are around I can be myself,
I dont have to try to be some one else.

Most of all your always there,
And you always show how much you care.

I wouldnt give you up for anything of anyone,
Becasue there is just so much that you have done.

We both may not agree on everything one will do,
But that doesnt matter because our love for each other is so true.

You have given me so much to live for,
Everyday I love you even more.

Now that I have you in my life with me,
You made me everytihng I wanted to be.

I hope we last forever,
I never want to lose you ever.

You mean so much to me my cutie pie,
And I hope Ill love you till the day I die.

Without you I dont know what I would do,
So my adorable one... I LOVE YOU!
