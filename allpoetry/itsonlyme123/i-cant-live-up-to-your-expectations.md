Im sorry Im not as perfect as you wanted me to be,
I try as hard as possible,
But I cant help it if Im just me.

Im sorry I dont live up to your expectations,
Of another perfect daughter,
I have flaws too, Its not like any one can walk on water.

Im sorry Im not as perfect as you wanted me to be,
I try as hard as possible,
But I cant help it if Im just me.

Im me and thats all I am,
I like it that way,
I dont want to be like one of them.

Im sorry Im not as perfect as you wanted me to be,
I try as hard as possible,
But I cant help it if Im just me.

I cant help it if I mess up then,
I try as hard as I can,
But I guess you just wont be happy with me again.

Im sorry Im not as perfect as you wanted me to be,
I try as hard as possible,
But I cant help it if Im just me.

Its not like I can count on you to help me,
Its not like your ever there,
Your always busy.

Im sorry Im not as perfect as you wanted me to be,
I try as hard as possible,
But I cant help it if Im just me.

Im sorry I dont do the things you wanted me to,
But you asked me to chose what I wanted to do,
I got to pick not you.

Im sorry Im not as perfect as you wanted me to be,
I try as hard as possible,
But I cant help it if Im just me.

Im sorry I dont say the right things,
You act like Im supposed to be a perfect angel,
With beautiful wings.

Im sorry Im not as perfect as you wanted me to be,
I try as hard as possible,
But I cant help it if Im just me.

Im sorry if I dont always do everything right,
But I cant be perfect,
And you dont always think thats right.

Im sorry Im not as perfect as you wanted me to be,
I try as hard as possible,
But I cant help it if Im just me.
