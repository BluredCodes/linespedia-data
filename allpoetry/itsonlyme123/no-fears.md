---
title: "No fears"
writer: "iTsOnLyMe123"

I get lost looking in you eyes,
I never realized,
I could love someone like you,
But I do.

I want to hold on to you so tight,
And I want to be with you day and through the night,
Your eyes are like stars in a dark night sky,
It makes me smile and I wonder why.

I know for you I would die,
I love you for each little thing you do,
The way you look at me too.

You make me have no worries,
No fears,
When you wipe away my tears.

Loving you is like breathing.
I will die if I stop,
Because without you my heart will just drop.

You put me in a place I always wanted to be,
Because its just you and me.
And your the only love i'll ever see.

No matter what happens I will always love you,
Whether were near or far,
It will always be true.
I love you.
