I love you more each day,
Its hard to explain the words I am trying to say,
The feeling I get when your holding me tight...
In my heart a feeling too light.

Your soft gentle lips, each time they touch,
Becasue I love you so much.
A sense i get each time I kiss you,
Every moment apart I begin to miss you.

It scares me to think if we were apart.
Id be lost without you,
I wouldnt know where to start.
You are my love, my everthing,
For you, I would do almost anything.

I would give up my world to be with you,
So Im telling you, I truly love you.
