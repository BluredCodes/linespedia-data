title: "I will always love you"

I love you with everything I have in me.
Without you I dont know who I would be.
Without you I would feel like I have died.
I would feel like I have nothing inside.
My life without you would be boring and gray.
I dont know how I would go through a day.

I love you with everything I have in me.
without you I dont know who I would be.
Since I met you I finally feel loved.
Because you my hunny were sent from above.
God picked you out among the rest.
Because he knew I would love you the best.

I love you with everything I have in me.
Without you I dont know who I would be.
We'll be together for a very long time.
Because I hope that God will let you always be mine.
I'll hold on to you very tight.
All day and maybe in the future through the night.

I love you with everything I have in me.
Without  you i dont know who I would be.
I promise I will never forget.
The first time we kissed or the day we met.
The sky may fall and the stars may too.
But in the end I will always love you.
