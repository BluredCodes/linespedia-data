title: "Im not ready to say goodbye"
writer: "iTsOnLyMe123"

Days are going by,
And I am wondering why...
Why do people have to die?
Where do they go?
Does anyone really know?
Will I ever see them again?
When you die is it the end?
What happens if I dont want to die?
What if Im not ready to say goodbye?
Im not ready to leave...
I dont want to go.
I want to stay here.
Its not fair,
I dont want to die...
Im not ready to say goodbye.
