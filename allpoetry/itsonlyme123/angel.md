I say your my guardian angel,
Sent from heaven above,
God gave you to me,
To save my life with your love.

Your my guardian angel,
You saved my life in so many ways,
If it wasnt for you,
I cant even begin to count the days.

Your my guardian angel,
You picked up the broken pieces of me,
And put me back together,
The way I should be.

Your my guardian angel,
You saved me with your love,
I have to thank you so much,
For looking out for me above.

I say your my guardian angel,
And I know God put us together,
Just so that we could love each other,
Forever and ever.
