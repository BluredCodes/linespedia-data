In a land far away,
Above the mountians and under the sea,
It's a place were everyone would always want to be.

Fairy's fly sprinkling magical dust around,
From way up high in the sky,
The dust floating down and hitting the ground.

The star's sparkle with a glimmer,
Day turns to night,
And the moon would start to shimmer.

Dragons breathe red hot fire and fly with their wings,
Knights slay them with sliver swords,
And the princess' would start to sing.

Kings and queens would be together,
No one would hate each other,
And everything would be perfect forever.

Princess' would kiss ugly fat toads,
Then he would turn into a handsome prince,
And they would love eachother until their old.

In a place far, far away,
Everything would always be,
Perfect in every way.

Kings and queens would live happly ever after,
Everyone would be happy,
And they'd all be filled with laughter.

But nothing would ever be,
As good people dreamed it to be,
Nothing will ever be as perfect as everyone would like to see.

This place far away isnt any where at all,
It's a place in my mind,
Not any real place at all.
