The stars are shinning in the sky,
I look up at God,
And I ask him why,
Why do people have to die?

Is there heaven in the sky?
Above the clouds and moon,
Do good people go there when they die?
Will I be going there soon?

Is there hell down below?
Where fire devils thrive on new souls to grow,
Is that where all the bad people go?
Will I be going there, dont you know?

Is there even a heaven or hell?
I will find out when time will tell,
God is there a heaven or a hell?
Please tell me when time will tell.

Do you become something new after you die?
Or is that the end?
Will you make me something different, please tell me why,
So I could just make people smile again?

Does everything just stop?
Like your just not there anymore?
And I'll never come back?
Not even as someone else?

God why do people die?
I hate it when I make them cry,
Will I ever see them again?
Or is that just the end?
