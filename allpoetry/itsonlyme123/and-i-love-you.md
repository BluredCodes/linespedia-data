---
title: "And I love you"
writer: "iTsOnLyMe123"
slug: "and-i-love-you"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58567-And-I-love-you-by-iTsOnLyMe123"
---

Your the sun that dries the rain drops away,
Your the guy that wipes my tears away.


And I love you.


Your the thorns that protect me,
Your the guy that pushes the evil away from me.


And I love you.


Your the clothes that cover me,
Your the guy that holds me.


And I love you.


Your the blankets on my bed,
Your the guy that keeps me warm at night.


And I love you.


Your my pillow on my bed,
Your the guy thats holds my head up when I cry. 


And I love you.


Your the prince charming Ive been dreaming about,
Your the prince in my dreams, that finally came true.


And I love you.


Your the shoes on my feet,
Your the guy that watchs what I do so I dont trip in my life.


And I love you.


Your my teddy bear,
Your the guy that hugs me when I am sad.


And I love you.


Your my tear drops,
You make all the sadness in me dissapear.


And I love you.


Your my hands,
Your the guy that picks me up when I am down.


And I love you.


Your my legs,
Your the guy that helps me walk away from the past, and into the future.


And I love you.


Your my heart,
Your the guy that filled my heart with your love.


And I love you.
