Im in my box,
My secert place,
My own little space,
Just for me, not for you,
Because whatever you try to do,
Probaly wont make me feel any better,
My eyes will just get a little wetter,
You can still try,
But I might just still cry,
No one but you trys to help me,
No one knows what I see,
You try to understand what I say,
What I think about everyday,
Where will I stay,
When everyone goes away,
Im sorry I put you through this too,
I just need you to help me through,
I know you dont fully understand,
But I know you try the best that you can,
I need you here with me,
I dont care what other people see,
Im sorry for all Ive done to you,
Please dont leave me whatever you do,
Your the only one that I can trust and that is true,
I just want to thank you,
For not giving up on messed up me,
Without you I dont know where I would be.
