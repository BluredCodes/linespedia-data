Your my guardian angel sent from above
I knew God gave you to me,
Just to love.

Your the nicest guy I will ever know,
I love the way you love me so,
That feeling I have will always show.

Your my guardian angel sent from above
I knew God gave you to me,
Just to love.

You protect me when evil comes my way,
I love the way you kiss me everyday,
I love you and thats what you make me say.

Your my guardian angel sent from above
I knew God gave you to me,
Just to love.

Your the sweetest guy on the earth,
You showed my how to love with out being hurt,
To loose your love its so not worth.

Your my guardian angel sent from above
I knew God gave you to me,
Just to love.
