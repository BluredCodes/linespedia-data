It's so hard now, 
even this dusty furniture reminds me of you.
I remember nights of sitting on the couch,
watching old black and white re-runs 
till two in the morning.  
Now the tv's broken 
and I can't see through the borded up windows.
After you left our house seemed to fall apart,
just like you and all of my memories. 
Now all I have left is three inches of dust
and a broken down house.
