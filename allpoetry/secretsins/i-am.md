---
title: "I Am"
writer: "SecretSins"
slug: "i-am"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59849-I-Am-by-SecretSins"
---

I am broken.
 (you broke me)
I am crying.
 (you are my tears)
I am falling away.
 (you pushed me over)
I am alone.
 (you left me)
I am dirty.
 (you soiled me)
I am afraid.
 (you scare me)
I am nothing.
 (you are everything)
