title: "All I Can See"

Are you still there?
I can't see your star.
Your voice plays in the background
of my memories,
and I can't hold you anymore,
cuz you've gone.
You're gone 
and I can't breath. 

What happens to me now?
You can't speak to me,
I can't speak at all. 
My life is changing,
cuz my world revolved around you.
And now, I'm so lost.

Come back, help me find my way.
Cuz I'm standing here alone,
with all of these people,
swallowing me into them.
And the only person I can see,
is you.
