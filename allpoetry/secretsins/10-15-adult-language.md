i'm gonna steal the life you stole from her
let me rape you of everything you feel holy 
let me spit on your self-confidence 
and push you into the ground
i'm gonna hold myself on top of you
until you beg for mercy you fucking pig
let me break you down
i'll even buy you the sleeping pills
you'll need to shut your eyes at night
just like she does
and i'm gonna pull you apart
till there's nothing left of youa little stress i had to release, seeing my friend fall apart over this dick's weakness is the hardest thing in the world for me
