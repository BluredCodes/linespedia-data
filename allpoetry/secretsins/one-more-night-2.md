title: "One More Night (2)"

We're just a little closer, 
to where we never thought we'd be. 
We're running out of time now,
it's almost over,
it's hard to believe.
We've been through so much,
so many tears, 
so many endless nights.
But now it's all closing up,
and it's all we've got,
just one more night.
