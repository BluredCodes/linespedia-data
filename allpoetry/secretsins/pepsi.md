I remember when you called your best friend Pepsi.
You'd talk aboout it as if it were no big deal,
but only to those who never gave two shits about you.
You loved your "friend" so much more than you ever loved me,
or your family, or your lover, even more than yourself. 
You always called your best friend Pepsi,
but never to me, cuz I cared about you.
And I never thought it wasn't all that bad.Sad memories of my best friend and her addiction.
