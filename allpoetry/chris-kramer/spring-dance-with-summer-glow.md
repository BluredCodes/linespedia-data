title: "Spring Dance With Summer Glow"

Sounds in the air signal tempo
Guiding movement across the land
Spring swept in a dance of romance
Spring kept in the palm of your hand

Dance across the floor of meadow
Hold firm to heart and touch soul
Wrap yourself in sweet season
Spring dance with summer glow

There lies one of life's mysteries
Joy adrift in a ripple of time
No matter the burdens from winter
Set aside, take off, and try

Dance across mountain and valley
Hold firm to heart and touch soul
Wrap yourself in sweet season
Spring dance with summer glow

Stir to the sound of songbird
Singing attention to himself
Move to the rhythm of ocean
In tune with the tall ones melt

Dance across the space of universe
Hold firm to heart and touch soul
Wrap yourself in sweet season
Spring dance with summer glow

Come close to the heartstring
Engage in a whirlwind spin
The world now moves around us
This dance of romance in spring

Dance in my arms and being
Join together heart and soul
Wrap yourself in our season
