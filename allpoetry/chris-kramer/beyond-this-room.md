Adventure awaits one who tries
To be close to soul mate and friend
Joined and fused by days gone by
Memories that will never end

I await now in thoughtful mood
Confiscated by thoughts of bliss
A midnight arrival in all likelihood
Then a kiss and then ... a kiss!

No part of her will escape my lips
And touch will cause surrender
I've waited and thought of all this ...
Moments captured in dream splendor

Sweet woman, dearest friend
Daylight in times of black
Such moments will never end
As our clocks tock a tick back

Arrival at the stroke straight up
An entrance into sweet
For there, exposed to eyes and touch
My dream awaits complete

A kiss here and a kiss there
Is but kindling to this event
Engaged in opening gates where
Fires melt by liquid sent

With a steady rhythm attuned in
We will burn and consume
Nothing to gain or to win
Except ourselves ... beyond this room ...(((( >>>  Wishful Thinking  <<< ))))   :0)
