I inhale deep; a steady breeze,

Each time you draw near ...

Certain time never ends,

as I take in sweet air.



You breathe life into me; 

You catalyze; cause reaction ...

Muscle tightens, heart grows,

a soul-full of satisfaction.



Lungs inflate to capacity

As thought of you arrives ...

A true age reversal;

feeling youth and alive.



When taken in measure,

I stand to gain ...

The essence of being

from you in my brain.Written in Oct/2001, revised since then a billion times---LoL--- I will breathe deeply from your commentary :0)
