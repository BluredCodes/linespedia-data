My heart feels torn 
i blow the horn
i say ill move on
casue your allready gone
i wish i could do something
but now there is nothing
wishing apon my star 
youve left a permanent scar
the times we shared
i thought you really cared
you say its not your fault
and that you got cought
to see your smile
i would walk a mile
im breathles with out a voice
you left me with no choice
now theres nothing left to do
but sit here and wish for you
if you could only see 
what you have done to me
i cried so many tears 
had so many fears
its not the same
was i only a game
did you lie 
to see me cry
i liked you more
than anyone before
i blink to see
if this is really happening to me
it feels like a bunch of dreams
thats how this all seems
i realy did try 
but i geuss now all ill do is cry.....
