---
title: "Who am i?"
writer: "ib6ub9"
slug: "who-am-i"
source: "AllPoetry"

I often feel afraid
afraid of you and afraid of my self
not that it means anything to you 
but its the things you do and that i do
if the world could look pass all this 
maybe they would see that underneath the make up
is a scared little girl crying inside
who has no one by her side
to look after her or see who she really is
i laugh to hide my pain u dont know how i feel
with everything i have done to you and to myself
im sorry in a way but happy in another 
so look beyond me into another time and place
will you still be there or will i be gone?
