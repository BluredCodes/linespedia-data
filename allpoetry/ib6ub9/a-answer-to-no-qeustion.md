why do you do this to me, is that why i am like this
we all know im not normal, i hate my self sometimes 
but mostly i hate when im like this, no one to talk to and no one to listin or here what i have to say
i feel so alone is it that im not pretty enough or that im not good enough to be with you
all i ever wanted was for you to like me back i would say i love you but my usless words are nothing 
is it that i hate my self or that i reallhy hate you not you but what you have done to me 
even though we have been over for a while i still hang on 
but latley there has been nothing to hold on to except pictures and memories .... i juss wanted to here you say you loved me back but instead all you did was let me down.... it was never love
