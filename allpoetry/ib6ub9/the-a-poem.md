my friend kim is so gay
she makes me say hey
and she makes me go outta my way
just like yesturday
when i was playing in the clay
and the things she would say 
and the games she would play
often sitting by the bay
feeding the horses hay
asking "do you have a quater aye"
some would say yes now go lay
by the horse who goes naaay
then id ask for money to pay
for the make up i bought at mary-kay
in santa fe
were nobody likes the kkk
and some people atend aa
until they they are ok
and thats probaly sometime in may
around the time when they pray
and dont ask me to stay
cause im going to see my friend jay
who likes the color grey
so amscray
before you get stung by a stingray
