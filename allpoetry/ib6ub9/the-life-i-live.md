title: "the life i live"
writer: "ib6ub9"

why must i wonder
what tomorrow will bring
while i sit here and ponder
and listin to the birds sing
if life was so easy
why does it bring so much pain
i often think its cheezy
and feel i need to be slain
when i think of everything that happens to me
i live in fear and look beyond all this
i wish i could see
everything that i miss
i often talk
but do u hear
let us take a walk 
and over come our fears 
now reach up to god
and ask and pray
though it might feel odd
u can hear him say
thoughts of happyness 
that he has bestoed uppon us
and here i sit again with less
then everything in my heart, thus
im here again to think
that maybe tomorrow will be
better than i blink
only to oppen my eyes and see
that this is the life i live
