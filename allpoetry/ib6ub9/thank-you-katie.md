thank you for always being there
thanks for always showing you care
thanks for all the times you called
thanks for all times you balled
thanks for all the things you said
thanks for all the times i could have been dead
thanks for being my friend
thanks for being there till the end
thanks for all the times we shared
thanks for all the times you cared
thanks for being by my side
thanks for all the times we cried
thanks for all the times you told me i could take it
thanks for all the times when you knew i could make it
thanks for all the things you do
thanks for showing we can make it through
thanks for all the times you wiped my tears away
thanks for always telling me it will be ok
thanks for always staying strong
thanks for always comming along
thanks for all the troubles with boys
thanks for always sharing your toys
thanks for always sticking up for me
thanks for all the things you made me see
thanks for everything youve done 
thanks i love you so much hun!
