She Comes,
	by Darmok
	4/10/02
	~~
	Pray tell,
	when comes this beauty,
	and by which name will she speak.
	Is she the moon's daughter
	wherein dreams we shall meet...
	~~
	or is she the sun's fury
	and o're the day we'll play....
	is she in front of me
	just beyond my reach...
	waiting...smiling...
	enticing me this way....
	~*~
	oh, bring me this....
	tis your joy,
	as I would share and bathe.
	In golden rays,
	draped beneath your summer's hair,
	released...
	the weight and bonds
	of care,
	slipping silently,
	quietly...
	away.
