Falling

~*~
I hear the Piper calling, 
young men weeping ...balling.
For a brother today,
lay sleeping this way....
Thomas O'shay continues falling.
~*~*~ 
From the blue and riddled skies, 
where his heart lay true, 
fought bravely for the red, white, and the blue, 
Thomas O'shay, flies away.....today,
continues his dream of flying,
falling.
~*~*~ 
If I could fly with Thomas O'shay, 
I would give most of what I have. 
Perhaps then upon rising to the day,
Night would not require emotional salve.
