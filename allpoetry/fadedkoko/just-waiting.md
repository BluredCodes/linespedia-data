Sitting Alone,
In a darkened room.

Just waiting.....

Waiting for you to come,
Save me from my darkest fears.
Hold me tight,
keeping me warm and safe.
Comfort me,
In my most darkest hours.

Sitting alone,
In a darkened room.

Just waiting....

Waiting for you to come,
Save me from my darkest fears.
Shinning the light that leads the way.
Whiping away al my tears.
Saying have no fear,
For I am here.

You shined the light
Into the darkened room.
No more tears,
No more frears.

As Long as your here,
Leading the way.
My Life will all ways be,
The most brightest,
Safest place for me.
