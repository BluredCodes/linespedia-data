A little girl
Cold and all a lone
Lost and yearning for a home
A loving family to call her own

Tears begen to fall
As she hears her tummy 
Begen to rummble 
Pering though a window
Watching a family 
As they sit down to dinner

Turning to walk away
A soft voice calls out to her
Come on in and have a seat 
Lets get you something to eat

A little girl 
No longer cold and all a lone
She found her way
To a nice warm home
An a loving family 
To call her own
