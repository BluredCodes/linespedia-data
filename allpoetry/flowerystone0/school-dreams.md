Sitting..
sitting..
sitting..
Thinking hard
still doing nothing
painfully restricted to a cold, hard seat
Tired
involuntarily waving
back and fourth
back and fourth
Asleep
unconcious,having a nightmare
of things yet to come
Scared
afraid of what I am to see next
but what is to come?
Do I really want to know?
Ring..
ring..
ring..
school is over
I will know soon enough.This one was written a long time ago,but I still like it :)
