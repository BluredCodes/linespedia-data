Imagine being so in love,that you really can't think
Racing thoughts of your true love everytime you blink
Loving so blindly no one else to lead you through,
except the one you're loving, whos just as blind as you
Every tune is a happy tune,no matter what was meant
Each new day seems to be a gift straight from Heaven sent
Then the unthinkable happens,your love is split apart
It seems youre left with nothing,but a crack inside your heart
You live your life through day to day,
in a lonely fashion,a lonely way.
"This tune is such a dead end song,
why does it have to be so long?"
I must say,if push came to shove,
I like it better being in love!This one is kinda old,but I still agree with it :)
