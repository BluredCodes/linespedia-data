What is this that's coming down?
Seem's it's hit the whole damn town!
There's a full moon out tonight.
And everyONE is in a fight.
Could it be something in the air?
Or that wise old moon's constant stare.
No one wants to apologize,NoBODY will admit they're wrong.
I am willing to take the blame,CAN'T WE ALL JUST GET ALONG?!?!Judy Baker,and myself
