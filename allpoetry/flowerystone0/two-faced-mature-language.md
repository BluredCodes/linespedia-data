I know the things you say when you think that I'm not there.
I know your secret betrayal,and I'm trying not to care.
You always talk about me,and all the things I lack.
Do you think that I dont feel it when you stab me in the back?
Running around and running your mouth "hey,guess what I just heard"
Spitting my life through your teeth with "do not say a word"
Telling all my secrets,as if I wanted you to
Giving away the trust I wholeheartedly gave to you
As a friend,youre nothing.Youre a total sell-out.
Now I'm gonna show you what I'M really about.
Youre a fake, youre a fraud,youre a plastic friend
Youll be getting what you deserve in the end
With my skeletons out,I've nothing to hide
I'll admit that it hurt,mostly my pride
I'll get over this soon and I'll be just fine
So you'd better start runnin cause your ass is mine!
Remember my promise to keep YOUR secrets inside?
Now it's MY turn,guess what bitch, I LIED!!
