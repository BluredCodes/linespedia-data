I had the weirdest dream last night
I found myself in viewing site
Inside a box,someone trapped me
For all the world to plainly see
A little boy switched the channel down,
Then there I danced,a beaming clown
A grumpy old lady changed it again
suddenly all i could speak of was sin
This balding old man turned this t.v off
Im glad he didnt decide on golfHmm...It could be better,hope it doesnt bomb massivly
