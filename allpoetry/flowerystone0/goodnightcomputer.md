Every night I go to bed,bidding my adieu
Tonight I just realized,I've always forgotten you!
I'm sorry my dear computer world,I meant you no disrespect.
I say GoodNight to everyone,but, best cyber friends,i neglect!
So with this thought,I'll say GoodNight,and hope you'll be
forgiving.
See,I sometimes fail to think about,the one behind my screen as
"living"
 Computers remind me of machines,well you know,really that's
what they are!
My body know's what year this is,guess my mind has'nt gotten
that far.
How amazing it seems,I can talk to you and say all that's on my mind,
without knowing your race or sex,it's almost like being blind!
My cyber friend,I've told you now,all the things I had to say
Oh,and I'd like you to know..I would'nt have you any other way!
The GREATEST GOODNIGHT,to you I'll now send
You're a wondrful "behind my screen"best friend!Oh,to be the dork that I am!If you only knew!This is just a funny something I threw out..Im not gonna kill myself,I'm not depressed,and I dont need a hug.Most of the poems I write are just that..things I'm making up.Well,except for Gavin.He's my miracle. :)
