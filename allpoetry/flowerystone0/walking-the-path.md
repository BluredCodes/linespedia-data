The path begins,and disappears into the horizon.
Walking..
Walking steadily with too much direction.
Straying..
Moving off the path in discovery of life.
Curious..
Seeking all the beauty of the wilderness inside.
Stopped..
Halted by society.
Walking..
Once again down this narrow path,some fools call life.
Thriving..
Growing fast on this narrow, dull path that bores me.
Refusing..
Refusing to live in a life without imagination.
Angry..
Pissed off by people lacking individuality.
Choices..
Choosing a life walking in my own direction.
Happy..
Happiness found in a life I can now call mine.
Fallen..
Falling off the path without warning.
Life lost,choices lost!
Where is the justice in this tragic ordeal,
stricken of choice and the possibility of life?
I ask you,where lies the justice?
Not on this path that they say we should walk.
