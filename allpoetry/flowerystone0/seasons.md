Love is life,and life can bring
many hardships in the spring.
In the winter we will laugh and sing.
In the summer we will have a fling.
Fall,whats a word that ends with ing?   

Happy the heart,
where memories dwell.
Happy the day,
you no longer smell.      :DJust goofin' off
