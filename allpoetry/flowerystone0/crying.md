Crying, I'm confused,tired and broken
Longing for words that should've been spoken
Hurting I feel like I'm about to drown
Begging for help,with no one around
Needing to touch you,the urge so strong
Wanting to tell you that I was wrong
Praying this all was just a nightmare
Hoping I'll wake,and still see you there
Feeling so empty,only you make me whole
Living without you is killing my soul
