Now I lay me down to sleep,
but for my child,I'll first weep.
If I should die before I wake,
straight to his heart,my love
please take

I pray thee Lord to keep him near
and please protect him-For I fear,
that harm may come while I'm away.
So please,dear Lord,to you I pray

In Jesus name,I humbly plea,
God,keep my baby close to me.
For I am empty without him.
Please hear my prayer,Dear God,

                       Amen.
