Everywhere you go,you act like you're "The Man"
I wish you'd finally realize,you are your biggest fan.
Always talking,yet everything you say,just proves
you have no brain.
You ask when I'll "kick it" with you,you must
think I'm insane!
You wear your pants so big,they hang below your
ass.
You think I would be seen with you,I'm sorry,but
I must pass.
What makes you think someone like you could treat
me better than another?
You have no job,you have no car,you're still 
living with your mother!
You want to take me out.How?You have no
money,
and "big pimpin" in your daddys ride,to me seem
very funny!
Give up little boy,I'll say it again,I will not date a 
slacker.
Especially one who's ego is much bigger than his teeny
tallywacker!!
