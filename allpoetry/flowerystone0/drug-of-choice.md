Lots of pills, can give you thrills
With amphetimine,you'll want to clean
But smoke some pot,and you will not
Ecstacy,some call it X,
This one makes you jones for sex
It's also one I've never done,
I'm scared that I'll hump EVERYONE
There are drugs that make you lazy
Some might even drive you crazy
I'm not listing anymore
Cause it's time for me to score
So with this line,I'll say good bye
