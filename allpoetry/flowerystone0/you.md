Lately you have seen me staring blankly at the floor,or gazing out the window,or looking towards the door?
I seem to lose my train of thought,my focus is getting bad.
I can't stop thinking about the love that we once had.
I'd like to think we share a thought,
together now,and then.
So tell me ,do you ever stop to wonder,
What might have been?
I doubt you'll ever look at me,but,
if you do I'll be,Waiting here,or waiting there..
but just a shell you'll see.

    My soul will flutter off to you...
       Wherever you may be.Jb
