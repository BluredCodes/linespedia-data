I give you directions each step of the way,
     why do I bother,you never obey.
     When you do what I say,without giving me hell,
     you're deleting my files and sending junk mail.
     When I finish a download, I thought wouldnt end,
     you then shut off,and say "download it again"
     "ERROR" you flash,"TURN OFF THIS MACHINE!"
     I know that you do this just to be mean.
     My mouse hardly moves,and my c-prompt can't see,
     My poor mega-hurtz now, so just giga-byte me!!
