---
title: "Another love gone bad"

Softly strummin'his guitar,he leaned against the wall
starin' at his glass,not seein' me at all
Relivin' those old memories,by singin' those old songs
his face reflected feelings he still held too strong.

   He sang about a woman,who'd touched his heart and soul
of the love they shared,before her flame burned low
The emptiness,he realized,would never go away
his whole life stripped of meaning,all in one day.

   He played until he noticed the tears on his guitar
then he put it down,and slowly left the bar.
Both hands in his pockets,his head bent low and sad
   A shadow of another love gone bad.
