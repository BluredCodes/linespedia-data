The rainbow,the rose,the sunshine and
the laughter.
The good things you think of that sometimes
comes after.
You reach for the rose,and you get punctured 
by the thorn,
but you wont let that stop you,just like cold
cant stop the warm.
The rain cant keep you down if the rainbow flaunts 
it's hues,
and it's hard to keep a frown,when someones grinning back
at you.
Laughing in the sunshine,or crying in the rain-filling a
broken heart with love,takes away the pain.
So if you think about it,you'll know there's a reason why..
For when we part,we leave our heart..that's the
Good in Good-Bye.
