title: "Mama's Boy"

Unconditional love; never ending, and deep
  Sacred-the place in my heart you will keep
  So precious and innocent, so sweet and so clever
  "Little One", I will adore you forever...
  I fear the day when you think you're to old
  and you believe you're too big for your mama to hold
  My baby boy it will happen,you'll grow so tough and strong.
  Please never forget that special place,in my arms where you belong.

           For my Gavin Lee
