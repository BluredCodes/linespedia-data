Maybe it's not such a bad thing,they say you go to a better place
When your time on earth is through,you give someone else your space
Everybody hopes it's never their time to go
and it's hardest to put our loved ones below
But life can sometimes be so cold
We all feel pain,we all grow old
I believe there's never an ending
Just the start of a new beginning
I'm scared for the time when my chapter must close
Why is it death we're afraid of,when life hurts the most?Not sure if i put this in the right category. hope I dont offend anybody.
