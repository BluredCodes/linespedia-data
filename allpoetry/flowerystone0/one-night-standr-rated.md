If we do this tonight,can you respect me 
tomorrow?
Will I wake in your arms,or greet the new
day with sorrow?
You'll love me tender tonight,but,tomorrow
then what?
Will you brag to your friends,tell them all
I'm a slut?
Will you lead me to heaven,or put me straight
through hell?
Would you fight for my honor,or just kiss
and tell?
This painful desire is making me weak.
I crave you inside me,but truth,
I must seek.
Can you penetrate my mind,and take my body 
much higher?
Make love to me now,I'm burning up 
with desire.
If you tell anybody,I'll call you
a liar.
