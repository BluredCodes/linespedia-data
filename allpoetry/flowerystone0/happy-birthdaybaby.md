Your gentle push motivates me
Your upper hand intimidates me
Your better knowledge educates me
Your tender heart always awaits me
You are the best,without a doubt
The one I couldnt live without
You are the greatest man that I know
Happy Birthday baby!You're my hero

     I love you,my Jamie...forever
