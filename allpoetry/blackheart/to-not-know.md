title: "'To not know.'"
writer: "blackheart"

To not know is worse.
The terror now in mind is...
Haunting.
Leaving me restless,
Worried,
Paniced,
Paranoid!
To not know is worse.

Give it to me! ... slap it in my face, i'll take it in my stride,
Realise i might be stronger than i thought i was right now!
Cannot hide. Truth is always there, eventually to catch you,
Unaware!

To not know is worse.
Have i told you that i love you...
It doesn't matter though,
i wouldn't let you near me,
Ever,
Forever,
Eternity,
Infinity!
To not know is worse.

i love you, and you love me, but... you do not deserve this.
You are an angel, so if the truth becomes a positive, then fly.
Remember me however as a man who loved you true, because my love,
i will never forget you.

To not know is worse.
Believe me!
