Reaching out...
	But you’re no longer near!
	So, i’ll fall...
			Spiral of contempt,
	Staircase for my stumbling descent!

Erase my chances,
	Cancel my success!
		Remind myself i’m worthless,
			Return me to contempt,
	Staircase for my stu...

	Been here before!
	SO MANY TIMES!

		Drives me wild,
		Realize you are bored…

End the drone.  The pointless song,
For you cannot hold me now...

	To tell me,

		i am perfect just the way i am!

So, imperfect here i stand...

		...Without you!
