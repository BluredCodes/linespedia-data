---
title: "'i am not well.'"
writer: "blackheart"
slug: "i-am-not-well"

i am simple.
Ready to unwind.
Strand, one strand, i’m stranded,
A pleasure from your cries!
Undertaken by myself,
i hold the line and finish last,
What does it mean to fail,
When winning watches me walk past!

i am not well.
Ready to sleep.
Hand the hand i’m handed,
Toss the feelings i can’t keep!
A wonder why i’m wondering, why…?
i test the ancient fools who fall.
Trusting your inadequacies,
When there is nothing left at all!

i am simple.
Always am.
i am not well,
So understand,
It’s not the end,
But close enough,
Good bye to none,
But the one i love.
You will remain,
Stuck in my heart,
No matter how,
It does not last,
So remember this,
For to tell,
i am simple,
i am not well.
