title: "'Today, I speak.'"
writer: "blackheart"

Shape me with the serendipity on your mellifluent tongue,
A folding vocal spiral you articulate... sees me come undone.
Digressing my intentions to perpetuate my smile,
I gain from sitting staring, into my loved ones eyes.
Tumultuous decorum? A twisted verse to pen,
Like the calmness in a hurricane that becomes a mans best friend.
How can one explain, the infinity found in dreams,
Delivering saturation; all is not what it might seem.

Lay your humerus, radius and ulna,
	Around my aching ribs.

The flesh wrapped ‘round these aching bones,
	Grows weak with every kiss!

	May these moments...
	...Stay more than memories, that could fade.

	Because this, my perfect angel...
	...Is the most wonderful of days.

		Today I say,

			"I LOVE YOU!"
