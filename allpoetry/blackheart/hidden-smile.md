title: "'Hidden smile.'"
writer: "blackheart"

How can i write happy verse,
When in my mind lays painful curse,
i’m growing ill,
Waiting still,
Colder,
Older.

How can one explain, the splinters stabbing at my brain,
Leaving me in pieces, i didn’t realize it would be this way.

This way; the end!

Yet still i’ll go on rhyming.
My love for you still runs.
For you i’ll go on living,
Regardless what may come...

Positive or negative,
My choice, it will not change.
It’s you i love my angel,
What more can i say?!

Happy verse this isn’t,
But it’s words contain my joy,
Knowing you are with me,
Has calmed this little boy.

Man i may appear,
But i’ve never felt as such.
Although i wish to disappear,
You are just enough.

i will not do the worst,
You have prevented that,
i love you completely,
With this heart so very black.
