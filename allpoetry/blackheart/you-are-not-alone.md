Written by Molly for me, with her permission i wanted to share it as i thought it was beautiful.


A young man was walking along the golden sands alone.
He looked down beside him and saw another set of foot prints,
Following him.  Silent by his side.
It was that angel.
They walked the many stages of his life,
Through pain and pure pleasure.
The young man noticed that when he walked through the pain and anger there was only one set of foot prints.
But through the pleasure and love he experienced,
Two sets of foot prints walked together.
The young man questioned the angel.
She slowly came into sight,
Beautiful, gentle and delicate,
She took his tired hand and said...
When you saw only one set of foot prints,
That was when i carried you.Thank you Molly... you have no idea what this means to me.
