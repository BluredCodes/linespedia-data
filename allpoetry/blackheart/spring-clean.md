---
title: "'Spring Clean.'"
writer: "blackheart"
slug: "spring-clean"
source: "AllPoetry"

Raining spring i see you,
i dance inside your waves,
Stars all fall around me,
To meet a watery grave.
Riding in the wind,
Like a plane, my arms outstretched!
Laughing at the fallen leaves,
As with me they are swept!
Splutter pass me by,
Cough, Cough!
High rise hell i tell you,
i wish away the lot!

Looming lines all over me, microscopic forest under toes,
Rays bend all around me, as the sea around me grows.
Taste this life... breathe in.
Remember who you are,
Begin.

Begin the age old process,
One step at a time.
A life to live you are bestowed!
So use it well this time!
No more mistakes to make,
You’ve done it all so soon,
So step ahead so gently,
But start this afternoon!

Raining spring I see you,
It’s time to start afresh.
A star held in time just waiting,
For me to make my wish...

Star light, star bright...
First star i see tonight.
i wish,
i wish,
i wish...
