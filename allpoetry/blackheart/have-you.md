---
title: "'Have you?'"
writer: "blackheart"
slug: "have-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

Have you looked around my mind,
Trudged through every hole and crack!
Did you notice how i fall apart,
When you have turned your back!

Grate the skin upon me,
Flay the flesh i keep,
Drain my blood and burn me,
i won’t complain... i will not weep!

Turn away and taunt me,
It amuses every time,
Hear my pleas for love,
Ignore, then empty, leave my life.

Have you noticed my enigma,
The perplexing little drive?
That stays me on your trail,
As you insist on keeping me alive!

Break the bones within,
Tell me i am dumb,
Surprise with praise... ... ...
Then turn away and do not come!

Take away my chances,
Delete my one last hope,
Remind me i am no-one,
Supply me with the rope!

Have you wondered why,
You wanted me this way?!
i guess it doesn’t matter now,
As again i fade away,

A little more, another day...

As i slowly fade away.
