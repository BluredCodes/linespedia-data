---
title: "'Alone, together.'"
writer: "blackheart"
slug: "alone-together"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

If angels were as beautiful as you,
Then to death, I, smiling walk.
On heavens wings I’ll reach on out,
To fall back; by love be caught.

	Refreshing melodies,
	Rhyming in my mind.
	Voice on angel, sing again,
	I am in love this time.
	Remember me a new star,
	That watched and loved you well,
	Never could I hurt you...
	Can you tell?
	Can you tell?

If flowers bloomed as perfectly as you,
Then in gardens sweet, I, happy wander.
On summers breeze I’ll swim and find...
...You.  Dancing on imaginary water!

	Silent wishes,
	Skipping through my ears,
	Beautiful diamond, shine again,
	I no more feel tears.
	Remember me as warm.
	A man who loved you deep.
	So as alone we lay at night,
	In dreams we’ll forever meet.
