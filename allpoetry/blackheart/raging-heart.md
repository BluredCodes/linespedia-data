---
title: "'Raging heart!'"
writer: "blackheart"
slug: "raging-heart"

Surviving an intention,
To wrap you up in cotton wool!
i just want to protect you...
If only i could!

i am a self destructive fool,
Jumping many flames,
Dancing with the devil,
At every stage...
Of my life (perhaps it could be ours?!)

Silently i’m stalking,
To jump on you and keep you!
But i’ve missed my mark again,
This is cruel!

i am transparent,
i want to be alone.
Hold my hand but hate me,
My, how i have grown.

Into a fucking cripple!
A bird with broken wing!
A shadow lost in darkness,
i get lost in everything!
A bleeding elevation,
As i sit upon this shelf,
i’ll never leave my mark,
Except upon myself!

Falling at your feet,
i realize i’m unworthy!
Laugh at me and walk away...
But...
Don’t leave?!

i am a self destructive fool,
Jumping many flames,
Dancing with the devil,
Full of rage...
From my life (don’t walk away now?!)

Surviving the retreat,
i’ll hold you down, can’t let go!
Love me please...
It’s only you!
Only you.
