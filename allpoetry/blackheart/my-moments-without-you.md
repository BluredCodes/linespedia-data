Wondering...
	Through fallen fog... deep in me,
		This is not what i expected,
		Of this life!

	Blinded by suspense,
	For some failure i might meet,
	Knowing you will leave me,
	Some day... for now i weep.

The greys around me smile...
	With a whitened tooth!
	Sparkle in those angel eyes,
	Soon red they will appear... That is the truth!

Rain amongst my fingers,
Tingle on my skin!

			Help me see a future,
			That both of us are in!

Daisy chain around me,
i’m helpless.  Bound by beauty.

			Together we were something, alone?
			I AM NOTHING!

		Prepare me for your extended holiday,
		That i know is sure to come.
		Frightened now, i cower,
		As i watch you walk away my love!

	If i had wings like you,
	i’d follow you wherever.
	But i am nearly legless!

However...

Light meets grey in eyes,
So dull to look upon.
A shine they soon will have,
For soon no more i’ll be alone.

	You will arrive to save me,
	To control the thoughts i think.
	Remind me that you love me,
	As under stars, again we wish.

		Wishing time would just stand still,
		To keep us smiling long.
		With all this joy and beauty,
		How on earth could this be wrong!

		
			Is it?!For : Molly... without you near my mind is twisted... somehow you flatten the kinks and make it all so clear! thank you.
