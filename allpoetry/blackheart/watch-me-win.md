---
title: "'Watch me win.'"
writer: "blackheart"
slug: "watch-me-win"

Darkness creeps in sleep,
A step into the realm;
Insanity breed.
Eyes bleed...
Make me feel,
The show and tell you keep.

Rabbit hole of thoughts,
Am in-between...
Raging in the waters,
Of my melancholy dreams.
Savior be no-one,
A pin stuck in my mind,
Fumbling for my sanity,
Is such a waste of time!
Perhaps you never noticed,
The disturbing overture,
That plays behind my life,
As my pain remains uncured!
Delivery of passion,
No longer on my brain,
Instead i turn me inside out,
Finding rain... Drying rain!
Constant in the firelight,
i hold my hands so thin,
Hoping to obliterate,
The devils caught within!
Calling out i step,
Into the tattered soul,
That haunts me every moment,
i walk inside this woe.
Terrifying justice,
Damaging my name,
Opening the flood gates,
To all of my mistakes!
Ready to assemble,
My very bitter end,
Noose upon my table,
Not one more day i’ll spend,
Waiting for the obvious,
My oblivion is near!
So i’ll beat it to the finish line,
Before it tastes my fear!
Enemy before me,
Reach inside your head,
Find that i am part of you,
So with me you are dead!
Weak into the strong,
i’ve become a god somehow,
Controlling everything inside,
i am capable now!

So tell me...

Will darkness creep in sleep,
A step into the realm?
Insanity breed?
Eyes bleed…?
Now i ... ignore,
The show and tell you keep.
