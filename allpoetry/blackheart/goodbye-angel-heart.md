---
title: "Goodbye, Angel Heart."
writer: "blackheart"
slug: "goodbye-angel-heart"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62801-Goodbye--Angel-Heart.-by-blackheart"
---

Chalk lines on the pavement,
Sun on window; glare.
Sunday grows to Monday,
i was unaware.
Prevail in the nightmare,
Saviour find me now.
Left to fold; i’m bankrupt,
Save me... do you dare?!

Heat bounce all around me,
Change is empty; poor.
Tomorrow is another day,
i’m trying to ignore.
Dance with me in graveyards,
Make love on fallen headstones,
Watch the temptress smile,
When all the angels know!

Break the bitter silence,
Make it clear; reflect.
What was yesterday?!
i’m sorry, i forget...
Veneer of trust across me,
i see through all the lies.
Do not, my love, misplace it,
For the angels will not hear your cries.

Chalk lines in my basement,
Moon under cloud; hide.
Police are at my door,
i apologize!
i didn’t mean to kill her,
Her grave mistakes were made...
The blood i just could not resist,
i love the way she tastes.

My angel heart, i loved you.

Good bye.
