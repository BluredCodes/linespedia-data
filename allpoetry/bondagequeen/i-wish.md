---
title: "I Wish"
writer: "BondageQueen"

There was a time when lust
Was a new thing to me
There was a time when your embrace
Made me blush and whooed me
There was a moment
When you mad me screeched wildly
There was a moment
When you filled me so tightly
As you entered I screamed
There was that crazy feeling
I wish I could feel again
