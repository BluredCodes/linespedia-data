Take me to your blessed palce

Take me inside your world

Kiss me on every inch of my body
Take me to your blessed place

Take me away with you
And bury me in your sweet divine
Take me to your special place
So I can cum more than one time
Take me within your heavenly embrace
And I shall lustfully embrace you within myself
