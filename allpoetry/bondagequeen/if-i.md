If I called you my lover
If I called you my friend
If I called you my everything
Would you call my name again

If I held your hand
And embraced you in my arms
If I touched you briskfuly
Would you take me back again

If I let you take me
Anywhere you'd like to go
If I let you touch me
Anywhere you'd like to touch me
Would that make everything okay
Would that make yo come to me

If I let you use me
Just as you did before
If I do anything you ask
Will that make you come back
Will that make you love me for me
