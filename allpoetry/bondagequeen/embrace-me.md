Drift away into the abyss
Drigt away into nothing but my kiss
Take my hand
And take me to the promised land
For your love is more than a feeling
It's the fuel to my being

Embrace me
With your masculent arms
Warm my cold flesh
With the warmth of your own
Place your lips apon mine
And make my dreams come true
Place your lips apon mine

Lie me down
Apon these silk sheets
Lie me down
Benieth your sweating limbs
Lie me down
And free me
As you entrap me from within

Your every being
Sores within me
Your gentle feeling
Makes my heart churn again
Your eyes are soft and confident
Staring deep within my soul
Your love takes me places
I've never been before
