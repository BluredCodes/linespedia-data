Your sex completes me
Fills the void within
I can't think about anything
When you've taken me from within
Your sex complestes me
Down deep on the south side
It fills me up within
It takes away the pain inside
Your sex completes me
When you take me in your arms
I feel your loucious body
Slide apon my sweaty skin
Your sex completes me
Never before could I believe in your eyes
But now I've felt your strength
And it's knotting my mind in ties
Your sex completes me
Your lust sets me free
I lie here dreaming
Of what next time will be
