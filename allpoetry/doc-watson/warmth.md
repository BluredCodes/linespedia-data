---
title: "warmth"
writer: "Doc Watson"
slug: "warmth"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56814-warmth-by-Doc-Watson"
---

look into my eyes and see that i'm lost without you
hear me speaking but know that it means nothing
if it's not somehow directly related to you
take this hand and walk with me
through the lands where we dance in daydreams
floating, flying, leaping tall
feel my heart beating and know that
i breathe every breath with the thought of you upon me
you run through my veins
my being is because of you
falling back down to earth without you
lift me up again to the skies of blue and beyond
soothe this aching feeling all through my body
and keep me warm
