title: "the gift"
writer: "Doc Watson"

Such a long time ago
man was born
of God or on his own
but with man came
a precious Gift
one that's lasted
and always will
man looked at Woman
and said, "My Dear,
i would want you to be always near
to myself, for this is true
i myself, i love You"
man has said that forever and always
man will continure
to repeat it to Her
for Her who stole his heart away
to the Only One he sees this day
to the Only Woman that means so much
the Only Woman he wants to stay
