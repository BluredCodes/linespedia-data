---
title: "me, my heart, and a lonely song"
writer: "Doc Watson"

the treasured things we've seen and done
our time together had just begun
but it was just another story in a dream
lost from your heart to mine, or somewhere in between
we had a special song, that no one else could hear
but now it plays inside my head, and brings with it a tear
left sitting outside, in the cold
with whispered promises, left untold
once again i'm all alone
with no one left to call my own
time stands still as i struggle along
just me, my heart, and a lonely song
