---
title: "is it?"
writer: "Doc Watson"
slug: "is-it"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

sometimes we wear masks to the table
when we lie or pretend we are invincible, invisible
or maybe simple;
but our beauty is this tiny world, this family
is this never ending bond between souls
that makes us friends

it may not be like how it was, but how it is
provides picture perfect opportunity to love
to lose, to find again
who we are as people, who we are as family
and that itself is our universe
each of us our own star
shining on, loving in our related hells
