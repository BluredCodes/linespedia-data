---
title: "the traveler's tale"
writer: "Doc Watson"
slug: "the-travelers-tale"
source: "AllPoetry"

weak...i'd guess you'd call me weak
in character, at least...
i'd like to be a noble man
i'm closer to a beast

deisres withing my heart do rage
illusions in my mind
i strive to keep them all in check;
it's difficult i find

i find i'm wandering in a maze
direction, all askew...
which doorway's right, and which is false
which one do i choose?

behind the one, the tiger waits
the other?...maiden fair
the craven quakes, the fool presumes
a brave man doesn't care

and so i take another turn
and if, or when i fail...
again i'll try, and yet again -

so goes the Traveler's Tale
