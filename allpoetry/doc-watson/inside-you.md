---
title: "Inside You"
writer: "Doc Watson"
slug: "inside-you"

Again last night you set ablaze my mind
once again i saw far inside
looking deep into Your Eyes
i saw love and starry skies
lying awake, can't fall alseep
i feel and hear my own heart beat
i'm thinking again, yes, it's true
of Someone Special, who would be (you)
i held your hand inside of mine
and now i realize that with time
love can make us one from two
you'll be me, and i'll be you
no matter where our roads will lie
no matter if we see bleak skies
or if our roads stay together
which i hope is true
always know that I Love You
