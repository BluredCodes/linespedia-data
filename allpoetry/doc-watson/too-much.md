---
title: "too much"
writer: "Doc Watson"
slug: "too-much"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/56818-too-much-by-Doc-Watson"
---

a soft piano and,
a tiny wish,
a careless Whisper
it always is
i try to find it
in my heart
to say the words
i find so hard
i couldn't talk
nor could i move
was i glued to the ground
or just passing through
passing through pain
or passing through you
i can't leave this place
i'll die if i do
cause i can't live
i won't stand on my own
i can't live without you
i can't live so alone
alone in the darkness
the darkness inside
inside of my tears
the ones that i hide
hide from your eyes
your wonderful kiss
i just want your love
is that too much to wish?
