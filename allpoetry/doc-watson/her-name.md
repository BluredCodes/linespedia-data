title: "her name"

i once had a good friend
Her name was Love
we were a pair, we fitted well;
i was the hand, and she, the glove
she showed me new, wondrous emotions
and made me king of my heart;
she took me to breath-taking heights
and promised that we'd never part
with her, i went everywhere;
when i talked of her, i swelled with pride
i leaned on her, she was my shoulder
i trusted her, she was my confidant and guide
then one day, she held my hand
and took me down this unknown track
she sat me down and blinded me
then walked away, and never came back
