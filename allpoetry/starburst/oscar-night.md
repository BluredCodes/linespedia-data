Tonight's the biggest
  Night of all
  For movies
  It's the crystal ball

  Actors and actresses
  From all around
  Some of them 
  Are Oscar-bound


Whoopi puns
 And cracks some jokes
 On hobbits, schitzos
 And all those folks

" I heard Frodo
  Was an Anti-Semite"
" Come and get me boys!"
 What a night

 And then we get
 To the awards
 Some of the speeches
 Make me bored

 Red Carpet- no Bloom
 I was sad
 When Jim beat Ian
 Oh, I was mad!

And then Peter Jackson
 By Howard was beat
 I started yelling 
At my TV

Sure we took home
 Some for make-up and tech
And the animated film
Went to Shrek :)

But at one in the morining
 When I'd stayed awake
 And we lost best movie
 Is when I began to quake

 Man I was angry
 And, heck, I still am!
 But, hey, we'll be back
 For two more years, man!
