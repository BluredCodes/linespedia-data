Striking, imposing forces that drive it out,
Feelings of hate spread as you shout,
Into the night closer you become,
Feeding on what's there, what keeps you numb.

Driven stakes into your heart so deep,
Feel your bones crushing in your sleep,
Exposed to the privilege of this pain,
Encourages you to turn completely insane.

Your voice spreads the hatred inside,
Lets out the decrepit demon hiding,
Arise to your feet. Feel what is inside,
The one you never want to meet.
