~*~

If I were the moon,
I'd embellish your eyes.
With the jewels made in heaven;
The stars in the sky.

I'd shine opal light,
Upon the beauty of your face.
Hold you ever so tenderly,
In my moonlit embrace.

I would shine within you,
All the wisdom the universe had taught.
My sprinklings of moon dust,
Would echo in your every thought.

Within my galaxy of dreams,
I would rest my cheek against yours.
Envelope you with the comfort,
That only Mother Nature can bring forth.

Whispering through the evening winds,
Calming stories to ease your mind;
If only I were the moon,
I’d be with you all the time.

~*~
