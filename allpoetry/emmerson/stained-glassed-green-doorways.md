I wonder what you would discover,
If you were to sit in front of me and stare.
As the reflective rays of the sun,
Shone done in my golden hair?

~*~

Looking in through
The stained glassed green doorways,
That lead down into my soul.
Caputuring little pieces of me
As walk on stairs of gold.

Feeling of me from the inside,
As you find yourself
In me lost.
Discovering what the
Price of beauty,
To me did really cost.

Touching with your palms
A heart that beats in time.
With the magical
Lessons of wisdom 
That it's learnt over time.

Would you taste 
My essence as your fingers
Across me did glide?
Or would all that you'd 
Discovered,
Make you want to hide?

Sitting in the sun,
Eyes relfecting into me.
Just wondering what you'd see;
If you really did in fact,
