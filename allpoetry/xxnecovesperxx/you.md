"I miss you pal" - 
i never knew one line in this letter
could mean so much to me.
you say you miss me as a pal,
i think you miss me and want me back.
i don't know why i distort its meaning. 
i want to love you, but
it hurts to know you can't ever feel the same.
you have HER
I have..him..
but when can i have YOU?..please do not rate..it was written for my friend's situation =) hope you like it hun
