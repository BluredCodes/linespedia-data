i chanced upon your ethereal hand
embessoning through my ebony hair.
you caressed your way to my heart
as you whispered the depths of your soul to me.
your voluptuous lips puckered 
in seductive endearment
as you called out my name and our pilgrim lips convened.
the taste of you was overwhelming
as you warmly showered me in fond regard.
your branching arms welcomed me in a gratifying embrace
the virile smell of you offered me comfort, 
as it was befitting a man.
then, the thought of you converged
into the definition of love......

..and from the nethermost part of this pathetic heart
i would like to thank you for giving me this dream..
