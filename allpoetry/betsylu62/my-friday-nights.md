A quiet house, alone with me;
I can read or write; I'm totally free.

I light a scented candle;
Plop down at my desk;
Start pecking at the keyboard;
And my mind does the rest.

Big dog at my side;
Eyes following every movement.
From earlier today...
Wow, what a vast improvement.

I love Friday nights;
Look forward to them all week;
For the quietness they afford me;
And the special solitude I seek.
