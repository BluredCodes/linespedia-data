She's a very quiet girl;
still waters can run deep.
In her sad heart she feels,
many rivers she could weep.
She spends way too much time
on introspective thinking.
Life is the sun; and she stares
long without blinking.
The world does it's best
to crush her further down;
But with each of life's new trials,
she rises without a sound.
Her spirit may be quiet;
but there's a strength inside.
A stormy life is hers;
but all the waves she'll ride.
She may not always understand
all the where's and why's...
But you watch out....because someday ...
That girl is going to fly!
