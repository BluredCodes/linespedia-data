The ever present rock and hard place
Desperately teaching them to find their way;
While I'm teetering at the top of the cliff;
Picking up pieces at the end of each day.

When did I become the great enemy?
I only signed up to be their mom.
They battle my every movement
As if I were out to do them wrong.

I must cling to my inner hope;
Which tells me they'll be okay;
Then choose my battles wisely
Before I jump into the fray.
