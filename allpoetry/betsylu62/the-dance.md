title: "The dance"

I caught his eye and
he reciprocated in-kind.
I could tell this man
was a truly special find.
Good looking to a fault;
but you can't blame him for that.
And just being so close
made me squirm where I sat.
When he asked me to dance,
I about fell on the floor.
While part of me wanted to
make a bolt for the door.
We danced once or twice;
and let's just say, "he moved well."
I longed for a slow song
but it would be to no avail.
Next thing I know, 
he was gone out the door!
Leaving me with thoughts
of the man on the dance floor.
