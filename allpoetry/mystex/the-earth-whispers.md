---
title: "The earth whispers"
writer: "Mystex"
slug: "the-earth-whispers"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59267-The-earth-whispers-by-Mystex"
---

A distant whispering is all I hear
The world talking to me.
Telling me secrets of its existance
Of it long life and existance.

Tales of love gone wrong,
Passionate and romantic evenings.
Stories of huge battles of man,
And trials of animals over nature.

Tribulations and sorrows
Successes and failures.
All has it seen
And experienced.

Nothing we can think
Is beyond it's scope of experience.
