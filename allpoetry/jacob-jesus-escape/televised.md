i am only eyes now

resting on a belly one could never grasp
without stabbing hard with your fork
even then lifting s so impossible
you d have to bend down
i think to eat this gut
off of its shocking bed 
dangerous as you would feel
that fat and thorns in your face
the cold of life and the heat of pain
as i await that turning off of  tongues
