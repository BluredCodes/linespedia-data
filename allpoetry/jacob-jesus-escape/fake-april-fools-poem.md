having nothing more to say
about the many ways i said
the same thing over again
i then take the book
leafing through 
its chambers

revolving until
a fire erupts
my fire spills

and a teardrop of burned foolishness
exposes the green shoots
of my idiot taking

from the air 
an audience eye
channels news of passing
and the beautiful black drop
a backdrop of burn points just as the gun
pointed out by redundant anchors like white trees
locking us in a gentle head waving shake
an act of pity and acting to be sure
so that we're all right with sorrow

the miracle of fire
caused by a rot of head
exposes this kiss of bullet touched off
flame quenched by no loss of blood
there was the glowing beauty
in that fooling death

how many times
must this anchor splash
into our dark ocean
before children
see the lies

i am not dead
that is the best joke
played on the world april foolthere is little reality here
