when sick the world 
turns like 
a program of failing like 
a programmed need 
a provided crutch 
or our
favorite false
 
escape of 
burdening youth 
release the christ 
dog to 
snap the heels of sinners turning 
them to worms for 

fishers of men bob 
past in our ocean blue bold 
as a glacier 
knowing the good 
melt 

of 
drowning experiment 
and 
freezing process 
ramble on again 
spit 

on again if you dare 
but mean it this timea response to a comment
