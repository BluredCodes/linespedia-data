again 
her abdomen 
dips to my pond s waters 
and sets free a union of flight 
to dwell in the dark pool waters killing 
a year before taking the sky 
before taking the flies 
before coupling 
againdragonflies are magnificent predators
280 million years ago there were species 
with wingspans 15 times greater than those living today...
75cm...ALMOST 30 INCHES!
