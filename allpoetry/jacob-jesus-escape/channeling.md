few hear the rumble
of crumbling 
trained promises
spelling tragedy

or piers calling out
watery curls 
talk clinging 
gulping air 
swallows 

across 
catfish mists
ruptured bellies of rocked gas
spring scent rips 
apart

passed earth s flowering

around the bends of green 
fields bodies twist
gifts all

swirling 
rap hiss 
thump forms crack 
creak round stumps float 
drown shallows 
flood
a river  calling
all to bow down in her wake
cuts a new channelchallenge submission
