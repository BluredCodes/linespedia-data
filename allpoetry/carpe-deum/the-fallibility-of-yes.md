---
title: "The Fallibility of 'Yes'"
writer: "Carpe Deum"
slug: "the-fallibility-of-yes"

No
Just two letters that carry
A whole heck of a lot of meaning
Depending on the tone they're said in
If you say it kindly 'no' can be
A gentle remonstration
A mother telling a child for the first time 'don't do that'
Harshly, 'no' can be 
"I've told you once and I'm not going to tell you again"
Jokingly 'no' can be
"I really do think your cute but I've just GOT to play hard-to-get"
No
Our society revolves around 'no'
No swimming, no skateboarding, no drugs, no drinking, no running
But poor, underappreciated 'no'
She doesn't get any attention 
Until someone says 'yes'
When they should have said 'no'
And then they blame poor 'no'
When it was yes's fault they got in trouble in the first place
We don't know no's importance
Until we see the fallibility of yes
