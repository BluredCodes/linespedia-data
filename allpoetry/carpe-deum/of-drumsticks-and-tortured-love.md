Sometimes I wonder
If everything tastes like chicken
What does chicken taste like?
Who decided that chicken tasted like chicken
And why?
And...
If 'shrimp' is a derogatory nickname meaning 'small'
Why are there jumbo shrimp?
And if ice is made of water 
Why is there dry ice?
And black light?
Tortured love?
And the ever popular 'Military intelligence'?
Why?
Maybe I ask too many questions
But I've always believed
That if light is light, it can't be black
That if shrimp is small, it can't be jumbo
And that if two people love each other enough
There cannot be tortured love
Everyone tells me
That's just the way it is
But still, I have to wonder...
What does chicken taste like?
