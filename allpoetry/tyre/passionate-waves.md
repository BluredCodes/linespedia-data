---
title: "Passionate Waves"
writer: "Tyre"
slug: "passionate-waves"
source: "AllPoetry"

Passionate waves rolling across the sandy shores
Delighting my soul with thoughts of loving you
As I engulf myself deep into its rich oceans
Where seagulls cry out to enliven sweet dreams

There, fluffly clouds don the misty skies above
Adding flavor to the splendor of your finess
Screaming with pleasure in rapturous motions
Of intense desires burning brightly within

Quickening is your spirit dancing inside
Where dawn awaits for a brand new beginning
To arise and greet new passions sweeping across
Vying for your affections to strengthen my soul

You are my source for loving enchantments
Indulging in dreams to fill the vast oceans
Where passionate waves roll across sandy shores
Delighting my soul with thoughts of loving you

Sweet passionate Angel of my soul's delight
Abide forevermore within everlasting dreams
As a constant source for our loving enchantments
To arise and greet every new passionate wave
