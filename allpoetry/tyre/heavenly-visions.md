---
title: "Heavenly Visions"
writer: "Tyre"
slug: "heavenly-visions"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54897-Heavenly-Visions-by-Tyre"
---

Heavenly visions of passionate dreams
Breathes life unto hopeful explorations
Delivered in truth of reality's quest
In bringing to pass the heart's desires

Such is the beauty your soul provides
With its heavenly visions of undying love
Wrapped within our protective cocoons
Blanketed in our harmonized symphonies

Let us explore our distant worlds together
Providing comfort in their enticing realms
Keeping their gifts for our lifetime of joys
That our visions of heaven may endure

Tenderly I implore your will to succeed
By seeking the truth behind our visions
Of heavenly dreams in need of living
And growing beyond our own expectations

There we may find our dreams come true
With our souls united in loving compassion
Proving that love is more than a dream
And life gives more within heavenly visions
