---
title: "My Passionate Angel"
writer: "Tyre"
slug: "my-passionate-angel"
source: "AllPoetry"

You are a shining vessel unto me 
With subtle reigns of purity's delight
Woven in the midst of passionate play
Deepening life's pleasures within your grace

You are my balance given to endure
The living adventure we now call life
That our hearts may blossom unto full bloom
And passion may enchant our living souls

You are the sun burning brightly in me
Quickening my soul with life giving floods
Of nurturing pleas for intimate waves
To brake upon the shores of our abode

You are my purpose for breathing the air
Wrapped in the pleasure of making love to you
Endlessly devouring your want for more
As I graze in your pastures tasting sweet

Oh passionate Angel abide in me
Rest upon the tapestries of my soul
In need of your passion's burning intent
To engulf my desires within your nest
