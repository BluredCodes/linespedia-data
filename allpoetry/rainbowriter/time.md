---
title: "Time"
writer: "Rainbowriter"
slug: "time"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

I almost fell into the hole in your life.
There is people all around,
But you only see me.
You say that I'm insensitive,
But our love is so young
It couldn't even stand on its own.

I fell down the hill of my insecurities.
You came tumbling after me. 
You say that I'm insensable.
I'm asking God for all the answers
That lie right in front of me.

You say that we are perfect
And you're the only one for me.
Am I blind?
I just can't seem to make up my mind.
I'm asking God for answers,
But God doesn't always come through.

Give me time, 
But don't give up on me.
I feel comfort in your arms.
Give me time, 
But don't fall in love with me.
I don't want to hurt you again.
I just can't seem to make up my mind.
