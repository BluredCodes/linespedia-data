title: "If You Could See Me Now"
writer: "Rainbowriter"

The hardest thing for me to do
Is to smile and say everything is alright.
If I can't read your thoughts
Then I feel empty.
When I'm not with you
I'm falling apart.
My world is crashing down
When your not here.
If you could see me now...
