I close my eyes.
Pretend this is not happening to me.
Waiting for the black to replace my blue.
Who is this man that stands before me?
He says he's my friend.
He says he won't hurt me.
He says alot of things.

This is harder than it seems.
These haunting dreams.
He hurt me more than I show,
But I can't scream.
I can't let you know.
He says he'll hurt me if I tell.

He liked it when he made me cry.
Is it my right to wish he would die?
Oh God, can I ask you 
Why you let this man make me scream?
