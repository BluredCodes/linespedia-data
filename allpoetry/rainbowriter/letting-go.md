---
title: "Letting Go"
writer: "Rainbowriter"
slug: "letting-go"

It doesn't mean much that you think 
My life is not a waste.
You strip away the ugliness that surrounds me.
I claw for solid ground,
But it crushes under my own weight.
Will I burn in heaven like I do down here?
I never thought i could feel so low.
Oh darkness, i feel like letting go.
With all the courage and the strength
I will fly away from this place.
No, I will not turn around.
I've crossed the last line 
From where I can't return.
It's much peaceful up here.
Are you an angel?
Am I that gone?
I hope that I won't disappoint you 
Down here on my knees.
When the sky starts to fall,
You won't see me cry.
You can't pick me up
When I'm stuck to the ground.
I watch the stars start to fall,
You won't see me cry.
It seems that there's no hope for me,
But you won't see me cry.
