title: "Walk Over Me"

Sometimes I cry myself to sleep.
Does anyone know why?
I put the damage on myself;
I beat myself up just like you always did.
Sometimes I ask myself why
I put up with this for so long.
You've caused enough damage for a lifetime.
and now I'm wishing...

That love is not a hopeless crime.
But now I have to worry
Because you still look pretty when you're
Walking over me.

I'm over the bridge now.
So how does it feel 
When the tables are turned?
This is easier than I thought it would be.
There is nothing left for you to do.
But you've caused enough damage for a lifetime.
and now I'm wishing...

That these wounds will heal in time.
But now I have to worry
Because you still look pretty when you're
Walking over me.

You say I can quit alot of things,
But I can't stop loving you.
Well, I'm here to prove you wrong.
I have my shoes on.
