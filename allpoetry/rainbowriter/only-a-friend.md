---
title: "Only A Friend"
writer: "Rainbowriter"

I get so weak in the knees.
I can hardly speak.
What do I have to do 
For you to notice me?
I can't let it go so easily,
I've never been this swept away.
I can't describe the way it felt to hit the floor.

Friends we've been for so long,
But I have never been honest to you.
You say you're in love;
You just turn away.
How can you love pain?
I don't understand.

I can't tell you how I feel.
I'm only a friend,
Nothing more.
And if you fall the way 
I fell for you,
Then you will see 
What you mean to me.
I'm in to deep.
There is no way out.
I fear I'm alone...
