---
title: "getting to the bottom"
writer: "to some i am a poet"
slug: "getting-to-the-bottom"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/53944-getting-to-the-bottom-by-to-some-i-am-a-poet"
---

the top is the bottom
but the bottom's not on top.
i wish these thoughts would just stop
just drop
but they do not.
from right to left
and left to right.
up and down
these thoughts are in sight...
they excite
but i'm getting tired.
the bottom's the top now
these thoughts have expired.
