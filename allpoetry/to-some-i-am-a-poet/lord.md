---
title: "Lord,"
writer: "to some i am a poet"

please show me a better day.
it seems like i only pray when things ain't goin my way.
but i want you to stay,
don't go away.
you know i'll need you here.
i'll need you to dry the tears.
i feel your presence near me but it seems like you don't hear me.
Lord,
there is so much i need to tell you.
well you may not know this but i can tell you do.
i sin,
and you see what i go through.
you want me to win and lead me in the right direction.
i need your protection Lord.
help me feel safe and sound.
sometimes Lord,
i don't feel you around.
those are the times when i'm feelin down.
i guess it's just to make me stronger. 
now i no longer think i'm a goner.
it's been a long time since i couldn't stand,
so whatever you do Lord,
when I die, take me to the promise land.
