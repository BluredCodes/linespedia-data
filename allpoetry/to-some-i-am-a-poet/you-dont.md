title: "you don't"
writer: "to some i am a poet"

no introductions please,
you don't need to know me.
it's only greed you show me.
wanting to be a part of this,
but you weren't here at the start of this.
it doesn't matter where i'm at now,
and it doesn't really matter where i'm headed to or how.
without you looking in this direction,
hooked on every imperfection..
makes it easier for me to see.
to be who i will when i can be.
so please..
don't act like you know me.
