title: "Melting"
writer: "drummerchick"

The way you smile
the way you talk
the amazing way you make me feel
I love to sit
and look into your eyes
almost as if
I can see your soul
I love you oh-so-much
I hate to be apart
I shiver when we touch
you
have a hold
upon my heart2002,
