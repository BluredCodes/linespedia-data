When I look into your eyes
or caress your soft skin,
I feel like I am flying,
Like an angel without sin.
Why do I deserve you,
I must do something right
I think about you all day
and dream of you at night.
and when we are together,
all time seems to stop
of all the world, 
I feel on top.
When we are apart, 
I go through withdrawl
its like I am addicted to you
more than an alcoholic is to alcohol.
You make me feel special
crazy inside
I tell you my secrets,
in you I confide.
I love you with all 
the love I have to give.
You are my everything,
you give me a reason to live.2002,
