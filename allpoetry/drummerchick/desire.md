I desire,
to be desired by you.
You light my heart on fire ;
all the day through.
I desire to be irresistable
and loved by you.
I desire for you to say “I LOVE YOU” 
and mean it too.
I wish you would look into my eyes 
and see that
I want you,
so much, so true.2002,
