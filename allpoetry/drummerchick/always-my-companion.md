Often times I sit around,
for nothing but to think. 
I wonder why things seem so hard,
why my heart appears to have lost a link.
Why does nothing go right?
It never does for me,
out of sight, out of mind,
memmories just let me be.
I miss the people I once had,
friends I could talk to.
How I yearn to tell you to your face,
I miss and love you.
You are such an enormous part of me,
our friendship once so strong.
Until the day everyhting changed,
nothing could ever go wrong.
I wont be here for you next year,
I will be quite far away.
You are silent for a while, 
You dont know what to say.
I wish you would say something,
anything’d be fine
You are my friend forever,
Yes! forever mine!2002,
