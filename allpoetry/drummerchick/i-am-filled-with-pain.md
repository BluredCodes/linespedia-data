I am filled with pain
my heart feels like its about to explode.
I can do nothing but cry....
how did this happen? 
How could I give you a hold of my emotions...
HOW?? 
More over...
Why are you messing with them..
why must you hurt me 
I hate that I cant hate you
please dont do this.
