---
title: "It is You"
writer: "drummerchick"
slug: "it-is-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

The glow in your eyes
the smile in your face
the love in your heart
and devotion in your soul
these combine
to make you the one
I would like to spend the rest of my life with
you make me feel important,
special inside.
The way you tell me
“I love you” 
how you look deep into my eyes
seeing my soul
melting my heart
you are the one who
reaches for my hand
and touches my heart
tells me it will be okay
when times are hard
you are
my soulmate2002,
