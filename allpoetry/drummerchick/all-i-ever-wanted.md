title: "all I ever wanted"
writer: "drummerchick"

All I ever wanted
was to be like you
be capable to do the things you do. 

All I ever wanted
was to be loved by you 
and have everyone know it too.

All I ever wanted
was a chance for you to see
how much you meant to me.

Now all time is gone,
I think of what I wish I had done,
Of all the times we may have had fun.

You left me yesterday
and dead in the casket you lay
I never got the chance to say

All I ever wanted 
was to be like you
be capable to do the things you do.

All I ever wanted 
was to be loved by you
and have everyone know it too.

All I ever wanted 
was a chance for you to see
how much you meant to me.
