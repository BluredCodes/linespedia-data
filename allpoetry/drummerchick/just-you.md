---
title: "Just You"
writer: "drummerchick"
slug: "just-you"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

you are my sunshine
warming me
surrounding every part of my body.
you are my moonlight,
watching me,
as I sleep through the night;
dreaming of you.
You are my heart,
lightly beating in my chest,
giving me this feeling I have never had.
You are my soul,
filled with emotion,
this amazing sense of devotion.
You are the air I breathe,
That keeps me alive.
You are everything thats of importance to me and my being.
You keep me going,
give me a reason to live.
SO I thank you
for being you
for you are
my everything.2002,
