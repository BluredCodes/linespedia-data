We were once so close
but since we have spread.
Growing apart,
too far apart.
I lie in bed 
wondering wehre it went.
our friendship once so great,
What happened?
Tore us apart? 
I know one thing however...
It becomes painful
more so each and every day.
You and me...
we made a lot of sense.
We talked about everything,
and I trusted you with all my heart.
When you went away,
I missed you.
I didnt tell myself to miss you
it just happened.
I know we arent great friends anymore
but still I love you
You WERE
my one true best friend2001,
