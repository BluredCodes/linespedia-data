When I first met you,
it wasent love at first sight.
You seemed so wrong,
but we were so right. Eventually we got closer,
Our friendship grew and grew.
Then I came to realize, 
I cant help loving you.2002, This is my favorite poem I have written so far... I hope you like it too.
