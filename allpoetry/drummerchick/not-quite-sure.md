I am not quite sure why
I feel the way I do
but I am so sure
these feelings are made 
strong by you.
When you touch me,
I shiver inside.
The softness of your skin
the look in your eyes
I have found the one,
my mate for life
my looking is done
THe softness of your kiss,
and sweetness of your lips
when they are upon mine
and the sound of your whisper 
stops the moment in time2002,
