When I first moved here
I could do nothing but tear
I missed my old friends,
all memories came to ends.
I didnt want a new one
my whole life was done.
Then
I met you
you were a drummer too.
Or frienship was a slow start,
we were always apart
WHo would have thought
you are the friend I sought.
nopw wer are always together,
we know our friendship is forever2002,
