This smile
that lies upon my face
Is a cover 
hiding the way I really feel
Making everyone think its Ok.

You
are the only one who knows
how I feel deep down inside 
how sometimes I wish 
dead is how I wish I could lay.

This smile
Covers up the saddness and sorrow
makes me think I am ok
I know I am not
one day this pain will catch up to me.

You 
help me see 
that this saddness is not how to live
you help me see its a cover
it is you that helps me see
