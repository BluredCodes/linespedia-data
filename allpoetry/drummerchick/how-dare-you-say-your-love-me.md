The more you say you love me,
it pushes me away.
You always say you want me near,
yet I year to be apart.
The way you tell people you love me,
makes me sick.
I often find myself wondering 
how can you say you love me?
We arent that close,
and barely talk.
I get angry 
in a way I cannot describe.
How dare you say you love me!
You incredibly pushy bastard.
WHen you say you love me
Do you notice.. I never say it back.
If you say you love me 
I want to ask you why?
how? 
You dont know who I am
or what I am about.
You know absolutely nothing
yet you insist you love me
YOU DONT LOVE ME DAMNIT!
STOP playing these games.
You can not love me
You dont love someone you dont know
you cant
exactly!
so stop!
Stop lieing to youself,
me,
and everyone around! 
NO!
 You dont love me2002,
