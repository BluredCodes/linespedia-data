I need a Place to go
a place I can run to
When I can't handle things
or when life gets too tough
We all need a place like this
A place where you wont feel anything but happiness
where there is no pain or hurt
a placce to sit and think in
ans have time to enjoy quiet and peacefullness
A place no one knows about
Dont I wish! 
A place like this however
does not exist2001,
