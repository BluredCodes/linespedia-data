Love is like the sun
rising each day
warming us
bringing life to new things
guiding us by day
showing us the way
and when the clouds come out,
covering the sun, 
the love is always present
Even while covered
nothing can stop the sun from coming out
as nothing can stop 
love from being all around. 
And as dusk moves in,
and the sun sets,
it goes to other people
letting them feel its warmth
and see its beauty
letting the entire world 
know how it feels 
to be loved.2002,
