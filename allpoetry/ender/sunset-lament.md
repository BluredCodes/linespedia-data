Sunset Lament

my heart's flown the skies of heaven, and dragged the grounds of hell.
i've spent many nights awake remembering how far i fell.
the road that brought me to this place long since made me weak of heart,
but the time and everything faded; and with it tore me apart.
even so, i'd give it all up if it meant i could see her face,
relinquish in the moment of that eternal embrace.
but withered is the rose that was that dream,
lost to my touch like leaves on a stream.
the storm has left me weary and beaten, but still i stand, as ever so alone.
a new hope risen that i'd long since forsaken, in the light that my own sunset shone.
