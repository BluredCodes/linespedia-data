The Sole Purpose of Betrayal


Listening,
to the rain falling down
tears of silence in which i drown
but i've heard it all before

Ironic,
you'd think that after all this time
of simple words in empty rhyme
there'd be something more

Remembering,
days of past and withered shame
that just of thinking rouse new pain
but again i push it away

Loving,
everything yet nothing at all
if like the rain, my love would fall
there'd be nothing left to betrayUnsatisfied with title..
