Velvet Wings

velvet wings frame a beauty
meant to be free for all eternity
soaring the skies with a passion
rivaling a sunset's fire within me

and as promised, came the day
when those wings were thrown away
gone from sight but burnt into my heart
the sunset gave way to the grey

days and nights brought such pain
but it faded like summer rain
and the sun seemed to shine once more
birthing a hope for happiness in store

the wings then returned, met with a gaze
that couldn't help but remember those days
of hoplessness and broken heart
but even so were the walls torn apart

and the wings were once more embraced
a love now untainted and beautifully graced
reaching heights not reached in the past
but hope yet wavered that it would last

though on and on untouched it went
doubt and uncertainty long since spent
which faded just like the pain had before
an emotion which didn't exist anymore

and then that heaven was abruptly replaced
with an agony not yet to be faced
for the sun that powered the flight
had set, unknowingly bringing the night

the beautiful wings majestic and fierce
now meaningless, how deeply they pierced
forcing a fall to a hell long forgotten
wasting away, long decayed and rottenThis... is a metaphorical poem.
