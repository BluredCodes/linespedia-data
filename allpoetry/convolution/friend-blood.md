---
title: "Friend Blood"
writer: "Convolution"
slug: "friend-blood"
source: "AllPoetry"

God is risin' from the ground
Silent people standin' all around
Sadness comes down from the sky
Just in time to see us wavin' bye

Silver statues screamin' "save the dawn"
All the morning in the glory's gone
Pushin' pedals just to be a man
I don't think you want to hold my hand

Leather eaters clawin' at your shoes
Everybody hates to watch you lose
Never wanted to meet you here
So dry your eyes with another tear

The pencil's bleedin' on the paper's back
I'm turnin' white to help you see the black
Everybody loves to help you fly
They never take the time to help you cry

Light-up people in the night's embrace
Love and fear are how we keep this pace
Muted chords of God's disharmony
Every kiss designed to steal from me

Several strings are ringin' out of tune
I cannot bleed in front of this new moon
Give me all your loss tonight
I'll take it all to give you one last flight
