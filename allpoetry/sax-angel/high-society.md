---
title: "High Society"
writer: "sax angel"
slug: "high-society"

As you drive around in your fancy car
do you ever stop and see
the people who would never fit 
in your high society?

The pregnant teenage dropout
or the prostitute on the street
who sells her soul for pennies
so that her kids can eat?

If perchance you notice,
do you lend a helping hand?
Do you judge and condemn
or do you try to understand?

Just pause for a moment and 
try to put yourself into their shoes.
The life that you are given 
isn't always what you'd choose.

The homeless may not wish to live
in boxes or in cars,
but still that choice is better 
than just blankets made of stars.

The disabled veteran may not have
any place that he can go, 
for he has seen and experienced things
that you and I will never know.

Are your children always
clothed and fed?
Do they sleep each night
in a nice warm bed?

Be thankful that you have
been so blessed.
Just don't forget to
help the rest.You may not always be the one who is blessed.
