---
title: "You & Me"
writer: "DarkDruidess"
slug: "you-me"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62647-You---Me-by-DarkDruidess"

Within my dreams I see a beautiful place
Where you and me are free to feel
Filled with the warmest sun and freshest breeze
With no tears and no sorrow
Full of laughter and love
Running hand in hand with you
Along the shore with waves kissing our feet
Sharing intimate looks of tenderness
Laced with a fiery passion
Embracing each other with all our desires
Exploring the depths of our love
Letting the whole world fall away
Till nothing matters but you and me
Together we are complete and unbound
Free to fulfill all our fantasies
And create a sanctuary made for us
In this beautiful place I envision
But I am only allowed glimpses
While I close eyes and sleep
The days seem so long and dreary
Waiting for the stars to shine
So I can escape and dream
Of my heaven of you and me
