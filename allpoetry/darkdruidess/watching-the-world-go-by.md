title: "Watching the World Go By"

I’ve been beaten and cowed 
I’ve stood straight and proud
I don’t like either extreme
Isn’t there somewhere in between
My will has been broken
My self control is just a token
I don’t want to live and I don’t want to die
Just sit here and take my pills to get high
Watching the world go by
While inside I slowly cry
Shedding tears of bitterness
For my state of loneliness
Cursing the lot of my fate
While the hour grows late
Hating my self inflicted pain
Yet yearning to do it again
They say I am falling apart
I say I am suffering from a broken heart
They say I am crazy
I’m just lazy
Watching the world go by
While inside I slowly die
