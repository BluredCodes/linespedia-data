title: "Loving Only You"

I feel a hunger welling up in me
Coursing through my veins like liquid fire
My body shakes and trembles
Begging for your tender touch
I slowly close my eyes
My eyelids start to flutter
Relishing in this desire
That only you can induce
Wondering if you can feel the heat
That radiates from me
As I cautiously peer at you
I see an enticing grin cross your face
Further fanning the flames
That burn so passionately 
As I reach out for you 
You take my hand and pull me close
Electric sensations shock me
Where our heated skin meets
Creating sparks that illuminate 
This steamy and fervid room
As my lips search for yours
I inhale the scent that is wholly yours
Causing my mind to spin dizzily 
Finding my destination I taste
The very essence of you
Almost unable to bear the sweetness
As it floods through me
I yearn for so much more
But time has slowed to a crawl
With every touch and caress
Etched upon my heart and soul
Ready to spend forever
In this beautiful night
Loving only you
