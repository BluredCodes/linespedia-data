Chains wrap the tender flesh
Hooks dig deep within the skin
Such exquisite and sweet pain
Salty blood streaked tears run freely
Tasting so bittersweet to my lips
As I lick your sweat soaked cheeks
Agony bringing out the pleasing song
That your tortured body sings
My ears ring with your delightful melody
Watching as you writhe in distress
My lips curl dripping with your fluid
That I so carefully and meticulously drain
As you beg and plead with a scream 
Your suffering excites my palate 
Saliva rushing to fill my mouth
Awaiting the fresh morsel I carve
Manipulating my blade with a smile
Your eyes sparkle with satisfying terror
As you spray ruby red droplets 
When each gut wrenching shriek 
Running my knife up your chest
Drawing a rapidly filling line
That arouses my darkened soul
My breathing becoming labored
As I reach the spot where your heart lays
Closing my eyes I relish the moment
When you realize what I am going to do
Feeling your body shake in denial
My eyes meet yours with intensity
That lights the room with horror
Pleasure quickening my pounding pulse
I slide my gleaming steel inside
Your body jerks driving the blade deeper
Plunging into your beating heart
I sigh with release as your eyes grow dim
Lowering my still wet lips for a final kiss
Savoring one last gentle caress 
Before I feast on your warm flesh
