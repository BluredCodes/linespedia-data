title: "Watching You Sleep"

As I sit here watching you sleep 
Thoughts randomly surface
I love the way your hair curls 
Around my finger so softly
The way those dark eyes
Can stare right into my soul
One touch from your hand
Sets my soul on fire
I beg my heart not to love you
For I see the leaving in your eyes
I sense the exit in your words
I sit trembling on the edge
Of this impending heartbreak
Soaking up all that you give to me
Building memories etched on my soul
Even knowing that you will say goodbye
My mind still spins fantasies
Where you and I are shooting stars
Dreams that sustain and keep me
I tenderly caress your handsome face
Living in this beautiful moment
I entreat the gods to let me stop time
And spend forever watching you sleep
