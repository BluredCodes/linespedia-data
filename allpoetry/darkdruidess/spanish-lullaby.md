Underneath a star filled sky
Dancing under the desert moon
To the sound of a Spanish guitar
The taste of margaritas still on my lips
Relishing the feel of your arms
Wrapped around me in tenderness
Swaying gently to the subtle rhythm
That our two souls make
Feeling the passion that runs deep
Making my skin prickle with your touch
Softly whispering in your ear
Uttered words for you only
Watching a smile cross your face
Fills me with such contentment 
Sending shivers through my body
To be the one here with you
Enjoying the magical quality 
That encircles you and me
Basking in the heat that we create
With each delicate and gentle caress 
Exploring the wonderful boundaries
That we have opened up 
Kindly teasing each other with loving strokes
Evoking a beautiful laughter from both
That rings through the night
And lights our eyes with a spark
Catching sight of a falling star
I make a simple and heartfelt wish
To always have you to hold
And listen to my Spanish lullaby
