title: "Learning To Breath Again"

Standing within your warm embrace
I feel the ice around my heart
Slowly start to thaw
I start to let go of all
The anger, bitterness and pain
That has become so much a part of me
I feel an awakening deep inside
That makes my soul sing
A beautiful melody full of your name
My lips beg to let loose 
With all the adoration 
That I have bottled up inside
But my fears have me silenced
Too scared to say the words
That would bring us closer
Frightened of the implications
That would come after my professions
I stand holding you close
With tears stinging in my eyes
Learning how to breath again
With your scent on my cheek
Imploring the heavens to give me strength
To let you know how much you mean to me
Before you walk out the door
Leaving me standing here
With your taste on my lips
And your last exhalation in my hair
Without ever knowing if you felt it tooBrad this is for you...I hope you see it and realize what it means...
