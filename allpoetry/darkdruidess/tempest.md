title: "Tempest"
writer: "DarkDruidess"

I stand on the edge 
Staring down into the abyss
Watching the storm build 
Clouds gathering rapidly
Black and churning  
Maliciousness in motion
The wind whipping my hair
Boisterous and unruly
Its coldness invading my skin
Lightening piercing the sky
Wicked streaks across the darkness
This tempest intensifying 
Jolting my senses
Tears forming and falling
Sliding down my cheeks
Rivulets of ice
Shivering and trembling
In awe of the rage
That wells up from my soul
Matching that of the squall
I raise my arms in supplication 
Entreating nature arduously 
To let me join in
This magnificent power
I throw back my head
And scream will all I have
With all my wrath and abhorrence
Of the ungrateful people 
That surround me
Wishing this storm 
Within and outside
Would cleanse my essence
Scour it back to innocence
Wash the vile thoughts
From my heart
And return me 
To happiness lost
