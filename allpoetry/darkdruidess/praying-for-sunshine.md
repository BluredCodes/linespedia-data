---
title: "Praying For Sunshine"
writer: "DarkDruidess"
slug: "praying-for-sunshine"

The rain clouds have begun to build again
Tall and threatening ready to release a deluge
That has the possibility of drowning my soul
I stand shaking wondering what it all means
The wind blows my hair in circles
Whipping it each and every way
I continue my ardent prayer for peace
But the storm is unfazed by my treaties
I shiver with the coldness that rises up
Forcing me to hold onto myself in protection
While lightening illuminates the sky
With great flashes and booming thunder
I feel so small and helpless to it all
The cacophony continues around me
Terrible power on display in the night
As my tears start to fall in silence
The rain begins to fall soaking me
Wet and trembling I still beg for solace
From that great higher power that hides
Behind the storm that is life
Deep understanding takes hold of me
Knowing that I need strength and conviction
To weather this tempest and stay whole
Aware that I am being tested for my love
Struggling to be the woman I need to be
Trying to be courageous and see the big picture
Not wanting to get lost in this storm
And lose sight of what is important to me
So I will stand brave in the face of this assault
Holding on to the love I feel
And arduously praying for the sunshine to return
