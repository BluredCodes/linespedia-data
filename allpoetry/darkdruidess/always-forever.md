title: "Always & Forever"
writer: "DarkDruidess"

How do I tell you what you mean to me
Without sounding cliché or like a sappy love song
The words seem to be stuck inside with no way out
I need a wise and intelligent stream of words
Pouring from my fingertips
But everything I write sounds so blasé 
The words don’t even come close to meaning what they should
My talent must be failing in my ability to communicate

I know when I look at you my heart flutters
My hands shake and my insides tremble
I get lost in your eyes and never want to find my way out
I would give up everything to feel your arms around me
To feel the safeness and stability that you bring to me
Spend forever locked in this friendship we have created
Relish in the warmth and shelter you encompass me with
Stay always in the tenderness and gentleness that you surround me with

But even these few verses don’t convey what I want
They lack the sparkle and flair that you deserve
They don’t mention what I think of you
And how much I respect and admire everything you do
Or how I think that sometimes my love for you will kill me
I don’t know how to tell you everything that I want to
I pray that someday I will find the words that you deserve
And until then all I can say is…I love you always and forever
