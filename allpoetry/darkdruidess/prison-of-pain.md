---
title: "Prison of Pain"
writer: "DarkDruidess"
slug: "prison-of-pain"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/54356-Prison-of-Pain-by-DarkDruidess"

Darkness has fallen upon my soul in droves 
All my energy has taken flight and left me comatose
Lying amidst shattered dreams and one broken heart
Breathless I try to pull the heavy air into my lungs
While the bitter tears fall unheeded by anyone
Moaning with the intense pain caused by this loneliness
Wishing for some noise to take away the cacophony of silence
Wondering how I will make it through another night
Knowing somehow that the ache will only get worse
My hands reach for the ghost of you that got left behind
Grasping at the memories of happier times
Teasingly they dance just out of reach, slipping away
Leaving behind only more suffering and distress
Pictures of you smile from their place on the wall
Causing the knife to twist deeper into my insides
Falling to my knees I pray from some sort of solace
Begging for a release from this prison of pain
That caged me the day you walked out the door
