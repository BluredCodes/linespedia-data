I hear the desperate cries of an Angel
That tears at my heart and soul
I close my eyes and picture his beauty
Yearning to somehow span the distance
And surround him with comfort
Smooth his exquisite wings with a soft touch
Brush the hair back from his brow
Bestow his handsome face with faint kisses
Engulf him with the utmost tenderness
Whisper gentle words of serenity
Allow him the release of his tears
While I compassionately hold him close
Freeing him to unburden his soul
Let loose with all his sorrow and pain
Permitting the peace and tranquility 
To once again become his 
I pray that this ethereal creature
Hears this impassioned plea that I wish
With all my heart and soul
So close your eyes Angel
And let my voice wash over you
Bringing you the love that I feel inside
And letting you know how much I careThis is for my Angel that has once helped me and now I wish to help him in any way possible. Brian, I only want the best for you in everything you do. Know that there is a person far away that cares very much for you. Love, Tami
