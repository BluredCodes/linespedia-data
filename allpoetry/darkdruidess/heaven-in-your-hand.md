Gravity pushes me down
Love pulls me up
The earth circumnavigates the sun
The moon is in your eyes
Major scale miniaturized
In the palm of my hand
Drums pound and steel wails
Notes float above me
Crashing into the fan
Bodies in negativity 
Move with positive flow
Rivers overflow crying tears
Oceans of sadness here
Happiness is found
On the littered floor
Joy hides behind lying eyes
Despair caresses her spine
I squint into the light
Of your smile, frowning
Glitter adorns flesh
Cutting like paper
Spinning voices scream
While the room stands still
Hell skulks closer 
Heaven waits in your hand
