---
title: "Please Stay"
writer: "DarkDruidess"

What can I say to make you stay?
Words slip and fall through my lips
How is it possible that you could make me feel this way?
My heart tells me to scream with my worship
To stop you from walking away
Make you see the adoration in my eyes
My feelings and emotions I can’t convey
Ready to shed my fragile guise
And show you what’s inside
All these thoughts and desires
But to reveal myself I’m petrified
What if it all backfires?
I want the courage to show you
All that is in my heart and soul
My respect and devotion too
Tell you how you make me whole
But I see you turning to leave
And a tear starts to stray
My heart is begging please
What can I say to make you stay?
