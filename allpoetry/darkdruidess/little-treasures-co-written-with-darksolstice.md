Love, Peace, Joy, and Hope
                       Passion, Bliss, Carnal Pleasure

Seeking, My soul longs to find this treasure

Wisps of smoke, Intangible things
                   That I aspire for, yet they remain distant

Elusive riches...buried somewhere in time
                      Beneath crumbled bridges and burnt ashes

Stumbling down the darkened alleys of melancholy way
	Exhausted, struggling just to live one more day

Despair has become my traveling partner

Whispering his evil thoughts inside my head
               Making me doubt my own sanity

Falling, Falling back into the pit of anguish
		Dragging me down as people standby and stare

		Looking into their eyes, I wonder
			If they really fucking care

Some say I'm going crazy
                     
                      Little do they know, I'm already there

Searching for life’s little treasure’s amidst the rubble
