title: "My Paradise"

A tropical wind blows ever so softly
Stirring up the jasmine in the air
The starlight speckles the sand
Creating prisms of light
That bounce back from your eyes
The moon reflected in the waves
Of the calm and tranquil ocean
Bathing your body in an aura
That warms my heart
And touches my soul
I am afraid to reach out and hold you
In all your splendor and glory 
Scared of shattering the illusion
That I have before me
My fingers tremble as they caress 
Your exquisite and handsome face
My breathing stops as you smile
Making my heart beat faster
Feeling your heat I shiver
Drawn enigmatically closer to you
I lay my head on your shoulder
Reveling in the sensations induced 
By the proximity of your brilliance
Knowing as I hold you
On this serene beach 
I have found my paradise
