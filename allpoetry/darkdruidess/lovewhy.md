---
title: "Love...Why?"
writer: "DarkDruidess"

Love can be a blissful state
Full of laughter and happiness
But it flies away at one wrong word
Hearts get broken in an instant
Dreams get shattered, hopes turn to dust
You get left standing in the midst of rubble
Of your once joyful and perfect love
While the darkness starts creeping in
Whispering the insane thoughts and deeds
That only the dark can conjure up
While you shake your head in denial
It slowly slides its way up your spine
Leaving a cold and slimy trail
You look for your voice to scream
But it grabs you by the throat and squeezes
Till you can’t draw a single breath
Hissing in your ear evil things
That are better left unsaid and unspoken
Droning into your brain causing panic
That makes you want to run far away
Screaming and tearing your hair out
Wondering why you ever loved at all
