as i look
into your eyes 
you showed me
how to love unselfishly

This is how I felt 
for you.
I know you have passed on
Have i?
Or am I here?
Did you leave because of me?
Or was it time to go.

Do you miss me?
Is this right?
Tell me!
As time goes on and on,
I still love you 
more and more.

I dream day to day,
that you loved me.
This is how I say Good-Bye!
