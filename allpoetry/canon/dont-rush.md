he treated her like a queen, he did what he could.
He never thought she'd leave him. no one thought she would.
Just a girl with a guy seemingly happy and smiling.
he had no idea on the weekends she was cheatin and whilin.
clubbin and lyin, she didnt appreciate everything he was tryin.
pretend complying, fake laughin and cryin, if he knew he'd be dyin
she waited for a good day for goodbyin.

She never thought he knew, he never thought he did.
he finally searched for the things she might of hid,
found not only was she cheating, she also had a kid,
not only was she a mother but she sexed for a bid.
he got tested and he contracted a demon he'd never rid.
cheating was one thing, but lying he'd forbid.

they were the perfect couple in a perfect circumstance.
went so far that she let him and he got into her pants.
She wanted money he wanted love, they got together and took a chance.
started so simple with a dinner, movie and then a slow dance.
slowly moved forward, they saw eachother more and began to hold hands.
he never cheated on a woman, but she seemed to have always cheated on her man.
they were together for so long, she did everything she shouldnt, he did the best he can.
