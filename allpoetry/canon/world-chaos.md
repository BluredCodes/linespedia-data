Look at our country, tell me what you see.
You sure as hell dont see happiness in you and me.
maybe in you but not in me.
look at our schools, most metal detected, cus of a kid
that was rejected, spit on, pushed and never respected.
His life was hectic, got some money and spent it..
bought a gun and let it, fire on the kids in school,
the kids that flaunt jewels, big house, nice cars and pools,
he saw the real them and they were fools, these weapons were his tools.

Now look where we are,
bombs on our chests, houses and even in our car.
columbine started something that no one expected,
something that wont be tolerated nor accepted.
we had something goin good for us, the people wrecked it.
No one did nothing to stop it they just let it.
I have concerns for life so dont say i never said it.

We blame it on our movies, we blame it on the beats.
dont knock what you dont know till you experience it on the streets.
lifes full of hustlers and cheats.
Look at those people that brought down our WTC
so I dont know what you find in your world but you dont find happiness in me.
