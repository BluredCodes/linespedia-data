I spoke to god once, He never spoke back.
Now I dont belive in him,
will I go to hell cus I wrote that,
I dont hold back, young and old rap.
My soul?.. I sold that, nothin but a cold rat in an old hat,
were you stand, I sat, everything he isnt, im that.

Satan, I never spoke to him,
I wanted to hear from him, so I wrote to him.
He never wrote back, but he spoke back.
told me to beat myself with a coat rack.
voodoo the jewish goats back,
wait, maybe it was the psycho that spoke that,oh crap.

Now who is gonna criticise me for what I believe,
dont write to me, just leave, you can believe what you need,
I dont believe in god but ill still do a good deed.
me and you, brothers indeed, who cares what we believe,
its all the same....the blood we bleed, you and me, can be who we be.
we are all the same, even the color of skin you see..
Brothas we all bleed the same blood...forget about beliefs and beefs just show some love.....
