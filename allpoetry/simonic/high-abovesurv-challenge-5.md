I rest high above the dirtied ground,
Free from the mire of this dark earth
Able to sway gently within the unseen winds

My life, is the life of the earth, and I
Am that which gives the world life, as
They gasp me in, ignorant to my existence.

From season to season, I reside upon a wooden
Vein, a stick of life that provides me with
Both food and drink, nutrition to the nutrients.

And as I sway high above the dirtied lands, I hear
A growling sound, and see in the distance a large
Yellow thing, plow into my sacred lands.

Screaming I ask them to stop, my hosts veins
Crying in pain, until finally that yellow creature
Approached my life giver, and began to rip.

I ached inside, as I felt the strong and powerful
Steel dig into my giver of life, and in defiance
It groaned, until finally giving up his deep roots.

Slowly, my world darkened, as the warm day’s air
Passed through my face as I saw the dirtied ground
Approach nearer, until finally I rest amid the muck.

I now lie, a dying leaf amid the industrial waste of
A once proud forest, now a industrial plant, but my
Death will not be in vain, for my life isn’t over.

For as I begin to seep into the ground, the nutrients
Which once bade me life, are now drunken in by the
Once dirtied soil, now flourishing with green grass.'tis a leaf....yeah.... :\
