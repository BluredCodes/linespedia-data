---
title: "Wailing Soundlessly"
writer: "Simonic"

When I was able to, push
Her out of my mind, things
Within collapsed and failed.
People, no longer cared for.

My blood, has began to chill,
While pumping within the hearts
Echoing rhythm. Frigid storms,
Wailing through veins, soundlessly.

I hear of other's desires, to hold my
Soul within theirs, to calm, or even
Appease the raging storms. Yet, I could
Turn them away, without thought or care.

For so long, they told me to forget
About her, to leave her within my
Past. Now, I look where it’s taken
Me, here, to this numbing existence.

I am a soul, thrown into the storms
Of the nihilist, and the blood within
Desires not another, though I once
Believed it would help my survival.

So, I’m here now, within this life
Left only to pine away, within these
Short days. Alone, all because I pushed
Her away, following their experiences.﻿...damn the instructions of those who ‘have been there’.
