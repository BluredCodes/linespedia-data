These are the Gods within my world I've created.  I have some stories written, but also some poems taken place in this world.  Mainly the ones w/ strange titles..."Kin Mar Simonic", and the tales of immortality and such.  Look at my authors page and you'll be able to tell which.  Anyways, this is just some info on ppl who have asked about some of the gods and such.


Greater Gods:
	
Braum - “Father Time” - Creator
     Ruler of all the lands.  Father of the gods, and ultimately the Will of all.

Serith - “Mother Nature”
     Ruler of the plants, and all things that can grow within the soil.

Rann - “The Great Wind”
     Ruler of the powerful winds.

Sagrim - “Death”
     The untouched god.  Not even Braum can touch him, he is the ruler of death, and the ruler of Man’oin, the City of Souls.

Iklan - “Father All”
     The father of the races upon the lands.  Keeper of love and relationship.  

Marzog - “War”
     Herald of War, and keeper of the Battle Cry.  He is the greatest fighter among the gods.

Lylirn - Lord of Magic - “Spell Lord” - “Hated of the gods”
     Ruler of all things magical within the realms.  His power is great,but always in check by the Will.  Thus, he’s often kept out of immortal meetings leaving him to find out in other ways...

Koi’on - Fate
     The ever seeing.  Simply, he sees things that many cannot.





﻿Gods:

Teslor - God of Hate - “Death’s Hand”
     Tempter of evil, and hate.  Allies with Sagrim, often seen as Death’s Hand.
﻿     - The Mantle of the Gods was placed upon him after he beguiled the elves to turn against the dwarves, introducing magic to the realms, Lylirn being the first to speak a spell.  Those elves who served him were cursed and labeled the Cursed of Teslor, the Aku’rus.

Penlee - Goddess of the Seas - “The Sea”
     Ruler of the seas, and all the creatures within.  She created many of the waters creatures without Iklan’s order.  

Athan - “Father Justice”
     The face of order and peace.  He is seen as the wisest of all the immortals, for his logic and reason surpasses all(some say even Braum's).

Ela - “Mother of All”
     She is the keeper of pleasure, sexuality, and fertility.  In marriage with Iklan.

Cilanik - “Father Fire” 
     Ruler of fire.  Keeper of the Passing Flame(day).  Killed by Ictar, when the great wizard was on his crusades for the realms.

Patro - “Forger of Crafts”
     He is the keeper and guide of crafts.  Mainly black smithing, carpentry and masonry.  He built many of the immortal’s palaces within their planes.

Lodrick - “Stone’s Heart”
     The ruler of the many mountain ranges within the lands.  He also is labeled as a great warrior among the gods, wielding armor made of stone and a hammer that can crush mountains.  His power and strength is never overlooked.

Bela - “Passing’s Watcher” - "Mother Fire"
     Daughter of Cilanik.  Watcher of the Passing Flame.  Known for her keen eye sight.  Once Ictar defeated Cilanik she rose to her father's former position.  She now holds all that Cilanik had possessed, though is still referred to as the Passing's Watcher, and still commended for her keen sight. - Was a Lesser God till her father's death.




﻿Lesser Gods:

Basi - “The Drink”
     Son of Penlee, watcher of the vital fluid mortals need to sustain life.  Water.

Aoi - “Lord of Lands”
     Son of Serith, he keeps the lands in order during his mother’s absence.

Artison - “Blade’s Blood”
     Watcher of War.  His origin is only known to Braum, but his strength and power amazes the gods and fear his wrath.  He is often quick to anger and shows no mercy once his wrath is breeched.

Ul - The firstborn among the “Sister’s of laughter”.
     Keeper of jests and jokes.

Ula - The second born among the “Sister’s of laughter”.
     Keeper of laughs.

Ule - The third born among the “Sister’s of Laughter”
     Keeper of happiness.

Qillo - “Luck”
     The keeper of luck, things that supposedly happen without Braum’s knowledge. - Often confused with Koi'on.

Macy - “Goddess of Magic”
     A step behind Lylrin, but she’s more sexually oriented, giving up her powerful position.





﻿Children of the Gods:

Kantrik - Son of Marzog and Manah.
Simonic - Soul stolen by Lylirn from Man’oin and “reborn”.
Ictar - Created by Lylirn, under Braum’s agreement.

Sekerv - Son of Qillo and Tison.
Guil’shan - Son of Penlee and Jharad.
