I saw
You standing there
While looking for truths.
Never getting what you wanted.
Never.

When you
Opened your eyes,
I heard you gasp out loud
Reaping in all the sights ahead.
Crying.

Did you
Hear me crying.
Staring at you shaking,
Realizing all the things you knew
Were false.

I am
Right here for you
When you need to be held.
When it appears to suffocate,
