Would you hold it against
Me, if I watched you die, not
Helping you or seeking aid?

Would you hold it against
Me, if I kicked your hot blood
Far away from where I stood?

Would you hate me if I were
To laugh, watching you choke
While you writhed on the ground?

Would you hate me if I were
To laugh, while I threw the knife
Away, removing my gloves?

Don’t hate me love. Your death was
Only in the mind, I will smile at you
Lovingly upon our next meeting.Guess it's poems like these that make people go and make 2nd accounts...
