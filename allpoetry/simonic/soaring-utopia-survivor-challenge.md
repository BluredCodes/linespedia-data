I stand at the edge of a disastrous life, wanting to
Drain my soul, fading from existence, surrendering
My once treasured life, now just a spew of chaotic
Thoughts, shifting within my head, exerting itself
Into my failing life, whirling violently amid 
This shadow, liquefying dreams, dripping into the
Vat of society, conforming all the lives
Of humanity, feeding upon the blissful dreams of
Life.  How can I, once so happy, find myself now
So dreadfully depressed, wishing for salvation, so 
That I might, die within peaceful arms, sending
My broken soul, into another realm, leaving this
Pitiful existence forever, gliding over ethereal planes my
Soul soars with inexplicable joy, and I wish that you
May one day feel this joy, soaring above all, able to 
Go where life had always denied entrance, back
On earth, grueling pains, fruitless efforts, 
And a tormented existence, always wishing to 
Rise within an eternal heaven, and
To fly high above it all, know that
It’s just not a mortal dream, soaring within this
Eternal hall, breathing in the crisp cool air of this
Utopia...finding it to be no where...Yeah...well...I think this works :\
