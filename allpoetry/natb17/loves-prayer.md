Now as I lay down to sleep
I pray you lord that you may keep
This love of mine so safe and warm
Away from pain
Away from harm
Should my day come to pass on
Should my time be done and gone
Hold my love entwined by grace
Rid his pain by love's embrace
Keep him safe and sound and whole
Sleeping in your palm of gold
For I'll love him through and through
Even in my life a new..
Amen.I wrote this because of hope. I will continue to recite it as the world turns..
