---
title: "...Over You"
writer: "natb17"

It's not enough for me to see you once again
cause now I'm needing something more..
Maybe I'm selfish
to blame for all I've painted
Maybe I can't let this go...
But you've got a way when you make me lose my head
now I'm stuck here bettin' high...
cause you were the one 
placing me above it all
laying it out
in the stillness of the weeping he says:
hold me down
let me be the one to tuck you in tonight
just, 
hold me down
got me in too deep 
when I'm losing sleep 
over you...
Something has lingered in the way we carry on
holding dear what went before
Now and again
when I think of what it seems
love is subsiding,
in the episodes of laughter he says:
hold me down
let me be the one to tuck you in tonight
just,
hold me down
got me in too deep
when I'm losing sleep 
over you...
and if the sun came crashing down
and I had nothing left to give
would he still save me?
would he break the ocean waves
that kept me stranded far from him?
Late in the twilight with the abscence of your face
I'm still needing something more...
I wanna tell you
If you'd let me take my place
right beside you,
in the multitude of grace
I say:
hold me down
let me be the one to tuck you in tonight
just,
hold me down
got me in too deep 
when I'm losing sleep
Over you...
love is subsiding....
