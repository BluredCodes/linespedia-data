title: "Pretend."
writer: "arosebyanyothername"

You’re not worth it.
I refuse to hold on.
I wont take this any longer.
You are beneath me.
You don’t deserve my love.
I gave you so much 
But didn’t take enough!
You never offered yourself to me.
Never even let me inside.
I showed you my soul.
Revealed to you my deepest thoughts.
You laugh and you mock me.
How dare you!
Who do you think you are?
I wont stand by and watch this.
I refuse to let you win.
You think I wont react.
You think I’m so desperate.
Never will I yearn for your love.
You showed affection, pretended that it’s more.
I know the truth, you don’t even CARE.
You think you can use me.
That I will come back again.
I know this isn’t love.
I don’t know who you are.
But I know that I don’t need you anymore.Eh...whatever.
