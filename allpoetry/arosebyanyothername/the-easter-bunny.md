The Easter Bunny didn’t come for you today.
Although, he told me what he wanted to say.
He said that although he didn’t bring you any candy.
There’s something there, that you didn’t see.

He brought you the true meaning of Easter.
Although, to some it might seem to have less luster.
For it isn’t something you can touch.
For it is something more, much.

More then you can even realize now.
For candy rots out your teeth, causing pain. OW!
However, the true gift of Easter is 
So much more then this.

The gift that you received is the Resurrection.
This causes an infection.
Deep within your heart,
But not of bad things, but of something you will never want to part.

The love of God is so powerful.
An intense feeling that is so wonderful.
It means so much more then chocolate or jellybeans.
It is better than any of your dreams.

So do not fret.
And do not be upset.
That the Easter bunny did not come to you.
For God loves you, and that will always be true!
