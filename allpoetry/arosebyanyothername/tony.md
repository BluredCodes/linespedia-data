title: "Tony."
writer: "arosebyanyothername"

Thank you for opening your heart to me.
Your heart is such a sight to see.
You gave me the key.
And it’s touched me so deeply.

You have an incredible heart.
Although I always knew you were smart.
I never noticed the art.
That set you apart.

You are unlike everyone I know.
Seeds of inspiration, inside me you sow.
I love your mind and it’s flow.
In your oceans of intellect, I could forever row.

I could be with you forever, and never get enough.
You give meaning to all things, that I had thought of as simple ‘stuff”.
Although on the outside you seem to be tough.
I know that there is truly a diamond beyond the rough.

I always thought I knew you.
But now it’s all been proven untrue.
Because you are wonderful too.
Not realizing this sooner, I do rue.

You are beautiful inside.
In life, I’d let you be my guide.
In you I know I can confide.
I wish you’ll always be by my side.

For you are a light.
And I wish to never fight.
Even when you’re mad, you’re an amazing sight.
To forever love you, I just might.

Although I won’t leave you ever.
Our friendship you just might sever.
And I know I’ll love you forever.
And to hate you, I know I could never.

Know that you will always have me as a friend.
Even with time, I friendship will never end.
My ears, heart, and shoulder I’ll always lend.
For the sorrows that I know, you always seem to mend.

But you are leaving me.
Maybe it’s something meant to be.
I want you to be near.
But never seeing you again, I fear.
