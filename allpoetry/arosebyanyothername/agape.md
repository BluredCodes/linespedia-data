Loving you
Is something I’m not used to.
Trying to understand,
I’m doing everything I can,
Thinking of you
No matter what I do.
I have no other care,
As long as you are there.
I know you listen to me.
Your heart, I begin to see.
I feel as if I have true love,
Something so wonderful, it must be from up above.
I know it will last until the end of time
Throughout my lifetime, I never thought I’d find
A love so pure and real.
For I know, never again, loneliness, will I feel.
