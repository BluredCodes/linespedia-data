---
title: "Wrong."
writer: "arosebyanyothername"

Confusion fills my head,
No empty space left,
All full of worries,
I don’t understand,
I can’t comprehend,
Why doesn’t anything make sense?
My life is a mess,
It’s all in disarray.
What did you say?
Don’t worry about it, HA!
How can I continue,
Through this maze,
With no light to guide me,
How can I cope,
With no sense of hope.
Everyone is pushing me down,
I can’t overcome,
I can’t go on any longer.
I can’t find my way through the clutter.
I can no longer speak.
Few words pass my lips barley a mutter.
Too much confusion,
It’s unfair and unjust.
It’s wrong.
I have no use for this life any more.Just random thoughts.
