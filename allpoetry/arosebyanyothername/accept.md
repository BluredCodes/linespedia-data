I watch as the rain
Falls down from the sky,
Filling up my window pane.
I close my eyes and am taken for a ride.

My world is spinning 
Round and round it goes.
Will it ever stop circulating?
No one really knows.

For life is a mystery 
That no one has ever solved.
We have all faced our own misery.
And problems that we can't resolve.

We have to accept the truths in our lives.
We can't hide from the reality.
We must realize
We lie to ourselves, to keep our sanity.

We have no choice but to relinquish
The power we never had to begin with.
This life is not controled by ourselves.
We look for the answers in garbage cans.

But we refuse to see
What has been there all along.
You think its me.
But you are so wrong.

You can not blame 
your ignorance on anyone.
It's not the same.
You just can't run.

We have to see what's right in front of us.
We have to embrace our true entities.
Our own fantasy has become too ambiguous.
And here we will find the key.

This will answer our questions,and makes us completely free.
From who we thought we ought to be.
