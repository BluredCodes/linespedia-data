Shadows in the corners

Shadows in the corners
In the middle of the night
As I lie unable to sleep
Waiting to hear the screams
I feel a presence
A lost soul, dark eyes searching through the night
It’s broken, it’s torn in two
Calling out, but what I cant hear
The beating of its lonely heart
So fragile, so much pain
Crying, trying to find somewhere to lie and rest

Pamela Massey
2-4-2002
