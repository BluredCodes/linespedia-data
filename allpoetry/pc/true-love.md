True Love

All I ever wanted was to hate and despise you
But all I can do is love you
It’s been many hills to climb
I hate to see you looking sad
But I know you have every right to feel guilty
And now is the time to hold your head up high
We both know you have sinned
But I ask now can you stand by me and be my strength
Fight the memories with me
Are you willing
Can we walk into the light
Don’t let us die

Pamela Massey
