Inner Beauty

How could I ever walk away from your beauty
Your beating heart
Trembling, calling out my name
Screaming out for just one touch
yours eyes crystal clear sparkling like a star
The warmth of the world inside
Arms so strong so safe.
Asking for nothing but to hold me in them forever
My heart will cherish you forever.

Pamela Massey
