Suffering

All the sacrificing people put up with
All the bridges we have to cross
All the pain we have to live with 
Why all this hate and anger
Why not peace and Love

The devil is looking all the time
Giving people with good in them 
So much pain, So much hate
Making all their dreams broken
All their hopes shattered
All their desires unanswered

Pamela Massey
