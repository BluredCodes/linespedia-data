Who

Folks like you and I wish to cross them Bridges
Which way do we go through all the lonely times alone
All the happy times with a loved one
Who do you turn to in the painful times
When the ghosts from the past are haunting you
Who is your savour
Who can give you all your hopes and all your dreams back
Who can stop your Suffering
Give you all the love you need and treat you with kindness
Someone to say kind words to you
Someone who make’s you smile, makes you laugh
Someone who can put the sparkle back in your eyes
Who can fill your heart with love not hate
Someone who will cherish you with all there heart
Someone you are at peace with.

Pamela Massey
