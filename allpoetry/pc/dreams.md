Dreams

Why has my heart fallen for one
Why do I have to cross those bridges of broken dreams
Where dose my peace lie
When dose my happiness start
All my ghosts are haunting me
But you are not here to stop the pain
Who is this one 
Who holds my dreams.

Pamela Massey
1999
