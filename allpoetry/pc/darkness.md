Darkness

There is a dark place in my heart
Where I keep all the memories
Where I keep all this pain
Where my loneliness lies
I call out to the one who holds my heart
I ask, I plead for you to take this place away
To put my pain at peace
To cover my memories with a shadow
So I never have to see them again
I call out to you to unlock my heart

Pamela Massey
