Leanna

Gracefully she walks, head held high
black hair shining, tousled by the breeze
Quiet dignity for so young a lass as she
as she waltzes through life with ease

Dark eyes dancing, slender body swaying
she’s like a young woman, yet still a child
Her smile brings sunshine to warm us all
this lovely girl, so free so wild

Her laughter fills our hearts with pleasure
the sound a measure of her glee
She glows with the light of a heart reborn
of love and happiness for all to see

In another moment she may be subdued
I wonder what’s on her mind
What can she be thinking, this young girl
perhaps of  days and friends left behind

But soon she’ll be up and running again
or tying up the phone for hours
My sweet granddaughter Leanna
a child, one of God’s beautiful flowersJanuary 6 2001, Just a little poem I wrote for my grandaughter shortly before her 13th birthday.
