You give soo much, but you don't give enough
For me, the one who cares, cause I can't feel for nobody else.

Disapoint, and confuse, are just a few of what i feel;
stop it soon, or i may abandon you.

You work, you are so nice, but you keep it in the back,
Hey jerk, work in public cause I don't like standing in my mack.

In the back of the road, waitin for the door to open,
so i can sneak in, and see what you've maken.

So don't feed me, your seens and your maybes,
but feed me, your now, your upcoming, and your unfinished.

or just make me happy.This is my tribute to how Marilyn Manson is making me feel that his website is soo reluctant to continue adding updates to the site!
i want pictures.
