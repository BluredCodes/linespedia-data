Let's jump off this bridge together,
Maybe we wont wake up this.
Shall we take this trip as a group,
how long can u make me wait here?

Desire, all you seek
Let's fuck desire and be us.

Follow the line if you want, or lets skip the line,
let's jump to the same place,
let's get there before them, but lets do something different.

Don't be a comformist man,
be a jumper, like me.
Let's not join the club.who knows?
