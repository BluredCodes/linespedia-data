I have always wanted to be a person of interest
a writer perhaps
like Poe, like Twain, any one other than myself.

I worked so hard for the words
the opportunity
the art...
nothing.

I have always wanted to be a writer
maybe a singer
like Billy Joel, like Elton, like any one other than myself.

I worked so hard for the voice
the magic
the art
it never came.

I am a writer and singer today
in my heart
in my soul, like no-one, for no-one, but me.Nothing special, just jotting down a few thoughts.
