Once upon a time there was a girl...

Holding me closely she'd whisper
the things that completed our souls
Those little dreams shared in the shadows
The places we wanted to go

Conversations of spain by the fire
o'er candle light scouring the globe
the couch that we shared so secure
on adventures to places untold.

So where is my coveted dream girl
with those eyes that shadowed the night
please take me away with your softness
if only a short little flight.

I miss you.Just missing someone
