As they prey, (yes prey) the world seems to converge.
I stand outside their doors, observing their unity.

Innocents?

Bastards!

Harbingers of sanctities untold.
Scrutiny for the masses 
Reprieve for the few
Scrutinizing me
Damning you.

While all the while 
the standards bend
for these most righteous
Holy men.

Brilliant illusions, 
adherence sought.
Lifetimes squandered
all for naught.


Why must there be only one way?

Leave me be.I know it doesn't do well for meter.
A dim light shone on organized religion?  Don't be hasty.
Zealot comments welcome (I dare you...)

Ever look up the definition of reprieve?
