title: "shrink wrapped"
writer: "blurred vision"

Covered in the arts of this century,
trying to grasp the inspiration of others
sliding through galleries of paint, she allows herself
to become the piece
To me she's a piece
a piece that is not up for auction or display but
wrapped up for hybernation
or maybe she's going to sleep

I hope so.
