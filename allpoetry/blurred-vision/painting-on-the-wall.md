People sit and look at it
that painting on the wall
they whisper "what the fuck is that"
"No I did not come from a vaccum pack"
Ya thats why I'm distorted like that
demented thoughts that can't be bought
oh woo
no hoo
It might be something I can't control
call the curator
make sure she's got the thought eliminator
It looks like he'll cause a hubufull
just stick me in a cell
no disturbances any more
I guess it's going to be a whore
but hey, it's better than society
trying to teach you domonos
if you believe it
why don't you be it
if you don't believe yourself
tie a car bomb to yourself
and think of a better place
I'm someone you cant facilitate
I'M THE PAINTING ON THE WALL
