The Star Princess

      Eternity cradles stars in twinkles of time
      laden within wings of sweet angelic chime.
      Wispy flutters float from a castle cloud
      a bluebird skips over skies amply endowed.
      Enchanted cherubim gather from all around
      to give breath to the heart of musical sound.

      A melody, the first glimmer in a stream,
      is sunshine~kissed by a blushing starbeam.
      A babe, wrapped in a gold pennance of light,
      cries the song of day transcribed by starnight.
      A princess birthed in the essence of love,
      handsung by choirs and choirs of stars above.I wrote this for my 14 year old daughter. ~:)
