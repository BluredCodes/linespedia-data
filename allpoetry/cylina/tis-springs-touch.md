Tis Spring's Touch


               Fledging feathers adorn each word     
               upon my arrow lifted high.                         
               Wind empowers the wings of birds             
               to milk April dreams from mother sky.          
               Dawning shadows alight the herds,               
               my tested bow closes winter’s eye.         


               Bye~bye wooly winter sky so shy 

                                    
              Clutching time’s waist in spring’s embrace      
              I free my newborn butterfly.   
              The furious sun melts earth’s face, 
              a virgin heat my heart can't deny.
              Flowing on love’s chantilly lace,                       
              wind wipes the tears I need to cry.                     
                    

              Bye~bye wooly winter sky so shy  


              I heard a song whispering wind
              like petals twirling in a sigh.
              The cold season  left me chagrined,  
              it hung me up to slowly dry.
              The spirit of spring has not sinned
              nor it’s wild beauty can we buy.


              Bye~bye wooly winter sky so shyMy ode to spring ~:)
