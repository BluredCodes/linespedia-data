Wings of Desire



                       A songbird alarms lovers
                          from exploring rest,
                          when tender lashes 
                        brush a beating breast.
      
                        An arced~bell, chorded
                        by the wings of desire,
                         vanquishes new love
                          with a single wire.

                        Chitter~chatter drifts
                         through the receiver;
                      the caller couldn't believe
                       her bird would leave her.

                          The songbird sings 
                         the lost song of May,
                          she cheeps a melody
                           that seems to say:

                          "My beautiful bird, 
                         hope is on the phone.
                        My heartwing is broken,
                         must I suffer alone?

                           My lonely bird,
                       let us fly for awhile;
                      I can teach you to sing
                        with only one smile."

                         The wings of desire
                     lift memories as they lay;
                           mid~spring blows 
                         into a nest far away.

                        Time holds her breath, 
                          helplessly baited,
                          silence chokes her
                           as tolling faded.

                      Man alit to meet his mate,
                         like a winged hound.
                    The bell ceased its ringing,
                       it left lanquish resound~

                         from throbbing flesh;

                     seeking silk within her chest

                       Yes, the bluebird returned,
                        he found journey's fill.
                         Phantom winds whistle,
                     howling and haunting him still.

                          Two birds made a nest,
                      one more made a guest arrival.
                           A reward was granted
                           to winter's survival.

                        The flutter of two hearts
                        sang a songbird's desire;
                         a three feathered love
                           set two nests afire.
