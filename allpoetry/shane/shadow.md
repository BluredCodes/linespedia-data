---
title: "Shadow"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "shadow"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

In my shadow I see myself,
But he does not exist
I try to focus my attention elsewhere
But I cannot resist

Outlined perfection
Is a portrait of myself the saint
It is shaped and toned
A replica superior, to my very self

Duplicating in the sun
Frustrated as the moon comes out
It quickly runs away
Not afraid to be afraid, he’ll be back today

Daylight comes and so does it
Returning from it’s sleep
Unlike the body growing old
The shadow remains forever fit

Resurrected by the light
Now it’s on a bus
The same as me, yet differently
His locations are random and unfamiliar

Portrait of perfection
A sun cast reflection
Of all that life should be
My shadow, a better version of me
