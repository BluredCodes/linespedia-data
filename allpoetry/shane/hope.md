title: "Hope"
writer: "Shane"

When nothing is everything
And you’ve got something to say
Just cast your thoughts and words along the way

And after all is said and done
And there’s nothing left to say
Take a break for all your worth
Stand up make your presence felt

Just take time to say,
Give something to those who care
But as you stare upon their face
Please try to disguise your hate

When everything once wrong is right
It’s up to you to say
Every single word that’s in your way

Take the time to make some sense
Of the thoughts in your head
What the crystal ball can’t tell us all
Is our reason to be

When I say that right is wrong
It’s wrong to say I’m right
I’m just seeing feel the love
And spread it to those in sight

Sometimes life makes little sense
As your friends are cast away
But as you land upon gods shore
Tell him not to kill no more

I just want to take the hand
Of everyone aware
That meaning is just meaningless
If the meaning isn’t care

Don’t go thinking I’m not wrong
‘Cos who am I to say
The words of god may fill my mind but say…
Give all you have to give and then live

One more time around,
Spread something good
Throughout your home town
Bring your friends don’t drag them down

Life is here to stay
And answers in the oceans heart
Think it’s safe to say
That we will live to see a better day
