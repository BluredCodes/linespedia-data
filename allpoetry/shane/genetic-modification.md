Out with the old, In with the new
Just do whatever they tell you to do
Replacing me with a newer you
Forgetting justice, ignoring truth

Defaced by cloning and changed DNA
You are complete and god made you that way
Existence monitored by chips in your head
One step further and we’ll all be dead

Modify nature as she’s not suffice
Nuclear holocaust and mutation the price
Losing all interest in the way things should be
What you believe is just not right you see

Granted an outcast ‘cos you tried to speak
Prevention is futile, the future looks bleak
You say you won’t be here, but your children will be
Seeing the things that you won’t let them see

Out with the new and old, in with the modified
Ideas of rebellion, all thoughts vaporised
Imprisoned for murder because of what you said
In a world un-natural I’d rather be dead
