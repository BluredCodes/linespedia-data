Things that are difficult, Often don’t make sense to me
Here she comes the love of my life, but she doesn’t know that’s me
She said “love that is beautiful, it’s a hard thing to see”
So I said “It’s even harder to find someone to be… with”

She said the food was good, I said she was good too
But just leave the food alone, ’cos unlike the food girl
I want to take you home

I said I can’t do anything, but I’ll do what I’ve go to do
To spend the rest of the days of my life, to spend them all on you
I know money don’t mean nothing, but I’ll spend all mine on you

The door bell rang and I said to myself “Well you just who it ain’t”
But strike me down with cupids arrow, there she was at my door
Well I was confused but don’t get me wrong, I had no complaints
She said “I loved dinner, now baby I want some more”

Well the rest of the story is history, a beautiful thing you just can’t see
So with the curtains closed and the clothes on the floor
I just can’t tell you anymore
But life is good for the rest of my days, with my woman beside me
