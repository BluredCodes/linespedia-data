I live for Neil Young
In the past he’s the man
I live for my time
And I live for Pearl Jam

Cobain, is finally free
I thank you for the sounds
That you’ve given to me
Nevermind, is life to me

Eddie Vedder, is an icon to me
Dave Mustaine is a father to me
Tells me how I have to be free
And they all know, that I’m feeling life right now

Metallica, Pantera, Soundgarden,
Get me through my life
As it hardens
Music saves my life

I tip my hat to those
Who worship Axl Rose
They from the music
And they dance so sweetly

We all live from they’re music
And we live completely"This is a work in progress, I think we all owe a lot to music, whether we chose to except that fact is down to us.
For me, Music is my soundtrack for life, it takes me where I go and keeps me company while I'm there" - Danw
