Love me, love me and myself or don’t love me at all
Innocence coated with lies
I’ll stare into the sun just to see who blinks first

We’re safe tonight, your safe in my arms
If I love you I won’t be denied
If I don’t then watch my scorched eyes cry
As alone my soul will dieAnother interlude
