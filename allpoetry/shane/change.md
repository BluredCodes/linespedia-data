---
title: "Change"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "change"

---

I know the words to everyone’s song
Who isn’t me
I shed my skin to change
I lost my friends, but they’re the same

I can’t deal with change

I thought I’d paint my insides
A midnight passion blue
So the sky reflects the feeling that’s me
And what I’m going through

Only a man can he change

Loosened my grip on you
So I could feel myself again
My words are now true
So tell me as I try again, tell this reborn man

Change in all of us is inevitable

Follow me into the centre of the sun
We’ll drown ourselves in alcohol
Not even that pain can stop our fun
I’ll change this time

Time defines the time we change in

Shot in my foot today
I need to take precautions
If I’m to change this way
And I know, what I know is not enough

Change me girl, God change me

I turned my back on me
I have stepped out of the shade
I’m burnt now but I don’t care
A changed man I am free
