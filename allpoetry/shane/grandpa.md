---
title: "Grandpa"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "grandpa"
source: "AllPoetry"

A wrinkled experienced hand,
A genuine plastic smile
Let me take you by your hand
And we’ll sit for a little while

Though life wears you out
Your loved throughout
Though life wears you out

An old man is watering
His eyes at the thought of him
Though they love that man to death
They sense his final breath

A beautiful head of hair
A silver main that shows he cares
He acts as is he’ll never die
He’ll never digest his pride

A slanted walk makes me cry
As I see gods pulling him
A touch of it from the sky
The old man just can’t win

A desperate hope to live on
To fix all the life he’s done wrong
But no matter what is said
I’ll still love you when your dead
