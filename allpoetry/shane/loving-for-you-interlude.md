title: "Loving For You (Interlude)"
writer: "Shane"

When I’m old I’ll leave this planet,
I’d like to leave sooner cos I just can’t stand it
But I still love my living time
Even though it kills me from time to time

This time I’ll say bye
But I won’t die, this time
This time is goodbye, but I won’t die

I will leave to see your smile
Running around my mind
A touch of beauty is your face
A cure for the blind

Lies will hurt us all everyday…
I don’t know what they said, but I love you
I can see you everyday
I know I still love you

We’ll live in the dark while the sun meets the moon
Don’t be afraid love, it’ll be back soon
So don’t forget what I said, that I’d rather be dead
Than be without you

There’s nothing more to say,
Except I love you
