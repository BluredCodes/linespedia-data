title: "Friendshit"
writer: "Shane"

My friends tell me
Cheer up slowly
I can’t tell them
How it makes me…

Sick

Repetition of everything
A waterfall
The waters sting
Moist water, wet
Wet water

If it gets to me it will get to you
Your getting to me, with the same old tune
Step inside and get into me
Get inside my mind

Frustrating as I hear them sing
Different fucking lyrics about the same old thing
Sure they change the words, they slow down the beat
I don’t give a damn, it’s a meal I don’t want to eat

They feed you bull shit
There’s no other kind
Friends are ignorant
And they leave you behind

And when they say
That’s it’s you who changed
Forget… about them all
You know deep down…
That they don’t care at all
