title: "Imagine"

Imagine that, the thief came back
He put all the money back

Imagine that, the whales hat
Is made from man skin

Imagine that, mysterious fruit
Money’s growing from a tree

Imagine that, we can’t fight back
While the animal loads his syringe

I get the feeling it’s aimed me *yikes*
Imagine that, a world that’s back, to front

Imagine that, A man on a leash
Walking with a canine

Imagine that, he falls flat on his back
Doesn’t land much like a feline, cat

How would you like it?
Imagine that!
