Well this is my plan
Of what I’m going to do
I’ll kill myself ,
To be better than you

You say you’ll hurt yourself
And do it because you just can’t face the facts
So you’ll try to damage your health
Just to get them back

Well I’ll take my gun
Point it at myself

Wait…
Before you do something
That you’ll just regret
Remember this one thing…
They ain’t got you yet

They only truly get you when your gone
It doesn’t mean I’m telling you
The truth is there’s no right or wrong
But reality is, that what you do is true

I will take my fist
Shake it like a man
In the direction of all my troubles
Now this is my plan
It’s all so much simpler now
Though the complications, they may have doubled

Two kids shot dead, one on a railroad track
The other lost his head on the way
Don’t let it be this way
There are better days, for your enemies
There are better ways to get them back
