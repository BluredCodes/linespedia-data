On a summers day
The sun shines like a gift from god
Providing warmth and love
On all of whom it shines

The morning sunlight like a new born life
Everything in existence stops to think
About the blessing that guides them
The wonder that brings sight to them

Never the horror of rain is on display
In the beautiful wonder of a summers day

The tree it bends towards the light
Speaking to the sun
Feeling it’s power and energy
Just wishing it was one

Though imitators try and try
Only the sun can light the sky
Never lost nor in the way
Sadness only comes, when summer goes away
