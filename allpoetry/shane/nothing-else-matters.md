Forever In debt to life itself
You realise life is a game
The more difficult things become
The more you are to blame

So free your mind
And free your soul
You’ll benefit in the end
There’s no use disguising your thoughts…

In this world it’s the truth
Or nothing at all
Existence delivered to us on a platter
Yet it is discarded like it is nothing

Life is the only thing to know
The only thing that there is
Love, soul, mind, hope
And nothing else matters
