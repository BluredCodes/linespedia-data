title: "Count Me In (Interlude)"

I’m for justice, I’m for everything I keep
I’m for painless, I’m for dying in my sleep

I don’t love you, but I love the things you say
I knew you’d find me, Thought you’d show me the way

I’m for being helped and doing things right
I just didn’t know I’d see the light
