---
title: "In Thought"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "in-thought"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59197-In-Thought-by-Shane"
---

In this world thoughts are free
In the sea thoughts will flow
They tumble down below
Thoughts of confined, in a mind forever free

Stuttering words are dead
Sense is gone
My thoughts are spent
Thoughts that think I’ve gone insane

In my eyes I can see
The innocence written in me
I see the reigns of my head
They’re the thoughts that keep me fed

My thoughts they keep me alive
I’m always here, as long as my thoughts survive
If they leave, I do too
Before I’m gone, I’ll be true
And pass my thoughts to you

Deep in thought, my onslaught
Of ideas hard to find
Help myself with my words
Thinking helps to pass the time

When I die in thoughts
I die forever, words left unspoken
A body lost that now is broken
Gift of life spent, memories of once free thought now bound in cement
