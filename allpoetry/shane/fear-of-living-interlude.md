title: "Fear of Living (Interlude)"

I’m not dead yet,
But I know I’ll be gone first
Lived through the heartbreak
I’ve lived throughout the worst

Beat on my ear,
With your complicated words
My lack of interest,
Is my intellectual curse

My fear of living
Is what drags me down
In this world of many waters
It’s reality in which I drown
