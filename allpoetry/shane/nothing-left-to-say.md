title: "Nothing Left To Say"

Trapped in thought, you’ve got nothing to say
Confined within your head, you have nothing to say
Thoughts unspoken, you’ve got nothing to say
Words are broken, when they don’t leave your head

Take your time to think, and don’t forget to say
Words just get in my way
Live in your head when you think
Live in your head as you feel, live, love, lust

Live in your head because
Your trapped in thought
Confined because
You have nothing to say

Confined, trapped, imprisoned
Nothing to say
Words unspoken, only within’
You have nothing to say

Chase your demons, make them go away
Within your head the demons rest and play 
As thoughts they eat your mind so you have…
Nothing to say!Prelude To… 'In Thought'
