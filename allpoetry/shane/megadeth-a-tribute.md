Without you I’m nothing
Taking you away from me
Was something I could not dream
An existence with out you, is nothing as it seems

With always I was never alone
You’d stay with me through living
Through the songs within my head
They can take our bodies, but the music is never dead

Goodbye is not the word
That you from I deserve
Something more fulfilled
For the life of mine you’ve filled

I’ll dedicate all of my being
To everything you said
I can live without you now
Though a part of me is dead

You brought hope
When all was lost
You guided me in winters frost
The summertime you sat with me

From the beginning to the end
It was bound to end
It was just a matter of when
From now to the grave, you’ll be with me then

The power of the music
Is something I cannot describe
To it there’s no equal
A verbal tribute denied

From 85 to 2002
The music spoke the words
Of thousands of the innocent
In it’s freedom they were birds

Though critically they’re shot down
They’ll kill us one by one
The truth we hold is Megadeth
The music is our gun

Battles with addiction, the poison in my vain
I beat the evil creatures, but they come back again
My family will not harm me, but they don’t feel the same
An endless drug in my brain, Only I feel the pain

Supporting me, trustfully
My fans they will my power
Criticised unjustly
I’ll never reach the top

My lies are the truth
Recognize the sound of youth?
Though I hear you hour after hour
