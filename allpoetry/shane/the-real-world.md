---
title: "The ‘Real’ World"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "the-real-world"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

You were a slave since the day you were born
You know life’s all been planned
The question’s how much can you take
Knowing that the air you breath and life you lead are fake

You can’t spell reality without a word of three
You can’t live a life of this world because life is not free
You were programmed to live and die
The word I speak is lie

God and Devil, good and evil everything’s the same
So for all your lack of fortunes the set up is to blame
Moral of the story harsh and true, but you know it’s all a lie
Destiny I set in stone, you can’t choose your fate so die
