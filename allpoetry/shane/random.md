---
title: "Random…"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "random"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/58743-Random--by-Shane"
---

I’m over here, your over there
But your still so far away
I’ve said this many times before
But what else can I say?

Don’t need a tribute ‘cos I’m not dead yet
Don’t need to hear a single thing
That you have to say
