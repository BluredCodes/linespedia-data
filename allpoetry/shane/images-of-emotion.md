I love looking at you, but I can’t bare to see myself
Your beautiful sunlight smile, my crooked teeth my ugly self
Your gentle lips, my aged withered hands
The beauty of your youth, this wrinkled sad old man

Your perfect hands you silky skin, My blistered hands my wrinkled skin
Your life filled eyes they draw me, my stone dead glare it scares you off
So I love you, so you hate me
I just want you to know that I exist, and how I feel I clench my fist

The hair upon your youth full head is a river of gold,
The hair upon my head is lost and dead, I’m old
