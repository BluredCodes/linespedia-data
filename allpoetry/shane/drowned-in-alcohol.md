---
title: "Drowned In Alcohol"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "drowned-in-alcohol"
source: "AllPoetry"

Close your eyes
Open your mouth
And drown in alcohol

Nothing matters when nothing makes sense
Drowned in alcohol

See yourself in perfect harmony
Wasting thoughts and killing memories

Rapidly feeling free, as your insides struggle to breathe
Hide your face, from you soul, you leave yourself ashamed
When you wake up in your head

Washed up in the morning tide,
Thoughts all buried beneath sands
As your waking up

Rembrandts of a memory
Remains of former glories
Washed away with the tide

Your life full, an empty glass
Is all that life will take
It’s all it takes
