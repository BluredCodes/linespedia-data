Yes I hold the secret,
To what I do not know
I refuse to reveal it
Why I do not know

No you can not see it
I don’t think that you’d believe it
I can’t tell you my secret
All I can do is tease itI'm sure you can all relate to this, just think of when a friend refuses to tell you something, we've all been there.
