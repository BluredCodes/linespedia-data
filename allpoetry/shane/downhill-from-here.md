Finally I am free, from what Imprisoned me
The state of mind, that I’ve left behind

I’ve been walking up a hill,
But now I’m coming down
I’m as happy as a dilusionary pill
I’ll spread it all around

I don’t know how or why
But I ran from happiness
And now it’s found me

Sitting scared, wishing fooled, I’m waiting
Just anticipating, the return of my depression
But no one here is evil, at least that’s my impression
In a world of beauty not even pointless evils have me hating

It’s all downhill now, but in a good way
And I’ll take it all, at my own pace
My  delightful feeling, I’ll say it all, until there’s none to say
Now I’m free and the world is mine to love, and my face

My face shows me a younger man
A man who knows this is his time
I won’t deny my reflection
Though it’s not entire perfection…

I take a little look and then…
Realise It’s mine now
Though the world may fall part again
I’ll still be happy somehow

Worlds and hearts may fall apart
But I’ll be mine
I’ll still be mine
Until it stops my heart
