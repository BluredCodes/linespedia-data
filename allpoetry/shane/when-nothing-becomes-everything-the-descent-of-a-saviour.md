---
title: "When Nothing Becomes Everything (The Descent of a Saviour)"
writer: "Shane"

Strolling beneath the sea
Are warriors of fortune
Together they quest for freedom
With Jesus himself at the helm

Travelling like a sound inaudible
Fighting for a cause so credible
Walking like ghosts in unison
They seek to bring peace to earth their realm

God cannot fail to deliver his message
With his only son at the helm
With the only one at the helm
A disciple of gods hand in our realm

Freedom stripped down to pain
Like a lonely dancer
Killed by an enemy to the soul
Crucified by a mind that is old

Dawning of a new age
Brings forth a new era
As the second resurrection
Gets ever nearer

Each life will continue to sin the same
Until the spirit of the lord will rise again
Each thought untrue not pure forever thought
Until the lord casts down again, the one true form of existence who’s mind and thoughts are one

Descent of a saviour
Cast down by the hand of god
The descent of a saviour
To a world no longer thankful

Cast down once again to save the soul
Of everything living a life
The saviour will see that hell rules us in pain
As he spends his pride sacrificing himself once again

The hand cast down will crush the wall
Between us
The sacrifice will save us all
Will free us
