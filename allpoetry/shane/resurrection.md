title: "Resurrection"
writer: "Shane"

Bathing with your toaster,
Cos things don’t look so great
You’ve gotta be tough enough
To take the strain

It’s raining so you hide for shelter
Your just afraid to be part of reality
You wan’t to live inside 
But you just can’t find the words to make you say

So scream it out, before you  fade out
Faded shadows on the Mississippi
Reflection of a coward
The fear inside it takes, your nightmares are reality

So get up and scream your lungs out
And use an ounce of pain
You have to take some damage
If you want to stand in the rain

Don’t fade away, until the sun comes today
Wake up your insides and step out
Don’t fade out
Get out of your shoebox, and see what comes your way

Resurrect your mind go seek what you can find
And as your wheels are turning forget all you’ve left behind
Go live your life ‘cos the world won’t mind
Because now’s the time, time to make things right, the time of your life
