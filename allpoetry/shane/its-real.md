---
title: "It's Real"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "its-real"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55217-Its-Real-by-Shane"
---

Don’t you dare cry for me
Because I’m already dead

Stuttering I’m a mess
I’m something that’s best left
Sunshine comes and it goes
It can’t clear away the pain

Withering in my sleep
It’s my dreams I pray to be
As I see my reflection die
I lose the will to try

When I’m black
You’ll forget
Everything that you held back
And I’ll be lost again

Don’t forget, once I’m gone
They’ll chose someone else
To condemn with pain
Once I’m gone, I’ll feel the same

Please awake me when I’m gone
And you’ll feel my suicide
Forget about myself, I know
That you already have

So take your steps
To feel so good
See my regrets
When I’m gone for good
