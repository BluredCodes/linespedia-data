It’s not if your winning, it’s if your taking part
If you try all you can, then you will win at heart

What’s the point in winning if you give it all you’ve got
A trophy for the memories of something that your not

Well forget about your attempts, at being a machine
Though you were born a loser, you still feel like a machine

Well you can’t work if you broken, and you can’t try till your chosen
On words placed in your mouth you are choking, ‘cos you know what it means to me

Victory is not a price to pay, misery is defeat that won’t go away
It’s a mystery how the loser seems to see, that winning ain’t important, when it means so much to me
