title: "Resting In False Environments"
writer: "Shane"

Sometimes I feel I waste my time
Just leaving my bed
Voices around me confuse
Except the ones in my head

The world is my oyster
It’s my choice sir tonight
Wake up and it goes wrong
Wake up and you’ll get denied

Crumbling walls with my head say
Desperate words are better said
The writings on my walls unread
In dreams I wish, I’ll wake up dead
