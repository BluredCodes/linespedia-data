title: "Meaning Of Life?"

What a pity
I’m no longer young and pretty
Like the fools cry
I just got old

Young and lonely 
I thought I was
At the bottom of the well
Seems to be that as you age

Your body goes to hell,
And your mind, don’t feel so well
I can’t think I’m aging now
Everything is lost somehow

But you never know
Until somebody tells you so
That’s the price you pay
For you life

It’s harsh, some may say
But that’s just life
So take a chance
And be a man before your sun sets

Take my thoughts take my hand
With a little thought of your own
You’ll be a better man
With a little life of you own, you’ll finally understand

The meaning of life,
You’ll personify
The meaning of life

If your nobody
And you wanna be somebody
Take a look at your self on a stormy day
With the sunshine raining down
You’ll hear the rainbow say

To be yourself, you have to believe yourself
To believe yourself is to be no-one else

If your nobody till somebody loves you
Well somebody better come to you
And make all the better things true
Because a man alone, is a man ruined through and through

Don’t forget what I tell you
‘Cos I’m the only friend that you’ve got
You think your more important
Until you’ve lost what you’ve got

I never want to live again
If everything is gonna be the same
Extend your finger and point the blame
You’ll realise, that yourself is the only target to aim
