---
title: "Just"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "just"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

I’m not student, I’m not worker
I’m free

I’m no client, I’m no employer
I’m just free

I’m not god, I’m no destroyer
I’m just me

I’m not a bad one, I’m not the good
But it feels good to be free

I’m not the worst one, I’m not the best one
But it’s great to be me
