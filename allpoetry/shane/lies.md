---
title: "Lies"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "lies"

---

Stating the obvious ain’t a habit of mine
I’d say your beautiful but you know what you are
Drinking drugs, now that’s a habit I like
Swallow it down and drown my pride

State of a confusion, he’s a friend of mine
He don’t need a reason, he just needs time
To co-exist to re-inhabit my mind
What you are inhabits your mind

Being unfriendly, well that’s what I do
Arrogant, ignorant oh yes it’s true
I am exactly what you see in me
I’m what you want from me

It’s everyone, it’s everything
It’s what you feel, it’s what they think
That makes me, that’s breaks me
So label me, I’ll label you, It’s just a word that makes it true
