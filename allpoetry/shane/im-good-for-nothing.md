title: "I’m Good For Nothing"

I used to play guitar till I broke all my strings
I tried to make a band but I don’t know how to sing
Something tells me, I’m good for nothing

I tried to save life’s, until I couldn’t breathe
Someone else had to save me
Something tells me, I’m good for nothing

I try so hard to be, all that I can
But it’s hard when you feel, more like mouse than man
Something tells me, I’m good for nothing

I want to be an equal, at least that’s what I’m told
As my eyes they grow stupid, my body grows old
Something tells me, I’m good for nothing

Open book, of my life you’ll see sirens warning
Take one look at my face and you’ll see god is yawning
Something tells me, I’m good for nothing

Take a page from my novel, and you will see
I am not what life lets, just what I can be
Something tells me, I’m good for nothing

As read all my letters, take a look and see
Through the ink and the pages, the emptiness is me
Something tells me, I’m good nothing

If your feeling good for nothing,
Then feel ashamed
You are wrong in my head
As my finger points the blame

Something tells me,
I just cannot be trusted
To live and lye awake
I’m good for nothing
