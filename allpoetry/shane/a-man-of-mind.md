---
title: "A Man Of Mind"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "a-man-of-mind"

I need to take a break from food,
I need to lose some excess parts of me
I need to change

I have to change the way I see
I have to love the people in front of me
I need to love

I have to modify the way I am
I have to modify myself
For someone else

I wish I was something better than this
I wish I was a hit, instead I’m just a miss
The target vanity

I want to see a different picture in the mirror
I want to see someone better than me
I want to be someone else

It’s time to change!

I guess it’s time to sell out
And stop my screaming
It’s strange, the lengths we got to
Just to fit in

Star sign says he don’t want to talk to me
I guess he knows I’ve wasted years on nothing
I tried to save life for myself
It got me nowhere

I’m lost now, 
Stubborn was the fool
The fool who lives alone now
I just have to accept, that of this man there’s nothing left
