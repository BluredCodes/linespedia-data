Ignorance personified, epitome of filth
Wretched face, evil mind
You I wish to kill

Genius within your mind
The truth is bittersweet
From your bowels all inside
The truth on you it eats

Attitude shockingly
Leaves your mind
Riddled with laughable simplicity

Reality means to be
For you it’s just ignored
Trying to be something
But your nothing and nothing forevermore

Friend a word that you curse
With your own preference
One is worth, one is not
A friend is yours until one better comes
Then a friend he’s not

Well die, simple man
I’ll kill you with my hands
Die, Now somehow
For your existence I can’t stand

I’m forever in debt to you
Well someone has to pay the price
I tried to negotiate with you acceptably
Now I’m sick of being nice

Dance to your tune,
Insanely around your padded room
You fucked with me you’ll pay the price
Die by the hands of my deadly deviceWell this is written about a$$holes, the world is filled with them, but when you best friend turns out to be one it pi$$e$ you off, and thats what this poem is for.
