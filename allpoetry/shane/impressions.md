---
title: "Impressions"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "impressions"

Don’t get the wrong one
Just get it right
It’s my impression
It’s part of my life

Look out for them critically
Beware of the first one’s
Don’t let them beat you up
Some are out of line

Give me one of those that I like best,
Give me love and give me all the rest
Tell me how great I am
Tell me, tell me before I explode

It’s my confession,
The first impression
That I don’t think I like you much
My impressions just a touch

The first one, the worst one
It’s just my fear, not what I want to hear
A bad one, the sad one, the first one
Your impression goes straight into my head and out of my ear
