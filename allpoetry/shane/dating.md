The first stage…
I’m trying to be what you want me to be
It’s over now…
Your lying, to kill what’s inside of me
Love long lost…
I’m crying out, for who I used to be
Life long love…
Words mean nothing to me
Long live love…
Is something I can’t believe
I love you…
You mean that I love me
