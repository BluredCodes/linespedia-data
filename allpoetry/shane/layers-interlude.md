There’s a pretty lady with an eye of mine
But she won’t take my hand, she won’t tell me the time
There’s a deadly favour that I have to ask
Some things in this world are just to hard to grasp
Is it safe to leave my shelter, or should I have
A fear to go outside without an oxygen mask

Pollution multiplying
There’s a dark moon that’s rising
Overhead the lights are blinding
As they penetrate the safety net we confide in
The ozone’s hole is overgrown there’s no life left in it
Scientist are not ashamed though they know we did it
Now there’s a dark moon on the horizon
