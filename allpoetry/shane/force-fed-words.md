---
title: "Force Fed Words"
writer: "Shane"
slug: "force-fed-words"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

Singing like a bird, to the same old tune again
Choking on the words that you put inside my mouth
Dancing like a marionette, wooden shoes again
Acting like a puppet with the same old strings again

Government juggernauts, stealing my thoughts again
Choking on the words that they put inside my mouth
Politicians in white shirts, stealing my rewards again
A pigeon on the floor they all think I’m diseased again

Fingers in my mouth, I’m biting my nails again
They all try to push me, but I’m biting down hard on them
Choke on my fingers as they’re pulling them out
As they’re filling my face, with their words in my mouth again

Pointing a gun, threatening to kill myself again
The words in my mouth are talking me out of it
As I’m pulling the trigger, my fingers come out again
The words in my mouth… speak no more!
