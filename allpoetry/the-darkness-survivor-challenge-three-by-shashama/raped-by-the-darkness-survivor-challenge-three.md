Raped by the darkness
She silently cries
Keeping her fears
Hidden inside

She looks straight ahead
Emotionless stare
Not showing him
How much she is scared

Raped by the darkness
She wants to be free
He doesn't know it
She won't let him see

She lets him in
Night after night
To be raped again
She can't make it right

Raped by the darkness
She silently cries
She's convincing herself
With all of her lies

Someday she'll leave him
But not quite yet
For she still thinks
It's as good as it gets

Raped by the darkness
She crawls away
Looking for safety
For all time, to stay

She realizes now
That she was so wrong
To stay with the darkness
That muted her song

Raped by the darkness
She'll no longer be
She can sing again
For now she is free
