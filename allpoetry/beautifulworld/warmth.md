---
title: "Warmth"
writer: "beautifulworld"
slug: "warmth"
source: "AllPoetry"

Sweet flame, oh come to me
Sing your soothing song to me
Make me wish for another day
oh come to me
I know I do not need you
But I do in every way
oh come to me
I glance at you from across the room
Distant your eyes
oh come to me
I will make it better
I will make it all better

IF I CAN
