The light from the window leads trails on the floor 
Lost immigrants, tired and lonely, search for the light 
Where are they going, the desperate few, what is it they are seeking? 
Dreams cloud the eyes of the disheartened, returning hope 
Weary are those who follow the path 
Lonely and tired, searching for escape 
Why do they strive for a whisper, when tomorrow is not so far? 
There is nothing wrong with seeing the window
