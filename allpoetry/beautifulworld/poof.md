---
title: "*POOF*"
writer: "beautifulworld"

source: "AllPoetry"

I've built it up
for so long
The heavy wall
of stone
Brick by brick
it grew
For years I was the sole builder
Then you
came along
The wall grew higher than I
care to tell
It became
an elaborate labrynth
I was nearly content laboring on the wall
Then you
came along
The wall grew higher still
despite your efforts
It became
an obsession
I was desperately trying to protect what had always been there
Then you
came along
You began breaking down the wall
brick by brick
And I ran
as far and as fast as I could
You saw through me and that scared me to tears
Since you
came along
The walls
have thinned
Transparent and weak
are the bricks
What perfect conditions
for a *POOF*
