title: "Too Soon"
writer: "daisy"

Everything happened so fast.
Everything happened so very fast.
One day, you were at school with me eating at my luch table,
The next, i was eating lunch with you by your bed in the hospital.
Why did all of this have to happen?
It all happened so fast.
When I first got the news,
I thought to myself,
What is going to happen to you?
Is she going to die?
How long is she going to live?
I soon found out.
I found out the answers to my questions way too soon.
Way too soon.
