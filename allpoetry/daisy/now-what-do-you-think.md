So now what do you think?
Do you really think you should of done what you did to start with?
Do you?
Was it really worth it?
Was it?
No, it wasn't, and you know that.
You left behind so many people who cared about you.
Think about it,
You hurt so many people.
They loved you, and they cared for you.
But you put them out of their misery, and you went and did something stupid.
Why did you do that to yourself?
You had such a good life ahead of you.
If you would've just forgot about your parents,
You could've made it through.
And you know that,
You just never took the time to realize it.
You never were patient with anything.
That was one of the things I learned about you.
You always used to yell at me when I was too slow with something.
What was wrong with that?
O well, it's all too late now.
I just wish you would've thought some
Before you killed yourself.
So now what do you think?
