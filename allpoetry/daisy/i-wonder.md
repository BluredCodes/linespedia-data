---
title: "I wonder"
writer: "daisy"

I wonder
Out under the sky
Why do people I care about
Always have to die?

Are you happy were you are,
Wherever that may be?
I wonder,
Do you still think of me?

Is it nice up there in heaven?
For I know you made it there.
Are the clouds really marshmallows?
Do you know that I still care?

I look up into the sky,
And shed a single tear.
I think of all the days gone by,
I'll always hold you.

I wonder
Out under the sky
Why do people I care about
Always have to die?
