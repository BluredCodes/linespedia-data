My angel guides me like a star in the sky
My angel cares about me and watches me succeed.
My angel never disagrees with me,
My angel never hurts me or puts me down.

Everyone has an angel somewhere who has a lot of love
That special angel gives you so much love and that special angel teaches you what is right.

Amands is my special angel
Who brightens my day, everyday
She is a gift to me.

Amanda is my angel
Amanda is my angel who has recently just recieved her wings.


Amanda Kratita 1985-2002--I love you;.
