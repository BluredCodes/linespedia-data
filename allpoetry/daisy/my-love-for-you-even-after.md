---
title: "My Love For You, Even After"
writer: "daisy"
slug: "my-love-for-you-even-after"
source: "AllPoetry"

Why have you done this to me?
Please tell me.
In don't seem to understand.
You told me you loved me.
Maybe you were lying to me the whole time.
Were you?
You could never understand what I am feeling right now.
You never will.
You choose not to understand me.
You told me you cared
Did you lie about that too?
Why are you doing this?
I love you.
Doesnt that mean anything to you?
I thought we had something that would live on.
Maybe I was wrong.
There is one thing I know,
I do not regret my choice in you.
I love you more than anything.
And that love for you will always live on.
Forever.
