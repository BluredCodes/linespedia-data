---
title: "Let Me Bring You Here"
writer: "daisy"

When the clouds above you start to pour with rain,
And all of the doubts you have rage like a storm
And you dont even know what or who you are anymore,
Let me be the one to help you find what you are searching for.
Somewhere in this life of yours there is a river
Where you can let everything run free.
Let me bring you here.
You think that no one sees this heavy weight you carry on your back,
But I do.
You can't fool me.
Let me be the one to show you the way.
Let me be the one to show you the way to happiness.
Give me a chance to be the giver,
Let me bring peace to your life.
