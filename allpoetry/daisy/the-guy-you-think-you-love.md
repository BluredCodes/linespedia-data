title: "The Guy You Think You Love"
writer: "daisy"

What is happening between you and this guy?
When is he going to realize that you are the one for him?
Or will he not.
He needs someone like you to love him.
Not just any girl, you.
For he has told me so.
And I know he has feeling for you somehow.
For is he were any other guy, he would've moved on.
But with you, he stays.
He is just afraid to show you his feelings.
Could you help him open up?
Then you will see, you are the one for him.
You would be more than just a girl, but someone to talk to.
He just needs a little push
And that little push he needs, is you.
