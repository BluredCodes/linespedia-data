---
title: "Where Did You Go?"
writer: "daisy"
slug: "where-did-you-go"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57939-Where-Did-You-Go--by-daisy"
---

You are the one who I feel for the most.
You are the one who I love the most.
You are the one who I love more.
I want you to love me as much as I love you.
Maybe more.
I want you to care for me as much as I care for you.
I want you to be there for me as much as I am for you.
I thought I was the luckiest one to be with you.
Anyone I know in this world would love you as much as I do.
But I am that special one who loves you.
I am.


And now, I don't feel so lucky anymore.
You aren't the one who I love the most anymore.
No.
You aren't the same person who I used to be in love with.
You are someone else.
You are not that special someone who I thought was the best ever.
You never cared, and you never loved.
You never learned how.
I do not feel special to be yours anymore.
You should feel special to be mine.

But before I leave you here in this lonely place all by yourself,
Please explain to me.
Where did you go?
