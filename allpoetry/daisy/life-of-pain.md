title: "Life of Pain"
writer: "daisy"

I know a girl
Who is living her life with pain and sorrow
The only thing she sees is fighting
Thats all she knows
She knows nothing else
Will someone please come and take her away?
She needs some help
She needs to learn
Some blame her mother
Even though she is no longer there
It is her father who causes her so much grief
He will not stop
He is too powerful
Will someone please teach her that he is bad?
For she loves him still.
Even though he hurts her.
She needs someone to help.
In a hurry.
