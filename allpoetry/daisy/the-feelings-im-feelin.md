title: "The Feelings I'm Feelin"
writer: "daisy"

i feel as if the world is spinning
and my life is dimming
myself i can no longer fight
and away went this bright light
i cant get you off my mind
my mind is racing
and my feet are pacing
i cant sit still
you stole my heart 
and you tore it apart
i had my warning
but i had no clue what was forlorning
tears are streaming down my face
you made my life a depressing case
why wont you hold me?
and feel the passion from me to you
why my love do you deny?
you turn around and tell me so many lies
i feel dead inside
and in you i only want to confide
was i wasting my time?
did you ever want to be mine?
