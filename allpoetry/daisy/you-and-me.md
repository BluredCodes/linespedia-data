---
title: "*~You And Me*~"
writer: "daisy"
slug: "you-and-me"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/57930---You-And-Me---by-daisy"
---

Here we are safe at last, it seems the storm has passed.
Through it all, no one knew that all the stars in heaven would bring me back to you.
No one I know imagined we would make it,
but only made us believe.
We were meant to be.
Mountains move and oceans part from eachother when they are standing in our way.
Angels stand watching over us and heaven shines upon us everyday.
You give me strength and you set me free.
It's because of you that I am all that I am.
The chance was so unlikely that we would ever be together.
Two stars among the heavens danced you right here to me.
You and me were meant to be.
Together forever.
