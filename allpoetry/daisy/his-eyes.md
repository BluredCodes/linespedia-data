---
title: "His eyes"
writer: "daisy"

I look over his body
And i was drawn to his eyes
I held his hand
But I stared into his eyes
I tried to look elsewhere
But every time I turned away, I looked into his eyes

I kissed him
But I was unable to close my eyes properly
Because I looked into his eyes
He walked away
But I was still staring into his eyes
That night when I feel asleep I had a dream

He lost his eyes
And I fell in love with him
But the next day I saw him again
And fell in love with 
His eyes.
