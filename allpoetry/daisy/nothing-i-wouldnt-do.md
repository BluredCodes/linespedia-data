---
title: "Nothing I Wouldnt Do"
writer: "daisy"

There's nothing in this world I wouldn't do for you.
You and your beautiful blue eyes.
I dont care what the others say,
Now that I've got you.
All those little things you do to me.
The way you touch me and caress me 
I love you.
It's all about you.

Every night I dream about you.
If only you knew how much I do,
Do need you.
Baby be mine, 
I love you Boo.
