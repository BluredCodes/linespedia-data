---
title: "My Everything"
writer: "daisy"
slug: "my-everything"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/55673-My-Everything-by-daisy"
---

I've finally found the one I love,
And I thank the Lord above.
He is so special to me, 
And he means the world to me.
He is my baby, 
I am his girl.
The way he kisses me makes me wonder,
The way he hugs me makes me ponder.
He has the most gentle touch,
I know he would never hurt me.
We share the same feelings, 
We share the same ideas.
When I am in a bad mood, 
He wil brighten my day.
It seems as though we were meant to be,
Together in this world as one.
He is just so wonderful,
I could never let him go.
He is my everything.
