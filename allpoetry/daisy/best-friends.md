Most people have a best friend, mine happens to be you.
All the money in the world couldnt build a friendship like you.
A friend is a special person, unique in there own ways.
They like you for who you are and couldnt settle for anything else.
There is just so much that we share that it is time we are aware of what best friends are.

When the road looks rough ahead and you are miles away from your bed,
just remember what your old pal said.
Hand in hand as we walk we share those special moments,
who would ever have thought?
We are the best of friends.
Always and Forever.
