---
title: "*I'll Always Be There*"
writer: "daisy"
slug: "ill-always-be-there"
source: "AllPoetry"

When the stars are falling, 
I'll keep calling.
I promise that you'll be my one my only and my everything.
I will never ever be untrue to you.
If you give me your love, I will give you mine.
Even through the roughest times, our love together will find a way.
When you're life seems to be fading away, 
I will be waiting here to help you through it.
And I will still love you.
You are my summer breeze,
My winter rain,
And my spring time soul.
You are the smile upon my heart,
The breath in my body.
Even when we are apart from eachother,
Our love will always be true.
Through time we may grow apart, thats the truth,
But I will still be loving you.
