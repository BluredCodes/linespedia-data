---
title: "~I Think of You~"
writer: "daisy"
slug: "i-think-of-you"

Staring up at the sky above me,
As the stars blink quickly,
The sounds of the waves on the shore,
They crash upon the rocks around me.
While I think of you.

Looking out the window to the near street,
As the cars go by,
The rain comes pouring down,
With thunder in the distance.
While I think of you.

Wondering why apples fall from trees,
And what is up in the sky,
And what makes the wind blow,
It always leads to you, and....
I sit and think of you every second of everyday.
