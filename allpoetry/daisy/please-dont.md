Don't think like you do
Don't let that thought into your mind
Life has meaning in it
That meaning, you will eventually find.

This journey will be long
A lifetime of pain
But if you look closer at life and its meaning
You might be happy again

My friend, I love you more than anything
Please don't ask me why
But God needs you here
And so do I.
