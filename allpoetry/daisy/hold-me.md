---
title: "Hold Me"
writer: "daisy"
slug: "hold-me"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

The best place for me,
I now realize
Is in your arms, 
Or at least in your eyes

For that is the one place 
I do feel secure
As time seems to fly
Without any cure

My heart beats so fast
And my head feels light
I'm glad that you are holding me
Or my sould might take flight

So hold me forever
As close as you can
For the reason, "I love you"
Is that you understand

When I am with you
My world is at peace
So please hold me dear
Until my fears pass
