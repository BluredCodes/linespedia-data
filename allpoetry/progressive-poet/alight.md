---
title: "Alight"
writer: "Progressive Poet"
slug: "alight"
source: "AllPoetry"

---

My heart it beats so slowly, barely raising a pulse
My nose it smells your perfume, something stirs inside
There will always be a part of me
That only belongs to you

My heart it flickers dimly, burning embers in your dark
My eyes they fix upon you, something comes alight
There will always be a part of me
That only lights up for you
