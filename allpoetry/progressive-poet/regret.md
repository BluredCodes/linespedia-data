If I bared my soul to you
What would you say to me?
If I poured out all my thoughts
Would you hear my words?

If I told you how I feel
How would you react?
And if I spoke right from the heart
Would you turn away?

If I took a risk on you
Would you pay me back?
And if I never say a word
Will I feel regret?Should the second to last line be "but if I never say a word"?  I couldn't choose between the but and and.
