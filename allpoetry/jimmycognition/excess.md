Surrounded by an excess of humanity
Impenetrable,
Indiscernible,
Powerfully complex
A myriad-branched sapling sprout of fertile minds

Across sea and field and mountain, a scarcity of privilege
Forces those to subsist on souls emaciated
Birth, Work, Death
Such is the way it is for the world that bleeds for us.written upon realizing that I was taking part in a state drama competition, while other humans will never get an opportunity to read a book.
