As I sit atop this lofty peak
my blood swimming with warm tea
and my mind swirling
with the intoxicating richness about me
I peer down at the treacherous path below
that brought me here
and laugh at the absurdity of every thought
forged in bitterness along the way.I'm aware that the meter is nonexistant, but I just wrote what I felt. Hopefully it makes a shred of sense.
