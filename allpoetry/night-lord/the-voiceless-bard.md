title: "The Voiceless Bard"

Drowning in sorrows bittersweet

Souls roasting over white fire

Deep whithin burning desire

Lost in an ocean of numbing lies

Adrift, lost in turmoil

Dreams vanquished blood begining to boil

Drifting helplessly under a midnight moon

Rolloing against waves of crystal shards

Sinking, becoming The Voiceless Bard

In terrifying temptation I sink

Crimson coals for eyes I cannot blink

Mountain streams crimson coals

Deep within becoming cold

Lungs turn green and begin to mold

In etearnal lonliness I relize

I've 

Become

The voiceless Bard.
