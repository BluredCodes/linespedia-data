The Mountain opens her mouth and rocks tumble down



Flowers bloom and sing to the sky



Young Kids run after each other like a bead necklace



Laughter filled the air

 

Humbleness inside castle like a Angel have visit



Sounds of nature calls to the wood



The Wind dances with the sun



Wings flutter and flirt with the Clouds



The sun winks in pleasure as he looks downThis was the first poem I have written in my life and I just founde it. *-*
