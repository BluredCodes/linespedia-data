Things aren't always going to be the same.
People around you change.
Do you still feel the same?
Do you feel the world around you?
It's still there still haunting you.
I'll always be inside you.
I'm apart of you.
I am you my love.
Love gone by so fast didn't even see you there.
You put me there in your head.
