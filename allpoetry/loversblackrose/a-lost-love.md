title: "A Lost Love"

We don't really feel.
Things are just what we are.
Players in a game the we'll never win.
This person would like to win.
I will do anything down to givin in to hate.
Even if i've already done so.
A love I remember tonight.
Just hit me like death to life.
I remember his touch and the way he smelled.
I could remember the pain I caused.
And the love he gave me.
I hate myself for the pain i've givin the world.
But now I will show the same love he gave me to some one else.
Things happen so fast.
But will life be taken tonight.
I hope not,everyone needs a chance to live even for a minute.
