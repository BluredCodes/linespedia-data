title: "A Cutters New Wish"

My new wish is to live again.
Is to stop the pain and hate.
And to try and put life back together.
Without the people I love to help me.
I will let them help but not with the important things.
I need to help myself before they try and help me.
I needed to see what I was doing to myself.
With the pills and the razors.
But now there out of my life.
A long with the person who started them.
Yes I miss this person with all my heart.
But for my wish to come true he has to leave.
I've left all of them in the past and tryed to forget my color.
So really my new wish is to no longer be a cutter.
I will make this wish come true......
