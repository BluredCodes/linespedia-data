title: "In Love Again"

Can't sleep just keep thinking bout things around me.
They say that when you go to sleep and wake up thinking bout the same person your in love.
Well I guess i'm in love and just scared to say it.
To him I guess,I could say it to anyone but him.
I could say it to my friends.
Cause no matter what they love me.
And I can tell my family they'll love me too.
But what if I tell him would he still say he loves me?
Would he still feel the same if he knew my feelings were more?
Would he still touch me the same way?
Could he still look at me with his eyes?
I can not tell him the truth cause i'm not sure he would still say I love you.
Even if I can't say it...I'm in love..........
Love can make and break you.
Please don't break me.
I can't believe it i'm in love again!!!!!!!!!!
