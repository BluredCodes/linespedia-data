title: "The Voices"

No one can understand what they can...No one can get inside my head better than them...There already there...One tells me the world is going to end....Another tells me that one day the love of my life will come back to me...One tells me of my dreams...And what I really want....One tells me of my heart my cuts and my pain...
But what is pain...I mean I feel nothingness...Is that pain the longing for someone you know loves you...But what of love its not real either...
