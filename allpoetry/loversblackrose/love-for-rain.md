---
title: "Love For Rain"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

It rained today but yet you where not next to me.
I cry for a while and said even if he's not here,
He's still in my heart.
I feel so alive when it rains.
But without you rain means nothing to me.
Come back to me love and watch the rain with me.
Its so beautful and means so much when we are together.
And taste my tears away,kiss the cuts and stop the blood.
Make me whole again with your love.
Listen to the rain and when you hear it.
Think of me and know i'm still alive.
Cause your heart I will never die.
Go outside and kiss the rain know that tomorrow bring more love and happiness.
