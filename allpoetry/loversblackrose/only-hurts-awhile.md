title: "Only Hurts Awhile"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

Tomorrow is here and he is not.
Take it it's was my fault.
So people think if you hurt your better.
They're wrong people who hurt turn to death.
Nothing good can come out of what is real.
Help is not what helps...Getting what you want helps.
But sometimes its more than that.
Some people turn to life to help them get around the love they lost.
Tears and death seem to kill whats unreal to me.
I liked the unreal...Its everything that can make things better.
If I could I'd still be lost in the unreal.
