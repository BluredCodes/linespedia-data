I saw the letter sitting on the desk.
I didn't know what to think.
Was that letter for me I ask?
But no one answers me.
I feel so lost in my own world.
But thats where I was safe.
I feel lost and alone.
A light was found along the way.
But et again I pasted by the light.
I read the letter love in my heart.
But knew its not real........But maybe I'll fall in love again.
