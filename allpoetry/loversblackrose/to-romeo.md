I love him so much but can't be with him.
I hate myself so for the pain I gave him.
I well never be the same with out him be side me.
I will never love the same after what happened.
I can only dream bout the love he gave me.
The pain I gave in return.
To him you'll never know how sorry I am.
Or how much I still love you.
To Romeo my love forever.....
