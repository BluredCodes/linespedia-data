The rain talks to me it tells me of my pain and hope.
But it also tells me of love that has been givin to me.
It tells me he not like any other boy.
He's love and thats what i have been waiting for all my life.
I've been looking for my romeo and now i've found him.
I was told I would never find him.
That I was helpless and hopeless.
I did anyways...I would not stop trying untill I found him.
And now when we are together I am in his arms and in his heart.
So with this said...I hope my romeo reads this...
This is my peom to you sweet romeo.
You can be loved.
