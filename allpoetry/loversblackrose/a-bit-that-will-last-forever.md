Secrets lies and pain.
Thats what my life has become.
They played with my head made me evil again.
It feels good to feel hate take over and not care anymore.
They've taklen from me everything i love and killed my heart.
THey took my soul and give it away to hell.
I had already givin it to hell but they wanted to make sure it got there.
He bit my neck and took the last of my blood only to give me a lifetime of pain in return.
