title: "Pain of Death"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

I've been in pain so long is there anything else?
I've hated and hurt some many?
I know i'm going to hell.
So much pain where does it stop?
Or where did it start all this pain?
Who gave me the power to do these things?
Why did I take it?
I remember a boy I knew so long ago.
He's dead now...Dead only to me.
The world knows he's alive but i'm the only one who knows the truth.
He's really dead inside and love is gone.
I tryed and tryed but he wouldn't love me.
So now he's dead but only to me.


If he reads this than he'll know how much I miss him...
How much things have changed...And even if I think of him dead....
He's soul is still in me.
