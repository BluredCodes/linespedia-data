Bloodstaind glass death to all.
I did everything I could to please you.
Will you kill me now.
All alone in your little world.
Scared to death of people and happiness.
My bloodstaind hands should be yours.
Help me out of the hell i've been given.
Take back the words that killed me.
Kiss me one last time before you kill me.
But kiss my neck and drink my blood.
Take my life and for ever live yours.
But you'll live it alone cause you've killed everyone you love.
