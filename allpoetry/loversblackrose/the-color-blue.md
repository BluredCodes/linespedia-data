It only hurts alittle.
Feel my heartbeat.
Then it stoped.

I gave my life for you.
Will you remember me tomorrow?
Or will I be the past gone by?

Injected blood feel my pain.
Take from me all my pain.
I feel no more.

Asleep to you.
Death to me.

Injected hate,loving fate.
Blood falls from your mouth.
My neck soaked in blood.

One thing could kill me.
Two pills take them,all better.
Three razors the taste of blood.

Could you understand the truth?
If I told you death was coming.
That you were next and life isn't worth it.

I hide away in hopes to find...
My new piece of mind.
But i've found nothing.

I take that back I did find something.
A love taken so young.
I killed her soul.

He showed me the way.
The truth even if it hurt.
And why I was here.

But he left me with nothing.
Not able to rememeber the answers to anything.
Light gone faith gone.

His voice haunts me.
But why,he wronged me?
All I did was love him.

He broke me.
Killed what was left of me.
KNowing pain would leave me.

He kissed me one last time.
Asked me one last thing.
Do you still love me?

This time I said no.
His eyes got wide.
The color blue faded from my heart...

I said one last thing but it was not heard.
Romeo is my love,no matter pain.
I love you romeo...I never stoped and never will!
