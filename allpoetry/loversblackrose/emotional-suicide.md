Emotional suicide,lost inside.
Feeling pain with out needing our game.
I can't believe I couldn't let myself be happy for even a little while.
I always seem to push everyone away for the real love I have.
I wish in a way I didn't love him or he loved me.
He'll never read this.....But I'm forever in pain for a love givin up on.
Scars of hate from our games....Lust is what we want.
In a way love and lust are my life....All I've got left is her pain.
I can be lost...But now I wish I wasn't.
