They all tell me my pain isn't real.
Its all in my head.
Well in a way it is in my head.

But its real no less.
Its pain what more can I say.
I know everyone has pain,Flea.

But no one has pain like me.
Sometimes I can't control myself.
Sometimes I get hurt.

But not by others.
I hurt myself I don't mean too.
It just happens.

Pain is not a game.
Will not to me.
Some people play life as a game.

Life really is a game but its not ment to be that way.
Pain can kill you.
Its a piece in the game.

Death is apart of life.
You can't get away from it.
Its there forever...


Or until you die.
