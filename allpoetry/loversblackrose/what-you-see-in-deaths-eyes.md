Dressed in black comes death.
I say goodbye with a tear in my eye.
I do not want to let go of his hand.
I looked into his eyes one last time.
I felt the pain in my heart grow.
I know that i'll never touch him again.
They cut my wrist with razors.
I felt tears come to be.
I closed my eyes to only see 
my true loves eyes looking back at me.
With this now no more pain.
But only loves eyes to light my way.
