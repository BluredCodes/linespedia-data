Have you ever looked at the world from anothers eyes.
It can be so different.
But alot like you eyes.
I know I should kill him,
But I can't I have to kiss him.
The dark is my new best friend.
It needs me like no one else.
It shows me what I want to see.
Misery one of my true loves.
Death the next,Pain the next.
The rain loves me as well.
He gave me all my loves.
Showed me the way to hate and pain.
But hate I gave to myself.
Mix them together hell would kill.
He poured his misery upon me and let everything go.
Feeling everything he had once felt.
Running in me all at once.
He kissed my neck softly then bit as hard as he could.
I knew then he had sucked me into he's world.
And I loved being there.
His world in me killing everything good and giving me a new meaning.
To hurt and kill all that stands in my way.
And to get what I want no matter what it takes.
No one is worth what we want.
The world is next in our little plan.
But will we be able to do it.
Don't be weak I need you.
