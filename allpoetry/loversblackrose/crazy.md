Lalalalala
You call me cazy but i'm just singing.
Whats so crazy bout me?
Is it because i'm not like you.
Is it cause I like pain and you don't?
Or cause I can take it,and you can't?
Sometimes I wonder am I really crazy or is it really you?
And your trying to make me think its me.
What comes after goodbye?
Who will be there for me now.
