Tears fall thats happened before...
But what if these are your last tears...
What if the love of your life was in your hands dying...
What could you do...
Would you die right after...
Would you just go on depressed and lonely...
Would you cry or try and hide it...
Would you live and try to be happy...
Or would your heart and soul die w/ them...
Do you even have a love...
Or just pain to hole your fears and tears...
What if love is just a mindless game they play...
What a game... would you win or lose...
Would you even play...
These are all things I could not answer for I have lost love...
And my life as I know it...
To those in love you will at some point feel pain...
Try not to think bout it...
It will just make thing wrose...
