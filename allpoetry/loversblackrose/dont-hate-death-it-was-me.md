title: "Don't Hate Death It Was Me"

I'm holding in my hand my way out.
But so many people would hate me if I did it.
But whats there pain next to mine?
Who would hurt more?
I bet it would be me if I stayed a minute longer.
I loss for words but they just keep pouring out.
I hold in my hand a pertty new razor to take the pain.
To bleed and die by the end of the day.
So hate me if you want I no longer care.
But if you do hate me remember it was you that put me here.
Holding my only way out!
