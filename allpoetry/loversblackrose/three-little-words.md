---
title: "Three Little Words"

I say it each day when we are together and even when we are apart.
The words come from my lips and the bottom of my heart.
I can't help but wonder am I what he thinks of when he awakes each day.
He is what I think of...He is what I need.
I long to know he's hopes and dreams...His pain and fear.
I love you...I need you.
Fate takes love away.
He gave love back.
After a broken heart I still love you.
