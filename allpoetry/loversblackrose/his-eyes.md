title: "His Eyes"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

Watching blue skys turn black with you.
True meaning but nothings there.
Do you really know someone?
I know its him when I look him in the eyes.
His eyes make the pain go away.
His lips kiss every tear away.
Does he know the truth that he hides?
I know my fears come out when he looks at me.
Scars of what once was come back in the night.
Bite marks pain.
Don't leave me alone I could die.
I'm to scared to die alone.
I'd be to scared with out you and to weak to go on.
Stay with me as you can.
But know when you leave me...
You'll leave love.
