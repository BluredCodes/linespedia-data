---
title: "Found Love"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"
slug: "found-love"

I've been looking for the longest time.
For the one soul that could keep death away.

I've found him now but I fear for loseing this soul.
Fear was all I had until he came.

My fear grows less with each day.
I think he knows of my fear.

Each day when I awake he is the only thing on my mind.
And the last thing I think of before I dream.

Sometimes I wonder why this person even looks at me?
But yet day by day he still tells me he loves me.

I could be with another that I called my soulmate,but I no longer love him.
He gave me only pain and misery.

I have found love again this time I wish not to lose it.
But if I do know this fate I may not live To see another day.
