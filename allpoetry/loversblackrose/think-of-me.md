---
title: "Think Of ME"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

Think of me when you all alone.
Think of me when there people around.
Think of me when rain comes down on you.
Think of me when we're together.
Think of me when we're apart.
Think of me when you think of love.
Think of me when you think of death.
Think of me when dying is on your mind.
For I know I could not take loseing you to fate.
Love me now in loving hate.
Take me now my soul and fate.
My love is only for you.
