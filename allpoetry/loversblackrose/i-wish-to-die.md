title: "I Wish To Die"

Nothing but black and red I see in my eyes.
Death to me I got what I wanted.
I got hurt for the last time.
No more pain only hate to keep me down.
I've gone back to being me the real me.
I let love take over and make me need it.
I won't let that happen again.
To death take me now...To life I no longer need you.
I wish to die by a vampires kiss....

FleaFlicker
