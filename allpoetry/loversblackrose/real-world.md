---
title: "Real World"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

I hate the world.
I hate the pain.
I hate the way they use to say that 
all my pain would go away.
I see them now everyday knowing 
there hate and seeing there pain.
I look into their eyes and say 
welcome to the real world.
