---
title: "Gone Forever"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

The two I love are gone forever.
Somehow I knew this was going to happen.
I put my self in the middle of a little plan.
Hating the world like always.
I can't help but think maybe he'll die while i'm gone.
But then he'll come to join me.
I will no longer be alone and scared.
But now my life will fade away.
And I will be forgotten just as I should have been before.
I wish everything I have away for a good love to come along and save me from this hell.
I'd like to run far away for here.
With the one they send to me...And forget that they ever forgot me.
