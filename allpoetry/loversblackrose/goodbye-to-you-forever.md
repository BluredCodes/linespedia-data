Its late at night and love is gone.
My tears will and fall and paint the floors.
With pertty colors of pain and hate.
I've taken something to eas the pain.
Something to eas my life.
Tomorrow I won't wake up.
And they'll all know my real pain.
I can't see that well and my body starts to move.
Pain fades so does me life.
It was no ones fault I took it myself.
But i'll be ok I won't be far.
I'm right next to you.
Don't fear me i'm here with love.
My hearts been broken for the longest time.
I wanted to die I took my own life.
So don't be mad don't be sad i'm still in your hearts.
I am still with you.


This is to the four ppls I love.If you read this and i'm still alive I won't be for long.....This goes to cande chuckie blueberry and grim.
