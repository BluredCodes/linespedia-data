title: "Death to a Young Girl!!!!!!!!!!"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

A young person...
Not knowing what was once good for her has now killed her.
The razor on the skin.
Feel the pain run in her.
Blood fall to the floor.
But where did it come from?
No fear when blood comes out.
It's time to go they say with smiles.
And she smiles back with a tear.
She went crazy watching her friends kill eachother.
Hopes killed by the night and days killed by sunlight.
She dressed in black...With rainbow hair.
Needed love she could not get.
She sat in the hallway slowly one by one.
70 cuts all so beautiful blood running down them.
One by one watching the blood fall to the floor.
Tasting the pain and fear.
Will this kill her will she be happy again.
