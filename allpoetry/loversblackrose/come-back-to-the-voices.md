Evil its all around can you feel it?
I put it there we want the real you back.
We want the evil thats in your heart to come back.
Love has made you weak and helpless.
We want you back stronger better.
We know your in there.
We're waiting for you to come back to us.
Please don't keep us waiting to long we're not as good w/out you.
We need you We love you
You need us You need me You love me....
