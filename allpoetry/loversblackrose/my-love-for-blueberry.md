In deaths eyes you'll find me waiting for you to come back to me.
I still miss you wait like a little kid...wanting something really bad.
But I can do this things will be ok til you get back.
Pain was what you gave me but yet I'm still here loving you.
I won't stop won't give in to their games.
I will only love who I wish to love.
And hate the rest of the world as he wished me too.
Why does he wish pain upon me?
Why was it so wrong for me to love him?
Thats all I ever did was love him...But he keeps pushing me away.
So I give up play your games....Hurt yourselfs I no longer care.
I've been healed I've gotten better.
No more razor to slice my skin...No more suicide.
I'm always going to be better than them now.
I won't be sucked back in not this time.
I left you be and now would you let me be.
You still haunt me...You biting and the taste of your skin.
The way you said my name and the way you always drink my blood.
Your a little part of me I wished not to give up but to live...I must.
I wish you would just love me and show me your truth and not leave me be.
But wishes don't come true...
