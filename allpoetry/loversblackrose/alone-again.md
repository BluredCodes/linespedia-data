---
title: "Alone Again"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

I don't know what to think,what to say.
I'm done with the world and its done with me.
I'm lost in a world of my own.
The one that would make things better.
That could help me.
That I could feel safe in.
I feel nothing but alone.
Like the world is done with me...
