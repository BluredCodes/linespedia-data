I love it when you say you love me and when you touch my hand.
Something keeps telling me to wake up when your around.
Cause you feel like a dream to me.
Things have gotten better in my life because you love me and I still can't believe your real.
I feel like romeo has found me after all this time of me looking for you.
Love can only go so far but you've gone way out there.
You know that I love you and always will.
Three small cuts.
Three small letters.
No pain when I cut,Just love to light my way.
