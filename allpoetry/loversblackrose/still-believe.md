I could have saved us you know its true.
I could have stop it I still believe in you.
It was all my pain and hate,But I pulled you in forever.
Most think love is a game,I know that love is what you feel never.
I felt you in the dark.
Your sweet kiss in the rain.
But no matter what I can never hear you name.
I find you again someday my love.
I'll get passed the pain and hate.
To find love waiting for me on the other side of fate.
I still believe that your waiting upon the day that I return to kill away the pain is gone.
Hate me now but love me then...
