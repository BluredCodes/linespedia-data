I could sit here for hours and listen to the rain.
But with out you it doesn't sound as beautiful.

I could watch each star each night.
Just to know you watching the same stars.

Everytime I look to pain to light my way.
I turn away and look at you.

I will not leave you.
on my life I swear to you.

My heart is forever your until you give it up.
Please never leave me.

Sometimes I wonder...
Sometimes I think where would I be if I wasn't with you.

But who cares.
I'm where I want to be and i'm happy.

Sometimes I wonder why you love me.
But I know you do.
