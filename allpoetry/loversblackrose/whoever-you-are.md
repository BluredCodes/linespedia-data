title: "Whoever You Are!!!!!!!"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

source: "AllPoetry"

Everyone around you is so happy.
What are you going to do?
Fake your smile then move on.
Make everyone around you happy and then love no more.
What makes you happy?
Why will you wake up tomorrow?
Will you wake up for love?
Will you wake up for me?
When you kissed my neck was it me you tasted?
Could you need me like you need her?
Were you the one I saw in my dreams w/ the heartache?
Are you the one that comes to me after she hurts you?
Who ever you are I love you!
