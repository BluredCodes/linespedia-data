I saw him today a little of my past.
It blew me away to see how he'd changed.
It scared me when he said my name.
I looked at him and didn't even know what to say.
An old crush staying before me.
I remembered my feeling but forgot it fast.
I remember the love I have now.
But I would like to see him again.
And ask him what became of him.
An old flame burned out.
A new flame going on.
