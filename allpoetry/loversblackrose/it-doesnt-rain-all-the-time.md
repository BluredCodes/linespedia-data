title: "It Doesn't Rain All The Time!"

I hear them in my head everyday.
They seem to say the same things.

Wake up flea the world is waiting for you.
I don't even notice when it gets dark.

Its always dark in my mind so whats the differents.
I wish in a way that it would rain forever.

Remember it doesn't rain all the time.
Well it does now...For a long time.

But i'm glad again...For the rain maybe my only real love anymore.
I know he loves me yes this I know.

But sometimes it doesn't feel that way.
I wish things would just get better on its own.

I fear I don't have what it takes to try again.
I wonder is this all a dream or just a nightmare of life?
