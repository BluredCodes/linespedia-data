Just becasue she hates everyone doesn't mean she evil.
Even if she trys to kill me that doesn't mean she can't love.
I call her vernoica.
She is not like everyone else.
She trys so hard to take my life I stoped trying to stop her.
She is so beautiful you just can't see it.
She has a soul like me we just share it.
She means the world to me cause she causes me pain.
Pain I can not give myself.
If you ever meet her don't piss her off.
You may not know it but it could take my life.
I'd like to live to see tomorrow cause now I have a reason a person to live for.
