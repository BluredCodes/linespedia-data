---
title: "Fighting Fate"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

She denys me the two things I need.
But I will get them.
She denys me love and my soulmate.
But no longer will I ask her for help.
I can only trust myself.
I can only look to me for help.
She gives and takes too much.
My pain is the only thing I get to keep.
But no more will I take this pain.
Give me love a reason to live.
If not then deny me life and take it from me.
I ask that you leave me be fate unless you want to bring happiness.
If not cut my wrists,and take my life its nothing without love.
I am nothing without love.
