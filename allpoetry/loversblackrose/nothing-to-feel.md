Nothing left to hold things are fallin around you.
Left to yourself not that fun is it.
All alone wanting needing something better than this.
Living a lie needing the truth.
Asking for help getting only pain.
BUt you like the pain anyway.
Why cry when the pain will soon be gone?
Needed me and things well be ok again.
The rest of the world knows it.
Why not you?
Who told you that love was real?
Whats so good bout the world?
THe pain you gave me I live for that.
To feel it again would kill me.
But I would take that chance again.
The pain in your heart can't be any worse than mine.
Your heart can only be so broken.
Put your life back together.
Take the time to really see the truth again.
Find me in your heart.
And love me for nothing.
Need life to love,love and to live.
Feel love in you but the evil take over your heart.
THe evil is the only thing that really loves you.
Nothing else can get to you like I can.
No one can get under your skin like me.
And thats where I am feeling your pain.
Thats all I wanted was to feel what you do.
But until you let me I feel only my pain.
