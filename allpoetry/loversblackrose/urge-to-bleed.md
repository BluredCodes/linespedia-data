title: "Urge To Bleed"

Have you ever had the urge to make yourself bleed?
To watch blood fall from your arms.
To taste blood from others as well from you.
Blood as sweet as chocolate.
Or as sour as your skin.
Do you not feel the urge inside of you?
The longing in your heart to feel pain.
They painted my heart black.
They took from me love...
They gave back the urge to bleed.
