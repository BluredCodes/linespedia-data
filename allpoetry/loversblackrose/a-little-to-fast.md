title: "A Little To Fast!!!!"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

I grew up just a little to fast.
Did things I shouldn't have.
Love the wrong people.
But I'm still here...Still wishing to be in his arms.
So alone in my own little world.
But happy again evil is back...Also my love for him.
People all the same in this world.
I long to be in another.
