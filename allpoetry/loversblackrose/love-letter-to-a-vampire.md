Dear Souless Vampire,
A bite mark just a little pain.
Things never seem to go away.
Day after day.
Life and love.
Thats not real.
Pain is all you feel.
Faithless is your heart.
Let me show you faith.
Your way back to love,To me.
Bloodstaind paper but can you read my words.
Pain is nothing when you know how to use it.
Some people stop,others go.
Pain will someday stop when your six feet under.
But why six feet?
Why not stay awhile?
Do not die until i've had a chance to say goodbye.
Come back tomorrow night.
My neck will be yours.
To bite and drink all thats left.
Make me pale and sickly like you.
Give me a chance to live forever.
Let me see what its like to be you.
Look into my eyes and say you don't love me.
I know you do.
Make me one of you.
Kill me make me whole again.
I long for your love and hate you when you leave.
I cry for you only a while.
Every night you come to me.
But you always leave me here.
Next time bite me.
Take me with you.
I want to be a vampire with a soul.
