title: "Death In Loves Arms"

I feel sick and helpless.
Your not here to save me.
Where have you gone?
Away forever so you won't have to watch me die.
I give to you my last goodbye and breath away my last words.
To you my love my life was givin.
But no longer will I live.
No longer will I take this world and the people in it.
You where my reason to live.
The room is getting dark and I feel things start to fall.
Don't leave me till my life is gone,please.
I need you just as you need me if only for a little while stay with me.
Remember after i'm gone that i'm am still here.
Don't cry for I haven't gone far.
I'm still in your heart.
No matter what you'll find me there.
You haven't lost me i'm just gone for a little while.
I'm waiting for you where ever I am.
To come back to me.
So we can be free of this world and the pain it's givin us.
I know you feel pain and hate cause I have left you but put those feelings away.
As long as you still love me I will still be in your arms.
You'll always feel my touch and hear my voice.
I'll never be lost to you if you remember.
Remember my life and love.
And never give up on me.
For i'm still here and still loving you.
Never miss what you don't know is gone.
A vampire's touch is how I wanted to die.
But now I just want to die next to my romeo!
