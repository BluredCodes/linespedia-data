---
title: "A Little Happiness"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

Its hard to look at him and think what could have happened?
What if he had died and I was left with nothing?

It's hard to look at him and see what could have been.
We could have been happy.

The three of us.
But now its just one.

How could I let that happen.
For the first time I was happy with bad news.

I'll just have to live knowing that if he's gone.
That little piece of him will no longer be mine.
