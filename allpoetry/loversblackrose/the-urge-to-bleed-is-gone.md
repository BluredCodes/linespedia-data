The urge to bleed is gone.
I no longer need to taste blood to live.
No cuts just scars to eas the pain.
The pain that grows without him here.
But I did what I had to do to live and love again.
But something tells me that i'm going to need me when he comes back again.
When life gets hard and pain is near I have a new love to be here.
Yes I do still miss him so,but I will have to grow up and get over it.
Growing up ok but getting over it i'm not sure I can do that.
I wish I still had the ugre to bleed but its best if it stays away and pain is not that great at all.
So to the person who gave me the urge to bleed give it back.
I ask you please!!!!!!


To a color bring back the urge!!!!!!!!
