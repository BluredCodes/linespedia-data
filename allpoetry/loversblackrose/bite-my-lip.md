---
title: "Bite My Lip"
writer: "LoversBlackRose"

Things come together in hopes to make it better.
But like life it doesn't work like that.
You get tired of people giving up on you.
You try and tell them that you givin up makes it worse.
But they won't listen to you.
Your just there...So they can play with your little head till their done with you.
I bite my lip and shut my mouth but really i'm screaming to get out.
Some people just can't even begin to understand your love or hate.
Someone told me to get what you want you have to work for it.
Well I feel that i'm doing all the work.
And in a way its killing me.
There will always going to be the better half for you.
But for me its lonelyness.
But the being alone no one can hurt me.
I don't want to live but i'm still here.
We're all little pieces in a game.
If you didn't know that its time you did.
This Girl would like to win...But is to weak and helpless cause everyone gives up.
