title: "Together In Our Hearts"

Goodbye are the hardest words to say.
And things can get to be that way.
But sometimes I wonder what do they really mean?
You can say goodbye and leave them be.
But you'll never know what you mean to me.
Things we've done the love and fighting.
The people we hurt with our little games.
They will never forget us,nor will I.
I wish you'd come back say you need me.
We could do so much together,
it would be so much fun you would see.
But we're no longer together you and me.
But in our hearts together will be.
