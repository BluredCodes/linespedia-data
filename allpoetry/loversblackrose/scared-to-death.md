So scared to death...needing help but the one I want it for is just as scared as I.
I wish I could make thigns better just like that.
I know it was our little thing.
But now its gone wrong.
I love him but I feel like I lost him.
So many things so wrong all at the same time.
I wish he loved me  it would help just a little bit with the pain.
