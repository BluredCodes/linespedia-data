It only lasted a minute.
And why i can not say.

But I feel it when he touches me.
It runs through me as blood.
But it's better than blood.

Could it be love or just me wshing it to be?
But does he feel it?
Can he even look at me?
When he looks at me what does he see?

I hope he can see me.
The love in my eyes and 
the longing to hold him once again.

Whatever he can see I hope that maybe it's me.
They took from me what he hasn't givin back.
Please love don't leave me again.
