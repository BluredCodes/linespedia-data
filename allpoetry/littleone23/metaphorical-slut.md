title: "Metaphorical Slut"

Why do I do it?
I don't even like you
I flirt
But not for you
I don't even know if it's for me
I'm hitting on you,
Deal with it
Why do I?
I have not shame
Nor do I contain tact
Am I a slut?
Or just a tease?
Could I even be either?
My legs
Metaphorically wide open
Right in your gaze
Licking my lips
And sucking your fingers
Wait,
Where's your hand?
Why do I?
I don't even like you,
Not really that way
