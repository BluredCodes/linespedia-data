What to do
What to say
I know not
Talking backwards
And thinking forwards
Confusion rots my soul
As words rot my brain
Clashing,
Trashing thoughts
Journeying through
Blinders on
Shields let down
Letting it in
All of it going throught
Paths merging12*11*01,
