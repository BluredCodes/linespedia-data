You are in the distant world, 
Your thoughts around me on the earth!
Calm was the day
Reciting of psalms in nearby church on;
Sounds around deter not my mind!
Hot sun could reach me not 
As under the tree sitting am I!

Children in play, gay and free;
Distant things to mind remind,
Nearby happenings unnoticed by mind,
Alone I am, amidst the crowd
Like a king without crown,
In meditation of love for you,
Revolving around you,
like the earth around the sun!

Birds in a row flying in the sky
To reach the land of summer camp;
Ducks in the lake squawking sounds make,
Herd of sheep following each other meek,
To reach the lake to quench the thirst!

Your touch, the cool winds remind,
With fragrance of flowers varied,
your songs sweet surround,
Myself enjoying your company divine
Hoping to spend rest of my life likewise
Till the day I meet you in Heaven!
