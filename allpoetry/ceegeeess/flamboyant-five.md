Born in mud though,
Is lotus clean and pure,
Like gentleman in a mob!

Still and steady waters
instill clean waters pure,
so do the clear minds!

Pretty little flowers,
Little colourful butterfly
Make lovely match indeed!

Flower to flower 
Buuterfly ever fly,
Like a flirting lover sly!

Trees and land
Barren each other stand
In no man's land!
