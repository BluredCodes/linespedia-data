……….Drench me in showers of love!

Like your curly hair, shall your song travel,
Like pearls your teeth glitter and shine!
Like flowing stream your smile ever shine,
Like promise serene, your love to me divine!

Like the sky is your heart big and broad,
Wherein I live forever with love grand!
When you smile, my mind beguile,
When you sing my blood dances in delight!

When you speak, pearls fall on ground as rain,
I always try to pick them up, in vain!
Your love, a gift from Heaven, by grace of God1
Your beauty and charm, makes me an instant poet!

You are like sparkling light
 Ubiquitous and permeate,
You are like rain in sunlight,
Tickling warm and soothing and 
Drench me in showers of love!Love is everything fine and every thing fine is love!
