The age of innocense is over.  
Vengeance shall be mine tonight
The lamb shall devour the lion
Mercy shall not be my sword
And forgiveness shall not be my shield
You brought darkness to my life
Only fair that I give it back
Your nightmare will be my pleasureDo not worry.  I am not really angry at anyone.  I just wrote this to see if I could sound convincing in the matter.  So don't worry...all is well.  This is a total fictional piece.
