Black rancid milk
On a hot summer day
Is what I dream of
To satisfy my thirst
POOP BE THY NAME

Swimming in a pool of vinegar
After being sunburn all over my body
Would be most tempting
On a day like today
POOP BE THY NAME

French kissing
A rabies infected dog
After its been highly agitated
Would be so refreshing
POOP BE THY NAME

Eating rotten eggs
That have been cooked
On the hood of a hot car
Would be yummy right about now
POOP BE THY NAME

POOP BE THY NAME?
What does this poem have to do
WITH POOP?
