---
title: "Leave me be"
writer: "Heart Broken Lover"
slug: "leave-me-be"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/59212-Leave-me-be-by-Heart-Broken-Lover"
---

You know how i feel
So why say what you do
It seems to be unreal
you seem to be untrue

And you rip me apart 
with the brutal things you say
I cant take anymore of this
I just look away

Stop with your lies
Just Leave me be
No matter how hard i try
you'll never ever see

Leave me be
pick up and go
Cant you see?
doesnt it show?

Leave me to myself
I can manage on my own
I dont need your help
you can Leave me alone

Maybe in time
you'll finally see
Finally understand.......
finally leave me be
