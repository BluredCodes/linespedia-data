That night not too long ago
When all these things started going wrong
My intentions were not that of the results
I havent been here very long

That night still haunts me
all the things that people said
Did i follow my heart?
Or did i follow my head?

In looking out for a friend
I betray anothers trust
she walks away in silence
Follow her i must

At that moment its hopeless
I cant make her speak
Slams the door in my face
My heart grows weak

Return to the circle
Of freinds who can save me
At this moment im told
she said that she hates me

From this point on
I couldnt bring myself to talk
she Refuses to leave,i scream and yell
Put on my coat and walk

A sleepless night
Too much on my mind
a hopeless fight
