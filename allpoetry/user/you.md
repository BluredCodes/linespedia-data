Seeing with my mind and not with my eyes,
Sleepwalking through life I scream.
The vociference makes me alert again.
Exiting the womb of delusion,
Visiting a place from the past,
I breathe in gasps of air purified by a transparent mind.
The opaqueness has vanished,
Leaving me in solitude,
To taste the bitterness of almonds,
And the excitement of lima beans.
I still feel you.
The mist of your cloud descends upon me,
Shades my reason,
Undresses my thought,
Like a surreal shower,
When I hang up my towel.
My hair stands tall again; soldiers on my flesh,
Engaging in war,
Resolutely prepared to battle the invited enemy.
