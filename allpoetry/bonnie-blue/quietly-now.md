Quietly now…
Don’t break
This peace,
This crystalline moment
In time and space,
Where I can 
See you
In my mind’s eye…
Sleeping…
Dreaming…
Hopefully of me…
Rest, my love.
Tomorrow, the world
Reclaims you;
Responsibility anchors you
To a ship slowly sinking.
I beg you, 
Be not the captain
Going down with his ship.
You will drown…
There is no hope there.

Someday,
Our time will be here,
Yours and mine;
I am ready to be your 
Heart’s dearest desire
And your helpmeet.
I can, you know…
You know my strength…
I’ve survived worse than this.
I’ll get through it.
But I am worried for you…

Let me be a part of
Your solution, your comfort;
Use me to lean on.
I want that…
I need to know
That you trust me enough
To take me in,
And let me show you
The depth and breadth
Of this precious love
I have for you.





Bonnie Cook
