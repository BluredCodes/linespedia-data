I’ve read too many posts lately
With titles like”last post”
And “not a poem”
Well I am ANGRY now
How DARE one single person here
Have the unmitigated AUDACITY
To judge the work of a poet’s pain
---the BLOOD on the page,
the TEARS of anguish, the AGONY
of despair---
WHO AMONG US CAN SAY THAT IS WRONG?

AND HOW DARE YOU????

Who decides what makes one work worthy
And another not, based upon what the poet
Reveals about her pain, his depression, 
A background of horrific abuse
Or the cumulative sorrow of repeated 
Abandonment?  Who?  Is there someone
So authoritative on what a poem is
Or is not
That that person is in a position to
Deliver a proclamation that 
“No one wants to hear all of your problems,
so pack it in, and save us the sad story?”
Huh?
WHO???

I have my suspicions.  I’ll keep them to myself,
But know this:
YOU KNOW WHO YOU ARE.
Are you proud of yourself now?

Another gifted poet has bidden us farewell,
Burdened further than she already was
By the thoughtless, insensitive weight 
Of some FOOL’S worthless opinion.
She didn’t deserve it.  
She deserved the best we can give her,
And I’m sad to say,
Though I did not know her well,
She will be missed.
We will all pay the price, 
And for me, it’s too damned high.

ANYONE ELSE???

Go ahead…give me your best shot.
I can take it.  I only wish she could have.



Bonnie Cook
