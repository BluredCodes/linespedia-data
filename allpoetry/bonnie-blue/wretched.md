Wretched is lonely
Pain on a pin
Sticking in heart
Holes leaking blood
Drips to floor
Of soul there puddling
Tears add to pain
Sobs bring agony
Tearing and ripping
Screams from throat
Touch me touch me please
Heart begs so keenly
Desperate soul longs
For communion 
With yours
Eyes spill sorrow
Salty tears burning
Carve into face
Their stained rivers of need
Lonely is wretched
All for thee



Bonnie Cook
