title: "I Try"
writer: "bonnie blue"

I try not to let my heart beat too fast
When your words grip my heart so tightly
That it feels they will pull it free of my chest…

I try to be calm, unheeding of the way
You glance my way, pretending not to look,
Yet knowing you see my longing for you.

I feign nonchalance when I hear your name,
So that no one guesses my sorrow at losing you;
I can’t let on how this pain undoes me

I want to be happy in all the gifts of life;
Though the one I miss most is denied me…
The light of your soul’s brightness…

I strive to forget…but I can’t…I still miss you…
I still need your love so deeply
That it shatters me from inside out…

I am trying to tell you, my love,
Of the loss, the aching loneliness,
The deep and profound anguish…of missing you...




Bonnie Cook
