Is this for real?
Am I only wishing?
Pretending you love me,
And hoping for the impossible?
My heart is waiting
Waiting for your touch
Waiting to breathe again
If only you love me…
I hear you whisper
But I need you to say it
So that I know it’s real.

And if it is…
I am joy…



Bonnie Cook
