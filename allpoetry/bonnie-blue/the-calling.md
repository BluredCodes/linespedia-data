Come to the table
Eat, and be filled
Drink, slake your thirst
This is the table 
Of humanity’s last supper
Our last chance
~the last resort~
On the highway of life.
No reservations are needed
Only come, come now.

Many are gathered here
There is plenty for all
An abundance of food
Wine flows for all
Come, hurry now…
This is our destiny, 
Now is the time.
The message is clear
You must hear it
We must not be late.

There be music
Song and dance for the soul
There be poetry
To fill the heart with love
There is wonder and awe
To keep us childlike in spirit
There be magic and stories
For we must not forget miracles.
Come now…it grows late.

This is our last chance.
Blessed be.



Bonnie Cook
