title: "A Fool, Still Missing You"
writer: "bonnie blue"

Call me a fool, for I still miss you;
Touching your  heart
Filled mine with such joy…
Now, more than a corner of it
Lies empty and aching,
Occupied only by lonely tears.
Perhaps I should not miss you so---
You probably have someone new
To while away the hours with,
Someone who excites you,
Much more than I could---
Or who is less real and human,
With lesser scars than mine.
I surely cannot blame you 
For your reluctance to get closer;
You feared my flame,
And perhaps rightly so…
Nothing I can say about it
Will convince you 
That I would never let it burn you,
Far from it…you came very close
To taming it, in fact,
Before you turned away.
Now my wanting feeds it yet again.
And it still burns so within me.

And oh…how I do still miss you.




Bonnie  Cook
