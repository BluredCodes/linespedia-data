A good day, this day…
Filled with friends and poetry
(my favorite obsessions;)
gleaning treasures from out
the accumulated debris of
words.  Holding each one
up to the light, peering closely
at it, deciding its worth in stars
and pronouncing it good or
somehow lacking.
Some will remain in the mind
And continue to speak to me
Many will be forgotten---
For who can truly remember
All of this in its entirety?
No I…

I hope I have touched someone;
I hope I have learnt something;
I hope I have painted a picture
Of my mind, that someone else
may see within me,  and share my bounty.

May tomorrow be as good
As this day has been.



Bonnie Cook
