Judge me not
So harshly that
You forget
We are all human…

Chain me not
Take not my wings
You and I are
Not so different…

Break not my heart
In careless passion
Turn it not 
To lifeless stone…

Pity me not
I’ve made my choices
Understand
The reasons why…

Rape not my soul
I will avenge it
I am the Phoenix
Risen from the ashes…

And now I burn…




Bonnie Cook
