Today the calm
Returns to reclaim me;
I am grateful to soak it up
Into every pore of my being.
A fleet glimpse of tranquility
…mid torrent…
it was more than I’d hoped for.

Outside my window,
Pale forsythia
Glows faintly yellow,
Lone flowers seeking
Spring sunlight;
Laid upon a background
Of grey-barked trees;
Footed by grass barely greening.

The sun washes all with
Light gold,
Paling the sky to delicate azure.




Bonnie Cook
