title: "Awakening: An Arrival"
writer: "bonnie blue"

God spoke, 

And I listened

and heard…

His voice reached me

through an online friend,

making a suggestion

that changed my life 

beyond imagination.

An invitation

to read his work

turned me 

onto a new path

and opened my heart

to its blooming

like morning’s scarlet glory.

(I would have laughed

at such a statement, 

had it been heard by

[hidden] inner mother.

who would have sneered.

‘You have only memories

of youthful flowered dreams, and

you were long since deflowered ‘

[the wry cruel snicker]

You have nothing to say!

For what is your life

if not ordinary?

And you would 

call yourself poet?

Ha!’)



Grateful am I 

that my ear 

was attuned elsewhere;

I did not hear these

thoughts

of self-mocking 

destruction.



Now

I cast my lot

on this ground;

my heart, given voice.

Home I have come

to share with my kin, 

bidden to give our gifts…

our souls: stirring the words

to paint portraits

of lives lived;

love gained…and lost;

adventures lived;

heroes: made…and unmade…

likely or unlikely;

nightmares screamed.

dreams envisioned.

Filled I am

with the beauty 

of simple breathing,

with the laughter 

and the tears

of these souls, my kindred;

with the awesome power

of winged hope

streaming from these hearts 

bruised and scarred, yet perfect;

impaired, suffering,

yet

flying 

as angels.



I am blessed.







Bonnie Cook
