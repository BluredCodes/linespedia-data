title: "The Darkness is Alive"

Tumble down a long flight of stairs
Into depression; here I am again.
Black tunnel.  No light.
No end anywhere in sight.
No hand held out to guide me.
No voice to comfort me.
No end to this bleeding
Needing sadness
Soaking my soul.

Yes…the darkness is alive
And it’s hungry
And I’m alone here
In the darkness
As it begins to
Digest me.
I can feel it
Drawing me 
Down its throat
Down the long slide of (depression)
Into its belly, deep
Where I can’t see out
The hungry darkness 
Loves me.
Caresses me.
Seduces me.

I never knew the bottomless pit
Of despair
Could be so tender a lover…

I have no wish
To rise from this
I have no strength
To fight it off
It feels like my only friend
The one whose faithfulness will never end…




Bonnie Cook
