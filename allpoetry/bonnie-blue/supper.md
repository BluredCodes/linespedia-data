Twirl me up like spaghetti
On your fork of desire
Gather me into your mouth
And chew me gently

Massage me with warm oil
Of your wanting
Season me with sav’ry herbs
And breathe me in

Garnish me with rosebuds
Like frosting on a cake
Take a fingerful of sweet
And lick me till I’m gone
