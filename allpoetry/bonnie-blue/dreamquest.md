Spirit take me

To wander sky

Cloudtop

Starshine

Light falls

Darkness 

Deep 

Infinitely blue

Reigns.

I am time:

Creeping across life;

Inching forward

Sometimes lurching

Never a surety.

Circle a globe

Enshrouded;

Spark to life.

Lightning ignites…

Thunder is broken,

Causing fall of rain

To ground;

My heart calls out

For flood…

Grant me one wish

In my dying:

Tell me

Where spirit goes

At day’s 

End.







Bonnie Cook
