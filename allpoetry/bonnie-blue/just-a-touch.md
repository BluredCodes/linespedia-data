---
title: "Just a Touch"
writer: "bonnie blue"

Just a touch
from your hand
sears me
with desire
so strong
that I
may die

In your arms
I feel
no hesitation
at all
I give
myself
completely
to you
open
laid bare

Handle me
with care
tenderly
my heart
is full
and
my need
so great
I will
perish
if you
let go
now




Bonnie Cook
