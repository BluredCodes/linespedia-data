title: "Portent of Evil"

The demon is come.
It creeps boldly in,
Brazenly showing itself
Just enough to be seen 
Out of corners of eyes.
It touches things
And leaves a mark,
An indelible stain:
Putrefaction.
Madness.
Watch closely its movements,
As it slithers about
In its sick quest.
It is very clever;
Be on guard for your very life.
It wants to know 
Your weaknesses.
It seeks your hidden entries.
I pray you, do not reveal them;
If you do not guard against it,
It will penetrate your defenses 
Before you know it’s invaded you.
If it touches you,
You will never be free
Of its stench.
It will own you.
Don’t let it in.  
Don’t let it near.
Keep your fences closed and
Your windows barricaded
Keep it out.
Keep it at bay.
Bind its hands 
And shackle its feet.
Cast it out,
Deprive it of life.
Demand its departure!
Drive out
This sinister being of evil!
