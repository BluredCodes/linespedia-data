title: "Gather Me In"

Gather me in
Make me warm
And safe
I need a cocoon
To conceal and protect me
Allow me room
To grow wings
To fly with you
To heights unexplored
Feelings untouched yet
I ask you to be
Open
As no other has
Though I have tried
With others before you
See me as possibility
Unrealized wonder
Not limitation
Or bondage
I am not a chain of steel
I am a soft silken ribbon
Wrap me around you
And gather me in.




Bonnie Cook
