---
title: "I Remember Your Sweetness"
writer: "bonnie blue"
slug: "i-remember-your-sweetness"
source: "AllPoetry"
url: "https://allpoetry.com/poem/62029-I-Remember-Your-Sweetness-by-bonnie-blue"

A deep pang
just surprised
my heart
a knife
thrust in clean
unexpectedly
Withdrawn
with the blood
of my love
still upon it

I remember
your sweetness
and how
you made
me feel
light
giddy
warm
elated

Remembering
those feelings
I long for them still
more than ever

Please stop my tears
these drops of sorrow
that slide
down my soul
leaving trails
of open wounds
that will never
close.

As long as I live
I will never
forget you
or stop hoping
that someday
the door
may open
to me again
And let me
into your heart.



Bonnie Cook
