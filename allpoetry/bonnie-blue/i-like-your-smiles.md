Precious, this…
Chance meeting across ether…
Spinning smiles and conversation
Shyly at first, then more boldly
Asking questions just to see the answers
Wondering always
What each other’s expression might be
What is being thought?
Exchanging information from mundane
To momentous, randomly
Somehow a sense of intimacy is built
Eventually;  and perhaps a new friendship
Will be born. 

* S *

I do like your smiles….:)



Bonnie Cook
