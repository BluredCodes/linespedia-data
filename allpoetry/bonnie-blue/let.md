Let the words come
Let the spirit rejoice
Let the day begin
Let me have peace
Let  my feelings show
Let my heart be free
Let my mind be open
Let me reach my potential
Let me understand
Let me have wisdom
Let my path be clear
Let there be adventure
Let me see light and hope
Let me share my light
Let love conquer hatred
Let me know love
Let me return that love
a hundred-fold
Let the sky be clear
Let me see the sun
And feel its warmth
Let me walk with the moonlight
Let the world be at peace
Let the hungry be fed
Let all wounds heal
Let me help the healing
Let me hear God speak
Let me help others hear Him
Let me see the sunset
Let me hear birds calling
Let the heavens sing
In their eternal glory
Let me rise to meet my destiny
Let life have its way with me
Let me sing
Let me laugh
Let me learn.



Bonnie Cook
