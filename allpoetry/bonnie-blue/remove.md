---
title: "Remove"
writer: "bonnie blue"
slug: "remove"
source: "AllPoetry"

Remove this tumor
This strangle of pain
That chokes my breath
And makes me wish for death
An end to sorrow
I’ve tried to live with it
But it demands too much of me
Pluck out this stone
This ruddy boulder
That burns my brain
Till I absorb its stain
I slept with it for years
But it only wants to smother me
With its weight
Cut from me this growth
This false appendage
That feigns intention
To claim the blood of glory
But leaves me wanting and broken
Not least, alone
Shave off this hair
This layer of hiding
That fails to shield me
From swords of repugnance
Reveal my sin
Reveal my beauty
Absolve my guilt
In my own undoing…




Bonnie Cook
