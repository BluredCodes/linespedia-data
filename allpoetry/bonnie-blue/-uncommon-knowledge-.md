Your mind

                 Skips all over the galaxy
                 
                 Delighting me…amazing me

E x p a n  d    i    n    g

[My boundaries]

Of  *knowing* 

It is enough to know today
That together we take this journey
Seeking 
            uncommon
                               knowledge.


             ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~



Bonnie Cook
