I never learned love,
Until you touched me,
Took my heart
With your smile,
So radiantly caring.
I lacked, though I knew not what
Or why.
I stumbled from heartache 
To heartbreak, blindly
Searching in vain for that
Someone who would stay true
Stand tall beside me
Make me smile with joy
Hold me when I cried
Touch me with passion’s fire
Love me till I died.
In a lifetime,
I did not expect 
To find a love like this.
Till you.




Bonnie Cook
