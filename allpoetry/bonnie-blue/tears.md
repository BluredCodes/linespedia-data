Tears, my love…
I have cried so many.
Heart heavy with sorrow,
Seeing only empty tomorrows.
A life without you in it
Felt like no life at all.
The silence stood between us
Like a steel door.
Cold.  Locked.  Impenetrable.

Tears.  Only tears…
Death could not have been worse.

Till today…
And now there is this
Bright jewel of knowing
That you still love me,
Still care…because you told me so…
A gift I did not expect;
One that will be cherished always
And will keep my heart filled
And open to you.

Still there are tears, my love…
But they are tears of the greatest joy
I have ever known.
I love you with all of my heart
Every fiber of my soul
Every breath I breathe.
I thank God for your love,
For I know it is only
With His blessing
That our hearts will be one.
Believe this, my love.
It is true.



Bonnie Cook
