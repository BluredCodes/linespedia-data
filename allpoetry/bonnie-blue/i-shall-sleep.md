I shall sleep…in time,
When this warmth fades away…
This desire has taken me
Into a place 
where fantasy lives
and plays…
my composure has disintegrated,
utterly evaporated…
but you know that, don’t you?
I smile at the thought of you,
knowing I won’t need a blanket tonight.




Bonnie Cook
