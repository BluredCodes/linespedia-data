Begun at center
Was I ever?
No.

Wind me up
you the master
of this puppet’s
inner machinery

tighten the spring
strengthen my spine
resolve
my void

pull me back
higher: test my fear
don’t let me hide

nudge me
toward motion
release my inertia
free me

arc
find the center
at the boundaries
of the pendulum’s
swing





Bonnie Cook
