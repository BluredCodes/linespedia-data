---
title: "I Am Perplexed"
writer: "bonnie blue"

I am perplexed by the notion
That love in its pain is all the more precious
For the stone it places in a sad heart.

I am perplexed at the idea
That hatred can grow so rampantly
In the human soul, where it is alien.

I am perplexed knowing the power
Of the human mind, with such potential;
Rarely reaches the zenith of its vision.

I am perplexed at our blatant disregard
For the earth, our Great Mother, who weeps
As we poison her body.

I am perplexed by our greed for material things
When we have been given everything
We will ever need, and then some.

I am perplexed by the lack of gratitude
That we give to God, who gave all to give us life
And the free choice of how to live it.

I am perplexed
I am saddened
I am afraid
I am wondering…




Bonnie Cook
