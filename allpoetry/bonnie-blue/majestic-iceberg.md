title: "Majestic: Iceberg"

Majestic floating mountain

Amputated from your glacial home

And set on a journey

Propelled by ancient current of ocean

You are but a fragment

Of history’s times frozen

And bound in ice;

Eons fractured in your

Frigid blue fissures,  upthrust

In steep virginal cleavage

Mammoth;  snow adorned.

Hidden below in clandestine chasms

Lie veiled caverns exultant

In glistening illumined lines 

Revealed beneath the ocean’s cloak

By sunlight’s piercing lance

Concealing a heart titanic 

Yet  unbeheld by human eyes

Till chance rendezvous---

This magnificent spectacle of nature’s creation.

I witness communion 

Of God with his earth

In awed obeisance.









Bonnie Cook
