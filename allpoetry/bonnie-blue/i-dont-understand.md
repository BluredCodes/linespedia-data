I don’t understand:
Why did you say that?
What signal did I give
Without knowing I had?
Was I smiling too much?
Oozing innuendo
Through the screen?
I am flustered, unsure
How to react to you,
For I like you, you see
Although we’ve only 
Spoken once or twice…
So why did you say that?
Can we please back up
And begin again, 
More slowly this time?



Bonnie Cook
