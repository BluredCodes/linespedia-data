title: "Pondering the Parallels"
writer: "bonnie blue"

Parallels keep running through my life,
And I ask why?  Why does this question
Appear again… and again?
Why are there so many coincidences 
that beg acknowledgement, yet
Aspire to be more than coincidence?
From where have these kindred spirits
Come…that lead me to the light?
Or is it, what are they, not from where?

Opportunity… so much depends upon
Our perception of this:  for you cannot
Receive what you fail to recognize.
Opportunity: it is this that God offers;
And the voice of God may take any form
That He wishes, to speak to us.
Any form at all…a bird singing in a certain tree;
A gentle breeze brushing against the cheek;
A raindrop, splashing onto thirsty ground;
The child next door playing in the sun;
Or the falling angelic snowflakes.
He may speak through the next person you meet,
Or the person typing the words 
On your screen…from the other side
Of somewhere, somewhen…
And since you can never know
When God may send the next opportunity
---a thing ever changing---
Be ready to know it, when it comes.  
Be listening always.
For all too soon, chances slip away
Lost forever; a path not discovered, 
Nor any foot set upon it,
Unless you are listening: 
Then will your path open.

These are things my journey
Have brought me to know:
Follow blindly not
Those who do not understand this:
That you receive not what you
Have never asked for;
Your request is answered,
Even before the question
Yet clothes itself in words.
Know that God understands
Deepest feelings, thoughts unexpressed,
Sorrows untold and joys yet to be born.
These are in His understanding
Because He gave them to us
The tools we would need in life
On all levels, in the here,
In the unseen beyond.
Know that the mind of God
Is our mind as well,
As borne of Him we are.
Made of His essence
Enlivened by His breath
Found in His excellence. 
Know that God above all
Is love,  in all its permutations
Those that we know of
And those we do not,
Those not yet achieved.
Love is the highest, purest
Form of energy existing;
Love will transform us,
When we truly let it.

These are my sacred truths.

So I go on, mindful of
The parallels in life;
Joining other souls, other lights,
Partaking of their gifts;
Sharing what I can give.
No longer am I surprised 
By the merging of threads 
at once both straight 
and intersecting; 
for I am learning
That all these lines lead me ever
Onward down my path
And I am enriched
In the pilgrimage.




Bonnie Cook
