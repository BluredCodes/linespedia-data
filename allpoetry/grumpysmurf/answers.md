---
title: "Answers"
writer: "grumpysmurf"

It hurts to look at you.
You're sad to see.
You have yet to achieve your goals.
Everything is wrong.
You look as though you've turned away from me.
It hurts to know what you do.
I've never tried to hurt you.
I will help with anything you'd like.
Please don't turn away.
I will always be with you.
You're unsure of where you are going.
You tend to push me away.
If you let me in your heart.
