---
title: "Death Please"
writer: "grumpysmurf"

I'm filled with rage
I hate myself
I hate him
I hate love
I hate life
The only way I'll feel better
Is if I am dead
Please kill me
I don't care how
I don't care where
Just please do it soon
Shoot Me
Cut Me
Make Me Bleed
Hang Me
Choke Me
For Death I Plea
Give me death
Take my life
That will fix my problems
If you love me
Show me so
Anyway you'd like
Shoot Me
Cut Me
Make Me Bleed
Hang Me
Choke Me
For Death I Plea
