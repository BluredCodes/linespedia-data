Have I disappointed you?
Have I let you down?
Did I not follow through with your plans?
Is there something wrong?
Do you not like the way I look?
Did I break your heart?
Or are you the one that broke mine?
Will you help me?
Do I need help?
Are you going to leave me?
Am I lost?
Am I losing you?
Will you stay?
