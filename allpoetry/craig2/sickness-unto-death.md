---
title: "‘sickness unto death’"

what he believed
he knew
what he knew
was true

this for him
pristine
a church bell
in a light snow
layering metaphysics

once replete
contagious
pact with reality
reworked

the truth poser
rang
pealed
the sanctity
of doubt

with an immoral 
aroma
of almost rain
sat him down
to one riddle
at a time
