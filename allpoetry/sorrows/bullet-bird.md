The Bullet Bird

The Bullet Bird, or Bulletis Ectium, is native to any war-stricken country. 
The scent of bloodshed attracts them
They are violently aggressive by nature
They possess a lush silver plumage
The Bullet Bird lives under a strange code of dependence
It cannot survive without first finding a host species
That species is most commonly the rare Gunne
The Bullet Bird is carnivorous and feeds mostly on humans
The bite of the Bullet Bird is venomous and most often deadly
 Unlike most species there are groups fighting to make the Bullet Bird extinct
The Bullet Bird isn’t born like other feathered creatures
It is born when the Hate Hare and War Warthog mate
They are born at full maturity
When hunting the Bullet Bird’s technique is to drive itself at an unsuspecting target
If it hits the human, the human will most likely die
If that’s the case then the Bullet Bird feeds off the other humans’ reaction of horror
Transforming it into energy to keep itself alive
Unfortunately Bullet Birds continue to thrive at an amazing population
We can only hope one day they will die out
But it doesn’t look likely
