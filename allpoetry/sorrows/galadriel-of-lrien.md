Inspired and written about a character from LOTR, which is © JRR Tolkien. 

Galadriel of Lórien

Her skin was rumored ivory-white
Her hair like gossamer gold
Ethereal and wise alike
or so the legends told

Fair lady of Lothlorien
I saw you once by chance
And when I eyed your features fair
I stayed my hoisted lance

I bid my pony to retreat
A frown upon my face
So lowly are us mortal men
