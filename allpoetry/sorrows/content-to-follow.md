Based on a character [Samwise] from LOTR, which is © JRR Tolkien.

Content to follow
[Samwise thinking about Frodo]

I never wanted glory
not any of my own
just to see you safe
to see you riding home

Each minuscule smile 
that crossed your tired face
made me this much happier
to keep myself in place

Never much for fighting
I'd rather sing a song
But I'll stay here beside you
While the battle rages on

Take my food if you are hungry
If your tired take my bed
I'll give you all I have to give
Until I'm left for dead
