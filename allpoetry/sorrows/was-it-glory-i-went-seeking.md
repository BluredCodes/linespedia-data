Was It Glory I Went Seeking?

Was it glory that I went seeking
when I ventured through the door
Now I’ve come to think my claim
is not as honest as before

For battle wasn’t what I sought
Though that I did declare
And ever my mind left the war
and you I pictured there

Your warm smile came to haunt me
As the foul armies clashed
And I lay down my mighty sword
And rode into the past

The cottage still stood lonely
Waves lapping at the shore
I said a prayer and held my breath
and knocked upon the door

Somehow you were waiting
and pushed the door ajar
inside I was welcomed
for I had traveled far

I did not bring home glory 
But in my heart I knew
Dishonor was the price to pay
To unearth my love for you
