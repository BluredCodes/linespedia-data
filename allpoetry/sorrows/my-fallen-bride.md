This isn’t a poem, but a Celtic song. The tempo is very quick and steady, with a cheerful air to it, although it doesn’t fit with the mood of the lyrics. Meant to be sung alongside a fiddle.

My Fallen Bride

There’s one thing I remember
when I wake to each new day
and that, love, is how pretty
you were that evening gray

Your cheeks were flushed with strawberry tint
although the day was cold
and on your shoulders there did rest
fair locks of glistening gold

A smile was on your lovely lips
and through it all could see
that even in the worst of times
you still had smile for me

So simple was your life before
I don’t think you deserved
to be exacted into things
and voice for whom you served

Battle never was your craft
And you never did excel
For ‘twas not but seven days
Before in fight you fell

Four seasons now it has been
since you last drew a breath
and still I sit beside your tomb
and love you despite death
