extensivly happy
thats how shes described
but thats not really her
inside lays something sinister
thats tired of wearing a smile
that hates being the cheerful one
that just wants to scream
just wants to cry
wants to get angry
this person inside is never let out
is completly different from what people see
she hates and she loves and she cries
and shes trapped
inside herself
