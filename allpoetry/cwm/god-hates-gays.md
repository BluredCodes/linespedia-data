"God hates gay's,"
that's what a religious man says,
but we aren't suppose to hate,
isn't hate a sin?

"God hates gay's,"
being gay is evil,
but how evil is gay?
Does being gay make you evil?

"God hates gay's,"
but dosen't God love us all?
Dosen't God love murderers?
Dosen't God love burgluars?

What about the pain,
homosexuals take,
the stress, the worries, of living.
Wheres the respect, love, for all men?

I have a friend, who's homosexual..
he loves his family,
he loves himself,
he gives to the homeless,
he gives to various charities.

Hate!!!
Is given to him,
being sent to hell,
is his soul purpose.

Who are we?
We are men,
we all have sinned,
we all have made mistakes,
we all have hurted someones feelings,
we all need somebodies hand.
That hand may be given,
from a homosexual,
a homo, whose heart is filled with love,
yet, God hates gay's.
