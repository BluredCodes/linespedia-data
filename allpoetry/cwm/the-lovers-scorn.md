No! the anguish of a lover's scorn,
thy secret strife, not known when born,
remembrance seeping with full of plight,
falling, dead mistletoes, fall through-out the night!

Pain, unbearable,savaging,
feel thy pain, from the cries of ravaging.
The nighttime tragedy, come to rend.
petals of tears, drown gloomy souls, till the very end!

~CWMUnbearable, the fall of love, ouch! ~CWM
