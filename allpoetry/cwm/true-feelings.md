Words written,
words spoken.
Tears falling,
anger provoking.
A child laughter,
a babies smile,
gettin up so early,
just to run five miles.

Love embracing,
love invoking hearts racing,
a fat old gump, sitting complaining,
as a lion roars, showing pride,
with his brown mane!

Hugs, hugs, hugs,
my shirt you continue to tug,
as we fall, happily unto the laying rug,
as a baby brother, to a big brother,
is as irratating as a pest a bug.

A dog chasing a cat,
a professional baseball player,
chasing a pitcher, with his bat,
Mary shows her love, for a guy name Jack!

True feelings feel the air,
hearts awake, tempers flare.
Not knowing, what awaits that day,
in your heart, true sentinments are born, to stay!
