A broken marriage,
a broken home,
vows forsaken,
love taken, from the throne.
Separation, segregation,
reckless anticipation
failed romance, complete frustration!!!

Husband fighting,
wife cursing,
tears cascading, scorn bursting!
Children separated,
court appointments, for child support,
a child, now from one parent,now deports.
As a father's anguish is known with deplore,
bickering, resentment, becoming a repititious bore!

Years of regret,
years you'll never forget.
J.R taken from his little brother,
now he has no fun.
Disagreements came,
bitter anger, still reamins,
marriage is over, now complain!

A broken marriage,
a broken home,
vows forsaken,
love, and a family, are taken,
from a throne.

~CWMMy father too, had this expierence, as a result, he only had a relationship with his father, when he was dying from cancer. And My father's father, I never got the chance to meet him. Separation, just destroys friendships, and family.
                                      ~CWM
