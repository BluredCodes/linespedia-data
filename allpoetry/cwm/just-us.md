I wish, we lived,
on a deserted island,
living close to the shore,
not worrying about our problems,
no problems, no more,
living together just us.

Why do so many distractions,
seem to follow you, follow me?
Why is the world, trying to separate,
true love?
Wish the world would disappear,
everyone but us, just us.

Sitting by the board walk,
feeling the sand, beneath my feet,
dreaming of our world,
our tower of dreams,
loving from dusk till dawn,
with us, just us.

Riding on this boat,
feeling the wind, blow our hair,
dreaming of our domain,
our tower of dreams,
our happiness, without a fuss,
with us, just us.

Maybe one day...
when fate cooperates,
and our hearts, bleed for gleem,
we'll live in a world,
without our pain, our sadness, 
with you and me, us, just us.

Let's live in world,
with us, just us, just us....

~CWMHate distractions, specially, distracting of love.
~CWM
