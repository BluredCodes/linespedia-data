Ah! anguish creeps,
into the darkness of the light,
my fragile soul, weeps,
as I cower, into the blackening mist!
Joyous delight, has been forge with depression,
as I reap, the distraught, of my unrequited passions!
Remorse is my excuse, plight, I used to abuse.
It hath displeased my drowning sentiments,
because of a single mistake of abandonment.

But I beg thee for one more day,
beg for forgiveness  as I wail in sorrow!!!
Let euphoria  fill this solemn day,
cast the joys of heedless bliss...
rebuke my guilt  so this irrevocably fate,
will seem  worth-while again!

Give me one more day,
So I can adjure
for I won't delude,
I won't fail,
I cannot  must not fail!
Eternal elation will elude  thy touch,
doing the twilight hours
if convincing isn't sufficient.

Oh! Terminate this animosity,
so I can escape the grips of insanity!!!!

~CWMMy plead - forgive me.
