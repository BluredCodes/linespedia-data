Songs Of Songs  2:16-17 N.I.V

"My lover is mine and I am his;
he browses among the lilies.
Until the day breaks
and shadows flee,
turn, my lover,
and be like a gazelle
or like a young stag
on the rugged hills."

It's true, my love,
my love is the tears, that flow,
the snow which falls,
and the rain, that drips...
Seek now, my love,
thy joy is within thy loving heart,
embrace thee, with full adoration, 
let's walk together, along with the herds of sheep,
let's kiss together, as the sun sparks, in this summer day.

~CWMDoing a project: was reading the "Holy" Bible last night, when I came across, these beautiful verse's.. so I decided, to reply, and explore into thier meaning... ~CWM
