Walk with me dear friend
I'll never leave you, through thick and thin.
Who cares about the past
Who cares who dies last
Walk with me, so we can have a blast!

Want to let you know,
I'll never let you go
I give you my heart and soul,
Even a gentle rose, a mistletoe
Let your smile show, let it glow!

Walk with me
Walk in unity
Together we can lead us, to victory,
Accomplishments, sucess, becomes reality
Let dreams come true, while walking with me.

Will you give me your hand?
How much punishment can you with stand?
Join, the march, as we preach respect, for every man.

~CWMThe hand is open. ~CWM
