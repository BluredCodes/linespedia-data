What does faith do,
when you lie in a death-bed?
What does faith do,
when your money falls through a large hole?
What does faith do,
whe opression, becomes depressing?
What's the rationality of faith?
The will to believe,
the hope for change,
the hope, that one day,
all troubles will cease!
Does these wishe's, pleads, trascend,
to a God, or do they fly in the surface in your mind?
Tragedies are played in movies,
tragedies are played in life,
without hope, why not give up?
Without hope, wheres the mark of change?
If life seems hopeless,
then the rationality of faith is surpported.
