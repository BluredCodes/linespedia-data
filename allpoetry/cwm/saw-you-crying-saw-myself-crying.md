title: "Saw You Crying, Saw Myself Crying"
writer: "Cwm"

I saw you cry today,
but I had no sympathy.
Saw you wail away today,
but I didn't care.
There you was,
crying over, little me.
Why? I'm not worth it,
look at how, i'm laughing at you, right now.

I saw you cry today,
but hey, it's not me crying,
so what do I care!
I'm a selfish, uncaring little boy,
who lives with no joy!
Yet, I see you smiling at me,
I see you trying to console me.
Forget it!
It's not going to work,
stop crying, get up,
and get the hell out!

I watched you walk away,
screaming, bewailing.
Suddenly I felt ashamed,
why did I mistreat, such wonderful soul...
I still don't know,
and I still see the flashbacks of her sickening sorrow.
I went to lay down, to cool off....
but as I sat, and I began to pray,
I noticed, it wasn't you crying anymore
Today, I saw myself crying.

~CWM
