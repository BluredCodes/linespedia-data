I'm sorry that you've moved here,
life just hasn't been the same,
sorry that your other friends, are no where near,
I know I haven't made it easier, so I take the blame.
If you would've never moved here,
then life would've been so much better,
sorry, that you now, live in fear,
sorry, also that I broke you two, from being together.

Alot of sorrow, and regrets,
so much worries, and remorse,
a huge mistake, was the day we met,
since moving here, you haven't had time to rejoice.
If I could change the time, I would,
must move on, while I see the tears,
falling in love, is your regret, changing I wish I could,
all I wanted was for us, to be happy for many years,
that's impossible, and i'm sorry you've moved here.

~CWM
