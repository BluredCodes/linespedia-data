After many days, and months of separation,
when my days, were filled with guilt,
tonight, this fateful night, all is forgiven.
Harsh words, words that tore us apart,
the weeps within were unheard,
as I layed in a puddle of my tears.
Now tears of joy, have cascade, down my face,
joy, of forgiveness,of regaining our once friendship.
Through many fights, and many regretable words, and actions,
lets start over, lets start anew,
lets show the world, that forgiveness is vitial.

Confusion, disarray,
anger provoked by others involving their-selves,
in matters that wasn't none of their business!
Distractions, came from every corner,
distarctions hid, in every closet.
Now, that my eyes are clear,
and now that my ears, are now open,
I seek the best, I seek to be on good terms.
Through thick, and then,
through ups, and downs,
patience, has been a virture,
through the traveling of fate,
and the forgivness, and kindness of the heart,
i've found, the best part is making up.
