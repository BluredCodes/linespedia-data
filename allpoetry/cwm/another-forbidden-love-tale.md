Our is love forbidden, curse with abomination!
Our love is a travesty, hiding out secrets,
from our families, living double lives,
wrecked with contradictory.
Your Mother, condemns us,
as she has already sent my demise, in hell!
Yet, we sneak away, thrive on forbidden love,
eat from forbidden fruit,drink from deceptive wine.
Holding hands, our implusive actions,
only held together from the grips,
of our hands and the love which bleeds!
Romeo and Juliet of the 21st-century-
Antigone, left alone, covering her fallen love.
Tragedy, agony, lust, love, desire, passion,
all of the emotion stuffed into a bottle,
shipped into the oceans,
and leaving the messages,
rolling into the sand, which sinks.
Tasted from the sweets, scorn from the bitter,
mixture of happiness and harmful plagues.
Finally Romeo dies and juliet is left alone,
frigthened, love so dear, she takes her own life
as we remember, as we look into our dreary, ill fate,
we realize our existence
is stained with the same forbidden curse!

~CWMSomething I wrote, last night, a short exploration. ~CWM
