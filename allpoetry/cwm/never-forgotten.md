title: "Never Forgotten"

Although the sea,
along with the high tides,
drove away our presence,
remembrance, which I thought had to flee,
a sign of hope, lingers, through our memories!

No longer are you by my side,
that reality, the present, I can no longer hide,
I wear your honor, very dear, with strong pride
remembrance, can't be deprived,
as long as we remember those, as long as we strive!

Clarified, by your definite presence,
memorials, past along the reminiscence,
tears stroll down, memories will never fade,
remaining forever in our hearts,
never let the memories drown, into the midst of the sea.

A glowing light, shines onto the sea
a bottle, with a message,
flows whirling, into the shore,
my loved one, last message, is surrounded by the tears,
of my overflowing love!

Preserving the message, before it's rotten,
clutching the passage, I vow, they'll never be forgotten!!!
