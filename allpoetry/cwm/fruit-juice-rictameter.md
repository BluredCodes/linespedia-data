Fruit juice,
sweetly, tasteful,
pure pleasure inside mouth,
many kinds, many fruits, yummy.
Apple juice, grape juice, orange juice,cranberry
mixed them together, more pleasure
pour it, into your mouth
healthy, fruity
fruit juice.YUUUM! Waht som of my juice??? :D ~CWM
