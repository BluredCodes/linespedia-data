It's all over dear friend,
I will not be here,
to say goodbye at the very end.
I'm packing up my bags,
and i'm going for a ride,
from you I chose to hide.

Knowing we'll never regain,
what we once had,
makes my choice, to be less sad.
I've done all I could do here,
wiped away, your tears, protected you,
from your many fears,
you wouldn't listen to me,
with open ears,
now I can't console you,
because i'm no where near.

Wishing you the best,
wishing you happiness, nothing less,
life of joy, so you'll be blessed.
I'm leaving now, I must go,
loved you, that you should know,
but theres nothing left,
today, becamed our last breath.
Meeting you, I don't regret,
wished I could've save us,
saved us from my large fishing net,
were through, why put up a big fuss?
