Thy secret joy, hides within the soul,
this precious joy, dangles as if from a long pole.
What's the secret,
is the secret hiding in the stars?
Or Perhaps it traveled farther, up to mars.
I bare my soul, from head to toe,
where is thou secret, I think i'll never know,
the fruits await for me to eat,
sweet taste of euphoric bliss, I long to greet.

~CWMA search within yourself, an exploration. ~CWM
