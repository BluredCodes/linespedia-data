Who am I to you?
Am I your friend,
or your fool?
Don't look surprised,
don't look stunned,
let me know, who I am,
and we all can start to have some fun!

Hey don't throw my hat,
on unto the ground like that,
treat me how I treat you, treat me with respect.
I see, you didn't call me last night,
you don't even recognize me, am I in your sight?
Hello, why won't you answer me?
Come on, wake up to reality...
because I standing here, without a clue,
this question is long passed due,
tell me, who am I to you?

~CWMNot my best effort,  but I think I got the mesage acrossed.
~CWM
