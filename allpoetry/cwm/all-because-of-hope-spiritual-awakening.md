Sometimes, everyone wants to cry,
some, so stressed, even want to die,
but a phenomenon, still exist,
even when i'm shackled and depressed,
I want to carry on, all because of hope.

Unemployed, no money, not a single dime,
life seems, (at worse) a wasted time.
But a phenomenon, still exist,
even when i'm shackled and depressed,
I want to carry on, all because of hope.

"What's next?" Is the quetion, of plight,
too much to bear, let me die tonight,
but a phenomenon still exist,
even when i'm shackled and depressed,
I want to carry on, all because of hope.

I'll lay my burdens down,
and toss my deepest sentinments, unto the ground.
I'm free, free from bane,
free, no longer, am I madly insane
don't know if this is a spiritual awakening,
but the phenomenon is here, it must be taken.

Maybe, this is a blessing,
something in life, i've been missing,
can I now believe in Gods angels,
can I now believe in God?

Hope is where I stand,
lead me, guide me, hold my hand,
lets walk together, and have hopes,
and our everyday plans.
