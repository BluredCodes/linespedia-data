You don't love me anymore,
I know this,
because that loving stare,
was not the look, that I saw in your eyes.
You don't love me anymore,
I feel this,
because those three words,
I used to hear, are not said anymore.

You don't love me anymore,
I saw this,
when I over-heard you talking,
about another man's arms, his kisses, his eyes.
You don't love me anymore,
I understand this,
because of how you treat me,
anger, frustration, hate is given, now to me.

Now, I don't love you anymore,
Listen to this,
you continued to forsake my love,
because of you fooling me, and yourself...
tears, no longer fall,
my heart no longer lingers for you,
i've moved on, and live without burden's.
You don't love me anymore, truthfully, I no longer care.
