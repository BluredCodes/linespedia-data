My love for you,
is still growing,
it started, the first time,
I saw you.
I always knew, we would be friends,
but it was more, love for me to send.
Don't care about the past, or before,
my only concern, is to love you more and more.
To me you're so precious,
but not only in only in my eyes,
others have seen you,
throught a diffrent light, a disguise.
I'm glad, it was me,
who you met first
i'm glad it was you,
that I met first.
Times when we are apart,
I feel vaule, missing from my heart...
a voice, I no longer hear,
is missing from my wanting ears.
I wished, I could erase, the distractions
ignoring the bad, changing my reactions.
Baby, in all I do, I can't help,
only to thank about you.
You know, from good to bad,
happy or sad,
deep down, together, that makes me glad.
Gosh! I never felt love like this,
never truely loved, when I kissed,
for when you leave, you'll be sorely missed.
Love you, so much, too much,
as my tears of joy, and sadness start to combine.
