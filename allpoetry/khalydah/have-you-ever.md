Have you ever been in love,
So much it pauses time?
Do they take your breath away?
Do they keep your heart in chime?

Have you ever had someone,
Turn your life around?
They always help you up when,
You're running into the ground?

Have you ever told them that,
They're the best thing in your life?
Or were you too afraid,
To look deeply in their eyes?

Have you ever dreamt at night,
About them being by your side?
And you wanted it so badly,
That you broke down and cried?

Have you ever reached out,
Wishing they were there?
But realised your mistake,
Just wanting them to care?

Have you ever needed them,
So much it hurts your heart?
Has it ever made you feel,
That it hurts to be apart?
